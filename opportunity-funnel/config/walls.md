# Walls

A wall is a point where a person with a perfect AI in their pocket still fails to reach the outcome.
Always cite walls by ID (`W1`…`W29`). The scripts check the IDs against this file.

## Machine walls (melt): W1–W16

Their own AI could close these as it improves.

### Before the model
- **W1 Trigger:** nobody asks, or the moment passes.
- **W2 Diagnosis:** they ask the wrong question.
- **W3 Access:** they can't use a chat box (language, voice, literacy, payment).
- **W4 Context:** the model lacks the inputs (hard-to-assemble records, live data).

### Inside the model
- **W5 Frames:** it gives the average view, not the right one.
- **W6 Policy:** it hedges or refuses on legitimate specifics.
- **W7 Quality:** right most of the time; the job needs right every time.
- **W8 Capacity:** the job needs the expensive tier, or thousands of runs at once.

### Around the answer
- **W9 Effort:** too many steps; nobody does the work.
- **W10 Form:** the answer isn't in a usable shape or inside their workflow.
- **W11 Amortization:** only worth building across thousands of buyers.

### After the answer
- **W12 Action:** it must become a deed in someone else's system (file, chase, call).
- **W13 Habit:** they won't keep doing it.
- **W14 Practice:** knowing doesn't become ability.
- **W15 Record:** the value only exists over time.

### Across everything
- **W16 Compliance:** their organization isn't allowed to use or buy it.

## Human walls (hold): W17–W29

They need something a model can't be.

- **W17 Risk transfer:** someone who pays if it's wrong (guarantee, liability, insurance).
- **W18 License:** only a licensed person or firm may deliver or sign it.
- **W19 Judgment:** someone whose pick you borrow and who owns the call.
- **W20 Accountability:** someone who watches, corrects, and whom you'd hate to let down.
- **W21 Advocacy:** someone who fights your battle and answers for it.
- **W22 Atoms:** physical work; the labour is the product.
- **W23 Private data:** outcomes across many customers that no one else can see.
- **W24 Network:** value that exists only when people are brought together.
- **W25 Peers:** people going through it with you.
- **W26 Facilitation:** a neutral outsider who helps several people agree.
- **W27 Ritual:** the ceremony of receiving is the product; it needs a credible officiant.
- **W28 Witness:** being seen by someone who knows you.
- **W29 Source:** they, or someone they must satisfy, want it from a person or a respected brand.

## Natural pairs (entry → hold)

| Entry (machine) | Hold (human) |
|---|---|
| W7 Quality | W17 Risk transfer |
| W13 Habit | W20 Accountability |
| W14 Practice | W25 Peers or W20 Accountability |
| W12 Action | W21 Advocacy |
| W4 Context / W15 Record | W23 Private data |
| W6 Policy | W18 License |
| W5 Frames | W27 Ritual or W29 Source |
| W2 Diagnosis | W19 Judgment |

These are common fits, not rules. A pair needs 3 or more machine walls next to each other on the path, plus one human wall next to them.
