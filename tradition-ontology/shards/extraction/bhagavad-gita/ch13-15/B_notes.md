# Bhagavad Gītā ch. 13–15 — extractor B: the six marks of purport
_(Returned as text by the subagent — subagents cannot write Markdown files in this harness — and saved verbatim by the orchestrator.)_


Refs use this edition's numbering. In ch. 13 this runs one higher than the 700-verse numbering from 13.2 on; 13.1 is the extra verse.

## 1. Opening and closing (upakrama–upasaṃhāra)
- **Ch. 13**
  - Opens with Arjuna's six topics (13.1: prakṛti and puruṣa, field and knower, knowledge and the knowable) and with Kṛṣṇa's definition: the body is the field, its knower is the kṣetrajña, and the Lord is the knower in all fields (13.2–13.3).
  - Closes on the same pair: those who know the difference of field and knower with the eye of knowledge go to the highest (13.35).
  - The chapter's aim is framed by the field–knower distinction: "knowledge of field and knower is knowledge" (13.3).
- **Ch. 14**
  - Opens with "the highest knowledge, best of knowledges", by which sages reached the highest perfection (14.1–14.2).
  - Closes with the way beyond the guṇas, unswerving bhakti-yoga (14.26), and "I am the foundation of brahman" (14.27).
- **Ch. 15**
  - Opens with the aśvattha that is to be cut (15.1–15.3).
  - Closes with knowing the Lord as puruṣottama and worshipping him with one's whole being (15.19). It then calls itself "the most secret teaching" (15.20).
- **The chunk as a whole** moves from the body as field (13.2) to the highest person beyond the perishable and the imperishable (15.18).

## 2. Repetition (abhyāsa)
- **Seeing rightly**
  - "yaḥ paśyati sa paśyati" (13.28, 13.30).
  - Other verbs of seeing: 13.25, 13.29, 13.31 (anupaśyati), 14.19 (anupaśyati), 15.10–15.11 (paśyanti / na paśyanti).
- **Non-agency**
  - Prakṛti is the cause of agency (13.21).
  - Actions are done by prakṛti alone (13.30).
  - The supreme self neither acts nor is stained (13.32–13.33).
  - There is no doer but the guṇas (14.19); "the guṇas act" (14.23).
- **The guṇas**
  - They are born of prakṛti (13.20, 13.22, 14.5).
  - They bind (13.22, 14.5–14.9).
  - They are to be gone beyond (14.20–14.26).
- **Knowledge leading beyond birth and death**
  - amṛtam aśnute (13.13, 14.20).
  - na bhūyo 'bhijāyate (13.24).
  - na nivartante (15.4, 15.6).
- **The Lord's state and indwelling**
  - madbhāva (13.19, 14.19).
  - The Lord / the knowable in the heart of all (13.18, 15.15).
- **Unswerving devotion**
  - bhakti avyabhicāriṇī (13.11).
  - avyabhicāreṇa bhakti-yogena (14.26).
  - bhajati sarvabhāvena (15.19).
- **Lists of evenness**
  - 13.10 and 14.24–14.25, echoing 12.13–12.19.

## 3. Novelty (apūrvatā)
- The field and its knower as a named pair, with an enumerated field (13.6–13.7). The list matches the twenty-four principles, though the verse gives no number.
- Twenty dispositions and practices, including devotion and attendance on the teacher, are themselves called "knowledge" (13.8–13.12).
- The knowable brahman is described through paradoxes (13.13–13.18), including "neither sat nor asat" (13.13).
- A full account of the guṇas:
  - their modes of binding (14.6–14.9);
  - their mutual overpowering (14.10);
  - their signs (14.11–14.13);
  - their destinies at death (14.14–14.18);
  - the marks and conduct of the guṇātīta (14.22–14.25).
- The inverted aśvattha, whose leaves are the Vedic metres and whose roots also stretch downward, binding through action (15.1–15.3).
- The Lord's eternal portion as the living being that carries the senses from body to body (15.7–15.9).
- Three puruṣas — kṣara, akṣara, and the highest person beyond both (15.16–15.18) — with the name puruṣottama said to be celebrated "in the world and in the Veda".
- The Lord's own claims:
  - knower in all fields (13.3);
  - seed-giving father (14.4);
  - foundation of brahman (14.27);
  - known by all the Vedas and maker of the Vedānta (15.15).

## 4. Stated result (phala)
- **Immortality:** 13.13, 14.20.
- **No further birth or return:**
  - not born again (13.24);
  - not born at creation nor troubled at dissolution (14.2);
  - the place and supreme abode of no return (15.4–15.6).
- **Beyond death / the highest goal:**
  - crossing beyond death (13.26);
  - the highest goal (13.29);
  - going to the highest (13.35).
- **Brahman:** reaching brahman (13.31); fitness for becoming brahman (14.26).
- **The Lord's state:**
  - madbhāva (13.19, 14.19);
  - the highest perfection and sameness of nature with the Lord (14.1–14.2).
- **Knowledge completed:**
  - "knowing all" and worshipping (15.19);
  - being possessed of understanding and having done what is to be done (15.20).

## 5. Praise (arthavāda)
- "jñānānāṃ jñānam uttamam" (14.1).
- "guhyatamaṃ śāstram" (15.20).
- "whoever knows it is a knower of the Veda" (15.1).
- "sa sarvavit" (15.19).
- "sarvathā vartamāno 'pi" (13.24).
- Even those who only worship after hearing from others "do cross death" (13.26).
- The Lord's self-description as the radiance of sun, moon and fire, sustainer of beings, soma and vaiśvānara (15.12–15.14).
- Candidates for P7 reading: 13.24 and 13.26 (praise of knowledge and of hearing).

## 6. Reasoned demonstration (upapatti)
- **Explicit reasons**
  - "anāditvān nirguṇatvāt": the supreme self neither acts nor is stained (13.32).
  - "sūkṣmatvāt tad avijñeyam" (13.16).
  - "yasmāt kṣaram atīto 'ham … ato 'smi … puruṣottamaḥ" (15.18).
- **Causal analysis**
  - Prakṛti is the cause of agency, puruṣa the cause of experiencing (13.21).
  - Attachment to the guṇas is the cause of births (13.22).
  - Every being arises from the union of field and knower (13.27).
  - The guṇas prevail by overpowering one another (14.10).
- **Inference from signs:** "vidyāt" (14.11–14.13).
- **Similes**
  - Space is unstained because of its subtlety (13.33).
  - One sun lights the whole world (13.34).
  - The wind carries scents (15.8).
  - The tree (15.1–15.3).
- **The teaching's own claim to reasons:** "hetumadbhir viniścitaiḥ" (13.5).

## Contribution to the text-level thesis (for the merger of the last chunk)
- In chs. 13–15 the means is knowledge that discriminates field from knower and sees the guṇas / prakṛti as the only agents (13.30, 14.19).
- This knowledge is joined to unswerving devotion:
  - devotion is counted inside "knowledge" (13.11);
  - bhakti-yoga is the answer to "how does one pass beyond the guṇas" (14.26);
  - knowing the Lord as puruṣottama issues in worship with one's whole being (15.19).
- The result is going beyond the guṇas to immortality or the Lord's state, and the place of no return.
- The Lord is presented as knower in all fields, foundation of brahman and puruṣottama.
- 13.25–13.26 set meditation, sāṃkhya, the yoga of action and hearing-and-worship side by side without ranking them (relevant to dsp:gita-primary-teaching).
