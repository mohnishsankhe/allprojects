## 2. Corrections to the MUST-COVER list
- The list is essentially accurate. Refinements:
  - **Limbs of surrender.** The Ahirbudhnya Saṃhitā verse lists a six-fold śaraṇāgati. Deśika counts five limbs (aṅga) with ātma-nikṣepa as the principal (aṅgin). The verse's wording is "rakṣiṣyatīti viśvāsaḥ"; "mahāviśvāsa" is Deśika's term.
  - **"Brahman as saguṇa."** The tradition reads Brahman as both "nirguṇa" (free of prākṛta, defiling qualities) and "saguṇa" (auspicious qualities). This is the ubhaya-liṅga, and it is recorded as such.
  - **Śrī as jīva-koṭi.** This is the common report of the Teṅkalai position. Some Teṅkalai authors reject the label, and that objection is noted.
  - **Monkey and cat.** The maxims are labels from the dispute literature, not arguments that Deśika or Piḷḷai Lokācārya make in those terms.
  - **Nāthamuni's works.** A third lost work, the Puruṣanirṇaya, is added.
  - **Vaṭakku Tiruvīti Piḷḷai's work is the Īṭu 36000**, his record of Nampiḷḷai's lectures.
