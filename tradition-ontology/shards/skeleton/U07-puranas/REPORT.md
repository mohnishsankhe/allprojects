### Corrections to the orchestrator's MUST-COVER list (checked in the e-texts)
- **Devī Māhātmya "700 verses".** 700 is the tradition's count of mantras, which includes speaker-lines, half-verses and refrains. The e-text has 588 numbered verses. Its 13 chapters are MkP 81–93 in this numbering (78–90 in other editions). Its sage is Medhas (MkP 81.9).
- **Mahākālī, Mahālakṣmī and Mahāsarasvatī** are named for the three episodes only in the dhyāna verses and the Rahasyas, not in the 13 chapters.
- **The Lalitā Sahasranāma** is not in the printed Lalitopākhyāna (Brahmāṇḍa Uttarabhāga chs. 5–44); it only claims that origin.
- **The Garuḍa's death section** is the Dharmakāṇḍa/Pretakalpa: 49 chapters in the Venkateshwara edition, 35 in others. The 16-chapter Sāroddhāra is what is recited at mourning.
- **Madālasā.** MkP 25.11 reads "śuddho 'si re tāta, na te 'sti nāma". The popular "śuddho 'si buddho 'si nirañjano 'si" was not found in this edition.
- **The twenty-four avatāras.** BhP 1.3 numbers 22 and 2.7 adds more. The count of 24 is later tradition, not a single verse.
- **Śiva or Vāyu.** Matsya 53.18 and Nārada 1.95 list the Vāyu, not the Śiva. The Kūrma names both and equates the Vāyavīya with the Brahmāṇḍa.
- **Guṇa classifications.** The texts give several schemes that disagree: the Matsya by the deity glorified, the Padma with six per guṇa, the Garuḍa Brahmakāṇḍa as a graded list. There is no single Vaiṣṇava/Śaiva/Brāhma division in the texts themselves.
- **The Garuḍa's account of dying.** GP 2.2 and 2.5 reuse MkP 10.46-78 verse for verse (recorded as a borrowing).
- **The length of the road to Yama.** It is 12 days in the Mārkaṇḍeya, a year in the Garuḍa, and 2–3 muhūrtas in BhP 3.30.24. Queued as dsp:duration-of-the-road-to-yama.
- **Numbering differences.** The VP samādhi verse is 6.7.92 in the edition checked (often cited as 6.7.90). BhP 10.33 runs one verse higher here.
