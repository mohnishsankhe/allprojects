## Corrections to the MUST-COVER list (as written from memory)
- "The Great Explanation of Chöd" is recorded as *phung po gzan skyur ba'i rnam bshad* (Harding's "Complete Explanation"). Machig's root text is a separate work, *bka' tshoms chen mo* (`src:katsom-chenmo`).
- Shākya Chokden was a Sakya scholar, not a Jonang follower. He held both rangtong and zhentong to be valid Madhyamaka. He is recorded as a reference only.
- Chöd is not institutionally a branch of Shije. The link is the "father lineage" through Padampa, and the two are paired only in the eight-chariots scheme.
- Four Tantras chapter counts are 6 + 31 + 92 + 27 (25 + 2 concluding) = 156.
- U30 already owns `brw:ayurveda-to-sowa-rigpa`. It was contributed to under the same id, not duplicated.

## Decisions
- `lin:sowa-rigpa` is family "shared": Buddhist in frame, Āyurvedic in much of its content, and also used by Bön physicians.
- `tch:buton` is filed under lin:sakya and lin:kalacakra, because there is no Zhalu lineage id.
- Bloodletting is `trm:targa`, not `trm:tarka`, which would collide with the Sanskrit *tarka* (reasoning) used by several units.
- Contribution entries (same id, name and category, this unit's definitions only) were written for shared ids owned elsewhere. The shared ids concerned:
