**How references are written**
- Mānas refs are kāṇḍa.dohā in the Gita Press numbering (e.g. `tea:ramcaritmanas:7.121-122`). Caupāī positions are approximate.
- Abhaṅgas and padas use incipit slugs, with the incipit in `location.ref` (e.g. `tea:tukaram-gatha:je-ka-ranjale-ganjale`).
- Where a verse number is not known, a chapter-level or descriptive ref is used and flagged in `notes`.

### Corrections and clarifications to the MUST-COVER list
1. **Three different "Bhakti" anthologies.** The Bhakti-ratnākara is Śaṅkaradeva's *Sanskrit* anthology. It is distinct from Mādhavadeva's Assamese Bhakti-ratnāvalī, which renders Viṣṇupurī's Sanskrit Bhakti-ratnāvalī. It is also distinct from Narahari Cakravartī's Bengali Gauḍīya Bhaktiratnākara. All three now have separate ids.
2. **The book on the guru-āsana** is the Bhāgavata in many sattras, but often the Kīrtana-ghoṣā or Nām-ghoṣā. Both are recorded.
3. **Dāmodaradeva** is claimed by the Mahāpuruṣīyā account as Śaṅkaradeva's disciple, while his own Brahma saṃhati stresses his independence. This is recorded as a dispute.
4. **Hanumān Cālīsā:** the attribution to Tulsīdās is traditional only. It is not among the twelve works accepted in early lists.
5. **Mahānubhāva and the other gods:** they do not deny that the devatās exist. They deny that the devatās can liberate, and forbid worshipping them.
6. **"Caṇḍīdās" is several poets** (Baḍu, Dvija, Dīna). The Śrīkṛṣṇakīrtana is known from a single manuscript found in 1909.
7. **Two different Lakṣmaṇa passages in the Mānas.** Rāma's teaching to Lakṣmaṇa at Pañcavaṭī (Araṇya 14–16; maiṃ aru mora…) is a different passage from U06's src:laksmana-gita (Ayodhyā 92–93, Lakṣmaṇa to Guha). It is recorded here under src:ramcaritmanas.
8. **Added items not on the list:** Muktābāī's Tāṭīce abhaṅga, Narahari Sonār, Kānhopātrā, Bhadrācala Rāmadās, and the Pāṇḍuraṅga-māhātmya (Puṇḍalīka).
