# Gaps — what is out of reach

Updated as the run proceeds. Three kinds of gap: (1) never written down, (2) written but not digitized or not reachable,
(3) digitized but under a licence that forbids storing it here.

## 1. Oral and initiatory teachings never written down
- Initiatory instructions of every tantric lineage (Śaiva, Śākta, Vaiṣṇava, Buddhist, Jain) that are transmitted only orally
  from teacher to initiate (e.g. the oral instructions, "upadeśa", "man ngag", that accompany dīkṣā/abhiṣeka).
- The oral lineages of the ascetic orders (Daśanāmī, Nāgā akhāṛās, Bairāgī, Udāsīn, Nāth, Aghora) beyond what ethnographies
  and the orders' own printed texts record.
- Village and regional goddess traditions, folk Nāth and Siddha lineages, and regional saint cults, except where documented in writing.

## 2. Not digitized, or not reachable from this environment
- The very large body of undigitized manuscripts (Sanskrit, Prakrit, Pali, Tamil, Tibetan and vernacular) in Indian,
  Nepalese, Tibetan and European collections.
- This run's container cannot reach GRETIL, SuttaCentral.net, archive.org, sacred-texts, Wikisource, Muktabodha, BDRC,
  84000, CBETA Online or Wikipedia directly (egress policy). Texts that exist only on those sites are listed here as the
  sweep finds them; texts mirrored on GitHub are used.

## 3. Licence-restricted
- 84000 translations (readable, but not openly licensed): references and our own paraphrase only.
- Modern copyrighted translations (e.g. Jaideva Singh, Bhikkhu Bodhi, Garfield, Conze): references only.
