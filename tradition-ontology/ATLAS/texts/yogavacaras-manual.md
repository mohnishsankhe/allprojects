A Sinhala-Pali manuscript manual of the old (borān) meditation: preliminary rites, the five kinds of rapture, the tranquillities and other qualities to be induced in order at points of the body, and the path attainments.

### intro <a id="tea-yogavacaras-manual-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The pupil prepares by taking refuge, making offerings and formally requesting the meditation subject from the teacher; then the five kinds of rapture are induced in order, each fixed at a particular place in the body.
