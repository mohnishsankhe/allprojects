## Teachings (6: skeleton 6)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-bodhisattvabhumi-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The lineage (gotra) is of two kinds: naturally present — a distinctive feature of the six sense-bases of bodhisattvas, transmitted from beginningless time and obtained by the nature of things — and developed, obtained by the prior cultivation of roots of good; it is also called seed, element and nature.

> samāsato gotraṃ dvividham | prakṛtisthaṃ samudānītañca

### 1.2 <a id="tea-bodhisattvabhumi-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The first arising of the mind of awakening is the bodhisattva's first right vow, embracing all other right vows; it has two objects — awakening and the welfare of beings — and is the foundation of the whole bodhisattva career.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: beginner · types: ethics, practice_

### 1.4 <a id="tea-bodhisattvabhumi-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Reality (tattvārtha) is fourfold: what is established by worldly convention, what is established by reasoning, the domain of knowledge purified of the afflictive obscuration, and the domain of knowledge purified of the cognitive obscuration — the supreme suchness, reached by knowledge equal to the knowable, free of conceptualization, with respect to the inexpressible nature of all dharmas.

> lokaprasiddho yuktiprasiddhaḥ kleśāvaraṇaviśuddhijñānagocaro jñeyāvaraṇaviśuddhijñānagocaraśca

### 1.8 <a id="tea-bodhisattvabhumi-1-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The bodhisattva seeks the five sciences — the inner science (the Buddha's word), the science of reasons, the science of language, medicine and the crafts; he studies the science of reasons in order to know exactly what is badly argued in (other) treatises, to refute others' doctrines, to bring faith to those without it and increase it in those who have it.

> adhyātmavidyā hetuvidyā śabdavidyā vyādhicikitsāvidyā śilpakarmasthānavidyā ca | itīmāni pañca vidyāsthānāni yāni bodhisattvaḥ paryeṣate

### 1.10 <a id="tea-bodhisattvabhumi-1-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The bodhisattva's discipline is threefold — the discipline of restraint, the discipline of gathering wholesome dharmas, and the discipline of acting for the welfare of beings; for one established in the vow there are four offences equivalent to defeat: praising oneself and disparaging others out of attachment to gain and honour; not giving wealth or the Dharma to the suffering out of avarice; not accepting another's apology in anger; and abandoning the bodhisattva canon while promoting a counterfeit dharma.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: action · stage: all · types: ethics_

### 2.4 <a id="tea-bodhisattvabhumi-2-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The bodhisattva's twelve abodes (vihāra) — of the lineage, of practice by resolve, of joy, of higher discipline, of higher mind, the three of higher wisdom, and the abodes connected with the factors of awakening, with the truths, with dependent origination, and the signless — culminate in a thirteenth, the unsurpassed abode of the Tathāgata.
