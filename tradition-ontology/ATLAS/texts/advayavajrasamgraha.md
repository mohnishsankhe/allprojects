## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2.mulapatti <a id="tea-advayavajrasamgraha-2-mulapatti"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The fourteen root downfalls of the mantra practitioner: despising the teachers; transgressing the Sugata's command; (anger toward vajra kin); abandoning love; abandoning bodhicitta; reviling the vehicles; revealing the secret to the unripe; abusing the aggregates (which are the Jinas); doubting the pure dharma; (misplaced) love for the wicked; superimposing (concepts) on non-dual dharmas; corrupting the minds of the faithful; not relying on the pledge substances when obtained; and disparaging women, who are of the nature of wisdom. Committing them, there are no attainments, Māra and a mass of suffering, and one experiences the intense pains of hell.

> ācāryeṣvavamānena sugatājñāvilaṅghane | heyāt gotraguṇāsthāne mahāmaitrovivarjane || bodhicittaparityāge yānacitayanindane | guhyākhyāne jane pakṣe jinātaskandhadūṣaṇe ||
