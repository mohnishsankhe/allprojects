A Mysore court encyclopaedia of deities and sacred lore whose illustrated section on postures (drawing on the Haṭhābhyāsapaddhati) preserves over a hundred āsanas, many of them dynamic.

_Notes: Posture count and dependence on the Haṭhābhyāsapaddhati recalled (N. E. Sjoman; J. Birch)._
