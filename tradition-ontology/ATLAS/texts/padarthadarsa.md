## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### on ŚT 16 (commentary) <a id="tea-padarthadarsa-16-japa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Rāghavabhaṭṭa quotes the Vāyavīya Saṃhitā: japa is the repetition of syllables and is loud, whispered or mental. Loud (vācika) japa utters the mantra with high, low and circumflex tones and clear words and syllables; whispered (upāṃśu) japa utters it slowly, the lips moving slightly, barely audible; mental (mānasa) japa recites with the intellect the series of letters, notes and words while remembering the meaning. Loud japa is ten times better than ritual sacrifice, whispered a hundred times, mental a thousand times. Elsewhere: in mental japa one should not move head, neck, tongue or lips.
