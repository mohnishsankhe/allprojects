# Kālasaṅkarṣiṇīmata

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Krama (the 'Sequence'; Mahānaya, Mahārtha)](../lineages/krama.md), [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md)
**Genre:** tantra (Kālīkula)
**Availability:** manuscript-only

A Kālīkula tantra related to the Jayadrathayāmala, said in its colophons to extend to 24,000 verses; known in Kashmir-related tradition through fragments and a commentary (ṭippaṇī) concerned mostly with the Kaula 'primal sacrifice' (ādiyāga).
