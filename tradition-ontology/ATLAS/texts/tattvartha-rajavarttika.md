## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-tattvartha-rajavarttika-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Knowledge without action is lost and action of the ignorant is lost: the blind man burns while running, the lame man while seeing — so view, knowledge and conduct must go together.

> hataṃ jñānaṃ kriyāhīnaṃ hatā cājñānināṃ kriyā / dhāvan kilāndhako dagdhaḥ paśyann api ca paṅgulaḥ

### 1.6 <a id="tea-tattvartha-rajavarttika-1-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

A statement that presents the whole thing through one property taken as representing all (sakalādeśa) belongs to valid knowledge; one that presents a single aspect (vikalādeśa) belongs to a standpoint; both are expressed in seven predications.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: intermediate · types: sound-language, consciousness-mind_
