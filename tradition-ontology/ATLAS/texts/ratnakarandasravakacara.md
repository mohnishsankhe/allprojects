The classic Digambara manual of lay conduct (c. 150 verses): right view with its eight limbs, free from three follies and eight kinds of pride; right knowledge (the four anuyogas); the lay vows (five aṇuvratas, three guṇavratas, four śikṣāvratas), the eight basic qualities (mūlaguṇa), sallekhanā with its conditions and faults, and the eleven pratimās.
