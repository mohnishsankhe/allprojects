# Gopatha Brāhmaṇa

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Genre:** brāhmaṇa
**Dates:** Scholarly account: late; regarded as later than the major Brāhmaṇas, parts perhaps post-Pāṇinian; (confidence low)
**Structure:** Pūrva-bhāga (5 prapāṭhakas) and Uttara-bhāga (6 prapāṭhakas)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The only surviving Brāhmaṇa of the Atharvaveda. It opens with Brahman, the self-existent, alone in the beginning, who creates a second god from himself (1.1.1), tells of the origin of Atharvan and the Aṅgirases and of the Atharvaveda as the Veda of the brahman priest, discusses Oṃ at length, gives rules for the Vedic student, and in its later part explains Śrauta rites, often in words shared with other Brāhmaṇas.
