Atiśa's summary of a bodhisattva's daily round according to the perfection vehicle: vows, moderation in food, guarding the senses, waking in the last part of the night, recollecting dharmatā, seeing appearances as illusion, offering food in four portions, the ten dharma practices, and resting in the elaboration-free at night.

### v10-13 <a id="tea-caryasamgrahapradipa-v10-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

At mealtime, thinking 'with this essenceless body I shall seek the essence', regard the body as a boat; eat not to fatten it nor from attachment to taste. Divide the food in four: first offer the pure portion to the deities, then give a vast torma to the dharma protectors; give what remains after eating and drinking to all spirits.

### v18-19 <a id="tea-caryasamgrahapradipa-v18-19"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

When the day's work is complete, in the first part of the night rest the mind, free of elaboration, in dharmatā free of elaboration; when midnight comes, with the notion of rising and of light, lie down as a lion lies and fall into virtuous sleep.

### v20-22 <a id="tea-caryasamgrahapradipa-v20-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · [AI-translated]

In general, when meditative equipoise is stable, do not make physical and verbal virtue the main thing; if equipoise is not clear, do what bodily and verbal virtue you can, to accord with the world. If the world's mind is not in accord, put first the good intention that 'this is not my dharma', and ask about the customs of the land.

### v4-8 <a id="tea-caryasamgrahapradipa-v4-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

Preceded by generating the mind, take the bodhisattva vow; look at all the sūtras and listen to all the treatises. Keep the vows of body, speech and mind unbroken and clean, purifying ethics. Know the measure in food, restrain the senses, do not sleep in the first and last parts of the night but strive in yoga; be very afraid of even the slightest wrongdoing. Divide the night in three and rise in the last part; washing the face or not, sit on a comfortable seat and recollect the dharmatā of phenomena.
