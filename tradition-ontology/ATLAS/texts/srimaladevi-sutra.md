Queen Śrīmālā, with the Buddha's approval, roars the lion's roar: her ten vows and three great aspirations; embracing the true dharma; the one vehicle into which the three vehicles merge; the arhat's nirvāṇa is not final; the tathāgatagarbha is the dharmakāya obscured by defilements; the two kinds of emptiness-knowledge of the garbha (empty of defilements, not empty of buddha-qualities); the dharmakāya's perfections of permanence, bliss, self and purity; saṃsāra and nirvāṇa depend on the garbha; the innately pure mind defiled is hard to understand.

## Teachings (11: skeleton 11)

### 1 <a id="tea-srimaladevi-sutra-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Śrīmālā praises the Tathāgata's true body, beyond compare and inconceivable; the Buddha predicts that she will become the buddha Samantaprabha in a land without evil destinies.

### 2 <a id="tea-srimaladevi-sutra-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Śrīmālā's ten great vows (ten acceptances): to keep the precepts, not to be proud toward elders, not to be angry, not to envy, not to be stingy, not to hoard for herself but to give to the poor, to gather beings by the four means of attraction, to rescue the suffering and lonely, to restrain those who break the precepts, and never to forget the true dharma.

### 3 <a id="tea-srimaladevi-sutra-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Three great aspirations: to gain knowledge of the true dharma in all lives, to teach it untiringly, and to protect it at the cost of body, life and wealth; all bodhisattva vows are contained in these.

### 4 <a id="tea-srimaladevi-sutra-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Embracing the true dharma is itself the perfections and the true dharma; to embrace it one gives up body, life and possessions; its merit is immeasurable.

### 5 <a id="tea-srimaladevi-sutra-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The śrāvaka and pratyekabuddha vehicles enter the great vehicle; the one vehicle is the buddha-vehicle. The arhats and pratyekabuddhas still have fear, a remainder of birth, and the ground of ignorance (avidyāvāsabhūmi) not yet removed; their nirvāṇa is a skillful means; only the Tathāgata attains final nirvāṇa.

### 7 <a id="tea-srimaladevi-sutra-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The tathāgatagarbha is the realm of the Tathāgata, not of śrāvakas or pratyekabuddhas; who has no doubt about the tathāgatagarbha wrapped in countless defilements has no doubt about the dharmakāya freed from them.

### 8 <a id="tea-srimaladevi-sutra-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The cessation of suffering is not the destruction of a dharma: it is the Tathāgata's dharmakāya, beginningless, uncreated, unborn, undying, permanent, stable, pure by nature; this dharmakāya not freed from defilements is called the tathāgatagarbha.

### 9 <a id="tea-srimaladevi-sutra-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There are two kinds of emptiness-knowledge of the tathāgatagarbha: the tathāgatagarbha empty of all the defilements that are separable, detached and different from it; and the tathāgatagarbha not empty of the buddha-qualities, more numerous than the Ganges' sands, that are inseparable, undetached and not different from it.

> 世尊！有二種如來藏空智。世尊！空如來藏，若離、若脫、若異一切煩惱藏。世尊！不空如來藏，過於恒沙不離、不脫、不異、不思議佛法。

### 10 <a id="tea-srimaladevi-sutra-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Of the four noble truths three are impermanent and conditioned; only the truth of cessation is permanent, the one truth and the one refuge, which is the Tathāgata.

### 12 <a id="tea-srimaladevi-sutra-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Beings with inverted views see the permanent as impermanent, the blissful as suffering, the self as no-self, the pure as impure; right view sees that the Tathāgata's dharmakāya is the perfection of permanence, bliss, self and purity; those who see thus are the Buddha's true children.

> 如來法身是常波羅蜜、樂

### 13 <a id="tea-srimaladevi-sutra-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Saṃsāra depends on the tathāgatagarbha, and so does nirvāṇa; the tathāgatagarbha is not a self, a being, a soul or a person; it is the self-nature pure mind, yet defiled by adventitious defilements — that the naturally pure mind is defiled is hard to understand, known only to the Buddha.

> 自性清淨心而有染者，難可了知
