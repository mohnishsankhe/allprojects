Hongzhi's verse on silent illumination: in silence words are forgotten and clarity appears; silence and illumination must not lose each other — if illumination loses silence, aggression appears; if silence loses illumination, there is murky, superfluous dharma; when they are complete, the lotus opens and the dream awakes.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 100a26 <a id="tea-mozhao-ming-100a26"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Silently, silently, words are forgotten; clearly, clearly it appears before you; when you mirror it, it is vast; where you embody it, it is spiritually alive; the numinous shines alone, and within the shining there is wonder.

> 默默忘言。 昭昭現前。 鑒時廓爾。 體處靈然

### 100b07 <a id="tea-mozhao-ming-100b07"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If within illumination silence is lost, aggression appears; if within silence illumination is lost, all becomes murky superfluous dharma; when silence and illumination are complete, the lotus opens and the dreamer awakes, the hundred streams flow to the sea.

> 照中失默。 便見侵凌。 證明問答。 相應恰恰。 默中失照。 渾成剩法
