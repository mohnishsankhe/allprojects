The Kerala manual of interrogation: the conduct and qualifications of the astrologer, omens at the time of the query, the aṣṭamaṅgala and other praśna methods, diagnosis of afflictions from curses (of gods, serpents, ancestors, teachers) and spirits, and the remedies for them.
