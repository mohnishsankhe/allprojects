The dispelling of king Ajātaśatru's remorse for killing his father: Mañjuśrī shows that the karmic obstruction is empty and the king obtains the acceptance of dharmas.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-ajatasatrukaukrtyavinodana-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

King Ajātaśatru's remorse for killing his father is dispelled by Mañjuśrī: the mind that did the deed and the remorse are empty and cannot be found; the king obtains faith and the acceptance of dharmas.
