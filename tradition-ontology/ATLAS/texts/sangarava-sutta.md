## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3-16 <a id="tea-sangarava-sutta-3-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When the mind is obsessed by sensual lust it is like water mixed with dye; by ill will like boiling water; by dullness and drowsiness like water covered with moss; by restlessness and remorse like water stirred by wind; by doubt like muddy water in the dark — one cannot see one's reflection or know one's own good; when the mind is free of the hindrances it is like clear still water.
