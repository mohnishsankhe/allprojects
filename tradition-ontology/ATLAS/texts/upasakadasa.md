The seventh Aṅga: ten chapters on ten wealthy lay disciples (upāsaka) of Mahāvīra — Ānanda, Kāmadeva, Culanīpitā, Surādeva, Cullaśataka, Kuṇḍakolika, Saddālaputra, Mahāśataka, Nandinīpitā and Sālihīpitā — who take the twelve lay vows, progress through the eleven lay stages (pratimā), withstand divine tests and die peacefully; the Saddālaputra chapter records Mahāvīra's argument against the Ājīvika fatalism.
