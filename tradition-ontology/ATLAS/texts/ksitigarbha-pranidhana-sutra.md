The sūtra of Kṣitigarbha's past vows, spoken in the Trāyastriṃśa heaven: in past lives as a brahmin girl and as a king he vowed to save beings from the hells and not to become a buddha until the hells are empty; it describes the hells, the merit of offerings for the dead during the forty-nine days, and the benefits of reciting his name.

### vows <a id="tea-ksitigarbha-pranidhana-sutra-vows"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Kṣitigarbha, in past lives as the brahmin girl whose mother was in hell and as a king, vowed to deliver all beings of the six destinies before becoming a buddha: 'only when the hells are empty will I become a buddha'; offerings and recitation for the dead during the forty-nine days help them.
