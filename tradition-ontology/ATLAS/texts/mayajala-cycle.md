# The Māyājāla (sgyu 'phrul drva ba) cycle of tantras

**Language:** Tibetan
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)
**Genre:** tantra cycle
**Part of:** [Nyingma Gyubum (rnying ma rgyud 'bum, the Collected Tantras of the Ancients)](nyingma-gyubum.md)
**Structure:** Local Derge Tōh 832-837 (vols. kha-ga of rnying rgyud); the 'eight' of tradition not enumerated here
**Availability:** digitized-original

The family of Mahāyoga tantras of the 'Magical Net' to which the Guhyagarbha belongs: in the Derge Old Tantra section the Guhyagarbha root (Tōh 832), the 'Mirror of All Secrets' (Tōh 833), a longer Guhyagarbha (Tōh 834), the 'Great Magical Net of the Goddess' (Tōh 836) and the 'Great Supreme Uttara' of the Guhyagarbha (Tōh 837). Tradition counts eight Māyājāla tantras (sgyu 'phrul sde brgyad).
