Nāropa's short treatise on the view: conventionally all phenomena are nothing but self-aware mind; ultimately that mind is beyond existence and non-existence; appearance and emptiness are inseparable like a moon in water; mind is luminous and non-conceptual wisdom obscured by adventitious stains; its many names (bodhicitta, sugatagarbha, great bliss, secret mantra, great Madhyamaka, the fourth empowerment…) all refer to it; a correct view makes meditation and conduct reach the fruit.

## Teachings (8: skeleton 8)

### 244a.6 <a id="tea-drstisamksipta-244a-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

All phenomena of appearance and existence are not other than self-aware mind, because they appear and are clear, like one's own experience of self-awareness; if they were not mind, being unrelated (to it) they would not appear. Thus the conventional is established: 'all phenomena abide in mind', it is said.

> snang zhing srid pa'i chos rnams kun/ /rang rig sems ni gud na med/ /snang zhing gsal ba yin pa'i phyir/ /dper na rang rig nyams myong bzhin/ /gal te sems de ma yin na/ /'brel pa med phyir mi snang thal/ /de ltar kun rdzob gtan la dbab/ /chos kun sems la gnas zhes gsungs/

### 244b.1 <a id="tea-drstisamksipta-244b-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The basis of phenomena is mind itself; examined by the four reasonings and the like, not even a sound-part of a thing (is found), like a sky-flower. Since existence is not established, not the least non-existence is established either, for they are mutually dependent, like this shore and the other. It is not both existence and non-existence, since each is refuted; not neither, since existence and non-existence directly exclude each other; not embodied, and not non-embodied either; therefore free of all elaborations. Thus the ultimate is established: 'mind abides in space', it is said.

> chos kyi dngos gzhi sems nyid de/ /rig pa bzhi sogs kyis dpyad kyang / /dngos po sgra yi cha tsam med/ /dper na nam mkha'i pad+ma bzhin/ /de ltar yod pa ma grub phyir/ /med pa cung zad grub mi 'gyur/ /phan tshun ltos pa nyid kyi phyir/ /tshu rol ma grub pha rol bzhin/ /yod med gnyis ka ma yin te/ /so so nyid du bkag phyir ro/ /gnyis ka med pa ma yin te/ /yod med dngos su 'gal ba'i phyir/ /lus can yang ni ma yin la/ /lus can min pa mi srid phyir/ /des na spros pa thams cad bral/ /de ltar don dam gtan la dbab/ /sems ni nam mkha' la gnas gsungs/

### 244b.3 <a id="tea-drstisamksipta-244b-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

That self-awareness free of elaboration appears and is empty, is empty and appears; therefore appearance and emptiness are inseparable, like the moon in water. Thus non-duality is established: 'space abides in nothing', it is said. That nature free of elaboration is clear because it is awareness, and non-conceptual because self-awareness cannot conceptualize itself — clear, non-conceptual wisdom, like the wisdom of the sugatas. Therefore, if one realizes mind itself as clear light and mind as wisdom, one does not seek the buddha elsewhere.

> rang rig spros bral de nyid kyang / /snang zhing stong la stong zhing snang / /de phyir snang stong dbyer med de/ /dper na chu yi zla ba bzhin/ /de ltar gnyis med gtan la dbab/ /nam mkha' ci la'ang mi gnas gsungs/ /rang bzhin spros bral de nyid ni/ /rig pa yin phyir gsal ba'o/ /rang rig min phyir mi rtog ste/ /rang rig rang la rtog mi srid/ /rnam par rtog bya min pa'i phyir/ /sems de'i rang bzhin mi rtog nyid/ /gsal ba mi rtog ye shes te/ /dper na bde gshegs ye shes bzhin/ /des na sems nyid 'od gsal la/ /sems ni ye shes yin rtogs na/ /sangs rgyas gzhan du mi btsal gsungs/

### 244b.5 <a id="tea-drstisamksipta-244b-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Yet that mind is afflicted by the adventitious stains of thought; like water, gold and space it has two conditions, pure and impure. Whether the naturally luminous mind and the adventitious stains of thought are one or different is extremely profound; because it is so profound the wise examine it — there is an explanation, but it is not written down.

### 244b.6 <a id="tea-drstisamksipta-244b-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

That emptiness, awareness, mind is also bodhicitta, the buddha-family, the sugatagarbha; tasting it as it is, it is great bliss; it is what is called secret mantra; it is method and wisdom, the profound and the vast, Samantabhadra father-mother; from the inner emptiness, the expanse and wisdom, it is called 'primordially awakened'. Self-awareness with stains, not depending on another, is self-arisen wisdom; it is the basis of saṃsāra and also nirvāṇa, the great Madhyamaka, what is to be seen, meditated and attained, the truth of valid cognition; it is known as the three tantras of cause, method and fruit and as ground, path and fruit; its situations are known as the causal root, the ālaya, the aggregates of saṃsāra, the dependent nature and so on.

### 245a.3 <a id="tea-drstisamksipta-245a-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

E ma ho! Mind itself with stains is the magical display of the six classes of beings, an inconceivable machinery of suffering as boundless as space. That self-awareness free of the stains of thought and of elaboration is non-abiding nirvāṇa; it is Vajrasattva, the sixth buddha, the sixth family, youthful Mañjuśrī, Vairocana; it is dharmakāya, great bliss and union; it is the fourth empowerment and co-emergent joy; it is natural purity — most of the synonyms known in sūtras and tantras rest on it and should be applied accordingly.

### 245a.6 <a id="tea-drstisamksipta-245a-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Deluded about their own mind, outer tīrthikas speak of a self, a primordial nature and the like and enter an ocean of wrong tenets; within our own schools, śrāvakas, pratyekabuddhas and mind-only followers grasp at the duality of apprehended and apprehender or take non-duality as truly real; others fall into the net of thought as 'true aspect' and 'false aspect' (proponents).

### 245b.1 <a id="tea-drstisamksipta-245b-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If the view is not mistaken, meditation and conduct accord with reality and awakening is attained, like a horse on a track that has been shown; if the view does not accord with reality, meditation and conduct go astray and the fruit is not attained as it is, like a blind man without a guide.
