### 884c25 <a id="tea-dahui-yulu-884c25"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Dahui: nowadays some teachers of heretical silent-illumination Chan, seeing officials troubled by worldly affairs, teach them to become cold ashes and dead wood, a strip of white silk, an incense burner in an old shrine — but while the 'monkey' is not dead, how can there be rest? When he first went to Fujian he opposed this style strongly, calling it cutting off the life of buddha-wisdom.

> 而今諸方有一般默照邪禪，見士大夫為塵勞所障方寸不寧，怗便教他寒灰枯木去

### 891a07 <a id="tea-dahui-yulu-891a07"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Those who merely learn words call Guizong's cutting the snake and Nanquan's cutting the cat 'wondrous function at the right moment'; they do not understand — without the eye that sees beyond, it is like divining with tiles and tortoise shells.

> 歸宗斬蛇，南泉斬猫兒，學語之流，多謂之當機妙用

### 891b05 <a id="tea-dahui-yulu-891b05"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Students of the Way should keep mind and consciousness calm at all times and sit quietly when unoccupied so that the mind does not scatter and the body does not stir — but to take quiet as the ultimate is to be captured by heretical silent-illumination Chan.

> 學道人，十二時中心意識常要寂靜，無事亦須靜坐，令心不放逸，身不動搖

### 903b29 <a id="tea-dahui-yulu-903b29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

While the feeling of doubt is not yet broken, just look at a critical phrase by which an ancient entered the Way; when deluded thoughts arise, just raise 'wu'; raising it again and again, suddenly the news is cut off — that is returning home and sitting at ease.

> 疑情未破，但只看箇古人入道底話頭

### 921c05 <a id="tea-dahui-yulu-921c05"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Letter to Fu Shumi: press down at once the deluded mind, the discriminating mind, the mind that loves life and hates death, the mind of views and understanding, the mind that likes quiet and dislikes noise, and right there look at the critical phrase: 'does a dog have buddha-nature?' — 'wu'. This one word is a weapon that smashes all wrong knowledge. Do not understand it as being or non-being, nor by reasoning, nor by deliberation, nor in raising the eyebrows, nor in words, nor sink into the shell of no-concern, nor accept it where it is raised, nor seek proof in texts; only at all times, in all postures, keep raising it.

> 但將妄想顛倒底心，思量分別底心，好生惡死底心，知見解會底心，欣靜厭鬧底心，一時按下，只就按下處看箇話頭
