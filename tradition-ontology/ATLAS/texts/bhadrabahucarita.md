## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### whole <a id="tea-bhadrabahucarita-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Bhadrabāhu interprets the sixteen dreams of King Candragupta as signs of a declining age, foresees the twelve-year famine, and leads twelve thousand monks south; Candragupta renounces, follows him and tends him at his death on the hill at Śravaṇabeḷagoḷa; the monks who remained in the north under Sthūlabhadra and others adopted the half-cloth and later the full white garment, becoming the Śvetāmbaras, from whom the Yāpanīyas later split.
