Twelve (or fourteen) verses ascribed to Hastāmalaka, answering Śaṅkara's question 'who are you?' by declaring himself the eternal consciousness that, like the sun, illumines mind and senses; a commentary is ascribed to Śaṅkara.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-hastamalakiya-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Asked who he is, the disciple answers: not a man, god or demon, not brāhmaṇa or other class, not a student, householder, forest-dweller or renunciant, but the self-knowing consciousness.
