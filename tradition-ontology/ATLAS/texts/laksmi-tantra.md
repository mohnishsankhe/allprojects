A 57-chapter Pāñcarātra text in which Śrī/Lakṣmī teaches Indra (Śakra), presenting herself as Nārāyaṇa's eternal Śakti who performs his five acts (12.12–14), whose body is the six guṇas (2.36), and who is the object of six-limbed surrender (17.56–62); it closes with the qualities required of one who receives it (57.51–54).

### 2.34-37 <a id="tea-laksmi-tantra-2-34-37"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Splendour (tejas) is called the sixth quality; some say it is the capacity to overcome others, others include it in lordship. These five qualities are pure outflowings of knowledge. These six qualities beginning with knowledge — the ṣāḍguṇya — are my body. From my wish to create there arises a minute part of me, by which the whole class of pure and impure is embraced; of these I shall explain the pure path, O lord of the gods.

### 12.12-14 <a id="tea-laksmi-tantra-12-12-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

I am Nārāyaṇa's power (śakti) — independent, the wondrous cause of all accomplishments, eternal, divine, ever arisen. Mine are five eternal acts, O lord of the gods: concealment (tirobhāva), creation, maintenance, dissolution and grace (anugraha) — this is called my fivefold work.

### 17.56-62 <a id="tea-laksmi-tantra-17-56-62"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Means and non-means laid down in the śāstra were made by me: the prescribed are means, the prohibited non-means; non-means lead down one who follows them, means lead up. One who gives up both means and non-means, keeps to the middle course and takes refuge in me alone, reaches me in the end. Hear that six-limbed means: the resolve to do what is agreeable, the avoidance of the disagreeable, the faith that 'he will protect', choosing (him) as protector, laying down oneself, and helplessness — the sixfold refuge. Having thus taken refuge in me, free of grief, fear and weariness, without undertakings or desires, without 'mine' and without ego, one crosses the ocean of saṃsāra.

### 57.51-54 <a id="tea-laksmi-tantra-57-51-54"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

One who does not revile the scriptures and knows the higher and lower rules, a believer with faith, dear to Lakṣmī and her Lord, who knows the divisions of rite and sacrifice and the rules of other tantras, who knows yoga with its limbs and the truth of all śāstras, the Veda and its auxiliaries and the doctrine of Paśupati, who knows reasoning, the means of knowledge, logic and the categories, and the truth of all spiritual śāstras, is fit to receive (this teaching).
