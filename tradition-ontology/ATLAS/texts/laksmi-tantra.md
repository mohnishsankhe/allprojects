# Lakṣmī Tantra

`src:laksmi-tantra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Lakṣmītantra, Pāñcarātrasāra (colophon)
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md)
**Genre:** saṃhitā (Pāñcarātra)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Bhagavān (Nārāyaṇa/Vāsudeva); one of the traditional 108 saṃhitās; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Scholarly account: later than the older saṃhitās; scholars have argued it draws on Kashmirian Śaiva works (c. 11th c. or later); (confidence low)
**Structure:** 57 adhyāyas
**Availability:** digitized-original

A 57-chapter Pāñcarātra text in which Śrī/Lakṣmī teaches Indra (Śakra), presenting herself as Nārāyaṇa's eternal Śakti who performs his five acts (12.12–14), whose body is the six guṇas (2.36), and who is the object of six-limbed surrender (17.56–62); it closes with the qualities required of one who receives it (57.51–54).
