### episode of Mādhavadeva's meeting <a id="tea-katha-gurucarit-madhavadeva-conversion"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Mādhavadeva, a worshipper of the Goddess, came to argue with Śaṅkaradeva over a goat vowed for sacrifice; after long debate Śaṅkaradeva cited the Bhāgavata — as watering the root of a tree nourishes trunk, branches and leaves, so worshipping Acyuta satisfies all the gods — and Mādhavadeva took refuge as his disciple.

### episodes at the Koch court <a id="tea-katha-gurucarit-brahmins-accuse"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Brahmins complained to the kings that Śaṅkaradeva preached a religion without rites or gods; summoned to the Koch court, he answered them from the Bhāgavata and King Naranārāyaṇa took his side.
