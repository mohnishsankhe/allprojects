## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 3-11 <a id="tea-mahasunnata-sutta-3-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A monk who delights in company cannot enter the liberation of mind; the Buddha dwells in emptiness internally by not attending to any signs; to do so one steadies the mind internally in the four jhānas and attends to emptiness internally, externally, and to imperturbability, and when walking, standing or speaking stays aware.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice_

terms: [suññatā](../terms/sunnata.md), [viveka](../terms/viveka.md) · concepts: [Emptiness in the suttas](../concepts/emptiness-early.md) · practices: [Dwelling in emptiness (suññatāvihāra)](../practices/sunnata-vihara.md)

### 27 <a id="tea-mahasunnata-sutta-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

I will not treat you as a potter treats raw damp clay; repeatedly restraining you I shall speak, repeatedly admonishing you; the sound core will stand the test.
