Record of Shenhui's public challenge at the Great Assembly of Huatai (732) to the Northern school: Shenxiu's line is collateral, Bodhidharma's robe passed to Huineng, and the Northern teaching of 'freezing the mind to enter samādhi, fixing the mind to view purity, stirring the mind to illumine outward, collecting the mind to realise inwardly' is gradual.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### huatai <a id="tea-nanzong-ding-shifei-lun-huatai"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

At the Huatai assembly Shenhui charged that Shenxiu's line was collateral, not the direct line — Bodhidharma's robe was with Huineng — and that its teaching was gradual: 'freezing the mind to enter samādhi, fixing the mind to view purity, activating the mind to illumine the external, concentrating the mind to realise internally'.
