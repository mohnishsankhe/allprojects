## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1 <a id="tea-mahavairocana-sutra-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Lord teaches that the gnosis of omniscience has bodhicitta as its cause, compassion as its root and skilful means as its culmination.

_level: bridging · standpoint: divine · path: knowledge, action · stage: all · types: practice, ethics_

concepts: [The three phrases of the Mahāvairocana](../concepts/three-phrases-mahavairocana.md)

### 1.mind <a id="tea-mahavairocana-sutra-1-mind"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

What is awakening? It is to know one's own mind as it truly is.

concepts: [The three phrases of the Mahāvairocana](../concepts/three-phrases-mahavairocana.md)

### 2 <a id="tea-mahavairocana-sutra-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Womb maṇḍala arisen from great compassion is taught, with the Buddha Mahāvairocana at the centre of an eight-petalled lotus and the families in enclosures around.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ritual · path: ritual · stage: intermediate · types: practice_

### a-syllable <a id="tea-mahavairocana-sutra-a-syllable"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The letter A is the gate of all dharmas because they are originally unborn; meditation on the letters, above all A, is taught as the way of mantra.
