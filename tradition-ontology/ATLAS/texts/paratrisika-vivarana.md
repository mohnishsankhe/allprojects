Abhinavagupta's long commentary on the Parātrīśikā: the Unsurpassed (anuttara) as the Heart, the emanation of the letters from A to KṢA within consciousness, the Heart-mantra SAUḤ, and the identity of the Goddess Parā with supreme speech; it also gives a hierarchy of revelations culminating in the Trika.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-paratrisika-vivarana-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Abhinavagupta explains the Goddess's question as the Supreme questioning itself: the Unsurpassed (anuttara) is consciousness in which question and answer, teacher and disciple, are one; the letter A is the Unsurpassed and the universe unfolds from A to HA as the 'aham' (I).

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge, sound · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, sound-language_

### scriptural-hierarchy <a id="tea-paratrisika-vivarana-scriptural-hierarchy"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Quoting other texts: the Trika is higher than the Kula; 'From the Veda the Śaiva, then the Vāma, then the Dakṣa, then the Kula, then the Mata, and then the Trika, supreme above all.'
