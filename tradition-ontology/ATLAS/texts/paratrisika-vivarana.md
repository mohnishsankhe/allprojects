Abhinavagupta's long commentary on the Parātrīśikā: the Unsurpassed (anuttara) as the Heart, the emanation of the letters from A to KṢA within consciousness, the Heart-mantra SAUḤ, and the identity of the Goddess Parā with supreme speech; it also gives a hierarchy of revelations culminating in the Trika.
