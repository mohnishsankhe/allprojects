After the death of the Nigaṇṭha Nātaputta and the split among his followers, Sāriputta recites the teaching in numerical lists from ones to tens so that the Saṅgha may recite together without dispute.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.6-1.7.7 <a id="tea-sangiti-sutta-1-6-1-7-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

After the Nigaṇṭha Nātaputta's death his disciples split and quarrelled; Sāriputta proposes that the monks recite the teaching together, in numbered lists, so that the holy life may last long for the welfare of the many.
