# Ṛṣibhāṣitāni

**Alternate titles:** Isibhāsiyāiṃ
**Language:** Ardhamāgadhī Prakrit
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Genre:** sayings of sages
**Dates:** Scholarly account: often regarded as very old (perhaps c. 3rd–1st c. BCE); low confidence; (confidence low)
**Availability:** digitized-original

A collection of sayings of forty-five 'seers' (ṛṣi) called pratyekabuddhas, among them figures known from Brahmanical and Buddhist sources — Nārada, Asita Devala, Aṅgiras, Yājñavalkya, Vajjiyaputta, Mahākāśyapa, Śāriputra — as well as Pārśva, Vardhamāna and Maṅkhali Gośāla. The Nandī and the niryukti lists name it, but it is not counted among the forty-five Āgamas.
