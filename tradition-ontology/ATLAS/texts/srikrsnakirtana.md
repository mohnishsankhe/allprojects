### Vaṃśī-khaṇḍa and Rādhā-viraha <a id="tea-srikrsnakirtana-vamsi-viraha"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

In the closing sections Rādhā, tormented by the sound of Kṛṣṇa's flute on the Yamunā's bank and then by his departure, laments in separation.
