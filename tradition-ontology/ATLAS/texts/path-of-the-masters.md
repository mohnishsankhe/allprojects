## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### The Path of the Masters, on saints and incarnations <a id="tea-path-of-the-masters-saints-and-avataras"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

The incarnations (avatāras) come from the regions of the negative power to restore order in the world; the Saints come from the highest region to take souls back home; hence the Saints are above the incarnations.

### The Path of the Masters, on the audible life stream <a id="tea-path-of-the-masters-audible-life-stream"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

The creative power of the Supreme flows out as a current of sound and light (the Word, Shabd, Nām); it can be heard within by a soul withdrawn to the eye centre under a living Master, and followed back to its source.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: sound, meditation · stage: all · types: sound-language, practice_
