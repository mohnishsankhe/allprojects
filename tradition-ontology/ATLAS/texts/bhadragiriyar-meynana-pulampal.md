# Pattirakiriyār: Meyññāṉap pulampal (lament for true knowledge)

`src:bhadragiriyar-meynana-pulampal` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tamil
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Genre:** kaṇṇi (couplets with refrain)
**Part of:** [Cittar pāṭalkaḷ (the songs of the Siddhars)](siddhar-padalgal.md)
**Authors:** 
  - [Pattirakiriyār (Bhadragiri)](../teachers/bhadragiriyar.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Dates:** Tradition's account: the disciple of Paṭṭiṉattār, formerly king of Ujjain; Scholarly account: undetermined; late-medieval; (confidence low)
**Structure:** invocation + 231 couplets (local e-text)
**Availability:** digitized-original

Two hundred and thirty-one couplets after an invocation, each asking 'when will the day come (ekkālam)?' — when will I burn the five senses, sleep without sleeping in Śiva-yoga, see women as mother, stop killing living beings, see the chiselled stone and moulded image as no ultimate thing, leave Vedānta and Veda to abide alone, give up ganja, opium and toddy, and know 'who am I, who are you'?
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Pattirakiriyār pāṭalkaḷ: Meyññāṉap pulampal (Project Madurai 1999; GRETIL Devanāgarī transliteration)
