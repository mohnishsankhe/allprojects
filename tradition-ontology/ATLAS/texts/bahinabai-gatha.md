The abhaṅgas of Bahiṇābāī of Śiūr, a brahmin woman who took Tukārām as guru in a dream: an autobiography in verse (Ātmanivedana) recounting her marriage, her husband's opposition and her previous births, songs on the true brahmin and the duties of a devoted wife, and the famous image of the saints as a temple.

### Ātmanivedana <a id="tea-bahinabai-gatha-atmanivedana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Bahiṇābāī's verse autobiography: married young to an older brahmin, she heard Tukārām's abhaṅgas in kīrtan, received his initiation in a dream, bore her husband's jealous anger until he relented, and remembers her previous births leading to this one.
