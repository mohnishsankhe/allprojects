144 verses on direct experience: the four qualifications; inquiry ('who am I? how did this arise?'); the self distinct from the body; and a fifteen-limbed (pañcadaśāṅga) yoga in which yama, niyama, posture, breath control and the rest are reinterpreted as knowledge-disciplines, with a list of the obstacles to samādhi; haṭha is said to be for those whose impurities are not yet burnt.

### 12 <a id="tea-aparoksanubhuti-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Who am I? How was this (world) born? Who is its maker? What is its material cause?' — such is inquiry (vicāra).

> ko 'haṃ katham idaṃ jātaṃ ko vai kartāsya vidyate / upādānaṃ kim astīha vicāraḥ so 'yam īdṛśaḥ

### 100-101 <a id="tea-aparoksanubhuti-100-101"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

For the sake of steady knowledge, fifteen limbs are taught in order: restraint, observance, renunciation, silence, place, time, posture, the root-lock, equipoise of the body, steadiness of vision, control of breath, withdrawal, holding, meditation on the self, and samādhi — each understood as a discipline of knowledge (e.g. restraint as control of the senses by knowing 'all is Brahman').

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice_

### 116 <a id="tea-aparoksanubhuti-116"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Making one's vision full of knowledge, one should see the world as Brahman; that is the noblest vision, not looking at the tip of the nose.

> dṛṣṭiṃ jñānamayīṃ kṛtvā paśyed brahmamayaṃ jagat / sā dṛṣṭiḥ paramodārā na nāsāgrāvalokinī

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice_

### 127-128 <a id="tea-aparoksanubhuti-127-128"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Obstacles arise in practising samādhi: lack of inquiry, laziness, desire for enjoyment, dullness (laya), darkness, distraction, relishing (rasāsvāda) and blankness (śūnyatā); the knower of Brahman should gradually abandon them.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice_

### 143-144 <a id="tea-aparoksanubhuti-143-144"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

This rāja-yoga (of knowledge) combined with haṭha-yoga is for those whose worldly desires are not fully burnt; for those whose minds are fully ripe, this rāja-yoga alone brings perfection.
