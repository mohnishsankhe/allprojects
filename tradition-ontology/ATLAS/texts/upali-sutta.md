## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3-12 <a id="tea-upali-sutta-3-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Nigaṇṭha Nātaputta declares bodily 'rods' (daṇḍa) most blameworthy for evil deeds; the Buddha speaks of 'deeds' (kamma) and declares mental deeds most blameworthy, and leads Upāli to agree through examples (a Jain who dies thirsting for cold water is reborn among the mind-bound gods).
