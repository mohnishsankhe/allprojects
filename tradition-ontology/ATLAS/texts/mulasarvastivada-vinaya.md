### bhiksuni-ordination <a id="tea-mulasarvastivada-vinaya-bhiksuni-ordination"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Full ordination of a bhikṣuṇī requires ordination by both assemblies — first by bhikṣuṇīs, then by bhikṣus — after training as a śikṣamāṇā; the nun accepts the eight weighty rules (gurudharma).
