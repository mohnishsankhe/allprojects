## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### women <a id="tea-raihai-tokuzui-women"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In seeking the Way one should bow to anyone who has attained it — man or woman, old or young, even a seven-year-old girl; to despise women or exclude them from sacred places is a foolish custom without basis in the buddha-dharma.
