### rokkakudo-verse <a id="tea-godensho-rokkakudo-verse"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In the dream at the Rokkakudō (1201) the bodhisattva Kannon (Guze Kannon, as Prince Shōtoku) said to Shinran: 'If the practitioner, by past karma, should be bound to a woman, I will take the form of a woman and be embraced; throughout life I will adorn his life and at death lead him to the Land of Bliss.'
