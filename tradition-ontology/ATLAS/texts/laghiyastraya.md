## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### pramana-pravesa <a id="tea-laghiyastraya-pramana-pravesa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Direct cognition is of two kinds: 'conventionally direct' (sāṃvyavahārika), the knowledge through senses and mind, and 'truly direct' (mukhya), clairvoyance, mind-reading and omniscience; memory, recognition, reasoning (tarka), inference and scripture are indirect.
