## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### introduction <a id="tea-dhavala-introduction"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Scripture was lost step by step after Mahāvīra; Dharasena, dwelling in the Candra cave at Girinagar and knowing part of the Pūrvas, feared its end and asked the monks' assembly for two able pupils. He tested them by giving each a spell to perfect, one with a syllable missing and one with a syllable too many; when goddesses of deformed form appeared, they corrected the spells. Then he taught them; the gods straightened one's teeth (Puṣpadanta) and the spirits honoured the other (Bhūtabali).
