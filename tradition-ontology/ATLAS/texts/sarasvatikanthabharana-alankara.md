### 5.1 <a id="tea-sarasvatikanthabharana-alankara-5-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Rasa is self-regard (abhimāna), the sense of I (ahaṃkāra), which is called śṛṅgāra; by its presence poetry becomes lovely.
