The songs ascribed to the Sahajiyā Caṇḍīdās: 'Listen, brother man — the truth of man is above all, nothing is above it'; everyone speaks of sahaja but who knows it?; the washerwoman's love is tested gold without a trace of lust.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### Rāgātmikā padas, on the difficulty of the sahaja path <a id="tea-candidas-ragatmika-padas-sahaja-path-is-hard"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _restricted: summary only_

The sahaja path is hard: like drawing a frog into a snake's mouth, or holding an elephant with a spider's thread — the lover must be near the fire and not burn.
