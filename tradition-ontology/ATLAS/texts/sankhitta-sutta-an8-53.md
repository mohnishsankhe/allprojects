## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-3 <a id="tea-sankhitta-sutta-an8-53-2-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Whatever qualities you know lead to passion not dispassion, bondage not release, accumulation not dismantling, many wishes not few, discontent not contentment, company not seclusion, laziness not energy, being burdensome not unburdensome — these are not the Dhamma, not the discipline, not the Teacher's instruction; their opposites are.
