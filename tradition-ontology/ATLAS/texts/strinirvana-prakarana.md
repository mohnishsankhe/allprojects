A short Sanskrit treatise in verse by the Yāpanīya grammarian Śākaṭāyana (Pālyakīrti) arguing that women can attain liberation: the cause, the three jewels, is not deficient in women; women can take the great vows, study, and have the highest stages of purity; clothing kept without attachment does not bar them.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-strinirvana-prakarana-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

There is liberation for women as for men, for the cause of liberation — the three jewels — is present in them without deficiency, and nothing in a woman is opposed to the three jewels. Women can hold right faith, learn scripture, keep the great vows and practise austerity; the garment they keep is not possession, for possession is attachment; that women cannot be reborn in the lowest hell does not show that they cannot reach the highest state; and the scripture itself speaks of women who reached liberation.
