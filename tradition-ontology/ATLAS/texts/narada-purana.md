# Nārada Purāṇa

`src:narada-purana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Nāradīya Purāṇa, Bṛhannāradīya Purāṇa (so named in the printed colophons)
**Original title:** नारदपुराण
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Genre:** purāṇa
**Authors:** 
  - [Vyāsa (Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana)](../teachers/vyasa.md) — role: compiler; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Nārada's teaching of the dharmas of the Bṛhat-kalpa (Matsya 53.23); the four Kumāras (Sanaka, Sanandana, Sanatkumāra, Sanātana) instruct Nārada.; scholarly: The Mahāpurāṇa is distinguished by scholars from a separate Bṛhannāradīya Upapurāṇa, but printed colophons of the Mahāpurāṇa use 'Bṛhannāradīya'.; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Vyāsa, end of Dvāpara; Scholarly account: late compilation (roughly late 1st to mid 2nd millennium CE); (confidence low)
**Structure:** Pūrvabhāga (four pādas, c. 125 chapters) and Uttarabhāga (c. 82 chapters); 25,000 verses by the traditional count
**Availability:** digitized-original

A Vaiṣṇava Purāṇa of dharma and ritual: devotion to Viṣṇu, the Vedāṅgas, mantra-śāstra, the tithi-vratas of the year, the contents of all eighteen Purāṇas, the Ekādaśī vow (with Yama's lament that his realm is emptied by it), and the glories of many tīrthas.

_Notes: Pūrvabhāga chs. 92-109: a summary-index (anukramaṇī) of all eighteen Purāṇas (the list there counts the Vāyu fourth); following chapters: tithi-vratas for the twelve months; Pūrva 3rd pāda: mantra-śāstra; Uttarabhāga: Ekādaśī/Dvādaśī-māhātmya with Rukmāṅgada and Mohinī, and tīrtha-māhātmyas. chapter checked in the GRETIL/Sansknet e-text of the Nārada Purāṇa_
