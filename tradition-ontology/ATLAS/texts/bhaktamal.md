# Bhaktamāl

**Language:** Braj Bhāṣā
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md), [Rasik Rāma-bhakti (Rasik sampradāya of the Rāmānandīs)](../lineages/rasik-ramanandi.md)

**Structure:** about 200 chappays with dohās (numbering differs between editions)

Nābhādās's 'garland of devotees': short chappay verses on some two hundred devotees of all ages and sects — from the Purāṇic devotees to the founders of the Vaiṣṇava sampradāyas, Rāmānanda and his twelve disciples, Kabīr, Mīrā, Sūrdās and Tulsīdās — opening with the teaching that devotee, devotion, Lord and guru are one.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Bhaktirasabodhinī](bhaktirasabodhini.md)

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### chappay on Mīrā <a id="tea-bhaktamal-mira"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Mīrā showed in the Kali age a love like the gopīs'; fearless, she sang the Lord's glory; the wicked plotted her death, yet not a hair was harmed and she drank the poison like nectar; beating the drum of devotion and ashamed before none, she cast off public shame and the chains of family and worshipped Giridhar.

### chappay on Rāmānanda (c. no. 36; numbering varies) <a id="tea-bhaktamal-ramananda"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Rāmānanda, like Raghunātha, built a second bridge by which the world crosses over; his disciples were Anantānanda, Kabīr, Sukhānanda, Surasurānanda, Padmāvatī, Naraharī, Pīpā, Bhāvānanda, Raidās, Dhanā, Sena and Surasurī (Surasurānanda's wife).

### chappay on Tulsīdās <a id="tea-bhaktamal-tulsi"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

For the deliverance of the crooked beings of the Kali age Vālmīki was born again as Tulsī; the Rāmāyaṇa of a hundred crore verses, one syllable of which destroys the greatest sin, he made easy for all.

### opening dohā <a id="tea-bhaktamal-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Devotee, devotion, the Lord and the guru — four names, one body; bowing to their feet destroys every obstacle.

> भक्त भक्ति भगवंत गुरु चतुर नाम बपु एक । इनके पद बंदन किए नासत बिघ्न अनेक ॥
