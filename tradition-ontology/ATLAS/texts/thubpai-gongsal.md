## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### structure <a id="tea-thubpai-gongsal-structure"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Sage's intent is the Mahāyāna path: awakening the potential (gotra), going for refuge, generating bodhicitta, and training in the six perfections — with calm and insight as the last two — through the grounds and paths to buddhahood.
