## Teachings (15: skeleton 15)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-ratnagotravibhaga-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Buddha, the dharma, the saṅgha, the element (dhātu), awakening, the qualities and the buddha-activity — these seven vajra points are, in brief, the body of the whole treatise.

> buddhaśca dharmaśca gaṇaśca dhāturbodhirguṇāḥ karma ca bauddhamantyam

### 1.27 <a id="tea-ratnagotravibhaga-1-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Because the buddha's knowledge is present within the host of beings, because that stainless nature is non-dual, and because the fruit is named after the buddha-lineage — therefore all embodied beings are said to have the buddha-nature (buddhagarbha).

> buddhajñānāntargamāt sattvarāśestannairmalyasyādvayatvāt prakṛtyā | bauddhe gotre tatphalasyopacārāduktāḥ sarve dehino buddhagarbhāḥ

### 1.28 <a id="tea-ratnagotravibhaga-1-28"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Because the perfect buddha-body radiates, because suchness is undifferentiated, and because of the lineage, all embodied beings always have the buddha-nature — the dharmakāya pervades them, the Tathāgata's suchness is undivided in them, and the Tathāgata-lineage exists in them.

> sambuddhakāyaspharaṇāt tathatāvyatibhedataḥ | gotrataśca sadā sarve buddhagarbhāḥ śarīriṇaḥ

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate_

### 1.29 <a id="tea-ratnagotravibhaga-1-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The element is explained under ten headings: its essence, cause, fruit, function, endowment, manifestation, states, all-pervasiveness, unchangeability and inseparability of qualities.

### 1.34 <a id="tea-ratnagotravibhaga-1-34"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The seed of the buddha's children is faith in the supreme vehicle, their mother is wisdom producing the buddha-qualities, their womb is the bliss of meditation, and their nurse is compassion.

> bījaṃ yeṣāmagrayānādhimuktirmātā prajñā buddhadharmaprasūtyai

### 1.35 <a id="tea-ratnagotravibhaga-1-35"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The fruit is the perfection of the qualities of purity, self, bliss and permanence; its function is aversion to suffering and the wish and aspiration to attain peace.

> śubhātmasukhanityatvaguṇapāramitā phalam

### 1.47 <a id="tea-ratnagotravibhaga-1-47"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

### 1.55-57 <a id="tea-ratnagotravibhaga-1-55-57"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As earth rests on water, water on wind and wind on space, while space rests on nothing, so the aggregates rest on karma and defilements, these on improper attention, and improper attention on the purity of mind — but the nature of mind rests on nothing.

> pṛthivyambau jalaṃ vāyau vāyurvyomni pratiṣṭhitaḥ | apratiṣṭhitamākāśaṃ vāyvambukṣitidhātuṣu

### 1.96-98 <a id="tea-ratnagotravibhaga-1-96-98"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The element hidden in beings is like a buddha in a bad lotus, honey among bees, kernels in husks, gold in filth, a treasure in the ground, a sprout in a small fruit, a Jina image in rotten rags, a king in the womb of a low woman, a precious image in clay: so abides this element in beings obscured by adventitious stains of defilement.

> buddhaḥ kupadme madhu makṣikāsu tuṣesu sārāṇya śucau suvarṇam | nidhiḥ kṣitāvalpaphale 'ṅkurādi praklinnavastreṣu jinātmabhāvaḥ

### 1.154-155 <a id="tea-ratnagotravibhaga-1-154-155"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Nothing is to be removed from it and nothing added; the real should be seen as real, and seeing the real one is freed. The element is empty of the adventitious stains, which have the mark of separability; it is not empty of the unsurpassed qualities, which have the mark of inseparability.

> nāpaneyamataḥ kiñcidupaneyaṃ na kiñcana | draṣṭavyaṃ bhūtato bhūtaṃ bhūtadarśī vimucyate || śūnya āgantukairdhātuḥ savinirbhāgalakṣaṇaiḥ | aśūnyo 'nuttarairdharmairavinirbhāgalakṣaṇaiḥ

### 1.156-157 <a id="tea-ratnagotravibhaga-1-156-157"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If everything is taught to be empty like clouds, dreams and illusions, why did the buddhas teach that the buddha-element exists in every being? To remove five faults: a faint heart, contempt for inferior beings, grasping the unreal, denying the real dharma, and excessive self-love.

> līnaṃ cittaṃ hīnasattveṣvavajñābhūtagrāho bhūtadharmāpavādaḥ | ātmasnehaścādhikaḥ pañca doṣā yeṣāṃ teṣāṃ tatprahāṇārthamuktam

### 2 <a id="tea-ratnagotravibhaga-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Awakening: the transformation of the basis (āśraya-parāvṛtti), in which the stainless element becomes manifest as the dharmakāya for one's own benefit and the two form-bodies (enjoyment and emanation) for others, like the sun freed from clouds.

### 3 <a id="tea-ratnagotravibhaga-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The qualities: thirty-two qualities of separation (the ten powers, four fearlessnesses, eighteen unshared qualities) belonging to the dharmakāya and thirty-two of maturation (the thirty-two marks) belonging to the form-bodies — sixty-four in all.

### 4 <a id="tea-ratnagotravibhaga-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The buddha-activity is effortless and uninterrupted, illustrated by nine examples: Indra's reflection in the jewel-ground, the divine drum, clouds, Brahmā, the sun, the wish-fulfilling jewel, an echo, space and the earth.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: divine · path: devotion · stage: realized · types: ultimate, world-fate_

### 5 <a id="tea-ratnagotravibhaga-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The benefit: one who has faith in this teaching of the buddha-element gains more merit than one who offers jewels to buddhas or keeps pure morality or meditates for aeons.
