# Māheśvara Sūtras (Śivasūtras of the grammarians)

**Language:** Sanskrit

**Lineages:** `lin:vyakarana`, [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md)
**Genre:** vyākaraṇa

**Availability:** digitized-original

The fourteen short lists of phonemes (a i u ṇ, ṛ ḷ k, … ha l) prefixed to the Aṣṭādhyāyī, from which Pāṇini forms his abbreviations (pratyāhāra) of sound-classes; by tradition they were the fourteen beats of Śiva's drum at the end of his dance.

_Notes: Not to be confused with Vasugupta's Śiva Sūtra (src:siva-sutra, Kashmir Śaivism)._
