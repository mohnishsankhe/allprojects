The Prakrit verse commentary (niryukti) on the Āvaśyaka, the most important of the ten niryuktis ascribed to Bhadrabāhu: besides explaining terms by the method of nikṣepa it narrates the lives of Ṛṣabha and Mahāvīra, the gaṇadharas' doubts and the schisms (nihnava), and states that the Arhat speaks the meaning and the gaṇadharas compose the sūtras.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 92 <a id="tea-avasyaka-niryukti-92"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Arhat speaks the meaning; the gaṇadharas skilfully compose the sūtras, for the benefit of the teaching; thus scripture proceeds.

### marici <a id="tea-avasyaka-niryukti-marici"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In a former birth as Marīci, grandson of Ṛṣabha, the future Mahāvīra left the hard discipline and became a tridaṇḍin ascetic; on hearing that he would be the last Tīrthaṅkara, a vāsudeva and a cakravartin, he danced with pride in his lineage, binding the karma of low status that later caused him to be conceived in a brāhmaṇa womb.
