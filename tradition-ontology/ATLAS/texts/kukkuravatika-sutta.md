Puṇṇa the cow-duty ascetic and Seniya the naked dog-duty ascetic ask about each other's destiny; the Buddha says the observance, if perfected, leads to rebirth among dogs or cattle, and if joined with wrong view to hell.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-5 <a id="tea-kukkuravatika-sutta-2-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Puṇṇa, who keeps the cow-vow, and Seniya, a naked ascetic who keeps the dog-vow and eats food thrown on the ground, ask about each other's destiny. The Buddha, after hesitation, says one who develops the dog-vow fully, with a dog's mind and habits, is reborn among dogs if it succeeds; if he holds that by it he will become a god, that is wrong view and leads to hell or the animal realm. The same holds for the cow-vow.
