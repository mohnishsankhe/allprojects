## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.12 <a id="tea-sutrakrtanga-vrtti-silanka-1-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Śīlāṅka derives the numbers: kriyāvāda — the nine categories (soul, non-soul, influx, stopping, bondage, wearing-away, merit, demerit, release), each 'from itself' or 'from another', eternal or non-eternal, and caused by time, God, the self, destiny or own-nature: 9×2×2×5 = 180; akriyāvāda — seven categories × itself/another × six causes (adding chance): 84; ajñāna — nine categories × seven modes (is, is not, is and is not, inexpressible, and the three inexpressible combinations) = 63, plus four on the arising of a state = 67, each voiced as 'who knows whether the soul exists, and what use is knowing it?'; vinaya — veneration of eight (gods, king, ascetics, kin, elders, the lowly, mother, father) by mind, speech, body and gifts = 32. The ajñānikas hold that ignorance is better (ajñānam eva śreyaḥ).
