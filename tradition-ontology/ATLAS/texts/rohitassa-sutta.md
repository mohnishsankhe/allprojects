## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 3-6 <a id="tea-rohitassa-sutta-3-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The seer Rohitassa, able to stride from ocean to ocean, walked for a hundred years to reach the end of the world and died without reaching it. The Buddha: the end of the world where one is not born, does not age, die or reappear cannot be reached by travelling; yet without reaching it there is no end of suffering; in this fathom-long body with its perception and mind he declares the world, its origin, cessation and the way.

### 5.1-5.2 <a id="tea-rohitassa-sutta-5-1-5-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There is no making an end of suffering without reaching the end of the world; it is in this fathom-long body with its perception and mind that I declare the world, its origin, its cessation and the way leading to its cessation.

> “Na kho panāhaṁ, āvuso, appatvā lokassa antaṁ dukkhassa antakiriyaṁ vadāmi. Api ca khvāhaṁ, āvuso, imasmiṁyeva byāmamatte kaḷevare sasaññimhi samanake lokañca paññapemi lokasamudayañca lokanirodhañca lokanirodhagāminiñca paṭipadanti.
