# Jaiminīya Upaniṣad Brāhmaṇa

**Alternate titles:** Talavakāra Upaniṣad Brāhmaṇa
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
**Genre:** brāhmaṇa/āraṇyaka
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 7th–6th c. BCE (estimate); (confidence low)
**Structure:** 4 adhyāyas divided into anuvākas and khaṇḍas (numbering differs between editions)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The 'secret' Brāhmaṇa of the Jaiminīya Sāmaveda, functioning as that school's Āraṇyaka: meditations on the sāman chant, the udgītha, Oṃ and the breath (prāṇa), the journey of the dead through the sun, and a teacher lineage (vaṃśa); the Kena Upaniṣad forms part of its fourth book.

_Notes: Correction to the task list: it is a Brāhmaṇa (an 'Upaniṣad Brāhmaṇa'), not an Āraṇyaka in name, though it plays the Āraṇyaka's role for the Jaiminīyas. The Kena Upaniṣad is usually cited as JUB 4.18–21 (4.10 in Oertel's anuvāka numbering) — locator not verified here._
