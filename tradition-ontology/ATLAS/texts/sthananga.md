The third Aṅga, a numerical compendium arranging doctrinal, cosmological and monastic topics in ten sections from 'ones' to 'tens'; among much else it lists the seven schismatics (nihnavas) and the ten 'wonders' (accheras) of the present era, such as a woman Tīrthaṅkara and the transfer of Mahāvīra's embryo.
