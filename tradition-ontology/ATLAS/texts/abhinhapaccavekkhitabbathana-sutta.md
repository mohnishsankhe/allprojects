## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-11 <a id="tea-abhinhapaccavekkhitabbathana-sutta-1-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Five subjects that a woman or a man, lay or ordained, should often reflect on: 'I am subject to ageing, not beyond ageing'; 'subject to illness'; 'subject to death'; 'I must be parted from all that is dear'; 'I am the owner of my deeds, heir of my deeds …; whatever deed I do, good or bad, of that I shall be the heir'; each reflection weakens pride of youth, health and life, desire and bad conduct.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, action · stage: all · types: practice, death-dying_
