## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.2 <a id="tea-padacandrika-anantadeva-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The activities are those of the mind, a transformation of sattva; their stilling is their dissolution into their cause, turned inward, when the outward-facing flow is cut off.
