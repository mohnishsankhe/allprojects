## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 2.1-2.4 <a id="tea-nibbanapanha-sutta-2-1-2-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Nibbāna, nibbāna' it is said, friend Sāriputta; what is nibbāna? — The destruction of lust, the destruction of hatred, the destruction of delusion: this is called nibbāna.

> “‘Nibbānaṁ, nibbānan’ti, āvuso sāriputta, vuccati. Katamaṁ nu kho, āvuso, nibbānan”ti? “Yo kho, āvuso, rāgakkhayo dosakkhayo mohakkhayo— idaṁ vuccati nibbānan”ti.

### 2.5-2.12 <a id="tea-nibbanapanha-sutta-2-5-2-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate
