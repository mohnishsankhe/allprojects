## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### whole <a id="tea-srutavatara-indranandi-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

After the nirvāṇa the three omniscients (Gautama, Sudharman, Jambū), the five śrutakevalins ending with Bhadrabāhu, and the successively lesser knowers of the Pūrvas and Aṅgas followed until the knowledge was all but lost; then Dharasena taught Puṣpadanta and Bhūtabali, who composed the Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama and completed it on the fifth of the bright half of Jyeṣṭha, when the saṅgha worshipped the book; Guṇadhara's Kaṣāyapāhuḍa followed.
