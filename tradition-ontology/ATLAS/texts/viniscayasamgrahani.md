## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.alaya-proofs <a id="tea-viniscayasamgrahani-1-alaya-proofs"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The store-consciousness must be accepted: without it the appropriation of a new body, the simultaneous functioning of several consciousnesses, the clarity of mental cognition, the planting of seeds, the karmic process, bodily experience, the mindless attainments and death could not be explained (the eight proofs).
