## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-mahavakya-upanisad-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Brahmā teaches that this knowledge is not to be given to the unqualified; ignorance is darkness — the body and world — and knowledge is the sun's orb.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: intermediate · types: teacher-transmission, ultimate_

### 3-4 <a id="tea-mahavakya-upanisad-3-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

'That sun is Brahman': meditating with the ajapā 'haṃsaḥ so'ham' one sees the self-luminous consciousness.

_level: bridging · standpoint: experiential · path: sound, meditation · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, sound-language_

### 5 <a id="tea-mahavakya-upanisad-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate
