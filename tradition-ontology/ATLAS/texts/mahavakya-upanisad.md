## Teachings (3: sourced 3)

### 1-5 <a id="tea-mahavakya-upanisad-1-2"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Brahmā teaches that this knowledge is not to be given to the unqualified; ignorance is darkness — the body and world — and knowledge is the sun's orb.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: intermediate · types: teacher-transmission, ultimate_

concepts: [Secrecy and eligibility](../concepts/secrecy-and-eligibility.md)

### 6 <a id="tea-mahavakya-upanisad-3-4"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

'That sun is Brahman': meditating with the ajapā 'haṃsaḥ so'ham' one sees the self-luminous consciousness.

_level: bridging · standpoint: experiential · path: sound, meditation · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, sound-language_

concepts: [Haṃsa — the swan / the self as breath-mantra](../concepts/hamsa.md) · practices: [Ajapā-japa (the haṃsa / so'ham breath-mantra)](../practices/ajapa-japa.md)

### 7 <a id="tea-mahavakya-upanisad-5"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

This is not samādhi, not attainment of yoga, not dissolution of mind — it is oneness with Brahman.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, dispute_

disputes: [Is knowledge alone sufficient for liberation, or must bodily yoga accompany it?](../debates/yoga-or-knowledge-for-liberation.md)
