### 5.2 <a id="tea-parayanavagga-5-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Ajita asks by what the world is shrouded and what its great fear is: ignorance shrouds it, craving is its pollution, suffering its fear; the currents flowing everywhere are dammed by mindfulness and cut off by wisdom; with the cessation of consciousness, name-and-form ceases without remainder.

### 5.7 <a id="tea-parayanavagga-5-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Upasīva asks for a support to cross the flood: relying on nothingness, mindful, with 'there is nothing', cross the flood, free of sensual desire; as a flame blown out by the wind goes to rest and cannot be reckoned, so the sage freed from name-and-form goes to rest and cannot be reckoned; for one who has gone out there is no measure; all ways of speaking are removed.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, practice_

### 5.16 <a id="tea-parayanavagga-5-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Mogharāja asks how one should look upon the world so that the King of Death does not see him: look upon the world as empty, always mindful, uprooting the view of self; so one crosses beyond death.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate, death-dying_

### 5.16/2 <a id="tea-parayanavagga-5-16-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Look upon the world as empty, Mogharāja, always mindful, having uprooted the view of self; so you would cross over death; one who looks upon the world thus the King of Death does not see.

> “Suññato lokaṁ avekkhassu, Mogharāja sadā sato; Attānudiṭṭhiṁ ūhacca, Evaṁ maccutaro siyā; Evaṁ lokaṁ avekkhantaṁ, Maccurājā na passatī”ti.
