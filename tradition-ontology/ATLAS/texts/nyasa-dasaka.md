## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-nyasa-dasaka-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The wise should offer the self saying: 'I myself, the burden of my protection, and the fruit of that protection are not mine but belong to the Lord of Śrī alone.'
