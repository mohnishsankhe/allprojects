## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1-4 <a id="tea-caturmudra-advayavajra-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

The four seals are action seal (karmamudrā), dharma seal, great seal (mahāmudrā) and pledge seal (samayamudrā): relying on the action seal one meditates the dharma seal; above it is the great seal, from which the pledge seal arises. The great seal is without own-being, free of the obscurations, like the clear midday autumn sky, the support of all perfections, the single nature of existence and nirvāṇa, whose body is objectless compassion and whose form is great bliss; the pledge seal is Vajradhara's radiating in the form of Heruka, with the saṃbhoga- and nirmāṇa-kāya, for beings.

> mahāmudrā niḥsvabhāvā jñeyādyāvaraṇavivarjitā śaradamalamadhyāhnagaganasaṅkāśā sakalasampadādhārabhūtā bhavanirvāṇaikasvarūpā anālambanakaruṇāśarīrā mahāsukhaikarūpā

### 4-verse <a id="tea-caturmudra-advayavajra-4-verse"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

No mantra recitation, no austerity, no fire offering, no maṇḍala dweller and no maṇḍala — that is the mantra recitation, that the austerity, that the fire offering, that the maṇḍala dweller and that the maṇḍala.

> na mantrajāpo na tapo na homo na māṇḍaleyaṃ na ca maṇḍalaṃ ca | sa mantrajāpaḥ sa tapaḥ sa homaḥ tanmāṇḍaleyaṃ tanmaṇḍalaṃ ca ||
