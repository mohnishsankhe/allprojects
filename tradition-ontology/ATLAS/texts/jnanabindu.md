## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### kevala <a id="tea-jnanabindu-kevala"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The three views of the omniscient's knowledge and perception — in sequence, simultaneous, and identical — are each valid from a particular standpoint and are not contrary when so understood.
