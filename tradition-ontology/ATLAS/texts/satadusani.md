### vāda on liberation while living <a id="tea-satadusani-jivanmukti"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Liberation while living (jīvanmukti) is impossible: while the body lasts, karma and bondage remain, and scripture places release after the body falls; the Advaitin's 'liberated-while-living' is still bound.
