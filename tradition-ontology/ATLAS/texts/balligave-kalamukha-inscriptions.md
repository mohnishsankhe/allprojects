Records of the Kōḍiya-maṭha attached to the Dakṣiṇa-Kedāreśvara temple at Balligāve, seat of the Śakti-pariṣad of the Kālāmukhas: grants, lineages of pontiffs, and praise of their learning and of the maṭha's feeding, teaching and healing.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-balligave-kalamukha-inscriptions-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Inscriptions of the Kōḍiya-maṭha at Balligāve praise its Kālāmukha ascetics as masters of the Lakula doctrine, of Pātañjala yoga, Nyāya, Vaiśeṣika, Mīmāṃsā, Sāṃkhya and Buddhist doctrine, grammar and poetry, and record the maṭha as a place of feeding, teaching and care of the sick, belonging to the Śakti-pariṣad.
