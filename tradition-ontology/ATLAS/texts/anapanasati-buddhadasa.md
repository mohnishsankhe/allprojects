### tetrads <a id="tea-anapanasati-buddhadasa-tetrads"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

The sixteen steps of mindfulness with breathing form four tetrads corresponding to the four foundations of mindfulness (body, feeling, mind, dhammas); practised fully they complete the seven factors of awakening and bring liberation.
