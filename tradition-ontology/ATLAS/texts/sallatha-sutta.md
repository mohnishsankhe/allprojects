## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-sallatha-sutta-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When touched by painful bodily feeling the uninstructed person sorrows and laments and so feels two feelings, bodily and mental, as if struck by a dart and then by a second dart; resisting the pain he seeks sensual pleasure as the only escape he knows, and the tendencies to aversion, lust and ignorance lie within him.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [vedanā](../terms/vedana.md) · concepts: [The two arrows](../concepts/two-arrows.md) · obstacles: [The seven latent tendencies (anusaya)](../obstacles/seven-anusayas.md)

### 3-6 <a id="tea-sallatha-sutta-3-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The instructed noble disciple feels only one feeling, bodily, not mental — struck by one dart only; he does not resist it, knows an escape other than sensual pleasure, understands the origin, passing, gratification, danger and escape of feelings, and feels them detached: he is called one detached from birth, ageing and death.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice, consciousness-mind_
