The 'armour' of the Goddess recited before the Saptaśatī: Brahmā teaches Mārkaṇḍeya the nine Durgās (Śailaputrī to Siddhidātrī) and places the Goddess's forms on each part of the body for protection.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3-5 <a id="tea-devi-kavaca-3-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Brahmā names the nine Durgās: first Śailaputrī, second Brahmacāriṇī, third Candraghaṇṭā, fourth Kūṣmāṇḍā, fifth Skandamātā, sixth Kātyāyanī, seventh Kālarātri, eighth Mahāgaurī, ninth Siddhidātrī.
