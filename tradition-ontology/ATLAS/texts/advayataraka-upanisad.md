### 1 (eligibility) <a id="tea-advayataraka-upanisad-1-eligibility"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

This teaching is for the renouncer who has conquered the senses and is endowed with the six qualities (calm and the rest).

### 1 (guru) <a id="tea-advayataraka-upanisad-1-guru"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The true teacher knows the Vedas, is devoted to Viṣṇu, free of envy, a yogin devoted to his own guru, who knows the Person. 'Gu' is darkness and 'ru' its remover: because he removes darkness he is called guru; the guru is the supreme Brahman.

> guśabdas tv andhakāraḥ syād ruśabdas tan nirodhakaḥ | andhakāranirodhitvād gurur ity abhidhīyate

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission_

concepts: [The guru in the minor Upaniṣads](../concepts/guru.md)

### 1 (three lakṣyas) <a id="tea-advayataraka-upanisad-1-three-laksyas"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Three targets: the inner (the kuṇḍalinī in the suṣumnā; the sound heard on closing the ears; a blue light between the eyes), the outer (colours of space seen at distances in front of the nose, rays and a light above the head), and the intermediate (a sequence of voids).

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: body-breath, meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice, powers-experiences_

concepts: [The three targets of meditation (lakṣya-traya)](../concepts/three-laksyas.md) · practices: [Contemplation of the three targets (lakṣya-traya)](../practices/laksya-traya.md)

### 1 (tāraka) <a id="tea-advayataraka-upanisad-1-taraka"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Tāraka is so called because it makes one cross the great fear of birth, old age and death; by 'not this, not this' one knows the Self as distinct from the body.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: intermediate · types: ultimate, practice_

concepts: [Tāraka and amanaska yoga](../concepts/taraka-amanaska.md) · practices: [Tāraka-yoga](../practices/taraka-yoga.md)

### 1 (voids) <a id="tea-advayataraka-upanisad-1-voids"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In the intermediate target the yogin sees in turn the space without qualities, the supreme space like dense darkness lit by stars, the great space like the fire of dissolution, the space of principles, and the space of the sun like crores of suns.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: body-breath, meditation · stage: advanced · types: powers-experiences_

concepts: [The five voids (vyoma-pañcaka)](../concepts/vyoma-pancaka.md)

### 1 (śāmbhavī) <a id="tea-advayataraka-upanisad-1-sambhavi"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Śāmbhavī mudrā is the gaze on the inner target while the eyes look outward without blinking; the place where such a yogin lives is purified.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice_
