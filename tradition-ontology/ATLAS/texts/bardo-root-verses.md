The verses of the six intermediate states (life, dream, meditation, dying, dharmatā, becoming), each exhorting the practitioner to recognize and use that bardo.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-6 <a id="tea-bardo-root-verses-1-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The root verses exhort the practitioner in each intermediate state: in the bardo of life, give up laziness and enter hearing, reflection and meditation; in the bardo of dream, give up the corpse-like sleep of ignorance and recognize the dream; in the bardo of meditation, rest undistracted; in the bardo of dying, give up clinging and enter the clear light; in the bardo of dharmatā, recognize the appearances as self-display; in the bardo of becoming, close the womb door and remember pure perception.
