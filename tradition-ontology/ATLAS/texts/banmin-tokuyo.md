### work-as-practice <a id="tea-banmin-tokuyo-work-as-practice"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Each occupation — farming, trade, craft, a warrior's service — performed wholeheartedly is buddha-practice; the farmer hoeing with the nenbutsu or with fierce energy is doing the work of the buddha.
