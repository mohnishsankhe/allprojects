## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### ch1 <a id="tea-tsigdon-dzod-ch1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The first vajra topic, the ground: its essence is primordially pure, its nature spontaneously present, its compassion all-pervading; it is neither saṃsāra nor nirvāṇa and is described as a youthful vase body — luminosity enclosed within.

### ch2 <a id="tea-tsigdon-dzod-ch2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

How delusion arises: when the ground-appearances emerge from the ground through the eight gateways of spontaneous presence, not recognizing them as one's own display is ignorance (in three kinds), and the dualistic apprehension of self and other unfolds into the realms of saṃsāra.
