### bhiksu-vibhanga <a id="tea-dharmaguptaka-vinaya-bhiksu-vibhanga"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The bhikṣu's prātimokṣa comprises 250 rules: 4 pārājika, 13 saṃghāvaśeṣa, 2 aniyata, 30 naiḥsargika-pātayantika, 90 pātayantika, 4 pratideśanīya, 100 śaikṣa and 7 adhikaraṇa-śamatha; the bhikṣuṇī's comprises 348.
