Cēkkiḻār's 'Great Purāṇa' (Tiruttoṇṭar Purāṇam): the lives of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs and the nine groups of devotees named in Cuntarar's Tiruttoṇṭattokai, framed by Cuntarar's own story; the twelfth Tirumuṟai and the charter of Tamil Śaiva sainthood.

## Teachings (10: skeleton 10)

### 1 <a id="tea-periya-puranam-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The opening word 'ulakelām' ('all the world'): the Lord whom all the world cannot know or tell, who dances in the hall, is praised.

The hunter Kaṇṇappar's worship with tasted meat, water from his mouth and flowers from his hair pleased Śiva more than the priest's correct rites; when the liṅga's eye bled he gave his own eye, and was stopped by Śiva from giving the second.
