The chapter of Kāśyapa (the 'Ratnakūṭa' proper): sets of four qualities that make or break a bodhisattva; the middle way as the true examination of dharmas — 'is' and 'is not', permanent and impermanent, self and no-self are two extremes; emptiness does not make dharmas empty, they are empty; better a view of self as large as Mount Sumeru than a view of emptiness, which is incurable, like a medicine that stays in the belly; the lamp that dispels darkness of a thousand years.

## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 56-60 <a id="tea-kasyapaparivarta-56-60"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Permanent' is one extreme, 'impermanent' the other; 'self' one extreme, 'no-self' the other; 'real mind' and 'unreal mind'; defilement and purification; 'is' and 'is not' — what is between these extremes, formless, unshowable, unsupported, without abode, is the middle way, the true examination of dharmas.

> astīti kāśyapa ayam eko 'ntaḥ nāstīty ayaṃ dvitīyo 'ntaḥ yad etayor dvayor antayor madhyam iyam ucyate kāśyapa madhyamā pratipad dharmāṇāṃ bhūtapratyavekṣā

### 63 <a id="tea-kasyapaparivarta-63"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The true examination of dharmas: emptiness does not make dharmas empty — dharmas themselves are empty; the signless does not make them signless — they are signless; and so for wishless, unconditioned, unborn, ungraspable and without own-being.

> yan na śunyatāyā dharmā śūnyā karoti dharmā eva śūnyā

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_

### 64 <a id="tea-kasyapaparivarta-64"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Emptiness is not for destroying the person; rely on emptiness, not the person; those who resort to emptiness by apprehending emptiness are lost to this teaching. Better a view of the person as large as Mount Sumeru than the view of emptiness of one who is proud of it: emptiness is the escape from all views, but by what will one escape the view of emptiness?

> varaṃ khalu puna kāśyapa sumerumātrā pudgaladṛṣṭir āśritā na tv evādhimānikasya śunyatādṛṣṭi

### 65 <a id="tea-kasyapaparivarta-65"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As a medicine that expels a sick man's disorders but itself stays in his belly makes him worse, so one for whom emptiness has become a view is, the Buddha says, incurable.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: practice_

obstacles: [The view of emptiness (śūnyatādṛṣṭi)](../obstacles/view-of-emptiness.md)

### 70-71 <a id="tea-kasyapaparivarta-70-71"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A lamp does not think 'I dispel darkness', yet darkness goes; darkness gathered for a thousand years does not refuse to leave when a lamp is lit — so karma and defilements gathered for countless aeons vanish with one wise attention and examination: the lamp is the noble faculty of wisdom.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation_
