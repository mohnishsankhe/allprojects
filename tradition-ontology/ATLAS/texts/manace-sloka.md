Two hundred and five verses in the bhujaṅgaprayāta metre addressed to one's own mind: go by the path of devotion, hold to Rāma's name, keep good company and inquire who in the world is truly happy.

### śloka 2 <a id="tea-manace-sloka-2"></a>

O good mind, go by the path of devotion; then Śrī Hari is reached of himself. Give up all that the world rightly blames; do with a full heart all that it rightly honours.
