## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 1-3 <a id="tea-progress-of-insight-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

In the first knowledges the meditator distinguishes the noted object from the noting mind (mind and matter), sees their conditionality, and comprehends them as impermanent, painful, not-self; unpleasant sensations are often prominent at the stage of comprehension.

### 4 <a id="tea-progress-of-insight-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

At the knowledge of arising and passing away, bright lights, rapture, tranquillity, happiness, strong faith and quick, easy noting may appear; the meditator may think he has attained the path; these are the imperfections of insight and must themselves be noted.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: powers-experiences, practice_

### 5-10 <a id="tea-progress-of-insight-5-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

Then only the vanishing of objects is seen (dissolution); formations appear fearful, full of danger and wearisome; there arises a desire for deliverance; at re-observation strong pains and restlessness may occur until the mind settles.

### 11 <a id="tea-progress-of-insight-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

At equanimity about formations noting proceeds by itself, smoothly and without effort, neither delighting nor fearing, and can last long.

### 14-16 <a id="tea-progress-of-insight-14-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

At the path and fruition the noted objects and the noting mind cease: the meditator experiences the cessation of formations (nibbāna); afterwards reviewing knowledge reflects on path, fruit, nibbāna and the defilements abandoned.
