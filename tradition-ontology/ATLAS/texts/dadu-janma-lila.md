### Dādū Janma Līlā, the meeting with Akbar (1585) <a id="tea-dadu-janma-lila-meeting-with-akbar"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Summoned to Fatehpur Sikri, Dādū spoke with the emperor Akbar over days about the one God beyond Hindu and Muslim; Akbar honoured him.

### Dādū Janma Līlā, the old man's blessing <a id="tea-dadu-janma-lila-buddhan-blessing"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

When Dādū was eleven an old man appeared to him, took pān from his hand and put it in Dādū's mouth, touched his head, and vanished; seven years later he appeared again and completed the teaching.
