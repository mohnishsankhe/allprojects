## Teachings (2: sourced 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-avyakta-upanisad-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

In the beginning there was the unmanifest light; it became twofold, green (māyā) and red (puruṣa); from their union came the golden egg and Parameṣṭhin, who asked 'what is my family, what my task?'; a voice told him to know by austerity.

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: world-fate_

### 2-7 (summary) <a id="tea-avyakta-upanisad-2-7-summary"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

After a thousand years of discipline he sees the supreme anuṣṭubh verse, and then the Lord Nṛsiṃha; by the mantra he creates.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language, world-fate_
