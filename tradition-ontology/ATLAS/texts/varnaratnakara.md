### siddha-list <a id="tea-varnaratnakara-siddha-list"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Maithili Varṇaratnākara includes, among its stock lists, a list of eighty-four siddhas that mixes names known from the Nāth tradition (Mīnanātha, Gorakṣanātha, Cauraṅgīnātha) with names known from the Buddhist siddha lists (among them Virūpa, Kāṇha, Jālandhara, Nāgārjuna, Carpaṭi).
