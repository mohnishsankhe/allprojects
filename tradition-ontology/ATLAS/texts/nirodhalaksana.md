## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1ff <a id="tea-nirodhalaksana-1ff"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Nirodha is the soul's absorption in the Lord through forgetting the world, brought about by the Lord's play; its highest form is the gopīs' longing in separation, and those absorbed in him are freed from saṃsāra.
