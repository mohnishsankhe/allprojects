The Brāhmaṇa of the Ṛgvedic hotṛ priests: it explains the recitations of the Soma sacrifice, the Agnihotra and the royal consecration, with the dīkṣā as rebirth of the sacrificer as an embryo (1.3), the legend of Śunaḥśepa recited at the rājasūya (7.13–18), the derivation of the vyāhṛtis and Oṃ from Prajāpati's brooding (5.32), and the rites of kingship and the royal chaplain (book 8).
