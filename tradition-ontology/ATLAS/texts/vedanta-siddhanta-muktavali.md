Prakāśānanda's treatise arguing that the world exists only in being perceived (dṛṣṭi-sṛṣṭi-vāda), that there is one jīva (eka-jīva-vāda), and that ignorance alone accounts for the appearance, with no need of a theory of creation.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-vedanta-siddhanta-muktavali-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The world exists only as perceived (dṛṣṭi-sṛṣṭi): its being is its being seen, as with dream objects; there is one jīva, whose ignorance projects the whole, and no separate account of creation is needed.
