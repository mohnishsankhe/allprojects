## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-3 <a id="tea-aksamalika-upanisad-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Prajāpati asks Guha about the rosary: its beads (coral, pearl, crystal, conch, silver, gold, sandal, lotus seed, rudrākṣa and others), threads of gold, silver and copper, and invoking the fifty letters from a to kṣa into the beads.
