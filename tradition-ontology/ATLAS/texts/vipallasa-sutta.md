## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-7 <a id="tea-vipallasa-sutta-1-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Four perversions of perception, mind and view: taking the impermanent as permanent, suffering as happiness, what is not-self as self, and the unattractive as attractive; beings so perverted, yoked by Māra's yoke, wander on; when Buddhas arise and teach, the wise regain their right mind.
