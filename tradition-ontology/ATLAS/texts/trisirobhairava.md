# Triśirobhairava

A Trika tantra (the 'three-headed Bhairava') cited by Abhinavagupta and Jayaratha; the Vijñāna Bhairava mentions the 'three-headed' (triśiras) form of Bhairava among the forms it transcends (VBT 3, 11).
