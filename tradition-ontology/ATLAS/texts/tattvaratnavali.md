The mantra way is not explained here because it is very profound, meant for those devoted to the profound, and extensive in its exposition of the four seals; as it is said: 'Though the goal is the same, the mantra treatise is superior, being free of confusion, rich in means, not difficult, and for those of sharp faculties.'

> ekārthatve'pyasammohāt bahūpāyādaduṣkarāt | tīkṣṇendriyādhikārācca mantraśāstraṃ viśiṣyate ||
