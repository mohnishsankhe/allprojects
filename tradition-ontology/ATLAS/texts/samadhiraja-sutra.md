The 'King of Samādhis' (also Candrapradīpa): the Buddha teaches the young Candraprabha the samādhi of the sameness of the nature of all dharmas, in verses that liken all dharmas to dreams, magical displays, the moon in water and mirages, reject 'is' and 'is not' as extremes, and praise recollection of the Buddha, patience and renunciation; Candraprabha's past life as the monk who gave up his life to preserve the dharma. Much quoted by Candrakīrti and by Gampopa for Mahāmudrā.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 4 <a id="tea-samadhiraja-sutra-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The recollection of the Buddha (buddhānusmṛti): one should call to mind the Tathāgata's body of gold, his marks and qualities, and so enter the samādhi; the buddha recollected is known as neither form nor formless.

### 9.17 <a id="tea-samadhiraja-sutra-9-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As a young woman in a dream sees a son born and die, rejoicing at his birth and grieving at his death — know all dharmas to be like that.

> yathā kumārī supināntarasmin sā putra jātaṃ ca mṛtaṃ ca paśyati

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: all · types: ultimate_

terms: [svapna](../terms/svapna.md), [māyopama](../terms/mayopama.md)

### 9.19 <a id="tea-samadhiraja-sutra-9-19"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As the moon is seen at night in clear, unstirred water, yet cannot be grasped, empty, void of moon — know all dharmas to be like that.

> agrāhya tuccho jala candraśūnya tathopamān jānatha sarvadharmān

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: all · types: ultimate_

### 9.27 <a id="tea-samadhiraja-sutra-9-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Is' and 'is not' are both extremes; 'pure' and 'impure' are extremes; therefore, abandoning both extremes, the wise one does not take a stand even in the middle.

> astīti nāstīti ubhe 'pi antā śuddhī aśuddhīti ime 'pi antā | tasmādubhe anta vivarjayitvā madhye 'pi sthānaṃ na karoti paṇḍitaḥ

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_
