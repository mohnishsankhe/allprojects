## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-20 <a id="tea-anupada-sutta-2-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

For a fortnight Sāriputta saw insight into states one by one as they occurred: in the first jhāna applied and sustained thought, rapture, bliss, unification of mind, contact, feeling, perception, volition, mind, zeal, decision, energy, mindfulness, equanimity and attention were defined; he knew them arising, present and disappearing — and so on through the attainments to cessation, from which he emerged mindful. He is the Buddha's true son, born of the Dhamma.
