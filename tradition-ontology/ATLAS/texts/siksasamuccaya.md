### k1 <a id="tea-siksasamuccaya-k1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When fear and suffering are as unwelcome to me as to others, what is special about myself that I protect it and not the other?

> yadā mama pareṣāṃ ca bhayaṃ duḥkhaṃ ca na priyam | tadātmanaḥ ko viśeṣo yattaṃ rakṣāmi netaram ||

### k2 <a id="tea-siksasamuccaya-k2"></a>

One who wishes to end suffering and reach the far shore of happiness should make firm the root of faith and set the mind firmly on awakening.

> duḥkhāntaṃ kartukāmena sukhāntaṃ gantumicchatā | śraddhāmūlaṃ dṛḍhīkṛtya bodhau kāryā matirdṛḍhā ||

### k4 <a id="tea-siksasamuccaya-k4"></a>

The giving up of one's body, one's enjoyments and one's merit of the three times to all beings, and the protecting, purifying and increasing of these (is the bodhisattva's training).

> ātmabhāvasya bhogānāṃ tryadhvavṛtteḥ śubhasya ca | utsargaḥ sarvasattvebhyastadrakṣā śuddhivardhanam ||
