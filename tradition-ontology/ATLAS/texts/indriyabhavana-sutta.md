## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-10 <a id="tea-indriyabhavana-sutta-2-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Pārāsariya teaches not seeing forms with the eye; then the blind and deaf would be developed. The noble development: when the agreeable, disagreeable or both arise, one understands 'this is conditioned, gross, dependently arisen; this is peaceful, sublime — equanimity', and they cease as quickly as an eyelid blinks; the trainee is repelled by them, the noble one with developed faculties abides perceiving the unrepulsive in the repulsive and vice versa, or equanimous.
