A Bhairava tantra of the cult of Amṛteśvara / Mṛtyuñjit, 'the Eye', whose mantra conquers death. Besides ritual for protection, healing and against possession, it teaches gross, subtle and supreme meditation, the latter two on the subtle body (cakras, ādhāras, lakṣyas, vyomans) and on the formless. Commented on by Kṣemarāja (Netroddyota).
