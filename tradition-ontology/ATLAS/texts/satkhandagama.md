The oldest Digambara scripture, in six parts (Jīvasthāna, Kṣudrakabandha, Bandhasvāmitvavicaya, Vedanā, Vargaṇā, Mahābandha), analysing souls through the fourteen stages of quality (guṇasthāna) and fourteen 'ways of search' (mārgaṇā), and the bondage of karma in minute detail. It opens with the Namaskāra mantra. Tradition says it preserves part of the Agrāyaṇīya Pūrva (the Karmaprābhṛta) taught by Dharasena.

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1.1.1 <a id="tea-satkhandagama-1-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The scripture opens with the fivefold salutation: homage to the Arhats, to the Siddhas, to the Ācāryas, to the Upādhyāyas, to all the Sādhus in the world.

> ṇamo arihaṃtāṇaṃ, ṇamo siddhāṇaṃ, ṇamo āiriyāṇaṃ, ṇamo uvajjhāyāṇaṃ, ṇamo loe savvasāhūṇaṃ

### 1.1.4 <a id="tea-satkhandagama-1-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Souls are to be investigated through fourteen 'ways of search' (mārgaṇā): destiny, sense-faculty, body-type, activity, sex, passion, knowledge, restraint, perception, soul-colour, capacity for liberation, right faith, rationality and intake of nourishment.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind, karma-liberation_

terms: [mārgaṇā](../terms/margana.md) · concepts: [The fourteen ways of search (mārgaṇā)](../concepts/fourteen-marganas.md), [The fourteen stages of quality (guṇasthāna)](../concepts/fourteen-gunasthanas.md)

### 1.1.93 <a id="tea-satkhandagama-1-1-93"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Of human females who have completed their development, the sūtra states in which of the stages of quality (guṇasthāna) they are found; the manuscripts' reading of whether the word 'restrained' (saṃjada) occurs here — and so whether women reach the stages of full renunciation and beyond — became the subject of a 20th-century Digambara controversy; the Dhavalā explains the passage as concerning psychological sex (bhāva-veda).
