## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### p58 <a id="tea-jnanasiddhi-p58"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Indrabhūti explains the opening of the Guhyasamāja: 'the heart' is gnosis; that very gnosis is the vajra-woman, being of the nature of the indivisible, and that very (gnosis) is the 'bhaga' because it destroys all afflictions.

> hṛdayaṃ jñānaṃ tadeva vajjayoṣit abhedyāpakṣāsvabhāvatvāt tadeva bhagaṃ sarvakleśabhañjanāt
