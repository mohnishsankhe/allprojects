## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-41 <a id="tea-khaggavisana-sutta-1-41"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Laying aside violence toward all beings, not wishing for sons or companions, seeing the danger born of affection, wander alone like the rhinoceros horn; if you find a wise companion of good conduct, live with him glad and mindful; if not, wander alone.
