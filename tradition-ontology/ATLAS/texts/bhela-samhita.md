## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### ci.8 <a id="tea-bhela-samhita-ci-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Five kinds of insanity have been told. Of the senses and their activity, the mind (manas), located between the head and the palate, is supreme over all the senses; seated there it knows the senses' objects near it and governs the three states (bhāvas); strength consisting of all the senses arises from the mind; the cause of all cognitions is citta, which rests in the heart; citta is the cause of all actions — those of good citta go on the good path, those of bad citta go astray.

> śirastālvantaragataṃ sarvendriyaparaṃ manaḥ
