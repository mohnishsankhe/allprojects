### verse-on-intent <a id="tea-locana-rocani-verse-on-intent"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Jīva remarks that some things in his writing were written by his own wish and some by the wish of others; what is consistent with what comes before and after is the former, the rest the latter.
