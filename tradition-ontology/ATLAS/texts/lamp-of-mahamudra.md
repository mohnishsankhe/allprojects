### view <a id="tea-lamp-of-mahamudra-view"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Mahāmudrā is taught as ground, path and fruition: the ground is the mind's nature, the path its recognition and sustaining, the fruition its full manifestation; Mahāmudrā and Dzogchen are of one essence.
