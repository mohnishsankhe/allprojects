A short work ascribed to Gorakṣanātha on the four yogas — mantra, laya, haṭha and rāja — in which haṭha practices (the great seal, great lock and great piercing) serve rājayoga, the goal; it describes the stages of practice and the sounds heard in them. Several of its verses reappear in the Haṭhapradīpikā.
