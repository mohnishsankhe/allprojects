# Lamdre root text: the Vajra Verses (lam 'bras bu dang bcas pa'i gdams ngag)

`src:lamdre` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Mārgaphalānvitāvavādaka, gsung ngag rin po che lam 'bras bu dang bcas pa'i gdams ngag, rdo rje tshig rkang (Vajra Verses), lam 'bras gzhung, lam zab bla ma dam tshig lus so sor brtags pa'i 'gog pa lam 'bras bu dang bcas pa'i gdams ngag dang man ngag du bcas pa (Derge colophon title)
**Language:** Tibetan
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md), [Ngor (Ngorpa) Sakya](../lineages/ngor.md), [Tshar (Tsharpa) Sakya](../lineages/tshar.md), [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md)
**Genre:** vajra verses (pith instruction)
**Authors:** 
  - [Virūpa](../teachers/virupa.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: spoken by Virūpa after the goddess Nairātmyā bestowed on him the four empowerments in her maṇḍala; memorized and later written down (the tradition says the text was long kept oral, first set down by Sachen Kunga Nyingpo); scholarly: attributed to Virūpa (Indian, c. 9th–10th c.?); the Tibetan text is known through Drokmi's transmission; included in the Derge Tengyur as Tōh 2284; confidence: moderate

**Structure:** short prose-verse text of about 230 lines in the Derge Tengyur (Tōh 2284, fols. 139a.6–142b.6)
**Availability:** digitized-original

Virūpa's terse instruction on 'the Path together with its Result': the three visions (impure, experiential, pure), the three continua (all-base causal, body-method, Mahāmudrā result), the four authorities, the obstacles and their protections, the gathering of the elements and the signs, and the progression through the grounds to the thirteenth, Vajradhara.

### 139a.7 <a id="tea-lamdre-139a-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

For sentient beings, the appearance of the afflictions is the impure vision; for the yogin in concentration, the appearance of experience is the experiential vision; the inexhaustible ornamental wheel of the body, speech and mind of the Sugatas is the pure vision.

### 139b.1-3 <a id="tea-lamdre-139b-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · [AI-translated]

Because the all-base causal continuum contains saṃsāra and nirvāṇa, it is the root continuum; the body-method continuum — with the causal empowerments, the path of the generation stage and the rest, keeping the commitments of equipoise, restoring breakages with the vajra-ḍākinī rites, pleasing with the sense-objects, meditating the four empowerments in the four sessions of the path so that, relying on the body, the obscurations to great bliss are cleared and awakening dawns — is the explanatory continuum; Mahāmudrā is the result continuum, in which omniscience comes through the four result-empowerments.

> kun gzhi rgyu rgyud la 'khor 'das [(]chang [,]tshang [)]bas rtsa rgyud/ lus thabs rgyud sogs la gdan sogs gsum pa bzhi yis rgyu'i dbang / lam du bskyed rim la sogs lnga pa bzhis bstan/ mnyam gzhag gi dam tshig sogs bsrung / rdo rje mkha' 'gro la sogs kyi nyams chags bskang / 'dod yon sogs lnga yis mnyes pa sogs bya/ lam gyi thun bzhi la dbang bzhi bsgoms pas lus la brten nas bde chen gyi sgrib pa 'dag cing 'tshang rgya bar gsal bas bshad rgyud/ phyag rgya chen po 'bras bu'i rgyud ni/ 'bras bu'i dbang bzhis thams cad mkhyen to/ /

_level: bridging · standpoint: analytic · path: ritual, meditation · stage: advanced (rgyud gsum (the three continua)) · types: ultimate, practice, body-layers_

### 139b.5-6 <a id="tea-lamdre-139b-5-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · [AI-translated]

### 139b.7 <a id="tea-lamdre-139b-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · [AI-translated]

The obstacles on the path of the yogin who has fallen to the side of method are guarded against by firm faith, the protection wheel, mantra recitation and mantra knots; (and by knowing that) phenomena are the appearance of mind itself, other-dependent dependent arising, being reflections — they are guarded against by not being separated from the meaning of the ocean of reality.

> thabs kyi phyogs su lhung ba'i rnal 'byor pa'i lam gyi bar chad ni/ dad pa brtan pos srung ba'i 'khor lo/ sngags zlos/ sngags mdud/ chos rnams sems nyid snang ba/ gzhan dbang rten 'brel/ gzugs brnyan yin phyir/ de kho na nyid rgya mtsho'i don dang ma bral bas bsrung /

### 140a.5-6 <a id="tea-lamdre-140a-5-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · [AI-translated]

The three visions of undeviating meditation — the seeing-appearance, dream and experience — appear throughout the three realms as preceded by conceptions. When earth dissolves into water there is (the appearance of) a mirage; water into fire, smoke; fire into wind, fireflies; wind into consciousness, a burning lamp; consciousness into clear light, like a cloudless sky.

> bsgom pa mi 'chor ba'i mthong snang rmi lam nyams gsum gsum ni rnam rtog sngon du song ba ltar khams gsum kun du snang ngo / / sa chu la thim na smig rgyu/ chu me la thim na du ba/ me rlung la thim na srin bu me khyer/ rlung rnam par shes pa la thim na mar me 'bar ba/ rnam par shes pa 'od gsal la thim na nam mkha' sprin med lta bu/

### 140b.5-6 <a id="tea-lamdre-140b-5-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · [AI-translated]

The three sets of outer dependent arisings of undeviating meditation (reversal of winds and the rest), the five inner ḍākinīs and the five bodies of the Tathāgatas create the dependent arising of great awakening; knowing that the accomplishments arise in oneself, the mind that hopes and asks for them is cleared away. Knowing that māras and deviations are one's own path and dependent arising, one raises faults into qualities and, whether stages appear or not, lets whatever arises go free.

### 141a.3-4 <a id="tea-lamdre-141a-3-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · [AI-translated]

The path of the inner Buddhas reaches the peak of existence; the patience for the unborn dharma, hard to bear, is the patience of emptiness; when the non-conceptual mind turns in the central channel, it is patience; having passed beyond the dharmas of saṃsāra, the dharmas of the path of nirvāṇa are complete, so it is the supreme among dharmas.

### 141b.2 <a id="tea-lamdre-141b-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _restricted: summary only_ · [AI-translated]

Without seeing inwardly one does not see outwardly; then the liṅga is firm; as the drop abides at the place of the co-emergent at the vajra-tip, the body is intoxicated and faints with bliss, one does not recognise self or other; the inner sign is that one finger-width of wind ceases, the outer sign that the drop is stabilised at half the secret place — this is the first ground.

### 142b.5 <a id="tea-lamdre-142b-5"></a>
