### selected <a id="tea-yogasutra-vrtti-yasovijaya-selected"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Commenting on selected sūtras of Patañjali, Yaśovijaya accepts the method of stilling the mind but corrects the doctrine: the self is a knower that changes in its modes, not an unchanging witness; the Lord is the liberated omniscient, not a creator; and liberation is the soul's own knowledge, not mere isolation.
