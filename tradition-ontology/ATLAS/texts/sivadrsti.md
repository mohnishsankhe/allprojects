# Śivadṛṣṭi

`src:sivadrsti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Original title:** शिवदृष्टि
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md), [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md)
**Genre:** prakaraṇa (verse treatise)
**Authors:** 
  - [Somānanda](../teachers/somananda.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 900-950 CE; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 7 āhnikas; Utpaladeva's vṛtti survives for the first chapters
**Availability:** digitized-original

Somānanda's foundational treatise of the Pratyabhijñā: everything is Śiva, endowed with the powers of will, knowledge and action (ch. 1); the grammarians' non-dualism of speech is refuted - their highest, paśyantī, is only the knowledge-power at the level of Sadāśiva (ch. 2); rival schools are criticized (chs. 3-6); Śivahood, once known with firm conviction, needs no further means (7.5-6); and the author's lineage from Durvāsas is told (7.107-122).
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: with Utpaladeva's Vṛtti, Kashmir Series of Texts and Studies (KSTS), Srinagar 54 (1934)
**Commentaries on this text:** [Śivadṛṣṭivṛtti](sivadrsti-vrtti.md)

## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 2 <a id="tea-sivadrsti-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Chapter 2 argues at length that paśyantī cannot be the ultimate: the grammarians' Brahman of speech is a limited, sequential level (knowledge-power at the Sadāśiva level), while the ultimate is Śiva, endowed with will, knowledge and action.

_level: conventional · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: dispute_

### 2.1 <a id="tea-sivadrsti-2-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

What for us is the power of knowledge, the state of Sadāśiva, is for the good grammarians paśyantī, [their] supreme state.

> athāsmākaṃ jñānaśaktir yā sadāśivarūpatā / vaiyākaraṇasādhūnāṃ paśyantī sā parā sthitiḥ

### 7.1-2 <a id="tea-sivadrsti-7-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Whether it is known or not, Śivahood is unobstructed: does fire, known or unknown, not give light? Is unrecognized gold a stone?

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate_

teachers: [Somānanda](../teachers/somananda.md)

### 7.5-6 <a id="tea-sivadrsti-7-5-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Once Śivahood, present in all, has been known with firm conviction - by a valid means, by scripture or by the teacher's word - there is nothing further to be done by any instrument or even by contemplation.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: advanced (anupāya (as read by the later tradition)) · types: karma-liberation, practice_

### 7.107-122 <a id="tea-sivadrsti-7-107-122"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When the Śaiva secrets were lost at the start of the Kali age, Śiva as Śrīkaṇṭha urged the sage Durvāsas to preserve them; Durvāsas created a mind-born son, Tryambakāditya; fourteen mind-born siddhas followed; the fifteenth married a brāhmaṇa woman; their descendant Saṅgamāditya came to Kashmir, followed by Varṣāditya, Aruṇāditya, Ānanda and Somānanda, who composed the Śivadṛṣṭi; this Tryambaka lineage ('Terambā') spread as a maṭhikā.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission_
