## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-distinguishing-consciousness-and-wisdom-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The eight consciousnesses are delusion; when their nature is recognized they are the wisdoms: the all-ground consciousness is mirror-like wisdom, the afflicted mind is the wisdom of equality, the mental consciousness is discriminating wisdom, the five sense consciousnesses are all-accomplishing wisdom, and their stainless expanse is dharmadhātu wisdom.
