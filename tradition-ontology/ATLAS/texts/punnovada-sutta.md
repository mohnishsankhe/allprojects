## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 5-6 <a id="tea-punnovada-sutta-5-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Puṇṇa will go to Sunāparanta; if they abuse him he will think them kind for not striking him, if they strike, kind for not using a knife, and if they kill him, that some seek a knife-wielder out of disgust with the body; the Buddha says he is fit for that land.
