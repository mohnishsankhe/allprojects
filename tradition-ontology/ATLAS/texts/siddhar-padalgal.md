The anthology of the Tamil Siddhars' songs, printed since the 19th c. under titles such as Cittar pāṭalkaḷ and Periya ñāṉakkōvai. It gathers Sivavākkiyar, Pattinattār, Pattirakiriyār, Pāmbāṭṭi, Iṭaikkāṭar, Kuṭampai, Aḻukaṇṇi, Kaṭuveḷi, Akappēy and others: songs on the inner yoga, the body, impermanence, the guru and Civam, and against caste, idols and ritual.
