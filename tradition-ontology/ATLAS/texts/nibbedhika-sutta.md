## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 33.3-33.4 <a id="tea-nibbedhika-sutta-33-3-33-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

It is intention (cetanā), monks, that I call kamma; having intended, one acts by body, speech and mind.

> Cetanāhaṁ, bhikkhave, kammaṁ vadāmi. Cetayitvā kammaṁ karoti—

### 33-38 <a id="tea-nibbedhika-sutta-33-38"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Kamma should be known: its source is contact; its diversity is kamma that leads to hell, the animal realm, the ghost realm, the human world and the deva world; its result is threefold — in this life, the next, or later; its cessation is the cessation of contact; the way is the noble eightfold path.

_level: conventional · standpoint: causal · path: action · stage: all · types: karma-liberation_
