Thirty-one chapters explaining the basis in detail: embryology, the similes, anatomy (channels, vital points), physiology, the classes of the body, the signs of death, the causes, conditions, onset, characteristics and classes of disease, daily, seasonal and occasional conduct, diet and its restrictions, the tastes and potencies of medicines, instruments, health preservation, diagnosis, treatment principles, and the qualities of the physician.

### 8 <a id="tea-gyushi-shegyu-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The distant cause of all disease is ignorance — the failure to understand selflessness. As a bird soaring in the sky is never parted from its shadow, so beings, even when happy, are never parted from it. From ignorance arise desire, hatred and delusion, and from them rlung, mkhris pa and bad kan respectively.

### 31 <a id="tea-gyushi-shegyu-31"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The physician must have six qualities: intelligence; a pure, altruistic intention — love, compassion, joy and equanimity towards patients; commitment to the physician's vows; skill in body, speech and mind; diligence in the work; and knowledge of worldly conduct. Such a physician is like the Medicine Buddha to beings.
