### root-downfalls <a id="tea-fruit-clusters-of-siddhis-root-downfalls"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The tantric pledges are guarded above all by avoiding the fourteen root downfalls, the first of which is disparaging one's vajra master; a root downfall destroys the vows and the basis of attainment unless restored by rite.
