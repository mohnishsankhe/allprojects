Kulaśekhara Āḻvār's 105 verses in ten decads: longing to live among devotees at Śrīraṅgam, to be born even as a fish or a step at Tiruvēṅkaṭam, clinging to the Lord of Vittuvakkōṭu though he cause grief, Devakī's and Daśaratha's laments, and a lullaby for Rāma.

### 4 <a id="tea-perumal-tirumoli-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Āḻvār asks to be born at Tiruvēṅkaṭam in any form at all — a crane in its pool, a fish in its streams, a bush or a stone on the hill, a stream, a path — only to be near the Lord.

### 4.9 <a id="tea-perumal-tirumoli-4-9"></a>

### 5.1 <a id="tea-perumal-tirumoli-5-1"></a>
