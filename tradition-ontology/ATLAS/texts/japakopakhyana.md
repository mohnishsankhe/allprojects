Bhīṣma teaches the path and destiny of the reciter (jāpaka) of the Veda/Sāvitrī, told through the story of a brāhmaṇa reciter who meets Sāvitrī, Dharma, Kāla, Mṛtyu and Yama, and of king Ikṣvāku's dispute with him over the gift of the fruit of recitation.
