An extensive commentary (vṛtti) on the Brahma Sūtras, known only from Rāmānuja's statement that earlier teachers abridged it and that he follows it; no manuscript is known.

_Notes: Cited at the opening of Rāmānuja's Śrībhāṣya. Some later tradition identifies Bodhāyana with Upavarṣa; not settled._
