# Prakaraṇapāda

**Alternate titles:** Abhidharma-prakaraṇa-śāsana, Pinlei zu lun 品類足論 (T1542), Zhong shifen apitan lun 眾事分阿毘曇論 (T1541)
**Language:** Sanskrit (lost or fragmentary), Chinese
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
**Genre:** abhidharma
**Authors:** 
  - [Vasumitra](../teachers/vasumitra.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Vasumitra (Yaśomitra; Chinese tradition); scholarly: Attribution to disciples of the Buddha is traditional; the works are dated by scholars to c. 3rd c. BCE – 1st c. CE; confidence: disputed
**Structure:** Eight chapters; T1542 18 fascicles (Xuanzang, 659); T1541 12 fascicles (Guṇabhadra and Bodhiyaśas, 5th c.)
**Availability:** digitized-translation

One of the six 'feet'; systematizes the dharmas into five groups (rūpa, citta, caitasika, cittaviprayukta, asaṃskṛta) — the classification later canonized in the seventy-five dharmas.

_Notes: Yaśomitra's list (Sphuṭārthā on AK 1.3, checked locally): Jñānaprasthāna – Kātyāyanīputra; Prakaraṇapāda – Vasumitra; Vijñānakāya – Devaśarman; Dharmaskandha – Śāriputra; Prajñaptiśāstra – Maudgalyāyana; Dhātukāya – Pūrṇa; Saṅgītiparyāya – Mahākauṣṭhila. Chinese attributions differ._
