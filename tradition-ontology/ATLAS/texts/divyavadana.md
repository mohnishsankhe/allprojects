### 12 <a id="tea-divyavadana-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The six tīrthika teachers, losing support to the Buddha, challenge him to a contest of wonders at Śrāvastī; the Buddha performs the great miracle and the tīrthikas are routed; Pūraṇa drowns himself.

### 28 <a id="tea-divyavadana-28"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

In the Aśoka cycle, when a follower of the Nirgrantha at Puṇḍravardhana drew the Buddha bowing at the Nirgrantha's feet, Aśoka ordered the Ājīvikas there to be killed; later his own brother Vītāśoka was killed in a similar persecution, after which Aśoka forbade such killing.
