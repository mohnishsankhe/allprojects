## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### mn10 <a id="tea-papancasudani-mn10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The four foundations of mindfulness are matched to four kinds of practitioner: contemplation of the body for the dull one of craving temperament, of feeling for the keen one of craving temperament, of mind for the dull one of speculative (view) temperament, of dhammas for the keen one of view temperament.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice_

terms: [satipaṭṭhāna](../terms/satipatthana.md) · concepts: [The six temperaments (carita)](../concepts/six-temperaments.md)

### mn10 <a id="tea-papancasudani-mn10-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Clear comprehension is fourfold: of purpose, of suitability, of the domain (the meditation subject), and of non-delusion.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice_

terms: [sampajañña](../terms/sampajanna.md)

### mn10 <a id="tea-papancasudani-mn10-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

'Ekāyano maggo' is glossed in several ways: a single path, not a forked one; a path to be travelled alone; the path of the One (the Buddha); a path found in this one teaching; and a path leading to the one goal, nibbāna.
