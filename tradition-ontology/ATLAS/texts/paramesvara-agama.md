# Pārameśvarāgama

`src:paramesvara-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Pārameśvara, Pārameśa
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Śrīdevī (Kiraṇa: Śrīrūpa), then the sage Uśanas (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Rudrabheda no. 26 in the Kāmika's order; 7 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** unknown

No. 26 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Rudrabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Śrīdevī (Kiraṇa: Śrīrūpa), then the sage Uśanas. The Kāmika assigns it 7 upabheda(s): Mataṅga, Yakṣiṇīpadma, Pārameśvara, Puṣkara, Suprayoga, Haṃsa, Sāmānya. Its best-known derivatives are the upāgamas Mataṅgapārameśvara and Pauṣkara. Reports of an early Nepalese 'Pārameśvara' manuscript were not verified in this pass.

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.14/10.19: Pārameśa to Śrīpūpa/Śrīrūpa → Uśanas. Śrīkaṇṭhīya: 'Pārameśvara'. In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the necklace (pūrva 1.93–101).
