### stages <a id="tea-shumon-mujinto-ron-stages"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Tōrei sets out the Zen path in stages: arousing faith, entering by seeing the nature, advancing practice after awakening, passing the barriers, full transmission and teaching others — insisting that seeing the nature is only the beginning.
