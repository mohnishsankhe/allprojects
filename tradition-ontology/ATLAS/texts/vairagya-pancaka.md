## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-5 <a id="tea-vairagya-pancaka-1-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Deśika declines the patronage of kings: his wealth is the Lord (Varadarāja) alone, inherited from his forebears; he will not praise the rich for a living.
