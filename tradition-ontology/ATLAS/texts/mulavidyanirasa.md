## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-mulavidyanirasa-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Śaṅkara's 'avidyā' is superimposition itself, a natural error, not a positive beginningless root-ignorance (mūlāvidyā) that is its material cause; the later doctrine of mūlāvidyā (Vivaraṇa and after) departs from Śaṅkara.
