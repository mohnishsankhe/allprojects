### sutete-koso <a id="tea-ippen-shonin-goroku-sutete-koso"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Asked how to say the nenbutsu, Ippen cites Kūya: 'only by casting away' — the practitioner casts away wisdom and ignorance, the distinctions of good and evil, noble and base, the fear of hell and even the wish for the Pure Land, and all the understanding of the schools, and simply says Namu Amida Butsu; then mountains, rivers, grasses and trees, even the sound of the wind and the waves, are all the nenbutsu.
