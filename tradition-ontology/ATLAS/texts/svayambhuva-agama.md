# Svāyambhuvāgama

`src:svayambhuva-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Svāyambhuva, Svayambhū, Svayambhuva (Śrīkaṇṭhīya)
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Nidhana, then Nalinodbhava (Brahmā) (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Rudrabheda no. 13 in the Kāmika's order; 3 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** unknown

No. 13 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Rudrabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Nidhana, then Nalinodbhava (Brahmā). The Kāmika assigns it 3 upabheda(s): Prajāpatimata, Padma, Svāyambhuva. An early 'Svāyambhuva' survives as the Svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgraha (src:svayambhuvasutrasangraha); later South Indian material under the title is not assessed here.

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.16/10.24: Nidhana → Padmasambhava. Śrīkaṇṭhīya: 'Svayambhuva'. In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the two breasts (pūrva 1.93–101).
