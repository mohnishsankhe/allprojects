## Teachings (8: skeleton 8)

### 5-13 <a id="tea-ariyapariyesana-sutta-5-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Two searches: the ignoble search, when one subject to birth, ageing, sickness, death, sorrow and defilement seeks what is likewise subject to them (wives, children, gold); the noble search, when one seeks the unborn, unageing, unailing, deathless, sorrowless, undefiled supreme security from bondage, nibbāna. The bodhisatta set out on the noble search.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, ultimate_

terms: [nibbāna](../terms/nibbana.md), [yogakkhema](../terms/yogakkhema.md) · concepts: [The noble search](../concepts/noble-search.md)

### 12.2 <a id="tea-ariyapariyesana-sutta-12-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

One who is himself subject to birth, seeing the danger in what is subject to birth, seeks the unborn supreme security from bondage, nibbāna … (and so for ageing, sickness, death, sorrow and defilement).

> Idha, bhikkhave, ekacco attanā jātidhammo samāno jātidhamme ādīnavaṁ viditvā ajātaṁ anuttaraṁ yogakkhemaṁ nibbānaṁ pariyesati, attanā jarādhammo samāno jarādhamme ādīnavaṁ viditvā ajaraṁ anuttaraṁ yogakkhemaṁ nibbānaṁ pariyesati, attanā byādhidhammo samāno byādhidhamme ādīnavaṁ viditvā abyādhiṁ anuttaraṁ yogakkhemaṁ nibbānaṁ pariyesati, attanā maraṇadhammo samāno maraṇadhamme ādīnavaṁ viditvā amataṁ anuttaraṁ yogakkhemaṁ nibbānaṁ pariyesati, attanā sokadhammo samāno sokadhamme ādīnavaṁ viditvā asokaṁ anuttaraṁ yogakkhemaṁ nibbānaṁ pariyesati, attanā saṅkilesadhammo samāno saṅkilesadhamme ādīnavaṁ viditvā asaṅkiliṭṭhaṁ anuttaraṁ yogakkhemaṁ nibbānaṁ pariyesati.

### 15-17 <a id="tea-ariyapariyesana-sutta-15-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The bodhisatta learned from Āḷāra Kālāma the attainment of the sphere of nothingness and from Uddaka Rāmaputta that of neither-perception-nor-non-perception (taught by Rāma); each offered him leadership, but he left, seeing that these did not lead to disenchantment, cessation, peace, direct knowledge, awakening and nibbāna.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: teacher-transmission, practice_

terms: [ākiñcaññāyatana](../terms/akincannayatana.md), [nevasaññānāsaññāyatana](../terms/nevasannanasannayatana.md) · concepts: [The four formless attainments](../concepts/formless-attainments.md) · teachers: [Āḷāra Kālāma](../teachers/alara-kalama.md), [Uddaka Rāmaputta](../teachers/uddaka-ramaputta.md)

### 18.3 <a id="tea-ariyapariyesana-sutta-18-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Knowledge and vision arose in me: unshakeable is my liberation; this is my last birth; there is now no renewed existence.

> ‘akuppā me vimutti, ayamantimā jāti, natthi dāni punabbhavo’ti.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: experiential · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation_

### 19-21 <a id="tea-ariyapariyesana-sutta-19-21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Dhamma he had attained is deep, hard to see, peaceful, sublime, beyond reasoning, to be experienced by the wise; this generation delights in attachment, so dependent origination and nibbāna are hard to see, and his mind inclined to inaction. Brahmā Sahampati implored him to teach; surveying the world he saw beings like lotuses at different depths, some with little dust in their eyes, and declared the doors to the deathless open.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission_

terms: [amata](../terms/amata.md) · concepts: [Brahmā's request](../concepts/brahma-yacana.md)

### 25 <a id="tea-ariyapariyesana-sutta-25"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

On the road between Gayā and Bodhgayā the Ājīvaka Upaka sees the newly awakened Buddha, remarks that his faculties are clear and his complexion bright, and asks who his teacher is. The Buddha declares himself self-awakened, without a teacher, the victor; Upaka says 'may it be so, friend', shakes his head and takes a by-path.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: experiential · path: general · stage: unmarked · types: teacher-transmission, powers-experiences_

teachers: [Upaka the Ājīvaka](../teachers/upaka-ajivaka.md), [Gotama Buddha](../teachers/gotama-buddha.md)

### 25-30 <a id="tea-ariyapariyesana-sutta-25-30"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

On the road to Benares the Ājīvika Upaka met him; the Buddha declared himself the one who has no teacher; at the deer park he taught the five monks, who attained the unborn, deathless nibbāna.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission_

concepts: [The first teaching and the first disciples](../concepts/first-teaching.md) · teachers: [Aññāsi Koṇḍañña](../teachers/kondanna.md), [Upaka](../teachers/upaka.md)

### 31-33 <a id="tea-ariyapariyesana-sutta-31-33"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ascetics who enjoy the five cords of sensual pleasure tied and infatuated are like a deer caught in a snare; those who enjoy them untied, seeing the danger, are like a deer lying on the snares but not caught; a monk who enters the jhānas and destroys the effluents has blindfolded Māra and gone beyond attachment to the world.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice, karma-liberation_

terms: [kāmaguṇa](../terms/kamaguna.md) · obstacles: [Māra](../obstacles/mara.md)
