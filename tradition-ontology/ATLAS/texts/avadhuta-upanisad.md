## Teachings (8: sourced 8)

### 1 <a id="tea-avadhuta-upanisad-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Sāṅkṛti asks Dattātreya who the avadhūta is; the name is explained: from imperishability (a), excellence (va), having shaken off (dhūta) the bonds of saṃsāra, and being the meaning of 'that you are' (ta).

> akṣaratvād vareṇyatvād dhūtasaṃsārabandhanāt | tattvamasyādilakṣyatvād avadhūta itīryate

### 2 <a id="tea-avadhuta-upanisad-2"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

The yogin who has transcended the castes and stages and abides always in the Self is called beyond castes and stages (ativarṇāśramin), the avadhūta.

`sourced` · confidence high

Bliss is his bird-body — joy its head, delight and great delight its wings, Brahman its support; not by works, offspring or wealth but by renunciation some have reached immortality.

`sourced` · confidence high

Let the body attend to worship, bathing, cleanliness and alms; let speech repeat the praṇava and read the Upaniṣads; I am the witness, I do nothing and cause nothing to be done.

### 27-32 <a id="tea-avadhuta-upanisad-27-32"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high
