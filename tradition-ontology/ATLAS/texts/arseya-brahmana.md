# Ārṣeya Brāhmaṇa

**Genre:** brāhmaṇa (index)

The Kauthuma Sāmaveda index of the seers (ṛṣi) of the sāman chants; the tradition holds that one who uses a chant without knowing its seer, metre and deity gains no fruit.
