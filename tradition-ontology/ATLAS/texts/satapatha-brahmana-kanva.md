# Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa (Kāṇva recension)

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Genre:** brāhmaṇa
**Part of:** [Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa (Mādhyandina)](satapatha-brahmana.md)
**Authors:** 
  - [Yājñavalkya](../teachers/yajnavalkya.md) — role: revealer; attribution: traditional
**Structure:** 17 kāṇḍas (c. 104 adhyāyas)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The second recension of the Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa, transmitted by the Kāṇva school of the White Yajurveda; its order of books differs from the Mādhyandina, and its last part is the Kāṇva Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad, the version on which Śaṅkara commented.

_Notes: Only partly edited in print for a long time; the Kāṇva BĀU is widely available._
