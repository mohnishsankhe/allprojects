Vācaspati Miśra's sub-commentary on Vyāsa's bhāṣya, the standard explanation of it. It answers the objection that Hiraṇyagarbha, not Patañjali, is the teacher of yoga by reading 'anuśāsana' as re-teaching (1.1), develops the theistic arguments of 1.24–26, and explains the objects of the samprajñāta samādhis in terms of the grasped, the grasping and the grasper.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Pātañjalarahasya](patanjala-rahasya.md)

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-tattvavaisaradi-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Answering the objection that the smṛti names Hiraṇyagarbha, not Patañjali, as the teacher of yoga ('hiraṇyagarbho yogasya vaktā nānyaḥ purātanaḥ'), Vācaspati explains 'anuśāsana' as a teaching that follows (anu) an earlier one: Patañjali re-teaches, he does not originate, the yoga śāstra.
