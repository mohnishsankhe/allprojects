Bṛhaspati's hymn on sacred speech and knowledge: the first speech arose when the ancients gave names; the wise fashioned speech with mind, sifting it like grain in a sieve; by sacrifice they followed speech's track and found her entered into the seers; one who sees does not see her, one who hears does not hear her, but to another she opens herself like a loving wife; knowing the words without their meaning is fruitless.
