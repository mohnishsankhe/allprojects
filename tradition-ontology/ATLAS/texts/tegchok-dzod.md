### trekcho-togal <a id="tea-tegchok-dzod-trekcho-togal"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _restricted: summary only_

Cutting through (khregs chod) releases the practitioner into primordial purity and suits those who prefer ease; direct crossing (thod rgal) brings spontaneous presence to manifestation through the four visions and suits the diligent; the first frees the mind, the second also exhausts the body's elements into light.
