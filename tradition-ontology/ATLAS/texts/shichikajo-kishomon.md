Hōnen's pledge, signed by his disciples, forbidding them to attack the doctrines of other schools or other buddhas and bodhisattvas, to dispute with learned men, to say that nenbutsu people need not keep the precepts and may freely do evil, to teach their own ideas as the master's, and similar conduct.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### articles <a id="tea-shichikajo-kishomon-articles"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Hōnen's disciples pledge: not to attack the Tendai and Shingon teachings or slander other buddhas and bodhisattvas; not to engage the learned or other practitioners in disputes; not to tell those of other practices to abandon them, or mock them; not to say that the nenbutsu has no precepts, encouraging lust, wine and meat, or that one who keeps precepts is a practitioner of miscellaneous practice and that relying on the vow one need not fear evil; not to spread their own ideas as the master's teaching; not to preach wrong doctrines to the ignorant; not to teach heresies in the master's name.
