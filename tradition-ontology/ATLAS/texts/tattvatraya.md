## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1 <a id="tea-tattvatraya-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Cit: the soul is distinct from body, senses, mind, breath and intellect; it is self-luminous, blissful, eternal, atomic, imperceptible, unthinkable, partless, unchanging, the substrate of knowledge, controlled, supported and owned by the Lord; souls are bound, freed or eternal.

### 2 <a id="tea-tattvatraya-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Acit: insentient reality is of three kinds — pure sattva (the substance of Vaikuṇṭha and the Lord's forms), mixed sattva (prakṛti with the three guṇas, the stuff of bodies and worlds) and that devoid of sattva (time).

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: world-fate_

### 3 <a id="tea-tattvatraya-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Īśvara: the Lord is opposed to all evil and has infinite auspicious qualities; he is the cause of the world, its self and support, present in five forms — the supreme, the vyūhas, the descents, the inner controller and the image.
