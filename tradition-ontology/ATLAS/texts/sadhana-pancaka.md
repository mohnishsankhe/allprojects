Five verses giving a graded sequence of about forty instructions, from daily Vedic study and duty, through association with the wise and renunciation, to inquiry into the mahāvākya and abiding as Brahman.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-sadhana-pancaka-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Let the Veda be studied daily and its prescribed acts well performed, offered as worship to the Lord; give up desire-prompted acts, wash away sin, see the faults of worldly pleasure, seek the self, and leave home — the first steps of a graded ladder ending in abiding as Brahman.
