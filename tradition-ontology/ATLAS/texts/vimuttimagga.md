Upatissa's 'Path of Freedom', a manual of virtue, concentration and wisdom closely parallel to (and earlier than) the Visuddhimagga, including ascetic practices, temperaments, the meditation subjects, the higher knowledges and the analysis for insight; the Pali original is lost and it survives complete only in Chinese.
