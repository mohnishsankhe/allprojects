The three 'sin-effacing' verses: ṛta and satya were born from kindled tapas; then night, then the ocean; from the ocean the year, which orders days and nights; the Disposer fashioned sun and moon as before (yathāpūrvam), and heaven, earth, atmosphere and the light.

_Notes: Recited while immersed in water at the bath and in the twilight worship (sandhyā) to efface sin._
