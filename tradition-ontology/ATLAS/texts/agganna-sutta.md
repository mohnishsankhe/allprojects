Against brahmin claims to be born from Brahmā's mouth, the Buddha tells of the evolution of the world and the origin of the social classes by conduct and custom; Dhamma, not birth, is best among people.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 3-9 <a id="tea-agganna-sutta-3-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The brahmins claim to be the best class, born from Brahmā's mouth, his heirs; yet brahmin women are seen menstruating, pregnant and giving birth. Members of all four classes do good and evil; the Dhamma, not birth, is best among people, in this life and the next, and whoever has gone forth and become an arahant is the foremost.

### 7.9 <a id="tea-agganna-sutta-7-9"></a>

### 10-26 <a id="tea-agganna-sutta-10-26"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

At the contraction of the world beings are mostly reborn in the Ābhassara realm, mind-made and self-luminous; as the world re-evolves they taste the savoury earth, lose their radiance, sun and moon appear, coarser foods and sex distinctions arise, property and theft follow, and people elect a king (Mahāsammata); the classes of khattiya, brahmin, vessa and sudda arise from occupations, not from Brahmā.

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: general · stage: all · types: world-fate, dispute_

terms: `trm:kappa` · concepts: `cpt:buddhist-cosmogony`, `cpt:world-cycles` · disputes: `dsp:caste-and-purity`

### 27-31 <a id="tea-agganna-sutta-27-31"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A member of any class who does evil in body, speech and mind goes to a bad destiny; one who does good goes to heaven; one who restrains himself and develops the seven sets of qualities leading to awakening attains nibbāna in this life.

_level: conventional · standpoint: causal · path: action · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, ethics_
