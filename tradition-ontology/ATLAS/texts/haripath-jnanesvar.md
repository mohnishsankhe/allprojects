## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-haripath-jnanesvar-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Whoever stands but a moment at God's door has won the four liberations; say 'Hari' with your mouth — who can count the merit?

> देवाचिये द्वारीं उभा क्षणभरी । तेणें मुक्ति चारी साधियेल्या ॥ हरि मुखें म्हणा हरि मुखें म्हणा । पुण्याची गणना कोण करी ॥
