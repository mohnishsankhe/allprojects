The Bengali songs of Rāmprasād Sen to Kālī (Śyāmā-saṅgīt) and to Umā (āgamanī–vijayā): the devotee as the Mother's child who pleads with, reproaches and surrenders to her; the mind addressed as a farmer who lets the field of human life lie fallow; the world as a 'frame of illusion'; Kālī's name as the refuge at death. The songs are transmitted orally and in 19th-century collections, so wording and attribution vary.
