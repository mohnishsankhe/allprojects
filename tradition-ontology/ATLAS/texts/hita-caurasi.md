Eighty-four Braj padas by Hita Harivaṃśa on the eternal love-play (nitya-vihāra) of Śyāmā (Rādhā) and Śyāma (Kṛṣṇa) in the groves of Vṛndāvana, witnessed by the sakhīs; the scripture of the Rādhāvallabha sampradāya.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 84 padas (summary) <a id="tea-hita-caurasi-passim"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The padas show Śyāmā and Śyāma in unending love-play in the groves of Vṛndāvana — glances, adornment, the swing in the rains, their playful exchange of roles — witnessed by the sakhīs; Rādhā is the centre, Kṛṣṇa her ever-devoted lover, and their love (hita) is never broken by separation.
