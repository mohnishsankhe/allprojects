## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 185a13 <a id="tea-lidai-fabao-ji-185a13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Musang's assemblies: first drawn-out recitation of the Buddha's name to the end of a breath, then the words 'no remembering, no thinking, no forgetting': no remembering is precepts, no thinking is samādhi, no forgetting is wisdom — these three phrases are the dhāraṇī gate. A thought not arising is like the face of a mirror reflecting all things; a thought arising is like the back of a mirror.

> 無憶無念莫妄。無憶是戒。無念是定。莫妄是惠。此三句語即是總持門

### 189a17 <a id="tea-lidai-fabao-ji-189a17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Asked by the minister Du Hongjian whether Musang's three phrases are one or three, Wuzhu says they are one: 'no thought' is complete precepts, samādhi and wisdom.

> 無憶是戒。無念是定。莫妄是惠。又云。念不起戒門
