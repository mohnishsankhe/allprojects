## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 474c25 <a id="tea-wuhui-nianfo-fashi-yizan-474c25"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The samādhi of recollecting the Buddha is the truly unsurpassed, profound and wondrous gate of meditation; Amitābha, king of the Dharma, performs the Buddha's work through the name of his forty-eight vows and saves beings by the power of his vow; the voices of the five-tone recitation reach the eternal palace. Those who reject the golden form as mere marks and the teaching as mere words fall into wrong views.

> 然念佛三昧是真無上深妙禪門矣。以彌陀法王四十八願名號為佛事，願力度眾生

### 476b27-c02 <a id="tea-wuhui-nianfo-fashi-yizan-476b27-c02"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The five assemblies of recitation: first, 'Namo Amituofo' slowly in level tone; second, slowly in level and rising tones; third, neither slow nor fast; fourth, gradually faster; fifth, the four syllables 'Amituofo' ever faster; then hymns of the jewel birds and others are chanted.

> 第一會平聲緩念 南無阿彌陀佛。第二會平上聲緩念 南無阿彌陀佛。第三會非緩非急念 南無阿彌陀佛。第四會漸急念 南無阿彌陀佛。第五會四字轉急念 阿彌陀佛。
