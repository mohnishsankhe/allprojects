Śrīmad Rājacandra's 142 Gujarati dohās (1896) establishing the six fundamentals — the soul exists, is eternal, is the doer and the enjoyer of its karma, liberation exists and there is a means to it — with the marks of the true teacher (sadguru) and of the sectarian (matārthī) and the self-seeker (ātmārthī).

### 1 <a id="tea-atmasiddhi-1"></a>
