## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-5 <a id="tea-bhaktivardhini-1-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

When the seed of devotion (given by grace) is made firm through renunciation, hearing and singing, it grows: love (sneha/prema) destroys attachment to other things, attachment (āsakti) makes the home distasteful, and when there is consuming absorption (vyasana) in Kṛṣṇa one has attained the goal.
