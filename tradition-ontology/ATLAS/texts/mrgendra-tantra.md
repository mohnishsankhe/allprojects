An upāgama of the Kāmika in the tradition's classification, and one of the most systematic Siddhānta scriptures, with four pādas. Its yogapāda lists seven auxiliaries of yoga (prāṇāyāma, pratyāhāra, dhāraṇā, dhyāna, vīkṣaṇa, japa, samādhi) with yoga itself as the eighth; its caryāpāda includes the annual pavitra rite.
