## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 2-5 <a id="tea-brahmanimantanika-sutta-2-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Baka the Brahmā held his realm to be permanent, eternal, complete, where one is not born, does not age or die, and beyond which there is no escape; the Buddha declares that he has fallen into ignorance.

_level: conventional · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: dispute, world-fate_

concepts: [The Brahmā worlds](../concepts/brahma-realms.md)

### 25.1-25.2 <a id="tea-brahmanimantanika-sutta-25-1-25-2"></a>
