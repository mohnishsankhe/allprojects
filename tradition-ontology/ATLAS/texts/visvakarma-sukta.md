Two hymns to Viśvakarman, the All-maker: the seer-priest who offered all these worlds; with eyes, faces, arms and feet on every side he forged heaven and earth; what was the wood, what the tree from which they were fashioned?; he is our father, the disposer, the one name-giver of the gods; you will not find him who made these things — something else stands between you — and the hymn-reciters wander wrapped in mist and chatter.
