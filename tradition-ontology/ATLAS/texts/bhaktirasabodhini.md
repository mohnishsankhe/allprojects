## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### kavitts on Mīrā <a id="tea-bhaktirasabodhini-mira"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Priyādās tells how the Rāṇā sent Mīrā poison, which she drank as Kṛṣṇa's prasād and which turned to nectar; a snake sent in a basket became a śāligrāma; in Vṛndāvana, told that Jīva Gosvāmī would not meet women, she asked whether there was any male in Vraja besides Kṛṣṇa.

### kavitts on Tulsīdās <a id="tea-bhaktirasabodhini-tulsi"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Rebuked by his wife that love for Rāma would save him as love for her could not, Tulsī renounced; a ghost he had watered told him how to meet Hanumān, who attends every telling of the Rāma story in disguise, and Hanumān promised him the vision of Rāma at Citrakūṭa.
