## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-7 <a id="tea-nalakalapi-sutta-2-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ageing and death, birth … are not self-made, not made by another, not both, not arisen by chance, but conditioned. Consciousness and name-and-form support each other like two sheaves of reeds leaning against each other: if one is pulled away, the other falls.
