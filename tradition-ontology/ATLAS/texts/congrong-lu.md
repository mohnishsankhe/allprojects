## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-congrong-lu-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Case 1: the World-Honoured One ascended the seat; Mañjuśrī struck the gavel: 'clearly observe the Dharma of the Dharma King; the Dharma of the Dharma King is thus'; the World-Honoured One got down from the seat. Hongzhi's verse: 'a stretch of true wind — do you see it? … woven into ancient brocade with the image of spring; nothing to be done if the lord of the east lets it leak out.'

> 世尊一日陞座。文殊白槌云。諦觀法王法。法王法如是。世尊便下座
