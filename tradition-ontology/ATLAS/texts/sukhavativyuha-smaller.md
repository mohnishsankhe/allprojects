The Smaller Sūtra (Amituo jing): the Buddha, unasked, describes Sukhāvatī to Śāriputra — its jewelled trees, lotus ponds and birds that sing the dharma; beings cannot be born there by few roots of merit, but whoever holds fast to the name of Amitābha for one to seven days with an undistracted mind will see him appear at death and be born there; the buddhas of the six directions confirm it.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1 <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-smaller-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Sukhāvatī: its beings know no suffering, only joys; it has seven rows of railings, jewel-trees, ponds of the eight virtues with lotuses as large as cartwheels, and birds conjured by Amitābha whose songs proclaim the faculties, powers and limbs of awakening, so that all who hear them recollect the Buddha, dharma and saṅgha.

### 10 <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-smaller-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

One cannot be born in that land with few good roots and little merit: a good man or woman who hears of Amitābha and holds fast to his name for one day, or up to seven days, with a mind undistracted, will see Amitābha and the holy assembly before them at the end of life, and dying without confusion will be born in Sukhāvatī.

> 聞說阿彌陀佛，執持名號，若一日、若二日、若三日、若四日、若五日、若六日、若七日，一心不亂。

### 11 <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-smaller-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The buddhas of the six directions, numberless as the Ganges' sands, extend their tongues over the trichiliocosm and testify to the truth of this teaching, 'protected and borne in mind by all buddhas'.

### skt.10 <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-smaller-skt-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Beings are not born in Amitāyus's field by a small root of good. Whoever hears the name of Amitāyus and keeps it in mind, keeping it in mind with undistracted thought for one night up to seven nights, will at death see Amitāyus standing before him with his assembly, will die with an unconfused mind and be born in Sukhāvatī; therefore one should earnestly aspire to that field.

> नावरमात्रकेण शारिपुत्र कुशलमूलेन अमितायुषस्तथागतस्य बुद्धक्षेत्रे सत्त्वा उपपद्यन्ते । यः कश्चिच्छारिपुत्र कुलपुत्रो वा कुलदुहिता वा तस्य भगवतो ऽमितायुषस्तथागतस्य नामधेयं श्रोष्यति, श्रुत्वा च मनसिकरिष्यति, एकरात्रं वा द्विरात्रं वा त्रिरात्रं वा चतूरात्रं वा पञ्चरात्रं वा षड्रात्रं वा सप्तरात्रं वाविक्षिप्तचित्तो मनसिकरिष्यति
