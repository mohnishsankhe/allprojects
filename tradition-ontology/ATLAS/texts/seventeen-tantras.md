The canonical tantras of the innermost secret instruction series (snying thig), taught by Samantabhadra and transmitted via Garab Dorje, Mañjuśrīmitra, Śrī Siṃha and Vimalamitra. They teach the ground, the lamps, the direct introduction, cutting through, direct crossing and its four visions, the bardos and the fruit; the Reverberation of Sound (Dra Thalgyur) is the root.
