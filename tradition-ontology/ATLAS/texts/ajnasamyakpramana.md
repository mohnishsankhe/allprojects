A ḍākinī instruction that opens with the distinction between gradual and simultaneous persons ('the great medicine of the gradual is great poison for the simultaneous, and vice versa') and then outlines the path: taming afflictions, generation stage, speech isolation and wind, the great-bliss and Mahāmudrā instructions, inner heat and clear light, the illusory body, transference, dream (five points) and the intermediate state.

## Teachings (8: skeleton 8)

### 271a.3 <a id="tea-ajnasamyakpramana-271a-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Homage to the great Vajradhara. By the difference of persons' minds there are the gradual and the simultaneous: this great medicine of the gradual is great poison for the simultaneous; the great medicine of the simultaneous becomes poison for the gradual. Therefore to one with the residue of (previous) training, teach the simultaneous; to beginner beings, teach this gradual (way).

### 271a.5 <a id="tea-ajnasamyakpramana-271a-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

First, to tame the afflictions, analyse the elements and meditate on the aggregates as disintegrating and liberated; the disciple is fully ripened on the path the guru teaches; the generation stage is learned from the texts; isolation of speech and wind meditation are learned from the guru's mouth; the instruction focusing on mind is the instruction of great bliss; the bodhicitta instruction is learned from the guru; the Mahāmudrā instruction is learned from the concise words; having trained in inner heat and clear light, meditate on the illusion-like body.

### 271b.1 <a id="tea-ajnasamyakpramana-271b-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

Transference is threefold (upward transference by the manner of the generation stage, by the actual illusion, and by clear light); the text sketches each and says clear-light transference is done at the time of death, leading to nirvāṇa in the bardo.

### 272a.1 <a id="tea-ajnasamyakpramana-272a-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Dream too has five aspects: recollection, knowing (it) as illusion, training, habituation, and transferring upward. For recollection: even before falling asleep, set the intention and with mindfulness watch again and again for the knowing that is falling asleep; as one falls asleep each time, (recognition) arises on waking for the best and middling.

### 272a.3 <a id="tea-ajnasamyakpramana-272a-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When mindfulness is stable, know (dreams) as illusion; even when not asleep, know appearance and existence as illusion — the actual illusion is the dream. If this is not known as illusion, and the bardo is not known as illusion, the becoming of the bardo is not blocked; one takes birth again and again and wanders in saṃsāra's three realms.

### 272b.5 <a id="tea-ajnasamyakpramana-272b-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

By habituating again and again, dream is blessed, and by that the bardo is blessed. Through the habituation of dream, making the knowing at death clear: when the signs of death are complete, set the mind in what was trained before and enter clear light; at death, the falling asleep — with mindfulness set beforehand — rest in the state of clear light.

### 272b.6 <a id="tea-ajnasamyakpramana-272b-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

(After a list of twelve terms — seed, birthplace, sequence, pervasion, ground, house, action, union, own power and own essence with the bindu — whoever always grows familiar with them becomes the vajra-holder of the siddhi of bliss.) Appearance dissolves, thought dissolves, coarse and subtle dissolve; by familiarity after dissolution natural clear light arises, then the body of union, learner and non-learner; on becoming a non-learner the fruit is said to be attained.

### 273a.1 <a id="tea-ajnasamyakpramana-273a-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The being of the intermediate state, with the form of its former existence and the power of karmic miracles: when it sees method and wisdom (a couple in union) before its eyes, it should regard them as the guru, recall the secret empowerment, abandon jealousy and hatred, and meditate on bliss or non-thought, or recall what it trained in before; recalling in time, the first becoming is blocked; thereafter, practising as above for seven weeks, when the forty-nine (days) pass, becoming in saṃsāra is blocked; abiding thus in illusion, purify by clear light and enter clear light.
