## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-foxing-lun-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Buddha-nature is the suchness revealed by the two emptinesses (of persons and dharmas); it is threefold — abiding in beings as their own nature, emerging through practice, and attained as the fruit; the view that some beings lack buddha-nature is refuted.
