## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 13-33 <a id="tea-canki-sutta-13-33"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The preservation of truth: a person with faith says 'this is my faith' but does not conclude 'only this is true, anything else is wrong'; the same for approval, oral tradition, reasoned reflection and acceptance of a view. Awakening to truth: examining a teacher for greed, hatred and delusion, placing faith, approaching, listening, memorising, examining meaning, pondering, willing, weighing and striving, one realises the ultimate truth with the body; repeated cultivation of these is the final arrival at truth.
