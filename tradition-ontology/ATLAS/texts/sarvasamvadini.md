## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### on-paramatma-sandarbha <a id="tea-sarvasamvadini-on-paramatma-sandarbha"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Because the Lord's powers are inconceivable, his relation to them — and to the souls and the world — is to be held as inconceivable difference-and-non-difference (acintya-bhedābheda), neither simple identity nor simple difference.
