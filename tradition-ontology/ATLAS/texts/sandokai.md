### 459b08 <a id="tea-sandokai-459b08"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The mind of the great sage of India was intimately transmitted west and east; people's faculties are sharp or dull, but the Way has no northern or southern ancestors; the numinous source shines bright and pure, the branch streams flow in the dark; grasping phenomena is delusion, merging with principle is not yet awakening.

> 竺土大仙心。東西密相付。人根有利鈍。道無南北祖。靈源明皎潔。枝派暗流注。執事元是迷。契理亦非悟

### 459b12 <a id="tea-sandokai-459b12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The four elements return to their own natures like a child to its mother: fire is hot, wind moves, water is wet, earth is solid; eyes and colours, ears and sounds, nose and smells, tongue and tastes — each dharma, like leaves from the root, branches out; root and branches must return to the source.

> 四大性自復。如子得其母。火熱風動搖。水濕地堅固。眼色耳音聲。鼻香舌鹹醋。然依一一法。依根葉分布

### 459b15 <a id="tea-sandokai-459b15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Right in light there is darkness — do not meet it as darkness; right in darkness there is light — do not see it as light; light and dark oppose each other like the front and back foot in walking. … I respectfully urge those who study the mystery: do not pass your days in vain.

> 當明中有暗。勿以暗相遇。當暗中有明。勿以明相覩。明暗各相對。比如前後步
