## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 27-33 <a id="tea-bhayabherava-sutta-27-33"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

With his mind concentrated, purified and imperturbable, in the first watch of the night the bodhisatta recollected his many past lives, in the middle watch saw with the divine eye beings dying and reappearing according to their deeds, and in the last watch knew the four truths and the effluents: ignorance was destroyed and knowledge arose, as happens in one who abides diligent, ardent and resolute.
