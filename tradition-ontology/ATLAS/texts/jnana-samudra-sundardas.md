### Jñān Samudra, the final ullāsa (non-duality) <a id="tea-jnana-samudra-sundardas-advaita-ullasa"></a>

At the end the disciple knows the self as Brahman, one without a second; the world is appearance, the guru, the disciple and the teaching dissolve in that knowledge.
