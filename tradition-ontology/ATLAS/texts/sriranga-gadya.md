## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-sriranga-gadya-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Rāmānuja surrenders himself to Raṅganātha at Śrīraṅgam and prays to become his eternal servant, whose one joy is service according to the Lord's pleasure.
