Appayya Dīkṣita's compendium, in four chapters, of the divergent views held by Advaita teachers on the locus of ignorance, the nature of Īśvara and the jīva (limitation, reflection, semblance, one-jīva), the means of knowledge, the role of works and of hearing, and liberation, prefaced by the principle that the ancients, intent only on the oneness of the self, were indifferent to how the empirical is explained.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-siddhantalesasangraha-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Collects the views on Īśvara and jīva: the Vivaraṇa (Īśvara the prototype, jīva the reflection in ignorance), the Saṃkṣepaśārīraka (Īśvara reflected in ignorance, jīva in the intellect), the semblance view of the Vārttika, the limitation view of Vācaspati, and the one-jīva view; and on whether hearing is enjoined and which of hearing and contemplation is principal.
