## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 13-19 <a id="tea-magandiya-sutta-13-19"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As a leper cauterising his sores over a charcoal pit finds relief in what is painful, and once cured would not envy another leper, so one who has abandoned craving for sensual pleasures and abides with inner peace does not envy those who indulge. 'Health is the highest gain, nibbāna the highest bliss; the eightfold path the best of paths, leading to the deathless.'
