### 7 <a id="tea-manahsiksa-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The shameless outcaste woman of the desire for prestige dances in my heart; how can pure love touch it? O mind, serve the beloved leader of Kṛṣṇa's devotees, who will drive her out and bring in pure love.
