The most famous biography of Padmasambhava, a treasure revealed by Orgyen Lingpa in 1352 at Crystal Cave (Shel brag): the Lotus-born's birth, deeds in India, the eight manifestations, the founding of Samye and the prophecies.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### birth <a id="tea-pema-kathang-birth"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Padmasambhava is born, without father or mother, from a lotus on Lake Dhanakośa in Oḍḍiyāna as an emanation of Amitābha; the childless King Indrabhūti finds him and adopts him as prince.

### samye <a id="tea-pema-kathang-samye"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Invited by Trisong Detsen on Śāntarakṣita's advice, Padmasambhava binds under oath the gods and spirits of Tibet who were obstructing the work, so that Samye can be built and consecrated.
