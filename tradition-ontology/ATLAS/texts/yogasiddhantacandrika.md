Nārāyaṇatīrtha's extensive vṛtti. Its opening verses give the transmission (Pitāmaha taught yoga; Śeṣa in the sūtras; Vyāsa in the bhāṣya; Vācaspati Miśra and Vijñānabhikṣu clarified the bhāṣya) and argue that Vyāsa's Brahma Sūtras refuted pradhāna's independence, not yoga as meditative cultivation; the introduction treats deep contemplation (nididhyāsana) as samādhi, 'also called rājayoga', and subsumes many named yogas under the eight-limbed yoga.
