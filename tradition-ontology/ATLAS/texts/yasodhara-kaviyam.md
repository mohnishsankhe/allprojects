A short Tamil Jain epic (one of the five minor epics) on King Yaśodhara and his mother, reborn for many lives as animals because they offered a cock made of flour to the goddess — the intention of killing bears fruit like killing; it condemns animal sacrifice.

### whole <a id="tea-yasodhara-kaviyam-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

King Yaśodhara and his mother, to avert an evil dream, offer to the goddess a cock made of flour instead of a living bird; for the intention of killing they are reborn again and again as animals — peacock and dog, fish, goat, cock — until a monk's teaching frees them; the story teaches that intended violence ripens as violence.
