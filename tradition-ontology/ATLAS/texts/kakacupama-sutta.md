## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 11-20 <a id="tea-kakacupama-sutta-11-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Others may speak to you in five ways — timely or untimely, true or false, gently or harshly, beneficially or not, with love or with hate; you should train: 'our minds will be unaffected, we will utter no evil words, we will abide compassionate with a mind of love.' Even if bandits sever you limb from limb with a two-handed saw, one who gives rise to a mind of hate on that account does not carry out my teaching.
