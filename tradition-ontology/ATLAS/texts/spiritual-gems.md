### Spiritual Gems, on the initiate's remaining births <a id="tea-spiritual-gems-four-lives"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _recent (post-1800)_

One who has been initiated by a true Master will not be reborn below the human form and, at most within four lives, will reach the true home, even if he practises little; practice shortens the way.

### Spiritual Gems, on the three kinds of karma <a id="tea-spiritual-gems-three-karmas"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Karma is of three kinds: the destiny (pralabdh) that made this body, which must be lived out; the stored (sinchit) karma, which the Master burns at initiation or through the disciple's meditation; and the new (kriyaman) karma made now, which the disciple should avoid by living by the vows and doing all as the Master's service.
