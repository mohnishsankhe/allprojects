## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3-15 <a id="tea-mahamalunkya-sutta-3-15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A tender infant has no notion of 'identity', 'teachings', 'precepts', 'sensual pleasures' or 'beings', yet the underlying tendencies to identity view, doubt, clinging to rules, sensual lust and ill will lie within it. The path to abandoning the five lower fetters: with the first jhāna (or higher attainment) one regards whatever exists there of form, feeling, perception, formations and consciousness as impermanent, suffering, a disease, empty, not-self, turns the mind away to the deathless element, and attains the destruction of the effluents or non-return.
