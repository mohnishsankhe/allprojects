## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-7 <a id="tea-padukapancaka-1-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

In the pericarp of the white thousand-petalled lotus is the triangle formed of the letters a-ka-tha, and in it the haṃsa and the guru's two sandals, luminous — the yogin meditates on the guru's sandals there, on the jewelled seat, as the culmination of the inner ascent.
