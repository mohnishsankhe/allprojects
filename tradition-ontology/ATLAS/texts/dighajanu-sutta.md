## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-13 <a id="tea-dighajanu-sutta-2-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Four things lead to a householder's welfare in this life: accomplishment in initiative, in protection of wealth, in good friendship and in balanced living (with four inlets and drains of wealth); four lead to welfare in future lives: accomplishment in faith, virtue, generosity and wisdom.
