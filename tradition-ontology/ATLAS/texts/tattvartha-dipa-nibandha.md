Vallabha's principal doctrinal work in verse with his own commentary; its first section (Śāstrārtha-prakaraṇa) sets out the meaning of the Gītā, which he counts with the Veda, the Brahma Sūtra and the Bhāgavata among the four authorities, reading it in the light of devotion and grace (puṣṭi).
