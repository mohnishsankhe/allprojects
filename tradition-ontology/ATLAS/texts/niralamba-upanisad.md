## Teachings (3: sourced 3)

### 1 (caste) <a id="tea-niralamba-upanisad-1-caste"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Caste belongs not to skin, blood, flesh or bone; the Self has no caste; caste is imagined for convention.

> na carmaṇo na raktasya na māṃsasya na cāsthinaḥ | na jātir ātmano jātir vyavahāraprakalpitā

### 1 (questions) <a id="tea-niralamba-upanisad-1-questions"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Questions are posed on Brahman, Īśvara, jīva, nature, the supreme self, Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra, the world, knowledge and ignorance, bondage and liberation, the teacher, the disciple, the learned, the deluded, the demonic, austerity, heaven and hell, the renouncer — and each is answered.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate, ethics_

### 1 (renouncer) <a id="tea-niralamba-upanisad-1-renouncer"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

The renouncer is one who, having abandoned all dharmas and 'I' and 'mine', takes refuge in Brahman, knowing 'I am Brahman', the supportless.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ethics, karma-liberation_
