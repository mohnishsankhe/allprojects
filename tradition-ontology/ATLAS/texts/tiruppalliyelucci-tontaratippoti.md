Ten verses awakening the Lord of Śrīraṅgam at dawn, the gods and sages waiting at his door; the Āḻvār asks to be made servant to the Lord's servants. Sung daily at dawn in Śrīvaiṣṇava temples.

### 1 <a id="tea-tiruppalliyelucci-tontaratippoti-1"></a>

The sun has touched the eastern peak, darkness has fled, flowers open, the gods and kings crowd at your door with their elephants and drums — Lord of Araṅgam, rise from your sleep.
