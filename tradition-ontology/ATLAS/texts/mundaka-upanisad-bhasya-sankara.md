## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.2.12 <a id="tea-mundaka-upanisad-bhasya-sankara-1-2-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Even one learned in scripture should not seek knowledge of Brahman independently; he should approach, with fuel in hand, a teacher who is learned in scripture (śrotriya) and established in Brahman (brahmaniṣṭha), who then teaches the calm, qualified disciple.
