## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.3 <a id="tea-laghu-jataka-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Whatever good and bad karma was accumulated in another birth, this śāstra makes manifest its ripening, as a lamp shows objects in the dark.

> yad upacitam anyajanmani śubhāśubhaṃ tasya karmaṇaḥ paṅktim | vyañjayati śāstram etat tamasi dravyāṇi dīpa iva ||
