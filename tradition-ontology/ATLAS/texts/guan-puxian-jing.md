The 'closing sūtra' of the Threefold Lotus: repentance of the six senses and visualization of Samantabhadra on his six-tusked white elephant, as practiced in Tiantai's Lotus samādhi.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-guan-puxian-jing-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Repentance of the six sense faculties before Samantabhadra: the sea of karmic obstructions arises from false thinking; one who wishes to repent should sit upright and contemplate the true mark (reality), and the frost and dew of offences are melted by the sun of wisdom.
