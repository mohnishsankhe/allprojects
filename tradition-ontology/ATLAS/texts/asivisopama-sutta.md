## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-17 <a id="tea-asivisopama-sutta-1-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A man pursued by four fierce vipers, five murderous enemies and a sixth hidden assassin enters an empty village plundered by bandits, reaches a great flood whose near shore is dangerous and far shore safe, builds a raft and crosses by his own effort: the vipers are the four elements, the enemies the five aggregates of clinging, the assassin delight and lust, the village the six internal sense bases, the bandits the six external, the flood the four floods, the raft the eightfold path, the far shore nibbāna, and the brahmin who has crossed is the arahant.
