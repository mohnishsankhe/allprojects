Nammāḻvār's hundred verses in the viruttam metre, a love-poem sequence in the akam mode; opens with the prayer to be freed from false knowledge, evil conduct and the impure body. Held to be the Ṛgveda in Tamil.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-tiruviruttam-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

O Lord who is born among all beings for their sake: hear the plea of your servant — may I no longer be joined to the false knowledge, the evil conduct and the impure body in which I have stood till now.
