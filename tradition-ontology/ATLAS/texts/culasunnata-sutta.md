## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 4-13 <a id="tea-culasunnata-sutta-4-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As Migāra's mother's mansion is empty of elephants and cattle but not of the monks, the monk attends to the perception of forest, not village; then of earth; then of the spheres of infinite space, infinite consciousness, nothingness, neither-perception-nor-non-perception, and the signless concentration of mind — each time seeing what is absent as empty and what remains as present ('there is this') — until, seeing even the signless as conditioned and impermanent, the mind is freed: this is the genuine, undistorted, pure descent into emptiness.
