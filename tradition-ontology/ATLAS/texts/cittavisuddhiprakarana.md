### 6 <a id="tea-cittavisuddhiprakarana-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

By whatever violent deeds beings are bound, by those very deeds, joined with means, they are freed from the bonds of existence.

> yena yena hi bandhyante jantavo raudrakarmaṇā | sopāyena tu tenaiva mucyante bhavabandhanāt ||

### 18 <a id="tea-cittavisuddhiprakarana-18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As one who knows the truth of poison eats poison seeing it for what it is and is not merely stupefied but becomes free of disease, (so the yogin enjoys objects).

> yathaiva viṣatattvajño viṣamālokya bhakṣayan | kevalaṃ muhyate nāsau rogamuktaśca jāyate ||

### 20 <a id="tea-cittavisuddhiprakarana-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Fools are attached to forms, the middling go to dispassion, and those of highest understanding, knowing the nature of form, are liberated.

> bālā rajyanti rūpeṣu vairāgyaṃ yānti madhyamāḥ | svabhāvajñā vimucyante rūpasyottamabuddhayaḥ ||
