## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### structure <a id="tea-three-visions-ngorchen-structure"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The common Lamdre path is taught under the three visions: under the impure vision, the sufferings of saṃsāra, the difficulty of obtaining leisure and endowment, death and impermanence, and karma and its results, to turn the mind from this life and from saṃsāra; under the experiential vision, love, compassion and bodhicitta, relative and ultimate, and the experiences that arise in meditation; under the pure vision, the qualities of buddhahood, to inspire effort.
