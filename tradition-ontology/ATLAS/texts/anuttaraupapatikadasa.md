The ninth Aṅga: stories of monks reborn in the highest (anuttara) heavens, from which they will reach liberation in one more birth — notably Dhanya, whose body wasted by austerities is described limb by limb.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3.1 <a id="tea-anuttaraupapatikadasa-3-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

Dhanya, a rich young man, became a monk and fasted with such rigour that his body is described limb by limb as withered like dry leaves and pods; Mahāvīra praised him to King Śreṇika as the greatest ascetic among his monks. He was reborn in the highest heaven.
