## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-aksi-upanisad-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Sāṅkṛti goes to the sun's world and praises the Sun with the Cākṣuṣmatī-vidyā; one who knows it has no eye disease and none in his family is blind.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language, practice_

### 2 <a id="tea-aksi-upanisad-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Sun teaches the seven stages of yoga — from good desire to the fourth — and the jīvanmukta who, seeing all as Brahman, is free.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: intermediate · types: consciousness-mind, karma-liberation_
