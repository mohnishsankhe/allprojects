## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-tattvartha-slokavarttika-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The authority of the teaching of the path rests on an omniscient teacher, whose existence the commentary defends at length against the Mīmāṃsaka denial.
