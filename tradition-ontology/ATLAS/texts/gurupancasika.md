## Teachings (6: skeleton 6)

### 6-7 <a id="tea-gurupancasika-6-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Teacher and disciple should first examine each other before forming the bond, for the fault of breaking the pledge falls on both; the wise should not take as guru one who is merciless, angry, cruel, rigid, frivolous, undisciplined or boastful — nor accept such a disciple.

> prāk śiṣyācāryasambandhaḥ kāryaḥ parīkṣya sūribhiḥ | samānasamayabhraṃśo doṣo hi guruśiṣyayoḥ || niṣkṛpaṃ krodhanaṃ krūraṃ stabdhaṃ laghumasaṃyatam | svotkarṣakaṃ ca no kuryād guruṃ śiṣyaṃ ca buddhimān ||

### 8-9 <a id="tea-gurupancasika-8-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The master should be steady, disciplined, intelligent, patient, straightforward, without deceit, knowing the application of mantras and tantras, compassionate, learned in the treatises, knowing the ten principles, skilled in drawing maṇḍalas and in rites, an explainer of mantra, serene and master of his senses.

> dhīro vinīto matimān kṣamāvānārjavo 'śaṭhaḥ | mantratantraprayogajñaḥ kṛpāluḥ śāstrakovidaḥ || daśatattvaparijñātā maṇḍalālekhyakarmavit | mantravyākhyākṛdācāryaḥ prasannaḥ syājjitendriyaḥ ||

### 10 <a id="tea-gurupancasika-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The disciple who, having become one, despises that protector (his master) despises all the buddhas and always meets suffering.

> taṃ nāthaṃ yo 'vamanyeta śiṣyo bhūtvā sacetanaḥ | sarvabuddhāpamānena sa nityaṃ duḥkhamāpnuyāt ||

### 22 <a id="tea-gurupancasika-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Those disciples are endowed with compassion, generosity, discipline and patience who do not imagine any difference between the guru and Vajradhara.

> te śiṣyāḥ karuṇotsargaśīlakṣāntiguṇānvitāḥ | ye nānyatvaṃ kalpayanti gurorvajradharasya ca ||

### 47 <a id="tea-gurupancasika-47"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Attainment always follows the master; on Vajradhara's word the guru is to be honoured with all things.

> sarvathā sarvadā siddhirācārya yānugāminī | gururvajradharasyokterārādhyaḥ sarvavastubhiḥ ||

### 49 <a id="tea-gurupancasika-49"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Then, having made him a vessel of the true dharma by giving mantra and the rest, (the master has the disciple) read and retain the fourteen root downfalls.

> tato mantrādidānena kṛtvā saddharmapātrakam | paṭhecca dhārayeccāpi mūlāpattīścaturdaśa ||
