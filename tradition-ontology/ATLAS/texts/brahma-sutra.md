**Original title:** ब्रह्मसूत्र
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md), [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md), [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md), [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md), [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md), [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md), [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md), [Śiva-viśiṣṭādvaita (Śaiva Vedānta of Śrīkaṇṭha)](../lineages/siva-visistadvaita.md)

  - [Bādarāyaṇa](../teachers/badarayana.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Bādarāyaṇa, identified with Vyāsa (Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana); scholarly: Bādarāyaṇa as final redactor of an older sūtra tradition; identification with Vyāsa not accepted; confidence: moderate

**Structure:** 4 adhyāyas × 4 pādas. Śaṅkara's recension: 555 sūtras (pāda counts 31, 32, 43, 28 | 37, 45, 53, 22 | 27, 41, 66, 52 | 19, 21, 16, 22). Other schools divide and number differently: Rāmānuja 545 sūtras, Madhva 564; Nimbārka 549 and Vallabha 554 (these two counts low confidence). Adhikaraṇa counts also differ (Śaṅkara c. 191–192; Rāmānuja c. 156; Madhva c. 223 — low confidence).
**Availability:** digitized-original

Bādarāyaṇa's aphorisms systematizing the Upaniṣads in four adhyāyas — samanvaya (all Upaniṣadic texts converge on Brahman), avirodha (no conflict with smṛti, reason or rival systems, which are critiqued in 2.2), sādhana (transmigration, the states of the self, the meditations, the auxiliaries of knowledge) and phala (repetition of meditation, the knower's freedom from karma, departure, the path of the gods, the liberated state). Every Vedānta school is founded on a commentary on it.
