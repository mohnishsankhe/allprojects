# Muṇḍamālā Tantra

`src:mundamala-tantra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md), [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md)
**Genre:** tantra (Mahāvidyā)
**Availability:** digitized-original

An eastern tantra that names the ten Mahāvidyās — Kālī, Tārā, Ṣoḍaśī, Bhuvaneśvarī, Bhairavī, Chinnamastā, Dhūmāvatī, Bagalā, Mātaṅgī and Kamalā — as 'siddha-vidyās' needing no preliminary tests (as quoted in Kṛṣṇānanda's Tantrasāra), and treats their worship, rosaries and mantras.
