### appearances-are-mind <a id="tea-aspiration-prayer-of-mahamudra-appearances-are-mind"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Appearances are mind, and emptiness is mind; realization is mind and delusion is mind; arising is mind and ceasing is mind — may all doubts about mind be cut.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind, ultimate_

### compassion <a id="tea-aspiration-prayer-of-mahamudra-compassion"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

When the unobstructed power of love arises for beings who suffer through not realizing this, the empty essence shines nakedly in the midst of compassion: may we practise the union of emptiness and compassion unceasingly.

### ground-path-fruit <a id="tea-aspiration-prayer-of-mahamudra-ground-path-fruit"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The ground of purification is mind itself, clarity and emptiness inseparable; the purifier is the great vajra yoga of Mahāmudrā; what is purified are the adventitious stains of delusion; the fruit of purification is the stainless dharmakāya.

_level: bridging · standpoint: analytic · path: meditation · stage: all · types: ultimate, practice_

### neither-existent-nor-nonexistent <a id="tea-aspiration-prayer-of-mahamudra-neither-existent-nor-nonexistent"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

It is not existent — even the victors do not see it; it is not non-existent — it is the basis of all saṃsāra and nirvāṇa; this is not a contradiction but the middle way of union — may we realize the nature of mind, free of extremes.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_

terms: [sems nyid (mind-as-such)](../terms/semnyi.md), [yuganaddha](../terms/yuganaddha.md) · concepts: [The unions: clarity-emptiness, appearance-emptiness, bliss-emptiness](../concepts/clarity-emptiness-union.md)

### not-realizing <a id="tea-aspiration-prayer-of-mahamudra-not-realizing"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Not realizing this, beings circle in saṃsāra; realizing it, buddhahood is nothing other than this; there is no 'this is it' or 'this is not it' — may we realize the nature of dharmatā, the all-ground.

_level: bridging · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, ultimate_

### samatha-vipasyana <a id="tea-aspiration-prayer-of-mahamudra-samatha-vipasyana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The waves of coarse and subtle thought subside in their own place and the river of mind rests unwavering in its natural state; free of the stains of dullness and torpor, may the ocean of śamatha be stable. Looking again and again at the mind that cannot be looked at, may we see vividly what is not seen, just as it is, and cut doubts about what is and is not.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice_

### view-meditation-conduct <a id="tea-aspiration-prayer-of-mahamudra-view-meditation-conduct"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Confidence in the view is cutting through doubts about the ground; the key of meditation is to sustain that without distraction; the supreme conduct is to train in the meaning of meditation in everything — may we have the view, meditation and conduct.
