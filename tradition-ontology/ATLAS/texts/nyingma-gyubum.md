The canon of Old Tantras not (or only partly) admitted to the Kangyur: the tantras of Atiyoga (mind, space and instruction series, incl. the Seventeen Tantras), Anuyoga (the root sūtras) and Mahāyoga (tantra section and sādhana section), with protector texts. Compiled in its extant form from the 15th c.
