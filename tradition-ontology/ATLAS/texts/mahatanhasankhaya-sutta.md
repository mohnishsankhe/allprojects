## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 2-7 <a id="tea-mahatanhasankhaya-sutta-2-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The monk Sāti held that 'it is this same consciousness that runs and wanders through the round of rebirths, not another' — the one that speaks and feels and experiences the results of deeds. The Buddha rebukes him: consciousness is dependently arisen; without a condition there is no origination of consciousness.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: dispute, consciousness-mind_

terms: [viññāṇa](../terms/vinnana.md) · teachers: [Sāti Kevaṭṭaputta](../teachers/sati-kevattaputta.md) · disputes: [Is it the same consciousness that runs and wanders through the rounds of rebirth?](../debates/consciousness-transmigrates.md), [Is there a self?](../debates/is-there-a-self.md)

### 8-13 <a id="tea-mahatanhasankhaya-sutta-8-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Consciousness is reckoned by the condition dependent on which it arises — eye-consciousness … mind-consciousness — as a fire is reckoned by its fuel: log fire, grass fire. 'Do you see: this has come to be? It originates with that as nutriment; with the cessation of that nutriment what has come to be is subject to cessation.'

_level: unmarked · standpoint: causal · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [viññāṇa](../terms/vinnana.md), [āhāra](../terms/ahara.md) · concepts: [Dependent origination](../concepts/dependent-origination.md)

### 14 <a id="tea-mahatanhasankhaya-sutta-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Purified and bright as this view is, if you cling to it, cherish it, treasure it and treat it as a possession, you would not understand the Dhamma as a raft taught for crossing over, not for grasping.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission_

concepts: [The simile of the raft](../concepts/raft-simile.md)

### 17-22 <a id="tea-mahatanhasankhaya-sutta-17-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Dependent origination in forward order (ignorance → formations → … → ageing and death) and its cessation in reverse order; knowing and seeing thus, would you run back to the past or forward to the future, or be perplexed about the present? Would you speak out of respect for the teacher? — only what you have known, seen and understood for yourselves.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: causal · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation_

terms: [paṭiccasamuppāda](../terms/paticcasamuppada.md) · concepts: [The twelve links (nidāna)](../concepts/twelve-links.md)

### 26-29 <a id="tea-mahatanhasankhaya-sutta-26-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

An embryo is conceived when three things meet: the mother and father come together, the mother is in season, and the gandhabba is present; the child grows up, delights in pleasant feelings, and craving and the whole mass of suffering arise.

_level: conventional · standpoint: causal · path: action · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, death-dying, body-layers_
