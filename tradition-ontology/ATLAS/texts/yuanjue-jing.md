## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-yuanjue-jing-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

All beings' ignorance is like flowers seen in the sky by diseased eyes, or a second moon: knowing that it is like flowers in the sky, there is no turning in birth and death; perfect enlightenment is the original mind, pure and unmoving.
