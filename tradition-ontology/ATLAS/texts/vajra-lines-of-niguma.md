### 1 <a id="tea-vajra-lines-of-niguma-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

By inner heat, bliss-warmth blazes by itself; by the illusory body, attachment and aversion are self-liberated; by dream, delusion is self-liberated; by clear light, ignorance is self-liberated; by transference and the intermediate state, one attains the enjoyment body.
