Six verses on the mind, each ending 'tan me manaḥ śivasaṅkalpam astu' ('may that mind of mine be of auspicious resolve'), counted as an Upaniṣad in some lists.

## Teachings (1: sourced 1)

### 1-6 <a id="tea-sivasankalpa-upanisad-1-6"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Each verse praises the mind — that which goes far while awake and asleep, the light of lights, by which the sacrifice is performed — and prays: 'may that mind of mine be of auspicious resolve'.
