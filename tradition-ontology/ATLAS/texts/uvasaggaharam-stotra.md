A five-verse Prakrit hymn to Pārśvanātha, 'remover of calamities', recited daily for protection; the tradition ascribes it to Bhadrabāhu, composed to protect the community from the vengeful spirit of his brother Varāhamihira.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-uvasaggaharam-stotra-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

I bow to Pārśva, remover of calamities, attended by [the yakṣa] Pārśva, freed from the dense mass of karma, destroyer of the poison of snakes, abode of auspiciousness and welfare.

> uvasaggaharaṃ pāsaṃ pāsaṃ vaṃdāmi kammaghaṇamukkaṃ / visaharavisaninnāsaṃ maṃgalakallāṇaāvāsaṃ
