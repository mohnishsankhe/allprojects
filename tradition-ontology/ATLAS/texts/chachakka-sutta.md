## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 10-15 <a id="tea-chachakka-sutta-10-15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If anyone says 'the eye is self' that is not tenable, for the arising and passing of the eye are discerned; if it were self it would follow that 'my self arises and passes away'. So for forms, eye-consciousness, contact, feeling and craving, and for all six senses.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate, dispute_

terms: [anattā](../terms/anatta.md) · concepts: [Not-self (anattā)](../concepts/anatta.md) · disputes: [Is there a self?](../debates/is-there-a-self.md)

### 28-39 <a id="tea-chachakka-sutta-28-39"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Pleasant feeling left to itself brings the underlying tendency to lust, painful feeling that to aversion, neutral feeling that to ignorance; that one could make an end of suffering here and now without abandoning these is impossible; seeing thus one becomes disenchanted and is liberated.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: meditation · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind, karma-liberation_
