Haribhadra's 228-verse treatise on yoga: three yogas (by desire, by scripture, by capacity), the eight views (dṛṣṭi) — mitrā, tārā, balā, dīprā, sthirā, kāntā, prabhā, parā — each correlated with one of Patañjali's eight limbs, the removal of a fault and the rise of a quality; the unity of the goal under many names; four kinds of yogins.
