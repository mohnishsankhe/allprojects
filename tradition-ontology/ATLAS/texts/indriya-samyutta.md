## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 48.10 <a id="tea-indriya-samyutta-48-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The five faculties: faith (in the Tathāgata's awakening), energy (the four right strivings), mindfulness (the four establishments), concentration (the four jhānas, made with relinquishment as object) and wisdom (the noble penetrative wisdom into rise and fall that leads to the complete destruction of suffering).
