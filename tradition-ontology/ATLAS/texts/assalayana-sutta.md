## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 5-18 <a id="tea-assalayana-sutta-5-18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Against the brahmins' claim to be the best class: brahmin women menstruate and give birth; in Yona and Kamboja there are only masters and slaves who can change places; a khattiya, vessa or sudda who kills or steals goes to hell as does a brahmin; any class can develop a mind of love, wash off dirt, or make fire from any wood; conduct, not birth, is decisive.
