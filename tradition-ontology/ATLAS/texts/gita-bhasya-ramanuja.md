Viśiṣṭādvaita commentary reading the Gītā as teaching Nārāyaṇa, the supreme Brahman, reached by bhakti-yoga, for which karma-yoga and jñāna-yoga are preparatory; the individual selves and matter are the Lord's body; on 18.66 it offers readings of 'abandoning all dharmas' in relation to bhakti and surrender.
