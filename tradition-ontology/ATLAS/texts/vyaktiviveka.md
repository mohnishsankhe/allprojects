## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 (opening) <a id="tea-vyaktiviveka-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Mahimabhaṭṭa's thesis: the word has only one power, denotation; all that Ānandavardhana calls suggestion is in fact inference (anumāna) from the expressed meaning; the book is written to show that every kind of dhvani falls under inference.
