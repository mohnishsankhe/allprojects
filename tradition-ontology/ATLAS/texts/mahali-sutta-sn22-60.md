Mahāli the Licchavi reports Pūraṇa Kassapa's view that there is no cause for the defilement or purification of beings; the Buddha explains the causes through the aggregates.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2 <a id="tea-mahali-sutta-sn22-60-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Mahāli reports that Pūraṇa Kassapa says there is no cause or condition for the defilement of beings, nor for their purification. The Buddha replies that if form, feeling, perception, formations and consciousness were entirely suffering beings would not be attached to them, and if entirely pleasant they would not be disenchanted: that is the cause of defilement and of purification.
