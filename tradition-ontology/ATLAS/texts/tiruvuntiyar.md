## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-45 <a id="tea-tiruvuntiyar-1-45"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Grace alone brings the soul to Śiva; the guru's glance destroys the bonds; the one who knows by grace becomes one with Śiva without losing himself, and should remain in stillness.
