## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-catuhsloki-vallabha-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

At all times and with one's whole being the Lord of Vraja is to be worshipped; this alone is one's dharma, and no other anywhere, ever.

> sarvadā sarvabhāvena bhajanīyo vrajādhipaḥ | svasyāyam eva dharmo hi nānyaḥ kvāpi kadācana ||
