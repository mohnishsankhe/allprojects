## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-vimalaprabha-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Puṇḍarīka's commentary explains that the Ādibuddha 'without beginning or end' is the gnosis of immutable bliss and not a creator; it also sets out the three 'bodhisattva commentaries' and the correct reading of the tantra in the sense of the definitive meaning.
