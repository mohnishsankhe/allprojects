Bhāskara's verse commentary on the Śiva Sūtra, by 'Bhāskara, son of Divākara'. Its opening gives the lineage Vasugupta - Kallaṭa - (his maternal cousin) Pradyumnabhaṭṭa - (his son) Prajñārjuna - (his pupil) Mahādevabhaṭṭa - (his son) Śrīkaṇṭhabhaṭṭa - Bhāskara (vv. 1.3-1.9), and says Kallaṭa explained three sections with his Spanda-sūtras and the last with the Tattvārthacintāmaṇi.
