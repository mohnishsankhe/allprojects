one of the thirty-seven works of Madhva collected as the Sarvamūla ('root of all'); a short work on the observance of Kṛṣṇa's birthday: when the eighth day of the dark fortnight falls with the Rohiṇī asterism at midnight it is called Jayantī, and it is to be kept by fasting by all, especially by Hari's devotees.
