one of the thirty-seven works of Madhva collected as the Sarvamūla ('root of all'); a short work on the observance of Kṛṣṇa's birthday: when the eighth day of the dark fortnight falls with the Rohiṇī asterism at midnight it is called Jayantī, and it is to be kept by fasting by all, especially by Hari's devotees.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-3 <a id="tea-jayantinirnaya-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When the dark eighth day falls with the Rohiṇī asterism at midnight it is called Jayantī, destroyer of all sins; since on it Hari, the unborn Lord, was born at midnight, that day is supremely holy, and it is always to be kept by fasting by all — especially by the twice-born and by his devotees.
