### 1 <a id="tea-praise-of-dependent-origination-1"></a>

I bow to the Conqueror who, seeing and teaching dependent arising, is the unsurpassed knower and teacher.

### colophon-verses <a id="tea-praise-of-dependent-origination-colophon-verses"></a>

The author tells how, after long searching, he came through the kindness of his teacher to see Nāgārjuna's system clearly by the light of Candrakīrti's commentary, and was filled with faith in the Buddha as teacher of dependent arising.

### theme <a id="tea-praise-of-dependent-origination-theme"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Whatever depends on conditions is empty of inherent existence; no teaching is more wondrous than this. Those who take emptiness to contradict dependent arising have not understood the Buddha; for him, emptiness is the very meaning of dependent arising.
