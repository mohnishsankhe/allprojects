The eighth Aṅga: stories of those who 'made an end' (antakṛt) of rebirth in that very life — among them Gajasukumāla, Kṛṣṇa's young brother, who kept equanimity while a brāhmaṇa set burning coals on his head, and the gardener Arjuna, a murderer who became a monk and bore abuse patiently.
