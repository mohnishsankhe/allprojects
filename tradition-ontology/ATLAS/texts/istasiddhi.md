Vimuktātman's treatise establishing the 'desired' (iṣṭa) conclusion that the self is pure experience (anubhūti) and that ignorance and its products are indescribable (anirvacanīya), with a critique of the rival theories of error in favour of indescribability.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-istasiddhi-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The self is pure experience (anubhūti), self-established and never an object; ignorance and its products are neither real nor unreal but indescribable, as the rival theories of error fail to explain illusion.
