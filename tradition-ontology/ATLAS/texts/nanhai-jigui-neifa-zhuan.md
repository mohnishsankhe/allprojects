## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### intro <a id="tea-nanhai-jigui-neifa-zhuan-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In the Western lands there are four principal nikāyas — the Āryamahāsāṃghika, the Āryasthavira, the Āryamūlasarvāstivāda and the Āryasaṃmitīya — with eighteen sub-schools; which are 'Mahāyāna' and which 'Hīnayāna' is not fixed, those who venerate bodhisattvas and read Mahāyāna sūtras being called Mahāyāna.
