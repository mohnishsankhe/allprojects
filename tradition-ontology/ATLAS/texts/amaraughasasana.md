A prose treatise ascribed to Gorakṣanātha, printed in the Kashmir Series with a commentary. It sets out the elements, breaths, channels, ten doors and seven bonds of the body, locates the 'desire, poison and spotless' (kāma, viṣa, nirañjana) at the root of the brahma-staff, rejects rival definitions of liberation (cutting off karma, Veda-recitation, the supportless, meditation on form, bindu and nāda, Kaula union, the movement of kuṇḍalinī) and defines liberation as the mind seeing the mind in sahaja-samādhi.
