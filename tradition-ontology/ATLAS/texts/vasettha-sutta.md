## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 7-13 <a id="tea-vasettha-sutta-7-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Among grasses, trees, insects, snakes, fish and birds the marks of species differ by birth, but among humans there is no such difference; the distinction is by designation. Whoever lives by farming is a farmer, by trade a merchant, and so on; I call him a brahmin who has cut all fetters, who is without clinging, patient, free of lust — one is a brahmin not by birth but by deeds (kamma).
