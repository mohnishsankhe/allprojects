## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-7 <a id="tea-yuganaddha-sutta-2-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Whoever declares final knowledge does so by one of four paths: developing insight preceded by calm, calm preceded by insight, calm and insight yoked together, or when the mind seized by agitation about the Dhamma settles inwardly and becomes unified; the path arises, the fetters are abandoned and the tendencies eliminated.
