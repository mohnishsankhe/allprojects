# Sahasrāgama

`src:sahasra-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Sahasra, Sahasraka (Kiraṇa)
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Kāla, then Bhīma, then Dharma (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Śivabheda no. 8 in the Kāmika's order; 10 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** manuscript-only

No. 8 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Śivabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Kāla, then Bhīma, then Dharma. The Kāmika assigns it 10 upabheda(s): Atīta, Maṅgala, Śuddha, Aprameya, Jātibhāk, Prabuddha, Vibudha, Hasta, Alaṅkāra, Subodhaka.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: IFP manuscript RE15540 (karṣaṇa-to-pratiṣṭhā section), digitized (Muktabodha I00042)

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.5/10.10: Kāla → Bhīma → Dharma. In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the hips (pūrva 1.93–101).
