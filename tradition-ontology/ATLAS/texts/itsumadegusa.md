### bell <a id="tea-itsumadegusa-bell"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In his early twenties, working on 'wu' at Eiganji, Hakuin fell into great doubt as if frozen in ice for days; at the distant sound of the temple bell it shattered and he thought no one had awakened like him in three hundred years; Shōju Rōjin crushed this pride and he practised on, with later awakenings.
