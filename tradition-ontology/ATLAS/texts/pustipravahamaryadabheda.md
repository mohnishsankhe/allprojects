## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1ff <a id="tea-pustipravahamaryadabheda-1ff"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

There are three creations of souls: those of grace (puṣṭi), chosen by the Lord for loving service; those of rule (maryādā), who follow scriptural discipline toward liberation; and those of the stream (pravāha), who pursue worldly ends (associated with the 'demonic' creation of Gītā 16); puṣṭi souls are further subdivided.
