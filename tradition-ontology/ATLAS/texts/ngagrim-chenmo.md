### empowerment-and-vows <a id="tea-ngagrim-chenmo-empowerment-and-vows"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Entry into the mantra path requires empowerment from a qualified master and the keeping of the tantric vows and pledges; without them, practice produces no attainment and leads to downfall.

### sutra-tantra-difference <a id="tea-ngagrim-chenmo-sutra-tantra-difference"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Mahāyāna has two divisions, the perfection vehicle and the mantra vehicle; they do not differ in the view of emptiness, which is the same, but in method: the mantra vehicle is distinguished by deity yoga, in which the wisdom realising emptiness itself appears as the deity's body.

_level: bridging · standpoint: analytic · path: ritual, meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice_

### two-stages <a id="tea-ngagrim-chenmo-two-stages"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In Highest Yoga Tantra the generation stage, visualising oneself and the environment as the deity and maṇḍala to purify ordinary appearance and conception, ripens the continuum for the completion stage, which works on the winds, channels and drops to manifest the clear light and the illusory body.
