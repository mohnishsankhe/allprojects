# Resolving Doubts about the Pure Land (Jingtu yibian)

Zhuhong's short reply to a Chan objector who calls the Pure Land a mere symbol: 'returning to the source the nature is not two; expedient means have many gates'; Chan and Pure Land reach the same end (quoting Zhongfeng: 'Chan is the Chan of Pure Land; Pure Land is the Pure Land of Chan'); if you truly saw all places as the Pure Land you would live in a latrine without distaste; those who are not sure of ending birth and death should not discard the Pure Land.

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 420a11-22 <a id="tea-jingtu-yibian-420a11-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Asked whether Pure Land is only a symbol and the wise should awaken directly through Chan: 'returning to the source the nature is not two; expedient means have many gates' — Chan and Pure Land reach the same end by different roads. As Zhongfeng said, 'Chan is the Chan of Pure Land, Pure Land is the Pure Land of Chan; those who practise must value going deep through one gate.' Mahāsthāmaprāpta entered the acceptance of non-arising through recollecting the Buddha; Samantabhadra vowed to be born in Sukhāvatī.

> 歸元性無二，方便有多門。曉得此意，禪宗、淨土，殊途同歸。……如中峰大師道：『禪者，淨土之禪；淨土者，禪之淨土。而修之者必貴一門深入。』

### 420a23-b14 <a id="tea-jingtu-yibian-420a23-b14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If 'the pure mind is the Pure Land, so there is no seven-jewelled world', then by the same token there would be no heavens and no hells. True understanding of principle knows there is no principle outside phenomena; the lands are of four kinds, not only the land of eternal quiescent light. If you have truly seen through your own mind so that every place is the Pure Land — would you live in a latrine, eat from the trough with dogs and pigs, sleep among rotting corpses, nurse lepers for months? If you can do so with joy and without a thought, I grant that you may say 'high mountains and flat ground are all the West'; otherwise pure and impure are not yet empty for you, and to deny the buddha-land is to deceive heaven and men.

> 且汝既了徹自心，隨處淨土。吾試問汝：『還肯即廁溷中作住止否？……』於斯數者，歡喜安隱，略不介意，許汝說高山平地總西方。

### 420b15-28 <a id="tea-jingtu-yibian-420b15-28"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If you have the strength and vow to go in and out of the sea of birth-and-death doing the bodhisattva work without fear, I will not press the Pure Land on you; but if you fear the strong winds of this world, the rarity of buddhas, the weakness of your acceptance, that you may not end birth-and-death in this body and that the road after death is dark — then to reject the Pure Land is no small loss. This teaching seems shallow but is deep, near but far, hard but easy, easy but hard; whether you practise Chan or recollection, go deep through one gate, but do not mistake brass for gold and slander the Pure Land.

> 此淨土法門似淺而深，似近而遠，似難而易，似易而難，他日汝當自知。汝今參禪、念佛，不妨隨便一門深入。
