A lost Yāpanīya scripture known from a Prakrit passage quoted by Haribhadra in the Lalitavistarā, which denies every ground for excluding women from liberation.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### lalitavistara-quote <a id="tea-yapaniya-tantra-lalitavistara-quote"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

A woman is not a non-soul, nor incapable of liberation, nor opposed to right faith, nor non-human, nor born in a non-Ārya land, nor of an immeasurably long life, nor of extremely cruel mind, nor unable to calm delusion, nor without pure conduct, nor of impure body, nor without the highest effort — so she is not excluded from liberation.
