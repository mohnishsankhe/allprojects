King Bhoja of Dhārā's concise verse manual of Siddhānta doctrine: Śiva as the one pervasive mass of consciousness who favours all; the three categories pati, paśu and pāśa; the classes of souls; the malas; the tattvas from the pure principles to earth; and liberation. Commented on by Aghoraśiva and by Śrīkumāra (Tātparyadīpikā).

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1 <a id="tea-tattvaprakasa-bhoja-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Victorious is Śambhu, the one pervasive, eternal, ever-risen, all-powerful, tranquil mass of consciousness, the sole seed of the world, who favours all beings.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: divine · path: general · stage: all · types: ultimate_

terms: [pati](../terms/pati.md), [śiva](../terms/siva.md) · teachers: [Bhoja](../teachers/bhoja.md)

### ch.1 <a id="tea-tattvaprakasa-bhoja-ch-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The categories are three: pati (Śiva), paśu (souls) and pāśa (bonds); Śiva's five acts — creation, maintenance, withdrawal, concealment and grace — are for the souls.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate_

concepts: [Pati, paśu, pāśa — the Lord, the bound soul, the bonds](../concepts/pati-pasu-pasa.md), [Śiva's five acts (pañcakṛtya)](../concepts/five-acts-of-siva.md) · teachers: [Bhoja](../teachers/bhoja.md)

### ch.2 <a id="tea-tattvaprakasa-bhoja-ch-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Souls are of three kinds: vijñānākalas, bound by mala alone; pralayākalas, bound by mala and karma; and sakalas, bound by mala, karma and māyā; each class is further divided by the degree to which its mala has ripened.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [sakala](../terms/sakala.md), [pralayākala](../terms/pralayakala.md), [vijñānākala](../terms/vijnanakala.md) · concepts: [The three classes of souls (sakala, pralayākala, vijñānākala)](../concepts/three-classes-of-souls.md) · teachers: [Bhoja](../teachers/bhoja.md)

### ch.3 <a id="tea-tattvaprakasa-bhoja-ch-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The bonds are mala, karma, māyā, and Śiva's concealing power (rodha-śakti), which is called a bond because it drives the others until the soul is ripe; some add the bond of bindu for souls in the pure worlds.
