## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 12 <a id="tea-sallekha-sutta-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Effacement should be practised thus: 'Others will be cruel, we shall not be cruel … others will kill, we shall abstain … others will be greedy, envious, deceitful, obstinate, of wrong view, we shall not' — forty-four such resolves, each also a path of turning the mind and of avoidance.
