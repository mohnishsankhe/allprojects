Twelve-fascicle essay rejecting the view that the awakened are free from causality: Baizhang's fox shows 'not obscuring cause and effect', and denial of causality is a grave wrong view.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### fox <a id="tea-jinshin-inga-fox"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The first old man's 'not falling into cause and effect' denies causality and so he became a fox; Baizhang's 'not obscuring cause and effect' shows deep faith in cause and effect; to deny causality is to deny the buddha-dharma.
