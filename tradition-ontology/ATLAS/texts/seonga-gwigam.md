## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### seon-and-teachings <a id="tea-seonga-gwigam-seon-and-teachings"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Seon is the Buddha's mind and the teachings are the Buddha's words; the student should first discern through the teachings the two meanings of unchanging and conditioned, then set the teachings aside and enter Seon through the live word of the hwadu, with great faith, great doubt and great determination.
