### 1 <a id="tea-osel-nyingpo-1"></a>

Mipham's overview of the Guhyagarbha: all phenomena are great purity — appearances are the deities and pure lands — and great equality — they are empty; the inseparability of the two is the 'superior two truths' of Mahāyoga, established by the pure-vision valid cognition.
