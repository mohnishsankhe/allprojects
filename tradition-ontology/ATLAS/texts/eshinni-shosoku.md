Letters of Shinran's wife to their daughter Kakushinni recounting Shinran's hundred-day retreat at the Rokkakudō, his going to Hōnen, her dream that Hōnen was Seishi (Mahāsthāmaprāpta) and Shinran Kannon, and his abandoned attempt to read the three sūtras a thousand times for the people's benefit.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### rokkakudo <a id="tea-eshinni-shosoku-rokkakudo"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Eshinni writes that Shinran left Mt Hiei, secluded himself at the Rokkakudō for a hundred days praying about the life to come, and at dawn of the ninety-fifth day received a verse from Prince Shōtoku; he then went to Hōnen for a hundred days, rain or shine, and heard that for good and evil people alike the way of release is simply to say the nenbutsu; and that Shinran used to say he would go wherever Hōnen went, even to hell.
