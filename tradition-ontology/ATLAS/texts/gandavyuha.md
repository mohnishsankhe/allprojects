The final part of the Avataṃsaka, also an independent sūtra: sent by Mañjuśrī, the youth Sudhana visits fifty-odd spiritual friends — monks, laymen and laywomen, a courtesan, kings, goddesses of the night, a boy teacher, the Buddha's mother Māyā and wife Gopā — each teaching one liberation; Maitreya shows him the tower of Vairocana in which all worlds are reflected; he returns to Mañjuśrī and finally merges with Samantabhadra, whose vows (the Bhadracarī) conclude the text.

## Teachings (7: skeleton 7)

### 3 <a id="tea-gandavyuha-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Mañjuśrī tells Sudhana that to accomplish the bodhisattva's conduct he must seek, serve and honor spiritual friends without weariness, and sends him south to the monk Meghaśrī; the bodhisattva's knowledge comes from good friends.

### 28 <a id="tea-gandavyuha-28"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The courtesan Vasumitrā teaches a liberation 'free of passion': beings who come to her with passion leave free of passion — by seeing her, speaking with her, holding her hand or embracing her they attain various samādhis.

### 30 <a id="tea-gandavyuha-30"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

On Mount Potalaka Avalokiteśvara teaches Sudhana the bodhisattva liberation of the door of great compassion, by which he appears before beings to free them from fear.

### 44 <a id="tea-gandavyuha-44"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Māyā, the Buddha's mother, has the liberation of the illusory manifestation of great knowledge (or great vow): she has been and will be the mother of all the buddhas of the aeon.

### 54 <a id="tea-gandavyuha-54"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Maitreya opens the great tower called Vairocanavyūhālaṃkāragarbha; entering it Sudhana sees within it innumerable towers, each containing all the others and all of Maitreya's past lives and deeds, without obstruction; when Maitreya snaps his fingers the vision ends.

### 55 <a id="tea-gandavyuha-55"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Mañjuśrī, stretching his hand across a hundred and ten yojanas, touches Sudhana's head: without faith the roots are weak and one does not know the dharma's depth; he then enables him to see Samantabhadra.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: devotion · stage: advanced · types: teacher-transmission_

### 56 <a id="tea-gandavyuha-56"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Sudhana sees Samantabhadra and enters his body, where he travels through countless worlds and becomes equal to Samantabhadra and the buddhas; the verses of Samantabhadra's vows of good conduct (Bhadracarī) follow.
