# Ṣaḍviṃśa Brāhmaṇa

`src:sadvimsa-brahmana` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Genre:** brāhmaṇa
**Part of:** [Pañcaviṃśa Brāhmaṇa](pancavimsa-brahmana.md)
**Structure:** 5 (or 6) prapāṭhakas; the last is the Adbhuta Brāhmaṇa
**Availability:** digitized-original

The 'twenty-sixth' chapter-book appended to the Pañcaviṃśa: rites of the Sāmaveda not covered there, sorcery and counter-sorcery rites, and in its final part (the Adbhuta Brāhmaṇa) the portents in the world — images of gods that laugh, weep or sweat, strange births and signs in the sky — with the propitiatory rites that avert them.

_Notes: Commentary: Sāyaṇa. Content of the Adbhuta section given from memory, chapter level._
