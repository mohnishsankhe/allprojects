Some three hundred abhaṅgas ascribed to Cokhāmeḷā, a Mahār (then 'untouchable') devotee of Maṅgaḷveḍhe, and to his family (Soyrābāī, Karmameḷā, Nirmaḷā, Bankā): love of Viṭṭhala, the pain of being kept from the temple, and the claim that outward birth does not touch inner devotion.

### abhaṅga 'ūsa ḍoṅgā' <a id="tea-cokhamela-abhangas-us-donga"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The sugarcane is crooked, but its juice is not; why be deceived by the outward colour? The river bends, but its water does not; Cokhā is crooked outside, but his devotion is not.

> ऊस डोंगा परि रस नोहे डोंगा । काय भुललासी वरलिया रंगा ॥

### abhaṅgas of complaint <a id="tea-cokhamela-abhangas-temple-door"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Cokhāmeḷā laments that he is kept from the temple and beaten for 'polluting' God, asks Viṭṭhala why he was born low, and yet says the Lord's name is all he needs.
