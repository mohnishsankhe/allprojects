## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### structure <a id="tea-adhyatmopanisad-structure"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The path is set out as four yogas — of scripture, of knowledge, of action and of equanimity — each needing the others; knowledge without action and action without knowledge are both rejected.
