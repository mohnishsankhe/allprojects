On meditation in three sections: the place, the person (the practitioner's qualities) and the practice (the experiences of bliss, clarity and non-thought and their pitfalls).

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### ch1 <a id="tea-samten-ngalso-ch1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The place for meditation: a solitary, pleasant place suited to the season and to one's practice — high and open for clarity, enclosed for calm — free of dangers and disturbances.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice_

### ch2 <a id="tea-samten-ngalso-ch2"></a>

The person who meditates: one with renunciation, faith and diligence, who has turned from worldly concerns, keeps the vows and relies on a qualified teacher.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice, teacher-transmission_

### ch3 <a id="tea-samten-ngalso-ch3"></a>

The practice: preliminaries, then the main meditations that bring the experiences of bliss, clarity and non-thought, each to be released without attachment lest it lead into the desire, form or formless realms; then non-conceptual wisdom.
