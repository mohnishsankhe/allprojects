## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### protect <a id="tea-renwang-jing-protect"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Kings who have the perfection of wisdom recited and honored protect their states from the seven calamities; the perfection of wisdom protects the fruits of buddhahood and the state.
