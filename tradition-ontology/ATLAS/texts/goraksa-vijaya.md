A Bengali narrative poem of the Nāth cycle telling how Gorakṣa, disguised as a dancer and drummer, awakens his guru Mīnanātha, who had forgotten himself among the women of the Kadalī kingdom, and brings him back to yoga.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### whole <a id="tea-goraksa-vijaya-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Mīnanātha, having forgotten himself among the women of the Kadalī kingdom, is near death; his disciple Gorakṣa enters the palace disguised as a dancer and by drum-words and riddles reminds him of who he is, awakening him and bringing him back to yoga.
