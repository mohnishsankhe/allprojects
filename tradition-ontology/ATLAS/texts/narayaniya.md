The section of the Mokṣadharma on the religion of Nārāyaṇa: Nārada visits Nara and Nārāyaṇa at Badarī and the White Island (Śvetadvīpa), where the one-pointed devotees (ekāntins) dwell; Nārāyaṇa reveals himself, teaches his four forms (Vāsudeva, Saṃkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna, Aniruddha) and his future manifestations; the Sātvata or Ekāntika dharma, its lineage of transmission, the bloodless sacrifice of Uparicara Vasu, and the five systems of knowledge (Sāṃkhya, Yoga, Pāñcarātra, Veda, Pāśupata).

### 12.321-326 <a id="tea-narayaniya-12-321-326"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Nārada visits Nara and Nārāyaṇa at Badarī, sees them worship, and asks whom Nārāyaṇa himself worships; told of the supreme self, he goes to the White Island (Śvetadvīpa) north of the milk ocean, where white, one-pointed devotees dwell, and after praising the Lord receives a vision of him in universal form and his teaching.

### 12.323-324 <a id="tea-narayaniya-12-323-324"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

King Uparicara Vasu, devoted to Nārāyaṇa, performs a great sacrifice without slaughter of animals; the offering is taken invisibly by Hari, angering Bṛhaspati; in a dispute between gods and seers whether 'aja' in the injunction 'one should sacrifice with aja' means a goat or seed-grain, Vasu sides with the gods (goat), falls into the earth, and is restored by his devotion.

### 12.326.72-94 <a id="tea-narayaniya-12-326-72-94"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Nārāyaṇa foretells his manifestations (prādurbhāva): as a boar he will raise the sunken earth and slay Hiraṇyākṣa; as the man-lion slay Hiraṇyakaśipu; born of Aditi he will win back the three worlds from Bali; in the Tretā he will be Rāma of the Bhṛgus and destroy the kṣatriyas; at the junction of Tretā and Dvāpara he will be Rāma son of Daśaratha and slay Rāvaṇa; at the junction of Dvāpara and Kali he will appear at Mathurā for Kaṃsa's sake, found Dvārakā and at last destroy his own kin; formerly he appeared as the Swan and as the Horse-headed one who restored the Veda.

### 12.332.13-18 <a id="tea-narayaniya-12-332-13-18"></a>

### 12.336 <a id="tea-narayaniya-12-336"></a>
