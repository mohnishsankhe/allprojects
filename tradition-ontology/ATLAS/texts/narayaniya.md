The section of the Mokṣadharma on the religion of Nārāyaṇa: Nārada visits Nara and Nārāyaṇa at Badarī and the White Island (Śvetadvīpa), where the one-pointed devotees (ekāntins) dwell; Nārāyaṇa reveals himself, teaches his four forms (Vāsudeva, Saṃkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna, Aniruddha) and his future manifestations; the Sātvata or Ekāntika dharma, its lineage of transmission, the bloodless sacrifice of Uparicara Vasu, and the five systems of knowledge (Sāṃkhya, Yoga, Pāñcarātra, Veda, Pāśupata).
