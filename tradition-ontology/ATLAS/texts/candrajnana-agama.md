# Candrajñānāgama

`src:candrajnana-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Candrajñāna, Candrabhāsa (Kiraṇa), Candrāṃśu (Śrīkaṇṭhīya)
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Ananta, then Bṛhaspati (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Rudrabheda no. 19 in the Kāmika's order; 14 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** digitized-original

No. 19 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Rudrabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Ananta, then Bṛhaspati. The Kāmika assigns it 14 upabheda(s): Sthira, Sthāṇu, Mahānta, Vāruṇa, Nandikeśvara, Ekapādapurāṇa, Śaṅkara, Nīlarudraka, Śivabhadra, Kalpabheda, Śrīmukha, Śivaśāsana, Śivaśekhara, Devyāmata. A 'Candrajñāna' also appears in the Vāmakeśvara's list of 64 tantras.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Muktabodha M00263, M00273 (the latter describes itself as a pratiṣṭhātantra); Vīraśaiva recension M00201

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.16/10.23: 'Candrabhāsa' of Ananta → the gods' priest (Bṛhaspati). Śrīkaṇṭhīya: 'Candrāṃśu'. In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the chest (pūrva 1.93–101).
