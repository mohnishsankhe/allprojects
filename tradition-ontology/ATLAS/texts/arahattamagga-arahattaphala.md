### passim <a id="tea-arahattamagga-arahattaphala-passim"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _recent (post-1800)_

At the last stage the citta becomes radiant and seemingly wondrous; this radiance is the lair of ignorance and must itself be investigated and destroyed — when it is, the pure citta remains, beyond conventions.
