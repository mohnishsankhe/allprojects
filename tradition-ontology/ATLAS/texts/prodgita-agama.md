# Prodgītāgama

`src:prodgita-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Prodgīta, Udgīta, Madgīta / Sadgīta (Śrīkaṇṭhīya readings)
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Śūlin, then Kavaca (Kiraṇa: Kaca) (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Rudrabheda no. 21 in the Kāmika's order; 16 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** digitized-original

No. 21 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Rudrabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Śūlin, then Kavaca (Kiraṇa: Kaca). The Kāmika assigns it 16 upabheda(s): Kavaca, Vārāha, Piṅgalāmata, Pāśabandha, Daṇḍadhara, Aṅkuśa, Dhanurdhara, Śivajñāna, Vijñāna, Śrīkālajñāna, Āyurveda, Dhanurveda, Sarpadaṃṣṭrīvibhedana, Gīta, Bharata, Ātodya. Its upabheda list includes arts and sciences (medicine, archery, music, dramaturgy).

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.14/10.20: Śūlin (Rudra) → the sage Kaca. Śrīkaṇṭhīya: 'Madgīta' (var. 'Sadgīta'). In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the tongue (pūrva 1.93–101).
