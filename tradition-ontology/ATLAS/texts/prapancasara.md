**Attribution:** tradition: composed by Śaṅkarācārya; scholarly: attribution to the Advaitin Śaṅkara is doubted by most scholars; confidence: disputed

A manual of mantra-śāstra attributed to Śaṅkara: the emergence of the world from the supreme through Śakti, sound and the letters (mātṛkā), mantras of many deities with their worship, initiation and daily rites. A foundational source for mantra-based worship used in Kerala.
