## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### intro <a id="tea-katha-upanisad-bhasya-sankara-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The word upaniṣad means the knowledge of Brahman, because for those who approach it with dispassion it loosens and destroys the seeds of saṃsāra such as ignorance, and leads them to Brahman.
