Haribhadra's twenty Prakrit verses (the 17th of his Viṃśativiṃśikā): all religious activity that joins one to liberation is yoga; its five kinds — posture (sthāna), sound (ūrṇa), meaning (artha), support (ālambana), the supportless (anālambana) — each in four grades (desire, practice, steadiness, perfection). Commented by Yaśovijaya.
