Tiruttakkatēvar's Tamil epic in thirteen cantos (ilampakam) on Prince Cīvakaṉ (Jīvaka / Jīvandhara): born in a cremation ground after his father's murder, he grows up in a merchant's house, wins eight wives and his kingdom, and finally renounces, becomes a monk under Mahāvīra and attains release. One of the five great Tamil epics, it first used the viruttam metre on an epic scale.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 13 <a id="tea-civakacintamani-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

In the last canto, Cīvakaṉ, having ruled, is moved by the sight of a forest-keeper snatching a jackfruit from a monkey to see kingship as seizure; he gives the kingdom to his son, goes to Mahāvīra's assembly, takes initiation, and after austerities attains release; his queens become nuns.
