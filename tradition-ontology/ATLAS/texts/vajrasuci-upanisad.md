'Who is a brāhmaṇa?' — not the jīva, not the body, not birth, not learning, not ritual action nor dharmic deeds; a brāhmaṇa is one who has directly realized the non-dual Self and is free of faults, desire and pride; this is the intent of śruti, smṛti, itihāsa and purāṇa.

## Teachings (5: sourced 5)

### prose (definition) <a id="tea-vajrasuci-upanisad-prose-definition"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

The brāhmaṇa is one who has directly realized, like a fruit in the hand, the non-dual Self — without caste, quality or action, being-knowledge-bliss — and lives fulfilled, free of desire and passion, endowed with calm and restraint, untouched by pride and ego; this is the intent of śruti, smṛti, purāṇa and itihāsa.

### prose (not birth) <a id="tea-vajrasuci-upanisad-prose-not-birth"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Not birth: many great seers were born of other beings — Ṛṣyaśṛṅga of a deer, Kauśika of kuśa grass, Jāmbūka of a jackal, Vālmīki of an anthill, Vyāsa of a fisherman's daughter, Gautama from a hare's back, Vasiṣṭha of Urvaśī, Agastya from a pot.

### prose (not jīva, not body) <a id="tea-vajrasuci-upanisad-prose-not-jiva-not-body"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Not the jīva, which is the same in past and future bodies; not the body, which is the same in all castes, and at whose burning the sons would incur the sin of killing a brāhmaṇa.

### prose (not knowledge, action or dharma) <a id="tea-vajrasuci-upanisad-prose-not-knowledge-action-or-dharma"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Not knowledge, for many kṣatriyas and others are seers of the highest truth; not action, for all act from their karma; not doing dharma, for kṣatriyas and others give gold.

### prose (question) <a id="tea-vajrasuci-upanisad-prose-question"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Of the four castes the brāhmaṇa is chief, say the Vedas and smṛtis; who then is a brāhmaṇa — the jīva, the body, birth, knowledge, action or the doer of dharma?
