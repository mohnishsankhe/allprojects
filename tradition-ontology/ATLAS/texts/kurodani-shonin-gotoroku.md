## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### how-to-live <a id="tea-kurodani-shonin-gotoroku-how-to-live"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Live your present life in whatever way lets you best say the nenbutsu; whatever hinders the nenbutsu, give it up. If you cannot say it as a wandering hijiri, say it married; if you cannot say it married, say it as a hijiri; if living alone hinders it, live with others; food, clothing and shelter are aids to the nenbutsu.

### one-calling-letter <a id="tea-kurodani-shonin-gotoroku-one-calling-letter"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Take it in faith that birth is attained by a single calling, yet in practice strive all your life; to doubt that one calling suffices is to doubt the Buddha's vow, and to neglect continued calling because one suffices is also wrong — believe with the one calling, practise with the whole life.
