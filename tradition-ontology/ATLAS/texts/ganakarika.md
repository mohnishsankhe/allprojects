Eight verses naming the nine groups (gaṇa) of the Pāśupata system: eight pentads - gains (lābha), impurities (mala), means (upāya), places (deśa), stages (avasthā), purifications (viśuddhi), factors of initiation (dīkṣākārin) and strengths (bala) - and one triad of livelihoods (vṛtti). The teacher is he who knows these nine groups and consecrates the disciple.

## Teachings (8: skeleton 8)

### 1 <a id="tea-ganakarika-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Eight pentads and one group of three are to be known; the one who knows these nine groups and consecrates [the disciple] is called the teacher.

> pañcakās tv aṣṭa vijñeyā gaṇaś caikas trikātmakaḥ | vettā navagaṇasyāsya saṃskartā gurur ucyate

### 2 <a id="tea-ganakarika-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The eight pentads are gains, impurities, means, places, stages, purifications, factors of initiation and strengths; the three are the livelihoods.

> lābhā malā upāyāś ca deśāvasthāviśuddhayaḥ | dīkṣākāribalāny aṣṭa pañcakās trīṇi vṛttayaḥ

### 3 <a id="tea-ganakarika-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Strength is fivefold: devotion to the teacher, clarity of mind, conquest of the pairs of opposites, merit, and vigilance.

> gurubhaktiḥ prasādaś ca mater dvandvajayas tathā | dharmaś caivāpramādaś ca balaṃ pañcavidhaṃ smṛtam

### 4 <a id="tea-ganakarika-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Purification is fivefold: loss of ignorance, of demerit, of what causes attachment, of falling away, and of the paśu-state.

> ajñānahāny adharmasya hāniḥ saṅgakarasya ca | cyutihāniḥ paśutvasya śuddhiḥ pañcavidhā smṛtā

### 5 <a id="tea-ganakarika-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The stages are the manifest, the unmanifest, conquest, cutting and completion as the fifth; the factors of initiation are material, time, rite, image and teacher as the fifth.

> vyaktāvyaktaṃ jayacchedo niṣṭhā caiveha pañcamī | dravyaṃ kālaḥ kriyā mūrtiḥ guruś caiveha pañcamaḥ

### 6 <a id="tea-ganakarika-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The places are the teacher, people, the cave, the cremation ground and Rudra; the gains are knowledge, austerity, permanence, steadiness and perfection as the fifth.

> gurur janaguhādeśaḥ śmaśānaṃ rudra eva ca | jñānaṃ tapo 'tha nityatvaṃ sthitiḥ siddhiś ca pañcamī

### 7 <a id="tea-ganakarika-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Residence, observance, muttering-and-meditation, constant remembrance of Rudra, and grace are the five sure means to the gains.

> vāsaś caryā japadhyānaṃ sadārudrasmṛtis tathā | prasādaś caiva lābhānām upāyāḥ pañca niścitāḥ

`skeleton` · confidence high

False knowledge, demerit, the cause of attachment, falling away and the root of the paśu-state - these five are the impurities to be abandoned in this system.

> mithyājñānam adharmaś ca saktihetuś cyutis tathā | paśutvamūlaṃ pañcaite tantre heyādhikārikāḥ
