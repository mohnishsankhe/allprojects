# Niśvāsamukha

`src:nisvasamukha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Niśvāsamukhatattvasaṃhitā, Mukhāgama
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** `lin:saiva-siddhanta`, [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** tantra (book of the Niśvāsatattvasaṃhitā)
**Part of:** [Niśvāsatattvasaṃhitā](nisvasatattvasamhita.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: later than the Mūla-, Uttara- and Nayasūtra (c. 6th–7th c.?); (confidence low)
**Availability:** unknown

Introductory book of the Niśvāsatattvasaṃhitā. It sets out a fivefold hierarchy of religious paths — worldly (laukika) religion, Vedic (vaidika) religion, the 'spiritual' (ādhyātmika) Sāṃkhya-Yoga, the Atimārga (Pāśupata and Lākula) and the Mantramārga — and is a primary source for the texts' own division between Atimārga and Mantramārga.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: N. Kafle (ed. & tr.), The Niśvāsamukha, IFP/EFEO 2020

_Notes: The Kāmika's list of the Niśvāsa's eight upabhedas includes Niśvāsottara, Niśvāsamukhodaya, Niśvāsanayana and Guhya, which match these books by name._
