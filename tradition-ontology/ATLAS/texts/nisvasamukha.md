Surveys Śaiva revelation as five streams: the worldly (laukika), the Vedic (vaidika), the spiritual (ādhyātmika: Sāṃkhya and Yoga), the Atimārga and the Mantra path. Its account of the Atimārga describes the Pāśupata observance 'beyond the āśramas' and the Lākula observance 'beyond the world' with skull and khaṭvāṅga.

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1-4 <a id="tea-nisvasamukha-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Niśvāsamukha sets out a series of religious paths — worldly (laukika) religion, Vedic (vaidika) religion, the 'spiritual' (ādhyātmika) path of Sāṃkhya and Yoga, the Atimārga (Pāśupata and Lākula) and the Mantramārga — presenting them in ascending order.

### 4 <a id="tea-nisvasamukha-4"></a>

Śaiva revelation is presented as five streams - the worldly, the Vedic, the spiritual (Sāṃkhya and Yoga), the Atimārga and the Mantra path - each higher than the one before.

### 4 <a id="tea-nisvasamukha-4-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Atimārga is of two kinds: the observance 'beyond the āśramas' of the Pāśupatas, with ash-bathing and the rest, and the observance 'beyond the world' of the Lākulas, who carry a skull and a skull-topped staff.
