## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### Kabīr Paracaī, Kabīr's initiation <a id="tea-kabir-paracai-ramananda-at-the-ghat"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Kabīr, a weaver's son refused as a disciple, lay on the steps of the ghāṭ before dawn; Rāmānanda, going down to bathe, stepped on him and cried 'Rām!'; Kabīr took this as his initiation into the Name.

### Kabīr Paracaī, the trial before Sikandar Lodī <a id="tea-kabir-paracai-trial-before-sikandar-lodi"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Accused by brahmins and qāzīs before Sultan Sikandar, Kabīr refuses to bow; he is chained and thrown into the river, into fire and before an elephant, and survives each ordeal, until the Sultan asks his pardon.
