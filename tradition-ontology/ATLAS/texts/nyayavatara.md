## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-nyayavatara-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Valid cognition (pramāṇa) is knowledge that illuminates itself and its object and is free from contradiction; it is direct or indirect.
