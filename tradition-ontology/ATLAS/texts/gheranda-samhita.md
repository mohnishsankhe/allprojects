# Gheraṇḍa Saṃhitā

`src:gheranda-samhita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Original title:** घेरण्डसंहिता
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Genre:** yoga dialogue
**Authors:** 
  - [Gheraṇḍa](../teachers/gheranda.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: the teaching of the sage Gheraṇḍa to Caṇḍakāpāli; scholarly: anonymous; Vaiṣṇava leanings; c. 1700 CE; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Gheraṇḍa's teaching; Scholarly account: c. late 17th – early 18th c. CE; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 7 upadeśas, c. 350 verses (Thomi ed.: 62 + 46 + 102 + 19 + 99 + 24 + 24 lines of numbering)
**Availability:** digitized-original

Teaches 'yoga of the pot' (ghaṭastha-yoga): the body, an unbaked pot, is fired in yoga through seven means — the cleansing acts (purification), postures (firmness), mudrās (steadiness), withdrawal (calm), breath-control (lightness), meditation (direct perception of the self) and samādhi (non-attachment, liberation). It lists 32 postures, 25 mudrās, many variants of the six acts, eight retentions, three kinds of meditation and six kinds of samādhi.
