**Availability:** digitized-original

Four hundred quatrains (veṇpā) of ethical teaching in three parts following the Tamil division of virtue, wealth and love, dwelling above all on the impermanence of wealth, youth and the body, the impurity of the body, renunciation, patience and non-anger, and the company of the good. One of the Eighteen Minor Classics (Patiṉeṇkīḻkkaṇakku).

### adh.1 <a id="tea-naladiyar-adh-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Wealth is impermanent: it comes and goes like the turning of a cart-wheel; share what you have while you have it, for it will not stay.
