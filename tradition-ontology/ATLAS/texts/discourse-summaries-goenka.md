## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### day10 <a id="tea-discourse-summaries-goenka-day10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

The course ends with loving-kindness: having purified the mind somewhat, the meditator shares merit and goodwill; the final goal is the experience beyond mind and matter, nibbāna.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, devotion · stage: all · types: practice, ultimate_

### day4 <a id="tea-discourse-summaries-goenka-day4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

After three days of observing respiration, the meditator moves attention part by part through the body, observing whatever sensation is there objectively and with equanimity, understanding its impermanence.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: beginner · types: practice_

practices: [Body scanning (observation of sensations)](../practices/body-scanning.md), [The ten-day Vipassana course (Goenka)](../practices/ten-day-vipassana-course.md)

### day5 <a id="tea-discourse-summaries-goenka-day5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Craving and aversion arise as reactions to bodily sensations (vedanā); by observing sensations with equanimity one stops generating new saṅkhāras and old ones arise and pass away.

_level: conventional · standpoint: causal · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice, karma-liberation_
