Jñāneśvar's letter of sixty-five ovīs to the aged yogī Cāṅgadev: the self is one, like gold in all ornaments; knower, knowing and known are one reality; addressing it as 'you' or 'I' already divides it.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### whole (65 ovīs) <a id="tea-cangadeva-pasasti-1-65"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

To Cāṅgadev: the Self hidden in the world is like gold hidden in ornaments; knower, knowing and known are one; to write a greeting one must posit a 'you' and an 'I', yet there is nothing apart to address — so this is the Self writing to itself.
