A short Sanskrit treatise attributed to Rāmānanda, answering his disciple Surasurānanda's questions on the supreme reality (Rāma with Sītā), the means (devotion and refuge), the mantra, the marks of the Vaiṣṇava and the goal; it holds refuge in Rāma open to all.

### whole (summary) <a id="tea-vaisnavamatabjabhaskara-questions"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Answering Surasurānanda's questions, Rāmānanda declares the supreme reality to be Rāma with Sītā, the means to be refuge in him through his mantra and devotion, and the goal to be eternal service; refuge is open to every class of person.
