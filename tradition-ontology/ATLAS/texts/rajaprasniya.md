# Rājapraśnīya

**Language:** Ardhamāgadhī Prakrit
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md), [Sthānakavāsī](../lineages/sthanakavasi.md), [Śvetāmbara Terāpanth](../lineages/terapanthi.md)
**Genre:** upāṅga (dialogue)
**Part of:** [Śvetāmbara Āgamas (the Jain canon)](svetambara-agamas.md)
**Location in parent:** Upāṅga 2
**Availability:** digitized-original

The second Upāṅga: the god Sūryābha's visit to Mahāvīra and his worship of Jina images in his celestial palace, and the dialogue in which Keśin, of Pārśva's line, convinces King Paesi (Pradeśin), who had tried to find the soul by experiments on criminals, that the soul is distinct from the body.

_Notes: The Paesi dialogue parallels the Pāyāsi Sutta (DN 23) of the Pali canon, where the Buddhist monk Kumāra Kassapa answers King Pāyāsi._
