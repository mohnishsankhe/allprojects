## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-drodon-khakhyabma-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

With refuge and bodhicitta, visualize Avalokiteśvara above the heads of oneself and all beings; his light purifies them; recite the six-syllable mantra; at the end rest with one's body and others' as the deity's body, sounds as mantra, and thoughts as the expanse of wisdom.
