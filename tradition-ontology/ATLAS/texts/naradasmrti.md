The first Dharmaśāstra devoted wholly to law and legal procedure (vyavahāra): its introduction teaches that when people were devoted to dharma and truthful there was no litigation, and that legal procedure arose when dharma was lost among humans (Mātṛkā 1.1–2); it then treats the eighteen titles of law.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### m1.1-2 <a id="tea-naradasmrti-m1-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When people were devoted solely to dharma and spoke the truth, there was no litigation, no hatred and no envy; when dharma was lost among men, legal procedure (vyavahāra) came into being.
