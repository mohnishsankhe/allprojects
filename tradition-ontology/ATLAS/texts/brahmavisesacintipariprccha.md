## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-brahmavisesacintipariprccha-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

All dharmas are the same in not arising; the bodhisattva does not seek nirvāṇa apart from saṃsāra.
