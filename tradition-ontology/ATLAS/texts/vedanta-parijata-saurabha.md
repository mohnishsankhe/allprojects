Nimbārka's brief commentary on the Brahma Sūtras establishing natural difference-and-non-difference: souls and world are Brahman's powers, different and non-different from him like sun and rays or snake and coils.

### 1.4.26 <a id="tea-vedanta-parijata-saurabha-1-4-26"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Brahman transforms itself into the world by its own power while remaining itself; the world is its real effect.

### 2.3.43 <a id="tea-vedanta-parijata-saurabha-2-3-43"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The soul is a part (aṃśa) of Brahman, since scripture declares both difference and non-difference between them; being a part it is both different and non-different from the whole.

### 3.2.27 <a id="tea-vedanta-parijata-saurabha-3-2-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

### 4.4.4 <a id="tea-vedanta-parijata-saurabha-4-4-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The released soul exists in non-separation (avibhāga) from Brahman, as seen in scripture, while remaining a distinct knower who enjoys Brahman's nature.
