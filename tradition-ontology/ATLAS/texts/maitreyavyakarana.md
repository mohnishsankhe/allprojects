## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-maitreyavyakarana-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Maitreya will be born when human lifespan is long, in the city Ketumatī, awaken under the nāga-flower tree and teach three great assemblies that free countless beings.
