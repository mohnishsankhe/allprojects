## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-bhasapariccheda-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Salutation to that Kṛṣṇa, lustrous like a fresh rain-cloud, stealer of the garments of the cowherd girls, the seed of the tree of saṃsāra.

> nūtana-jaladhara-rucaye gopabadhūṭī-dukūla-caurāya / tasmai kṛṣṇāya namaḥ saṃsāramahīruhasya bījāya

_level: unmarked · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate_

terms: [īśvara](../terms/isvara.md)

### 2 <a id="tea-bhasapariccheda-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Substance, quality, motion, universal with particularity, inherence and absence — seven categories are declared.

> dravyaṃ guṇas tathā karma sāmānyaṃ saviśeṣakam / samavāyas tathābhāvaḥ padārthāḥ sapta kīrtitāḥ

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: unmarked · types: world-fate_
