**Structure:** Tantrasāra: sāmānyapāda, mantrapāda; Siddhāntasāra (uparibhāga): kriyāpāda, yogapāda
**Availability:** digitized-original

A large ritual compendium in two parts — the Tantrasāra (general section and a mantra section covering Śaiva, Vaiṣṇava, Śākta and other deities and many applied rites) and the Siddhāntasāra (kriyāpāda on Śaiva Siddhānta ritual: initiation, worship, installation; and a yogapāda). It is a principal authority behind Kerala temple tantra.
