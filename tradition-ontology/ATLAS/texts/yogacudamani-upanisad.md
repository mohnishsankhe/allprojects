### 2-3 <a id="tea-yogacudamani-upanisad-2-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Posture, breath-restraint, withdrawal, concentration, meditation and absorption are the six limbs of yoga; of postures siddha and kamala (lotus) are chief. The yogin should know the six centres, sixteen supports (ādhāra), three targets (lakṣya) and five voids (vyoma).

### 27-30 <a id="tea-yogacudamani-upanisad-27-30"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The jīva is tossed up and down by prāṇa and apāna like a ball struck by the bat, and like a hawk tied by a string it is drawn back.

### 31-35 <a id="tea-yogacudamani-upanisad-31-35"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The jīva repeats the ajapā-gāyatrī 21,600 times a day; it arises from the kuṇḍalinī; it is the knowledge of prāṇa and the great knowledge.

### 43-44 <a id="tea-yogacudamani-upanisad-43-44"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Moderate diet is food that is sweet and unctuous, leaving a quarter of the stomach empty, eaten for Śiva's pleasure. The kuṇḍalinī is bondage for the ignorant and liberation for yogins.

### 71-88 <a id="tea-yogacudamani-upanisad-71-88"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Praṇava and the four states; sa means 'you' and ha 'that' — their union is 'that you are'; bound by the senses and 'mine' one is jīva, by 'not mine' kevala; one who repeats the praṇava, pure or impure, is untouched like a lotus leaf by water.
