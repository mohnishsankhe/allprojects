# Niśītha Sūtra

**Alternate titles:** Nisīha, Ācāraprakalpa, Āyārapakappa
**Language:** Ardhamāgadhī Prakrit
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md), [Sthānakavāsī](../lineages/sthanakavasi.md), [Śvetāmbara Terāpanth](../lineages/terapanthi.md)
**Genre:** chedasūtra (monastic law)
**Part of:** [Śvetāmbara Āgamas (the Jain canon)](svetambara-agamas.md)
**Location in parent:** Chedasūtra
**Structure:** 20 uddeśakas
**Availability:** digitized-original

The chief Chedasūtra: in twenty sections it lists the transgressions of monks and nuns and the atonement (prāyaścitta) fixed for each. Originally the fifth appendix (cūlā) of the Ācārāṅga ('Ācāraprakalpa'); its bhāṣya and Jinadāsa's cūrṇi are major sources for monastic law and early Jain history.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Niśītha Cūrṇi](nisitha-curni.md)

_Notes: Chedasūtras were traditionally restricted to mature monks; their study by laypeople was discouraged._
