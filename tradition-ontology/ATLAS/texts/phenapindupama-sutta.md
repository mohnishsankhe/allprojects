## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 2-15 <a id="tea-phenapindupama-sutta-2-15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As a lump of foam on the Ganges, a water bubble in the rain, a mirage in the hot season, a plantain trunk without heartwood and a magician's illusion are seen as void and without substance, so form, feeling, perception, formations and consciousness are seen as void and without substance; when vitality, heat and consciousness leave this body it lies cast aside as food for others.
