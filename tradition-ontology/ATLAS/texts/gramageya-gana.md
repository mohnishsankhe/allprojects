# Grāmageya-gāna (Veyagāna)

`src:gramageya-gana` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Prakṛtigāna, Veyagāna
**Original title:** ग्रामगेयगानम्
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Kauthuma śākhā (Sāmaveda)](../lineages/sakha-kauthuma.md), [Rāṇāyanīya śākhā (Sāmaveda)](../lineages/sakha-ranayaniya.md)
**Genre:** gāna (song-book)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The first song-book of the Kauthuma–Rāṇāyanīya Sāmaveda: the melodies on the Pūrvārcika verses 'to be sung in the village', i.e. in public ritual; it shows how each verse is transformed into a sāman with stretched vowels, repetitions and stobhas.
