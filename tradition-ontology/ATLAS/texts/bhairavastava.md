### 2 <a id="tea-bhairavastava-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Now, by your grace, all this shines as made of you; and you, Maheśa, are always my own self; therefore everything is my own self.

### 4-5 <a id="tea-bhairavastava-4-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Death, do not cast your terrible angry glance on me: steady in the service and contemplation of Śaṅkara, I am made of the fearsome power of Bhairava; with the darkness torn by the rays of awareness made of you, I fear neither death, Yama nor the goblins of karma.
