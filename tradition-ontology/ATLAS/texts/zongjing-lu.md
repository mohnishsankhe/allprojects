### 415a18 <a id="tea-zongjing-lu-415a18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Yanshou's preface: taking the one mind as the source (zong) and illumining the myriad dharmas as a mirror; with the buddha as mirror one sees precepts, samādhi and wisdom as the source of all good; with beings as mirror, greed, hatred and delusion as the source of all evil.

> 所謂舉一心為宗。照萬法為鑑矣
