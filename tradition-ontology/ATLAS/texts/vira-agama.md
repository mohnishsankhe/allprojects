# Vīrāgama

`src:vira-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Vīra, Vīraja (Kiraṇa), Vīrabhadra (some lists)
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Tejas, then Prajāpati (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Rudrabheda no. 15 in the Kāmika's order; 13 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** digitized-original

No. 15 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Rudrabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Tejas, then Prajāpati. The Kāmika assigns it 13 upabheda(s): Prastāra, Phulla, Malla, Prabodha, Bodhabodhaka, Amoha, Mohasamaya, Hākaṭa, Śākaṭādhika, Hala, Vilekhana, Bhadra, Vīra.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: IFP transcript T0029 (Muktabodha M00253); a Vīraśaiva 'Vīrāgama' is also catalogued (M00608)

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.16/10.24: 'Vīraja' of Tejas. The Śrīkaṇṭhīya list has no 'Vīra' as such (it has 'Visara' and 'Vīrabhadra'). In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the throat (pūrva 1.93–101).
