# Pramāṇavārttika

**Original title:** प्रमाणवार्त्तिक
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
**Genre:** kārikā (vārttika on the Pramāṇasamuccaya)

**Authors:** 
  - [Dharmakīrti](../teachers/dharmakirti.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 600–660 CE; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 4 chapters (c. 1450 verses): svārthānumāna (with autocommentary), pramāṇasiddhi, pratyakṣa, parārthānumāna. Numbered as in the local DSBC/GRETIL e-text, whose chapter order is 1 Pramāṇasiddhi (the two opening maṅgala verses counted as 1.1–2), 2 Pratyakṣa, 3 Svārthānumāna, 4 Parārthānumāna. In the Tibetan / Miyasaka order usually cited (1 Svārthānumāna, 2 Pramāṇasiddhi, 3 Pratyakṣa, 4 Parārthānumāna) e-text 1.n ≈ PV 2.(n−2), 2.n = PV 3.n, 3.n ≈ PV 1.n; the e-text's own numbering has small irregularities.
**Availability:** digitized-original

Dharmakīrti's great verse treatise re-founding Buddhist epistemology: the reason's necessary connection by identity or causality, exclusion, the proof that the Buddha is a person of valid cognition (with the arguments for rebirth, the cultivability of compassion and the path), perception and reflexive awareness with the ultimately non-dual nature of cognition, and the theory of proof and refutation in debate.
