## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### Sahaj Prakāś ('rām tajūṃ pai guru na bisārūṃ …') <a id="tea-sahaj-prakas-ram-tajun-pai-guru-na-bisarun"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

I may give up Rām but I will not forget the guru; I do not regard Hari as equal to the guru: Hari gave me birth into the world, the guru cut the round of birth and death; Hari gave me the five thieves, the guru freed me from them.

### Sahaj Prakāś, on death and detachment <a id="tea-sahaj-prakas-sahaj-and-death"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The body is a guest for a few days; the world is a fair that breaks up at evening; do not be proud of youth or wealth — remember the Name before death comes.
