one of the thirty-seven works of Madhva collected as the Sarvamūla ('root of all'); a commentary on selected verses of the Bhagavad Gītā. Its introduction says Vyāsa composed the Mahābhārata out of compassion so that women, śūdras and others not entitled to the Veda might also reach liberation through dharma and knowledge; it reads the Gītā as teaching Kṛṣṇa's supremacy, difference and devoted action.
