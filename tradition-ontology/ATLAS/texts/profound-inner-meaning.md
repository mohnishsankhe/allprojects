## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### ch.1 <a id="tea-profound-inner-meaning-ch-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Mind, beginningless and luminous, is the ground; through confusion about it the aggregates, elements and body with its channels, winds and drops are formed, and the same principles that confuse are what the completion-stage practice purifies.

_level: bridging · standpoint: causal · path: meditation, body-breath · stage: advanced · types: body-layers, consciousness-mind_

### ch.death <a id="tea-profound-inner-meaning-ch-death"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The treatise describes the dissolution of the elements and winds at death, the arising of the clear light, and the correspondence between the processes of death, intermediate state and rebirth and those of sleep, dream and waking.
