## Teachings (11: skeleton 11)

### 6 <a id="tea-vibhanga-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In the sutta-method analysis of dependent origination, ignorance is not-knowing suffering, its origin, its cessation and the way; the formations conditioned by it are meritorious, demeritorious and imperturbable formations and bodily, verbal and mental formations.

> Tattha katamā avijjā? Dukkhe aññāṇaṁ, dukkhasamudaye aññāṇaṁ, dukkhanirodhe aññāṇaṁ, dukkhanirodhagāminiyā paṭipadāya aññāṇaṁ— ayaṁ vuccati “avijjā”. Tattha katame avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā? Puññābhisaṅkhāro, apuññābhisaṅkhāro, āneñjābhisaṅkhāro, kāyasaṅkhāro, vacīsaṅkhāro, cittasaṅkhāro.

_level: conventional · standpoint: causal · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation_

terms: [paṭiccasamuppāda](../terms/paticcasamuppada.md), [avijjā](../terms/avijja.md), [saṅkhāra](../terms/sankhara.md) · concepts: [Dependent origination in the Abhidhamma and commentaries](../concepts/dependent-origination-abhidhamma.md)

### 6 <a id="tea-vibhanga-6-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In the Abhidhamma-method analysis the links are shown occurring within a single moment of (e.g. unwholesome) consciousness: with ignorance as condition, formation (singular); with formation, consciousness; with consciousness, mentality; with mentality, the sixth base; then contact, feeling, craving, clinging, existence, birth, ageing-and-death.

> Avijjāpaccayā saṅkhāro, saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇaṁ, viññāṇapaccayā nāmaṁ, nāmapaccayā chaṭṭhāyatanaṁ, chaṭṭhāyatanapaccayā phasso, phassapaccayā vedanā, vedanāpaccayā taṇhā, taṇhāpaccayā upādānaṁ, upādānapaccayā bhavo, bhavapaccayā jāti, jātipaccayā jarāmaraṇaṁ.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: karma-liberation, consciousness-mind_

concepts: [Dependent origination in the Abhidhamma and commentaries](../concepts/dependent-origination-abhidhamma.md) · disputes: [Does dependent origination describe rebirth across three lives, or a process in present experience?](../debates/dependent-origination-three-lives-or-present.md)

### 7 <a id="tea-vibhanga-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Vibhaṅga's analysis of the foundations of mindfulness has the practitioner contemplate the body internally, externally and both; its body contemplation reviews the body from the soles up and the hair down as full of many kinds of impurity.

> Kathañca bhikkhu ajjhattaṁ kāye kāyānupassī viharati? Idha bhikkhu ajjhattaṁ kāyaṁ uddhaṁ pādatalā adho kesamatthakā tacapariyantaṁ pūraṁ nānappakārassa asucino paccavekkhati—

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice_

terms: [satipaṭṭhāna](../terms/satipatthana.md) · practices: [Mindfulness directed to the body (kāyagatāsati)](../practices/kayagatasati.md)

### 12 <a id="tea-vibhanga-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The sutta-method analysis of jhāna begins with virtue, sense restraint, moderation in food, wakefulness and clear comprehension; the practitioner resorts to a secluded dwelling, sits cross-legged with body erect and mindfulness established, abandons the five hindrances and enters the first jhāna.

> So araññagato vā rukkhamūlagato vā suññāgāragato vā nisīdati pallaṅkaṁ ābhujitvā ujuṁ kāyaṁ paṇidhāya parimukhaṁ satiṁ upaṭṭhapetvā.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice_

terms: [jhāna](../terms/jhana.md), [nīvaraṇa](../terms/nivarana.md) · concepts: [The fourfold and fivefold jhāna schemes](../concepts/five-jhanas-abhidhamma.md)

### 12 <a id="tea-vibhanga-12-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In the Abhidhamma method jhāna is also counted fivefold: the fifth jhāna, attained e.g. on the earth kasiṇa, has two factors (equanimity and one-pointedness).

> Idha bhikkhu yasmiṁ samaye rūpūpapattiyā maggaṁ bhāveti sukhassa ca pahānā …pe… pañcamaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharati pathavīkasiṇaṁ, tasmiṁ samaye duvaṅgikaṁ jhānaṁ …

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: practice, consciousness-mind_

terms: [jhāna](../terms/jhana.md) · concepts: [The fourfold and fivefold jhāna schemes](../concepts/five-jhanas-abhidhamma.md)

### 13 <a id="tea-vibhanga-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The immeasurables are analysed: the practitioner dwells pervading one direction, then the second, third and fourth, above, below, across and everywhere, with a mind of loving-kindness, compassion, gladness and equanimity, abundant, exalted, measureless.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice, ethics_

terms: [brahmavihāra](../terms/brahmavihara.md) · practices: [Loving-kindness (mettā-bhāvanā)](../practices/metta-bhavana.md), [Development of compassion (karuṇā)](../practices/karuna-bhavana.md), [Development of rejoicing (muditā)](../practices/mudita-bhavana.md), [Development of equanimity (upekkhā)](../practices/upekkha-bhavana.md)

### 16 <a id="tea-vibhanga-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The analysis of knowledge classifies knowledge from one kind up to ten kinds, ending with the ten powers of a Tathāgata.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: powers-experiences, consciousness-mind_

### 18 <a id="tea-vibhanga-18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The 'heart of the Dhamma' begins by counting all: five aggregates, twelve bases, eighteen elements, four truths, twenty-two faculties, nine roots, four nutriments, seven contacts, feelings, perceptions, volitions and consciousnesses.

> Kati khandhā, kati āyatanāni, kati dhātuyo, kati saccāni, kati indriyāni, kati hetū, kati āhārā, kati phassā, kati vedanā, kati saññā, kati cetanā, kati cittāni? Pañcakkhandhā, dvādasāyatanāni, aṭṭhārasa dhātuyo, cattāri saccāni, bāvīsatindriyāni, nava hetū, cattāro āhārā, satta phassā, satta vedanā, satta saññā, satta cetanā, satta cittāni.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [khandha](../terms/khandha.md), [āyatana](../terms/ayatana.md), [dhātu](../terms/dhatu.md), [indriya](../terms/indriya.md), [hetu](../terms/hetu.md)
