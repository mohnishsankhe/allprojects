A vast Vaiṣṇava-leaning compendium: creation, tīrthas (Puṣkara and others), vratas (Ekādaśī, Kārttika, Māgha), the glory of the Bhāgavata and the Gītā, Rāma narratives, the worship of the name and of the śālagrāma and tulasī, and the guṇa-classification of the eighteen Purāṇas.

### 6.236.18-21 <a id="tea-padma-purana-6-236-18-21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Śiva tells Umā the classification of the Purāṇas: the Matsya, Kūrma, Liṅga, Śiva, Skanda and Agni are tāmasa; the Viṣṇu, Nāradīya, Bhāgavata, Garuḍa, Padma and Varāha are sāttvika; the Brahmāṇḍa, Brahmavaivarta, Mārkaṇḍeya, Bhaviṣya, Vāmana and Brahma are rājasa.

### nama-aparadha <a id="tea-padma-purana-nama-aparadha"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The ten offences against the name: reviling the saints, regarding Śiva's names and qualities as independent of Viṣṇu's, disregarding the teacher, deriding scripture, taking the name's glory as exaggeration, giving interpretations of it, sinning on the strength of the name, equating it with ordinary pious acts, teaching it to the faithless, and lacking love for it though hearing its glory.
