**Attribution:** tradition: Compiled by Vyāsa (Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana) from the one primeval Purāṇa and taught through Romaharṣaṇa and his line. Told of the time when the world was a golden lotus (Matsya 53.14).; scholarly: Survives in two main recensions - a Bengal recension in five khaṇḍas and a South Indian/Ānandāśrama recension in six khaṇḍas (the Venkateshwara print arranges Sṛṣṭi, Bhūmi, Svarga, Brahma, Pātāla, Uttara and Kriyāyogasāra); many layers of different date.; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Vyāsa, end of Dvāpara; Scholarly account: layered compilation, c. 8th-15th c. CE; (confidence low)
**Structure:** khaṇḍas differing by recension; 55,000 verses by the traditional count (BhP 12.13.4; Matsya 53.14)
**Availability:** digitized-original

A vast Vaiṣṇava-leaning compendium: creation, tīrthas (Puṣkara and others), vratas (Ekādaśī, Kārttika, Māgha), the glory of the Bhāgavata and the Gītā, Rāma narratives, the worship of the name and of the śālagrāma and tulasī, and the guṇa-classification of the eighteen Purāṇas.

_Notes: Contains the Bhāgavata-māhātmya (Uttarakhaṇḍa; colophon checked in the local e-text), the Gītā-māhātmya, the Śiva Gītā (U06), the classification of the Purāṇas by the three guṇas (Uttarakhaṇḍa; ch. number edition-dependent, often cited as 236.18-21), and the list of ten offences against the name as cited by Gauḍīya authors._
