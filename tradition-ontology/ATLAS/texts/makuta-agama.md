# Makuṭāgama

`src:makuta-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Makuṭa, Mukuṭa, Maukuṭa (Śrīkaṇṭhīya)
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Śiva (the Rudra so named), then Mahādeva (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Rudrabheda no. 17 in the Kāmika's order; 2 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** digitized-original

No. 17 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Rudrabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Śiva (the Rudra so named), then Mahādeva. The Kāmika assigns it 2 upabheda(s): Makuṭottara, Makuṭa.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Grantha edition by C. Swaminatha Gurukkal, Devakottai series (Muktabodha M00036); Mukuṭāgama (M00424)
  - kind: original; name: Vīraśaiva recension, kriyā- and caryā-pādas (Muktabodha M00202)

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.17/10.25: Śiva/Īśāna → Mahādeva. The Śrīkaṇṭhīya lists 'Maukuṭa' among the ten Śivabheda, not the Rudrabheda. In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the crown (pūrva 1.93–101).
