## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 3-4 <a id="tea-vakkali-sutta-3-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

To the sick Vakkali who longs to see him: 'Enough, Vakkali! Why do you want to see this foul body? Whoever sees the Dhamma sees me; whoever sees me sees the Dhamma'; and the aggregates are impermanent, so not fit to be regarded as self.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge, devotion · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, ultimate_

concepts: ['Whoever sees the Dhamma sees me'](../concepts/dhammakaya-early.md) · teachers: [Vakkali](../teachers/vakkali.md)

### 8-11 <a id="tea-vakkali-sutta-8-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Vakkali, sick and in pain, uses the knife; the Buddha sees a cloud of smoke moving in all directions — Māra searching for Vakkali's consciousness — and declares that he attained final nibbāna with consciousness unestablished.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: experiential · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: death-dying, karma-liberation_
