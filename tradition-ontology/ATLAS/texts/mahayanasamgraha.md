## Teachings (6: skeleton 6)

### 1 <a id="tea-mahayanasamgraha-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The basis of the knowable is the store-consciousness, called ālaya because all defiled dharmas are stored in it as effects and it in them as cause, and 'appropriating consciousness' (ādāna) because it appropriates the body; it is proved by scripture and reasoning, and its seeds are momentary, simultaneous with their fruit, continuous, determinate, dependent on conditions and productive of their own fruit.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: intermediate · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [ālaya-vijñāna](../terms/alaya-vijnana.md), [ādāna-vijñāna](../terms/adana-vijnana.md), [bīja](../terms/bija.md) · concepts: [The store-consciousness (ālaya-vijñāna)](../concepts/alaya-vijnana.md), [The six characteristics of seeds](../concepts/six-characteristics-of-seeds.md) · teachers: [Asaṅga](../teachers/asanga.md)

### 2 <a id="tea-mahayanasamgraha-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The characteristic of the knowable is the three natures: the dependent nature is cognition-only arising from the seeds of the store; the imagined is its appearing as objects though there are none; the perfected is the dependent's complete absence of the imagined object. The dependent partakes of both defilement and purification.

_level: bridging · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: intermediate · types: ultimate_

concepts: [The three natures (sūtra layer)](../concepts/three-natures.md) · teachers: [Asaṅga](../teachers/asanga.md)

### 3 <a id="tea-mahayanasamgraha-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Entry into the characteristic of the knowable is entry into cognition-only: hearing the Mahāyāna teaching, the bodhisattva at the stage of practice by resolve first understands that names, meanings, own-natures and particulars are mere designations (four investigations and four exact knowledges), then that objects are cognition-only, then that even cognition-only does not appear, and enters the path of seeing.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, knowledge · stage: intermediate (adhimukticaryā → darśana-mārga) · types: practice_

practices: [The four investigations and four exact knowledges](../practices/four-investigations.md), [Entry into cognition-only (vijñaptimātratā-praveśa)](../practices/entry-into-cognition-only.md) · teachers: [Asaṅga](../teachers/asanga.md)

### 8 <a id="tea-mahayanasamgraha-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Higher wisdom is non-conceptual cognition (nirvikalpa-jñāna): preparatory, fundamental and subsequently obtained; it is not mere absence of thought, nor like the unconsciousness of sleep or of the mindless attainments, but a cognition that does not construct its object.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: experiential · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind, practice_

terms: [nirvikalpa-jñāna](../terms/nirvikalpa-jnana.md), [pṛṣṭhalabdha-jñāna](../terms/prsthalabdha-jnana.md) · concepts: [Non-conceptual wisdom (nirvikalpa-jñāna)](../concepts/non-conceptual-wisdom.md) · teachers: [Asaṅga](../teachers/asanga.md)

### 9 <a id="tea-mahayanasamgraha-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The fruit as abandonment is the transformation of the basis — the dependent nature turned from its defiled to its purified side — and non-abiding nirvāṇa, which abides neither in saṃsāra nor in (a quiescent) nirvāṇa.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation_

terms: [āśraya-parāvṛtti](../terms/asraya-paravrtti.md), [apratiṣṭhita-nirvāṇa](../terms/apratisthita-nirvana.md) · concepts: [Transformation of the basis (āśraya-parāvṛtti)](../concepts/asraya-paravrtti.md), [Non-abiding nirvāṇa](../concepts/apratisthita-nirvana.md) · teachers: [Asaṅga](../teachers/asanga.md)

### 10 <a id="tea-mahayanasamgraha-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The fruit as wisdom is the three bodies of a buddha: the essential body (dharmakāya), the enjoyment body and the transformation body, the first being the support of the other two.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: divine · path: general · stage: realized · types: ultimate_
