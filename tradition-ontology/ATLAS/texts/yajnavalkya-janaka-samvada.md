Yājñavalkya teaches king Daivarāti Janaka the evolution of the principles (tattvas), the guṇas, the yoga practices and the time of dissolution, relates his answers to the Gandharva Viśvāvasu's twenty-four questions and a twenty-fifth on reasoning (12.306.27), and says knowledge may be received even from a śūdra (12.306.85).
