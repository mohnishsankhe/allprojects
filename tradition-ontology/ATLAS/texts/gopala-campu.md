Jīva's prose-and-verse narrative of Kṛṣṇa's Vraja pastimes in two parts, in which the gopīs' love is presented as ultimately svakīyā (culminating in Rādhā's marriage to Kṛṣṇa).

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### uttara <a id="tea-gopala-campu-uttara"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The narrative ends with Kṛṣṇa's return to Vraja and his marriage to Rādhā and the gopīs, showing their love to be svakīyā in truth.
