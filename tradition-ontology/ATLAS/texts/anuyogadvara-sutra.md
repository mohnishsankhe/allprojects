A Cūlikāsūtra on the method of exposition, taking 'āvaśyaka' as its example: the four doors (upakrama, nikṣepa, anugama, naya), the fourfold placement of a word's meaning (name, representation, substance, actual state), measures and numbers, the seven notes and the nine sentiments, and the standpoints.
