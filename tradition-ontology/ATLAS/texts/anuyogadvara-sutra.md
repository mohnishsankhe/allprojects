A Cūlikāsūtra on the method of exposition, taking 'āvaśyaka' as its example: the four doors (upakrama, nikṣepa, anugama, naya), the fourfold placement of a word's meaning (name, representation, substance, actual state), measures and numbers, the seven notes and the nine sentiments, and the standpoints.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### niksepa <a id="tea-anuyogadvara-sutra-niksepa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Any word is to be understood by fourfold placement (nikṣepa): as a name (nāma), as a representation (sthāpanā) — in wood, painting, clay or the like, whether resembling the thing or not — as the substance without the actual state (dravya), and as the actual state (bhāva); 'āvaśyaka' is analysed in these four ways.
