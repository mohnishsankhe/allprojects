Five verses, set in the tradition's story of Śaṅkara meeting an outcaste (caṇḍāla) at Kāśī, affirming that whoever has firmly realized the one consciousness — outcaste or twice-born — is the guru: 'this is my conviction'.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-manisa-pancaka-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Whoever has firmly realized that the one consciousness shines in waking, dream and deep sleep and pervades all beings from Brahmā to the ant — whether he be an outcaste or a twice-born — he is my guru: this is my conviction.

> caṇḍālo 'stu sa tu dvijo 'stu gurur ity eṣā manīṣā mama
