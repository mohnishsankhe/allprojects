## Teachings (11: skeleton 11)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-udana-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Seated for seven days under the Bodhi tree, in the first watch the Buddha attended to dependent origination in forward order ('when this exists, that comes to be') and exclaimed: when phenomena become manifest to the ardent meditating brahmin, all his doubts vanish, since he understands states with their causes.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: experiential · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation_

terms: [paṭiccasamuppāda](../terms/paticcasamuppada.md) · concepts: [Dependent origination](../concepts/dependent-origination.md), [The awakening of the Buddha](../concepts/awakening-of-the-buddha.md)

### 1.3 <a id="tea-udana-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In the last watch he attended to dependent origination forward and backward: when phenomena become manifest to the ardent meditating brahmin he stands dispersing Māra's army, like the sun illuminating the sky.

### 3.10 <a id="tea-udana-3-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

This world, born of torment and afflicted by contact, calls disease 'self'; whatever it conceives, it is otherwise than that. Attached to becoming, afflicted by becoming, it delights in becoming; what it delights in is a fear. Ascetics who say liberation from becoming is by becoming, or escape from becoming by non-becoming, are not released; this suffering arises dependent on all acquisitions.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: experiential · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, dispute_

terms: [bhāva](../terms/bhava.md), [upādhi](../terms/upadhi.md)

### 4.1 <a id="tea-udana-4-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When the liberation of mind is immature, five things lead to its maturity: good friends, virtue, talk on few wishes and seclusion, energy, and wisdom into rise and fall; and further one should develop foulness to abandon lust, love to abandon ill will, mindfulness of breathing to cut off thinking, and the perception of impermanence to uproot the conceit 'I am'; one who perceives impermanence perceives not-self and attains nibbāna here and now.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice_

### 5.1 <a id="tea-udana-5-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Having searched all directions with the mind, one finds no one dearer than oneself; so too each person holds himself dearest; therefore one who loves himself should not harm another.

> “Sabbā disā anuparigamma cetasā, Nevajjhagā piyataramattanā kvaci; Evaṁ piyo puthu attā paresaṁ, Tasmā na hiṁse paramattakāmo”ti.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: action · stage: all · types: ethics_

concepts: [Not harming others](../concepts/non-harming.md) · teachers: [Pasenadi](../teachers/pasenadi.md), [Mallikā](../teachers/mallika.md)

### 5.5 <a id="tea-udana-5-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

As the great ocean has eight wonderful qualities — it slopes gradually, keeps to its shore, casts out corpses, all rivers lose their names in it, it neither fills nor empties, it has one taste of salt, it holds many treasures and great beings — so this Dhamma and discipline has a gradual training, disciples do not transgress the rules, the Saṅgha does not live with the immoral, the four classes who go forth lose their former names, nibbāna is neither filled nor emptied, it has one taste, the taste of liberation, it holds many treasures (the thirty-seven qualities) and great beings (the eight noble persons).

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, karma-liberation_

terms: [vimuttirasa](../terms/vimuttirasa.md) · concepts: [The gradual training (anupubbasikkhā)](../concepts/gradual-training.md), [The eight noble persons](../concepts/eight-noble-persons.md)

### 6.4 <a id="tea-udana-6-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Rival ascetics quarrel about whether the world is eternal, the soul the same as the body, the Tathāgata existing after death; the Buddha tells of a king who had men born blind touch an elephant — each, touching a part, declared the elephant to be like a pot, a winnowing basket, a ploughshare, a post — and they came to blows; so the sectarians, blind, seeing only one side, quarrel.

_level: conventional · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: dispute_

concepts: [The blind men and the elephant](../concepts/blind-men-and-elephant.md) · disputes: [Should the questions about the eternity and extent of the world, soul and body, and the Tathāgata after death be answered?](../debates/avyakata.md)

### 8.1 <a id="tea-udana-8-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There is that sphere where there is neither earth nor water nor fire nor air, neither the sphere of infinite space, nor of infinite consciousness, nor of nothingness, nor of neither-perception-nor-non-perception, neither this world nor another nor both, neither sun nor moon; there I say there is no coming, no going, no staying, no passing away, no arising; it is without support, without occurrence, without object: this is the end of suffering.

> “Atthi, bhikkhave, tadāyatanaṁ, yattha neva pathavī, na āpo, na tejo, na vāyo, na ākāsānañcāyatanaṁ, na viññāṇañcāyatanaṁ, na ākiñcaññāyatanaṁ, na nevasaññānāsaññāyatanaṁ, nāyaṁ loko, na paraloko, na ubho candimasūriyā. Tatrāpāhaṁ, bhikkhave, neva āgatiṁ vadāmi, na gatiṁ, na ṭhitiṁ, na cutiṁ, na upapattiṁ; appatiṭṭhaṁ, appavattaṁ, anārammaṇamevetaṁ. Esevanto dukkhassā”ti. Paṭhamaṁ.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: ultimate_

terms: [āyatana](../terms/ayatana.md), [nibbāna](../terms/nibbana.md) · concepts: [Nibbāna](../concepts/nibbana.md)

### 8.2 <a id="tea-udana-8-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The uninclined is hard to see, the truth is not easy to see; craving is pierced in one who knows, for one who sees there is nothing.

> “Duddasaṁ anataṁ nāma, na hi saccaṁ sudassanaṁ; Paṭividdhā taṇhā jānato, passato natthi kiñcanan”ti.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: ultimate_

concepts: [Nibbāna](../concepts/nibbana.md)

### 8.3 <a id="tea-udana-8-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There is an unborn, unbecome, unmade, unconditioned; if there were not, no escape would be discerned from the born, become, made, conditioned; but because there is, an escape is discerned.

> “Atthi, bhikkhave, ajātaṁ abhūtaṁ akataṁ asaṅkhataṁ. No cetaṁ, bhikkhave, abhavissa ajātaṁ abhūtaṁ akataṁ asaṅkhataṁ, nayidha jātassa bhūtassa katassa saṅkhatassa nissaraṇaṁ paññāyetha. Yasmā ca kho, bhikkhave, atthi ajātaṁ abhūtaṁ akataṁ asaṅkhataṁ, tasmā jātassa bhūtassa katassa saṅkhatassa nissaraṇaṁ paññāyatī”ti. Tatiyaṁ.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: ultimate_

terms: [ajāta](../terms/ajata.md), [asaṅkhata](../terms/asankhata.md) · concepts: [Nibbāna](../concepts/nibbana.md)

### 8.4 <a id="tea-udana-8-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

For one who is dependent there is wavering; for the independent there is no wavering; without wavering there is calm; with calm there is no inclination; without inclination there is no coming and going; without coming and going no passing away and arising; then there is neither here nor beyond nor between the two: this is the end of suffering.

> “Nissitassa calitaṁ, anissitassa calitaṁ natthi. Calite asati passaddhi, passaddhiyā sati nati na hoti. Natiyā asati āgatigati na hoti. Āgatigatiyā asati cutūpapāto na hoti. Cutūpapāte asati nevidha na huraṁ na ubhayamantarena.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: ultimate_
