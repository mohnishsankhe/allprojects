Tirumaṅkai Āḻvār's 1084 verses in eleven centums: the discovery of the name 'Nārāyaṇa' after a life of wrongdoing, pilgrimage hymns to some eighty sites, the Lord's avatāras, and love-longing as Parakāla-nāyakī.

### 1.1.1 <a id="tea-periya-tirumoli-1-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

I withered, withered and suffered in my mind; I was caught in the great sorrow of birth, running after young women and their pleasures; then I searched, searched, and found for myself — the name 'Nārāyaṇa'.
