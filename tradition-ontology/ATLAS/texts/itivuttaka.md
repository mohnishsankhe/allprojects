## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 1 <a id="tea-itivuttaka-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Abandon one thing and I guarantee you non-return: greed; beings overcome by greed go to a bad destination; those with insight abandon it and never return to this world.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: karma-liberation_

### 27 <a id="tea-itivuttaka-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

All grounds for making merit are not worth a sixteenth part of the liberation of mind by love, which outshines them as the moon outshines the stars; one who develops boundless love, seeing the destruction of acquisitions, has fetters worn thin; loving even one being with a pure heart is wholesome.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice_

concepts: [The benefits of loving-kindness](../concepts/benefits-of-metta.md) · practices: [Loving-kindness (mettā-bhāvanā)](../practices/metta-bhavana.md)

### 43 <a id="tea-itivuttaka-43"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There is an unborn, unbecome, unmade, unconditioned; if there were not, no escape from the born, become, made, conditioned would be known.

> “Atthi, bhikkhave, ajātaṁ abhūtaṁ akataṁ asaṅkhataṁ. No cetaṁ, bhikkhave, abhavissa ajātaṁ abhūtaṁ akataṁ asaṅkhataṁ, nayidha jātassa bhūtassa katassa saṅkhatassa nissaraṇaṁ paññāyetha. Yasmā ca kho, bhikkhave, atthi ajātaṁ abhūtaṁ akataṁ asaṅkhataṁ, tasmā jātassa bhūtassa katassa saṅkhatassa nissaraṇaṁ paññāyatī”ti.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: ultimate_

terms: [ajāta](../terms/ajata.md), [asaṅkhata](../terms/asankhata.md) · concepts: [Nibbāna](../concepts/nibbana.md)

### 44 <a id="tea-itivuttaka-44"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There are two nibbāna-elements: with residue remaining — the arahant whose five faculties remain, who still experiences the agreeable and disagreeable, but in whom lust, hatred and delusion are destroyed; and without residue remaining — the arahant for whom all that is felt, not delighted in, will become cool here itself.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: ultimate, death-dying_

terms: [nibbānadhātu](../terms/nibbana-dhatu.md), [saupādisesa](../terms/saupadisesa.md), [anupādisesa](../terms/anupadisesa.md) · concepts: [Nibbāna](../concepts/nibbana.md), [The two elements of nibbāna](../concepts/two-nibbana-dhatus.md)

### 49 <a id="tea-itivuttaka-49"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Held by two kinds of views some gods and humans hold back, some overreach, and those with vision see: some delight in becoming and hold back when the Dhamma of cessation is taught; some, repelled by becoming, delight in non-becoming ('the self is annihilated at death — this is peaceful'); those with vision see what has come to be as come to be and practise for its disenchantment and cessation.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: dispute, ultimate_
