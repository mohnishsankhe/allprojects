## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 7-14 <a id="tea-saleyyaka-sutta-7-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Unrighteous conduct is threefold in body (killing, stealing, sexual misconduct), fourfold in speech (false, divisive, harsh, idle) and threefold in mind (covetousness, ill will, wrong view); righteous conduct is the abstention from these; by the former beings go to a bad destination, by the latter to a good one, and one of righteous conduct who wishes may even attain the destruction of the effluents.
