## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1.3.4 <a id="tea-tantravarttika-1-3-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Texts not accepted by those who know the three Vedas — those of the Sāṃkhya, Yoga, Pāñcarātra, Pāśupata and Śākya (Buddhist) schools that treat dharma and adharma — are aimed at gaining followers, profit, honour and fame, rest on perception, inference and the like mixed with a little Vedic-seeming dharma (non-violence, truth, self-control, giving, compassion) and on occasional success of mantras and herbs; they have no authority for dharma.

> sāṃkhyayogapāñcarātrapāśupataśākyagranthaparigṛhītadharmādharmanibandhanāni

### 1.3.4/2 <a id="tea-tantravarttika-1-3-4-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Buddha's very transgression is praised by his followers as an ornament: he said 'may the evils done in the Kali age fall on me, and may the world be freed'; for the world's good he transgressed a kṣatriya's dharma, took up the brāhmaṇa's role of teaching, and taught outsiders a dharma the brāhmaṇas had not taught, accepting harm to his own dharma; his followers act against the dharma of śruti and smṛti.

> kalikaluṣakṛtāni yāni loke mayi nipatantu vimucyatāṃ tu lokaḥ

### 1.3.6 <a id="tea-tantravarttika-1-3-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Even teachings of the Buddha and others that do not contradict the Veda — building monasteries and parks, dispassion, meditation, non-violence, truth, self-restraint, giving, compassion — are not authoritative for dharma, because the branches of knowledge accepted as sources of dharma are limited (fourteen or eighteen); and only those whose teaching the Veda sanctions, like the Manus of each age, compose authoritative dharma-texts.

### 1.3.24-29 <a id="tea-tantravarttika-1-3-24-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The obligatory and occasional rites fixed for each stage of life and varṇa are also to be performed, for destroying sins done earlier and for avoiding the future demerit that non-performance would cause.

> pratyāśramavarṇaniyatāni nityanaimittikakarmāṇyapi pūrvakṛtaduritakṣayārthamakaraṇanimittānāgatapratyavāyaparihārārthaṃ ca kartavyāni
