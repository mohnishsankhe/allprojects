## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### method2 <a id="tea-keeping-the-breath-in-mind-method2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Begin by repeating 'bud-' with the in-breath and '-dho' with the out-breath; then observe the breath, let it become comfortable, locate its resting spots (tip of the nose, middle of the head, palate, base of the throat, breastbone, navel), and spread the breath energy throughout the body until the whole body is filled with comfortable breath.
