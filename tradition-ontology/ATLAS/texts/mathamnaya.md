A short text, transmitted in the Daśanāmī maṭhas and ascribed to Śaṅkara, assigning to each of the four āmnāya maṭhas its direction, Veda, mahāvākya, presiding deities, first ācārya, and the sannyāsa names attached to it; it is the tradition's charter for the order.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-mathamnaya-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The tradition's charter: Śaṅkara established four āmnāya maṭhas — Śṛṅgeri (south; Yajurveda; 'ahaṃ brahmāsmi'; Sureśvara), Govardhana at Purī (east; Ṛgveda; 'prajñānaṃ brahma'; Padmapāda), Śāradā at Dvārakā (west; Sāmaveda; 'tat tvam asi'; Hastāmalaka) and Jyotirmaṭh (north; Atharvaveda; 'ayam ātmā brahma'; Toṭaka) — each with its sannyāsa names.
