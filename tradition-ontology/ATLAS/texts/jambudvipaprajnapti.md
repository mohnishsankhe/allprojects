## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2 <a id="tea-jambudvipaprajnapti-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Describes the six ages of the descending half-cycle and the life of Ṛṣabha, first king and first Tīrthaṅkara, who in the transition from the age of wish-fulfilling trees taught people the seventy-two arts for men and sixty-four for women, and cooking, pottery and the other crafts, then renounced and founded the dharma.
