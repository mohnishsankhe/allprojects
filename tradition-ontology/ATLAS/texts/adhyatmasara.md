### 1.2 <a id="tea-adhyatmasara-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Jinas call adhyātma the pure activity undertaken with reference to the self by those who have thrown off the rule of delusion.

### vairagya <a id="tea-adhyatmasara-vairagya"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Detachment is threefold: born of suffering, born of delusion, and born of knowledge; only the last is true and lasting.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: general · stage: all · types: practice, consciousness-mind_

### yoga <a id="tea-adhyatmasara-yoga"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Yoga is of two kinds, action-yoga and knowledge-yoga; action purifies and supports knowledge, and knowledge makes action fruitful; the adept rests in knowledge while action falls away of itself.
