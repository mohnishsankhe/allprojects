Mipham's 'Lion's Roar' exposition of buddha-nature, arguing that the tathāgatagarbha is the union of luminosity and emptiness and that the buddha-qualities are primordially present, avoiding both a merely negative emptiness and an existent self.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-sugatagarbha-lions-roar-mipham-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Buddha-nature is the union of luminosity and emptiness: its emptiness keeps it from being an existent self, its luminosity means the buddha-qualities are primordially present rather than newly produced; to take it as a mere absence or as a permanent entity are both errors.
