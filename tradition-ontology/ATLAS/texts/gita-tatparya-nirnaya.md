one of the thirty-seven works of Madhva collected as the Sarvamūla ('root of all'); a fuller treatment of the purport of the Gītā, chapter by chapter, opening with the claim that the Bhārata is the essence of all śāstras and that within it the Viṣṇusahasranāma and Kṛṣṇa's Gītā are its essence.
