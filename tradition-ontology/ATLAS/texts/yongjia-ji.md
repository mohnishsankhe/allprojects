## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 389b29 <a id="tea-yongjia-ji-389b29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Alert and calm is right; indeterminate calm is wrong; calm and alert is right; confused-thought alertness is wrong.

> 惺惺寂寂是。無記寂寂非。寂寂惺惺是。亂想惺惺非

### 390c04 <a id="tea-yongjia-ji-390c04"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Confused thinking is a sickness and indeterminate dullness is a sickness; calm is a medicine and alertness is a medicine: calm cures confused thinking but, alone, produces dullness; alertness cures dullness but, alone, produces confused thinking; so calm is the aid and alertness the main thing.

> 亂想是病。無記亦病。寂寂是藥。惺惺亦藥
