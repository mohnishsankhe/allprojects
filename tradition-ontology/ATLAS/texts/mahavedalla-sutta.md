## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 3-9 <a id="tea-mahavedalla-sutta-3-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Wisdom and consciousness are conjoined and cannot be separated; what one understands (with wisdom) one cognises (with consciousness); wisdom is to be developed, consciousness to be fully understood. Feeling, perception and consciousness are conjoined: what one feels one perceives, what one perceives one cognises.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind_

### 13-14 <a id="tea-mahavedalla-sutta-13-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Two conditions for the arising of right view: the voice of another and wise attention; right view assisted by virtue, learning, discussion, calm and insight has liberation of mind and by wisdom as its fruit.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: practice, teacher-transmission_

### 21-25 <a id="tea-mahavedalla-sutta-21-25"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The five sense faculties have different fields and resort to mind; they depend on vitality, vitality on heat and heat on vitality, like a lamp's flame and light. One who has died has vitality exhausted, heat dissipated and faculties broken up; one in the cessation of perception and feeling has bodily, verbal and mental formations ceased, but vitality is not exhausted, heat not dissipated and the faculties are clear.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: body-layers, death-dying, consciousness-mind_

terms: [āyu](../terms/ayu.md), [usmā](../terms/usma.md), [indriya](../terms/indriya.md), [saññāvedayitanirodha](../terms/sannavedayitanirodha.md) · concepts: [The attainment of cessation](../concepts/nirodha-samapatti.md), [Death and the attainment of cessation distinguished](../concepts/death-and-cessation.md)

### 26-37 <a id="tea-mahavedalla-sutta-26-37"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The kinds of liberation of mind — immeasurable, through nothingness, through emptiness and signless — differ in meaning and name, or are one in meaning, depending on how they are explained; lust, hatred and delusion are 'makers of measure', 'something' and 'makers of signs', and the unshakeable liberation of mind is the foremost, empty of them.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: meditation, knowledge · stage: advanced · types: karma-liberation, consciousness-mind_
