## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1-3 <a id="tea-titthayatana-sutta-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Three sectarian tenets end in inaction when pressed: that whatever one experiences is due to past deeds (pubbekata-hetu), to God's creation (issara-nimmāna-hetu), or to no cause (ahetu-appaccaya). If so, one would kill, steal and lie because of past deeds, God or chance; for those who hold them there is no desire, effort or sense of what should and should not be done, and they cannot rightly be called ascetics.

### 1-8 <a id="tea-titthayatana-sutta-1-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Three sectarian tenets, if pursued, end in inaction: 'whatever a person experiences is all caused by past deeds', 'is all caused by God's creation (issaranimmāna)', 'is all without cause or condition'. For whoever holds them there is no desire or effort about what should and should not be done, since killing and the rest would also be due to past deeds, God's creation or chance.

_level: conventional · standpoint: polemical · path: action · stage: all · types: dispute, karma-liberation_

### 9-13 <a id="tea-titthayatana-sutta-9-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Dhamma taught by the Buddha is irrefutable and uncensured by wise ascetics: the six elements, the six bases of contact, the eighteen mental explorations and the four noble truths; with the six elements as condition there is descent into the womb, name-and-form, and so feeling, for which the four truths are declared.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, body-layers_
