# Lālan-gīti (the songs of Lalon Fakir)

`src:lalon-giti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

**Alternate titles:** Lālan Gītikā, Lalon's songs, Lālan Śāher gān
**Language:** Bengali
**Family:** shared
**Lineages:** [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md)
**Genre:** Bāul songs (oral; later notebooks and printed collections)
**Authors:** 
  - [Lalon Fakir (Lālan Sāṃi)](../teachers/lalon-fakir.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: the songs of Lalon Sāṃi, each signed with his and his guru Sirāj Sāṃi's name; scholarly: several hundred songs; transmitted orally and in disciples' notebooks; the Shilaidaha notebook used by Rabindranath Tagore's circle is an early written witness; authenticity of individual songs varies; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Lalon's life (died 17 October 1890 at Cheuṛiyā); Scholarly account: 19th c.; (confidence moderate)
**Availability:** oral

Lalon's songs ask where the unknown bird comes and goes in the cage of the body, long for union with the Man of the Heart, mock the question of caste, place the original Mecca in the human body, call the neighbour in the mirror-city whom one never sees, and urge worship of the human through the guru. They are the classic corpus of Bāul teaching.

### song 'bāṛir kāche āraśinagar, sethā ek paṛśi basat kare …' <a id="tea-lalon-giti-barir-kache-arsinagar"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Near my house is the city of mirrors (āraśinagar) where a neighbour lives; I have not seen him even for a day. The village is surrounded by bottomless water with no shore and no boat. If that neighbour would touch me, all the torments of death would go far away. He and Lalon live in one place, yet between them lies a gap of a hundred thousand leagues.

> bāṛira kāche āraśinagara, sethā eka paṛaśī basata kare; āmi ekadina-o nā dekhilāma tāre

### song 'khāṃcār bhitar acin pākhi kemne āse yāy …' <a id="tea-lalon-giti-khancar-bhitar-acin-pakhi"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

How does the unknown bird come and go inside the cage? If I could catch it I would put the fetters of my mind on its feet. The cage has eight rooms and nine doors, with lattices here and there; above them the main hall, the mirror-palace. Lalon says: the cage is of green bamboo and will break one day.

> khāṃcāra bhitara acina pākhi kemane āse yāya; dharate pārale manobeṛi ditāma pākhira pāya

### song 'sab loke kay lālan ki jāt saṃsāre …' <a id="tea-lalon-giti-sab-loke-kay-lalon-ki-jat-sansare"></a>
