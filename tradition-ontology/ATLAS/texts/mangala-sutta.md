## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-12 <a id="tea-mangala-sutta-2-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The highest blessings: not associating with fools, associating with the wise, honouring those worthy of honour; a suitable place, past merit, right aspiration; learning, craft, discipline, good speech; supporting mother and father, wife and children, peaceful work; giving, righteous conduct, helping relatives, blameless deeds; abstaining from evil and intoxicants, heedfulness; reverence, humility, contentment, gratitude, timely hearing of Dhamma; patience, meekness, seeing ascetics, timely discussion; austerity, the holy life, seeing the noble truths, realising nibbāna; a mind unshaken by the worldly conditions, sorrowless, stainless, secure.
