### 1.1 <a id="tea-krsnakarnamrta-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Victory to Cintāmaṇi, my guru, and to Somagiri; victory to Kṛṣṇa of the peacock-feather crown, whose lotus feet are the wish-fulfilling tree on which the maiden Victory (jaya-śrī) enjoys the relish of choosing her husband.
