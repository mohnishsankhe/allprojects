Tulsīdās's 'letter of petition': some 279 devotional songs, opening with hymns to Gaṇeśa, Sūrya, Śiva, Devī, Gaṅgā, Hanumān and others before turning to Rāma, confessing the poet's faults and pleading for acceptance.

### pada 111 (Gita Press; verify) <a id="tea-vinaya-patrika-111"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

O Keśava, what can one say? Your creation is beyond telling — a picture drawn without colour on the wall of the void by a painter without a body; it cannot be washed away, and fearing it one suffers. Some call it true, some false, some both; Tulsī says whoever gives up all three errors knows himself.
