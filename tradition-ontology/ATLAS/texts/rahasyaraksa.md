## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### on the Catuḥślokī <a id="tea-rahasyaraksa-catuhsloki"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Commenting on Yāmuna's Catuḥślokī, Deśika argues that Śrī, like the Lord, is all-pervading and his equal in lordship, a co-refuge and co-goal, while remaining dependent on him as his consort.
