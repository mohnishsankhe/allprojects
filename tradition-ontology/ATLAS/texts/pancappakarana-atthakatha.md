Buddhaghosa's commentary on the last five Abhidhamma books (Dhātukathā, Puggalapaññatti, Kathāvatthu, Yamaka, Paṭṭhāna); its Kathāvatthu section names the schools holding each refuted view.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### kathavatthu-intro <a id="tea-pancappakarana-atthakatha-kathavatthu-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Kathāvatthu commentary names, for each refuted thesis, the schools that held it — among them the Puggalavādins (Vajjiputtakas and Sammitīyas), Sabbatthivādins, Mahāsaṅghikas, Andhakas (Pubbaseliyas, Aparaseliyas, Rājagirikas, Siddhatthikas), Mahiṃsāsakas, Uttarāpathakas and Vetulyakas.
