## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 15.2-15.4 <a id="tea-anapanasati-sutta-15-2-15-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Mindfulness of breathing developed and cultivated fulfils the four establishments of mindfulness; these fulfil the seven factors of awakening; these fulfil true knowledge and liberation.

> Ānāpānassati, bhikkhave, bhāvitā bahulīkatā cattāro satipaṭṭhāne paripūreti. Cattāro satipaṭṭhānā bhāvitā bahulīkatā satta bojjhaṅge paripūrenti. Satta bojjhaṅgā bhāvitā bahulīkatā vijjāvimuttiṁ paripūrenti.

### 17-22 <a id="tea-anapanasati-sutta-17-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The monk, gone to the forest, the root of a tree or an empty hut, sits cross-legged, body erect, mindfulness established, and ever mindful breathes in and out: knowing long and short breaths; training to breathe experiencing the whole body, calming the bodily formation; experiencing rapture, bliss, the mental formation, calming the mental formation; experiencing the mind, gladdening it, concentrating it, liberating it; contemplating impermanence, fading away, cessation and relinquishment — the sixteen steps.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, body-breath · stage: all · types: practice_

terms: [ānāpānasati](../terms/anapanasati.md), [kāyasaṅkhāra](../terms/kayasankhara.md), [cittasaṅkhāra](../terms/cittasankhara.md) · practices: [Mindfulness of breathing (ānāpānasati)](../practices/anapanasati.md)

### 23-28 <a id="tea-anapanasati-sutta-23-28"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

How each tetrad fulfils one establishment of mindfulness: the first contemplates the body ('in-and-out breathing is a certain body among bodies'), the second feelings ('close attention to breathing is a certain feeling'), the third mind ('there is no development of mindfulness of breathing for one who is muddled'), the fourth dhammas, looking on with equanimity after seeing with wisdom the abandoning of covetousness and grief.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: meditation, body-breath · stage: all · types: practice_

concepts: [The four establishments of mindfulness](../concepts/four-satipatthanas.md) · practices: [Mindfulness of breathing (ānāpānasati)](../practices/anapanasati.md), [The four establishments of mindfulness (satipaṭṭhāna)](../practices/satipatthana.md)

### 26.6 <a id="tea-anapanasati-sutta-26-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

I do not say that there is development of mindfulness of breathing for one who is muddled and not clearly comprehending.

> Nāhaṁ, bhikkhave, muṭṭhassatissa asampajānassa ānāpānassatiṁ vadāmi.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, body-breath · stage: all · types: practice_

terms: [sampajañña](../terms/sampajanna.md) · practices: [Mindfulness of breathing (ānāpānasati)](../practices/anapanasati.md)

### 29-43 <a id="tea-anapanasati-sutta-29-43"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When the four establishments of mindfulness are developed, the seven factors of awakening arise in sequence — mindfulness, investigation of dhammas, energy, rapture, tranquillity, concentration, equanimity — each supported by seclusion, dispassion and cessation, ripening in relinquishment; so developed they fulfil true knowledge and liberation.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice, karma-liberation_
