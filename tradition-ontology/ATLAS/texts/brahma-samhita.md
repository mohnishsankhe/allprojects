The fifth chapter of a Pāñcarātra-style saṃhitā, Brahmā's hymn to Govinda as the supreme Lord whose form is being, consciousness and bliss, residing in Goloka among his own potencies; tradition says Caitanya found it at the Ādikeśava temple in south India (CC 2.9); commented on by Jīva.

## Teachings (7: skeleton 7)

### 5.1 <a id="tea-brahma-samhita-5-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Kṛṣṇa, called Govinda, is the supreme Lord; his form is being, consciousness and bliss; he is without beginning, the beginning of all, the cause of all causes.

> īśvaraḥ paramaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ sac-cid-ānanda-vigrahaḥ | anādir ādir govindaḥ sarva-kāraṇa-kāraṇam ||

### 5.29 <a id="tea-brahma-samhita-5-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

I worship Govinda, the primeval person, tending the wish-fulfilling cows in abodes of wish-fulfilling gems surrounded by wish-fulfilling trees, served with reverence by hundreds of thousands of goddesses of fortune.

### 5.37 <a id="tea-brahma-samhita-5-37"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Govinda, the soul of all, dwells in Goloka itself with those (gopīs) who are his own forms and potencies, imbued with rasa of bliss and consciousness.

### 5.38 <a id="tea-brahma-samhita-5-38"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The saints, their eyes of devotion anointed with the salve of love, always see him within their hearts: Śyāmasundara, of inconceivable qualities.

### 5.40 <a id="tea-brahma-samhita-5-40"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The undifferentiated, infinite, complete Brahman, differentiated into the glories of countless universes, is the effulgence of his body — I worship Govinda.

### 5.44 <a id="tea-brahma-samhita-5-44"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Durgā — the one power that accomplishes creation, maintenance and dissolution — sustains the worlds like his shadow and acts according to his will: I worship Govinda, the primal Person.

> सृष्टिस्थितिप्रलयसाधनशक्तिरेका छायेव यस्य भुवनानि विभर्त्ति दुर्गा। इच्छानुरूपमपि यस्य च चेष्टते सा गोविन्दमादिपुरुषं तमहं भजामि॥

### 5.48 <a id="tea-brahma-samhita-5-48"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The lords of the universes, born from the pores of Mahāviṣṇu's body, live for the duration of one of his exhalations; that Mahāviṣṇu is a portion of a portion of Govinda.
