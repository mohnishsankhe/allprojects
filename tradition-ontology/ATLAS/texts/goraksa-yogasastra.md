# Gorakṣayogaśāstra (Mūlasāra)

**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)

**Authors:**

A short stotra-like work ascribed to Gorakṣa listing the contents of the subtle body — channels, sun, moon and fire, centres and Kaula seats, the fourteen worlds, oceans and rivers within the body, and the moon on the inner Meru raining nectar — to be recited daily.
