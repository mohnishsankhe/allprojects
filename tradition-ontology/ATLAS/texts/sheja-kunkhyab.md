## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### cosmology <a id="tea-sheja-kunkhyab-cosmology"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The universe is described three times over — according to the Abhidharma (Mount Meru and the four continents), according to the Kālacakra, and according to the Great Perfection — each presentation valid for its own vehicle and purpose rather than one replacing the others.

### eight-lineages <a id="tea-sheja-kunkhyab-eight-lineages"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The esoteric instructions of Tibet are presented as eight great chariots of practice lineages — Nyingma, Kadam, the Path and Result, Marpa Kagyu, Shangpa Kagyu, Pacification and Chöd, the six-branch yoga of Kālacakra, and the approach-and-accomplishment of the three vajras — each a complete path with its own instructions.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, practice_

### tenets <a id="tea-sheja-kunkhyab-tenets"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Among the tenet systems the Madhyamaka is presented in two forms: the self-emptiness Madhyamaka of Nāgārjuna's reasonings (Svātantrika and Prāsaṅgika), and the other-emptiness Great Madhyamaka of the Maitreya treatises and the definitive third turning, set out as the summit of the sūtra tenets. Each is valid in its own sphere: the self-empty approach cuts through conceptual elaboration in study and reflection, the other-empty view is suited to meditative experience.
