**Availability:** digitized-original

The Goddess declares herself the one consciousness, supreme Brahman, whose inseparable power is māyā; she shows her cosmic form, teaches that ignorance is the root of rebirth and knowledge its end, expounds eight-limbed and kuṇḍalinī yoga, meditation on herself as Brahman (with Muṇḍaka verses), three kinds of devotion by the guṇas and supreme devotion, her sacred seats and vows, and her external (Vedic and tantric) and internal worship.
