## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-attakari-sutta-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A brahmin holds that one acts neither of one's own doing nor another's. The Buddha: how can one who comes and goes of himself say so? There is an element of initiative, of persistence, of exertion, of endurance, of steadiness, of undertaking; beings are seen to initiate; so there is doing by oneself and by others.
