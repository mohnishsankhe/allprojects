Vacanas addressed to Cennamallikārjuna as her only husband: bridal devotion, longing and union, the renunciation of worldly marriage, clothing and social shame, and equanimity under praise and blame.

## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### vacana 'Bettada mēle maneya māḍi…' <a id="tea-akka-mahadevi-vacanas-bettada-mele-maneya-madi"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Having built a house on the hill, why fear the beasts? Having built a house on the seashore, why fear the waves? Having built a house in the market, why be ashamed of noise? Born into the world, when praise and blame come one must not get angry but stay calm, Cennamallikārjuna.

### vacana 'Hasivādaḍe ūroḷage bhikṣānnagaḷuṇṭu…' <a id="tea-akka-mahadevi-vacanas-hasivadade-urolage"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

If hungry, there is alms-food in the villages; if thirsty, tanks, streams and wells; for sleep, the ruined temples; and for the soul's company I have you, Cennamallikārjuna.

### vacana 'Sāvilladā kēḍilladā…' <a id="tea-akka-mahadevi-vacanas-savillada-kedillada"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

I love the Beautiful One who has no death, no decay and no form, no place, no end, no birth; I love him, mother; Cennamallikārjuna is my husband — take these husbands who die and decay and feed them to your kitchen fires.

### vacana 'Teraṇiyahuḷu…' <a id="tea-akka-mahadevi-vacanas-teraniyahulu"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Like the silkworm that builds its house out of its own love and dies wound round and round in its own threads, I burn with the desires of my heart; Lord, cut through my heart's greed and show me the way out, Cennamallikārjuna.

### vacana on clothing and nakedness (incipit not recalled) <a id="tea-akka-mahadevi-vacanas-v-clothes-and-nakedness"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

You can take away the money in the hand, but can you take away the body's glory? You can strip off every cloth one wears, but can you strip the Nothing that covers and veils? For one who wears the light of Cennamallikārjuna, what need is there of cover and jewel?
