Nāgeśa's middle-length summary of the Mañjūṣā, the classic source for the grammarians' mapping of the four levels of speech (parā, paśyantī, madhyamā, vaikharī) onto places in the body and for the sphoṭa as identical with madhyamā-level word.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### sphoṭa-nirūpaṇa <a id="tea-laghumanjusa-sphota"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Nāgeśa: speech at rest in the root centre (mūlādhāra), unmanifest, is parā; moved by breath and manifest at the navel it is paśyantī; in the heart, joined to the intellect, it is madhyamā — the sphoṭa that conveys meaning; issuing through the throat and mouth it is vaikharī, heard by others.
