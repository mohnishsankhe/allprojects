What should be done by one skilled in good who wishes to attain the peaceful state: the qualities of such a person and the wish 'may all beings be happy', cultivating a boundless mind towards all beings as a mother protects her only child; not holding to views, he does not come again to a womb.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1-10 <a id="tea-metta-sutta-1-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

One skilled in good who wishes to attain the peaceful state should be able, upright, gentle, content, of few duties and light living, not doing even a slight thing the wise would censure; may all beings be happy and secure; let none deceive or despise another or wish harm in anger; as a mother would protect her only child with her life, so let one cultivate a boundless heart towards all beings, above, below and across; standing, walking, sitting or lying down, let one keep this mindfulness — this is called a divine abiding here; not falling into views, virtuous and endowed with vision, one does not come again to a womb.

### 7 <a id="tea-metta-sutta-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As a mother would protect her only child with her life, so let one cultivate a boundless heart towards all beings, and love for the whole world.

> Mātā yathā niyaṁ puttam Āyusā ekaputtamanurakkhe; Evampi sabbabhūtesu, Mānasaṁ bhāvaye aparimāṇaṁ. Mettañca sabbalokasmi, Mānasaṁ bhāvaye aparimāṇaṁ; Uddhaṁ adho ca tiriyañca, Asambādhaṁ averamasapattaṁ.
