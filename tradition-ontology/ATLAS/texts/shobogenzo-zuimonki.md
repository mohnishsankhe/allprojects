## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### just-sitting <a id="tea-shobogenzo-zuimonki-just-sitting"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Students should just sit (shikan taza) and not spend time on kōan stories or scriptures alone; the ancients awakened through sitting; give up body and life for the Dharma and abandon fame and profit.
