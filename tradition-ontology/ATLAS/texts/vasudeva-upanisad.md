## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1-3 <a id="tea-vasudeva-upanisad-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Nārada asks Vāsudeva about the ūrdhvapuṇḍra; gopīcandana clay from Dvārakā is applied with mantras; householders mark twelve places, students and forest-dwellers fewer; the ascetic marks head, forehead and heart; the paramahaṃsa one mark on the forehead.

### 4-5 <a id="tea-vasudeva-upanisad-4-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low
