## Teachings (2: sourced 2)

### 1-3 <a id="tea-vasudeva-upanisad-1-3"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Nārada asks Vāsudeva about the ūrdhvapuṇḍra; gopīcandana clay from Dvārakā is applied with mantras; householders mark twelve places, students and forest-dwellers fewer; the ascetic marks head, forehead and heart; the paramahaṃsa one mark on the forehead.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ritual · path: ritual · stage: all · types: practice_

practices: [Ūrdhvapuṇḍra (vertical Vaiṣṇava mark)](../practices/urdhvapundra.md) · teachers: [Nārada](../teachers/narada.md)

### 4-5 <a id="tea-vasudeva-upanisad-4-5"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

One should meditate on the flame in the heart as Vāsudeva, the self of all.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice, ultimate_
