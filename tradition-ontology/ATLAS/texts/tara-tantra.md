## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.2-4 <a id="tea-tara-tantra-1-2-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Goddess asks how Buddha and Vasiṣṭha, the two Kula-Bhairavas, were perfected by the mantra; Bhairava: the supreme God Janārdana in the form of Buddha, by repeating the five-syllable great mantra of Ugratārā, became the doer of creation and the rest and attained freedom from age and death; Vasiṣṭha too, worshipping her, reached the world of the stars.
