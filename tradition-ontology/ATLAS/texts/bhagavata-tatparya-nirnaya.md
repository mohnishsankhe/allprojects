### 10.87.14 <a id="tea-bhagavata-tatparya-nirnaya-10-87-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

(Quoting a scripture on Bhāgavata 10.87.14:) Two defective prakṛtis abide in each person — one covering the soul's own qualities, the other covering the Supreme; the Supreme Person destroys the first and removes the second, and grants the state of liberation. Both are unborn; the one covering Hari does not cover Viṣṇu but covers the knowledge of him. Śrī, Bhū and Durgā are the (higher) prakṛti called śakti.
