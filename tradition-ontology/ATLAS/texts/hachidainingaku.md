### eight <a id="tea-hachidainingaku-eight"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The eight awarenesses of great beings, from the Buddha's last teaching: having few desires, knowing contentment, enjoying tranquillity, diligent effort, not forgetting mindfulness, practising samādhi, cultivating wisdom, and not engaging in idle talk.
