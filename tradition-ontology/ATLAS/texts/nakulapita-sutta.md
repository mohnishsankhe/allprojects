## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-18 <a id="tea-nakulapita-sutta-2-18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The aged Nakulapitā is told to train: 'though my body is afflicted, my mind shall not be afflicted'. Sāriputta explains: the uninstructed regards form, feeling, perception, formations and consciousness as self, self as possessing them, them in self or self in them, and when they change sorrow arises; the noble disciple does not, and though the body is afflicted the mind is not.
