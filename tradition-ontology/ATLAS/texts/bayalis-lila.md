### passim <a id="tea-bayalis-lila-prema"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Dhruvadās: the pair's love is eternal, without beginning or separation; the sakhīs find their joy only in the pair's joy; beside this rasa, Vaikuṇṭha and liberation are nothing.
