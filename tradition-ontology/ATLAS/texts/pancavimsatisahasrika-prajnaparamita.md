The Perfection of Wisdom in 25,000 lines: the Large Sūtra, expanding the 8,000 with systematic lists — the twenty (or sixteen/eighteen) emptinesses, the 108 samādhis, the dhāraṇī-doors, the stages — and a revised version rearranged to follow the eight topics of the Abhisamayālaṃkāra. It is the sūtra commented on in the Chinese Dazhidu lun and the Abhisamayālaṃkāra tradition.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-pancavimsatisahasrika-prajnaparamita-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A bodhisattva who wishes to train in the emptinesses should train in the perfection of wisdom: the emptiness of the internal, of the external, of both, of emptiness, the great emptiness, of the ultimate, of the conditioned, of the unconditioned, the absolute emptiness, of the beginningless, of non-rejection, of essential nature, of all dharmas, of own-marks, of the non-apprehended, of the own-being of non-being, of being, of non-being, of own-being and of other-being (twenty).

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, practice_

terms: [śūnyatā](../terms/sunyata.md) · concepts: [The twenty (sixteen, eighteen) emptinesses](../concepts/twenty-emptinesses.md), [Emptiness in the Perfection of Wisdom sūtras](../concepts/emptiness-prajnaparamita.md)

### 5 <a id="tea-pancavimsatisahasrika-prajnaparamita-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The sameness of bodhisattvas is the emptinesses: form is empty of form, feeling of feeling, and so for the aggregates, elements, sense-fields, links of dependent origination, the perfections, the wings of awakening, the truths, the powers and the buddha-qualities — each empty of itself.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_

terms: [śūnyatā](../terms/sunyata.md), [svabhāva](../terms/svabhava.md) · concepts: [Emptiness in the Perfection of Wisdom sūtras](../concepts/emptiness-prajnaparamita.md)
