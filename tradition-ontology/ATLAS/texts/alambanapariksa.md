# Ālambanaparīkṣā

**Alternate titles:** Examination of the Object, dmigs pa brtag pa, Guan suoyuan yuan lun 觀所緣緣論 (T1624, Xuanzang); Wuxiang si chen lun (T1619, Paramārtha)
**Original title:** आलम्बनपरीक्षा
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md), [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)

**Authors:** 
  - [Dignāga](../teachers/dignaga.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
**Structure:** 8 verses with vṛtti
**Availability:** digitized-translation

Dignāga's eight verses showing that the object-condition of cognition cannot be external atoms, singly or aggregated, since what causes a cognition does not appear in it and what appears in it is not causally real; the object is the knowable form inside cognition appearing as if outside, and the sense faculty is a potency within consciousness.
