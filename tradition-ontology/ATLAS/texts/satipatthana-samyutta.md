### 47.6 <a id="tea-satipatthana-samyutta-47-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A quail seized by a hawk while roaming outside its own range escapes by luring the hawk to its own domain among the clods of a ploughed field. So roam in your own domain, the four establishments of mindfulness; outside it, the five cords of sensual pleasure, Māra gets his chance.

### 47.13 <a id="tea-satipatthana-samyutta-47-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When Sāriputta attains final nibbāna, Ānanda is shaken; the Buddha: have I not taught that we are parted from all that is dear? Sāriputta did not take away virtue, concentration, wisdom or liberation. Therefore dwell as islands to yourselves with the Dhamma as island — by the four establishments of mindfulness.
