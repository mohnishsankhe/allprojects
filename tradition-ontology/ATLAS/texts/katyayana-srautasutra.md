### 1.1.1-8 <a id="tea-katyayana-srautasutra-1-1-1-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Now the qualification (for Śrauta rites): rites bearing fruit are for all without distinction — or rather for humans, since they can undertake them — excepting those lacking a limb, those not learned in the Veda, eunuchs and śūdras; for brāhmaṇas, rājanyas and vaiśyas, because the Veda so states; and for women too, since no distinction is made, and because it is seen (in scripture).
