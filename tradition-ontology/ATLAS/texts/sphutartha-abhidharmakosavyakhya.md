## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1.3 <a id="tea-sphutartha-abhidharmakosavyakhya-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The authors of the Abhidharma treatises are known: Kātyāyanīputra of the Jñānaprasthāna, Vasumitra of the Prakaraṇapāda, Devaśarman of the Vijñānakāya, Śāriputra of the Dharmaskandha, Maudgalyāyana of the Prajñaptiśāstra, Pūrṇa of the Dhātukāya, Mahākauṣṭhila of the Saṅgītiparyāya. Sautrāntikas are those who take the sūtras, not the treatises, as authority; for them the Abhidharma basket consists of particular sūtras such as the Arthaviniścaya in which the characteristics of dharmas are taught.

> ye sūtraprāmāṇikā na śāstraprāmāṇikāḥ| te sautrāntikāḥ|

### 1.3/2 <a id="tea-sphutartha-abhidharmakosavyakhya-1-3-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Vaibhāṣikas say: as the elder Dharmatrāta arranged in chapters the verses (udāna) spoken by the Buddha here and there, so the Abhidharma, the teaching of the characteristics of dharmas spoken by the Blessed One here and there according to his disciples, was gathered and set out by the elder Kātyāyanīputra and others in the Jñānaprasthāna and the rest. They are called Vaibhāṣikas because they know or live by the Vibhāṣā.

> vibhāṣayā dīvyanti caranti vā vaibhāṣikāḥ|
