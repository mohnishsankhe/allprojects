## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.five-abhisambodhis <a id="tea-sarvatathagatatattvasamgraha-1-five-abhisambodhis"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The tathāgatas come to the bodhisattva Sarvārthasiddhi, seated in the 'unmoving' samādhi of austerities, and ask how he will awaken while ignorant of the reality of all tathāgatas; they teach him five manifest awakenings with natural mantras: penetrating one's own mind ('oṃ I penetrate the mind'), raising bodhicitta, seeing a moon disc in the heart and a vajra on it ('stand, vajra!'), making it firm ('I am of vajra nature') and seeing himself as the Buddha body ('as all tathāgatas are, so am I'); consecrated as Vajradhātu, he becomes the perfect Buddha.

> oṃ cittaprativedhaṃ karomi … oṃ bodhicittam utpādayāmi … oṃ tiṣṭha vajra … oṃ vajrātmako 'ham … oṃ yathā sarvatathāgatās tathāham
