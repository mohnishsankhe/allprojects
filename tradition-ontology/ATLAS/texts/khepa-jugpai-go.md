### three-activities <a id="tea-khepa-jugpai-go-three-activities"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The learned are known through three activities: composition, exposition and debate; each has its rules — for composition, grammar and poetics; for exposition, the proper explanation of a text; for debate, the correct method of proof and refutation.
