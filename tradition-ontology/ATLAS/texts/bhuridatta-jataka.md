## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 158-160 <a id="tea-bhuridatta-jataka-158-160"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If Brahmā is lord of the whole world, master of beings, why did he order the world to misfortune and not make it happy? Why did he make it with deceit, lies and pride, and without justice? The lord of beings is unjust who, where there is dharma, ordained adharma.

### 161-173 <a id="tea-bhuridatta-jataka-161-173"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Bhūridatta rejects the brāhmaṇas' claims: if the one who kills in sacrifice is purified and the victim goes to heaven, let brāhmaṇas kill brāhmaṇas; animals do not ask to be killed; the sacrificial post does not yield wishes; the priests are like crows mobbing an owl, cheats who take the seen for the unseen.
