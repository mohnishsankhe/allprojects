## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 12-31 <a id="tea-dantabhumi-sutta-12-31"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

As an elephant tamer ties a wild elephant to a post to subdue its forest habits, the Tathāgata trains the disciple through the gradual training and the four establishments of mindfulness — tethers of the mind — until he is patient under cold, heat, hunger and abuse, a field of merit for the world.
