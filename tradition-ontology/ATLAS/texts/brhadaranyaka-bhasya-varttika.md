### 1.4.402 <a id="tea-brhadaranyaka-bhasya-varttika-1-4-402"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Whatever method brings people to understand the inner self is the right method here; and it is not fixed.

> yayā yayā bhavet puṃsāṃ vyutpattiḥ pratyagātmani / sā saiva prakriyeha syāt sādhvī sā cānavasthitā

### abhasa <a id="tea-brhadaranyaka-bhasya-varttika-abhasa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The jīva is a semblance (ābhāsa) of consciousness in the intellect, neither simply real nor simply identical with the original; when the semblance is known as such, only consciousness remains.

_level: bridging · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind_

### bhartrprapanca <a id="tea-brhadaranyaka-bhasya-varttika-bhartrprapanca"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Sureśvara reports and refutes an earlier commentator's (Bhartṛprapañca's) teaching that Brahman and the world are both different and non-different and that knowledge must be combined with works throughout life.
