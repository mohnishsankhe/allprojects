The 'opening sūtra' of the Threefold Lotus: innumerable meanings arise from one dharma, which is without marks; it contains the statement that in the more than forty years of teaching the truth had not yet been revealed.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2 <a id="tea-wuliangyi-jing-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Innumerable meanings arise from one dharma, which is without marks; the Buddha says that in more than forty years of teaching he had not yet revealed the truth, because beings' natures and desires differ.
