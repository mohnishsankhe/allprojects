**Availability:** digitized-original

Hemacandra's treatise in twelve chapters (prakāśa) written for king Kumārapāla: yoga as the three jewels; the conduct and vows of the householder; conquest of the passions and senses, the twelve reflections, equanimity and the four friendly contemplations; postures; breath, the winds and signs of death (with the warning that breath-control does not serve liberation); the four objects of meditation (piṇḍastha, padastha, rūpastha, rūpātīta) with the five concentrations; virtuous and pure meditation; and a closing chapter on his own experience of the no-mind state.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Yogaśāstra-svopajñavṛtti](yogasastra-svopajna-vrtti.md)
