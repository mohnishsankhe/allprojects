## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 15-31 <a id="tea-upakkilesa-sutta-15-31"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Before his awakening the bodhisatta perceived light and a vision of forms that then vanished; he found the imperfections of concentration that caused it — doubt, inattention, sloth and torpor, fear, elation, inertia, excess energy, slack energy, longing, perception of diversity, excessive meditation on forms — abandoned each, and developed concentration in three ways (with applied and sustained thought, with sustained thought only, with neither), with rapture, without, with pleasure and with equanimity.
