### bare-attention <a id="tea-heart-of-buddhist-meditation-bare-attention"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

Bare attention is the clear and single-minded awareness of what actually happens to and in us at the successive moments of perception, registering facts without reacting to them by deed, speech or mental comment.
