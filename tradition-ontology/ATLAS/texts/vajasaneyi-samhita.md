# Vājasaneyi Saṃhitā (Mādhyandina)

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Mādhyandina śākhā (White Yajurveda)](../lineages/sakha-madhyandina.md)

**Availability:** digitized-original

The White Yajurveda Saṃhitā: the adhvaryu's formulas and verses without explanatory prose (which is given separately in the Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa). Its forty adhyāyas run through the new- and full-moon rites, agnihotra and seasonal rites, the soma sacrifice, the royal rites, the fire altar (11–18, with the Śatarudrīya in 16), the sautrāmaṇī and the horse sacrifice (22–25), then supplementary books with the Puruṣa Sūkta (31), the hymn of the One (32), the Śivasaṅkalpa verses on mind (34.1–6), the funeral (35), peace chants (36) and the pravargya, and closes with the Īśa Upaniṣad (40).

### 1.1 <a id="tea-vajasaneyi-samhita-1-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

The first formula of the White Yajurveda: (you twigs) for food, for strength; you are winds; may god Savitṛ impel you to the best of works; swell, inviolable cows, with Indra's share, rich in offspring, free from sickness and consumption; let no thief rule over you.
