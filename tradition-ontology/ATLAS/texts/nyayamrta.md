**Structure:** 4 paricchedas
**Availability:** digitized-original

Vyāsatīrtha's great polemic against Advaita, in four chapters that follow the four chapters of the Brahmasūtras: (1) the reality of the world — a refutation of the five Advaita definitions of falsity (mithyātva) and of the inferences from perceivability, insentience and limitedness; (2) the Upaniṣads do not teach an impartite, attributeless meaning, and the flawless Brahman is absolutely different from the flawed souls; (3) the means — against the Vivaraṇa view of hearing, reflection and meditation, the Lord is to be won by direct realization born of meditation; (4) liberation is not the mere cessation of ignorance, and the liberated, from Brahmā and Rudra down, are graded. Madhusūdana Sarasvatī's Advaitasiddhi answers it point by point.
