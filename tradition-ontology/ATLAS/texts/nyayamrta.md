# Nyāyāmṛta

**Lineages:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)

**Authors:**

**Structure:** 4 paricchedas
**Availability:** digitized-original

Vyāsatīrtha's great polemic against Advaita, in four chapters that follow the four chapters of the Brahmasūtras: (1) the reality of the world — a refutation of the five Advaita definitions of falsity (mithyātva) and of the inferences from perceivability, insentience and limitedness; (2) the Upaniṣads do not teach an impartite, attributeless meaning, and the flawless Brahman is absolutely different from the flawed souls; (3) the means — against the Vivaraṇa view of hearing, reflection and meditation, the Lord is to be won by direct realization born of meditation; (4) liberation is not the mere cessation of ignorance, and the liberated, from Brahmā and Rudra down, are graded. Madhusūdana Sarasvatī's Advaitasiddhi answers it point by point.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Nyāyāmṛta-āmoda (Laghvāmoda)](nyayamrta-amoda.md), [Nyāyāmṛta-taraṅgiṇī](nyayamrta-tarangini.md)

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1 <a id="tea-nyayamrta-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The world is real: the five Advaita definitions of falsity (mithyātva) are refuted one by one, and so are the probans 'perceivable', 'insentient' and 'limited'; by the principles of the first chapter of the Brahmasūtras, the Upaniṣads are authoritative about a Brahman who has infinite qualities such as being the maker of the real world.

### 2 <a id="tea-nyayamrta-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Upaniṣadic sentences ('satyaṃ jñānam anantam', 'tat tvam asi') do not have an impartite (akhaṇḍa) meaning; by the principles of the second chapter, the seeker is to pursue direct realization of the flawless Brahman, which is absolutely different from the flawed souls.

### 3 <a id="tea-nyayamrta-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Against the Vivaraṇa view that reflection and meditation are subsidiaries of hearing: by the principles of the third chapter, for liberation the Lord (is to be won) through direct realization produced by meditation, which is brought about by hearing and the rest.

### 4 <a id="tea-nyayamrta-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Liberation is not the mere cessation of ignorance; by the principles of the fourth chapter, the Lord, husband of Śrī, is the supreme controller of the liberated — Brahmā, Rudra and the rest — who stand in gradation even in liberation.
