Hemacandra's appendix to the Triṣaṣṭi: the lives of the elders from Jambū, the last omniscient, through Prabhava, Śayyambhava, Bhadrabāhu and Sthūlabhadra to Vajrasvāmin, including the twelve-year famine, the council at Pāṭaliputra and the story of Cāṇakya and Candragupta.
