## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 8 <a id="tea-parisistaparvan-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The story of Cāṇakya, a Jain layman's son, who raised Candragupta to the throne of Pāṭaliputra and overthrew the Nandas; Candragupta later became a Jain, and Cāṇakya ended his life in a fast.

### 9 <a id="tea-parisistaparvan-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

During a twelve-year famine the monks scattered and the scripture began to be forgotten; afterwards the saṅgha assembled at Pāṭaliputra and put together the eleven Aṅgas, but no one knew the Dṛṣṭivāda except Bhadrabāhu, then in Nepal practising the 'great breath' meditation. Sthūlabhadra went to learn the Pūrvas from him; after Sthūlabhadra displayed his power by taking the form of a lion before his sisters, Bhadrabāhu refused to teach him the meaning of the last four Pūrvas, giving only the text, so that their meaning was lost.
