## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-6 <a id="tea-unmai-neri-vilakkam-1-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The ten acts (tacakāriyam): seeing the form of the tattvas, seeing that they are insentient, purifying oneself of them; seeing the form of the soul, seeing the soul, purifying the soul; seeing the form of Śiva, seeing Śiva, union with Śiva (śiva-yoga) and enjoyment of Śiva (śiva-bhoga).
