The songs of Narsinh Mehta of Junāgaḍh: dawn songs (prabhātiyāṃ), songs of the rāsa and of Kṛṣṇa's love, songs of non-dual knowledge ('in the whole universe you alone, Śrī Hari'), the marks of the true Vaiṣṇava, and autobiographical poems (the Hāramālā, Māmeruṃ, Huṇḍī episodes).

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### pada 'akhila brahmāṇḍamāṃ' <a id="tea-narsinh-mehta-padas-akhila-brahmandamam"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In the whole universe you alone, Śrī Hari, appear in endless different forms — as the Self in the body, as light in the sky; as gold in many ornaments, one in the many.

> akhila brahmāṇḍamāṃ eka tuṃ śrīhari, jūjave rūpe ananta bhāse

### pada 'jyāṃ lagī ātmā tattva cīnyo nahīṃ' <a id="tea-narsinh-mehta-padas-jyam-lagi-atma"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As long as the principle of the Self is not known, all spiritual practice is false: bathing, pilgrimage, rites, rosaries and marks on the brow are in vain without it.

> jyāṃ lagī ātmā tattva cīnyo nahīṃ, tyāṃ lagī sādhanā sarva jūṭhī

### pada 'vaiṣṇava jana to' <a id="tea-narsinh-mehta-padas-vaisnava-jana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Call him a Vaiṣṇava who knows the pain of others, who helps those in sorrow and lets no pride enter his mind; who honours all and speaks ill of none, steady in word, deed and mind; who sees all alike, has given up craving, regards others' wives as mothers, speaks no untruth and touches no one's wealth; free of delusion, greed, deceit, lust and anger — seeing such a one, seventy-one generations are saved.

> vaiṣṇava jana to tene kahīe je pīḍa parāī jāṇe re, para duḥkhe upakāra kare toye mana abhimāna na āṇe re
