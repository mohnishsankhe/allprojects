# Tiruviḷaiyāṭal Purāṇam

**Language:** Tamil
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md), [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)

**Authors:** 
  - [Parañcōti Muṉivar (author of the Tiruviḷaiyāṭal Purāṇam)](../teachers/parancoti-tiruvilaiyatal.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 16th–17th c.; (confidence low)

Parañcōti Muṉivar's purāṇam of the sixty-four 'sacred sports' of Śiva at Madurai, including the stories of Māṇikkavācakar (the foxes turned into horses, the Lord carrying earth for the Vaikai embankment) and of Campantar's contest with the Jains.

_Notes: An earlier Tiruvālavāyuṭaiyār Tiruviḷaiyāṭal Purāṇam (Perumpaṟṟappuliyūr Nampi, c. 13th c.) is noted at low confidence._
