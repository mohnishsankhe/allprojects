Rūpa's theology of the Lord's forms: Kṛṣṇa as the original form (svayaṃ-rūpa), his identical expansions (tad-ekātma-rūpa: vilāsa and svāṃśa) and empowered descents (āveśa); the classes of avatāras; and (second part) the devotees in ascending order.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-laghubhagavatamrta-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Lord appears as his original form (svayaṃ-rūpa: Kṛṣṇa), as forms identical with him (tad-ekātma-rūpa: vilāsa and svāṃśa expansions) and as empowered souls (āveśa); the avatāras are classed as puruṣa, guṇa, līlā, manvantara, yuga and empowered avatāras.
