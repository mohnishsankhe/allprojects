Thirty-two couplets (kaṇṇi), each asking 'why … , Kuṭampai?' ('ētukkaṭi kutampāy'): for one who has seen the true thing, the open space (veṭṭa veḷi) and the root, what need of charters, kaṟpam (rejuvenation), mantra, seals, yoga, hymns, rituals, fame or a house? A catalogue of means made redundant by realization.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Cittar pāṭalkaḷ tokuppu I (Project Madurai 1999; GRETIL Devanāgarī transliteration)
