The 'four hymns' ascribed to Nāgārjuna — to the Buddha as beyond the world (Lokātītastava), as inconceivable (Acintyastava), as incomparable (Niraupamyastava) and to the ultimate (Paramārthastava) — praising the Buddha for teaching that all things are dependently arisen and empty.
