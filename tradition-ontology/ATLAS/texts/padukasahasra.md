## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### Prastāva-paddhati <a id="tea-padukasahasra-prastava-paddhati"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The sandals (pādukā) of Raṅganātha are praised as Śaṭhakopa (Nammāḻvār) and as the teacher who, like Bharata's sandals ruling Ayodhyā, brings the Lord to those who cannot reach him.
