An upāgama of the Pārameśvara (the Kāmika lists 'Mataṅga' first among the Pārameśvara's upabhedas), taught by Śiva to the sage Mataṅga. All four pādas survive; Bhaṭṭa Rāmakaṇṭha commented on it.
