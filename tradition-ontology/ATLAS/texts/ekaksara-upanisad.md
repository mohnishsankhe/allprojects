## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-13 <a id="tea-ekaksara-upanisad-1-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The one imperishable syllable is praised as the lord of beings, Agni, the Kumāra, the sun in the sky, Viṣṇu, time, the Vedas and the supreme imperishable.
