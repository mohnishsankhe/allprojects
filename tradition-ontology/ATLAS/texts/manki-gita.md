**Availability:** digitized-original

Maṅki, having lost the young bulls on which his hopes rested, renounces desire: 'Desire, I know your root — you arise from intention (saṃkalpa); I will not intend you, and you will be gone with your root' (12.171.25). The chapter also quotes Janaka's saying that nothing of his burns when Mithilā burns.
