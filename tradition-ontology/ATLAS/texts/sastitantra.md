# Ṣaṣṭitantra

**Language:** Sanskrit

**Genre:** tantra (systematic doctrine)
**Authors:**

**Attribution:** tradition: The extensive doctrine (tantra) of the supreme sage, of which the SK is a condensation (SK 72; Yuktidīpikā introductory verses; Jayamaṅgalā on SK 1).; scholarly: Lost. Authorship proposals include Pañcaśikha and Vārṣagaṇya; some have taken 'ṣaṣṭitantra' as a name of the system rather than of a single book.; confidence: disputed
**Structure:** sixty topics: ten fundamental topics (mūlikārtha) and the fifty categories of the creation of cognitions (5 errors, 28 incapacities, 9 contentments, 8 accomplishments)
**Availability:** lost

The lost 'doctrine of sixty topics' that the SK claims to summarize without its illustrative tales and polemics (SK 72). The later commentaries list its sixty topics; the Pāñcarātra Ahirbudhnya Saṃhitā (ch. 12) gives a different, theistic account of a Kāpila Ṣaṣṭitantra.
