Keizan's practical instructions on zazen: posture, eyes, diet, clothing; ordinarily rest the mind in the left palm — if the mind sinks, place it at the hairline or between the eyebrows, if it scatters, at the tip of the nose or below the navel; warns against visionary and other deviant states.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### faults <a id="tea-zazen-yojinki-faults"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Keizan warns that in zazen strange states may appear — light, visions of buddhas, sounds, sensations, extraordinary understanding — and that these are illnesses arising from imbalance of breath and mind; one should not cling to them but return to right sitting.
