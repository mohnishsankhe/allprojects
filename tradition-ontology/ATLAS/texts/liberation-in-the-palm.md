### four-point-analysis <a id="tea-liberation-in-the-palm-four-point-analysis"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Meditation on selflessness by four essential points: identifying the object of negation (the self as it appears to the innate self-grasping); ascertaining that if such a self existed it would have to be either one with the aggregates or different from them; ascertaining that it is not one; and ascertaining that it is not different.
