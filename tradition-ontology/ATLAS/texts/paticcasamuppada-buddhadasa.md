Buddhadāsa's presentation of dependent origination as arising in everyday experience from sense-contact, criticizing the exclusively three-lives reading.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### intro <a id="tea-paticcasamuppada-buddhadasa-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Dependent origination runs its full course in daily life whenever contact occurs without mindfulness — ignorance, feeling, craving, clinging, 'becoming' and the 'birth' of the ego; to read it only as three lives spread over past, present and future makes it useless for practice.
