## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### Die to Live, on dying daily <a id="tea-die-to-live-dying-while-living"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Meditation is the practice of dying while living: withdrawing the attention from the body to the eye centre as at death, the disciple learns daily to cross the threshold, and so no longer fears death.

### Die to Live, on the Master at the disciple's death <a id="tea-die-to-live-master-at-death"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _recent (post-1800)_

At the death of an initiate the Master comes to receive the soul himself; the angel of death has no power over him.
