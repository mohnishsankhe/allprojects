The hymn with the refrain 'to which god shall we offer with oblation?' (kasmai devāya haviṣā vidhema): the Golden Germ arose in the beginning, born the one lord of what exists; he holds earth and heaven; he gives self and strength; his shadow is immortality and death; the great waters bearing the germ brought forth Agni, and from them arose the one life-breath of the gods; the last verse names him Prajāpati.

_Notes: The tradition reads the interrogative 'ka' (who?) as a name of Prajāpati. Verse 10 is widely held to be a later addition and is omitted in the Padapāṭha (as usually reported). Also VS 23.1 and TS 4.1.8 (checked)._
