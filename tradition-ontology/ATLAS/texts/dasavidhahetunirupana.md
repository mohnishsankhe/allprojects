Śrīnivāsa Dīkṣita's exposition of ten reasons why the Vaikhānasa Sūtra is the best of all sūtras and its followers the highest: composed by Vikhanas, cause of the world; first of all sūtras; following the Vedic path in all rites; teaching all rites with mantras; having the rite of conception and the eighteen bodily saṃskāras; and more.
