## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### p2.1-12 <a id="tea-dasavidhahetunirupana-p2-1-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ten reasons are set out why the Vaikhānasa Sūtra — composed by the four-faced Brahmā called Vikhanas, born of Nārāyaṇa, the supreme Brahman possessing truth and all auspicious qualities — is the best of all sūtras, and those who follow its dharma the most excellent of all: because it was composed by Vikhanas, the cause of the whole world; because it is the first of all sūtras; because in all rites it follows the path of the śruti; because it teaches all rites with mantras; because it has the rite of impregnation and the other saṃskāras; because it teaches the eighteen bodily saṃskāras; because it has the full set of rites with their subsidiaries; and further reasons — each established from śruti, smṛti and purāṇa.

### p2.14-23 <a id="tea-dasavidhahetunirupana-p2-14-23"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Is Vikhanas some sage, or Nārāyaṇa? Nārāyaṇa alone: no mere sage is taught to be denoted by 'Vaikhānasa', whereas śruti, smṛti and purāṇa show that the supreme Self Nārāyaṇa is denoted by 'Vikhanas', 'Vaikhānasa' and the like — as in the khila-śruti: 'the cow (is the source) of draught animals, Aditi of the gods, Brahmā of the Ṛbhus, Vikhanas of the sages'.
