# Saṃyuktāgama (Chinese)

`src:samyuktagama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Za ahan jing 雜阿含經 (T99)
**Language:** Chinese
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Mūlasarvāstivāda](../lineages/mulasarvastivada.md), [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
**Genre:** āgama
**Authors:** 
  - [Guṇabhadra](../teachers/gunabhadra.md) — role: translator; attribution: accepted
**Dates:** Scholarly account: translated 435–443 CE from a manuscript Faxian brought from Sri Lanka; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 50 fascicles, 1362 sūtras in the Taishō numbering (the order of the fascicles is disturbed; reconstructed by Lü Cheng and Yinshun)
**Availability:** digitized-translation

The Chinese 'connected discourses', affiliated with the (Mūla)sarvāstivāda, parallel to the Saṃyutta Nikāya (e.g. SĀ 379 ↔ SN 56.11; SĀ 34 ↔ SN 22.59; SĀ 301 ↔ SN 12.15).
