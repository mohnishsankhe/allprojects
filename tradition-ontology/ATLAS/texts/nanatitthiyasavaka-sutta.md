## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-14 <a id="tea-nanatitthiyasavaka-sutta-2-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Deities who were disciples of rival teachers speak before the Buddha: one praises Pūraṇa, who saw no evil in injuring and killing nor merit for oneself; one praises Makkhali, well restrained by austerity and scruple, truthful, shunning quarrels; one praises the Nigaṇṭha, scrupulous, restrained by the fourfold restraint; one praises Pakudha and the others as not far from the good. Another deity answers that the jackal never equals the lion; Māra, possessing him, praises those devoted to austerity and heaven, and the Buddha exposes Māra's bait.
