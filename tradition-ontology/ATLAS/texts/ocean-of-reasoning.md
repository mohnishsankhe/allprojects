## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### ch.24 <a id="tea-ocean-of-reasoning-ch-24"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Commenting on 'for whom emptiness is possible, everything is possible' (MMK 24.14), Tsongkhapa argues that the opponent who charges emptiness with destroying the four truths has it backwards: only because things are empty of inherent existence can arising, ceasing, the truths and the path be posited.
