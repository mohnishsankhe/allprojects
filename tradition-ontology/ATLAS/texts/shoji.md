## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### life-of-buddha <a id="tea-shoji-life-of-buddha"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Because there is buddha within birth and death there is no birth and death; this birth-and-death is the life of the buddha; if one hates and discards it one loses the buddha's life; simply release body and mind, throw them into the house of the buddha, and without effort one becomes buddha.
