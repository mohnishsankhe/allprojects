# Kitāb Pātanǧal (al-Bīrūnī's Arabic Patañjali)

`src:kitab-patanjal` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Kitāb Bātanǧal al-Hindī fī al-ḫalāṣ min al-irtibāk (The Book of Patañjali the Indian on Liberation from Bondage)
**Original title:** كتاب باتنجل
**Language:** Arabic
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Genre:** translation
**Authors:** 
  - [al-Bīrūnī](../teachers/al-biruni.md) — role: translator; attribution: accepted
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 1017–1030 CE (made in India; used in al-Bīrūnī's India, completed c. 1030); (confidence moderate)
**Availability:** unknown

An Arabic rendering, recast as questions and answers, of a Pātañjala yoga text together with a commentary that is not Vyāsa's; known from a single manuscript in Istanbul, edited by H. Ritter (1956). An early witness to the Yoga Sūtra's reception outside India.
