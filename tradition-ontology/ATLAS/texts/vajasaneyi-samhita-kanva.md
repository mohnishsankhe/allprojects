# Vājasaneyi Saṃhitā (Kāṇva)

`src:vajasaneyi-samhita-kanva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Original title:** काण्वसंहिता
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Kāṇva śākhā (White Yajurveda)](../lineages/sakha-kanva.md)
**Genre:** saṃhitā (formulas and verses)
**Attribution:** tradition: Yājñavalkya's Yajus as transmitted by Kaṇva's line; scholarly: White Yajurveda recension; confidence: high
**Structure:** 40 adhyāyas (c. 2,086 kaṇḍikās, as usually counted)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The Kāṇva recension of the White Yajurveda Saṃhitā, parallel to the Mādhyandina with differences of order and wording; its last adhyāya is the Kāṇva form of the Īśa Upaniṣad.
