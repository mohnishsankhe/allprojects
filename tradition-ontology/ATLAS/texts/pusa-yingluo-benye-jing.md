Sets out the forty-two stages of the worthies and sages (ten abodes, ten practices, ten dedications, ten grounds, equal and wondrous awakening), preceded by the ten faiths, with six 'natures' (gotra) of practitioners; also bodhisattva precepts.

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1012b25 <a id="tea-pusa-yingluo-benye-jing-1012b25"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The 'natures' (lineages of the path) are six: the nature of habituation, the nature of innate disposition, the nature of the path, the sagely nature, the nature of equal awakening and the nature of wondrous awakening — also called the six firmnesses (firm faith, dharma, practice, virtue, summit, awakening) and the six patiences.

> 佛子！性者，所謂習種性、性種性、道種性、聖種性、等覺性、妙覺性。

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: general · stage: all (liu zhongxing 六種性) · types: practice_

### 1014b29 <a id="tea-pusa-yingluo-benye-jing-1014b29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Practising the ten faiths for one, two, up to ten kalpas, one enters the ten abodes; from the first to the sixth abode, if the sixth perfection's right contemplation appears and one is protected by buddhas, bodhisattvas and good friends, one goes on (to the stage of non-retrogression).

> 若一劫二劫乃至十劫修行十信得入十住

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: beginner (shixin 十信 → shizhu 十住) · types: practice_

### 1015c09 <a id="tea-pusa-yingluo-benye-jing-1015c09"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The forty-second ground is called the ground of wondrous awakening, the mind of quiescence: always abiding in one mark, supreme and limitless, clear like space; all-knowing wisdom illumines the unborn.

> 第四十二地名寂滅心妙覺地

_level: ultimate · standpoint: experiential · path: general · stage: realized (miaojue 妙覺) · types: karma-liberation_
