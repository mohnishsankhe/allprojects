### sutra.2 <a id="tea-civanana-mapatiyam-sutra-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Civañāṉa Muṉivar explains 'advaita' in the Civañāṉa Pōtam as 'non-separateness' (ananya), not numerical identity, and argues against the Māyāvādin reading of the Upaniṣadic identity sayings.
