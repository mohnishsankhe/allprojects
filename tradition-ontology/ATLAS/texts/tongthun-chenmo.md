## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### other-emptiness-refuted <a id="tea-tongthun-chenmo-other-emptiness-refuted"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Khedrup Je refutes the view that the ultimate is truly established, permanent and empty only of other: whatever is truly established would be an object for grasping at true existence, and such a view falls to the extreme of permanence, indistinguishable from the self asserted by non-Buddhists.

_level: bridging · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: dispute, ultimate_

### view <a id="tea-tongthun-chenmo-view"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Khedrup Je argues that the object of negation must be precisely identified and that Madhyamaka reasoning negates inherent existence, not conventional existence; he criticises other Tibetan readings of the view.
