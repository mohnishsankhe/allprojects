## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### faith-vow-practice <a id="tea-amituo-jing-yaojie-faith-vow-practice"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Faith, vow and holding the name are the three provisions for birth, like the three legs of a tripod. Whether one is born depends entirely on having faith and vow; the height of one's grade depends entirely on the depth of holding the name. Faith is to believe in oneself, in others, in cause, in effect, in phenomena and in principle; vow is to loathe this Sahā world and delight in the Land of Bliss.
