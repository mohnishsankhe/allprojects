The first Mūlasūtra, thirty-six chapters of verse and some prose: discipline toward the teacher, the twenty-two hardships, the four rare things, the ignorant and the wise death, the renunciation of Nami, the leaf of the tree ('Gautama, be not careless for a moment'), the outcaste monk Harikeśa, Nemi and Rājīmatī, the dialogue of Keśin and Gautama, the eight 'mothers of the doctrine', the true brāhmaṇa, the path to liberation, austerities, the kinds of karma, the six leśyās and the classes of living and non-living things. Tradition holds that Mahāvīra recited it in his last hours.

### 15.7 <a id="tea-uttaradhyayana-sutra-15-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

He who does not live by the sciences of cuts (in cloth), sounds, earth-signs, sky-signs, dreams, bodily marks, staffs, sites, limbs and the cries (of animals) — he is a monk.

> chinnaṃ saraṃ bhomam antalikkhaṃ sumiṇaṃ lakkhaṇadaṇḍavatthuvijjaṃ | aṅgaviyāraṃ sarassa vijayaṃ je vijjāhiṃ na jīvai sa bhikkhū ||
