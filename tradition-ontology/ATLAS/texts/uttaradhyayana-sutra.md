**Availability:** digitized-original

The first Mūlasūtra, thirty-six chapters of verse and some prose: discipline toward the teacher, the twenty-two hardships, the four rare things, the ignorant and the wise death, the renunciation of Nami, the leaf of the tree ('Gautama, be not careless for a moment'), the outcaste monk Harikeśa, Nemi and Rājīmatī, the dialogue of Keśin and Gautama, the eight 'mothers of the doctrine', the true brāhmaṇa, the path to liberation, austerities, the kinds of karma, the six leśyās and the classes of living and non-living things. Tradition holds that Mahāvīra recited it in his last hours.

### 5 <a id="tea-uttaradhyayana-sutra-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There are two ways of dying: the death against one's will (akāma-maraṇa) of the ignorant, who cling to pleasures and die in fear, and the death with one's will (sakāma-maraṇa) of the wise and restrained, who die calm and are reborn among the gods or reach liberation. ### 23 <a id="tea-uttaradhyayana-sutra-23"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

At Śrāvastī, Keśin of Pārśva's line and Gautama of Mahāvīra's meet and ask why Pārśva taught a fourfold law and allowed an under- and upper garment while Vardhamāna taught a fivefold law and nakedness. Gautama answers that the first Jina's disciples were simple and slow, the last Jina's crooked and slow, and those between simple and wise; so the law was stated in the way each could keep it; outward marks exist for recognition in the world, but the true means to liberation are knowledge, faith and conduct. Keśin then questions him in figures — the enemy, the fetters, the creeper, the fire, the unruly horse, the boat, the light, the safe place — and, satisfied, accepts the fivefold law.

`skeleton` · confidence high

### 28 <a id="tea-uttaradhyayana-sutra-28"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The path to liberation (mokkhamaggagaī) consists of knowledge, faith, conduct and austerity. Knowledge is of five kinds; the substances are six (the media of motion and rest, space, time, matter and souls); the real categories are nine (soul, non-soul, bondage, merit, demerit, inflow, stopping, shedding, liberation); faith is belief in these as they truly are. By knowledge one knows things, by faith one believes, by conduct one restrains, by austerity one is purified.

### 30 <a id="tea-uttaradhyayana-sutra-30"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Austerity is of two kinds: external — fasting, eating less than one's fill, limiting the kinds of alms accepted, giving up tasty food, bodily endurance, and seclusion; internal — atonement, humility, service, study, meditation and abandonment of the body. As a lake whose inflow is stopped dries up, so the karma of the restrained is burned away by austerity.

### 36 <a id="tea-uttaradhyayana-sutra-36"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The analysis of the living and the non-living ends with the liberated: they stop where the medium of motion ends, at the summit of the world; they are liberated from the female, male and neuter sex, from the Jain monk's garb, from other garb and from the householder's state.
