## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 110 padas (summary) <a id="tea-kelimal-passim"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Kuñjabihārī and Śyāmā play in the grove — singing, dancing, quarrelling in love and making up — served by the sakhīs; the singer is a sakhī who witnesses and serves, never a partner.

### pada 1 (incipit 'māī rī sahaja jorī pragaṭa bhaī') <a id="tea-kelimal-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

O friend, the natural pair (sahaja jorī) has appeared — fair and dark, like lightning with a cloud; they were never joined and never apart, but are together eternally.
