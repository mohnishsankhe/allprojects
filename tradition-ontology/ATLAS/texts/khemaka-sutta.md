## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 9-12 <a id="tea-khemaka-sutta-9-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Though the sick Khemaka does not regard any aggregate as self, the conceit 'I am', the desire and tendency 'I am', remain unuprooted in the aggregates, like the smell of cleaning agents in a washed cloth that the cloth still carries until it is put away in a scented box; attending to the rise and fall of the aggregates it is uprooted. While he spoke, sixty elders and Khemaka were liberated.
