Three kinds of wonder — of psychic power, of mind-reading and of instruction — the first two disparaged; the story of the monk who asked the gods up to Great Brahmā where the four elements cease, answered by the Buddha: in consciousness that does not manifest.

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 4-7 <a id="tea-kevaddha-sutta-4-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Of the three wonders the Buddha sees danger in the wonder of psychic power (and of revealing others' minds) and is horrified, repelled and disgusted by them, since an unbeliever may ascribe them to a charm; the wonder of instruction — teaching the gradual training to its end — is the one he praises.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: powers-experiences_

terms: [iddhi](../terms/iddhi.md), [pāṭihāriya](../terms/patihariya.md) · concepts: [The three wonders](../concepts/three-patihariya.md)

### 67.3-85.9 <a id="tea-kevaddha-sutta-67-3-85-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A monk asked the gods in turn, up to Great Brahmā, where earth, water, fire and air cease without remainder; Brahmā, unable to answer, sent him to the Buddha, who said the question should be where the elements, long and short, fine and coarse, name and form find no footing — answered: in consciousness that does not manifest, infinite, radiant all round; there, with the cessation of consciousness, all this ceases.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, consciousness-mind_

terms: [viññāṇaṃ anidassanaṃ](../terms/vinnana-anidassana.md), [nāmarūpa](../terms/namarupa.md) · concepts: [Nibbāna](../concepts/nibbana.md)

### 85.10-85.27 <a id="tea-kevaddha-sutta-85-10-85-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The verse question and answer: where do the four elements, long and short, fine and coarse, fair and foul, and name-and-form cease without remainder? In consciousness non-manifesting, infinite, radiant all round; with the cessation of consciousness they cease there.

> Evañca kho eso, bhikkhu, pañho pucchitabbo: ‘Kattha āpo ca pathavī, tejo vāyo na gādhati; Kattha dīghañca rassañca, aṇuṁ thūlaṁ subhāsubhaṁ; Kattha nāmañca rūpañca, asesaṁ uparujjhatī’ti. Tatra veyyākaraṇaṁ bhavati: ‘Viññāṇaṁ anidassanaṁ, anantaṁ sabbatopabhaṁ; Ettha āpo ca pathavī, tejo vāyo na gādhati. Ettha dīghañca rassañca, aṇuṁ thūlaṁ subhāsubhaṁ; Ettha nāmañca rūpañca, asesaṁ uparujjhati; Viññāṇassa nirodhena, etthetaṁ uparujjhatī’”ti.
