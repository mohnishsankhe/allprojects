## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1 <a id="tea-mumuksuppadi-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The tirumantra: 'oṃ' shows the soul to be the Lord's alone (ananyārha-śeṣatva); 'namaḥ' removes the sense of belonging to oneself and of being one's own means; 'nārāyaṇāya' is the prayer for service to Nārāyaṇa in all states.

### 2 <a id="tea-mumuksuppadi-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The dvaya: the first half shows Śrī as mediator (puruṣakāra) and the Lord's feet, with his qualities of motherly love (vātsalya), lordship (svāmitva), gracious condescension (sauśīlya) and accessibility (saulabhya), as the means; the second half is the prayer for service to the divine couple, removing any sense that the service is for oneself.

### 3 <a id="tea-mumuksuppadi-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The carama-śloka: 'abandoning all dharmas' means giving up every other means, including the idea that one's own surrender is a means; 'me alone' means the Lord himself, already accomplished, is the only means; 'do not grieve' — all burden is his.
