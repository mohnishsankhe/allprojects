A tract framed as a Śiva–Gaurī dialogue said to belong to the Kalpadrumavivekinī: a first chapter on the birth and deeds of Gorakṣa (with its own account of his origin and ear-splitting) followed by liturgical hymns to Gorakṣanātha — twelve names, armour (kavaca), heart, five-jewel praise, 108 and 1000 names.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.4-24 <a id="tea-goraksa-gutika-1-4-24"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

In this telling Gorakṣa is born in the Kṛta age of Brahmā's seed and named by the sage Viśravas; as a student his ear is cut in punishment, and he declares: 'I will not follow the brāhmaṇa-dharma; I take up the Gorakṣa-dharma' — taking the guru's garb, the split ear and the cut top-knot; with Jālandhara, Matsyendra and Dattātreya he makes known the supreme Gorakṣa-dharma.
