# Gorakṣaguṭikā

`src:goraksa-gutika` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Genre:** stotra / māhātmya
**Availability:** digitized-original

A tract framed as a Śiva–Gaurī dialogue said to belong to the Kalpadrumavivekinī: a first chapter on the birth and deeds of Gorakṣa (with its own account of his origin and ear-splitting) followed by liturgical hymns to Gorakṣanātha — twelve names, armour (kavaca), heart, five-jewel praise, 108 and 1000 names.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Muktabodha e-text M00291

_Notes: Shows the Nāth liturgy of guru-worship; its origin story differs from the better-known vernacular accounts._
