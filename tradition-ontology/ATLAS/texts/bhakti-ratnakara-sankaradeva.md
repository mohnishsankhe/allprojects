### whole (summary) <a id="tea-bhakti-ratnakara-sankaradeva-exclusive-devotion"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Verses from the Bhāgavata arranged to show that exclusive devotion to Kṛṣṇa, above all hearing and singing his name, is the highest dharma.
