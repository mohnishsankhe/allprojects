## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-tripuratapani-upanisad-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Gāyatrī is read as the Śrīvidyā of Tripurā; its words disclose the syllables of her mantra.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language_

concepts: [The Goddess as Brahman](../concepts/devi-as-brahman.md)

### 2-5 (summary) <a id="tea-tripuratapani-upanisad-2-5-summary"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Her mantras, cakra, worship and meditation are taught, culminating in knowledge of Tripurā as the supreme Brahman.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion · stage: all · types: practice, ultimate_
