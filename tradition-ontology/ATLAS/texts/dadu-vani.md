# Dādū Vāṇī (Dādū Dayāl kī Bāṇī)

`src:dadu-vani` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Śrī Dādū Vāṇī, Aṅgabandhu (Rajjab's arrangement)
**Language:** Hindi (Rajasthani/Dhūṇḍhāṛī Sant Bhāṣā)
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Dādū Panth](../lineages/dadu-panth.md), [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md)
**Genre:** Sant verse (sākhī, pad)
**Authors:** 
  - [Dādū Dayāl](../teachers/dadu.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
  - [Rajjab](../teachers/rajjab.md) — role: compiler; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Dādū's utterances, arranged by Rajjab in aṅgas; scholarly: compiled shortly after 1603; broadly authentic; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Dādū's lifetime, 1544–1603; Scholarly account: late 16th–early 17th c.; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** Some 2,600 sākhīs in 37 aṅgas (from Gurudev kau aṅg, Sumiran kau aṅg, Birah kau aṅg, Paracā kau aṅg … to Abihaṛ kau aṅg) and some 440 padas in 27 rāgas (approximate counts)
**Availability:** digitized-original

Dādū's sākhīs and songs on the guru met in the unseen, remembrance of Rām, separation and encounter, the body as the true mosque and temple, impartiality between Hindu and Turk, the middle way, and dying to self while alive. It opens with an invocation to Nirañjan, the gurudev and all sādhus.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Dādū Dayāl kī Bāṇī (Belvedere Press, Allahabad; Santbānī series); licence: public domain in some jurisdictions
  - kind: original; name: Śrī Dādū Vāṇī, ed. Svāmī Nārāyaṇdās (Jaipur); licence: copyrighted

`skeleton` · confidence low

The arrow of separation has struck: the devotee cannot eat or sleep and lives only in longing; this burning is itself the Beloved's gift and the way to meeting.

`skeleton` · confidence low

Dādū stays in the middle: neither Hindu nor Turk, neither indulgence nor harsh renunciation, taking no side (nipakh); the one Lord is found by the one who walks between the two.
