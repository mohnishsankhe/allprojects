## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### Uttara-kāṇḍa, kavitt c. 106 (number to verify) <a id="tea-kavitavali-7-106"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Call me a rogue or an ascetic, a Rajput or a weaver — I need marry my son to no one's daughter nor spoil anyone's caste; Tulsī is Rāma's slave and known as such; let people say what they like: I will beg and eat, sleep in the mosque, and owe nothing to anyone.
