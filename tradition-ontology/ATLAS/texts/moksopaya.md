**Attribution:** tradition: the same Vasiṣṭha-Rāma dialogue ascribed to Vālmīki ('the Rāmāyaṇa of the means to liberation'); scholarly: the older Kashmiri text of which the Yoga Vāsiṣṭha is a revision (Walter Slaje and the Halle project); composed anonymously in Kashmir c. 10th c. **Dates:** Tradition's account: Vālmīki (Tretā age); Scholarly account: c. 10th c. CE (Kashmir); (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 6 books (Vairāgya, Mumukṣuvyavahāra, Utpatti, Sthiti, Upaśānti, Nirvāṇa); Book 6 has 252 sargas; critical edition in 6 volumes (2011-2019)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The Kashmiri recension of the Vasiṣṭha-Rāma dialogue: the same six-book teaching on mind, consciousness, effort, the stages of knowledge and liberation while living, carried by the same stories, without some Vedāntic additions of the vulgate.
**Editions / translations:**

_Notes: Lineage placement is by reception only (the Vedāntic recension was absorbed by Advaita); the Halle editors regard the Mokṣopāya as neither Advaita nor Śaiva. It is scholarly metadata for the Yoga Vāsiṣṭha as well as a text in its own right; U06 anchors Book 4 and Book 6 teachings on it._
