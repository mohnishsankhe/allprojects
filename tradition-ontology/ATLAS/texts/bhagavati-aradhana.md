Śivārya's large Prakrit work on the four 'accomplishments' (ārādhanā) of faith, knowledge, conduct and austerity as they culminate in a disciplined, meditative death; it classifies kinds of death, treats the monastic sign in its normal (naked) and exceptional forms, and gives the conditions, supervision and inner discipline for the renunciation of food at the end of life (restricted theme: summary only).

## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 2 <a id="tea-bhagavati-aradhana-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The four accomplishments (ārādhanā) — of right faith, right knowledge, right conduct and austerity — are to be perfected together and above all at the end of life.

### 26 <a id="tea-bhagavati-aradhana-26"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Death is classified as the death of the wise-among-the-wise (the omniscient), of the wise (the monk), of the wise-ignorant (the layman with vows), of the ignorant (one with right faith but no vows) and of the ignorant-among-the-ignorant (one of wrong faith).

### conditions <a id="tea-bhagavati-aradhana-conditions"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _restricted: summary only_

The fast unto death is to be undertaken only when death cannot be averted, with the teacher's permission, gradually, under the care of attending ascetics (niryāpaka) who strengthen the dying one with instruction and recitation; it is ruined by fear, by longing for life or death, by desire for rewards, and by attachment.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: action · stage: advanced · types: death-dying, teacher-transmission_

### linga <a id="tea-bhagavati-aradhana-linga"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The sign of renunciation is normally the natural one (autsargika-liṅga): nakedness, the plucked head and the whisk; for those with a just cause (such as a bodily defect or public shame) an exceptional sign (āpavādika-liṅga) with cloth is allowed, which may be given up at the approach of death.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: action · stage: advanced · types: ethics, dispute, death-dying_

concepts: [Monastic nudity and clothing](../concepts/monastic-nudity.md) · disputes: [Is complete nudity required of the renunciant (and for liberation)?](../debates/monastic-nudity.md)

### marana <a id="tea-bhagavati-aradhana-marana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _restricted: summary only_

Death is of five kinds — the 'wise-wise' death of the omniscient, the wise death of the restrained ascetic, the wise-foolish death of the layman, the foolish death of one with right view but no vows, and the foolish-foolish death of the wrong-believer; the wise death, by one of three kinds of renunciation of food, is praised.
