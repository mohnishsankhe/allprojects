## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1.2-5 <a id="tea-nirvana-tantra-1-2-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Caṇḍikā asks how the formless, attributeless, eternal maker of all, beyond praise and blame, beyond letters, can take form; Śaṅkara answers that the supreme reality is the Goddess beyond letters, abode of qualities yet beyond them, without form and free of passion, who produces herself (in form) for the sake of worship.

### 1.6-9 <a id="tea-nirvana-tantra-1-6-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

From ether arises wind, from wind the sun, from the sun water, from water earth; the universes (brahmāṇḍas) are made of the five elements; countless universes like grains of sand rest on the head of Ananta upon the tortoise that moves in the ocean of compassion, and Śiva protects them with his trident again and again.

### 10.38-43 <a id="tea-nirvana-tantra-10-38-43"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In the thousand-petalled lotus one should contemplate the Lord as a mountain is seen within a mirror; it is the abode of the supreme Brahman, of liberation, of the attributeless, of Mahākālī; there the eternal changeless one of supreme bliss dwells with the Goddess, Kālī who illumines time, primal Śakti, giver of nirvāṇa; as trees rise from the earth and dissolve into it, and a bubble arises and dissolves in water, so Brahmā and the gods are born from Kālikā and at dissolution dissolve back into her.

### 14.1-10 <a id="tea-nirvana-tantra-14-1-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

On the avadhūta: the vīra follows the renunciant's outer form, shaven or with untended hair, wearing a bone or rudrākṣa rosary, naked or in a loincloth, smeared with ash, holding 'I am Śiva, the bliss of Bhairava, liberated, the lord of the Kula'; by the vīra disposition a brāhmaṇa attains nirvāṇa-liberation and the full fruit, a kṣatriya becomes a great yogin with three parts of the fruit, a vaiśya attains likeness of form and a śūdra the same world.
