Māṇikkavācakar's 400-verse love poem in the kōvai genre, set at Tillai (Chidambaram); the tradition reads the heroine's love as the soul's for Śiva. Tradition: Śiva asked for it after writing down the Tiruvācakam.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-400 <a id="tea-tirukkovaiyar-1-400"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The situations of love — meeting, union, separation, longing and marriage — set at Tillai, which the tradition reads as the soul's love for Śiva.
