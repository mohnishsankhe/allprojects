## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 61 <a id="tea-tiruccanta-viruttam-61"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

To the Lord reclining at Kuṭantai: did your feet ache from walking (as Vāmana, as Rāma)? did your body tire from lifting the earth as the boar? — rise and speak to me, Lord who lies at Kuṭantai.
