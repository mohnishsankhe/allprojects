A concise commentary on SK 1–69. It presents Kapila as one of Brahmā's seven mind-born sons, born with virtue, knowledge, dispassion and power, who taught the twenty-five principles to Āsuri; names the three kinds of suffering; explains dharma through compassion, giving and the Pātañjala yamas and niyamas; lists the eight powers; details the fifty categories of the creation of cognitions; and argues that neither Īśvara, nature (svabhāva) nor time, but prakṛti, is the world's cause.
