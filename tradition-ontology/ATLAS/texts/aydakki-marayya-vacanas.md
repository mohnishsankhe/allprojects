## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### vacana 'Kāyakadalli niratanādaḍe…' <a id="tea-aydakki-marayya-vacanas-kayakadalli-niratanadare"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Absorbed in one's work (kāyaka), one must forget even the sight of the guru and even liṅga-worship, and must break off even if a jaṅgama stands before one; because work is Kailāsa, even Amarēśvaraliṅga is within work.
