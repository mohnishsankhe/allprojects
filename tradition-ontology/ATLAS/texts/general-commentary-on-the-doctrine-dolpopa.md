### whole <a id="tea-general-commentary-on-the-doctrine-dolpopa-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

In the form of a supplication, the whole doctrine is summarized: the conventional is self-empty and the ultimate other-empty; the definitive sūtras and tantras agree in this; the vajra-yoga is the path to its realization.
