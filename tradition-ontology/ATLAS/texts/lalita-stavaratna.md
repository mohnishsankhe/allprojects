# Lalitāstavaratna (Āryādviśatī)

`src:lalita-stavaratna` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Āryādviśatī
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Genre:** stotra
**Authors:** 
  - [Durvāsas](../teachers/durvasas.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Availability:** unknown

A hymn of about two hundred āryā verses ascribed to Durvāsas, describing Lalitā's city (Śrīnagara) enclosure by enclosure with its guardians and the Goddess at the centre; Bhāskararāya quotes it.

_Notes: A verse on Kubera in the region before the city is quoted from 'Durvāsas' in the Saubhāgyabhāskara (checked)._
