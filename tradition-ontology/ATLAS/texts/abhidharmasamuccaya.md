## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1.1.alaya <a id="tea-abhidharmasamuccaya-1-1-alaya"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The consciousness-aggregate is mind, mentation and consciousness; mind (citta) is the store-consciousness, perfumed by the imprints of aggregates, elements and bases, holding all seeds, also called maturation-consciousness and appropriating consciousness; mentation (manas) is what always takes the store-consciousness as 'I', associated with four afflictions: self-view, self-love, the conceit 'I am' and ignorance.

> skandhadhātvāyatanavāsanāparibhāvitaṃ sarvavījakamālayavijñānaṃ vipākavijñānamādānavijñānamapi tat

### 2.1.five-paths <a id="tea-abhidharmasamuccaya-2-1-five-paths"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The path is fivefold: the path of accumulation, the path of preparation, the path of seeing, the path of cultivation and the path of completion. The path of seeing consists of sixteen moments of mind — eight acceptances and eight knowledges regarding the four truths.

> saṃbhāramārgaḥ prayogamārgaḥ darśanamārga bhāvanāmārgaḥ niṣṭhāmārgaśca
