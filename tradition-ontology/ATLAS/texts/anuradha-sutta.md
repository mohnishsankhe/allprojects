Anurādha is questioned by wanderers about the Tathāgata after death; the Buddha shows that the Tathāgata is not found in truth in the aggregates even here: 'I teach only suffering and its cessation'.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 7-13 <a id="tea-anuradha-sutta-7-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Tathāgata is not regarded as form, feeling …, nor as in them or apart from them, nor as without them; since he is not apprehended in truth and reality even in this life, is it fitting to declare him in terms of the four alternatives after death? 'Formerly and now I make known just suffering and the cessation of suffering.'
