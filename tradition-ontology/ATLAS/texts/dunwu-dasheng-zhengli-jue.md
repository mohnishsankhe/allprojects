Wangxi's Chinese record of the Chan master Moheyan's answers to Indian critics at the Tibetan court (late 8th c.): not thinking, not examining (bu si bu guan) as the way to reveal the originally pure mind.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### no-examining <a id="tea-dunwu-dasheng-zhengli-jue-no-examining"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Moheyan's replies: all beings are bound by deluded discriminating thought; when thoughts arise one does not follow them, 'not thinking, not examining', and the originally pure mind appears; the six perfections are included in this non-examining.
