# Yogaviṣaya

**Availability:** digitized-original

A short tract of thirty-three verses attributed to Mīnanātha (Matsyendra): the qualified disciple, the six centres from ādhāra to ājñā, the three channels, the three knots of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra, the names of kuṇḍalinī, the nectar raining from the brahma-place, inner worship with the flower of the mind, and the haṃsa repeated 21,600 times as 'so 'ham'.

## Teachings (7: skeleton 7)

### 3-5 <a id="tea-yogavisaya-3-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The disciple is one of good family and conduct, endowed with virtues, devoted to the guru and wise; the supreme principle was taught by the compassion of Ādinātha to Mīnanātha, who in turn taught his true disciple.

> तस्य सिध्यन्ति पुरुषा आदिनाथे व्यवस्थिताः। कुलजातिसमायुक्तः सुचरित्रगुणान्वितः गुरुभक्तियुतो धीमान् स शिष्य इति कथ्यते। आकुलेनादिनाथेन केजापूपीनवासिना कृपयैव परं तत्त्वं मीननाथोऽपि बोधतः। मीननाथोऽपि सच्छिष्यं प्रत्युवाच समाहितः

### 8-10 <a id="tea-yogavisaya-8-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The six auspicious Kaula centres are ādhāra, svādhiṣṭhāna, maṇipūra, anāhata, viśuddhi and ājñā — at the anus, the genitals, the navel, the heart, the throat and between the brows; knowing the piercing of the centres, one knows the spotless one beyond the centres.

> आधारः स्वाधिष्ठानञ्च मणिपूरसनाहतम्। विशुद्धिराज्ञा कौलानि षट् चक्राणि शुभानि च आधारश्च गुदे तस्थौ स्वाधिष्ठानञ्च लिङ्गके। मणिपूरं नाभिगतं हृदये चाप्यनाहतम् विशुद्धिः कण्ठदेशे च आज्ञाचक्रं भ्रुवोर्मुखम्। चक्रभेदमिति ज्ञात्वा चक्रातीतं निरञ्जनम्

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: body-breath, meditation · stage: intermediate · types: body-layers_

terms: [cakra](../terms/cakra.md), [nirañjana](../terms/niranjana.md) · concepts: [The six centres (Nāth enumeration)](../concepts/six-cakras-natha.md)

### 17 <a id="tea-yogavisaya-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The knot of Brahmā is below, the knot of Viṣṇu in the heart, the knot of Rudra between the brows; the three are released.

> ब्रह्मग्रन्थिरधष्कारे विष्नुग्रन्थिर्हृदि स्थितः। रुद्रग्रन्थिर्भ्रुवोर्मध्ये विमुच्यन्ते त्रयस्तथा

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: body-breath · stage: intermediate · types: body-layers_

concepts: [The three knots (granthi)](../concepts/three-granthis.md)

### 22-23 <a id="tea-yogavisaya-22-23"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Kuṇḍalī's names are Praṇavā, Gudanālā, Nalinī, Sarpiṇī, Baṅkanālī, Śaurī and Kuṇḍalī; the breath stirs kuṇḍalī, and when the circle of the moon is pierced the adamantine powers are gained; the nine doors are to be closed.

> प्रणवा गुदनाला च नलिनी सर्पिणी तथा। बङ्कनालि क्शया शौरी कुण्दली कुन्दलाः स्मृताः कुण्दलीं चालयेत् प्राणो भेदिते शशिमण्डले। सिध्यन्ति वज्रगुम्भानि नव द्वाराणि बन्धयेत्

terms: [kuṇḍalinī](../terms/kundalini.md) · concepts: [Kuṇḍalinī](../concepts/kundalini.md)

### 25-27 <a id="tea-yogavisaya-25-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Lighting the lamp of knowledge above the six centres, one worships the deity within the body: bathing the conscious Lord with the moon's nectar in devotion and offering the flower of the mind, one should worship the supreme Śiva; beholding him as one's own self, knowledge in form and free of disease, the all-pervading spotless one is seen in the form of the body.

> षट्चक्रमण्डलोद्धारं ज्ञानदीपं प्रकाशयेत्। सर्वेषां स्नापनं देहे क्रियते देवतार्चनम् चन्द्रामृतेन चिद्रुपमीश्वरं स्नाप्य भक्तितः। मनःपुष्पं तथा देयमर्चतेत्परमं शिवम् आत्मरूपं तमालोक्य ज्ञानरूपं निरामयम्। दृश्यते देहरूपेण सर्वव्यापी निरञ्जनः

_level: bridging · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice, ultimate_

concepts: [The nectar of the moon (amṛta)](../concepts/amrta-natha.md), [The body as microcosm (piṇḍa and brahmāṇḍa)](../concepts/pinda-brahmanda.md) · practices: [Mental worship of the body-liṅga with eight flowers](../practices/deha-linga-manasa-puja.md)

### 28-29 <a id="tea-yogavisaya-28-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Haṃsa, haṃsa' is the word abiding in the bodies of breathing beings, the knot of prāṇa and apāna; uttered twenty-one thousand six hundred times always, the haṃsa is called 'so 'ham'.

> हंस हंस पदे वाक्यं प्राणिनां देहमाश्रितः। सम्प्राणापानयोर्ग्रन्थिरूपे ॥ त्यभिधीयते सहस्रमेकञ्च युतं षट्छतं चैव सर्वदा। उच्चारपदतो हंसः सोऽहमित्यभिधीयते
