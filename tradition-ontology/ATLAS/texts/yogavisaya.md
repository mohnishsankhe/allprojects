# Yogaviṣaya

**Lineages:** [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), `lin:hatha-yoga`

**Availability:** digitized-original

A short tract of thirty-three verses attributed to Mīnanātha (Matsyendra): the qualified disciple, the six centres from ādhāra to ājñā, the three channels, the three knots of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra, the names of kuṇḍalinī, the nectar raining from the brahma-place, inner worship with the flower of the mind, and the haṃsa repeated 21,600 times as 'so 'ham'.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: P. C. Bagchi (ed.), Kaulajñānanirṇaya and some minor texts of the school of Matsyendranātha, Calcutta Sanskrit Series 3 (Calcutta, 1934)
  - kind: original; name: Muktabodha e-text M00121

_Notes: Colophon in the e-text reads 'matsyendranāthaviracitaṃ ...'; the text calls its author Mīnanātha, son of Umā and Śaṅkara (v. 7). Date uncertain._
