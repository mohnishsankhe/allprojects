Sakya Paṇḍita's verse treatise and autocommentary on epistemology restoring Dharmakīrti's intent: only particulars are real; universals, relations and 'real' generalities posited by the earlier Tibetan (Sangphu) logicians are conceptual constructions; perception is non-conceptual; the treatise organises Buddhist logic in eleven topics.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### universals <a id="tea-tshad-ma-rigs-gter-universals"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Only particulars, which can perform functions, are ultimately (i.e. really) existent; universals are mere conceptual constructs made by the exclusion of what is other, not real entities as the earlier Tibetan logicians held.
