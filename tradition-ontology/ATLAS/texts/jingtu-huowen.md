## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 295a13 <a id="tea-jingtu-huowen-295a13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Thus every land and every mote is the Land of Bliss of mind-only, every buddha in every mote is the Amitābha of one's own nature. Why then is the Sahā world impure? Ordinary beings' karma makes the pure impure, while the Buddha's eye sees the impure as pure. If the mind is defiled the land is defiled, if pure the land is pure — and to purify the mind there is no method apart from Pure Land practice, which has three gates: contemplation, recollection and the many practices, all taking Amitābha of the Land of Bliss as lord.

> 此則知諸剎諸塵塵塵皆唯心之極樂也，一塵一佛佛佛皆本性之彌陀也。……心垢土垢、心淨土淨。……夫欲淨其心有，捨淨土之修法，他無能焉。
