# Tevijjavacchagotta Sutta

The Buddha tells Vacchagotta that in ninety-one aeons he recalls only one Ājīvaka who went to heaven, and he taught the efficacy of deeds.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 14-15 <a id="tea-tevijjavacchagotta-sutta-14-15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Asked whether any Ājīvaka goes to heaven, the Buddha says that recollecting the past ninety-one aeons he finds none except one, and that one taught the efficacy of deeds (kammavādī kiriyavādī); Vacchagotta concludes that this sectarian fold is empty even of heaven, and the Buddha agrees.
