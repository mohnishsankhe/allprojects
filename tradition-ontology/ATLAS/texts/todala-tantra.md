## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1.3-19 <a id="tea-todala-tantra-1-3-19"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Śiva names the Bhairava to be worshipped at the right side of each Mahāvidyā: Mahākāla with Dakṣiṇakālī; Akṣobhya — so called because he drank the Hālāhala poison churned from the ocean without agitation — with Tārā; the five-faced Śiva with Mahātripurasundarī (hence she is 'Pañcamī'); Tryambaka with Bhuvanasundarī; Dakṣiṇāmūrti with Bhairavī; Kabandha with Chinnamastā; Dhūmāvatī is a widow; the one-faced Mahārudra with Bagalā; Mataṅga with Mātaṅgī; Sadāśiva in Viṣṇu's form with Kamalā; also the ten-faced Śiva with Annapūrṇā and Nārada with Durgā.

### 1.20-25 <a id="tea-todala-tantra-1-20-25"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Goddess asks why the first, supreme Vidyā, mother of the three worlds, rides a corpse. Śiva: the first Goddess is Time itself, dwelling in Śiva's heart-lotus as the form of dissolution; when Kālī becomes manifest as dissolution, Sadāśiva at once becomes a corpse; with Śakti he is Śiva, without Śakti he is a corpse (śava), though he does not lose his being the Person.

> शक्त्या युक्तो यदा देवि तदैव शिवरूपकः । शक्तिहीनः शवः साक्षात् पुरुषत्वं न मुञ्चति ॥

_level: ultimate · standpoint: divine · path: knowledge, devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate, world-fate_

terms: [śava](../terms/sava.md) · concepts: [Kālī standing on Śiva as a corpse](../concepts/kali-on-siva-as-corpse.md), [Śiva and Śakti inseparable](../concepts/siva-sakti-inseparable.md)

### 10.9-12 <a id="tea-todala-tantra-10-9-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The ten avatāras: Tārā is the Fish (the e-text reads 'the blue one'), Bagalā the Tortoise, Dhūmāvatī the Boar, Chinnamastā Nṛsiṃha, Bhuvaneśvarī Vāmana, Mātaṅgī Rāma, Tripurā Jāmadagnya (Paraśurāma), Bhairavī Balabhadra, Mahālakṣmī the Buddha, Durgā Kalki, and Bhagavatī Kālī herself arose as the form of Kṛṣṇa; by worshipping these one becomes equal to Mahādeva.

> स्वयं भगवती काली कृष्णमूर्तिः समुद्भवा ॥
