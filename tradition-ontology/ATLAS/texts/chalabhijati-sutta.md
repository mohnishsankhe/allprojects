# Chaḷabhijāti Sutta

Ānanda reports Pūraṇa Kassapa's six classes of birth — black, blue, red, yellow, white and supremely white, the last being Nanda Vaccha, Kisa Saṅkicca and Makkhali Gosāla; the Buddha sets out his own six classes of dark and bright birth and deed.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1-9 <a id="tea-chalabhijati-sutta-1-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ānanda reports that Pūraṇa Kassapa describes six classes of birth: black (butchers, hunters, bandits, executioners and those of cruel livelihood), blue (mendicants who live 'by thorns' and others who teach deeds and action), red (Nigaṇṭhas with one cloth), yellow (white-clothed lay disciples of the naked ones), white (Ājīvaka men and women), and supremely white (Nanda Vaccha, Kisa Saṅkicca and Makkhali Gosāla). The Buddha says Pūraṇa laid this down without the consent of the world, like forcing a piece of meat on a poor man and demanding payment.

### 10-17 <a id="tea-chalabhijati-sutta-10-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Buddha's own six classes: born in a dark (low) state one may produce a dark result, a bright result, or nibbāna that is neither dark nor bright; born in a bright (high) state one may likewise produce a dark result, a bright result, or nibbāna — the last by going forth, abandoning the five hindrances, establishing mindfulness and developing the awakening factors.
