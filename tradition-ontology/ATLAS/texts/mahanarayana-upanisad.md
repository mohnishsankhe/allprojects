# Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad

The tenth prapāṭhaka of the Taittirīya Āraṇyaka: a collection of prayers and meditations (to Nārāyaṇa as the supreme, on truth, austerity and renunciation) that includes the mantras spoken when food is offered into the five breaths before a meal.
