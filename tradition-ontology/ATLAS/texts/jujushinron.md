## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### fasc.1-10 (whole) <a id="tea-jujushinron-ten-abodes"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The mind of beings is set out in ten abodes, one per fascicle, from the goat-like ordinary mind driven by appetite, through the child who keeps fasts and the fearless infant (worldly ethics and religion), the minds of the śrāvaka, the pratyekabuddha, the Hossō, Sanron, Tendai and Kegon teachings, to the tenth, the secret adorned mind of the Shingon maṇḍala.
