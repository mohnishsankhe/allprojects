## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-yogayatra-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

May the Sun make our speech good — he who is the eye of the world, the thousand-rayed abode of splendours, the opened door of liberation, the dispeller of darkness, the self of all embodied beings, the maker of time, beyond compare.

### 1.3-4 <a id="tea-yogayatra-1-3-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Karma, good and bad, produced in another birth is fate (daiva); by itself it gives only such things as birth in a good family. After childhood, discipline, excellence and worthiness arise from human effort together with fate, as in farming. A king obtains fruit through his feudatories, the time, the land, effort, counsel, servants and fate, as a farmer does; if even one is defective, everything is lost, as water leaks from a hole in a skin bag.

### 1.5-6 <a id="tea-yogayatra-1-5-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If mere favourableness of planets, asterisms, lunar days, rising sign, horā, decan and the like were the cause of success for one who sets out, the astrologer himself would be king. If mere mantra, consecration, amulets, pacificatory rites, oblations, fasts, worship of gods and recitation were the cause of success for one who would conquer his enemies, why would the king's priest not be king?
