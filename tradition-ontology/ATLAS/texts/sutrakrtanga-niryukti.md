## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.12 <a id="tea-sutrakrtanga-niryukti-1-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

There are one hundred and eighty kinds of kriyāvādins, eighty-four of akriyāvādins, sixty-seven of ajñānikas and thirty-two of vainayikas.

> asiyasayaṃ kiriyāṇaṃ akiriyavāīṇa hoi culasīī / annāṇiya sattaṭṭhī veṇaiyāṇaṃ ca battīsā
