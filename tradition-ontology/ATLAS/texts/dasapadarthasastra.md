Candramati's Vaiśeṣika treatise of ten categories — substance, quality, motion, universal, particularity, inherence, capacity (śakti), incapacity (aśakti), the universal-particular (sāmānyaviśeṣa) and absence — lost in Sanskrit and preserved only in Xuanzang's Chinese translation (648 CE).
