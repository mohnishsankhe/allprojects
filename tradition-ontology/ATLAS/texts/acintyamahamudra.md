A cycle of Tilopa's instructions given to different people (a yogin in a royal city, a sorcerer, a singer, a courtesan and others), each called 'inconceivable': the unborn luminous dharmatā is the path of Mahāmudrā, free of identification and mentation; mind-itself is self-arisen wisdom; rest undistracted in the uncontrived mind; seeing it directly is the guru's kindness.

### 245b.4 <a id="tea-acintyamahamudra-245b-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Homage to glorious Vajrasattva. The clear light of dharmatā without arising or ceasing, beyond speech, thought and expression, is the path of Mahāmudrā: free of identification, not engaging the mind; homage to the profound, peaceful, unconditioned.

### 245b.5 <a id="tea-acintyamahamudra-245b-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Bodhicitta is the root of all phenomena; because its nature is non-dual, mind itself is self-arisen wisdom, the unmixed, complete mind of the victors; realizing it just as it is, is what is called 'buddha'.

> chos kun rtsa ba byang chub sems/ /rang bzhin gnyis su med pa'i phyir/ / sems nyid rang byung ye shes te/ /ma 'dres yongs rdzogs rgyal ba'i thugs/ /de nyid ji bzhin rtogs gyur na/ /sangs rgyas zhes ni de la bya/

### 246a.4 <a id="tea-acintyamahamudra-246a-4"></a>

### 246a.7 <a id="tea-acintyamahamudra-246a-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Abiding in oneself it is not elsewhere; though it abides continually its depth is hard to fathom; free of effort and accomplishment it pervades everything; seeing it directly is the guru's kindness. Mind itself, unborn and free of elaboration, is free of colour, shape and location, profound, vast and clear; do thus with all that is still or moving. All these beings are buddhas; though (it is) clearly present, they do not realize it; mind itself, the precious jewel, is like a garuḍa chick in its shell.

> rang la gnas pas gzhan du min/ /rgyun du gnas kyang gting dpag dka'/ /rtsol sgrub bral bar yongs la khyab/ /dngos su mthong ba bla ma'i drin/ /ma skyes spros bral sems nyid ni/ /kha dog dbyibs dang gnas rig bral/ /zab cing rgya che gsal bar ldan/ /brtan g.yo ma lus de ltar byos/ /'gro ba 'di kun sangs rgyas te/ /gsal bar yod kyang rtogs pa med/ /sems nyid nor bu rin chen de/ /sbubs na mkha' lding phru gu 'dra/

### 248a.6 <a id="tea-acintyamahamudra-248a-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Qualities are illusion, so faults are not real; there is no certainty of place, so no transition or change; mind has no birth or death — it is the state of clear light; the various recollections are the play of wisdom's energy. Knowing this, there is no grasping at rejecting and adopting; without moving from the equality of awareness's essence, practise the conduct of a madman in the great charnel ground — thus I prophesy for yogins.

> yon tan sgyu ma yin pas skyon la dngos ma grub/ /gnas la nges pa med pas 'pho 'gyur nyam mi nga / /sems la skye shi med de 'od gsal ngang / /dran rig sna tshogs ye shes rol pa'i rtsal/ /de ltar shes pas spang blang 'dzin pa med/ /rig pa'i ngo bo mnyam las ma g.yos par/ /dur khrod chen por smyon pa'i spyod pa gyis/ /rnal 'byor rnams la nga yis lung bstan no/
