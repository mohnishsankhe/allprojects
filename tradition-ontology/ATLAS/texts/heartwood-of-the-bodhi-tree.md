### passim <a id="tea-heartwood-of-the-bodhi-tree-passim"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Voidness (suññatā) — the mind void of 'I' and 'mine' — is the heartwood of the teaching; one should work with a void mind and offer the fruits of work to voidness.
