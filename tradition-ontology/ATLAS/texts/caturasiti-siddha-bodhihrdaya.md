# Caturaśītisiddhabodhihṛdaya (the realization songs of the eighty-four)

`src:caturasiti-siddha-bodhihrdaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** grub thob brgyad cu rtsa bzhi'i rtogs pa'i snying po (Tibetan)
**Language:** Tibetan
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md)
**Genre:** songs (realization verses)
**Authors:** 
  - [Vīraprabhāsvara](../teachers/viraprabhasvara.md) — role: compiler; attribution: accepted
**Availability:** digitized-translation

A collection of the realization songs of the eighty-four siddhas, in almost the same order as Abhayadatta's lives, each introduced 'from the mouth of guru X'; compiled by the master Vīraprabhāsvara (dpa' bo 'od gsal).
**Editions / translations:**

### 1 <a id="tea-caturasiti-siddha-bodhihrdaya-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

As honey smeared on the nose of an untamed wild dog makes it bite in all directions, the guru's instruction given to the dull and unfit burns their own mind-stream; the fortunate who realize the unborn destroy concepts at the mere meeting with appearances, as a sword tied to the trunk of a steady, maddened elephant routs an army.
