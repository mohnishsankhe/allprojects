**Availability:** digitized-original

Abhinavagupta's encyclopaedic exposition of the Trika on the basis of the Mālinīvijayottara, integrating Kaula and Krama. After defining bondage as ignorance and knowledge as the sole cause of liberation (1.22-23), it orders all Śaiva practice by the four means (chs. 2-5), sets out time, space, the tattvas and the paths of manifestation (6-12), the descent of grace (13), the whole ritual of initiation, consecration, funerary and daily rites (14-28), the secret Kula ritual (29), mantra, maṇḍala and mudrā (30-32), and ends with unification, the harmony of the scriptures, the lineage and the author's account of himself (33-37).

### 1.18 <a id="tea-tantraloka-1-18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The teaching of the Lord, divided into ten, eighteen and eight-times-eight, has as its essence the Trika scripture, and the essence of that is the Mālinīmata.

### 1.22-23 <a id="tea-tantraloka-1-22-23"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In all the scriptures it is proclaimed that ignorance is the cause of saṃsāra and knowledge the sole cause of liberation; as the Mālinīvijayottara says, 'they hold that impurity is ignorance, the cause of the sprouting of saṃsāra'.

### 4.148-172 <a id="tea-tantraloka-4-148-172"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Consciousness, eager to manifest, projects creation (Sṛṣṭi); colours what is manifest (Raktā); withdraws its persistence (Sthitināśā); gives rise to and removes the doubt of Yama; withdraws [the object] into the fire of the self (Saṃhāra); devours even that dissolution (Mṛtyu); fixes and melts impressions (Rudra); withdraws the twelve faculties (Mārtaṇḍa); dissolves the instrument into the agent (Paramārka); dissolves the constructed agent (Kālāgnirudra); dissolves that into Mahākāla, full of 'I am all this'; and finally dissolves full consciousness into the Akula abode - that independence is the supreme Goddess.

### 4.268-270 <a id="tea-tantraloka-4-268-270"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

### 5.43-52 <a id="tea-tantraloka-5-43-52"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate
