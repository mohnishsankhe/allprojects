# Āgneyāgama

`src:agneya-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Āgneya, Anala
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Vyoman, then Hutāśana (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Rudrabheda no. 14 in the Kāmika's order; 1 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** unknown

No. 14 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Rudrabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Vyoman, then Hutāśana. The Kāmika assigns it 1 upabheda(s): Āgneya.

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.18/10.27: Vyoman → Hutabhuj. Śrīkaṇṭhīya: 'Āgneya'. In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the eyes (pūrva 1.93–101).
