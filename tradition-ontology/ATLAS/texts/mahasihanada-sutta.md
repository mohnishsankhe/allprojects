## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 9-21 <a id="tea-mahasihanada-sutta-9-21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The ten powers of a Tathāgata by which he claims the bull's place and sets rolling the Brahmā-wheel: knowing the possible and impossible, the results of deeds, the ways leading everywhere, the world of many elements, the diverse inclinations of beings, the faculties of beings, the defilement and purification of jhānas and attainments, past lives, the passing away and rebirth of beings, and the destruction of the effluents.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: devotion · stage: realized · types: powers-experiences_

terms: [tathāgatabala](../terms/tathagatabala.md) · concepts: [The ten powers of a Tathāgata](../concepts/ten-tathagata-balas.md)

### 22-28 <a id="tea-mahasihanada-sutta-22-28"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The four confidences (vesārajja) of the Tathāgata: none can rightly charge that he is not fully awakened to certain things, that certain effluents are not destroyed, that the things he calls obstructions do not obstruct, or that his teaching does not lead one who practises it to the end of suffering.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: devotion · stage: realized · types: powers-experiences, teacher-transmission_

terms: [vesārajja](../terms/vesarajja.md)

### 35-43 <a id="tea-mahasihanada-sutta-35-43"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The five destinations — hell, the animal realm, the realm of ghosts, humans and gods — and nibbāna, with the ways leading to each, which the Tathāgata knows; the similes of the charcoal pit, cesspit, tree, mansion and lotus pond.

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: action · stage: all · types: world-fate, karma-liberation_

terms: [gati](../terms/gati.md) · concepts: [The five destinations](../concepts/five-gati.md), [The realms of rebirth](../concepts/rebirth-realms.md)

### 44-56 <a id="tea-mahasihanada-sutta-44-56"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

The Buddha recounts that he once practised the fourfold holy life of asceticism, coarseness, scrupulousness and seclusion to the extreme, and purification by food; yet by that conduct and those austerities he did not attain superhuman distinction of knowledge and vision, because he had not attained noble wisdom.

_level: conventional · standpoint: polemical · path: general · stage: all · types: dispute, practice_

terms: [tapas](../terms/tapas.md) · concepts: [The middle way](../concepts/middle-way.md) · disputes: [Is suffering ended by burning off past kamma through austerity, and which action is most blameworthy?](../debates/austerity-and-past-kamma.md)

### 57 <a id="tea-mahasihanada-sutta-57"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There are ascetics and brahmins who say 'purity comes through transmigration'. The Buddha: it is not easy to find a realm not already passed through in this long time, except the Pure Abodes, from which he would not have returned.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: polemical · path: general · stage: unmarked · types: karma-liberation, dispute_
