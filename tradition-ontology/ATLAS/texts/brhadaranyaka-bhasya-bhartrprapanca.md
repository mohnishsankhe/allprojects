## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### bau-5.1.1 <a id="tea-brhadaranyaka-bhasya-bhartrprapanca-bau-5-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Bhartṛprapañca (as reported by Śaṅkara, identified by Ānandagiri) held that Brahman is both dual and non-dual, one and many, like the ocean and its waves: the manifold world is a real modification of the one Brahman.

### bau-intro <a id="tea-brhadaranyaka-bhasya-bhartrprapanca-bau-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Bhartṛprapañca (as reported and refuted by Śaṅkara and Sureśvara) taught that liberation requires the combination of knowledge with the Vedic works prescribed for one's station, throughout life.
