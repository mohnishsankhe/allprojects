## Teachings (1: sourced 1)

### 1-5 <a id="tea-mantrika-upanisad-1-5"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

The eight-footed haṃsa, the unborn she-goat of red, white and black, and the one Lord who is seen by some as the twenty-fifth, by others as the twenty-sixth principle.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate, world-fate_

concepts: [Haṃsa — the swan / the self as breath-mantra](../concepts/hamsa.md)
