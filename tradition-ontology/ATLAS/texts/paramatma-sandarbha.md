## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### jiva-and-world-section <a id="tea-paramatma-sandarbha-jiva-and-world-section"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The souls are the Lord's marginal power, eternally distinct from yet dependent on him; the world is a real transformation (pariṇāma) of his external power while he remains unchanged; the Advaita doctrine of unreal appearance (vivarta) is rejected.
