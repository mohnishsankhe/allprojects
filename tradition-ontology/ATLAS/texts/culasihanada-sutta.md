## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-16 <a id="tea-culasihanada-sutta-2-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Only here is there a first, a second, a third and a fourth ascetic; other doctrines are empty of ascetics.' Others may profess to understand the four kinds of clinging — to sensual pleasures, views, rules and vows, and a doctrine of self — but do not describe the full understanding of all of them; the Tathāgata does, and clinging has craving as its source.
