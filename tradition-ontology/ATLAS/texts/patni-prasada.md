Aṅkīyā nāṭ on the Brahmin wives who fed Kṛṣṇa against their husbands' ritual scruples: devotion over ritual. Performed as bhāonā with a sūtradhāra who sings, dances and explains.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### whole <a id="tea-patni-prasada-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The priests, busy with sacrifice, refuse food to the hungry Kṛṣṇa and his friends; their wives carry food to him and are blessed, and the priests learn too late that rites without devotion are empty.
