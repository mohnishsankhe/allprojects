# Sanatkumāra Saṃhitā (one of the five Śubhāgamas)

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md)
**Genre:** saṃhitā (Śubhāgama)
**Authors:** 
  - [Sanatkumāra](../teachers/sanatkumara.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional

One of the five 'auspicious āgamas' which, according to Lakṣmīdhara, teach the Vedic Samaya way of Śrīvidyā; ascribed to the sage Sanatkumāra. Known only through Lakṣmīdhara's citations.

_Notes: Not the haṭha Vasiṣṭha Saṃhitā (src:vasistha-samhita) nor Pāñcarātra saṃhitās of similar name. Named in Lakṣmīdhara on SL 31 (checked)._
