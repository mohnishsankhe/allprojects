## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 17-20 <a id="tea-salayatanavibhanga-sutta-17-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There is equanimity based on diversity (towards forms, sounds …) and equanimity based on unity (the formless spheres); by depending on the latter one abandons the former, and by non-identification (atammayatā) one abandons even that.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind, practice_

terms: [upekkhā](../terms/upekkha.md), [atammayatā](../terms/atammayata.md)

### 21-26 <a id="tea-salayatanavibhanga-sutta-21-26"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Teacher establishes mindfulness in three situations — when disciples do not listen, when some listen and some do not, and when all listen — remaining unmoved, neither displeased nor elated, mindful and aware: this makes him fit to instruct a group; he is the incomparable trainer of persons to be tamed.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: general · stage: realized · types: teacher-transmission_

concepts: [The teacher–disciple relationship](../concepts/teacher-disciple-relation.md)
