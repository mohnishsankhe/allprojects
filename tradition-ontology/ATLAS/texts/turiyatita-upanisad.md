## Teachings (2: sourced 2)

### 1 (conduct) <a id="tea-turiyatita-upanisad-1-conduct"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

He lives in the way of the cow, taking food without hands; he burns his learning; hides his nature; behaves like a child, a madman or a ghost; and abandons the body as the praṇava.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: ethics, death-dying_

concepts: [The avadhūta](../concepts/avadhuta.md)

### 1 (sequence) <a id="tea-turiyatita-upanisad-1-sequence"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Brahmā asks Ādi-Nārāyaṇa about the path of the avadhūta beyond the fourth; the rare one passes from kuṭīcaka to bahūdaka, haṃsa and paramahaṃsa, and then, abandoning all marks into water, becomes the avadhūta.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation_
