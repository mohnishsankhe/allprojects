# Kauṣītaki Āraṇyaka (Śāṅkhāyana Āraṇyaka)

**Alternate titles:** Śāṅkhāyana Āraṇyaka
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
**Genre:** āraṇyaka
**Structure:** 15 adhyāyas (3–6 = Kauṣītaki Upaniṣad)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The Āraṇyaka of the Kauṣītaki/Śāṅkhāyana Ṛgveda: after chapters on the Mahāvrata it contains the Kauṣītaki Upaniṣad (adhyāyas 3–6, including Pratardana's 'inner Agnihotra', ŚĀ 4.5 = KU 2.5), a Saṃhitopaniṣad on recitation, the contest of the breath with the senses, an interiorized Agnihotra, and a closing teacher lineage.
