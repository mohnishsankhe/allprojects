The Āraṇyaka of the Kauṣītaki/Śāṅkhāyana Ṛgveda: after chapters on the Mahāvrata it contains the Kauṣītaki Upaniṣad (adhyāyas 3–6, including Pratardana's 'inner Agnihotra', ŚĀ 4.5 = KU 2.5), a Saṃhitopaniṣad on recitation, the contest of the breath with the senses, an interiorized Agnihotra, and a closing teacher lineage.
