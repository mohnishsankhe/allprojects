The fourth Upāṅga, a systematic treatise in thirty-six sections (pada) on the classification of beings, their locations, lifespans, senses, bodies, sex (veda), passions, leśyās, karma, knowledge and the rest; its first pada lists fifteen kinds of liberated beings, including those liberated in a woman's body, as householders and in the garb of other sects.
