## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 478 <a id="tea-kartikeyanupreksa-478"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Dharma is the nature of a thing; dharma is the tenfold [virtue] beginning with forbearance; dharma is the three jewels; dharma is the protection of living beings.

> dhammo vatthusahāvo khamādibhāvo ya dasaviho dhammo / rayaṇattayaṃ ca dhammo jīvāṇaṃ rakkhaṇaṃ dhammo

### loka <a id="tea-kartikeyanupreksa-loka"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

In the reflection on the universe the text describes the uncreated, beginningless world of six substances in the shape of a standing man, with the hells below, the middle world and the heavens above, and the abode of the liberated at the summit.
