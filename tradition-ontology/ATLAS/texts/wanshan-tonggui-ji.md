## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 966b26-c10 <a id="tea-wanshan-tonggui-ji-966b26-c10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Objection: the mind-only pure land pervades the ten directions; why lodge in a lotus in Sukhāvatī, giving rise to choosing and rejecting — how is that the gate of non-arising, where is equality? Yanshou: the mind-only buddha-land is born only when the mind is understood; the sūtra says a bodhisattva who knows all is mind-only may be born quickly in Abhirati or Sukhāvatī. Though one believes in equality and non-arising, one's power is not yet sufficient, contemplation is shallow, the mind floats, objects are strong and habits heavy — so one must be born in a buddha-land, relying on its superior conditions, where acceptance is easily achieved and the bodhisattva path swiftly practised (citing the Awakening of Faith).

> 問：「唯心淨土，周遍十方。何得託質蓮臺，寄形安養，而興取捨之念，豈達無生之門？欣厭情生，何成平等？」答：「唯心佛土者，了心方生。……又平等之門，無生之旨，雖即仰教生信，其乃力量未充，觀淺心浮，境強習重；須生佛國，以仗勝緣，忍力易成，速行菩薩道。
