## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-ramarahasya-upanisad-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Sanaka and the sages, with Prahlāda and others, ask Hanumān which is the supreme truth among the Vedas; Hanumān: Rāma is the supreme Brahman, the tāraka.
