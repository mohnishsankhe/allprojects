# Yogajāgama

`src:yogaja-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Yogaja
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Sudhā, then Bhasma, then Vibhu (Kiraṇa: Prabhu) (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Śivabheda no. 2 in the Kāmika's order; 5 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** digitized-original

No. 2 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Śivabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Sudhā, then Bhasma, then Vibhu (Kiraṇa: Prabhu). The Kāmika assigns it 5 upabheda(s): Vīṇāśikhottara, Tāra, Santa, Santati, Ātmayoga. Transmitted in South Indian manuscripts; mainly ritual (kriyā) content.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: e-text from a manuscript of M.K.S. Bhattar, Madurai (Muktabodha M00250); IFP MS 39691

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.4/10.7: Sudhā → Bhasma → Prabhu. Śrīkaṇṭhīya: 'Yogaja'. In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the ankles (pūrva 1.93–101).
