## Teachings (1: sourced 1)

### 1-5 (summary) <a id="tea-hayagriva-upanisad-1-5-summary"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

Nārada asks Brahmā for the knowledge that gives liberation; Brahmā gives the Hayagrīva mantras with seers, metres and seed-syllables; one who knows them attains knowledge and liberation.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language_

teachers: [Nārada](../teachers/narada.md), [Brahmā (as first teacher)](../teachers/brahma.md)
