## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2.1-2.2 <a id="tea-ambattha-sutta-2-1-2-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Buddha repeats a verse of Brahmā Sanaṅkumāra: the khattiya is best among people who rely on clan, but one accomplished in knowledge and conduct is best among gods and humans; knowledge and conduct are the gradual training from virtue to the destruction of the effluents.
