## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### gunasthana-adhikara <a id="tea-samayasara-nataka-gunasthana-adhikara"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

In a closing chapter added by Banārsīdās, the fourteen guṇasthānas are described so that the reader, knowing the self by niścaya, also knows where he stands on the path and does not neglect conduct.

### utthanika <a id="tea-samayasara-nataka-utthanika"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The poet sets the Samayasāra as a drama in which the conscious self (cetana), deluded into playing the roles of non-self, recognises its own nature; he names Rājamalla's commentary as his guide.
