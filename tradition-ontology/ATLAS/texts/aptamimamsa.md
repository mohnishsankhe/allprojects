Samantabhadra's 114-verse critical hymn (Devāgamastotra) examining who is a trustworthy teacher (āpta): outward miracles do not prove greatness; the omniscient, faultless teacher is established by reasoning; one-sided (ekānta) views of being and non-being, identity and difference, permanence and momentariness, and monism are refuted by the sevenfold predication, and syādvāda is defined.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Aṣṭasahasrī](astasahasri.md), [Aṣṭaśatī](astasati.md)
