## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### structure <a id="tea-three-continua-ngorchen-structure"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The uncommon Lamdre is the three continua: the causal continuum, the all-base as the union of clarity and emptiness in which saṃsāra and nirvāṇa are inseparable; the method continuum, the practices of empowerment and the two stages that ripen and liberate; and the result continuum, Mahāmudrā.
