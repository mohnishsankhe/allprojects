# Lalitāgama

`src:lalita-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Lalita
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Ālaya, then Lālita (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Rudrabheda no. 22 in the Kāmika's order; 3 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** digitized-original

No. 22 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Rudrabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Ālaya, then Lālita. The Kāmika assigns it 3 upabheda(s): Lalita, Lalitottara, Kaumāra.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: IFP transcript T0202 (Muktabodha M00252)

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.17/10.26: Ālaya → Rudra in Bhairava form. Śrīkaṇṭhīya: 'Lalita'. In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the cheeks (pūrva 1.93–101).
