Āryadeva's 'Four Hundred Verses' on the yoga practice of bodhisattvas: the first eight chapters overcome the four errors (taking the impermanent as permanent, the painful as pleasant, the impure as pure, and the selfless as self), the afflictions and attachment to objects, and prepare the student; the last eight refute permanent entities, the self, time, views, the senses and objects, extreme views and conditioned characteristics, and settle disputes between teacher and student.

### 1 <a id="tea-catuhsataka-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Chapter on abandoning the belief in permanence: one who has the three worlds' lord of death as his master should not sleep as if he were immortal; since all who are born must die, clinging to the impermanent as permanent brings grief, and seeing death clearly one turns from craving.

### 8.15 <a id="tea-catuhsataka-8-15"></a>

First turning away from demerit, in the middle turning away from the self, afterwards turning away from everything — whoever knows this is wise.

### 8.19 <a id="tea-catuhsataka-8-19"></a>

### 10 <a id="tea-catuhsataka-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Chapter refuting the self: a permanent self could neither act nor be harmed, so non-harm would be meaningless; the self cannot be the body, the mind or apart from them, and what is called 'I' arises only through ignorance, as a snake is imputed to a rope.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: unmarked · types: dispute, consciousness-mind_

### 12.23 <a id="tea-catuhsataka-12-23"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Tathāgatas describe the Dharma, in brief, as non-harming, and emptiness alone as nirvāṇa; here there are only these two.

> dharmaṃ samāsato'hiṃsāṃ varṇayanti tathāgatāḥ | śūnyatāmeva nirvāṇaṃ kevalaṃ tadihobhayam ||

`skeleton` · confidence high

One who has no position — 'existent', 'non-existent' or 'both existent and non-existent' — cannot be refuted even over a long time.

> sadasatsadasacceti yasya pakṣo na vidyate | upālambhaścireṇāpi tasya vaktuṃ na śakyate ||
