The 'Vows of Good Conduct' of Samantabhadra, some 60 verses that conclude the Gaṇḍavyūha: homage, offering, confession, rejoicing, requesting the teaching, requesting the buddhas to remain, dedication, and the vow to follow Samantabhadra's conduct until the end of the world and be reborn in Sukhāvatī; the basis of the seven-branch worship and, in East Asia, of 'Samantabhadra's ten great vows'.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1-12 <a id="tea-bhadracaripranidhana-1-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

With body, speech and mind I bow to all the buddhas of the three times in all worlds; I make offerings; I confess all wrongs done through desire, anger and delusion; I rejoice in the merit of all beings and buddhas; I request the buddhas to turn the wheel; I request those about to pass into nirvāṇa to remain for aeons; I dedicate all merit to awakening.

### ten-vows <a id="tea-bhadracaripranidhana-ten-vows"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Samantabhadra's ten great vows (40-fascicle Avataṃsaka, T293 fasc. 40): to pay homage to all buddhas; praise the Tathāgatas; make vast offerings; repent karmic obstructions; rejoice in merit; request the turning of the wheel; request the buddhas to remain; always follow the buddhas' teaching; always accord with beings; dedicate all merit — until space, beings, karma and afflictions end; at death they lead one to Sukhāvatī.
