The third 'secret': the forms of the Goddess foretold in the Devī Māhātmya's eleventh chapter (Nandā, Raktadantikā, Śākambharī, Durgā, Bhīmā, Bhrāmarī) and their meditation.

_Notes: Recited as a limb (aṅga) of the Durgā Saptaśatī (Devī Māhātmya, U07); U23 records the Śākta liturgy._
