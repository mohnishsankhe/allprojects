# Cintyāgama

`src:cintya-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Cintya, Acintya (Kiraṇa), Acintyaviśvasādākhya (title of a surviving text; relation uncertain)
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by (Su)dīpta, then Gopati, then Ambikā (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Śivabheda no. 3 in the Kāmika's order; 6 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** unknown

No. 3 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Śivabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by (Su)dīpta, then Gopati, then Ambikā. The Kāmika assigns it 6 upabheda(s): Sucintya, Subhaga, Vāma, Pāpanāśa, Parodbhava, Amṛta. No complete text securely identified with the Cintya is recorded here.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Acintyaviśvasādākhya (Muktabodha M00272) — relation to the Cintya not established here

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.4/10.8: 'Acintya' to Dīpta; Dīptarudra → Gopati → Ambikā. Śrīkaṇṭhīya: 'Cintya'. In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the toes (pūrva 1.93–101).
