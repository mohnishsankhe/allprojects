# Dharmaskandha

**Language:** Sanskrit (lost or fragmentary), Chinese
**Family:** ascetic

**Attribution:** tradition: Śāriputra (Yaśomitra); Mahāmaudgalyāyana (Chinese tradition); scholarly: Attribution to disciples of the Buddha is traditional; the works are dated by scholars to c. 3rd c. BCE – 1st c. CE; confidence: disputed
**Structure:** 21 chapters; 12 fascicles (Xuanzang, 659); Sanskrit fragments from Gilgit (ed. Dietz 1984)
**Availability:** digitized-translation

One of the six 'feet', held to be among the oldest: an analysis of the training rules, the fruits, the noble lineages, the right efforts, the bases of power, the applications of mindfulness, the truths, the meditations, the formless states, the faculties, the aggregates and dependent origination, closely comparable to the Pali Vibhaṅga.
