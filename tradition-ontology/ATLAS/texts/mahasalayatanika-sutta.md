## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3-10 <a id="tea-mahasalayatanika-sutta-3-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Not knowing and seeing the eye, forms and so on as they are, one is inflamed with lust and the five aggregates of clinging accumulate; knowing and seeing them as they are, craving is abandoned, and in such a one the noble eightfold path comes to fulfilment together with the four establishments of mindfulness and all the thirty-seven qualities, and calm and insight occur yoked evenly together.
