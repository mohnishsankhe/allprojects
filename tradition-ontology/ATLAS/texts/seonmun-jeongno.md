## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### donodonsu <a id="tea-seonmun-jeongno-donodonsu"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Seongcheol argues that the awakening of the Chan patriarchs is 'realization-awakening' (jeungo) in which cultivation is also complete — sudden awakening, sudden cultivation — and that Jinul's sudden awakening followed by gradual cultivation describes only 'understanding-awakening' (haeo), which the Chan school rejects as intellectual understanding.
