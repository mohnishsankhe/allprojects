Kṣemarāja's primer of the Pratyabhijñā in 20 sūtras with his own commentary: consciousness, free, is the cause of the world (1); it unfolds the world on its own screen (2); the individual is contracted consciousness (4) and the mind is consciousness descended (5); every philosophy is a stage of it (8); the individual too performs the five acts (10-11); ignorance of this is saṃsāra (12), knowledge of it liberation in life (13-16); the means are the unfolding of the centre (17-18), culminating in ever-present samādhi and lordship over the wheel of powers (19-20).
