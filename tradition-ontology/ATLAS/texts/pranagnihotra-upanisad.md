## Teachings (3: sourced 3)

### 1 <a id="tea-pranagnihotra-upanisad-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

This teaching frees from saṃsāra even without agnihotra, Sāṃkhya or yoga: the body itself is the sacrifice.

### 1-4 <a id="tea-pranagnihotra-upanisad-1-4"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

The sacrifice in the body: food is purified with mantras and offered into the breaths as into the sacrificial fires; the body's fires are named, and the self, intellect, mind and senses are identified with the sacrificer, his wife and the priests; the one who performs this bodily sacrifice is freed from saṃsāra.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: ritual, knowledge · stage: intermediate · types: practice, body-layers_

concepts: [Prāṇāgnihotra — the fire-offering into the breaths](../concepts/pranagnihotra.md), [The internalized sacrifice](../concepts/internalized-sacrifice.md) · practices: [Prāṇāgnihotra (offering food into the breaths)](../practices/pranagnihotra.md)

### 2-4 <a id="tea-pranagnihotra-upanisad-2-4"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Before eating one sprinkles the food with mantras and offers five oblations to prāṇa, apāna, vyāna, udāna and samāna; the parts of the body are the fire, the priests and the ritual implements.
