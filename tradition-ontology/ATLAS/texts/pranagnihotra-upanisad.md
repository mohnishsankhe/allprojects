# Prāṇāgnihotra Upaniṣad

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** `lin:upanisadic`
**Genre:** upaniṣad (sāmānya-vedānta)
**Availability:** digitized-original

A short Upaniṣad of the Muktikā canon (attached to the Atharvaveda) teaching the 'sacrifice in the body' (śārīra-yajña): food eaten with mantras is offered into the breaths as into fires, and the parts and powers of the body and mind are identified with the priests, implements and fires of the sacrifice.
