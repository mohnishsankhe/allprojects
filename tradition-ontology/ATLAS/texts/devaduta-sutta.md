## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-29 <a id="tea-devaduta-sutta-2-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

King Yama asks the evil-doer whether he did not see the divine messengers — an infant, an old person, a sick person, a criminal being punished, a corpse — and reminds him that his evil deeds were done by himself, not by mother, father or gods; the hell-wardens then lead him through the hells.
