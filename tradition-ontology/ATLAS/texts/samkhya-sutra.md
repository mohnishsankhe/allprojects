**Availability:** digitized-original

The sūtra text of later Sāṃkhya. It restates the Kārikā's system, supplies the illustrative tales the Kārikā omitted, and argues at length against rivals: that Īśvara is not established as creator, that bondage is not due to avidyā, that things are not momentary, that consciousness is not a product of the elements, that the Veda is neither eternal nor authored yet self-valid; it accepts liberation while living and explains meditation as the removal of passion.
