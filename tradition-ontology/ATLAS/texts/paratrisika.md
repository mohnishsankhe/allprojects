# Parātrīśikā

`src:paratrisika` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Parātriṃśikā, Trīśikā, Anuttaraprakriyā
**Original title:** परात्रीशिका
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md), [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md), [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)
**Genre:** tantra (fragment of a Yāmala, dialogue)
**Attribution:** tradition: Spoken by Bhairava; presents itself as belonging to the Rudrayāmala (final verse).; scholarly: Anonymous; transmitted in Kashmir with the commentaries of Abhinavagupta and others.; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Scholarly account: before c. 950 CE (commented on by Somānanda, now lost); (confidence low)
**Structure:** c. 35 verses (GRETIL numbering, with a gap at 34)
**Availability:** digitized-original

A short Kaula-Trika dialogue: the Goddess asks how the Unsurpassed (anuttara) grants immediate Kaula perfection. Bhairava teaches the emanation of the letters as the tattvas (vv. 5-9), the Heart of Bhairava as the 'third Brahman' (10), the powers of reciting and remembering it (11-20), the tattvas from Kālāgni to Śiva (21-23), the universe resting in the Heart-seed as a banyan tree in its seed (24), initiation for one who knows this (25), and a worship procedure (26-33). It closes 'this is the Rudrayāmala' (35).

## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 1-4 <a id="tea-paratrisika-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Goddess asks how the Unsurpassed (anuttara) grants immediate Kaula perfection, by merely knowing which one attains equality with the Khecarī, and asks about the power abiding in the heart, the Kaulinī, mistress of the Kula; Bhairava promises to tell the Kaula rule that dwells in the space of his heart.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, ultimate_

terms: [anuttara](../terms/anuttara.md), [kula](../terms/kula.md), [hṛdaya](../terms/hrdaya.md)

### 5-9 <a id="tea-paratrisika-5-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The vowels ending with the bindu are the lunar days; within them, by the union of time, are sun and moon; the tattvas from earth to puruṣa are in the five classes of consonants from ka to ma; the four concentrations on air, fire, water and Indra (the semivowels); above them the five 'Brahmans' beginning with śa; this emanation, ending in kṣa, is the womb of all mantras and vidyās.

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: sound · stage: all · types: sound-language, world-fate_

terms: [mātṛkā](../terms/matrka.md), [varṇa](../terms/varna.md) · concepts: [The letters: mātṛkā and mālinī](../concepts/matrka-and-malini.md)

### 10-20 <a id="tea-paratrisika-10-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The third Brahman joined with the fourteenth and the end of the lunar-day lords is the Heart of Bhairava, not attained by one not born of a yoginī or not a Rudra; when it is uttered, the whole host of mantras and seals turns toward the reciter; remembering it brings powers, and by knowing it, without seeing a maṇḍala, one becomes initiated and a yogin.

_level: bridging · standpoint: divine · path: sound · stage: advanced · types: sound-language, powers-experiences_

terms: [hṛdaya-bīja](../terms/hrdaya-bija.md), [mantravīrya](../terms/mantravirya.md)

### 24 <a id="tea-paratrisika-24"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As the great tree lies as power within the banyan seed, so this moving and unmoving universe lies within the seed of the Heart.

> yathā nyagrodhabījasthaḥ śaktirūpo mahādrumaḥ / tathā hṛdayabījasthaṃ jagad etac carācaram

_level: ultimate · standpoint: cosmic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate, world-fate_

terms: [hṛdaya](../terms/hrdaya.md), [hṛdaya-bīja](../terms/hrdaya-bija.md) · concepts: [The heart and the space within it](../concepts/hrdaya-heart.md)

### 25 <a id="tea-paratrisika-25"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

For one who knows this in truth, initiation leading to nirvāṇa happens without doubt, without oblations of sesame and ghee.
