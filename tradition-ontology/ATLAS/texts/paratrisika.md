# Parātrīśikā

**Structure:** c. 35 verses (GRETIL numbering, with a gap at 34)
**Availability:** digitized-original

A short Kaula-Trika dialogue: the Goddess asks how the Unsurpassed (anuttara) grants immediate Kaula perfection. Bhairava teaches the emanation of the letters as the tattvas (vv. 5-9), the Heart of Bhairava as the 'third Brahman' (10), the powers of reciting and remembering it (11-20), the tattvas from Kālāgni to Śiva (21-23), the universe resting in the Heart-seed as a banyan tree in its seed (24), initiation for one who knows this (25), and a worship procedure (26-33). It closes 'this is the Rudrayāmala' (35).
