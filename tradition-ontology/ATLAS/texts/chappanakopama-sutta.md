## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-6 <a id="tea-chappanakopama-sutta-1-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Six animals — snake, crocodile, bird, dog, jackal and monkey — tied to one rope pull each to its own domain; tied to a strong post they tire and lie down by it. So the six senses pull toward agreeable objects unless tied to the post of mindfulness directed to the body.
