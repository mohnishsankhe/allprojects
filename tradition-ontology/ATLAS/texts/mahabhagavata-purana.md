## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 8 <a id="tea-mahabhagavata-purana-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

When Śiva refuses Satī leave to attend her father Dakṣa's sacrifice, she grows angry and takes a terrifying form; as Śiva turns to flee, she blocks every direction with ten forms — the ten Mahāvidyās — and, when he asks who they are, names them; he then consents to her going.
