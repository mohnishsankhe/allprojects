## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-3 <a id="tea-kesakambala-sutta-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As a hair-blanket is the worst of woven cloths — cold in cold, hot in heat, ugly, smelly, rough — so Makkhali's doctrine is the worst of the ascetics' doctrines: he says there is no deed, no action, no energy (natthi kammaṃ natthi kiriyaṃ natthi viriyaṃ), contradicting all past, future and present Buddhas, who teach deeds, action and energy. He is like a fish-trap set at a river mouth, for the harm of many.
