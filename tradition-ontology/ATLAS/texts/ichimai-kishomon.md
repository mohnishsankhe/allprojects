## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### whole <a id="tea-ichimai-kishomon-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The nenbutsu I teach is not the contemplation of the Buddha discussed by the learned of China and Japan, nor the recitation that comes from studying and understanding its meaning; it is only to say 'Namu Amida Butsu' without doubt that one will be born, and so to be born. The three minds and four modes are all contained in the conviction that one will be born by saying Namu Amida Butsu. If I held some deeper teaching besides this, I would lose the compassion of Śākyamuni and Amida and fall out of the vow. Those who believe in the nenbutsu, even if they have thoroughly learned all the teachings of Śākyamuni's lifetime, should behave as ignorant people who know not a single letter, as unlettered nuns and lay-folk, and without showing learning just say the nenbutsu single-heartedly.
