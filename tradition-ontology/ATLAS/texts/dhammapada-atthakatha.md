### 14.2 <a id="tea-dhammapada-atthakatha-14-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

At the time of the Twin Miracle at Sāvatthī the rival teachers were put to shame; Pūraṇa Kassapa, disgraced, tied a pot of sand to his neck and drowned himself in a pond.
