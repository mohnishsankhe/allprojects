## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 5-14 <a id="tea-anathapindikovada-sutta-5-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

To the dying Anāthapiṇḍika Sāriputta teaches: 'I will not cling to the eye and my consciousness will not be dependent on the eye' — and so for all senses, objects, consciousnesses, contacts, feelings, elements, aggregates, formless spheres, this world and the next, and whatever is seen, heard, sensed, cognised; Anāthapiṇḍika weeps, never having heard such a talk, which is usually not given to lay people.
