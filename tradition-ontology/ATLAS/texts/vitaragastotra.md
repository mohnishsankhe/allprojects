## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### ch.19 <a id="tea-vitaragastotra-ch-19"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Obeying your command is higher than worshipping you; your command, kept or broken, leads to liberation or to worldly existence. For all time your command concerns what to abandon and what to adopt: inflow is always to be abandoned, stopping to be adopted.
