### 1 <a id="tea-dhyanabindu-upanisad-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Even if sin were piled high as a mountain many yojanas wide, it is destroyed by the yoga of meditation, and by nothing else ever.

### 41-43 <a id="tea-dhyanabindu-upanisad-41-43"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Posture, breath-restraint, withdrawal, concentration, meditation and absorption are the six limbs. There are as many postures as beings; four are named — siddha, bhadra, siṃha and padma.

### 43-54 <a id="tea-dhyanabindu-upanisad-43-54"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The body's centres: the ādhāra with four petals and the triangle (yoni) and liṅga; the svādhiṣṭhāna; the maṇipūra, a twelve-spoked wheel where the jīva wanders driven by merit and sin; the kanda from which 72,000 channels spread, ten of them principal (iḍā, piṅgalā, suṣumnā, gāndhārī, hastijihvā, pūṣā, yaśasvinī, alambusā, kuhū, śaṅkhinī).

Khecarī mudrā and the retention of bindu are described: bindu is twofold, white (śukla, Śiva) and red (rajas, Śakti), and their union in the body is the yogin's aim. Recorded as a summary only.

### 93-94 (petal moods) <a id="tea-dhyanabindu-upanisad-93-94-petal-moods"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In the heart lotus the moods follow the petals: east (white) dharma, south-east (red) sleep and sloth, south (black) hatred, south-west (blue) sin and violence, west (crystal) play, north-west (ruby) going and dispassion, north (yellow) pleasure, north-east (lapis) charity; at the junctions, disease; at the centre, knowing all.
