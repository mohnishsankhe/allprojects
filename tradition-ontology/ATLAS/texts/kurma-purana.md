**Attribution:** tradition: Spoken by Viṣṇu as the Tortoise (Kūrma) to Indradyumna and the sages; the fifteenth Purāṇa, in four saṃhitās (Brāhmī, Bhāgavatī, Saurī, Vaiṣṇavī) of which the Brāhmī (6,000 verses) is this text (KūP 1.1.21-23).; scholarly: A Pāśupata-Śaiva redaction of an originally Vaiṣṇava Purāṇa; teaches the identity of Śiva and Viṣṇu.; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Vyāsa, end of Dvāpara; Scholarly account: c. 6th-8th c. CE (Pāśupata redaction); (confidence low)
**Structure:** Pūrvabhāga c. 51-53 chapters, Uparibhāga c. 44-46 chapters; 17,000 verses by BhP 12.13.8, 6,000 in the Brāhmī saṃhitā by its own count
**Availability:** digitized-original

A Purāṇa of Śiva-Viṣṇu identity: creation and cosmography, the Pāśupata way, the Īśvara Gītā on yoga and knowledge, the Vyāsa Gītā on dharma, tīrthas and the rites for the dead; it lists the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas and eighteen Upapurāṇas.
