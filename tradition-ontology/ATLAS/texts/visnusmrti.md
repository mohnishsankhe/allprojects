# Viṣṇusmṛti (Vaiṣṇava Dharmaśāstra)

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic

**Genre:** smṛti

A Dharmaśāstra framed as Viṣṇu's teaching to the Earth goddess, in mixed prose and verse (100 chapters), covering the classes and orders, law, impurity, śrāddha and holy places for it, vows and fasts, expiation, the duties of women (including the widow's choice of chastity or ascending the pyre, 25.14), and devotion and meditation on Vāsudeva.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Vaijayantī of Nandapaṇḍita](vaijayanti.md)

## Teachings (1: sourced 1)

### 25.14 <a id="tea-visnusmrti-25-14"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

When her husband has died, (a woman should practise) chastity, or ascend (the pyre) after him.
