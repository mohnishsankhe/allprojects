Śaunaka's metrical treatise on the deities of the Ṛgveda hymns, hymn by hymn, with the legends (itihāsa) behind them; it lists the women seers (brahmavādinī) of the Ṛgveda and discusses how the one Ātman is praised under many deities.

## Teachings (1: sourced 1)

### 2.82-84 <a id="tea-brhaddevata-2-82-84"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

The women who spoke brahman (brahmavādinī) in the Ṛgveda are named: Ghoṣā, Godhā, Viśvavārā, Apālā, Upaniṣad, Niṣad, Juhū Brahmajāyā, Agastya's sister, Aditi; Indrāṇī and Indra's mother, Saramā, Romaśā, Urvaśī, Lopāmudrā, the Rivers, Yamī, the wife (Śaśvatī); Śrī, Lākṣā, Sārparājñī, Vāc, Śraddhā, Medhā, Dakṣiṇā, Rātrī and Sūryā Sāvitrī.
