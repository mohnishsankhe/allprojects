## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 (summary) <a id="tea-annapurna-upanisad-1-summary"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Nidāgha asks Ṛbhu how he attained the state; Ṛbhu tells of his worship of Annapūrṇā and her mantra, and of the five delusions (difference, doership, attachment, reality of the world, separateness) she removed.
