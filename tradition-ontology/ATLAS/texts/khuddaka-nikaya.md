**Availability:** digitized-original

The 'minor collection', a miscellany of verse and prose books ranging from the oldest strata (Aṭṭhaka and Pārāyana vaggas of the Sutta Nipāta, parts of the Dhammapada, Udāna, Itivuttaka, Thera- and Therīgāthā) to late works (Apadāna, Buddhavaṃsa, Cariyāpiṭaka, Niddesa, Paṭisambhidāmagga) and, in the Burmese canon, the Nettippakaraṇa, Peṭakopadesa and Milindapañha.
