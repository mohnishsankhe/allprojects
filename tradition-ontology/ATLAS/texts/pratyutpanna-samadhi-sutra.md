The sūtra of the samādhi in which the buddhas of the present stand before one: by recollecting Amitābha single-mindedly for seven days and nights the practitioner sees him (and all buddhas) face to face, as in a dream; the buddhas seen are understood as made by mind, neither coming nor going. One of the earliest Mahāyāna sūtras in Chinese.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3 <a id="tea-pratyutpanna-samadhi-sutra-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Recollecting Amitābha single-mindedly for seven days and nights, the practitioner sees him face to face, in samādhi or in a dream; then he understands: the buddha did not come and I did not go; this triple world is made by mind; what I think, I see; mind makes the buddha, mind sees itself.
