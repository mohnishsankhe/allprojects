## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-buddhapalita-vrtti-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Things do not arise from themselves, because their arising would be pointless and because of the fault of absurd consequence: for things already existing in their own being there is no purpose in arising again, and if what exists were to arise, it would never cease arising.

> na svata utpadyante bhāvāḥ, tadutpādavaiyarthyāt, atiprasaṅgadoṣācca | na hi svātmanā vidyamānānāṃ padārthānāṃ punarutpāde prayojanamasti | atha sannapi jāyeta, na kadācinna jāyeta iti ||
