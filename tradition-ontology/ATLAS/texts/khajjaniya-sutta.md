## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 7-14 <a id="tea-khajjaniya-sutta-7-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The noble disciple reflects: I am now being devoured by form, as I was in the past and would be in the future if I delighted in it; he becomes indifferent to past and future form and practises for disenchantment with present form. He is one who dismantles and does not build up, abandons and does not cling, scatters and does not amass, extinguishes and does not kindle.
