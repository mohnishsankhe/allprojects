## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-11 <a id="tea-kimatthiya-sutta-2-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The purpose and benefit of wholesome virtue is non-remorse; of non-remorse, gladness; of gladness, rapture; of rapture, tranquillity; of tranquillity, happiness; of happiness, concentration; of concentration, knowledge and vision of things as they are; of that, disenchantment and dispassion; of these, the knowledge and vision of liberation. So wholesome virtues gradually lead to the highest.
