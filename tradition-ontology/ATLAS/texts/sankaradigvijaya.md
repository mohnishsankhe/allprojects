The most widely read hagiography of Śaṅkara (16 cantos): birth at Kālaṭi, renunciation, discipleship under Govinda, the meeting with Vyāsa, Kumārila on the pyre, the debate with Maṇḍana judged by Ubhaya Bhāratī, the disciples, the conquest of the quarters and the Sarvajña-pīṭha, and his departure.

### 5 <a id="tea-sankaradigvijaya-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The tradition's account: as a boy Śaṅkara, seized by a crocodile, obtained his mother's permission to renounce, promised to return at her death, and went north to Govinda on the Narmadā, who initiated him.

### 8-10 <a id="tea-sankaradigvijaya-8-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The tradition's account: at Māhiṣmatī Śaṅkara debated Maṇḍana Miśra, champion of Vedic ritual, on whether knowledge or works liberates, with Ubhaya Bhāratī as judge; she declared Śaṅkara the victor when Maṇḍana's garland faded first, and Maṇḍana became Śaṅkara's disciple Sureśvara.

_level: conventional · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge, action · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, dispute_

teachers: [Śaṅkara (Ādi Śaṅkarācārya)](../teachers/sankara.md), [Maṇḍana Miśra](../teachers/mandana-misra.md), [Ubhaya Bhāratī](../teachers/ubhaya-bharati.md) · disputes: [Is liberation won by ritual works, by knowledge alone, by knowledge combined with works, by devotion, or by the Lord's grace — and does grace wait on the soul's surrender (the 'monkey') or carry it without cause (the 'cat')?](../debates/works-knowledge-grace.md), [Can liberating knowledge be combined with ritual action (jñāna-karma-samuccaya), or does knowledge alone liberate?](../debates/jnana-karma-samuccaya.md)

### 11 <a id="tea-sankaradigvijaya-11"></a>

At Śrīśaila the Kāpālika Ugrabhairava asks Śaṅkara for his head as an offering to win Bhairava's favour; Śaṅkara consents, but his disciple Padmapāda, possessed by Narasiṃha, kills the Kāpālika.
