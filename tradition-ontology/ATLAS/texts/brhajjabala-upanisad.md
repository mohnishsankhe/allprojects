Bhusuṇḍa asks Kālāgnirudra about sacred ash: its origin from Sadāśiva's five faces, its five names (vibhūti, bhasita, bhasma, kṣāra, rakṣā) and their meanings, preparation, ash-bath and tripuṇḍra, and the merit and liberation it confers.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-brhajjabala-upanisad-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Bhusuṇḍa asks Kālāgnirudra the greatness of ash: from Sadāśiva's five faces arise five elements, five kalās and five cows whose dung makes the five kinds of ash — vibhūti, bhasita, bhasma, kṣāra and rakṣā.

### 3-8 (summary) <a id="tea-brhajjabala-upanisad-3-8-summary"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Ash burns sins, gives protection and prosperity; bathing in ash and wearing the tripuṇḍra lead to union with Śiva.
