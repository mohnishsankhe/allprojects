The 'Basket Array': the compassion and saving deeds of Avalokiteśvara, who descends into the hells and the realms of hungry ghosts, and whose body contains worlds in its pores; it first reveals the six-syllable great vidyā oṃ maṇipadme hūṃ as his heart, obtained with difficulty and bestowing liberation.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### p291 <a id="tea-karandavyuha-p291"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Hard to obtain is even the name of Avalokiteśvara: those who recollect his six-syllable vidyā are born in his pores and no longer wander in saṃsāra.

### p297 <a id="tea-karandavyuha-p297"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The six-syllable great vidyā, oṃ maṇipadme hūṃ, is Avalokiteśvara's innermost heart, hard to obtain; whoever knows it is freed; even the buddhas sought it.

> oṃ maṇipadme hūṃ
