# Kulānandatantra

  - [Matsyendranātha](../teachers/matsyendranatha.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Structure:** short tract (Bagchi 1934, pp. 107–113)
**Availability:** digitized-original

A brief Bhairava–Devī dialogue on centres in the body (the sixty-four-petalled lotus in the brahma-place and others) and the quickly convincing signs of Kaula practice: bewitching, piercing, shaking, trembling, moving in the sky, samarasa and the removal of wrinkles and grey hair.
