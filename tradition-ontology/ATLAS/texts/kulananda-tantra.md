A brief Bhairava–Devī dialogue on centres in the body (the sixty-four-petalled lotus in the brahma-place and others) and the quickly convincing signs of Kaula practice: bewitching, piercing, shaking, trembling, moving in the sky, samarasa and the removal of wrinkles and grey hair.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 4 <a id="tea-kulananda-tantra-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Goddess asks for the quickly convincing signs of practice within the body: binding by fascination, piercing, shaking, trembling, moving in the sky, samarasa and the destruction of wrinkles and grey hair.

> पाशस्तोभञ्च वेध(न)ञ्च धूननं कम्पनं तथा । खेचरं समरसञ्चैव बलीपलितनाशनम् ॥ सर्वं तत्तु सुरेश्वर कथयस्व मम प्रभो

### 6-7 <a id="tea-kulananda-tantra-6-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Restraining the mind in the sixty-four-petalled lotus at the brahma-place, the one under the vow should observe the flames of a lamp; he sees in the whole body with divine sight, and by that mere observation the highest success arises.

> ब्रह्मस्थाने यत् कमलं चतुःषष्टिदलान्वितम् । तत्रैव मनसा रोध्य लक्षयेद्दीपशिखान् व्रती पश्यति सर्वदेहे तु दिव्यदृष्टिर्वरानने । तेन लक्षितमात्रेण जायते सिद्धिरुत्तमा
