The teaching on the conventional and ultimate truths, spoken to Mañjuśrī: the ultimate is beyond all expression, even that of the Buddha.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-samvrtiparamarthasatyanirdesa-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The ultimate truth is beyond all activity of speech and thought — so much so that the Tathāgata's own teaching of it is conventional; it is known by not seeing.
