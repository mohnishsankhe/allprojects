## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 8-21 <a id="tea-mahakammavibhanga-sutta-8-21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Four persons: one who kills … and is reborn in hell; one who kills and is reborn in heaven; one who abstains and is reborn in heaven; one who abstains and is reborn in hell. Those with the divine eye who see only part generalise wrongly; the Tathāgata knows that a rebirth may follow an earlier deed, a later deed, or the view taken at the time of death, and that deeds are incapable and capable, seemingly capable and seemingly incapable.
