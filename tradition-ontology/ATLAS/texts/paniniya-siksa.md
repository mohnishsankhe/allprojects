# Pāṇinīya Śikṣā

**Availability:** digitized-original

The general phonetic manual ascribed to Pāṇini: places and efforts of articulation, the Vedic accents and the conduct of recitation — naming six worst and six best reciters (32–33) and warning that a mantra wrong in accent or letter becomes a verbal thunderbolt that harms the sacrificer, as 'Indra-śatru' did through a fault of accent (52).
