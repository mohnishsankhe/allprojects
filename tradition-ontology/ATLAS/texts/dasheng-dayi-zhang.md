## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### pratyutpanna-question <a id="tea-dasheng-dayi-zhang-pratyutpanna-question"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Huiyuan asks Kumārajīva whether the buddha seen in the pratyutpanna samādhi — said by the sūtra to be like things seen in a dream — is produced by one's own mind or is a real buddha coming from outside; if made by the mind, how can it answer questions and resolve doubts? Kumārajīva answers that the sūtra's dream simile shows only that one sees as clearly as in a dream, not that the vision is false; the samādhi is attained by the power of the Buddha, of the samādhi and of one's own merit.
