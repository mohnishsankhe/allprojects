## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2 <a id="tea-dhyanasataka-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

A steady state of mind is meditation (dhyāna); a moving one is thought (citta), which is either cultivation, reflection or reasoning.
