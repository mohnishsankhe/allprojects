## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.54 <a id="tea-pramanaviniscaya-1-54"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Because blue and the cognition of blue are necessarily apprehended together, they are not different; their difference is seen by deluded cognitions, as a double moon is seen in the single moon.
