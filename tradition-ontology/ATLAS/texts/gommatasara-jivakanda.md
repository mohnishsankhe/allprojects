## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 9-10 <a id="tea-gommatasara-jivakanda-9-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The fourteen stages of quality are: wrong view, the tasting (sāsādana), the mixed, unrestrained right view, partial restraint, careless restraint, careful restraint, the unprecedented (apūrvakaraṇa), the non-returning (anivṛtti), subtle passion, subsided delusion, destroyed delusion, the omniscient with activity and without.

### 507-508 <a id="tea-gommatasara-jivakanda-507-508"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Six travellers lost in a forest see a tree full of fruit: one thinks to uproot it, one to cut the trunk, one the branches, one the twigs, one to pluck the fruit, and one to gather what has fallen — so the six soul-colourings, from black to white, show in thought, word and deed.
