**Structure:** preface (pāyiram) + 9 tantras, c. 3,047 verses; the tradition links the nine tantras to nine Āgamas
**Availability:** digitized-original

Tirumūlar's c. 3,000 verses in nine sections (tantras) and a preface: ethics, impermanence and the guru; Śiva's greatness, his five acts and the classes of souls; eightfold yoga and its powers; mantras, cakras and the Goddess; the Śaiva schools and the four paths; the guru and grace; the centres, liṅga and the realized; the states of the soul and the tattvas; and the Dance, the five letters and the experience of Śiva. It declares 'love is Śiva' and 'one family, one God', and joins Vedānta and Siddhānta.

### tantra.1 <a id="tea-tirumantiram-tantra-1"></a>
