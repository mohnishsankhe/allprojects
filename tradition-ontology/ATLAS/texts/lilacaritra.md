The biography of Cakradhar Svāmī in some fifteen hundred short episodes (līḷās), gathered by Mhāimbhaṭ from the disciples' memories after Cakradhar's departure: his acts, conversations, teachings and parables. Revered as scripture by the Mahānubhāvas and counted the earliest prose work in Marathi.

### Pūrvārdha <a id="tea-lilacaritra-govindaprabhu-grace"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

At Ṛddhipur Cakradhar receives the grace (śakti) of Govindaprabhu, which the Mahānubhāvas read as the passing of the Supreme's power from one incarnation to the next.

### Pūrvārdha, opening episodes <a id="tea-lilacaritra-haripala"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Prince Haripāla of Bharuch fell dead; Cāṅgadeva Rāüḷ, laying down his own body at Dvārakā, entered Haripāla's, who rose and, turning from royal life, wandered away and became Cakradhar.

### Uttarārdha, closing episodes <a id="tea-lilacaritra-departure"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Threatened by the Yādava court — by Mahānubhāva account seized and beheaded, yet living — Cakradhar departed northward, charging Nāgadeva with the disciples and assuring them of his continued presence in the teaching.
