### Āgamasāra, on the hidden abode within the body <a id="tea-agamasara-sahaja-pura-within"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The eternal Vṛndāvana is within the body: its lakes and lotuses are there, and the divine couple sport there; the outer Vṛndāvana is its image.
