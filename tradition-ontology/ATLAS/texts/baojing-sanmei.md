## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 515a17 <a id="tea-baojing-sanmei-515a17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Precious Mirror Samādhi: this Dharma is intimately entrusted by buddhas and patriarchs; now you have it, keep it well — a silver bowl filled with snow, a heron hidden in the moonlight; like facing a precious mirror, form and reflection behold each other: 'you are not it, it is just you'; the hexagram of double fire, the real and apparent interchanging, folds into three and turns into five; practise hiddenly and intimately, like a fool, like an idiot — to continue this is called the host within the host.

> 如是之法，佛祖密付，汝今得之，宜善保護。銀盌盛雪，明月藏鷺
