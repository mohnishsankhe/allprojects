# Āvaśyaka Sūtra

**Language:** Ardhamāgadhī Prakrit
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md), [Sthānakavāsī](../lineages/sthanakavasi.md), [Śvetāmbara Terāpanth](../lineages/terapanthi.md)
**Genre:** mūlasūtra (liturgy)
**Part of:** [Śvetāmbara Āgamas (the Jain canon)](svetambara-agamas.md)
**Location in parent:** Mūlasūtra (32nd text for Sthānakavāsīs)
**Structure:** 6 chapters (the six āvaśyakas)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The Mūlasūtra of the six obligatory daily duties (āvaśyaka): equanimity (sāmāyika), praise of the twenty-four Jinas (caturviṃśatistava, the 'Logassa'), veneration of the teacher (vandana), confession and repentance (pratikramaṇa), abandoning the body in standing meditation (kāyotsarga), and renunciation of specified things for a set time (pratyākhyāna). Its formulas, beginning with the Namaskāra, are the core of Jain liturgy; its niryukti and commentaries carry much narrative.
