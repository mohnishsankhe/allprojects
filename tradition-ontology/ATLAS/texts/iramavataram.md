Kampaṉ's Tamil Rāmāyaṇa in six kāṇṭams, a devotional retelling of Rāma's story that opens by taking refuge in the Lord who creates, sustains and dissolves the worlds in play; by tradition first recited (araṅkēṟṟam) before Raṅganātha at Śrīraṅgam.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### invocation-1 <a id="tea-iramavataram-invocation-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

We take refuge in him — the Lord who, in endless play, creates, sustains and removes all the worlds that are, and to whom alone we belong.
