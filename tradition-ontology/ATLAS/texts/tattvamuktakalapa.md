Deśika's systematic poem of about 500 verses in five sections (sara): insentient substance, the soul, the Lord (nāyaka), cognition (buddhi) and non-substances (adravya), refuting rival schools; with his own commentary Sarvārthasiddhi.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 2 <a id="tea-tattvamuktakalapa-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Jīva section: the soul is eternal, atomic, self-luminous as 'I', a knower having attributive consciousness that contracts and expands, many in number; it is not the body, senses or mind.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind_

concepts: [The individual living self (jīva)](../concepts/jiva.md), [Attributive consciousness (dharmabhūta-jñāna)](../concepts/dharmabhuta-jnana.md) · teachers: [Vedānta Deśika](../teachers/vedanta-desika.md)

### 3 <a id="tea-tattvamuktakalapa-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Nāyaka section: the Lord, qualified by souls and matter, is the one cause of the world, with infinite auspicious qualities; the rival accounts of God (Advaita's, Nyāya's) are refuted.
