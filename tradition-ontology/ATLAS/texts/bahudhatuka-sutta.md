## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 12-19 <a id="tea-bahudhatuka-sutta-12-19"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

It is impossible that one accomplished in view should treat any formation as permanent, pleasant or self, kill his mother, father or an arahant, shed a Tathāgata's blood or split the Saṅgha, or acknowledge another teacher; impossible that two Buddhas arise in one world-system at once; impossible that a woman should be an arahant fully awakened Buddha, a wheel-turning monarch, Sakka, Māra or Brahmā; impossible that bad conduct should ripen as pleasant.
