## Teachings (2: sourced 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-dattatreya-upanisad-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Brahmā asks Nārāyaṇa for the tāraka; Nārāyaṇa: 'I, Dattātreya, have been given (datta)'; meditating on Dattātreya one is freed.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: ultimate, sound-language_

teachers: [Nārāyaṇa](../teachers/narayana.md), [Dattātreya](../teachers/dattatreya.md)

### 2-3 (summary) <a id="tea-dattatreya-upanisad-2-3-summary"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

His seed-syllable and mantras of various lengths are given with limbs and fruits.
