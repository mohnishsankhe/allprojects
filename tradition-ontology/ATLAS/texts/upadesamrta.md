# Upadeśāmṛta

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)

**Structure:** 11 verses

Rūpa's eleven verses of practical instruction: control of the six urges, six things that destroy and six that nourish devotion, six loving exchanges among devotees, how to honour three kinds of Vaiṣṇavas, the essence of practice (remembering the name, form and play while living in Vraja under those who love him), and the pre-eminence of Rādhākuṇḍa.

## Teachings (7: skeleton 7)

### 1 <a id="tea-upadesamrta-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A steady person who can tolerate the urges of speech, of the mind, of anger, of the tongue, of the belly and of the genitals can make disciples of the whole world.

> vāco vegaṃ manasaḥ krodha-vegaṃ jihvā-vegam udaropastha-vegam | etān vegān yo viṣaheta dhīraḥ sarvām apīmāṃ pṛthivīṃ sa śiṣyāt ||

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: all · types: ethics, teacher-transmission_

teachers: [Rūpa Gosvāmī](../teachers/rupa-gosvami.md)

### 2 <a id="tea-upadesamrta-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Devotion is destroyed by six things: overeating (or over-collecting), over-endeavour, idle talk, rigid attachment to rules (or neglect of them), worldly company and greed/fickleness.

> atyāhāraḥ prayāsaś ca prajalpo niyamāgrahaḥ | jana-saṅgaś ca laulyaṃ ca ṣaḍbhir bhaktir vinaśyati ||

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: devotion · stage: beginner · types: ethics, practice_

obstacles: [Six things that destroy devotion](../obstacles/six-spoilers-of-bhakti.md) · teachers: [Rūpa Gosvāmī](../teachers/rupa-gosvami.md)

### 3 <a id="tea-upadesamrta-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Devotion succeeds through six things: enthusiasm, conviction, patience, performing the prescribed activities, giving up bad company and following the conduct of the saints.

> utsāhān niścayād dhairyāt tat-tat-karma-pravartanāt | saṅga-tyāgāt sato vṛtteḥ ṣaḍbhir bhaktiḥ prasidhyati ||

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: devotion · stage: beginner · types: ethics, practice_

teachers: [Rūpa Gosvāmī](../teachers/rupa-gosvami.md)

### 4 <a id="tea-upadesamrta-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Giving and receiving gifts, revealing and inquiring about confidential matters, eating and feeding — these six are the signs of love among devotees.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ethics_

teachers: [Rūpa Gosvāmī](../teachers/rupa-gosvami.md)

### 5 <a id="tea-upadesamrta-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

One who utters the name 'Kṛṣṇa' is to be honoured in the mind; one who has taken initiation and worships, with obeisances; one who is advanced in exclusive worship and free of fault-finding, with service and association.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ethics, teacher-transmission_

### 8 <a id="tea-upadesamrta-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The essence of all instruction: engaging the tongue and mind in turn in singing and remembering his name, form, qualities and play, one should pass all one's time living in Vraja, following those who are attached to him.

> tan-nāma-rūpa-caritādi-sukīrtanānusmṛtyoḥ krameṇa rasanā-manasī niyojya | tiṣṭhan vraje tad-anurāgi-janānugāmī kālaṃ nayed akhilam ity upadeśa-sāram ||

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: devotion, sound, meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice_

### 9-11 <a id="tea-upadesamrta-9-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Above Vaikuṇṭha is Mathurā, above it Vṛndāvana, above it Govardhana, and above all Rādhākuṇḍa, the pond as dear to Kṛṣṇa as Rādhā herself; bathing there awakens the highest love.
