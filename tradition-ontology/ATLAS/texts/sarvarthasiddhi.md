Pūjyapāda Devanandin's commentary on the Tattvārthasūtra, which fixes the Digambara recension of the sūtras; it expounds the fourteen guṇasthānas under 1.8, argues that sallekhanā is not self-killing (7.22) and reads 9.11 so that the Jina is free of hunger and thirst.

### 1.1 <a id="tea-sarvarthasiddhi-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Right view and right knowledge arise together, as the light and heat of a lamp arise together; right conduct may follow after.

### 1.8 <a id="tea-sarvarthasiddhi-1-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In explaining the eight doors of inquiry, the commentary names the fourteen stages of quality (guṇasthāna) — from wrong view to the omniscient without activity — and examines each soul-type through them.

### 7.22 <a id="tea-sarvarthasiddhi-7-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

Sallekhanā is not self-killing: it is undertaken free of attachment when death has become inevitable, as a merchant who does not wish his house destroyed still saves his goods when it is burning; the one who kills himself does so from passion.

### 8.1 <a id="tea-sarvarthasiddhi-8-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Wrong faith taught by others is of four kinds — the doctrines of action, of non-action, of ignorance and of discipline — or, again, of five kinds: one-sided (ekānta), perverse (viparīta), doubting (saṃśaya), discipline-alone (vainayika) and ignorant (ajñāna).

### 9.11 <a id="tea-sarvarthasiddhi-9-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The eleven hardships are said of the Jina only figuratively: feeling karma, without the support of delusion, cannot produce hunger and the rest in the omniscient.

### 10.9 <a id="tea-sarvarthasiddhi-10-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

On the 'sign' (liṅga) of the liberated: with respect to physical sex (dravya-veda) liberation is only from the male; with respect to psychological sex (bhāva-veda) it may be from any of the three; with respect to garb, liberation is only in the state without possessions (nirgrantha-liṅga), though the past garb may be considered either way.

### mangala <a id="tea-sarvarthasiddhi-mangala"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

I bow to the leader on the path of liberation, the breaker of the mountains of karma, the knower of all realities, in order to attain those very qualities.
