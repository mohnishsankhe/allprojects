## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 381b02 <a id="tea-jingtu-sheng-wusheng-lun-381b02"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Chuandeng sets out ten gates (one true dharma-realm; the arising of body and land; mind and land identical; beings and buddhas not two; ...). The first verse: within the one true dharma-nature the ten dharma-realms are complete; the environment and the person are fundamentally interfused; beings and buddhas do not differ. The one true dharma-realm is beings' inherent mind-nature, endowed with countless virtues.

> 一真法性中，具足十法界，依正本融通，生佛非殊致。
