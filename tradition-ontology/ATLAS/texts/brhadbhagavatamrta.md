## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-brhadbhagavatamrta-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Nārada searches for the greatest recipient of Kṛṣṇa's grace — from a king to Indra, Brahmā, Śiva, Prahlāda, Hanumān, the Pāṇḍavas, the Yādavas and Uddhava — and is led to the gopīs of Vraja, whose love surpasses all.

### 2 <a id="tea-brhadbhagavatamrta-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The cowherd Gopakumāra, chanting his guru's mantra, rises through the worlds, liberation in Brahman and Vaikuṇṭha, to Goloka, where Kṛṣṇa's intimate play is attained through spontaneous love.
