### abhaṅga 'rūpa pāhatāṃ locanīṃ' <a id="tea-jnanesvar-gatha-rupa-pahata-locani"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Seeing the form with my eyes, O friend, happiness arose: this is the lovely Viṭṭhala, the lovely Mādhava — my heart is fixed on him.
