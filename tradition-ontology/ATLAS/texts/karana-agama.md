# Kāraṇāgama

`src:karana-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Kāraṇa, Karaṇāgama (Muktabodha spelling)
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Kāraṇa, then Śarva(rudra), then Prajāpati (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Śivabheda no. 4 in the Kāmika's order; 7 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** digitized-original

No. 4 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Śivabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Kāraṇa, then Śarva(rudra), then Prajāpati. The Kāmika assigns it 7 upabheda(s): Kāraṇa, Pāvana, Daurga, Māhendra, Bhīma, Māraṇa, Dveṣṭa (reading uncertain). The surviving South Indian Kāraṇa (Pūrva and Uttara parts) is a major authority for Tamil temple ritual.
**Editions / translations:**

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.4/10.8: Kāraṇa → Śarva → Prajāpati. In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the shanks (pūrva 1.93–101).
