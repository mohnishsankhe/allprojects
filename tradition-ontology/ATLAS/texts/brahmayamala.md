# Brahmayāmala (Picumata)

`src:brahmayamala` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

**Alternate titles:** Picumata, Brahmayāmalatantra
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Vidyāpīṭha (the Seat of Vidyās)](../lineages/vidyapitha.md)
**Genre:** tantra (Vidyāpīṭha, Yāmala)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: Nepalese manuscript dated 1052 CE; text perhaps 7th–8th c.; (confidence low)
**Structure:** about 12,000 verses in some 100 paṭalas (approximate; not verified)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The chief early Yāmala tantra of the Vidyāpīṭha, teaching the cult of Kapālīśabhairava and Caṇḍā Kāpālinī with yoginīs, Kāpālika observances and powers; first in the Śrīkaṇṭhīya's Yāmala group, and the source ('Picu') of the Maṅgala group's Bhairavī.
**Editions / translations:**

  - kind: original; name: Cs. Kiss, The Brahmayāmalatantra or Picumata, vol. II, IFP/EFEO/Hamburg 2015
  - kind: original; name: Muktabodha e-text M00319

_Notes: Contains Kāpālika and sexual rites; summarised only._
