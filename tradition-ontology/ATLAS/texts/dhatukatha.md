## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-dhatukatha-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The method of the Discourse on Elements: inclusion and non-inclusion, included with not-included, not-included with included, included with included, not-included with not-included, association and dissociation.

> Saṅgaho asaṅgaho Saṅgahitena asaṅgahitaṁ Asaṅgahitena saṅgahitaṁ Saṅgahitena saṅgahitaṁ Asaṅgahitena asaṅgahitaṁ Sampayogo vippayogo
