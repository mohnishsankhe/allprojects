**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)

**Attribution:** tradition: Its colophon ascribes it to Śaṅkara Bhagavat, pupil of Govinda Bhagavatpāda.; scholarly: The ascription to (Ādi) Śaṅkara is doubted; the opening salutation to a sage 'who teaches the supramundane' (lokottaravādin) has been read as pointing to another author.; confidence: disputed
**Dates:** Scholarly account: uncertain (perhaps 7th–9th c. CE); (confidence low)
**Availability:** digitized-original

A commentary on the Sāṃkhyasaptati: liberation comes from knowing the twenty-five principles; the Saptati was written because the Ṣaṣṭitantra is too extensive; it quotes the verse that the knower of the twenty-five principles is liberated in whatever stage of life.
