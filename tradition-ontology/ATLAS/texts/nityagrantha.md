## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-nityagrantha-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Nityagrantha lays down the daily worship of the Lord at home for the surrendered: purifications, meditation on the teacher lineage, worship of the Lord's image with offerings, recitation and prostration, all done as service without desire for fruit.
