## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 5 <a id="tea-dipavamsa-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Vajjiputtakas defeated at the Second Council held another council and formed the Mahāsaṅgītika, altering the texts; from them came the Gokulika, Ekabyohārika, Paññatti, Bahussutaka and Cetiya; from the Theravāda came the Mahiṃsāsaka and Vajjiputtaka, from these the Dhammuttariya, Bhaddayānika, Chandāgārika, Sammitiya, Sabbatthavāda, Dhammaguttika, Kassapiya, Saṅkantika and Suttavāda — eighteen with the Theravāda; later six more arose.
