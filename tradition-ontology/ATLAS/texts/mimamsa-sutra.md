Jaimini's aphorisms on dharma known from Vedic injunction, in twelve adhyāyas (sixty pādas): (1) means of knowing dharma, (2) distinction of rites, (3) subsidiarity, (4) what prompts performance, (5) sequence, (6) eligibility, (7–8) general and specific transfer, (9) modification, (10) cancellation, (11) single performance for several, (12) incidental benefit. Its first pāda (the tarkapāda) grounds the authority of the authorless, eternal Veda.

### 1.1.5 <a id="tea-mimamsa-sutra-1-1-5"></a>

### 1.1.6-11 <a id="tea-mimamsa-sutra-1-1-6-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Prima facie view: some hold that the word is a product (karma), because it is seen to arise from effort; because it does not persist; because one speaks of 'making' a sound; because it is heard simultaneously in different places; because it undergoes modification; and because it grows louder with more speakers.

### 1.1.12-17 <a id="tea-mimamsa-sutra-1-1-12-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Reply: the seeing (of arising and ceasing) applies equally to manifestation; the non-perception after utterance is because the object (the manifesting cause) is no longer present; 'making' means employing; simultaneity is like that of the sun seen in many places; the changed form is another letter, not a modification; the increase belongs to the sound (nāda), not to the word.
