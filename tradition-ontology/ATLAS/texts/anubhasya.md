Vallabha's commentary on the Brahma Sūtras establishing Śuddhādvaita: Brahman is the material and efficient cause and becomes the world without change (avikṛta-pariṇāma); it is the locus of contradictory attributes. Vallabha's own text breaks off in the third adhyāya and was completed by his son Viṭṭhalanātha.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Bhāṣyaprakāśa](bhasyaprakasa.md)

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1.4.26 <a id="tea-anubhasya-1-4-26"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Brahman, by transforming itself, is the material cause of the world; this transformation involves no change in Brahman (avikṛta-pariṇāma), as gold remains gold in its ornaments.

### 3.2 <a id="tea-anubhasya-3-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Brahman is the locus of contradictory attributes (viruddha-dharmāśraya): scripture's statements that it is with and without qualities, near and far, moving and unmoving are all true of it, and are not to be divided into higher and lower truths.
