Hakuin's vernacular hymn: all beings are originally buddhas, like water and ice; not knowing it is near they seek it far away; the Mahāyāna zazen is beyond praise; this very place is the Lotus Land, this very body the Buddha.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### closing <a id="tea-zazen-wasan-closing"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Praising the Mahāyāna zazen, which includes all the perfections, recitation and repentance: if one turns inward and bears witness to one's own nature, which is no-nature, the gate of the oneness of cause and effect opens; this very place is the Lotus Land, this very body is the Buddha.

### opening <a id="tea-zazen-wasan-opening"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

All sentient beings are originally buddhas; as with water and ice, there is no ice apart from water and no buddhas apart from beings; not knowing it is near, beings seek it far away, like one in water crying from thirst.
