# Ṣaṭ Sandarbha (Bhāgavata Sandarbha)

`src:sat-sandarbha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)
**Authors:** 
  - [Jīva Gosvāmī](../teachers/jiva-gosvami.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
  - [Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī](../teachers/gopala-bhatta-gosvami.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Structure:** six sandarbhas divided into anucchedas
**Availability:** digitized-original

Jīva Gosvāmī's six 'compositions' systematising Gauḍīya theology from the Bhāgavata: four on the relation (sambandha: Tattva, Bhagavat, Paramātma, Kṛṣṇa), one on the means (abhidheya: Bhakti) and one on the goal (prayojana: Prīti). Jīva states that he rearranged and completed a draft by a southern Bhaṭṭa (Gopāla Bhaṭṭa), friend of Rūpa and Sanātana.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Sarvasaṃvādinī](sarvasamvadini.md)
