## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### deha-devali <a id="tea-pahuda-doha-deha-devali"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The god is not in the temple, not in stone, not in plaster or painting; the Jina dwells in the temple of the body — seek him there by knowing the self, not by pilgrimage and outer rites.
