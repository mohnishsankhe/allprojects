**Availability:** digitized-original

Pāṇini's grammar of Sanskrit in 'eight chapters': nearly four thousand ordered rules (sūtra) that derive every correct word of the spoken language (bhāṣā) and of the Veda (chandas) from roots, stems and affixes by means of technical terms, metarules and abbreviations (pratyāhāra) built on the fourteen Māheśvara sūtras. It defines, among much else, the agent as 'the independent one' (1.4.54), the three accents (1.2.29–31) and the three vowel quantities (1.2.27), and it names ten earlier grammarians.
