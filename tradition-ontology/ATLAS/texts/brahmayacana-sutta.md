## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-10 <a id="tea-brahmayacana-sutta-2-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Having awakened, the Buddha reflects that the Dhamma, going against the stream, is hard for those delighting in attachment to see, and inclines to inaction; Brahmā Sahampati appears and implores him: there are beings with little dust in their eyes who will understand. Surveying the world with the Buddha-eye he sees beings like lotuses and declares: 'Open are the doors to the deathless for those who have ears to hear'.
