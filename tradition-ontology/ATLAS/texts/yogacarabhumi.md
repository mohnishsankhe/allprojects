The encyclopaedic foundation of Yogācāra: an analysis of consciousness, the grounds of meditation and the paths of the three vehicles, in which the store-consciousness (ālaya-vijñāna) is first argued for (in the Viniścayasaṃgrahaṇī) and the bodhisattva's training, discipline and grounds are laid out.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 0.uddana <a id="tea-yogacarabhumi-0-uddana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Yogācārabhūmi is summed up in seventeen stages: that associated with the five sense-consciousnesses; the mental stage; those with initial and sustained thought, with sustained thought only, and with neither; the concentrated and the unconcentrated; with mind and without mind; those made of hearing, reflection and cultivation; the stages of the disciples, the solitary buddhas and the bodhisattvas; and those with and without remainder.
