Gampopa's graded-path treatise, organized under six headings: the cause (buddha-nature), the basis (the precious human life), the condition (the spiritual friend), the method (the spiritual friend's instructions — impermanence, the suffering of saṃsāra, karma, love and compassion, refuge, bodhicitta and its trainings, the six perfections, the five paths and ten grounds), the result (the kāyas of buddhahood) and the activity (the Buddha's non-conceptual benefit of beings).

## Teachings (17: skeleton 17)

### ch.1 <a id="tea-jewel-ornament-of-liberation-ch-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

All beings have buddha-nature, because the dharmakāya of the buddhas pervades them, because suchness is undifferentiated, and because the potential (gotra) is present (following the Ratnagotravibhāga). The potential is presented in five kinds — cut off, uncertain, śrāvaka, pratyekabuddha and Mahāyāna — and even the 'cut off' are not cut off forever.

### ch.2 <a id="tea-jewel-ornament-of-liberation-ch-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The precious human life is the working basis: it has the eight freedoms and ten endowments, is hard to obtain and easily lost; it must be joined with three kinds of faith (clear, longing, confident).

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: beginner · types: karma-liberation, ethics_

### ch.3 <a id="tea-jewel-ornament-of-liberation-ch-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Without a spiritual friend one cannot reach buddhahood, as a traveller needs a guide, an escort and a ferryman. The kind of spiritual friend matches the disciple's level: for a beginner, an ordinary person as spiritual friend; for one whose karmic obscurations are largely purified, a bodhisattva on the grounds; on the great path of accumulation, a nirmāṇakāya buddha; on the grounds, a saṃbhogakāya buddha.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: devotion, general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission_

### intro <a id="tea-jewel-ornament-of-liberation-intro"></a>
