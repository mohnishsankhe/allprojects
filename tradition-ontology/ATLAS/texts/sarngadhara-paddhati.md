A 14th-c. verse anthology whose yoga section is among the earliest datable witnesses to haṭha: it distinguishes two kinds of haṭha — one practised by Gorakṣa and others, another by the son of Mṛkaṇḍu (Mārkaṇḍeya) and others — and quotes rājayoga and haṭha verses shared with the Dattātreyayogaśāstra and Amaraughaprabodha.
