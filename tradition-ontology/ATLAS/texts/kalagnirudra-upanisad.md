# Kālāgnirudra Upaniṣad

`src:kalagnirudra-upanisad` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
**Genre:** upaniṣad
**Attribution:** tradition: Śruti: revealed, without human author (apauruṣeya); the Muktikā presents it as one of the 108 Upaniṣads that Rāma teaches Hanumān, each belonging to a Vedic śākhā; scholarly: anonymous; assigned to a Veda by the Muktikā list, not by any surviving śākhā transmission; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Śruti: eternal and authorless; no human date; Scholarly account: Sectarian Śaiva Upaniṣads are generally placed by scholars in the medieval period, with wide uncertainty; a few (e.g. Atharvaśiras, Kaivalya) are usually thought older than the rest.; (confidence low)
**Availability:** digitized-original

Sanatkumāra asks Kālāgnirudra about the tripuṇḍra: ash applied with the five brahma-mantras and Vedic verses; each of the three lines corresponds to a fire, a letter of Oṃ, a guṇa, a world, a self, a power, a Veda, a time of day and a deity; prescribed for all four āśramas; leads to union with Śiva.

## Teachings (3: sourced 3)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-kalagnirudra-upanisad-1-2"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Sanatkumāra asks about the tripuṇḍra; Kālāgnirudra: take ash with the five brahma-mantras, apply with 'fire is ash…', 'mā nas toke' and 'tryāyuṣam' in three lines across the forehead, chest and shoulders.

### 3 <a id="tea-kalagnirudra-upanisad-3"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Whoever wears the tripuṇḍra — student, householder, forest-dweller or renouncer — is purified of all sins and attains union with Śiva.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ritual · path: ritual · stage: all · types: practice, karma-liberation_

practices: [Tripuṇḍra (three lines of ash)](../practices/tripundra.md)

### 2 (three lines) <a id="tea-kalagnirudra-upanisad-2-three-lines"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

The first line is the gārhapatya fire, the letter a, rajas, earth, the outer self, the power of action, the Ṛgveda, the morning pressing and Maheśvara; the second the dakṣiṇa fire, u, sattva, mid-space, the inner self, the power of will, the Yajurveda, midday and Sadāśiva; the third the āhavanīya fire, m, tamas, sky, the supreme self, the power of knowledge, the Sāmaveda, the third pressing and Mahādeva.
