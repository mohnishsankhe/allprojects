## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-kalagnirudra-upanisad-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Sanatkumāra asks about the tripuṇḍra; Kālāgnirudra: take ash with the five brahma-mantras, apply with 'fire is ash…', 'mā nas toke' and 'tryāyuṣam' in three lines across the forehead, chest and shoulders.

### 3 <a id="tea-kalagnirudra-upanisad-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Whoever wears the tripuṇḍra — student, householder, forest-dweller or renouncer — is purified of all sins and attains union with Śiva.

### 2 (three lines) <a id="tea-kalagnirudra-upanisad-2-three-lines"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The first line is the gārhapatya fire, the letter a, rajas, earth, the outer self, the power of action, the Ṛgveda, the morning pressing and Maheśvara; the second the dakṣiṇa fire, u, sattva, mid-space, the inner self, the power of will, the Yajurveda, midday and Sadāśiva; the third the āhavanīya fire, m, tamas, sky, the supreme self, the power of knowledge, the Sāmaveda, the third pressing and Mahādeva.
