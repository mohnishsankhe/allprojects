Speech (Vāc) proclaims herself: she moves with the Rudras, Vasus, Ādityas and All-gods and bears Mitra-Varuṇa, Indra-Agni and the Aśvins; she is the queen, the gatherer of treasures, first of those worthy of worship, placed by the gods in many places; through her one eats, sees, breathes and hears, and those who do not know her dwell beside her; whom she loves she makes formidable, a brahman, a seer, a sage; she bends Rudra's bow; she gives birth to the father on the world's head; her womb is in the waters; she blows forth like the wind, beyond heaven and earth.

_Notes: The Sarvānukramaṇī gives the deity as 'ātman': the seer Vāc Āmbhṛṇī praises herself as the self of all. Recited in Śākta worship with the Devī Māhātmya._
