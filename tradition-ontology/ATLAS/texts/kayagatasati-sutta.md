## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 4-17 <a id="tea-kayagatasati-sutta-4-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Mindfulness of the body is developed through breathing, the postures, clear comprehension, reviewing the body from the soles up and the hair down as full of impurities (like a bag of mixed grains), reviewing the elements (like a butcher with the carcass divided), and comparing one's body with a corpse in the charnel ground; thereby memories and intentions of the household life are abandoned and the mind becomes steady and unified.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, body-breath · stage: all · types: practice, body-layers_

### 18-22 <a id="tea-kayagatasati-sutta-18-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The four jhānas as mindfulness of the body, with the similes of bath-powder, the spring-fed lake, the lotuses and the white cloth.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice, powers-experiences_

### 23-31 <a id="tea-kayagatasati-sutta-23-31"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Whoever has developed mindfulness of the body includes all wholesome states that partake of true knowledge, as one who fills the great ocean includes all rivers; Māra finds no opening in him, as a heavy stone ball is not dented by a ball of string.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice_

practices: [Mindfulness directed to the body (kāyagatāsati)](../practices/kayagatasati.md) · obstacles: [Māra](../obstacles/mara.md)

### 32-43 <a id="tea-kayagatasati-sutta-32-43"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The ten benefits of mindfulness of the body: one conquers discontent and delight, fear and dread, endures cold, heat and pain, attains the four jhānas at will, wields psychic powers, hears with the divine ear, knows others' minds, recollects past lives, sees with the divine eye, and realises the destruction of the effluents.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice, powers-experiences_
