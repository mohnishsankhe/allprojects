## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 379c18 <a id="tea-chuanxin-fayao-379c18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

All buddhas and sentient beings are only the one mind; there is no other dharma. This mind has since beginningless time never been born or perished; it is neither blue nor yellow, without form, beyond existence and non-existence, new and old, long and short; it is like space. This one mind is buddha; buddha and beings are not different — beings seek outside with forms and lose it; 'using the buddha to seek the buddha, using the mind to grasp the mind'.

> 諸佛與一切眾生。唯是一心。更無別法。此心無始已來。不曾生不曾滅

### 380a13 <a id="tea-chuanxin-fayao-380a13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In this one mind not a speck of dharma can be attained; this very mind is buddha. Making offerings to all buddhas of the ten directions is not as good as offering to one person of the Way without mind (wuxin daoren).

> 唯此一心更無微塵許法可得。即心是佛

### 380b02 <a id="tea-chuanxin-fayao-380b02"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Only be without mind (wuxin) and that is the ultimate; if students of the Way do not become without mind right now, they will not accomplish the Way however many kalpas they practise; some attain no-mind in a single thought on hearing the Dharma, others only after passing through the ten faiths, abodes, practices and dedications — the attainment is equal, the latter only laboured longer.

> 但能無心。便是究竟。學道人若不直下無心。累劫修行終不成道

### 381c05 <a id="tea-chuanxin-fayao-381c05"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

At the time of death one should only contemplate that the five aggregates are empty and the four elements without self; the true mind is formless, neither coming nor going; if auspicious signs of buddhas coming to welcome appear, do not follow them; if frightening signs appear, do not fear them; only forget the mind and be one with the dharma-realm.

> 凡人臨欲終時。但觀五蘊皆空四大無我。真心無相不去不來
