## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### Puṇḍalīka episode <a id="tea-panduranga-mahatmya-pundalika-brick"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Kṛṣṇa came to Puṇḍalīka while he was serving his aged parents; unwilling to break off his service, Puṇḍalīka tossed him a brick to stand on until he was free; pleased, the Lord stood there with hands on hips and remains as Viṭṭhala.
