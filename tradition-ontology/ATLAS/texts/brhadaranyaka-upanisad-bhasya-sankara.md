## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 2.3.6 <a id="tea-brhadaranyaka-upanisad-bhasya-sankara-2-3-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The double negation 'not so, not so' excludes every specification by adjuncts; Brahman cannot be described by name, form, action, distinction, genus or quality, and is indicated only by negation.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_

### 4.4.22 <a id="tea-brhadaranyaka-upanisad-bhasya-sankara-4-4-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The text's 'desiring this world (the self) alone, renunciants wander forth' enjoins renunciation; sacrifice, gifts and austerity produce the desire to know (vividiṣā), and the knower renounces all works.

### 5.1.1 <a id="tea-brhadaranyaka-upanisad-bhasya-sankara-5-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Against the view that knowledge must be combined with works and that the world is really both one and many (difference-in-identity), Śaṅkara argues that difference and non-difference cannot both be real of one thing.
