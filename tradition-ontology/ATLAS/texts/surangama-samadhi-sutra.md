The sūtra of the 'heroic-going' concentration, which only bodhisattvas of the tenth ground attain and which contains all other samādhis; it tells of Māra's conversion and predictions, of Mañjuśrī's past lives as a pratyekabuddha and a buddha, and of bodhisattvas who display nirvāṇa without abandoning beings.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### whole <a id="tea-surangama-samadhi-sutra-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Śūraṅgama ('heroic going') samādhi is attained only by bodhisattvas of the tenth ground; it contains all samādhis, and one abiding in it can display every kind of life — even entering nirvāṇa — without abandoning beings; hearing of it with faith is a cause of awakening.
