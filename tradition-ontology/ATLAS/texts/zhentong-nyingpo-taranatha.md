## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### three-natures <a id="tea-zhentong-nyingpo-taranatha-three-natures"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Of the three natures, the imputational (kun btags) does not exist even conventionally and is empty of its own nature; the other-powered (gzhan dbang), the dualistic mind of apprehended and apprehender, exists conventionally but is likewise self-empty; the thoroughly established (yongs grub) is the ultimate, empty of the other two. Thus the first two are self-empty and the last is other-empty.

### ultimate <a id="tea-zhentong-nyingpo-taranatha-ultimate"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The thoroughly established nature is self-aware, self-illuminating primordial wisdom free of the duality of apprehended and apprehender. It does not change, it is what is realized in meditative equipoise, and it is not empty of itself.
