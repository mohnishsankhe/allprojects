The second 'secret': how Mahālakṣmī, Mahākālī and Mahāsarasvatī are to be worshipped in their derived forms, with the eighteen-armed Mahālakṣmī at the centre.

_Notes: Recited as a limb (aṅga) of the Durgā Saptaśatī (Devī Māhātmya, U07); U23 records the Śākta liturgy._
