## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 5-7 <a id="tea-angulimala-sutta-5-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Buddha walks on while the bandit Aṅgulimāla, running with all his strength, cannot catch him; the Buddha explains: 'I have stopped, having laid aside violence towards all beings; you are unrestrained'; Aṅgulimāla throws away his weapons and goes forth.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: general · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, powers-experiences_

teachers: [Aṅgulimāla](../teachers/angulimala.md)

### 5.8-5.9 <a id="tea-angulimala-sutta-5-8-5-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Stop, ascetic!' 'I have stopped, Aṅgulimāla; you too stop.'

> “tiṭṭha, tiṭṭha, samaṇā”ti. “Ṭhito ahaṁ, aṅgulimāla, tvañca tiṭṭhā”ti.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: karma-liberation_

teachers: [Aṅgulimāla](../teachers/angulimala.md), [Gotama Buddha](../teachers/gotama-buddha.md)

### 15-16 <a id="tea-angulimala-sutta-15-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

At the Buddha's bidding Aṅgulimāla makes an act of truth for a woman in difficult labour: 'Since I was born with the noble birth I do not recall intentionally depriving a living being of life; by this truth may you and your child be well' — and she was well.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ritual · path: sound, devotion · stage: all · types: sound-language, powers-experiences_

terms: [saccakiriyā](../terms/saccakiriya.md) · practices: [Act of truth (saccakiriyā)](../practices/saccakiriya.md), [Protective recitation (paritta)](../practices/paritta.md) · teachers: [Aṅgulimāla](../teachers/angulimala.md)

### 17-18 <a id="tea-angulimala-sutta-17-18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Struck by clods and stones on alms round, Aṅgulimāla is told: bear it, brahmin; you are experiencing here and now the result of deeds for which you might have been tormented in hell for many years.

_level: conventional · standpoint: causal · path: action · stage: all · types: karma-liberation_
