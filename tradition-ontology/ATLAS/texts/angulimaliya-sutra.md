The Mahāyāna retelling of the converted murderer Aṅgulimāla, who teaches famous disciples that the tathāgatagarbha is the permanent self; it criticizes a nihilistic reading of emptiness and forbids meat.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-angulimaliya-sutra-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Aṅgulimāla, converted, instructs the great disciples: the one refuge is the Tathāgata; the self (ātman) is the tathāgatagarbha, permanent and not empty; those who teach that everything is empty without exception are like hail that destroys the crops.
