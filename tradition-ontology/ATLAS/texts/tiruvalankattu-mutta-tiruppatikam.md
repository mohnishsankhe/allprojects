## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.1-11 <a id="tea-tiruvalankattu-mutta-tiruppatikam-1-1-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Lord dances at Tiruvālaṅkāṭu in the cremation ground at night among ghouls and jackals, with fire in his hand, while the ghoul-poet (the singer herself) watches and sings.
