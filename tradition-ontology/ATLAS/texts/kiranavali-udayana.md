# Kiraṇāvalī (Udayana)

`src:kiranavali-udayana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md), [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
**Genre:** ṭīkā
**Commentary on:** [Padārthadharmasaṅgraha](padarthadharmasangraha.md)
**Authors:** 
  - [Udayana](../teachers/udayana.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
**Dates:** Scholarly account: late 10th – 11th c. CE; (confidence low)
**Availability:** unknown

Udayana's commentary on Praśastapāda's Padārthadharmasaṅgraha; it explains why absence is not listed among the categories there (because it is determined through its counterpositive, not because it is unreal) and gives the six impediments to a genuine universal (jātibādhaka).

_Notes: Sub-commentaries: Vardhamāna's Kiraṇāvalīprakāśa, Raghunātha's Dīdhiti on the Prakāśa._
