### Paltū Sāhib's verses against outer worship <a id="tea-paltu-sahib-bani-against-outer-worship"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Paltū mocks the one who bathes at pilgrim places and worships stones while his heart is full of deceit; the Lord is found by the one who turns within and hears the unstruck sound.
