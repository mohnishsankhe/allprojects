The earliest Chan manual of seated meditation: arouse compassion and vow, regulate food and sleep, sit in the full or half lotus with the body upright, let go of all involvements; 'when a thought arises, be aware of it; when you are aware of it, it vanishes'; meditation is the Dharma gate of ease and joy.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### awareness <a id="tea-zuochan-yi-awareness"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Sit with the body upright, neither leaning nor slumped, ears in line with shoulders, nose with navel; let go of all involvements; do not think of good or evil; when a thought arises, be aware of it; when aware of it, it vanishes; long practice forgets objects and one becomes unified; seated meditation is the Dharma gate of ease and joy.
