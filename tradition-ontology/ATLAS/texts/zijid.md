## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### nine-ways <a id="tea-zijid-nine-ways"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The nine ways of Bön: four causal ways — of prediction (phywa gshen), of the visible world (snang gshen), of magical power ('phrul gshen) and of existence (srid gshen) — and five fruitional ways — of lay followers (dge bsnyen), of ascetics (drang srong), of the white A (a dkar), of the primordial shen (ye gshen) and the unsurpassed way (bla med, Dzogchen).
