Advayavajra's 'Destruction of Wrong Views', defending ordinary Mahāyāna preliminary practice (ādikarma) even for those in the 'mad' observance.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### end <a id="tea-kudrstinirghatana-end"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Therefore the beginner's practice (ādikarma) — generosity to the point of the body, discipline, patience, effort, meditation and wisdom — goes on even for those established in the mad observance.

> tasmādunmattavratasthitānāmapyādikarma pravartata eva |

### tippani <a id="tea-kudrstinirghatana-tippani"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Of the three states — cause (from bodhicitta to the seat of awakening), fruit and activity for beings — the one in the causal state must perform all the beginner's practices; for one in the states of fruit and activity, like Śākyamuni, the beginner's practice proceeds without effort.

> tatra hetvavasthāsthitena sarvamādikarma kartavyam | phalāvasthāsatvārthakriyāvasthāsthitasya ca śākyamuneriva anābhogena ādikarma pravartata eva iti |
