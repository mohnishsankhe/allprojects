## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-8 <a id="tea-upanisa-sutta-2-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The destruction of the effluents is for one who knows and sees; it has knowledge of destruction as proximate cause, which has liberation, which has dispassion, disenchantment, knowledge and vision of things as they are, concentration, happiness, tranquillity, rapture, gladness and faith, and faith has suffering as proximate cause; suffering has birth, and so back to ignorance. As rain on a mountain fills the clefts, pools, lakes, rivers and the ocean, so each stage fills the next.
