### 1.2.6-7 <a id="tea-kavyalankara-sutra-1-2-6-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Style (rīti) is the soul of poetry; style is a special arrangement of words, its speciality consisting in the qualities (guṇa).

> rītir ātmā kāvyasya | viśiṣṭā padaracanā rītiḥ |
