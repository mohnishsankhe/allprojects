# The Oral Instruction Tantra (man ngag rgyud) of the Four Tantras

**Language:** Tibetan

**Genre:** medical tantra
**Part of:** [The Four Tantras (rgyud bzhi): the Secret Instruction Tantra on the Eight Branches, the Essence of Nectar (bdud rtsi snying po yan lag brgyad pa gsang ba man ngag gi rgyud)](gyushi.md)

**Dates:** Tradition's account: the word of the Medicine Buddha, translated in the 8th c. by Vairocana, hidden at Samye and recovered as treasure by Drapa Ngönshé in 1038; Scholarly account: compiled in the 12th c. by Yuthok Yönten Gönpo the Younger, drawing on the Aṣṭāṅgahṛdaya and other sources; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 92 chapters
**Availability:** digitized-original

Ninety-two chapters on the treatment of particular diseases: disorders of the three nyepa, internal diseases, fevers, diseases of the upper body and organs, diseases of the private parts, miscellaneous and spreading diseases, children's and women's diseases, spirit (gdon) afflictions, wounds, poisons, and the rejuvenation (bcud len) and virility chapters.
