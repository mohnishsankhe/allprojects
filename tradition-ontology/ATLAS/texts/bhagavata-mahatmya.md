Bhakti, with her sons Knowledge and Detachment grown old in the Kali age, is revived by the Bhāgavata; the seven-day hearing surpasses sacrifice, vows, yoga and meditation; the preta Dhundhukārī is freed by hearing it from his brother Gokarṇa.
