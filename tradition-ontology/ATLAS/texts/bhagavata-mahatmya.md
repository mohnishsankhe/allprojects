## Teachings (1: sourced 1)

### 1-6 <a id="tea-bhagavata-mahatmya-1-6"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Bhakti, a young woman whose sons Knowledge and Detachment have grown old and weak in the Kali age, meets Nārada; the Kumāras tell him that the Bhāgavata heard in seven days (saptāha) revives them; the seven-day hearing surpasses sacrifice, vows, yoga, meditation and knowledge; Dhundhukārī, who became a great preta through his crimes, is freed by hearing the Bhāgavata in seven days from his brother Gokarṇa.
