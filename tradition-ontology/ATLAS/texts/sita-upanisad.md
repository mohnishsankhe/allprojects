The gods ask Prajāpati who Sītā is: she is primordial nature (mūlaprakṛti) and Rāma's power, with three forms and the powers of will, action and direct manifestation; the syllables of her name; she is the source of Vedas and worlds.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1-3 <a id="tea-sita-upanisad-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The gods ask Prajāpati who Sītā is; she is the primordial nature (mūlaprakṛti), called praṇava-prakṛti, the power of Rāma; the syllables of her name are explained.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate, world-fate_

concepts: [The Goddess as Brahman](../concepts/devi-as-brahman.md) · teachers: [Prajāpati (as teacher)](../teachers/prajapati.md)

### 4-10 <a id="tea-sita-upanisad-4-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

She has three forms and three powers: will (as Śrī, Bhū and Nīlā), action (from nāda, bindu, Oṃ and the Vedas) and direct manifestation; she is the power of yoga, enjoyment and heroism.
