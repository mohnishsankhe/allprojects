Four lines spoken by Mañjuśrī to the twelve-year-old Sachen Kunga Nyingpo in a vision: attachment to this life, to saṃsāra, to one's own purpose, and grasping, are each incompatible with dharma practice, renunciation, bodhicitta and the view — a summary of the whole path.

### context <a id="tea-parting-from-four-attachments-context"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

At twelve, during a six-month retreat on Mañjuśrī under Bari Lotsawa, Sachen Kunga Nyingpo saw Mañjuśrī, who spoke the four lines of Parting from the Four Attachments; Sachen understood that the whole of the path of the perfections is contained in them.
