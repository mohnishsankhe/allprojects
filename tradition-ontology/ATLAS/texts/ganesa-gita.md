Gajānana teaches Vareṇya the yoga that leads to liberation, redefining yoga as union with the supreme (not with wealth, kin, powers or heavens), and follows the Bhagavad Gītā's sequence of action, knowledge, renunciation, meditation, devotion, the cosmic form, the field and its knower, and the threefold classification by guṇas.

### 1.7-12 <a id="tea-ganesa-gita-1-7-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

Gajānana to Vareṇya: union with wealth, sense-objects, mother, father, kin or sons is not yoga; union with the eight powers, with a wondrous woman, with kingdom, elephants and horses is not yoga; union with Indra's rank or Brahmā's world, with the realm of Śiva or of Viṣṇu, with the state of sun, moon, Kubera, wind, fire, the gods or time, or with world-sovereignty, is not yoga.

### 1.13-19 <a id="tea-ganesa-gita-1-13-19"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

The knowers who practise yoga become free of craving, conquer food and seed, purify all the worlds and teach some out of compassion; liberated while living, immersed in the bliss in the heart, they see Brahman with closed eyes and regard all beings as equal to themselves, wandering the earth for the world's good, equal toward clod, stone and gold.

### 1.21-24 <a id="tea-ganesa-gita-1-21-24"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

The undivided understanding (abheda-buddhi) regarding Śiva, Viṣṇu, Śakti, the Sun and me is yoga — right yoga in my view. I alone create, protect and, taking many guises, withdraw the world in play; I am Mahāviṣṇu, Sadāśiva, Mahāśakti and the Sun; I, the one lord of men, became fivefold of old, and through ignorance people do not know me, the cause of causes.

> śive viṣṇau ca śaktau ca sūrye mayi narādhipa / yā'bhedabuddhiryogaḥ sa samyagyogo mato mama
