**Availability:** digitized-original

Gajānana teaches Vareṇya the yoga that leads to liberation, redefining yoga as union with the supreme (not with wealth, kin, powers or heavens), and follows the Bhagavad Gītā's sequence of action, knowledge, renunciation, meditation, devotion, the cosmic form, the field and its knower, and the threefold classification by guṇas.
