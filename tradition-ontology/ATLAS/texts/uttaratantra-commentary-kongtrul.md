Jamgön Kongtrul's commentary on the Ratnagotravibhāga ('The Unassailable Lion's Roar'), presenting it as a text of the definitive third turning in a shentong-leaning reading.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### view <a id="tea-uttaratantra-commentary-kongtrul-view"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Kongtrul explains the Uttaratantra as a treatise of the Great Madhyamaka of the final turning: the element, buddha-nature, is empty of the separable adventitious stains and not empty of the inseparable qualities; this is its definitive meaning.
