## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-apohasiddhi-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Exclusion is the meaning of words. By 'exclusion' is meant neither a bare external thing, nor the form in the mind, nor mere negation: verbal cognition such as 'there is fire on the mountain' appears positive; the meaning of a word is a positive thing qualified by the exclusion of others.

> apohaḥ śabdārtho nirucyate
