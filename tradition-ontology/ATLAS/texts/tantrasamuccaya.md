Cēnnās Nārāyaṇan Nampūtirippāṭ's manual of Kerala temple ritual, the standard authority of Kerala tantris. In twelve paṭalas it covers temple construction rites, the installation (pratiṣṭhā) and worship of seven deities — Viṣṇu, Śiva, Śaṅkaranārāyaṇa, Durgā, Subrahmaṇya, Gaṇapati and Śāstā — festivals, renewal and expiatory rites.
