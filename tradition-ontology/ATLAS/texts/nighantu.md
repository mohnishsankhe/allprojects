# Nighaṇṭu

`src:nighantu` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md), [Nairukta school (the Vedic etymologists)](../lineages/nairukta.md)
**Genre:** nighaṇṭu (glossary)
**Attribution:** tradition: handed down (samāmnāta) from the seers; some later authorities ascribe it to Yāska; scholarly: older than Yāska's Nirukta; confidence: moderate
**Structure:** 5 adhyāyas: naighaṇṭuka (1–3), naigama (4), daivata (5)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The traditional word-list (samāmnāya) on which the Nirukta comments: three chapters of synonyms (names of earth, water, speech, and so on), one of single difficult Vedic words, and one of the names of the deities by region.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Nighaṇṭu-nirvacana of Devarāja Yajvan](nighantu-nirvacana.md)
