## Teachings (8: skeleton 8)

### 1.easy-path <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-zhu-1-easy-path"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Tanluan begins from Nāgārjuna: a bodhisattva seeking non-retrogression in an age of the five turbidities and without a buddha finds two paths, the difficult and the easy. The difficult is like walking overland — outsiders' seeming good confuses, śrāvakas' self-benefit obstructs great compassion, evil people destroy others' merit, results obtained by delusion ruin pure practice, and there is only self-power without support from other-power. The easy path is simply to aspire to birth in the Pure Land through faith in the Buddha; riding on the power of the Buddha's vow one is born there, and upheld by the Buddha's power one enters the rightly established group of the Mahāyāna — like sailing on water.

### 1.eight-questions <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-zhu-1-eight-questions"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In eight questions Tanluan asks who is born: the Larger Sūtra excludes those guilty of the five grave offences and slander of the Dharma, while the Contemplation Sūtra lets even five-offence evil-doers be born by ten recitations; he answers that one guilty of both offences and slander cannot be born, one who has only committed the grave offences but not slandered can be; slander is the gravest since it rejects the very Dharma; the ten continuous recitations outweigh a lifetime's evil because their weight lies in the mind, the object and decisiveness, not in length of time; 'ten recitations' need not be counted — one simply continues without other thoughts; the count is known to the sages.

### 2.birth-of-non-birth <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-zhu-2-birth-of-non-birth"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

To the objection that seeking birth contradicts the Mahāyāna teaching of non-arising, Tanluan answers that birth in that land is the 'birth of non-birth' arising from Amitābha's pure original vow, not the deluded birth of the three existences; even one of lower grade who calls the name without knowing this, once born there, finds the attachment to birth dissolved — as ice placed on fire melts and the fire goes out.

### 2.bodhicitta <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-zhu-2-bodhicitta"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In all three grades of the Larger Sūtra the aspirants arouse the unsurpassed aspiration for awakening; this aspiration is the mind that vows to become a buddha, which is the mind that vows to save beings, which is the mind that takes beings in and brings them to be born in a buddha-land. One who aspires to birth only for the endless pleasures there will not be born.

### 2.dharmakaya <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-zhu-2-dharmakaya"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

All buddhas and bodhisattvas have two dharma-bodies: the dharma-body of dharma-nature and the dharma-body of expedient means. From the dharma-body of dharma-nature arises the dharma-body of expedient means, and through the dharma-body of expedient means the dharma-body of dharma-nature is manifested; the two are different but cannot be separated, one but cannot be identified. Therefore the broad (twenty-nine adornments) and the brief (the one dharma-phrase) enter into each other.

### 2.other-power <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-zhu-2-other-power"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Seeking the root of it all, Amitābha is the dominant condition: birth there, the activity of the bodhisattvas and humans and gods there all arise from the power of his original vow. As proof, the eighteenth vow makes birth possible through ten recitations, the eleventh ensures that those born dwell in the rightly established group and so avoid retrogression, the twenty-second lets them surpass the ordinary stages and practise Samantabhadra's virtue. By this, other-power is the dominant condition. As an inferior man who cannot mount a donkey by himself can travel through space by joining the retinue of a wheel-turning king, so beings ride the Buddha's power; students should not think only of self-power.

### 2.praise-gate <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-zhu-2-praise-gate"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Commenting on 'calling that Tathāgata's name in accord with ... the meaning of the name, wishing to practise in accord with reality': the name of the Tathāgata of Unhindered Light can break all the ignorance of beings and fulfil all their aspirations; yet some call the name and ignorance remains because they do not practise in accord with reality, not knowing that the Tathāgata is the body of reality and the body for beings; and because of three kinds of non-accord: faith that is not genuine, faith that is not single, and faith that is not continuous. The three mutually entail one another; their opposites are the three faiths.

### 2.two-transfers <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-zhu-2-two-transfers"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Transfer of merit has two aspects: in the going aspect one transfers one's merit to all beings, vowing to be born together with them in Amitābha's land; in the returning aspect, having been born there and attained śamatha and vipaśyanā and the power of skilful means, one returns to the dense forest of birth-and-death to teach all beings and turn them to the Buddha's path.
