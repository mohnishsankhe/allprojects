The Gorakṣaśataka in the recension printed by G. W. Briggs (1938) and found as the first hundred verses of the Gorakṣapaddhati: after praising Mīnanātha it teaches the six limbs, the two postures, the knowledge of the six centres, sixteen supports, three aims and five voids of one's own body, the channels and breaths, and ends with the purification of the channels by alternate breathing.
