## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 145c11 <a id="tea-wangsheng-jingtu-jueyi-xingyuan-ermen-145c11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Because mind, buddha and beings are one in substance, turning one's spirit to a land ten trillion lands away is really to be born within one's own mind, and being conceived in the nine-grade lotus does not escape the present instant; unless phenomena and principle interpenetrate, how could a five-offence ordinary being reach the jewelled land by ten recitations?

> 我心既然，生佛體等，如此則方了迴神億剎，實生乎自己心中；孕質九蓮，豈逃乎剎那際內。

### 147a17-22 <a id="tea-wangsheng-jingtu-jueyi-xingyuan-ermen-147a17-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The gate of ten recitations: each morning after dressing, stand facing west, join the palms and call Amitābha's name continuously for the length of one breath — that is one 'recitation'; ten breaths are the ten recitations; the number of names follows the breath's length, not a count; the voice neither high nor low, neither slow nor hurried. It is called ten recitations because the breath is used to bind the mind. Then one makes the vow of dedication, recalling the eighteenth vow, asking that at death one know the time, be free of pain and craving, and be welcomed by the Buddha and holy ones.

> 十念門者，每日清晨服飾已後，面西正立，合掌連聲稱阿彌陀佛，盡一氣為一念。如是十氣，名為十念。但隨氣長短，不限佛數。……名此為十念者，顯是藉氣束心也。
