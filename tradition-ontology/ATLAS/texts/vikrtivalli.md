# Vikṛtivallī

A short treatise defining the eight 'modified' recitations (vikṛti) of the Veda built on the krama — jaṭā, mālā, śikhā, rekhā, dhvaja, daṇḍa, ratha and ghana.

_Notes: Ascription to Vyāḍi and the verse listing the eight vikṛtis are recalled from memory — low confidence._
