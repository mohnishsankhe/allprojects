# Vikṛtivallī

`src:vikrtivalli` · `sourced` · confidence low

**Original title:** विकृतिवल्ली
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md)
**Genre:** śikṣā (recitation)
**Authors:** 
  - [Vyāḍi](../teachers/vyadi.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Availability:** digitized-original

A short treatise defining the eight 'modified' recitations (vikṛti) of the Veda built on the krama — jaṭā, mālā, śikhā, rekhā, dhvaja, daṇḍa, ratha and ghana.

_Notes: Ascription to Vyāḍi and the verse listing the eight vikṛtis are recalled from memory — low confidence._
