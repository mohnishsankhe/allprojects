# Kīlaka Stotra

A hymn explaining that Śiva 'pinned' (kīlita) the power of the Saptaśatī and how the reciter releases it through devotion and self-offering.

_Notes: Recited as a limb (aṅga) of the Durgā Saptaśatī (Devī Māhātmya, U07); U23 records the Śākta liturgy._
