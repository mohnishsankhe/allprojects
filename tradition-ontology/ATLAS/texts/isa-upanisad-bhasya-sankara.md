## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-isa-upanisad-bhasya-sankara-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Verse 1 teaches the knower's renunciation of all desire and possession, covering the world with the Lord (the self); verse 2 ('performing works here one should wish to live a hundred years') is for those unable to take up knowledge, for whom works without attachment are the path.
