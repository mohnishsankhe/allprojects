## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1119b01 <a id="tea-chixiu-baizhang-qinggui-1119b01"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Of Baizhang: in work he shared the labour equally with the assembly and always said, 'a day without work, a day without food'.

> 至於作務。猶與眾均其勞。常曰。一日不作一日不食

### 1144a27 <a id="tea-chixiu-baizhang-qinggui-1144a27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The procedure of the universal invitation (puqing): high and low equally lend their strength; the work board is hung, all except those on duty, the old and the sick go to the work site, 'mindful of the ancients' warning: a day without work, a day without food'.

> 普請之法蓋上下均力也
