The phonetic manual of the Sāmaveda: it names the seven sāman notes and sets them against the notes of the flute (1.5.1–2: the first of the Sāman singers is the flute's madhyama, the second gāndhāra, the third ṛṣabha, the fourth ṣaḍja, the fifth dhaivata, the sixth niṣāda, the seventh pañcama), gives the places of the notes in the body and on the fingers (1.7), and teaches the gestures and faults of chanting.

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1.5.1-2 <a id="tea-naradiya-siksa-1-5-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The first (note) of the Sāman singers is the flute's madhyama; the second is gāndhāra, the third ṛṣabha, the fourth ṣaḍja, the fifth dhaivata, the sixth niṣāda, the seventh pañcama.

> yaḥ sāmagānāṃ prathamaḥ sa veṇor madhyamaḥ svaraḥ

### 1.5.3-4 <a id="tea-naradiya-siksa-1-5-3-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The peacock sounds ṣaḍja, cows low ṛṣabha, goats and sheep gāndhāra, the krauñca bird madhyama, the cuckoo in the flowering season pañcama, the horse dhaivata, the elephant niṣāda.

### 1.7.1-4 <a id="tea-naradiya-siksa-1-7-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The places of the Sāman notes: kruṣṭa at the head, prathama on the forehead, dvitīya between the brows, tṛtīya at the ears, caturtha in the throat, mandra in the chest, atisvārya in the heart; on the hand the kruṣṭa is shown at the top of the thumb, prathama on the thumb, gāndhāra (dvitīya) on the forefinger, then the others on the following fingers.
