## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 2-7 <a id="tea-bahiya-sutta-2-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Bāhiya, honoured as an arahant, is told by a deity, a former relative, that he is neither an arahant nor on the path to it, and that the Buddha teaches at Sāvatthī; he travels there and three times begs the Buddha, who is on alms round, to teach him at once, since life is uncertain.

### 8 <a id="tea-bahiya-sutta-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Then, Bāhiya, you should train thus: in the seen there will be only the seen, in the heard only the heard, in the sensed only the sensed, in the cognised only the cognised. When this is so, you will not be 'by that'; when you are not 'by that', you will not be 'in that'; when you are not 'in that', you will be neither here nor beyond nor in between the two: just this is the end of suffering.

> “Tasmātiha te, bāhiya, evaṁ sikkhitabbaṁ: ‘diṭṭhe diṭṭhamattaṁ bhavissati, sute sutamattaṁ bhavissati, mute mutamattaṁ bhavissati, viññāte viññātamattaṁ bhavissatī’ti. Evañhi te, bāhiya, sikkhitabbaṁ. Yato kho te, bāhiya, diṭṭhe diṭṭhamattaṁ bhavissati, sute sutamattaṁ bhavissati, mute mutamattaṁ bhavissati, viññāte viññātamattaṁ bhavissati, tato tvaṁ, bāhiya, na tena. Yato tvaṁ, bāhiya, na tena; tato tvaṁ, bāhiya, na tattha. Yato tvaṁ, bāhiya, na tattha,

### 9-14 <a id="tea-bahiya-sutta-9-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Through this brief teaching Bāhiya's mind was at once freed from the effluents; soon after, he was killed by a cow with a young calf; the Buddha had his body cremated and a shrine built, declared him the foremost of those who understand quickly, and uttered: where water, earth, fire and air find no footing, there the stars do not shine nor the sun give light, the moon does not appear, there is no darkness; when a sage has known this by himself, he is freed from form and formless, from pleasure and pain.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: ultimate, death-dying_
