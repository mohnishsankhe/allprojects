## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### intro <a id="tea-pradipoddyotana-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Guhyasamāja is to be read by the six alternatives: words of provisional and of definitive meaning, intentional and non-intentional speech, literal and non-literal — so that statements such as those of chapter 5 are interpreted through the completion stage rather than taken at face value.
