The teaching of 'neither decrease nor increase': the realm of beings neither decreases nor increases; the sattvadhātu is the tathāgatagarbha, the tathāgatagarbha is the dharmakāya; the dharmadhātu has three aspects — endowed from beginningless time with inseparable pure qualities, covered by separable defilements, and equal to the end of time.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-anunatvapurnatvanirdesa-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The realm of beings neither decreases nor increases: those who think beings decrease (by nirvāṇa) or increase hold wrong views. The realm of beings is the tathāgatagarbha, the tathāgatagarbha is the dharmakāya — one dharmadhātu, which has three aspects: endowed from beginningless time with inseparable pure qualities; covered by beginningless separable defilements; and equal to the end of time, permanent.
