Śrīnivāsadāsa's primer of Viśiṣṭādvaita in ten chapters (avatāra): perception, inference and scripture as the means of knowledge; then the realities — prakṛti, time, the eternal realm, attributive consciousness, the soul, the Lord — and the non-substances.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1-3 <a id="tea-yatindramatadipika-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

There are three means of valid knowledge — perception, inference and scripture; comparison and the rest are included in these; all valid cognition is of the real.

### 4-10 (the categories) <a id="tea-yatindramatadipika-dravya"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Reality is substance (dravya) or non-substance (adravya). The six substances are prakṛti, time, pure sattva (the eternal realm), attributive consciousness, the soul and the Lord; the ten non-substances are the three guṇas (sattva, rajas, tamas), the five sense qualities (sound, touch, form, taste, smell), conjunction and potency (śakti).

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: world-fate_

### 8 <a id="tea-yatindramatadipika-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Souls are bound, freed or eternal; bound souls seek enjoyment or liberation; seekers of liberation seek either isolation of the self (kaivalya) or the Lord; seekers of the Lord are devotees (bhakta) or the surrendered (prapanna).

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: general · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind, karma-liberation_

terms: [mumukṣu](../terms/mumuksu.md), [prapanna](../terms/prapanna.md) · concepts: [The classes of souls](../concepts/classes-of-souls.md) · teachers: [Śrīnivāsadāsa](../teachers/srinivasadasa.md)

### 9 <a id="tea-yatindramatadipika-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Lord has five forms: the supreme (para) in Vaikuṇṭha; the four vyūhas (Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna, Aniruddha), each manifesting pairs of the six qualities for creation, maintenance, dissolution and for meditation; the descents; the inner controller; and the image (arcā).
