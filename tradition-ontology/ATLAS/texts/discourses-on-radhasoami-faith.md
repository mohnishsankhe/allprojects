### Discourses on Radhasoami Faith, on spirit, mind and matter <a id="tea-discourses-on-radhasoami-faith-spirit-mind-matter"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _recent (post-1800)_

Creation consists of three grand divisions: the purely spiritual above, the spiritual-material (mind) in the middle, and the material below; the spirit current descends through them and must re-ascend.
