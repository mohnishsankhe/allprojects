### 1 <a id="tea-torch-of-certainty-1"></a>

The preliminaries are the foundation of Mahāmudrā: the four thoughts turn the mind from saṃsāra, and the four special preliminaries — refuge with prostrations and bodhicitta, Vajrasattva, maṇḍala offering and guru yoga — purify obscurations, gather merit and open the mind to the guru's blessing.
