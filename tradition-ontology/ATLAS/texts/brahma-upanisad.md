Śaunaka asks Pippalāda; teaches prāṇa as the self, the four seats of the person (navel, heart, throat, head) with waking, dream, deep sleep and the fourth, and — in its second part — that the true topknot and sacred thread are knowledge, so the knower who renounces wears the 'thread' of the Self and the ekadaṇḍin's sandhyā is meditation.

## Teachings (3: sourced 3)

### 1 (four seats) <a id="tea-brahma-upanisad-1-four-seats"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

The person has four seats — navel, heart, throat and head — where waking (Brahmā), dream (Viṣṇu), deep sleep (Rudra) and the fourth, the imperishable, shine.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind, body-layers_

concepts: [The fourth and beyond the fourth (turīya, turīyātīta)](../concepts/turiya-turiyatita.md)

### 1 (prāṇa) <a id="tea-brahma-upanisad-1-prana"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Śaunaka asks Pippalāda who dwells in the divine city of Brahman; Pippalāda: prāṇa is the Self; it controls all like the queen bee; the person goes forth and returns as a spider with its thread.

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate, body-layers_

teachers: [Śaunaka Mahāśāla](../teachers/saunaka-mahasala.md), [Pippalāda](../teachers/pippalada.md)

### 1 (thread) <a id="tea-brahma-upanisad-1-thread"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

The wise should abandon the outer thread; the thread of knowledge is the true one; the renouncer who wears it is pure; the ekadaṇḍin's twilight rite is meditation without water.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ethics, ultimate_

concepts: [The inner topknot and sacred thread](../concepts/inner-sikha-yajnopavita.md)
