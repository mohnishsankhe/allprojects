## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-8 (summary) <a id="tea-tripadvibhuti-mahanarayana-upanisad-1-8-summary"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

After a thousand years of austerity Brahmā is taught by Mahāviṣṇu the supreme truth: the realm of three quarters beyond māyā (tripād-vibhūti), cosmogony, the vision of Vaikuṇṭha, and liberation through knowledge and devotion.
