# Mahāmudropadeśa of Tilopa (the Gaṅgā Mahāmudrā)

**Language:** Tibetan
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)

**Availability:** digitized-translation

Tilopa's mahāmudrā instruction to Nāropa on the bank of the Gaṅgā: mahāmudrā cannot be shown, yet leave the mind uncontrived like space; clouds dissolve in space, thoughts in seeing one's mind; the kings of view, meditation, conduct and fruit; practice as a river from torrent to ocean; seek a skilled guru.
