## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### pointing-out <a id="tea-pointing-out-the-dharmakaya-pointing-out"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The pointing-out is given in stages: mind in stillness and in movement is pointed out as mind; appearances are pointed out as mind; mind is pointed out as empty; emptiness is pointed out as spontaneously present; and spontaneous presence as self-liberated.
