A hymn to the sequence (krama) of the Kālīs of cognition - Sṛṣṭi, Raktā, Sthitināśā, Yama, Saṃhāra, Mṛtyu and onward to Mahākālī and the Mahābhairavī - composed by Abhinavagupta in Mārgaśīrṣa of the [Laukika] year 66 (c. 990/91 CE).

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 15-27 <a id="tea-kramastotra-abhinavagupta-15-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Abhinavagupta praises in turn the goddesses of the sequence: Sṛṣṭi, Raktā, Sthitināśā, [Yama], Saṃhāra, Mṛtyu, and the rest, up to Mahākālī who reckons all time and the goddess of perfect fullness in whom the knower, the means, the measure and the whole world rest.
