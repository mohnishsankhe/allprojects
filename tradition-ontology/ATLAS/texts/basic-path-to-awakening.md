## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### downfalls <a id="tea-basic-path-to-awakening-downfalls"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The bodhisattva vow taken by the rite of the Bodhisattvabhūmi is lost through root downfalls — praising oneself and disparaging others out of desire for gain, not giving dharma or wealth out of stinginess, not accepting another's apology out of anger, abandoning the Mahāyāna — committed with full entanglement; lesser faults are the secondary infractions.
