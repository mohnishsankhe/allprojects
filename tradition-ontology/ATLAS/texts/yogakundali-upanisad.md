## Teachings (17: skeleton 17)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-yogakundali-upanisad-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Mind has two causes, vāsanā and breath; when one is destroyed both are destroyed.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind_

concepts: [Destruction of vāsanā, dissolution of mind and knowledge of truth](../concepts/vasana-ksaya-manonasa-tattvajnana.md)

### 1.2 <a id="tea-yogakundali-upanisad-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Of these, the breath is conquered by moderate diet, posture and the moving of the power (śakticālana).

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: body-breath · stage: intermediate · types: practice_

practices: [Moderate diet (mitāhāra) in the haṭha texts](../practices/mitahara.md), [Śakticālana (moving the power)](../practices/sakticalana.md)

### 1.7-18 (summary) <a id="tea-yogakundali-upanisad-1-7-18-summary"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

Śakticālana — moving the kuṇḍalī from her place toward the brows — and sarasvatī-cālana are described; the text says they cure abdominal diseases. Recorded summary-only.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: body-breath · stage: advanced · types: practice_

practices: [Śakticālana (moving the power)](../practices/sakticalana.md)

### 1.19-39 (summary) <a id="tea-yogakundali-upanisad-1-19-39-summary"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

Kumbhaka is twofold, sahita and kevala; sahita is of four kinds: sūrya(bheda), ujjāyī, śītalī and bhastrī. Methods and counts are not reproduced here.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: body-breath · stage: advanced · types: practice_

practices: [Sahita-kumbhaka (retention joined with inhalation and exhalation)](../practices/sahita-kumbhaka.md), [Kevala-kumbhaka (retention alone)](../practices/kevala-kumbhaka.md)

### 1.56-58 <a id="tea-yogakundali-upanisad-1-56-58"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Disease arises from sleeping by day, keeping awake at night, excess of sex, crowds, suppressing the urges, unwholesome food and strain; the diseased practitioner then gives up yoga, thinking it caused the illness — this is the first obstacle.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: body-breath · stage: all · types: practice_

### 1.59-61 <a id="tea-yogakundali-upanisad-1-59-61"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Ten obstacles: disease, doubt, heedlessness, sloth, sleep, withdrawal of interest, delusion, and further obstacles (readings uncertain) ending with failure to attain the truth of yoga; the wise should overcome them.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: body-breath · stage: all · types: practice_

### 1.62-86 <a id="tea-yogakundali-upanisad-1-62-86"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The kuṇḍalinī, roused like a serpent struck with a stick, enters the suṣumnā, pierces the knots of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra and the six lotuses, and unites with Śiva in the thousand-petalled lotus; then the world is seen to arise and dissolve falsely, like silver in mother-of-pearl.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: body-breath, meditation · stage: advanced · types: body-layers, powers-experiences, karma-liberation_

### 2.1-13 <a id="tea-yogakundali-upanisad-2-1-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The khecarī-vidyā is to be learnt from a guru who knows it from text, meaning and practice; its mantra (melana) comes only from the guru's mouth; it must be kept secret.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, practice_

### 2.28-49 (summary) <a id="tea-yogakundali-upanisad-2-28-49-summary"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

Bodily procedures of khecarī are described; they are not reproduced here. The text warns that they must be done gradually, never all at once, or the body perishes, and gives a long period of practice.
