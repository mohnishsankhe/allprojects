### 496c10 <a id="tea-linji-lu-496c10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Linji in the hall: on the lump of red flesh there is a true person of no rank, always going in and out of the gates of your faces; those who have not yet confirmed it, look! A monk asked what the true person of no rank is; Linji grabbed him — 'speak, speak!' — and when he hesitated pushed him away: 'what a dried shit-stick is the true person of no rank!'

> 赤肉團上有一無位真人，常從汝等諸人面門出入，未證據者看看

### 497a19 <a id="tea-linji-lu-497a19"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Each phrase must contain three mysterious gates, each mysterious gate three essentials, with provisional means and function; (the first phrase makes one a teacher of buddhas and patriarchs, the second of humans and gods, the third cannot even save oneself).

> 一句語須具三玄門，一玄門須具三要，有權、有用

### 497a29 <a id="tea-linji-lu-497a29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Students today must seek true insight; with true insight birth and death do not stain you and you are free to go or stay. The sickness is not trusting yourself: if you can stop the mind that seeks outward moment after moment, you are no different from the patriarchs and buddhas. Do you want to know them? They are just you, listening to the Dharma right in front of me.

> 今時學佛法者，且要求真正見解。若得真正見解，生死不染、去住自由

### 497b17 <a id="tea-linji-lu-497b17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The pure light in your single thought is the Dharma-body buddha in your own house; the non-discriminating light is the reward-body buddha; the undifferentiated light is the transformation-body buddha. These three bodies are you listening to the Dharma now; the scholars take the three bodies as ultimate, but they are only names and dependencies, 'light and shadow'.

> 爾一念心上清淨光，是爾屋裏法身佛；爾一念心上無分別光，是爾屋裏報身佛；爾一念心上無差別光，是爾屋裏化身佛

### 497c04 <a id="tea-linji-lu-497c04"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The mind-dharma has no form and pervades the ten directions: in the eye it is seeing, in the ear hearing, in the nose smelling, in the mouth speaking, in the hands grasping, in the feet running; originally one pure radiance, divided into six harmonious functions; when the one mind is absent, one is liberated wherever one is.

> 心法無形，通貫十方，在眼曰見、在耳曰聞、在鼻嗅香、在口談論、在手執捉、在足運奔。本是一精明，分為六和合

### 498a16 <a id="tea-linji-lu-498a16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In the buddha-dharma there is no place to apply effort; just be ordinary with nothing to do — shitting, pissing, wearing clothes, eating food, lying down when tired. Fools laugh at me, the wise understand. Be master wherever you are, and wherever you stand is true; circumstances cannot then sway you.

> 佛法無用功處，秖是平常無事——屙屎、送尿、著衣、喫飯、困來即臥

### 498c02 <a id="tea-linji-lu-498c02"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

True insight: entering all realms — ordinary and holy, defiled and pure, buddha-lands, Maitreya's tower, Vairocana's realm — one sees all dharmas empty; only the person of the Way who depends on nothing, listening to the Dharma, is the mother of all buddhas; buddhas are born from non-dependence; awakening to non-dependence, even buddha is not attained.

> 唯有聽法無依道人，是諸佛之母，所以佛從無依生。若悟無依，佛亦無得

### 500b22 <a id="tea-linji-lu-500b22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If you want insight according to the Dharma, do not be deceived by others; whatever you meet within or without, kill it: meeting a buddha kill the buddha, meeting a patriarch kill the patriarch, meeting an arhat kill the arhat, meeting parents kill parents, meeting kin kill kin — only then are you liberated, not bound by things. 'I have no dharma to give people; I only cure sickness and untie bonds.'

> 逢佛殺佛、逢祖殺祖、逢羅漢殺羅漢、逢父母殺父母、逢親眷殺親眷——始得解脫

### 502a12 <a id="tea-linji-lu-502a12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Right at the words, turn your light around and shine it back, and seek nothing else; knowing that body and mind are no different from patriarchs and buddhas, with nothing to do right now — that is called obtaining the Dharma.

> 儞言下便自回光返照，更不別求。知身心與祖佛不別，當下無事，方名得法

### 504a26 <a id="tea-linji-lu-504a26"></a>

> 有時一喝如金剛王寶劍、有時一喝如踞地金毛師子、有時一喝如探竿影草、有時一喝不作一喝用

### 504c16 <a id="tea-linji-lu-504c16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Linji's awakening: three times he asked Huangbo the exact meaning of the buddha-dharma and three times was struck; at Dayu's he asked whether he had been at fault; Dayu: 'Huangbo was so grandmotherly with you, and you come asking about fault!' Linji awoke: 'there isn't much to Huangbo's buddha-dharma after all', and punched Dayu three times in the ribs.

> 某甲三度問佛法的的大意，三度被打。不知某甲有過？無過
