### 1 <a id="tea-vivekadhairyasraya-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Discrimination (viveka), fortitude (dhairya) and refuge (āśraya) in the Lord are always to be guarded: discrimination that the Lord does all by his will, fortitude in bearing suffering, refuge in him alone.
