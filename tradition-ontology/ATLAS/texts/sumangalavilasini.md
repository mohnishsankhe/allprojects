## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### dn2 <a id="tea-sumangalavilasini-dn2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Buddhaghosa explains the six names: Pūraṇa, a slave, 'completed' a hundred slaves; Makkhali, told 'don't stumble' (mā khali) while carrying an oil-pot, ran off naked; Ajita wore a blanket of human hair; he glosses Makkhali's lists, including the 'eight stages of a person' as stages of life from the dull newborn to the one who has gone forth and beyond.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: general · stage: unmarked · types: teacher-transmission, world-fate_

### nidana <a id="tea-sumangalavilasini-nidana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The introduction tells how the canon was recited at the First Council and entrusted to reciter lineages (bhāṇaka), and records that the Dīgha-reciters and Majjhima-reciters classified the Khuddaka books differently.
