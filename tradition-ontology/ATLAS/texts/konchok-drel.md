### 1 <a id="tea-konchok-drel-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Rongzom reads the Guhyagarbha as teaching the great purity of all phenomena as the deities and their great equality in emptiness, so that the maṇḍala is not imagined but is how things are.
