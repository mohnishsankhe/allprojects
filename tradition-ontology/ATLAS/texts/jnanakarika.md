# Jñānakārikā

**Authors:** 
  - [Matsyendranātha](../teachers/matsyendranatha.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Structure:** 3 paṭalas
**Availability:** digitized-original

A short work in three chapters — on liberation (mokṣādhikāra), on dharma and adharma, and on conduct (caryā) — teaching that bondage is support (āśraya) and liberation is supportlessness, that dharma and adharma are two nooses to be cut with the sword of knowledge, and that the 'fourth' mind beyond the three guṇas is marked by nirvāṇa.
