## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 4-27 <a id="tea-nandakovada-sutta-4-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Nandaka questions the nuns: are the eye, forms, eye-consciousness permanent or impermanent? like a lamp whose oil, wick and flame are all impermanent, so its light; like a butcher separating skin and flesh with a sharp knife, so noble wisdom severs the defilements. All five hundred nuns attain at least stream-entry.
