## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1 <a id="tea-biyan-lu-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Case 1: Emperor Wu asks the first principle of the holy truths — Bodhidharma: 'vast emptiness, nothing holy'; 'who faces me?' — 'I don't know'. Yuanwu comments that Bodhidharma came by sea because he saw Mahāyāna capacities here, 'singly transmitting the mind-seal, not establishing words and letters, pointing directly at the human mind, seeing the nature and becoming buddha'.

> 舉梁武帝問達磨大師如何是聖諦第一義磨云。廓然無聖帝曰。對朕者誰磨云。不識

### 12 <a id="tea-biyan-lu-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Case 12, 'three pounds of flax': Yuanwu warns that many misunderstand this case — some say Dongshan was weighing flax, others that he answered east when asked west; words are only vehicles of the Way; seeking the meaning in the phrase one will not see it even in a dream until Maitreya comes.

> 麻三斤。這箇公案。多少人錯會。直是難咬嚼。無爾下口處

### 12.pointer <a id="tea-biyan-lu-12-pointer"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Yuanwu's pointer to case 12: the sword that kills and the sword that gives life is the ancient standard and today's pivot; in killing not a hair is harmed, in giving life one loses body and life; 'the one road upward the thousand sages do not transmit' — yet why so many entangling cases? Case 12 follows: 'what is buddha?' — Dongshan: 'three pounds of flax'.

> 殺人刀活人劍。乃上古之風規。亦今時之樞要。若論殺也。不傷一毫。若論活也。喪身失命
