# Kramadīpikā

**Alternate titles:** Tattvasamāsasūtravṛtti
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Genre:** commentary
**Commentary on:** [Tattvasamāsa](tattvasamasa.md)
**Availability:** digitized-original

An anonymous commentary on the Tattvasamāsa (its colophon: 'tattvasamāsākhyā sūtravṛttiḥ'), reckoned at 300 ślokas: a brahmin afflicted by the threefold suffering approaches Kapila, who explains each topic — the five functions of buddhi, the five sources of action, the five winds, the five agents, the five-jointed avidyā, the sixty topics, threefold bondage and liberation.
