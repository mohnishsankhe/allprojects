Ten verses saluting the guru as Dakṣiṇāmūrti (Śiva as silent teacher): the universe is seen within the self like a city in a mirror, projected outward by māyā as in dream; on waking, one sees the non-dual self. Commented on by Sureśvara's Mānasollāsa.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-daksinamurti-stotra-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Salutation to the guru Dakṣiṇāmūrti, who sees the universe — existing within himself like a city seen in a mirror yet appearing outside through māyā, as in sleep — and who, on awakening, directly realizes his own non-dual self.
