# Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa

`src:brahmanda-purana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Brahmāṇḍa, Vāyu-prokta Brahmāṇḍa
**Original title:** ब्रह्माण्डपुराण
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Genre:** purāṇa
**Authors:** 
  - [Vyāsa (Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana)](../teachers/vyasa.md) — role: compiler; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Proclaimed by Vāyu ('vāyuprokta' in the colophons), the eighteenth Purāṇa, 12,000 verses; the Lalitopākhyāna is its appended Uttarabhāga told by Hayagrīva to Agastya.; scholarly: Shares its core with the Vāyu Purāṇa; the Lalitopākhyāna is a much later Śākta/Śrīvidyā addition; the Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa also claims a Brahmāṇḍa origin.; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Vyāsa, end of Dvāpara; Scholarly account: core c. 4th-6th c. CE; Lalitopākhyāna much later; (confidence low)
**Structure:** four pādas (Prakriyā, Anuṣaṅga, Upodghāta, Upasaṃhāra) in three bhāgas; the 12,000-verse saṃhitā ends at Uttarabhāga ch. 4; Lalitopākhyāna = Uttarabhāga chs. 5-44
**Availability:** digitized-original

A classic Purāṇa of creation, cosmography, time, manvantaras and dynasties (with śrāddha and Paraśurāma narratives), closing with the dissolution and the city of Śiva, to which the Lalitopākhyāna is appended.
