# Bāṇī of Ravidās (Raidās kī Bāṇī)

The songs of Ravidās: the city without sorrow (Begampura); the Lord who raises the low-born; his own caste of leatherworkers who carry dead cattle round Banaras; the unity of self and God like gold and bracelet; and the offering of the mind when every outward offering is already defiled.

### dohā 'ravidās janm ke kāraṇe, hot na kou nīc …' <a id="tea-ravidas-bani-ravidas-janm-ke-karane"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

No one is low because of birth; what makes a man low is the mud of low deeds.

### pad 'aisī lāl tujh binu kaunu karai …' (Ādi Granth, rāg Mārū) <a id="tea-ravidas-bani-aisi-lal-tujh-binu-kaunu-karai"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Who but you, my Beloved, could do such a thing? Lord of the poor, you have set the royal canopy over my head — you make pure the one whose touch the world thinks polluting; you raise the low without fear of anyone. Nāmdev, Kabīr, Trilocan, Sadhnā and Sain crossed over; Ravidās says, hear, O sants: Hari does everything.

### pad 'begam purā sahar ko nāu …' (Ādi Granth, rāg Gauṛī) <a id="tea-ravidas-bani-begam-pura-sahar-ko-nau"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The city's name is Begampura (the city without sorrow): there is no pain or anxiety there, no tax on goods, no fear, no fault, no decline; there is no second or third rank, all are one; it is ever settled and its king is steady; people go where they please, the palace is open to them; Ravidās the liberated tanner says: whoever is a citizen there is my friend.

### pad 'dūdh ta bachrai thanahu biṭāriu …' (Ādi Granth, rāg Gūjrī) <a id="tea-ravidas-bani-dudh-ta-bachrai-thanahu-bitariu"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The calf has sullied the milk in the udder, the bee the flower, the fish the water; what shall I offer you in worship? I offer you my body and mind and the guru's grace, and so I find the stainless Lord.

My caste are the cutters and binders of leather who carry dead cattle around Banaras every day; now the chief brahmins bow before me — Ravidās your servant has taken refuge in your name.

### pad 'nāgar janāṃ merī jāti bikhiāt chamāraṃ …' (Ādi Granth, rāg Malār) <a id="tea-ravidas-bani-nagar-jana-meri-jati-bikhiat-chamaran"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Townsfolk, my caste is known to be Camār; yet in my heart I keep the Lord's virtues; the wine made of Gaṅgā water is not taken by the sants, and the Gaṅgā's water poured into wine is not wine — so a low family does not stain the devotee.

### pad 'prabhu jī tum candan ham pānī …' (Ādi Granth, rāg Sorath) <a id="tea-ravidas-bani-prabhu-ji-tum-candan-ham-pani"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Lord, you are sandalwood and I am water; your fragrance has entered every limb; you are the cloud and I the peacock, you the moon and I the partridge; you are the lamp and I the wick, you the pearl and I the thread; you are the master and I the servant — such is Ravidās's devotion.

### pad 'tohī mohī mohī tohī antaru kaisā …' (Ādi Granth, Sirī rāg) <a id="tea-ravidas-bani-tohi-mohi-mohi-tohi-antaru-kaisa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Between you and me, me and you, what difference is there? As between gold and the bracelet, water and the wave. If I did not sin, how would you be called the purifier of sinners?

### saying 'man cangā to kaṭhautī meṃ gaṅgā' <a id="tea-ravidas-bani-man-canga-to-kathauti-men-ganga"></a>
