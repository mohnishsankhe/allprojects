**Language:** Sanskrit

Keśava Miśra's primer of syncretic Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika, arranged by the sixteen categories of the Nyāya Sūtra and absorbing the Vaiśeṣika ontology into the treatment of the objects of knowledge; widely taught with many commentaries. (Distinct from Mokṣākaragupta's Buddhist Tarkabhāṣā.)
