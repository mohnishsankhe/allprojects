# Tirukkuṟaḷ

`src:tirukkural` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Kuṟaḷ, Muppāl (the three-part work), Tamiḻ Maṟai, Poyyāmoḻi, Uttaravētam, Ulakap Potumaṟai (common scripture of the world)
**Original title:** திருக்குறள்
**Language:** Tamil
**Family:** shared
**Lineages:** [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Genre:** ethical aphorisms (kuṟaḷ-veṇpā)
**Authors:** 
  - [Tiruvaḷḷuvar](../teachers/tiruvalluvar.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
**Attribution:** tradition: Tiruvaḷḷuvar; presented at the Madurai academy (Caṅkam), where the plank accepted only it (Tiruvaḷḷuva Mālai); Jain tradition identifies the author with Elācārya/Kundakunda; scholarly: a single author, Tiruvaḷḷuvar, of unknown affiliation; possibly Jain-influenced; confidence: high
**Dates:** Tradition's account: a Sangam-age work; the Tiruvaḷḷuvar era used in Tamil Nadu reckons from 31 BCE (fixed by Tamil scholars in 1921); Scholarly account: c. 5th–6th c. CE (Zvelebil and others); proposals range from c. 300 BCE to the 6th c. CE; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 3 books (pāl), 133 chapters (atikāram) × 10 couplets = 1,330 kuṟaḷs; Aṟam 38, Poruḷ 70, Iṉpam 25 chapters
**Availability:** digitized-original

Tiruvaḷḷuvar's 1,330 couplets (kuṟaḷ-veṇpā) in 133 chapters of ten, in three books: Aṟam (virtue, 38 chapters — preface, the householder's life, the renunciant's life, fate), Poruḷ (wealth and polity, 70 chapters) and Iṉpam/Kāmam (love, 25 chapters). It names no sectarian deity; its ethics of non-killing, abstention from meat, truthfulness, love, gratitude and renunciation are claimed by Śaivas, Jains, Vaiṣṇavas, Buddhists, and as non-sectarian ('the common scripture').
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Project Madurai e-text; url: https://www.projectmadurai.org
  - kind: translation; name: G. U. Pope, The Sacred Kurral of Tiruvalluva-Nayanar (1886)
**Commentaries on this text:** [Maṇakkuṭavar's commentary on the Tirukkuṟaḷ](manakkutavar-urai.md), [Parimēlaḻakar's commentary on the Tirukkuṟaḷ](parimelalakar-urai.md)
