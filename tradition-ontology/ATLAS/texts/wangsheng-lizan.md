## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 438c01-13 <a id="tea-wangsheng-lizan-438c01-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

How should one settle the mind, undertake practice and carry it out so as surely to be born? By the three minds of the Contemplation Sūtra: the sincere mind — true in the three acts of worship, praise and contemplation; the deep mind — true entrusting, knowing oneself to be an ordinary being full of passions, with scant roots of good, transmigrating in the three realms and not leaving the burning house, and knowing that by Amitābha's original universal vow and the calling of his name, down to ten or even one calling, one will surely be born, without a single thought of doubt; and the mind that dedicates merit and aspires to birth. With these three one is surely born; lacking one, one is not.

> 問曰：今欲勸人往生者，未知若為安心起行作業定得往生彼國土也？答曰：必欲生彼國土者，如《觀經》說者，具三心必得往生。……二者深心，即是真實信心，信知自身是具足煩惱凡夫、善根薄少，流轉三界、不出火宅。今信知彌陀本弘誓願及稱名號，下至十聲一聲等定得往生，乃至一念無有疑心，故名深心。

### 439a08-23 <a id="tea-wangsheng-lizan-439a08-23"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Four modes of practice spur the three minds: reverent practice (worshipping that Buddha and the holy ones); exclusive practice (only calling that Buddha's name, recollecting, worshipping and praising him, mixing in no other practice); uninterrupted practice (continuing without other practices or passions intervening, repenting at once when one lapses); and each 'until the end of life, vowing never to stop', which is the long-lasting practice.

> 一者恭敬修，所謂恭敬禮拜彼佛及彼一切聖眾等，故名恭敬修。……二者無餘修，所謂專稱彼佛名，專念專想專禮專讚彼佛及一切聖眾等，不雜餘業，故名無餘修。……三者無間修，所謂相續恭敬禮拜、稱名讚歎、憶念觀察、回向發願，心心相續不以餘業來間，故名無間修。

### 439a20-b01 <a id="tea-wangsheng-lizan-439a20-b01"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Citing the Mañjuśrī Prajñāpāramitā's single-practice samādhi (dwell alone, fix the mind on one buddha, do not contemplate his features, only call his name): why not contemplation, but only calling the name? Because beings' hindrances are heavy, the object is subtle while the mind is coarse, consciousness flies and spirit scatters, and contemplation is hard to achieve; the great sage in compassion directly urged exclusive calling of the name; because calling is easy, continuing it one is born.

> 問曰：何故不令作觀，直遣專稱名字者，有何意也？答曰：乃由眾生障重，境細心麁、識颺神飛，觀難成就也。是以大聖悲憐，直勸專稱名字。正由稱名易故，相續即生。

### 439b17-c08 <a id="tea-wangsheng-lizan-439b17-c08"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If one continues moment after moment until the end of life, ten of ten are born, a hundred of a hundred — for no outside conditions disturb right mindfulness, it accords with the Buddha's vow, the teaching and the Buddha's words. If one abandons exclusive practice for mixed practices, hardly one or two in a hundred, three or five in a thousand are born — because mixed conditions disturb right mindfulness, it does not accord with the vow, recollection is not continuous, the dedication is not earnest, passions interrupt, there is no shame or repentance, no thought of repaying the Buddha's kindness, pride and pursuit of fame and gain, no reliance on good companions. Whoever practises wholeheartedly, ten of ten are born; of mixed and insincere practice, not one in a thousand.

> 若能如上念念相續、畢命為期者，十即十生、百即百生。……若欲捨專修雜業者，百時希得一二、千時希得三五。……但使專意作者，十即十生；修雜不至心者，千中無一。

### 447c23-26 <a id="tea-wangsheng-lizan-447c23-26"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Shandao paraphrases the eighteenth vow as: 'If, when I become a buddha, beings of the ten directions who call my name even down to ten voicings are not born, may I not attain perfect awakening'; that Buddha has now become a buddha in the world, so know that the grave original vow is not in vain: beings who call and recollect will surely be born.

> 又如《無量壽經》云：若我成佛，十方眾生稱我名號，下至十聲，若不生者，不取正覺。彼佛今現在世成佛，當知本誓重願不虛，眾生稱念必得往生。
