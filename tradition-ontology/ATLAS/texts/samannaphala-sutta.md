# Sāmaññaphala Sutta

**Alternate titles:** The Fruits of the Contemplative Life

King Ajātasattu asks the fruit of the contemplative life visible here and now; he reports the answers of the six rival teachers; the Buddha answers with the whole gradual training from going forth, virtue and sense restraint through the four jhānas to the six higher knowledges and the destruction of the effluents.

## Teachings (11: skeleton 11)

### 16-32 <a id="tea-samannaphala-sutta-16-32"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ajātasattu reports the answers of the six teachers: Pūraṇa Kassapa's doctrine that there is no evil or merit in deeds (non-action), Makkhali Gosāla's purification through wandering on in saṃsāra by fate, Ajita Kesakambalī's annihilationism (nothing survives death), Pakudha Kaccāyana's seven unmade bodies, the Nigaṇṭha Nātaputta's fourfold restraint and Sañjaya Belaṭṭhaputta's evasion; each answered a question about fruits with something else.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: polemical · path: general · stage: all · types: dispute, karma-liberation_

teachers: `tch:purana-kassapa`, `tch:makkhali-gosala`, `tch:ajita-kesakambali`, `tch:pakudha-kaccayana`, `tch:mahavira`, `tch:sanjaya-belatthiputta` · disputes: `dsp:kiriyavada-akiriyavada`

### 35-36 <a id="tea-samannaphala-sutta-35-36"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The first visible fruit: a slave or servant who goes forth and lives restrained is no longer treated as a slave by the king but is honoured and protected.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: all · types: ethics_

terms: `trm:pabbajja`

### 40-42 <a id="tea-samannaphala-sutta-40-42"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A Tathāgata arises in the world and teaches; a householder hears the Dhamma, gains faith, reflects that household life is crowded and the holy life hard to live at home 'like a polished shell', and goes forth; he lives restrained by the Pātimokkha, seeing danger in the slightest fault.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: beginner · types: practice, teacher-transmission_

terms: `trm:saddha`, `trm:pabbajja`, `trm:patimokkha`

### 43-63 <a id="tea-samannaphala-sutta-43-63"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The monk's virtue: abstaining from killing, taking what is not given, unchastity, false, divisive, harsh and idle speech, from damaging plants, eating at wrong times, entertainments, luxuries, trading, and from wrong livelihood by divination and low arts; endowed with this noble virtue he experiences a blameless happiness within.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: action · stage: beginner · types: ethics_

terms: [śīla](../terms/sila.md) · concepts: `cpt:sila`

### 64-66 <a id="tea-samannaphala-sutta-64-66"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Guarding the doors of the senses without grasping at signs and features; mindfulness and clear comprehension in all activities; contentment with robes and alms-food, taking only what is needed as a bird takes only its wings.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: beginner · types: practice_

terms: `trm:indriya-samvara`, `trm:sampajanna`, `trm:santutthi` · practices: `prc:indriya-samvara`, `prc:sampajanna`

### 67-74 <a id="tea-samannaphala-sutta-67-74"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Resorting to a secluded dwelling, sitting cross-legged with mindfulness established, he cleanses his mind of covetousness, ill will, dullness and drowsiness, restlessness and remorse, and doubt. The five hindrances are like a debt, a disease, a prison, slavery and a desert journey; their abandoning is like freedom from debt, health, release, emancipation and reaching safety — and on seeing them abandoned, gladness and joy arise.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice, consciousness-mind_

terms: `trm:nivarana` · concepts: `cpt:five-hindrances` · obstacles: `obs:five-hindrances`

### 75-82 <a id="tea-samannaphala-sutta-75-82"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The four jhānas with their similes: the first pervades the body with rapture and bliss born of seclusion like bath-powder kneaded with water; the second with rapture and bliss born of concentration like a lake fed by a spring; the third with bliss free of rapture like lotuses immersed in cool water; the fourth pervades the body with pure bright mind like a man wrapped in white cloth.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice, consciousness-mind, powers-experiences_

terms: `trm:jhana`, `trm:piti`, [sukha](../terms/sukha.md), `trm:upekkha` · concepts: `cpt:four-jhanas`

### 83-84 <a id="tea-samannaphala-sutta-83-84"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

With the mind concentrated, purified and malleable he directs it to knowledge and vision: he knows 'this body of mine is material, made of the four elements … and this consciousness of mine is supported here and bound up here', as one sees a thread strung through a clear beryl gem.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: experiential · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind, powers-experiences_

terms: `trm:nanadassana`, `trm:vinnana`

### 85-96 <a id="tea-samannaphala-sutta-85-96"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

He creates a mind-made body; wields the kinds of psychic power; hears with the divine ear; knows the minds of others; recollects his many past lives; and sees with the divine eye beings passing away and reappearing according to their deeds — each with its simile.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: powers-experiences_

terms: `trm:abhinna`, `trm:iddhi`, `trm:dibbacakkhu`, `trm:pubbenivasanussati`, `trm:manomayakaya` · concepts: `cpt:six-abhinnas`

### 97-98 <a id="tea-samannaphala-sutta-97-98"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

He directs the mind to the knowledge of the destruction of the effluents: he knows as it really is 'this is suffering … its origin … its cessation … the way', and 'these are the effluents …'; his mind is freed from the effluents of sensuality, becoming and ignorance, and he knows 'birth is ended, the holy life fulfilled, done is what was to be done, there is no more coming back to any state of being' — like one who sees shells, pebbles and fish in a clear lake.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: experiential · path: knowledge · stage: realized (arahatta) · types: karma-liberation, powers-experiences_

terms: `trm:asava`, `trm:asavakkhaya`, `trm:arahant` · concepts: `cpt:three-vijjas`, `cpt:four-noble-truths` · obstacles: `obs:asavas`

### 99-102 <a id="tea-samannaphala-sutta-99-102"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ajātasattu confesses the killing of his father; the Buddha accepts his confession as restraint for the future, and afterwards says that had the king not killed his father, the stainless eye of the Dhamma would have arisen in him on that very seat.

_level: conventional · standpoint: causal · path: action · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, ethics_

terms: `trm:kamma`, `trm:dhammacakkhu` · teachers: `tch:ajatasattu`
