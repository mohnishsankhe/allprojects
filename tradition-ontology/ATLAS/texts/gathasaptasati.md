## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 408 <a id="tea-gathasaptasati-408"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The newly initiated female Kāpālika, her limbs sweating with the pleasure of touching the ash born from her lover's cremation, does not finish beginning to smear herself.

> jāramasāṇasamubbhavabhūisuhapphaṃsasijjiraṅgīe | ṇa samappaï ṇavakāvāliṇīa uddhūlaṇārambho
