### 6.conversion <a id="tea-honen-shonin-gyojo-ezu-6-conversion"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Having read the whole canon several times without finding a way for someone like himself who could keep neither precepts nor meditation nor wisdom, Hōnen in 1175 came upon Shandao's line 'single-heartedly and exclusively recollecting the name of Amida ... this is the rightly determined act, because it accords with that Buddha's vow', and at once abandoned all other practices for the nenbutsu.

### ohara <a id="tea-honen-shonin-gyojo-ezu-ohara"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

At Ōhara, before Tendai and Nara scholars, Hōnen explained that the holy path suits those of superior capacity while the Pure Land path is for ordinary people of this age, and that his own capacity was too poor for the former; the listeners were moved and joined in the nenbutsu for days.
