## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### conduct <a id="tea-kaula-upanisad-conduct"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

One should not tell one's own secret; one should tell it to a disciple. Inwardly a Śākta, outwardly a Śaiva, among people a Vaiṣṇava: this is the conduct. Liberation comes from knowledge of the self. One should not censure the (other) worlds/views.

> antaḥ śāktaḥ bahiḥ śaivaḥ loke vaiṣṇavaḥ / ayam evācāraḥ / ātmajñānān mokṣaḥ / lokān na nindyāt

### opening <a id="tea-kaula-upanisad-opening"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Now the inquiry into dharma. Knowledge and understanding; knowledge is the sole cause of liberation; liberation is the accomplishment of being the self of all; the five objects are the world-expanse; beings are of the nature of knowers; yoga is liberation.

> athāto dharmajijñāsā / jñānaṃ buddhiś ca / jñānaṃ mokṣaikakāraṇam / mokṣaḥ sarvātmatāsiddhiḥ

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: karma-liberation, ultimate_

### paradoxes <a id="tea-kaula-upanisad-paradoxes"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The very ignorance that is the cause of non-dharma is knowledge; the world is the Lord; the impermanent is permanent; ignorance is knowledge; non-dharma itself is dharma; this is liberation.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_

### pinda-moksa <a id="tea-kaula-upanisad-pinda-moksa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The five bonds are forms of knowledge; from the body comes birth; in that very body is liberation.
