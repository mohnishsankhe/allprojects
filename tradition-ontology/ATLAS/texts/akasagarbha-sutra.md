## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### downfalls <a id="tea-akasagarbha-sutra-downfalls"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Root downfalls of kings, ministers and beginner bodhisattvas — among them teaching emptiness to the unprepared, turning others from the great vehicle, abandoning the prātimokṣa, disparaging the śrāvaka vehicle — and their confession to Ākāśagarbha, who appears in dreams.
