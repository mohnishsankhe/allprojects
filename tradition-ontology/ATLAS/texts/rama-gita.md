After one's duties have purified the mind, one should approach a true guru: ignorance is the root of rebirth and only knowledge removes it, not action, so knowledge cannot be combined with ritual action; the meaning of 'that thou art' is grasped by partial implication; the self is distinct from the three bodies and three states; superimposition is explained; one meditates on the Self, dissolves the syllables of Oṃ into it, lives out prārabdha free of ego and merges in Rāma. Until all is seen as Rāma, one should worship him.
