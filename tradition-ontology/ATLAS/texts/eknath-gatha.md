### abhaṅga 'mājheṃ māher paṇḍharī' <a id="tea-eknath-gatha-maze-maher-pandhari"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Paṇḍharī on the bank of the Bhīmā is my mother's home: Viṭṭhala is my father, Rakhumāī my mother, Puṇḍalīka my brother, and the saints my kin.

> माझें माहेर पंढरी । आहे भिवरेच्या तीरीं ॥
