## Teachings (15: skeleton 15)

### 1.4-1.5 <a id="tea-mahaparinibbana-sutta-1-4-1-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The seven conditions of non-decline for the Vajjī republic: meeting often in concord, not abolishing ancient laws, honouring elders, protecting women, venerating shrines, and supporting arahants.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: action · stage: all · types: ethics_

concepts: [Conditions of non-decline](../concepts/aparihaniya-dhamma.md)

### 1.6-1.11 <a id="tea-mahaparinibbana-sutta-1-6-1-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Seven conditions of non-decline for the monks: frequent meetings in concord, keeping the training rules laid down, honouring elders, not falling under craving, delighting in forest dwellings, establishing mindfulness; with further sets of seven (the seven factors of awakening, seven perceptions) and six.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: all · types: ethics, teacher-transmission_

concepts: [Conditions of non-decline](../concepts/aparihaniya-dhamma.md)

### 2.8-2.9 <a id="tea-mahaparinibbana-sutta-2-8-2-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The mirror of the Dhamma: a noble disciple with confirmed confidence in the Buddha, the Dhamma and the Saṅgha, and with the virtues dear to the noble ones, may declare of himself that he is a stream-enterer, no longer bound for the lower realms, sure of awakening.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: devotion, action · stage: intermediate (sotāpanna) · types: karma-liberation_

terms: [sotāpanna](../terms/sotapanna.md), [aveccappasāda](../terms/aveccappasada.md) · concepts: [The factors of stream-entry](../concepts/sotapattiyanga.md)

### 2.25-2.26 <a id="tea-mahaparinibbana-sutta-2-25-2-26"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Tathāgata has taught without an inner and an outer: he has no teacher's closed fist in respect of the teachings. Therefore live as islands to yourselves, as your own refuge, with no other refuge, with the Dhamma as your island and refuge — that is, by the four establishments of mindfulness.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, practice_

terms: [dīpa](../terms/dipa.md), [ācariyamuṭṭhi](../terms/acariya-mutthi.md) · concepts: [Oneself as island, the Dhamma as island](../concepts/attadipa.md) · practices: [The four establishments of mindfulness (satipaṭṭhāna)](../practices/satipatthana.md)

### 2.26.1 <a id="tea-mahaparinibbana-sutta-2-26-1"></a>

> Tasmātihānanda, attadīpā viharatha attasaraṇā anaññasaraṇā, dhammadīpā dhammasaraṇā anaññasaraṇā.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission_

### 3.50-3.51 <a id="tea-mahaparinibbana-sutta-3-50-3-51"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The qualities the Buddha has taught from direct knowledge, to be learned, cultivated and developed so that the holy life may last long for the welfare of the many — the four establishments of mindfulness, four right efforts, four bases of power, five faculties, five powers, seven factors of awakening and the noble eightfold path; and: 'conditioned things are subject to vanishing; accomplish the goal by heedfulness'.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: practice, teacher-transmission_

terms: [bodhipakkhiyā dhammā](../terms/bodhipakkhiya-dhamma.md), [appamāda](../terms/appamada.md) · concepts: [The thirty-seven qualities conducive to awakening (bodhipakkhiyā dhammā)](../concepts/thirty-seven-wings.md)

### 4.5-4.12 <a id="tea-mahaparinibbana-sutta-4-5-4-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The four great references: if a monk claims to have heard a teaching from the Buddha, from a Saṅgha with elders, from many learned elders or from one learned elder, it should neither be accepted nor rejected, but compared with the discourses and checked against the discipline; if it does not fit, it is to be rejected.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, sound-language_

terms: [mahāpadesa](../terms/mahapadesa.md), [buddhavacana](../terms/buddhavacana.md) · concepts: [The four great references](../concepts/mahapadesa.md)

### 4.13-4.20 <a id="tea-mahaparinibbana-sutta-4-13-4-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Cunda the smith offers the Buddha a meal including 'sūkaramaddava'; the Buddha tells him to bury the rest, since no one but the Tathāgata could digest it; afterwards he falls severely ill with bloody dysentery and deadly pains, which he endures mindfully.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: general · stage: all · types: death-dying_

teachers: [Cunda the smith](../teachers/cunda-kammaraputta.md)

### 4.42 <a id="tea-mahaparinibbana-sutta-4-42"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Lest Cunda feel remorse, Ānanda is to tell him that two offerings of food are of equal fruit, greater than any other: the one after which the Tathāgata awakened and the one after which he attained final nibbāna; Cunda has made merit leading to long life, beauty, happiness, fame, heaven and sovereignty.

_level: conventional · standpoint: causal · path: action · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, death-dying_

practices: [Giving (dāna)](../practices/dana.md) · teachers: [Cunda the smith](../teachers/cunda-kammaraputta.md)

### 5.8 <a id="tea-mahaparinibbana-sutta-5-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Four places a faithful person should see with emotion: where the Tathāgata was born, where he awakened, where he set rolling the wheel of Dhamma, and where he attained final nibbāna; whoever dies on such a pilgrimage with a confident heart will be reborn in heaven.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion · stage: all · types: practice, death-dying_

concepts: [The four places of pilgrimage](../concepts/four-places-of-pilgrimage.md) · practices: [Pilgrimage to the four places](../practices/pilgrimage-four-places.md)

### 5.26-27 <a id="tea-mahaparinibbana-sutta-5-26-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The wanderer Subhadda asks whether the six famous teachers all have direct knowledge, or none, or some. The Buddha sets the question aside and teaches that in whatever teaching and discipline the noble eightfold path is not found, there is no first, second, third or fourth ascetic; other doctrines are empty of true ascetics.

_level: conventional · standpoint: polemical · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, dispute_

concepts: [The six rival teachers (cha satthāro)](../concepts/six-teachers.md)

### 5.27 <a id="tea-mahaparinibbana-sutta-5-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

To the wanderer Subhadda: in whatever teaching and training the noble eightfold path is not found, no first, second, third or fourth ascetic is found; in this teaching it is found, so here alone are they; the other doctrines are empty of ascetics.

_level: conventional · standpoint: polemical · path: general · stage: all · types: dispute, karma-liberation_

concepts: [The noble eightfold path](../concepts/noble-eightfold-path.md), [The four stages of awakening and the eight noble persons](../concepts/four-stages-of-awakening.md) · teachers: [Subhadda the wanderer](../teachers/subhadda-paribbajaka.md)

### 6.1-6.3 <a id="tea-mahaparinibbana-sutta-6-1-6-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

After his passing, the Dhamma and Vinaya he has taught will be their Teacher; monks should address each other according to seniority; if it wishes, the Saṅgha may abolish the lesser and minor training rules; the higher penalty is to be imposed on Channa.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, death-dying_

terms: [dhammavinaya](../terms/dhammavinaya.md) · concepts: [The Dhamma and discipline as teacher](../concepts/dhamma-vinaya-as-teacher.md)

### 6.7.1-6.7.3 <a id="tea-mahaparinibbana-sutta-6-7-1-6-7-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Buddha's last words: conditioned things are liable to pass away; accomplish [the goal] through heedfulness.

> Atha kho bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi: “handa dāni, bhikkhave, āmantayāmi vo, vayadhammā saṅkhārā appamādena sampādethā”ti.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: death-dying, practice_

terms: [appamāda](../terms/appamada.md), [saṅkhāra](../terms/sankhara.md) · concepts: [The three marks](../concepts/three-marks.md)

### 6.8-6.9 <a id="tea-mahaparinibbana-sutta-6-8-6-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Buddha passes up through the four jhānas and four formless attainments to the cessation of perception and feeling, descends again to the first jhāna, ascends to the fourth, and emerging from the fourth jhāna attains final nibbāna.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: realized · types: death-dying, karma-liberation_

terms: [parinibbāna](../terms/parinibbana.md), [jhāna](../terms/jhana.md) · concepts: [Parinibbāna (final passing)](../concepts/parinibbana.md)
