### 49 <a id="tea-jataka-49"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The goal passes by the fool who waits on the lucky star; the goal is the goal's own star — what can the stars do?

> nakkhattaṃ paṭimānentaṃ attho bālaṃ upaccagā | attho atthassa nakkhattaṃ kiṃ karissanti tārakā ||
