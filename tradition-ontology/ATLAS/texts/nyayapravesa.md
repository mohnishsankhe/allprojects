## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-nyayapravesa-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Proof and refutation, with their fallacies, serve to convince others; perception and inference, with their fallacies, serve one's own understanding.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: dispute_

concepts: [The three characteristics of a valid reason (trairūpya)](../concepts/trairupya.md) · teachers: [Śaṅkarasvāmin](../teachers/sankarasvamin.md)

### 2 <a id="tea-nyayapravesa-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The reason has three aspects: it is a property of the subject, present in similar cases and absent from dissimilar cases; the thesis, reason and example with their fallacies (e.g. 'sound is eternal' contradicted by perception or by common knowledge) are enumerated.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: dispute_
