# Gītādhyāna (meditation verses on the Gītā)

Nine verses customarily recited before the Gītā, invoking Vyāsa and Kṛṣṇa and comparing the Upaniṣads to cows milked by Kṛṣṇa, with Arjuna as the calf and the Gītā as the nectar-milk.

_Notes: The ascription to Madhusūdana is a popular report, recorded as doubtful._
