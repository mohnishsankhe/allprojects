A principal Mantrapīṭha tantra of the Bhairava corpus, centred on Svacchandabhairava (Aghora) and Aghoreśvarī; the mainstay of Kashmirian Śaiva ritual (worship, initiation), with a large cosmography of the worlds (bhuvanas) and teachings on yoga and the subtle body. Kṣemarāja's Uddyota reads it in the light of non-dual doctrine.

### 10.1169-1171 <a id="tea-svacchanda-tantra-10-1169-1171"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Those of conquered senses who practise the divine Pāśupata observance, devoted to ash, muttering and meditation, go to the level of Īśvara, where the Lord, impelled by Śiva's will, holds office.

### 11.71-72 <a id="tea-svacchanda-tantra-11-71-72"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In the Pāśupata observance the highest state taught is the level of Īśvara; for the Mausulas and Kārukas it is the Māyā principle; the lord of the Vaimalas is Tejeśa; in the Pramāṇa [system] the state is Dhruva.

> vrate pāśupate proktam aiśvaraṃ paramaṃ padam | mausule kāruke caiva māyātattvaṃ prakīrtitam

### 11.182-185 <a id="tea-svacchanda-tantra-11-182-185"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

What lies beyond the states of the intellect is called the Atimārga; that knowledge beyond the world is the Atimārga. The worlds are the paśus on the path of creation and dissolution; those in the Atimārga are beyond them. The skull-observers and the Pāśupatas are not created again, being established in the Īśvara and in the Dhruva, since they go to release, which is the cause of non-rebirth; for the worldly there is creation and dissolution again.

> kapālavratino ye ca tathā pāśupatāś ca ye | sṛṣṭir na vidyate teṣām īśvare ca dhruve sthitāḥ

### 11.189-190 <a id="tea-svacchanda-tantra-11-189-190"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

All that is to be attained in the worldly and other paths up to the end of the Atimārga, one attains with ease in the great knowledge of Śiva; that [knowledge] is not attained by all, being exceedingly pure.
