## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-9 <a id="tea-paramapada-sopanam-1-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Nine steps lead to the supreme abode: discrimination (viveka), disgust with saṃsāra (nirveda), dispassion (virakti), fear of rebirth (bhīti), winning the Lord's favour through surrender (prasādana), departure from the body (utkramaṇa), the path of light (arcirādi), arrival in the divine realm (divyadeśa-prāpti) and attainment (prāpti).
