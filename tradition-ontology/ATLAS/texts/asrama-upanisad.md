## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-4 <a id="tea-asrama-upanisad-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

There are sixteen kinds among the four stages: students (gāyatra, brāhma, prājāpatya, bṛhat/naiṣṭhika), householders (vārtāka, śālīna, yāyāvara, ghora-saṃnyāsika), forest-dwellers (vaikhānasa, audumbara, vālakhilya, phenapa) and wandering ascetics (kuṭīcara, bahūdaka, haṃsa, paramahaṃsa), each defined by livelihood and marks.
