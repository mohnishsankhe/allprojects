## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### whole <a id="tea-catuhsarana-prakirnaka-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Four things are auspicious, four are highest in the world, in four I take refuge: the Arhats, the Siddhas, the Sādhus and the dharma taught by the omniscient. Taking these refuges, one confesses one's wrongs and rejoices in the good done by others.
