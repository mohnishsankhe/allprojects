The esoteric 'heart' of the Vāmakeśvara teaching in three 'codes' (saṅketa): the code of the cakra (the Śrīcakra as the emanation of the supreme Śakti and the universe of principles), the code of the mantra (six levels of the mantra's meaning), and the code of worship (three kinds of worship, inner and outer rites). Knowledge of these codes is said to make one liberated while living.
