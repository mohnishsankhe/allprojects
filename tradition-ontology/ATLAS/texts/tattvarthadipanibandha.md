## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1 <a id="tea-tattvarthadipanibandha-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

In the first part (Śāstrārtha-prakaraṇa) Vallabha teaches that the world is Brahman's own real form produced without change in Brahman, that only saṃsāra — the soul's ignorance-made 'I and mine' — is to be destroyed, and that souls are parts of Brahman whose bliss is concealed.

### 1 <a id="tea-tattvarthadipanibandha-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Ignorance (avidyā) has five joints — forgetting one's own nature and the false identification with body, senses, vital breath and inner organ — and knowledge (vidyā) has five parts, among which devotion to Kṛṣṇa is chief.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge, devotion · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind, karma-liberation_

### 1.7 <a id="tea-tattvarthadipanibandha-1-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The means of valid knowledge are four: the Vedas, the words of Śrī Kṛṣṇa (the Gītā), the sūtras of Vyāsa, and Vyāsa's 'language of trance' (the Bhāgavata).

> vedāḥ śrīkṛṣṇavākyāni vyāsasūtrāṇi caiva hi | samādhibhāṣā vyāsasya pramāṇaṃ tac catuṣṭayam ||
