### 1 <a id="tea-bhavanakrama-1"></a>

### 1/3 <a id="tea-bhavanakrama-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Wisdom arising from hearing should first be produced, by which one grasps the meaning of scripture; then by wisdom arising from reflection one discerns the definitive and the provisional meaning; having ascertained it, one should cultivate what is real and not what is unreal, for otherwise, cultivating the mistaken and not removing doubt, correct knowledge will not arise.

> tatra prathamaṃ tāvat śrutamayī prajñotpādanīyā | tayā hi tāvad āgamārtham avadhārayati | tataścintāmayyā prajñayā nītaneyārthaṃ nirvedhayati |

### 1/4 <a id="tea-bhavanakrama-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In brief every concentration has six faults — laziness, forgetting the object, laxity and excitement, non-application and over-application — against which eight remedial factors are cultivated: faith, aspiration, effort and pliancy against laziness; mindfulness against forgetting the object; introspection against laxity and excitement; application when they are not being quelled; and equanimity when the mind flows calmly.

> saṃkṣepeṇa sarvasyaiva samādheḥ ṣaḍ doṣā bhavanti | kausīdyam ālambanasaṃpramoṣaḥ, layauddhatyam, anābhogaḥ, ābhogateti | teṣāṃ pratipakṣeṇāṣṭau prahāṇasaṃskārā bhāvanīyāḥ | tad yathā-śraddhā, chandaḥ, vyāyāmaḥ, praśrabdhiḥ, smṛtiḥ, samprajanyam, cetanā, upekṣā ceti |

### 1.cittamatra <a id="tea-bhavanakrama-1-cittamatra"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Kamalaśīla explains the Laṅkāvatāra's verses as stages of meditation: the yogin first analyses form as nothing apart from mind, then examines mind itself and finds it without own-nature, then abandons even the image of non-duality, and abides in the imageless wisdom that sees the Mahāyāna.

### 2 <a id="tea-bhavanakrama-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Neither method without wisdom nor wisdom without method is the bodhisattva's liberation; the perfections of giving and the rest must be joined with the wisdom that understands emptiness, and that wisdom with the method of great compassion.

### 3 <a id="tea-bhavanakrama-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There is no way other than correct analysis (bhūtapratyavekṣā) by which to achieve non-recollection and non-attention; mere absence of recollection and attention cannot be the cause of non-conceptuality, or one who has fainted would enter it. When the yogin, analysing with correct wisdom, sees no dharma arisen in the three times, he enters non-conceptual wisdom, pacifying all elaboration, penetrates emptiness and is freed from the net of wrong views.

> na ca bhūtapratyavekṣāṃ vinā'nya upāyo'sti yena prakāreṇāsmṛtyamanasikārau kuryāt |
