### 14.1 <a id="tea-platform-sutra-dunhuang-14-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

One-practice samādhi is practising a straight mind at all times; the deluded who hold that sitting motionless and not letting deluded thoughts arise is one-practice samādhi make the Dharma the same as insentient things — a cause obstructing the Way; hundreds teach people to sit viewing mind and viewing purity — a great error.

> 若如是，此法同無情，却是障道因緣

### 17.1 <a id="tea-platform-sutra-dunhuang-17-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

From the beginning, both sudden and gradual have taken no-thought as tenet, no-form as substance and non-abiding as root. If a single thought were cut off, the Dharma body would leave the physical body. 'No' means free of the dual characteristics and all afflictions; 'thought' means thinking of the original nature of suchness.

> 頓漸皆立無念為宗，無相為體，無住為本

### 38.1 <a id="tea-platform-sutra-dunhuang-38-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The compilers' note: the tenet is transmitted by handing on the Platform Sūtra itself; without receiving it, with place, date and name, one has no transmission and is not a disciple of the Southern school; those who have not received it may preach the sudden teaching but will not escape disputes.

> 若不得《壇經》，即無稟受

### 44.1 <a id="tea-platform-sutra-dunhuang-44-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Shenhui asks whether Huineng, in sitting meditation, sees or does not see; Huineng strikes him three times and asks whether it hurts: 'it hurts and does not hurt' — 'I too see and do not see: I always see my own faults and do not see others' faults; seeing and not seeing are two extremes, hurting and not hurting are arising and ceasing.'

> 吾打汝，痛不痛

### 49.1 <a id="tea-platform-sutra-dunhuang-49-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Asked who will receive robe and Dharma, Huineng says the Dharma has been entrusted; more than twenty years after his death, when false teachings confuse his tenet, someone will come forward regardless of his life to settle right and wrong in the buddha-teaching and establish the tenet — that will be his true Dharma; the robe should not be passed on.

> 吾滅後二十餘年，邪法撩亂，惑我宗旨。有人出來，不惜身命，定佛教是非，竪立宗旨，即是吾正法
