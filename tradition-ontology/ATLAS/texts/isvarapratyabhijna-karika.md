**Availability:** digitized-original

Utpaladeva's verse exposition of the 'recognition of the Lord': in the section on knowledge he answers the Buddhist logician's denial of an enduring self (1.2) and shows that cognition, memory and exclusion require one conscious subject (1.3-1.4) whose essence is reflexive awareness (1.5); the section on action shows action and relation to be the Lord's; the section on scripture sets out the tattvas, the pramātṛs and the three malas (3.1-3.2); the summary section (4) closes with the simile of the unrecognized lover (4.17).
