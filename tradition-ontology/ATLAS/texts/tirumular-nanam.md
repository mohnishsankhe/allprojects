Nine verses ascribed to Tirumūlar, distinct from the Tirumantiram: the letter 'a' (akāram) as the root and ground of all; the root centre with the three flames and three channels; gazing at the middle between the eyes, nose and forehead; the guru and the Lord are one; the unspoken mantra; the light seen as the Lord's form.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1 <a id="tea-tirumular-nanam-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Being the base, and beyond the worlds, the letter 'a' (akāram) is the foot; being crown, middle and root, being the three things and the first, beyond the ladder of the three voids is the supreme light's abode; the root at the base is akāram; the state standing as He and She is to be approached.

> अडियागि अण्डरण्डत् तप्पाल् आगि / अगारमॆऩुमॆऴुत् तदुवे पादमागि / मुडियागि नडुवागि मूलऩ् तऩ्ऩिल् / मुप्पॊरुळुऩ् ताऩागि मुदलुमागिप् / पडियाय्मुप् पाऴऱ्ऱुप् पडिक्कु मप्पाऱ् / पडिगडन्द परञ्जोदिप् पदियुमागि / अडियागु मूलमदे अगार मागि / अवऩवळाय् निऩ्ऱऩिलै यणुव तामे।

### 4 <a id="tea-tirumular-nanam-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

What is known as akāram is the Supreme itself (taṟparam); ukāram, which has knowledge, is supreme consciousness (ciṟparam); what is born is the whole world; know the foot called akāram as foot and crown, and gaze on it daily.

> अरिन्ददुवुऩ् तऱ्परमे अगार मागुम् / अऱिवुडैय उगारंसिऱ् परम तागुम् / पिऱन्ददुवु मुलगमॆलाञ् जमयऩ् ताऩाम् / पेदमॆऩुङ् गरुविवगै यॆल्ला मागुम् / अऱिन्ददुवुम् अगारमॆऩुम् पादऩ् तऩ्ऩै / अडिमुडियॆऩ्ऱु अऩुदिऩमुम् अऱिन्दु नोक्के।

### 5 <a id="tea-tirumular-nanam-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Gazing at the root-foot, look through the subtle supreme consciousness; passing beyond the supports, look at the middle of the eyes, nose and forehead; those able to hold this state become sons of the matted-haired Lord; gazing daily on the guru like the flame of grace, they become teachers.

> नोक्कमुडऩ् मूलमॆऩुम् पादऩ् तऩ्ऩै / नुण्बॊरुळाञ् जिऱ्परत्तिऩूडे नोक्कु / तीर्क्कमुड ऩादार वगैयुऩ् ताण्डित् / तिरुनयऩम् नासिऩॆऱ्ऱि नडुवे पार्त्तुप् / पोक्कऱिन्दिङ् गिन्दऩिलै नोक्क वल्लार् / पुरिसडैयोऩ् तऩ्ऩुडैय पुदल्व रावार् / आक्कमुडऩ् अरुट्चुडर्बोऱ् कुरुवैत् ताऩे / अऩुदिऩमुम् नोक्कि निऱ्पार् आसानामे।

### 6 <a id="tea-tirumular-nanam-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The teacher (ācāṉ) and the Lord (īcāṉ) are one; He and She are one, and it is That; this is the mantra that is not spoken; it is the form of the great light; those who see the Lord as the teacher reach Kailāsa in joy.

> आसाऩु मीसाऩु मॊऩ्ऱे यागुम् / अवऩवळु मॊऩ्ऱागुम् अदु ताऩागुम् / पेसाद मन्दिरमु मिदुवे यागुम् / पेरॊळियिऩ् वडिवागुम् पेरु मागुम् / नेसारुङ् गलैगळॆलाऩ् ताऩे यागुम् / निलैयाऩ ओङ्गार पूड मागुम् / ईसाऩै आसाऩाय्क् काणुम् पेर्क्किङ् / गिऩ्बमुडऩ् कयिलास मॆय्द लामे।
