Nine verses ascribed to Tirumūlar, distinct from the Tirumantiram: the letter 'a' (akāram) as the root and ground of all; the root centre with the three flames and three channels; gazing at the middle between the eyes, nose and forehead; the guru and the Lord are one; the unspoken mantra; the light seen as the Lord's form.
