### 6.14.2 <a id="tea-chandogya-upanisad-bhasya-sankara-6-14-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Knowledge does not immediately end the body: 'for him the delay lasts only as long as he is not released (from the body); then he is merged' — the karma that has begun to bear fruit continues, like an arrow already released, until it is spent.
