The root tantra of Heruka Cakrasaṃvara and Vajravārāhī (Herukābhidhāna): the maṇḍala of the wheels of body, speech and mind with the twenty-four sacred sites, the vows and signs (chommā) of the yoginīs, feast (gaṇacakra) and conduct in the cremation grounds, and mantra; much of it coded and completed by its explanatory tantras.

### 1.1 <a id="tea-cakrasamvara-tantra-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Now I shall explain the secret, briefly and not at length: the union with Śrī Heruka, which accomplishes all desired aims.

### pitha-list <a id="tea-cakrasamvara-tantra-pitha-list"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The twenty-four sites of the Heruka maṇḍala are named — among them Pullīramalaya, Jālandhara, Oḍḍiyāna, Arbuda, Godāvarī, Rāmeśvara, Devīkoṭa, Mālava, Kāmarūpa, Oḍra, Triśakuni, Kośala, Kaliṅga, Lampāka, Kāñcī, Himālaya, Pretapurī, Gṛhadevatā, Saurāṣṭra, Suvarṇadvīpa, Nagara, Sindhu, Maru and Kulatā — arranged in the wheels of mind, speech and body, each with its ḍāka and yoginī, and (in the commentaries) mapped onto the body.
