A manual in ten paṭalas by Nārāyaṇa Paṇḍitācārya on yoga as the Dvaita tradition understands it: yoga is the supreme worship of Hari with devotion through prescribed action; one remembers Hari as the real agent within one's limbs; it describes the training of a devotee, study and reflection on Madhva's works, initiation by the ācārya, worship with mantra and nyāsa, contemplation of oneself as the reflection (pratibimba) of Nārāyaṇa in the heart, a graded list of disciplines crowned by hearing, reflection, meditation, samādhi and bhakti, and the presence of Hari in both the worshipper and the image.

## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 1.2-5 <a id="tea-yogadipika-narayana-pandita-1-2-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Yoga is the supreme worship (of the Lord) with devotion by a person who does the prescribed acts and avoids the forbidden — 'skill in action'. Hari, independent, abides in me and pervades my limbs; self-governed, the Unborn moves me by his will; the activities of my mind, speech and body are his worship, and I should remember Hari as their doer and their aim. This yoga fully pleases Viṣṇu and grants release from bondage.

### 1.6-14 <a id="tea-yogadipika-narayana-pandita-1-6-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A father should train his son from childhood in devotion, dharma and vows; the twice-born worships his teacher, keeps pure conduct and daily rites, studies the Veda and Madhva's Mahābhārata-tātparya-nirṇaya and, to still obstacles to knowledge, worships Narasiṃha and Kṛṣṇa. One with beginningless devotion to the Supreme, sinless, trained in grammar and reasoning, learned in the Veda, austere and under vows, gains the supreme knowledge from the teacher's grace. Having heard the Sūtrabhāṣya and other works he reflects on them with companions. The ācārya initiates the prepared devotee, has him sip the Lord's foot-water, and the initiate keeps the vows the teacher gives.

### 3.55-58 <a id="tea-yogadipika-narayana-pandita-3-55-58"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When one sits with the body straight the suṣumnā shines, lit above the crown by the sun's rays; in the lotus of the heart one remembers Nārāyaṇa, and before him one's own self as his reflection, praying: 'You are the original (bimba), I am the reflection, though there is a difference between us; flawless Lord, separate my faults from me — homage to you.' So entreated by his servant, he removes the sin.

### 3.84-88 <a id="tea-yogadipika-narayana-pandita-3-84-88"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Purity, posture, breath-control, non-possession, non-violence, truth, non-stealing, continence, austerity, contentment, sacrifice and the other duties, study, worship of Hari, fixation, hearing, reflection, meditation, samādhi, exposition of the Upaniṣads and devotion are to be practised, each later one with special weight. If hearing and the like are available the others may be reduced; if one wishes to reduce them further one should renounce; hearing and the rest are never to be reduced; and the vow of Nārāyaṇa's day (Ekādaśī) is to be kept with special care.

### 10.43-46 <a id="tea-yogadipika-narayana-pandita-10-43-46"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Hari, present in the image worshipped, is the enjoyer of his own form; present in the offerings — scent, flowers, food, fan, umbrella, music — Viṣṇu accepts himself as present in the image; Viṣṇu within the worshipper and Hari in the image are (the same Lord) in the forms of worshipped and worshipper, enjoyer and enjoyed.
