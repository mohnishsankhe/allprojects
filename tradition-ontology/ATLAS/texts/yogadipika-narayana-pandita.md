A manual in ten paṭalas by Nārāyaṇa Paṇḍitācārya on yoga as the Dvaita tradition understands it: yoga is the supreme worship of Hari with devotion through prescribed action; one remembers Hari as the real agent within one's limbs; it describes the training of a devotee, study and reflection on Madhva's works, initiation by the ācārya, worship with mantra and nyāsa, contemplation of oneself as the reflection (pratibimba) of Nārāyaṇa in the heart, a graded list of disciplines crowned by hearing, reflection, meditation, samādhi and bhakti, and the presence of Hari in both the worshipper and the image.
