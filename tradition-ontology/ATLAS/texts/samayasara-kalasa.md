## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-samayasara-kalasa-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Homage to the essence of the self (samayasāra), which shines by self-experience, whose nature is consciousness, and which cuts through all other states.

> namaḥ samayasārāya svānubhūtyā cakāsate / citsvabhāvāya bhāvāya sarvabhāvāntaracchide

_level: ultimate · standpoint: devotional · path: knowledge, devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate_

### 131 <a id="tea-samayasara-kalasa-131"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Whoever are liberated are liberated through discriminative knowledge; whoever are bound are bound through its absence.

> bhedavijñānataḥ siddhāḥ siddhā ye kila kecana / asyaivābhāvato baddhā baddhā ye kila kecana

_level: ultimate · standpoint: causal · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation_
