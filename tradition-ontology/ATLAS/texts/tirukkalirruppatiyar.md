## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-100 <a id="tea-tirukkalirruppatiyar-1-100"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Expanding the Tiruvuntiyār, it teaches the dissolution of the sense of 'I' in grace, the guru as Śiva, and the conduct of the jīvanmukta who acts without attachment.
