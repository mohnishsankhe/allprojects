## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 13-20 <a id="tea-kassapasihanada-sutta-13-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There is a path — the noble eightfold path — by which one sees for oneself that the Buddha speaks at the right time and truly. Going naked, food restrictions and other mortifications do not make one an ascetic or brahmin; one who develops a mind of love free of enmity and realises the liberation of mind and of wisdom is called an ascetic and a brahmin.
