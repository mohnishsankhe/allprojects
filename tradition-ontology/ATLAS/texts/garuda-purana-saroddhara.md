**Structure:** 16 chapters
**Availability:** digitized-original

A sixteen-chapter digest of the Garuḍa's teaching on death: the miseries of sinners, the road and city of Yama, the hells, rebirth and embryonic suffering, gifts and rites at death, the ten days, sapiṇḍīkaraṇa, and the conduct that leads to heaven or liberation.

_Notes: Compiler's name and date not verified - low confidence; kept as the text actually recited in mourning in North India._
