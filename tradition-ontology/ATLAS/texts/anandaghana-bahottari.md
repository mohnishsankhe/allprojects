## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### rama-kaho <a id="tea-anandaghana-bahottari-rama-kaho"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Call him Rāma or Rahīm, Kṛṣṇa or Mahādeva, Pārśvanātha or Brahmā — it is Brahman itself; as vessels differ but the clay is one, so the names differ while the self is one in nature.
