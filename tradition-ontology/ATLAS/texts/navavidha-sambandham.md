## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-navavidha-sambandham-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The tirumantra contains nine relations between the Lord and the soul: father and son, protector and protected, master (śeṣin) and servant, husband and wife, knower and known, owner and owned, support and supported, self and body, enjoyer and enjoyed.
