A song rebuking attachment and pedantry — 'worship Govinda, you fool; at the time of death grammar rules will not save you' — urging dispassion, good company and worship; tradition says Śaṅkara composed the refrain and first stanzas and his disciples added one each.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-bhaja-govindam-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Worship Govinda, worship Govinda, worship Govinda, you fool; when the appointed time (of death) comes, (the grammar rule) 'ḍukṛñ karaṇe' will not save you.

> bhaja govindaṃ bhaja govindaṃ govindaṃ bhaja mūḍhamate / samprāpte sannihite kāle nahi nahi rakṣati ḍukṛñ karaṇe

### 9 <a id="tea-bhaja-govindam-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

From good company comes non-attachment; from non-attachment, freedom from delusion; from freedom from delusion, steadiness in truth; from steadiness in truth, liberation while living.

> satsaṅgatve nissaṅgatvaṃ nissaṅgatve nirmohatvam / nirmohatve niścalatattvaṃ niścalatattve jīvanmuktiḥ
