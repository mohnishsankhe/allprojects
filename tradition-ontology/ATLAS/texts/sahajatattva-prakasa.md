### Sahajatattva-prakāśa, on worshipping the Kartā alone <a id="tea-sahajatattva-prakasa-worship-of-the-karta-alone"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _recent (post-1800)_

The Kartābhajā worships the one Kartā (Master) and no other god; the many gods and rites of the world are outward custom, kept only for the world's sake.
