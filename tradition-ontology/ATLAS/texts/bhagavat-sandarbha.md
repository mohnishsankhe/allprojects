On Bhagavān: the personal Lord with his inherent powers is the full manifestation of the non-dual reality, of which impersonal Brahman is an incomplete manifestation.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### brahman-section <a id="tea-bhagavat-sandarbha-brahman-section"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The non-dual reality appears as Brahman when realised without its distinctions and powers — an incomplete manifestation — and as Bhagavān when realised with all his inherent powers; Bhagavān is therefore the full manifestation.
