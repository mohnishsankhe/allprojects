The aspiration spoken by the primordial buddha: one ground, two paths and two results — awareness and ignorance; Samantabhadra was liberated by recognizing the ground, beings wander by not recognizing it; the prayer turns each of the five poisons into its wisdom.

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1 <a id="tea-kuntuzangpo-monlam-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

All things of saṃsāra and nirvāṇa have one ground, two paths and two results — the magical display of awareness and of ignorance. By Samantabhadra's aspiration may all beings recognize the ground and attain perfect buddhahood in the palace of the dharmadhātu.

### 2 <a id="tea-kuntuzangpo-monlam-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Samantabhadra's account of his own liberation: the ground is uncompounded, self-arisen, inexpressible, beyond saṃsāra and nirvāṇa; recognizing it, one is buddha; not recognizing it, beings wander. He, Samantabhadra, recognized that ground-appearances are his own display and so, without having done a single virtue, was liberated; for beings who did not recognize, ignorance arose as a blank unawareness and a conceptual ignorance, and from them saṃsāra.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate, karma-liberation_

concepts: [Ground-appearances and how delusion arises](../concepts/ground-appearances-and-delusion.md), [The three kinds of ignorance (ma rig pa gsum)](../concepts/three-kinds-of-ignorance.md)

### 3 <a id="tea-kuntuzangpo-monlam-3"></a>

The aspiration then takes up each afflictive state — desire, anger, pride, jealousy and dullness — describing the realm into which it leads, and prays that, by relaxing into the natural state without grasping or rejecting, each be recognized and liberated as its wisdom.
