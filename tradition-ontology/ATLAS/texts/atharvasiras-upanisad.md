## Teachings (4: sourced 3, skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-atharvasiras-upanisad-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

The gods go to heaven and ask Rudra 'who are you?'; Rudra: 'I alone was in the beginning, I am and I will be; there is none other than me'; he is all things, eternal and non-eternal, Brahman and not-Brahman.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate_

### 3-4 (names) <a id="tea-atharvasiras-upanisad-3-4-names"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

His names are explained: oṃkāra (it raises up the breaths), praṇava (it bows the breaths), all-pervading, infinite, tāra (it saves from the great fear of birth and death), śukla, sūkṣma, vaidyuta, Parabrahman, one, Rudra, Īśāna, Maheśvara.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language, ultimate_

concepts: [The measures of Oṃ (mātrā)](../concepts/omkara-matras.md)

### 5 <a id="tea-atharvasiras-upanisad-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Having abandoned the root of the net of causes, and with the intellect fixed the collected [mind] on Rudra, they call Rudra the oneness. Fire is ash, wind is ash, water is ash, earth is ash, sky is ash - all this is ash; since this vow of touching the limbs with ash is pāśupata, it is Brahman; this is the pāśupata, for release from the bond of the paśu.

> agnir iti bhasma vāyur iti bhasma jalam iti bhasma sthalam iti bhasma vyometi bhasma

_level: unmarked · standpoint: ritual · path: ritual, meditation · stage: all · types: practice, ultimate_

terms: [bhasma](../terms/bhasma.md), [pāśupata](../terms/pasupata.md) · practices: [The Vedic Pāśupata vow of the Atharvaśiras](../practices/atharvasiras-pasupata-vrata.md)

### 5 (pāśupata vow) <a id="tea-atharvasiras-upanisad-5-pasupata-vow"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Taking ash with 'fire is ash, wind is ash, water is ash, earth is ash, sky is ash…' and smearing the body — this is the pāśupata vow, for releasing the bound soul (paśu) from its bonds (pāśa).

_level: conventional · standpoint: ritual · path: ritual · stage: all · types: practice, karma-liberation_

concepts: [Paśu and Paśupati](../concepts/pasu-pasupati.md) · practices: [The pāśupata vow](../practices/pasupata-vrata.md), [Ash-bath (bhasma-snāna)](../practices/bhasma-snana.md)
