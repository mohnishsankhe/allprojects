## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### atmartha <a id="tea-kriyakramadyotika-atmartha"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Aghoraśiva distinguishes worship for oneself (ātmārtha), which every initiate performs daily, from worship for others (parārtha) in temples, reserved to qualified Ādiśaiva priests.

### diksa <a id="tea-kriyakramadyotika-diksa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Aghoraśiva teaches that initiation removes the bonds by Śiva's own power acting through the ācārya, who has become Śiva by ritual identification (śivatva of the officiant), while the disciple's prārabdha karma continues until the body falls.
