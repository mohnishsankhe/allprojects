# Kutūhalasālā Sutta

Vacchagotta reports a discussion in the debating hall: each of the six teachers declares where his dead disciples are reborn, even the highest; the Buddha does not so declare of the highest, and speaks of rebirth fuelled by grasping and craving.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-4 <a id="tea-kutuhalasala-sutta-2-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In the debating hall (kutūhalasālā) wanderers said that each of the six teachers, when a disciple dies, declares where he is reborn, even of his highest disciple; the ascetic Gotama declares the rebirth of ordinary disciples but says the highest has cut off craving and made an end of suffering. The Buddha explains that he declares rebirth for one with fuel (grasping), not for one without.
