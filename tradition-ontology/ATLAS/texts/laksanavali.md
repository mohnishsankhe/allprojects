Udayana's short manual of definitions of the Vaiśeṣika categories, which divides the categories into positive ones (the six) and absence as a seventh; its closing verse dates it to Śaka 906 (984 CE).

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### opening division of the categories <a id="tea-laksanavali-padartha-vibhaga"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

A category is of two kinds, positive and absence; substance and the rest are the six positive ones, absence the seventh.
