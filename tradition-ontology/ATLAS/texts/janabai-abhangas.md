Some three hundred abhaṅgas ascribed to Janābāī, maidservant in Nāmdev's household: Viṭṭhala grinds and pounds grain with her, and songs of work become worship; some songs are non-dual.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### abhaṅga 'daḷitāṃ kāṇḍitāṃ' <a id="tea-janabai-abhangas-dalita-kandita"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

While grinding and pounding grain I will sing of you, O Infinite One.

> दळितां कांडितां । तुज गाईन अनंता ॥

### abhaṅgas on Viṭṭhala's help <a id="tea-janabai-abhangas-vitthala-at-the-mill"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Janābāī sings that Viṭṭhala himself sits at the grindstone with her, carries water, and combs and washes her hair, since she has no one else.
