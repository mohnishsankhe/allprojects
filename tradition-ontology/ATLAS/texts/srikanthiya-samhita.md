# Śrīkaṇṭhīyasaṃhitā

`src:srikanthiya-samhita` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Śrīkaṇṭhī, Śrīkaṇṭhīya
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** saṃhitā (scriptural catalogue and doctrine)
**Availability:** lost

A lost Śaiva scripture known through quotations, above all Jayaratha's on Tantrāloka 1.18, which preserve its catalogue of the 28 Siddhānta tantras (10 Śiva- and 18 Rudra-bhedas) and of the 64 Bhairava tantras in eight groups of eight (Bhairava, Yāmala, Mata, Maṅgala, Cakra, Bahurūpa, Vāgīśa, Śikhā).
