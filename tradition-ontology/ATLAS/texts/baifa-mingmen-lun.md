# Baifa mingmen lun (Treatise on the Gate of Understanding the Hundred Dharmas)

`src:baifa-mingmen-lun` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** 大乘百法明門論 (T1614), theg pa chen po'i chos brgya gsal ba'i sgo'i bstan bcos (D4063, from Chinese)
**Language:** Chinese, Tibetan
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md), `lin:faxiang`
**Authors:** 
  - [Vasubandhu](../teachers/vasubandhu.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Availability:** digitized-translation

A list of the hundred dharmas of Yogācāra in five groups — 8 minds, 51 mental factors, 11 form-dharmas, 24 factors dissociated from mind, 6 unconditioned — with the two selflessnesses; a basic text of Faxiang study.
