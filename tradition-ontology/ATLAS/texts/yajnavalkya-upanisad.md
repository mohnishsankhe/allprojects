## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1 <a id="tea-yajnavalkya-upanisad-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Janaka asks Yājñavalkya about renunciation; the Jābāla's teaching is repeated (renounce the very day one is dispassionate), with Atri's question on the thread.

### 4 <a id="tea-yajnavalkya-upanisad-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Lord has entered beings as the jīva; the renouncer should bow down even to horse, caṇḍāla, cow and donkey.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ethics, ultimate_

### 5-14 <a id="tea-yajnavalkya-upanisad-5-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Warnings to the renouncer against the attraction of women, voiced in the ascetic idiom of disgust.

### 19-21 <a id="tea-yajnavalkya-upanisad-19-21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate
