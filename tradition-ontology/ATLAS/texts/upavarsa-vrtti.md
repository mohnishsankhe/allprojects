**Availability:** lost

A lost commentary by the 'revered Upavarṣa', cited by Śabara for the view that the word 'gauḥ' is just the letters g, au and ḥ; Śaṅkara reports that Upavarṣa, in the first (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā) treatise, deferred the proof of the self to the Śārīraka, implying he commented on both Mīmāṃsās.

_Notes: Title is descriptive; no original title survives. Evidence: Śabara on MS 1.1.5; Śaṅkara, BSBh 3.3.53._
