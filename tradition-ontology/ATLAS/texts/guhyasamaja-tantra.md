### 1.1 <a id="tea-guhyasamaja-tantra-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Thus have I heard: at one time the Lord dwelt in the 'bhagas of the vajra-women', the heart of the body, speech and mind of all tathāgatas, with countless bodhisattvas (named the samaya-, body-, speech-, mind-vajra and the rest).

> evaṃ mayā śrutam | ekasmin samaye bhagavān sarvatathāgatakāyavākcittahṛdayavajrayoṣidbhageṣu vijahāra

### 2.3 <a id="tea-guhyasamaja-tantra-2-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

(The bodhicitta taught by the Lord, vajra of body, speech and mind:) in the absence of being there is no meditation; meditation is not meditation; thus being is not being, and meditation is not apprehended.

> abhāve bhāvanābhāvo bhāvanā naiva bhāvanā | iti bhāvo na bhāvaḥ syād bhāvanā nopalabhyate ||

### 2.3p <a id="tea-guhyasamaja-tantra-2-3p"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Vairocana's bodhicitta: free of all entities, devoid of aggregates, elements, sense-fields and of grasped and grasper, by the sameness of the selflessness of phenomena one's own mind is primordially unborn, of the nature of emptiness.

> sarvabhāvavigataṃ skandhadhātvāyatanagrāhyagrāhakavarjitaṃ dharmanairātmāsamatayā svacittamādyanutpannaṃ śūnyatābhāvam

### 5.3-9 <a id="tea-guhyasamaja-tantra-5-3-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

Chapter 5 ('the conduct') declares that caṇḍālas, bamboo-workers and others intent on killing, and even those who have committed the heinous deeds, succeed in this supreme vehicle, while those who revile their master never succeed; it lists killing, lying, coveting others' wealth, constant desire, the eating of impure substances and sexual transgression with kinswomen as not preventing the non-conceptual practitioner's success. The assembled bodhisattvas are astonished and ask why the Lord speaks such ill words; the tathāgatas reply: 'Do not say so — this is the pure dharmatā of the buddhas who know the essence, arisen from the meaning of the essential dharma: the path of awakening.'

> caṇḍālaveṇukārādyā māraṇārthārthacintakāḥ | sidhyanti agrayāne'smin mahāyāne hyanuttare ||

### 18.138 <a id="tea-guhyasamaja-tantra-18-138"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The six-limbed yoga is: withdrawal (pratyāhāra), meditation (dhyāna), breath control (prāṇāyāma), retention (dhāraṇā), recollection (anusmṛti) and absorption (samādhi); without this 'service' in six limbs the supreme attainment does not arise.

> pratyāhārastathā dhyānann prāṇāyāmo'tha dhāraṇā | anusmṛtiḥ samādhiśca ṣaḍaṅgo yoga ucayte ||

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, body-breath · stage: advanced · types: practice_

practices: [The six-branch yoga (ṣaḍaṅgayoga)](../practices/sadanga-yoga.md)

### 18.139-145 <a id="tea-guhyasamaja-tantra-18-139-145"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

Withdrawal is the ten senses each staying in its own place; meditation is the conception of the five objects of desire as the five buddhas, fivefold (with reasoning, investigation, joy, bliss and one-pointedness); breath control is to draw out the breath — of the five gnoses and five elements — in the form of a ball and imagine it at the tip of the nose as a five-coloured jewel; retention is to meditate one's mantra in the heart and place it in the drop of life-wind, restraining the senses.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, body-breath · stage: advanced · types: practice_

### 18.146-149 <a id="tea-guhyasamaja-tantra-18-146-149"></a>

### 18.150-152 <a id="tea-guhyasamaja-tantra-18-150-152"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Recollection is to recollect the form one has visualized — there an appearance arises; absorption: gathering all things by the union of wisdom and means and meditating them within the image, the sudden arising of gnosis is called samādhi.

> jhaṭiti jñānaniṣpattiḥ samādhiriti saṅkṣitā |

### 18.152-155 <a id="tea-guhyasamaja-tantra-18-152-155"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

By withdrawal one is blessed by all mantras; attaining the gnosis of meditation one gains the five superknowledges; by breath control one is blessed by the bodhisattvas; by the power of retention Vajrasattva enters; by recollection a halo of light arises; merely by mastery of absorption one becomes free of obscurations.

> dhyānajñānaṃ samāpadya pañcābhijñatvamāpnuyāt | prāṇāyāmena niyataṃ bodhisattvairadhiṣṭhyate ||
