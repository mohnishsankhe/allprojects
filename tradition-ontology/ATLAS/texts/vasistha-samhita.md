An eight-limbed yoga for the twice-born framed by Vedic orthodoxy: yamas and niyamas, a few postures, the channels and kuṇḍalinī, breath-control, a withdrawal (pratyāhāra) that moves the breath through the eighteen vital points (marma), concentration on the elements, meditation and samādhi. It is among the early texts that teach kuṇḍalinī outside a tantric frame and is named as an authority on postures in HYP 1.18.

### topic.kundalini <a id="tea-vasistha-samhita-topic-kundalini"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The channels arise from the bulb; kuṇḍalinī lies coiled at the mouth of suṣumnā, blocking it, and is moved upward by breath-control so that the breath can enter the central channel.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: body-breath · stage: all · types: body-layers_

### topic.pratyahara-marmas <a id="tea-vasistha-samhita-topic-pratyahara-marmas"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Withdrawal is taught as moving the breath and attention through the eighteen vital points (marmasthāna) from the toes to the crown.
