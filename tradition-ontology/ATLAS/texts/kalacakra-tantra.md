The abridged Kālacakra tantra in five chapters — the world (loka), the inner person (adhyātma), consecration (abhiṣeka), practice (sādhana) and gnosis (jñāna) — correlating cosmos, body and deity, with astronomy and calendar, the Śambhala narrative, the six-branch yoga and the goal of immutable bliss.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1 <a id="tea-kalacakra-tantra-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The first chapter (the world) sets out the cosmos, its measures and astronomy, and the narrative of Śambhala, including the prophecy that the kalkin Raudra Cakrin will in the future defeat the barbarians (mlecchas) and restore the dharma.

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: general · stage: all · types: world-fate_

concepts: [Śambhala and its kalkins](../concepts/sambhala.md), [Outer, inner and other Kālacakra](../concepts/outer-inner-other-kalacakra.md)

### 2 <a id="tea-kalacakra-tantra-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The second chapter (the inner) teaches the body as a microcosm of the world: channels, winds, drops and the cycle of breaths correspond to the days, months and years of outer time.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: body-breath · stage: advanced · types: body-layers, world-fate_

concepts: [Outer, inner and other Kālacakra](../concepts/outer-inner-other-kalacakra.md), [The Buddhist subtle body (channels, winds, drops)](../concepts/buddhist-subtle-body.md)

### 4 <a id="tea-kalacakra-tantra-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The fourth chapter (practice) gives the generation of the Kālacakra maṇḍala and, for the completion stage, the six-branch yoga.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, ritual · stage: advanced · types: practice_

practices: [The six-branch yoga (ṣaḍaṅgayoga)](../practices/sadanga-yoga.md)

### 5 <a id="tea-kalacakra-tantra-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The fifth chapter (gnosis) teaches immutable bliss joined with empty form, the reversal of karmic winds, and the attainment of the Ādibuddha's gnosis.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: ultimate, karma-liberation_

concepts: [Empty form (śūnyatābimba)](../concepts/empty-form.md), [Immutable bliss (akṣarasukha)](../concepts/aksara-sukha-kalacakra.md)
