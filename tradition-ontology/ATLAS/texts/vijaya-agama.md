# Vijayāgama

`src:vijaya-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Vijaya
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Anādirudra, then Parameśa (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Rudrabheda no. 11 in the Kāmika's order; 8 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** unknown

No. 11 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Rudrabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Anādirudra, then Parameśa. The Kāmika assigns it 8 upabheda(s): Vijaya, Udbhava, Saumya, Aghora, Mṛtyunāśana, Kubera, Mahāghora, Vimala. The early Vijaya is not securely extant.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: a 'Vijayottaratantra' is catalogued by Muktabodha (E00010); relation to the Vijaya not established

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.13/10.19: to Anādi; Anādi → Parameśvara. Śrīkaṇṭhīya: 'Vijaya'. In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the belly (pūrva 1.93–101).
