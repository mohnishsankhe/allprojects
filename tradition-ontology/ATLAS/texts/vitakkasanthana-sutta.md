## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 2-8 <a id="tea-vitakkasanthana-sutta-2-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When a monk pursuing the higher mind is troubled by unwholesome thoughts connected with desire, hate or delusion, he should (1) attend to another, wholesome sign, as a carpenter knocks out a coarse peg with a fine one; (2) examine the danger in those thoughts, as a young person would be disgusted by a carcass hung round the neck; (3) forget them and not attend to them; (4) attend to stilling the thought-formation, as one walking fast slows down, stands, sits, lies down; (5) with teeth clenched and tongue on the palate, beat down mind with mind. Then he becomes master of the courses of thought: he will think what he wishes and not think what he does not.
