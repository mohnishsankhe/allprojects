Tāranātha's list of twenty-one points on which the other-emptiness (shentong) Madhyamaka differs from the self-emptiness (rangtong) Madhyamaka — on the two truths, the three natures, buddha-nature, the qualities, and what is established in meditative equipoise.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### whole <a id="tea-twenty-one-differences-taranatha-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Self-emptiness and other-emptiness Madhyamaka agree that every conventional phenomenon is empty of its own nature and that in meditative equipoise all elaborations cease. They differ over the ultimate: other-emptiness holds that the dharmatā, self-aware wisdom, is truly established and not empty of itself, self-emptiness holds that it too is empty of own-nature — and twenty-one further differences follow from this.
