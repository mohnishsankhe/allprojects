## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 136c03 <a id="tea-rujing-xu-yulu-136c03"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In the supplementary record: Rujing, entering the hall and scolding a sleeping monk, said 'practising Chan is body and mind dropping off — why just sleep?'; the narrator awoke, came to the abbot's room and said 'body and mind have dropped off'; Rujing: 'body and mind dropped off; dropped-off body and mind'.

> 夫參禪者身心脫落只管打睡作麼
