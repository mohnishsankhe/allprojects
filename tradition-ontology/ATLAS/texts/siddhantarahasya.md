## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1 <a id="tea-siddhantarahasya-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

What was spoken directly by the Lord at midnight on the eleventh day of the bright fortnight of Śrāvaṇa is now told word for word.

> śrāvaṇasyāmale pakṣe ekādaśyāṃ mahāniśi | sākṣād bhagavatā proktaṃ tad akṣaraśa ucyate ||

### 2-3 <a id="tea-siddhantarahasya-2-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

By making the relation with Brahman (brahmasambandha), all faults of body and soul are removed. The faults are of five kinds: innate, arising from place and time, those determined by worldly and Vedic rule, those from association and those from contact; none of them should be thought to remain.

### 4-8 <a id="tea-siddhantarahasya-4-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Therefore what has not been offered is to be avoided; those who have dedicated themselves should do everything only after offering it, as a gift already given is not given again; like the Gaṅgā purifying what enters it, offering to the Lord removes faults.
