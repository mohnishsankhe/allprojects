### q1 <a id="tea-ngeshe-dronme-q1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Whether the ultimate is an affirming or non-affirming negation: for the provisional, enumerated ultimate the emptiness that negates true existence is a non-affirming negation; the actual ultimate, realized in meditative equipoise, is the union of appearance and emptiness beyond all four extremes — neither kind of negation.

### q4 <a id="tea-ngeshe-dronme-q4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Whether to analyse or to rest: analytical meditation produces certainty, and resting meditation lets that certainty settle without concepts; the two are to be alternated, the analysis of the paṇḍita and the resting of the kusāli supporting one another.

`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Whether Madhyamaka has a thesis: from the standpoint of the ultimate free of extremes, there is no thesis to hold; conventionally, the Mādhyamika asserts, teaches and refutes.
