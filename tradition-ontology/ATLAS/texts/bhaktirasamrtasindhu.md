**Structure:** 4 vibhāgas divided into laharīs
**Availability:** digitized-original

Rūpa Gosvāmī's treatise on devotion as rasa, in four divisions (east, south, west, north) subdivided into waves (laharī): (1) devotion in general, sādhana-, bhāva- and prema-bhakti, vaidhī and rāgānugā practice and the 64 limbs; (2) the components of rasa (vibhāva, anubhāva, sāttvika, vyabhicārin, sthāyin); (3) the five primary devotional rasas; (4) the seven secondary rasas and their incompatibilities.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Durgamasaṅgamanī](durgama-sangamani.md)

### 1.1.11 <a id="tea-bhaktirasamrtasindhu-1-1-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The highest devotion (uttamā bhakti) is the cultivation of (activity for) Kṛṣṇa in a favourable spirit, free of desire for anything else and not covered by knowledge (aiming at impersonal oneness), ritual works and the like.

> anyābhilāṣitā-śūnyaṃ jñāna-karmādy-anāvṛtam | ānukūlyena kṛṣṇānuśīlanaṃ bhaktir uttamā ||

### 2.1 <a id="tea-bhaktirasamrtasindhu-2-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Rūpa sets out the components of bhakti-rasa: the permanent emotion (sthāyibhāva, love for Kṛṣṇa) becomes rasa when combined with its excitants (vibhāva: the object, Kṛṣṇa and his devotees, and the stimuli), its outward effects (anubhāva), the involuntary bodily signs (sāttvika) and the transient emotions (vyabhicārin); Kṛṣṇa's sixty-four qualities are described, four unique to him.

### 3 <a id="tea-bhaktirasamrtasindhu-3"></a>
