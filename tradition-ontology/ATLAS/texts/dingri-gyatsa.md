Padampa Sangye's parting advice to the people of Dingri in about a hundred couplets, each ending with an address to the Dingri people: impermanence, the value of human life, renouncing worldly concerns, relying on the guru, practising now, and recognizing the mind.

### guru <a id="tea-dingri-gyatsa-guru"></a>

### impermanence <a id="tea-dingri-gyatsa-impermanence"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Life passes like dew on grass; wealth, relatives and this body are all left behind at death. People of Dingri, do not cling to them.

### karma <a id="tea-dingri-gyatsa-karma"></a>

### mind <a id="tea-dingri-gyatsa-mind"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

### practice-now <a id="tea-dingri-gyatsa-practice-now"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Having found a human life with its freedoms, practise the Dharma now; putting it off until later is deceiving oneself, for death gives no warning.
