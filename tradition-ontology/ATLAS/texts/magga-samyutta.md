## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 45.8 <a id="tea-magga-samyutta-45-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The noble eightfold path defined: right view is knowledge of the four truths; right intention is the intention of renunciation, non-ill-will and harmlessness; right speech the abstention from false, divisive, harsh speech and idle chatter; right action from killing, stealing and sexual misconduct; right livelihood the abandoning of wrong livelihood; right effort the four right strivings; right mindfulness the four establishments of mindfulness; right concentration the four jhānas.
