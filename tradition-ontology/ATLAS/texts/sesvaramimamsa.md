## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.1.1 <a id="tea-sesvaramimamsa-1-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Mīmāṃsā of ritual and the Mīmāṃsā of Brahman are one science with one purport; rightly read, the ritual texts presuppose the Lord as the one worshipped in all rites and the giver of their fruits.
