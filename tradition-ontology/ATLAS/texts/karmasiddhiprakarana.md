Vasubandhu's treatise establishing how karma works without enduring agents: action is volition, its result is carried by seeds in a mental series, and for the absorptions without mind a maturation-consciousness (vipāka-vijñāna, i.e. the ālaya) is required.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### ch.maturation-consciousness <a id="tea-karmasiddhiprakarana-ch-maturation-consciousness"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Bodily and verbal action are not real entities; action is volition, which leaves an impression in the mental series; since mind is interrupted in the mindless attainments, a maturation-consciousness holding the seeds must continue through them, and this is the ālaya-vijñāna.
