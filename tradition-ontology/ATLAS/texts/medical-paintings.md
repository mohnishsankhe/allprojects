# The medical paintings (thangkas) illustrating the Blue Beryl

**Availability:** digitized-translation

A series of painted scrolls commissioned by Desi Sangye Gyatso to illustrate the Blue Beryl: the medicine city, the medicine trees, embryology, anatomy and channels, the vital points, conduct and diet, the materia medica, instruments, pulse and urine diagnosis, and the signs of death.

_Notes: Not a text; included because the tradition treats the set as a teaching commentary._
