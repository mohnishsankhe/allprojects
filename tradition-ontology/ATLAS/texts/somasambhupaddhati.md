## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### diksa <a id="tea-somasambhupaddhati-diksa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Initiation is of three kinds: samaya-dīkṣā (qualifying the samayin for worship and study), viśeṣa-dīkṣā (the 'special' initiation), and nirvāṇa-dīkṣā (the liberating initiation, in which the guru purifies the six paths — kalā, tattva, bhuvana, varṇa, pada, mantra — and burns the disciple's bonds); it may be with 'seed' (post-initiatory observances binding) or 'seedless' (for children, the old, women, kings and the incapable); it is followed for some by consecration as ācārya or sādhaka.

### nitya <a id="tea-somasambhupaddhati-nitya"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The daily rite of the initiate: bath (with water and ash), twilight worship, then worship of Śiva: purification of the elements (bhūtaśuddhi), installing mantras in the body (nyāsa), inner worship, outer worship of Śiva in the liṅga with the five brahma-mantras and six limb-mantras, fire offering, and recitation.
