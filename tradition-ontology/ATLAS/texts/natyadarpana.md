A Jain dramaturgical treatise by two disciples of Hemacandra, notable for holding that rasa is of the nature of both pleasure and pain (sukha-duḥkhātmaka), against the view that every rasa is bliss.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3 <a id="tea-natyadarpana-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Rasa has the nature of both pleasure and pain (sukha-duḥkhātmaka): the erotic, comic, heroic, marvellous and peaceful are pleasant, the compassionate, furious, odious and terrible painful; spectators nonetheless enjoy the skill of the poet and actors.
