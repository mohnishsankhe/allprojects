## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 37 <a id="tea-culavamsa-37"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Buddhaghosa, a brahmin from near the Bodhi tree, was converted by the elder Revata, came to Lanka in the reign of Mahānāma, wrote the Visuddhimagga as a test, and was then given the Sinhala commentaries to render into Pali.
