## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 4-17 <a id="tea-lonakapalla-sutta-4-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As a lump of salt makes a small cup of water undrinkable but not the river Ganges, the same trivial evil deed leads one of undeveloped body, virtue, mind and wisdom to hell, while for one who is developed and limitless it ripens here only slightly, if at all. Were kamma to be experienced exactly as done, there would be no living of the holy life.
