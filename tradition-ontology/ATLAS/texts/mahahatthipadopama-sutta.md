## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 2 <a id="tea-mahahatthipadopama-sutta-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As the footprints of all walking creatures fit into an elephant's footprint, all wholesome states are included in the four noble truths.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation_

concepts: [The four noble truths (cattāri ariyasaccāni)](../concepts/four-noble-truths.md) · teachers: [Sāriputta](../teachers/sariputta.md)

### 6-27 <a id="tea-mahahatthipadopama-sutta-6-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The internal and external earth, water, fire and air elements are simply elements: 'this is not mine, I am not this, this is not my self'. Even the great external elements vanish; what then of this body that lasts but a while? If others abuse or strike him, the monk understands that painful feeling born of ear-contact has arisen, dependently arisen, and recollecting the Buddha, Dhamma and Saṅgha stays unshaken.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: all · types: body-layers, practice_

### 28.5-28.6 <a id="tea-mahahatthipadopama-sutta-28-5-28-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Whoever sees dependent origination sees the Dhamma; whoever sees the Dhamma sees dependent origination.

> “yo paṭiccasamuppādaṁ passati so dhammaṁ passati; yo dhammaṁ passati so paṭiccasamuppādaṁ passatī”ti.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: causal · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, ultimate_
