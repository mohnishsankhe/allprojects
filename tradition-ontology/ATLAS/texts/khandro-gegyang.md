## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### whole <a id="tea-khandro-gegyang-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Longchen Nyingthig Chöd: with the syllable phaṭ the consciousness leaves the body and appears in the sky as the wrathful black ḍākinī; the body left behind becomes a vast offering transformed into nectar and is shared in the feasts for the four kinds of guests; at the end one rests in the view in which giver, gift and recipient are not found. (Restricted practice (visualized body-offering): summary only, no procedure.)
