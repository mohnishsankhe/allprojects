## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### intro <a id="tea-setubandha-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Nityāṣoḍaśikārṇava and the Yoginīhṛdaya are the prior and latter 'four-hundreds' of one Vāmakeśvara tantra, and their unity of purport is beyond dispute: the nyāsas taught in the first are presupposed in the second, and what the second extracts is not given in the first. For those firm in inner worship the Yoginīhṛdaya's inner worship suffices even though few outer subsidiaries are stated.

### yh1.2 <a id="tea-setubandha-yh1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Secret' (guhya) means what lies in the cave: the identity of the individual self and the supreme self, as the Upaniṣads say; because worship has that as its fruit, it too is secret, and it is twofold, outer and inner.
