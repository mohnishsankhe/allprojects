## Teachings (6: skeleton 6)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-bahvrca-upanisad-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Goddess alone was in the beginning; she created the world-egg; she is known as Kāmakalā and as Śṛṅgārakalā; from her were born Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra, the Maruts, the celestial musicians and all beings of every kind.

> devī hy ekāgra evāsīt / saiva jagadaṇḍam asṛjat / kāmakaleti vijñāyate / śṛṅgārakaleti vijñāyate

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: world-fate, ultimate_

concepts: [Emanation in Śrīvidyā](../concepts/srividya-cosmogony.md), [Kāmakalā](../concepts/kamakala.md)

### 3 <a id="tea-bahvrca-upanisad-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

She is the supreme Śakti; she is the Śāmbhavī vidyā, called the Kādi vidyā, the Hādi vidyā or the Sādi vidyā — this is the secret.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: sound · stage: intermediate · types: sound-language, teacher-transmission_

### 4 <a id="tea-bahvrca-upanisad-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

She alone, pervading the three cities — the three bodies — illumines them outside and within; free of attachment to place, time and other things, Mahātripurasundarī is the inward consciousness.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, body-layers_

terms: [tripurā](../terms/tripura.md) · concepts: [The three bodies (śarīra-traya)](../concepts/three-bodies.md), [The three cities (tripura) and their triads](../concepts/tripura-triads.md)

### 4-9 <a id="tea-bahvrca-upanisad-4-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

She is Mahātripurasundarī, the inner consciousness; the great sayings ('prajñānaṃ brahma', 'ahaṃ brahmāsmi') are her; her vidyās are named — Ṣoḍaśī, Bālā, Mātaṅgī, Bhuvaneśvarī and others.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: intermediate · types: ultimate, sound-language_

concepts: [The Goddess as Brahman](../concepts/devi-as-brahman.md), [The four great sayings (mahāvākya)](../concepts/mahavakyas.md)

### 5 <a id="tea-bahvrca-upanisad-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

She alone is the self; what is other than her is unreal, not-self. She is the awareness of Brahman, free of the digits of being and non-being, the wave of being-consciousness-bliss; you and I and the whole universe and all the gods are Mahātripurasundarī; the one real thing called Lalitā is the non-dual, partless supreme Brahman.

> saivātmā tato 'nyad asatyam anātmā

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_
