## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1-3 <a id="tea-bahvrca-upanisad-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Goddess alone was in the beginning; she created the egg; from her were born Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra and all; she is the supreme power, the Self.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate, world-fate_

concepts: [The Goddess as Brahman](../concepts/devi-as-brahman.md)

### 4-9 <a id="tea-bahvrca-upanisad-4-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

She is Mahātripurasundarī, the inner consciousness; the great sayings ('prajñānaṃ brahma', 'ahaṃ brahmāsmi') are her; her vidyās are named — Ṣoḍaśī, Bālā, Mātaṅgī, Bhuvaneśvarī and others.
