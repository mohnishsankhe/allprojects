# Skambha hymns (Atharvaveda 10.7–8)

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Śaunaka śākhā (Atharvaveda)](../lineages/sakha-saunaka.md)

**Location in parent:** 10.7 (Skambha) and 10.8 (jyeṣṭha brahman)
**Availability:** digitized-original

Two long hymns asking in which limb of Skambha, the 'frame' or 'pillar' of the world, tapas, ṛta, vrata, faith and truth abide; they identify Skambha with the highest Brahman (jyeṣṭha brahman) to whom homage is paid, teach that those who know Brahman in man know the Supreme, and end with the self, desireless, wise, immortal, self-born, knowing whom one does not fear death.
