# Pāśupata Sūtra

`src:pasupata-sutra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Pāśupatasūtra, Pañcādhyāyī ('the five-chapter text', Yāmuna), Yogapāśupataśāstra (Kauṇḍinya's colophon)
**Original title:** पाशुपतसूत्र
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md), [Atimārga](../lineages/atimarga.md)
**Genre:** sūtra
**Authors:** 
  - [Lakulīśa](../teachers/lakulisa.md) — role: revealer; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Taught by the Lord (Bhagavat, Paśupati) who took a brahmin's body at Kāyāvataraṇa and taught Kuśika at Ujjayinī (Kauṇḍinya); later sources call the speaker Nakulīśa / Lakulīśa (Sarvadarśanasaṃgraha ch. 6).; scholarly: An anonymous sūtra text preserved only with Kauṇḍinya's commentary; composed before Kauṇḍinya. Its attribution to a historical Lakulīśa is the tradition's.; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: revealed by Śiva incarnate as Lakulīśa; Scholarly account: before Kauṇḍinya (4th–6th c.); often placed in the early centuries CE; (confidence low)
**Structure:** five adhyāyas of short sūtras - 44, 27, 26, 24 and 47 in the Sagar-based numbering (168 in all, with a lacuna at 5.29); 167 in the Sastri-based numbering. Each adhyāya closes with one of the five brahma-mantras (Sadyojāta, Vāmadeva, Aghora, Tatpuruṣa, Īśāna).
**Availability:** digitized-original

The root text of the Pāśupatas. Adhyāya 1 prescribes the first-stage observance in the temple (ash-bathing thrice daily, lying in ash, wearing the used garland, the six-limbed offering of laughter, song, dance, the ḍuṇḍuṅkāra sound, prostration and muttering) and lists the powers that arise; adhyāyas 2-4 teach worship of Rudra alone, courting dishonour by feigned madness and the transfer of merit and sin, and non-return; adhyāya 5 teaches the solitary yogin in empty houses, caves and cremation grounds, meditation on Oṃ in the heart, constant remembrance of Rudra and the end of suffering by the Lord's grace.
