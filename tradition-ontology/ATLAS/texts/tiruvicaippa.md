Hymns of nine poets forming the ninth Tirumuṟai — Tirumāḷikaittēvar, Cēntaṉār, Karuvūrttēvar, Pūnturutti Nampi Kāṭanampi, Kaṇṭarātittar, Vēṇāṭṭaṭikaḷ, Tiruvāliyamutaṉār, Puruṭōttama Nampi and Cētirāyar — many addressed to Naṭarāja at Chidambaram and to the Rājarājeśvaram at Tañcāvūr.

_Notes: The nine names are given at moderate confidence; identification of Karuvūrttēvar with the Siddhar Karuvūrār is traditional._
