A verse doxography. Its 'Śaiva' (= Yauga/Naiyāyika) section describes the ascetics' dress and greeting, lists the eighteen avatāras of Śaṅkara worshipped as tīrtheśas (Nakulīśa, Kauśika, Gārgya, Maitrya, Kauruṣa, Īśāna, Pāragārgya, Kapilāṇḍa, Manuṣyaka, Aparakuśika, Atri, Piṅgalākṣa, Puṣpaka, Bṛhadācārya, Agasti, Santāna, Rāśīkara, Vidyāguru), names Akṣapāda as their guru, and says bharaṭas of any varṇa serve as priests.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### saiva-section <a id="tea-saddarsanasamuccaya-rajasekhara-saiva-section"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Śaiva (Yauga) ascetics' god Śaṅkara has eighteen avatāras whom they worship at every step as tīrtheśas - from Nakulīśa, Kauśika, Gārgya, Maitrya and Kauruṣa to Rāśīkara (seventeenth) and Vidyāguru; Akṣapāda is their guru; bharaṭas of any varṇa who have devotion to Śiva take the vow and serve as priests in all their tīrthas.
