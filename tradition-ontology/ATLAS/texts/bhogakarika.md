## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-100 <a id="tea-bhogakarika-1-100"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Experience (bhoga) arises when the soul, its agency partly awakened by kalā and its knowing by vidyā, coloured by rāga and limited by kāla and niyati, meets objects through the intellect; experience is needed so that karma is consumed and mala ripens.
