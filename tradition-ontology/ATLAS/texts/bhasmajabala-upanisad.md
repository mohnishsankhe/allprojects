### 1 <a id="tea-bhasmajabala-upanisad-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Bhusuṇḍa Jābāla asks Mahādeva about ash: its preparation with homa and mantras, its application, and the fruit.

### 2 <a id="tea-bhasmajabala-upanisad-2"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

At Avimukta Śiva teaches the dying the tāraka; the Śaiva mantras (the five- and six-syllable 'oṃ namaḥ śivāya') are praised as the means.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language, death-dying_
