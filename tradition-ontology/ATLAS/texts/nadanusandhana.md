A short dialogue in which Śiva teaches the unstruck inner sound in ten forms (ciṇī, ciñciṇī, ciri, conch, strings, a ringing, bronze cymbals, cloud, roaring water, kettle-drum) — the first nine to be left and the tenth giving liberation — and correlates the sounds with the elements and their maṇḍalas and with the dissolution of the principles.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1-5 <a id="tea-nadanusandhana-1-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Śiva teaches a proof as sweet as nectar that gives immediate conviction: the unstruck sound in ten forms — first 'ciṇī', second 'ciñciṇī', third 'ciri', fourth the conch, fifth the sound of strings, sixth a ringing, seventh bronze cymbals, eighth the cloud, ninth the roar of water and tenth the kettle-drum; abandoning the nine, the tenth gives liberation; without striking, nāda makes this tenfold sound, emerging in the opening of the ear.

### 7-13 <a id="tea-nadanusandhana-7-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The sounds are correlated with the elements and their maṇḍalas: the 'ciṇī' sound with earth, square and yellow, giving freedom from cold and disease; the conch and related sounds with water in its crescent; the flute-like sounds with fire in its triangle; the bronze and cloud sounds with air in its hexagon; the kettle-drum with space; attention through them brings the dissolution of the principles in order.
