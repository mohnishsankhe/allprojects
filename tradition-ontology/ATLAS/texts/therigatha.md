### 1.1 <a id="tea-therigatha-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Sleep in comfort, little nun, well wrapped in the robe you made; your passion has been stilled like herbs dried in a pot.

> “Sukhaṁ supāhi therike, Katvā coḷena pārutā; Upasanto hi te rāgo, Sukkhaḍākaṁ va kumbhiyan”ti.

### 1.12 <a id="tea-therigatha-1-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

One who is eager, resolved, pervaded by mind, whose mind is not bound to sensual pleasures, is called 'one who goes upstream'.

> “Chandajātā avasāyī, Manasā ca phuṭā siyā; Kāmesu appaṭibaddhacittā, Uddhaṁsotāti vuccatī”ti.

### 3.5 <a id="tea-therigatha-3-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Ubbirī, weeping in the charnel ground for her daughter Jīvā, is told that 84,000 women named Jīvā have been burned there — which one does she mourn? The dart is drawn from her heart; grief is dispelled.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: general · stage: realized · types: death-dying, karma-liberation_

### 3.8 <a id="tea-therigatha-3-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Somā: what does womanhood matter when the mind is well concentrated and knowledge flows, for one who rightly sees into the Dhamma; delight is everywhere destroyed, the mass of darkness is torn.

> “Yaṁ taṁ isīhi pattabbaṁ, ṭhānaṁ durabhisambhavaṁ; Na taṁ dvaṅgulapaññāya, sakkā pappotumitthiyā”. “Itthibhāvo no kiṁ kayirā, cittamhi susamāhite; Ñāṇamhi vattamānamhi, sammā dhammaṁ vipassato. Sabbattha vihatā nandī, Tamokhandho padālito; Evaṁ jānāhi pāpima, Nihato tvamasi antakā”ti.

### 5.9 <a id="tea-therigatha-5-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Bhaddā Kuṇḍalakesā, once a debater with hair plucked out, seeing faults in the faultless and none in the faulty, met the Buddha on Vulture's Peak; he said 'Come, Bhaddā' and that was her ordination; for fifty years she wandered free of debt, eating the country's alms.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: general · stage: realized · types: teacher-transmission_

### 5.10 <a id="tea-therigatha-5-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Paṭācārā: seeing the water she washed her feet with flow down the slope, she concentrated her mind like a thoroughbred horse; taking a lamp she entered her cell, looked at the bed, sat, and with a needle drew down the wick: at the lamp's going out her mind was liberated.

### 6.3 <a id="tea-therigatha-6-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Khemā answers Māra, who tempts her with youth and music: this rotting body disgusts me; sensual pleasures are like swords and stakes; ignorance torn apart, I once worshipped stars and fire in the forest, not knowing; now I worship the Awakened One and am freed from all suffering.

_level: conventional · standpoint: polemical · path: general · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation, dispute_

obstacles: [Māra](../obstacles/mara.md) · teachers: [Khemā](../teachers/khema.md)

### 6.6 <a id="tea-therigatha-6-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī's homage to the Buddha: all suffering is understood, craving dried up, the eightfold path developed and cessation touched; I was mother, son, father, brother, grandmother before; Māyā gave birth to Gotama for the sake of many; see the disciples in harmony — that is the homage to the Buddhas.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation, teacher-transmission_

teachers: [Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī](../teachers/mahapajapati-gotami.md)

### 10.1 <a id="tea-therigatha-10-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Kisāgotamī: the Sage praised good friendship; painful is the state of women, said the tamer of men — co-wives, childbirth, some who cut their throats; on the road giving birth she found her husband dead, her children dead, her family burned; she developed the eightfold path leading to the deathless, realised nibbāna, and looked into the mirror of the Dhamma.

### 11.1 <a id="tea-therigatha-11-1"></a>

Uppalavaṇṇā: seeing the danger in sensual pleasures she went forth; she knows past lives, has the divine eye, knows others' minds, and has psychic power; to Māra she replies that even a hundred thousand rogues would not shake her, she is master of her mind and has destroyed the mass of darkness.
