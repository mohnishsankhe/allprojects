The verses of the elder nuns: 73 poems (522–524 verses) by or about arahant nuns — Paṭācārā, Kisāgotamī, Khemā, Uppalavaṇṇā, Mahāpajāpatī, Ambapālī, Sumedhā — on grief, renunciation, meditation and release; the earliest collection of women's religious poetry in India.
