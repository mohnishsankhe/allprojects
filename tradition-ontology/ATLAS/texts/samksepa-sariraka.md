## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1.319 <a id="tea-samksepa-sariraka-1-319"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Partless, pure consciousness alone is what possesses the status of being both the locus and the object (of ignorance).

> āśrayatvaviṣayatvabhāginī nirvibhāgacitir eva kevalā

### creation <a id="tea-samksepa-sariraka-creation"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The scriptural accounts of creation are provisional superimpositions (adhyāropa), taught only in order to be withdrawn (apavāda), to lead to the attributeless Brahman.
