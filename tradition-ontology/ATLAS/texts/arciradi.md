## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-arciradi-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The surrendered soul, leaving the body, is led by the Lord along the path of light through the worlds of the escorting deities, crosses the Virajā river, sheds the subtle body, receives a divine form and is welcomed in Vaikuṇṭha to eternal service.
