The verse to Savitṛ in the gāyatrī metre: 'we meditate on that excellent radiance of the god Savitṛ; may he impel our thoughts (dhī)'. Prefixed with Oṃ and the three vyāhṛtis (bhūr bhuvaḥ svaḥ) as in VS 36.3, it is the mantra given at initiation (upanayana) and repeated in the twilight worship; the tradition calls it the mother of the Vedas.
