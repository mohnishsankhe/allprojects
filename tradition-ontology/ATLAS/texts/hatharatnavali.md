A haṭha manual that often argues with the Haṭhapradīpikā: it teaches eight cleansing acts (aṣṭakarma, adding cakrī and gajakaraṇī), the retentions and ten mudrās, names eighty-four postures and describes a selection, and treats the channels, centres, the inner sound and samādhi. It holds that mantra, laya, haṭha and rāja are one 'great yoga'.

### 1.topic.astakarma <a id="tea-hatharatnavali-1-topic-astakarma"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The cleansing acts are eight, not six: cakrī, nauli, dhauti, neti, basti, gajakaraṇī, trāṭaka and mastakabhrānti (kapālabhāti).
