A verse manual (c. 580 verses) in dialogue form: the rarity of human birth, the four qualifications, approaching a teacher, the teacher's exposition of the three bodies and five sheaths, the self as witness, māyā and its powers, the three vāsanās, meditation and nirvikalpa samādhi, prārabdha, and the marks of the jīvanmukta.

### 22-27 <a id="tea-vivekacudamani-22-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The six virtues defined: calm (śama) is resting the mind on its goal after detaching it from objects; self-control (dama) is turning the organs back to their sources; withdrawal (uparati) is the mind's not depending on external things; forbearance (titikṣā) is enduring all afflictions without seeking redress and without anxiety; faith (śraddhā) is firm trust in the words of scripture and teacher; concentration (samādhāna) is fixing the intellect steadily on Brahman; longing for liberation (mumukṣutva) is the desire to be freed from bondage by knowing one's true nature.
