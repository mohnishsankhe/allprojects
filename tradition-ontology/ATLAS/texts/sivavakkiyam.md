About five hundred verses in a swift eight-foot metre, the most radical of the Siddhar songs: the Lord dwells within; the planted stone does not speak; temples and tanks are within the mind; caste has no basis in flesh and bone; Vedic recitation and ritual without knowledge are empty; one famous verse denies that the dead are reborn.

### four-vedas <a id="tea-sivavakkiyam-four-vedas"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Reciting the four Vedas, bathing in sacred waters and performing rites do not bring one to the Lord who is within; without knowing the One in the body, all such learning is empty.

### karanta-pal <a id="tea-sivavakkiyam-karanta-pal"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Milk once drawn does not return to the udder, churned butter does not return to the buttermilk, the sound of the broken conch and the life do not return to the body, fallen flowers and fruit do not go back to the tree: the dead are not born again — no, no, no.

Running and running, searching and searching for the light that is mingled within, the days pass; withering and withering, crores upon crores of men have died.
