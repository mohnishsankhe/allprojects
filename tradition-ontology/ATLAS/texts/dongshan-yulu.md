## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 513c10 <a id="tea-dongshan-yulu-513c10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In the age of the final Dharma many have dry wisdom; to test true and false there are three leaks: the leak of view (the function does not leave its position and falls into a sea of poison), the leak of feeling (stuck in likes and dislikes, one-sided view), the leak of words (seeking the wondrous and losing the tenet).

> 末法時代人多乾慧，若要辨驗真偽，有三種滲漏

### 525c01 <a id="tea-dongshan-yulu-525c01"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The five ranks of lord and minister in verse: the apparent within the real (midnight before the moon shines — meeting without recognising); the real within the apparent (an old woman who overslept meets an ancient mirror); coming from within the real (a road out of nothingness, beyond the dust); arriving within both (crossed swords, a lotus in fire); arrived within both (not falling into being or non-being, returning to sit among the coals).

> 正中偏，三更初夜月明前，莫怪相逢不相識，隱隱猶懷舊日嫌

### 525c09 <a id="tea-dongshan-yulu-525c09"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The five ranks of merit: turning toward, serving, merit, shared merit, merit of merits — asked what each is, Dongshan answers with everyday acts ('when eating', 'when putting down the hoe').

> 向時作麼生？奉時作麼生？功時作麼生？共功時作麼生？功功時作麼生
