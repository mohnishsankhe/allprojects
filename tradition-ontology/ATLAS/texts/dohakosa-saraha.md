# Dohākoṣa of Saraha (the 'People Dohā')

`src:dohakosa-saraha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Dohākoṣagīti, do ha mdzod kyi glu (Tibetan), mi'i do ha (the 'People' Dohā)
**Language:** Apabhraṃśa, Tibetan
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Genre:** dohā (songs in couplets)
**Authors:** 
  - [Saraha](../teachers/saraha.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: sung by Saraha; scholarly: a collection that grew over time; the Apabhraṃśa and Tibetan recensions differ in order and content; confidence: moderate
**Structure:** the Derge Tibetan (Tōh 2224) prepared as 136 four-line stanzas
**Availability:** digitized-original

Saraha's Treasury of Songs: a critique of Brahmins, Śaiva ascetics, Jain monks, Buddhist monks and scholars who miss the innate; the innate is found through the true guru, is the Buddha dwelling in the body, and is neither thing nor no-thing; mind itself is the seed of saṃsāra and nirvāṇa.

`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]
