## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-sadacaradipika-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

(Quoting the Varāha:) The wearing of (Viṣṇu's) emblems is of two kinds, heated and with clay; heated emblems are to be worn at set times of the year and at the thread ceremony, in the presence of the great teacher.
