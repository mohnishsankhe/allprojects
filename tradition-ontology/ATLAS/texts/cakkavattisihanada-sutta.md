Be an island to yourselves, roaming in your own pasture (the four satipaṭṭhānas); the tale of the wheel-turning kings, the decline of human lifespan through the neglect of Dhamma, its renewal, and the coming of the Buddha Metteyya.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1 <a id="tea-cakkavattisihanada-sutta-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Live as islands to yourselves with the Dhamma as island; roam in your own pasture, the domain of your fathers — the four establishments of mindfulness — and Māra will find no opening.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice_

concepts: [Oneself as island, the Dhamma as island](../concepts/attadipa.md) · practices: [The four establishments of mindfulness (satipaṭṭhāna)](../practices/satipatthana.md)

### 10-24 <a id="tea-cakkavattisihanada-sutta-10-24"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When the king neglects to give wealth to the needy, poverty arises, then theft, violence, lying and the other wrongs, and human lifespan and beauty decline to ten years; after a 'sword-interval' people turn back to wholesome conduct and lifespan rises again.

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: action · stage: all · types: world-fate, ethics_

concepts: [Decline and renewal of human life](../concepts/decline-and-renewal.md)

### 25 <a id="tea-cakkavattisihanada-sutta-25"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When human life reaches eighty thousand years, the Blessed One Metteyya will arise in the world, perfected and fully awakened, leading a Saṅgha of many thousands of monks.

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: devotion · stage: all · types: world-fate, teacher-transmission_

concepts: [Metteyya, the future Buddha](../concepts/future-buddha-metteyya.md) · teachers: [Metteyya](../teachers/metteyya.md)

### 28 <a id="tea-cakkavattisihanada-sutta-28"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

For a monk, long life means the four bases of psychic power; beauty means virtue; happiness the four jhānas; wealth the four immeasurables; power the destruction of the effluents.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice, powers-experiences_

terms: [iddhipāda](../terms/iddhipada.md) · concepts: [Basis of power (iddhipāda)](../concepts/iddhipada.md)
