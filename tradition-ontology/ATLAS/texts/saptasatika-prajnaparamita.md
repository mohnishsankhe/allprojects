## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### ekavyuha <a id="tea-saptasatika-prajnaparamita-ekavyuha"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The single-practice samādhi (ekavyūha-samādhi): one who, in a quiet place, sits facing the direction of a buddha, fixes the mind on that one buddha and recites his name without break, sees in that one buddha all buddhas of past, present and future, and knows the sameness of the dharmadhātu.
