## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 395c09 <a id="tea-zhengdao-ge-395c09"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Have you not seen the leisurely person of the Way, beyond learning and without contrivance, who neither removes deluded thoughts nor seeks the true? The real nature of ignorance is buddha-nature; this illusory, empty body is the Dharma body.

> 絕學無為閒道人。 不除妄想不求真。無明實性即佛性。 幻化空身即法身

### 396a10 <a id="tea-zhengdao-ge-396a10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Walking too is Chan, sitting too is Chan; in speaking and silence, moving and still, the substance is at ease; even facing swords one is calm, even given poison one is at leisure.

> 行亦禪坐亦禪。 語默動靜體安然
