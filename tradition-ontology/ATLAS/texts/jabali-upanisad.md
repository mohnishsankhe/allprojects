## Teachings (2: sourced 2)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-jabali-upanisad-1-2"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Paippalādi asks Jābāli about the supreme; the teaching came from Īśāna through Ṣaḍānana; Paśupati is the all-knowing Lord who does the five acts; the jīva, bound by ego, is the paśu.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate, teacher-transmission_

concepts: [Paśu and Paśupati](../concepts/pasu-pasupati.md) · teachers: [Jābāli](../teachers/jabali.md), [Paippalādi](../teachers/paippaladi.md), [Skanda / Guha](../teachers/skanda.md)

### 3-4 <a id="tea-jabali-upanisad-3-4"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Beings are called paśus because they eat grass, lack discrimination, are driven by others and are bound; they are freed by wearing ash and the tripuṇḍra.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ritual · path: ritual · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, practice_

concepts: [Paśu and Paśupati](../concepts/pasu-pasupati.md) · practices: [Tripuṇḍra (three lines of ash)](../practices/tripundra.md), [Ash-bath (bhasma-snāna)](../practices/bhasma-snana.md)
