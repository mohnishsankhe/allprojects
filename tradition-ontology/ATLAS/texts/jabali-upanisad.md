## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-jabali-upanisad-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Paippalādi asks Jābāli about the supreme; the teaching came from Īśāna through Ṣaḍānana; Paśupati is the all-knowing Lord who does the five acts; the jīva, bound by ego, is the paśu.

### 3-4 <a id="tea-jabali-upanisad-3-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Beings are called paśus because they eat grass, lack discrimination, are driven by others and are bound; they are freed by wearing ash and the tripuṇḍra.
