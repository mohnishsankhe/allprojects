# Tiruvaḷḷuvar ñāṉam (the wisdom of Tiruvaḷḷuvar)

**Language:** Tamil
**Family:** vedic

**Genre:** kaṭṭaḷaik kalittuṟai, veṇpā
**Part of:** [Cittar pāṭalkaḷ (the songs of the Siddhars)](siddhar-padalgal.md)

  - [Tiruvaḷḷuvar](../teachers/tiruvalluvar.md) — role: author; attribution: doubtful
**Dates:** Scholarly account: undetermined; the Siddhar song corpus is usually placed c. 1400–1800; (confidence low)
**Structure:** 1 invocation + 19 verses (local e-text)
**Availability:** digitized-original

Nineteen verses ascribed to Tiruvaḷḷuvar as a Siddhar: invocation of Agastya and the coiled power; the body's impermanence; knowledge of the whirl (cuḻi) and the root; the Veda's essence as ghee in milk; kāya-siddhi removing karma; salt, 'muppu' and the vāci breath making the body endure.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Cittar pāṭalkaḷ tokuppu I (Project Madurai 1999; GRETIL Devanāgarī transliteration)

### 4 <a id="tea-tiruvalluvar-nanam-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

O men who rise in this world, what becomes of the stir you took for real in your life? Having lived without doubt, adorned with manliness — this life called a lie ends in the cremation ground.

> वैयमदि लेयुदिक्कु माण्बर्गऩे युङ्गळुयिर् / मॆय्यॆऩ् ऱिरुन्दसैवु वॆळिप्पडुव तॆऩ्ऩविदम्? / अय्यमिल्ला वाऴ्न्दुलगिल् आण्मैयाय्प् पूण्डमदिप् / पॊय्यॆऩुमिव् वाऴ्क्कैयदु पोगुञ्जुडु काडुळदे!

### 10 <a id="tea-tiruvalluvar-nanam-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

For those who know wisdom there is no Yama; when wisdom is seen, body, life and frame become strong; if you drink, drink the essence of nectar (amirta racam).

> ञाऩमऱिऩ् तोर्क्कु नमऩिल्लै नाळ्दोऱुम् / पाऩमदै युण्डु पसियिऩाल् - ञाऩमदु / कण्डाल् उडलुयि रुङ्गायम् वलुवागुम् / उण्डाल् अमिर्दरस मुण्।

### 12 <a id="tea-tiruvalluvar-nanam-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

If the Veda and scripture become true knowledge and inner experience, by the Lord's grace the (highest) state is reached; of the meanings of the four Vedas, see the good meaning as ghee is to milk.

> वेद मऱैञाऩ मॆय्युणर्वु ताऩागिल् / नादऩरु ळाल्बदवि नाडुमे - वेदमऱै / ऩालु पॊरुळुळ् नऱ्पॊरुळिऩ् आऱ्ऱियप् / पालुमदु नॆय्यॆऩवुम् पार्।

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: intermediate · types: dispute, karma-liberation_

### 13 <a id="tea-tiruvalluvar-nanam-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Burning the fate that was there from the first in the three voids, he no longer sought good or bad deeds and sent off the life of hard karma.

### 14 <a id="tea-tiruvalluvar-nanam-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

After perfection of the body (kāyacitti) removed his karma, the power over illusion and the root-whirl came within reach; the first initiation by kāyacitti came through the 'root tamarind'; see it within two years.

### 17 <a id="tea-tiruvalluvar-nanam-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _restricted: summary only_

When the dross of salt changes by steeping, before it becomes muppu-calx — by that salt the body is made strong for crores of aeons, the body abiding in the vāci that flows with the word.

### kappu <a id="tea-tiruvalluvar-nanam-kappu"></a>
