## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 4.99 <a id="tea-mandukya-karika-bhasya-sankara-4-99"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

On 'this was not spoken by the Buddha': the knowledge of the enlightened one, like space, does not go out to other things, nor do the selves; the ultimate reality — non-dual, free of the distinction of knowledge, known and knower, like space, unchanging, partless, eternal — was not taught by the Buddha. Although the rejection of external objects and the view that there is only cognition were said to be close to the non-dual reality, this ultimate non-dual reality is to be known only in the Vedānta (the Upaniṣads).

> ज्ञानज्ञेयज्ञातृभेदरहितं परमार्थतत्त्वमद्वयम् एतन्न बुद्धेन भाषितम् । यद्यपि बाह्यार्थनिराकरणं ज्ञानमात्रकल्पना चाद्वयवस्तुसामीप्यमुक्तम् । इदं तु परमार्थतत्त्वमद्वैतं वेदान्तेष्वेव विज्ञेयमित्यर्थः ।
