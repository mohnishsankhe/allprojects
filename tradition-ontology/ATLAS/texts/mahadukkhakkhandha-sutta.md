## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 7-36 <a id="tea-mahadukkhakkhandha-sutta-7-36"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The gratification in sensual pleasures is the pleasure arising from the five cords of sensuality; the danger is toil, loss, quarrels, war, punishment and a bad destiny; the escape is the removal of desire for them. Likewise for forms (a beautiful girl who ages and becomes a corpse) and feelings (even the jhānas are impermanent).
