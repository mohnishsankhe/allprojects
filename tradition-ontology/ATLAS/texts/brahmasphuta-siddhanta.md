## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1.7-9 <a id="tea-brahmasphuta-siddhanta-1-7-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Four million three hundred twenty thousand solar years make a caturyuga, with the twilights and twilight-portions for each of Kṛta and the rest; a tenth of the yuga multiplied by four is Kṛta, by three Tretā, by two Dvāpara, by one Kali. Āryabhaṭa's statement that the four quarters of the yuga, Kṛta and the rest, are equal — not one of them agrees with what the smṛti says.

> yugapādān āryabhaṭaś catvāri samāni kṛtayugādīni | yad abhihitavān na teṣāṃ smṛtyuktasamānam ekam api ||

### 11.17 <a id="tea-brahmasphuta-siddhanta-11-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

If the earth moved a minute of arc in a prāṇa, from where and along what path would it go? If the earth rotated, why do lofty things not fall?
