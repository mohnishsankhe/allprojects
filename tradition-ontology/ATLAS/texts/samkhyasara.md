Vijñānabhikṣu's manual of Sāṃkhya, written to supply what the Kārikā left unsaid and to summarize his Sāṃkhya Bhāṣya; it opens by saluting Viṣṇu as the Lord, the self-born 'sprout of the world' called mahat, and presents liberation as the direct realization of the discrimination of self and non-self.
