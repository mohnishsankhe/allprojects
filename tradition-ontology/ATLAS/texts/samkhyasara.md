## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1.1.1 <a id="tea-samkhyasara-1-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Salutation to Viṣṇu, the Self of all, all-conquering — the Lord, the self-born one called mahat, the sprout of the world.

> mahadākhyaḥ svayambhūr yo jagadaṅkura īśvaraḥ / sarvātmane namas tasmai viṣṇave sarvajiṣṇave

### 1.1.2-4 <a id="tea-samkhyasara-1-1-2-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Kārikā discriminated the self-principle only briefly and mostly gathered the Sāṃkhya method; so this work sets out, briefly, what it left unsaid, having explained prakṛti and the rest at length in the Sāṃkhya Bhāṣya. Liberation comes from direct realization of the discrimination of self and non-self, through which all identifications such as agency cease.
