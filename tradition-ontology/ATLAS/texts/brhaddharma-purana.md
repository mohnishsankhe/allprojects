A Bengali Upapurāṇa in three khaṇḍas: its Madhyakhaṇḍa tells how Satī, refused leave by Śiva to attend Dakṣa's sacrifice, reveals herself as the root Nature and shows him her ten Mahāvidyā forms in the ten directions (madhya 6); it also treats the Gaṅgā, the ages and dharma.

### madhya.6.78-87 <a id="tea-brhaddharma-purana-madhya-6-78-87"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Śiva, seeing Satī dark and fearsome, asks who she is; Satī: 'I am the subtle Nature, born to Dakṣa as golden-limbed to win you, the supreme Person; when you three — Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva — were born, I came to you in the form of a corpse; the other two ignored me in that deformed shape, you alone took me, so I am yours. When I hear you blamed I must abandon this body.'

### madhya.6.98-124 <a id="tea-brhaddharma-purana-madhya-6-98-124"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Satī explains creation: seeing that creation would not occur, the Goddess divided the Person into jīva and the supreme; then the root Nature made herself threefold — māyā, vidyā and the supreme eternal one; deluding Mahāmāyā sets creation going, while as vidyā she told the three Persons to create, protect and dissolve; she made Brahmā the creator, Viṣṇu the protector and Śiva the destroyer; 'I, without the three guṇas, shine through the one with guṇas; I took refuge in you, Śiva, and in Brahmā and Viṣṇu by a portion'; the root Nature is subtler even than she.

### madhya.6.125-134 <a id="tea-brhaddharma-purana-madhya-6-125-134"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'See these ten goddesses, my forms, called the Mahāvidyās: Kālī, Tārā, Ṣoḍaśī, Bhuvaneśvarī, Bhairavī, Chinnamastā, Sundarī, Bagalāmukhī, Dhūmāvatī and Mātaṅgī.' Kālī stands naked before him, dark Tārā in the sky above, Chinnamastā to the right, Bhuvaneśvarī to the left, Bagalāmukhī behind, Dhūmāvatī in the south-east, Sundarī in the south-west, Mātaṅgī in the north-west, Ṣoḍaśī in the north-east, and Satī herself as Bhairavī in his body; all the Mahāvidyās give liberation to worshippers and accomplish the magical acts they desire; this is secret and not to be revealed.
