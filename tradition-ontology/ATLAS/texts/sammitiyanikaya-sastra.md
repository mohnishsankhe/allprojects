## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### person <a id="tea-sammitiyanikaya-sastra-person"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The treatise sets out the arguments of those who deny a self and replies that the person exists, being designated by its support (the aggregates), by its passing from life to life, and by its cessation (in nirvāṇa); it is neither identical with the aggregates nor different from them.
