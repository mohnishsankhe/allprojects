The Contemplation Sūtra: Queen Vaidehī, imprisoned by her son Ajātaśatru, asks for a world without sorrow; the Buddha shows her the pure lands and teaches sixteen contemplations — the setting sun, water, the ground, the trees, ponds, towers, the lotus throne, the images, Amitābha's body, Avalokiteśvara, Mahāsthāmaprāpta, one's own birth there, and the three grades each of upper, middle and lower birth (nine grades); even the lowest evil-doer who at death calls 'namo Amitābha Buddha' ten times is born in a lotus there.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1-13 <a id="tea-amitayurdhyana-sutra-1-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Sixteen contemplations taught to Vaidehī: (1) the setting sun, (2) water becoming ice and lapis ground, (3) the ground, (4) the jewel trees, (5) the ponds, (6) the jewel towers, (7) the lotus throne, (8) the images of the Buddha and bodhisattvas, (9) the true body of Amitāyus, (10) Avalokiteśvara, (11) Mahāsthāmaprāpta, (12) oneself born in a lotus there, (13) the mixed contemplation of the small and great bodies.

### 8 <a id="tea-amitayurdhyana-sutra-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When you contemplate the Buddha, this very mind is the thirty-two marks and eighty minor marks; this mind makes the Buddha, this mind is the Buddha; the ocean of the buddhas' right and universal knowledge arises from the mind.

> 是心作佛，是心是佛。

### 14 <a id="tea-amitayurdhyana-sutra-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Those who arouse three minds — the sincere mind, the deep mind, and the mind that dedicates merit and aspires to be born — will be born there; with these three they are born in the highest grade of the highest birth.

> 一者、至誠心。二者、深心。三者、迴向發願心。

### 16 <a id="tea-amitayurdhyana-sutra-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The lowest of the low grade: a person who has done the five grave offences and the ten evil deeds, about to fall into hell, meets at death a good friend who tells him to call the name; unable to think of the Buddha, he says 'namo Amitābha Buddha' ten times with a sincere voice; with each recitation the offences of eighty koṭis of kalpas are removed, and he is born in a golden lotus, which opens after twelve great kalpas.

> 具足十念，稱南無阿彌陀佛。
