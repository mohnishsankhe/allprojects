### 8 <a id="tea-amitayurdhyana-sutra-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When you contemplate the Buddha, this very mind is the thirty-two marks and eighty minor marks; this mind makes the Buddha, this mind is the Buddha; the ocean of the buddhas' right and universal knowledge arises from the mind.

> 是心作佛，是心是佛。

### 9 <a id="tea-amitayurdhyana-sutra-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ninth contemplation, of Amitāyus's true body: he has eighty-four thousand marks, each with eighty-four thousand minor marks, each with eighty-four thousand rays of light; each ray shines throughout the worlds of the ten directions and embraces, never abandoning, the beings who recollect the Buddha. One who sees this sees all buddhas; this is the samādhi of recollecting the Buddha; seeing the Buddha's body one sees the Buddha's mind, which is great compassion.

> 無量壽佛有八萬四千相；一一相中，各有八萬四千隨形好；一一好中復有八萬四千光明；一一光明遍照十方世界，念佛眾生攝取不捨。

### 14 <a id="tea-amitayurdhyana-sutra-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Those who arouse three minds — the sincere mind, the deep mind, and the mind that dedicates merit and aspires to be born — will be born there; with these three they are born in the highest grade of the highest birth.

> 一者、至誠心。二者、深心。三者、迴向發願心。

### 16 <a id="tea-amitayurdhyana-sutra-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The lowest of the low grade: a person who has done the five grave offences and the ten evil deeds, about to fall into hell, meets at death a good friend who tells him to call the name; unable to think of the Buddha, he says 'namo Amitābha Buddha' ten times with a sincere voice; with each recitation the offences of eighty koṭis of kalpas are removed, and he is born in a golden lotus, which opens after twelve great kalpas.

> 具足十念，稱南無阿彌陀佛。

### closing <a id="tea-amitayurdhyana-sutra-closing"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Merely hearing the names of the Buddha and the two bodhisattvas removes the sins of countless kalpas, how much more recollecting them. One who recollects the Buddha is a white lotus (puṇḍarīka) among people; Avalokiteśvara and Mahāsthāmaprāpta are his friends; he will sit at the seat of awakening. The Buddha tells Ānanda: hold these words well — to hold these words is to hold the name of Amitāyus.

### closing <a id="tea-amitayurdhyana-sutra-closing-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Hearing this, Vaidehī and her five hundred attendants at once see the vast Land of Bliss, the Buddha's body and the two bodhisattvas; rejoicing, she is suddenly awakened and gains the acceptance of the non-arising of dharmas; the attendants arouse the aspiration for awakening and are foretold birth there.

> 韋提希與五百侍女，聞佛所說，應時即見極樂世界廣長之相，得見佛身及二菩薩。心生歡喜，歎未曾有，豁然大悟，得無生忍。

### intro.1 <a id="tea-amitayurdhyana-sutra-intro-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Vaidehī, imprisoned by her son, throws herself down weeping before the Buddha: what past sin made her bear such a son? She begs him to teach her of a place without sorrow where she may be born, for she no longer delights in this defiled and evil Jambudvīpa full of hells, hungry ghosts and animals.

> 唯願世尊，為我廣說無憂惱處，我當往生，不樂閻浮提濁惡世也。

### intro.2 <a id="tea-amitayurdhyana-sutra-intro-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Shown all the pure lands of the ten directions in the Buddha's light, Vaidehī chooses to be born in the Land of Bliss of Amitābha and asks to be taught how to think on it and how to receive it rightly.

### intro.3 <a id="tea-amitayurdhyana-sutra-intro-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Amitābha is not far from here. Those who wish to be born there should cultivate three meritorious acts: caring for parents, serving teachers, not killing out of compassion, practising the ten good deeds; taking the three refuges, keeping the precepts, not breaching deportment; arousing the aspiration for awakening, deeply believing in cause and effect, reciting the Mahāyāna sūtras and encouraging others. These are the pure acts that are the right cause of the buddhas of the three times.

> 欲生彼國者，當修三福：一者、孝養父母，奉事師長，慈心不殺，修十善業。二者、受持三歸，具足眾戒，不犯威儀。三者、發菩提心，深信因果，讀誦大乘，勸進行者。如此三事名為淨業。

### intro.4 <a id="tea-amitayurdhyana-sutra-intro-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Buddha tells Vaidehī: you are an ordinary being, your mind and thought are weak, you lack the divine eye and cannot see far; the Tathāgatas have special means to let you see. She asks how beings after the Buddha's passing, defiled and pressed by the five sufferings, will see Amitābha's land.

> 汝是凡夫，心想羸劣，未得天眼，不能遠觀；諸佛如來有異方便，令汝得見。
