## Teachings (6: skeleton 6)

### 5-6 <a id="tea-mahasaccaka-sutta-5-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

Saccaka, asked what 'development of the body' means, names Nanda Vaccha, Kisa Saṅkicca and Makkhali Gosāla: they go naked, lick their hands, refuse food brought or prepared for them or invitations, and eat at fixed intervals of days; pressed by the Buddha, he admits they also at times eat rich foods and so rebuild the body.

### 17-20 <a id="tea-mahasaccaka-sutta-17-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Three similes occurred to the bodhisatta: as a wet sappy log in water, or on land, cannot be made to give fire, those who do not live withdrawn in body and mind from sensual pleasures cannot attain awakening, whether they feel painful feelings or not; only those withdrawn, like a dry log away from water, are capable of it.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: practice_

concepts: [The middle way](../concepts/middle-way.md)

### 20-30 <a id="tea-mahasaccaka-sutta-20-30"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

The bodhisatta then practised forcibly subduing mind with mind, the breathless meditation and severe reduction of food, experiencing extreme painful feelings and emaciation, yet these did not overpower his mind; he concluded that no ascetic has felt more pain, but that by this gruelling practice he did not attain superhuman knowledge and vision, and asked whether there might be another path to awakening.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: body-breath · stage: advanced · types: practice, dispute_

concepts: [The middle way](../concepts/middle-way.md) · disputes: [Is suffering ended by burning off past kamma through austerity, and which action is most blameworthy?](../debates/austerity-and-past-kamma.md)

### 31-33 <a id="tea-mahasaccaka-sutta-31-33"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

He recalled sitting in the cool shade of a jambu (rose-apple) tree while his father the Sakyan was working, and entering the first jhāna; recognising 'that is the path to awakening', he saw that he need not fear that pleasure, which has nothing to do with sensual pleasures; he took solid food to regain strength, and the five monks left him in disgust.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice, powers-experiences_

terms: [jhāna](../terms/jhana.md), [sukha](../terms/sukha.md) · concepts: [The four jhānas](../concepts/four-jhanas.md), [The middle way](../concepts/middle-way.md)

### 34-44 <a id="tea-mahasaccaka-sutta-34-44"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

He entered the four jhānas and, with the mind concentrated, attained the three knowledges in the three watches of the night; the pleasant and painful feelings that arose did not invade his mind.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation, knowledge · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation, powers-experiences_

concepts: [The three true knowledges (tevijjā)](../concepts/three-vijjas.md), [The four jhānas](../concepts/four-jhanas.md)

### 48 <a id="tea-mahasaccaka-sutta-48"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Saccaka says he has debated Pūraṇa Kassapa, Makkhali Gosāla, Ajita, Pakudha, Sañjaya and the Nigaṇṭha, and each dodged the issue, changed the subject and showed anger, whereas the Buddha's complexion only brightens under attack.
