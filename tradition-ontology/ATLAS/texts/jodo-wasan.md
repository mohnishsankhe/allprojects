## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### genze-riyaku <a id="tea-jodo-wasan-genze-riyaku"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

When one says Namu Amida Butsu, the benefits in this present life are boundless: evil karma of transmigration is removed, untimely death is averted, the gods of heaven and earth and the guardian kings protect the person day and night, the demons are afraid.

### shinjin-buddha-nature <a id="tea-jodo-wasan-shinjin-buddha-nature"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Those who rejoice in shinjin are said to be equal to the Tathāgatas; great shinjin is buddha-nature, buddha-nature is the Tathāgata.

_level: bridging · standpoint: divine · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate, consciousness-mind_
