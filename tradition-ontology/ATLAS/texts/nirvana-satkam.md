## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-nirvana-satkam-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

I am not mind, intellect, ego or memory; not the ears, tongue, nose or eyes; not space, earth, fire or air; I am of the form of consciousness and bliss — I am Śiva, I am Śiva.

> mano buddhy ahaṅkāra cittāni nāhaṃ na ca śrotrajihve na ca ghrāṇanetre / na ca vyomabhūmir na tejo na vāyuḥ cidānandarūpaḥ śivo 'haṃ śivo 'ham
