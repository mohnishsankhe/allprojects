**Availability:** digitized-original

one of the thirty-seven works of Madhva collected as the Sarvamūla ('root of all'); a long verse work that re-expounds the Brahmasūtras adhikaraṇa by adhikaraṇa, supplementing the Brahmasūtrabhāṣya with extended argument against Advaita, Buddhist, Jain, Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika, Sāṃkhya and other positions; it defends the reality of the world through the witness (sākṣin) and ends with Madhva's verse naming himself Vāyu's third form. Jayatīrtha's Nyāyasudhā is its great commentary.
