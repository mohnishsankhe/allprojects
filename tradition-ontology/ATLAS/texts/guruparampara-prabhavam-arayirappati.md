The Maṇipravāḷa hagiography of the Āḻvārs and ācāryas attributed to Piṉpaḻagiya Perumāḷ Jīyar: the source of the stories of Nāthamuni's recovery of the Prabandham, Yāmuna's three folded fingers, Rāmānuja's teachers and the six sayings of Varadarāja.

## Teachings (6: skeleton 6)

### Kūreśa <a id="tea-guruparampara-prabhavam-arayirappati-kuresa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

When a Cōḻa king demanded that Rāmānuja sign that nothing is higher than Śiva, Kūreśa went in his teacher's place, disguised, refused, and was blinded; Periya Nambi, taken with him, died.

### Nāthamuni <a id="tea-guruparampara-prabhavam-arayirappati-nathamuni"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Nāthamuni, hearing the Tiruvāymoḻi decad ending 'these ten of the thousand', sought the whole; at Kurukūr he recited Madhurakavi's Kaṇṇinuṇ Ciṟuttāmpu twelve thousand times and Nammāḻvār appeared to him and gave him all four thousand hymns with their meanings, which he arranged and set to music.

### Tirukkōṭṭiyūr Nambi <a id="tea-guruparampara-prabhavam-arayirappati-tirukkottiyur"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

After making Rāmānuja come eighteen times, Tirukkōṭṭiyūr Nambi taught him the secret meaning of the tirumantra, charging him to keep it secret; Rāmānuja proclaimed it to all from the temple tower, saying that if he went to hell for disobedience, so many would be saved, and his teacher embraced him as 'Emperumāṉār'.

### Yajñamūrti <a id="tea-guruparampara-prabhavam-arayirappati-yajnamurti"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Advaita renouncer Yajñamūrti debated Rāmānuja at Śrīraṅgam for eighteen days; on the eighteenth, after Rāmānuja prayed to the Lord, he conceded and became Rāmānuja's disciple as Aruḷāḷa Perumāḷ Emperumāṉār.

### Yāmuna <a id="tea-guruparampara-prabhavam-arayirappati-yamuna"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

When Yāmuna died before Rāmānuja could meet him, three fingers of his hand remained folded; Rāmānuja vowed to write a commentary on the Brahma Sūtras, to honour Parāśara and Vyāsa, and to honour Nammāḻvār, and the fingers unfolded.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, death-dying_

teachers: [Yāmunācārya](../teachers/yamuna.md), [Rāmānuja](../teachers/ramanuja.md)

### the six sayings of Varadarāja <a id="tea-guruparampara-prabhavam-arayirappati-six-sayings"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Through Tirukkacci Nambi, Varadarāja of Kāñcī answered Rāmānuja's doubts with six sayings: I am the supreme reality; difference is the true doctrine; surrender (prapatti) is the means; the surrendered need no last remembrance at death; liberation follows at the end of this body; take Periya Nambi as your teacher.
