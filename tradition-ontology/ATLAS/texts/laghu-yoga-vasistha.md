# Laghu Yoga Vāsiṣṭha

`src:laghu-yoga-vasistha` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Yogavāsiṣṭhasāra (sometimes), Mokṣopāyasāra (scholarly identification, low)
**Original title:** लघुयोगवासिष्ठ
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Genre:** upadeśa
**Authors:** 
  - [Abhinanda (Gauḍa Abhinanda)](../teachers/abhinanda.md) — role: compiler; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: an abridgement of the Yoga Vāsiṣṭha made by (Gauḍa) Abhinanda of Kashmir; scholarly: a Kashmiri abridgement (c. 10th-11th c.) of the Mokṣopāya/Yoga Vāsiṣṭha; earlier scholars sometimes took it to be older than the full text; confidence: low
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 10th-11th c. CE (Kashmir); (confidence low)
**Structure:** about 6,000 verses in the same six prakaraṇas
**Availability:** unknown

A condensed version (about a fifth of the whole) of the Yoga Vāsiṣṭha keeping its six books, main stories and doctrines of mind, effort, the seven stages and liberation while living; it circulated widely and was translated into Persian in the Mughal period.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Vāsiṣṭhacandrikā](vasistha-candrika.md)

_Notes: The Persian 'Jūg Bāsisht' made for Prince Salīm (1597) is recalled as based on the Laghu version (low); commentaries: Ātmasukha's Vāsiṣṭhacandrikā (low)._
