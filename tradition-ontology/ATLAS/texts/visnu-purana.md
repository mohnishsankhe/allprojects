# Viṣṇu Purāṇa

**Alternate titles:** Vaiṣṇava, Viṣṇu Mahāpurāṇa
**Original title:** विष्णुपुराण
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Genre:** purāṇa
**Authors:** 
  - [Parāśara](../teachers/parasara.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
  - [Vyāsa (Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana)](../teachers/vyasa.md) — role: compiler; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Spoken by Parāśara to Maitreya (VP 1.1), the narrative of the Vārāha-kalpa (Matsya 53.16-17); within the eighteen compiled by Vyāsa.; scholarly: One of the most unified Purāṇas; closely matches the classical five marks; Book 5 parallels the Harivaṃśa.; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Parāśara's teaching; Vyāsa's compilation at the end of Dvāpara; Scholarly account: commonly placed in the first half of the 1st millennium CE (estimates vary widely); (confidence low)
**Structure:** 6 aṃśas (books), 126 chapters in the common vulgate; c. 23,000 verses by the traditional count (BhP 12.13.4) but c. 6,000-7,000 in the extant text
**Availability:** digitized-original

The model Purāṇa of the five marks: creation from pradhāna and puruṣa as forms of Viṣṇu, Bhū-maṇḍala and the worlds and hells, manvantaras and the Vyāsas, varṇāśrama and śrāddha, the solar and lunar dynasties, Kṛṣṇa's life, the Kali age and the four dissolutions, and the yoga of Keśidhvaja with Viṣṇu as its support.
