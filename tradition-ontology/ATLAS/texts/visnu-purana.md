## Teachings (39: skeleton 39)

### 1.2 <a id="tea-visnu-purana-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Viṣṇu, the supreme Brahman, exists in four forms: primal matter (pradhāna), spirit (puruṣa), the manifest (vyakta) and time (kāla); time is the form by which pradhāna and puruṣa are joined and separated in creation and dissolution.

### 1.2.66-67 <a id="tea-visnu-purana-1-2-66-67"></a>

> sṛṣṭisthityantakaraṇīṃ brahmaviṣṇuśivātmikām / sa saṃjñāṃ yāti bhagavān eka eva janārdanaḥ
