# The eighteen scriptures of the mind series (sems sde bco brgyad)

**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md), [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)

**Part of:** [Nyingma Gyubum (rnying ma rgyud 'bum, the Collected Tantras of the Ancients)](nyingma-gyubum.md)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The canonical set of mind-series scriptures: five early translations by Vairocana (Cuckoo of Awareness, Great Potency, Soaring Garuḍa, Gold Refined from Ore, Never-Falling Banner) and thirteen later translations by Vimalamitra with Yudra Nyingpo (among them Rtse mo byung rgyal, Nam mkha'i rgyal po, Bde ba 'phra bkod, Rdzogs pa spyi chings, Byang chub sems tig, Bde ba rab 'byams, Srog gi 'khor lo, Thig le drug pa, Rdzogs pa spyi spyod, Yid bzhin nor bu, Kun 'dus rig pa, Rje btsan dam pa, Sgom pa don grub).
