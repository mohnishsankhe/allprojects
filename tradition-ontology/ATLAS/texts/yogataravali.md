A short poem on rājayoga reached through absorption in the inner sound: nādānusandhāna is the foremost of the layas, the three bandhas and spontaneous (kevala) retention lead to the mindless state (amanaska) and the natural state (sahaja), in which the yogin is freed from the world while living.
