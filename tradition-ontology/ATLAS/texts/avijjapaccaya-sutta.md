# Avijjāpaccayā Sutta

The view 'the soul is the body' and the view 'the soul is one thing, the body another' both make the holy life impossible; the Tathāgata teaches by the middle, dependent origination.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-avijjapaccaya-sutta-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If one holds 'the soul is the same as the body' there is no living of the holy life; if one holds 'the soul is one thing, the body another' there is no living of the holy life. Avoiding both extremes the Tathāgata teaches by the middle: with ignorance as condition, formations, and so on.
