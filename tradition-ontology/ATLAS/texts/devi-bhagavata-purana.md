# Devī Bhāgavata Purāṇa

The Goddess as the supreme Brahman and Mother of the Trimūrti: her revelation to Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva in Maṇidvīpa, her avatāras and battles, the Navarātra vow, Prakṛti's forms, the Devī Gītā on yoga and knowledge, sadācāra and Gāyatrī, and the description of her island-paradise Maṇidvīpa.

## Teachings (4: sourced 4)

### 1.3 <a id="tea-devi-bhagavata-purana-1-3"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

The list of the eighteen Purāṇas given by the Devī Bhāgavata (with the mnemonic 'madvayaṃ bhadvayaṃ caiva bratrayaṃ vacatuṣṭayam ...'), in which the 'Bhāgavata' is understood as the Goddess's Bhāgavata and the Vāyu is counted.

### 3.3-6 <a id="tea-devi-bhagavata-purana-3-3-6"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva are carried in a celestial car to Maṇidvīpa, see the Goddess, find themselves turned into women in her presence, and are taught that she is the one Brahman from whom they receive their powers of creation, maintenance and destruction.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: divine · path: devotion, knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate, world-fate_

concepts: [The Goddess as Mahāmāyā and the supreme](../concepts/devi-as-mahamaya.md), [Maṇidvīpa and Śrīpura](../concepts/manidvipa.md), [The three forms (Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra)](../concepts/trimurti.md)

### 3.26 <a id="tea-devi-bhagavata-purana-3-26"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

The Navarātra vow of the Goddess: worship for nine nights in autumn (and spring), with the worship of young girls (kumārī-pūjā), is taught to Janamejaya as bestowing all aims.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ritual · path: ritual, devotion · stage: all · types: practice_

terms: [navarātra](../terms/navaratra.md) · practices: [The Navarātri observance](../practices/navaratri-vrata.md)

### 12.10-12 <a id="tea-devi-bhagavata-purana-12-10-12"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Maṇidvīpa, the island of jewels beyond the worlds, the Goddess's highest abode, is described with its many walls of metals and gems, its guardians and her palace, the Cintāmaṇi-gṛha, where she sits with Śiva.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: devotion, meditation · stage: all · types: world-fate, karma-liberation_
