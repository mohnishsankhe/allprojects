# Devī Bhāgavata Purāṇa

**Alternate titles:** Śrīmad Devī Bhāgavatam, Devībhāgavata
**Original title:** देवीभागवतपुराण
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

  - [Vyāsa (Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana)](../teachers/vyasa.md) — role: compiler; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Vyāsa's Bhāgavata of the Goddess; Śākta tradition counts it as the Mahāpurāṇa 'Bhāgavata' of the lists, the Viṣṇu Bhāgavata being an Upapurāṇa (see dsp:which-bhagavata-is-the-mahapurana); told to Janamejaya.; scholarly: A Śākta Purāṇa usually treated as an Upapurāṇa by modern scholars; draws on the Brahmavaivarta (Book 9) and the Devī Māhātmya.; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Vyāsa, end of Dvāpara; Scholarly account: c. 11th-14th c. CE (estimates vary); (confidence low)
**Structure:** 12 skandhas, 318 chapters, c. 18,000 verses
**Availability:** digitized-original

The Goddess as the supreme Brahman and Mother of the Trimūrti: her revelation to Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva in Maṇidvīpa, her avatāras and battles, the Navarātra vow, Prakṛti's forms, the Devī Gītā on yoga and knowledge, sadācāra and Gāyatrī, and the description of her island-paradise Maṇidvīpa.
