The standard diagnostic manual, compiled from the sayings of many sages (1.2): the five means of knowing a disease — cause, prodrome, manifest signs, response to trial therapy and pathogenesis (1.4) — then disease by disease.

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1.1-3 <a id="tea-madhava-nidana-1-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Bowing to Śiva, cause of the world's origin, maintenance and dissolution, door to heaven and liberation, refuge of the three worlds, this 'Determination of Disease' with its complications, fatal signs, causes and signs is compiled briefly from the words of various sages at the request of good physicians, so that physicians of little intelligence without many tantras may easily know disease.

### 1.4 <a id="tea-madhava-nidana-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Cause (nidāna), prodromal signs (pūrvarūpa), manifest signs (rūpa), response to trial (upaśaya) and pathogenesis (samprāpti) — the knowledge of diseases is fivefold.

> nidānaṃ pūrvarūpāṇi rūpāṇyupaśayastathā | samprāptiśceti vijñānaṃ rogāṇāṃ pañcadhā smṛtam

### 1.5-10 <a id="tea-madhava-nidana-1-5-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Nidāna is called by the synonyms occasion, cause, basis, condition, origin; the prodrome is the unmanifest sign of an impending disease not yet fixed in a particular doṣa; the same become manifest is the form (rūpa); upaśaya is the agreeable use of medicine, food and conduct contrary to cause or disease (or acting contrarily) — it is suitability (sātmya), its opposite unsuitability; samprāpti is the way the disease comes into being by the doṣa vitiated and spreading.
