Vijñānabhikṣu's extensive commentary on the Sāṃkhya Sūtra, reading Sāṃkhya as compatible with a theistic Vedānta: the sūtras' denial that Īśvara is established is treated as a concessive argument, and Sāṃkhya is presented as the discipline of discrimination within a wider scriptural whole.
