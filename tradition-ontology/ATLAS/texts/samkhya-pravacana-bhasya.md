## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### introduction (bhūmikā) <a id="tea-samkhya-pravacana-bhasya-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Vijñānabhikṣu criticises the doctrine of the 'modern' (ādhunika) self-styled Vedāntins — that difference is unreal and the world māyā — as not the purport of the Brahmasūtras, and, quoting the Padma Purāṇa's verses on māyāvāda as 'Buddhism in disguise', counts it among heterodox teachings; he himself teaches non-separation (avibhāga) of the selves from the Lord with real distinction.
