Āpadeva's introductory manual of Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā ('Āpadevī'): the Veda as injunction, mantra, name, prohibition and arthavāda, the kinds of injunction, the means of determining subsidiarity and sequence, and eligibility. It opens with a salutation to Govinda and closes by teaching that dharma done as an offering to Govinda becomes a cause of the highest good.

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### mangala <a id="tea-mimamsanyayaprakasa-mangala"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Salutation to Govinda, affectionate to devotees, by a mere particle of whose grace the four aims of life are attained.

> yatkṛpāleśamātreṇa puruṣārthacatuṣṭayam | prāpyate tamahaṃ vande govindaṃ bhaktavatsalam ||

_level: unmarked · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate_

### upasamhara <a id="tea-mimamsanyayaprakasa-upasamhara"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

This dharma, performed with the aim for which it is enjoined, is the cause of that; performed with the thought of offering it to Śrī Govinda, it is the cause of the highest good — the Gītā verse on offering all acts to the Lord being the authority.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: action, devotion · stage: all · types: karma-liberation_
