### 1-8 <a id="tea-kundika-upanisad-1-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

After studentship, marriage and the forest, one renounces with the permission of kin; the fires are abandoned; he takes initiation and ochre, and the strainer to save creatures in water.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ritual · path: ritual · stage: advanced · types: practice, ethics_

### 9-10 <a id="tea-kundika-upanisad-9-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

He may keep water-pot, drinking-bowl, sling, a three-legged stand, sandals, patched cloth, loincloth, strainer, bathing cloth and upper cloth, and should avoid anything beyond these.
