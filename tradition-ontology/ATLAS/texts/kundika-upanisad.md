## Teachings (6: sourced 6)

### 1-8 <a id="tea-kundika-upanisad-1-8"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

After studentship, marriage and the forest, one renounces with the permission of kin; the fires are abandoned; he takes initiation and ochre, and the strainer to save creatures in water.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ritual · path: ritual · stage: advanced · types: practice, ethics_

concepts: [The rite of renunciation](../concepts/sannyasa-rite.md) · practices: [The renunciation rite (saṃnyāsa-dīkṣā)](../practices/sannyasa-diksa.md)

### 9-10 <a id="tea-kundika-upanisad-9-10"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

He may keep water-pot, drinking-bowl, sling, a three-legged stand, sandals, patched cloth, loincloth, strainer, bathing cloth and upper cloth, and should avoid anything beyond these.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: advanced · types: ethics_

concepts: [The renouncer's marks (liṅga)](../concepts/renunciant-marks.md)

### 11-13 <a id="tea-kundika-upanisad-11-13"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

He wanders, lives on alms, sleeps on sand by rivers, is indifferent to praise and blame.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: advanced · types: ethics_

practices: [Wandering (pārivrājya) and the rains retreat](../practices/parivrajya.md)

### 14-17 <a id="tea-kundika-upanisad-14-17"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

'I am the Lord, destroyer of hell and of the cities, the self of all, without a lord over me' — the renouncer's declaration of identity.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_

### 18-22 (summary) <a id="tea-kundika-upanisad-18-22-summary"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low · _restricted: summary only_

A yogic departure at death through the crown is described; recorded summary-only.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: death-dying, practice_

### 23-28 <a id="tea-kundika-upanisad-23-28"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

The Self is the witness, untouched by the states and the body.
