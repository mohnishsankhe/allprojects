# Eight Verses of Mind Training (blo sbyong tshig brgyad ma)

**Alternate titles:** Blo sbyong tshig brgyad ma, Eight Verses for Training the Mind
**Language:** Tibetan
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md), [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md)
**Genre:** mind-training verses
**Authors:** 
  - [Langri Tangpa Dorje Senge](../teachers/langri-tangpa.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
**Dates:** Scholarly account: late 11th – early 12th c. CE; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** eight four-line verses
**Availability:** digitized-translation

Langri Tangpa's eight verses: holding all beings dear as more precious than a wish-fulfilling jewel, regarding oneself as lowest, confronting afflictions at once, cherishing the difficult, taking defeat and offering victory, seeing the one who harms as teacher, secretly sending and taking, and keeping all this free of the eight worldly concerns while seeing all as illusion.
