# Caraṇavyūha

`src:caranavyuha` · `sourced` · confidence low

**Original title:** चरणव्यूहः
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md)
**Genre:** pariśiṣṭa (lists of śākhās)
**Attribution:** tradition: Śaunaka (or Kātyāyana); scholarly: late compilation; its counts of śākhās are traditional; confidence: low
**Availability:** digitized-original

A short treatise listing the branches (śākhās, caraṇas) of the four Vedas and their extent — e.g. the five Ṛgveda śākhās (Śākala, Bāṣkala, Āśvalāyana, Śāṅkhāyana, Māṇḍūkāyana) — ascribed to Śaunaka or Kātyāyana; the Atharvaveda has its own Caraṇavyūha among its Pariśiṣṭas.
