**Alternate titles:** Araṇyegeyagāna, Āraṇyakagāna
**Original title:** आरण्यगेयगानम्
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Kauthuma śākhā (Sāmaveda)](../lineages/sakha-kauthuma.md), [Rāṇāyanīya śākhā (Sāmaveda)](../lineages/sakha-ranayaniya.md)
**Genre:** gāna (song-book)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The song-book of melodies 'to be sung in the forest', regarded as more powerful and secret, learned apart from the village; it includes the melodies of the Āraṇyaka section.
