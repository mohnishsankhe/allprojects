Uddyotakara's sub-commentary defending the Nyāya Sūtra and Bhāṣya against Dignāga and other Buddhist critics ('bad reasoners'); it enumerates the six kinds of sense-object contact, defends the self, the whole, universals and Īśvara, and criticizes Buddhist definitions of perception and inference.
