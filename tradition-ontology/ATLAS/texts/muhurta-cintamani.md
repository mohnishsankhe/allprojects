Rāma Daivajña's standard manual of electional timing in 13 sections: good and bad times (tithis, weekdays, their lords and classes); the asterisms and their classes; solar ingresses; transits; the sacraments (saṃskāra); marriage with the eightfold matching; the bride's entry; the second coming; establishing the fires; royal consecration; journeys; building; and entering a house.

### 1.3-4 <a id="tea-muhurta-cintamani-1-3-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The lords of the lunar days (from the first) are Fire, Brahmā, Gaurī, Gaṇeśa, the Serpent, Guha, the Sun, Śiva, Durgā, Yama, the All-gods, Hari, Kāma, Śiva and the Moon. The lunar days fall into five classes — nandā, bhadrā, jayā, riktā and pūrṇā — which in the bright fortnight are inauspicious, middling and good (by thirds of the month) and in the dark fortnight the reverse; with Friday, Wednesday, Tuesday, Saturday and Thursday respectively they give success.

### 2.2-8 <a id="tea-muhurta-cintamani-2-2-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The three Uttaras, Rohiṇī and Sunday are fixed (dhruva): for sowing, houses, pacification and gardens. Svātī, Punarvasu, Śravaṇa and the two after it, with Monday, are movable (cara): for riding and journeys. The three Pūrvas, Bharaṇī and Maghā, with Tuesday, are fierce (ugra): for killing, fire and deceit. Viśākhā and Kṛttikā, with Wednesday, are mixed. Hasta, Aśvinī, Puṣya and Abhijit, with Thursday, are swift (kṣipra): for trade, love, learning, ornaments and arts. Mṛgaśiras, Revatī, Citrā and Anurādhā, with Friday, are soft (mṛdu): for song, dress, play and friendship. Mūla, Jyeṣṭhā, Ārdrā and Āśleṣā, with Saturday, are sharp (tīkṣṇa): for spells of harm, killing, splitting and taming animals.

### 6.21 <a id="tea-muhurta-cintamani-6-21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

(In matching for marriage) caste (varṇa), control (vaśya), star (tārā), animal-type (yoni), friendship of the lords (grahamaitra), temperament (gaṇa), sign-relation (bhakūṭa) and channel (nāḍī) — each of these carries more points than the one before.
