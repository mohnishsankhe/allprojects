### 33 <a id="tea-mahavamsa-33"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

It relates that King Vaṭṭagāmaṇī Abhaya built the Abhayagiri monastery, and that in his time the monks, fearing the loss of the oral tradition, had the canon and commentary written down.
