In a sleepless night before the war, Vidura counsels Dhṛtarāṣṭra on wise and foolish conduct, the faults to be abandoned, policy, truth, forgiveness and non-violence, the golden rule, the self as a sacred river, and the fate of a man after death — his relatives turn back from the pyre and only his deeds follow him.
