Eisai's defence (1198) of Zen against the Tendai establishment: Zen is the source of all Buddhist teachings and was part of Saichō's own heritage; strict observance of the precepts is its foundation (against the Daruma school); promoting Zen protects the state.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### precepts <a id="tea-kozen-gokoku-ron-precepts"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Zen takes the precepts as its foundation: one who does not keep the precepts cannot practise Zen; the Daruma school's claim that there are no precepts to keep and no practice to do is wrong view.
