## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### deathbed-three-essentials <a id="tea-yinguang-wenchao-deathbed-three-essentials"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Three essentials for the dying: first, skilfully instruct and comfort the dying person so that right faith arises; second, let family and friends recite the Buddha's name in shifts to support the dying one's pure recollection; third, strictly avoid moving the body or weeping, lest the dying one be disturbed and lose right mindfulness.

### summary-of-teaching <a id="tea-yinguang-wenchao-summary-of-teaching"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Fulfil your human relations and duties, guard against evil and keep sincerity, do no evil and practise all good; truly for the sake of birth-and-death arouse the aspiration for awakening, and with deep faith and vow hold the Buddha's name.
