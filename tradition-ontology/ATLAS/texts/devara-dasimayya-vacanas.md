Vacanas signed Rāmanātha, reckoned by the tradition among the earliest; they speak of Śiva's gift of the world, of the devotee's work, and deny that the self is male or female.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### vacana of the split bamboo (incipit not recalled) <a id="tea-devara-dasimayya-vacanas-v-bamboo-and-fire"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Cut a tall bamboo in two; make the bottom a woman and the top a man; rub them together till they burn — tell me, is the fire that is born male or female, O Rāmanātha?

### vacana on the self as neither man nor woman (incipit not recalled) <a id="tea-devara-dasimayya-vacanas-v-breasts-and-long-hair"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

If they see breasts and long hair coming they call it woman; if beard and whiskers, man; but the self that hovers between is neither man nor woman, O Rāmanātha.
