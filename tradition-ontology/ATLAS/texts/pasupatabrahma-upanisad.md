Vaiśravaṇa asks Brahmā (Svayambhū): the inner sacrifice of the haṃsa, the praṇava as thread, Paśupati as the witness who empowers the senses, and non-dual knowledge; non-injury as the true sacrifice; purity of food purifies mind.

## Teachings (5: sourced 5)

### 1.1-5 <a id="tea-pasupatabrahma-upanisad-1-1-5"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Vaiśravaṇa, son of Brahmā and a Vālakhilya, asks Svayambhū about the supreme; Oṃ's measures and the three deities — Rudra (tamas), Viṣṇu (sattva), Brahmā (rajas) — are explained.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, sound-language_

teachers: [Vaiśravaṇa (Vālakhilya)](../teachers/vaisravana.md), [Brahmā (as first teacher)](../teachers/brahma.md)

### 2.18-19 <a id="tea-pasupatabrahma-upanisad-2-18-19"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Māyā exists only from the standpoint of empirical usage; in truth there is none.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_

### 2.36-37 <a id="tea-pasupatabrahma-upanisad-2-36-37"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Purity of food brings purity of mind; the knower sees 'I am the food and the eater'.

### 1 (ahiṃsā) <a id="tea-pasupatabrahma-upanisad-1-ahimsa"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

Non-injury is the sacrifice of dharma; the paramahaṃsa is its priest.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: beginner · types: ethics_

concepts: [The inner sacrifice](../concepts/inner-sacrifice.md)

### 1 (inner sacrifice) <a id="tea-pasupatabrahma-upanisad-1-inner-sacrifice"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Rudra-Paśupati is the doer of the inner sacrifice; its fire is the haṃsa 'so'ham', its thread the praṇava; attention to nāda is the rite; without meditation on haṃsa and praṇava there is no liberation.
