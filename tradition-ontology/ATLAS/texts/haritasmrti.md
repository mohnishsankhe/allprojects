# Hārīta Dharmasūtra / Hārītasmṛti (fragments)

`src:haritasmrti` · `sourced` · confidence low

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)
**Genre:** dharmasūtra (fragments)
**Availability:** partly-lost

An old Dharma text of Hārīta, lost and known from quotations (later metrical Laghu- and Vṛddha-Hārīta texts exist); it is often cited for the distinction between women who are 'speakers of Brahman' (brahmavādinī) and those who marry directly (sadyovadhū).

_Notes: The brahmavādinī/sadyovadhū quotation is known through later digests; wording not verified here._
