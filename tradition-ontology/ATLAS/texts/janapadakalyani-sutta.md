## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-3 <a id="tea-janapadakalyani-sutta-2-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A man must carry a bowl brimful of oil between a crowd and the country's beauty queen while a man with a drawn sword follows, ready to cut off his head if he spills a drop: the bowl of oil is mindfulness directed to the body.
