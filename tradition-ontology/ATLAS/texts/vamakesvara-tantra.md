### 1.13-22 <a id="tea-vamakesvara-tantra-1-13-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Goddess says to Bhairava: you have revealed to me all mantras and the sixty-four tantras of the Mothers, the best — Mahāmāyā, Śambara, Yoginījālaśambara, Tattvaśambara, the eight Bhairava tantras, the eight Bahurūpas, the eight Yāmalas, Candrajñāna, Vāsuki, Mahāsammohana, Mahocchuṣma, Vātula, Vātulottara, Hṛdbheda, Tantrabheda, Guhyatantra, Kāmika, Kalāvāda, Kalāsāra, Kubjikāmata, Tantrottara, Vīṇākhya, Trotala, Trotalottara, Pañcāmṛta, Rūpabheda, Bhūtoḍḍāmara, Kulasāra, Kuloḍḍīśa, Kulacūḍāmaṇi, Sarvajñānottara, Mahākālīmata, Mahālakṣmīmata, Siddhayogeśvarīmata, Kurūpikāmata, Devarūpikāmata, Sarvavīramata, Vimalāmata, the Eastern, Western, Southern, Northern and Niruttara, Vaiśeṣika, Jñāna, Vīrāvali, Aruṇeśa, Mohinīśa and Viśuddheśvara — these and crores of other śāstras, all made of knowledge, have been told by you.

### 4.12-16 <a id="tea-vamakesvara-tantra-4-12-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Tripurā goes to the supreme Person, markless, without qualities, devoid of the form of kula; then, free in her own form, taking the Lord of the world to herself and pleased by that path, she, alone, sports by herself — unmanifest Tripurā come to manifestation.

### nityas <a id="tea-vamakesvara-tantra-nityas"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Around Tripurasundarī, the sixteenth, are the fifteen Nityās, each with her mantra, form and rite, worshipped in the triangle of the cakra.

### sricakra <a id="tea-vamakesvara-tantra-sricakra"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The tantra teaches how the Śrīcakra is drawn — the central point, the triangles, the eight- and sixteen-petalled lotuses and the square — and how it is worshipped enclosure by enclosure with its deities, placements (nyāsa) and ten mudrās.
