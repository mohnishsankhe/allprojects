# Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad

**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Śaunaka śākhā (Atharvaveda)](../lineages/sakha-saunaka.md)

**Availability:** digitized-original

Aṅgiras teaches Śaunaka the higher knowledge by which the imperishable is grasped, above the lower knowledge of the Vedas and their limbs; sacrifices are unsafe boats; one must go to a teacher fuel in hand; all comes from the imperishable like sparks from fire; Om is the bow, the self the arrow, brahman the target; the two birds on one tree; truth alone conquers; renunciants purified by saṃnyāsa-yoga are released.

### 1.2.12-13 <a id="tea-mundaka-upanisad-1-2-12-13"></a>
