## Teachings (3: sourced 3)

### 1-6 <a id="tea-parabrahma-upanisad-1-6"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Śaunaka asks Pippalāda; the one seeking liberation bears the inner topknot and thread, the ritualist householder the outer; the haṃsa is the topknot, the praṇava the thread, nāda the union.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ethics, ultimate_

### 9 <a id="tea-parabrahma-upanisad-9"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

### 14 <a id="tea-parabrahma-upanisad-14"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low
