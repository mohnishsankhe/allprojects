one of the thirty-seven works of Madhva collected as the Sarvamūla ('root of all'); the sixth of the ten short treatises (daśa-prakaraṇa); it an eleven-verse enumeration of all reality: independent (Viṣṇu) and dependent; the dependent as positive and negative; sentient beings by their relation to sorrow and fitness for liberation; the insentient as eternal, eternal-and-non-eternal, and non-eternal; and Viṣṇu as the agent of creation, sustenance, dissolution, control, ignorance, knowledge, bondage and release of all of it.

## Teachings (6: skeleton 6)

### 1 <a id="tea-tattvasankhyana-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Reality (tattva) is held to be of two kinds, independent and dependent. The independent is the Lord Viṣṇu; the other, the dependent, is of two kinds, positive (bhāva) and negative (abhāva).

> svatantram asvatantraṃ ca dvividhaṃ tattvam iṣyate | svatantro bhagavān viṣṇuḥ bhāvābhāvau dvidhetarat ||1||

### 2 <a id="tea-tattvasankhyana-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Negation is of three kinds — prior non-existence, destruction and constant non-existence; positive being is of two kinds, sentient and insentient.

### 3-4 <a id="tea-tattvasankhyana-3-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The sentient is of two kinds: untouched by sorrow and touched by sorrow. Ramā (Lakṣmī) is eternally untouched by sorrow; all others are touched by it. Those touched by sorrow are either liberated or still in sorrow; those in sorrow are either fit for liberation or unfit.

### 5-7 <a id="tea-tattvasankhyana-5-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The liberated are of five kinds — gods, sages, ancestors, rulers and humans — and so are those fit for liberation. Those unfit for liberation are of two kinds: those going to darkness and those who remain in transmigration. Those fit for darkness are of four kinds — daityas, rākṣasas, piśācas and the lowest of humans — and they are further of two kinds: those who have reached blinding darkness and those still in transmigration.

### 7-10 <a id="tea-tattvasankhyana-7-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The insentient is of three kinds by the division of eternal and non-eternal. The Vedas are eternal; the Purāṇas and the like, time and prakṛti are eternal-and-non-eternal; the non-eternal is of two kinds, uncompounded and compounded. The uncompounded are the mahat, the I-principle (aham), buddhi, manas, the ten senses, the (five) subtle elements and the five elements; the compounded is the cosmic egg and all that is within it.

`skeleton` · confidence high

Creation, maintenance, dissolution, control, ignorance, awakening, bondage, release, happiness, sorrow, covering and light — of this whole (dependent reality) these come from Viṣṇu, in brief and in detail.
