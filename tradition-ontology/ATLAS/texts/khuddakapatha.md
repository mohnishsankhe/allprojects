### 1 <a id="tea-khuddakapatha-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Homage to the Blessed One; I go for refuge to the Buddha, to the Dhamma, to the Saṅgha, for the second and the third time.

> Namo tassa Bhagavato Arahato Sammāsambuddhassa. Buddhaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi, Dhammaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi, Saṅghaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi. Dutiyampi buddhaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi, Dutiyampi dhammaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi, Dutiyampi saṅghaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi. Tatiyampi buddhaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi, Tatiyampi dhammaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi, Tatiyampi saṅghaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi.

### 2 <a id="tea-khuddakapatha-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The ten training rules of the novice: abstaining from killing, taking what is not given, unchastity, false speech, intoxicants, eating at the wrong time, entertainments, garlands and adornment, high and luxurious beds, and accepting gold and silver.

> Pāṇātipātā veramaṇisikkhāpadaṁ samādiyāmi. Adinnādānā veramaṇisikkhāpadaṁ samādiyāmi. Abrahmacariyā veramaṇisikkhāpadaṁ samādiyāmi. Musāvādā veramaṇisikkhāpadaṁ samādiyāmi. Surāmerayamajjapamādaṭṭhānā veramaṇisikkhāpadaṁ samādiyāmi. Vikālabhojanā veramaṇisikkhāpadaṁ samādiyāmi. Naccagītavāditavisūkadassanā veramaṇisikkhāpadaṁ samādiyāmi. Mālāgandhavilepanadhāraṇamaṇḍanavibhūsanaṭṭhānā veramaṇisikkhāpadaṁ samādiyāmi. Uccāsayanamahāsayanā veramaṇisikkhāpadaṁ samādiyāmi. Jātarūparajatapaṭiggahaṇā veramaṇisikkhāpadaṁ samādiyāmi.

### 3 <a id="tea-khuddakapatha-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In this body there are head hairs, body hairs, nails, teeth, skin, flesh, sinews, bones, marrow, kidneys, heart, liver, diaphragm, spleen, lungs, intestines, mesentery, stomach contents, faeces, brain, bile, phlegm, pus, blood, sweat, fat, tears, grease, saliva, snot, synovial fluid, urine.

> Atthi imasmiṁ kāye—Kesā lomā nakhā dantā taco, maṁsaṁ nhāru aṭṭhi aṭṭhimiñjaṁ vakkaṁ, hadayaṁ yakanaṁ kilomakaṁ pihakaṁ papphāsaṁ, antaṁ antaguṇaṁ udariyaṁ karīsaṁ matthaluṅgaṁ, pittaṁ semhaṁ pubbo lohitaṁ sedo medo, assu vasā kheḷo siṅghāṇikā lasikā muttanti.

### 4 <a id="tea-khuddakapatha-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The novice's questions: what is one? — all beings subsist on nutriment; two — name and form; three — three feelings; four — the four noble truths; five — the five aggregates of clinging; six — the six internal sense bases; seven — the seven factors of awakening; eight — the noble eightfold path; nine — the nine abodes of beings; ten — one with ten factors is called an arahant.

### 7 <a id="tea-khuddakapatha-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Outside the walls the departed stand at junctions and doorways, returning to their old homes; when a great feast is prepared, none remember them. Those who have compassion give pure food and drink at the right time, saying 'may this be for our relatives, may they be happy'; there is no farming or trade there, the departed live on what is given here; the offering placed in the Saṅgha benefits them long.
