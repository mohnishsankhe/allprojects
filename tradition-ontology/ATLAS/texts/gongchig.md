### single-intent <a id="tea-gongchig-single-intent"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

All the teachings of the Buddha — the vehicles, vows and practices — share a single intent; no teaching is to be rejected, each being a means toward awakening.

### three-vows <a id="tea-gongchig-three-vows"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The vows of individual liberation, of the bodhisattva and of the mantra are one in essence, differing in their aspects according to the vehicle; they are kept together as a single discipline.
