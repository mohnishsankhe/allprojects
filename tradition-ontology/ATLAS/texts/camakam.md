# Camakam (Camaka-praśna)

The Yajurveda litany that follows the Śatarudrīya: a long series of petitions 'and … for me' (… ca me) — food and impulse, the breaths, mind, speech, eye and ear, strength and life, truth and faith, well-being, the Vedic rites and their implements, and finally the numbers — each to be made fit by the sacrifice (yajñena kalpantām).

_Notes: Recited with the Śatarudrīya (Namakam-Camakam); ritually the formulas accompany the 'stream of wealth' (vasordhārā) offering of the agnicayana (moderate confidence)._
