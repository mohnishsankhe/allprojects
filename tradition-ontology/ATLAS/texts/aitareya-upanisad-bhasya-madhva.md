one of the thirty-seven works of Madhva collected as the Sarvamūla ('root of all'); Madhva's commentary on the Aitareya Upaniṣad, reading it as teaching Viṣṇu's supremacy and his difference from souls and matter, often by etymological explanation of names and by quotations from other scriptures. The tradition remembers the Aitareya as the text Madhva was expounding when he passed from view at Uḍupi.
