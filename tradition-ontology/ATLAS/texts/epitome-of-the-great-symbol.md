### 1 <a id="tea-epitome-of-the-great-symbol-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Great Symbol is practised through preliminaries, then the ordinary practice of calming and insight, then the extraordinary practice in which appearances and mind are realized as one, and it is completed in the four yogas.
