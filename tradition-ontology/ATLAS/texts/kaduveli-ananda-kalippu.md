# Kaṭuveḷic cittar: Āṉantak kaḷippu (the Joy-song)

`src:kaduveli-ananda-kalippu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tamil
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Genre:** song (pallavi–caraṇam)
**Part of:** [Cittar pāṭalkaḷ (the songs of the Siddhars)](siddhar-padalgal.md)
**Authors:** 
  - [Kaṭuveḷic cittar](../teachers/kaduveli.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Dates:** Scholarly account: undetermined; the Siddhar song corpus is usually placed c. 1400–1800; (confidence low)
**Structure:** pallavi + 34 caraṇams (local e-text)
**Availability:** digitized-original

A pallavi and thirty-four caraṇams in popular song form: 'Do no sin, O mind — tomorrow Yama in his anger will carry you off.' The body is a bubble on water; the mendicant who begged a pot for months danced and broke it; will karma go by visiting Kāśī or bathing in the Gaṅgā? Devotion is highest; the eight-limbed yoga; the many castes are one; avoid ganja and toddy, false garb and cruelty; seek the pure Vedānta space.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Cittar pāṭalkaḷ tokuppu I (Project Madurai 1999; GRETIL Devanāgarī transliteration)
