## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### hevajra-prostration <a id="tea-marpa-namthar-hevajra-prostration"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

When Nāropa manifested the maṇḍala of Hevajra, Marpa prostrated to the deity rather than to his guru; Nāropa said that without the guru even the name of buddha would not be heard, and foretold that Marpa's family line would scatter while his Dharma lineage would continue.

### three-journeys <a id="tea-marpa-namthar-three-journeys"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Marpa gathered gold and travelled to India three times (by the usual account), receiving the tantras, the six dharmas and Mahāmudrā from Nāropa, Maitrīpa and other masters.
