## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 4.1 <a id="tea-culakammavibhanga-sutta-4-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Beings are owners of their deeds, heirs of their deeds; deeds are their womb, their kin, their refuge; it is deeds that distinguish beings as inferior and superior.

> “Kammassakā, māṇava, sattā kammadāyādā kammayonī kammabandhū kammappaṭisaraṇā.

### 4-20 <a id="tea-culakammavibhanga-sutta-4-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Beings are owners of their deeds, heirs of their deeds; deeds are their womb, kin and refuge; it is deeds that distinguish beings as inferior and superior. Killing leads to a short life, harming to sickness, anger to ugliness, envy to little influence, not giving to poverty, arrogance to low birth, not asking the wise to stupidity — and the opposites to the opposites.
