A short work of Mipham explaining in what sense the Nyingma accept 'other-emptiness': the ultimate as the unconditioned wisdom realized in meditative equipoise, empty of the adventitious, while still not established by its own nature.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### whole <a id="tea-zhentong-khelen-sengei-ngaro-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

In the sense in which the Nyingma accept it, 'other-emptiness' means that the ultimate — the unconditioned wisdom realized in meditative equipoise — is empty of the adventitious, conditioned phenomena; this does not conflict with self-emptiness, for that ultimate too is not established by its own nature: appearance and emptiness are a unity.
