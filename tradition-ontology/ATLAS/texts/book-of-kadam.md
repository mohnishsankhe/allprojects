## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### seven-divine-dharmas <a id="tea-book-of-kadam-seven-divine-dharmas"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Kadam practitioner is to hold the seven divine dharmas: the four deities — Śākyamuni as the teacher, Avalokiteśvara as the deity of compassion, Tārā as the protectress of activity, Acala as the guardian against obstacles — and the three baskets as the teaching.

### sixteen-drops <a id="tea-book-of-kadam-sixteen-drops"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The sixteen drops (thig le bcu drug) of the Kadam: a guru-yoga meditation in which the practitioner visualises nested spheres containing the lineage teachers and deities, culminating in the drop of awakening, as the heart practice of the Kadam oral instructions.
