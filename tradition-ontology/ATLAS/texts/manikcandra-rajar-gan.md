# Mānikcandra Rājār Gān

A North Bengal ballad of Queen Mayanāmatī, disciple of Gorakṣa, and her son King Gopīcandra, whom she makes renounce the kingdom and take initiation from Hāḍipā (Jālandhara) to escape early death.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: G. A. Grierson, 'The Song of Mānik Candra', Journal of the Asiatic Society of Bengal (1878)

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### whole <a id="tea-manikcandra-rajar-gan-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Queen Mayanāmatī, who has Gorakṣa's great knowledge, foresees that her son King Gopīcandra will die young and compels him to renounce his kingdom and queens and take initiation from the siddha Hāḍipā; after trials he is initiated.
