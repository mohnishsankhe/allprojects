## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 16.8 <a id="tea-theragatha-16-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Aṅgulimāla's verses: 'I have stopped, you stop'; once heedless, now heedful, he lights up the world like the moon freed from a cloud; his evil deed is covered by the good; he was called 'Harmless' though he killed, now he is truly harmless; tamed without rod or sword by the Buddha, he bears the fruit of his deeds.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: general · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation_

teachers: [Aṅgulimāla](../teachers/angulimala.md)

### 18.1 <a id="tea-theragatha-18-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Mahākassapa's verses: one should not frequent families for honour; he ate the morsel a leper put into his bowl, content with alms-food, fermented urine as medicine, the root of a tree as lodging and rag-robes; the rocky crags delight him.
