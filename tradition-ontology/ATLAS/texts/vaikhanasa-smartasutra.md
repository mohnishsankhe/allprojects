# Vaikhānasa Smārtasūtra

**Alternate titles:** Vaikhānasa Gṛhyasūtra, Vaikhānasasūtra
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md)
**Genre:** sūtra (gṛhya and dharma)
**Authors:** 
  - [Vikhanas](../teachers/vikhanas.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: composed by Vikhanas, the first of all sūtra authors (Daśavidhahetunirūpaṇa); scholarly: a late Taittirīya sūtra; date uncertain; confidence: low
**Structure:** 10 praśnas (domestic rules followed by the dharma section)
**Availability:** unknown

The domestic and dharma sūtra of the Vaikhānasas (Taittirīya branch) ascribed to Vikhanas: saṃskāras from conception (including the Vaikhānasa prenatal rite for Viṣṇu), daily rites including the worship of Viṣṇu, and the rules of the āśramas.
