### 2-9 <a id="tea-brahmavidya-upanisad-2-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Oṃ's body: a is the Ṛgveda, the gārhapatya fire, the earth and Brahmā; u the Yajurveda, the dakṣiṇa fire, the mid-space and Viṣṇu; m the Sāmaveda, the āhavanīya fire, the sky and Īśvara.

### 16-26 <a id="tea-brahmavidya-upanisad-16-26"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The jīva repeats the haṃsa with every breath (sa going in, ha going out); haṃsa-vidyā is the only means to liberation.

### 27-31 <a id="tea-brahmavidya-upanisad-27-31"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The disciple should do whatever the guru says without deliberating; then, having gained knowledge, he may abandon caste, stage of life and even the Vedas like dust; the guru is Hari himself.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission_

### 51-53 <a id="tea-brahmavidya-upanisad-51-53"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Three kinds of teacher: the codaka who points out the path, the bodhaka who shows the place, and the mokṣada who gives the supreme; addressed to Gautama.

### 81-110 <a id="tea-brahmavidya-upanisad-81-110"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate
