Āṇṭāḷ's thirty verses of the Mārkaḻi vow (pāvai nōṉpu): the cowherd girls of Āyarpāṭi rise before dawn, wake one another, go to Nandagopa's house, wake Nappiṉṉai and Kṛṣṇa, and ask for the 'drum' (paṟai) — which in the end is revealed as unbroken kinship with him and service to him alone through seven times seven births.

### 1 <a id="tea-tiruppavai-1"></a>

In the month of Mārkaḻi, on this good day of the full moon, you girls of the rich cowherd village who will come to bathe, come! Nārāyaṇa himself, the son of Nandagopa, dark-bodied and lotus-eyed, will give us the drum (paṟai).

### 2 <a id="tea-tiruppavai-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The observances of the vow: we sing the feet of the Lord who sleeps on the milk ocean; we take no ghee and no milk; we bathe before dawn; we put no kohl on our eyes and no flowers in our hair; we do not do what should not be done; we do not carry evil tales; we give alms and help as much as we can.

### 5 <a id="tea-tiruppavai-5"></a>

The wondrous one, the son of northern Mathurā, the lamp of the cowherd clan — if we come pure, strew pure flowers, worship him, sing with our mouths and think of him in our minds, then the faults already done and those still to come will burn like cotton in fire; so sing his name.

### 28 <a id="tea-tiruppavai-28"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

We are cowherds who follow the cattle into the forest and eat there, a clan without knowledge; yet we have the merit of your being born among us; the relationship between you and us cannot be broken here even if you try; do not be angry, Govinda, that out of love and ignorance we called you by small names — grant us the drum.

### 29 <a id="tea-tiruppavai-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

We came at dawn to worship your golden lotus feet; hear why: not to take the drum today, Govinda — for all time, for seven times seven births, we shall be kin to you alone and serve you alone; change every other desire of ours.
