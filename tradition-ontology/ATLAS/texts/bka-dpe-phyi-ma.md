# bka' dpe phyi ma (Nāropa's instructions received from Tilopa)

`src:bka-dpe-phyi-ma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** The Later Book of Instructions (tentative rendering)
**Original title:** bka' dpe phyi ma
**Language:** Tibetan
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md), [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md)
**Genre:** instruction (six dharmas)
**Authors:** 
  - [Nāropa](../teachers/naropa.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
  - [Tilopa](../teachers/tilopa.md) — role: revealer; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Nāropa's instructions from Tilopa (the text's opening); scholarly: Indian or early Tibetan compilation of the Nāropa instructions; author as given in catalogues not checked here; confidence: moderate
**Structure:** c. 46 Derge lines (273a.4–276a.7); each section ends with a 'concise word' (tshig sbram) naming the kind of awakening it gives
**Availability:** digitized-translation

Nāropa's record of the instructions he received from Tilopa after reciting the Cakrasaṃvara mantra a hundred thousand times: the natural state of body and mind, the key points of body, time, object and wind; inner heat with its signs and warnings; illusory body; dream (four points); clear light (five kinds, five times); the kinds of transference with the warning that transference at the wrong time 'kills the deities'; the intermediate state with the stages of dissolution and the learner's and non-learner's union; 'entering another's body'; the method path; the rule of secrecy (the lioness's milk in an unfit vessel).
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: translation; name: Derge Tengyur, Tōh 2332 (read locally; Esukhia/Barom digital edition); licence: public domain; url: local: sources_raw/derge-tengyur

### 273a.4 <a id="tea-bka-dpe-phyi-ma-273a-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The great guru Nāropa recited the secret mantra of Cakrasaṃvara one hundred thousand times; the earth shook and he received a prophecy; he then received instructions in the presence of Tilopa: on the natural state of things, the path, and the stages by which the fruit arises.

### 273a.5 <a id="tea-bka-dpe-phyi-ma-273a-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

### 273b.6 <a id="tea-bka-dpe-phyi-ma-273b-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The milk of the white lioness is not to be poured into ordinary bad vessels, only into gold or silver ones; if poured, the vessel breaks and the milk is lost. If this instruction of the accomplished gurus is given to bad vessels other than fortunate disciples, the pledge is broken and one goes to hell; therefore cherish those with trained karma. (This is) the concise word of inner heat, 'awakening through meditation': the instruction for the diligent to awaken in this life.

### 273b.7 <a id="tea-bka-dpe-phyi-ma-273b-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Taking the illusory body as path: this appearance and all beings are here explained as like illusion; abiding in the illusion-like samādhi, see everything as such — outer and inner, all is nothing but illusion. Know this illusory body by the image in a mirror; colours are like a rainbow; pervasion is (shown) by the moon in water; like the image of Vajrasattva well drawn in a stainless mirror. Whatever the yogin focuses on, examine as illusion — mirror image, dream, magic, bubble, conjuring, mirage, city of gandharvas. The pure illusory body of the deity and the fully ripened illusory body: form, sound, smell, taste and touch entering the eye and the other senses are examined as illusion; in this Vajra Vehicle what need is there to say more? The concise word of the illusory body: 'awakening through purified tendencies'.

### 274a.5 <a id="tea-bka-dpe-phyi-ma-274a-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The instruction on carrying dream onto the path: phenomena are like dreams, unborn in essence; within that pure nature delusion is said to be mistaken. Five points are explained in detail (recollection, knowing as illusion, training, habituation, guiding), and four points put it into practice: first seize the dream; then train it and learn it as illusion; then abandon fear; then meditate on the meaning of suchness. Knowing phenomena as dreams, engage for the benefit of beings; carrying (experience) onto the path as illusion, one awakens as illusion. The concise word: 'awakening through dream for one with tendencies'.

### 274b.1 <a id="tea-bka-dpe-phyi-ma-274b-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Taking clear light as the path: phenomena are by nature clear light, pure from the beginning like space; the three wisdoms are pure, the cause of the three appearances; having entered all objects, one enters clear light. It is not empty and not non-empty, not even observed as a middle. As fish leap quickly from a clear river, so the net of illusion arises and ceases from clarity-emptiness. Clear light is fivefold: natural clear light, the dawning of wisdom clear light, samādhi clear light, suchness clear light, and realization clear light. Its times are five: death, abiding in the central channel, sleep after absorption, when absorption and post-absorption become one taste, and the time of insight-realization. From clear light made manifest, union dawns. The concise word: 'awakening through deep sleep'.

### 275a.1 <a id="tea-bka-dpe-phyi-ma-275a-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Taking the intermediate state as path has four points: first, recognizing the clear light through the dissolution of the three appearances; second, the method of awakening in the intermediate state by way of the pure and impure illusion-like (body); third, blocking the womb door; fourth, closing the womb door. In the bardo of birth and death one trains in inner heat and the illusory body; when familiar, one mixes dream and illusory body and trains the dream bardo; when these are mastered, in the bardo of dying the illusory body mixes with clear light; when the tendencies are burnt away by the fire of clear light, one awakens in the body of union.

### 275a.4 <a id="tea-bka-dpe-phyi-ma-275a-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

For the person who enters gradually, knowledge arises thus: appearance dissolves and thought dissolves, the coarse dissolves and the subtle dissolves; by familiarity after the dissolution natural clear light arises, and after it union dawns. In detail: form dissolves into sound, sound into smell, smell into taste, and so on to touch; earth, water, fire and wind dissolve in sequence into consciousness; consciousness dissolves into appearance — the outer sign is like moonrise, the inner sign like smoke; appearance dissolves into increase — outer sign like sunrise, knowledge like a lamp; increase into attainment — outer sign like darkness, wisdom dawns like a lamp; attainment into clear light — outer sign like the dawn at daybreak, inner knowing arises like a cloudless sky.

### 275b.1 <a id="tea-bka-dpe-phyi-ma-275b-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Rising from that clear light, the union is to be identified: while the clear-light fire of full awakening purifies all tendencies it is called the learner's union; when nothing remains to be purified and familiarity is complete it is the non-learner's union — the fruit attained. From there, in the bardo of becoming, the illusory body becomes the body of union and the saṃbhogakāya is attained; by familiarity the womb door is closed. If union is not known, birth in existence is not blocked; then abandon all appearances of attachment and aversion, connect with mindfulness, practise closing the womb, and by the transference of delight be born where you wish. Death is emptiness, dharmakāya; the bardo is saṃbhogakāya; birth in existence is nirmāṇakāya. The concise word: 'awakening in the bardo of becoming'.
