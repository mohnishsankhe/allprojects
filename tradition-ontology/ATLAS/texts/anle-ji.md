## Teachings (13: skeleton 13)

### 10a11-24 <a id="tea-anle-ji-10a11-24"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Against teachers of the Mahāyānasaṃgraha who called the Contemplation Sūtra's promise of birth by ten recitations an 'intention for another time' (only a cause for a distant future birth, as one coin buys a thousand only in time), Daochuo holds that a treatise may not contradict the sūtra: the Buddha, leading future evil-doers, hides their past causes and speaks only of the result; those who complete the ten recitations all have past causes, as the Nirvāṇa Sūtra shows, and so are born at once.

> 今此經中但說一生造罪，臨命終時十念成就即得往生。不論過去有因、無因者，直是世尊引接當來造惡之徒，令其臨終捨惡歸善，乘念往生，是以隱其宿因。

### 10c16-29 <a id="tea-anle-ji-10c16-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

To the objection that karma is like a scale where the heavier side pulls first, so how can ten recitations at death outweigh a lifetime of evil: the weight lies in the mind, the object and decisiveness, not in length of time. In the mind: the evil arose from a false, inverted mind; the ten recitations arise from hearing the Dharma of reality through a good teacher's skilful comfort — one real, one false; as light entering a room dark for a thousand years dispels the dark at once.

> 正明在心、在緣、在決定，不在時節久近、多少也。

### 11a22-27 <a id="tea-anle-ji-11a22-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

How long is 'ten recitations'? Not the scriptural measure of moments: simply recollecting Amitābha, by his general or particular marks as one contemplates, through ten thoughts without other thoughts intervening is called ten recitations. 'Ten' is a number known to the sages; one only accumulates recollection without other concerns until the karmic path is complete, without troubling to count; practitioners of long standing may do so, while beginners may well count.

> 但憶念阿彌陀佛，若總相、若別相，隨所緣觀，逕於十念，無他念想間雜，是名十念。又云：『十念相續者，是聖者一數之名耳。』但能積念凝思，不緣他事，使業道成辦，便罷不用，亦未勞記之頭數也。又云：『若久行人念，多應依此；若始行人念者，記數亦好。』

### 11b19-29 <a id="tea-anle-ji-11b19-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Do not put off practice to the deathbed: ordinary minds are like wild horses and monkeys; build the habit now, for when the 'wind-knife' of death comes with a hundred pains the recitation cannot be managed if it was not practised. Let three or five like-minded people make a pact in advance: at the time of death they take turns reminding the dying one and calling Amitābha's name, voice following voice, so that the ten recitations are completed — as a wax seal pressed in clay: when the seal breaks the pattern is formed; the moment life ends is the moment of birth in the Land of Peace and Bliss.

> 各宜同志三、五預結言要，臨命終時，迭相開曉，為稱彌陀名號，願生安樂國，聲聲相次，使成十念也。譬如蠟印印泥，印壞文成。此命斷時，即是生安樂國時。

### 11c08-14 <a id="tea-anle-ji-11c08-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If birth is the root of all troubles, is not seeking birth in the Pure Land abandoning birth only to seek birth? No: that land is the birth of non-birth from Amitābha's pure original vow, not the deluded, attached birth of beings in the three existences; dharma-nature is pure and ultimately unborn — 'birth' is said only from the standpoint of those who are born. (Earlier: 'beings are ultimately unborn like space' is true, and birth by conditions is nominal birth, hence non-birth.)

> 然彼淨土乃是阿彌陀如來清淨本願，無生之生。非如三有眾生愛染，虛妄執著生也。何以故？夫法性清淨畢竟無生，而言生者，得生者之情耳。

### 13c05-22 <a id="tea-anle-ji-13c05-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Why do beings who all have buddha-nature and have met many buddhas still transmigrate? Because they have not taken either of two excellent dharmas: the holy path and birth in the Pure Land. The holy path is hard to realise now, because the great sage is long gone and its principle is deep while understanding is shallow; the Candragarbha says that in the last age not one of countless practitioners attains. Now in the last age, this evil world of five turbidities, only the gate of the Pure Land is passable. The Larger Sūtra says even one who has done evil all his life, if at death he calls the name ten times continuously, will be born. Even a lifetime's evil-doer who fixes his mind and recollects the Buddha will have all obstacles removed and be born.

> 何者為二？一謂聖道；二謂往生淨土。其聖道一種今時難證。一、由去大聖遙遠；二、由理深解微。……當今末法，現是五濁惡世，唯有淨土一門，可通入路。

### 4a26-b03 <a id="tea-anle-ji-4a26-b03"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The first great gate: teaching arises according to the time and the capacity of beings. If the teaching fits the time and the capacity, it is easy to practise and easy to awaken; if capacity, teaching and time are at odds, it is hard to practise and hard to enter — like drilling wet wood for fire.

> 若教赴時機，易修易悟；若機教時乖，難修難入。

### 4b03-22 <a id="tea-anle-ji-4b03-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Citing the Candragarbha (Mahāsaṃnipāta) sūtra's five periods of five hundred years after the Buddha — wisdom, concentration, learning, building stūpas and repentance, then disputes — Daochuo reckons that beings now are in the fourth five hundred years, the time for repentance, merit and calling the Buddha's name; one calling of Amitābha removes the sins of eighty koṭis of kalpas of birth-and-death. When the sage was near, meditation and wisdom were the main practice and calling the name secondary; now that the sage is far, calling the name is main and the others secondary.

> 計今時眾生，即當佛去世後第四五百年，正是懺悔、修福，應稱佛名號時者。若一念稱阿彌陀佛，即能除却八十億劫生死之罪。

### 5c11-15 <a id="tea-anle-ji-5c11-15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Asked what body the present Amitābha is and what land Sukhāvatī is, Daochuo answers: Amitābha now is a reward-body buddha and the jewel-adorned Land of Bliss is a reward land; the old tradition that he is a transformation body in a transformation land is a great error — for then where would the reward body dwell? Citing a sūtra, all who become buddhas in pure lands are reward bodies, those in defiled lands transformation bodies.

> 現在彌陀是報佛，極樂寶莊嚴國是報土。然古舊相傳皆云：『阿彌陀佛是化身，土亦是化土。』此為大失也。

### 7a25-b02 <a id="tea-anle-ji-7a25-b02"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Pure Land is excellent and wondrous, in substance beyond the world; the three realms are the dark house of ordinary beings in birth-and-death, a long river of defilement where cause and effect breed falsehood, deeply to be loathed; therefore the Pure Land is not included in the three realms.

> 淨土勝妙，體出世間。此三界者，乃是生死凡夫之闇宅……是故淨土非三界攝。

### 9b22-c09 <a id="tea-anle-ji-9b22-c09"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

To those who prefer birth in Maitreya's Tuṣita to the West, Daochuo answers that they are only slightly alike and fundamentally different in four ways: in Tuṣita many are attached to pleasure and retrogress; life there is four thousand years and then one falls; its birds and trees serve the gods' sensual pleasure and do not aid the holy path, while in the Pure Land all are non-retrogressive, beyond the three realms, of life equal to the Buddha's, and even its birds and trees preach the Dharma.

> 願生兜率，不願歸西。……此義不類，少分似同，據體大別，有其四種。
