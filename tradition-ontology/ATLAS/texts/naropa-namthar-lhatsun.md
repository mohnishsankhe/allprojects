## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### dakini-question <a id="tea-naropa-namthar-lhatsun-dakini-question"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

While Nāropa was a scholar at Nālandā an ugly old woman asked whether he understood the words or the meaning; when he said the words she laughed, when he said the meaning she wept, telling him that only her brother Tilopa understood the meaning; he left to seek Tilopa.

### twelve-hardships <a id="tea-naropa-namthar-lhatsun-twelve-hardships"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Nāropa underwent twelve great and twelve lesser hardships at Tilopa's command (leaping from a temple roof, into fire, and so on), each followed by an instruction; finally Tilopa struck him with a sandal and he realized the nature of mind.
