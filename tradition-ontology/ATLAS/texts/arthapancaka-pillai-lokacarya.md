Piḷḷai Lokācārya's short work on the five things a seeker must know — the self, the Lord, the means, the goal and the obstacle — each in five kinds.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-arthapancaka-pillai-lokacarya-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Five things must be known, each fivefold: the self (eternal, freed, bound, isolated and seeking release), the Lord (supreme, vyūha, descent, inner controller, image), the means (karma, jñāna, bhakti, prapatti, the teacher's regard), the goal (dharma, wealth, pleasure, self-enjoyment, the Lord's service) and the obstacles (to right knowledge of the self, of the Lord's supremacy, of the means, of the goal, and to attainment).
