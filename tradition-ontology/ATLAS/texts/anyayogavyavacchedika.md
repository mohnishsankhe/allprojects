### 6 <a id="tea-anyayogavyavacchedika-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

'There is a maker of the world; he is one, all-pervading, independent and eternal' — such obstinate fancies belong to those whose teacher you are not.

### 30 <a id="tea-anyayogavyavacchedika-30"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

As the other doctrines are jealous of one another, standing as thesis and counter-thesis, your doctrine, which accepts all standpoints without distinction, is not partisan.

> anyonyapakṣapratipakṣabhāvād yathā pare matsariṇaḥ pravādāḥ / nayān aśeṣān aviśeṣam icchan na pakṣapātī samayas tathā te
