### 20 <a id="tea-brahmajnanavalimala-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Brahman is real, the world is false, the jīva is Brahman alone and not other; by this the right teaching is to be known — this is the drum-beat of Vedānta.

> brahma satyaṃ jagan mithyā jīvo brahmaiva nāparaḥ / anena vedyaṃ sacchāstram iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ
