Yāmuna's four verses in praise of Śrī (Lakṣmī), declaring that even the Lord cannot measure her greatness and that she is the Lord's consort, whose servants are all the gods; later the Vaṭakalai cite it for Śrī's supremacy.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-catuhsloki-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Addressed to Śrī: your lover is the supreme Person, the serpent-king your couch and seat, Garuḍa (the Veda embodied) your mount, world-enchanting māyā your curtain, the gods from Brahmā and Śiva down with their consorts your servants and maids — 'Śrī' alone is your name; how can we praise you?

> kāntas te puruṣottamaḥ phaṇipatiḥ śayyāsanaṃ vāhanaṃ vedātmā vihageśvaro yavanikā māyā jagan-mohinī | brahmeśādi-sura-vrajaḥ sa-dayitas tvad-dāsa-dāsī-gaṇaḥ śrīr ity eva ca nāma te bhagavati brūmaḥ kathaṃ tvāṃ vayam ||

### 2 <a id="tea-catuhsloki-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Even the Lord, her beloved, cannot measure the boundless greatness of Śrī, as he cannot measure his own; she is ever in accord with him.
