Kṛṣṇa as the supreme Person in Goloka with Rādhā as his śakti; Prakṛti's five principal forms (Durgā, Lakṣmī, Sarasvatī, Sāvitrī, Rādhā); Gaṇeśa's birth; Kṛṣṇa's life with a strong Rādhā-centred devotion.
