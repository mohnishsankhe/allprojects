## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-raga-vartma-candrika-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The sole qualification for spontaneous devotion is eager longing (lobha) for the emotions of the Vraja residents, arising by the grace of Kṛṣṇa or of a devotee who has it; scripture then serves only to show the method, not to compel.
