Āṇṭāḷ's 143 verses in 14 decads: prayer to Kāma to unite her with Kṛṣṇa, the dream of her wedding ('vāraṇam āyiram'), questions to the conch about the taste of his lips, messages to clouds and birds, the agony of separation, and at the end the sight of him in Vṛndāvana.

### 1 <a id="tea-nacciyar-tirumoli-1"></a>

Āṇṭāḷ prays to Kāma, god of love, to unite her with the Lord of Vēṅkaṭam: if she is spoken of as destined for a mortal man, she will not live.
