Forty stanzas: 32 in kalittāḻicai with the refrain 'eṉ kaṇṇammā!' and eight couplets with 'āttāṭi', in veiled imagery of the root (mūlam), the six supports, the ninety-six principles and alchemical drugs; they tell how the guru came, showed his own form, burnt the six supports and the fifty-one letters, and left the heart standing empty without bath-offerings or flowers.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Cittar pāṭalkaḷ tokuppu I (Project Madurai 1999; GRETIL Devanāgarī transliteration)

## Teachings (13: skeleton 13)

### 1 <a id="tea-azhukanni-padalgal-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In the root-abode within the house of 'three-times-two', in the abode of milk where a pillar has grown like fire, the singer longs to see, in the upper abode, the 'play' — addressed to 'my Kaṇṇammā'.

> मूलप् पदियडियो मूविरण्डु वीडदिले / कोलप् पदियडियो कुदर्क्कत् तॆरुऩडुवे / पालप् पदिदऩिले तणलाय् वळर्त्तगम्बम् / मेलप् पदिदऩिले ऎऩ् कण्णम्मा! / विळैयाट्टैप् पारेऩो!

### 8 <a id="tea-azhukanni-padalgal-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

This oozing body is a pot that once held salt; the medicine to be born otherwise has not come to me — if it came, I would leave this oozing body and reach your feet.

> ऊऱ्ऱैच् चडलमडि उप्पिरुन्द पाण्डमडि / माऱ्ऱिप् पिऱक्क मरुन्दॆऩक्कुक् किट्टुदिल्लै / माऱ्ऱिप् पिऱक्क मरुन्दॆऩक्कु किट्टुमॆऩ्ऱाल् / ऊऱ्ऱैच् चडलम् विट्टेऎऩ् कण्णम्मा! / उऩ्बादञ् जेरेऩो!

### 9 <a id="tea-azhukanni-padalgal-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Told that the plantain would hurt his mouth, he ate the screw-pine fruit and death came to him; if he left that fruit and 'died without dying', would life not come to him?

> वाऴैप् पऴन्दिऩ्ऱाल् वाय्ऩोगु मॆऩ्ऱुसॊल्लित् / ताऴैप् पऴत्तिऩ्ऱु सावॆऩक्कु वन्ददडि / ताऴैप् पऴत्तैविट्टुच् चागामऱ् सागवल्लो / वाऴैप् पऴन्दिऩ्ऱाल् ऎऩ् कण्णम्मा! / वाऴ्वॆऩक्कु वारादो!

### 10 <a id="tea-azhukanni-padalgal-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Born from the town of the sack into the ruined town, he does not know the house of Vedānta by which to go to the true town; if he knew it, both the sack-town and the true town would end in the void.

> पैयूरि लेयिरुन्दु पाऴूरिले पिऱन्दु / मॆय्यूरिल् पोवदऱ्कु वेदान्द वीडऱियेऩ्, / मॆय्यूरिऱ् पोवदऱ्कु वेदान्द वीडऱिन्दाल् / पैयूरुम् मॆय्यूरुम् ऎऩ् कण्णम्मा! / पाऴाय् मुडियावो!

### 19 <a id="tea-azhukanni-padalgal-19"></a>

### 33 <a id="tea-azhukanni-padalgal-33"></a>

### 34 <a id="tea-azhukanni-padalgal-34"></a>

To cut off birth, he took him as guru; he came as the cause to cut off the thieving senses.

### 37 <a id="tea-azhukanni-padalgal-37"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

### 38 <a id="tea-azhukanni-padalgal-38"></a>

### 39 <a id="tea-azhukanni-padalgal-39"></a>
