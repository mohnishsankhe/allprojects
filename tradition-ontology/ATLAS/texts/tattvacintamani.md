# Tattvacintāmaṇi

`src:tattvacintamani` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Maṇi, Cintāmaṇi
**Original title:** तत्त्वचिन्तामणि
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md), [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
**Genre:** prakaraṇa
**Authors:** 
  - [Gaṅgeśa Upādhyāya](../teachers/gangesa.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
**Dates:** Scholarly account: first half of the 14th c. CE (Mithilā); (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 4 khaṇḍas: Pratyakṣa, Anumāna, Upamāna, Śabda; each divided into vādas
**Availability:** digitized-original

Gaṅgeśa Upādhyāya's 'wish-gem of truth', the founding text of Navya-Nyāya, in four books (khaṇḍa) on perception, inference, comparison and verbal testimony. It treats validity as extrinsic, error as misplaced apprehension, sets out and rejects five provisional definitions of pervasion (vyāptipañcaka) before its own definition, defines subjecthood (pakṣatā) and consideration (parāmarśa), proves Īśvara by inference, and defines verbal testimony and the conditions of verbal cognition.

## Teachings (10: skeleton 10)

### Anumāna-khaṇḍa, Pakṣatā <a id="tea-tattvacintamani-anumana-paksata"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Subjecthood (pakṣatā) is the absence of certainty (of the probandum in the subject) unaccompanied by a desire to infer; hence one can infer even what is known if one wishes to, and cannot infer what is certain if one does not.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: unmarked · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [pakṣatā](../terms/paksata.md), [siṣādhayiṣā](../terms/sisadhayisa.md) · concepts: [Subjecthood (pakṣatā)](../concepts/paksata.md)

### Anumāna-khaṇḍa, Parāmarśa <a id="tea-tattvacintamani-anumana-paramarsa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Inferential cognition is produced by 'consideration' (parāmarśa) — the cognition of the probans as pervaded by the probandum and present in the subject — not by the bare memory of pervasion.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: unmarked · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [parāmarśa](../terms/paramarsa.md), [anumiti](../terms/anumiti.md) · concepts: [Consideration (parāmarśa) as the instrument of inference](../concepts/paramarsa.md)

### Anumāna-khaṇḍa, Siddhāntalakṣaṇa <a id="tea-tattvacintamani-anumana-siddhanta-laksana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Gaṅgeśa's own definition: pervasion is the co-location of the probans with a probandum that is not delimited by the delimitor of the counterpositiveness of any absolute absence which resides in the locus of the probans and is not co-located with its own counterpositive.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: unmarked · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [vyāpti](../terms/vyapti.md), [avacchedaka](../terms/avacchedaka.md), [pratiyogin](../terms/pratiyogin.md), [atyantābhāva](../terms/atyantabhava.md) · concepts: [Pervasion (vyāpti)](../concepts/vyapti.md), [The delimitor (avacchedaka)](../concepts/avacchedaka.md)

### Anumāna-khaṇḍa, Vyāptipañcaka <a id="tea-tattvacintamani-anumana-vyaptipancaka"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Five provisional definitions of pervasion are examined — e.g. 'not occurring in what possesses the absence of the probandum', 'not occurring in what possesses the absence of the probandum and differs from what possesses the probandum', 'not co-located with a mutual absence whose counterpositive is what possesses the probandum', 'being the counterpositive of an absence located in all that possesses the absence of the probandum', 'not occurring in what is other than what possesses the probandum' — and rejected, chiefly because they fail where the probandum is present everywhere (kevalānvayin).

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: unmarked · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [vyāpti](../terms/vyapti.md), [kevalānvayin](../terms/kevalanvayin.md), [pratiyogin](../terms/pratiyogin.md), [sāmānādhikaraṇya](../terms/samanadhikaranya.md) · concepts: [The five provisional definitions of pervasion (Vyāptipañcaka)](../concepts/vyaptipancaka.md), [Pervasion (vyāpti)](../concepts/vyapti.md)

### Anumāna-khaṇḍa, Īśvarānumāna <a id="tea-tattvacintamani-anumana-isvaranumana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Earth, sprouts and similar effects have a maker, because they are effects, like a pot; the maker of the world must know its material causes and the merit and demerit of selves — an omniscient Lord.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: divine · path: knowledge · stage: unmarked · types: ultimate, dispute_

### Pratyakṣa-khaṇḍa (on error) <a id="tea-tattvacintamani-pratyaksa-anyathakhyati"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

An erroneous cognition is the apprehension of a thing as having a property it lacks (e.g. mother-of-pearl as 'this is silver') — misplaced apprehension — not a mere failure to grasp the difference between two cognitions.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: unmarked · types: consciousness-mind, dispute_

terms: [anyathākhyāti](../terms/anyathakhyati.md), [viparyaya](../terms/viparyaya.md) · concepts: [Error as misplaced apprehension (anyathākhyāti)](../concepts/anyathakhyati.md) · disputes: [What happens in erroneous cognition (khyāti)?](../debates/theory-of-error.md)

### Pratyakṣa-khaṇḍa, Prāmāṇyavāda <a id="tea-tattvacintamani-pratyaksa-pramanyavada"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Validity is not grasped by the cognition that apprehends a cognition (otherwise doubt about validity could not arise); it is known extrinsically, by inference — e.g. from the success of the activity it prompts — and it is produced by an excellence in the causes of the cognition.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: unmarked · types: consciousness-mind, dispute_

terms: [prāmāṇya](../terms/pramanya.md), [anuvyavasāya](../terms/anuvyavasaya.md) · concepts: [Extrinsic validity (parataḥ-prāmāṇya)](../concepts/paratah-pramanya.md) · disputes: [Is the validity of a cognition intrinsic (svataḥ) or extrinsic (parataḥ)?](../debates/svatah-paratah-pramanya.md)

### Śabda-khaṇḍa (on the validity of the Veda) <a id="tea-tattvacintamani-sabda-veda-pramanya"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Therefore the validity of the Veda, ascertained from its acceptance by the great, rests solely on its being rooted in the perception of Īśvara, which is the ground of trust and depends on nothing else.

> tasmād āśvāsabījaparānapekṣeśvarapratyakṣamūlakatvād eva vedasya prāmāṇyaṃ mahājanaparigrahād avadhāryate

_level: unmarked · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: unmarked · types: sound-language, dispute_

### Śabda-khaṇḍa (opening) <a id="tea-tattvacintamani-sabda-definition"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Now verbal testimony is examined: a word (statement) produced by true knowledge of the meaning that is the cause of its utterance is a means of knowledge.

> atha śabdo nirūpyate / prayogahetubhūtārthatattvajñānajanyaḥ śabdaḥ pramāṇam

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: unmarked · types: sound-language_

terms: [śabda](../terms/sabda.md), [āpta](../terms/apta.md), [pramāṇa](../terms/pramana.md)

### Śabda-khaṇḍa (Ākāṅkṣā-, Yogyatā-, Āsatti- and Tātparyavāda) <a id="tea-tattvacintamani-sabda-akanksa-yogyata-asatti"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Verbal cognition arises from words when there is expectancy, compatibility and proximity among them and the speaker's intention is known.
