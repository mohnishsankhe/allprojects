## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-bahyarthasiddhikarika-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

External objects exist: the regularity of cognitions, their dependence on the presence of objects and the failure of the cognition-only arguments (dream, co-apprehension) show that cognition has objects outside it.
