## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 50 <a id="tea-istopadesa-50"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The soul is other, matter is other — this is the summary of reality; whatever else is said is only its elaboration.

> jīvo 'nyaḥ pudgalaś cānya ity asau tattvasaṃgrahaḥ / yad anyad ucyate kiṃcit so 'stu tasyaiva vistaraḥ
