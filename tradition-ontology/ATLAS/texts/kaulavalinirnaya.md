Jñānānanda Paramahaṃsa's digest of Kaula practice in 21 ullāsas, on the Kula worship, the conduct of the Kaula and the honouring of women: women are goddesses and life-breath and are never to be struck or reviled (10.87-88); the Kaulas wander 'inwardly Śākta, outwardly Śaiva, Vaiṣṇava in the assembly' (10.94-95).

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 10.87-88 <a id="tea-kaulavalinirnaya-10-87-88"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Striking, blaming, deceiving or displeasing women is never to be done, otherwise perfection is blocked; women are goddesses, women are the life-breath, women are the ornament.

> स्त्रियो देव्यः स्त्रियः प्राणाः स्त्रिय एव विभूषणम्॥
