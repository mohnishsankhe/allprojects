### 1.2 <a id="tea-sandilya-upanisad-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Niyamas: worship of the Lord is worshipping Viṣṇu, Rudra and the others with a clear mind; hearing the doctrine is inquiring into Vedānta; japa is loud, whispered (a thousand times better) or mental (a crore times better).

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: beginner · types: ethics, practice_

### 1.15 <a id="tea-sandilya-upanisad-1-15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Fourteen principal channels; the suṣumnā is the path to liberation; the moon (tamas, nectar) moves in iḍā, the sun (rajas, poison) in piṅgalā; suṣumnā devours time.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: body-layers_

### 1.16 <a id="tea-sandilya-upanisad-1-16"></a>

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: practice_

### 1.41 <a id="tea-sandilya-upanisad-1-41"></a>

### 1.42-53 <a id="tea-sandilya-upanisad-1-42-53"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Ways of stilling the vibration of prāṇa (shared with the Yoga Vāsiṣṭha): study, association with the wise, dispassion, practice, fixing on one object, restraint of breath, meditation on the heart-space, and others.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice, consciousness-mind_

### 1.54 <a id="tea-sandilya-upanisad-1-54"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The kuṇḍalinī rising above the throat gives liberation to yogins; remaining below it binds the ignorant.

### 1.60-68 <a id="tea-sandilya-upanisad-1-60-68"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Saṃyama on parts and principles gives powers: on the body's parts knowledge of the worlds; on merit and demerit knowledge of past and future; on the throat freedom from hunger; on the kūrma-nāḍī steadiness; on the light in the head vision of siddhas; and others.

### 1.69 <a id="tea-sandilya-upanisad-1-69"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Five pratyāhāras: forcibly withdrawing the senses; seeing all as the Self; renouncing the fruits of daily acts; turning from all objects; and holding (the breath) at the eighteen vital points in ascending and descending order.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice_

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice, ultimate_

### 2 <a id="tea-sandilya-upanisad-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Śāṇḍilya, not finding brahmavidyā in the four Vedas, asks Atharvan; Brahman is truth, knowledge, infinite, and 'that you are'.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, teacher-transmission_

### 3 <a id="tea-sandilya-upanisad-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Brahman has three forms — with parts, without parts, and with-and-without parts; its most manifest form is Dattātreya, who gave himself to Atri and was born of Anasūyā; his meditation form is the naked avadhūta smeared with ash.
