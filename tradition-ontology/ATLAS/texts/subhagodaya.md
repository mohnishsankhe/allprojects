## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### sakti-rahita <a id="tea-subhagodaya-sakti-rahita"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Even the supreme, when without Śakti, cannot do anything; O great Goddess, he becomes able only when joined with Śakti.

> paro 'pi śaktirahitaḥ śaktaḥ kartuṃ na kiñcana / śaktaḥ syāt parameśāni śaktyā yukto bhaved yadi
