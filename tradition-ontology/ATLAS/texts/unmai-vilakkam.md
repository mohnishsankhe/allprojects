## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 36 <a id="tea-unmai-vilakkam-36"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The dance of the Lord is the five acts: creation from the drum, protection from the hand that grants fearlessness, destruction from the fire, concealment from the planted foot, and release (grace) from the raised foot.

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: devotion, knowledge · stage: all · types: world-fate, ultimate_

### pancaksara-section <a id="tea-unmai-vilakkam-pancaksara-section"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In the five letters na-ma-śi-vā-ya, 'na' is concealment (tirōtam), 'ma' is mala, 'śi' is Śiva, 'vā' is his grace (aruḷ) and 'ya' is the soul; the soul standing between mala and Śiva should turn toward grace; placing 'śi' first (śivāya nama) is the subtle form.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: sound, knowledge · stage: intermediate · types: sound-language_

### tattva-section <a id="tea-unmai-vilakkam-tattva-section"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The body and world are made of thirty-six tattvas: twenty-four of the soul (ātma-tattva: the elements, subtle elements, organs and inner organs), seven of knowledge (vidyā-tattva: māyā, kalā, kāla, niyati, vidyā, rāga, puruṣa) and five of Śiva (śiva-tattva); the soul must know them as not itself.
