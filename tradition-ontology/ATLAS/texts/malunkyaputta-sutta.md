## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 10.1-10.8 <a id="tea-malunkyaputta-sutta-10-1-10-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Regarding what is seen, heard, sensed and cognised: in the seen there will be merely the seen, in the heard merely the heard, in the sensed merely the sensed, in the cognised merely the cognised; then you will not be 'by that'; then you will not be 'in that'; then you will be neither here nor beyond nor in between: this is the end of suffering.

> “Ettha ca te, mālukyaputta, diṭṭhasutamutaviññātabbesu dhammesu diṭṭhe diṭṭhamattaṁ bhavissati, sute sutamattaṁ bhavissati, mute mutamattaṁ bhavissati, viññāte viññātamattaṁ bhavissati. Yato kho te, mālukyaputta, diṭṭhasutamutaviññātabbesu dhammesu diṭṭhe diṭṭhamattaṁ bhavissati, sute sutamattaṁ bhavissati, mute mutamattaṁ bhavissati, viññāte viññātamattaṁ bhavissati; tato tvaṁ, mālukyaputta, na tena. Yato tvaṁ, mālukyaputta, na tena; tato tvaṁ, mālukyaputta, na tattha. Yato tvaṁ, mālukyaputta, na tattha; tato tvaṁ, mālukyaputta, nevidha, na huraṁ, na ubhayamantarena. Esevanto dukkhassā”ti.

### 11-24 <a id="tea-malunkyaputta-sutta-11-24"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Māluṅkyaputta's verses: seeing a form with mindfulness lost, attending to the pleasing sign, one experiences it with an infatuated mind; many feelings born of it grow and the mind is harmed; far from nibbāna is he. One who is not inflamed by forms, seeing with mindfulness, dwells near nibbāna; he later became an arahant.
