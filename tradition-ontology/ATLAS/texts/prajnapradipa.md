## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-prajnapradipa-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Buddhapālita's argument is inadequate: it states no reason and example, it does not answer the faults the opponent would raise, and being a statement of consequence it would, by reversal, imply the opposite thesis — that things arise from another — contrary to the Mādhyamika's own tenet.

### 1.1/2 <a id="tea-prajnapradipa-1-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ultimately the inner sense-bases do not arise from themselves, because they exist, like consciousness.
