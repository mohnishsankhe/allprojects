## Teachings (7: skeleton 7)

### 1 <a id="tea-tripura-upanisad-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Three cities, three paths, the all-ruling one: here the imperishable syllables are set; presiding over them is the unaging, ancient one, the greatest glory of the gods.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: cosmic · path: knowledge, sound · stage: all · types: ultimate, sound-language_

### 1-11 <a id="tea-tripura-upanisad-1-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Tripurā pervades three cities (worlds, bodies, states); her cakra has nine triangles (yoni) with their yoginīs; the fifteen nityās; the verse 'desire, womb, kalā…' encodes her fifteen-syllable mantra.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language, ultimate_

concepts: [The Goddess as Brahman](../concepts/devi-as-brahman.md) · practices: [Contemplation of the body as the Śrīcakra](../practices/sricakra-bhavana.md)

### 2 <a id="tea-tripura-upanisad-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

They held nine yonis, nine cakras, nine yogas and nine yoginīs; the kindly lords of the nine cakras, nine mudrās, nine blessed ones of the earth.

### 14 <a id="tea-tripura-upanisad-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Bhaga is Śakti and the blessed Kāma is the Lord; both here are givers of good fortune; they are equal in primacy, equal in being, equal in vigour; their Śakti is unaging, the womb of the universe.
