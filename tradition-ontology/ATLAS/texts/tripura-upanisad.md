## Teachings (7: skeleton 4, sourced 3)

### 1 <a id="tea-tripura-upanisad-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Three cities, three paths, the all-ruling one: here the imperishable syllables are set; presiding over them is the unaging, ancient one, the greatest glory of the gods.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: cosmic · path: knowledge, sound · stage: all · types: ultimate, sound-language_

terms: [tripurā](../terms/tripura.md) · concepts: [The three cities (tripura) and their triads](../concepts/tripura-triads.md)

### 1-11 <a id="tea-tripura-upanisad-1-11"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Tripurā pervades three cities (worlds, bodies, states); her cakra has nine triangles (yoni) with their yoginīs; the fifteen nityās; the verse 'desire, womb, kalā…' encodes her fifteen-syllable mantra.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language, ultimate_

concepts: [The Goddess as Brahman](../concepts/devi-as-brahman.md) · practices: [Contemplation of the body as the Śrīcakra](../practices/sricakra-bhavana.md)

### 2 <a id="tea-tripura-upanisad-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

They held nine yonis, nine cakras, nine yogas and nine yoginīs; the kindly lords of the nine cakras, nine mudrās, nine blessed ones of the earth.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: ritual · path: ritual · stage: intermediate · types: practice_

concepts: [The nine enclosures (āvaraṇa) of the Śrīcakra](../concepts/nine-avaranas.md), [The ten mudrās of Śrīvidyā worship](../concepts/ten-mudras-srividya.md)

### 10 <a id="tea-tripura-upanisad-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The great lunar day, the fifteen Nityās, together with the sixteenth, bears the centre of the city.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: cosmic · path: ritual · stage: intermediate · types: world-fate_

concepts: [The fifteen Nityās](../concepts/fifteen-nityas.md), [The Nityās as the lunar days and time](../concepts/nityas-as-time.md)

### 13-16 <a id="tea-tripura-upanisad-13-16"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

Knowing her, one becomes the supreme; this is the knowledge of the Upaniṣad.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_

### 14 <a id="tea-tripura-upanisad-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Bhaga is Śakti and the blessed Kāma is the Lord; both here are givers of good fortune; they are equal in primacy, equal in being, equal in vigour; their Śakti is unaging, the womb of the universe.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: divine · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate_

concepts: [The fivefold equality of Śiva and Śakti (pañcavidha-sāmya)](../concepts/fivefold-equality.md), [The union of Śiva and Śakti (śiva-śakti-aikya, sāmarasya)](../concepts/siva-sakti-union.md) · disputes: [Is Śakti supreme, is Śiva supreme, or are they equal?](../debates/siva-sakti-primacy.md)
