The collection of Piḷḷai Lokācārya's eighteen short Maṇipravāḷa works on the secret doctrine, including the Mumukṣuppaṭi, Tattvatrayam, Śrīvacanabhūṣaṇam, Arthapañcakam and Arcirādi.
