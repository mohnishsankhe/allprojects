### verse <a id="tea-anavataptanagarajapariprccha-verse"></a>

What is born from conditions is unborn; it has no arising by own-nature; what depends on conditions is called empty; whoever knows emptiness is heedful.

> yaḥ pratyayairjāyati sa hyajāto no tasya utpādu sabhāvato 'sti
