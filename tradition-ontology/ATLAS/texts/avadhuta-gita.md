An ecstatic statement of non-duality from the standpoint of the realized: only by the Lord's grace does the inclination to non-duality arise; the self alone is, formless and all-pervading; there is no one to bow to, no bondage or liberation, no meditator and meditated. Chapter 8 defines the avadhūta syllable by syllable, repeats the Bhāgavata's list of the sage's virtues and warns the renunciant against sexual association and drink.

### 1.1 <a id="tea-avadhuta-gita-1-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

Only by the grace of the Lord does the inclination toward non-duality (advaita-vāsanā) arise in wise men, protecting them from great fear.

### 1.2-6 <a id="tea-avadhuta-gita-1-2-6"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

How shall I bow to the formless, indestructible Śiva as if different, when the Self has filled all this by itself in itself? The world of five elements is like a mirage; to whom should I bow, I who am one and stainless? The Self alone is all; there is neither difference nor non-difference; how can I say 'it is' or 'it is not'? Wonder shines in me. The essence of Vedānta, knowledge and realization, is: I am the Self, formless, all-pervading by nature.

### 1.8-14 <a id="tea-avadhuta-gita-1-8-14"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

No good or evil deed of mind, body or speech is mine; I am the nectar of knowledge, pure, beyond the senses. The mind is like space, faces everywhere, is beyond mind — in truth there is no mind. Know the Self as one everywhere; I the meditator and the supreme object of meditation — how can the undivided be divided? You were never born nor died, you never had a body; scripture says in many ways 'all is Brahman'; why do you run about like a ghost?

teachers: [Dattātreya](../teachers/dattatreya.md)

### 8.1 <a id="tea-avadhuta-gita-8-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

The avadhūta's apology: by pilgrimage I denied your all-pervasiveness, by meditation your being beyond mind, by praise your being beyond speech — forgive these three offences.

_level: bridging · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, knowledge · stage: all · types: practice, ultimate_

### 8.2-4 <a id="tea-avadhuta-gita-8-2-4"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

The qualities of the sage: undisturbed by desires, self-controlled, gentle, pure, possessionless, without striving, moderate in food, peaceful, steady, taking refuge in the Lord, vigilant, deep, resolute, master of the six waves, humble, giving honour, friendly, compassionate, wise, harming none, forbearing, true, blameless, equal and beneficent to all.

### 8.5-9 <a id="tea-avadhuta-gita-8-5-9"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

The syllables of 'avadhūta': 'a' — free of the noose of hope, pure in beginning, middle and end, ever in bliss; 'va' — free of latent tendencies, speaking what is wholesome, living in the present; 'dhū' — limbs grey with dust, mind cleansed, free of concentration and meditation; 'ta' — holding to the thought of truth, free of anxiety and effort, free of darkness and ego.

### 8.10-26 <a id="tea-avadhuta-gita-8-10-26"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

The chapter warns the renunciant, in harsh terms, against sexual association — calling woman a bond for all embodied beings and the gate of hell, and the union of man and woman like ghee near fire — and against liquor, declaring both to be given up by the sage devoted to truth.

### 8.27-28 <a id="tea-avadhuta-gita-8-27-28"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

The body bound by elements is overcome by anxiety; when the mind is ruined the elements perish, so the mind must be guarded on every side — in a healthy mind understanding arises. Those who read or hear this, made by Dattātreya the Avadhūta, whose form is bliss, are not born again.
