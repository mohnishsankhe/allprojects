### 260c19-24 <a id="tea-fozu-tongji-260c19-24"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The seven patriarchs of the Lotus society: first, the Dharma master of Lushan (Huiyuan); second, the master of Guangming in Chang'an (Shandao); third, the pratyutpanna master of Nanyue (Chengyuan); fourth, the five-tone master of Chang'an (Fazhao); fifth, the master of Taiyan in Xinding (Shaokang); sixth, the Chan master Zhijue of Yongming (Yanshou); seventh, the master Yuanjing of Zhaoqing (Shengchang).

### 262c16-26 <a id="tea-fozu-tongji-262c16-26"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Huiyuan lived thirty years on the mountain without entering the world, diligent only in recollection for the Pure Land. In the first eleven years, with clear mind and fixed thought, he three times saw the holy form but kept silent. In the nineteenth year (of Yixi, 416), on the last evening of the seventh month, rising from samādhi in the eastern niche of the Prajñā terrace he saw Amitābha filling space, with transformation buddhas in his halo and the two bodhisattvas; streams of light in fourteen branches proclaimed suffering, emptiness, impermanence and no-self; the Buddha said: 'By the power of my original vow I have come to comfort you; in seven days you will be born in my land.' He also saw deceased companions who asked why he was so late.

> 師居山三十年。迹不入俗。唯以淨土克勤於念。初十一年澄心繫想。三覩聖相沈厚不言。後十九年七月晦夕。於般若臺之東龕方從定起。見阿彌陀佛身滿虛空。

### 263a22-b18 <a id="tea-fozu-tongji-263a22-b18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Shandao, having seen Daochuo's nine-grade assembly, said this contemplation gate alone quickly transcends birth-and-death; he recited kneeling until exhausted, sweating even in the cold, did not lie down for over thirty years, kept the precepts minutely, copied the Amitābha Sūtra a hundred thousand times and painted three hundred Pure Land tableaux. Asked whether recollection brings birth, he said 'as you recollect, so your wish is fulfilled', and at each recitation a light came from his mouth. His verse: 'the skin grows wrinkled, the hair white ... only the direct path of practice: just recollect Amitābha.' Later, saying 'this body is loathsome, I shall go west', he climbed a willow, prayed facing west that the Buddha receive him without loss of right mindfulness, and threw himself down; the emperor named his temple Guangming.

> 或問念佛生淨土耶。師曰。如汝所念遂汝所願。乃自念一聲有一光明從其口出。十至於百光亦如之。……唯有徑路修行。但念阿彌陀佛。後忽謂人曰。此身可厭吾將西歸。乃登柳樹向西願曰。願佛接我菩薩助我。令我不失正念得生安養。言已投身自絕。
