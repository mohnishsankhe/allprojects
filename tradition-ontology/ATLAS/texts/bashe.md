## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### samye-debate <a id="tea-bashe-samye-debate"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Testament of Ba reports that under Trisong Detsen the followers of the Chinese master Moheyan, teaching sudden non-conceptuality, and the Indian party led by Kamalaśīla, teaching the gradual path of the perfections, debated before the king, who ruled that Tibetans should follow Nāgārjuna's view and the gradual path (tradition's account).
