## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-ketaka-mipham-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

In the wisdom chapter commentary Mipham holds that Prāsaṅgika analysis negates all extremes without leaving a qualified remainder, so that in equipoise nothing — not even emptiness — is grasped; Gelug scholars answered that this blurs the object of negation.
