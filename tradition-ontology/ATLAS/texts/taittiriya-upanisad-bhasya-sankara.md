## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 2.1.1 <a id="tea-taittiriya-upanisad-bhasya-sankara-2-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Satyaṃ jñānam anantaṃ brahma' is a definition (lakṣaṇa) of Brahman's own nature: each word excludes what is opposed to it (the unreal, the insentient, the finite), and together they indicate Brahman without making it an object with attributes.

### 2.5.1 <a id="tea-taittiriya-upanisad-bhasya-sankara-2-5-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The five sheaths are taught progressively, each inner one presented as the self of the outer, so that the seeker is led from the food-sheath to Brahman, the support ('tail') of the bliss-sheath, which is not itself a sheath.
