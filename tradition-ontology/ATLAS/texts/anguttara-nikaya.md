# Aṅguttara Nikāya

**Structure:** 11 nipātas (Ekaka to Ekādasaka); the commentarial count is 9,557 suttas, modern counts c. 8,000 (abbreviated repetitions)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The 'numerical discourses', arranged in eleven books by the number of items treated (ones to elevens): the luminous mind (AN 1.51–52), the Kālāma teaching (AN 3.65), the four kinds of concentration development (AN 4.41), kamma as intention (AN 6.63), the eight worldly conditions, women's ordination (AN 8.51), the ten perceptions (AN 10.60), and much lay ethics.
