The oldest 'cord-rules': how to lay out with cord and pegs the sacrificial grounds, fire-altars and bricks of prescribed shapes and areas — squares, rectangles, the falcon-shaped altar and its enlargements — including the rule relating the diagonal of a rectangle to its sides, and transformations of one figure into another of equal area.
