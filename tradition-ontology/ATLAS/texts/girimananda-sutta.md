## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3-13 <a id="tea-girimananda-sutta-3-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The ten perceptions: impermanence (of the aggregates), not-self (of the sense bases), unattractiveness (the thirty-one parts of the body), danger (the many diseases of the body), abandoning (of unwholesome thoughts), dispassion and cessation (nibbāna as the peaceful), non-delight in the whole world, impermanence in all formations, and mindfulness of breathing in sixteen steps; on hearing them Girimānanda's illness subsided.
