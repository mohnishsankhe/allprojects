Jinul's primer: the buddha is one's own mind; the empty and calm, numinous awareness that asks the question is one's original face; the path is sudden awakening followed by gradual cultivation, like an infant who has all its faculties but must grow; cultivation after awakening is the balanced keeping of samādhi and prajñā; supernatural powers are secondary and appear only through gradual cultivation.

## Teachings (10: skeleton 10)

### 1005c21 <a id="tea-susim-kyol-1005c21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The three realms burn like a house on fire; to escape transmigration nothing is better than seeking buddha, and buddha is the mind — not far away, not apart from the body. The physical body is provisional, arising and ceasing; the true mind is like space, neither cut off nor changing. People seek buddha outside with austerities — burning the body, piercing arms, writing sūtras in blood, never lying down — but without knowing their own mind, it is wasted labour.

> 三界熱惱，猶如火宅。其忍淹留，甘受長苦。欲免輪迴，莫若求佛。若欲求佛，佛即是心

### 1006b15 <a id="tea-susim-kyol-1006b15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Though there are many gates into the Way, in essence they are two: sudden awakening and gradual cultivation. Even the 'sudden awakening, sudden cultivation' of the highest faculties rests on gradual cultivation in past lives; all sages first awaken, then cultivate, and through cultivation realise. As the sūtra says, 'principle is awakened suddenly, and with awakening all is dissolved; phenomena are not removed at once but exhausted step by step'; Zongmi: knowing the frozen pond is all water, one still needs the sun's warmth to melt it.

> 夫入道多門，以要言之，不出頓悟、漸修兩門耳

### 1006b28 <a id="tea-susim-kyol-1006b28"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Supernatural powers appear through gradual cultivation after awakening, not at the moment of awakening; for one who has penetrated, powers in the realm of phenomena are 'uncanny matters', the trivial edge of sainthood — even if they appear, one should not make use of them. Those who think a moment of awakening brings countless powers do not know first and last.

> 況事上神通，於達人分上，猶為妖怪之事，亦是聖末邊事，雖或現之，不可要用

### 1006c11 <a id="tea-susim-kyol-1006c11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Sudden awakening: while deluded, ordinary people take the four elements as body and deluded thoughts as mind, not knowing their own nature is the true Dharma body and their own numinous knowing is the true buddha; when a good friend points the way and in one thought they turn back the light and see their original nature, it lacks nothing — that is sudden awakening. Gradual cultivation: habit energies from beginningless time cannot be removed at once, so relying on awakening one cultivates gradually, like a newborn infant who has all faculties but takes years to grow up.

> 頓悟者，凡夫迷時，四大為身，妄想為心，不知自性是真法身，不知自己靈知是真佛也

### 1007a01 <a id="tea-susim-kyol-1007a01"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

All dharmas are like a dream; deluded thoughts are originally quiescent and objects originally empty; where all dharmas are empty, numinous knowing is not obscured — this mind of empty, quiescent, numinous knowing is your original face and the Dharma-seal secretly transmitted by all buddhas and patriarchs.

> 諸法皆空之處靈知不昧，即此空寂靈知之心，是汝本來面目

### 1007a08 <a id="tea-susim-kyol-1007a08"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'What is my empty, numinous knowing mind?' — 'The one asking me now is it. Why not trace back the radiance instead of seeking outside? From morning to night, seeing, hearing, laughing, speaking, angry or pleased — who is it that does all this? Not the physical body, for at death the body does not see or hear.'

> 汝今問我者，是汝空寂靈知之心。何不返照，猶為外覓

### 1007c21 <a id="tea-susim-kyol-1007c21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Contemplate killing, stealing, lust and lying as arising from the nature — arising, they do not arise, and right there are still; 'do not fear thoughts arising, only fear being slow to notice'; 'when a thought arises, be aware of it; aware of it, it is gone'.

> 所以云：『不怕念起，唯恐覺遲。』又云：『念起即覺，覺之即無。』

### 1008a06 <a id="tea-susim-kyol-1008a06"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Samādhi and prajñā are the substance and function of the self-nature, the empty calm and numinous knowing: samādhi is substance, prajñā function; because function is of the substance, prajñā is not apart from samādhi; because substance is of the function, samādhi is not apart from prajñā; calm yet always knowing, knowing yet always calm — this is the sudden school's dual cultivation of samādhi and prajñā.

> 定是體，慧是用也。即體之用故，慧不離定；即用之體故，定不離慧

### 1008a11 <a id="tea-susim-kyol-1008a11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

To use calm first to cure distraction and then alertness to cure dullness, balancing the two in sequence to enter stillness, is the practice of inferior faculties in the gradual gate; for the one who has penetrated, the balance of samādhi and prajñā is effortless — seeing and hearing, dressing and eating, all is 'just thus', like an empty boat riding the waves.

> 先以寂寂，治於緣慮；後以惺惺，治於昏住。先後對治，均調昏亂，以入於靜者，是為漸門劣機所行也
