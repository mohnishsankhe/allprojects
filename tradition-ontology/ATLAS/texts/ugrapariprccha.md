The Inquiry of the householder Ugra: the lay and monastic bodhisattva's conduct; the householder should see his household as a burning house and practice the perfections; praises the forest life for the renunciant bodhisattva.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### home <a id="tea-ugrapariprccha-home"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The householder bodhisattva should regard his home as a place of attachment and bondage, his family as obstacles to be served with detachment, and practice the perfections, the precepts and the fortnightly eight precepts; the renunciant bodhisattva should dwell in the forest.
