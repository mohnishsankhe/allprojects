## Teachings (2: sourced 2)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-devi-upanisad-1-2"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

The gods ask the Goddess 'who are you?'; she: 'I am of the form of Brahman; from me arises the world of prakṛti and puruṣa, the void and the non-void; I am bliss and non-bliss, knowledge and ignorance'.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate_

concepts: [The Goddess as Brahman](../concepts/devi-as-brahman.md)

### 14-17 (summary) <a id="tea-devi-upanisad-14-17-summary"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

Her mantra is given in coded form; one who knows it crosses sorrow.
