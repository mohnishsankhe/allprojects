The scripture of Radhasoami: in verse (Nazm) and prose (Nasr), Soamiji teaches that Radhasoami is the supreme being, that the soul (surat) is a drop of that ocean fallen into the regions of Kāl and māyā, and that it returns by surat-śabd yoga under a Sant Satguru through Sahasdal Kanwal, Trikuṭī, Sunn, Mahāsunn, Bhanwar Guphā and Sat Lok to Radhasoami Dhām.

### Sār Bachan (prose), on the Sant Satguru <a id="tea-sar-bachan-necessity-of-sant-satguru"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _recent (post-1800)_

Without a living Sant Satguru the soul cannot cross the regions of Kāl; the Sant alone knows the way, gives the secret of the sound and protects the disciple within.

### Sār Bachan (prose), on the soul's descent <a id="tea-sar-bachan-descent-of-the-surat"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _recent (post-1800)_

The soul descended from the pure spiritual regions through the realms of Brahm and māyā into the body, where it is bound by mind and matter and scattered through the nine doors; it must be gathered at the eye centre and return by the current by which it came.
