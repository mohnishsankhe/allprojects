A Kannada stone inscription at Abalūr recording Ēkānta Rāmayya's contest with the Jainas, in which he is said to have offered his own head to Śiva and received it back, and the conversion of the Jaina shrine to Śiva's worship.

### the inscription's narrative <a id="tea-abalur-inscription-record"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Ēkānta Rāmayya, challenged by the Jainas of Abalūr, wagers his head on Śiva's supremacy, cuts it off before Śiva and receives it back whole; the Jainas yield and their shrine is given to Śiva's worship.
