# Abalūr inscription of Ēkānta Rāmayya

**Language:** Kannada
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)
**Genre:** inscription
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 1200 (late 12th–early 13th c.); (confidence low)
**Availability:** unknown

A Kannada stone inscription at Abalūr recording Ēkānta Rāmayya's contest with the Jainas, in which he is said to have offered his own head to Śiva and received it back, and the conversion of the Jaina shrine to Śiva's worship.

_Notes: The Jaina side is known here only through this Śaiva record (reported by the other side)._
