## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-4 <a id="tea-mudgala-upanisad-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Puruṣa-sūkta is explained: the Person's pervasion in space and time, his giving liberation, his fourfold form as Aniruddha and the vyūhas; Vāsudeva taught this to Indra.
