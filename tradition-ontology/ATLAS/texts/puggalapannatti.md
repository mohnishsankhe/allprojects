## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-puggalapannatti-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There are six designations: of aggregates, of bases, of elements, of truths, of faculties and of persons; this book deals with the designation of persons.

> Cha paññattiyo— khandhapaññatti, āyatanapaññatti, dhātupaññatti, saccapaññatti, indriyapaññatti, puggalapaññattīti.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind_

### 1.1 <a id="tea-puggalapannatti-1-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The single-type persons include the one temporarily and the one permanently liberated, the worldling, the one at change-of-lineage, the trainee, the one beyond training, the fully and the privately enlightened Buddhas, the one liberated both ways, by wisdom, the body-witness, the one attained to view, the one liberated by faith, the Dhamma-follower, the faith-follower, and the kinds of stream-enterer and non-returner.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: general · stage: all · types: karma-liberation_

### 2.1 <a id="tea-puggalapannatti-2-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Who is liberated both ways? One who has touched with the body the eight liberations and whose taints are destroyed by seeing with wisdom. Who is liberated by wisdom? One who has not touched the eight liberations with the body, but whose taints are destroyed by seeing with wisdom.

> Katamo ca puggalo ubhatobhāgavimutto? Idhekacco puggalo aṭṭha vimokkhe kāyena phusitvā viharati; paññāya cassa disvā āsavā parikkhīṇā honti— ayaṁ vuccati puggalo “ubhatobhāgavimutto”. Katamo ca puggalo paññāvimutto? Idhekacco puggalo na heva kho aṭṭha vimokkhe kāyena phusitvā viharati; paññāya cassa disvā āsavā parikkhīṇā honti. Ayaṁ vuccati puggalo “paññāvimutto”.
