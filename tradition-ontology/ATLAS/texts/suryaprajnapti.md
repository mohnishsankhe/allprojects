# Sūryaprajñapti

**Alternate titles:** Sūrapaṇṇatti
**Language:** Ardhamāgadhī Prakrit
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md), [Sthānakavāsī](../lineages/sthanakavasi.md), [Śvetāmbara Terāpanth](../lineages/terapanthi.md)
**Genre:** upāṅga (astronomy)
**Part of:** [Śvetāmbara Āgamas (the Jain canon)](svetambara-agamas.md)
**Location in parent:** Upāṅga 5 (6th in some lists)
**Availability:** digitized-original

An Upāṅga on astronomy and calendar: the motions of the two suns and two moons of Jambūdvīpa, the lunar mansions and the five-year yuga; nearly identical in content with the Candraprajñapti.

_Notes: Lists differ on the order of the Sūrya-, Jambūdvīpa- and Candra-prajñaptis (5th–7th Upāṅgas)._
