### letting-go <a id="tea-food-for-the-heart-letting-go"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Virtue, concentration and wisdom are one path; peace is found by letting go — if you let go a little you have a little peace, a lot, a lot of peace; seeing everything as uncertain ('mai nae') frees the heart from clinging.
