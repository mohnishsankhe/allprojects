## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 24 <a id="tea-balapandita-sutta-24"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If a man threw a yoke with one hole into the ocean and a blind turtle came up once every hundred years, the turtle would put its neck through that hole sooner than the fool who has gone once to the lower world would regain the human state, for there is no conduct by Dhamma there, only mutual devouring.
