## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-6 <a id="tea-puttamamsa-sutta-2-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Edible food is to be regarded as a couple eating their son's flesh to cross a desert; contact like a flayed cow attacked by creatures wherever it stands; mental volition like a charcoal pit into which a man is dragged; consciousness like a criminal struck morning, noon and evening by a hundred spears. When each nutriment is fully understood, the lust for the five cords of sensuality, the three feelings, the three cravings and name-and-form are fully understood.
