An encyclopedia of Purāṇic knowledge: the avatāras, temple building and image installation, dīkṣā and worship, tīrthas (Gayā), vratas and gifts, astrology, medicine, polity, poetics and grammar, and closing chapters on the hells, the eight limbs of yoga and non-dual knowledge of Brahman.
