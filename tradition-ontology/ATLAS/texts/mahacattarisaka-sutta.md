## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3-36 <a id="tea-mahacattarisaka-sutta-3-36"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Noble right concentration is unification of mind supported by the other seven path factors. Right view comes first: of each factor there is a mundane kind, affected by effluents, siding with merit and ripening in attachment, and a noble, supramundane kind that is a factor of the path; right view, effort and mindfulness circle around each factor. The trainee has eight factors; the arahant ten, with right knowledge and right liberation.
