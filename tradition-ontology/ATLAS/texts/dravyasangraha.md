## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 2 <a id="tea-dravyasangraha-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The soul is characterised by cognition, is formless, is the doer, is the size of its body, is the enjoyer, is in worldly existence, is perfected, and naturally moves upward.

> jīvo uvaogamao amutti kattā sadehaparimāṇo / bhottā saṃsārattho siddho so vissasoḍḍhagaī

### 47 <a id="tea-dravyasangraha-47"></a>
