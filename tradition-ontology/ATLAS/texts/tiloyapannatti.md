Yativṛṣabha's Prakrit treatise on the three worlds (Trilokaprajñapti) in nine great chapters: the shape and measures of the universe, the hells, the human world with its time-cycles and the lives, emblems, parents and dates of the Tīrthaṅkaras and other great persons, the gods, and the Siddhas; also a Digambara chronology of teachers after Mahāvīra.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 4 <a id="tea-tiloyapannatti-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The chapter on the human world gives for each of the twenty-four Tīrthaṅkaras the birthplace, parents, emblem, height, lifespan, colour, the dates of the five auspicious events, the numbers of their gaṇadharas, monks and nuns, and their places of nirvāṇa; and describes the samavasaraṇa and the Jina's divine sound.

### 4/2 <a id="tea-tiloyapannatti-4-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

After Mahāvīra's nirvāṇa came three omniscients in 62 years, five knowers of all scripture in 100 years, eleven knowers of ten Pūrvas in 183 years, five knowers of the eleven Aṅgas in 220 years, and four knowers of the Ācārāṅga in 118 years — 683 years in all, after which no one knew even one Aṅga complete.
