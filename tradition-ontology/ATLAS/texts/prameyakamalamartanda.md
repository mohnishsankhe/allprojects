## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### kevalibhukti <a id="tea-prameyakamalamartanda-kevalibhukti"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Prabhācandra argues that the omniscient does not eat: eating presupposes hunger, which is a form of suffering and desire incompatible with infinite bliss and the destruction of delusion; the omniscient's body is sustained by subtle matter (nokarma) without morsel-eating (kavalāhāra); and eating would require perceiving impure things, which does not befit the Lord.

### strimukti <a id="tea-prameyakamalamartanda-strimukti"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Prabhācandra argues that women cannot be liberated: they cannot give up clothing and so cannot be without possessions; they lack the strongest bodily constitution needed for the highest pure meditation; as they cannot fall to the lowest hell they cannot rise to the highest state; and the scripture's 'female siddhas' refers to psychological, not physical, sex.
