# Aruṭpulampal (Paṭṭiṉattār)

`src:arut-pulampal-pattinattar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tamil
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Part of:** [Paṭṭiṉattār pāṭalkaḷ (Tiruppāṭal tiraṭṭu)](pattinattar-padalgal.md)
**Authors:** 
  - [Paṭṭiṉattār (Tiruveṇkāṭar)](../teachers/pattinattar.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Availability:** digitized-original

Two sets of plaintive couplets ('lament for grace'), the first addressed to the Primal One (mutalvaṉ muṟaiyīṭu), the second in the voice of a girl to her friend, longing to see Śiva and to know herself.
