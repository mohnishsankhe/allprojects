### pada 'e sakhi hāmari dukhaka nāhi ora' <a id="tea-vidyapati-padavali-bhadra-viraha"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Rādhā's lament in the rains: it is the month of Bhādra, clouds heavy, my house empty; the thunder roars, and the lord of my life is far away — how shall I pass the days?
