## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 8 <a id="tea-abhayarajakumara-sutta-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Tathāgata does not speak what is untrue or unbeneficial; what is true and beneficial but unwelcome he speaks at the right time; what is true, beneficial and welcome he likewise knows the time to speak, because he has compassion for beings.
