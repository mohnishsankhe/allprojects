The Marathi abhaṅgas ascribed to Nāmdev (some 2,500) — childhood intimacy with Viṭṭhala, the name and kīrtan, the saints of his circle, the Tīrthāvaḷī (pilgrimage with Jñāneśvar) and the Samādhi songs on Jñāneśvar's entering samādhi — together with his Hindi padas, some of which are preserved in the Sikh scripture (context only).

### Samādhi abhaṅgas <a id="tea-namdev-gatha-samadhi"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Nāmdev's songs of Jñāneśvar's samādhi at Āḷandī: with Nivṛtti, the saints and Viṭṭhala present, Jñāneśvar enters the chamber, sits in meditation, closes his eyes, and the stone is set in place; Nāmdev grieves as for a mother.

### abhaṅga 'nācūṃ kīrtanāce raṅgīṃ' <a id="tea-namdev-gatha-nacu-kirtanace-rangi"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

We will dance in the joy of kīrtan and light the lamp of knowledge in the world.

> नाचूं कीर्तनाचे रंगीं । ज्ञानदीप लावूं जगीं ॥

### āratī 'yugeṃ aṭṭhāvīsa viṭevarī ubhā' <a id="tea-namdev-gatha-yuge-atthavisa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Viṭṭhala āratī: for twenty-eight yugas he has stood on the brick, Rakhumāī at his left, radiant; Puṇḍalīka's devotion keeps him there.

> युगें अठ्ठावीस विटेवरी उभा । वामांगीं रखुमाई दिसे दिव्य शोभा ॥
