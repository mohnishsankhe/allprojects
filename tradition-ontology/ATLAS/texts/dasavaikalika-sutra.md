The second Mūlasūtra, the first text learned by a new Śvetāmbara monk: dharma as non-harm, restraint and austerity; the bee that takes nectar without hurting the flower; the six classes of living beings and the vows; the rules of alms-seeking and speech; conquering the four passions; and the marks of the true monk.
