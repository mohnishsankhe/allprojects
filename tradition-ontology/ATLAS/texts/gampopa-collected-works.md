### ordinary-mind <a id="tea-gampopa-collected-works-ordinary-mind"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Ordinary mind is one's own present awareness, uncontrived by anything, unspoiled by any (spiritual or worldly) conception; resting in it as it is, is the dharmakāya recognized.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, practice_

### sutra-mahamudra <a id="tea-gampopa-collected-works-sutra-mahamudra"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Gampopa gave the Mahāmudrā pointing-out, grounded in devotion and the guru's blessing and in the sūtra teaching on buddha-nature, also to disciples who had not received tantric empowerment, besides teaching it within the path of method.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, devotion · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, dispute_

### three-coemergents <a id="tea-gampopa-collected-works-three-coemergents"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Co-emergent union: mind-as-such, co-emergent, is the dharmakāya; appearances, co-emergent, are the light of the dharmakāya; thoughts, co-emergent, are the waves of the dharmakāya.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, consciousness-mind_

### two-streams <a id="tea-gampopa-collected-works-two-streams"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Gampopa's assembly teachings join the Kadam graded path — renunciation, compassion and bodhicitta — with the Mahāmudrā instruction, so that the view is pointed out to a mind prepared by the path.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, practice_
