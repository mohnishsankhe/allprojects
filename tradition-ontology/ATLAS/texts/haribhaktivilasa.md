The Gauḍīya code of Vaiṣṇava ritual and conduct in twenty 'vilāsas': the guru and disciple and their testing, initiation, daily purity and worship, deity worship with nyāsa and bhūta-śuddhi, the name and offences, the Ekādaśī fast and festivals, temple construction and consecration.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1 <a id="tea-haribhaktivilasa-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The guru should be of good family and conduct, learned, pure, devoted and self-controlled, and the disciple sincere and faithful; each should examine the other before initiation.

### 2 <a id="tea-haribhaktivilasa-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Initiation (dīkṣā) is so called because it gives divine knowledge and destroys sin; by it the disciple receives the mantra and becomes fit for deity worship.
