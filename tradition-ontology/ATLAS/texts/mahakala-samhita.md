A large Kālīkula saṃhitā whose surviving parts include the Guhyakālī section (on the ten-faced Guhyakālī of the Nepalese Uttarāmnāya) and the Kāmakalākhaṇḍa (on Kāmakalākālī); it contains hymns, mantras and rites of Kālī.
