An account of the body: its elements and components, the embryo's month-by-month growth, what determines sex and defects, the embryo's memory of past births and vow to seek liberation (through Sāṃkhya-yoga and the Lord), its forgetting at birth, and counts of the body's parts.

## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 1 <a id="tea-garbha-upanisad-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The body is made of the five elements, rests on six supports, has six flavours, seven constituents, three humours and two kinds of birth.

### 2 <a id="tea-garbha-upanisad-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The embryo grows month by month: after one night a mixture, seven nights a bubble, a fortnight a lump, a month firm; head in the second month, feet in the third, and so on; in the ninth it is complete.

### 3 <a id="tea-garbha-upanisad-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Excess of the father's seed gives a male, of the mother's a female, equal a eunuch; defects arise from the parents' state.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: body-layers_

### 4 <a id="tea-garbha-upanisad-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In the ninth month the embryo remembers its past births and vows: if freed from the womb it will resort to Sāṃkhya-yoga, to Maheśvara or Nārāyaṇa, and meditate on Brahman; touched at birth by the Vaiṣṇava wind, it forgets.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, death-dying_

### 5 <a id="tea-garbha-upanisad-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Counts of the body: 107 vital points, 180 joints, 109 sinews, 700 veins, 360 bones, and so on.
