A six-limbed yoga (āsana, prāṇāyāma, pratyāhāra, dhāraṇā, dhyāna, samādhi) that joins Kaula body-mapping to haṭha method: siddhāsana and padmāsana, the centres, the channels and breaths, kuṇḍalinī, the seals and locks (mahāmudrā, nabhomudrā/khecarī, uḍḍiyāna, jālandhara, mūlabandha), viparītakaraṇī for the nectar, the five element concentrations, meditation with and without qualities and samādhi.

### topic.knowledge-of-own-body <a id="tea-vivekamartanda-topic-knowledge-of-own-body"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

How can yogins succeed who do not know in their own body the six centres, the sixteen supports (ādhāra), the three targets (lakṣya) and the five voids (vyoma)?

### topic.six-limbs <a id="tea-vivekamartanda-topic-six-limbs"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Yoga has six limbs: posture, breath-control, withdrawal, concentration, meditation and samādhi (the yamas and niyamas are not counted among the limbs).
