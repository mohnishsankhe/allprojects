## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### su.1.1-mangala <a id="tea-ayurveda-dipika-su-1-1-mangala"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Homage to the lord of serpents (Śeṣa), who removed the faults of mind, speech and body by the Pātañjala (Yoga), the Mahābhāṣya and the redaction of Caraka; and homage to Brahmā, Dakṣa, the Aśvins, Indra, Bharadvāja, Punarvasu, Agniveśa, Caraka and the others.

> pātañjalamahābhāṣyacarakapratisaṃskṛtaiḥ | manovākkāyadoṣāṇāṃ hartre 'hipataye namaḥ
