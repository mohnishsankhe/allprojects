## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 8-16 <a id="tea-maranassati-sutta-8-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Monks who develop mindfulness of death thinking 'may I live a day', 'a meal', 'while I chew a few mouthfuls' dwell negligently; those who think 'may I live for the time of one breath in and out, that I may attend to the Buddha's teaching' dwell diligently and develop it keenly for the destruction of the effluents. Mindfulness of death merges in the deathless and culminates in the deathless.
