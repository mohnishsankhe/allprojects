### 1.4 <a id="tea-pancasiddhantika-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Of the five siddhāntas the Pauliśa is accurate, the Romaka close to it, the Sāvitra (Saura) more accurate, and the other two (Paitāmaha and Vāsiṣṭha) far from the truth.
