## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### guru-yoga <a id="tea-longchen-nyingthig-ngondro-guru-yoga"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

At the end of guru yoga the practitioner receives the four empowerments: from the guru's forehead (oṃ) white light purifies the body and gives the vase empowerment; from the throat (āḥ) red light purifies speech and gives the secret empowerment; from the heart (hūṃ) blue light purifies mind and gives the wisdom empowerment; and from all three the fourth, the word empowerment; then guru and disciple mingle and one rests in the nature of mind.
