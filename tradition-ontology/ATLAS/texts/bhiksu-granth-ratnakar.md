## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### anukampa <a id="tea-bhiksu-granth-ratnakar-anukampa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Compassion that preserves a being's worldly life — by rescuing it by force, or by feeding and giving to those without vows — is worldly (laukika) compassion and not spiritual dharma; spiritual (lokottara) compassion and giving are those that help a soul toward self-restraint; dharma lies in renunciation and restraint, not in the maintenance of worldly life.
