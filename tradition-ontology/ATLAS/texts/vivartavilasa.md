## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### Vivartavilāsa (and Sahajiyā manuals), on turning kāma into prema <a id="tea-vivartavilasa-kama-into-prema"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _restricted: summary only_

Kāma (desire for one's own pleasure) is not to be destroyed but transformed: as the Caitanya-caritāmṛta says, kāma is wanting the pleasure of one's own senses and prema wanting Kṛṣṇa's; the Sahajiyā holds that prema is refined out of kāma through the discipline of the couple, as gold from ore.

### Vivartavilāsa, on the hidden meaning of the Caitanya-caritāmṛta <a id="tea-vivartavilasa-hidden-meaning-of-caitanya-caritamrta"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Caitanya-caritāmṛta has an outer and an inner meaning; its inner meaning, known to Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja and the Gosvāmīs, is the Sahajiyā doctrine of the innate Man and of love practised with a partner.
