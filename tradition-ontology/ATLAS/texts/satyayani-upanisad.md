### 1-3 <a id="tea-satyayani-upanisad-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Mind is the cause of bondage and liberation — attached to objects it binds, free of them it frees.

### 6-8 <a id="tea-satyayani-upanisad-6-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Triple staff, thread, cloth or loincloth with girdle, sling and strainer — these he keeps till death, and is buried with them.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: advanced · types: ethics, death-dying_

### 9-11 <a id="tea-satyayani-upanisad-9-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Viṣṇu's mark is twofold, manifest and unmanifest; abandoning either one falls; the triple staff is the Vaiṣṇava mark. All four kinds of renouncer bear Viṣṇu's mark, topknot and thread, and perform the five sacrifices; they differ in livelihood.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ethics, dispute_

concepts: [The renouncer's marks (liṅga)](../concepts/renunciant-marks.md), [Four kinds of renunciation](../concepts/four-kinds-of-sannyasa.md) · disputes: [Should the renouncer keep outer marks — triple staff, topknot, sacred thread — or abandon them (one staff, or none)?](../debates/renunciant-marks-ekadanda-tridanda.md)

### 12-17 <a id="tea-satyayani-upanisad-12-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The renouncer's sacrifices are inner: japa, the yoga of service to Hari and guru, austerity as non-injury, study of the Upaniṣads, and the sacrifice of knowledge, the best; knowledge is his staff, topknot and thread.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, knowledge · stage: advanced · types: practice, ethics_

### 26-29 <a id="tea-satyayani-upanisad-26-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

One who abandons this Vaiṣṇava discipline is like a killer of heroes and brāhmaṇas, not purified even by expiations — one who has fallen from the height.

_level: conventional · standpoint: polemical · path: general · stage: advanced · types: ethics, dispute_

### 30-32 <a id="tea-satyayani-upanisad-30-32"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The renouncer uplifts a hundred generations before and three hundred after; even uttering 'I have renounced' with the last breath in the throat saves the ancestors.
