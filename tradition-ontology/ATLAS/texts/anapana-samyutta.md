### 54.1 <a id="tea-anapana-samyutta-54-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

One thing developed and cultivated is of great fruit and benefit: mindfulness of breathing, practised in sixteen steps.

### 54.9 <a id="tea-anapana-samyutta-54-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

At Vesālī the Buddha praised the meditation on foulness and went into seclusion for half a month; the monks, practising it, became repelled and disgusted by their own bodies, and many took their lives. On his return the Buddha had the Saṅgha assembled and taught concentration by mindfulness of breathing as peaceful, sublime, a pleasant dwelling, which disperses unwholesome states on the spot as the first rain of the season settles the dust.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, body-breath · stage: all · types: practice, death-dying_
