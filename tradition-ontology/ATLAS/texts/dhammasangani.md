## Teachings (13: skeleton 13)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-dhammasangani-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The matrix opens with the triad of the wholesome: dhammas are wholesome, unwholesome, or indeterminate (neither).

> Kusalā dhammā. Akusalā dhammā. Abyākatā dhammā.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind, ethics_

terms: [kusala](../terms/kusala.md), [akusala](../terms/akusala.md), [abyākata](../terms/abyakata.md), [dhamma (Abhidhamma sense)](../terms/dhamma-abhidhamma.md), [mātikā](../terms/matika.md) · concepts: [The Abhidhamma matrix (mātikā)](../concepts/dhammasangani-matika.md)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-dhammasangani-1-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The matrix sets out twenty-two triads (wholesome, feeling, result, clung-to, defiled, applied thought, rapture, to be abandoned by seeing, accumulation, trainee, limited, inferior, fixed destiny, path-object, arisen, past, internal, visible-resistant, etc.) and one hundred Abhidhamma dyads grouped in clusters (roots, taints, fetters, ties, floods, bonds, hindrances, misapprehension, clinging, defilements) by which every dhamma can be classified.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [mātikā](../terms/matika.md) · concepts: [The Abhidhamma matrix (mātikā)](../concepts/dhammasangani-matika.md)

### 1.3 <a id="tea-dhammasangani-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A further forty-two dyads are taken from the suttas (Suttantika-duka-mātikā), beginning with dhammas that partake of knowledge and those that partake of ignorance.

> Vijjābhāgino dhammā. Avijjābhāgino dhammā.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind_

concepts: [The Abhidhamma matrix (mātikā)](../concepts/dhammasangani-matika.md)

### 2.1.1 <a id="tea-dhammasangani-2-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Defines wholesome dhammas by the first sense-sphere wholesome consciousness: whenever a sense-sphere wholesome consciousness has arisen, accompanied by joy and associated with knowledge, with any of the six kinds of object, at that time there are contact, feeling, perception, volition, consciousness, applied and sustained thought, rapture, pleasure, one-pointedness, the faculties, path factors, powers and the other factors listed.

> Katame dhammā kusalā? Yasmiṁ samaye kāmāvacaraṁ kusalaṁ cittaṁ uppannaṁ hoti somanassasahagataṁ ñāṇasampayuttaṁ rūpārammaṇaṁ vā saddārammaṇaṁ vā gandhārammaṇaṁ vā rasārammaṇaṁ vā phoṭṭhabbārammaṇaṁ vā dhammārammaṇaṁ vā yaṁ yaṁ vā panārabbha, tasmiṁ samaye— Phasso hoti, vedanā hoti, saññā hoti, cetanā hoti, cittaṁ hoti, Vitakko hoti, vicāro hoti, pīti hoti, sukhaṁ hoti, cittassekaggatā hoti,

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind, ethics_

terms: [citta](../terms/citta.md), [cetasika](../terms/cetasika.md), [phassa](../terms/phassa.md), [cetana](../terms/cetana.md), [kāmāvacara](../terms/kamavacara.md) · concepts: [The 89 / 121 types of consciousness](../concepts/classes-of-consciousness.md), [The fifty-two mental factors (cetasika)](../concepts/fifty-two-cetasikas.md)

### 2.1.5 <a id="tea-dhammasangani-2-1-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The supramundane wholesome consciousness is the jhāna developed as the path that leads out and dismantles the round, for abandoning views and reaching the first stage; at that time, besides the jhāna factors, the faculty 'I shall know the unknown', all eight path factors, and calm and insight together are present.

> Yasmiṁ samaye lokuttaraṁ jhānaṁ bhāveti niyyānikaṁ apacayagāmiṁ diṭṭhigatānaṁ pahānāya paṭhamāya bhūmiyā pattiyā vivicceva kāmehi …pe… paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharati dukkhapaṭipadaṁ dandhābhiññaṁ, tasmiṁ samaye phasso hoti, vedanā hoti, saññā hoti, …

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: karma-liberation, consciousness-mind_

terms: [lokuttara](../terms/lokuttara.md), [magga](../terms/magga.md), [samatha](../terms/samatha.md), [vipassanā](../terms/vipassana.md) · concepts: [The 89 / 121 types of consciousness](../concepts/classes-of-consciousness.md), [The four paths and four fruits](../concepts/four-paths-and-fruits.md)

### 2.2.1 <a id="tea-dhammasangani-2-2-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The indeterminate dhammas are the results of wholesome and unwholesome dhammas in all spheres, functional dhammas that are neither wholesome nor unwholesome nor results, all matter, and the unconditioned element.

> Katame dhammā abyākatā? Kusalākusalānaṁ dhammānaṁ vipākā kāmāvacarā rūpāvacarā arūpāvacarā apariyāpannā vedanākkhandho saññākkhandho saṅkhārakkhandho viññāṇakkhandho, ye ca dhammā kiriyā neva kusalā nākusalā na ca kammavipākā, sabbañca rūpaṁ, asaṅkhatā ca dhātu— ime dhammā abyākatā.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, consciousness-mind_

terms: [abyākata](../terms/abyakata.md), [vipāka](../terms/vipaka.md), [kiriyā](../terms/kiriya.md), [asaṅkhata](../terms/asankhata.md), [rūpa](../terms/rupa.md) · concepts: [The four ultimate realities (paramattha-dhamma)](../concepts/four-paramattha-dhammas.md)

### 2.2.1 <a id="tea-dhammasangani-2-2-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Dhammasaṅgaṇī's analysis of matter enumerates the great elements and the derived matter (sense faculties, objects, femininity, masculinity, life faculty, intimation, space, lightness, malleability, wieldiness, growth, continuity, ageing, impermanence, edible food) without naming a 'heart-base'.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: body-layers_

terms: [mahābhūta](../terms/mahabhuta.md), [upādā-rūpa](../terms/upada-rupa.md), [hadaya-vatthu](../terms/hadaya-vatthu.md) · concepts: [The twenty-eight kinds of matter](../concepts/twenty-eight-rupas.md)

### 2.3.2 <a id="tea-dhammasangani-2-3-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Lists the ten fetters of the Abhidhamma: sensual lust, aversion, conceit, view, doubt, clinging to rules and observances, lust for existence, envy, avarice, ignorance.

### 2.3.2 <a id="tea-dhammasangani-2-3-2-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Lists four taints: of sensual desire, of existence, of views, and of ignorance (the Abhidhamma adds the taint of views to the three of the suttas).

> Katame dhammā āsavā? Cattāro āsavā— kāmāsavo, bhavāsavo, diṭṭhāsavo, avijjāsavo.

### 2.3.2 <a id="tea-dhammasangani-2-3-2-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Misapprehension (parāmāsa) is wrong view grasping at theses such as 'the world is eternal', 'not eternal', 'finite', 'infinite', 'soul and body are the same', 'different', and the four positions on the Tathāgata after death.

> Katame dhammā parāmāsā? Diṭṭhiparāmāso. Tattha katamo diṭṭhiparāmāso? Sassato lokoti vā, asassato lokoti vā, antavā lokoti vā, anantavā lokoti vā …
