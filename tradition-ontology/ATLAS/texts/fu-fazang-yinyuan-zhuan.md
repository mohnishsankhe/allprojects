# Account of the Causes and Conditions of the Transmission of the Dharma Treasury (Fu fazang yinyuan zhuan)

Chinese account (5th–6th c.) of 23/24 Indian masters who successively held the Dharma treasury from Mahākāśyapa to Siṃha, whose murder ended the line; used by Tiantai and the early Chan historians and later expanded by the Chan 28-patriarch list.
