### 2 <a id="tea-atma-bodha-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Compared to other means, knowledge is the one direct means to liberation; as cooking is impossible without fire, liberation is impossible without knowledge.

> bodho 'nyasādhanebhyo hi sākṣān mokṣaikasādhanam / pākasya vahnivaj jñānaṃ vinā mokṣo na sidhyati

### 3 <a id="tea-atma-bodha-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Action, not being opposed to ignorance, cannot remove it; knowledge destroys ignorance as light destroys the mass of darkness.

> avirodhitayā karma nāvidyāṃ vinivartayet / vidyāvidyāṃ nihanty eva tejas timirasaṅghavat

### 7 <a id="tea-atma-bodha-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The world appears real, like silver in the shell, as long as Brahman, the non-dual substratum of all, is not known.

> tāvat satyaṃ jagad bhāti śuktikārajataṃ yathā / yāvan na jñāyate brahma sarvādhiṣṭhānam advayam
