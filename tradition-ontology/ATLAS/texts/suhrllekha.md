## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 29 <a id="tea-suhrllekha-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Gain and loss, pleasure and pain, fame and disgrace, praise and blame — the eight worldly concerns: the one who knows the world makes them equal, not objects of the mind.
