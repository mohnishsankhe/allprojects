## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 44 <a id="tea-mahadevastotra-44"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Homage to him in whom attachment and the other [passions], which make the sprout of the seed of rebirth grow, have been destroyed — whether he be Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Hara or the Jina.

> bhavabījāṅkurajananā rāgādyāḥ kṣayam upāgatā yasya / brahmā vā viṣṇur vā haro jino vā namas tasmai
