Bhāskararāya's treatise, with his own commentary Prakāśa, on the Śrīvidyā mantra: the first part on its form, syllables and the subtle stages of pronouncing its final sounds; the second part on japa and on the necessity of knowing its meanings, of which fifteen are expounded (the Gāyatrī meaning, the six Yoginīhṛdaya meanings and others).

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 3 <a id="tea-varivasya-rahasya-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Victorious is that great light: when it is seen, nothing else is seen; and yet the Veda says that when it is known all is known — how can that be?

> sa jayati mahān prakāśo yasmin dṛṣṭe na dṛśyate kim api / katham iva tasmiñ jñāte sarvaṃ vijñātam ucyate vede

### 54-56 <a id="tea-varivasya-rahasya-54-56"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Uttering a word without knowing its meaning bears no fruit, as oblation thrown on ash without fire does not blaze; those who recite many words without knowing their sense are like an ass carrying sandalwood; those who want the aims of life must learn the meanings — for those who disregard meaning there is no gain but harm.

### 57-59 <a id="tea-varivasya-rahasya-57-59"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The meanings of the full Gāyatrī (the Pañcadaśī) are fifteen, equal to its syllables: the first (the Gāyatrī meaning), then the literal, traditional, hidden (the fourth), Kaula, secret and great-principle meanings, the meaning of the name, of the sound-form, of part of the name, of the Śaktas, of fusion (sāmarasya), the collective, the qualified, and the meaning of the great saying.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: sound, knowledge · stage: intermediate · types: sound-language_

### nada-kalas <a id="tea-varivasya-rahasya-nada-kalas"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In pronouncing the mantra's final sounds the voice passes through subtle phases — bindu, ardhacandra, rodhinī, nāda, nādānta, śakti, vyāpikā, samanā and unmanā — each lasting half as long as the one before, from 128 units down to one for samanā; unmanā has no time at all.
