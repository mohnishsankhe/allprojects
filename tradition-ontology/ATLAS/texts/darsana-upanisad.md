Dattātreya (Viṣṇu) teaches Sāṅkṛti eight-limbed yoga in ten sections: ten yamas and niyamas with inner meanings, postures, channels, winds, inner time and inner tīrthas, purification, breath-control, five withdrawals, dhāraṇā, dhyāna, and samādhi as knowledge of identity.

### 1.6-24 <a id="tea-darsana-upanisad-1-6-24"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ten yamas are defined twice over — outwardly and inwardly: non-injury is not harming (except as the Veda enjoins), and supremely, knowing the Self as all-pervading; truth is speech that is true, and supremely 'all is Brahman'; purity is external, and inward as 'I am pure'.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: beginner · types: ethics, ultimate_

### 1.21-22 <a id="tea-darsana-upanisad-1-21-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The body is utterly impure, the embodied Self utterly pure; one who abandons the purity of knowledge for external purity abandons gold for a clod.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ethics, ultimate_

### 2.1-16 <a id="tea-darsana-upanisad-2-1-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ten niyamas (austerity, contentment, faith, charity, worship of the Lord, hearing the settled doctrine, modesty, discernment, japa, vows); worship of the Lord is a heart free of passion, speech free of falsehood, action free of violence; japa is spoken (loud or whispered) or mental, the whispered worth a thousand loud, the mental a thousand whispered.

### 4.39-47 <a id="tea-darsana-upanisad-4-39-47"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Time within the body: the moon moves in iḍā, the sun in piṅgalā; passage from piṅgalā to iḍā is the northern course, the reverse the southern; their junction is the new moon; the mūlādhāra and the head are the equinoxes; the eclipses happen within.

### 4.48-59 <a id="tea-darsana-upanisad-4-48-59"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The sacred places are in the body — Śrīparvata in the head, Kedāra on the forehead, Vārāṇasī between brows and nose, Kurukṣetra in the breast, Prayāga in the heart lotus, Cidambaram in the heart; one who leaves the inner tīrtha for the outer throws away a gem for glass; an impure mind is not cleansed by bathing, as a liquor pot is not by washing. Yogins see Śiva in the Self, not in images; images are imagined for the ignorant.

### 7 <a id="tea-darsana-upanisad-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Five pratyāhāras: forcibly withdrawing the senses; seeing all that is seen as the Self; offering all acts' fruits to Brahman; doing daily and occasional rites as worship of Brahman; and moving the breath through eighteen vital points and fixing it there, the self in the Self.

### 10 <a id="tea-darsana-upanisad-10"></a>
