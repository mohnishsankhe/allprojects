## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 11-26 <a id="tea-culahatthipadopama-sutta-11-26"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The gradual training: a Tathāgata arises; a householder hears, goes forth, undertakes virtue, guards the senses, practises mindfulness and clear comprehension, contentment, seclusion, abandons the five hindrances and enters the four jhānas, and recollects past lives and sees beings' passing — each of which is still only 'a footprint of the Tathāgata', not the conclusion.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, action, knowledge · stage: all · types: practice_

### 27 <a id="tea-culahatthipadopama-sutta-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Only when he knows the four truths, his mind is freed from the effluents and he knows 'birth is ended' has the noble disciple come to the conclusion: the Blessed One is fully awakened, the Dhamma well taught, the Saṅgha practising well.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: experiential · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation, teacher-transmission_
