## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 3.kumano <a id="tea-ippen-hijiri-e-3-kumano"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

After a monk refused the talisman saying he had no faith, Ippen prayed at the Kumano Hongū; the deity (the avatar of Amida) appeared and said: 'Holy man spreading the nenbutsu, why do you spread it mistakenly? It is not by your recommending that beings come to be born; the birth of all beings in the ten directions was settled as Namu Amida Butsu at Amida's perfect awakening ten kalpas ago. Distribute the talismans without choosing between those with and without faith, and without shunning the pure and the impure.'

### 4.dancing <a id="tea-ippen-hijiri-e-4-dancing"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

At Saku in Shinano the dancing nenbutsu began: Ippen and his followers, beating time, danced as they recited in joy at their birth being settled; later performed on raised stages before great crowds.

### 12.death <a id="tea-ippen-hijiri-e-12-death"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Before dying Ippen burned the books he carried, saying that all the sacred teachings of his lifetime had come to an end in Namu Amida Butsu; he asked that no successor be set up and that his body be left in the fields for the beasts; he died reciting.
