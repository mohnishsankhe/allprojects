# Mūlapariyāya Sutta

**Genre:** sutta

**Location in parent:** MN 1

The root of all things: the ordinary person perceives earth … nibbāna and 'conceives' them (as self, in self, as mine) and delights in them; the trainee should not conceive; the arahant and the Tathāgata do not, having fully understood — 'delight is the root of suffering'. The monks did not rejoice in this teaching.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 3-26 <a id="tea-mulapariyaya-sutta-3-26"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The uninstructed ordinary person perceives earth as earth, and having perceived it conceives it — conceives in earth, from earth, conceives 'earth is mine', delights in earth — and likewise water, fire, air, beings, gods, Brahmā, the formless spheres, the seen, heard, sensed and cognised, unity, diversity, all, and nibbāna, because he has not fully understood them.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind, ultimate_

### 26.1-26.4 <a id="tea-mulapariyaya-sutta-26-1-26-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

He perceives nibbāna as nibbāna; having perceived nibbāna, he conceives nibbāna, conceives in nibbāna, conceives from nibbāna, conceives 'nibbāna is mine', delights in nibbāna. Why? Because he has not fully understood it.

> Nibbānaṁ nibbānato sañjānāti; nibbānaṁ nibbānato saññatvā nibbānaṁ maññati, nibbānasmiṁ maññati, nibbānato maññati, nibbānaṁ meti maññati, nibbānaṁ abhinandati. Taṁ kissa hetu? ‘Apariññātaṁ tassā’ti vadāmi.

### 27-50 <a id="tea-mulapariyaya-sutta-27-50"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The trainee directly knows earth as earth and should not conceive it … nor delight in it, so that he may fully understand it.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: intermediate (sekha) · types: practice, consciousness-mind_

terms: [sekha](../terms/sekha.md) · concepts: [Conceiving (maññanā)](../concepts/conceiving.md)

### 147-194 <a id="tea-mulapariyaya-sutta-147-194"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Tathāgata directly knows earth as earth and does not conceive it nor delight in it, because he has understood that delight is the root of suffering, that from becoming comes birth, and that for what has come to be there is ageing and death; with the complete destruction of cravings he has awakened to the supreme awakening.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation, ultimate_
