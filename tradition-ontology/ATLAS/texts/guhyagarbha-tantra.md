**Structure:** 22 chapters (counted from the colophons of the local Derge text)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The root tantra of Mahāyoga in 22 chapters: in Akaniṣṭha the buddhas teach that all phenomena are primordially enlightened, that the aggregates and elements are the male and female buddhas, and set out bodhicitta, the wheel of syllables, the peaceful and wrathful maṇḍalas, empowerment, samaya and the secret instruction. It is the most commented Nyingma tantra (Rongzom, Longchenpa, Lochen Dharmaśrī, Mipham).
