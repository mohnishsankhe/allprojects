The root tantra of Mahāyoga in 22 chapters: in Akaniṣṭha the buddhas teach that all phenomena are primordially enlightened, that the aggregates and elements are the male and female buddhas, and set out bodhicitta, the wheel of syllables, the peaceful and wrathful maṇḍalas, empowerment, samaya and the secret instruction. It is the most commented Nyingma tantra (Rongzom, Longchenpa, Lochen Dharmaśrī, Mipham).

## Teachings (7: skeleton 7)

### 1 <a id="tea-guhyagarbha-tantra-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The setting: the Bhagavat, the great enjoyment body, whose nature is the vajra body, speech and mind of all the tathāgatas of the ten directions and four times, abides in Akaniṣṭha in a boundless palace of blazing wisdom-jewels; around him are the tathāgatas named the king of consciousness, the king of form, the king of feeling, the king of perception and so on — the aggregates themselves as the buddhas.

### 2 <a id="tea-guhyagarbha-tantra-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The generation of the primordially enlightened mind as wisdom: 'E ma'o! This wondrous, marvellous dharma, the secret of all perfect buddhas: from the unborn all is born, and in being born there is no birth.' The same is said of ceasing, abiding, objects of observation, and coming and going: from what does not cease, abide, observe or come and go, everything ceases, abides, is observed, comes and goes — and in doing so does not.

> e ma'o ngo mtshar rmad kyi chos/ /rdzogs pa'i sangs rgyas kun gyi gsang / /skye ba med las thams cad skye/ /skyes pa nyid na'ang skye ba med/

### 2 <a id="tea-guhyagarbha-tantra-2-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Out of great compassion, since beings, though of one nature with the buddhas, wander through the five classes by the conceptuality of ignorance: 'E ma! From the sugata-essence, by one's own conceptual karma are emanated bodies, enjoyments, abodes, sufferings and the rest, and one grasps them as I and mine. No one binds; there is no bondage and nothing to be bound. Grasping a self through conceptuality, one forcibly ties knots in space. The buddha-dharma, spontaneously perfect from the beginning, without bondage or liberation, displays varied activity in order to be taught.'

> sus kyang ma bcings bcings med de/ /bcing bar bya ba yod ma yin/ /rnam rtog bdag tu 'dzin pa yis/ /nan gyis mkha' la mdud pa dor/

### 3 <a id="tea-guhyagarbha-tantra-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The six sages emanated from the buddhas' body, speech and mind teach, according to those to be tamed, the vehicles of gods and humans, of śrāvakas, of pratyekabuddhas, of bodhisattvas and the unsurpassed vehicle: eighty-four thousand teachings as antidotes to the eighty-four thousand afflictions arising from ignorant conceptuality. All of them show that grasped and grasper arise interdependently, that karma and its result are not wasted, and ultimately that one is not stained by karma and its result.

_level: bridging · standpoint: divine · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, karma-liberation_

### 4 <a id="tea-guhyagarbha-tantra-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The wheel of the garland of syllables: without moving from the samādhi in which all phenomena are primordially enlightened, the tathāgatas bring forth the syllables in which all phenomena abide as mere names. From the stable white A, extremely subtle A's radiate, filling the ten directions, and are gathered without increase or decrease; from them all groups of names blaze forth and are gathered — the cause of the stable wisdom of vajra accomplishment. The alphabet is uttered, the six worlds quake, and all phenomena become mere marks of names. 'A is neither empty nor not empty, and no middle is found either; all buddhas, mere names, abide in the garland of syllables; A itself appears as the variety, ka and the rest.'

> a ni stong dang mi stong gi/ /dbu ma'ang dmigs su yod ma yin/ /thams cad ming tsam sangs rgyas kun/ /yi ge phreng ba nyid la gnas/

### 13 <a id="tea-guhyagarbha-tantra-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The most secret instruction: the maṇḍala of Samantabhadra, gazed upon by the five wisdoms, is spontaneously perfect; this is the great secret fruit taken as the path, by which the five kāyas are spontaneously perfected — there is no more secret definitive meaning in all the victors' maṇḍalas. It is to be given only to those with the eye of wisdom trained in hearing, reflection and meditation, fit vessels of good character who offer body and wealth — never otherwise; if it is proclaimed to the deluded, their life ends untimely and they remain long burning and scorched.
