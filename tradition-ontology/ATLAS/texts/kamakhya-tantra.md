A tantra of 9 paṭalas in praise of Kāmākhyā, 'the great vidyā in the form of the yoni' (1.4), who is dharma, artha, kāma and liberation and without whom nothing exists (1.9-13); on Kaula worship and the vīra.

### 1.4 <a id="tea-kamakhya-tantra-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Kāmākhyā, the great Vidyā in the form of the yoni, is the giver of boons.

### 1.9-13 <a id="tea-kamakhya-tantra-1-9-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Kāmākhyā is always dharma, Kāmākhyā is wealth, Kāmākhyā the fulfilment of desire, Kāmākhyā liberation; she is the supreme goal, granting the same world and the same form; everything is the own form of the great Goddess Kāmākhyā; without Kāmākhyā nothing exists in the three worlds.
