# Tirumuṟai

`src:tirumurai` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Paṉṉiru Tirumuṟai, the twelve Tirumuṟai, the Tamil Veda (Tamiḻ maṟai) of the Śaivas
**Language:** Tamil
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md), [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
**Genre:** canon (collection)
**Authors:** 
  - [Nampi Āṇṭār Nampi](../teachers/nampi-antar-nampi.md) — role: compiler; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Nampi Āṇṭār Nampi, at the request of the Cōḻa king Rājarāja (Apayakulacēkaraṉ), recovered the hymns from a sealed room in the Chidambaram temple and arranged books 1–11; the Periya Purāṇam was added as the twelfth (Tirumuṟaikaṇṭa Purāṇam); scholarly: compiled in stages c. 10th–12th c.; the identity of the king and the exact stages are debated; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Scholarly account: contents c. 6th–12th c.; compilation c. 10th–12th c.; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 12 books (tirumuṟai): 1–3 Campantar (c. 384 patikams), 4–6 Appar (c. 312), 7 Cuntarar (c. 100), 8 Tiruvācakam + Tirukkōvaiyār, 9 Tiruvicaippā + Tiruppallāṇṭu, 10 Tirumantiram, 11 forty works by twelve authors, 12 Periya Purāṇam. Hymn counts as usually given (moderate).
**Availability:** digitized-original

The twelve-book canon of Tamil Śaiva sacred literature: (1–3) Tiruñāṉacampantar's hymns; (4–6) Tirunāvukkaracar's (Appar's) hymns; (7) Cuntarar's hymns — books 1–7 together are the Tēvāram; (8) Māṇikkavācakar's Tiruvācakam and Tirukkōvaiyār; (9) the Tiruvicaippā of nine poets and Cēntaṉār's Tiruppallāṇṭu; (10) Tirumūlar's Tirumantiram; (11) forty works by twelve poets (Kāraikkāl Ammaiyār, Cēramāṉ Perumāḷ, Nakkīrar, Paṭṭiṉattup Piḷḷaiyār, Nampi Āṇṭār Nampi and others); (12) Cēkkiḻār's Periya Purāṇam.
