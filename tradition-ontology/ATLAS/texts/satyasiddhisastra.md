Harivarman's 'establishment of the truth', which organizes Abhidharma around the four truths, rejects the Sarvāstivāda's three-time existence and many real entities, and teaches the emptiness of persons and of dharmas; the basis of the Chinese Chengshi school.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### structure <a id="tea-satyasiddhisastra-structure"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Harivarman arranges the whole Abhidharma under the four truths and teaches that liberation comes by seeing the emptiness of the person (pudgala-śūnyatā) and of dharmas (dharma-śūnyatā), rejecting the real existence of past and future.
