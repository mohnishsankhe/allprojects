## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-tantrasara-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Knowledge is the cause of liberation because it opposes ignorance, the cause of bondage; ignorance is twofold: in the intellect (bauddha), whose nature is uncertainty or wrong certainty, and in the person (pauruṣa), whose nature is contracted manifestation - the root of saṃsāra.
