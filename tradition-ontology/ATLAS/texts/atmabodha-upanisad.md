## Teachings (2: sourced 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-atmabodha-upanisad-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Oṃ namo nārāyaṇāya: one who knows the eight-syllable mantra goes to Vaikuṇṭha; the heart lotus, the Lord shining like lightning, devoted to brāhmaṇas.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language_

practices: [Recitation of the eight-syllable Nārāyaṇa mantra](../practices/narayana-astaksara-japa.md)

### 2-31 <a id="tea-atmabodha-upanisad-2-31"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

'Prajñānaṃ brahma'; first-person verses: 'I am free of delusion, of ignorance; for me there is no waking, dream or sleep; I am the witness'.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate_
