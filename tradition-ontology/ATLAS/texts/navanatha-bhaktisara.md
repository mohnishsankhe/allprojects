# Navanātha Bhaktisāra

A Marathi verse narrative of the nine Nāths — Macchindra, Gorakh, Jālandhar, Kānif, Carpaṭ, Nāg, Bhartarī, Revaṇ and Gahinī — presented as incarnations of the nine Nārāyaṇas (the nine Yogendras of the Bhāgavata), with Dattātreya as their teacher.

_Notes: Author recalled as Dhuṇḍisuta Mālu (Narahari Mālu); year recalled, not checked._
