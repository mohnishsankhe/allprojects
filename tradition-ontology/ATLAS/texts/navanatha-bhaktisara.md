A Marathi verse narrative of the nine Nāths — Macchindra, Gorakh, Jālandhar, Kānif, Carpaṭ, Nāg, Bhartarī, Revaṇ and Gahinī — presented as incarnations of the nine Nārāyaṇas (the nine Yogendras of the Bhāgavata), with Dattātreya as their teacher.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### whole <a id="tea-navanatha-bhaktisara-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The nine Nāths — Macchindra, Gorakh, Jālandhar, Kānif, Carpaṭ, Nāg, Bhartarī, Revaṇ and Gahinī — are the nine Nārāyaṇas (the Yogendras of the Bhāgavata) born to spread yoga in the Kali age, taught by Dattātreya.
