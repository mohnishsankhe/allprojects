## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### purva.3.1-5 <a id="tea-sarngadhara-samhita-purva-3-1-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The vessel at the root of the thumb is the witness of life; by its movement the learned know the body's happiness and suffering. In provoked vāta the pulse moves like a leech or a snake; in pitta like a sparrow, crow or frog; in kapha like a swan or pigeon; in all three together like a quail or partridge.

### purva.5.49-52 <a id="tea-sarngadhara-samhita-purva-5-49-52"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The heart is the seat of consciousness and the abode of ojas. The breath (prāṇa) seated at the navel, touching the lotus of the heart, goes out of the throat to drink the nectar of Viṣṇu's step (the sky), and having drunk the ambrosia of the sky returns swiftly, pleasing the whole body and enlivening the fire of the belly. Life is the union of body and breath; their separation in time is called death ('going to the five').
