## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### vijnana <a id="tea-pancaskandhaprakarana-vijnana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Consciousness is the cognition of objects; it is also called mind because it is variegated and because it is accumulated with seeds; in the principal sense it is the store-consciousness, and mentation (manas) is that which takes it as its object and is always accompanied by the afflictions of self.
