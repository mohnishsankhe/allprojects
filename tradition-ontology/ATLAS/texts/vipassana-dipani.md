## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### vipallasa <a id="tea-vipassana-dipani-vipallasa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Perversions of perception, thought and view — taking the impermanent as permanent, the painful as pleasant, the not-self as self, the foul as beautiful — are removed by insight into the three characteristics.
