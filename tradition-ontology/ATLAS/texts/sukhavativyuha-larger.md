# Sukhāvatīvyūha-sūtra (larger)

The Larger Sūtra of Immeasurable Life: the monk Dharmākara, before the buddha Lokeśvararāja, makes forty-eight vows (Chinese T360; the Sanskrit has a different count) to create a pure land, and after aeons of practice becomes the buddha Amitābha / Amitāyus in Sukhāvatī to the west; the eighteenth vow promises birth there to all who with sincere faith wish to be born there and call him to mind even ten times, excepting those who committed the five grave offences or slandered the dharma; it describes the land, the three grades of aspirants and the vulnerability of beings in this world.

## Teachings (21: skeleton 21)

### crosswise-cutting <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-crosswise-cutting"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Strive and you will surely transcend and go to be born in the land of Peace and Sustenance, cutting crosswise through the five evil destinies, which close of themselves; the ascent on the way is without limit; it is easy to go there, yet there is no one who goes.

> 必得超絕去，往生安養國。橫截五惡趣，惡趣自然閉；昇道無窮極，易往而無人。

### entrustment <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-entrustment"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Buddha tells Maitreya: whoever hears that Buddha's name and rejoices, even for a single thought, should be known to have gained the great benefit — to be endowed with unsurpassed merit.

> 其有得聞彼佛名號，歡喜踊躍，乃至一念，當知此人為得大利，則是具足無上功德。

### five-evils <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-five-evils"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Buddha tells Maitreya that the buddha-lands elsewhere are easy to transform because their beings do good of themselves, whereas he has become a buddha in this world amid the five evils, five pains and five burnings, the most severe suffering; he teaches beings to abandon them and keep the five goods.

> 今我於此世間作佛，處於五惡、五痛、五燒之中，為最劇苦。教化群生令捨五惡、令去五痛、令離五燒

Fulfilment of the vow: all beings who hear his name, rejoice with faith even for a single thought, sincerely dedicate it and wish to be born there attain birth and dwell in non-retrogression — except those who commit the five grave offences and slander the true dharma.

### hardest-of-hard <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-hardest-of-hard"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

It is hard to meet a Tathāgata, hard to hear the scriptures, hard to hear the perfections, hard to meet a good teacher and practise; but to hear this sūtra and joyfully entrust oneself and hold to it — of all difficulties none surpasses this.

> 若聞斯經信樂受持，難中之難無過此難。

### skt.vow18 <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-skt-vow18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Sanskrit vow 18: beings in other worlds who have aroused the thought of awakening, heard his name and recollect him with a serene mind — at the time of their death he will stand before them surrounded by the assembly of monks, so that their minds are not distracted.

### skt.vow19 <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-skt-vow19"></a>

### skt.womb <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-skt-womb"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Buddha tells Ajita (Maitreya): those who plant roots of good for birth in Sukhāvatī with doubt dwell there within the lotus as in a womb; those free of doubt, who trust the unobstructed knowledge of the buddhas, appear seated cross-legged in lotuses by apparitional birth.

> ye te 'jita bodhisattvā anyeṣu buddhakṣetreṣu sthitāḥ sukhāvatyāṃ lokadhātāv upapattaye vicikitsām utpādayanti, tena cittena kuśalamūlāny avaropayanti, teṣām atra garbhāvāso bhavati

### sutra-remains <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-sutra-remains"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In the age to come when the scriptures and the way have perished, out of compassion the Buddha will keep this sūtra alone for a hundred years more; beings who meet it will all be saved according to their wish.

> 當來之世經道滅盡，我以慈悲哀愍特留此經止住百歲，其有眾生值斯經者，隨意所願皆可得度。

### three-grades <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-three-grades"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Three grades of those who aspire to Sukhāvatī: the highest renounce the household, arouse the thought of awakening, single-mindedly think of Amitāyus and cultivate merit; the middle, unable to renounce, still arouse the thought of awakening and do good; the lowest, who can do no merit, arouse the thought of awakening and single-mindedly think of him even ten times; all are welcomed at death.

### verse-arrogance <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-verse-arrogance"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Those who are arrogant, corrupt and lazy find it hard to believe this teaching; those who have seen buddhas in past lives delight to hear it; the ocean of the Tathāgata's wisdom is beyond the measure of śrāvakas and bodhisattvas; only a buddha fully knows it.

> 憍慢弊懈怠，難以信此法；宿世見諸佛，樂聽如是教。

### vow11 <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-vow11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The eleventh vow: if, when he attains buddhahood, the humans and gods of his land do not dwell in the rightly established group and necessarily reach extinction (nirvāṇa), may he not attain perfect awakening.

> 設我得佛，國中人天不住定聚必至滅度者，不取正覺。

### vow12-13 <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-vow12-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The twelfth and thirteenth vows: his light shall not be limited so as not to illuminate hundreds of thousands of koṭis of nayutas of buddha-lands, and his life shall not be limited to hundreds of thousands of koṭis of nayutas of kalpas — else may he not attain awakening.

> 設我得佛，光明有能限量，下至不照百千億那由他諸佛國者，不取正覺。……設我得佛，壽命有能限量，下至百千億那由他劫者，不取正覺。

### vow22 <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-vow22"></a>

The twenty-second vow: bodhisattvas from other lands born in his land will certainly reach the stage of one life from buddhahood — except those who by their own vow freely choose to teach, donning the armour of great vows for beings' sake, accumulating merit to save all, travelling to buddha-lands and practising the virtue of Samantabhadra.

### vows <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-vows"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The monk Dharmākara, before the buddha Lokeśvararāja, surveys the qualities of countless buddha-fields and, after five aeons of reflection, makes forty-eight vows (T360) for a land without evil destinies, whose beings will have golden bodies, superknowledges, non-retrogression and life without limit, and which will receive all who aspire to it; he practiced for countless aeons and became Amitābha, who has now dwelt in Sukhāvatī for ten aeons.

### womb-birth <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-womb-birth"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Those who cultivate merit and aspire to birth while doubting the Buddha's inconceivable and unequalled wisdom — yet believing in sin and merit and cultivating good roots — are born in palaces there where for five hundred years they do not see the Buddha, hear the teaching or see the holy assembly; this is called womb-birth; those who clearly believe in the Buddha's wisdom are born by transformation, seated in lotuses.

> 若有眾生以疑惑心修諸功德，願生彼國，不了佛智、不思議智、不可稱智、大乘廣智、無等無倫最上勝智，於此諸智疑惑不信；然猶信罪福，修習善本，願生其國。此諸眾生生彼宮殿，壽五百歲，常不見佛、不聞經法、不見菩薩聲聞聖眾，是故於彼國土謂之胎生。
