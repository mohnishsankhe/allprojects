The Larger Sūtra of Immeasurable Life: the monk Dharmākara, before the buddha Lokeśvararāja, makes forty-eight vows (Chinese T360; the Sanskrit has a different count) to create a pure land, and after aeons of practice becomes the buddha Amitābha / Amitāyus in Sukhāvatī to the west; the eighteenth vow promises birth there to all who with sincere faith wish to be born there and call him to mind even ten times, excepting those who committed the five grave offences or slandered the dharma; it describes the land, the three grades of aspirants and the vulnerability of beings in this world.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### fulfilment <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-fulfilment"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Fulfilment of the vow: all beings who hear his name, rejoice with faith even for a single thought, sincerely dedicate it and wish to be born there attain birth and dwell in non-retrogression — except those who commit the five grave offences and slander the true dharma.

### three-grades <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-three-grades"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Three grades of those who aspire to Sukhāvatī: the highest renounce the household, arouse the thought of awakening, single-mindedly think of Amitāyus and cultivate merit; the middle, unable to renounce, still arouse the thought of awakening and do good; the lowest, who can do no merit, arouse the thought of awakening and single-mindedly think of him even ten times; all are welcomed at death.

### vow18 <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-vow18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The eighteenth vow: 'If, when I attain buddhahood, beings of the ten directions who sincerely and joyfully entrust themselves, wish to be born in my land and think of me even ten times, should not be born there, may I not attain perfect awakening — excepting those who commit the five grave offences and slander the true dharma.'

> 設我得佛，十方眾生至心信樂欲生我國，乃至十念，若不生者，不取正覺，唯除五逆、誹謗正法。

### vows <a id="tea-sukhavativyuha-larger-vows"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The monk Dharmākara, before the buddha Lokeśvararāja, surveys the qualities of countless buddha-fields and, after five aeons of reflection, makes forty-eight vows (T360) for a land without evil destinies, whose beings will have golden bodies, superknowledges, non-retrogression and life without limit, and which will receive all who aspire to it; he practiced for countless aeons and became Amitābha, who has now dwelt in Sukhāvatī for ten aeons.
