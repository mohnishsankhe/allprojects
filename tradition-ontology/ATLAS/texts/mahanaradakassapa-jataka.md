King Aṅgati of Videha consults the naked ascetic Guṇa of the Kassapa clan, who teaches that there is no fruit of right conduct, no other world, no teacher, no strength or energy, that beings are fixed like the stern-rope of a boat, that seven eternal bodies cannot be cut, and that all are purified by transmigration in 84 great aeons; the king gives up his duties until his daughter Rujā and the Brahmā Nārada correct him.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 34-45 <a id="tea-mahanaradakassapa-jataka-34-45"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The naked ascetic Guṇa of the Kassapa clan teaches King Aṅgati: there is no fruit, good or bad, of living by dharma; there is no other world — who has come back from it?; there are no ancestors, no mother or father, no teacher to tame the untamed; beings are all equal, there is no honouring of elders; there is no strength or energy, still less effort; beings are fixed (niyatāni) like the stern-rope of a boat; a man gets what he is to get, so what fruit has giving? Giving was laid down by fools. Seven bodies are eternal and cannot be cut — fire, earth, water, air, pleasure, pain and soul — so the sword only passes between them; all are purified by wandering through 84 great aeons, and before that time even the restrained is not purified: 'we do not pass beyond destiny, as the ocean does not pass its shore'.
