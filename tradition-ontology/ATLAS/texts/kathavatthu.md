## Teachings (17: skeleton 17)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-kathavatthu-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The opponent (identified by the commentary as the Vajjiputtaka and Sammitiya personalists) affirms that the person is apprehended as a real and ultimate fact; the Theravādin presses the consequences: if so, it must be apprehended as other real and ultimate facts are — which the opponent cannot grant — so the thesis fails.

> Puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenāti? Āmantā.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: dispute_

terms: [puggala](../terms/puggala.md), [paramattha](../terms/paramattha.md), [pudgala](../terms/pudgala.md) · concepts: [Conventional and ultimate truth (sammuti- and paramattha-sacca)](../concepts/two-truths-theravada.md), [The doctrine of the person (pudgalavāda)](../concepts/pudgala-doctrine.md) · disputes: [Is the person (puggala) found as a real and ultimate fact?](../debates/kv-puggala.md), [Is there a person (pudgala) that is neither the same as nor different from the aggregates?](../debates/pudgala.md), [Is there a self (ātman, puruṣa, jīva) — an enduring conscious subject distinct from body and changing cognitions — or only a stream of impersonal factors designated 'person'?](../debates/is-there-a-self.md)

### 1.2 <a id="tea-kathavatthu-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Examines the thesis that an arahant can fall away from arahantship (held, per the commentary, by Sammitīyas, Vajjiputtakas, Sabbatthivādins and some Mahāsaṅghikas); the Theravādin argues that one in whom the fetters are destroyed cannot fall away.

> Parihāyati arahā arahattāti? Āmantā. Sabbattha arahā arahattā parihāyatīti?

### 1.6 <a id="tea-kathavatthu-1-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Examines the Sabbatthivāda thesis that 'everything exists' (past, future and present dhammas alike); the Theravādin refutes it through its consequences for time and change.

> Sabbamatthīti? Āmantā. Sabbattha sabbamatthīti?

### 2.1-5 <a id="tea-kathavatthu-2-1-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Five theses about the arahant (attributed by the commentary to the Pubbaseliyas and Aparaseliyas): that deities can cause him to emit semen, that he can have ignorance and doubt, that others can inform him, and that one utters speech while in jhāna; the Theravādin refutes each as incompatible with the destruction of the fetters.

> Atthi arahato asuci sukkavissaṭṭhīti? Āmantā. Atthi arahato rāgo kāmarāgo kāmarāgapariyuṭṭhānaṁ kāmarāgasaṁyojanaṁ kāmogho kāmayogo kāmacchandanīvaraṇanti?

_level: unmarked · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: dispute_

terms: [arahant](../terms/arahant.md) · disputes: [Does the arahant have imperfections (the 'five points')?](../debates/kv-arahant-imperfections.md)

### 2.1-6 <a id="tea-kathavatthu-2-1-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

A group of debates on the arahant: whether impurity can be imputed to an arahant by others (parūpahāra), whether he has ignorance (aññāṇa) and doubt (kaṅkhā), whether he is surpassed by others in knowledge (paravitāraṇa), whether one in attainment has speech (vacībheda), and whether uttering 'suffering' brings the path (dukkhāhāra) — the Theravādin rejecting each.

> Atthi arahato asuci sukkavissaṭṭhīti? Āmantā.
