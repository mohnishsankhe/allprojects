# Vātulāgama

`src:vatula-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Vātula, Saurabheya (Kiraṇa, Śrīkaṇṭhīya), Vātulaśuddhākhya (associated title)
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Śiva (the Rudra so named), then Mahākāla (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Rudrabheda no. 28 in the Kāmika's order; 12 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** digitized-original

No. 28 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Rudrabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Śiva (the Rudra so named), then Mahākāla. The Kāmika assigns it 12 upabheda(s): Vātula, Uttara, Kālajñāna, Prarohita, Sarva, Dharmātmaka, Śreṣṭha, Nitya, Śuddha, Mahānana, Viśva, Viśvātmaka. The Kālottara corpus is associated with it (the Kāmika lists 'Uttara' and 'Kālajñāna' among its upabhedas).
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: IFP manuscripts (Muktabodha I00012, I00023, I00031); Vātulottara e-text (M00219)

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.18/10.27: 'Saurabheya' of Śiva, received by Mahākāla — the same slot. Śrīkaṇṭhīya: 'Saurabheya'. In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the garment (pūrva 1.93–101).
