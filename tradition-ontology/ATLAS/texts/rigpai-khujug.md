The first of the mind-series scriptures, six lines on the non-dual nature of variety, Samantabhadra as all appearance, and resting spontaneously without the sickness of effort. It appears as chapter 31 ('the six vajra lines') of the Kunjed Gyalpo.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-6 <a id="tea-rigpai-khujug-1-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Cuckoo of Awareness: the nature of variety is non-dual; each thing is free of elaboration; though not conceived as 'suchness', the all-good appears everywhere; being already complete, give up the sickness of effort; remaining spontaneously is the settling.
