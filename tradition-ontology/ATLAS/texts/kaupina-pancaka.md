## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-kaupina-pancaka-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Always delighting in the words of Vedānta, content with the food got by begging, wandering with a mind free of sorrow — fortunate indeed are the wearers of the loincloth.
