A verse doxography ascribed to Śaṅkara that sets out the doctrines of the schools from the Lokāyata upward. Its Lokāyata section states in verse that the four elements are the only principles, that consciousness arises from them like intoxicating power, that pleasure from embraces is the human aim and pain from thorns is hell, that the lord is the king known to the world and liberation is the destruction of the body.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2 <a id="tea-sarvasiddhantasangraha-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

In the Lokāyatika view the principles are four elements only; consciousness arises from them like intoxicating power from ingredients, the self being known in 'I am fat', 'I am thin'; pleasure born of embraces is the human aim, pain from thorns is hell; the lord is the king known to the world; liberation is the body's destruction, not knowledge; perception alone is a means of knowledge.
