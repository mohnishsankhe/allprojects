Twenty verses to Śiva of Tillai (Chidambaram): the one Lord makes us eat, sleep and act and drives us by the rope of the two karmas; never to forget the Dancer's feet is the medicine against death; chanting the five letters yields birthless release.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 11 <a id="tea-tiruttillai-pattinattar-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The one who makes us eat and makes us sleep, who joins one thing to another and makes us strive, who shows us our deeds and makes us dance by the rope of the two karmas — there is One in the hall of Tillai.

> ऊट्टुविप्पाऩु मुऱङ्गुविप्पाऩुमिङ् गॊऩ्ऱो टॊऩ्ऱै / मूट्टुविप्पाऩु मुयङ्गुविप्पाऩु मुयऩ्ऱ विऩै / काट्टुविप्पाऩु मिरुविऩैप् पासक् कयिऱ्ऱिऩ्वऴि / याट्टुविप्पाऩु मॊरुवऩुण् डेदिल्लै यम्बलत्ते।

### 15 <a id="tea-tiruttillai-pattinattar-15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

However much one boasts, climbs on stilts, dances on a pole, shouts and argues, or quarrels with one's brothers, will anything come of it? There are no deeds other than by the word of the Lord who fills Tillai.

> वित्तारम् पेसिऩुञ् जोङ्गेऱिऩुङ् गम्बमीदिरुन्दु / तत्तारवॆऩ् ऱोदिप् पवुरिगॊण्डाडिऩुऩ् तम्मुऩ्दम्बि / यॊत्तासैबेसिऩु मावदुण्डो? तिल्लैयुण्णिऱैन्द / कत्ताविऩ् सॊऱ्पडियल्लादु वेऱिल्लै कऩ्मङ्गळे।

### 16 <a id="tea-tiruttillai-pattinattar-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

One must win the boon of not being born; once born, is there a medicine against dying? O mind, never forget the lotus feet of the Lord of the hall at Tillai — that is the good medicine for you.

> पिऱवादिरुक्क वरम्बॆऱल् वेण्डुम्, पिऱन्दुविट्टा / लिऱवादिरुक्क मरुन्दुण्डु काणिदु वॆप्पडियो / अऱमार् पुगऴ्त्तिल्लै यम्बलवाण रडिक्कमल / मऱवा तिरुमऩमे, यदुगाणऩल् मरुन्दुऩक्के।

### 17 <a id="tea-tiruttillai-pattinattar-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Do not waver, O heart: if you chant the five letters to the Dancer of the golden hall at Tillai, birthless liberation will be attained.
