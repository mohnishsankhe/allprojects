# Kabīr Granthāvalī

`src:kabir-granthavali` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Original title:** कबीर ग्रंथावली
**Language:** Hindi (western: Rajasthani-Braj Sant language)
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md), [Dādū Panth](../lineages/dadu-panth.md)
**Genre:** Sant song anthology (western recension)
**Authors:** 
  - [Kabīr](../teachers/kabir.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Kabīr's sākhīs and padas as preserved in Rajasthani (Dādūpanthī) manuscripts; scholarly: the western recension; the Dādūpanthī manuscripts (from c. 1600 onward) are among the oldest witnesses of Kabīr; editions by Śyāmsundar Dās (1928) and Pārasnāth Tivārī (1961); confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Scholarly account: manuscripts from c. 1600 (Fatehpur 1582 manuscript of Sūr and others; Dādūpanthī Pañcvāṇī manuscripts 17th c.); (confidence low)
**Structure:** Sākhīs arranged in some 59 aṅgas (topics: gurudev, sumiraṇ, birah, gyān-birah, paracā, ras, jarṇā, hairān, lai, nihakarmī-pativratā, citāvaṇī, man, māyā, sahaj, sāṃc, bhram-bidhauṃsaṇ, bheṣ, saṅgati, sādh, madhi, sārgrāhī, sabad, jīvan-mṛtak, sūrātan, kāl, kastūriyā-mṛg, nindā, bīnatī, belī, etc.), padas (some 400) in rāgas, and ramainīs
**Availability:** digitized-original

The western (Rajasthani) collection of Kabīr, arranged by topic (aṅg) as in the Dādūpanthī anthologies: the guru, remembrance of the Name, the pain of separation, the encounter (paracā) with the Beloved, the mind, māyā, the sahaj state, the true sādhu, the valour of the spiritual warrior and death. It is warmer in devotional tone than the Bījak.
