# Argalā Stotra

A hymn recited before the Saptaśatī, praising the Goddess by her deeds with the refrain asking for beauty, victory and fame and the removal of enemies.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### refrain <a id="tea-argala-stotra-refrain"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Each verse praises the Goddess by one of her deeds and asks: give beauty, give victory, give glory, destroy enemies.

> rūpaṃ dehi jayaṃ dehi yaśo dehi dviṣo jahi
