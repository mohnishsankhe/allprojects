## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-sunyatasaptati-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Arising, abiding and ceasing, existence and non-existence, the inferior, middling and excellent are spoken of by the Buddha in accordance with worldly convention, not in reality.

### 64-65 <a id="tea-sunyatasaptati-64-65"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Teacher called ignorance the conceiving of things born from causes and conditions as real; from it the twelve links arise. When one sees well that things are empty one is not deluded; ignorance ceases and the twelve links cease.
