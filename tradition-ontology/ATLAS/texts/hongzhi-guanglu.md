## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 73c05 <a id="tea-hongzhi-guanglu-73c05"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The field is empty and vast, what has been there from the beginning; purify and polish away all deluded conditions and illusory habits, and you arrive at the pure, round, bright place: empty and imageless, standing alone, relying on nothing; only illumine the original truth, leaving external objects behind.

> 田地虛曠。是從來本所有者。當在淨治揩磨去。諸妄緣幻習。自到清白圜明之處。空空無像
