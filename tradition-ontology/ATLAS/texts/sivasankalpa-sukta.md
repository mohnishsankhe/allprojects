# Śivasaṅkalpa Sūkta

**Language:** Sanskrit

**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Mādhyandina śākhā (White Yajurveda)](../lineages/sakha-madhyandina.md)

**Location in parent:** VS 34.1–6
**Availability:** digitized-original

Six verses on the mind (manas), each ending 'may that mind of mine be of auspicious resolve (śivasaṅkalpa)': the divine mind that goes far in waking and returns in sleep, the one light of lights; the inner immortal light in beings without which no act is done; the mind that holds past, present and future and in which the Vedas are set like spokes in a hub; the heart-seated charioteer that guides men like horses.

_Notes: A form of these verses also circulates among the Ṛgveda khilas (reference only)._
