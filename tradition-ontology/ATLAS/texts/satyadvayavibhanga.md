## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 8-12 <a id="tea-satyadvayavibhanga-8-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Things as they appear, arising from causes, are the correct conventional truth when they are able to function as they appear; the incorrect conventional (such as a mirage taken as water, or imagined entities) is not; though alike in appearance, they differ in efficacy. The negation of arising accords with the ultimate and is itself called ultimate.
