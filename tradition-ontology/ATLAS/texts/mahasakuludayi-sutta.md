The wanderer Sakuludāyī reports a debate among ascetics about which of the famous teachers is honoured by his disciples; the six are said not to be, the Buddha is.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 6 <a id="tea-mahasakuludayi-sutta-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In a gathering of wanderers the question is raised which of the famous teachers is honoured by his disciples; of Pūraṇa and the others it is said that their disciples do not honour them and leave them, while the Buddha's disciples, even when he is interrupted, fall silent to hear him.
