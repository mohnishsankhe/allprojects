## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### stillness-movement <a id="tea-ocean-of-definitive-meaning-stillness-movement"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In stillness look at what is still; in movement look at what moves; ask whether the still and the moving mind are one or different, and recognize the awareness that knows both.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice, consciousness-mind_
