Jayanta Bhaṭṭa's large independent exposition of Nyāya in twelve āhnikas, organized around the means of knowledge and the objects of knowledge; it defends the authority of the Veda and of the Lord, criticizes Buddhist, Mīmāṃsaka and Cārvāka positions, and discusses the validity of the Āgamas.

### āhnika 1 <a id="tea-nyayamanjari-1-veda-raksa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Among the sciences, Nyāya is foremost because it protects the authority of the Veda, on which the other sciences depend, against those who attack it.
