A Kerala manual of Bhāṭṭa epistemology (means of knowledge, māna) and ontology (objects, meya); the first part by Nārāyaṇa Bhaṭṭa, the second completed by a later Nārāyaṇa.

_Notes: Identity of the author of the meya part (called Nārāyaṇa Paṇḍita / Nārāyaṇa Sudhī) uncertain._
