## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-santanantaradusana-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Once it is established (by co-apprehension and similar proofs) that all appearances are one's own mind, like a dream, the inference of another mind-stream from speech and action fails at the ultimate level; other streams are admitted only conventionally.
