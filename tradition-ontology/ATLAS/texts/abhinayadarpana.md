A short manual of dance and gesture (hand-gestures, glances, head and neck movements) ascribed to Nandikeśvara, basic to classical dance teaching; it states that where the hand goes the eye follows, the mind follows the eye, feeling (bhāva) the mind, and rasa arises from feeling.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 37 <a id="tea-abhinayadarpana-37"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Where the hand goes, there the eyes follow; where the eyes go, the mind; where the mind goes, feeling (bhāva); where there is feeling, rasa arises.
