## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### opening <a id="tea-muktivada-raghudeva-opening"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Ācāryas (Udayana) say that liberation is the absolute cessation of pain; its absoluteness is its not being contemporaneous with any prior absence of pain in the same locus — i.e. liberation is a destruction of pain not contemporaneous with any prior absence of pain in the same self.

> ātyantikī duḥkhanivṛttir muktir ity ācāryāḥ
