### pt.1 <a id="tea-milarepa-namthar-pt-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

After his father's death his relatives seized the family's wealth; at his mother's urging Milarepa learned sorcery, caused a house to collapse at a wedding feast killing thirty-five people, and destroyed the villagers' harvest with hail; seized by remorse, he resolved to seek the Dharma.

### pt.2 <a id="tea-milarepa-namthar-pt-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Marpa made Milarepa build towers and tear them down again, beat and humiliated him, and withheld initiation, so as to purify the karma of his evil deeds; Milarepa's despair and Marpa's later explanation that each ordeal cleansed a great sin.
