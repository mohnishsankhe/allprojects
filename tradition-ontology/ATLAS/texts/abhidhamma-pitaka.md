# Abhidhamma Piṭaka

**Language:** Pali
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md), [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md)
**Genre:** piṭaka
**Attribution:** tradition: The tradition's account (Atthasālinī, Nidānakathā): the Buddha taught the Abhidhamma in the Tāvatiṃsa heaven during the rains retreat after the Twin Miracle, to the gods led by his mother; each day he gave the method (naya) to Sāriputta at Lake Anotatta, who taught it to his five hundred pupils; it was recited at the First Council.; scholarly: a later systematization of sutta lists by monastic scholars; distinct Abhidharma canons developed in each early school; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: taught by the Buddha (5th c. BCE on the traditional chronology); Scholarly account: compiled over several centuries c. 3rd–1st c. BCE (the Paṭṭhāna and Kathāvatthu among the later strata); (confidence low)
**Structure:** seven books: Dhammasaṅgaṇī, Vibhaṅga, Dhātukathā, Puggalapaññatti, Kathāvatthu, Yamaka, Paṭṭhāna
**Availability:** digitized-original

The third basket of the Pali canon: seven treatises that analyse experience into ultimate constituents (dhammas), classify them by matrices of triads and dyads, and set out their conditional relations; for the Theravāda the Buddha's teaching 'in terms of ultimate reality'.
