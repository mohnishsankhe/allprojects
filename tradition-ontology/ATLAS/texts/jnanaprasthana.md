# Jñānaprasthāna

**Alternate titles:** Abhidharma-jñānaprasthāna-śāstra, Fazhi lun 發智論 (T1544), Aṣṭagrantha / Ba jiandu lun 八犍度論 (T1543)
**Language:** Sanskrit (lost), Chinese
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md), [Vaibhāṣika](../lineages/vaibhasika.md)
**Genre:** abhidharma
**Authors:** 
  - [Kātyāyanīputra](../teachers/katyayaniputra.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Composed by Kātyāyanīputra (Yaśomitra; Xuanzang); regarded as the 'body' (śarīra) of the Abhidharma, the other six being its 'feet'; scholarly: Accepted as the central Sarvāstivāda Abhidharma treatise; confidence: high
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Three hundred years after the parinirvāṇa (Xuanzang); Scholarly account: c. 2nd–1st c. BCE; (confidence low)
**Structure:** Eight sections (skandhaka/grantha): miscellaneous, fetters, knowledge, action, great elements, faculties, concentration, views. T1544: 20 fascicles (Xuanzang, 657–660); T1543: 30 fascicles (Saṅghadeva and Zhu Fonian, 383).
**Availability:** digitized-translation

The root treatise of the Sarvāstivāda Abhidharma, commented on by the Mahāvibhāṣā. It analyses the fetters, knowledges, actions, elements, faculties, concentrations and views, and states the Abhidharma definition of the word of the Buddha as speech (Yaśomitra's quotation).
