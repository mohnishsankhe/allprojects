## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1a05-14 <a id="tea-luelun-anle-jingtu-yi-1a05-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Asked which of the three realms includes the Land of Peace and Bliss: as the (Mahāprajñāpāramitā) treatise says, such a pure land is not included in the three realms — not the desire realm, being without desire; not the form realm, being on the ground; not the formless realm, having form; it was gained by Dharmākara's special karma through vows and the perfections over countless kalpas.

> 如釋論言：『如斯淨土，非三界所攝。』何以故？無欲故，非欲界；地居故，非色界；有形色故，非無色界。

### 2a02-17 <a id="tea-luelun-anle-jingtu-yi-2a02-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Besides the three grades there is a kind of birth for those who cultivate merit with a doubting mind, not understanding the Buddha's inconceivable wisdom; they dwell in jewelled palaces, happy as in the Trāyastriṃśa heaven, but for five hundred years do not see the Buddha or hear the Dharma. This is called the borderland (its difficulty) and womb-birth (its darkness) — names borrowed by analogy, for all in that land are born by transformation and after five hundred years they see and hear the Three Jewels.

> 安樂國土謂之邊地，亦曰胎生。邊地者，言其五百歲中不見聞三寶，義同邊地之難；……胎生者，譬如胎生人初生之時，人法未成。邊言其難，胎言其闇

### 2b05-17 <a id="tea-luelun-anle-jingtu-yi-2b05-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

To the doubt how ten continuous recitations can outweigh a lifetime of evil and take one out of the three realms without cutting the fetters, the answer is the Buddha's inconceivable wisdom, which can make little much and much little, the heavy light and the light heavy: a hundred years of firewood piled by a hundred men is burned in half a day by a bean-sized fire; a lame man carried on another's ship with the wind reaches a thousand li in a day.

> 不思議智者，謂佛智力能以少作多，以多作少；以近為遠，以遠為近；以輕為重，以重為輕……譬如百夫、百年聚薪，積高千仞，豆許火焚，半日便盡。……又如躄者寄載他船，因風帆勢，一日至千里。
