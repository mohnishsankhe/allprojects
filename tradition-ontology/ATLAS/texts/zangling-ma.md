The earliest full treasure biography of Padmasambhava, revealed by Nyangral Nyima Özer: his lotus birth in Oḍḍiyāna, training, arrival in Tibet, the building of Samye, the taming of spirits, his teachings to the king and disciples and his departure to the land of the rākṣasas.

### departure <a id="tea-zangling-ma-departure"></a>

Before leaving Tibet Padmasambhava gives prophecies and final advice, conceals teachings as treasures for future times, and departs on a horse through the sky to the south-west land of the rākṣasas to subdue them.
