# Jñānārṇava Tantra (Śākta)

`src:jnanarnava-tantra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Original title:** ज्ञानार्णवतन्त्र
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Genre:** tantra
**Authors:** 
  - [Śiva](../teachers/siva.md) — role: revealer; attribution: traditional
**Structure:** 26 paṭalas (Muktabodha e-text of S. Malaviya's ed.)
**Availability:** digitized-original

A Śrīvidyā tantra 'spoken by Īśvara' in twenty-six paṭalas, on the Bālā and Tripurasundarī vidyās, the forms of Śrīvidyā associated with its twelve great devotees, Śrīcakra worship, nyāsa and initiation. Distinct from Śubhacandra's Jain Jñānārṇava.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: ed. Sudhakar Malaviya (Muktabodha M00069)

_Notes: Bhāskararāya refers to the Jñānārṇava for the derivation of the twelve forms of the vidyā (Saubhāgyabhāskara). Not the Jain Jñānārṇava (src:jnanarnava)._
