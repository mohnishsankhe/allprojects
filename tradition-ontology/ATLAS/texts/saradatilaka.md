Lakṣmaṇa Deśika's manual of mantra-śāstra in 25 paṭalas: the emergence of Śakti, Nāda and Bindu from the supreme Lord (1.7), the letters and their deities, initiation, and the mantras and worship of many deities, ending with yoga. Widely used as an authority for mantra practice, including in Kerala.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.7 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-1-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

From the supreme Lord, having the splendour of being, consciousness and bliss, and possessed of parts (sakala), arose Śakti; from her Nāda; from Nāda arose Bindu.

> सच्चिदानन्दविभवात् सकलात् परमेश्वरात् । आसीच्छक्तिस्ततो नादो नादाद् बिन्दुसमुद्भवः ॥
