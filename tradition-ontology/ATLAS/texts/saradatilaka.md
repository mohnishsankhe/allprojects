U31 contribution (lineage owner): the most authoritative manual of mantra-śāstra. Paṭala 1 gives the cosmogony of sound — Śakti, nāda and bindu from the supreme Lord (1.7), the threefold bindu (1.8–9), śabda-brahman as the consciousness of all beings (1.11–13), dwelling as kuṇḍalinī and manifesting as the letters (1.14) — and the fifty letters with their deities; paṭala 2 gives the classes of mantras, their fifty defects (2.64–70), the ten purifications (2.112–122), and the marks of guru and disciple and the period of testing (2.141–153); later paṭalas give the mantras, worship and puraścaraṇa of many deities, and the last paṭala treats yoga.

### 1.7 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-1-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

From the supreme Lord, having the splendour of being, consciousness and bliss, and possessed of parts (sakala), arose Śakti; from her Nāda; from Nāda arose Bindu.

> सच्चिदानन्दविभवात् सकलात् परमेश्वरात् । आसीच्छक्तिस्ततो नादो नादाद् बिन्दुसमुद्भवः ॥

_level: unmarked · standpoint: cosmic · path: sound · stage: all · types: sound-language, world-fate_

terms: [nāda](../terms/nada.md), [bindu](../terms/bindu.md)

### 1.8-9 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-1-8-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

That (bindu), consisting of the supreme Śakti, divides again threefold: bindu, nāda and bīja are its divisions. Bindu is of the nature of Śiva, bīja is Śakti, and nāda is declared by those learned in all the Āgamas to be their mutual union.

> परशक्तिमयः साक्षात् त्रिधाऽसौ भिद्यते पुनः। बिन्दुर्नादो बीजमिति तस्य भेदाः समीरिताः॥ बिन्दुः शिवात्मको बीजं शक्तिर्नादस्तयोर्मिथः। समवायः समाख्यातः सर्वागमविशारदैः॥

_level: bridging · standpoint: cosmic · path: sound, knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate, sound-language_

terms: [bindu](../terms/bindu.md), [nāda](../terms/nada.md), [bīja](../terms/bija.md) · concepts: [Śakti, nāda, bindu: the emanation of sound](../concepts/nada-bindu-kala.md)

### 1.10-11 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-1-10-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

From bindu arose Raudrī, from nāda Jyeṣṭhā, from bīja Vāmā; from them arose Rudra, Brahmā and Viṣṇu, of the nature of will, knowledge and action; from the supreme bindu as it divided arose sound, unmanifest in nature (avyaktātmā ravaḥ).

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: sound · stage: all · types: world-fate, sound-language_

### 1.12-13 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-1-12-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Those learned in all the Āgamas call it śabda-brahman. Some say śabda-brahman is the meaning of words, others the word itself; neither is established, for both are insentient. My view is that śabda-brahman is the consciousness of all beings.

> शब्दब्रह्मेति तं प्राहुः सर्वागमविशारदाः। शब्दब्रह्मेति शब्दार्थं शब्दमित्यपरे जगुः॥ न हि तेषां तयोः सिद्धिर्जडत्वादुभयोरपि। चैतन्यं सर्वभूतानां शब्दब्रह्मेति मे मतिः॥

### 1.14 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-1-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

That (consciousness), having taken the form of kuṇḍalinī in the middle of the body of living beings, manifests as the letters, in the forms of prose, verse and the rest.

> तत् प्राप्य कुण्डलीरूपं प्राणिनां देहमध्यगम्। वर्णात्मनाऽविर्भवति गद्यपद्यादिभेदतः॥

### 1.15-16 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-1-15-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

From Śambhu, of the nature of bindu, the kinsman of time, made of kalās, was born Sadāśiva, witness of the world, all-pervading; from Sadāśiva came Īśa, then Rudra, then Viṣṇu, then Brahmā.

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: sound · stage: all · types: world-fate_

### 2.57-59 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-2-57-59"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Mantras are of three kinds by the division of mantra and vidyā: mantras have male deities, vidyās female deities; (those ending in svāhā are female, those ending in huṃ and phaṭ male) and those ending in namaḥ are neuter — so the mantras are threefold.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: sound, ritual · stage: all · types: sound-language_

terms: [mantra](../terms/mantra.md), [vidyā (female mantra)](../terms/vidya-mantra.md) · concepts: [The classes of mantras](../concepts/classes-of-mantras.md)

### 2.60-63 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-2-60-63"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Mantras are of the nature of fire and moon, for fierce and gentle rites: those abounding in the syllables of fire are fiery, those abounding in the syllables of moon and nectar lunar. Fiery mantras awaken when the breath moves in the right (channel), lunar ones when it moves in the other; when the breath goes in both channels all awaken, and awakened they always give fruit to the practitioners.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: sound, body-breath, ritual · stage: intermediate · types: sound-language, practice, body-layers_

concepts: [The classes of mantras](../concepts/classes-of-mantras.md)

### 2.64-70 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-2-64-70"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Mantras defiled by defects such as being 'cut' do not protect the practitioner. The defects are: cut (chinna), obstructed, powerless, turned away, deaf, blind, nailed (kīlita), paralysed, burnt, frightened, fearful, dirty, scorned, split, asleep, drunk, fainted, robbed of potency, deficient, destroyed, child, youth, adult, mature, old, merciless, seedless, without accomplishment, sluggish, deceitful, without portion, without essence, squinting, without seed-syllable, smoky, embraced, deluded, hungry, over-proud, limbless, very angry, very cruel, bashful, of calmed mind, displaced, crippled, very old, loveless and afflicted — forty-nine in all.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: sound, ritual · stage: intermediate · types: sound-language, practice_

terms: [mantra-doṣa](../terms/mantra-dosa.md) · obstacles: [The defects of mantras (mantra-doṣa)](../obstacles/mantra-dosas.md)

### 2.112-123 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-2-112-123"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ten purifications (saṃskāra) of mantras, giving success, are taught: generation (janana), enlivening (jīvana), striking (tāḍana), awakening (bodhana), consecration (abhiṣeka), cleansing (vimalīkaraṇa), strengthening (āpyāyana), satiation (tarpaṇa), kindling (dīpana) and guarding (gupti) — guarding being the non-disclosure of the mantra being repeated. These ten, kept secret in all mantras, are to be done according to tradition, by which the mantra-knower obtains what he desires.

> मन्त्राणां दशसंस्काराः कथ्यन्ते सिद्धिदायिनः। जननं जीवनं पश्चात् ताडनं बोधनन्तथा॥ अथाभिषेको विमलीकरणाप्यायने पुनः। तर्पणं दीपनं गुप्तिर्दशैता मन्त्रसंस्क्रियाः॥

_level: conventional · standpoint: ritual · path: sound, ritual · stage: intermediate · types: practice, sound-language, teacher-transmission_

terms: [mantra-saṃskāra](../terms/mantra-samskara.md), [gopana (gupti)](../terms/gopana.md) · practices: [The ten purifications of a mantra (mantra-saṃskāra)](../practices/mantra-samskara.md)

### 2.141-144 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-2-141-144"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

To attain the aims of man a good disciple should resort to a guru who is pure on mother's and father's side, pure in disposition, master of his senses, knowing the essence of all Āgamas and the meaning of all śāstras, devoted to helping others and to japa and worship, of unfailing speech, calm, versed in the Veda and its meaning, following the path of yoga, dear to the deity's heart — such a guru is approved by the Āgama.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: ritual, sound · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission_

concepts: [Mantra initiation, testing and secrecy](../concepts/mantra-diksa-and-secrecy.md)

### 2.145-153 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-2-145-153"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The disciple should be of good family, pure-minded, intent on the aims of man, learned in the Veda, capable, free of lust, ever wishing the good of beings, a believer, devoted to his own dharma and to his parents, serving the guru with speech, mind, body and wealth, without pride of birth, learning or wealth, ready to give his life to fulfil the guru's command. A brāhmaṇa becomes fit after one year's service, a king after two, a vaiśya after three, a śūdra after four; such a willing server is to be accepted for initiation, sacrifice and vows.

### 23.106-110 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-23-106-110"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The mudrās beginning with āvāhanī delight all the deities: the añjali filled with flowers is āvāhanī (invitation); the same turned downward is for installing (sthāpana); the two fists joined with thumbs raised is sannidhāna (making present); the same with the thumbs inside is sannirodha (holding there); the two fists turned upward is sammukhīkaraṇa (turning to face); placing the six limbs on the deity's body is sakalīkaraṇa.

### 23.111-114 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-23-111-114"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Avaguṇṭhana: a fist of the right hand with the index finger extended downward, rotated around. Dhenu: the little and ring fingers, and the index and middle fingers, of the two hands joined facing each other — with it the teacher performs amṛtīkaraṇa (making nectar). Mahāmudrā: the thumbs interlocked and the fingers extended — used for paramīkaraṇa in the worship of the deity.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ritual · path: ritual · stage: all · types: practice_

practices: [The invocation gestures (āvāhanī and the rest)](../practices/avahanadi-mudras.md), [Dhenu mudrā (the cow gesture)](../practices/dhenu-mudra.md), [Mahāmudrā as a hand-gesture (paramīkaraṇa)](../practices/mahamudra-hasta.md)

### 23.114 <a id="tea-saradatilaka-23-114-comm"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Rāghavabhaṭṭa's commentary: mudrā is so called because it gives joy (mudaṃ rāti) to the deities; the fingers from little finger to thumb are earth, water, fire, air and space, and their mutual joining makes the deity present; the mudrās are not to be shown in a crowd or to the uninitiated, lest the deities be disturbed and the rite fruitless; for seekers of liberation the mudrās may be made mentally.
