## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-8 <a id="tea-abhisanda-sutta-1-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Going for refuge to the Buddha, Dhamma and Saṅgha are three streams of merit; abstaining from killing, stealing, sexual misconduct, lying and intoxicants are five great gifts, primal and undisputed, by which one gives fearlessness, non-enmity and non-oppression to countless beings and shares in them.
