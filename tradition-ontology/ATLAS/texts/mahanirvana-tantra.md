# Mahānirvāṇa Tantra

**Structure:** 14 ullāsas

A tantra in 14 ullāsas in dialogue of Sadāśiva and Pārvatī, presenting the worship of Brahman (ch. 3) and of the primordial Kālī (Ādyā Kālikā) as the religion for the Kali age: the name and nature of Kālī (4.31-32); formless and form meditation (5.137-140) and inner worship (5.143-146); the five tattvas with their Kali-age restrictions and substitutes (5-8); the duties of classes and stages of life (only householder and renunciant in the Kali age, 8.8); the bhairavī and tattva circles; saṃskāras; law and punishment (11-12); the symbolism of Kālī's form (13.5-13); and the avadhūtas (14).

### 2.7-15 <a id="tea-mahanirvana-tantra-2-7-15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Truly, without the path of the Āgama there is no way in the Kali age; in this age the gods are to be worshipped by the Āgama's rule; the mantras taught in the tantras are effective and quickly fruitful in the Kali age, whereas the Vedic ones are powerless like snakes without poison — fruitful in the Satya and later ages, as if dead in the Kali.

### 4.31-32 <a id="tea-mahanirvana-tantra-4-31-32"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Mahākāla is so called because he devours all beings; because you devour Mahākāla you are the supreme, primordial Kālikā. Because she devours Time she is Kālī, the original form of all; because she is Time and the beginning, she is sung as Ādyā Kālī.

> कलनात् सर्वभूतानां महाकालः प्रकीर्तितः । महाकालस्य कलनात् त्वमाद्या कालिका परा ॥ कालसङ्ग्रसनात् काली सर्वेषामादिरूपिणी । कालत्वादादिभूतत्वादाद्या कालीति गीयते ॥

### 5.137-140 <a id="tea-mahanirvana-tantra-5-137-140"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Meditation is of two kinds, with form and formless; the formless meditation on you is beyond speech and mind, unmanifest, all-pervading, not 'this' or 'thus', unattainable except by yogins through great restraint; therefore, to steady the mind, to reach the goal quickly and to awaken subtle meditation, a form is imagined for the formless Kālikā, mother of Time, according to her qualities and acts.

### 13.5-13 <a id="tea-mahanirvana-tantra-13-5-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The symbolism of Kālī's form: as white, yellow and other colours disappear in black, so all beings enter Kālī — hence the formless power of Time is described as black; the moon on her forehead signifies her immortality; her three eyes are moon, sun and fire, by which she sees the whole world born of time; her boon- and fear-dispelling hands protect beings in danger and impel them to their tasks; she is seated on a red lotus because she stands on the worlds born of rajas; pure consciousness, witness of all, she watches Time playing drunk on the wine of delusion; thus forms are imagined according to her qualities for the good of devotees of little understanding.

### 14.142-149 <a id="tea-mahanirvana-tantra-14-142-149"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The avadhūtas are of four kinds; those consecrated by the complete consecration (pūrṇābhiṣeka) are Śaiva avadhūtas; Brāhma and Śaiva avadhūtas follow the conduct of their stage and take no food or water not offered to Brahman or in the circle; bathing, twilight rites, eating, drinking, giving and protecting one's wife are all by the Āgama's rule; the avadhūta is twofold, complete (the paramahaṃsa) and incomplete (the wanderer).
