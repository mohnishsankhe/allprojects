### 230c17-20 <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-230c17-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

World-honoured One, with single mind I take refuge in the Tathāgata of Unhindered Light filling the ten directions and aspire to be born in the Land of Peace and Bliss. Relying on the sūtras and their true, virtuous marks, I set out these verses of aspiration, a summary in accord with the Buddha's teaching.

> 世尊我一心， 歸命盡十方，無礙光如來， 願生安樂國。我依修多羅， 真實功德相，說願偈總持， 與佛教相應。

### 231a14 <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-231a14"></a>

It is a realm of Mahāyāna good roots, all equal, without even the name of anything reproachable; women, those with deficient faculties and those of the two-vehicle kind are not born there. ### 231a24-25 <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-231a24-25"></a>

### 231b08-24 <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-231b08-24"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Whoever perfects the five gates of mindfulness will certainly be born in the Land of Peace and Bliss and see Amitābha: (1) worship — bowing to Amitābha with the body, intending birth there; (2) praise — with speech calling the Tathāgata's name, in accord with the Tathāgata's light, the mark of his wisdom, and the meaning of his name, wishing to practise in accord with reality; (3) aspiration — with the mind always aspiring single-mindedly to birth, to practise śamatha truly; (4) contemplation — with wisdom and right mindfulness contemplating the adornments of the land, the Buddha and the bodhisattvas, to practise vipaśyanā truly; (5) transfer — not abandoning any suffering being, making the transfer of merit foremost, to fulfil the mind of great compassion.

> 若善男子善女人，修五念門成就者，畢竟得生安樂國土，見彼阿彌陀佛。何等五念門？一者禮拜門；二者讚歎門；三者作願門；四者觀察門；五者迴向門。

### 231b24-232b04 <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-231b24-232b04"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The object of contemplation is twenty-nine adornments: seventeen of the land (purity, measure, nature, form, manifold things, wondrous colour, touch, the adornments of water, ground and space, rain, light, sound, the lord, the retinue, enjoyment, freedom from difficulties, the gate of the great meaning, fulfilment of all wishes), eight of the Buddha (seat, body, speech, mind, assembly, leader, lord, sustaining power that is not in vain) and four of the bodhisattvas (unmoving yet manifesting everywhere, in one instant, making offerings everywhere, establishing the Three Jewels where they are absent).

> 觀察彼佛國土功德莊嚴者，有十七種事應知。……觀佛功德莊嚴成就者，有八種應知。……觀彼菩薩有四種正修行功德成就應知。

### 232b22-25 <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-232b22-25"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

These three kinds of accomplishment are adornments of the vow-mind; in brief they enter the one dharma-phrase, which is the pure phrase, which is the uncreated dharma-body of true wisdom. This purity is of two kinds: the purity of the vessel-world (the seventeen adornments of the land) and the purity of the world of beings (the adornments of the Buddha and bodhisattvas).

> 此三種成就願心莊嚴，略說入一法句故。一法句者，謂清淨句。清淨句者，謂真實智慧無為法身故。

### 232c03-233a04 <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-232c03-233a04"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

By broad and brief practice of śamatha and vipaśyanā the bodhisattva gains a pliant mind and skilful transfer: he does not seek his own pleasure with the merit gathered by the five practices but vows to take in all beings and be born together with them in that land. He thus leaves the three things contrary to awakening (attachment to self, not giving beings peace, seeking honour for himself) through the gates of wisdom, compassion and skilful means, and fulfils the three pure minds that accord with awakening.

> 謂說禮拜等五種修行所集一切功德善根，不求自身住持之樂，欲拔一切眾生苦故，作願攝取一切眾生，共同生彼安樂佛國。

### 233a05-25 <a id="tea-wangsheng-lun-233a05-25"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Five gates of merit are gradually accomplished: by worship one enters the first gate (approach), born in the Land of Peace and Bliss; by praise and calling the name, the second (joining the great assembly); by single-minded aspiration and the tranquil samādhi of śamatha, the third (the dwelling: the lotus-store world); by vipaśyanā, the fourth (the chamber: enjoying the flavours of the dharma). The first four are gates of entering, of benefiting oneself; by the fifth, going out, one looks with great compassion on suffering beings, manifests transformation bodies and re-enters the garden of birth-and-death and the forest of passions, playing with superknowledges at the stage of teaching, by the power of the original vow. Thus benefiting self and others the bodhisattva swiftly attains perfect awakening.

> 出第五門，以大慈悲觀察一切苦惱眾生，亦應化身迴入生死園煩惱林中，遊戲神通至教化地，以本願力迴向故，是名出第五門。
