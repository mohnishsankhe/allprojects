## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-isvarasadhanadusana-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Naiyāyika proof that the world, being an effect, has an intelligent maker fails: 'being an effect' is established only for things like pots whose making is observed; for mountains and bodies it is ambiguous, and a single permanent maker could not produce successive effects.
