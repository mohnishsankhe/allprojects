# Shamenguo jing 沙門果經 (Dīrgha-āgama 27)

The Chinese Dīrgha-āgama parallel of the Sāmaññaphala Sutta (T 1, no. 27, fasc. 17), in which King Ajātaśatru reports the answers of the six heterodox teachers (liushi waidao 六師外道) before hearing the Buddha. The six and their doctrines broadly parallel DN 2; the wording differs.

_Notes: Not checked: the CBETA volume T01 is not in sources_raw. Scholars report that some doctrines are assigned to different teachers in the Chinese and Sanskrit versions; this unit could not verify which, so no teaching entries are made. Other parallels: T 22 Jizhi guo jing 寂志果經; Ekottarika-āgama 43.7; the Sanskrit Śrāmaṇyaphala-sūtra in the Mūlasarvāstivāda Saṅghabhedavastu._
