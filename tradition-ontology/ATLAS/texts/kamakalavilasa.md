Puṇyānanda's short treatise in āryā verses on the metaphysics of Śrīvidyā: Śiva as light (prakāśa) and Śakti as the mirror of self-awareness (vimarśa), their union as the great point and the 'I' (aham), the kāmakalā of the white and red points, the emanation of the Śrīcakra, and liberation by knowing this vidyā.

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1-3 <a id="tea-kamakalavilasa-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

May Maheśa protect us, whose body is pure light, within whom self-awareness lies merged, who delights in the play of the arising, abiding and dissolving of all worlds. Victorious is the primal Śakti, whose form is her own eternal incomparable bliss, the seed of all that moves and does not move, the stainless mirror in which Śiva's form is reflected. The supreme Śakti is the sprout of the seed that is the manifest union of Śiva and Śakti.

> sakalabhuvanodayasthitilayamayalīlāvinodanodyuktaḥ / antarlīnavimarśaḥ pātu maheśaḥ prakāśamātratanuḥ

### 4-5 <a id="tea-kamakalavilasa-4-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When the rays of the sun that is Paraśiva are reflected in the clear mirror of self-awareness, the great point (mahābindu) enters the wall of consciousness; the 'I' made of consciousness, the fused form of the letters 'a' and 'ha', the lump of Śiva and Śakti in union, which has swallowed the circle of the worlds, is victorious.

### 6-8 <a id="tea-kamakalavilasa-6-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The pair of points, white and red, is Śiva and Śakti separated, contracting and expanding, entering one another, the cause of the creation of word and meaning; the point that is the 'I' is the sun, the fused form of this pair; kāma (desire) because it is lovely, and kalā — the two points that are fire and moon. This is the kāmakalā-vidyā, whose nature is the sequence of the Goddess's cakra; whoever knows it is liberated, becoming Mahātripurasundarī.
