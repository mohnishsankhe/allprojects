## Teachings (7: skeleton 7)

### 2.1-2.4 <a id="tea-dhammacakkappavattana-sutta-2-1-2-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There are two extremes not to be followed by one gone forth: devotion to sensual pleasure, low, vulgar, of the ordinary person, ignoble, unbeneficial; and devotion to self-mortification, painful, ignoble, unbeneficial. Without veering to either, the Tathāgata has awakened to the middle way, which gives vision and knowledge and leads to peace, direct knowledge, awakening and nibbāna.

> “Dveme, bhikkhave, antā pabbajitena na sevitabbā. Katame dve? Yo cāyaṁ kāmesu kāmasukhallikānuyogo hīno gammo pothujjaniko anariyo anatthasaṁhito, yo cāyaṁ attakilamathānuyogo dukkho anariyo anatthasaṁhito. Ete kho, bhikkhave, ubho ante anupagamma majjhimā paṭipadā tathāgatena abhisambuddhā cakkhukaraṇī ñāṇakaraṇī upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati.

### 3.1-3.4 <a id="tea-dhammacakkappavattana-sutta-3-1-3-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

And what is that middle way? It is this noble eightfold path: right view, right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration.

> Katamā ca sā, bhikkhave, majjhimā paṭipadā tathāgatena abhisambuddhā cakkhukaraṇī ñāṇakaraṇī upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati? Ayameva ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo, seyyathidaṁ— sammādiṭṭhi sammāsaṅkappo sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvo sammāvāyāmo sammāsati sammāsamādhi. Ayaṁ kho sā, bhikkhave, majjhimā paṭipadā tathāgatena abhisambuddhā cakkhukaraṇī ñāṇakaraṇī upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: practice_

### 4.1-4.10 <a id="tea-dhammacakkappavattana-sutta-4-1-4-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The noble truth of suffering: birth, ageing, illness and death are suffering; union with the disliked, separation from the liked, not getting what one wants is suffering; in brief the five aggregates of clinging are suffering. The origin: craving that leads to renewed existence, accompanied by delight and lust, seeking delight here and there — craving for sensual pleasures, for becoming, for non-becoming. The cessation: the remainderless fading away and cessation of that same craving, giving it up, release, non-attachment. The way: the noble eightfold path.

> Idaṁ kho pana, bhikkhave, dukkhaṁ ariyasaccaṁ— jātipi dukkhā, jarāpi dukkhā, byādhipi dukkho, maraṇampi dukkhaṁ, appiyehi sampayogo dukkho, piyehi vippayogo dukkho, yampicchaṁ na labhati tampi dukkhaṁ—saṅkhittena pañcupādānakkhandhā dukkhā. Idaṁ kho pana, bhikkhave, dukkhasamudayaṁ ariyasaccaṁ— yāyaṁ taṇhā ponobbhavikā nandirāgasahagatā tatratatrābhinandinī, seyyathidaṁ— kāmataṇhā, bhavataṇhā, vibhavataṇhā. Idaṁ kho pana, bhikkhave, dukkhanirodhaṁ ariyasaccaṁ— yo tassāyeva taṇhāya asesavirāganirodho cāgo paṭinissaggo mutti anālayo. Idaṁ kho pana, bhikkhave, dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā ariyasaccaṁ— ayameva ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo, seyyathidaṁ— sammādiṭṭhi …pe… sammāsamādhi.

### 5-8 <a id="tea-dhammacakkappavattana-sutta-5-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Regarding each truth, vision, knowledge, wisdom, true knowledge and light arose in things unheard before: 'this is suffering', 'it is to be fully understood', 'it has been fully understood'; the origin is to be abandoned and has been abandoned; the cessation is to be realised and has been realised; the path is to be developed and has been developed.

### 10.1-10.3 <a id="tea-dhammacakkappavattana-sutta-10-1-10-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When my knowledge and vision of these four noble truths in their three turnings and twelve aspects was quite purified, I claimed to have awakened to the unsurpassed perfect awakening; and knowledge and vision arose: unshakeable is my liberation; this is my last birth; there is now no renewed existence.

> Yato ca kho me, bhikkhave, imesu catūsu ariyasaccesu evaṁ tiparivaṭṭaṁ dvādasākāraṁ yathābhūtaṁ ñāṇadassanaṁ suvisuddhaṁ ahosi, athāhaṁ, bhikkhave, sadevake loke samārake sabrahmake sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiyā pajāya sadevamanussāya ‘anuttaraṁ sammāsambodhiṁ abhisambuddho’ti paccaññāsiṁ. Ñāṇañca pana me dassanaṁ udapādi: ‘akuppā me vimutti, ayamantimā jāti, natthi dāni punabbhavo’”ti.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: experiential · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation_

### 11.1-11.2 <a id="tea-dhammacakkappavattana-sutta-11-1-11-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

While this discourse was being spoken, the dust-free, stainless eye of the Dhamma arose in the venerable Koṇḍañña: 'whatever is subject to arising is all subject to cessation'.

> Imasmiñca pana veyyākaraṇasmiṁ bhaññamāne āyasmato koṇḍaññassa virajaṁ vītamalaṁ dhammacakkhuṁ udapādi: “yaṁ kiñci samudayadhammaṁ sabbaṁ taṁ nirodhadhamman”ti.

### 12-14 <a id="tea-dhammacakkappavattana-sutta-12-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When the wheel of Dhamma was set rolling, the earth-gods cried out that it could not be stopped by any ascetic, brahmin, god, Māra or Brahmā, and the cry rose through the heavens to the Brahmā world; the ten-thousandfold world-system shook and a boundless light appeared. The Buddha exclaimed 'Koṇḍañña has understood!'
