# Kaṭharudra Upaniṣad

`src:katharudra-upanisad` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Kaṇṭhaśruti (in some lists)
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md), [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Genre:** upaniṣad
**Attribution:** tradition: Śruti: revealed, without human author (apauruṣeya); the Muktikā presents it as one of the 108 Upaniṣads that Rāma teaches Hanumān, each belonging to a Vedic śākhā; scholarly: anonymous; assigned to a Veda by the Muktikā list, not by any surviving śākhā transmission; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Śruti: eternal and authorless; no human date; Scholarly account: Olivelle (1992) separates an older group (Āruṇi, Laghu-Saṃnyāsa/Kuṇḍikā, Kaṭhaśruti, Paramahaṃsa, Jābāla, Brahma), probably from around the turn of the common era, from a later medieval group (c. 12th–15th c.), largely composed in Advaita milieus in South India.; (confidence low)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The gods ask Prajāpati about renunciation: the rite in which the father tells his son 'you are Brahman…', the relatives' conduct, abandoning vessels; the eightfold avoidance of sexual thought; then Vedāntic teaching on the five sheaths and sevenfold consciousness.

### 1-2 <a id="tea-katharudra-upanisad-1-2"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

He abandons water-pot, bowl, sling, stand, sandals, patched cloth, loincloth, strainer, bath-cloth, upper cloth, thread and the Vedas.

### 5-6 <a id="tea-katharudra-upanisad-5-6"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Sexual union is eightfold — seeing, touching, play, talk, secret talk, resolve, determination and the act; the renouncer avoids all eight.

### 9 <a id="tea-katharudra-upanisad-9"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

### 17-22 <a id="tea-katharudra-upanisad-17-22"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

### 37-41 <a id="tea-katharudra-upanisad-37-41"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Consciousness is sevenfold — pure, Īśvara, jīva, the knower, the means of knowing, the known and the result; all are the one consciousness.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind, ultimate_

### 1 (rite) <a id="tea-katharudra-upanisad-1-rite"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

The gods ask Prajāpati about renunciation; after the rite the father removes topknot and thread and tells his son 'you are Brahman, you are the sacrifice…'; the son answers 'I am Brahman…'; relatives must not shed tears, which would harm the line, and return without looking back.
