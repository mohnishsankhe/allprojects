### 2-3 <a id="tea-sabhiya-sutta-2-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The wanderer Sabhiya put a deity's questions to the six famous teachers; unable to answer, they showed annoyance and questioned him back, and he thought of returning to lay life.
