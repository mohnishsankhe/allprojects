# Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa (Mādhyandina)

`src:satapatha-brahmana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Original title:** शतपथब्राह्मणम्
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Genre:** brāhmaṇa
**Authors:** 
  - [Yājñavalkya](../teachers/yajnavalkya.md) — role: revealer; attribution: traditional
  - [Śāṇḍilya](../teachers/sandilya.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: the White Yajus 'from the Sun' proclaimed by Yājñavalkya Vājasaneya (ŚB 14.9.4.33 = BĀU 6.5.3); scholarly: a compilation of the Vājasaneyin school; books 6–10 cite Śāṇḍilya as their authority, books 1–5 and 11–14 Yājñavalkya; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 8th–6th c. BCE (late Brāhmaṇa period; estimates vary); (confidence low)
**Structure:** Mādhyandina recension: 14 kāṇḍas, 100 adhyāyas; cited kāṇḍa.adhyāya.brāhmaṇa.kaṇḍikā
**Availability:** digitized-original

The 'Brāhmaṇa of a hundred paths', the great Brāhmaṇa of the White Yajurveda. It explains the whole Śrauta ritual — new- and full-moon, fire establishment, Agnihotra, ancestral offering, seasonal rites, Soma, royal consecration, the fire-altar (Agnicayana, books 6–10, with its 'secret' in book 10), horse, human and all-sacrifice, the funeral mound, the Pravargya — and teaches the sacrifice as the cosmic Person and year: Prajāpati fell apart after emitting creatures and is rebuilt in the altar. It records the teaching of Śāṇḍilya (10.6.3), the debates of Yājñavalkya at Janaka's court, the debts, the five great sacrifices, upanayana, Vedic self-study, the flood, and ends with the Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad (14.4–9).
