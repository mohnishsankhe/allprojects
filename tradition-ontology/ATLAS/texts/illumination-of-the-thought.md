In commenting on the sixth chapter of the Madhyamakāvatāra, Tsongkhapa sets out the tenets unique to Prāsaṅgika — rejection of a store-consciousness distinct from the six and of reflexive awareness, acceptance of external objects conventionally, and the rest.
