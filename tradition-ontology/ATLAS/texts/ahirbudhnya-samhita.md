# Ahirbudhnya Saṃhitā

**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Bhagavān (Nārāyaṇa/Vāsudeva); one of the traditional 108 saṃhitās; confidence: moderate
**Structure:** 60 adhyāyas
**Availability:** digitized-original

A 60-chapter Pāñcarātra saṃhitā in which Ahirbudhnya (Śiva) teaches Nārada, centred on Sudarśana as Viṣṇu's will-power (saṅkalpa). It defines the six guṇas (2.53–62), derives the three vyūhas from pairs of guṇas (5.16–19), lists 39 vibhavas (5.50–56), summarises the Sāṃkhya Ṣaṣṭitantra as Kapila's Vaiṣṇava teaching (12.18–30), calls the Lord's grace śaktipāta (14.28–34) and defines six-limbed surrender (37.27–29).
