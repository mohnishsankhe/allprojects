# Śiva Saṃhitā

`src:siva-samhita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Original title:** शिवसंहिता
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Genre:** yoga dialogue (Śiva–Pārvatī)
**Authors:** 
  - [Ādinātha](../teachers/adinatha.md) — role: revealer; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: the words of Śiva (Īśvara) to Pārvatī; scholarly: anonymous, probably from the region of Vārāṇasī, c. 15th c.; draws on Śrīvidyā and Advaita; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Śiva's revelation; Scholarly account: c. 15th c. CE; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 5 paṭalas (c. 510–645 verses depending on edition; the Muktabodha e-text has c. 514)
**Availability:** digitized-original

A yoga manual addressed also to householders. Ch. 1 surveys and rejects rival doctrines and teaches a non-dual Vedānta; ch. 2 maps the body as a microcosm (Meru, moon and sun, 350,000 channels, fourteen principal); ch. 3 the breaths, the guru, the conditions and four stages of practice and the postures; ch. 4 the mudrās; ch. 5 the obstacles, the four yogas and four grades of aspirant, the centres, rājayoga at the thousand-petalled lotus (Kailāsa) and a Śrīvidyā mantra practice.

## Teachings (54: skeleton 54)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-siva-samhita-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Knowledge alone is one, eternal, without beginning or end; nothing else is real. The differences seen in the world are due to the limiting adjuncts of the senses; knowledge itself does not appear otherwise.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate_

### 1.4-9 <a id="tea-siva-samhita-1-4-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Some praise truth, others austerity, purity, forbearance or straightforwardness; some gifts, ancestral rites, action or dispassion; some the duties of the householder, the Agnihotra, mantrayoga or pilgrimage — people settled in such views are deluded and wander helplessly through the round of birth and death.

_level: conventional · standpoint: polemical · path: general · stage: all · types: dispute, karma-liberation_

### 1.10-16 <a id="tea-siva-samhita-1-10-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Others hold that there are many eternal all-pervading selves; others that only what is perceived exists and there is no heaven; others that there is a stream of knowledge, or the void; others that reality is twofold, prakṛti and puruṣa; some say the world is without a Lord, others with a Lord — these contentious views cannot all be stated; those who wander among them are shut out of the path of liberation.

### 3.1-9 <a id="tea-siva-samhita-3-1-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

### 3.20-26 <a id="tea-siva-samhita-3-20-26"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

### 3.68-83 <a id="tea-siva-samhita-3-68-83"></a>

### 4.1-11 <a id="tea-siva-samhita-4-1-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Yoni mudrā: first by inhalation one fills the base (ādhāra) with the mind and contracts the yoni between the anus and the genitals; one meditates on Kāma in the brahma-yoni, like a ball, bright as millions of suns and cool as millions of moons, above it a subtle flame of consciousness, which goes by the path of Brahman through the three liṅgas to the nectar in heaven and returns to the kula by the measured yoga. By this binding there is nothing that cannot be accomplished: even mantras that are 'cut', 'nailed', 'paralysed', 'burnt' or otherwise defective become effective; one bound by yoni mudrā is not stained by the killing of a thousand brahmins, of the guru, drinking liquor, theft or the violation of the guru's bed. It is to be practised daily by those seeking liberation and not given to anyone even at the cost of life.
