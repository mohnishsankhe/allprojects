### 551b27 <a id="tea-xu-gaoseng-zhuan-551b27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Daoxuan's biography of Bodhidharma: a South Indian brahmin who came via the Song to the Wei and taught meditation; Daoyu and Huike served him four or five years; he taught them to pacify the mind by wall-gazing, to practise the four practices, to accord with others and guard against slander, and to be without attachment.

> 菩提達摩。南天竺婆羅門種

### 552b20 <a id="tea-xu-gaoseng-zhuan-552b20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Bodhidharma gave Huike the four-fascicle Laṅkāvatāra saying that in China only this sūtra should be relied on; Huike's arm was later cut off by bandits and he bore it through the Dharma without pain; his followers Na and Man carried the Laṅkāvatāra as the essence of mind.

> 初達摩禪師以四卷楞伽授可曰。我觀漢地惟有此經。仁者依行自得度世

### 684a11-18 <a id="tea-xu-gaoseng-zhuan-684a11-18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Recently a mountain monk Shandao, seeking the way everywhere, met Daochuo's community at Xihe and practised only the Pure Land work of recollecting Amitābha; in the capital he spread it widely and copied the Amitābha Sūtra tens of thousands of times. While he was preaching at the Guangming si, a man asked whether reciting the name would surely bring birth in the Pure Land; Shandao said it would. The man bowed, left the temple reciting 'Namo Amituofo' voice after voice, climbed a willow, joined his palms facing west, threw himself down and died.

> 近有山僧善導者。周遊寰寓求訪道津。行至西河遇道綽部。惟行念佛彌陀淨業。既入京師廣行此化。寫彌陀經數萬卷。士女奉者其數無量。時在光明寺說法。有人告導曰。今念佛名定生淨土不。導曰。念佛定生。其人禮拜訖。口誦南無阿彌陀佛聲聲相次出光明寺門。上柳樹表。合掌西望。倒投身下。至地遂死。
