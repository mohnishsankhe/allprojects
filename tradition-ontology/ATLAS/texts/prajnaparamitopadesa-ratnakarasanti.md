## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-prajnaparamitopadesa-ratnakarasanti-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The images of objects are false, projections of beginningless imprints; the yogin who recognizes this rests in luminous awareness without images, which is the perfection of wisdom and the meaning of both the Middle Way and mind-only.
