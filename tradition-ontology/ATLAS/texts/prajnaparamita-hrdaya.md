The Heart Sūtra: Avalokiteśvara, coursing in the deep perfection of wisdom, sees the five aggregates empty of own-being; form is emptiness and emptiness is form; all dharmas are marked by emptiness, unborn and unceasing; in emptiness there are no aggregates, elements, links, truths, knowledge or attainment; relying on the perfection of wisdom bodhisattvas are without obstruction and buddhas awaken; it ends with the mantra gate gate pāragate pārasaṃgate bodhi svāhā. Short (without frame) and long (with frame) versions exist.

## Teachings (8: skeleton 8)

### long.1 <a id="tea-prajnaparamita-hrdaya-long-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Longer recension frame: at Rājagṛha on the Vulture Peak the Buddha enters the samādhi called 'perception of the profound'; Śāriputra, through the Buddha's power, asks Avalokiteśvara how a son or daughter of good family who wishes to course in the deep perfection of wisdom should train; Avalokiteśvara answers that one should see the five aggregates as empty of own-being.

### long.2 <a id="tea-prajnaparamita-hrdaya-long-2"></a>

### s3 <a id="tea-prajnaparamita-hrdaya-s3"></a>

### s4 <a id="tea-prajnaparamita-hrdaya-s4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Here, Śāriputra, form is emptiness and emptiness is form; emptiness is not other than form, form not other than emptiness; what is form is emptiness, what is emptiness is form. So too feeling, perception, formations and consciousness (s5).

> iha śāriputra rūpaṃ śūnyatā, śūnyataiva rūpam | rūpānna pṛthak śūnyatā, śūnyatāyā na pṛthag rūpam | yadrūpaṃ sā śūnyatā, yā śūnyatā tadrūpam

### s6 <a id="tea-prajnaparamita-hrdaya-s6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

All dharmas have the mark of emptiness: unborn, unceasing, not stained, not stainless, not deficient, not complete. Therefore in emptiness there is no form, feeling, perception, formations or consciousness; no eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mind; no forms, sounds, smells, tastes, touchables, dharmas; no eye-element up to no mind-element.

> sarvadharmāḥ śūnyatālakṣaṇā anutpannā aniruddhā amalā na vimalā nonā na paripūrṇāḥ

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, consciousness-mind_

terms: [anutpāda](../terms/anutpada.md), [śūnyatā](../terms/sunyata.md) · concepts: [Emptiness in the Perfection of Wisdom sūtras](../concepts/emptiness-prajnaparamita.md) · teachers: [Avalokiteśvara](../teachers/avalokitesvara.md), [Sāriputta](../teachers/sariputta.md)

### s7 <a id="tea-prajnaparamita-hrdaya-s7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

No knowledge, no ignorance, no destruction of knowledge or of ignorance, up to no old age and death and no destruction of old age and death; no suffering, origin, cessation or path; no knowing and no attaining.

> na duḥkhasamudayanirodhamārgā na jñānaṃ na prāptitvam

### s8 <a id="tea-prajnaparamita-hrdaya-s8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Relying on the perfection of wisdom the bodhisattva dwells without obstruction of mind; having no obstruction he is unafraid, has passed beyond inversions and reaches final nirvāṇa; relying on it all buddhas of the three times fully awaken to unsurpassed perfect awakening.

> cittāvaraṇanāstitvādatrasto viparyāsātikrānto niṣṭhanirvāṇaḥ

### s9 <a id="tea-prajnaparamita-hrdaya-s9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Therefore the perfection of wisdom should be known as the great mantra, the mantra of great knowledge, the unsurpassed mantra, the unequalled mantra, allayer of all suffering, true because not false; the mantra spoken in the perfection of wisdom is: gate gate pāragate pārasaṃgate bodhi svāhā.
