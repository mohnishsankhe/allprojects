## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.1.1 <a id="tea-bhavarthadipika-1-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Śrīdhara reads the Bhāgavata's opening ('we meditate on the supreme truth') as indicating the non-dual Brahman, while the whole Purāṇa is devotion to that Lord; devotion and knowledge are not opposed.
