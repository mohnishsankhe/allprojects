Guṇadhara's concise Prakrit verses on the passions (kaṣāya) — attachment (pejja) and aversion (dosa) — and the deluding karma, their bondage, rise, subsidence and destruction; with the Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama the second Digambara 'āgama', held to derive from the fifth Pūrva (Jñānapravāda).

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-kasayapahuda-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The work declares its source: in the fifth Pūrva, in its tenth section (vastu), the third prābhṛta, called 'Pejja' (attachment), is the Prābhṛta of the Passions; it treats attachment and aversion (pejja, dosa) and the deluding karma — their bondage, duration, rise, subsidence and destruction.
