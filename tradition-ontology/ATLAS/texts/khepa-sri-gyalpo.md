### 1 <a id="tea-khepa-sri-gyalpo-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

Patrul Rinpoche opens by naming the lineage in terms of the path: the view is Longchen Rabjam (vast expanse), the meditation is Khyentse Özer (rays of wisdom and love), the conduct is Gyalwai Nyugu (the victors' offspring) — whoever practises thus attains buddhahood without effort.

### 2 <a id="tea-khepa-sri-gyalpo-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Commenting on the three statements: relax body and mind, then with a sudden forceful 'phaṭ' cut through thought; in the startled openness awareness is naked — this is the introduction; decide there is nothing else; and let thoughts arising be liberated as they arise, like drawings on water, so that meditation and post-meditation are not divided.
