Āśvalāyana asks Parameṣṭhin (Brahmā) for brahmavidyā; taught faith, devotion, meditation and yoga, that 'not by works, offspring or wealth but by renunciation some reached immortality', meditation on the three-eyed Lord with Umā in the heart, the self as the one witness of the three states, and 'I am That' verses; closes with the Śatarudrīya and Avimukta.

### 1.1-2 <a id="tea-kaivalya-upanisad-1-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Āśvalāyana approaches Parameṣṭhin for the highest knowledge of Brahman; he is told to know it by faith, devotion, meditation and yoga.

### 1.3 <a id="tea-kaivalya-upanisad-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Not by works, offspring or wealth; by renunciation alone some have reached immortality; beyond heaven, hidden in the cave, it shines, which the renouncers enter.

> na karmaṇā na prajayā dhanena tyāgenaike amṛtatvam ānaśuḥ

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: karma-liberation, ethics_

concepts: [Renunciation (saṃnyāsa) in the principal Upaniṣads](../concepts/sannyasa.md)

### 1.5-7 <a id="tea-kaivalya-upanisad-1-5-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Seated in solitude, pure, with head, neck and body straight, in the last stage of life, the renouncer meditates in the heart lotus on the Lord with Umā — three-eyed, blue-throated, tranquil.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: meditation, devotion · stage: intermediate · types: practice_

### 1.8-10 <a id="tea-kaivalya-upanisad-1-8-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

He is Brahmā, Śiva, Indra, the imperishable, the supreme, self-luminous; he is Viṣṇu, prāṇa, time, fire and moon; seeing oneself in all beings and all beings in oneself one reaches Brahman.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate_

### 1.11 <a id="tea-kaivalya-upanisad-1-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Making the self the lower fire-stick and the praṇava the upper, by the churning of knowledge the wise burn the bond.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: intermediate · types: practice, sound-language_

`skeleton` · confidence high

Deluded by māyā, the self in the body does everything in waking, enjoys in dream, and in deep sleep, overcome by darkness, attains happiness; from it arise the three worlds.

`skeleton` · confidence high

### 1.17-23 <a id="tea-kaivalya-upanisad-1-17-23"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'I am subtler than the subtle, greatest; I am the manifold universe; without hands and feet, of unthinkable power; I see without eyes, hear without ears; I am to be known by all the Vedas.'

### 2 <a id="tea-kaivalya-upanisad-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

One who recites the Śatarudrīya is purified as by fire; he resorts to Avimukta; the one beyond the stages (atyāśramin) should recite it; by this knowledge he attains kaivalya.
