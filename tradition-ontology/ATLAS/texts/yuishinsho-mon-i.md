## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### tathagata-fills-world <a id="tea-yuishinsho-mon-i-tathagata-fills-world"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Buddha-nature is dharma-nature, dharma-body; this Tathāgata pervades the countless worlds — the hearts of the ocean of all beings; it is said that plants, trees and land all attain buddhahood. Because this heart of all beings entrusts itself to the vow of the dharma-body, that shinjin is buddha-nature. From this formless dharma-body appeared the form called Dharmākara, who made the vows and became Amida of Unhindered Light.
