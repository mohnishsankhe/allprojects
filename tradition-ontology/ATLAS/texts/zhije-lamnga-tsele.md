### five-paths <a id="tea-zhije-lamnga-tsele-five-paths"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Padampa Sangye's Pacification teaching is arranged as instruction for the five paths — accumulation, joining, seeing, meditation and no more learning — each with its preliminaries, main practice and conclusion, so that suffering is pacified by the realization of the Perfection of Wisdom.
