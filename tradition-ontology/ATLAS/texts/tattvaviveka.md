one of the thirty-seven works of Madhva collected as the Sarvamūla ('root of all'); the seventh of the ten short treatises (daśa-prakaraṇa); it a thirteen-verse discrimination of realities: independent and dependent; Śrī as eternally free; souls graded in liberation and in bondage; eternal and non-eternal insentient things; and the relation of a thing to its properties (no difference for lasting properties; difference-and-identity for temporary ones). It ends: one who knows all this dependent reality to be always under Hari's control is freed from saṃsāra.
