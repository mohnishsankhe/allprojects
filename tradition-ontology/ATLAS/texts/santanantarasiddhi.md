## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-santanantarasiddhi-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Seeing in another body actions and speech that in one's own case are preceded by intention, one infers another's mind; the argument holds whether objects are external or cognition-only, since in the latter case the appearances of others' actions still need a cause other than one's own mind-stream.
