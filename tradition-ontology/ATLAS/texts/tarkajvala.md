## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3.26 <a id="tea-tarkajvala-3-26"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The ultimate is of two kinds: one that is supramundane, free of elaboration and beyond effort; and one accordant with it, involving effort and the collections, which is pure worldly knowledge and the object of reasoning — the nominal ultimate.
