# Nārada Bhakti Sūtra

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md)

  - [Nārada](../teachers/narada.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Spoken by the divine sage Nārada ('nārada-proktam', NBS 84).; scholarly: Anonymous; usually dated after the Bhāgavata Purāṇa, c. 10th–13th c. CE or later.; confidence: low

**Structure:** 84 sūtras (numbering varies slightly between editions); sometimes divided into five chapters (adhyāyas) in modern editions
**Availability:** digitized-original

Eighty-four aphorisms attributed to the sage Nārada defining devotion as supreme love (parama-prema) of the nature of nectar, 'the fruit itself', higher than action, knowledge and yoga; they give earlier teachers' definitions and the gopīs as the model, the means (renunciation, unceasing worship, hearing and singing, above all the grace of great souls), the things to avoid, the signs of the lover of God, the eleven forms of attachment, and the line of bhakti teachers.
