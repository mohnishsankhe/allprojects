# Kulārṇava Tantra

`src:kularnava-tantra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Original title:** कुलार्णवतन्त्र
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md), [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md), [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md)
**Genre:** tantra (Kaula)
**Attribution:** tradition: Spoken by Īśvara (Śiva) to the Goddess; styled 'the door to nirvāṇa and liberation, great secret, highest of all āgamas' in its colophons; scholarly: anonymous; confidence: high
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 11th–14th c. (Muktabodha catalogue: Kaula, 'later, post-1100 AD'); (confidence low)
**Structure:** 17 ullāsas, c. 2,100 verses in the Malaviya edition
**Availability:** digitized-original

The classic exposition of the Kaula path in 17 ullāsas, as a dialogue of Īśvara and the Goddess: the rarity of human birth and the supremacy of knowledge (1); the greatness of Kula and its seven modes of conduct, Kaula the highest (2); the five āmnāyas and the supreme Ūrdhvāmnāya (3); mantras; the Kula substances and their consecration, with repeated warnings against their abuse (5-8); the seven ullāsas; the Kaula yogin (9); special days; Kula conduct (11); the guru, the pādukā and devotion to the guru (12-13); testing of guru and disciple and the kinds of initiation (14); japa and puraścaraṇa (15-16); and the etymologies of Kaula terms (17).
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Kulārṇava Tantra, ed. Tārānātha Vidyāratna with intro. by A. Avalon (Tantrik Texts 5, 1917)
  - kind: original; name: Kulārṇavatantram, ed. Sudhakar Malaviya (Muktabodha M00031)

### 1.7-8 <a id="tea-kularnava-tantra-1-7-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There is Śiva, of the nature of the supreme Brahman, partless, all-knowing, the doer of all, lord of all, stainless and without a second, self-luminous, without beginning or end, changeless, beyond the highest, without qualities, being–consciousness–bliss; the beings called jīvas are his parts.

### 1.9-12 <a id="tea-kularnava-tantra-1-9-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Covered by beginningless ignorance like sparks in a fire, divided by adjuncts such as the body, bound by their own merits and demerits, the jīvas take birth after birth in bodies fitted to their kind, with a lifespan and experience born of karma; the subtle body lasts until liberation.

### 1.14-17 <a id="tea-kularnava-tantra-1-14-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Among the eighty-four hundred thousand kinds of bodies, only in the human is knowledge of reality gained; human birth is won rarely, by accumulated merit, and is the stairway to liberation; one who, having obtained it, does not save himself — who could be more sinful? — he is a self-slayer.

### 2.7-8 <a id="tea-kularnava-tantra-2-7-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Vedas are above all; the Vaiṣṇava is higher than the Vedic, the Śaiva higher than the Vaiṣṇava, the Dakṣiṇa higher than the Śaiva; the Vāma is higher than the Dakṣiṇa, the Siddhānta higher than the Vāma, the Kaula higher than the Siddhānta; there is nothing beyond the Kaula.

### 2.23-24 <a id="tea-kularnava-tantra-2-23-24"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If one is a yogin one is not an enjoyer, and if an enjoyer one does not know yoga; the Kaula is of the nature of both enjoyment and yoga, therefore it is above all. In the Kula dharma enjoyment becomes yoga directly, sin becomes merit, and saṃsāra becomes liberation.

> योगी चेन्नैव भोगी स्याद्भोगी चेन्नैव योगवित् । भोगयोगात्मकं कौलं तस्मात् सर्वाधिकं प्रिये ॥ भोगो योगायते साक्षात् पातकं सुकृतायते । मोक्षायते च संसारः कुलधर्मे कुलेश्वरि ॥

### 2.30-35 <a id="tea-kularnava-tantra-2-30-35"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Kula knowledge shines only for those whose sins are worn away by past austerity, gifts, sacrifice, pilgrimage, japa and vows, who please both the God and the Goddess, who are pure-minded, calm, active, serving the guru, deeply devoted and discreet, with firm devotion to the guru, the Kula scriptures and the Kaulas; it is attained through faith, humility and good conduct and by keeping the guru's command.

### 9.40-43 <a id="tea-kularnava-tantra-9-40-43"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The yogin who knows the highest is free of attachment, beyond adjuncts and residual impressions, immersed in his own nature. The body is the temple, the jīva is the God Sadāśiva; he should cast off ignorance like stale offerings and worship with the sense 'I am He'. The jīva is Śiva and Śiva is the jīva; the jīva is purely Śiva: bound by fetters it is called jīva, freed from them it is Sadāśiva — as rice in its husk is paddy and without the husk is grain; bound by karma one is jīva, freed from karma, Sadāśiva.

> देहो देवालयो देवि जीवो देवः सदाशिवः । त्यजेदज्ञाननिर्माल्यं सोऽहम्भावेन पूजयेत् ॥ जीवः शिवः शिवो जीवः स जीवः केवलः शिवः । पाशबद्धः स्मृतो जीवः पाशमुक्तः सदाशिवः ॥
