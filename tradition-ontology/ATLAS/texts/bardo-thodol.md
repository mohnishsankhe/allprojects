The part of Karma Lingpa's cycle read aloud to the dying and dead: it introduces the clear light at the moment of death, the peaceful and wrathful deities of the bardo of dharmatā day by day as one's own projections, and the ways to close the womb door or choose a birth in the bardo of becoming.

## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### chikhai <a id="tea-bardo-thodol-chikhai"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

At the moment of death the dying person is called by name and introduced to the clear light: 'O noble one, what is called death has now come; the luminosity of dharmatā is dawning — recognize it as your own awareness, empty and luminous, and rest in it'; recognition at this point is liberation.

### chonyi-peaceful <a id="tea-bardo-thodol-chonyi-peaceful"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In the bardo of dharmatā the peaceful deities appear day by day — the five buddhas with their consorts, the bodhisattvas and the rest — radiating brilliant wisdom lights, while dull lights of the six realms appear beside them; the deceased is urged not to fear the brilliant lights nor to be drawn to the dull ones, and to recognize everything as the display of their own awareness.

### chonyi-wrathful <a id="tea-bardo-thodol-chonyi-wrathful"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Later the wrathful deities — the herukas with their consorts, the gaurīs, piśācīs, gatekeepers and īśvarīs — appear in terrifying forms; they are the same deities in wrathful aspect, and recognizing them as one's own projections liberates.

### colophon <a id="tea-bardo-thodol-colophon"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The text is to be read aloud to the dying and dead over the forty-nine days; merely hearing it, the tradition says, can liberate even without meditation, and it should also be studied in life.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ritual · path: ritual, sound · stage: all · types: death-dying, sound-language_

practices: [Liberation through hearing (thos grol)](../practices/liberation-through-hearing.md)

### sidpa <a id="tea-bardo-thodol-sidpa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In the bardo of becoming the deceased has a mental body driven by karma, sees the Lord of Death weigh deeds in the mirror of karma, and is urged to close the womb door by the methods taught or, failing that, to choose a favourable birth or a pure land.
