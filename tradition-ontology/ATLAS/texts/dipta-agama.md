# Dīptāgama

`src:dipta-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Dīpta, Sudīptaka (Kiraṇa)
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Īśa, then Īśānamūrti (Kiraṇa: Trimūrti), then Hutāśana (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Śivabheda no. 6 in the Kāmika's order; 9 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** unknown

No. 6 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Śivabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Īśa, then Īśānamūrti (Kiraṇa: Trimūrti), then Hutāśana. The Kāmika assigns it 9 upabheda(s): Ameya, Śabda, Ācchādya, Asaṅkhya, Amitaujas, Ānanda, Mādhavodbhūta, Adbhuta, Akṣata. A South Indian ritual text (temple rites, installation, festivals).
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Dīptāgama, ed. M.-L. Barazer-Billoret, B. Dagens, V. Lefèvre, IFP/EFEO (3 vols, 2004–2009)

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.5/10.9: Īśa → Trimūrti → Hutāśana. Śrīkaṇṭhīya: 'Dīpta' (list lacunose after it). In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the thighs (pūrva 1.93–101).
