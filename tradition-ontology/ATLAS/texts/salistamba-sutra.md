## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-salistamba-sutra-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Seeing a rice seedling, the Buddha said: 'whoever sees dependent origination sees the dharma; whoever sees the dharma sees the Buddha', and fell silent; Maitreya explains dependent origination outwardly (seed, sprout, leaf) and inwardly (the twelve links and six elements), neither eternal nor annihilated.

> yo bhikṣavaḥ pratītyasamutpādaṃ paśyati, sa dharmaṃ paśyati | yo dharmaṃ paśyati, sa buddhaṃ paśyati
