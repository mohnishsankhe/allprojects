## Teachings (13: skeleton 13)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-three-principal-aspects-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Paying homage to the venerable teachers, I shall explain as best I can the essence of all the Conqueror's teachings, the path praised by the holy ones, the entrance for fortunate ones seeking liberation; you who are not attached to saṃsāra's pleasures, who strive to make leisure and endowment meaningful and rely on the path that pleases the Conquerors — listen with a clear mind.

### 3 <a id="tea-three-principal-aspects-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Without pure renunciation there is no way to calm the pursuit of pleasures in the ocean of existence; since embodied beings are bound by craving for existence, first seek renunciation.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice, karma-liberation_

### 4 <a id="tea-three-principal-aspects-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Leisure and endowment are hard to find and life has no time to spare — accustoming the mind to this reverses attachment to this life; repeatedly thinking of the infallibility of karma and its results and of saṃsāra's sufferings reverses attachment to future lives.

### 5 <a id="tea-three-principal-aspects-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

When, having meditated so, not even a moment's wish for saṃsāra's splendours arises and the mind seeks liberation day and night, renunciation has arisen.

### 6 <a id="tea-three-principal-aspects-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Renunciation not held by pure bodhicitta does not become the cause of unsurpassed awakening's perfect bliss; therefore the wise generate the supreme mind of awakening.

### 7-8 <a id="tea-three-principal-aspects-7-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Swept by the currents of the four powerful rivers, bound by the tight bonds of karma hard to undo, caught in the iron net of self-grasping, enveloped by the thick darkness of ignorance, endlessly born and reborn in existence and tormented without pause by the three sufferings — thinking of the mothers in this condition, generate the supreme mind.

### 9 <a id="tea-three-principal-aspects-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Even if you have renunciation and bodhicitta, without the wisdom realising the way things are you cannot cut the root of existence; therefore strive in the means of realising dependent arising.

### 10 <a id="tea-three-principal-aspects-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Whoever sees the cause and effect of all phenomena of saṃsāra and nirvāṇa as never failing, and destroys every mode of apprehension (of inherent existence), has entered the path that pleases the Buddhas.

### 10-12 <a id="tea-three-principal-aspects-10-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Seeing cause and effect of saṃsāra and nirvāṇa as unfailing while destroying all apprehension of inherent existence, and holding the understanding of appearances as dependent arising and of emptiness as free of assertion together, not alternately, is the completion of the analysis of the view.

### 11 <a id="tea-three-principal-aspects-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As long as the two understandings — of appearances as unfailing dependent arising and of emptiness as free of assertion — seem separate, you have not yet realised the Sage's intent.

### 12 <a id="tea-three-principal-aspects-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

When they arise simultaneously, not alternately, and from merely seeing dependent arising as unfailing, certain knowledge destroys every mode of grasping at an object, the analysis of the view is complete.

### 13 <a id="tea-three-principal-aspects-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Moreover, appearance dispels the extreme of existence and emptiness dispels the extreme of non-existence; when you know how emptiness appears as cause and effect, you will not be captivated by extreme views.

`skeleton` · confidence moderate

When you have understood the essential points of the three principal aspects of the path, rely on solitude, generate the power of effort and quickly accomplish the final goal, my child.
