# Saddharmapuṇḍarīka-sūtra

`src:saddharmapundarika` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Lotus Sūtra, Miaofa lianhua jing 妙法蓮華經 (T262, Kumārajīva 406), Zheng fahua jing 正法華經 (T263, Dharmarakṣa 286), Tianpin miaofa lianhua jing (T264), Hokekyō / Myōhō-renge-kyō, dam chos pad ma dkar po (Toh 113)
**Original title:** सद्धर्मपुण्डरीकसूत्र
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), `lin:tiantai`, `lin:tendai`, `lin:nichiren`, `lin:newar-vajrayana`, [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md), [Zen (Japanese Chan)](../lineages/zen.md)

**Location in parent:** Nepal's nine dharmas; Threefold Lotus (with T276 and T277) in East Asia
**Attribution:** tradition: Spoken by the Buddha Śākyamuni (tradition's account: buddhavacana) on the Vulture Peak (the assembly moving into the air and back); scholarly: Anonymous; composed in stages c. 1st c. **Dates:** Tradition's account: the Buddha's last years (Tiantai: the fifth period, the Lotus-Nirvāṇa period); Scholarly account: c. 50 BCE-150 CE; Dharmarakṣa 286, Kumārajīva 406; Gilgit, Nepalese and Kashgar mss; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 28 chapters in Kumārajīva's version (with the Devadatta chapter as ch. 12); 27 in the Sanskrit (Vaidya), where Devadatta is part of ch. 11 and the order of the later chapters differs
**Availability:** digitized-original

The Lotus Sūtra: the Buddha reveals that the three vehicles were skillful means and there is only the one buddha-vehicle; all who hear it, even with a single flower or a child's drawing of a stūpa, will become buddhas; the śrāvakas receive predictions of buddhahood. Through the parables of the burning house, the prodigal son, the medicinal herbs, the conjured city, the jewel in the robe and the physician's sons it teaches skillful means; the stūpa of the past buddha Prabhūtaratna appears; the nāga princess becomes a buddha instantly; bodhisattvas rise from the earth; the Buddha reveals that he awakened incalculable aeons ago and his lifespan is immeasurable; it praises the dharma-preacher, Sadāparibhūta, Bhaiṣajyarāja's self-offering, Avalokiteśvara's rescue and Samantabhadra's protection.

## Teachings (26: skeleton 26)

### 1 <a id="tea-saddharmapundarika-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

At the Vulture Peak the Buddha, after teaching the 'Immeasurable Meanings', enters samādhi; flowers rain, the earth shakes in six ways and a ray from the tuft between his brows lights up eighteen thousand worlds to the east, showing their beings, buddhas and stūpas. Maitreya asks why; Mañjuśrī recalls that the past buddha Candrasūryapradīpa showed the same sign before teaching the Lotus.

### 2 <a id="tea-saddharmapundarika-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The buddhas appear in the world for one great purpose only: to open beings to the Tathāgata's knowledge and vision, to show it, to make them awaken to it and to make them enter its path. The buddhas' 'intentional speech' (saṃdhābhāṣya) is hard to understand; the dharma is beyond reasoning, known only to Tathāgatas.

> ekakṛtyena śāriputra ekakaraṇīyena tathāgato 'rhan samyaksambuddho loka utpadyate mahākṛtyena mahākaraṇīyena

### 2 <a id="tea-saddharmapundarika-2-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Buddha teaches the dharma to beings with reference to one vehicle only, the buddha-vehicle ending in all-knowledge; there is no second or third vehicle anywhere in the ten directions. When buddhas appear in times of the five turbidities — of the aeon, of beings, of defilements, of views, of lifespan — they teach that one buddha-vehicle by skillful means as three vehicles.

> ahamapi śāriputra ekameva yānamārabhya sattvānāṃ dharmaṃ deśayāmi yadidaṃ buddhayānaṃ sarvajñatāparyavasānam

### 3 <a id="tea-saddharmapundarika-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The parable of the burning house: a rich man's old house catches fire while his children play inside, heedless; he lures them out by promising goat-, deer- and ox-carts they love, and when they are out gives each the same great, jewelled ox-cart. So the Tathāgata, father of the world, draws beings from the burning house of the three realms by the three vehicles and gives them all the one great vehicle — and is not guilty of falsehood.

> ādīptamidaṃ gṛhaṃ mahatā agniskandhena

### 4 <a id="tea-saddharmapundarika-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The parable of the lost son, told by the great disciples: a son who ran away lives as a poor laborer; his rich father recognizes him but, knowing his low self-esteem, hires him for menial work and raises him gradually until at death he declares him heir. The disciples were content with a day's wage of nirvāṇa; the Buddha by skillful means led them to the inheritance of buddhahood.

### 5 <a id="tea-saddharmapundarika-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The parable of the medicinal herbs: one cloud rains the same rain on all plants, which each take it according to their kind; so the Tathāgata's teaching is of one taste — liberation — though beings receive it by their capacity. The Sanskrit adds the physician who cures a man born blind with four herbs, which are emptiness, the signless, the wishless and the door of nirvāṇa.

> yathā catasra oṣadhayastathā śūnyatānimittāpraṇihitanirvāṇadvāraṃ ca draṣṭavyam

### 7 <a id="tea-saddharmapundarika-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The chapter tells the past of the buddha Mahābhijñājñānābhibhū and his sixteen sons, one of whom was Śākyamuni.

### 10 <a id="tea-saddharmapundarika-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Whoever keeps, reads, recites, explains or copies even one verse of this sūtra, or honors it, is a messenger of the Tathāgata; where it is kept a stūpa should be built, needing no relics, for the Tathāgata's whole body is there. The preacher should enter the Tathāgata's room (compassion), wear his robe (patience) and sit on his seat (the emptiness of all dharmas).

### 12 <a id="tea-saddharmapundarika-12-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The eight-year-old daughter of the nāga king Sāgara, whom Mañjuśrī taught in the sea, is declared able to awaken quickly; Śāriputra objects that a woman's body cannot attain buddhahood. She gives the Buddha a jewel and, before the whole assembly, her female organ vanishes, a male one appears, she is a bodhisattva, goes south to the world Vimalā and there appears as a buddha teaching the dharma.

> tat strīndriyamantarhitaṃ puruṣendriyaṃ ca prādurbhūtaṃ bodhisattvabhūtaṃ cātmānaṃ sandarśayati

When bodhisattvas from other worlds offer to protect the sūtra, the Buddha declines: countless bodhisattvas rise from the earth beneath, whom he says he has taught since his awakening; Maitreya asks how, in forty-odd years, he could have taught so many.

### 16 <a id="tea-saddharmapundarika-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Tathāgata did not first awaken at Gayā: it is countless hundreds of thousands of myriads of koṭis of aeons since he awakened; his lifespan is immeasurable, he always abides, and he appears to enter nirvāṇa as a skillful means so that beings will not become complacent. Like a physician whose poisoned sons refuse the antidote until he sends word that he has died abroad — then they take it and recover — he is not guilty of lying.

> bahūni mama kalpakoṭīnayutaśatasahasrāṇyanuttarāṃ samyaksambodhimabhisambuddhasya

### 20 <a id="tea-saddharmapundarika-20"></a>

### 25 <a id="tea-saddharmapundarika-25"></a>
