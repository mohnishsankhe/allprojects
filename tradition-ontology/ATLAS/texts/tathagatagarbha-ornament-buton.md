Butön Rinchen Drub's treatise on the tathāgatagarbha teaching, arguing that it is a teaching with an intention (dgongs pa can) — its basis being emptiness — and so of provisional meaning, against readings that take buddha-nature to be a permanent, non-empty entity.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### intention <a id="tea-tathagatagarbha-ornament-buton-intention"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The teaching that beings possess a tathāgatagarbha endowed with the buddha-qualities is a teaching with an intention: its basis is emptiness, its purpose is to remove the five faults such as discouragement, and taken literally — a permanent essence full of qualities within beings — it would not differ from the non-Buddhists' self.
