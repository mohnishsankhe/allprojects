## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-7 <a id="tea-bhara-sutta-2-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The burden is the five aggregates of clinging; the bearer of the burden is the person (puggala) of such a name and clan; the taking up of the burden is craving; the laying down of the burden is the remainderless fading and cessation of that craving.
