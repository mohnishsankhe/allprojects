## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-3 <a id="tea-atma-upanisad-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Aṅgiras teaches that the self is threefold: the outer (the body of skin, flesh, bone), the inner (the subtle knower who perceives through the senses) and the supreme (the imperishable Brahman beyond all attributes).
