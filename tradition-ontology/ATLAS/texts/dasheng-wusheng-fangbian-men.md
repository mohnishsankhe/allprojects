### T85n2834 p.1273c01-c27 <a id="tea-dasheng-wusheng-fangbian-men-1273c01"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

(In a ritual dialogue between the preceptor and the disciples:) The buddhas and tathāgatas have a great expedient for entering the Way: with one thought of the pure mind one suddenly transcends to the buddha-ground. No form is to be grasped; 'viewing the mind, if it is pure, is called the ground of the pure mind'; the disciples look far, evenly, into all of space with the pure mind-eye without interruption and, asked what they see, answer 'not a single thing'. Awakening means the mind's substance free of thought (li nian), equal to space, pervading everywhere, the one mark of the dharma-realm, the Tathāgata's dharma-body, called original awakening.

> 諸佛如來有入道大方便。一念淨心頓超佛地 … 看心若淨名淨心地
