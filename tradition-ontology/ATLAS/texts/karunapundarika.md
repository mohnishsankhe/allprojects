## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### vows <a id="tea-karunapundarika-vows"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Śākyamuni, in a past life as the brahmin Samudrareṇu, vowed to become a buddha in an impure world of the five turbidities where others would not go, and is praised as a white lotus of compassion among buddhas.
