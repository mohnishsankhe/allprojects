## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 44.10 <a id="tea-abyakata-samyutta-44-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Vacchagotta asks 'is there a self?' and 'is there no self?' and the Buddha is silent. To Ānanda he explains: to answer 'there is a self' would side with the eternalists; 'there is no self' would side with the annihilationists; the first would not accord with the knowledge that all phenomena are not-self, and the second would bewilder the already bewildered Vacchagotta: 'did I not formerly have a self, which now does not exist?'
