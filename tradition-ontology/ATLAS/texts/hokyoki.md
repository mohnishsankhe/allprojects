## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### dropping-off <a id="tea-hokyoki-dropping-off"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Rujing tells Dōgen that practising Chan is body and mind dropping off; burning incense, bowing, reciting the Buddha's name, repentance and reading sūtras are not needed — only just sit.
