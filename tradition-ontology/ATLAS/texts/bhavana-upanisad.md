## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 1-5 <a id="tea-bhavana-upanisad-1-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The guru is the power (śakti); the body with its nine openings is the Śrīcakra; the channels are the powers of the cakra, the winds and elements its deities; the knower is the priest, knowledge the fire, the known the offering; worship is contemplation of non-difference.

### guru-body-cakra <a id="tea-bhavana-upanisad-guru-body-cakra"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The guru is the Śakti that is the cause of all; so the body with its nine openings is the Śrīcakra of nine cakras; Vārāhī is the father, Kurukullā the mother; the aims of life are the oceans; the body is the island of nine jewels; the fourteen channels are the deities of the fourteen triangles; the ten vital airs are the outer ten, the ten fires the inner ten; cold, heat, pleasure, pain, desire and the three qualities are Vaśinī and the other goddesses of speech; the five subtle elements are the five flower-arrows, mind the sugarcane bow, attachment the noose, aversion the goad; the unmanifest, mahat and ahaṃkāra are Kāmeśvarī, Vajreśvarī and Bhagamālinī in the inner triangle; the fifteen Nityās are the lunar days, the unfolding of time.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: meditation, ritual · stage: intermediate · types: body-layers, practice_

### phala <a id="tea-bhavana-upanisad-phala"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

One who is thus absorbed in this contemplation for three muhūrtas becomes liberated while living; identity with the deity is accomplished; what he intends comes about without effort; he is called a Śiva-yogin. The bhāvanā has been taught according to the Kādi and Hādi doctrines.

_level: bridging · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: karma-liberation, powers-experiences_

concepts: [Liberation while living (jīvanmukti)](../concepts/jivanmukti.md), [The Kādi and Hādi doctrines (mata)](../concepts/kadi-hadi-matas.md)

### sricakra-puja <a id="tea-bhavana-upanisad-sricakra-puja"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The knower is the offerer (hotṛ), knowledge is the fire, the known is the oblation; contemplating the non-difference of knower, knowing and known is the worship of the Śrīcakra.

> jñātā hotā jñānam agnir jñeyaṃ haviḥ / jñātṛjñānajñeyānām abhedabhāvanaṃ śrīcakrapūjanam

### upacaras <a id="tea-bhavana-upanisad-upacaras"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The offerings are inward: the readiness of the inner and outer senses is the invocation; their grasping one object the seat; the union of the three states the betel; the movement from the base to the crown and back the circumambulation; the fourth state the prostration; the immersion of the body-identified knower the bali-offering; the dissolving of 'is', 'is not', 'to be done' and 'not to be done' in the eternal self the fire-offering; immersion in her sandals the complete meditation.
