### 1 <a id="tea-vatulanatha-sutra-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The attainment of one's own nature is by the mode of 'great daring' (mahāsāhasa).

> mahāsāhasavṛttyā svarūpalābhaḥ
