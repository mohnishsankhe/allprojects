## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### frightening-places <a id="tea-katsom-chenmo-frightening-places"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Go to frightening, haunted places. Whatever terrifying appearance or fear arises, do not suppress it and do not seek protection: look at the one who is afraid, find no one, and rest in that emptiness.

### hope-and-fear <a id="tea-katsom-chenmo-hope-and-fear"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Hope for help from gods and fear of harm from demons are both forms of clinging; cutting both, the practitioner neither seeks the one nor avoids the other.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice, consciousness-mind_

### root-of-mind <a id="tea-katsom-chenmo-root-of-mind"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

What is cut is the root of the mind — the clinging to a self. Demons are not outside: the grasping at 'I' is itself the demon; where there is ego-clinging there are demons, where it is cut there are none.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, knowledge · stage: intermediate · types: consciousness-mind, practice_

### view <a id="tea-katsom-chenmo-view"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Chöd is the practice of the Perfection of Wisdom: the view is that all phenomena are unborn and empty, and this view is applied directly to cut clinging at its root rather than held as a theory.
