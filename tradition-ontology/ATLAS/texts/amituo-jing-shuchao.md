## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### holding-the-name <a id="tea-amituo-jing-shuchao-holding-the-name"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Holding the name is of two kinds: holding it in phenomena (shichi) — hearing of the Buddha and reciting his name continuously, remembering and not forgetting, with faith but without yet penetrating principle; and holding it in principle (lichi) — seeing that the reciting mind and the Buddha recited are not two. Each matures into the undistracted single mind in its phenomenal and principled forms.
