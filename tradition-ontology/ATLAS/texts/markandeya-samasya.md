The long-lived sage Mārkaṇḍeya instructs the exiled Pāṇḍavas: on karma, on the ages of the world and the dissolution — including his vision in the cosmic waters of a child on a banyan branch in whose body he saw the worlds — on the devoted wife and the righteous meat-seller, and other stories.
