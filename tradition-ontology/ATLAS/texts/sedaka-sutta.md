## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-3 <a id="tea-sedaka-sutta-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

An acrobat tells his apprentice 'you protect me and I will protect you'; the apprentice says each should protect himself. The Buddha: that is the way — protecting oneself one protects others by cultivating the establishments of mindfulness; protecting others one protects oneself by patience, harmlessness, love and sympathy.
