## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 21-23 <a id="tea-sonadanda-sutta-21-23"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Soṇadaṇḍa agrees that of the qualities of a brahmin only virtue and wisdom are indispensable; the Buddha affirms that wisdom is purified by virtue and virtue by wisdom — where one is, the other is, as one washes one hand with the other.
