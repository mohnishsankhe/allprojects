## Teachings (2: sourced 2)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-ganapati-upanisad-1-2"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Gaṇapati is declared the manifest reality: 'you alone are the doer, the sustainer, the destroyer; you alone are Brahman'.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate_

### 7-9 <a id="tea-ganapati-upanisad-7-9"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

His seed-mantra and Gāyatrī ('ekadantāya vidmahe…') and his meditation form (one-tusked, four-armed, with noose and goad) are given; recitation removes obstacles and grants the four aims.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language, practice_
