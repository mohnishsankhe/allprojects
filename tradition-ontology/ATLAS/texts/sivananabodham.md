## Teachings (12: skeleton 12)

### 1 <a id="tea-sivananabodham-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The world, spoken of as 'he, she and it', undergoes origination, maintenance and dissolution; therefore it has an agent. It resolves into Hara and, because of mala, comes forth again from him; hence the learned say that he who is its end is also its beginning.

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate, world-fate_

### 2 <a id="tea-sivananabodham-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Lord is one with souls and yet other than them (like the soul with the body, the vowel with the consonants); by his command (āṇai, his power) he stands inseparably with them and makes them come and go through births according to their twofold deeds (iruviṉai).

_level: bridging · standpoint: divine · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate, karma-liberation_

terms: [iruviṉai](../terms/iruvinai.md), [aruḷ](../terms/arul.md) · concepts: [The Siddhānta's 'advaita' of inseparability](../concepts/siddhanta-advaita.md), [Grace (anugraha) as the Lord's fifth act](../concepts/grace-anugraha.md) · disputes: `dsp:souls-one-or-distinct`

### 3 <a id="tea-sivananabodham-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

There is a soul within the body distinct from it: it denies that it is this or that ('not this'), it says 'my body', it knows through the five senses, it knows in dream, it does not experience in deep sleep, it is awakened, and it knows only when made to know.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [paśu](../terms/pasu.md) · concepts: [The soul as sadasat](../concepts/soul-as-sadasat.md) · disputes: [Is there a self?](../debates/is-there-a-self.md)

### 4 <a id="tea-sivananabodham-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The soul is not one of the inner organs (antaḥkaraṇa); bound by its innate mala it cannot know by itself, and it knows through the inner organs as a king through his ministers; it passes through the five states (avasthā).

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [āṇava mala](../terms/anava-mala.md), [avasthā](../terms/avastha.md) · concepts: [The five states (avasthā) in the Siddhānta](../concepts/five-avasthas.md)

### 5 <a id="tea-sivananabodham-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The senses know their objects only through the soul yet do not know the soul; the soul likewise knows only through Śiva, who moves it as a magnet moves iron while himself remaining unchanged.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind, ultimate_

terms: [aruḷ](../terms/arul.md)

### 6 <a id="tea-sivananabodham-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

What is known by the senses is changing and perishing (acat); what cannot be known at all would be non-existent; the Real (Śiva) is neither — it is known by the soul through grace, not as an object.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_

terms: [cemporuḷ / meypporuḷ](../terms/cemporul.md)

### 7 <a id="tea-sivananabodham-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Before the Real, the unreal does not appear; the unreal cannot know the Real; the soul, which knows both, is neither — it is sadasat (cat-acat), taking on the nature of whatever it is joined to.

_level: bridging · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: intermediate · types: consciousness-mind, ultimate_

terms: [sadasat](../terms/sadasat.md) · concepts: [The soul as sadasat](../concepts/soul-as-sadasat.md)

### 8 <a id="tea-sivananabodham-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

To the soul that has done penance in former lives, the Lord comes as the guru and teaches: 'You have lived among the five senses like a prince brought up among hunters'; the soul then leaves them, knows it is not other than the Lord, and reaches his feet.

_level: bridging · standpoint: divine · path: knowledge, devotion · stage: advanced · types: teacher-transmission, karma-liberation_

terms: [guru](../terms/guru.md), [aruḷ](../terms/arul.md) · concepts: [Śiva comes as the guru](../concepts/guru-as-siva.md), [Descent of power / grace (śaktipāta)](../concepts/saktipata.md)

### 9 <a id="tea-sivananabodham-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Seeing Śiva in one's own soul with the eye of knowledge given by grace, knowing the senses' world as a mirage (pēyttēr), taking refuge in the Lord's shade, and meditating on the five letters as prescribed, the soul is purified.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge, sound, meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice, sound-language_

terms: [pañcākṣara](../terms/pancaksara.md) · concepts: [The five letters (pañcākṣara)](../concepts/pancaksara.md) · practices: [Repetition of the five-syllable mantra](../practices/pancaksara-japa.md), [Jñāna (knowledge of Śiva)](../practices/saiva-jnana.md)

### 10 <a id="tea-sivananabodham-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

As the Lord is one with the soul, so the soul becoming one with him, acting only as his servant, is no longer touched by mala, māyā and the two deeds.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge, devotion · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation_

terms: [iruviṉai](../terms/iruvinai.md), [mala](../terms/mala.md) · concepts: [The Siddhānta's 'advaita' of inseparability](../concepts/siddhanta-advaita.md)

### 11 <a id="tea-sivananabodham-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As the soul makes the eye see and itself sees, so Śiva makes the soul know and himself knows; by unforgetting love the soul reaches his feet.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: divine · path: devotion, knowledge · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation, consciousness-mind_

terms: [aṉpu](../terms/anpu.md) · concepts: [The Siddhānta's 'advaita' of inseparability](../concepts/siddhanta-advaita.md)

### 12 <a id="tea-sivananabodham-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Having washed away the three malas that hide the Lord's feet, the freed soul should join the true devotees and worship their holy garb and the temple as the Lord himself.
