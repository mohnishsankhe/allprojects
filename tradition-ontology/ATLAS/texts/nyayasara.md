# Nyāyasāra

**Structure:** 3 paricchedas (pratyakṣa, anumāna, āgama)
**Availability:** unknown

Bhāsarvajña's manual in three chapters (perception, inference, verbal testimony): it accepts only three means of knowledge, treats yogic perception, and holds — against the mainstream — that liberation is the absolute cessation of pain together with the experience of eternal bliss; its last part links the path to Śaiva yoga.
