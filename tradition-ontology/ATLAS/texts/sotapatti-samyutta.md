## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 55.5 <a id="tea-sotapatti-samyutta-55-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The factors for stream-entry are association with good persons, hearing the true Dhamma, wise attention and practice in accordance with the Dhamma; the stream is the noble eightfold path; one who possesses it is a stream-enterer.
