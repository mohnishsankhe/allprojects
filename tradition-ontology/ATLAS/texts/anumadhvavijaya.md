## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 18-19 <a id="tea-anumadhvavijaya-18-19"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Having expounded the bhāṣya and removed the stain of Kali, he installed at Rūpya (Uḍupi) the immovable image of Kṛṣṇa, carried in play though thirty youths could not move it, and went again to bow to Vyāsa; he crossed the Gaṅgā without a boat, won over the Yavana king by his own words, turned robbers on the road against one another, and at times by yoga became like a rock.
