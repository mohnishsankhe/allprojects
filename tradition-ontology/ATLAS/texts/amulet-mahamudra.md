## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### three-self-liberations <a id="tea-amulet-mahamudra-three-self-liberations"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Leave the mind uncontrived and it is self-liberated; whatever thoughts arise, look at them and they are self-liberated; the four faults (of deviation) are self-liberated; the fruit is the four kāyas, spontaneously present — ground, path and fruit held together like the two halves of an amulet box.
