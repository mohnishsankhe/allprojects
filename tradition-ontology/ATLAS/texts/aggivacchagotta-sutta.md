To Vacchagotta: the ten speculative views are a thicket the Tathāgata has abandoned; like an extinguished fire, the liberated one cannot be said to be reborn or not; he is deep like the ocean.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 3-15 <a id="tea-aggivacchagotta-sutta-3-15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Asked whether he holds any of the ten views, the Buddha says no: each is a thicket, a wilderness, a contortion, a vacillation, a fetter of views, beset by suffering, not leading to peace or nibbāna. The Tathāgata has put away speculative views; what he has seen is: such is form, its origin, its passing away … such is consciousness; therefore he is liberated through not clinging, with the destruction of all conceivings and 'I-making'.

### 16-18 <a id="tea-aggivacchagotta-sutta-16-18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Where is a monk with mind so liberated reborn? 'Reborn' does not apply, 'not reborn' does not apply, 'both' and 'neither' do not apply. Vaccha is bewildered; the Buddha: this Dhamma is deep, hard to see, peaceful, sublime, beyond reasoning, subtle, to be experienced by the wise.

### 19-20 <a id="tea-aggivacchagotta-sutta-19-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A fire burning with grass and sticks as fuel, when it goes out, cannot be said to have gone east, west, north or south; it is reckoned simply as 'extinguished'. So the form (and feeling, perception, formations, consciousness) by which one might describe the Tathāgata has been abandoned, cut off at the root; liberated from reckoning by form, he is deep, immeasurable, hard to fathom like the ocean.

### 20.1-20.9 <a id="tea-aggivacchagotta-sutta-20-1-20-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Liberated from reckoning in terms of form, the Tathāgata is deep, immeasurable, hard to fathom like the ocean; 'reborn', 'not reborn', 'both', 'neither' do not apply.

> “Evameva kho, vaccha, yena rūpena tathāgataṁ paññāpayamāno paññāpeyya taṁ rūpaṁ tathāgatassa pahīnaṁ ucchinnamūlaṁ tālāvatthukataṁ anabhāvaṅkataṁ āyatiṁ anuppādadhammaṁ. Rūpasaṅkhayavimutto kho, vaccha, tathāgato gambhīro appameyyo duppariyogāḷho— seyyathāpi mahāsamuddo. Upapajjatīti na upeti, na upapajjatīti na upeti, upapajjati ca na ca upapajjatīti na upeti, neva upapajjati na na upapajjatīti na upeti. Yāya vedanāya tathāgataṁ paññāpayamāno paññāpeyya sā vedanā tathāgatassa pahīnā ucchinnamūlā tālāvatthukatā anabhāvaṅkatā āyatiṁ anuppādadhammā. Vedanāsaṅkhayavimutto kho, vaccha, tathāgato gambhīro appameyyo duppariyogāḷho— seyyathāpi mahāsamuddo. Upapajjatīti na upeti, na upapajjatīti na upeti, upapajjati ca na ca upapajjatīti na upeti, neva upapajjati na na upapajjatīti na upeti. Yāya saññāya tathāgataṁ paññāpayamāno paññāpeyya sā saññā tathāgatassa pahīnā ucchinnamūlā tālāvatthukatā anabhāvaṅkatā āyatiṁ anuppādadhammā.
