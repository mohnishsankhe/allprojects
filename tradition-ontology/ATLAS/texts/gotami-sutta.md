## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1-10 <a id="tea-gotami-sutta-1-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī three times asks the Buddha at Kapilavatthu that women may go forth, and is refused; with shaven head and yellow robes she follows him to Vesālī with other Sakyan women; Ānanda intercedes, asking whether women who go forth can realise stream-entry up to arahantship — they can — and reminds the Buddha that she was his foster-mother; the Buddha grants going forth if she accepts eight principles of respect.

### 11-30 <a id="tea-gotami-sutta-11-30"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The eight principles of respect: a nun even a hundred years ordained bows to a monk ordained that day; she does not spend the rains where there is no monk; each fortnight she asks the monks for the uposatha and exhortation; she holds the invitation before both Saṅghas; for a serious offence she undergoes penance before both; a trainee is ordained by both Saṅghas after two years; a nun never abuses a monk; nuns may not admonish monks. If women had not gone forth the holy life would have lasted a thousand years; now it will last five hundred.
