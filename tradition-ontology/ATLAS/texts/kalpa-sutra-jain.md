**Availability:** digitized-original

Originally the eighth chapter of the Daśāśrutaskandha: (1) the Jinacaritra — the lives of Mahāvīra (in detail: descent, the fourteen dreams, the transfer of the embryo, birth, renunciation, twelve years of austerity, omniscience, rainy seasons, nirvāṇa), Pārśva, Ariṣṭanemi and Ṛṣabha, with the intervals between the other Jinas; (2) the Sthavirāvalī, the line of elders from Mahāvīra's gaṇadharas; (3) the Sāmācārī, rules for monks during the rainy season. Mūrtipūjakas read it aloud during Paryuṣaṇa; richly illustrated manuscripts survive from the 14th c. onward.
