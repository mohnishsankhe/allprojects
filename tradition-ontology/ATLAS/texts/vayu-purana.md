# Vāyu Purāṇa

**Alternate titles:** Vāyavīya Purāṇa, Vāyavya
**Original title:** वायुपुराण
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Genre:** purāṇa
**Authors:** 
  - [Vyāsa (Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana)](../teachers/vyasa.md) — role: compiler; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Proclaimed by Vāyu, the wind god, in the Śveta-kalpa, joined with the glory of Rudra (Matsya 53.18); several lists count it fourth in place of the Śiva Purāṇa (Matsya 53.18; Nārada 1.95).; scholarly: Regarded by most scholars as among the oldest Purāṇas; it shares a common core with the Brahmāṇḍa (the Kūrma list calls the eighteenth 'Vāyavīya ... named Brahmāṇḍa').; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Vyāsa, end of Dvāpara; Scholarly account: core c. 4th-5th c. CE (estimates vary); (confidence low)
**Structure:** four pādas (prakriyā, anuṣaṅga, upodghāta, upasaṃhāra); 24,000 verses by the traditional count (Matsya 53.18)
**Availability:** digitized-original

An early Śaiva-leaning Purāṇa of the classic type: creation, cosmography, time, manvantaras and genealogies, Pāśupata yoga with its obstacles and powers, śrāddha, and (in an appended portion) the glory of Gayā for the ancestors.
