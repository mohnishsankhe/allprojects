# Kaulajñānanirṇaya

`src:kaulajnananirnaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Jñānanirṇaya, Yoginīkaula

**Availability:** digitized-original

A dialogue of Bhairava and the Goddess in twenty-four chapters, 'brought down' by Macchaghnapāda (Matsyendra) from Candradvīpa as the Yoginīkaula. It teaches creation and dissolution within the body, the body-liṅga worshipped with inner flowers, the eleven centres of the body, nectar-meditations that conquer death and old age, the Kula and Akula, the eight-by-eight yoginīs, the qualifications of disciple, Śakti and hero, Kaula rites, and the story of how Bhairava as a fisherman recovered the scripture from a fish's belly.

### 2.4-5 <a id="tea-kaulajnananirnaya-2-4-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The seven nether worlds and the heavens above them — the fourteen worlds — are to be known as situated within the body in the form of principles (tattvas); all dissolves in the domain of Śakti.

### 2.6-7 <a id="tea-kaulajnananirnaya-2-6-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In dissolution Śakti goes into Śiva, and Śiva abides within action; action is absorbed in knowledge and dissolves through will; the power of will goes to dissolution where the supreme Śiva is the light. So dissolution is told by its stages.

### 3.2-5 <a id="tea-kaulajnananirnaya-3-2-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Kula aim: where the gaze is, there is the mind. Place, meditation, letter and aim are four: the place is called the body (piṇḍa), meditation the state (pada), the letter is form (rūpa), and the aim is beyond form (rūpātīta); each of these is fourfold in every place.

### 3.9-13 <a id="tea-kaulajnananirnaya-3-9-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Above the thousand-petalled and ten-million-petalled lotuses is the all-pervading, ever-risen, undivided, independent and spotless one; by its will creation arises and in it dissolution occurs — hence it is called the liṅga in which all that moves and stands dissolves. Not knowing it is bondage, knowing it one is released. It is beyond mind (unmana), free from meditation and concentration, always directly present, made of all letters yet devoid of letters, reached by knowledge and handed down in succession.

### 3.24-29 <a id="tea-kaulajnananirnaya-3-24-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Kaulika who desires success should worship this liṅga with mental flowers: the first flower is non-harming, the second restraint of the senses, the third compassion, the fourth feeling (bhāva), the fifth patience, the sixth conquest of anger, the seventh meditation, the eighth knowledge. Knowing this rule and worshipping the liṅga in the body, one gains enjoyment and liberation; this liṅga of success abiding in the body is the mark of the Kaula body-liṅga.

### 5.6-11 <a id="tea-kaulajnananirnaya-5-6-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The conquest of death: one who contemplates the white, cool, fragrant, radiant nectar that gladdens like the moon, coming from the middle of the sky and flowing through the many openings, soon conquers death; for him there is no old age, death or disease. The wise one should contemplate himself seated on a vast white lotus in the ocean of milk, covered above by a like lotus and sprinkled with its drops; he is freed from diseases and the bonds of old age and death and takes whatever form he wishes.

### 5.31-33 <a id="tea-kaulajnananirnaya-5-31-33"></a>

### 6.4-7 <a id="tea-kaulajnananirnaya-6-4-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The jīva is the supreme, partless, eternal, free from disease and spotless; it is called the lord (nātha), the all-pervading supreme Śiva; that jīva is the haṃsa, the 'person' of Śakti; it is mind, breath, intellect and thought, the air that fills all beings. As long as it abides in the body it is called jīva; as soon as the body is left it is called the supreme Śiva.

> शृणु त्वं वीरचामुण्डे वरं जीवस्य निर्णयम् । स(ः)परं निष्कलं नित्यं निरामयनिरञ्जनम् परमाणुमुच्यते नाथो स शिवो व्यापकः परः । स(ः) जीवः परतरो यस्तु स(ः) हंसः शक्तिपुद्गल(ः) स(ः) मनो मत्परं प्राणः स(ः) बुद्धिश्चित्तमेव च । समीरपूरको वायुः सर्वजीवेषु संस्थितम्(ः) देहस्थस्तिष्ठते यावत्तावज्जीवोऽपि गीयते । स देहत्यक्तमात्रेण परं शिवो निगद्यते

### 7.20-24 <a id="tea-kaulajnananirnaya-7-20-24"></a>
