# Pramāṇasamuccaya

`src:pramanasamuccaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Compendium of Valid Cognition, tshad ma kun las btus pa
**Original title:** प्रमाणसमुच्चय
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
**Genre:** kārikā with autocommentary
**Authors:** 
  - [Dignāga](../teachers/dignaga.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 500–540 CE; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 6 chapters: 1 perception, 2 inference for oneself, 3 inference for others, 4 examples (dṛṣṭānta), 5 exclusion (anyāpoha), 6 futile rejoinders (jāti)
**Availability:** partly-lost

Dignāga's culminating work, which founded the Buddhist pramāṇa school: two means of knowledge because two kinds of object; perception free of conceptual construction; reflexive awareness as the result; the triple character of the reason; the meaning of words as exclusion of others; and criticism of the Nyāya, Vaiśeṣika, Sāṃkhya and Mīmāṃsā accounts.
