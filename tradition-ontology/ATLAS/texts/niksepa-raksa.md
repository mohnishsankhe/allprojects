## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### opening <a id="tea-niksepa-raksa-opening"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Prapatti (nikṣepa) is taught by scripture — the Upaniṣads, Itihāsas, Purāṇas and Pāñcarātra — as an independent means to liberation, and the objections that it is not a means, or not a proper Vedic practice, are answered.
