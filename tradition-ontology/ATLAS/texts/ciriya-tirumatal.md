Tirumaṅkai Āḻvār's shorter maṭal poem: Parakāla-nāyakī, spurned by the Lord, threatens to 'ride the maṭal' (the palmyra-stem horse) through the streets to shame him — a threat that Tamil convention allowed only to men.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-ciriya-tirumatal-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Spurned by the Lord, the heroine declares she will ride the maṭal — the palmyra-stem horse of the jilted lover — through the streets, proclaiming his cruelty, though Tamil custom forbids a woman to do so.
