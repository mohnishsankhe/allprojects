The first 'secret' appended to the Devī Māhātmya: Medhas tells the king that the supreme Mahālakṣmī, possessing the three qualities, is the origin of all; from her arise Mahākālī and Mahāsarasvatī, and from the three arise the divine couples who create, preserve and destroy.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-12 <a id="tea-pradhanika-rahasya-1-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Medhas to the king: the supreme Mahālakṣmī, possessing the three qualities, the first of all, pervades the manifest and unmanifest; from her tamas she brought forth Mahākālī and from her sattva Mahāsarasvatī; from the three arose three divine pairs, and Mahālakṣmī gave each god his consort, so that the world is created, preserved and destroyed.
