# Bhikkhunī Saṃyutta

`src:bhikkhuni-samyutta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Genre:** saṃyutta
**Part of:** [Saṃyutta Nikāya](samyutta-nikaya.md)
**Location in parent:** SN 5
**Availability:** digitized-original

Ten verse dialogues in which Māra challenges nuns (Āḷavikā, Somā, Kisāgotamī, Vijayā, Uppalavaṇṇā, Cālā, Upacālā, Sīsupacālā, Selā, Vajirā), and each refutes him — including Somā on women's capacity for wisdom and Vajirā on the 'being' as a convention like 'chariot'.
