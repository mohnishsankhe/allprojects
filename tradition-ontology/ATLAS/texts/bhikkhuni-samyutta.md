## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 5.2 <a id="tea-bhikkhuni-samyutta-5-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Māra taunts the nun Somā that the state of the seers cannot be reached by a woman with 'two-finger wisdom'; she answers: what does womanhood matter when the mind is well concentrated and knowledge is present, for one who rightly sees into the Dhamma? One who thinks 'I am a woman' or 'I am a man' is fit for Māra to address.

### 5.10 <a id="tea-bhikkhuni-samyutta-5-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Māra asks the nun Vajirā who made this being; she answers: why do you assume 'a being'? This is a heap of mere formations; no being is found here. As 'chariot' is spoken of when the parts are assembled, so 'a being' is a convention when the aggregates are present; only suffering comes to be and passes away.

### 5.10/2 <a id="tea-bhikkhuni-samyutta-5-10-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As 'chariot' is spoken of when its parts are assembled, so the convention 'a being' is used when the aggregates are present.
