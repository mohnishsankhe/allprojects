Punjabi verse romance of Pūraṇ Bhagat, son of Rājā Salvāhan of Sialkot, maimed at his stepmother Lūṇā's false accusation, restored by Gorakhnāth and made a yogī — identified in Nāth tradition with Cauraṅgīnāth.

_Notes: Best-known version by Qādaryār (19th c., recalled). Context for the Cauraṅgī legend._
