## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-astakaprakarana-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The one in whom there is no attachment giving rise to affliction, no hatred burning beings, no delusion covering right knowledge — he is called Mahādeva, whether [named] Brahmā, Viṣṇu or Śiva.
