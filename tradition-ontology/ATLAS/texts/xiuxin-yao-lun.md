## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 377b17 <a id="tea-xiuxin-yao-lun-377b17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Asked whether guarding one's own mind is better than recollecting another buddha: constant recollection of that buddha does not escape birth and death; guarding one's own original mind reaches the other shore; 'guarding the original true mind surpasses recollecting other buddhas'.

> 常念彼佛不免生死。守我本心則到彼岸

_level: bridging · standpoint: polemical · path: meditation, devotion · stage: all · types: practice_

### 377c03 <a id="tea-xiuxin-yao-lun-377c03"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Guarding the original true mind, deluded thoughts do not arise, the sense of 'mine' ceases, and one is naturally equal to the buddha; guarding the mind is the root of nirvāṇa, the essential gate for entering the Way, the tenet of the twelve divisions of scripture and the ancestor of the buddhas of the three times.

> 守本真心妄念不生。我所心滅自然與佛平等無二
