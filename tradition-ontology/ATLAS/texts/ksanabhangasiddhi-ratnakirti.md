## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-ksanabhangasiddhi-ratnakirti-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Whatever exists is momentary, like a pot; and these disputed things exist. The pervasion is shown positively: an existent must be causally efficacious; an enduring thing could produce its effects neither successively nor simultaneously; therefore existence is pervaded by momentariness.

> yat sat tat kṣaṇikaṃ yathā ghaṭaḥ santaścāmī vivādāspadībhūtāḥ padārthā iti
