**Availability:** digitized-original

The root text of Tibetan medicine, in four tantras and 156 chapters, framed as a dialogue in which the sage Rigpé Yeshe (the Medicine Buddha's mind-emanation) answers Yilé Kyé: the Root Tantra gives the whole system as three trees; the Explanatory Tantra covers embryology, anatomy, physiology, the causes and classes of disease, conduct, diet, pharmacology, diagnosis, treatment principles and the physician; the Oral Instruction Tantra treats the diseases one by one; the Subsequent Tantra gives pulse and urine diagnosis, the preparation of medicines and the evacuative and external therapies.
