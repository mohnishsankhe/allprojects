The complete classical commentary on the Ṛgveda, produced at Vijayanagara under Sāyaṇa's direction: it glosses every word, explains each hymn chiefly by its use in the sacrifice (yajña), draws on Yāska, the Brāhmaṇas and earlier commentators, and in its introduction defines the Veda and defends its authority. Most traditional and modern readings of the Ṛgveda start from it.
