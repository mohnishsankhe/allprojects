The first Aṅga, on the conduct (ācāra) of the mendicant. Book I (Bambhacerāiṃ, 'the discipline of chastity'; nine lectures, the seventh, Mahāparijñā, lost) teaches non-harm to the six classes of living beings (earth, water, fire, plants, mobile beings, wind), detachment and vigilance, and ends with the Upadhānaśruta, the account of Mahāvīra's austerities. Book II (four cūlās) gives detailed rules on alms, lodging, walking, speech, clothing and bowls, and a life of Mahāvīra with the twenty-five bhāvanās of the five great vows.
