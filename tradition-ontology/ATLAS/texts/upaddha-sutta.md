## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-3 <a id="tea-upaddha-sutta-2-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Not so, Ānanda! Good friendship, good companionship, good comradeship is the whole of the holy life.' A monk with a good friend can be expected to develop the noble eightfold path; by relying on the Buddha as good friend, beings subject to birth, ageing and death are freed from them.
