# Ātmatattvaviveka

**Alternate titles:** Bauddhādhikkāra
**Original title:** आत्मतत्त्वविवेक
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
**Genre:** prakaraṇa
**Authors:** 
  - [Udayana](../teachers/udayana.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
**Dates:** Scholarly account: late 10th – 11th c. CE; (confidence low)
**Availability:** unknown

Udayana's defence of an enduring self against Buddhist positions, organized as refutations of momentariness (kṣaṇabhaṅga), of the denial of external objects (bāhyārthabhaṅga), of the denial of the difference between quality and qualified (guṇaguṇibhedabhaṅga), and of the argument from the non-apprehension of a self (anupalambha); it closes with the knowledge of the self and liberation.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Ātmatattvavivekadīdhiti](atmatattvaviveka-didhiti.md)
