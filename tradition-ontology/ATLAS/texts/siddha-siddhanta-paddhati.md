# Siddhasiddhāntapaddhati

**Structure:** 6 upadeśas

The principal doctrinal treatise of the Nāth tradition, ascribed to Gorakṣanātha, in six upadeśas: the origin of the body (piṇḍotpatti) from the Supreme through its own power; the knowledge of the body (nine centres, sixteen supports, three aims, five voids); the vision of the cosmos in the body (piṇḍa-saṃvitti); the support of the body (Śakti); the samarasa of the body with the supreme state (pada); and the marks of the avadhūta yogī.
**Editions / translations:**

  - kind: translation; name: M. L. Gharote and G. K. Pai (Lonavla: Lonavla Yoga Institute, 2005)
**Commentaries on this text:** [Siddhasiddhāntasaṅgraha](siddhasiddhanta-sangraha.md)

## Teachings (6: skeleton 6)

### 1 <a id="tea-siddha-siddhanta-paddhati-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The origin of the body (piṇḍotpatti): from the nameless supreme its own power (nijā śakti) comes forth, and from it the powers Parā, Aparā, Sūkṣmā and Kuṇḍalinī; through them the supreme and cosmic bodies arise, and finally the human body born of the womb.

### 2 <a id="tea-siddha-siddhanta-paddhati-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The knowledge of the body (piṇḍa-vicāra): the nine centres, the sixteen supports, the three aims and the five voids are set out; one who does not rightly know them is a yogī in name only.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: body-breath, meditation · stage: intermediate · types: body-layers_

### 3 <a id="tea-siddha-siddhanta-paddhati-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The vision of the cosmos in the body (piṇḍa-saṃvitti): the worlds, mountains, rivers, gods and beings are all located within the body, so that the one who knows the body knows the universe.

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: all · types: body-layers, world-fate_

terms: [piṇḍa-saṃvitti](../terms/pinda-samvitti.md), [brahmāṇḍa](../terms/brahmanda.md) · concepts: [The body as microcosm (piṇḍa and brahmāṇḍa)](../concepts/pinda-brahmanda.md)

### 4 <a id="tea-siddha-siddhanta-paddhati-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The support of the body (piṇḍādhāra): the one Śakti — the Lord's own power, called kuṇḍalinī in the body — upholds the body and the cosmos; Śiva and Śakti are not two.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: substance · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate, body-layers_

concepts: [Śiva and Śakti inseparable](../concepts/siva-sakti-inseparable.md), [Kuṇḍalinī](../concepts/kundalini.md)

### 5 <a id="tea-siddha-siddhanta-paddhati-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The union of body and supreme state (piṇḍa-pada-samarasa): by the grace of the true guru the body and the supreme state (pada) come to one taste (samarasa); this is attained not by study or reasoning but by the guru.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: experiential · path: knowledge, devotion · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation, teacher-transmission_

terms: [sāmarasya](../terms/samarasya.md), [pada](../terms/pada.md) · concepts: [Equal taste (samarasa)](../concepts/samarasa.md), [The guru in the Nāth tradition](../concepts/natha-guru.md)

### 6 <a id="tea-siddha-siddhanta-paddhati-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The marks of the avadhūta yogī: he has shaken off all conventions and bonds, abides in the supreme state, and stands above the other schools and their marks; the Nāth path is presented as including and surpassing them.
