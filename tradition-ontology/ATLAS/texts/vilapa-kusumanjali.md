### text <a id="tea-vilapa-kusumanjali-text"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

As a maidservant (mañjarī) of Rādhā, Raghunātha Dāsa laments his separation and prays for particular services to her in the divine play, seeking no direct union with Kṛṣṇa but only service of the couple under Rādhā.
