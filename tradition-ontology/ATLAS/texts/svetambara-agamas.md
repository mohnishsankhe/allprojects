# Śvetāmbara Āgamas (the Jain canon)

**Alternate titles:** Siddhānta, Āgama, Gaṇipiṭaka, Duvālasaṃga (for the Aṅgas)
**Language:** Ardhamāgadhī Prakrit, Jaina Māhārāṣṭrī Prakrit (some texts)
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md), [Sthānakavāsī](../lineages/sthanakavasi.md), [Śvetāmbara Terāpanth](../lineages/terapanthi.md)
**Genre:** canon (āgama / siddhānta)
**Attribution:** tradition: The Aṅgas go back to the gaṇadharas' composition of Mahāvīra's teaching; the aṅgabāhya texts to later elders (sthaviras); recited at the councils of Pāṭaliputra, Mathurā and Valabhī and written down at Valabhī under Devarddhigaṇi (980 or 993 years after the nirvāṇa).; scholarly: Compiled from materials of very different ages (c. 4th c. CE); the grouping into Upāṅgas, Chedas, Mūlas and Prakīrṇakas and the count of 45 are medieval (the Nandī classifies differently: aṅga-praviṣṭa / aṅga-bāhya, kālika / utkālika).; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: taught by Mahāvīra (d. 527 BCE by the traditional reckoning); written down at Valabhī 453 or 466 CE; Scholarly account: oldest layers c. 4th–3rd c. BCE; closed in the 5th c. CE; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 45 texts (Mūrtipūjaka) or 32 (Sthānakavāsī, Terāpanthī); see cpt:svetambara-canon-45 and cpt:sthanakavasi-canon-32
**Availability:** digitized-original

The canonical scriptures of the Śvetāmbara Jains: the twelve-limbed 'basket of the gaṇin' (duvālasaṅga gaṇipiḍaga) of which eleven Aṅgas survive, together with the 'outside the limbs' (aṅgabāhya) texts. Mūrtipūjakas count forty-five texts (11 Aṅgas, 12 Upāṅgas, 10 Prakīrṇakas, 6 Chedasūtras, 4 Mūlasūtras, 2 Cūlikāsūtras); Sthānakavāsīs and Terāpanthīs accept thirty-two. The Digambaras hold that the original Aṅgas were lost and do not accept this collection.
