# Taṇḍulavaicārika

`src:tandulavaicarika` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Taṃdulaveyāliya
**Language:** Jaina Māhārāṣṭrī Prakrit
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md)
**Genre:** prakīrṇaka (body, detachment)
**Part of:** [Śvetāmbara Āgamas (the Jain canon)](svetambara-agamas.md)
**Location in parent:** Prakīrṇaka
**Availability:** digitized-original

A Prakīrṇaka on the human body: conception and development in the womb, the ten decades of a hundred-year life, the impurity of the body, and the amount of rice (taṇḍula) a person eats in a lifetime — as grounds for detachment.
