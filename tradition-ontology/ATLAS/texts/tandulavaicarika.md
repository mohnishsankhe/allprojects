## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### whole <a id="tea-tandulavaicarika-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Describes the formation of the body in the womb month by month, the ten decades of a hundred-year life from childhood to decrepitude, the impure contents of the body, and the vast quantity of rice eaten in a lifetime, urging the reader to use the rare human birth for dharma.
