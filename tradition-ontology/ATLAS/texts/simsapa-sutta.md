## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-4 <a id="tea-simsapa-sutta-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The leaves in the Buddha's hand are few compared with those in the siṃsapā grove; so what he has directly known but not taught is much more, because it is unbeneficial and does not lead to nibbāna; what he has taught is the four noble truths, because they are beneficial.
