### 1 <a id="tea-abhidhammatthasangaha-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The things taught in the Abhidhamma are, in the ultimate sense, fourfold: consciousness, mental factors, matter and nibbāna.

Consciousness is classified by plane: fifty-four types of the sense sphere, fifteen of the fine-material sphere, twelve of the immaterial sphere and eight supramundane — eighty-nine; or one hundred and twenty-one when each supramundane path and fruit is counted with the five jhānas.

### 1 <a id="tea-abhidhammatthasangaha-1-3"></a>

The twelve unwholesome types: eight rooted in greed (accompanied by joy or equanimity, with or without wrong view, unprompted or prompted), two rooted in hatred (with displeasure and aversion, unprompted or prompted), two rooted in delusion (with doubt, with restlessness).

### 4 <a id="tea-abhidhammatthasangaha-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A five-door cognitive process for a very great object runs through seventeen mind-moments: past life-continuum, vibration and arrest of the life-continuum, five-door adverting, sense consciousness, receiving, investigating, determining, seven impulsions and two registrations, after which the mind lapses into the life-continuum.

`skeleton` · confidence high

In the absorption process, after mind-door adverting, impulsions of preparation, access, conformity and change-of-lineage are followed by absorption; in the path process the path moment is followed by two or three moments of fruition.

### 4 <a id="tea-abhidhammatthasangaha-4-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Impulsion is where kamma is made; at the time of death impulsion runs only five moments, and in arahants the impulsions are functional, producing no result.

### 5 <a id="tea-abhidhammatthasangaha-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There are thirty-one planes in four groups: the four woeful planes, the seven happy sense-sphere planes (human and six heavens), sixteen fine-material planes and four immaterial planes.

### 5 <a id="tea-abhidhammatthasangaha-5-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Kamma is classified fourfold by function (productive, supportive, obstructive, destructive), by order of ripening (weighty, death-proximate, habitual, reserve), by time of ripening (in this life, in the next, in some later life, defunct) and by place of ripening (unwholesome, sense-sphere wholesome, fine-material wholesome, immaterial wholesome).

_level: conventional · standpoint: causal · path: action · stage: all · types: karma-liberation_

### 5 <a id="tea-abhidhammatthasangaha-5-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Death comes through the expiry of the life-span, of the productive kamma, of both, or through a destructive kamma; to the dying a kamma, a sign of kamma or a sign of destiny appears, and the rebirth-linking consciousness of the next life takes the same object as the last process of this life.

_level: conventional · standpoint: causal · path: general · stage: all · types: death-dying, karma-liberation_

### 5 <a id="tea-abhidhammatthasangaha-5-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Rebirth-linking is fourfold — into the woeful planes, the happy sense-sphere planes, the fine-material planes and the immaterial planes — and the same type of resultant consciousness serves as rebirth-linking, life-continuum and death consciousness within one existence.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: karma-liberation, consciousness-mind_

### 6 <a id="tea-abhidhammatthasangaha-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Matter is of twenty-eight kinds: eighteen concretely produced (the four great elements, five sensitive organs, four objective fields, two sex faculties, the heart-base, the life faculty and nutriment) and ten non-concretely produced (space, two intimations, three mutabilities, four characteristics).

`skeleton` · confidence high

Matter originates in four ways — from kamma, consciousness, temperature and nutriment — and occurs in material groups (kalāpa) that arise and cease together; a material phenomenon lasts seventeen mind-moments.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: body-layers_

### 6 <a id="tea-abhidhammatthasangaha-6-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Nibbāna is supramundane, realized by the knowledge of the four paths, the object of the paths and fruits; it is one by its intrinsic nature, twofold as with and without residue remaining, and threefold as the void, the signless and the desireless; the great sages call it deathless, unconditioned, unsurpassed.

The compendium of categories groups the unwholesome (taints, floods, bonds, bodily ties, clingings, hindrances, latent tendencies, fetters by the sutta and Abhidhamma lists, defilements), the mixed categories (roots, jhāna factors, path factors, faculties, powers, predominants, nutriments), the thirty-seven requisites of awakening, and the whole (aggregates, bases, elements, truths).

Conditionality is expounded by two methods: the method of dependent origination and the method of the Paṭṭhāna; all twenty-four conditions are included in four — object, decisive support, kamma and presence.

Concepts (paññatti) are twofold: concept as meaning, which is made known, and concept as name, which makes known; names are of six kinds, such as a concept of the real, of the unreal, of the unreal by means of the real, and so on.

Calm is developed on the forty subjects, suited to the six temperaments, through three stages of development (preliminary, access, absorption) and three signs (preliminary, learning, counterpart); from the fifth jhāna the direct knowledges can be developed.
