The sixth Aṅga, teaching by parables and stories: Prince Megha's past life as an elephant, the two tortoises, the four daughters-in-law and the rice grains, the princess Mallī who became the nineteenth Tīrthaṅkara (a woman, in Śvetāmbara belief), Draupadī's story, and others; the second book tells of goddesses who worshipped Mahāvīra.
