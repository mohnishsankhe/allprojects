**Availability:** digitized-original

The songs ascribed to the renouncer Paṭṭiṉattār (Tiruveṇkāṭar): the Tiruvēkampamālai to Śiva at Kāñcī, the Tillai verses, three Kōyil tiruvakavals, the Kacci tiruvakaval and the Aruṭpulampal, with further 'general' songs and the songs at his mother's cremation. Themes: impermanence, the body as a pot of filth, renunciation, women as snare, karma, and refuge in Śiva's feet.
