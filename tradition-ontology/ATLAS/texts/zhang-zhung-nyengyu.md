### outer-cycle <a id="tea-zhang-zhung-nyengyu-outer-cycle"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The general view: the base of all (kun gzhi) is empty and clear; awareness (rig pa) is inseparable from it; their inseparability is the natural state, uncreated and without deviation.

### secret-cycle <a id="tea-zhang-zhung-nyengyu-secret-cycle"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Seeing awareness nakedly: the master introduces the disciple to naked awareness beyond the conceptual mind, likened to the sky, the sun and its rays; one then rests in it without correction.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice_

concepts: [The base of all and awareness in Bön Dzogchen](../concepts/bon-kunzhi-and-rigpa.md) · practices: [Direct introduction to awareness (ngo sprod)](../practices/direct-introduction.md)

### tapihritsa <a id="tea-zhang-zhung-nyengyu-tapihritsa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Tapihritsa, appearing as a boy, questions the learned Gyerpung Nangzher Löpo and shows him that his pride in scholarship and powers obstructs the natural state, then transmits the teaching that became the Oral Transmission.
