A verse treatise ascribed to Nāgārjuna explaining the awakening mind in its ultimate aspect as emptiness free of the self of persons and phenomena and in its relative aspect as compassion, criticizing the Sāṃkhya, Vaiśeṣika, Abhidharma and Yogācāra views.
