## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 51.20 <a id="tea-iddhipada-samyutta-51-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The four bases of power — concentration due to desire, to energy, to mind and to investigation, with formations of striving — are developed without being too slack or too tense, neither constricted inwardly nor scattered outwardly, perceiving before and after, below and above, by night as by day, with an open mind that develops a luminous mind; so developed, they yield the powers and the destruction of the effluents.
