## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-bhairava-padmavati-kalpa-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _restricted: summary only_

The practitioner of these mantras must be devoted to the Jina and to his teacher, truthful, self-controlled, free of anger and deceit, courageous and discreet; the mantras given without these qualities do not bear fruit.
