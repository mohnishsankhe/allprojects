The body of instructions on inner heat (gtum mo), illusory body (sgyu lus), dream (rmi lam), clear light ('od gsal), the intermediate state (bar do) and transference ('pho ba) — with 'entering another's body' (grong 'jug) and the consort practice in some lists — that Tilopa gathered from four Indian lineages and Nāropa passed to Marpa; the Kagyu 'path of method', on which every Kagyu school has its manuals (Gampopa, Phagmodrupa, Rangjung Dorje, Pema Karpo, Tsongkhapa's Gelug commentary).
