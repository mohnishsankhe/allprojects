The householder's discipline: the six directions re-interpreted as parents, teachers, spouse, friends, workers and ascetics, with mutual duties; the four defilements of action, four biases, six drains on wealth, and true and false friends.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 3-6 <a id="tea-sigalovada-sutta-3-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The noble disciple gives up the four defilements of action (killing, stealing, sexual misconduct, lying), does no evil from the four biases (desire, hatred, delusion, fear), and avoids the six drains on wealth.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: action · stage: beginner · types: ethics_

terms: [agati](../terms/agati.md) · concepts: [Lay ethics](../concepts/lay-ethics.md) · obstacles: [The four biases (agati)](../obstacles/four-agati.md)

### 7-14 <a id="tea-sigalovada-sutta-7-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The six drains on wealth — drink, roaming the streets at night, festivals, gambling, bad friends, laziness — each with six dangers.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: action · stage: beginner · types: ethics_

concepts: [Lay ethics](../concepts/lay-ethics.md)

### 15-26 <a id="tea-sigalovada-sutta-15-26"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

False friends (the taker, the talker, the flatterer, the companion in waste) and good-hearted friends (the helper, the one who is the same in good times and bad, the one who points to what is good, the one who sympathises).

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: action · stage: beginner · types: ethics_

concepts: [Good friendship](../concepts/kalyanamittata.md)

### 27-34 <a id="tea-sigalovada-sutta-27-34"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The six directions are to be honoured as parents (east), teachers (south), wife and children (west), friends (north), workers and servants (below) and ascetics and brahmins (above), each with five duties on each side.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: action · stage: beginner · types: ethics_
