The 'Great Drum' sūtra: the permanence of the Tathāgata and of nirvāṇa and the one vehicle, proclaimed like a great drum; it prophesies a future preacher of the teaching.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-mahabheriharaka-sutra-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Like the sound of a great drum, the Buddha proclaims that the Tathāgata is permanent and that there is one vehicle; nirvāṇa is not nothingness.
