# Rauravāgama

`src:raurava-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Raurava
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Brāhmaṇeśa, then Nandikeśa (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Rudrabheda no. 16 in the Kāmika's order; 6 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** unknown

No. 16 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Rudrabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Brāhmaṇeśa, then Nandikeśa. The Kāmika assigns it 6 upabheda(s): Kālaghna, Kalātīta, Raurava, Rauravottara, Mahākālamata, Aindra. The first volume of Bhatt's edition includes the Rauravasūtrasaṅgraha (src:rauravasutrasangraha).
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Rauravāgama, ed. N. R. Bhatt, Institut Français d'Indologie (3 vols, 1961, 1972, 1988)

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.17/10.25: Brahmaṇeśa → Nandikeśvara. Śrīkaṇṭhīya: 'Raurava'. In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the ears (pūrva 1.93–101).
