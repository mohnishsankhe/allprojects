## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### prayojana-section <a id="tea-priti-sandarbha-prayojana-section"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Love for the Lord (prīti) is the supreme goal of human life, surpassing liberation; liberation without love is not sought by devotees, and prīti reaches its summit in the gopīs of Vraja.
