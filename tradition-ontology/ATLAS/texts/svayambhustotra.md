Samantabhadra's hymn to the twenty-four Tīrthaṅkaras (Bṛhat-svayambhūstotra), rich in doctrinal statements; in the Naminātha hymn non-violence is called the supreme Brahman known in the world. Tradition connects its recitation with the appearance of the image of Candraprabha at Vārāṇasī.
