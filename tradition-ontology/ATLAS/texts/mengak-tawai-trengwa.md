A short doxography attributed to Padmasambhava, commenting on chapter 13 of the Guhyagarbha: it ranks the views of worldly people, non-Buddhists, the vehicles of śrāvakas, pratyekabuddhas and bodhisattvas, the outer tantras (kriyā, ubhaya, yoga) and the inner (Mahāyoga's generation, Anuyoga's completion, Atiyoga's Great Perfection).

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### atiyoga <a id="tea-mengak-tawai-trengwa-atiyoga"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

In the Great Perfection all phenomena of saṃsāra and nirvāṇa are realized to have been enlightened from the beginning as the nature of the maṇḍala of body, speech and mind, so that there is nothing to be sought or accomplished; this realization, not a meditation that produces it, is the view of Atiyoga.

### views <a id="tea-mengak-tawai-trengwa-views"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Garland of Views ranks views from the lowest up: worldly people without a view of karma, non-Buddhist eternalists and nihilists, śrāvakas, pratyekabuddhas and bodhisattvas; then the outer tantras (kriyā, ubhaya, yoga); then the inner: Mahāyoga's generation, Anuyoga's completion and Atiyoga's Great Perfection — each higher view including what the lower sought.
