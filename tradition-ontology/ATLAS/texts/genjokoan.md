When all dharmas are buddha-dharma there is delusion and awakening; carrying the self forward to verify the myriad things is delusion, the myriad things advancing and verifying the self is awakening; to study the buddha-way is to study the self, to study the self is to forget the self, to forget the self is to be verified by the myriad dharmas; firewood does not become ash; awakening is like the moon reflected in water; the fan of Mayu Baoche — the wind-nature is everywhere, yet one fans.

### fan <a id="tea-genjokoan-fan"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Mayu Baoche was fanning himself; a monk asked why, since the nature of wind is permanent and reaches everywhere; Baoche kept fanning. The realization of the buddha-dharma, its vital path, is like this: because the wind-nature is everywhere one fans.

### firewood-and-ash <a id="tea-genjokoan-firewood-and-ash"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Firewood becomes ash and cannot become firewood again; yet do not view ash as after and firewood as before: firewood abides in its dharma-position with before and after, cut off from them; so birth does not become death — this is called 'no-birth', and death does not become birth — 'no-death'.

### moon-in-water <a id="tea-genjokoan-moon-in-water"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Attaining awakening is like the moon reflected in water: the moon does not get wet and the water is not broken; the whole moon and sky are reflected in a dewdrop on a blade of grass.

### opening <a id="tea-genjokoan-opening"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When all dharmas are the buddha-dharma, there are delusion and awakening, practice, birth and death, buddhas and beings; when the myriad dharmas are without self, there is no delusion, no awakening, no buddhas, no beings; because the buddha-way leaps beyond abundance and lack, there are birth and death, delusion and awakening; yet flowers fall amid our longing and weeds flourish amid our dislike.

### to-study-the-self <a id="tea-genjokoan-to-study-the-self"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

To carry the self forward and verify the myriad things is delusion; that the myriad things come forward and verify the self is awakening. To study the buddha-way is to study the self; to study the self is to forget the self; to forget the self is to be verified by the myriad dharmas; to be verified by the myriad dharmas is to let the body and mind of oneself and others drop off; no trace of awakening remains, and this traceless awakening continues endlessly.
