About 1,450 stanzas in some fifty-six sections (Paraparakkaṇṇi, Āṉantakkaḷippu, Tējōmayāṉantam, Ciṉmayāṉantakuru, Mauṉakuru vaṇakkam, Cittarkaṇam and others) praising the all-pervading Supreme (Paraparam), the silent guru and stillness (cummā iruttal); they join Vedānta and Śaiva Siddhānta in a 'harmony' (camaracam), warn that stilling the mind is harder than any power, and pray that all beings be happy.
