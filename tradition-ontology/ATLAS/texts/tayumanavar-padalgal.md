## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 1.1 (aṅkiṅkeṉātapaṭi) <a id="tea-tayumanavar-padalgal-ankinkenatapati"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

That which, without being here or there, is everywhere as light, the fullness of bliss, filled with grace, holding all the worlds as its life — that is what we contemplate.

### cittarkaṇam (vētānta cittānta camaracam) <a id="tea-tayumanavar-padalgal-camaracam"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Praise to the host of skilled Siddhas who have attained the good state of harmony (camaracam) of Vedānta and Siddhānta.

### kantuk kaṭaṅkā (Tējōmayāṉantam) <a id="tea-tayumanavar-padalgal-kantuk-katankata"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

One may tame a rutting elephant, bind the mouth of bear and tiger, ride a lion, play with a cobra, turn metals to gold, walk unseen, command the gods, stay ever young, enter another's body, walk on water and sit in fire, and win every peerless power — but the skill of stilling the mind and simply being (cummā iruttal) is rare.

### passim (cummā iru) <a id="tea-tayumanavar-padalgal-cumma-iru"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The guru's word 'be still' (cummā iru) is the whole teaching: in stillness, without the mind's movement, is bliss.
