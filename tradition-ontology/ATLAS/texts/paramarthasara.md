# Paramārthasāra

`src:paramarthasara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Original title:** परमार्थसार
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md), [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md)
**Genre:** prakaraṇa (verse)
**Authors:** 
  - [Abhinavagupta](../teachers/abhinavagupta.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
**Structure:** 105 āryās
**Availability:** digitized-original

Abhinavagupta's 105 āryā verses retelling, 'by the view of Śiva's teaching' (v. 3), the older Ādhārakārikās / Paramārthasāra ascribed to Ādiśeṣa: the four eggs of manifestation, the Lord as the embodied enjoyer, the mirror image of the 36 tattvas, the kañcukas and the three coverings, bondage and liberation as Śiva's play, liberation as the manifestation of one's own power, the conduct and inner worship of the liberated, and the knower's death.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: with Yogarāja's Vivṛti, Kashmir Series of Texts and Studies (KSTS), Srinagar 7 (1916)
