# Mantra Brāhmaṇa (Chāndogya Brāhmaṇa)

`src:mantra-brahmana` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Chāndogya Brāhmaṇa, Upaniṣad Brāhmaṇa
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Genre:** brāhmaṇa (gṛhya mantras)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The Kauthuma Sāmaveda collection of mantras for the domestic rites — marriage, conception, birth and the like — whose continuation (prapāṭhakas 3–10 of the 'Chāndogya Brāhmaṇa') is the Chāndogya Upaniṣad.
