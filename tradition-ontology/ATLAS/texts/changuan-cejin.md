## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1100b02 <a id="tea-changuan-cejin-1100b02"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Xueyan Zuqin recounts that a senior told him his practice was stagnant water: 'in practising Chan one must raise the feeling of doubt; small doubt, small awakening; great doubt, great awakening'; he changed his critical phrase to 'dried shit-stick' and struggled against dullness and scattering.

> 參禪須是起疑情。小疑小悟。大疑大悟
