# Patiṉoṉṟām Tirumuṟai (the eleventh book)

`src:patinonram-tirumurai` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Pirapantamālai
**Language:** Tamil
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Genre:** collection
**Part of:** [Tirumuṟai](tirumurai.md)
**Location in parent:** Tirumuṟai 11
**Authors:** 
  - [Nampi Āṇṭār Nampi](../teachers/nampi-antar-nampi.md) — role: compiler; attribution: traditional
**Structure:** 40 works by 12 authors (moderate confidence)

Forty works by twelve poets: Kāraikkāl Ammaiyār's three poems, Cēramāṉ Perumāḷ's three, Aiyaṭikaḷ Kāṭavarkōṉ's Kṣēttirat Tiruveṇpā, Nakkīrar's Tirumurukāṟṟuppaṭai and other poems, Kallāṭar, Kapilar, Paraṇar, Iḷampperumāṉ Aṭikaḷ, Atiravāciyaṭikaḷ, Paṭṭiṉattup Piḷḷaiyār's five poems, and Nampi Āṇṭār Nampi's ten works including the Tiruttoṇṭar Tiruvantāti.

_Notes: Paṭṭiṉattup Piḷḷaiyār of this book is often distinguished by scholars from the later Siddhar Paṭṭiṉattār (tch:pattinattar, U22); the tradition often identifies them._
