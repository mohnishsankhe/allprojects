## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### intro <a id="tea-sutrarthabodhini-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Of the four human aims, liberation is highest, attained by hearing, reflection and deep contemplation; for this and for powers such as minuteness, Patañjali made the sūtras in four pādas — samādhi, sādhana, vibhūti, kaivalya; the blessed Bādarāyaṇa wrote the bhāṣya on them, and Vācaspati [explained it].
