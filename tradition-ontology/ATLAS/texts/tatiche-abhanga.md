A short cycle of abhaṅgas (about a dozen) with the refrain 'open the door, Jñāneśvar', teaching that the saint answers the world's insults with a cool, forgiving mind.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### refrain 'tāṭī ughaḍā jñāneśvarā' <a id="tea-tatiche-abhanga-tati-ughada"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Muktābāī pleads with Jñāneśvar, who has shut himself in his hut after being insulted: the saint bears the world's blows with a cool mind; if the world becomes fire, the saint must become water; one who is a yogī has a pure mind and forgives — open the door, Jñāneśvar.

> tāṭī ughaḍā jñāneśvarā
