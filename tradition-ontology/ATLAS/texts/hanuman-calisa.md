## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### caupāīs 1–40 (summary) <a id="tea-hanuman-calisa-caupai"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Hanumān is praised as Rāma's messenger and servant, ocean of wisdom and virtue, who carries out Rāma's work, grants the eight powers and nine treasures by Sītā's boon, and keeps evil spirits and fear away from whoever recites his name.

### opening dohā 1 <a id="tea-hanuman-calisa-doha1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Cleansing the mirror of my mind with the dust of the guru's lotus feet, I describe the pure glory of Raghuvara, which bestows the four fruits.

> श्रीगुरु चरन सरोज रज निज मनु मुकुरु सुधारि । बरनउँ रघुबर बिमल जसु जो दायकु फल चारि ॥
