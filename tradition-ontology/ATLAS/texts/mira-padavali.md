The songs ascribed to Mīrābāī: love for Giridhar Gopāl as her only husband, defiance of family honour (lok-lāj), the pain of separation, the poison sent by the Rāṇā, the saints' company, and the name as treasure. Sung across north and west India.

### pada 'he rī maiṃ to prema divānī' <a id="tea-mira-padavali-prema-divani"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

I am mad with love; no one knows my pain; only the wounded know the wound; my bed is on the gallows — how can I sleep? Mīrā's pain will go only when the dark one becomes the physician.

> हे री मैं तो प्रेम दिवानी मेरो दरद न जाणै कोय ।

### pada 'mere to giradhara gopāla' <a id="tea-mira-padavali-mere-to-giridhara"></a>

Mine is Giridhar Gopāl, no one else — he who wears the peacock crown is my husband; I have given up family honour; sitting with sādhus I have lost the world's shame.
