## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-tegchen-tsuljug-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Rongzom argues that all appearances are established only as appearances, like illusions, and that the Great Perfection's claim that all is primordially enlightened does not deny appearance but recognizes it as pure from the beginning; errors come from taking appearances as real.
