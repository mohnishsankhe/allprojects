## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 326a12 <a id="tea-lushan-lianzong-baojian-326a12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Mao Ziyuan, after awakening in samādhi at a crow's cry, emulated Huiyuan's Lotus society: he urged people to take refuge and keep the five precepts, reciting Amitābha's name five times to confirm the five precepts, so that they might purify the five roots, gain the five powers and leave the five turbidities; he compiled a dawn repentance liturgy of the Lotus school and founded a repentance hall at Dianshan lake.

> 勸人歸依三寶、受持五戒，一不殺、二不盜、三不婬、四不妄、五不酒，念阿彌陀佛五聲以證五戒，普結淨緣，欲令世人淨五根、得五力、出五濁也。
