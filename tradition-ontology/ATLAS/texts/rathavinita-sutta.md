Puṇṇa Mantāṇiputta explains to Sāriputta that the seven purifications are like a relay of chariots: each serves the next, and the holy life is lived for final nibbāna without clinging.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 9-15 <a id="tea-rathavinita-sutta-9-15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The holy life is not lived for purification of virtue, of mind, of view, of overcoming doubt, of knowledge and vision of what is and is not the path, of knowledge and vision of the way, or of knowledge and vision, but for final nibbāna without clinging. As King Pasenadi reached Sāketa by a relay of seven chariots, each purification serves the next up to the final goal.
