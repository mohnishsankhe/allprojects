## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1001a17 <a id="tea-jinsim-jikseol-1001a17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ten ways of practising no-mind: 1 awareness (as soon as a thought arises, break it by awareness — 'do not fear thoughts arising, only fear being slow to notice'), 2 resting (stop the mind whenever it arises, think neither good nor evil), 3 extinguishing mind while keeping objects, 4 extinguishing objects while keeping mind, 5 extinguishing both, 6 keeping both, 7 inside and outside as the whole substance, 8 inside and outside as the whole function, 9 substance identical with function, 10 transcending substance and function. One need not use all ten; success in one gate is enough, chosen by one's affinity.

> 從來諸師說。做無心功夫類各不同。今總大義略明十種
