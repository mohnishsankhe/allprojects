### ch1 <a id="tea-choying-dzod-ch1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Longchenpa sings of the dharmadhātu as the primordial expanse: all phenomena of saṃsāra and nirvāṇa arise within it as its display, like the sky's contents within the sky, uncreated and unchanging; the mind that realizes this has nothing to add or remove.
