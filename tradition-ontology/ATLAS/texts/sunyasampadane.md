A dialogic anthology that sets vacanas in a narrative frame centred on Allama Prabhu presiding at the Anubhava Maṇṭapa of Kalyāṇa: Allama examines and is examined by Basava, Cennabasava, Siddharāma, Akka Mahādēvi, Muktāyakka, Gorakṣa and others, so that each 'attainment of the void' (śūnya-sampādane) unfolds as a debate on devotion, work, powers, renunciation and realization.

### closing sampādane (dispersal of the śaraṇas) <a id="tea-sunyasampadane-departure"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

After the events at Kalyāṇa the śaraṇas disperse: Basava to Kūḍalasaṅgama, Cennabasava to Uḷavi, Allama to Kadaḷi on Śrīśaila, each becoming one with the liṅga.

### sampādane of Akka Mahādēvi <a id="tea-sunyasampadane-akka-mahadevi"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Allama questions Akka Mahādēvi: having renounced everything, why does she still cover her body with her hair? She answers that until the fruit ripens within, the rind does not fall, and that she covers the body's mark so as not to trouble others; the assembly acknowledges her attainment and she departs for Śrīśaila.

### sampādane of Allama's arrival at Kalyāṇa <a id="tea-sunyasampadane-allama-at-kalyana"></a>

Allama comes to Kalyāṇa; Basava and the śaraṇas receive him, and he is seated on the throne of the void (śūnya-siṃhāsana) of the Anubhava Maṇṭapa, where each devotee's attainment is examined in dialogue.

### sampādane of Gorakṣa <a id="tea-sunyasampadane-goraksa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Gorakṣa, proud of a body made adamantine by yoga, is met by Allama; the blade that rings off Gorakṣa's body passes through Allama's as through empty space, and Gorakṣa acknowledges that the body dissolved in the liṅga surpasses the body made indestructible.

### sampādane of Muktāyakka <a id="tea-sunyasampadane-muktayakka"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Muktāyakka, grieving her brother Ajagaṇṇa and silent, is drawn into dialogue by Allama on speech and silence; through the exchange she recognizes the state her brother had attained and is freed of grief.

### sampādane of Siddharāma <a id="tea-sunyasampadane-siddharama"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

At Sonnalige Allama mocks Siddharāma's building of tanks and temples as bondage and asks where the true liṅga is; Siddharāma comes to Kalyāṇa and receives liṅga-initiation from Cennabasava.

### the Śūnyasampādane's culminating teaching of the void <a id="tea-sunyasampadane-the-void"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Every attainment tested in the book — devotion, work, knowledge, powers — is brought to the void (śūnya, bayalu), in which liṅga, aṅga and their union are no longer two; Allama is its embodiment.
