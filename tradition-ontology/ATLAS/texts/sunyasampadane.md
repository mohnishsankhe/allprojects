A dialogic anthology that sets vacanas in a narrative frame centred on Allama Prabhu presiding at the Anubhava Maṇṭapa of Kalyāṇa: Allama examines and is examined by Basava, Cennabasava, Siddharāma, Akka Mahādēvi, Muktāyakka, Gorakṣa and others, so that each 'attainment of the void' (śūnya-sampādane) unfolds as a debate on devotion, work, powers, renunciation and realization.
