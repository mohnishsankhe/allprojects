## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-20 <a id="tea-sarvasara-upanisad-1-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Definitions: bondage is identifying self with non-self; liberation is its cessation; ignorance is that through which this happens; knowledge that which removes it; the states, sheaths, jīva, witness, kūṭastha, inner controller, pratyagātman, paramātman and māyā are each defined.
