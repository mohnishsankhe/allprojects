Vacanas signed Guhēśvara, marked by paradox and enigmatic imagery (beḍagu) and by the teaching of the void (śūnya, bayalu); they criticize external observance, the pursuit of powers and attachment, and locate māyā in the mind's desire rather than in objects.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### vacana 'Honnu māyeyembaru…' <a id="tea-allama-prabhu-vacanas-honnu-mayeyembaru"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

They say gold is māyā — gold is not māyā; they say woman is māyā — woman is not māyā; they say land is māyā — land is not māyā; the desire standing before the mind is māyā, O Guhēśvara.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind, ethics_

### vacana 'Āsege sattudu kōṭi…' <a id="tea-allama-prabhu-vacanas-asege-sattudu-koti"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Millions died of desire, millions of greed, millions for gold, woman and land; O Guhēśvara, I see none who died for you.
