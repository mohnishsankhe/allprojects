Sages led by Śaunaka ask Mārkaṇḍeya how he lives long in bliss: through knowledge of Śiva as Dakṣiṇāmūrti — his mantras, meditation form with the gesture of silence, the lamp of knowledge fed by dispassion and devotion; 'dakṣiṇā' means understanding (śemuṣī).

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1 <a id="tea-daksinamurti-upanisad-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Sages led by Śaunaka at Brahmāvarta, under a great banyan, ask Mārkaṇḍeya how he lives long in bliss; he: by the secret knowledge of Śiva (Śiva-tattva).

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission_

teachers: [Mārkaṇḍeya](../teachers/markandeya.md), [Śaunaka Mahāśāla](../teachers/saunaka-mahasala.md)

### 2-3 <a id="tea-daksinamurti-upanisad-2-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The deity is Dakṣiṇāmūrti; his mantras are given with meditation forms — the teacher seated under the banyan, showing the gesture of knowledge in silence.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language, teacher-transmission_

practices: [Recitation of Śiva mantras](../practices/siva-mantra-japa.md)

### 4-6 <a id="tea-daksinamurti-upanisad-4-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The lamp of knowledge burns with the oil of dispassion and the wick of devotion; 'dakṣiṇā' means understanding (śemuṣī), and he who faces it is Dakṣiṇāmūrti; by this knowledge Brahmā gained power to create.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: intermediate · types: ultimate, practice_
