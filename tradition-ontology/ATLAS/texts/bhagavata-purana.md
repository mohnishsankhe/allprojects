# Bhāgavata Purāṇa

**Alternate titles:** Śrīmad Bhāgavatam, Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa, Bhāgavata, Pāramahaṃsya-saṃhitā
**Original title:** श्रीमद्भागवतपुराण
**Language:** Sanskrit

Given first by the Lord to Brahmā on the lotus (BhP 12.13.10; 2.9 catuḥślokī).; scholarly: Usually dated c. 9th-10th c. **Availability:** digitized-original

The foremost Vaiṣṇava Purāṇa: Kṛṣṇa as Bhagavān himself; bhakti as the supreme dharma; the ten marks; creation, avatāras and cosmography (worlds, Bhū-maṇḍala, hells); the stories of Dhruva, Prahlāda, Ajāmila, Gajendra, Jaḍa Bharata and Ṛṣabha; Kṛṣṇa's life in Vraja with the rāsa-līlā and the gopīs' love in separation (Book 10); the Uddhava Gītā on yoga, powers and devotion; the Kali age, the dissolutions and Śuka's final non-dual teaching.

### 1.1.1 <a id="tea-bhagavata-purana-1-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Opening invocation: we meditate on the supreme truth (satyaṃ param) from which the birth, maintenance and dissolution of this world proceed, who knows all things directly and is self-luminous, who revealed the Veda to the first poet (Brahmā) through the heart, in whom the threefold creation is real (not false) as fire, water and earth are mistaken for one another, and who by his own light is ever free of deception.

### 1.2.16-21 <a id="tea-bhagavata-purana-1-2-16-21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A sequence: from serving great souls and holy places arises a taste for hearing of Vāsudeva; Kṛṣṇa, dwelling in the heart of those who hear, shakes off what is inauspicious; with the inauspicious almost destroyed by constant service to the Bhāgavata, steady devotion arises; then the mind, no longer pierced by rajas, tamas, lust and greed, rests in sattva and becomes clear; to the clear mind, free of attachment, knowledge of the reality of Bhagavān arises; when the Lord is seen as the self, the knot of the heart is cut, all doubts are severed and karmas are exhausted.

### 2.2.22-31 <a id="tea-bhagavata-purana-2-2-22-31"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

### 2.9.32-35 <a id="tea-bhagavata-purana-2-9-32-35"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Lord's four verses to Brahmā: I alone was before (creation), nothing else, neither the gross nor the subtle nor their cause; after it, I am what this is; what remains is I. Whatever appears without being real and is not perceived in the self, know that as my māyā, like a reflection or darkness. As the great elements have entered beings and yet have not entered, so I am in them and not in them.
