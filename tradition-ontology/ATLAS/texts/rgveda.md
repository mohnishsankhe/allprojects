# Ṛgveda Saṃhitā

`src:rgveda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Ṛksaṃhitā, Ṛgveda (Śākala recension)
**Original title:** ऋग्वेदसंहिता
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Śākala śākhā (Ṛgveda)](../lineages/sakha-sakala.md)
**Genre:** saṃhitā (hymns)
**Authors:** 
  - [Vyāsa (Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana)](../teachers/vyasa.md) — role: compiler; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: authorless (apauruṣeya) revelation seen by the ṛṣis; arranged by Vyāsa; each hymn's seer (ṛṣi), deity and metre are recorded by the Anukramaṇīs; scholarly: composed by priestly poet families over several centuries and redacted into the Śākala recension; confidence: high
**Dates:** Tradition's account: beginningless; the hymns were 'seen' by the ṛṣis and handed down (Nirukta 1.20); Scholarly account: c. 1500–1000 BCE; family books (2–7) earliest, books 1 and 10 latest; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 10 maṇḍalas; 1,028 sūktas counting the 11 Vālakhilya hymns (8.49–59); c. 10,550 verses (counts differ slightly). Books 2–7 are the family books: 2 Gṛtsamada, 3 Viśvāmitra, 4 Vāmadeva, 5 Atri, 6 Bharadvāja, 7 Vasiṣṭha; book 8 chiefly the Kāṇvas (and Āṅgirasas); book 9 the hymns to Soma Pavamāna gathered from all families; books 1 and 10 are collections of many seers and are regarded as later. A second division into 8 aṣṭakas × 8 adhyāyas (64 adhyāyas) and vargas is used for recitation.
**Availability:** digitized-original

The oldest Vedic collection: hymns (sūktas) of praise and invocation to Agni, Indra, Soma, Varuṇa, Mitra, the Aśvins, Uṣas, Rudra, Viṣṇu and others, recited by the hotṛ priest. Beside the liturgy it contains speculative hymns on the One (1.164, 10.129, 10.121, 10.90, 10.125), on speech, on the self and on death and the fathers (10.14–18).
