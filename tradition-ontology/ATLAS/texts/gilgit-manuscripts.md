# Gilgit manuscripts

`src:gilgit-manuscripts` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Mūlasarvāstivāda](../lineages/mulasarvastivada.md), [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)
**Genre:** manuscript corpus
**Dates:** Scholarly account: found 1931 (and 1938) at Naupur near Gilgit; manuscripts c. 5th–7th c. CE; (confidence moderate)
**Availability:** digitized-original

Birch-bark manuscripts from a stūpa near Gilgit: the Mūlasarvāstivāda Vinaya, the Dharmaskandha, Ekottarāgama fragments, a Dīrghāgama and Mahāyāna sūtras (Lotus, Samādhirāja, Vajracchedikā, Bhaiṣajyaguru). Edited by Nalinaksha Dutt; facsimiles by Raghu Vira and Lokesh Chandra.
