## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### one-hand <a id="tea-sekishu-onjo-one-hand"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Two hands clap and there is a sound; what is the sound of one hand? Hakuin says that raising the great doubt with this kōan leads to seeing the nature more readily than 'wu'.
