Tanluan's hymn of homage in seven-character verses, each followed by 'may I with all beings be born in the Land of Peace and Bliss': Amitābha's attainment ten kalpas ago, his twelve names of light, the land and its assembly, and homage to Nāgārjuna; the model of Shinran's Jōdo wasan.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 420c18-421a11 <a id="tea-zan-amituo-fo-ji-420c18-421a11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Tanluan's hymn: in the West, ten trillion lands from here, is the Land of Peace and Bliss whose Buddha is Amitābha; he became a buddha ten kalpas ago and his life is immeasurable; his dharma-body's wheel of light pervades the dharma-realm; for his wisdom-light he is called Immeasurable Light, Boundless Light, Unhindered Light, Incomparable Light (and so on through twelve names); those touched by his light are freed of being and non-being, their karmic bonds removed. Each verse ends: may I with all beings be born in the Land of Peace and Bliss.

> 現在西方去此界，十萬億剎安樂土，佛世尊號阿彌陀，我願往生歸命禮。……智慧光明不可量，故佛又號無量光，有量諸相蒙光曉，是故稽首真實明。
