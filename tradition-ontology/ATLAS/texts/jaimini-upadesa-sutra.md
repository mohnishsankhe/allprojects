A sūtra text in four adhyāyas of four pādas on the Jaimini system of horā: aspects by sign (rāśi-dṛṣṭi), the variable significators (cara-kāraka) headed by the ātmakāraka, the kārakāṃśa, ārūḍha and upapada, sign-based periods such as the cara-daśā, and longevity. Numbers and signs are encoded by the kaṭapayādi system. It names the signs in the chart that indicate liberation (kaivalya).

### 1.1.2-3 <a id="tea-jaimini-upadesa-sutra-1-1-2-3"></a>

The signs aspect the sign facing them, and the signs at its sides too — so that each movable sign aspects three fixed signs (all but the one adjacent to it), each fixed sign three movable signs, and the dual signs one another.

> abhipaśyanty ṛkṣāṇi | pārśvabhe ca |
