## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3.47 <a id="tea-uttararamacarita-3-47"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

There is only one rasa, compassion (karuṇa); through difference of causes it takes on separate forms, as water takes the forms of eddies, bubbles and waves, though all of it is only water.
