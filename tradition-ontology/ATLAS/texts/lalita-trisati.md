A hymn of three hundred names of Lalitā in which each of the fifteen syllables of the Śrīvidyā mantra begins twenty names; presented as the most secret hymn, given by Hayagrīva to Agastya after the Sahasranāma. It is used above all in worship with offerings of vermilion (kuṅkumārcana).

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### secrecy <a id="tea-lalita-trisati-secrecy"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Hayagrīva calls the Triśatī the most secret of the Goddess's hymns and teaches it only after Agastya's persistent request, restricting it to the devoted and qualified.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission_

### structure <a id="tea-lalita-trisati-structure"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Triśatī gives three hundred names of Lalitā, twenty beginning with each of the fifteen syllables of the Śrīvidyā mantra, so that the hymn as a whole unfolds the mantra.
