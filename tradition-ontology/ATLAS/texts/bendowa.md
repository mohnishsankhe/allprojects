The self-fulfilling samādhi (jijuyū zanmai) transmitted by buddhas is right sitting; practice and realization are one (shushō ittō), so even a beginner's wholehearted practice is the whole of original realization; rejects the view that the mind-nature is permanent while the body perishes (the Śreṇika heresy); answers eighteen questions.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### practice-realization <a id="tea-bendowa-practice-realization"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

To think that practice and realization are not one is a non-Buddhist view; in the buddha-dharma practice and realization are one (shushō ittō); because practice is practice-in-realization, the beginner's wholehearted practice is the whole of original realization; teachers therefore say not to look for realization apart from practice.

### srenika <a id="tea-bendowa-srenika"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Rejects the view that the mind-nature is permanent while the body perishes, so that on death the nature leaves the body and returns to the ocean of nature: this is the view of the heretic Śreṇika; in the buddha-dharma body and mind are one, nature and form are not two.
