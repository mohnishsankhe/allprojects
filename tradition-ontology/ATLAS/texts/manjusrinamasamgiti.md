The 'Chanting the Names of Mañjuśrī': Vajrapāṇi requests and the Buddha proclaims the names of Mañjuśrī as the gnosis-being (jñānasattva) — the syllable A as supreme (v. 28), the primordial buddha without beginning or end (v. 100), the five gnoses; recited daily across Vajrayāna and classified variously as yoga or non-dual tantra.

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 28-29 <a id="tea-manjusrinamasamgiti-28-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Lord, the Buddha, the perfect Buddha, arisen from the letter A; A, foremost of all letters, of great meaning, the supreme imperishable; the great breath, unborn, free of utterance, the chief cause of all expression, illuminating all speech.

> tad yathā bhagavān buddhaḥ sambuddho 'kārasambhavaḥ | akāraḥ sarvavarṇāgryo mahārthaḥ paramākṣaraḥ || mahāprāṇo hy anutpādo vāgudāhāravarjitaḥ | sarvābhilāpahetvagryaḥ sarvavāksuprabhāsvaraḥ ||

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: sound, knowledge · stage: all · types: sound-language, ultimate_

terms: [akāra](../terms/a-syllable.md) · concepts: [The syllable A](../concepts/syllable-a.md)

### 30 <a id="tea-manjusrinamasamgiti-30"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Great, great, great passion bringing delight to all beings; great, great, great hatred, the great enemy of all afflictions.

> mahāmahamahārāgaḥ sarvasattvaratiṅkaraḥ | mahāmahamahādveṣaḥ sarvakleśamahāripuḥ ||

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, consciousness-mind_

terms: [mahārāga](../terms/maharaga.md) · concepts: [The passions as the path](../concepts/passions-as-path.md)

### 100 <a id="tea-manjusrinamasamgiti-100"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Without beginning or end is the Buddha, the primordial buddha without lineage, with gnosis as the single eye, stainless, the embodiment of gnosis, the tathāgata.

> anādinidhano buddha ādibuddho niranvayaḥ | jñānaikacakṣur amalo jñānamūrtis tathāgataḥ ||
