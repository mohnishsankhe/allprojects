A ten-fascicle sūtra transmitted in China: after Ānanda is nearly seduced through a spell, the Buddha shows that the mind is not located in seven places and that the true, perceiving nature (the 'wondrous bright true mind', the tathāgatagarbha) neither comes nor goes; twenty-five sages describe their perfect penetrations, and Mañjuśrī selects Avalokiteśvara's turning of hearing back to hear the self-nature as best for this world; the four clear instructions on purity (no sex, killing, stealing, false claims), the Śūraṅgama mantra and altar, the stages of the path, the destinies, and the fifty demonic states arising from the five aggregates in meditation.

### 1 <a id="tea-surangama-sutra-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Ānanda, caught by the spell of the Mātaṅga woman, is rescued by the Buddha's mantra; asked where his mind is, he places it in seven locations — inside the body, outside, hidden in the sense-organ, and so on — and the Buddha refutes each.

### 2 <a id="tea-surangama-sutra-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The nature of seeing does not move, age or perish: King Prasenajit saw the Ganges at three and at sixty-two with the same seeing; what changes perishes, what does not change is without birth and death. All aggregates, sense-fields and elements are the wondrous true suchness nature of the tathāgatagarbha.

### 5.mahasthamaprapta <a id="tea-surangama-sutra-5-mahasthamaprapta"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Mahāsthāmaprāpta tells how he entered samādhi by recollecting the Buddha: as a mother remembers her child, if beings remember the Buddha they will surely see him now or later; gathering in all six faculties, with pure recollection continuing moment after moment, he attained samādhi — for him the best of the penetrations — and he now receives recollecting beings into the Pure Land.

### 6 <a id="tea-surangama-sutra-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Twenty-five sages tell the method by which each attained perfect penetration (yuantong) — through each of the six sense objects, six faculties, six consciousnesses and seven elements; Mañjuśrī judges Avalokiteśvara's method the best for beings of this world: turning the hearing back to hear the self-nature until the self-nature becomes the supreme way.

> 反聞聞自性，性成無上道。

### 6 <a id="tea-surangama-sutra-6-2"></a>

### 8 <a id="tea-surangama-sutra-8"></a>

The stages of the path: dry wisdom, ten faiths, ten abodes, ten practices, ten dedications, four additional practices, ten grounds, equal awakening and wondrous awakening; and the ten destinies of beings produced by emotion and thought.

### 9-10 <a id="tea-surangama-sutra-9-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The fifty demonic states (five for each of the five aggregates, ten per aggregate in all): as form, feeling, perception, formations and consciousness are penetrated in meditation, lights, visions, sudden compassion, boldness, joy, pride or false views of nirvāṇa arise; if one does not take them as sagehood they are good states, if one takes them as sagehood one falls prey to demons.

> 不作聖心，名善境界；若作聖解，即受群邪。

### 10 <a id="tea-surangama-sutra-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The aggregates arise from consciousness and cease from form: in principle they are awakened to all at once, and with that awakening all is dissolved; in practice they are not removed all at once but exhausted in sequence.

> 理則頓悟，乘悟並銷；事非頓除，因次第盡。
