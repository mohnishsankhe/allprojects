Eight verses ascribed to Caitanya, his only writing: the glory of congregational chanting of Kṛṣṇa's name, the name's full potency, the humility with which to chant, the prayer for causeless devotion, and the longing of separation.

## Teachings (8: skeleton 8)

### 1 <a id="tea-siksastaka-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Victory to the congregational chanting of Kṛṣṇa's names, which cleanses the mirror of the mind, extinguishes the forest fire of worldly existence, spreads the moonlight of welfare, is the life of the bride of knowledge, increases the ocean of bliss, gives a taste of full nectar at every step and bathes the whole self.

> ceto-darpaṇa-mārjanaṃ bhava-mahā-dāvāgni-nirvāpaṇaṃ śreyaḥ-kairava-candrikā-vitaraṇaṃ vidyā-vadhū-jīvanam | ānandāmbudhi-vardhanaṃ prati-padaṃ pūrṇāmṛtāsvādanaṃ sarvātma-snapanaṃ paraṃ vijayate śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam ||

### 2 <a id="tea-siksastaka-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

You have made many names and placed all your powers in them, with no fixed rule of time for remembering them; such is your mercy, O Lord, yet such is my misfortune that I have no attraction for them.
