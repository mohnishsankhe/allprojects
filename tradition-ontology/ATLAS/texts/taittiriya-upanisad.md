# Taittirīya Upaniṣad

`src:taittiriya-upanisad` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Taittirīyaka Upaniṣad
**Original title:** तैत्तिरीयोपनिषद्
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Taittirīya śākhā (Black Yajurveda)](../lineages/sakha-taittiriya.md)
**Genre:** upanisad
**Part of:** [Taittirīya Āraṇyaka](taittiriya-aranyaka.md)
**Location in parent:** Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 7-9 (TA 10 is the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad)
**Attribution:** tradition: śruti, 'seen' by the sages whose teaching it records; not a human composition; scholarly: anonymous compilation within the Vedic schools (śākhās), incorporating older materials; confidence: high
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Revealed scripture (śruti): in the Mīmāṃsā and Vedānta view without human author and beginningless; the texts themselves trace their teaching through lineages of teachers back to Brahma/Prajāpati and brahman, the self-existent; Scholarly account: c. 6th-5th c. BCE (rough estimate); (confidence low)
**Structure:** 3 vallis in prose with verses: Siksavalli (12 anuvakas), Brāhmaṇandavalli or Anandavalli (9 anuvakas), Bhṛguvalli (10 anuvakas)
**Availability:** digitized-original

Opens with phonetics (siksa), meditations on combinations and on the vyahrtis, the precepts to the departing student (speak the truth, follow dharma, treat mother, father, teacher and guest as gods); the second part teaches brahman as truth, knowledge, infinite and the five selves made of food, breath, mind, understanding and bliss, with the calculus of bliss; in the third Bhṛgu learns from Varuṇa by tapas that brahman is food, breath, mind, understanding and bliss.

### 1.11.1-4 <a id="tea-taittiriya-upanisad-1-11-1-4"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Having taught the Veda, the teacher instructs the pupil: speak the truth; practise dharma; do not neglect your study; having given the teacher the wealth he likes, do not cut off the line of offspring; do not neglect truth, dharma, welfare, prosperity, study and teaching, or the rites to gods and fathers. Let your mother be a god to you, your father, your teacher, your guest. Do only blameless acts, follow only our good conduct. Give with faith, not without faith; give with grace, modesty, awe and understanding. If you are in doubt about an act or conduct, behave as the thoughtful, devoted, gentle brahmins there would behave. This is the rule, the instruction, the secret of the Veda.

> satyaṃ vada | dharmaṃ cara | svādhyāyān mā pramadaḥ

### 2.1.1 <a id="tea-taittiriya-upanisad-2-1-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

The knower of brahman attains the highest. On this there is a verse: brahman is truth, knowledge, infinite (satyaṃ jñānam anantaṃ brahma); whoever knows it hidden in the cave, in the highest heaven, attains all desires together with the wise brahman. From this self space arose, from space wind, from wind fire, from fire water, from water earth, from earth plants, from plants food, from food the person: this person consists of the essence of food.

> brahmavid āpnoti param | satyaṃ jñānam anantaṃ brahma

### 2.2-5 <a id="tea-taittiriya-upanisad-2-2-5"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Different from and within the self made of the essence of food is the self made of breath, which fills it; it too has the shape of a person (head prāṇa, right side vyāna, left side apāna, trunk space, tail earth). Within that is the self made of mind (head the yajus, sides the ṛc and sāman, trunk the teaching, tail the Atharvāṅgiras); within that the self made of understanding (head faith, sides right and truth, trunk yoga, tail greatness); within that the self made of bliss (head joy, sides delight and great delight, trunk bliss, tail — the foundation — brahman). Each inner self is the embodied self of the preceding one.

### 2.7.1 <a id="tea-taittiriya-upanisad-2-7-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

In the beginning this was non-being (asat); from it being was born; it made itself by itself; therefore it is called well-made. That which is well-made is the essence (raso vai saḥ); obtaining the essence one becomes blissful; who could breathe, who could live, if this bliss were not in space? When one finds fearless support in that which is invisible, selfless, undefined, without support, one has reached fearlessness; but when one makes the slightest gap in it, there is fear — the fear of one who knows but does not reflect.

> raso vai saḥ

### 3.1.1 <a id="tea-taittiriya-upanisad-3-1-1"></a>

Bhṛgu Vāruṇi approached his father Varuṇa: 'Sir, teach me brahman.' ### 3.2.1-3.6.1 <a id="tea-taittiriya-upanisad-3-2-1-3-6-1"></a>

Bhṛgu understood in turn that food is brahman, then breath, then mind, then understanding, each time returning to his father, who said: 'seek to know brahman by tapas; tapas is brahman'. ### 3.7.1-3.10.4 <a id="tea-taittiriya-upanisad-3-7-1-3-10-4"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Vows (vrata) concerning food: one should not despise food; one should not reject food; one should make much food; one should turn no one away from one's dwelling, and so should obtain much food and say 'food is ready'.
