# Taittirīya Upaniṣad

`src:taittiriya-upanisad` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Taittirīyaka Upaniṣad
**Original title:** तैत्तिरीयोपनिषद्
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Taittirīya śākhā (Black Yajurveda)](../lineages/sakha-taittiriya.md)
**Genre:** upanisad
**Part of:** [Taittirīya Āraṇyaka](taittiriya-aranyaka.md)
**Location in parent:** Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 7-9 (TA 10 is the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad)
**Attribution:** tradition: śruti, 'seen' by the sages whose teaching it records; not a human composition; scholarly: anonymous compilation within the Vedic schools (śākhās), incorporating older materials; confidence: high
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Revealed scripture (śruti): in the Mīmāṃsā and Vedānta view without human author and beginningless; the texts themselves trace their teaching through lineages of teachers back to Brahma/Prajāpati and brahman, the self-existent; Scholarly account: c. 6th-5th c. BCE (rough estimate); (confidence low)
**Structure:** 3 vallis in prose with verses: Siksavalli (12 anuvakas), Brāhmaṇandavalli or Anandavalli (9 anuvakas), Bhṛguvalli (10 anuvakas)
**Availability:** digitized-original

Opens with phonetics (siksa), meditations on combinations and on the vyahrtis, the precepts to the departing student (speak the truth, follow dharma, treat mother, father, teacher and guest as gods); the second part teaches brahman as truth, knowledge, infinite and the five selves made of food, breath, mind, understanding and bliss, with the calculus of bliss; in the third Bhṛgu learns from Varuṇa by tapas that brahman is food, breath, mind, understanding and bliss.

### 1.11.1-4 <a id="tea-taittiriya-upanisad-1-11-1-4"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Having taught the Veda, the teacher instructs the pupil: speak the truth; practise dharma; do not neglect your study; having given the teacher the wealth he likes, do not cut off the line of offspring; do not neglect truth, dharma, welfare, prosperity, study and teaching, or the rites to gods and fathers. Let your mother be a god to you, your father, your teacher, your guest. Do only blameless acts, follow only our good conduct. Give with faith, not without faith; give with grace, modesty, awe and understanding. If you are in doubt about an act or conduct, behave as the thoughtful, devoted, gentle brahmins there would behave. This is the rule, the instruction, the secret of the Veda.

> satyaṃ vada | dharmaṃ cara | svādhyāyān mā pramadaḥ

### 2.2-5 <a id="tea-taittiriya-upanisad-2-2-5"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Different from and within the self made of the essence of food is the self made of breath, which fills it; it too has the shape of a person (head prāṇa, right side vyāna, left side apāna, trunk space, tail earth). Each inner self is the embodied self of the preceding one.

### ch2 <a id="tea-taittiriya-upanisad-ch2"></a>

The Brahmānandavallī (2.1.1-2.9.1): it defines brahman as truth, knowledge, infinite and traces emergence from the self (2.1); gives the five selves made of food, breath, mind, understanding and bliss, each within the last and person-shaped, with brahman as the tail and support of the innermost (2.1-2.5); asks whether the ignorant or the knower reaches that world, and gives the emergence and entry of the one who wished to be many (2.6); the essence and fearlessness (2.7); the scale of bliss by hundredfold steps up to brahman (2.8); and the fearlessness of the one who knows the bliss of brahman (2.9).
