Twenty-two letters of Shinran: the welcoming at death belongs to birth by various practices, while the person of shinjin is already among the truly settled; do not take poison because there is an antidote; the letter on jinen hōni; the person of shinjin is equal to the Tathāgatas.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1 <a id="tea-mattosho-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Buddha's coming to welcome at death belongs to those who seek birth by various practices, being practitioners of self-power; waiting for the moment of death and relying on the welcoming apply to them. The person of true shinjin, because he is embraced and never abandoned, dwells in the rightly established state; his birth is settled when shinjin is settled; he need not wait for the moment of death or rely on being welcomed.

### 5 <a id="tea-mattosho-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Ji' means 'of itself', not through the practitioner's calculation; 'nen' means 'to be made so', by the Tathāgata's vow; 'hōni' means being so by virtue of the Dharma, the working of the vow. Since the practitioner does not calculate, 'no working is true working'. The vow is to make us become the supreme Buddha; the supreme Buddha is formless, and because formless is called jinen; Amida Buddha is the means by which we are made to know jinen. Having understood this, one should not keep discussing jinen, lest 'no working' become again a working.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: devotion, knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_

terms: `trm:jinen-honi`, `trm:hakarai` · concepts: `cpt:jinen-honi` · teachers: [Shinran](../teachers/shinran.md)

### antidote <a id="tea-mattosho-antidote"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Because there is medicine, do not delight in poison: some say that since the vow saves evil people one may do evil as one pleases; this is utterly wrong — as one hears the teaching and entrusts oneself, one comes to reject evil, and those who deliberately do what should not be done in the name of the vow harm the community and the teacher.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ethics, dispute_

obstacles: `obs:licensed-evil` · teachers: [Shinran](../teachers/shinran.md)

### equal-to-tathagata <a id="tea-mattosho-equal-to-tathagata"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The person of true shinjin, being certain to attain supreme awakening, is 'the same as Maitreya' and 'equal to the Tathāgatas' — though the body is that of a foolish being, the heart is already equal to the Tathāgatas.
