A shorter poem by Nārāyaṇa Paṇḍitācārya narrating, from the Mādhva side, the history before Madhva: the propagation of Vedic teaching by Durvāsas and others at the command of Kṛṣṇa and Bhīma, and the rise of the teaching of māyā, which it presents polemically as the work of a demon (Maṇimān) reborn. It is the tradition's polemical account; the Advaita tradition rejects it.
