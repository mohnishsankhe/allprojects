# Gorakṣamudgara

`src:goraksa-mudgara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Genre:** kāvya
**Authors:** 
  - [Gorakṣanātha](../teachers/goraksanatha.md) — role: author; attribution: doubtful
**Availability:** partly-lost

An ornate poem ascribed to Gorakṣa, of which the first chapter ('destruction of ignorance and delusion') survives: it praises the one who realises his own nature within his body and preaches renunciation through seeing the transience of life and attachment.
