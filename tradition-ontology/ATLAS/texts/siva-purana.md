**Authors:** 
  - [Vyāsa (Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana)](../teachers/vyasa.md) — role: compiler; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Originally of twelve saṃhitās and a hundred thousand verses, abridged by Vyāsa into seven saṃhitās of 24,000 verses; counted fourth of the eighteen in VP 3.6.21 and BhP 12.7.23.; scholarly: A late compilation relative to the Vāyu (which several lists count fourth); the Vāyavīya Saṃhitā may be older than the rest.; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Vyāsa, end of Dvāpara; Scholarly account: compiled c. 10th-14th c. CE (Vāyavīya possibly earlier); (confidence low)
**Structure:** seven saṃhitās (Venkateshwara): Vidyeśvara, Rudra (Sṛṣṭi, Satī, Pārvatī, Kumāra, Yuddha khaṇḍas), Śatarudra, Koṭirudra, Umā, Kailāsa, Vāyavīya (pūrva and uttara); 24,000 verses
**Availability:** digitized-original

The great Śaiva Purāṇa: Śiva as niṣkala (worshipped in the liṅga) and sakala (in the image); the means of hearing, singing and reflecting; the liṅga of fire; Satī and Pārvatī; the jyotirliṅgas; the pañcākṣara and Śaiva initiation; and Upamanyu's five kinds of yoga, their obstacles and powers.

_Notes: Vidyeśvara: śravaṇa-kīrtana-manana, liṅga worship, the name, bhasma, rudrākṣa; Koṭirudra: the twelve jyotirliṅgas and the Śivarātri hunter; Umā: hells and Yama's road (not checked); Kailāsa: praṇava and sannyāsa; Vāyavīya: Upamanyu's teaching to Kṛṣṇa - dīkṣā, pañcākṣara (7.2.12-13), yoga (7.2.37-39). verse number checked in the GRETIL e-text of the Śiva Purāṇa books 1 and 7 (Venkateshwara ed.)_
