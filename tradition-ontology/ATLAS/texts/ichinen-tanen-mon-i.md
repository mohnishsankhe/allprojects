Shinran's glosses on passages concerning one and many callings, concluding that one should not insist on either; explains 'immediately attain birth and dwell in non-retrogression' as attainment in this life at the moment of shinjin.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### conclusion <a id="tea-ichinen-tanen-mon-i-conclusion"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

One should not insist on 'one-calling' or 'many-calling' as the true teaching; the nenbutsu of the primal vow includes both, and to set one against the other goes against the teaching. 'Immediately attaining birth' means that at the moment shinjin arises one dwells in non-retrogression, without waiting for another time or another day.
