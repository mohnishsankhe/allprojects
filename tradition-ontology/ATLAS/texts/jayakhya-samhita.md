# Jayākhya Saṃhitā

`src:jayakhya-samhita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Jayā, Jayākhyatantra
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md)
**Genre:** saṃhitā (Pāñcarātra)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Bhagavān (Nārāyaṇa/Vāsudeva); one of the traditional 108 saṃhitās; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Scholarly account: before the 10th c.: quoted by Utpala Vaiṣṇava's Spandapradīpikā (Kashmir, 10th c.); (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 33 paṭalas
**Availability:** digitized-original

One of the three gems of the Pāñcarātra, in 33 paṭalas. Sages ask Śāṇḍilya, who relates Bhagavān's teaching to Nārada. It distinguishes Brahmā's, primordial matter's and the pure creation (paṭalas 2–4), and treats mantras, initiation, worship, meditation and yoga (paṭala 33). An additional passage in the printed text names the three gems and their expansions (Pādma, Pārameśvara, Īśvara) and assigns them to Kāñcī, Śrīraṅgam and Melkote.
