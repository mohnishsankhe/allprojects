## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 369c21 <a id="tea-two-entrances-four-practices-369c21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There are many ways into the Way but essentially two: entry by principle and entry by practice. Entry by principle is to awaken to the tenet through the teaching, deeply believing that all living beings share one true nature, covered by adventitious dust and deluded thought; if one abandons the false, returns to the true, and abides firmly in wall-gazing, self and other, ordinary and holy are one, one no longer follows the written teachings, and one is in silent accord with principle.

> 理入者。謂藉教悟宗。深信含生同一真性。俱為客塵妄想所覆。不能顯了。若也捨妄歸真。凝住壁觀。無自無他。凡聖等一

### 369c25 <a id="tea-two-entrances-four-practices-369c25"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Entry by practice consists of four practices that include all others: requiting enmity (accepting present suffering as the ripening of past wrongs, without resentment), according with conditions (gain and loss follow conditions; the mind neither increases nor decreases), seeking nothing (all seeking is suffering), and according with the Dharma (the principle of the pure nature).

> 行入者。謂四行。其餘諸行悉入此中。何等四耶。一報冤行。二隨緣行。三無所求行。四稱法行

### 370a01 <a id="tea-two-entrances-four-practices-370a01"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

On requiting enmity: when suffering comes the practitioner reflects that through countless kalpas he has followed the branches and abandoned the root, creating enmity and harm; though innocent now, this is the ripening of past wrongs, which neither gods nor humans impose; he accepts it willingly without complaint — 'meeting suffering without distress, because one understands'.

> 今雖無犯。是皆宿殃。惡業果熟。非天非人所能見與。甘心忍受。都無冤訴
