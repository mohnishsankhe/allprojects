# Kūṭadanta Sutta

The Buddha tells of King Mahāvijita's sacrifice in which no living being was killed, and of sacrifices of higher fruit: regular giving, dwellings for the Saṅgha, refuge, precepts, going forth, jhāna and knowledge; Kūṭadanta releases his animals.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 8-21 <a id="tea-kutadanta-sutta-8-21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

King Mahāvijita's great sacrifice, performed on his brahmin chaplain's advice: no cattle, goats, fowl or pigs were killed, no trees felled; workers were not forced; it was offered with ghee, oil, butter, curd, honey and molasses, with the giver's mind glad before, during and after.

### 22-27 <a id="tea-kutadanta-sutta-22-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Sacrifices of greater fruit than that: regular giving to virtuous renunciants, a dwelling for the Saṅgha of the four quarters, going for refuge, taking the five precepts, and — highest — going forth, virtue, the jhānas and the knowledge of the destruction of the effluents.
