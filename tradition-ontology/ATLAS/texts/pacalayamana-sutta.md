## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-11 <a id="tea-pacalayamana-sutta-2-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

To overcome drowsiness Moggallāna should not attend to the perception that brings it, reflect on the Dhamma, recite it, pull his ears and rub his limbs, get up and wash his eyes and look at the stars, attend to the perception of light, walk back and forth with senses turned inward, or lie down mindfully intending to rise. He should not go among families with conceit, nor engage in contentious talk; in brief, 'nothing is worth clinging to'.
