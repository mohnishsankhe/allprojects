## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-acarya-hrdayam-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Nammāḻvār's Tiruvāymoḻi is the essence of the four Vedas in Tamil; the Tamil Veda, uttered by one born outside the brahmin class, is equal in authority to the Sanskrit and reveals its heart.
