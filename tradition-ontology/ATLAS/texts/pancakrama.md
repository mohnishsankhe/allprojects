The 'Five Stages' of the Ārya school: vajra recitation, the purification of all (the four empties and the natural concepts), self-consecration (illusory body), full awakening (clear light) and union (yuganaddha); the text states that the stages are 'a staircase' for those established in the generation stage.

### 1.5-8 <a id="tea-pancakrama-1-5-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Established in vajra recitation, the mantrin attains mind-isolation; abiding in the illusion-like samādhi he enters the reality-limit; rising from it he attains non-dual gnosis; abiding in the samādhi of union he has nothing more to learn; this is called the perfected yoga, he is the great Vajradhara, omniscient, and sees the three times in an instant through the purity of clear light.

### 2.7-12 <a id="tea-pancakrama-2-7-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

### 2.20-27 <a id="tea-pancakrama-2-20-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The very empty (light-appearance, ālokābhāsa) has forty natural concepts (passion, delight, joy, amazement, embrace, courage, pride, violence, deceit and others), called 'day', the sun; the great empty (light-attainment, ālokopalabdhi) has seven moments of ignorance (forgetting, confusion, silence, weariness, laziness, dullness); these subtle natures, one hundred and sixty, run day and night by the movement of the wind.

### 3.10-12 <a id="tea-pancakrama-3-10-12"></a>

The stage of self-consecration shows conventional truth; it is obtained through the grace of the guru's feet and not otherwise; for the practitioner who does not obtain it, labour in sūtras, tantras and rituals is in vain; obtaining it, he attains buddhahood in this very birth without doubt.

### 3.15-17 <a id="tea-pancakrama-3-15-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The whole world is dependent, nothing independent arises; its cause is clear light, and the all-empty is clear light; by the same mind through which fools are bound in saṃsāra, yogins go to the state of the sugatas; here no one is born and no one dies; saṃsāra is to be known as standing in the shape of mind.

### 5.2-5 <a id="tea-pancakrama-5-2-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Where saṃsāra and nirvāṇa become one by abandoning the two concepts, that is called union; who knows defilement and purification as one in the ultimate knows union; who joins the conception of form and of formlessness knows union; where grasped and grasper are not two, that is union.

### 5.29-35 <a id="tea-pancakrama-5-29-35"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The great yogin established in union should meditate on union and the conduct that goes with it: as oneself so the enemy, as wife so daughter, as mother so courtesan, as the outcaste woman so the brahmin woman, as cloth so hide, as jewel so chaff, as urine so wine, as food so excrement, as praise so abuse, as Rudra so the Vajrī, as night so day, as pleasure so pain, as hell so heaven; knowing thus the wise one acts without hesitation, and by keeping the hidden observance all attainments are accomplished.
