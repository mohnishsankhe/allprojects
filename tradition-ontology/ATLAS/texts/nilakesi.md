A Tamil Jain polemical epic in ten chapters: Nīlakēci, a fierce goddess converted by the monk Municandra, travels and debates the Buddhist nun Kuṇṭalakēci, Buddhist teachers and the Buddha himself, an Ājīvika, and Sāṃkhya, Vaiśeṣika, Vedic and materialist (bhūtavāda) teachers, refuting each. It answers the Buddhist Kuṇṭalakēci.

### whole <a id="tea-nilakesi-whole"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Nīlakēci, once a fierce goddess who received animal sacrifice, is converted by the monk Municandra to non-violence and sets out to defend the Jina's teaching, defeating in argument the Buddhist Kuṇṭalakēci, Buddhist teachers and the Buddha, an Ājīvika teacher, and exponents of Sāṃkhya, Vaiśeṣika, Vedic sacrifice and materialism.
