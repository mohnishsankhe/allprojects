# Kaṭhaśruti Upaniṣad

`src:kathasruti-upanisad` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Kaṭhaśrutyupaniṣad
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md)
**Genre:** upaniṣad
**Dates:** Scholarly account: Olivelle (1992): one of the older Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads; (confidence low)
**Availability:** digitized-original

An older Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣad of the northern lists, on the renunciation rite and the renouncer's life; it corresponds closely to the first chapter of the (southern) Sannyāsa Upaniṣad ('yo 'nukrameṇa sannyasati…').

_Notes: Not the Kaṭharudra (src:katharudra-upanisad), despite similar names in some lists._
