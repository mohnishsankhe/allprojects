## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### who-hears <a id="tea-enzan-wadei-gassui-shu-who-hears"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Ask yourself: what is it that now sees and hears? Doubt deeply who the master of hearing is, without seeking an answer in thought; when the doubt is exhausted, the self-nature is seen.
