A 'grinding to powder' of the sixteen categories of the Nyāya school (means of knowledge, objects of knowledge, doubt, purpose, example, tenet, the members of inference, confutation, ascertainment, debate, wrangling, cavil, fallacies, quibble, futile rejoinders and points of defeat): their definitions are shown to depend on one another and to be unestablished by their own nature.
