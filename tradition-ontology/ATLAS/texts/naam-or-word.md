### Naam or Word, on the Word in all scriptures <a id="tea-naam-or-word-the-word-in-all-religions"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

The Word — Nām, Shabd, Kalma, Logos — is spoken of in all scriptures as the power that created and sustains all; it is not the spoken or written name but the inner sound and light, contacted through a living Master.
