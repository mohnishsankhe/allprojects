## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-vaikuntha-gadya-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

One who has surrendered by the Lord's grace should meditate on Vaikuṇṭha — its divine city and hall, the Lord seated with Śrī, Bhū and Nīlā, the eternal servants (nityasūris) ever serving him — and long to join that service.
