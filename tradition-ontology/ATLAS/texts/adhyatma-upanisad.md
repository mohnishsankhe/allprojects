## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-adhyatma-upanisad-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Within the cave of the body dwells the one unborn, whose body is earth… (as in the Subāla); he is Nārāyaṇa.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate_

### 2-70 (summary) <a id="tea-adhyatma-upanisad-2-70-summary"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Verses on removing the superimposition of 'I' on body and mind, abandoning vāsanās, samādhi, and liberation while living and without body; many are shared with the Vivekacūḍāmaṇi.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind, karma-liberation_
