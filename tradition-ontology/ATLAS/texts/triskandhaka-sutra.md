## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-triskandhaka-sutra-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Confession before the thirty-five buddhas of all evil done in this and past lives, rejoicing in all merit, and dedicating it to awakening — the three 'heaps' of practice.
