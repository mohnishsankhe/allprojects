The great Bengali digest of tantric worship in paricchedas: initiation and its preliminaries (including the checks of a mantra's suitability, which the Mahāvidyās are said not to need — a statement Kṛṣṇānanda reads as praise), then the mantras, meditations and worship of Viṣṇu, Śrīvidyā, the Mahāvidyās and above all Dakṣiṇakālī (Śyāmā), quoting the Kālī Tantra's meditation verse; yoginī-sādhana, homa, mudrās.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1.siddhadi-sodhana <a id="tea-tantrasara-krsnananda-1-siddhadi-sodhana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

On testing a mantra's suitability (siddha-sādhya etc.): the Muṇḍamālā Tantra says the ten Mahāvidyās — Kālī, Tārā, Ṣoḍaśī, Bhuvaneśvarī, Bhairavī, Chinnamastā, Dhūmāvatī, Bagalā, Mātaṅgī and Kamalā — are 'siddha' vidyās, for which there is no need to test compatibility, examine lunar mansions or purify the times, and which are unaffected by the faults of the Kali age. Kṛṣṇānanda comments: 'In truth this statement is praise; examination is always needed, because the fault of a hostile mantra has been seen even when a mantra was received in a dream' — so say the holders of the tradition.

> इत्यादिवचनादेषु विचारो नास्ति । वस्तुतस्तु इदं प्रशंसापरम् ।

### 2.syama-dhyana <a id="tea-tantrasara-krsnananda-2-syama-dhyana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The meditation on Dakṣiṇakālī quoted from the Kālī Tantra: terrible-faced, dreadful, hair loose, four-armed, adorned with a garland of heads, holding in her left hands a freshly severed head and a sword and showing with her right hands fearlessness and boon-giving; dark as a great cloud, naked, smeared with blood dripping from the garland of heads at her neck, with two corpses as ear-ornaments, a girdle of dead men's hands, a smiling face with blood streaming from its corners, three eyes like the rising sun; she dwells in the cremation ground, stands on the heart of Mahādeva lying as a corpse, surrounded by howling jackals, united with Mahākāla in reversed union, her face happy and pleased — so one should meditate on Kālī, giver of all desires.

> करालवदनां घोरां मुक्तकेशीं चतुर्भुजाम् । कालिकां दक्षिणां दिव्यां मुण्डमालाविभूषिताम् ।
