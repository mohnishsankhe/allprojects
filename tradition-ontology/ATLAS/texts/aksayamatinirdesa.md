The teaching of the bodhisattva Akṣayamati on eighty 'inexhaustible' qualities of the bodhisattva; it defines sūtras of definitive meaning (nītārtha) as those teaching emptiness, signlessness, no self, no being, and sūtras of provisional meaning (neyārtha) as those teaching self, being, person and the like, and gives the four reliances (on the dharma not the person, meaning not letter, definitive not provisional, gnosis not consciousness).

### nitartha <a id="tea-aksayamatinirdesa-nitartha"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Which sūtras are of definitive meaning (nītārtha) and which of provisional meaning (neyārtha)? Those that teach the conventional — the establishment of the path, of self, being, soul, person, agent — are provisional; those that teach emptiness, the signless, the wishless, the unconditioned, the unborn, no self, no being, no soul, no person, no owner, are definitive.

### pratisarana <a id="tea-aksayamatinirdesa-pratisarana"></a>

The four reliances: rely on the meaning, not the letter; on the dharma, not the person; on gnosis (jñāna), not consciousness (vijñāna); on sūtras of definitive meaning, not provisional meaning.
