Cāmarasa's bhāminī-ṣaṭpadi poem presenting Allama Prabhu as Śiva's descent: Pārvatī's Māyā sent to seduce him fails, Gorakṣa's adamantine body meets Allama's void body, Siddharāma and Muktāyakka are awakened, and Allama joins the śaraṇas at Kalyāṇa before his final union at Kadaḷi (Śrīśaila).

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### episode of Gorakṣa <a id="tea-prabhulingalile-episode-goraksa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Gorakṣa displays a body made adamantine (vajrakāya) that no sword can cut; the sword passes through Allama's body without meeting anything, and Gorakṣa recognizes the void body as higher than the imperishable one.

### episode of Māyā (Māyādēvi) <a id="tea-prabhulingalile-episode-maya"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Māyā, drawn to Allama as he plays the drum in the temple, pursues him; he remains untouched, and her desire cannot bind one who has no second.

### frame of the poem (opening cantos) <a id="tea-prabhulingalile-frame-descent"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Allama is Śiva's own descent into the world; Pārvatī, doubting his detachment, sends her tāmasic aspect as Māyā, born as a princess of Banavāsi, to win him.
