# Prabhuliṅgalīle (Cāmarasa)

`src:prabhulingalile` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Original title:** ಪ್ರಭುಲಿಂಗಲೀಲೆ
**Language:** Kannada
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md), [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md)
**Genre:** ṣaṭpadi kāvya
**Authors:** 
  - [Cāmarasa](../teachers/camarasa.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 1430 (court of Dēvarāya II of Vijayanagara); (confidence moderate)
**Availability:** digitized-original

Cāmarasa's bhāminī-ṣaṭpadi poem presenting Allama Prabhu as Śiva's descent: Pārvatī's Māyā sent to seduce him fails, Gorakṣa's adamantine body meets Allama's void body, Siddharāma and Muktāyakka are awakened, and Allama joins the śaraṇas at Kalyāṇa before his final union at Kadaḷi (Śrīśaila).
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: translation; name: Sanskrit Prabhuliṅgalīlā (print 1903; Muktabodha M00621) — see src:prabhulingalila-sanskrit

_Notes: Rendered into Tamil in the 17th c. (Turaimaṅkalam Śivaprakāśar's Pirapuliṅkalīlai, low confidence) and into Sanskrit._
