## Teachings (9: sourced 9)

### 1.1-3 <a id="tea-tejobindu-upanisad-1-1-3"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

One should meditate on the 'point of light' (tejobindu), subtle and beyond the senses, hard to see and reach; it is for the one who has conquered food, anger, association, the senses and the pairs of opposites.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: intermediate · types: ultimate, practice_

### 1.15-16 <a id="tea-tejobindu-upanisad-1-15-16"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

The limbs are fifteen: yama, niyama, tyāga, mauna, deśa, kāla, āsana, mūlabandha, dehasāmya, dṛksthiti, prāṇasaṃyamana, pratyāhāra, dhāraṇā, ātmadhyāna and samādhi.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice_

### 1.17-32 <a id="tea-tejobindu-upanisad-1-17-32"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Each limb is knowledge: yama is restraint of the senses by the knowledge 'all is Brahman'; niyama the flow of like thoughts; silence is not mere silence of speech (that is for children); the true seat is where Brahman-contemplation flows; the true root-lock is Brahman, root of the worlds; evenness of body is dissolving in the even Brahman, not the straightness of a dry tree; the true gaze is seeing the world as Brahman, not looking at the nose-tip; prāṇāyāma is stopping all modifications by seeing all as Brahman — negating the world is exhalation, 'I am Brahman' inhalation, steadiness retention.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: practice, ultimate_

concepts: [Limbs of yoga redefined as knowledge](../concepts/jnana-limbs.md) · practices: [Breath-control as knowledge](../practices/jnana-pranayama.md)

### 1.33 <a id="tea-tejobindu-upanisad-1-33"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

This (inner prāṇāyāma) is for the awakened; pressing the nose is for the ignorant.

### 1.40-41 <a id="tea-tejobindu-upanisad-1-40-41"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

In practising samādhi obstacles arise: lack of inquiry, sloth, desire for enjoyment, dissolution (laya), darkness, distraction, radiance (tejas), sweating and blankness; the knower of Brahman should avoid them.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: body-breath, meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice, powers-experiences_

### 1.42-47 <a id="tea-tejobindu-upanisad-1-42-47"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

By thinking of an existent one becomes it, by thinking of the void, void; by the thought of Brahman, fullness; those who merely talk of Brahman without that inner state, attached to pleasure, are born again and again.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: intermediate · types: consciousness-mind, karma-liberation_

### 2 (akhaṇḍaikarasa) <a id="tea-tejobindu-upanisad-2-akhandaikarasa"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Kumāra asks Śiva about the partless, single-flavoured pure consciousness; Śiva teaches that everything — self, world, teacher, scripture — is that consciousness alone.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_

teachers: [Śiva](../teachers/siva.md)

### 4 (jīvanmukta and videhamukta) <a id="tea-tejobindu-upanisad-4-jivanmukta-and-videhamukta"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Kumāra asks about the one liberated in life and the one liberated without body: the jīvanmukta abides as pure consciousness, free of 'I' and 'mine', while the body continues; the videhamukta is Brahman without remainder.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: karma-liberation_

concepts: [Liberation while living (jīvanmukti)](../concepts/jivanmukti.md), [Bodiless liberation (videhamukti)](../concepts/videhamukti.md)

### 5 (ātma-anātma-viveka) <a id="tea-tejobindu-upanisad-5-atma-anatma-viveka"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Nidāgha asks Ṛbhu to teach discrimination of self and not-self; Ṛbhu teaches that the Self is beyond the three states, bodies and sheaths, and all else is not-self and unreal.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, body-layers_

teachers: [Ṛbhu](../teachers/rbhu.md), [Nidāgha](../teachers/nidagha.md)
