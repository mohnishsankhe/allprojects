### gopi-section <a id="tea-krsna-sandarbha-gopi-section-2"></a>

The gopīs are Kṛṣṇa's own internal potencies, eternally his; Rādhā is foremost among them.

### paribhasa-section <a id="tea-krsna-sandarbha-paribhasa-section"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

'These are portions and partial portions of the Person, but Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān himself' (BhP 1.3.28) is the governing statement (paribhāṣā) that rules the interpretation of all other statements about the Lord's forms.
