Śrīpati Paṇḍita's Vīraśaiva commentary on the Brahma Sūtras, naming the doctrine Śakti-viśiṣṭādvaita: the non-duality of Śiva (liṅga) and the soul (aṅga), both qualified by Śakti, realised as their union (liṅgāṅga-sāmarasya).
