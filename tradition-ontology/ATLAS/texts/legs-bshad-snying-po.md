# Essence of Eloquence (drang nges legs bshad snying po)

Tsongkhapa's treatise distinguishing provisional and definitive meaning — through the Cittamātra hermeneutics of the Saṃdhinirmocana and then the Svātantrika and Prāsaṅgika Madhyamaka — insisting that the definitive must be established by reasoning, not by scripture alone.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### cittamatra-other-emptiness <a id="tea-legs-bshad-snying-po-cittamatra-other-emptiness"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Tsongkhapa, explaining the Saṃdhinirmocana's three natures, rejects the reading that the thoroughly established nature is an ultimate empty only of other things and not empty of itself: in the sūtra's own system the thoroughly established is the other-powered nature's emptiness of the imputational, and in the Madhyamaka nothing whatsoever, the ultimate included, is established by its own nature.

_level: bridging · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: dispute, ultimate_

### intro <a id="tea-legs-bshad-snying-po-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Because the scriptures themselves differ — some to be taken literally, others requiring interpretation — which is definitive cannot be settled by citing scripture alone (else endless regress); it must be established by stainless reasoning, following the great charioteers Nāgārjuna and Asaṅga.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: teacher-transmission, dispute_

### madhyamaka <a id="tea-legs-bshad-snying-po-madhyamaka"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Svātantrika hold that phenomena are posited by appearing to a non-defective awareness together with existing by their own character conventionally; the Prāsaṅgika hold that phenomena are posited only by the force of conceptual designation, with no existence from their own side even conventionally — this is the subtle difference in the object of negation.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, dispute_

concepts: [The object of negation (dgag bya)](../concepts/object-of-negation.md) · teachers: [Tsongkhapa Lobsang Drakpa](../teachers/tsongkhapa.md) · disputes: [Prāsaṅgika against Svātantrika](../debates/prasangika-svatantrika.md)

### madhyamaka-ultimate <a id="tea-legs-bshad-snying-po-madhyamaka-ultimate"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The ultimate truth is the mere absence of inherent existence — a non-affirming negation — and emptiness itself is empty; there is no phenomenon, the ultimate included, that is ultimately established.
