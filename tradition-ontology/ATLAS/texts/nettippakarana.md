# Nettippakaraṇa

**Alternate titles:** Netti
**Language:** Pali
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Genre:** hermeneutics

**Dates:** Tradition's account: taught by Mahākaccāna and approved by the Buddha; Scholarly account: c. 1st c. BCE – 1st c. CE; (confidence moderate)
**Availability:** digitized-original

A hermeneutical guide ('the guide') for explaining the suttas, by sixteen modes of conveying (hāra), five methods (naya) and eighteen root terms; ascribed to Mahākaccāna. Canonical only in the Burmese Khuddaka Nikāya.
