**Availability:** digitized-original

'Conditional Relations' (the Mahāpakaraṇa): sets out twenty-four modes of conditionality (paccaya) and applies them, positively and negatively, to all the triads and dyads of the Dhammasaṅgaṇī matrix — the Abhidhamma's full account of how dhammas condition one another.

## Teachings (10: skeleton 10)

### 1.1.1 <a id="tea-patthana-1-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The twenty-four conditions: root, object, predominance, proximity, contiguity, conascence, mutuality, support, decisive support, prenascence, postnascence, repetition, kamma, result, nutriment, faculty, jhāna, path, association, dissociation, presence, absence, disappearance and non-disappearance.

### 1.1.2 <a id="tea-patthana-1-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Root condition: the roots are conditions, by way of root, for the dhammas associated with them and for the matter they originate.

> Hetupaccayoti—hetū hetusampayuttakānaṁ dhammānaṁ taṁsamuṭṭhānānañca rūpānaṁ hetupaccayena paccayo.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: karma-liberation_

### 1.1.2 <a id="tea-patthana-1-1-2-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Object condition: the visible-form base is a condition for eye-consciousness and its associates, and so for each sense; all dhammas are object conditions for mind-consciousness; whatever mental dhammas arise with reference to whatever dhamma, that dhamma is their object condition.

> Yaṁ yaṁ dhammaṁ ārabbha ye ye dhammā uppajjanti cittacetasikā dhammā, te te dhammā tesaṁ tesaṁ dhammānaṁ ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo.

### 1.1.2 <a id="tea-patthana-1-1-2-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Decisive-support condition: earlier wholesome, unwholesome and indeterminate states are decisive supports for later ones; climate and food, a person, and a lodging are also decisive-support conditions.

> Utubhojanampi upanissayapaccayena paccayo. Puggalopi upanissayapaccayena paccayo. Senāsanampi upanissayapaccayena paccayo.

### 1.1.2 <a id="tea-patthana-1-1-2-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Kamma condition: wholesome and unwholesome kamma is a condition for resultant aggregates and for kamma-born matter; volition is a condition for its associated dhammas and the matter they originate.

> Kammapaccayoti—kusalākusalaṁ kammaṁ vipākānaṁ khandhānaṁ kaṭattā ca rūpānaṁ kammapaccayena paccayo. Cetanā sampayuttakānaṁ dhammānaṁ taṁsamuṭṭhānānañca rūpānaṁ kammapaccayena paccayo.

### 1.1.2 <a id="tea-patthana-1-1-2-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Support condition: the eye base supports eye-consciousness, and so on; and the matter in dependence on which the mind-element and mind-consciousness-element occur supports them and their associated dhammas.

> Yaṁ rūpaṁ nissāya manodhātu ca manoviññāṇadhātu ca vattanti, taṁ rūpaṁ manodhātuyā ca manoviññāṇadhātuyā ca taṁsampayuttakānañca dhammānaṁ nissayapaccayena paccayo.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: body-layers, consciousness-mind_

### 1.1.2 <a id="tea-patthana-1-1-2-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Conascence and mutuality: the four immaterial aggregates condition one another; the four great elements condition one another; at the moment of descent into the womb, mentality and materiality condition one another.

> Aññamaññapaccayoti—cattāro khandhā arūpino aññamaññapaccayena paccayo. Cattāro mahābhūtā aññamaññapaccayena paccayo. Okkantikkhaṇe nāmarūpaṁ aññamaññapaccayena paccayo.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: body-layers, karma-liberation_

### 1.1.2 <a id="tea-patthana-1-1-2-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Absence and disappearance: mental dhammas that have just ceased are conditions for the present mental dhammas by way of absence and of disappearance.

> Natthipaccayoti—samanantaraniruddhā cittacetasikā dhammā paṭuppannānaṁ cittacetasikānaṁ dhammānaṁ natthipaccayena paccayo.

> Pacchājātapaccayoti—pacchājātā cittacetasikā dhammā purejātassa imassa kāyassa pacchājātapaccayena paccayo.

### 1.1.3 <a id="tea-patthana-1-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The question method applies each condition to each class: 'Might a wholesome dhamma arise dependent on a wholesome dhamma by root condition? an unwholesome? an indeterminate? …' — the permutations of the matrix are worked through for all conditions.

> Siyā kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca kusalo dhammo uppajjeyya hetupaccayā. Siyā kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca akusalo dhammo …
