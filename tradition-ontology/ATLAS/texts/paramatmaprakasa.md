## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### book-1 <a id="tea-paramatmaprakasa-book-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The soul is of three kinds; in essence every soul is the supreme self, of knowledge, and is seen when the outer self (identified with body, possessions and sectarian marks) is given up and the inner self turns to it; knowing it, one is liberated.
