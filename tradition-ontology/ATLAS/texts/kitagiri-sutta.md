## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 14-21 <a id="tea-kitagiri-sutta-14-21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Seven kinds of persons: one freed both ways (touching the formless liberations and with effluents destroyed by wisdom), one freed by wisdom, the body-witness, one attained to view, one freed by faith, the Dhamma-follower and the faith-follower; for the last five there is still work to be done with diligence.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: general · stage: advanced · types: karma-liberation_

### 22-27 <a id="tea-kitagiri-sutta-22-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Final knowledge is not attained all at once but by gradual training, gradual practice, gradual progress: one with faith visits a teacher, attends, listens, remembers, examines, ponders, gains enthusiasm, will, weighs, strives and realises the ultimate truth with the body and sees it with wisdom.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: practice, teacher-transmission_
