## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### examination <a id="tea-fulfilling-hopes-of-disciples-examination"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Before entering the tantric relationship, teacher and disciple should examine each other carefully — the texts speak of taking even years — since a disciple who later disparages the master incurs grave harm; once accepted, the master is to be relied on as the Buddha.
