## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3-15 <a id="tea-yamaka-sutta-3-15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Yamaka holds that a monk with effluents destroyed is annihilated and perishes at death; Sāriputta leads him to see that the Tathāgata is not form, not in form, not apart from it … and since the Tathāgata is not apprehended as true and real even here in this life, it is not fitting to say he is annihilated; Yamaka answers that form, feeling … are impermanent, what is suffering has ceased and gone.
