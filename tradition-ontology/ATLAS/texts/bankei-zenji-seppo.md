Bankei Yōtaku's vernacular sermons: everyone is born with the unborn buddha-mind, which illuminates everything; do not turn it into anger or desire; no need for kōan struggle — simply abide in the Unborn.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### no-koan <a id="tea-bankei-zenji-seppo-no-koan"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Bankei tells his audience there is no need to raise a great doubt or to work on old cases; turning the buddha-mind into a 'doubt-mass' substitutes a doubt for the buddha-mind; simply abide in the Unborn.

### unborn <a id="tea-bankei-zenji-seppo-unborn"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

What everyone has from their parents is only the unborn buddha-mind; the unborn buddha-mind illuminates everything — while listening to me, if a crow caws or a sparrow chirps you know it without intending to; that is hearing with the Unborn. Do not trade the buddha-mind for anger, desire or self-concern.
