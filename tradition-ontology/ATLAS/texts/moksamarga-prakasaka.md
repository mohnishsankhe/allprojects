## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 7 <a id="tea-moksamarga-prakasaka-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Among Jains themselves wrong faith appears in two forms: those who cling only to the ultimate standpoint, talk of the pure self and neglect conduct, and those who cling only to the conventional standpoint, doing rites and vows without knowing the self; right faith holds both standpoints in their places.

### ch.7 <a id="tea-moksamarga-prakasaka-ch-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Among Jains themselves there are wrong views: those who hold only to the ultimate standpoint and neglect conduct (niścayābhāsī), those who hold only to outward conduct (vyavahārābhāsī), and those who mix both wrongly; the true path is knowing both in their places.
