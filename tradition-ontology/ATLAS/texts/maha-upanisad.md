## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-maha-upanisad-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Nārāyaṇa alone existed — not Brahmā, not Īśāna, not waters, fire and moon, heaven and earth; from his meditation arose fourteen persons and a maiden; later from his forehead came the three-eyed trident-bearer, and his sweat became the waters in which the golden egg arose, from which came Brahmā.

### 6.71 <a id="tea-maha-upanisad-6-71"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'This one is our own, that one a stranger' is the reckoning of the small-minded; for those of noble conduct the whole earth is one family.

> ayaṃ bandhur ayaṃ neti gaṇanā laghucetasām | udāracaritānāṃ tu vasudhaiva kuṭumbakam

_level: ultimate · standpoint: ethical-social · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ethics_

### 5 (fourteen stages) <a id="tea-maha-upanisad-5-fourteen-stages"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Ṛbhu teaches seven stages of ignorance (seed-waking, waking, great waking, waking-dream, dream, dream-waking, deep sleep) and seven stages of knowledge.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: intermediate · types: consciousness-mind_
