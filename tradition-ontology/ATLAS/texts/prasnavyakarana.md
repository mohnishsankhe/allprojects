The tenth Aṅga. The extant text treats the five 'doors of inflow' (āsrava: violence, falsehood, theft, unchastity, possessiveness) and the five 'doors of stopping' (saṃvara: the five great vows), with a celebrated list of names and praises of non-harm (ahiṃsā). The Sthānāṅga, Samavāyāṅga and Nandī describe a different original content (questions and answers, including on spells and prognostication); the commentators note the difference.
