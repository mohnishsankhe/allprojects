### 1 <a id="tea-milarepa-gurbum-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Returning to his cave at Red Rock Jewel Valley, Milarepa found demons who would not leave when he tried to drive them away; when he sang that demons are his own mind's projections and that he had no fear of them, they vanished.

### eight-worldly-concerns <a id="tea-milarepa-gurbum-eight-worldly-concerns"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In many songs Milarepa urges giving up the eight worldly concerns — gain and loss, pleasure and pain, praise and blame, fame and disgrace — and living in solitude.

### kailash <a id="tea-milarepa-gurbum-kailash"></a>

At Mount Kailash (Tise) Milarepa contested the Bön priest Naro Bönchung in displays of power and won; the tradition tells it as the mountain passing to the Buddhists.
