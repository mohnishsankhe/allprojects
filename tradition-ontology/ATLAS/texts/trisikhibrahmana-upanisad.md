A brāhmaṇa named Triśikhi visits Āditya's world: cosmogony; yoga of knowledge and of action; ten yamas and ten niyamas; seventeen postures; body, fire-centre, navel wheel, kuṇḍalī, channels and ten winds; grades of breath-control; death omens; withdrawal through eighteen vital points; element concentrations with vyūha deities; states mapped to body places; samādhi.

## Teachings (13: skeleton 13)

### 1-3 <a id="tea-trisikhibrahmana-upanisad-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The brāhmaṇa Triśikhi goes to Āditya's world and asks what the body is, what the life-breath, what the cause and what the Self; the Sun answers: all is Śiva.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, ultimate_

teachers: [Triśikhi](../teachers/trisikhi.md), [Āditya (the Sun as teacher)](../teachers/aditya.md)

### 19-27 <a id="tea-trisikhibrahmana-upanisad-19-27"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

From yoga comes knowledge and from knowledge yoga; yoga is twofold — of knowledge and of action; the yogin established in both never perishes.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice_

concepts: [Yoga and knowledge together](../concepts/yoga-jnana-samuccaya.md)

### 28-31 <a id="tea-trisikhibrahmana-upanisad-28-31"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In the yoga of knowledge the limbs are redefined: yama is dispassion toward body and senses; niyama constant attachment to the supreme; āsana indifference to all things; prāṇāyāma knowing the world as unreal; pratyāhāra turning the mind inward; dhāraṇā motionless mind; dhyāna 'I am pure consciousness'; samādhi the forgetting of meditation.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: practice, ultimate_

concepts: [Limbs of yoga redefined as knowledge](../concepts/jnana-limbs.md)

### 32-33 <a id="tea-trisikhibrahmana-upanisad-32-33"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ten yamas — non-injury, truth, non-stealing, celibacy, compassion, rectitude, forbearance, fortitude, moderate diet, purity; ten niyamas — austerity, contentment, faith, charity, worship of Hari, hearing Vedānta, modesty, discernment (mati), japa, vows.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: beginner · types: ethics_

concepts: [Ten yamas and ten niyamas](../concepts/ten-yamas-ten-niyamas.md)

### 34-52 <a id="tea-trisikhibrahmana-upanisad-34-52"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Seventeen postures are described — svastika, gomukha, vīra, yoga, padma, baddhapadma, kukkuṭa, uttāna-kūrma, dhanur, siṃha, bhadra, mukta, mayūra, matsya, siddha, paścimatāna and sukha; conquering posture conquers the three worlds.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: body-breath · stage: intermediate · types: practice_

practices: [Postures of the Yoga Upaniṣads](../practices/asanas-yoga-upanisads.md)

### 54-75 <a id="tea-trisikhibrahmana-upanisad-54-75"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The body is ninety-six digits; the fire-centre has a different shape in each species; at the navel is a twelve-spoked wheel ('I turn it by my māyā'), where the jīva moves like a spider in its web; kuṇḍalī lies coiled eightfold; the channels spread from the kanda.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: body-layers_

concepts: [The channels (nāḍī)](../concepts/nadis.md), [Kuṇḍalinī](../concepts/kundalini.md)

### 76-86 <a id="tea-trisikhibrahmana-upanisad-76-86"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The ten vital winds and their functions: prāṇa, apāna, samāna, udāna, vyāna; nāga for belching, kūrma for blinking, kṛkara for sneezing and hunger, devadatta for sleep and yawning, dhanañjaya which does not leave even the corpse.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: body-layers_

concepts: [The ten vital winds (vāyu)](../concepts/ten-vayus.md)

### 103-105 <a id="tea-trisikhibrahmana-upanisad-103-105"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Prāṇāyāma has three grades: the lowest produces sweat, the middle trembling, the highest rising of the body.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: body-breath, meditation · stage: advanced · types: powers-experiences, practice_

### 119-127 <a id="tea-trisikhibrahmana-upanisad-119-127"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Omens of death: when throbbing ceases in certain places of the limbs the remaining life is a year, six months, three months and so on; not seeing the tip of one's tongue, a few days.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: body-breath, meditation · stage: advanced · types: death-dying_

concepts: [Omens of death (ariṣṭa)](../concepts/death-omens.md)

### 128-133 <a id="tea-trisikhibrahmana-upanisad-128-133"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Pratyāhāra: holding the breath successively at eighteen vital points (marma) of the body, from the toes upward, and back.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: body-breath · stage: intermediate · types: practice, body-layers_

practices: [Withdrawal through the eighteen vital points (marmasthāna)](../practices/marmasthana-dharana.md)

### 134-143 <a id="tea-trisikhibrahmana-upanisad-134-143"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Element-concentrations with the vyūha deities: earth with Aniruddha, water with Nārāyaṇa, fire with Pradyumna, air with Saṅkarṣaṇa, space with Vāsudeva.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice_

practices: [The five element-concentrations (pañca-dhāraṇā) in the haṭha texts](../practices/element-dharanas.md)

### 148-151 <a id="tea-trisikhibrahmana-upanisad-148-151"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The states are placed in the body: waking between navel and heart, dream in the throat, deep sleep in the palate, the fourth between the brows, the beyond-fourth in the brahmarandhra.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind, body-layers_

concepts: [The fourth and beyond the fourth (turīya, turīyātīta)](../concepts/turiya-turiyatita.md)

### 160-161 <a id="tea-trisikhibrahmana-upanisad-160-161"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Samādhi is the knowledge 'I am the supreme Brahman'; the yogin becomes Brahman.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, karma-liberation_
