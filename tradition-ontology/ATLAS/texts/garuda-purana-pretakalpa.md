The classical Purāṇic manual of dying and the afterlife: the signs and pains of death, the thumb-sized person led off by Yama's messengers, the airy conveyance-body and the body built by the ten days' piṇḍas, the year-long road, the Vaitaraṇī, judgment, the preta state and its release through the sons' rites, gifts to the dying, and the means of liberation.
