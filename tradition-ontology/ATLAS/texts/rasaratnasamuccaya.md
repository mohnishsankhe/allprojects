A systematic compendium of mercurial and mineral medicine by 'the son of Siṃhagupta' (1.9): the twenty-seven siddhas of rasa (1.2-5), the minerals, gems and metals, the qualities of teacher and disciple and the laboratory (6), the impurities of mercury (11), and mercurial therapeutics. ### 1.2-9 <a id="tea-rasaratnasamuccaya-1-2-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

The twenty-seven giving success in rasa are Ādima, Candrasena, Laṅkeśa, Viśārada, Kapālin, Matta, Māṇḍavya, Bhāskara, Śūrasena, Ratnakośa, Śambhu, Sāttvika, Naravāhana, Indrada, Gomukha, Kambali, Vyāḍi, Nāgārjuna, Surānanda, Nāgabodhi, Yaśodhana, Khaṇḍa, Kāpālika, Brahmā, Govinda, Lampaka and Hari; further Rasāṅkuśa, Bhairava, Nandī, Svacchandabhairava, Manthānabhairava, Kākacaṇḍīśvara, the sage Vāsudeva, Śṛṅga the compiler of the kriyā-tantras, Rasendratilaka the yogin, Bhālukī, Maithila, Mahādeva, Narendra, Vāsudeva and Harīśvara; having consulted their tantras, the son of Siṃhagupta compiles this Rasaratnasamuccaya.

### 1.22-30 <a id="tea-rasaratnasamuccaya-1-22-30"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

Maheśvara, the first god of the worlds, was born as the great rasa to save the world. The merit of a hundred horse-sacrifices, of crores of cows and thousands of gold gifts, of bathing at all fords, comes from seeing mercury; who worships a mercurial liṅga with devotion gains the fruit of worshipping the liṅgas of the three worlds; eating, touching, giving, meditating on and worshipping mercury — this fivefold worship destroys great sins; whoever scorns the lord of mercury, Śambhu's supreme radiance, falls into a terrible hell; one who gives mercury, purified and properly prepared, to the sick gains the eternal fruit of great gifts and sacrifices.

### 5.47-73 <a id="tea-rasaratnasamuccaya-5-47-73"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

Impure copper destroys life and takes away lustre, potency and strength; nausea, purging, giddiness, burning and delusion are the hard-to-bear faults of (improperly prepared) copper; impure iron is not wholesome to use and certainly destroys life, strength and lustre.

### 6.2-10 <a id="tea-rasaratnasamuccaya-6-2-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

No science without the right sequence, no sequence without the science; one who acts knowing the science with its sequence succeeds. The teacher of rasa-knowledge is learned, skilled, versed in the rasa-śāstra, accomplished in mantra, heroic, steadfastly devoted to Śiva and the Goddess, knowing all the traditions and skilled in the work. The disciples are devoted to the teacher, of good conduct, truthful, firm in vows, diligent, knowing their own dharma, obedient, free of hypocrisy and envy, initiated, calm and devoted to mantra. Those who are unbelievers, of bad conduct, who cadge from other teachers, or seek the knowledge by theft, deceit or trickery gain no success with gems, mantras or herbs, and if they try out of delusion they destroy their own wealth, finding no happiness here or hereafter; so only when the teacher is pleased by devotion should the disciple receive the rasa-knowledge.

### 6.11-14 <a id="tea-rasaratnasamuccaya-6-11-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

The work of mercury should be carried out in a land free of affliction, in a righteous and pleasant kingdom, in a prosperous auspicious town graced by Umā and Maheśvara; the hall is to be spacious and pleasant, well provided with windows, and beside it a secluded rasa-pavilion.

### 11.17-25 <a id="tea-rasaratnasamuccaya-11-17-25"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

Mercury is not to be used where a vital point (marma) has been severed, nor in burns from caustic or fire. Purified it bears gentle fire; swooned it destroys disease; 'killed' it gives life and health. Poison, fire and dirt are its three natural impurities, causing death, burning and fainting; two from lead and tin cause dullness, distension and skin disease; and seven adventitious 'coverings' (kañcuka), from earth, rock and water, cause skin disease, dullness and wind-disorders — twelve impurities in all, declared by the experts in rasa.
