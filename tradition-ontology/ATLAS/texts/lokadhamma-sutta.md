## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-9 <a id="tea-lokadhamma-sutta-1-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Gain and loss, fame and disrepute, praise and blame, pleasure and pain turn around the world and the world turns around them; they come to the uninstructed and the noble disciple alike, but the uninstructed is attracted by the desirable and repelled by the undesirable and is not freed from suffering, while the noble disciple reflects that they are impermanent and does not cling.
