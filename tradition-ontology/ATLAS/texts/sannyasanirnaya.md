## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1ff <a id="tea-sannyasanirnaya-1ff"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

In the path of devotion renunciation is not the knowledge-path renunciation of the world as false; it is appropriate only when love in separation (viraha) makes household life impossible, and taken otherwise in the Kali age it can become an obstacle.
