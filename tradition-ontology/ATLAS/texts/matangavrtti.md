## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### vidyapada <a id="tea-matangavrtti-vidyapada"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The descent of Śiva's power (śaktipāta) occurs when the soul's mala has matured; its signs include devotion to Śiva and the wish for release; the soul so marked should be initiated.
