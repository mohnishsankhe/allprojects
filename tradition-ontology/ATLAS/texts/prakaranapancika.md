### 1 <a id="tea-prakaranapancika-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Śālikanātha opens by undertaking, following the Guru Prabhākara, an inquiry into the meaning of the injunction 'one's own Veda is to be studied' (svādhyāyo 'dhyetavyaḥ), to establish why Mīmāṃsā must be undertaken.

> svādhyāyavidhivākyārthavicāre prayatāmahe | prabhākaragurordṛṣṭyā mīmāṃsārambhasiddhaye

### 3 <a id="tea-prakaranapancika-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Error is not the appearance of something unreal: in 'this is silver' the 'this' is perceived and 'silver' remembered, and the two cognitions, though different from a true cognition of silver, are not grasped as different; memory here has lost its 'that' character (smṛti-pramoṣa). Ignorance is non-apprehension, not the appearing of the non-existent.

### 6 <a id="tea-prakaranapancika-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

How many means of knowledge are there? 'Fivefold is the means of knowledge': perception, inference, verbal testimony (śāstra), comparison and postulation — this is the Guru's view (excluding both fewer and more, e.g. six).

### 6/2 <a id="tea-prakaranapancika-6-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Memory is not a means of knowledge, because it depends on an earlier cognition; every experience (anubhūti) is. 'This is silver' is not one cognition but two — a grasping ('this'), which is valid, and a remembering ('silver'); only the silver-cognition is faulty, since it leads to action that fails.

> rajatamidamiti nedamekaṃ vijñānam, kintu dve ete vijñāne grahaṇasmaraṇarūpe |

### 7 <a id="tea-prakaranapancika-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The relation of word and meaning is not made by a person; it is known from the beginningless usage of elders, from which children learn the meanings of words, as their elders did before them.

### 7/2 <a id="tea-prakaranapancika-7-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There is no proof that everything arises or perishes at one time; things arise and perish gradually, and earlier people, like people now, were born of parents. Dharma and adharma are directed by the self to whom they belong; an Īśvara could not know others' dharma and adharma without senses, and the fruit comes from dharma and adharma together with place, time and circumstance, needing no conscious director. The Vaiśeṣika Īśvara who moves atoms by will is likewise rejected.

### 8 <a id="tea-prakaranapancika-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The self is distinct from cognition, senses and body; all-pervading, permanent, different in each body; it shines forth in the cognitions of objects — as their subject, not their object, as the goer is agent of going.

### 8/2 <a id="tea-prakaranapancika-8-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Release is the absolute ending of embodiment, caused by the complete exhaustion of dharma and adharma: the self, freed from body and senses, has all the sorrow of saṃsāra uprooted. It is a human goal as the cessation of suffering; a supreme bliss is not established, for scriptures do not make known accomplished things, and the discerning regard worldly pleasure itself as sorrow.

> ātyantikastu dehocchedo niśśeṣadharmādharmaparikṣayanibandhano mokṣa iti |

### 8/3 <a id="tea-prakaranapancika-8-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

One who, troubled by the sorrows of saṃsāra and without longing even for the pleasures mixed with them, rises for release, turns away from prohibited acts (causes of bondage) and from desire-prompted acts (means to prosperity); he exhausts the dharma and adharma already produced by experiencing them, and by self-knowledge supported by calm, restraint, celibacy and the like — enjoined for non-return ('he does not return') — he destroys the whole store of karma and is released. Self-knowledge is not subsidiary to the injunctions of rites.

### 8/4 <a id="tea-prakaranapancika-8-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Some (Maṇḍana) say release is the setting of ignorance (avidyā); this enters the Mahāyāna position and is an error of the Brahman-teachers, for ignorance is non-apprehension, not the appearance of an unreal world. Release is therefore not the setting of ignorance.
