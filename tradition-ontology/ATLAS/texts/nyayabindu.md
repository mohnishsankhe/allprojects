### 1.1 <a id="tea-nyayabindu-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The accomplishment of every human aim is preceded by right cognition; therefore it is expounded.

### 1.4-6 <a id="tea-nyayabindu-1-4-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Perception is free of conceptual construction and non-erroneous. Construction is a cognition whose appearance is fit to be joined with verbal expression; perception is cognition free of it and without an error caused by eye-disease, rapid motion, travelling in a boat, disturbance and the like.

> tatra pratyakṣaṃ kalpanāpoḍham abhrāntam / abhilāpasaṃsargayogyapratibhāsā pratītiḥ kalpanā

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind_

### 1.7-11 <a id="tea-nyayabindu-1-7-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Perception is fourfold: sense-cognition; mental cognition, produced by a sense-cognition as its immediately preceding condition together with the moment of the object immediately following its own object; the self-awareness of all minds and mental factors; and the cognition of yogins, born from the culmination of meditative cultivation of a real object.

> sarvacittacaittānām ātmasaṃvedanam / bhūtārthabhāvanāprakarṣaparyantajaṃ yogijñānaṃ ca

### 1.12-17 <a id="tea-nyayabindu-1-12-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Perception's object is the particular (svalakṣaṇa) — that object whose presence or absence makes a difference in the appearance of the cognition; it alone is ultimately real, because the mark of a real thing is the capacity for causal efficacy. Anything else is the general character, the object of inference.

> tad eva paramārthasat / arthakriyāsāmarthyalakṣaṇatvād vastunaḥ

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate_

terms: [svalakṣaṇa](../terms/svalaksana.md), [sāmānyalakṣaṇa](../terms/samanyalaksana.md), [arthakriyā](../terms/arthakriya.md), [paramārthasat](../terms/paramarthasat.md) · concepts: [Particular and universal (svalakṣaṇa / sāmānyalakṣaṇa)](../concepts/svalaksana-samanyalaksana.md) · teachers: [Dharmakīrti](../teachers/dharmakirti.md) · disputes: [Are universals (jāti, sāmānya) real?](../debates/reality-of-universals.md)

### 1.18-21 <a id="tea-nyayabindu-1-18-21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

That same perceptual cognition is the result of the means of knowledge, since it has the form of understanding the object; its likeness to the object is the means, since by virtue of it the understanding of the object is established.

> arthasārūpyam asya pramāṇam

### 2.3-5 <a id="tea-nyayabindu-2-3-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Inference for oneself is the cognition of the inferable object from a reason with three characteristics; the three characteristics are: the reason's certain presence in the inferable subject, its presence only in similar cases, and its certain absence from dissimilar cases.

> trairūpyaṃ punar liṅgasya anumeye sattvam eva sapakṣa eva sattvam asapakṣe ca asattvam eva niścitam

### 2.10-11 <a id="tea-nyayabindu-2-10-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Reasons with the three characteristics are only three: non-perception, own-nature and effect.

> trirūpāṇi ca trīṇy eva liṅgāni / anupalabdhiḥ svabhāvaḥ kāryaṃ ceti
