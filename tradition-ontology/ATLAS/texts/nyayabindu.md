# Nyāyabindu

`src:nyayabindu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Drop of Reasoning, rigs pa'i thigs pa
**Original title:** न्यायबिन्दु
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
**Genre:** sūtra-style prose manual
**Authors:** 
  - [Dharmakīrti](../teachers/dharmakirti.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
**Structure:** 3 chapters: perception, inference for oneself, inference for others
**Availability:** digitized-original

Dharmakīrti's concise manual: right cognition precedes the achievement of every human aim; it is of two kinds; perception is free of conceptual construction and non-erroneous, of four kinds; its object is the unique particular, the ultimately real; the three characteristics and three kinds of reason; and the fallacies of proof.
