## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 4 <a id="tea-vyakhyayukti-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Against the objection that the Mahāyāna sūtras are not the Buddha's word, Vasubandhu argues that much of the Buddha's teaching has been lost, that the Mahāyāna sūtras accord with the Dharma's marks and lead to awakening, and that the disciples' own canon contains passages presupposing them.
