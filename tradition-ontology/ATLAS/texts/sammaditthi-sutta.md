## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 3-8 <a id="tea-sammaditthi-sutta-3-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Right view is understanding the unwholesome (killing … wrong view) and its roots — greed, hatred and delusion — and the wholesome and its roots; so understanding, one abandons the underlying tendency to lust, removes aversion, extirpates the view and conceit 'I am', and makes an end of suffering here and now.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ethics, karma-liberation_

### 10-12 <a id="tea-sammaditthi-sutta-10-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Right view is also understanding the four nutriments that sustain beings — edible food, contact, mental volition and consciousness — their origin in craving, their cessation and the way.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: causal · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind, karma-liberation_

terms: [āhāra](../terms/ahara.md) · concepts: [The four nutriments](../concepts/four-aharas.md)

### 19-65 <a id="tea-sammaditthi-sutta-19-65"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Right view is understanding each link of dependent origination — ageing-and-death, birth, becoming, clinging, craving, feeling, contact, the six sense bases, name-and-form, consciousness, formations, ignorance — its origin, cessation and the way to its cessation (the noble eightfold path).

_level: unmarked · standpoint: causal · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation_

terms: [paṭiccasamuppāda](../terms/paticcasamuppada.md), [nāmarūpa](../terms/namarupa.md), [upādāna](../terms/upadana.md), [bhāva](../terms/bhava.md), [jāti](../terms/jati.md) · concepts: [The twelve links (nidāna)](../concepts/twelve-links.md), [Dependent origination](../concepts/dependent-origination.md)

### 66-70 <a id="tea-sammaditthi-sutta-66-70"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ignorance originates from the effluents and the effluents from ignorance: the three effluents of sensuality, becoming and ignorance, their origin, cessation and the way.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: causal · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation_
