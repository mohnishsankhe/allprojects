### 4.1.6.28-45 <a id="tea-skanda-purana-4-1-6-28-45"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Agastya to Lopāmudrā on the inner (mānasa) tīrthas: truth, forbearance, sense-control, compassion for all beings, straightforwardness, giving, self-restraint, contentment, celibacy, kind speech, knowledge, steadiness and austerity are tīrthas, and the supreme tīrtha is the purity of the mind; bathing the body in water is not bathing - the greedy, cruel and hypocritical remain impure after bathing in every tīrtha, as fish born and dying in water do not reach heaven; where one who has mastered the senses lives, there are Kurukṣetra, Naimiṣa and Puṣkara; the earthly tīrthas are holy through the power of earth and water and the presence of sages, so one should resort to both.

> satyaṃ tīrthaṃ kṣamā tīrthaṃ tīrtham indriyanigrahaḥ / sarvabhūtadayā tīrthaṃ tīrtham ārjavam eva ca

### 7.1.130 <a id="tea-skanda-purana-7-1-130"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

At Prabhāsa the liṅga Pāśupateśvara is the abode of the Pāśupatas; there the Pāśupata siddhas, brahmins devoted to celibacy, calm and self-controlled, reached supreme perfection and went to Śiva's abode in this very body; the chapter cites the ash formula 'fire is ash, wind is ash'.
