## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 2 <a id="tea-siddhantamuktavali-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Service (sevā) is the mind flowing toward him; to accomplish it there is service with body and with wealth; from that comes cessation of the sorrow of saṃsāra and the knowledge of Brahman.

> cetas tat-pravaṇaṃ sevā tat-siddhyai tanu-vittajā | tataḥ saṃsāra-duḥkhasya nivṛttir brahma-bodhanam ||

### 3-6 <a id="tea-siddhantamuktavali-3-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The one reality appears in three ways, like the Gaṅgā: to the worldly as mere water, to the ritualist as a sacred river conferring merit, to the devotee as a goddess; so Brahman is seen as the world, as Akṣara Brahman and as Puruṣottama.
