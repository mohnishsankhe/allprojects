# Tiruvāymoḻi

`src:tiruvaymoli` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Draviḍopaniṣad, Dramiḍopaniṣad, the Tamil Sāmaveda, Bhagavad-viṣaya (as expounded)
**Original title:** திருவாய்மொழி
**Language:** Tamil
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md)
**Part of:** [Nālāyira Divya Prabandham](nalayira-divya-prabandham.md)
**Location in parent:** Nāṉkām Āyiram (fourth thousand)
**Authors:** 
  - [Nammāḻvār](../teachers/nammalvar.md) — role: author; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Sung by Nammāḻvār at Kurukūr; held to be the Sāmaveda in Tamil.; scholarly: Nammāḻvār, c. 9th c. CE.; confidence: high
**Dates:** Tradition's account: born in the first year of the Kali age (hagiographies; one account gives the 43rd day); Scholarly account: c. 9th c. CE (some scholars 8th); (confidence low)
**Structure:** 1102 verses (pācurams) as counted in the Prabandham
**Availability:** digitized-original

Nammāḻvār's hundred decads (1102 verses), the fourth thousand of the Prabandham and the 'Tamil Upaniṣad' (Draviḍopaniṣad) of the Śrīvaiṣṇavas: the Lord's supremacy and accessibility, the gift of knowledge-and-love, surrender at Tiruvēṅkaṭam, service, love-longing in the voice of the girl Parāṅkuśa-nāyakī, her mother and friend, and in the last decads the soul's journey on the path of light and final union.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Ārāyirappaṭi (the 'Six Thousand')](arayirappati.md), [Irupattunālāyirappaṭi (the 'Twenty-four Thousand')](irupattunalayirappati.md), [Īṭu Muppattāṟāyirappaṭi (the 'Thirty-six Thousand')](itu-muppattarayirappati.md), [Oṉpatiṉāyirappaṭi (the 'Nine Thousand')](onpatinayirappati.md), [Paṉṉīrāyirappaṭi (the 'Twelve Thousand')](pannirayirappati.md)
