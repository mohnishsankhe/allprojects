The 'connected discourses on views' (SN 24): each wrong view, including the four doctrines of DN 2, arises because form, feeling, perception, formations and consciousness exist and are grasped.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 24.5 <a id="tea-ditthi-samyutta-24-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The view 'there is no gift … fool and wise alike are annihilated at death' arises when form, feeling, perception, formations and consciousness exist, by grasping and insisting on them; seen as impermanent and suffering, it does not arise.
