Vācaspati Miśra's commentary, the standard Sāṃkhya text of later tradition. It defines the three kinds of suffering; defends the impurity of sacrificial killing with a saying of Pañcaśikha; describes the path from hearing scripture through reasoning to long, uninterrupted, reverent practice ending in the knowledge 'I am not'; classifies inference; reduces other proposed means of knowledge to three; and explains how buddhi, taking on the reflection of consciousness, appears conscious.
