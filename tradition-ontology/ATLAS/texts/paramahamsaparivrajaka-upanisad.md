## Teachings (5: sourced 5)

### 1 (conduct) <a id="tea-paramahamsaparivrajaka-upanisad-1-conduct"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

One night in a village, three at a tīrtha, five in a town, seven at a sacred field; living in the way of the cow; the four rainy months in one place.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: advanced · types: ethics_

practices: [Wandering (pārivrājya) and the rains retreat](../practices/parivrajya.md)

### 1 (eligibility) <a id="tea-paramahamsaparivrajaka-upanisad-1-eligibility"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Brahmā asks Ādi-Nārāyaṇa about the wandering paramahaṃsa; the one with the fourfold means, having passed through the stages (or directly on dispassion, per the Jābāla rule), renounces with the consent of family or disciple.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: all · types: ethics_

concepts: [Who may renounce](../concepts/who-may-renounce.md) · teachers: [Brahmā (as first teacher)](../teachers/brahma.md), [Nārāyaṇa](../teachers/narayana.md)

### 1 (rite) <a id="tea-paramahamsaparivrajaka-upanisad-1-rite"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

He performs his own śrāddha and the virajā homa, transfers his powers to son, disciple or self, dissolves the Sāvitrī, and utters the declaration; if not fit for naked renunciation he keeps staff, girdle, loincloth, water-pot and cloth; the guru gives him 'tat tvam asi' with the praṇava.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ritual · path: ritual · stage: advanced · types: practice, teacher-transmission_

concepts: [The rite of renunciation](../concepts/sannyasa-rite.md) · practices: [The renunciation rite (saṃnyāsa-dīkṣā)](../practices/sannyasa-diksa.md)

### 1 (sixteen measures) <a id="tea-paramahamsaparivrajaka-upanisad-1-sixteen-measures"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

The Brahma-praṇava of sixteen measures corresponds to sixteen states from waking-in-waking to the fourth-in-the-fourth; it is worshipped by the paramahaṃsa, turīyātīta and avadhūta.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: sound-language, consciousness-mind_

concepts: [The measures of Oṃ (mātrā)](../concepts/omkara-matras.md), [The fourth and beyond the fourth (turīya, turīyātīta)](../concepts/turiya-turiyatita.md)

### 1 (thread and topknot) <a id="tea-paramahamsaparivrajaka-upanisad-1-thread-and-topknot"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

His thread is non-dual knowledge, his topknot steadfastness in meditation; 'know him as myself'.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, ethics_

concepts: [The inner topknot and sacred thread](../concepts/inner-sikha-yajnopavita.md)
