## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 7-17 <a id="tea-dhatuvibhanga-sutta-7-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A person consists of six elements — earth, water, fire, air, space, consciousness — six bases of contact, eighteen kinds of mental exploration and four foundations; each element is seen 'this is not mine, I am not this, this is not my self', and the mind becomes dispassionate towards it.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: body-layers, consciousness-mind_

terms: [dhātu](../terms/dhatu.md) · concepts: [The six elements](../concepts/six-dhatus.md) · teachers: [Pukkusāti](../teachers/pukkusati.md)

### 18-29 <a id="tea-dhatuvibhanga-sutta-18-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

What remains is consciousness, pure and bright; through it one knows pleasant, painful and neutral feelings as arising dependent on contact; pure equanimity remains, which he could direct to the formless spheres, but he knows those too are conditioned; he neither forms nor wills for becoming or non-becoming, clings to nothing, and is personally extinguished — like a lamp going out when its oil and wick are used up.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation, knowledge · stage: advanced · types: karma-liberation, consciousness-mind_

terms: [upekkhā](../terms/upekkha.md) · concepts: [Nibbāna](../concepts/nibbana.md)

### 30-31 <a id="tea-dhatuvibhanga-sutta-30-31"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'I am' is a conceiving, 'I am this', 'I shall be' … are conceivings; conceiving is a disease, a tumour, a dart. Going beyond all conceivings one is called a sage at peace: he is not born, does not age, does not die, is not shaken and does not long; where the currents of conceiving do not flow he is called a sage at peace.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: ultimate, karma-liberation_

terms: [maññanā](../terms/mannana.md), [muni](../terms/muni.md) · concepts: [Conceiving (maññanā)](../concepts/conceiving.md)
