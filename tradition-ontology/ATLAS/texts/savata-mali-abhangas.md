### abhaṅga 'kāndā muḷā bhājī' <a id="tea-savata-mali-abhangas-kanda-mula-bhaji"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Onion, radish and greens — all of it is my mother Viṭhābāī; garlic, chilli and coriander — all have become my Hari.

> कांदा मुळा भाजी । अवघी विठाबाई माझी ॥
