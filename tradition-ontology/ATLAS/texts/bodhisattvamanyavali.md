### v1-2 <a id="tea-bodhisattvamanyavali-v1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

Abandoning all doubt, value striving in practice; abandon sleep, dullness and laziness and always make effort. With mindfulness, alertness and conscientiousness always guard the doors of the senses; day and night, three times, again and again examine the mind-stream.

### v15-18 <a id="tea-bodhisattvamanyavali-v15-18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

Always be averse to wrongdoing. When a conceited mind arises, crush pride and remember your teacher's instructions; when a discouraged mind arises, raise your spirits; always meditate on emptiness. When objects of attachment or aversion arise, look on them as illusions and emanations; when you hear unpleasant words, regard them as echoes; when harm comes to the body, see it as the result of previous actions.

### v25-28 <a id="tea-bodhisattvamanyavali-v25-28"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · [AI-translated]

By way of the ten dharma practices strive day and night; in company examine your speech, alone examine your mind. Dedicate whatever roots of virtue are gathered in the three times to great unsurpassed awakening and spread the merit to beings; always make the great aspiration prayers of the seven limbs. Doing so, the two great accumulations of merit (and wisdom) are completed and the two obscurations exhausted; the human body obtained becomes meaningful and unsurpassed awakening is attained.

### v3-4 <a id="tea-bodhisattvamanyavali-v3-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

### v7-8 <a id="tea-bodhisattvamanyavali-v7-8"></a>

### v9-12 <a id="tea-bodhisattvamanyavali-v9-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · [AI-translated]

Always abandon busyness and stay in solitude; abandon idle talk and restrain speech. When you see your teacher or preceptor, serve them with respect; toward persons with the eye of dharma and toward beginners, generate the notion of teacher; when you see any being, generate the notion of father, mother, son or grandchild. Abandon bad friends and rely on spiritual friends; leave places of hostility and discomfort and go where it is happy.
