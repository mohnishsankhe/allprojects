## Teachings (83: skeleton 83)

### 1 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Asked by a deity how the inner and outer tangle of craving is disentangled, the Buddha answered: a wise man established in virtue, developing consciousness (concentration) and understanding, ardent and prudent, disentangles this tangle; the Visuddhimagga expounds that verse as virtue, concentration and understanding.

> sīle patiṭṭhāya naro sapañño, cittaṃ paññañ ca bhāvayaṃ, ātāpī nipako bhikkhu, so imaṃ vijaṭaye jaṭan ti.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: practice, karma-liberation_

terms: [sīla](../terms/sila.md), [samādhi](../terms/samadhi.md), [paññā](../terms/panna.md) · concepts: [Virtue, concentration, understanding as the frame of the Visuddhimagga](../concepts/three-trainings-vism.md)

### 1 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Purification' is to be understood as nibbāna, which is devoid of all stains and utterly pure; the path of purification is the means of reaching it.

> tattha visuddhī ti sabbamalavirahitaṃ, accantaparisuddhaṃ nibbānaṃ veditabbaṃ. tassā visuddhiyā maggo ti visuddhi-maggo.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: general · stage: all · types: ultimate, karma-liberation_

terms: [visuddhi](../terms/visuddhi.md), [nibbāna](../terms/nibbana.md) · concepts: [Nibbāna in the Abhidhamma and commentaries](../concepts/nibbana-abhidhamma.md)

### 1 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

What is virtue? The states beginning with volition present in one who abstains from killing and so on or who fulfils the practice of duties; as the Paṭisambhidāmagga says, volition is virtue, concomitants are virtue, restraint is virtue, non-transgression is virtue.

> kiṃ sīlan ti? cetanā sīlaṃ, cetasikaṃ sīlaṃ, saṃvaro sīlaṃ, avītikkamo sīlan ti.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: action · stage: all · types: ethics_

terms: [sīla](../terms/sila.md), [cetana](../terms/cetana.md) · practices: [The fourfold purity of virtue (catupārisuddhi-sīla)](../practices/catuparisuddhi-sila.md)

### 1 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Virtue is fourfold purity: restraint by the Pātimokkha, restraint of the sense faculties, purification of livelihood, and virtue concerning the requisites (using robes, food, lodging and medicine only after reflection on their purpose).

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: action · stage: all (bhikkhu) · types: ethics_

terms: [pātimokkha](../terms/patimokkha.md), [indriyasaṃvara](../terms/indriya-samvara.md) · practices: [The fourfold purity of virtue (catupārisuddhi-sīla)](../practices/catuparisuddhi-sila.md)

### 1 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-1-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Wrong livelihood for a monk includes scheming (kuhanā), talking (lapanā), hinting (nemittikatā), belittling (nippesikatā) and pursuing gain with gain — ways of obtaining requisites by deceit or pressure.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: action · stage: all · types: ethics_

terms: [ājīva](../terms/ajiva.md) · practices: [The fourfold purity of virtue (catupārisuddhi-sīla)](../practices/catuparisuddhi-sila.md)

### 2 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Blessed One allowed thirteen ascetic practices to clansmen who have given up worldly things and are without regard for body and life: refuse-rag wearing, triple-robe wearing, alms-food eating, house-to-house seeking, one-sessioner, bowl-food eating, later-food refusing, forest dwelling, tree-root dwelling, open-air dwelling, charnel-ground dwelling, any-bed-user and sitter's practice (never lying down).

> terasa dhutaṅgāni anuññātāni, seyyathīdaṃ: paṃsukūlikaṅgaṃ, tecīvarikaṅgaṃ, piṇḍapātikaṅgaṃ, sāpadānacārikaṅgaṃ, ekāsanikaṅgaṃ, pattapiṇḍikaṅgaṃ, khalupacchābhattikaṅgaṃ, āraññikaṅgaṃ, rukkhamūlikaṅgaṃ, abbhokāsikaṅgaṃ, sosānikaṅgaṃ, yathāsanthatikaṅgaṃ, nesajjikaṅgan ti.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: action · stage: intermediate · types: practice, ethics_

terms: [dhutaṅga](../terms/dhutanga.md) · practices: [The thirteen ascetic practices (dhutaṅga)](../practices/dhutanga.md)

### 2 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-2-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The ascetic practices suit one of greedy and one of deluded temperament: they are a painful way and an abiding in effacement; greed subsides through the painful way, and delusion is abandoned by the diligent through effacement.

> kassa dhutaṅgasevanā sappāyā ti rāgacaritassa c' eva mohacaritassa ca. kasmā? dhutaṅgasevanā hi dukkhāpaṭipadā c' eva sallekhavihāro ca.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice_

concepts: [The six temperaments (carita)](../concepts/six-temperaments.md) · practices: [The thirteen ascetic practices (dhutaṅga)](../practices/dhutanga.md)

### 3 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Concentration is wholesome one-pointedness of mind; it concentrates in the sense of placing consciousness and its concomitants evenly and rightly on a single object.

> kusalacittekaggatā samādhi. ken' aṭṭhena samādhī ti? samādhānaṭṭhena samādhi. kim idaṃ samādhānaṃ nāma? ekārammaṇe cittacetasikānaṃ samaṃ sammā ca ādhānaṃ ṭhapanan ti

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: practice, consciousness-mind_

terms: [samādhi](../terms/samadhi.md), [ekaggatā](../terms/ekaggata.md) · concepts: [The three kinds of concentration](../concepts/three-samadhis.md)

### 3 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-3-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Subjects suit temperaments: for the greedy, the ten foulnesses and mindfulness of the body; for the hating, the four divine abidings and the four colour kasiṇas; for the deluded and the speculative, mindfulness of breathing; for the faithful, the first six recollections; for the intelligent, mindfulness of death, the recollection of peace, the defining of the elements and the perception of repulsiveness in nutriment; the remaining kasiṇas and the formless states suit all.

> rāgacaritassa tāva:- ettha dasa asubhā kāyagatāsatī ti ekādasa kammaṭṭhānāni anukulāni. dosacaritassa cattāro brahmavihārā cattāri vaṇṇakasiṇānī ti aṭṭha.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice_

concepts: [The six temperaments (carita)](../concepts/six-temperaments.md), [The forty meditation subjects](../concepts/forty-kammatthanas.md)

### 3 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-3-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A monastery unfavourable to concentration has one of eighteen faults, such as being large, new, dilapidated, near a road, having a pond, leaves, flowers or fruit, being sought after, near a city, timber or fields, having incompatible persons, near a port, a border or a frontier, being unsuitable, and lacking good friends.

> tatr' ime aṭṭhārasa dosā:- mahattaṃ, navattaṃ, jiṇṇattaṃ, panthanissitattaṃ, soṇḍī, paṇṇaṃ, pupphaṃ, phalaṃ, patthanīyatā, nagarasannissitatā, dārusannissitatā, khettasannissitatā, visabhāgānaṃ puggalānaṃ atthitā, paṭṭanasannissitatā, paccantasannissitatā, rajjasīmasannissitatā, asappāyatā, kalyāṇamittānaṃ alābho ti

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice_

obstacles: [The eighteen faults of a monastery](../obstacles/eighteen-monastery-faults.md)

### 3 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-3-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Concentration is twofold as access and absorption: the unification reached through the six recollections, mindfulness of death, recollection of peace, the perception of repulsiveness in nutriment and the defining of the elements, and the unification preceding absorption, is access concentration; the unification immediately following the preliminary work is absorption.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: practice_

### 3 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-3-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

One who would develop concentration must first sever whichever of the ten impediments he has: a dwelling, a family, gain, a following, building work, travel, kin, affliction, books, and supernormal powers.

> āvāso ca kulaṃ lābho, gaṇo kammañ ca pañcamaṃ; addhānaṃ, ñāti, ābādho, gantho, iddhī ti te dasā ti ime dasa paḷibodhā nāma.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice_

### 3 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-3-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The worldling's supernormal power is hard to keep up, like an infant lying on its back or young corn, and is broken by a trifle; it is an impediment for insight but not for concentration, since it is reached through concentration — so one who seeks insight should sever it.

> iddhī ti pothujjanikā iddhi. sā hi uttānaseyyakadārako viya, taruṇasassaṃ viya ca dupparihārā hoti, appamattaken' eva bhijjati. sā pana vipassanāya paḷibodho hoti, na samādhissa

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: powers-experiences, practice_

terms: [iddhi](../terms/iddhi.md) · concepts: [The six direct knowledges](../concepts/six-abhinnas.md) · obstacles: [The ten impediments (paḷibodha)](../obstacles/ten-palibodhas.md)

### 3 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-3-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The meditation subject is to be taken from a good friend who gives it: one who is lovable, respected, esteemed, a counsellor, patient with speech, who can give deep talk and does not urge to what is useless; the Buddha is the best such friend, for relying on him beings subject to birth are freed from birth.

> piyo garu bhāvanīyo vattā ca vacanakkhamo, gambhīrañ ca kathaṅkattā, no ca ṭhāne niyojaye ti.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: beginner · types: teacher-transmission_

### 3 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-3-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There are six temperaments — greedy, hating, deluded, faithful, intelligent and speculative; some posit fourteen by combining them, but combinations would be endless, so in brief there are six; 'temperament', 'nature' and 'predominance' mean the same.

> cariyā ti cha cariyā: rāgacariyā, dosacariyā, mohacariyā, saddhācariyā, buddhicariyā, vitakkacariyā ti. keci pana rāgādīnaṃ saṃsaggasannipātavasena aparāpi catasso, tathā saddhādīnan ti imāhi aṭṭhahi saddhiṃ cuddasa icchanti.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: meditation · stage: beginner · types: consciousness-mind, practice_

### 3 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-3-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Because faith is strong in the wholesome moments of one of greedy temperament, faith is to the wholesome side what greed is to the unwholesome: as greed seeks sense objects, faith seeks virtue and other special qualities.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: meditation, devotion · stage: beginner · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [saddhā](../terms/saddha.md) · concepts: [The six temperaments (carita)](../concepts/six-temperaments.md)

### 3 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-3-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The forty meditation subjects: ten kasiṇas (earth, water, fire, air, blue, yellow, red, white, light, limited space), ten foulnesses (bloated, livid, festering, cut-up, gnawed, scattered, hacked-and-scattered, bleeding, worm-infested, skeleton), ten recollections (Buddha, Dhamma, Saṅgha, virtue, generosity, deities, death, mindfulness of the body, mindfulness of breathing, peace), four divine abidings, four formless states, one perception and one defining.

> dasa kasiṇā, dasa asubhā, dasa anussatiyo, cattāro brahmavihārā, cattāro āruppā, ekā saññā, ekaṃ vavatthānan ti.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice_

### 3 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-3-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ten subjects bring only access — the eight recollections other than mindfulness of the body and of breathing, the perception of repulsiveness in nutriment and the defining of the elements; the rest bring absorption: the ten kasiṇas and mindfulness of breathing all four jhānas, the ten foulnesses and body-mindfulness the first jhāna, the first three divine abidings three jhānas, the fourth divine abiding and the four formless states the fourth.

> ṭhapetvā kāyagatāsatiñ ca ānāpānassatiñ ca avasesā aṭṭha anussatiyo, āhāre paṭikūlasaññā, catudhātuvavatthānan ti imān' eva h' ettha dasa kammaṭṭhānāni upacārāvahāni; sesāni appanāvahānī ti

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: practice_

### 4 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Developing the earth kasiṇa: when the disk, adverted to with eyes shut, comes into focus as when the eyes were open, the learning sign has arisen; as he keeps striking at it with thought, the hindrances are suppressed, the defilements subside, the mind is concentrated with access concentration and the counterpart sign arises — unlike the learning sign it shows no kasiṇa fault, like a mirror disk drawn from its case.

> tass' evaṃ karontassa anukkamena nīvaraṇāni vikkhambhanti, kilesā sannisīdanti, upacārasamādhinā cittaṃ samādhiyati, paṭibhāganimittaṃ uppajjati.

### 4 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-4-3"></a>

For absorption, ten kinds of skill are to be applied (making the basis clean, balancing the faculties, skill in the sign, exerting, restraining and gladdening the mind when needed, looking on with equanimity, avoiding the unconcentrated, cultivating the concentrated, resolution upon concentration), with evenness of energy.

### 4 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-4-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Rapture is fivefold: minor rapture only raises the hairs; momentary rapture is like flashes of lightning; showering rapture breaks over the body again and again like waves on the shore; uplifting rapture is strong enough to lift the body into the air; pervading rapture suffuses the whole body.

> sā pan' esā khuddikā pīti, khaṇikā pīti, okkantikā pīti, ubbegā pīti, pharaṇā pītī ti pañcavidhā hoti. tattha khuddikā pīti sarīre lomahaṃsamattam eva kātuṃ sakkoti. khaṇikā pīti khaṇe khaṇe vijjuppātasadisā hoti.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: powers-experiences, practice_

### 4 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-4-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Rapture, maturing, perfects bodily and mental bliss; bliss, maturing, perfects the threefold concentration — momentary, access and absorption.

> sukhaṃ gabbhaṃ gaṇhantaṃ paripākaṃ gacchantaṃ tividhaṃ samādhiṃ paripūreti, khaṇikasamādhiṃ, upacārasamādhiṃ, appanāsamādhin ti.

### 4 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-4-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Before going on, the meditator should master the first jhāna in five ways: mastery in adverting, in attaining, in resolving (its duration), in emerging and in reviewing.

> tatr' imā pañcavasiyo: -āvajjanavasī, samāpajjanavasī, adhiṭṭhānavasī, vuṭṭhānavasī, paccavekkhaṇavasī ti.

### 5 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-5"></a>

The remaining kasiṇas are developed like the earth kasiṇa, each on its own device (water in a vessel, a fire seen through a hole in a screen, wind moving leaves, coloured disks or flowers, light cast through a hole on a wall, space seen through a hole); they are the basis for the corresponding supernormal powers.

### 6 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The foulness subject is grasped in eleven ways: the learning sign arises through looking at the corpse with open eyes; applying the mind to it, the counterpart sign arises; there he reaches absorption, and standing in absorption and developing insight he realizes arahantship.

> ekādasavidhena pana nimittaggāhaṃ sampādento kammaṭṭhānaṃ upanibandhati: tassa hi cakkhūni ummīletvā olokanapaccayā uggahanimittaṃ uppajjati, tasmiṃ mānasaṃ cārentassa paṭibhāganimittaṃ uppajjati; tattha mānasaṃ cārento appanaṃ pāpuṇāti; appanāya ṭhatvā vipassanaṃ vaḍḍhento arahattaṃ sacchikaroti.

### 6 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-6-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The ten foulnesses are ten kinds of corpse — bloated, livid, festering, cut up, gnawed, scattered, hacked and scattered, bleeding, worm-infested and a skeleton — each counteracting a particular kind of lust; one should go to them with care (informing the senior monk, noting the route and surroundings), and a body of the opposite sex is not suitable for a beginner.

### 19 <a id="tea-visuddhimagga-19"></a>

Purification by overcoming doubt: as a skilled physician seeks the origin of a disease, the meditator seeks the causes and conditions of mentality-materiality and, seeing that it occurred by conditions in the past and will in the future as it does now, abandons doubt about the three periods.
