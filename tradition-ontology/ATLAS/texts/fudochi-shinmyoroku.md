## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### no-stopping <a id="tea-fudochi-shinmyoroku-no-stopping"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Immovable wisdom is a mind that does not stop anywhere: when the mind stops on the opponent's sword it is taken by the sword; letting the mind fill the whole body without abiding, it responds freely.
