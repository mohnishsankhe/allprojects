## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-20 <a id="tea-cankarpa-nirakaranam-1-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Umāpati refutes nine neighbouring views of soul, bonds and release — among them that the freed soul becomes equal to Śiva in every respect (Śivasamavāda), that the soul passes over into Śiva (Saṅkrāntavāda), that bonds remain like stone (Pāṣāṇavāda) — and upholds inseparable union in which the soul enjoys Śiva's bliss.
