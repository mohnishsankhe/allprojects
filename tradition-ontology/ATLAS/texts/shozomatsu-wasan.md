Hymns composed in old age on the three ages of the Dharma, doubt of the Buddha's wisdom, and Shinran's lament over his own untruthful heart; with the appended note on jinen hōni in some versions.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### lament <a id="tea-shozomatsu-wasan-lament"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Although I have taken refuge in the true Pure Land teaching, a true and sincere mind is hard to have; I am false and untrue, without the least pure mind. Outwardly each of us shows wisdom, goodness and diligence; inwardly greed, anger, wrongdoing and deceit abound, like a serpent or scorpion.

### mappo <a id="tea-shozomatsu-wasan-mappo"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

More than two thousand years have passed since Śākyamuni entered nirvāṇa; the true and semblance ages are over — disciples of the last age, lament! In the last age of five turbidities beings cannot practise and realise; the teachings the Buddha left have all gone to the Dragon Palace; but Amida's universal vow remains.
