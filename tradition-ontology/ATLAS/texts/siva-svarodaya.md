The science of the flow of breath (svara) in the left (iḍā, moon), right (piṅgalā, sun) and central (suṣumnā) channels: the daily alternation of the nostrils, the five elements (tattvas) that arise in each flow, which actions suit which flow, and the use of the breath to foretell events and death. The central flow suits only meditation.

### topic.actions <a id="tea-siva-svarodaya-topic-actions"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Steady and auspicious acts are to be done when the moon (left) flows, active and harsh acts when the sun (right) flows; when suṣumnā flows, worldly acts fail and only meditation and yoga should be done.

### topic.three-flows <a id="tea-siva-svarodaya-topic-three-flows"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The breath flows through iḍā (left, moon), piṅgalā (right, sun) or suṣumnā (both); the flows alternate through the day in a regular order; the knowledge of svara is the most secret of sciences.
