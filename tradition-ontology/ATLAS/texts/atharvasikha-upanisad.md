## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-atharvasikha-upanisad-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Pippalāda, Aṅgiras and Sanatkumāra ask Atharvan what is the first meditation; Atharvan: Oṃ, the imperishable, with its measures corresponding to worlds, Vedas, fires and deities (Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra, and the half-measure).

### 2-3 <a id="tea-atharvasikha-upanisad-2-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Śiva alone is to be meditated upon, the doer of good, all else abandoned; this is the end of the Atharvaśikhā.
