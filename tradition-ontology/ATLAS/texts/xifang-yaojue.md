## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 104a19-22 <a id="tea-xifang-yaojue-104a19-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Those who do this work single-mindedly recollect Amitābha and dedicate all roots of good to birth there; Amitābha's original vow is to save the Sahā world — from a whole life's practice down to ten recitations at death, all are certainly born; since students doubt because scriptures seem to conflict, fourteen points are harmonised.

> 作斯業者，專念彌陀，一切善根迴生彼國；彌陀本願，誓度娑婆，上盡現生一形、下至臨終十念，俱能決定，皆得往生。
