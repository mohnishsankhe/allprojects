## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### passim <a id="tea-radha-sudhanidhi-dasi"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The poet asks not for Kṛṣṇa directly but to become Rādhā's maidservant, serving her in the bower; her grace is the only gate to the love of the pair.

### verse 1 <a id="tea-radha-sudhanidhi-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Obeisance even to the direction where dwells the daughter of Vṛṣabhānu — by the breeze from whose playing garment-hem Madhusūdana himself, whom the lords of yogīs cannot reach, feels his life fulfilled.
