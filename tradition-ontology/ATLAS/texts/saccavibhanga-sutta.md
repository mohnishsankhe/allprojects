## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 5-31 <a id="tea-saccavibhanga-sutta-5-31"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Sāriputta is like one who gives birth, Moggallāna like one who nurses the newborn: Sāriputta trains others to the fruit of stream-entry, Moggallāna to the supreme goal. Sāriputta then analyses the four noble truths in detail.
