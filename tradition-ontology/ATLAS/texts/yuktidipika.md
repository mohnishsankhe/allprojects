**Structure:** introductory verses and a commentary on the SK in four prakaraṇas and eleven āhnikas
**Availability:** digitized-original

The most extensive old commentary on the SK. It defends Sāṃkhya against rival schools, preserves the divergent views of earlier Sāṃkhya teachers (Vārṣagaṇya, Vindhyavāsin, Pañcādhikaraṇa, Paurika, the Sāṃkhya teacher Patañjali), enumerates the sixty topics of the Ṣaṣṭitantra and gives a list of teachers between Pañcaśikha and Īśvarakṛṣṇa.
