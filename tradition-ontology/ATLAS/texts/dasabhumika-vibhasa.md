## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 9 <a id="tea-dasabhumika-vibhasa-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Among the ways of the buddha-dharma there is a difficult path, like travelling by land on foot, and an easy path, like sailing by boat on water: for some, the easy practice of faith — calling on the names of buddhas such as Amitābha and keeping them in mind — quickly brings them to the stage of non-retrogression.

### 41b02-06 <a id="tea-dasabhumika-vibhasa-41b02-06"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Asked whether there is an easy way to reach non-retrogression quickly, the teacher first rebukes the question as fit for the weak, then says: the Buddha's teaching has countless gates; as worldly roads are hard or easy — walking overland is painful, sailing on water is pleasant — so in the bodhisattva path some advance by strenuous effort, others by the easy practice of faith as a skilful means quickly reach non-retrogression.

> 佛法有無量門，如世間道，有難、有易；陸道步行則苦，水道乘船則樂。菩薩道亦如是：或有勤行精進，或有以信方便易行疾至阿惟越致者。

### 41b13-17 <a id="tea-dasabhumika-vibhasa-41b13-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Naming ten buddhas of the ten directions, the verse says: whoever wishes quickly to reach the stage of non-retrogression should with a reverent mind hold to and call their names; a bodhisattva wishing to reach non-retrogression in this body should recollect these buddhas and call their names.

> 若人疾欲至， 不退轉地者，應以恭敬心， 執持稱名號。

### 43a09-20 <a id="tea-dasabhumika-vibhasa-43a09-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Among the many buddhas to be recollected, Amitābha's original vow is this: 'If anyone thinks of me, calls my name and takes refuge in me, he at once enters the stage of certainty and attains perfect awakening'; therefore one should always recollect him. In verses of praise the author says: one who can think of this Buddha of immeasurable power and majesty at once enters the stage of certainty; therefore I always think of him.

> 阿彌陀佛本願如是：「若人念我——稱名、自歸，即入必定，得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提。」是故常應憶念。……人能念是佛， 無量力威德，即時入必定， 是故我常念。

### 43b18-19 <a id="tea-dasabhumika-vibhasa-43b18-19"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If one plants good roots but doubts, the lotus does not open; for one whose faith is pure, the lotus opens and he sees the Buddha.

> 若人種善根， 疑則華不開，信心清淨者， 華開則見佛。
