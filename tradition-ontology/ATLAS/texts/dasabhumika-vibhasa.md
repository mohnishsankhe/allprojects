## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 9 <a id="tea-dasabhumika-vibhasa-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Among the ways of the buddha-dharma there is a difficult path, like travelling by land on foot, and an easy path, like sailing by boat on water: for some, the easy practice of faith — calling on the names of buddhas such as Amitābha and keeping them in mind — quickly brings them to the stage of non-retrogression.
