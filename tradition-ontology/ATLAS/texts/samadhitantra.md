### 4 <a id="tea-samadhitantra-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In all embodied beings the self is of three kinds: external, internal and supreme; one should attain the supreme by means of the internal, and give up the external.

> bahir antaḥ paraś ceti tridhātmā sarvadehiṣu / upeyāt tatra paramaṃ madhyopāyād bahis tyajet

### 5 <a id="tea-samadhitantra-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The external self is the sense of self in the body; the internal self is the self-sense in the self, free of mental error; the supreme self is utterly pure.
