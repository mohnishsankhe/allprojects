## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-17 <a id="tea-uraga-sutta-1-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The monk who removes anger as one removes snake venom with herbs, who has cut off lust, craving and conceit, who has not overreached nor fallen short, who knows 'all this is unreal', gives up the near and the far shore as a snake sheds its worn-out skin.
