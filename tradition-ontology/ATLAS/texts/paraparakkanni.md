### aṉpar paṇi <a id="tea-paraparakkanni-anpar-pani"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

If you make me a servant for the service of your devotees, the state of bliss will come of itself, O Supreme.

### ellōrum iṉpuṟṟu <a id="tea-paraparakkanni-ellorum-inpurru"></a>
