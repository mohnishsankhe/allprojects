## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3 <a id="tea-harsacarita-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

King Puṣpabhūti, a devout Māheśvara, hears of Bhairavācārya, a great Śaiva from the south famed for many kinds of knowledge and power, with thousands of disciples; he honours him and helps him in a night rite at the great cremation ground, which succeeds and brings the king a boon.
