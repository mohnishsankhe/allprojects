**Availability:** digitized-original

Aṣṭāvakra answers Janaka's question how knowledge, liberation and dispassion are attained: you are not the elements or the body but the one witness, pure consciousness; rest in awareness and you are free now; bondage and freedom are only notions ('as one thinks, so one becomes'). Janaka voices his awakening (ch. 2 and ch. 6-20 in part), and the text describes the liberated one (18) who is beyond effort, meditation, duty and even the idea of liberation.

### 1.1-2 <a id="tea-astavakra-gita-1-1-2"></a>

Janaka asks how knowledge is gained, how liberation comes and how dispassion is attained; Aṣṭāvakra: if you seek liberation, shun sense-objects like poison and drink forgiveness, sincerity, compassion, contentment and truth like nectar.

### 1.3-6 <a id="tea-astavakra-gita-1-3-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

You are not earth, water, fire, air or ether; know the Self as the witness of these, pure consciousness, for liberation. If you separate yourself from the body and rest in consciousness, you will at once be happy, peaceful and free. You belong to no caste or stage of life, are not an object of the senses; unattached, formless, witness of all, be happy. Dharma and adharma, pleasure and pain belong to the mind, not to you; you are neither doer nor enjoyer — you are ever free.

> yadi dehaṃ pṛthakkṛtya citi viśrāmya tiṣṭhasi / adhunaiva sukhī śānto bandhamukto bhaviṣyasi

### 1.7-10 <a id="tea-astavakra-gita-1-7-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

You are the one seer of all, ever free; your only bondage is that you see the seer as other. Bitten by the black serpent of the ego 'I am the doer', drink the nectar of faith 'I am not the doer' and be happy; burn the forest of ignorance with the fire of conviction 'I am the one pure awareness'; that in which the universe appears, imagined like a snake in a rope, is you — bliss, supreme bliss, awareness.

### 1.14-15 <a id="tea-astavakra-gita-1-14-15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

Long have you been bound by the noose of identification with the body; cut it with the sword of knowledge 'I am awareness' and be happy. You are unattached, actionless, self-luminous, stainless; this alone is your bondage, that you practise samādhi.

### 2.1-4 <a id="tea-astavakra-gita-2-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

Janaka: Ah, I am stainless, peaceful awareness beyond nature; all this time I was deceived by delusion. As I alone illumine this body, so I illumine the world; so the whole world is mine, or nothing is. Having given up the body and the world, by some skill I now see the supreme Self. As waves, foam and bubbles are not other than water, the world that has come out of the Self is not other than the Self.

### 3.1-8 <a id="tea-astavakra-gita-3-1-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

Aṣṭāvakra tests Janaka: knowing the Self indestructible and one, how can you delight in acquiring wealth? From ignorance of the Self comes attraction to objects, as greed for silver in a mother-of-pearl; it is a wonder that one who has heard of the Self still clings to sex, still feels 'mine', still fears liberation.

The pure-minded is fulfilled by even a casual instruction; another stays bewildered after inquiring all his life. Liberation is distaste for sense-objects; bondage is relish for them — this much is knowledge; now do as you please.

### 16.1-11 <a id="tea-astavakra-gita-16-1-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

Recite or hear many scriptures, yet you will not abide in yourself (svāsthya) without forgetting everything; everyone is miserable through effort; the dispassionate hates objects, the passionate craves them, the one beyond grasping and releasing is neither; as long as desire lives, the notion of what to take and what to leave is the sprout of the tree of saṃsāra; even if Hara, Hari or Brahmā be your teacher, there is no abiding in oneself without forgetting all.

### 18.1-6 <a id="tea-astavakra-gita-18-1-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

Salutation to that one bliss, peace and light, on whose awakening delusion becomes like a dream; without giving up everything no one is happy; this existence is only imagination, nothing in reality; the state of the Self is neither far nor attained by contraction — it is already attained, beyond thought, effortless, changeless, stainless; when delusion alone ceases, the ones whose sight is unveiled shine free of sorrow.

### 18.96-100 <a id="tea-astavakra-gita-18-96-100"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

The liberated one is neither happy nor miserable, neither dispassionate nor attached, neither seeker nor liberated; not distracted in distraction, not absorbed in samādhi, not dull in dullness, not learned in learning; content, equal everywhere, not remembering what was done or left undone; not pleased when praised nor angry when blamed, not fearing death nor delighting in life, seeking neither crowd nor forest, he remains the same wherever he is.

### 19.1-8 <a id="tea-astavakra-gita-19-1-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

Janaka: with the tongs of the knowledge of truth I have pulled out the thorn of manifold opinions from my heart; for me abiding in my own glory, where is dharma, pleasure or wealth, duality or non-duality, past or future, waking, dream, sleep or the fourth, death or life, dissolution or samādhi? Enough of talk of the three aims, of yoga and of knowledge, for me at rest in the Self.

### 20.1-14 <a id="tea-astavakra-gita-20-1-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

Janaka: in my stainless nature where are the elements, body, senses or mind, the void or despair; where knowledge or ignorance, bondage or liberation, prārabdha karma, liberation while living or bodiless isolation; where the doer or enjoyer, the world, the seeker, the yogin, the knower; where teaching or scripture, disciple or teacher? For me, attributeless Śiva, nothing arises at all.
