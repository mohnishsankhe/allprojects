## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-sivastotravali-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Homage to the one rich in devotion, for whom, without meditation, without repetition of mantra, without any prescribed procedure, the manifestation of Śiva simply occurs.

> na dhyāyato na japataḥ syād yasyāvidhipūrvakam / evam eva śivābhāsas taṃ namo bhaktiśālinam

_level: bridging · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion · stage: all · types: practice, karma-liberation_
