Bhṛgu's Vaikhānasa text on temple ritual; it opens with homage to Vikhanas 'whose father is Nārāyaṇa, whose mother is Haripriyā, whose disciples are Bhṛgu and the other sages' (1.1) and explains the fixed and movable images as Viṣṇu's partless and partite forms (11.148–152).
