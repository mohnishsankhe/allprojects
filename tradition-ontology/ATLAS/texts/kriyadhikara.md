### 11.147-152 <a id="tea-kriyadhikara-11-147-152"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

After worshipping the fixed image (dhruva) with eight services by mantra, one should perform worship with thirty-two services in the kautuka image. The supreme Brahman, Nārāyaṇa, is called the highest Self; its highest form is subtle, imperishable and partless (niṣkala), and the fixed image stands in its place; since partless worship is impossible, worship of the fixed image is only the placing of flowers. Viṣṇu's form with parts (sakala), gross and the cause of all, is represented by the kautuka image, and there worship is to be done, because offerings of flowers and the like can be made to it; worship is prescribed where there is a support and parts, while the fixed image, being partless, is without support.
