## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-tarkabhasa-moksakaragupta-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

People who act with forethought follow a means of valid cognition to achieve every human aim; a means of valid cognition is right cognition whose object is not previously known; it is that by which the object is measured.

> pramāṇaṃ samyagjñānamapūrvagocaram

### 3.tenets <a id="tea-tarkabhasa-moksakaragupta-3-tenets"></a>

The treatise closes with the four Buddhist tenet systems — Vaibhāṣika, Sautrāntika, Yogācāra and Mādhyamika — as a graded ascent in understanding.
