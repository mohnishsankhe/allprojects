### being-time <a id="tea-uji-being-time"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

'Being-time' (uji) means that time itself is already being and all being is time; each moment and each thing abides in its own dharma-position; being-time passes (kyōryaku) from today to tomorrow and from today to yesterday; the self set out in array is the whole world.
