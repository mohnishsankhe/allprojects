## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 2-8 <a id="tea-vattha-sutta-2-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As a dirty cloth takes dye badly and a clean cloth well, a defiled mind can expect a bad destination and an undefiled mind a good one. The defilements of mind are covetousness, ill will, anger, hostility, contempt, domineering, envy, avarice, deceit, fraud, obstinacy, presumption, conceit, arrogance, vanity and negligence; knowing them as defilements the monk abandons them and gains confidence in the Buddha, Dhamma and Saṅgha.

### 18-20 <a id="tea-vattha-sutta-18-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

To the brahmin Sundarika Bhāradvāja who believes in purification by bathing in the Bāhukā and other rivers: a fool may bathe there forever but will not cleanse his dark deeds; bathe here, in not harming, truthfulness and generosity.
