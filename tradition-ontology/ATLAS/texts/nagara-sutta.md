## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-8 <a id="tea-nagara-sutta-2-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Before his awakening the bodhisatta reflected: with what existing does ageing and death exist? — and by wise attention came to the insight 'birth', down to 'consciousness turns back from name-and-form and goes no further'. He found an ancient path travelled by the Buddhas of old, like a man who in the forest discovers an ancient path to an ancient city; that path is the noble eightfold path.
