### expedient-prayer <a id="tea-muchu-mondo-expedient-prayer"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Asked whether prayers for worldly benefit are proper, Musō answers that the buddhas grant worldly benefits as expedient means to lead people to the Way; seeking them for their own sake is not the purpose of the Dharma.
