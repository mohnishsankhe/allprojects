A modern Sanskrit commentary on the Yoga Sūtras. Its introduction surveys the darśanas, gives the count of 195 sūtras (51, 55, 55, 34), notes that some manuscripts mark the end of the work after pāda 3, cites the verse from Bhoja's vṛtti on Patañjali's three kinds of purification, and compares the Vivaraṇa, Tattvavaiśāradī and Yogavārttika on particular sūtras.
