## Teachings (2: sourced 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-tarasara-upanisad-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Bṛhaspati asks Yājñavalkya about Avimukta; there the tāraka — 'oṃ namo nārāyaṇāya' — is to be known.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language, death-dying_

concepts: [Avimukta and the tāraka](../concepts/avimukta-taraka.md) · practices: [Recitation of the eight-syllable Nārāyaṇa mantra](../practices/narayana-astaksara-japa.md)

### 2-3 <a id="tea-tarasara-upanisad-2-3"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

Oṃ's subtle parts and the syllables are identified with Rāma, Sītā and Rāma's companions; knowing this one is freed.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language_

concepts: [The measures of Oṃ (mātrā)](../concepts/omkara-matras.md)
