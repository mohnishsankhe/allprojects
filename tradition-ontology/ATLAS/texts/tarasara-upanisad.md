## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-tarasara-upanisad-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Bṛhaspati asks Yājñavalkya about Avimukta; there the tāraka — 'oṃ namo nārāyaṇāya' — is to be known.

### 2-3 <a id="tea-tarasara-upanisad-2-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Oṃ's subtle parts and the syllables are identified with Rāma, Sītā and Rāma's companions; knowing this one is freed.
