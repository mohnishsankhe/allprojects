### 437c19 <a id="tea-jingde-chuandeng-lu-437c19"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A monk from the south reports the teachers there: this very mind is buddha; you have the nature of seeing, hearing, feeling and knowing, which raises the eyebrows and moves the body; the body arises and perishes, but the mind-nature has never arisen or perished — like a dragon changing bones or a snake shedding skin. > 即心是佛佛是覺義。汝今悉具見聞覺知之性

### 440a03 <a id="tea-jingde-chuandeng-lu-440a03"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Mazu's sermon: the Way needs no cultivation — only do not defile it. Defilement is any mind of birth and death that contrives and aims; to meet the Way directly, the ordinary mind is the Way — without contrivance, right and wrong, grasping and rejecting, permanence and annihilation, ordinary and holy. Walking, standing, sitting and lying, responding to situations — all is the Way; all dharmas are mind-dharmas.

> 道不用修但莫污染。何為污染。但有生死心造作趣向皆是污染。若欲直會其道平常心是道
