## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### maxim 'guru satya' (the guru is truth) <a id="tea-kartabhaja-sayings-guru-satya"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

The guru is the truth: the Kartā is present in the living guru (Mahāśay), and devotion to him is devotion to God.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, ultimate_

### maxim 'loker madhye lokācār, sadgurur madhye ekākār' <a id="tea-kartabhaja-sayings-loke-madhye-lokacar"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Among people, follow people's customs; with the true guru, be one (with him): keep outward conventions of caste and religion in the world while inwardly worshipping the Kartā.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ethics, teacher-transmission_

### maxim 'mānuṣ bhaja' (worship the human) <a id="tea-kartabhaja-sayings-manus-bhaja"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Worship the human: God is not in images or distant heavens but in the human being, above all in the guru.
