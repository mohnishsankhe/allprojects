## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### bodhicitta <a id="tea-zaijarin-bodhicitta"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Myōe charges that the Senchakushū commits two great errors: it discards the aspiration for awakening (bodhicitta), which all the Pure Land sūtras, including the three grades of the Larger Sūtra, make the basis of birth; and it likens the practitioners of the holy path to the bandits of the two-rivers parable. The nenbutsu without the mind of awakening is not the Buddha's teaching.
