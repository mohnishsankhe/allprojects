### Satsaṅg Yog, the graded practice <a id="tea-satsang-yog-graded-practice"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _recent (post-1800)_

Practice proceeds from mental repetition (mānas jap) and mental contemplation of form (mānas dhyān) to fixing the gaze at a point (dṛṣṭi yoga), which opens the inner light, and then to the meditation on sound (nādānusandhān), which leads to the soundless.
