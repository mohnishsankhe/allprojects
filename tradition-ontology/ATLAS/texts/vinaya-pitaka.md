### cv.5.33.2 <a id="tea-vinaya-pitaka-cv-5-33-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When the group-of-six monks learned and taught low arts (tiracchāna-vijjā), the Buddha laid down: monks, low arts are not to be learned — whoever learns them commits an offence of wrong-doing; nor are they to be taught.
