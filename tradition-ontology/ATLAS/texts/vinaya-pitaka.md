## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### cv.5.8 <a id="tea-vinaya-pitaka-cv-5-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A Rājagaha merchant hung a sandalwood bowl on a bamboo pole for 'any ascetic or brahmin who is an arahant with powers'. Pūraṇa Kassapa, Makkhali Gosāla, Ajita, Pakudha, Sañjaya and the Nigaṇṭha each came claiming 'I am an arahant with powers — give me the bowl', and were told to take it down; Piṇḍola Bhāradvāja rose into the air and took it. The Buddha rebuked him and forbade monks to display superhuman powers to lay people.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: unmarked · types: powers-experiences, ethics_

concepts: [The six rival teachers (cha satthāro)](../concepts/six-teachers.md), [Claims and display of powers among the orders](../concepts/display-of-powers.md)

### cv.5.33.2 <a id="tea-vinaya-pitaka-cv-5-33-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When the group-of-six monks learned and taught low arts (tiracchāna-vijjā), the Buddha laid down: monks, low arts are not to be learned — whoever learns them commits an offence of wrong-doing; nor are they to be taught.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: all (bhikkhu) · types: ethics, world-fate_

teachers: [Gotama Buddha](../teachers/gotama-buddha.md) · disputes: [May monks and renunciants practise astrology or live by it?](../debates/astrology-for-renunciants.md)

### mv.1.23-24 <a id="tea-vinaya-pitaka-mv-1-23-24"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Sañjaya the wanderer lived at Rājagaha with 250 wanderers, among them Sāriputta and Moggallāna. After Sāriputta heard the verse on causes from Assaji, the two went to the Buddha with the 250; Sañjaya refused to come, and hot blood gushed from his mouth.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: unmarked · types: teacher-transmission_

teachers: [Sañjaya Belaṭṭhiputta](../teachers/sanjaya-belatthiputta.md), [Sāriputta](../teachers/sariputta.md), [Mahāmoggallāna](../teachers/moggallana.md)
