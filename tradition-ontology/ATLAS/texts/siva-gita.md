# Śiva Gītā

**Language:** Sanskrit

**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Genre:** gītā

**Location in parent:** claimed for the Padma Purāṇa, 'uparibhāga' (Uttarakhaṇḍa), per its colophons
**Attribution:** tradition: Śiva teaches Rāma (Rāghava) in the forest after Sītā's abduction, at the sage Agastya's prompting; part of the Padma Purāṇa; scholarly: an independent Śaiva Vedānta Gītā whose Padma Purāṇa attribution is not borne out by the printed Padma Purāṇa (low confidence); confidence: low

**Availability:** digitized-original

Śiva, worshipped by Rāma, grants him weapons and teaches Śaiva devotion and Vedānta: the Virajā initiation with sacred ash, Śiva's glories and cosmic form, the formation of the body in the womb, the nature and course of the jīva after death, the five sheaths, liberation by knowledge of Śiva as the Self, and devotion as the means.

### 1 <a id="tea-siva-gita-1"></a>

The excellence of devotion to Śiva: in the Padma Purāṇa frame the sages learn that this secret teaching was given by Śiva to Rāma; devotion to Śiva is praised as the root of all good.

### 3 <a id="tea-siva-gita-3"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low · [AI-translated]

The Virajā initiation: Rāma, grieving for Sītā, is instructed to take the Pāśupata vow of the Virajā rite — smearing the body with sacred ash while reciting the Virajā mantras — and to worship Śiva, after which Śiva appears to him.
