**Structure:** 16 chapters (titles verified in the 1915 Gītāsaṅgraha): 1 excellence of devotion to Śiva, 2 dispassion, 3 the Virajā initiation, 4 Śiva's appearance, 5 boon to Rāma, 6 vibhūti, 7 the cosmic form, 8 origin of the body, 9 the nature of the body, 10 the nature of the jīva, 11 the jīva's course, 12 fruits of worship and knowledge, 13 liberation, 14 the five sheaths, 15 bhakti-yoga, 16 the qualified hearer
**Availability:** digitized-original

Śiva, worshipped by Rāma, grants him weapons and teaches Śaiva devotion and Vedānta: the Virajā initiation with sacred ash, Śiva's glories and cosmic form, the formation of the body in the womb, the nature and course of the jīva after death, the five sheaths, liberation by knowledge of Śiva as the Self, and devotion as the means.
