## Teachings (6: skeleton 6)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-tiruvarutpayan-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As the vowel 'a' pervades all letters, the Lord, without equal, pervades everything as knowledge.

> அகர உயிர்போல் அறிவாகி எங்கும் நிகரில் இறைநிற்கும் நிறைந்து

_level: ultimate · standpoint: divine · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate_

concepts: [The Siddhānta's 'advaita' of inseparability](../concepts/siddhanta-advaita.md)

### ch.3 <a id="tea-tiruvarutpayan-ch-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The chapter on the dark mala (iruḷ-mala-nilai): āṇava is one, beginningless and hides the soul's knowledge as darkness hides objects — but, unlike darkness, it hides itself as well; it is removed not by the soul's effort but by grace.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, consciousness-mind_

terms: [āṇava mala](../terms/anava-mala.md) · obstacles: [Āṇava mala](../obstacles/anava-mala.md)

### ch.4 <a id="tea-tiruvarutpayan-ch-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The chapter on grace (aruḷatu-nilai): grace is always present with the soul, as the sun's light is to the eye, yet the soul does not see it while covered by mala; grace is Śiva's own power.

_level: bridging · standpoint: divine · path: general · stage: all · types: ultimate, karma-liberation_

terms: [aruḷ](../terms/arul.md) · concepts: [Grace (anugraha) as the Lord's fifth act](../concepts/grace-anugraha.md)

### ch.5 <a id="tea-tiruvarutpayan-ch-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The chapter on grace embodied (aruḷ-uru-nilai): as a deer is caught with a decoy deer, Śiva takes human form as the guru to catch souls who know only human forms.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission_

concepts: [Śiva comes as the guru](../concepts/guru-as-siva.md)

### ch.9 <a id="tea-tiruvarutpayan-ch-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The chapter on the five letters (aintheḻuttu-aruḷ-nilai): the right order of the five letters places grace between Śiva and the soul, and meditating them so leads the soul to Śiva.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: sound · stage: intermediate · types: sound-language, practice_

concepts: [The five letters (pañcākṣara)](../concepts/pancaksara.md)

### ch.10 <a id="tea-tiruvarutpayan-ch-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The chapter on those who have attained (aṇaintōr taṉmai): the freed live without 'I' and 'mine', their acts are Śiva's acts, and they neither seek nor reject.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: experiential · path: general · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation, ethics_
