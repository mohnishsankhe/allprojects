## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-23 (summary) <a id="tea-ksurika-upanisad-1-23-summary"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

The Kṣurikā ('razor') teaches a concentration (dhāraṇā) by which the mind, joined with breath, cuts through the vital points of the body from the feet upward and all the channels except the suṣumnā; with the razor of mind sharpened on the stone of dispassion the yogin severs the bonds of rebirth. Recorded as a summary only; no steps.
