# Cheng weishi lun (*Vijñaptimātratāsiddhi)

The foundational text of East Asian Yogācāra: refutes self and external dharmas, sets out the eight consciousnesses and their mental factors, seeds (both innate and newly perfumed), the four divisions of cognition (seen part, seeing part, self-witnessing part, witness of the self-witnessing), the three natures, the five stages of the path and the four wisdoms.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1 <a id="tea-cheng-weishi-lun-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Self and dharmas are refuted: they are mere designations based on the transformations of consciousness; the self of the non-Buddhists and the real dharmas of the Hīnayāna schools are both rejected.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: intermediate · types: dispute, ultimate_

teachers: [Xuanzang](../teachers/xuanzang.md) · disputes: [Is there a self?](../debates/is-there-a-self.md)

### 2 <a id="tea-cheng-weishi-lun-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Every cognition has four parts: the seen part (image), the seeing part, the self-witnessing part and the witnessing of the self-witnessing part (Dharmapāla's view, against those who admit one, two or three parts); seeds are both originally present and newly perfumed.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [svasaṃvedana](../terms/svasamvedana.md), [bīja](../terms/bija.md) · concepts: [The four parts of cognition (Dharmapāla)](../concepts/four-parts-of-cognition.md), [Seeds and imprints (bīja, vāsanā)](../concepts/bija-vasana.md) · teachers: [Dharmapāla (Yogācāra)](../teachers/dharmapala.md), [Xuanzang](../teachers/xuanzang.md)

### 9 <a id="tea-cheng-weishi-lun-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The path to cognition-only has five stages: accumulation, preparation (with the four stages heat, peak, patience and supreme mundane quality, in which the grasped and then the grasper are seen as empty), penetration (the path of seeing), cultivation (through the ten grounds), and final attainment.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: meditation, knowledge · stage: all (five stages (wuwei)) · types: practice_

teachers: [Xuanzang](../teachers/xuanzang.md)

### 10 <a id="tea-cheng-weishi-lun-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

At buddhahood the eight consciousnesses are transformed into four wisdoms: the eighth into great mirror-wisdom, the seventh into equality-wisdom, the sixth into discerning wisdom, the five senses into the wisdom that accomplishes what is to be done; the sixth and seventh begin to turn at the path of seeing, the eighth and the five senses only at buddhahood.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: consciousness-mind, karma-liberation_

concepts: [The four wisdoms](../concepts/four-wisdoms.md), [Transformation of the basis (āśraya-parāvṛtti)](../concepts/asraya-paravrtti.md) · teachers: [Xuanzang](../teachers/xuanzang.md)
