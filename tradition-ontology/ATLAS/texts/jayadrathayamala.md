The great Kālīkula scripture, in four hexads (ṣaṭka) of about 6,000 verses each, teaching the worship of many forms of Kālī, above all Kālasaṅkarṣiṇī; the principal scriptural source of the Krama's Kālī cult and of Kālasaṅkarṣiṇī's place in Abhinavagupta's Trika.
