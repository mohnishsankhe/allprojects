Rūpa Gosvāmī's supplement on the 'brilliant' (ujjvala) or amorous rasa (madhura) toward Kṛṣṇa: the hero and heroines, Rādhā and the gopīs, their groups and messengers, the stages of love from prema to mahābhāva, and love in union and separation; holds paramour love in Vraja to be the highest.

### 1.21 <a id="tea-ujjvalanilamani-1-21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The poets do not approve of another's wife as heroine in the principal rasa — except for the lotus-eyed women of Gokula, since Kṛṣṇa, crown of connoisseurs, brought them down (into the world) to taste rasa.

### radha-prakarana <a id="tea-ujjvalanilamani-radha-prakarana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Rādhā is the greatest of the gopīs, the embodiment of mahābhāva and Kṛṣṇa's hlādinī-śakti, endowed with innumerable qualities; the sakhīs and mañjarīs serve her.

### sthayibhava-prakarana <a id="tea-ujjvalanilamani-sthayibhava-prakarana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Love (prema) intensifies through stages — affection (sneha), love-pique (māna), intimacy (praṇaya), passion (rāga), ever-new passion (anurāga) and great emotion (mahābhāva) — the last found only in the gopīs of Vraja and, in its highest forms, in Rādhā alone.
