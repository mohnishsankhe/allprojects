## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### stabaka-3 <a id="tea-sastravartasamuccaya-stabaka-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The doctrine that Īśvara is the maker can be accepted in a true sense: the soul that attains the highest perfection is 'Īśvara', and every soul is the 'maker' of its own condition through its karma; a Lord who creates the world is refuted.
