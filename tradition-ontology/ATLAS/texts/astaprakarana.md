# Aṣṭaprakaraṇa

`src:astaprakarana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
**Genre:** collection
**Dates:** Scholarly account: collection of works of c. 700–1050; commentaries 10th–12th c.; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** eight short treatises with commentaries
**Availability:** digitized-original

The 'eight treatises' — the canon of short doctrinal works of the Siddhānta: Bhoja's Tattvaprakāśa; Sadyojyoti's Tattvasaṅgraha, Tattvatrayanirṇaya, Bhogakārikā, Mokṣakārikā and Paramokṣanirāsakārikā; Śrīkaṇṭha's Ratnatraya; and the Nādakārikā attributed to Rāmakaṇṭha. Aghoraśiva commented on six (all but the Mokṣakārikā and Paramokṣanirāsakārikā, which carry Rāmakaṇṭha's commentaries).
