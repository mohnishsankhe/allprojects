**Availability:** digitized-original

Kapila teaches his mother Devahūti that the mind attached to the guṇas binds and turned to the Lord frees; that devotion without motive (animittā, ahaitukī bhakti) is greater than liberation itself; the twenty-four principles with time as the twenty-fifth; eight-limbed yoga culminating in meditation on the Lord's form; devotion graded by the guṇas; the misery of household attachment, death, the hells, the embryo's life and prayer in the womb, and the paths of smoke and light. Devahūti attains liberation.

### 3.25.13-14 <a id="tea-kapila-gita-3-25-13-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

Kapila: in my view the yoga concerning the self (adhyātma-yoga), in which pleasure and pain completely cease, leads people to the highest good; I will tell you this yoga, complete in all its parts, which I once taught the sages.

### 3.25.15-18 <a id="tea-kapila-gita-3-25-15-18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

The mind is held to be the cause of the self's bondage and of its liberation: attached to the guṇas it binds, devoted to the Person it frees. When the mind is cleansed of lust, greed and the other impurities born of 'I' and 'mine', and becomes equal in pleasure and pain, the person sees the self as alone, beyond prakṛti, undivided, self-luminous, subtle, and prakṛti as powerless, by a self endowed with knowledge, dispassion and devotion.

> cetaḥ khalvasya bandhāya muktaye cātmano matam / guṇeṣu saktaṃ bandhāya rataṃ vā puṃsi muktaye

### 3.25.19-20 <a id="tea-kapila-gita-3-25-19-20"></a>

### 3.25.21-25 <a id="tea-kapila-gita-3-25-21-25"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

The holy are forbearing, compassionate, friends to all beings, without enemies, peaceful; with unswerving devotion to me they give up actions and kin for my sake and hear and tell my stories; they should be sought, for they remove the faults of attachment. In their company talks of my prowess arise, delightful to heart and ear; by relishing them faith, attraction and devotion quickly follow in order on the path to liberation.

### 3.25.32-33 <a id="tea-kapila-gita-3-25-32-33"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

Devotion (bhakti) is the natural inclination, without ulterior motive, of the senses — which perceive objects and act by the Veda — of one whose mind is fixed on the Lord alone; such causeless devotion is greater than liberation (siddhi), and quickly dissolves the subtle body as the digestive fire consumes food.

> devānāṃ guṇaliṅgānāmānuśravikakarmaṇām / sattva evaikamanaso vṛttiḥ svābhāvikī tu yā // animittā bhāgavatī bhaktiḥ siddhergarīyasī / jarayatyāśu yā kośaṃ nigīrṇamanalo yathā

### 3.28.39-44 <a id="tea-kapila-gita-3-28-39-44"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

### 3.29.11-14 <a id="tea-kapila-gita-3-29-11-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

The mark of attributeless (nirguṇa) devotion: at the mere hearing of my qualities the mind flows to me, dwelling in all hearts, unbroken like the Gaṅgā's waters to the sea — devotion without motive and without interruption to the Supreme Person; such devotees do not accept sālokya, sārṣṭi, sāmīpya, sārūpya or even oneness if offered, without my service; this is called the ultimate bhakti-yoga, by which one transcends the three guṇas and attains my state.

### 3.29.21-25 <a id="tea-kapila-gita-3-29-21-25"></a>

### 3.31.12-21 <a id="tea-kapila-gita-3-31-12-21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

The embryo's prayer: with folded hands the frightened jīva praises the Lord who took many bodies to protect the world, who dwells pure and undivided in the heart though the self seems bound by karma; who else could restore the memory lost on the path of saṃsāra? ### 3.31.44-48 <a id="tea-kapila-gita-3-31-44-48"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

The jīva moves from world to world with its body of elements, senses and mind; death is the cessation of that body's function, birth its appearance — death is when the body can no longer perceive objects, birth is taking 'I am this' toward it — as when the eye can no longer see, the seer loses the power of seeing through it. So the wise should not fear, be wretched or confused, but move in the world free of attachment, with right vision, yoga and dispassion.
