**Availability:** digitized-original

Kapila teaches his mother Devahūti that the mind attached to the guṇas binds and turned to the Lord frees; that devotion without motive (animittā, ahaitukī bhakti) is greater than liberation itself; the twenty-four principles with time as the twenty-fifth; eight-limbed yoga culminating in meditation on the Lord's form; devotion graded by the guṇas; the misery of household attachment, death, the hells, the embryo's life and prayer in the womb, and the paths of smoke and light. Devahūti attains liberation.
