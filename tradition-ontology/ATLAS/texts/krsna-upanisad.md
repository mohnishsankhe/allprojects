## Teachings (2: sourced 2)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-krsna-upanisad-1-2"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

The sages who wished to embrace Rāma in the forest are told they will be born as gopīs when he comes as Kṛṣṇa.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion · stage: all · types: world-fate_

### 3-25 <a id="tea-krsna-upanisad-3-26"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

An allegory: Nanda is supreme bliss, Yaśodā liberation, Devakī Brahma-knowledge, Vasudeva the Veda, the gopīs and cows the ṛcs, the flute Rudra, Gokula Vaikuṇṭha, the trees ascetics, the demons vices, Balarāma Śeṣa, Kṛṣṇa Brahman, the 16,108 wives the ṛcs and Upaniṣads, Kaṃsa the Kali age.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate_
