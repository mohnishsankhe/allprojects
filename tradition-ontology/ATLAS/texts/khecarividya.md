The fullest text on khecarī mudrā: a secret mantra that must be received from a guru, a physical practice on the tongue so that it can enter the cavity above the palate, the drinking of the nectar of immortality from the moon in the head, the places and kalās of the nectar, and (in its last chapter) herbal preparations. RESTRICTED: recorded here as summary with the text's own warnings only; no procedure is given.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1.topic.mantra-and-guru <a id="tea-khecarividya-1-topic-mantra-and-guru"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

The khecarī mantra, whose syllables are concealed in the text, must be received from a guru; the practice succeeds only through the guru and the mantra together, and the knowledge is to be kept secret and given only to a qualified disciple.

### 1.topic.physical-practice <a id="tea-khecarividya-1-topic-physical-practice"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

RESTRICTED. Paṭala 1 describes a gradual physical preparation of the tongue so that it can be turned back into the cavity above the palate, done over a long period and only under a guru; the text warns that it is not to be attempted without instruction. No procedure is recorded here.

### 2.topic.nectar <a id="tea-khecarividya-2-topic-nectar"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

With the tongue inserted above the palate the yogin drinks the nectar (amṛta) flowing from the moon in the head; the text describes places and kalās of the nectar and claims freedom from disease, old age and death.
