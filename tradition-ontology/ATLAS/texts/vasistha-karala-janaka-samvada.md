Vasiṣṭha teaches king Karāla Janaka the Sāṃkhya-Yoga account of the perishable and the imperishable: the 24 principles, the 25th (the unawakened/awakening self) and the 26th (the awakened), and the difference between the knowledge of plurality and of unity (nānātva, ekatva).
