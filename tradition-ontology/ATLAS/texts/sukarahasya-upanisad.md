## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-2 <a id="tea-sukarahasya-upanisad-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Vyāsa asks Śiva to teach Śuka at his initiation; Śiva teaches the four great sayings as a secret teaching with seer, metre, deity and limbs, and explains 'prajñānaṃ brahma', 'ahaṃ brahmāsmi', 'tat tvam asi' and 'ayam ātmā brahma'.
