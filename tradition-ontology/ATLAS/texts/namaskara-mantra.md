# Namaskāra-mantra (Navakāra, Pañca-parameṣṭhi-namaskāra)

`src:namaskara-mantra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Navakāra-mantra, Ṇamokāra-mantra, Pañcamaṅgala-mahāśrutaskandha (Śvetāmbara name for the mantra with cūlikā)
**Original title:** णमोक्कार मंत
**Language:** Prakrit
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Genre:** mantra
**Attribution:** tradition: eternal (anādi); no author; scholarly: the five homages appear at the opening of the Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama and the Bhagavatī; an early form of the first homages occurs in Khāravela's Hāthīgumphā inscription; confidence: moderate
**Structure:** 5 lines of homage (35 syllables) + 4-line cūlikā (33 syllables) = 68 syllables in 9 lines (Śvetāmbara)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The fundamental Jain mantra in Prakrit: homage to the Arhats, the Siddhas, the Ācāryas, the Upādhyāyas and all Sādhus in the world; the Śvetāmbara form adds a four-line cūlikā declaring that this fivefold homage destroys all evil and is the first of all auspicious things.

### 1-5 <a id="tea-namaskara-mantra-1-5"></a>

Homage to the Arhats; homage to the Siddhas; homage to the Ācāryas; homage to the Upādhyāyas; homage to all the Sādhus in the world.

> ṇamo arihaṃtāṇaṃ / ṇamo siddhāṇaṃ / ṇamo āyariyāṇaṃ / ṇamo uvajjhāyāṇaṃ / ṇamo loe savva-sāhūṇaṃ
