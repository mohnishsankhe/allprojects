### nine-vehicles <a id="tea-dudjom-chojung-nine-vehicles"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

Dudjom Rinpoche sets out the nine vehicles in three triads: the vehicles that lead out from the origin of suffering (śrāvaka, pratyekabuddha, bodhisattva); the vehicles of Vedic austerity (kriyā, ubhaya/caryā, yoga); and the vehicles of overpowering means (mahāyoga, anuyoga, atiyoga).

### terma <a id="tea-dudjom-chojung-terma"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Treasure teachings pass through the three common transmissions (the victors' mind-transmission, the vidyādharas' symbolic transmission, the oral transmission of persons) and three special ones (prophetic authorization, empowerment through aspiration, entrustment to the ḍākinīs), and are revealed by the treasure revealer at the destined time.
