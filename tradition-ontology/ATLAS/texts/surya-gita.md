### 1.5-6 <a id="tea-surya-gita-1-5-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · [AI-translated]

Before the arising of world, jīva and Īśvara there was only Brahman — stainless, without attributes, actionless, one without a second; how then can the Veda speak of Brahman as creator of jīva and Lord, and ascribe the act of creation to the actionless?
