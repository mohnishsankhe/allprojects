One of the eight Saṅgam anthologies (Eṭṭuttokai): hymns in the paripāṭal metre, of which about two dozen survive, several to Tirumāl (Viṣṇu), others to Cevvēḷ (Murukaṉ) and the river Vaiyai; its Tirumāl hymns praise him as the source and substance of all things and name his hill-shrine Tirumāliruñcōlai. A precursor of Āḻvār devotion, not an Āḻvār text.
