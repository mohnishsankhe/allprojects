## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### antah-sakta <a id="tea-kaula-upanisad-bhasya-bhaskararaya-antah-sakta"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

On 'inwardly a Śākta': the worship of Śakti is to be known by the inner organ alone; the outer Śākta marks are to be covered by Śaiva marks such as ash, since Śiva is not different from Śakti; in assemblies one shows Viṣṇu-worship, Viṣṇu being a manifest form of Tripurasundarī — which is why the Sahasranāma ranks Viṣṇu, Śiva and Śakti devotion as ever more secret.

### lokan-na-nindyat <a id="tea-kaula-upanisad-bhasya-bhaskararaya-lokan-na-nindyat"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

On 'one should not censure': the many schools, beginningless in their flow, are all valid for their respective qualified followers; censuring them breeds doubt in those followers, who then lose faith in their own school while not qualified for the Kaula — falling from both; hence Kṛṣṇa's 'one should not unsettle the minds of the ignorant' and Paraśurāma's 'no censure of any school'.
