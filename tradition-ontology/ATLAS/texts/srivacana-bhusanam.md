Piḷḷai Lokācārya's chief work, in aphorisms: the glory of Śrī's mediation, the Lord as the sole means, the conduct of the surrendered, service to devotees, and finally the saving power of the teacher's regard (ācārya-abhimāna).

## Teachings (6: skeleton 6)

### 1 <a id="tea-srivacana-bhusanam-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Śrī's mediation (puruṣakāra): in separation and in union she turns the soul toward the Lord and the Lord toward the soul — the soul by her grace, the Lord by her beauty — as shown by Sītā's intercession even for those who wronged her.

### 2 <a id="tea-srivacana-bhusanam-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The glory of the means: the Lord himself is the means; surrender (prapatti) is not a means but the soul's acceptance fitting its nature, and to regard it as a means would make it one more act of one's own; the means is 'already accomplished' (siddha).

### 2/2 <a id="tea-srivacana-bhusanam-2-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Lord does not merely overlook the faults of those who come to him but relishes them (doṣa-bhogya), as a cow licks the dirt from its newborn calf, out of motherly love (vātsalya).

### 2/3 <a id="tea-srivacana-bhusanam-2-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Lord's grace is without cause (nirhetuka): he saves by his own will; the soul's surrender is not what earns it.

### 3 <a id="tea-srivacana-bhusanam-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The conduct of the surrendered: to avoid offence against the Lord, against his devotees (bhāgavata-apacāra — the gravest, including judging devotees by birth) and the unbearable offence (asahya-apacāra); to serve devotees; to keep scriptural conduct as service and for the example of the world.

### 6 <a id="tea-srivacana-bhusanam-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Finally, for one who can do nothing, the gracious regard of the teacher (ācārya-abhimāna) is itself what saves: to be held as his own by a true teacher is the last refuge.
