## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 46.51 <a id="tea-bojjhanga-samyutta-46-51"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The nutriment for sensual desire is unwise attention to the sign of the beautiful; for ill will to the sign of the repulsive; for dullness and drowsiness to discontent, lethargy, lazy stretching, sleepiness after meals and sluggishness of mind; for restlessness and remorse to unsettledness of mind; for doubt to things that are the basis for doubt. The awakening factors are nourished by wise attention to their bases; the hindrances are denourished by wise attention to the sign of foulness, the liberation of mind by love, the elements of arousal, exertion and endeavour, peace of mind, and wholesome and unwholesome qualities.

### 46.53 <a id="tea-bojjhanga-samyutta-46-53"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When the mind is sluggish it is the wrong time to develop tranquillity, concentration and equanimity and the right time for investigation, energy and rapture (as one fans a small fire); when the mind is restless the reverse (as one puts out a fire with wet grass); mindfulness is always useful.
