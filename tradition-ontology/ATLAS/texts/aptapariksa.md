## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### isvara-pariksa <a id="tea-aptapariksa-isvara-pariksa"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Lord of Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika cannot be the maker of the world: an unembodied maker cannot act, an embodied one would need a maker for his body, and the world as a whole is not shown to be an effect; nor can such a Lord be the teacher of the path, since only one freed of faults by his own effort is trustworthy.
