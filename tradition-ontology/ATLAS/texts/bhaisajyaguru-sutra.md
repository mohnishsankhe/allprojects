The sūtra of the Medicine Buddha, Bhaiṣajyaguru Vaiḍūryaprabha, whose eastern land is of lapis; his twelve great vows to heal bodily and mental ills, provide for the poor and free beings from wrong views; hearing and keeping his name prevents evil rebirth; rites of lamps, banners and recitation to prolong the life of the sick and dying.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### vows <a id="tea-bhaisajyaguru-sutra-vows"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When a bodhisattva, Bhaiṣajyaguru made twelve great vows: that his body's light would illuminate countless worlds; that beings would have complete faculties, be healed of sickness and poverty, be freed from wrong views, from punishment, from hunger and from lack of clothing, and that women wishing to be free of the woman's state would obtain it.

> imāni dvādaśa mahāpraṇidhānāni
