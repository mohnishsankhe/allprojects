## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-4 <a id="tea-anuruddhamahavitakka-sutta-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Anuruddha thought: this Dhamma is for one of few wishes, content, secluded, energetic, mindful, concentrated, wise; the Buddha adds the eighth: this Dhamma is for one who delights in non-proliferation, not for one who delights in proliferation.
