## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### an1.49 <a id="tea-manorathapurani-an1-49"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

'This mind is luminous' is explained: 'luminous' means clear and pure, and 'mind' here means the life-continuum (bhavaṅga) consciousness; it is defiled by adventitious defilements as a clean person is called impure because of the misconduct of parents or teachers.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind_

### antaradhana <a id="tea-manorathapurani-antaradhana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The dispensation disappears in five stages: the disappearance of attainment, then of practice, then of learning (the texts), then of the outward signs (the robe), and finally of the relics.

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: general · stage: all · types: world-fate_
