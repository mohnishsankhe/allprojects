# Atharvaveda Saṃhitā (Śaunaka)

`src:atharvaveda-saunaka` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Atharvāṅgirasaḥ, Bhṛgvaṅgirasaḥ, Brahmaveda, Atharvaveda
**Original title:** अथर्ववेदसंहिता
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Śaunaka śākhā (Atharvaveda)](../lineages/sakha-saunaka.md)
**Genre:** saṃhitā (hymns and spells)
**Authors:** 
  - [Atharvan](../teachers/atharvan.md) — role: revealer; attribution: traditional
  - [Aṅgiras (teacher of Śaunaka)](../teachers/angiras.md) — role: revealer; attribution: traditional
  - [Sumantu](../teachers/sumantu.md) — role: compiler; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: authorless (apauruṣeya) revelation seen by the ṛṣis; arranged by Vyāsa; the Atharvaveda was received by Sumantu (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 1.4.22); scholarly: a collection of popular-ritual and speculative hymns, parts as old as the late Ṛgveda; confidence: high
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 1200–900 BCE for most hymns (low confidence); (confidence low)
**Structure:** 20 kāṇḍas, c. 730 sūktas, c. 6,000 verses; books 1–7 short hymns grouped by length, 8–12 long hymns, 13 Rohita, 14 marriage, 15 Vrātya (prose), 16–17 protective formulas, 18 funeral, 19 supplement, 20 Indra hymns for the soma rite (mostly from the Ṛgveda, with the Kuntāpa hymns). Books 9.9–10 reproduce RV 1.164.
**Availability:** digitized-original

The Veda of the Atharvans and Aṅgirases: hymns and spells for healing (bhaiṣajya), long life, protection against demons and sorcery, concord in family and assembly, marriage, royal power, expiation and prosperity; speculative hymns on Skambha the frame of the world (10.7–8), the human body as the city of the gods (10.2), Prāṇa (11.4), the Brahmacārin (11.5), the Remainder (11.7), the Earth (12.1), Time (19.53–54); the prose book of the Vrātya (15); the funeral book (18).
