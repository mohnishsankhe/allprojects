The most widely recited Kagyu supplication: it invokes the lineage from Vajradhara, Tilopa, Nāropa, Marpa, Milarepa and Gampopa, and states the path in four verses — revulsion is the feet of meditation, devotion its head, non-distraction its main body, and the nature of thought is dharmakāya.

### 1 <a id="tea-dorje-chang-thungma-1"></a>

Great Vajradhara, Tilo, Nāro, Marpa, Mila, and lord of Dharma Gampopa; Karmapa, knower of the three times, and the holders of the four great and eight lesser lineages … to you we pray: hold us with your blessing, we who keep your practice lineage.

### 2 <a id="tea-dorje-chang-thungma-2"></a>

Revulsion is said to be the feet of meditation: to this meditator without attachment to food and wealth, who has cut the ties to this life, grant your blessing that attachment to honour and gain may cease.

Non-distraction is said to be the main body of meditation: to this meditator who rests, without contrivance, in the fresh essence of whatever thought arises, grant your blessing that meditation may be free of mental fabrication.
