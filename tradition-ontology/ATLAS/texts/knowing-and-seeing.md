### dependent-origination <a id="tea-knowing-and-seeing-dependent-origination"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Having discerned mentality-materiality, the meditator discerns their causes back into past lives and forward into future ones, verifying dependent origination directly before developing the insight knowledges.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice, karma-liberation_

### four-elements <a id="tea-knowing-and-seeing-four-elements"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Four-elements meditation discerns twelve characteristics in the body — hardness, roughness, heaviness, softness, smoothness, lightness (earth); flowing, cohesion (water); heat, cold (fire); supporting, pushing (air) — until the body is seen as elements and concentration arises.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice, body-layers_

practices: [Defining the four elements (catudhātuvavatthāna)](../practices/catudhatuvavatthana.md), [Discerning material groups (Pa-Auk)](../practices/rupa-kalapa-discernment.md)

### intro <a id="tea-knowing-and-seeing-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Insight must know and see ultimate mentality and materiality and their causes; for that, concentration — access or jhāna — must be strong enough that its light reveals the material groups and the mind-moments.
