**Availability:** lost

The lost root text of the Lokāyata, ascribed to Bṛhaspati. Only fragments survive, quoted by others: the principles are earth, water, fire and air; their aggregate is called body, senses and objects; from them arises consciousness, like the intoxicating power (from fermenting ingredients); the person is the body qualified by consciousness; since there is no one who goes to another world, there is no other world.
