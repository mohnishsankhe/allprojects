# Amanaska (Amanaskayoga)

The principal text of a rājayoga called 'mindlessness' (amanaska): by a gaze without object and the guru's grace the mind dissolves effortlessly, the breath stops of itself and the yogin attains liberation while living. Its second chapter dismisses laborious methods — breath-control, mudrās, mantra-repetition, meditation on objects, pilgrimage, scriptural study — as useless or harmful beside this natural state.

### 2.topic.critique <a id="tea-amanaska-2-topic-critique"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Breath-control, the practice of mudrās and bandhas, mantra-repetition, meditation on objects, pilgrimage, ritual and scriptural study are laborious; they do not bring the mindless state and may cause pain or disease; the wise do not resort to them.

### 2.topic.effortless <a id="tea-amanaska-2-topic-effortless"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The mindless state (amanaska) arises without effort through the grace of the guru and a gaze that rests on no object; then the breath ceases of itself, the body becomes still, and the yogin is liberated while living.

### 2.topic.signs <a id="tea-amanaska-2-topic-signs"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Signs of the mindless state are described: the body becomes still as a log, sense-objects are not grasped, the breath stops, and the mind neither sleeps nor wakes.
