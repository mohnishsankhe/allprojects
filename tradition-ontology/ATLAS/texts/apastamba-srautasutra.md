The Śrauta manual of the Āpastamba Taittirīyas (praśnas 1–24 of the Kalpasūtra); its general rules define the Veda as mantra and brāhmaṇa (24.1.31) and the Brāhmaṇa as injunctions to action.

## Teachings (1: sourced 1)

### 24.1.30-33 <a id="tea-apastamba-srautasutra-24-1-30-33"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Mantra and Brāhmaṇa are the authority for the sacrifice; 'Veda' is the name for mantra and Brāhmaṇa together. The Brāhmaṇas are injunctions to rites; the rest of the Brāhmaṇa is explanatory statement (arthavāda): blame, praise, the deed of another, and ancient precedent.
