The hundreds of inscriptions on Candragiri and Vindhyagiri at Śravaṇabeḷagoḷa (edited in Epigraphia Carnatica vol. 2), including a c. 7th-c. record of Bhadrabāhu's coming south with the saṅgha, epitaphs of monks and nuns who died by sallekhanā, and the record of Cāmuṇḍarāya's colossal Bāhubali (Gommaṭeśvara).

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-sravanabelagola-inscriptions-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

An early inscription on Candragiri records that Bhadrabāhu, foreseeing a twelve-year calamity in Ujjayinī, the saṅgha went to the south, and that a teacher named Prabhācandra ended his life by fasting on this hill with a single disciple in attendance.
