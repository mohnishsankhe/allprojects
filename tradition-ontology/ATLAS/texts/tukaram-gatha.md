The collected abhaṅgas of Tukārām (about 4,500 in the standard editions): confession and struggle, the name 'Rāma Kṛṣṇa Hari', Viṭṭhala standing on the brick, scathing attacks on hypocrisy and caste pride, the saints' company, and songs of fulfilment. The third of the Vārkarīs' three foundational books, sung daily in bhajan and kīrtan.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Tukārām Gāthā, Indu Prakash / government edition, Bombay 1873
