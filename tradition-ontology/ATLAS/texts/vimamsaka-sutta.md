## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-16 <a id="tea-vimamsaka-sutta-2-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

A monk should investigate the Tathāgata over time as to whether defiled states seen and heard are found in him, whether mixed states are found, whether bright states are found, whether he has had this wholesome state long, whether dangers arise from his fame, whether he is fearless; faith so rooted in reasons, and confirmed by hearing the Dhamma, is unshakeable.
