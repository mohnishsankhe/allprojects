A Kālī tantra in 35 paṭalas in dialogue of Īśāna and the Goddess, on Kālī's worship in the Kali age: in this age japa and worship are to be done in the paśu disposition by all four classes and the vīra and divya dispositions do not exist (4.2-3); it assigns the dispositions to the ages (6.10-16) and describes the Kaulas' concealed conduct (7.12).

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 4.2-6 <a id="tea-kalivilasa-tantra-4-2-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Īśāna: in the Kali age japa and worship are to be done in the paśu disposition by brāhmaṇas, kṣatriyas, vaiśyas, śūdras and mixed castes; the divya and vīra dispositions do not exist in the Kali age; worship three times a day with bathing, pure food and no meat; a śūdra must not drink intoxicants; or, in brief, the wise one should always practise mentally, remembering the mantra alone, pure or impure, walking, standing or sleeping, for in the Kali age there is no (true) puraścaraṇa, since people are afflicted by hunger and sleep.

> दिव्यभावो वीरभावो नास्ति नास्ति कलौ युगे।

### 6.10-16 <a id="tea-kalivilasa-tantra-6-10-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The paśu disposition is always pure; the divya disposition held from the Satya to the Tretā age, the vīra from the Tretā to the Dvāpara; Sanaka and the others were perfected in the paśu disposition, Paraśurāma as a vīra and in the divya, Rāma lord of Jānakī in the paśu, and so too Ugrasena, Kaṃsa and Vasudeva.

### 7.11-12 <a id="tea-kalivilasa-tantra-7-11-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Worship of the Śiva-liṅga is allowed to śūdras in the Kali age; worship should be hidden and more hidden, for open worship destroys perfection; inwardly Śāktas, outwardly Śaivas, in the assembly Vaiṣṇavas, the Kaulas wander the earth in many forms.

> अन्तःशाक्ता बहिः शैवाः सभायां वैष्णवा मताः। नानारूपधराः कौला विचरन्ति महीतले॥
