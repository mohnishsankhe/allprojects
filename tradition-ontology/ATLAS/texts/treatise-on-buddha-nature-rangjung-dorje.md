## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-treatise-on-buddha-nature-rangjung-dorje-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Opening from the scripture's verse that the beginningless element is the basis of all phenomena and of the attainment of nirvāṇa, the treatise identifies that element with mind's nature — clarity and emptiness — obscured by adventitious stains; purified, it is buddhahood.
