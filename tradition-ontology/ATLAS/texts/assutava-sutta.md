## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-6 <a id="tea-assutava-sutta-2-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

It would be better for the uninstructed to take the body as self than the mind, for the body may last a hundred years, while what is called mind, thought or consciousness arises as one thing and ceases as another by day and by night, as a monkey roaming the forest seizes one branch, lets it go and seizes another. The noble disciple attends to dependent origination and becomes disenchanted with both.
