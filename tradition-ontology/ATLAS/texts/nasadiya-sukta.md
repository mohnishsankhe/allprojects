The hymn of the beginning: then there was neither non-being nor being, neither death nor immortality; That One breathed, windless, by its own power; darkness hidden by darkness; That One was born by the power of heat (tapas); desire, the first seed of mind, arose; seers searching in the heart found the bond of being in non-being; the gods came later than this creation, so who knows whence it came — he who oversees it in the highest heaven surely knows, or perhaps he does not know.

_Notes: Recited also in the Taittirīya Brāhmaṇa (2.8.9, reference from memory)._
