Patrul Rinpoche's oral-style guide to the Longchen Nyingthig preliminaries as taught by Jigme Gyalwai Nyugu: Part One the common outer preliminaries (freedoms and advantages, impermanence, the defects of saṃsāra, karma, the benefits of liberation, following a teacher); Part Two the uncommon inner preliminaries (refuge, bodhicitta, Vajrasattva, maṇḍala, kusāli, guru yoga); Part Three the transference of consciousness (phowa) as an auxiliary.

### 1.1 <a id="tea-kunzang-lamai-shelung-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

The difficulty of finding the freedoms and advantages: a human life free of the eight unfree states and endowed with the ten advantages (five personal, five circumstantial) is rarer than a turtle putting its head through a yoke on the ocean; it is also threatened by the eight intrusive circumstances and eight incompatible propensities.

### 3 <a id="tea-kunzang-lamai-shelung-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_ · _restricted: summary only_

Transference of consciousness (phowa), 'buddhahood without meditation', is taught as an auxiliary. Of its kinds (dharmakāya, sambhogakāya, nirmāṇakāya, the ordinary transference with three recognitions, and the transference for the dead by the hook of compassion), only the ordinary one is practised in training, and never applied before the signs of death are complete: to transfer untimely is like killing the deities.

### intro <a id="tea-kunzang-lamai-shelung-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

How to listen: avoid the three defects of the vessel (an upturned pot that hears nothing, a leaking pot that forgets, a pot with poison that mixes the teaching with bad motives) and the six stains (pride, lack of faith, lack of interest, outward distraction, inward tension, weariness); listen with the vast attitude of bodhicitta and the skilful attitude of secret mantra, seeing teacher, place and teaching as pure.
