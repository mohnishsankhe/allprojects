Madhurakavi Āḻvār's eleven verses addressed not to Viṣṇu but to his teacher Nammāḻvār: saying 'the Nampi of southern Kurukūr' is sweeter than approaching Kṛṣṇa himself; 'I know no other god'. The model of devotion to the teacher; the text Nāthamuni recited to recover the Prabandham.

### 1 <a id="tea-kanninun-ciruttampu-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Rather than approach the great wondrous one (Kṛṣṇa) who let himself be bound with a fine knotted cord, when I say 'the Nampi of southern Kurukūr', nectar wells up on my tongue.
