## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-adhyardhasatika-prajnaparamita-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The 'guide to the principle': all dharmas are pure by nature; the sixteen or seventeen pure states — including the states of desire, touch and embrace — are the pure state of the bodhisattva, because all dharmas are empty and pure in their nature.
