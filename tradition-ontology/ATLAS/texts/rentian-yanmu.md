### 304a11 <a id="tea-rentian-yanmu-304a11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Linji's four relations of illumination and function: sometimes illumination first and function after, sometimes function first and illumination after, sometimes both at once, sometimes not at once.

> 我有時先照後用。有時先用後照。有時照用同時。有時照用不同時

### 311b16 <a id="tea-rentian-yanmu-311b16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Rentian yanmu's summary of the Linji devices, including the four guest-host relations: a teacher who has 'nostrils' is host-within-host, a student who has them is guest-within-host, a teacher without them host-within-guest, a student without them guest-within-guest (different from the Caodong guest-host).

> 四賓主者。師家有鼻孔。名主中主。學人有鼻孔。名賓中主

### 312a07 <a id="tea-rentian-yanmu-312a07"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Yunmen said: 'containing heaven and earth; measuring the capacities of those before him; not involved in the myriad conditions — how will you take this on?' No one answered; he answered for them: 'one arrow breaks three barriers.' Deshan Yuanmi later divided this into three phrases: the phrase containing heaven and earth, the phrase cutting off the myriad streams, the phrase following the waves.

> 函蓋乾坤。目機銖兩。不涉萬緣。作麼生承當

### 321c09 <a id="tea-rentian-yanmu-321c09"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

On the origin of circle-figures: they began with National Teacher Huizhong of Nanyang, who gave them to his attendant Danyuan; Danyuan passed them to Yangshan, so they became the style of the Guiyang house; Wufeng Liang made forty of them, with six names.

> 圓相因起圓相之作。始於南陽忠國師。以授侍者耽源

### 324a01 <a id="tea-rentian-yanmu-324a01"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The guiding principle established by Fayan: the three realms are only mind, the myriad dharmas only consciousness; the Fayan house also used the Huayan six characteristics (totality and particularity, sameness and difference, formation and disintegration) to avoid views of annihilation and permanence.

> 三界惟心。萬法惟識。此法眼所立綱宗也
