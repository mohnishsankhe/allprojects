### 1.4 <a id="tea-sutta-nipata-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

To the brahmin farmer Bhāradvāja: faith is my seed, austerity the rain, wisdom my yoke and plough, conscience the pole, mind the yoke-tie, mindfulness my ploughshare and goad; energy is my draught ox; this ploughing has the deathless as its fruit.

### 1.7 <a id="tea-sutta-nipata-1-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Not by birth is one an outcaste, not by birth a brahmin; by deeds one is an outcaste, by deeds a brahmin — as the outcaste Mātaṅga won fame and the Brahmā world while brahmins born in reciters' families are reproached for evil deeds.

### 3.2 <a id="tea-sutta-nipata-3-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The bodhisatta striving by the Nerañjarā is approached by Namuci (Māra), who urges him to live and make merit; he answers that he has faith, energy and wisdom, and names Māra's ten armies — sensual desire, discontent, hunger and thirst, craving, sloth and torpor, fear, doubt, hypocrisy and obstinacy, gain, fame and honour wrongly got, self-praise and contempt of others; 'better to die in battle than live defeated'. Māra, finding no opening, departs like a crow pecking at a stone mistaken for fat.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: general · stage: all · types: powers-experiences, practice_

concepts: [Māra](../concepts/mara.md) · obstacles: [Māra](../obstacles/mara.md), [The ten armies of Māra](../obstacles/ten-armies-of-mara.md) · teachers: [Gotama Buddha](../teachers/gotama-buddha.md)

### 4.3 <a id="tea-sutta-nipata-4-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Some dispute with malicious or truthful intent, but the sage does not enter a dispute; how could one transcend one's own view, led by desire and fixed in preference? The purified one has no formulated view anywhere about existence or non-existence; having abandoned illusion and conceit, uninvolved, by what would he be judged?

_level: ultimate · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: dispute, ultimate_

### 4.8 <a id="tea-sutta-nipata-4-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

'Here alone is purity,' they say, denying purity in other teachings; eager for debate they plunge into the assembly, calling each other fools, seeking praise, and are dejected by defeat; the one who has put aside views does not dispute — what adversary would the Pasūra find in those who hold nothing as 'the highest'?

_level: ultimate · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: dispute_

### 4.9 <a id="tea-sutta-nipata-4-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Purity is not spoken of as by view, learning, knowledge, virtue or vows, nor without them; letting go of these, not grasping, peaceful, independent, one would not long for becoming. The sage does not think 'I am equal, superior or inferior'; the brahmin of this kind does not dispute and is not attached to views.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: polemical · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: dispute, ultimate_

concepts: [Not clinging to views](../concepts/non-attachment-to-views.md) · obstacles: [Conceit (māna)](../obstacles/mana.md), [Clinging to rules and observances](../obstacles/silabbata-paramasa.md)

### 4.11 <a id="tea-sutta-nipata-4-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Where do quarrels and disputes come from? From what is dear; what is dear from desire; desire from the pleasant and unpleasant; these from contact; contact from name-and-form; when perception and form cease — neither percipient of perceptions nor non-percipient — the concept of proliferation, which has perception as its source, ceases.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: causal · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind, dispute_

terms: [papañca](../terms/papanca.md) · concepts: [Proliferation (papañca)](../concepts/papanca.md), [Dependent origination](../concepts/dependent-origination.md)

### 4.14 <a id="tea-sutta-nipata-4-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

One should cut off the whole root of the concept of proliferation, the thought 'I am the thinker', and train to remove whatever cravings arise within; one should be settled within, like the ocean without a wave; with eyes not roving, ears guarded, not greedy for tastes, not grasping at anything in the world.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice_

concepts: [Proliferation (papañca)](../concepts/papanca.md) · practices: [Sense restraint (indriyasaṃvara)](../practices/indriya-samvara.md) · obstacles: [Conceit (māna)](../obstacles/mana.md)

### 4.15 <a id="tea-sutta-nipata-4-15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Fear is born from taking up the rod: seeing people struggling like fish in a drying pool, contending with each other, the sage saw the one dart lodged in the heart, hard to see, which when pulled out, one neither runs nor sinks; not lingering in the old, not delighting in the new, he does not grieve for what is lost.

### 927 <a id="tea-sutta-nipata-927"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

My follower should not practise Atharvan spells, dream-reading, bodily marks, nor astrology (nakkhatta); nor interpret animal cries, cause conception, or practise medicine.

> āthabbaṇaṃ supinaṃ lakkhaṇaṃ no vidahe athopi nakkhattaṃ | virutañca gabbhakaraṇaṃ tikicchaṃ māmako na seveyya ||
