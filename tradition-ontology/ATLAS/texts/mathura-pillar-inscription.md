Records that Uditācārya, tenth in succession from Kuśika, installed in a teachers' shrine two liṅgas named after his teachers (Kapileśvara and Upamiteśvara). The earliest dated evidence for the Pāśupata lineage of Kuśika and the basis of the scholarly dating of Lakulīśa.
