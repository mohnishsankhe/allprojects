A distinct text transmitted under the same title, identified and translated by J. Mallinson as the original Gorakṣaśataka: a manual of about a hundred verses teaching yoga through the control of the breath, the stirring of Sarasvatī (sarasvatīcālana), several kinds of breath retention and the three locks; several of its verses reappear in the Haṭhapradīpikā.
**Editions / translations:**
