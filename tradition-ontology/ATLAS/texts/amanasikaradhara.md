## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### end <a id="tea-amanasikaradhara-end"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Non-mentation' (amanasikāra) does not mean mere absence of mind: the 'a' may be read as the unborn (as the letter A is the door of all dharmas, being originally unborn, per the Hevajra and the Nāmasaṃgīti), as the seed of selflessness, or as the state of clear light while 'manasikāra' is self-consecration — so that amanasikāra denotes the union of clear light and self-consecration, inseparable emptiness and compassion.

> yadi vā a iti prabhāsvarapadam manasikāra iti svādhiṣṭhānapadam aścāsau manasikāraśceti amanasikāraḥ
