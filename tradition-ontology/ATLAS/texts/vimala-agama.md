# Vimalāgama

`src:vimala-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Vimala, Bhadra (Kiraṇa)
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Sarvātman, then Vīrabhadra (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Rudrabheda no. 18 in the Kāmika's order; 16 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** unknown

No. 18 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Rudrabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Sarvātman, then Vīrabhadra. The Kāmika assigns it 16 upabheda(s): Ananta, Bhoga, Ākrānta, Vṛṣapiṅga, Vṛṣodara, Vṛṣādbhuta, Sudanta, Raudra, Bhadravidha, Arevata, Atikrānta, Aṭṭahāsa, Alaṅkṛta, Arcita, Dhāraṇa, Vimala.

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.16/10.23 calls it 'Bhadra' of Sarvātman, received by the gaṇa Vīrabhadra. The Śrīkaṇṭhīya has both 'Vīrabhadra' and 'Vimala'. In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the arms (pūrva 1.93–101).
