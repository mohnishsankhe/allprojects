## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### whole (summary; on BhP 11.2–5) <a id="tea-nimi-navasiddha-samvada-bhagavata-dharma"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The nine sages teach King Nimi the devotee's religion: fearless refuge in the Lord, hearing and singing his names without shame, seeing the Lord in all beings; the best devotee sees the Lord in all and all in the Lord.
