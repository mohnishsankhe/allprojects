## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 5 <a id="tea-brhatkalpa-sutra-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

It is not allowed for a nun to be without clothing (acela); nor for a nun to stay alone, or to practise certain exposed postures outside the dwelling.
