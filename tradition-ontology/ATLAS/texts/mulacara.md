## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1.2-3 <a id="tea-mulacara-1-2-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The twenty-eight basic qualities of the monk are the five great vows, five carefulnesses, control of the five senses, the six essential duties, plucking the hair, nakedness, not bathing, sleeping on the ground, not cleaning the teeth, eating standing, and one meal a day.

### 4 <a id="tea-mulacara-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The rules for nuns (āryikā): they live together, wear a white garment, go out only in groups with an elder nun, do not stay alone or in a monk's dwelling, and keep the same root discipline as far as it applies to them.
