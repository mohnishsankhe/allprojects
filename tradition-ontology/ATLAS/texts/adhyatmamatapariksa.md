## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### structure <a id="tea-adhyatmamatapariksa-structure"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Against the 'Adhyātma' followers of Banārsīdās: the pure self is indeed the goal, but outer conduct, worship and the monastic order are its means; and the omniscient does take food, as scripture says.
