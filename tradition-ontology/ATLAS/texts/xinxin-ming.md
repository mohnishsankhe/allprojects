A poem in four-character lines on non-duality: 'The supreme Way is not difficult; it only dislikes picking and choosing.' Grasping, rejecting and dualistic views are the mind's sickness; when one mind does not arise, the myriad dharmas are without fault; faith in mind is non-dual.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 376b20 <a id="tea-xinxin-ming-376b20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The supreme Way is not difficult; it only dislikes picking and choosing. Just do not love or hate and it is clear; a hair's breadth of difference and heaven and earth are set apart; to have it appear, hold no for or against — the conflict of for and against is the mind's sickness.

> 至道無難 唯嫌揀擇 但莫憎愛 洞然明白 毫釐有差 天地懸隔

### 376c05 <a id="tea-xinxin-ming-376c05"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

As soon as there is right and wrong, the mind is lost in confusion; two exist because of one, yet do not keep even the one; when the one mind does not arise, the myriad dharmas are without fault.

> 纔有是非 紛然失心 二由一有 一亦莫守 一心不生 萬法無咎

### 376c15 <a id="tea-xinxin-ming-376c15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Wishing to go by the one vehicle, do not dislike the six sense objects; not disliking them is the same as true awakening; the wise do nothing, the foolish bind themselves; to use the mind to grasp the mind — is that not a great error?

> 欲趣一乘 勿惡六塵 六塵不惡 還同正覺

> 信心不二 不二信心 言語道斷 非去來今
