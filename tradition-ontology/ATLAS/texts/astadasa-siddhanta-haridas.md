Eighteen doctrinal songs by Svāmī Haridās: live as Hari keeps you, do not delay the name while death hunts with drawn bow, the world's pursuits are empty.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### siddhānta pada (incipit 'hari ke nāma kau ālasa') <a id="tea-astadasa-siddhanta-haridas-hari-ke-nama"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Why are you lazy about Hari's name? Death wanders with his arrow fitted to the string; all the world's pursuits are empty.
