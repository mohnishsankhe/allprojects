## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### abhaṅga 'avaghā raṅga eka jhālā' <a id="tea-soyrabai-abhangas-avagha-ranga"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

All colours have become one colour; the Lord is dyed in the dye; the distinction of 'I' and 'you' has gone.

> अवघा रंग एक झाला । रंगीं रंगला श्रीरंग ॥

### abhaṅga on bodily impurity <a id="tea-soyrabai-abhangas-deha-asuddha"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Soyrābāī asks: the body is called impure, but whose body is pure? Every body is born of the same flesh and blood; the Lord who dwells within is untouched by pollution.
