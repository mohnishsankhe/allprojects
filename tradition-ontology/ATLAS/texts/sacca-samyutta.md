## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 56.48 <a id="tea-sacca-samyutta-56-48"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

If the earth were one mass of water and a blind turtle surfaced once a century, it would put its neck through a yoke with one hole sooner than a being gone to the lower realms would regain the human state; therefore exert yourselves to understand 'this is suffering'.
