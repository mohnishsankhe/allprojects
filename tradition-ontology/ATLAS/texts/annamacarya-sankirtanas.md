## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### saṅkīrtana 'brahmam okkaṭe' <a id="tea-annamacarya-sankirtanas-brahmam-okkate"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Brahman is one, the supreme Brahman is one: the king's sleep and the servant's sleep are the same; the ground the elephant treads and the ground the dog treads are the same earth; for high and low the Lord Veṅkaṭeśvara is one.

> brahmam okkaṭe parabrahmam okkaṭe
