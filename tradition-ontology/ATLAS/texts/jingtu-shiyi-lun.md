### 77b23 <a id="tea-jingtu-shiyi-lun-77b23"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

First doubt: bodhisattvas' task is great compassion, so should they not vow to be born in the three realms to save beings, rather than seek the Pure Land's ease? Answer: bodhisattvas who have gained the acceptance of non-arising should indeed do so; but beginners and ordinary beings must first stay near the Buddha until their power of acceptance is complete — 'that an ordinary being in bondage with great compassion should vow to be born in the evil world to save beings is impossible' — like a child who must not leave its mother, or a bird with unfledged wings.

> 具縛凡夫，有大悲心願生惡世，救苦眾生者，無有是處。

### 78a01-b01 <a id="tea-jingtu-shiyi-lun-78a01-b01"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Second doubt: if all dharmas are empty and unborn, is seeking birth in the West not contrary to principle, and does the sūtra not say 'as the mind is pure the land is pure'? Answer: to cling to staying here is as much a fault as clinging to going there, and to seek neither is annihilationism. The buddhas teach by the two truths without destroying provisional names. The wise ardently seek birth in the Pure Land while understanding that the nature of birth cannot be found — this is true non-arising, and this is 'the mind being pure, the buddha-land is pure'; the foolish, bound by birth, take 'birth' and 'non-birth' literally, not knowing that birth is non-birth and non-birth is birth.

> 智者熾然求生淨土，達生體不可得，即是真無生，此謂心淨故即佛土淨。愚者為生所縛，聞生即作生解，聞無生即作無生解，不知生者即是無生，無生即是生。

### 79b03 <a id="tea-jingtu-shiyi-lun-79b03"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

How can ordinary beings in bondage be non-retrogressive once born? For five reasons: Amitābha's compassionate vow holds them; the Buddha's light always shines on them so the aspiration for awakening grows; the waters, birds, trees and music all preach suffering and emptiness and move them to recollect the Three Jewels; their companions are only bodhisattvas, with no evil conditions outside and no passions arising within; and their life there is as long as that of the bodhisattvas and buddhas.

> 得生彼國，有五因緣不退。云何為五？一者、阿彌陀佛大悲願力攝持，故得不退；二者、佛光常照，故菩提心常增進不退；三者、水鳥、樹林，風聲、樂響，皆說苦、空……四者、彼國純諸菩薩以為良友……五者、生彼國即壽命永劫，共菩薩、佛齊等，故不退也。

### 79b17 <a id="tea-jingtu-shiyi-lun-79b17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Seventh doubt: why not seek Maitreya's Tuṣita? They seem alike but differ greatly: even keeping the ten goods one may not be born in Tuṣita, which has no vow-power to receive beings, whereas Amitābha's vow and light embrace all who recollect him; Tuṣita is in the desire realm where many fall back and heavenly women arouse sensual attachment, while in the West all is Mahāyāna and passions never arise.

> 求生兜率，一日聞道見佛，勢欲相似，若細比校，大有優、劣。

### 80b05 <a id="tea-jingtu-shiyi-lun-80b05"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Ninth doubt: the West is ten trillion lands away, and the Rebirth Treatise says women and those with deficient faculties are not born there. Answer: distance is spoken of for ordinary eyes — the mind settled at death is already the mind born there, as in a dream one is elsewhere at once; and the verse means that in that land there are no women and no blind or deaf, not that women or the disabled of this world cannot be born — Vaidehī and her five hundred women were promised birth; once born they no longer take such bodies.

> 但論生彼國，無女人及無盲、聾、瘖、瘂人，不道此間女人、根缺人不得生。

### 80b30 <a id="tea-jingtu-shiyi-lun-80b30"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Tenth doubt: what practice ensures birth, and can householders who have not ended sexual desire be born? Two practices: loathing — contemplating the body as impure (seven kinds of impurity, from seed to death) so as gradually to turn from the five desires even if one cannot yet give up one's wife; and joyful aspiration — aspiring to the Pure Land, contemplating the Buddha and dedicating all merit.

> 欲決定生西方者，具有二種行，定得生彼：一者、厭離行；二者、欣願行。
