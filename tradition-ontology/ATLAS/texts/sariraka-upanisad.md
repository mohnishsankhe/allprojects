## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-5 <a id="tea-sariraka-upanisad-1-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The body is compounded of earth, water, fire, air and space; the senses of knowledge and action, their objects; mind, intellect, ego and citta with their seats; the three guṇas and states.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: body-layers_
