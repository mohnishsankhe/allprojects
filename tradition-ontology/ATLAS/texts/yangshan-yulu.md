## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 582a19 <a id="tea-yangshan-yulu-582a19"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Danyuan gave Yangshan the 97 circle-figures handed down from the six patriarchs through National Teacher Huizhong; Yangshan read them and burned them — 'having seen them once I know their meaning; one must be able to use them, not cling to the text' — and redrew them from memory when asked.

> 國師當時傳得六代祖師圓相，共九十七箇
