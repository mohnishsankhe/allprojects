## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-300 <a id="tea-ratnatraya-srikantha-1-300"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Śiva is the agent, his śakti the instrument, and bindu — pure māyā — the material cause of the pure universe, of the bodies of the pure beings and of sound; Śiva himself undergoes no change.
