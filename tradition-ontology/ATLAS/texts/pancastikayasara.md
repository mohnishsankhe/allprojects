Kundakunda's account of the five 'extended' substances (soul, matter, the medium of motion, the medium of rest, space) and time; substance defined by existence, which is conjoined with origination, destruction and permanence; the sevenfold predication; then the nine categories and the path to liberation.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 10 <a id="tea-pancastikayasara-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Substance (dravya) is characterized by existence; it is conjoined with origination, destruction and permanence; it is the substrate of qualities and modes.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic (dravyarthika) · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate_

terms: [dravya](../terms/dravya.md) · concepts: [The six substances (ṣaḍ-dravya)](../concepts/six-dravyas.md), [Many-sidedness (anekāntavāda)](../concepts/anekantavada.md)

### 14 <a id="tea-pancastikayasara-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

A substance can be described in seven ways according to the respect intended: in some respect it is, is not, is and is not, is inexpressible, is and is inexpressible, is not and is inexpressible, is, is not and is inexpressible.
