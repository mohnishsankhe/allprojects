## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### three-minds <a id="tea-tenzo-kyokun-three-minds"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The cook should work with three minds: a joyful mind, a nurturing (parental) mind and a great mind; preparing food is itself the buddha-way; Dōgen recounts the old Chinese tenzo who told him that practice is not something others can do for you.
