About three hundred bhāruḍs — dramatic allegorical folk songs in the voices of village characters (the scorpion-bitten man, the Jogī, the madwoman, the Mahār) — teaching dispassion, devotion and knowledge to ordinary people.

### bhāruḍ 'viñcū cāvalā' <a id="tea-eknath-bharuds-vincu-cavala"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

In the bhāruḍ of the scorpion's sting, a man bitten by the scorpion of anger and delusion (tamas) cries out as the venom climbs to his head; the guru's remedy is the sattva quality and the Lord's name.
