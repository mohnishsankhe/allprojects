## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-gopalatapani-upanisad-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Salutation to Kṛṣṇa, whose form is being, consciousness and bliss, who acts without effort, who is known through Vedānta, the guru and the witness of the intellect.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate_

### pūrva (bhakti) <a id="tea-gopalatapani-upanisad-purva-bhakti"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Devotion is worship of him: fixing the mind on him with no desire for this world or the next; this alone is freedom from action (naiṣkarmya).

> bhaktir asya bhajanaṃ tad ihāmutropādhinairāśyenaivāmuṣmin manaḥkalpanam etad eva ca naiṣkarmyam

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion · stage: all · types: practice, karma-liberation_

### pūrva 1 <a id="tea-gopalatapani-upanisad-purva-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

'Kṛṣ' denotes existence and 'ṇa' bliss; their union is Kṛṣṇa, the supreme Brahman.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate, sound-language_

### pūrva 2-3 <a id="tea-gopalatapani-upanisad-purva-2-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The sages ask Brahmā who is the supreme god, whom death fears; Brahmā: Kṛṣṇa (Govinda) is the supreme god; his meditation form and his mantra are given.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate, teacher-transmission_

practices: [Recitation of the Gopāla mantra](../practices/gopala-mantra-japa.md) · teachers: [Brahmā (as first teacher)](../teachers/brahma.md)

### uttara (summary) <a id="tea-gopalatapani-upanisad-uttara-summary"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The gopīs ask Durvāsas; Kṛṣṇa is the Self, ever celibate though amid them; Mathurā is described as a maṇḍala; Kṛṣṇa is the one Lord in all.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate_
