### 6.7 <a id="tea-padmanandi-pancavimsatika-6-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Worship of the god, attendance on the teacher, study, restraint, austerity and giving are the six daily duties of householders.

> devapūjā gurūpāstiḥ svādhyāyaḥ saṃyamas tapaḥ / dānaṃ ceti gṛhasthānāṃ ṣaṭ karmāṇi dine dine
