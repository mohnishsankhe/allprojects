## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-lalitavistara-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Buddha's descent from Tuṣita, birth, renunciation and awakening are the play (lalita) of a being who had long accomplished the path, displayed for the world's benefit.
