# Samādhibhāvanā Sutta

Four developments of concentration: for a pleasant abiding here and now (the jhānas), for knowledge and vision (perception of light), for mindfulness and clear comprehension (feelings, perceptions and thoughts known as they arise, stay and vanish), and for the destruction of the effluents (rise and fall of the aggregates).

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-6 <a id="tea-samadhibhavana-sutta-1-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Four developments of concentration: the four jhānas lead to a pleasant abiding here and now; attention to the perception of light, resolving on 'day' by night as by day, leads to knowledge and vision; knowing feelings, perceptions and thoughts as they arise, remain and vanish leads to mindfulness and clear comprehension; dwelling contemplating rise and fall in the five aggregates leads to the destruction of the effluents.
