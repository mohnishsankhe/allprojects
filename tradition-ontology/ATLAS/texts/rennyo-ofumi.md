## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 5.1 <a id="tea-rennyo-ofumi-5-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Lay men and women of this last age, ignorant of the teaching, should abandon all miscellaneous practices and single-heartedly and deeply rely on Amida, asking him to save them in the life to come; then, without doubt, they will be saved; after that, say the nenbutsu in gratitude for as long as you live.

### 5.16 <a id="tea-rennyo-ofumi-5-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When we consider the fleeting nature of human life, it is like an illusion from beginning to end; in the morning we may have rosy cheeks, in the evening be white ashes; when the winds of impermanence blow, eyes close, breath stops, the face loses its colour; family and relatives weep, but nothing can be done; the body is cremated and only white ashes remain. So everyone should take to heart the one great matter of the life to come, rely deeply on Amida Buddha, and say the nenbutsu.

### heresies <a id="tea-rennyo-ofumi-heresies"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Rennyo rejects the view that birth was already settled ten kalpas ago when Amida attained buddhahood so that no entrusting is now needed (jikkō anjin), the secret teachings transmitted in private (hiji bōmon), and the view that simply saying the name without shinjin brings birth; what is required is to rely on Amida single-heartedly.

_level: conventional · standpoint: polemical · path: devotion · stage: all · types: dispute_

obstacles: [The heresy of 'settled ten kalpas ago' (jikkō anjin)](../obstacles/jikko-anjin.md), [Secret teachings (hiji bōmon)](../obstacles/hiji-bomon.md) · teachers: [Rennyo](../teachers/rennyo.md)

### obo <a id="tea-rennyo-ofumi-obo"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Keep the laws of the land as fundamental (ōbō ihon) and humanity and justice first; do not disparage other buddhas, bodhisattvas and kami, for they are included in Amida; do not speak of the teaching indiscriminately in public; keep shinjin deep within.
