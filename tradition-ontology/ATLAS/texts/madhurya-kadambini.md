## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 3 <a id="tea-madhurya-kadambini-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Unwanted things (anartha) are of four kinds: those arising from sin, from pious acts, from offences and from devotion itself (desire for gain, honour and prestige); unsteady practice passes through six phases: false enthusiasm, alternating zeal and slackness, indecision, struggle with sense objects, inability to keep vows, and delight in the external benefits of devotion.

### 4 <a id="tea-madhurya-kadambini-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Steadiness (niṣṭhā) is reached when five obstacles no longer arise in practice: sleepiness (laya), distraction (vikṣepa), inability or indifference (apratipatti), residual impurity (kaṣāya) and relish of other pleasures (rasāsvāda).
