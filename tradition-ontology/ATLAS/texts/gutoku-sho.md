## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### two-pairs-four-levels <a id="tea-gutoku-sho-two-pairs-four-levels"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The teachings divide into two pairs and four levels: vertical and crosswise, going out and transcending. Vertical transcendence is the sudden teaching of the Mahāyāna (becoming a buddha in this body); vertical going out is the gradual teaching of the Mahāyāna (and of the two vehicles); crosswise going out is the Pure Land teaching of various practices and self-power nenbutsu (nineteenth and twentieth vows); crosswise transcendence is the other-power shinjin of the primal vow (eighteenth).
