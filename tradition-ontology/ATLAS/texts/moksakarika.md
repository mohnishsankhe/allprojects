Sadyojyoti's verses on liberation: the soul is by nature a knower and agent whose powers are covered by beginningless mala; Śiva's power, acting through initiation, removes the mala at the right time, and the released soul manifests omniscience and omnipotence equal to Śiva's (śivatva) while remaining a distinct soul.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Mokṣakārikāvṛtti](moksakarikavrtti.md)

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-150 <a id="tea-moksakarika-1-150"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Liberation is not the soul's destruction or its becoming unconscious, but the full manifestation (abhivyakti) of its innate powers of knowing and acting, which mala had covered; when Śiva's power removes the mala, the soul becomes equal to Śiva (a 'Śiva').
