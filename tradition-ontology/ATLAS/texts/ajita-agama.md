# Ajitāgama

`src:ajita-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Ajita
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
**Genre:** āgama (Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgama)
**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Sadāśiva from the upper (Īśāna) face; first received by Suśiva, then Śiva (Kiraṇa: Umeśa), then Acyuta (Kāmika pūrva 1.30–92; Kiraṇa vidyāpāda 10); scholarly: texts now transmitted under this title are mostly South Indian redactions centred on temple ritual; their relation to the early scripture of the same name is not established here; confidence: low
**Structure:** Śivabheda no. 5 in the Kāmika's order; 4 upabhedas in the Kāmika's list
**Availability:** unknown

No. 5 of the 28 Śaiva Siddhānta mūlāgamas (Śivabheda). According to the Kāmika it was first received by Suśiva, then Śiva (Kiraṇa: Umeśa), then Acyuta. The Kāmika assigns it 4 upabheda(s): Prabhūta, Parodbhūta, Pārvatī, Padmasaṃhitā. A ritual-centred South Indian text (kriyā and caryā).
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Ajitāgama, ed. N. R. Bhatt, Institut Français d'Indologie, Pondicherry (4 vols, 1964–1991)

_Notes: Kiraṇa 10.5/10.9: Suśiva → Umeśa → Acyuta. In the Kāmika's image of the scriptures as Sadāśiva's body it is the knees (pūrva 1.93–101).
