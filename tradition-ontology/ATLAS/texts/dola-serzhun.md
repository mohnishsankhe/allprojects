Mañjuśrīmitra's verse treatise on cultivating bodhicitta as the non-conceptual path: the awakened mind is beyond conceptual examination and verbal convention, worldly conventions of valid cognition are not needed on this yogic path, and those who seek other paths are 'like gold in ore' — worthy of compassion. One of the five early mind-series translations; preserved in the Tengyur.
