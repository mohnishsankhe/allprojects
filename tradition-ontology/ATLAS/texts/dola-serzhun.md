Mañjuśrīmitra's verse treatise on cultivating bodhicitta as the non-conceptual path: the awakened mind is beyond conceptual examination and verbal convention, worldly conventions of valid cognition are not needed on this yogic path, and those who seek other paths are 'like gold in ore' — worthy of compassion. ## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 2a <a id="tea-dola-serzhun-2a"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

This path of the great sage is subtle and hard to know: non-conceptual, beyond conceptions, hard to examine and to teach, free of verbal convention and not within the reach of the immature — one looks to the Teacher's definitive scripture and the instructions of holy masters. Analysis that takes perception and the like as valid means follows an endless stream of conceptions and cannot reach the essence; therefore the worldly conventions of examination are not needed on this yogic path.

> de phyir 'jig rten brtags pa'i tha snyad rnal 'byor lam 'dir mi dgos so/

### 4b <a id="tea-dola-serzhun-4b"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Those deceived by conceptions, following the stream of delusion like dreamers taking dreams as true, who abandon this path and teach other paths as the unmistaken one, are like gold in ore — worthy of compassion. May this unsurpassed non-dual path, established by valid means through the power of familiarity, spread for the benefit of beings.
