## Teachings (13: skeleton 13)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-mahavagga-vinaya-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The newly awakened Buddha sat seven days at the foot of the Bodhi tree experiencing the bliss of liberation, and in the three watches of the night attended to dependent origination in forward and reverse order, uttering the three verses on the manifestation of phenomena to the ardent meditating brahmin.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: experiential · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation, teacher-transmission_

concepts: [Dependent origination](../concepts/dependent-origination.md), [The awakening of the Buddha](../concepts/awakening-of-the-buddha.md)

### 1.11.1.4 <a id="tea-mahavagga-vinaya-1-11-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Wander forth, monks, for the welfare of the many, for the happiness of the many, out of compassion for the world, for the good, welfare and happiness of gods and humans.

> Caratha, bhikkhave, cārikaṁ bahujanahitāya bahujanasukhāya lokānukampāya atthāya hitāya sukhāya devamanussānaṁ.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission_

### 1.23-1.24 <a id="tea-mahavagga-vinaya-1-23-1-24"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The wanderer Sāriputta, pupil of Sañjaya, sees the monk Assaji on alms round, asks his teacher and teaching, hears the 'ye dhammā' verse and gains the eye of Dhamma; he and Moggallāna go forth with 250 of Sañjaya's pupils; the Buddha declares them his chief pair of disciples.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: general · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission_

teachers: [Sāriputta](../teachers/sariputta.md), [Mahāmoggallāna](../teachers/moggallana.md), [Assaji](../teachers/assaji.md), [Sañjaya Belaṭṭhiputta](../teachers/sanjaya-belatthiputta.md)

### 1.23.5 <a id="tea-mahavagga-vinaya-1-23-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Assaji's summary to Sāriputta: of things that arise from a cause, the Tathāgata has told the cause, and also their cessation: such is the teaching of the Great Ascetic.

> “Ye dhammā hetuppabhavā, Tesaṁ hetuṁ tathāgato āha; Tesañca yo nirodho, Evaṁvādī mahāsamaṇo”ti.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: causal · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, teacher-transmission_

terms: [hetu](../terms/hetu.md) · concepts: [Dependent origination](../concepts/dependent-origination.md) · teachers: [Assaji](../teachers/assaji.md), [Sāriputta](../teachers/sariputta.md)

### 2.1-2.3 <a id="tea-mahavagga-vinaya-2-1-2-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

At the suggestion of King Bimbisāra, who saw other sects meeting on the fourteenth, fifteenth and eighth of the fortnight, the Buddha allows the monks to assemble and speak Dhamma on those days, and then to recite the Pātimokkha as their uposatha observance.
