The East Asian bodhisattva-precept scripture: the ten major and forty-eight minor precepts (no killing, stealing, sexual misconduct, lying, selling alcohol, speaking of others' faults, praising self and disparaging others, stinginess, anger, reviling the three jewels; and minor rules including not eating meat or the five pungent plants); the buddha-nature precepts binding lay and ordained alike.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### major-precepts <a id="tea-fanwang-jing-major-precepts"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The ten major precepts of the bodhisattva: not to kill, steal, engage in sexual misconduct, lie, sell intoxicants, speak of the faults of the fourfold assembly, praise oneself and disparage others, be stingy, be angry and refuse apology, or slander the three jewels; with forty-eight minor precepts, including not eating meat; all beings who receive the precepts enter the rank of buddhas.
