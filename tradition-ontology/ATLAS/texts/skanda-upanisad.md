## Teachings (3: sourced 3)

### 6-7 <a id="tea-skanda-upanisad-6-7"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

The jīva is Śiva and Śiva is the jīva; the jīva is Śiva alone; bound by the husk it is paddy, without husk it is rice — so the bound one is jīva, freed from karma Sadāśiva.

> jīvaḥ śivaḥ śivo jīvaḥ sa jīvaḥ kevalaḥ śivaḥ | tuṣeṇa baddho vrīhiḥ syāt tuṣābhāvena taṇḍulaḥ

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate, karma-liberation_

concepts: [Paśu and Paśupati](../concepts/pasu-pasupati.md)

### 8-9 <a id="tea-skanda-upanisad-8-9"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Salutation to Śiva in the form of Viṣṇu and to Viṣṇu in the form of Śiva; Viṣṇu is the heart of Śiva and Śiva of Viṣṇu; as Viṣṇu is full of Śiva, so Śiva is full of Viṣṇu.

_level: conventional · standpoint: divine · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate, dispute_

concepts: [Identity of Śiva and Viṣṇu](../concepts/siva-visnu-identity.md) · disputes: [Which deity is the supreme Brahman — Śiva, Viṣṇu/Nārāyaṇa, or the Goddess?](../debates/which-deity-is-supreme.md)

### 10-12 <a id="tea-skanda-upanisad-10-12"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

The body is the temple and the jīva is Śiva; worship with 'I am He' (as in Maitreya 2.1–3).
