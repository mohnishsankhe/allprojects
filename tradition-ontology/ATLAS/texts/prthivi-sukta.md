The sixty-three-verse hymn to the Earth: she is upheld by truth, great order, consecration, tapas, brahman and sacrifice; 'the Earth is mother, I am her son'; she bears peoples of many speeches and many dharmas; the digger asks that what he digs may grow again and that he not strike her vitals or heart.
