## Teachings (3: sourced 3)

### 1 (conduct at will) <a id="tea-nirvana-upanisad-1-conduct-at-will"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Conduct at will (svecchācāra) is his nature; liberation is the cutting of bondage.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: karma-liberation_

concepts: [Conduct at will (svecchācāra)](../concepts/svecchacara.md)

### 1 (marks) <a id="tea-nirvana-upanisad-1-marks"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

'I am the paramahaṃsa'; his patched cloak is courage, his loincloth indifference, his staff inquiry; his vow is the ajapā, his mudrā khecarī; the fourth is his sacred thread.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ethics, ultimate_

concepts: [The renouncer's marks (liṅga)](../concepts/renunciant-marks.md), [The inner topknot and sacred thread](../concepts/inner-sikha-yajnopavita.md)

`sourced` · confidence moderate

This is to be taught only to a disciple or a son.
