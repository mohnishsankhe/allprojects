### 2.7 <a id="tea-milindapanha-2-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

King Milinda asks Pūraṇa Kassapa who protects the world; Pūraṇa says 'the earth protects the world'; asked why beings going to Avīci hell pass beyond the earth, he can neither swallow nor spit out the question and sits silent.

### 2.8 <a id="tea-milindapanha-2-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Milinda asks Makkhali Gosāla whether there are good and bad deeds and their fruit; Gosāla says there are none: those who are khattiyas, brāhmaṇas, vessas, suddas, caṇḍālas or pukkusas here will be the same in the next world. Milinda asks whether then one whose hand is cut off here will have it cut off there too; Gosāla falls silent.

### 3.1.1 <a id="tea-milindapanha-3-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Asked his name, Nāgasena says he is known as 'Nāgasena' but that no person is found here; the king objects that then there is no one who keeps precepts or does evil. Nāgasena asks whether the pole, axle, wheels or body of the king's chariot is the chariot, or something apart from them; the king admits that 'chariot' is a designation dependent on the parts; so 'Nāgasena' is a designation, a mere name dependent on the parts of the body and the five aggregates, but in the ultimate sense (paramatthato) no person is found — as the nun Vajirā said before the Buddha.

### 3.1.1/2 <a id="tea-milindapanha-3-1-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Nāgasena' is a reckoning, a designation, a concept, a usage, a mere name dependent on the parts and aggregates; but in the ultimate sense no person is found here.

> evameva kho, mahārāja, mayhampi kese ca paṭicca lome ca paṭicca …pe… matthake matthaluṅgañca paṭicca rūpañca paṭicca vedanañca paṭicca saññañca paṭicca saṅkhāre ca paṭicca viññāṇañca paṭicca ‘nāgaseno’ti saṅkhā samaññā paññatti vohāro nāmamattaṁ pavattati, paramatthato panettha puggalo nūpalabbhati.
