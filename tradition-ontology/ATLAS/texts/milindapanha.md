The Questions of King Milinda: the Indo-Greek king Menander questions the monk Nāgasena on not-self (the chariot), rebirth without transmigration (the flame), kamma, nibbāna and apparent inconsistencies in the texts (dilemmas). Canonical in the Burmese Khuddaka Nikāya.
