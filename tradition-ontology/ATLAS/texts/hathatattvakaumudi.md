# Haṭhatattvakaumudī

`src:hathatattvakaumudi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Original title:** हठतत्त्वकौमुदी
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Genre:** yoga compendium
**Authors:** 
  - [Sundaradeva](../teachers/sundaradeva.md) — role: author; attribution: accepted
**Attribution:** tradition: by Sundaradeva of Kāśī, son of Govindadeva, grandson of Viśvanāthadeva, of the Kāśyapa gotra (colophon); scholarly: 18th c. CE; confidence: high
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 18th c. CE; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 56 chapters (udyota) grouped in ullāsas, the last (9th) on rājayoga (Lonavla edition)
**Availability:** digitized-original

A large compendium of haṭha that quotes and systematizes earlier texts: yamas and niyamas, the eight cleansing acts, postures, many retentions (including raising and lowering kinds), the three bandhas and mudrās, vajrolī, kuṇḍalinī and śakticālana, the channels and breaths, the sixteen supports, the centres, nāda, saguṇa and nirguṇa meditation, saṃyama and its powers, rājayoga, the signs of approaching death and the cheating of death.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Haṭhatattvakaumudī of Sundaradeva, ed. M. L. Gharote, P. Devnath, V. K. Jha (Lonavla 2007); licence: copyright
