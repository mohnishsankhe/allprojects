Mānatuṅga's Sanskrit hymn to Ṛṣabha — 44 verses in the Śvetāmbara text, 48 in the Digambara — recited by all Jains; the tradition tells that each verse broke a fetter when the king imprisoned the poet. A later tradition attaches a mantra, a yantra and a benefit to each verse.

## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 1 <a id="tea-bhaktamara-stotra-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Having duly bowed to the feet of the first Jina — which light up the jewels in the crowns of the bowing gods, dispel the darkness of sin, and are the support of beings sinking in the ocean of existence — I will praise him.

### 23 <a id="tea-bhaktamara-stotra-23"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The sages call you the supreme person, sun-coloured, stainless, beyond darkness; by rightly attaining you alone they conquer death — there is no other auspicious path to the auspicious state.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate, karma-liberation_

teachers: [Mānatuṅga](../teachers/manatunga.md), [Ṛṣabha (Ṛṣabhanātha, Ādinātha)](../teachers/rsabha.md)

### 25 <a id="tea-bhaktamara-stotra-25"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

You are the Buddha, since the gods honour your awakened understanding; you are Śaṅkara, since you bring well-being to the three worlds; you are the Creator (Dhātṛ), since you laid down the rule of the path to bliss; clearly you alone, Lord, are the Supreme Person (Puruṣottama).

> buddhas tvam eva vibudhārcitabuddhibodhāt tvaṃ śaṅkaro 'si bhuvanatrayaśaṅkaratvāt / dhātāsi dhīra śivamārgavidher vidhānāt vyaktaṃ tvam eva bhagavan puruṣottamo 'si
