## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 577c04 <a id="tea-guishan-yulu-577c04"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Asked whether one who has suddenly awakened still cultivates: when truly awakened one knows for oneself; cultivating and not cultivating are two-sided words. A beginner who through conditions suddenly awakens to principle in one thought still has beginningless habit energies not yet purified at once, and must clear away the currents of present karmic consciousness — that is cultivation. 'In the ground of true reality not a speck of dust is accepted; in the gate of the myriad practices not a single dharma is discarded.'

> 如今初心雖從緣得，一念頓悟自理，猶有無始曠劫習氣未能頓淨，須教渠淨除現業流識，即是修也
