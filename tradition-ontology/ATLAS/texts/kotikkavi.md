## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-4 <a id="tea-kotikkavi-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Four verses on the meaning of raising Śiva's flag: the soul's bonds are cut and grace is proclaimed; tradition says the stuck festival flag at Chidambaram rose as they were sung.
