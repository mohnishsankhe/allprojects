## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 527a05 <a id="tea-caoshan-yulu-527a05"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Caoshan on the five ranks: the real position is the realm of emptiness, originally without things; the apparent position is the realm of form with the myriad images; the apparent within the real turns from principle to phenomena, the real within the apparent from phenomena to principle; the combined rank responds to all conditions without falling into existence — lord (real) and minister (apparent), minister facing lord, lord regarding minister, lord and minister united.

> 正位即空界，本來無物；偏位即色界，有萬象形
