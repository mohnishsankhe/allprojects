## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 8-18 <a id="tea-ambalatthikarahulovada-sutta-8-18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Like a mirror for reflection, one should reflect again and again before, during and after every bodily, verbal and mental action: 'does it lead to my affliction, to others', to both? is it unwholesome, with painful result?' If so, abandon it, confess it to the teacher or wise companions, and restrain in future; if not, continue in joy. All past, future and present ascetics purify their actions in this way.
