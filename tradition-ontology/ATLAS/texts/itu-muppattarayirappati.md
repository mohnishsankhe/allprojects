## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 6.10.10 <a id="tea-itu-muppattarayirappati-6-10-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

On Tiruvāymoḻi 6.10.10 ('I cannot bear to part even for a moment,' says she of the lotus, dwelling on your chest), the commentary reads Nammāḻvār's taking refuge at Tirumala as surrender to the Lord's feet with Śrī, ever on his chest, as mediator — the meaning of the dvaya.
