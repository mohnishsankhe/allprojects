# Barābar cave inscriptions of Aśoka

`src:barabar-cave-inscriptions` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Prakrit
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md)
**Genre:** inscription
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 257 and 250 BCE (12th and 19th regnal years); (confidence moderate)
**Availability:** digitized-original

Short dedicatory inscriptions in the Barābar hills (Bihar) recording that King Piyadasi gave the Nigrodha cave and the cave in the Khalatika hill to the Ājīvikas in his 12th regnal year, and a further cave in his 19th; the word 'Ājīvikehi' was later chiselled out in places.

_Notes: Scholarly metadata only: records donations, teaches nothing. The defacement is reported by epigraphists._
