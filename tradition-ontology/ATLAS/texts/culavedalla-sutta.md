## Teachings (6: skeleton 6)

### 2-8 <a id="tea-culavedalla-sutta-2-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Identity (sakkāya) is the five aggregates of clinging; its origin is craving; its cessation the fading of craving; the way the noble eightfold path. Identity view arises when the uninstructed person regards form (and each aggregate) as self, self as possessing form, form in self, or self in form.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind, ultimate_

terms: [sakkāya](../terms/sakkaya.md), [sakkāyadiṭṭhi](../terms/sakkaya-ditthi.md), [upādānakkhandha](../terms/upadanakkhandha.md) · concepts: [The five aggregates](../concepts/five-aggregates.md) · obstacles: [Identity view (sakkāyadiṭṭhi)](../obstacles/sakkaya-ditthi.md) · teachers: [Dhammadinnā](../teachers/dhammadinna.md)

### 11-12 <a id="tea-culavedalla-sutta-11-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The three aggregates of training are not included in the eightfold path, but the path is included in them: right speech, action and livelihood in the aggregate of virtue; right effort, mindfulness and concentration in the aggregate of concentration; right view and intention in the aggregate of wisdom. Unification of mind is concentration, the four establishments of mindfulness are its signs.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: general · stage: all · types: practice_

concepts: [The three trainings (tisso sikkhā)](../concepts/three-trainings.md), [The noble eightfold path](../concepts/noble-eightfold-path.md) · teachers: [Dhammadinnā](../teachers/dhammadinna.md)

### 14.2 <a id="tea-culavedalla-sutta-14-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In-and-out breathing is bodily formation; applied and sustained thought are verbal formation; perception and feeling are mental formation.

> “Assāsapassāsā kho, āvuso visākha, kāyasaṅkhāro, vitakkavicārā vacīsaṅkhāro, saññā ca vedanā ca cittasaṅkhāro”ti.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: meditation · stage: all · types: body-layers, consciousness-mind_

terms: [saṅkhāra](../terms/sankhara.md), [kāyasaṅkhāra](../terms/kayasankhara.md) · teachers: [Dhammadinnā](../teachers/dhammadinna.md)

### 16-21 <a id="tea-culavedalla-sutta-16-21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

One entering the cessation of perception and feeling does not think 'I shall enter' but his mind has been developed so as to lead to it; verbal formation ceases first, then bodily, then mental; on emerging, mental formation arises first; he is touched by three contacts — empty, signless and undirected — and his mind inclines to seclusion.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind, powers-experiences_

terms: [saññāvedayitanirodha](../terms/sannavedayitanirodha.md) · concepts: [The attainment of cessation](../concepts/nirodha-samapatti.md)

### 25-28 <a id="tea-culavedalla-sutta-25-28"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The underlying tendency to lust underlies pleasant feeling, to aversion painful feeling, and to ignorance neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling — but not every such feeling; through the first jhāna lust is abandoned, through longing for the peaceful liberation aversion, through the fourth jhāna ignorance.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [anusaya](../terms/anusaya.md), [vedanā](../terms/vedana.md) · obstacles: [The seven latent tendencies (anusaya)](../obstacles/seven-anusayas.md)

### 29-30 <a id="tea-culavedalla-sutta-29-30"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Asked the counterpart of nibbāna, Dhammadinnā answers that the question goes too far: the holy life is grounded in nibbāna, culminates in nibbāna, ends in nibbāna. The Buddha says: Dhammadinnā is wise; had you asked me, I would have answered in the same way.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: ultimate, teacher-transmission_
