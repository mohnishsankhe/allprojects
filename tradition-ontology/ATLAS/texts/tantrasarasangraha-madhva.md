one of the thirty-seven works of Madhva collected as the Sarvamūla ('root of all'); a manual in four chapters on the Lord's mantras and their derivation from the eightfold praṇava, the fifty letters as designating fifty forms of Viṣṇu, and on worship, images and temples, drawing on Pañcarātra material.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1.3-4 <a id="tea-tantrasarasangraha-madhva-1-3-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

(The Lord says:) I alone am full of all qualities; my designator is the praṇava, which, from 'a' to 'atiśānta', is eightfold; it designates my forms Viśva, Taijasa, Prājña, Turīya, Ātman, Antarātman, Paramātman and Jñānātman.

### 1.5-14 <a id="tea-tantrasarasangraha-madhva-1-5-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Lord has fifty further forms, designated by the fifty letters as differentiated from the praṇava — Aja, Ānanda, Indra, Īśa, Ugra, Ūrja, Ṛtambhara and the rest down to the last; from the praṇava arise the eight-syllable Nārāyaṇa mantra, the vyāhṛtis, the twelve-syllable mantra with the Keśava forms as its deities, and the Gāyatrī.
