## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### intro <a id="tea-trimsikabhasya-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Thirty Verses were composed to teach the selflessness of persons and of dharmas to those who misunderstand or do not understand them; teaching them serves to abandon the afflictive and the cognitive obscurations, since the afflictions arise from self-view and the cognitive obscuration is removed by knowing the selflessness of dharmas.

> pudgaladharmanairātmyayor apratipannavipratipannānām aviparītapudgaladharmanairātmyapratipādanārthaṃ triṃśikāvijñaptiprakaraṇārambhaḥ
