## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### bardo <a id="tea-yeshe-lama-bardo"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Instructions for those not liberated in life: at death recognize the ground luminosity (the meeting of mother and child luminosity); failing that, recognize the lights and deities of the bardo of dharmatā as one's own display; failing that, take a birth in a pure land through the bardo of becoming.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: death-dying, practice_

### rushen <a id="tea-yeshe-lama-rushen"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The extraordinary preliminaries: the practices of separating saṃsāra from nirvāṇa (ru shan) through body, speech and mind — exhausting ordinary activity, training with the syllable hūṃ, and searching for where mind comes from, stays and goes — so that one does not mistake ordinary mind for awareness.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, sound · stage: advanced · types: practice_

practices: [Separating saṃsāra and nirvāṇa ('khor 'das ru shan)](../practices/rushen.md), [Searching for the mind (sems 'tshol)](../practices/mind-searching.md) · teachers: [Jigme Lingpa (rig 'dzin 'jigs med gling pa)](../teachers/jigme-lingpa.md)

### togal <a id="tea-yeshe-lama-togal"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

Direct crossing is taught only to those stable in cutting through: relying on the key points given orally by the master, the lamps bring forth the four visions, culminating in the exhaustion of phenomena in dharmatā. The text insists that the instructions be received from a qualified master and practised under guidance.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice, powers-experiences_

practices: [Direct crossing (thod rgal)](../practices/togal.md) · teachers: [Jigme Lingpa (rig 'dzin 'jigs med gling pa)](../teachers/jigme-lingpa.md)

### trekcho <a id="tea-yeshe-lama-trekcho"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Cutting through: after the introduction by the master, awareness is recognized as fresh, naked and unfabricated; one rests in it without meditating, and whatever arises is liberated by itself; the faults of stupor, agitation and fabrication are recognized and left.
