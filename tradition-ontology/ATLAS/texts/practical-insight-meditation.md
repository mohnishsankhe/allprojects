## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### basic <a id="tea-practical-insight-meditation-basic"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

Sitting with attention on the abdomen, note its rising and falling as 'rising', 'falling'; when the mind wanders, note 'thinking', 'imagining' and so on and return; note pains, sounds and other objects as they become prominent, and then return to the abdomen.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: beginner · types: practice_

practices: [Noting (the Mahāsi method)](../practices/mahasi-noting.md), [Noting the rising and falling of the abdomen](../practices/rising-falling.md) · teachers: [Mahāsi Sayadaw](../teachers/mahasi-sayadaw.md)

### concentration <a id="tea-practical-insight-meditation-concentration"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

When noting is continuous and precise, momentary concentration grows strong enough for insight into the arising and passing of phenomena, without first developing jhāna.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice, dispute_

terms: [khaṇika-samādhi](../terms/khanika-samadhi.md) · concepts: [Momentary concentration (khaṇika-samādhi)](../concepts/khanika-samadhi.md), [Noting and bare attention](../concepts/bare-noting.md) · disputes: [Must jhāna precede insight, or can insight proceed on momentary concentration (dry insight)?](../debates/dry-insight-or-jhana-first.md)

### walking <a id="tea-practical-insight-meditation-walking"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

In walking meditation note each step — at first 'left', 'right', then 'lifting', 'pushing', 'dropping' — and throughout the day note every action (bending, stretching, eating) so that mindfulness is continuous.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: beginner · types: practice_
