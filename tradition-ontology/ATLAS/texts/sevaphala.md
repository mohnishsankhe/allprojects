## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-3 <a id="tea-sevaphala-1-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Service bears three fruits — a supernatural capacity (to enjoy the Lord's bliss), union (sāyujya), and a body fit for service in the Lord's abode — and meets three obstacles: agitation (udvega), hindrance (pratibandha) and enjoyment (bhoga).
