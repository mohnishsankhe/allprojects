# Kāśyapa Saṃhitā

`src:kasyapa-samhita` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Vṛddhajīvakīya Tantra
**Original title:** काश्यपसंहिता
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** shared
**Lineages:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Authors:** 
  - [Mārīca Kaśyapa](../teachers/marica-kasyapa.md) — role: revealer; attribution: traditional
  - [Vṛddha Jīvaka](../teachers/vrddha-jivaka.md) — role: compiler; attribution: traditional
  - [Vātsya (redactor of the Kāśyapa Saṃhitā)](../teachers/vatsya.md) — role: compiler; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: Mārīca Kaśyapa's teaching to Vṛddha Jīvaka, restored by Vātsya.; scholarly: Known from one fragmentary Nepalese manuscript, published 1938.; confidence: moderate
**Structure:** Sthānas on the pattern of Caraka plus a Khilasthāna; large parts lost
**Availability:** partly-lost

The principal classical text of the branch of children's medicine (kaumārabhṛtya): care of infant and wet-nurse, children's diseases, the seizing spirits of children (the Revatī chapter), and preparations for infants.
