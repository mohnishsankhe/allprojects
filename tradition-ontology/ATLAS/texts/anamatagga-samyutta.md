## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 15.3 <a id="tea-anamatagga-samyutta-15-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

This saṃsāra is without discoverable beginning; the tears shed while wandering on through the long course, weeping at being united with the disliked and separated from the liked, are more than the water in the four great oceans.
