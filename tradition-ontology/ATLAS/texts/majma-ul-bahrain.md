Dārā Shikoh sets side by side, topic by topic (elements, senses, the names and attributes of God, the four worlds, the cosmic ages, liberation), the terms of the Sufis and of 'the monotheists of India', holding that the two differ only in wording. Recorded as a document of exchange, not as a source of Sufi doctrine.
