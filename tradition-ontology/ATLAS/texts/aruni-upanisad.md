Āruṇi goes to Prajāpati's world and asks how to leave all works; he is taught to abandon sons, relatives, topknot, thread, sacrifice and Vedic study, take staff, loincloth and cloth, internalize the fires and the Gāyatrī, declare 'I have renounced', grant fearlessness to all beings, and live on alms guarding chastity, non-injury, non-possession and truth.

## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 1 <a id="tea-aruni-upanisad-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Āruṇi goes to Prajāpati's world and asks how he can abandon all works; Prajāpati: give up sons, brothers, relatives, topknot, sacred thread, sacrifice, Vedic study and the worlds above and below; take staff, loincloth and cloth.

### 2 <a id="tea-aruni-upanisad-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The renouncer places the fires in the stomach-fire and the Gāyatrī in his speech; he abandons the thread in water or on the ground; he may renounce from any stage.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ritual · path: ritual · stage: advanced · types: practice_

concepts: [The rite of renunciation](../concepts/sannyasa-rite.md)

### 3 <a id="tea-aruni-upanisad-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'I am Brahman'; the thread is Brahman as that which 'points out' (sūcana); saying three times 'I have renounced', he grants 'fearlessness to all beings from me'; he takes a bamboo staff with the mantra 'friend, protect me'; he eats as medicine and guards celibacy, non-injury, non-possession and truth.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ritual · path: ritual · stage: advanced · types: practice, ethics_

concepts: [The rite of renunciation](../concepts/sannyasa-rite.md), [The inner topknot and sacred thread](../concepts/inner-sikha-yajnopavita.md) · practices: [The renunciation rite (saṃnyāsa-dīkṣā)](../practices/sannyasa-diksa.md)

### 4 <a id="tea-aruni-upanisad-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

He uses a bowl of earth, gourd or wood; he stays in one place in the rains and wanders the other eight months alone or with one companion.

_level: conventional · standpoint: ethical-social · path: general · stage: advanced · types: ethics_

practices: [Wandering (pārivrājya) and the rains retreat](../practices/parivrajya.md)

### 5 <a id="tea-aruni-upanisad-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Knowing this, he abandons the palāśa, bilva or udumbara staff, deerskin, girdle and thread (of studentship); this is the highest place of Viṣṇu which the sages ever see (quoting the Ṛgveda).

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ethics, ultimate_
