After the war Arjuna asks Kṛṣṇa to repeat what he taught on the battlefield; Kṛṣṇa says he cannot tell it again in full and relates instead the teaching of a perfected brāhmaṇa to Kāśyapa (death, rebirth and liberation), the dialogue of a brāhmaṇa and his wife on the inner sacrifice of the senses and breaths, king Alarka's conquest of the senses by yoga, and Brahmā's teaching to the sages (guru–śiṣya) on the guṇas, the principles, the āśramas and the tree of Brahman.
