## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### nine-errors <a id="tea-kofukuji-sojo-nine-errors"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Kōfukuji asks the court to stop Hōnen's exclusive nenbutsu for nine errors: establishing a new school without imperial sanction; making a new image (the maṇḍala of the embracing light that shows only nenbutsu people embraced); slighting Śākyamuni; hindering the myriad good practices; turning against the kami; being ignorant of the Pure Land (the grades of birth include other practices); misunderstanding the nenbutsu (reducing it to oral calling); harming the Buddhist order (despising precepts); and disturbing the nation.
