## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### bahiranidana <a id="tea-samantapasadika-bahiranidana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The external introduction narrates the three councils; at the third, under Asoka, Moggaliputta Tissa purified the Saṅgha, recited the Kathāvatthu and sent elders to the border countries — Mahinda to Lanka and Soṇa and Uttara to Suvaṇṇabhūmi.
