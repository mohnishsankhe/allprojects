# Candrāvalokana

`src:candravalokana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Original title:** चन्द्रावलोकन
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Genre:** yoga tract
**Availability:** digitized-original

A short dialogue in which Maheśvara instructs Matsyendra on the śāmbhavī seal (inner aim, outer unwinking gaze), the dissolution of breath and mind, the 'new-moon, first-day and full-moon' of the eyes, desire (kāma), poison (viṣa) and the spotless (nirañjana), the mind as the seed from which bindu arises, and the six centres.
**Editions / translations:**

## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### 1 <a id="tea-candravalokana-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Inner aim with outer gaze, free of blinking — this is the śāmbhavī seal, hidden in all the tantras.

> अन्तर्लक्ष्यं त्वहिर्दृष्टिर्निमेषोन्मेष वर्जितम् । इयं सा शाम्भवी मुद्रा सर्वन्तेषु गोपिता

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice_

practices: [Śāmbhavī mudrā (Śambhu's seal)](../practices/sambhavi-mudra.md)

### p.1 <a id="tea-candravalokana-p-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

See within the body the consciousness (citi) that witnesses the rise and fall of all beings, full, dense with bliss, immortal; if Śiva is in one place, the mind in another, Śakti in another and the breath in another, there is no success even in hundreds of crores of aeons.

_level: bridging · standpoint: experiential · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind_

concepts: [Śiva and Śakti inseparable](../concepts/siva-sakti-inseparable.md)

### p.2 <a id="tea-candravalokana-p-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

How can there be knowledge while the mind lives, and the mind does not die while the breath lives; when both breath and mind dissolve, a man attains liberation, not otherwise. Until the moving breath enters the middle path, until the bindu is firm, until the natural (sahaja) state arises, all talk of knowledge is hypocritical prattle.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: body-breath, meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice, consciousness-mind_

concepts: [Dissolution of breath and mind (laya)](../concepts/laya-natha.md), [The innate (sahaja)](../concepts/sahaja.md) · obstacles: [Hypocrisy of the unrealised (dambha)](../obstacles/dambha.md)

### p.3 <a id="tea-candravalokana-p-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

'New moon', 'first day' and 'full moon' are the closed eye, the opened eye and the half-open gaze; 'desire' (kāma) is longing for objects and 'poison' (viṣa) its dissolution — abandoning both, one should resort to the spotless (nirañjana); placing the mind in the midst of Śakti and Śakti in the midst of mind, seeing the mind by the mind, one should contemplate the supreme state.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: advanced · types: practice, consciousness-mind_

concepts: [Desire, poison and the spotless](../concepts/kama-visa-niranjana.md) · practices: [Seeing the mind with the mind](../practices/manasa-mana-alokana.md), [Śāmbhavī mudrā (Śambhu's seal)](../practices/sambhavi-mudra.md)

### p.3/2 <a id="tea-candravalokana-p-3-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The mind alone is the seed, the cause of arising and persistence; from the mind bindu arises, as ghee from milk.

_level: conventional · standpoint: causal · path: meditation · stage: intermediate · types: consciousness-mind, body-layers_

terms: [bindu](../terms/bindu.md) · concepts: [The mind as seed](../concepts/mind-as-seed.md), [Bindu and its retention](../concepts/bindu-natha.md)
