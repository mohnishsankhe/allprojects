Somadeva's manual of lay conduct forming the last books of his Yaśastilaka; it teaches that in worldly (laukika) matters Jains may follow common custom so long as right view is not lost and the vows are not violated.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### laukika-vidhi <a id="tea-upasakadhyayana-somadeva-laukika-vidhi"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

For Jains all worldly custom is authoritative, provided right view is not lost and the vows are not violated.
