## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### the taniyaṉ given by Raṅganātha <a id="tea-yatindrapravana-prabhavam-taniyan"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

When Maṇavāḷa Māmuni completed a year's exposition of the Īṭu before Raṅganātha, the Lord, appearing as a child, recited: 'I bow to the sage Ramyajāmātṛ, object of the grace of Śrīśaileśa, ocean of wisdom, devotion and other virtues, devoted to Yatīndra (Rāmānuja).'

> śrīśaileśa-dayāpātraṃ dhī-bhakty-ādi-guṇārṇavam | yatīndra-pravaṇaṃ vande ramya-jāmātaraṃ munim ||
