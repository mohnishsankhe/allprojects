Kātyāyana's critical remarks (vārttika) on Pāṇini's rules — supplements, restrictions and corrections — preserved within Patañjali's Mahābhāṣya. The first vārttika declares that word, meaning and their relation are established (siddha) and that grammar restricts usage for the sake of dharma.

## Teachings (1: sourced 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-varttika-katyayana-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence high

Since word, meaning and their relation are established (siddha) — as known from the world, where words are used to convey meanings — grammar makes a restriction for the sake of dharma, as in worldly and Vedic matters.
