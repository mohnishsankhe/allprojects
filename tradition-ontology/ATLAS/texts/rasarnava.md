A Śaiva tantra in the form of a dialogue of Bhairava and the Goddess: jīvanmukti in a stable body, mercury as Śiva's seed, worship of the mercurial liṅga, the teacher and initiation, the laboratory, the processing of mercury and minerals, metal transmutation and finally the transformation of the body (dehavedha). ### 1.5-8 <a id="tea-rasarnava-1-5-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

The Goddess asks Bhairava about jīvanmukti, hinted at in all the tantras but never revealed. Bhairava: knowledge of identity with Śiva for one whose body is ageless and deathless is liberation in life, hard to attain even for the gods.

> ajarāmaradehasya śivatādātmyavedanam | jīvanmuktirmahādevi devānāmapi durlabhā

### 1.9-13 <a id="tea-rasarnava-1-9-13"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

Liberation at the falling of the body is meaningless: when the body falls even a donkey is freed; if liberation came from sexual excitement or from consuming bodily fluids, dogs and pigs would be liberated. In the six systems liberation is shown at the body's fall, but it is not perceived like a myrobalan in the hand; therefore one should protect the body with rasas and rasāyanas.

> piṇḍapāte ca yo mokṣaḥ sa ca mokṣo nirarthakaḥ

### 1.18-21 <a id="tea-rasarnava-1-18-21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

The stability of the body is gained by the discipline of action (karmayoga), which is twofold: mercury and breath. 'Swooned, it removes disease; dead, it revives itself; bound, it gives the power of moving in the sky — mercury and breath, O Bhairavī.' Liberation is from knowledge, knowledge from holding the breath; the body is stable where mercury is lord in stabilizing it; soon the body becomes ageless and deathless (the second line of 1.21, on meditation of the mind and the yoga of mercury, is of uncertain sense and not paraphrased).

> karmayogena deveśi prāpyate piṇḍadhāraṇam | rasaśca pavanaśceti karmayogo dvidhā mataḥ

### 1.23-31 <a id="tea-rasarnava-1-23-31"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

Until the descent of power (śaktipāta) and the cutting of the fetters, how can understanding of mercury arise? Rasa-knowledge is very hard for those whose minds are ruined by constant wine, meat and sexual rites; for those without the Kula teaching mercury does not succeed — they drink a mirage. Without eating the seed of Hara (mercury) how can there be liberation or preservation of the body? Deluded by Śiva's māyā, those addicted to wine and meat babble 'we are liberated, we shall go to Śiva's abode'; the whole world is coloured by partial knowledge. Practise constantly that knowledge of rasa by which one gains sky-going and Śivahood in one's own body.

### 1.34-36 <a id="tea-rasarnava-1-34-36"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

You are the mother of all beings and I the eternal father; the rasa born of our great union is called pārada because the best adepts proclaim it 'for the sake of others' (para-artha); this sūta is equal to me, born from my limbs; since it is the rasa of my body it is called rasa.

> sūto'yaṃ matsamo devi mama pratyaṅgasambhavaḥ | mama deharaso yasmāt rasastenāyamucyate

### 1.37-43 <a id="tea-rasarnava-1-37-43"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

From seeing, touching, eating, remembering, worshipping and giving mercury a sixfold fruit is seen: the merit of seeing all the liṅgas on earth comes from seeing mercury; eaten it destroys the three sins, the three sufferings and the diseases of the three doṣas; remembering it frees from sins of former births; worship of the mercurial liṅga yields a crore-fold the fruit of worshipping thousands of self-born liṅgas.

### 1.44-52 <a id="tea-rasarnava-1-44-52"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

The alchemy of the sky (khaga-vāda) is lowest, that of caves (bila-vāda) middling, that of mantra high, and the alchemy of mercury (rasavāda) highest; those who, knowing mantra and tantra, disparage the yoga of mercury go to hell, cutting off their store of merit. Rasa-knowledge is the supreme knowledge, giving enjoyment and liberation, to be given only to the qualified; success comes to the believer, not to one who says 'it is not'; the slanderer of rasa is reborn for countless births as dog, cat, donkey, crow, worm, cock and vulture, and whoever talks with or touches such slanderers suffers for a thousand births.

### 1.54-59 <a id="tea-rasarnava-1-54-59"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

First worship the guru with a pure inner self; when the guru is pleased the rays (of the teaching) grant the lineage-tradition (sampradāya). The fool who works without serving the guru finds his work fruitless as wealth found in a dream. This great tantra is most rare, to be kept secret, obtained through the guru's grace for the attainment of its fruit; having gained the rasa-works one should not become proud; permitted by the guru, having received the command, one should accomplish the Rasa-Bhairava.
