# Taittirīya Saṃhitā

`src:taittiriya-samhita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Original title:** तैत्तिरीयसंहिता
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Taittirīya śākhā (Black Yajurveda)](../lineages/sakha-taittiriya.md)
**Genre:** saṃhitā (formulas and prose)
**Authors:** 
  - [Vaiśampāyana](../teachers/vaisampayana.md) — role: compiler; attribution: traditional
**Attribution:** tradition: authorless (apauruṣeya) revelation seen by the ṛṣis; arranged by Vyāsa; handed down from Vaiśampāyana through his pupils, the Taittirīyas (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.5); scholarly: Black Yajurveda Saṃhitā, mantra and brāhmaṇa strata; confidence: high
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 1000–800 BCE (prose portions); mantras partly older; (confidence low)
**Structure:** 7 kāṇḍas, 44 prapāṭhakas, 651 anuvākas (as usually counted)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The Black Yajurveda Saṃhitā of the Taittirīya school: the adhvaryu priest's formulas (yajus) and verses for the new- and full-moon rites, the soma sacrifice, the royal consecration, the building of the fire altar (agnicayana) and the horse sacrifice, interleaved with brāhmaṇa prose explaining them. Book 4 contains the Śatarudrīya (4.5) and the Camaka (4.7).
