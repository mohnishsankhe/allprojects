## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 98a29 <a id="tea-zuochan-zhen-hongzhi-98a29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The essential function of buddhas and the functioning essence of patriarchs: it knows without touching things, it illumines without facing objects; the knowing is subtle, never discriminating; the illumination is wondrous, without the slightest sign; the water is clear to the bottom, fish swim slowly; the sky is vast without limit, birds fly far away.

> 佛佛要機祖祖機要。 不觸事而知。 不對緣而照
