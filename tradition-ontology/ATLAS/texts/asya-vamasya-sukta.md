Dīrghatamas' hymn of riddles (brahmodya): the one-wheeled chariot of seven names; the twelve-spoked wheel of ṛta with its 720 sons; the two birds on one tree, one eating the sweet fig, the other looking on; the seer who does not know what he is until the first-born of ṛta comes to him; the immortal of one origin with the mortal; the syllable in the highest heaven on which the gods sit; the four quarters of speech, three hidden; and the one Being that the inspired call by many names.

_Notes: Reproduced in the Atharvaveda (Śaunaka 9.9–10). An adhyātma (inner) commentary on this hymn by one Ātmānanda is reported in the secondary literature (low confidence; not entered as a source)._
