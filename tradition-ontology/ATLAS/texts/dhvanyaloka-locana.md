## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.5 <a id="tea-dhvanyaloka-locana-1-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Abhinavagupta: the grief of the first poet was not his personal sorrow — had it been, he would only have suffered; the stable emotion of grief, relished through the determinants (the slain bird, the lamenting mate), became the rasa of compassion, which overflowed as verse; hence rasa is the soul of poetry.
