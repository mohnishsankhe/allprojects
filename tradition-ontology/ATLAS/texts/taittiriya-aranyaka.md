# Taittirīya Āraṇyaka

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), `lin:upanisadic`
**Genre:** āraṇyaka
**Structure:** 10 prapāṭhakas (Āndhra recension; the Drāviḍa recension differs)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The Āraṇyaka of the Taittirīya Black Yajurveda: the Āruṇaketuka fire-altar (1); the book of Vedic self-study with the twilight worship, the wind-girdled seers who were śramaṇas (2.7) and the five great sacrifices (2.10); the cāturhotra altar (3); the Pravargya mantras and explanation (4–5); the funeral and ancestral mantras (6); the Taittirīya Upaniṣad (7–9); and the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad (10), which contains the mantras of the food-offering into the breaths.

_Notes: Commentaries: Sāyaṇa; Bhaṭṭa Bhāskara._
