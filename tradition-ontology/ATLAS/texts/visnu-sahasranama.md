The thousand names of Viṣṇu, taught by Bhīṣma to Yudhiṣṭhira in answer to the question which deity and which recitation frees a person from saṃsāra; it begins 'viśvaṃ viṣṇur vaṣaṭkāraḥ' and closes with the merits of recitation.
