## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 5-6 <a id="tea-yuktisastika-5-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Those who do not see reality conceive saṃsāra and nirvāṇa; those who see reality conceive neither. Existence and nirvāṇa are not two: the full understanding of existence is called nirvāṇa.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: karma-liberation, ultimate_

### 35 <a id="tea-yuktisastika-35"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

When the Victors have said that nirvāṇa alone is true, what wise person would think that the rest is not false?

_level: bridging · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, ultimate_

concepts: [The two truths (satyadvaya)](../concepts/two-truths.md), [Nirvāṇa in Madhyamaka](../concepts/nirvana-in-madhyamaka.md) · teachers: [Nāgārjuna](../teachers/nagarjuna.md)

### 46-48 <a id="tea-yuktisastika-46-48"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

When things are accepted as real, fierce attachment and aversion and the grip of evil views arise, and disputes spring from them; that acceptance is the cause of all views, and without it afflictions do not arise; when it is fully understood, views and afflictions are exhausted — and it is understood by seeing dependent origination.

_level: bridging · standpoint: causal · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: karma-liberation, consciousness-mind_

`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The great-souled have no thesis and no dispute; how could there be an opposing thesis for those who have no thesis?
