# Mālinīvijayottaratantra

`src:malinivijayottara-tantra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Mālinīvijaya, Śrīpūrvaśāstra / Pūrvatantra (Abhinavagupta's name), Mālinīmata
**Original title:** मालिनीविजयोत्तरतन्त्र
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md), [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Vidyāpīṭha (the Seat of Vidyās)](../lineages/vidyapitha.md)
**Genre:** tantra (Vidyāpīṭha, Trika)
**Attribution:** tradition: Revealed by the Supreme Lord (MVT 1.7: 'issued from the mouth of the Supreme Lord').; scholarly: Anonymous Vidyāpīṭha tantra, later than the Siddhayogeśvarīmata.; confidence: moderate
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 6th-9th c. CE (uncertain); (confidence low)
**Structure:** 23 adhikāras (chapters) in the KSTS edition
**Availability:** digitized-original

The root scripture of Abhinavagupta's Trika ('the essence of the Trika', TĀ 1.18). Presented as the teaching given to the sages Sanatkumāra, Nārada, Agastya and others (MVT 1.1-7), it covers emanation and the tattvas, the mālinī order of letters, the three kinds of immersion (āṇava, śākta, śāmbhava; 2.21-23), the five signs of the descent of Rudra's power (2.14-16), initiation and its signs (ānanda, udbhava, kampa, nidrā, ghūrṇi; 11.35), and a long yoga of concentrations on elements, subtle elements and senses (chs. 12-17).
