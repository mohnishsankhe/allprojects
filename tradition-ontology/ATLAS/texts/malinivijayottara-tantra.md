# Mālinīvijayottaratantra

**Alternate titles:** Mālinīvijaya, Śrīpūrvaśāstra / Pūrvatantra (Abhinavagupta's name), Mālinīmata

The root scripture of Abhinavagupta's Trika ('the essence of the Trika', TĀ 1.18). Presented as the teaching given to the sages Sanatkumāra, Nārada, Agastya and others (MVT 1.1-7), it covers emanation and the tattvas, the mālinī order of letters, the three kinds of immersion (āṇava, śākta, śāmbhava; 2.21-23), the five signs of the descent of Rudra's power (2.14-16), initiation and its signs (ānanda, udbhava, kampa, nidrā, ghūrṇi; 11.35), and a long yoga of concentrations on elements, subtle elements and senses (chs. 12-17).

### 1.23 <a id="tea-malinivijayottara-tantra-1-23"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

They hold that impurity (mala) is ignorance, the cause of the sprouting of saṃsāra.

> malam ajñānam icchanti saṃsārāṅkurakāraṇam

### 2.13-17 <a id="tea-malinivijayottara-tantra-2-13-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Where immersion in Rudra's power is established, these signs are to be observed: first, unwavering devotion to Rudra; second, success in mantra that brings immediate confirmation; third, control over all beings; fourth, completion of what one undertakes; fifth, poetic power, graceful and ornamented, with sudden knowledge of the meaning of all scriptures.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: general · stage: all · types: powers-experiences, teacher-transmission_

terms: [śaktipāta](../terms/saktipata.md) · concepts: [The grades of the descent of power (śaktipāta)](../concepts/kinds-of-saktipata.md)

### 2.21-23 <a id="tea-malinivijayottara-tantra-2-21-23"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The immersion (samāveśa) that arises through meditation on breath (uccāra), the bodily seals (karaṇa), visualization (dhyāna), letters (varṇa) and the positing of places (sthāna) is called the āṇava; the immersion reached by contemplating with the mind alone a reality free of such utterance is the śākta; the immersion that arises for one who thinks of nothing, through the awakening given by the teacher, is the śāmbhava.

_level: bridging · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation, knowledge · stage: all (āṇava / śākta / śāmbhava samāveśa) · types: practice_

terms: [samāveśa](../terms/samavesa.md), [upāya](../terms/upaya.md) · concepts: [The four means (upāya)](../concepts/four-upayas.md)

### 11.35 <a id="tea-malinivijayottara-tantra-11-35"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

[In initiation] the wise teacher should carefully observe in the disciple being purified the group of signs beginning with bliss: bliss (ānanda), leaping up (udbhava), trembling (kampa), sleep (nidrā) and whirling (ghūrṇi) as the fifth.

_level: conventional · standpoint: experiential · path: ritual · stage: all · types: powers-experiences, teacher-transmission_
