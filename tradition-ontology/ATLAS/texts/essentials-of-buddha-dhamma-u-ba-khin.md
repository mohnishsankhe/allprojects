## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### anicca <a id="tea-essentials-of-buddha-dhamma-u-ba-khin-anicca"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

The essence of the Buddha-dhamma is to experience impermanence (anicca) within one's own body as the arising and dissolving of subtle material units; this awareness leads to the understanding of suffering and not-self and, maintained, to nibbāna.
