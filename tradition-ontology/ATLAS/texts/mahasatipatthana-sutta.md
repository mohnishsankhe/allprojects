## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 18-21 <a id="tea-mahasatipatthana-sutta-18-21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Under the contemplation of dhammas the four noble truths are analysed: suffering (birth, ageing, death, sorrow … in brief the five aggregates of clinging); its origin (craving for sensual pleasure, becoming and non-becoming, arising wherever there is the pleasant and agreeable in the world); its cessation (the fading and ending of that craving); and the path (the noble eightfold path, each factor defined).

### 22 <a id="tea-mahasatipatthana-sutta-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Whoever develops the four establishments of mindfulness in this way for seven years — or even seven days — can expect one of two fruits: final knowledge in this very life or, if there is a residue, non-return.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: practice, karma-liberation_
