## Teachings (3: sourced 3)

### pūrva 1 <a id="tea-nrsimhatapani-upanisad-purva-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Prajāpati, doing austerity, saw the anuṣṭubh king of mantras of Narasiṃha, by which he created all this.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language, world-fate_

practices: [Recitation of the Narasiṃha mantra-king](../practices/nrsimha-mantraraja-japa.md) · teachers: [Prajāpati (as teacher)](../teachers/prajapati.md)

### pūrva 2-5 (summary) <a id="tea-nrsimhatapani-upanisad-purva-2-5-summary"></a>
`sourced` · confidence low

The words of the mantra are explained, its limbs, seers and uses; it is the essence of the four Vedas.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language_

### uttara 1-9 <a id="tea-nrsimhatapani-upanisad-uttara-1-9"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

The gods ask Prajāpati about the Self as Oṃ; its four quarters are expounded as in the Māṇḍūkya, and the fourth is identified with Nṛsiṃha, the Self beyond the three states.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, consciousness-mind_

concepts: [The fourth and beyond the fourth (turīya, turīyātīta)](../concepts/turiya-turiyatita.md), [The measures of Oṃ (mātrā)](../concepts/omkara-matras.md)
