### 1.3-7 <a id="tea-rasahrdayatantra-1-3-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

Swooned, mercury removes disease; having experienced binding it gives liberation; well 'killed' it makes immortal — who else is so compassionate as mercury? It allays even diseases held incurable that arise from sins of harming gods, teachers, cows and brahmins. Those who, without abandoning the body, have gained the body born of Hara and Gaurī's creation are the rasa-siddhas to be revered, whose servants are the hosts of mantras.

### 1.8-10 <a id="tea-rasahrdayatantra-1-8-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

Good deeds bring birth in a good family and intelligence, and with them mastery of the earth, wealth and enjoyments; but enjoyments are in the body and it is impermanent — all is vain. Knowing wealth, body and enjoyments to be impermanent, one should always strive for liberation; liberation comes from knowledge, knowledge from practice, and practice is possible in a stable body.

> iti dhanaśarīrabhogān matvānityān sadaiva yatanīyam | muktau sā ca jñānāt taccābhyāsāt sa ca sthire dehe

### 1.11-14 <a id="tea-rasahrdayatantra-1-11-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

No rejuvenative of roots or metals can give that stability, being itself unstable — burnable, dissolvable, dryable. Herbs dissolve in lead, lead in tin, tin in copper, copper in silver, silver in gold and gold in mercury — as all beings dissolve in the supreme self; this one king of rasas makes the body ageless and deathless. As yogins dissolved in the form of Hara attain immortality, so gold and the metals in the king of rasas that has swallowed mica.

### 1.15-19 <a id="tea-rasahrdayatantra-1-15-19"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

With a stable body, by practice, one gains knowledge with its eight qualities and reaches the state of brahman, free of the suffering of rebirth; the supreme light, three quarters of which are nectar, is not easily gained by dispassion alone. The subtle brahman cannot be worshipped with a body subject to disease, old age and death, perishing in a moment; without a body who could even name the success of yoga? The highest good — beyond sacrifice, giving, austerity, Vedic study, restraint and good conduct — is self-knowledge through yoga.

### 1.20-23 <a id="tea-rasahrdayatantra-1-20-23"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

Even when the bliss of consciousness, free of all conceptions, has flashed forth, what good is it to creatures for one whose body has not shone forth? For some of meritorious vision the conscious light between the eyebrows opens, illuminating the world like fire, lightning or sun: supreme bliss, of the nature of light, free of conception, free of all afflictions, peaceful, self-known; placing the mind there and seeing the whole world as consciousness, one whose bonds of karma are cut attains brahmanhood here itself.

### 1.24-29 <a id="tea-rasahrdayatantra-1-24-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

Freed from passion and hatred, truthful in conduct, free of falsehood, people become the same toward all through the touch of consciousness-brahman; those who have attained the deathless state of brahman live on, their bodies shining, joyful, endowed with the subtle powers, their work done. The body is the abode of knowledge and the root of dharma, artha, kāma and mokṣa — what higher good is there than an ageless deathless body? A body worn by old age and subject to cough and breathlessness is unfit for samādhi.
