## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-20 <a id="tea-irupa-irupatu-1-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

In question and answer with his guru Meykaṇṭār, Aruṇanti asks how the soul, pure by nature, came to be bound, why mala clings to it, and how grace works; the answers teach that āṇava is beginningless and that only the guru's grace releases the soul.
