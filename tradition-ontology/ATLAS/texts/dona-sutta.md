## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-6 <a id="tea-dona-sutta-1-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Seeing the wheel marks on the Buddha's footprints, the brahmin Doṇa asks whether he will be a god, a gandhabba, a yakkha or a human; he answers no to each, for the effluents by which he would become one are abandoned; as a lotus born in water rises above it untouched, 'remember me as a Buddha'.
