Early Chan lineage history presenting Guṇabhadra, translator of the Laṅkāvatāra, as first master and Bodhidharma as second, through Huike, Sengcan, Daoxin, Hongren and Shenxiu; includes Daoxin's teaching on guarding the one and one-practice samādhi.
