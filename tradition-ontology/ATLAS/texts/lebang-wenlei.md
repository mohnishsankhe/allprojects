## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 192c18 <a id="tea-lebang-wenlei-192c18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Since the Lotus society took the master of Lushan (Huiyuan) as its first patriarch, in the 809 years to 1199 five masters have continued the way: Shandao, Fazhao, Shaokang, Shengchang and Zongze.

> 蓮社之立既以遠公為始祖，自師歸寂，抵今大宋慶元五年己未，凡八百九年矣，中間繼此道者乃有五師：一曰善導師、二曰法照師、三曰少康師、四曰省常師、五曰宗賾師。
