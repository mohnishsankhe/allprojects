Sayings in Kashmiri verse (vākh) of the 14th-century yoginī Lal Ded (Lalleśvarī): the search for Śiva ending in her own body and home, breath-discipline lighting the lamp of knowledge, the equality of self and other, liberation in life, and indifference to sectarian names.

`skeleton` · confidence moderate

By practice the visible is dissolved into the void; what remains is the witness; when that too is stilled, the stainless remains: this is the teaching.
