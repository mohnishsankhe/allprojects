### 2 <a id="tea-paumacariya-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

King Śreṇika, doubting the popular Rāmāyaṇa — how could the mighty rākṣasas be defeated by monkeys, how could Kumbhakarṇa sleep six months, how could Rāvaṇa, a learned man, eat flesh? — asks Gautama for the true story; Gautama tells it: the 'monkeys' and 'demons' are human vidyādhara clans, and the other versions are false.
