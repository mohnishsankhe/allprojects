**Availability:** digitized-original

The ritual code of Śrīvidyā in sūtra form, in ten sections: initiation, the worship of Gaṇapati, the preliminaries (Śrīkrama), the worship of Lalitā, the nine-enclosure worship of the Śrīcakra, the worship of Śyāmā, of Vārāhī and of Parā, fire offering, and rules common to all. It opens with a statement of doctrine and conduct (the thirty-six principles, the jīva as Śiva covered, self-awareness as the goal, faith in the tradition) and calls itself a great Upaniṣad of the Traipura doctrine.

The blessed Paramaśiva, having in play composed the eighteen sciences beginning with the Veda and all the philosophical schools, taking on the corresponding states, was questioned by the Goddess Bhairavī, made of consciousness and not different from himself, and through his five faces set forth the five transmissions (āmnāya), the essence of the highest truth.

### 1.14-25 <a id="tea-parasurama-kalpasutra-1-14-25"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The conduct of the initiate: no censure of any philosophical school; disregard of no one; the secret told only to a worthy disciple; constant holding of the vidyā; constant immersion in Śiva-hood; avoidance of desire, anger, greed, delusion, pride, envy, unsanctioned violence, theft and what the world detests; worship of one guru without doubt; non-possessiveness everywhere; action done without regard to fruit; omission of non-obligatory rites; continuation of the daily worship even when the five 'M's are unavailable; fearlessness everywhere.
