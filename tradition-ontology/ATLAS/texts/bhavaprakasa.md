### purva.1.5-17 <a id="tea-bhavaprakasa-purva-1-5-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Brahmā, revealing the Āyurveda that is the essence of the Atharva, made a saṃhitā in his own name of a hundred thousand verses and taught it to Dakṣa Prajāpati; Dakṣa taught the two heavenly physicians, the Aśvins, who made their own saṃhitā; they rejoined Brahmā's head cut off by Bhairava, healed the gods' wounds, Indra's arm, the Moon's consumption, Pūṣan's teeth and Bhaga's eyes, and made Cyavana young; Indra begged Āyurveda from them and taught Ātreya and many sages.

### purva.1.18-34 <a id="tea-bhavaprakasa-purva-1-18-34"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Seeing the world full of disease, the compassionate Ātreya went himself to Indra, learnt Āyurveda with its limbs and returned; he made a saṃhitā in his own name and taught Agniveśa, Bheḍa, Jatūkarṇa, Parāśara, Kṣīrapāṇi and Hārīta; Agniveśa first made a tantra, then Bheḍa and the others, and read them to Ātreya, who rejoiced.

### purva.1.57-65 <a id="tea-bhavaprakasa-purva-1-57-65"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

When Hari as the Fish rescued the Veda, Śeṣa received the Veda with its limbs and the Āyurveda contained in the Atharva; once, coming to see the earth like a spy (cara), he saw people tormented by disease and dying, and, grieved, was born as the son of a pure sage learned in the Vedas; because he had come like a spy unknown to anyone he became famous as Caraka, a portion of the thousand-headed one; he redacted the tantras of Ātreya's disciples and made the book 'Caraka' in his own name.

### purva.1.66-73 <a id="tea-bhavaprakasa-purva-1-66-73"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Once Indra, seeing men sorely afflicted by disease, his heart moved by compassion, told Dhanvantari: go to earth and become king in Kāśī, and reveal Āyurveda to counter diseases; Dhanvantari, having learnt the whole Veda of life from Indra, was born in a kṣatriya house at Kāśī, famous on earth as Divodāsa, and even as a boy was dispassionate and practised great austerity.
