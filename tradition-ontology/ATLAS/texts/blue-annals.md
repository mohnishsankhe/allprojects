### chod <a id="tea-blue-annals-chod"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Chöd lineages are traced both from Padampa Sangye (the 'father' strand) and from Machig Labdrön (the 'mother' strand), with her sons and disciples as holders.

### mahamudra-sutra-basis <a id="tea-blue-annals-mahamudra-sutra-basis"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Gö Lotsāwa reports that the Mahāmudrā taught by Gampopa has Indian precedent: Maitrīpa and his disciple Sahajavajra taught a Mahāmudrā joined with the pāramitā path, apart from tantric initiation, and Gampopa gave it on that basis to some disciples.

_level: conventional · standpoint: polemical · path: general · stage: all · types: dispute, teacher-transmission_

concepts: [Sūtra, mantra and essence Mahāmudrā](../concepts/three-kinds-of-mahamudra.md) · teachers: [Gö Lotsawa Zhönnu Pel](../teachers/go-lotsawa.md), [Gampopa Sönam Rinchen (Dakpo Lhaje)](../teachers/gampopa.md), [Maitrīpa (Advayavajra)](../teachers/maitripa.md) · disputes: [Can Mahāmudrā be introduced and realized apart from tantric empowerment — Gampopa's 'sūtra Mahāmudrā', the 'white self-sufficient remedy' — or is Mahāmudrā only the wisdom arising from empowerment and the two stages?](../debates/sutra-mahamudra.md)

### shije <a id="tea-blue-annals-shije"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Pacification teaching of Padampa Sangye was passed on in three transmissions — early, middle and later — the later one through Dampa Kunga at Dingri.
