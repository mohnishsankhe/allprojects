## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### mahamudra-sutra-basis <a id="tea-blue-annals-mahamudra-sutra-basis"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Gö Lotsāwa reports that the Mahāmudrā taught by Gampopa has Indian precedent: Maitrīpa and his disciple Sahajavajra taught a Mahāmudrā joined with the pāramitā path, apart from tantric initiation, and Gampopa gave it on that basis to some disciples.
