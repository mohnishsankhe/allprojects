## Teachings (1: sourced 1)

### 1-10 <a id="tea-rudraksajabala-upanisad-1-10"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

The rudrākṣa arose from Kālāgnirudra's tears after he closed his eyes to destroy Tripura; beads are of various sizes and colours and have one to fourteen faces, each with a presiding deity; wearing and consecrating them with mantras removes sins; sages such as Nidāgha, Jaḍabharata, Dattātreya and others wore them.
