### 1.4-1.16 <a id="tea-mahapadana-sutta-1-4-1-16"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Seven Buddhas have arisen within ninety-one aeons — Vipassī, Sikhī, Vessabhū, Kakusandha, Koṇāgamana, Kassapa and Gotama — with their aeons, castes, clans, lifespans, trees of awakening, chief disciples, assemblies, attendants, parents and cities.

### 1.17-1.30 <a id="tea-mahapadana-sutta-1-17-1-30"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

It is the norm (dhammatā) for a bodhisatta that he descends from Tusita mindful into his mother's womb, that the ten-thousandfold world-system quakes and an immeasurable light appears, that his mother keeps the precepts, that he is born standing and proclaims 'I am the foremost in the world; this is my last birth'.

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: devotion · stage: all · types: world-fate, powers-experiences_

terms: [bodhisatta](../terms/bodhisatta.md), [dhammatā](../terms/dhammata.md) · concepts: [The norms of a bodhisatta](../concepts/dhammata-bodhisatta.md)

### 2.1-2.15 <a id="tea-mahapadana-sutta-2-1-2-15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Prince Vipassī sees an old man, a sick man, a dead man and a renunciant, is stirred, and goes forth.

### 2.18-2.22 <a id="tea-mahapadana-sutta-2-18-2-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In seclusion Vipassī reflects: this world has fallen into trouble — it is born, ages, dies and is reborn, and knows no escape. Through wise attention he penetrates by wisdom: when what exists does ageing-and-death exist? — birth … down to consciousness and name-and-form, which condition each other; and cessation in the same way.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: causal · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: karma-liberation, consciousness-mind_

### 3.1-3.7 <a id="tea-mahapadana-sutta-3-1-3-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

After his awakening Vipassī is inclined not to teach because the Dhamma is deep and people delight in attachment; Great Brahmā appeals to him, and seeing beings like lotuses at different stages he resolves: 'Open are the doors to the deathless'.
