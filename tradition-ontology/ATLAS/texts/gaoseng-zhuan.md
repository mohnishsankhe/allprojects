## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 358c17-29 <a id="tea-gaoseng-zhuan-358c17-29"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Lay men of letters such as Liu Yimin, Lei Cizong, Zhou Xuzhi and Zong Bing gathered round Huiyuan; before the image of Amitāyus in the monastery he held a feast and made a vow with them, together aspiring to the West. Liu Yimin wrote the vow: in the year of shetige (402), on the 28th of the seventh month, Huiyuan and 123 like-minded persons gathered before the Amitābha image at the Prajñā terrace on Lushan and, offering incense and flowers, vowed — knowing that retribution follows karma and that the evil paths are hard to escape — to help one another to birth there.

> 遠乃於精舍無量壽像前建齋立誓，共期西方。乃令劉遺民著其文曰：惟歲在攝提秋，七月戊辰朔，二十八日乙未。法師釋慧遠貞感幽奧，宿懷特發。乃延命同志息心貞信之士，百有二十三人，集於廬山之陰，般若臺精舍阿彌陀像前，率以香華敬廌而誓焉
