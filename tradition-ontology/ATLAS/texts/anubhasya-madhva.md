## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-anubhasya-madhva-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Viṣṇu alone is to be inquired into: the maker of all, made known by scripture, (shown) by the concordance of the texts and by his 'seeing' (before creation), full of bliss, the inner (controller), (named) 'space' and the like.
