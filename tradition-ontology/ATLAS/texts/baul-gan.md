The living song corpus of the Bāuls beyond Lalon: songs of the Man of the Heart, the body, the guru and the path, and of the futility of temple and mosque.

### Bāul maxim 'je āche bhāṇḍe, tā āche brahmāṇḍe' <a id="tea-baul-gan-je-ache-bhande-ta-ache-brahmande"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

What is in the vessel (the body) is in the universe: the whole cosmos, its gods, rivers and pilgrim places, is present in the human body, so the search is conducted in the body.

### Bāul songs on the 'four moons' (summary only) <a id="tea-baul-gan-cari-candra-secret-and-warning"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _restricted: summary only_

The songs speak in code of 'four moons' (cāri candra) in the body that the practitioner must come to know under the guru; they warn that without a guru and without mastery of desire the practice leads to ruin and to falling into lust, and that it must be kept secret.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: seeker · path: body-breath · stage: advanced · types: practice, body-layers_

### Bāul songs on the Uncatchable Man and the tide (summary only) <a id="tea-baul-gan-adhar-manus-and-the-tide"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _restricted: summary only_

The Uncatchable Man (adhar mānuṣ) is said in coded songs to come and go in the body with the tide at a certain time, like a fish swimming upstream; only the practitioner guided by the guru, going against the current (ujān), can catch him; the songs warn that the one who goes with the current is lost.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: experiential · path: body-breath, devotion · stage: advanced · types: practice, powers-experiences_

### Bāul teaching on bartamān and anumān <a id="tea-baul-gan-bartaman-and-anuman"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Bāuls call themselves followers of the present (bartamān): they accept what can be known in the living body here and now, and set aside inference and hearsay (anumān) — scripture, heaven, the gods of the books — as unverified.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge, body-breath · stage: all · types: dispute, consciousness-mind_
