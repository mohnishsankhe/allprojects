## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.8 <a id="tea-prameyaratnavali-1-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Baladeva lists nine tenets: Viṣṇu is the supreme reality, known through all the Vedas; the world is real; difference is real; souls are Hari's servants and are graded; liberation is attaining Kṛṣṇa's feet; pure devotion is its cause; and perception, inference and revelation are the means of knowledge.
