## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### four-superiorities <a id="tea-nayatrayapradipa-four-superiorities"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Though the perfection way and the mantra way share one goal, the mantra way is superior by four features: without confusion, with many means, without difficulty, and for those of sharp faculties.
