## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-cintra-prasasti-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The eulogy recounts that Śiva became incarnate as Lakulīśa at Kāyāvarohaṇa and that his four disciples - Kuśika, Gārgya, Kauruṣa and Maitreya - founded four lines, to one of which the Pāśupata ascetic Tripurāntaka of Somanātha belonged.
