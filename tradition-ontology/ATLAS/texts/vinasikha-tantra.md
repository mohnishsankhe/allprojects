# Vīṇāśikhatantra

`src:vinasikha-tantra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Vīṇāśikha
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Vāma stream (the left current of Tumburu)](../lineages/vama-srotas.md)
**Genre:** tantra (Vāma stream)
**Availability:** digitized-original

The one surviving scripture of the Vāma (left) stream: worship of Tumburu and his four sisters, with rites for powers. The Vīṇāśikha heads the four Vāma texts named in the Sdok Kak Thom inscription.
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: T. Goudriaan (ed. & tr.), The Vīṇāśikhatantra, Motilal Banarsidass 1985
