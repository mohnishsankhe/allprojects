The abridged Bhūṣaṇa, studied as the standard introduction to grammatical philosophy; its sections (nirṇaya) treat the meaning of roots, verbal endings, case endings, stems, compounds, the power of words (śakti), particles, and close with the settlement of the sphoṭa (sphoṭa-nirṇaya).

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### sphoṭa-nirṇaya <a id="tea-vaiyakarana-bhusana-sara-sphotanirnaya"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The sphoṭa is counted as eightfold — letter-, word- and sentence-sphoṭa, each as individual or universal, and the partless word and partless sentence; following Bhaṭṭoji's kārikā, on final analysis only the sentence-sphoṭa is real, since only the sentence conveys a complete meaning.
