### intro <a id="tea-bodhipakkhiya-dipani-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

Now, while the Buddha's dispensation lasts, is the rare opportunity to develop the thirty-seven requisites of awakening; one should not merely do meritorious deeds wishing to meet a future Buddha, but strive for the path in this life, beginning with firm virtue and mindfulness of the body.
