## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### Ghaṭ Rāmāyaṇ, the Rāmāyaṇa of the body <a id="tea-ghat-ramayan-inner-ramayana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low · _recent (post-1800)_

The true Rāmāyaṇa is within the body (ghaṭ): the soul must go up through the eye centre and the inner regions, hearing their sounds, to the true abode; Tulsī Sāhib says he told this in a former life as Tulsīdās, but the outer Rāmāyaṇa was taken instead.
