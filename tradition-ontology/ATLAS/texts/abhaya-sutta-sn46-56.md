# Abhaya Sutta (SN 46.56)

**Language:** Pali

**Location in parent:** SN 46.56

Prince Abhaya reports Pūraṇa Kassapa's view that there is no cause for knowing and seeing or for not knowing and seeing; the Buddha answers with the hindrances and the awakening factors.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-abhaya-sutta-sn46-56-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Prince Abhaya reports that Pūraṇa Kassapa says there is no cause or condition for not knowing and not seeing, nor for knowing and seeing. The Buddha shows the hindrances as the cause of not knowing and the awakening factors as the cause of knowing.
