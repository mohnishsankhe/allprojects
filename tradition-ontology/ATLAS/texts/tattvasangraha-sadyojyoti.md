## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-40 <a id="tea-tattvasangraha-sadyojyoti-1-40"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Māyā, one and eternal, is the material cause from which the impure principles arise — kalā, vidyā, rāga, kāla, niyati, then the prakṛti with its guṇas, buddhi, ahaṅkāra, mind, the senses, the subtle and gross elements — as the soul's instruments and objects; above māyā lie the pure principles of the pure universe.
