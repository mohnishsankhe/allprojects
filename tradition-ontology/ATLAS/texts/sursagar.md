The 'ocean of Sūr': Braj Bhāṣā songs on Kṛṣṇa's childhood, the gopīs' love and their separation (the bhramargīt), attributed to Sūrdās. The Puṣṭimārga counts Sūrdās its foremost aṣṭachāp poet.
