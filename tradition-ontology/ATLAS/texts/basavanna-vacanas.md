Vacanas signed Kūḍalasaṅgamadēva: devotion and humility before devotees (dāsōha), sevenfold inner and outer purity ('do not steal, do not kill, do not lie…'), the body as the temple of the moving (jaṅgama) against the fixed (sthāvara) temple, compassion as the root of dharma, and the unity of all devotees regardless of birth.

### vacana 'Dayavillada dharmavāvudayyā…' <a id="tea-basavanna-vacanas-dayavillada-dharmavavudayya"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

What kind of dharma is it that has no compassion? Compassion is needed toward all living beings; compassion is the root of dharma; the Lord of the meeting rivers accepts nothing without it.

### vacana 'Ivanāruva, ivanāruva…' <a id="tea-basavanna-vacanas-ivanaruva-ivanaruva"></a>

Let me not say of a devotee 'Whose is he? whose is he?' — let me say 'He is ours, ours, ours'; Kūḍalasaṅgamadēva, make me known as a son of your house.

When they see a snake of stone they pour milk on it; when they see a real snake they say 'Kill it!'; to the devotee of the god, who could eat, they say 'Go away'; to the god's image, which cannot eat, they offer food.

### vacana 'Kaḷabēḍa, kolabēḍa…' <a id="tea-basavanna-vacanas-kalabeda-kolabeda"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Do not steal, do not kill, do not lie, do not rage, do not be disgusted with others, do not praise yourself, do not blame others: this is inner purity, this is outer purity; this is the way to please our Kūḍalasaṅgamadēva.
