Vacanas signed Kūḍalasaṅgamadēva: devotion and humility before devotees (dāsōha), sevenfold inner and outer purity ('do not steal, do not kill, do not lie…'), the body as the temple of the moving (jaṅgama) against the fixed (sthāvara) temple, compassion as the root of dharma, and the unity of all devotees regardless of birth.
