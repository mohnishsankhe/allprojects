# Paitāmaha Siddhānta

`src:paitamaha-siddhanta` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)
**Genre:** siddhānta
**Authors:** 
  - [Brahmā (as first teacher)](../teachers/brahma.md) — role: revealer; attribution: traditional
**Availability:** lost

The siddhānta ascribed to Pitāmaha (Brahmā); its early form, close to the Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa, is summarized in the Pañcasiddhāntikā; a later form is preserved in the Viṣṇudharmottara Purāṇa and underlies Brahmagupta's work.

_Notes: Known chiefly through Varāhamihira's Pañcasiddhāntikā._
