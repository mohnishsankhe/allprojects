# Sātvata Saṃhitā

**Attribution:** tradition: revealed by Bhagavān (Nārāyaṇa/Vāsudeva); one of the traditional 108 saṃhitās; confidence: moderate
**Structure:** 25 paricchedas (as usually reported)
**Availability:** unknown

One of the 'three gems' (ratnatraya) of the Pāñcarātra, regarded as among the oldest saṃhitās. It treats the worship of the vyūha and vibhava forms, initiation and ritual; the Ahirbudhnya refers to 'the Sātvata teaching' for the details of the 39 vibhavas (AS 5.57–59). The Īśvara Saṃhitā is its ritual expansion (Melkote).
**Editions / translations:** 
  - kind: original; name: Sātvata Saṃhitā with Alaśiṅga Bhaṭṭa's bhāṣya (Kāñcī, early 20th c.; Varanasi reprint)
