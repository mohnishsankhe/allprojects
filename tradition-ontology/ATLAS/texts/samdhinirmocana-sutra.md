# Saṃdhinirmocana-sūtra

`src:samdhinirmocana-sutra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Sūtra Explaining the Thought, Jie shenmi jing 解深密經 (T676, Xuanzang 647), Shenmi jietuo jing (T675, Bodhiruci 514), dgongs pa nges par 'grel pa (Toh 106)
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** ascetic
**Lineages:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md), `lin:faxiang`, [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md)
**Genre:** sūtra
**Attribution:** tradition: Spoken by the Buddha Śākyamuni (tradition's account: buddhavacana) (Tibetan: the third turning of the wheel); scholarly: Anonymous; layered composition c. 2nd-4th c.; quoted whole in the Yogācārabhūmi; confidence: high
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 200-350 CE; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 10 chapters in Tibetan (Lamotte's ed.): 1 introduction; 2-4 the ultimate (Gambhīrārthasaṃdhinirmocana, Suviśuddhamati, Subhūti); 5 mind (Viśālamati); 6 the characteristics (Guṇākara); 7 naturelessness (Paramārthasamudgata); 8 yoga (Maitreya); 9 perfections and grounds (Avalokiteśvara); 10 the Tathāgata's activity (Mañjuśrī). Xuanzang's version has 8 chapters (2-4 joined)
**Availability:** digitized-translation

The 'Sūtra Unravelling the Intent': the ultimate is inexpressible, non-dual, beyond identity and difference and of one taste; the ādāna- or ālaya-vijñāna holding all seeds; the three characteristics (imagined, dependent, perfected) and three naturelessnesses as the Buddha's intent behind 'all dharmas lack own-being'; the three turnings of the dharma-wheel of which the third, 'well-distinguished', is definitive; calm abiding and insight and 'the image is cognition only'; the perfections and grounds; the bodies of the Tathāgata.
**Commentaries on this text:** [Commentary on the Maitreya chapter of the Saṃdhinirmocana](samdhinirmocana-maitreya-chapter-commentary.md), [Commentary on the Saṃdhinirmocana-sūtra (Wŏnch'ŭk)](samdhinirmocana-sutra-tika-woncheuk.md)

## Teachings (10: skeleton 10)

### 2 <a id="tea-samdhinirmocana-sutra-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The ultimate is inexpressible and non-dual: 'conditioned' and 'unconditioned' are words imagined by the teacher; the ultimate is realized by the noble ones' self-realized knowledge, beyond reasoning.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: apophatic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_

terms: [paramārtha](../terms/paramartha.md), [advaya](../terms/advaya.md)

### 3 <a id="tea-samdhinirmocana-sutra-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The ultimate is neither the same as nor different from the formations: if the same, ordinary beings would see it; if different, seeing it would not free one from the formations.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_

concepts: [The two truths (satyadvaya)](../concepts/two-truths.md)

### 4 <a id="tea-samdhinirmocana-sutra-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The ultimate is of one taste everywhere, like space among forms: Subhūti hears of monks who proclaim their realization by the aggregates, sense-fields and so on, missing the single taste.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_

teachers: [Subhūti](../teachers/subhuti.md)

### 5 <a id="tea-samdhinirmocana-sutra-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The appropriating consciousness (ādānavijñāna), also called ālaya and citta, holds all the seeds and the body; profound and subtle, it flows like a torrent with all its seeds; the Buddha did not teach it to the foolish lest they imagine it to be a self.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge, meditation · stage: advanced · types: consciousness-mind_

terms: [ādāna-vijñāna](../terms/adana-vijnana.md), [ālayavijñāna](../terms/alayavijnana.md), [bīja](../terms/bija.md) · concepts: [The eight consciousnesses](../concepts/eight-consciousnesses.md) · disputes: [Is there a self (ātman, puruṣa, jīva) — an enduring conscious subject distinct from body and changing cognitions — or only a stream of impersonal factors designated 'person'?](../debates/is-there-a-self.md)

### 6 <a id="tea-samdhinirmocana-sutra-6"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The three characteristics of dharmas: the imagined (parikalpita) — names and conventions; the dependent (paratantra) — dependent origination; the perfected (pariniṣpanna) — suchness. Like the hairs seen by one with cataract, the eye's disorder, and the clear eye's natural object; like a crystal taking on the colors placed beside it.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate, consciousness-mind_

terms: [tri-svabhāva](../terms/trisvabhava.md), [parikalpita](../terms/parikalpita.md), [paratantra (asvatantra)](../terms/paratantra.md), [pariniṣpanna](../terms/parinispanna.md) · concepts: [The three natures (sūtra layer)](../concepts/three-natures.md)

### 7 <a id="tea-samdhinirmocana-sutra-7"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

What the Buddha intended by 'all dharmas lack own-being': three naturelessnesses — of characteristic (the imagined), of arising (the dependent), and ultimate naturelessness (the perfected, suchness, the ultimate that is the absence of self in dharmas).

_level: ultimate · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: advanced · types: ultimate_

terms: [trividhā niḥsvabhāvatā](../terms/trinihsvabhavata.md), [niḥsvabhāva](../terms/nihsvabhava.md) · concepts: [The three naturelessnesses](../concepts/three-naturelessnesses.md) · teachers: [Paramārthasamudgata](../teachers/paramarthasamudgata.md)

### 7 <a id="tea-samdhinirmocana-sutra-7-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The three turnings of the wheel: first, at Vārāṇasī, the four noble truths for śrāvakas — wonderful, yet surpassable, provisional and a basis for dispute; second, for those in the great vehicle, the teaching of naturelessness, non-arising and original peace in a hidden manner — surpassable, provisional, a basis for dispute; third, for all vehicles, the same teaching 'well distinguished' — unsurpassed, definitive (nītārtha) and not a basis for dispute.

_level: bridging · standpoint: analytic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission, dispute_

terms: [dharmacakra](../terms/dharmacakra.md), [neyārtha](../terms/neyartha.md), [nītārtha](../terms/nitartha.md) · concepts: [The three turnings of the dharma-wheel](../concepts/three-turnings.md), [Provisional and definitive meaning](../concepts/neyartha-nitartha.md) · teachers: [Paramārthasamudgata](../teachers/paramarthasamudgata.md) · disputes: [Which turning of the dharma-wheel is of definitive meaning (nītārtha): the teaching of emptiness (second) or the 'well-distinguished' teaching of the three natures and of buddha-nature (third)?](../debates/which-turning-is-definitive.md)

### 8 <a id="tea-samdhinirmocana-sutra-8"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Maitreya asks about calm abiding (śamatha) and insight (vipaśyanā): their objects are images with and without discrimination, the limit of things and the accomplishment of the aim; the image seen in samādhi is not other than the mind — 'the object of consciousness is manifested by consciousness only (vijñaptimātra)'; the mind sees the mind, as a face is seen in a mirror.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: intermediate · types: practice, consciousness-mind_

terms: [samatha](../terms/samatha.md), [vipaśyanā](../terms/vipasyana.md), [vijñapti-mātra](../terms/vijnaptimatra.md) · concepts: [Mind only (sūtra layer)](../concepts/mind-only.md) · practices: [Calm and insight as taught in the Saṃdhinirmocana](../practices/samatha-vipasyana-samdhinirmocana.md) · teachers: [Maitreya (the bodhisattva, future buddha)](../teachers/maitreya-bodhisattva.md)

### 9 <a id="tea-samdhinirmocana-sutra-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Avalokiteśvara asks about the grounds and perfections: ten perfections — the six plus skillful means, vow, power and knowledge — with the ten grounds and a Buddha-ground.

_level: conventional · standpoint: analytic · path: general · stage: advanced · types: practice_

concepts: [The ten perfections](../concepts/ten-paramitas.md), [The ten grounds (bhūmi) of the Daśabhūmika](../concepts/ten-bhumis.md) · teachers: [Avalokiteśvara](../teachers/avalokitesvara.md)

### 10 <a id="tea-samdhinirmocana-sutra-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Mañjuśrī asks about the dharmakāya: it is the transformation of the basis, beyond discursive elaboration and effortless; the Tathāgata's emanation bodies arise like the sun's rays.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: divine · path: knowledge · stage: realized · types: ultimate_
