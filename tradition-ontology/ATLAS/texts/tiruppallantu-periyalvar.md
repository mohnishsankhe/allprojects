Periyāḻvār's twelve-verse benediction 'many years, many years' for the Lord's safety — the devotee, fearing the evil eye on the Lord who appeared on Garuḍa, prays for his welfare (maṅgaḷāśāsana) and invites servants of the Lord to join; it opens the Prabandham and daily worship.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 <a id="tea-tiruppallantu-periyalvar-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Many years, many years, many thousands of years, many crores of hundred thousands of years — O gem-hued one with strong shoulders that felled the wrestlers, may your red feet be kept safe.

### 3-5 <a id="tea-tiruppallantu-periyalvar-3-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Āḻvār calls those who are bound in service to come and sing the benediction with him, and also those who seek the Lord's wealth and those who seek the self (kaivalya), if they will give up those aims and join.
