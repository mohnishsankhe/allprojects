# Meykaṇṭa Śāstras (the fourteen)

`src:meykanta-sastras` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate titles:** Cittānta Cāttiram patiṉāṉku, Meykaṇṭa Cāttiram
**Language:** Tamil
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md)
**Genre:** collection of doctrinal treatises
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 1147–1313; (confidence moderate)
**Structure:** 14 treatises: 2 by the two Uyyavanta Tēvanāyaṉārs, 1 by Meykaṇṭār, 2 by Aruṇanti, 1 by Maṉavācakaṅkaṭantār, 8 by Umāpati (one disputed)

The fourteen doctrinal treatises (cittānta cāttiram) of Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta: (1) Tiruvuntiyār, (2) Tirukkaḷiṟṟuppaṭiyār, (3) Civañāṉa Pōtam, (4) Civañāṉa Cittiyār, (5) Irupā Irupatu, (6) Uṇmai Viḷakkam, (7) Civappirakācam, (8) Tiruvaruṭpayaṉ, (9) Viṉāveṇpā, (10) Poṟṟippahṟoṭai, (11) Koṭikkavi, (12) Neñcuviṭu Tūtu, (13) Uṇmai Neṟi Viḷakkam, (14) Caṅkaṟpa Nirākaraṇam.

_Notes: Named after Meykaṇṭār though two precede him; the canonical order is the tradition's._
