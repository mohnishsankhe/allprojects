# Yakṣapraśna (the Yakṣa's questions)

**Structure:** 2 chapters in the Critical Edition (about 100 verse units); the vulgate chapter is much longer and contains several famous questions absent from the critical text
**Availability:** digitized-original

At a lake guarded by a yakṣa, Yudhiṣṭhira's brothers fall dead after drinking without answering; Yudhiṣṭhira answers the yakṣa's riddling questions on the world, dharma, happiness and the self, chooses his half-brother Nakula to be revived out of fairness to both mothers, and the yakṣa reveals himself as Dharma.
