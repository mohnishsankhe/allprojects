A vṛtti on the sūtras by the grammarian Nāgeśa Bhaṭṭa. On 1.1 it says the śāstra expounds in detail the yoga taught by Hiraṇyagarbha and others; on 1.2 it glosses citta as the inner organ (antaḥkaraṇa) and nirodha as a special, supersensible effort that dissolves the activities.

_Notes: Nāgeśa is reported to have written a longer and a shorter vṛtti (Bṛhatī, Laghvī) — which one the local raw_etexts file contains is not verified._
