A vṛtti on the sūtras by the grammarian Nāgeśa Bhaṭṭa. On 1.1 it says the śāstra expounds in detail the yoga taught by Hiraṇyagarbha and others; on 1.2 it glosses citta as the inner organ (antaḥkaraṇa) and nirodha as a special, supersensible effort that dissolves the activities.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1.1 <a id="tea-yogasutravrtti-nagesa-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Atha' is auspicious in itself and marks a beginning; 'yogānuśāsana' is the śāstra by which yoga is taught analytically; here the yoga already taught by Hiraṇyagarbha and others is set out in detail.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: meditation · stage: all · types: teacher-transmission_

concepts: [Īśvara, Hiraṇyagarbha and the transmission of Yoga](../concepts/isvara-as-first-teacher.md) · teachers: [Hiraṇyagarbha](../teachers/hiranyagarbha.md)

### 1.2 <a id="tea-yogasutravrtti-nagesa-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Citta is the inner organ (antaḥkaraṇa); nirodha of its activities is their turning back — a special, supersensible effort of restraining the mind that dissolves the activities, or the state of the mind in which only impressions of its activities remain.

_level: unmarked · standpoint: analytic · path: meditation · stage: all · types: consciousness-mind_
