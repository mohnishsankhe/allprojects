## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-sivajnanabodha-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The Sanskrit first verse argues from the world's threefold character and its three functions that it has a maker, into whom it is withdrawn and from whom it comes forth again.
