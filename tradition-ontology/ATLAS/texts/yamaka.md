## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1.1.1 <a id="tea-yamaka-1-1-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Book of Pairs settles the scope of terms by converse questions: 'Are all wholesome dhammas wholesome roots? Or are all wholesome roots wholesome dhammas?'

> Ye keci kusalā dhammā, sabbe te kusalamūlā? Ye vā pana kusalamūlā, sabbe te dhammā kusalā?

### 7.1 <a id="tea-yamaka-7-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

There are seven latent tendencies — sensual lust, aversion, conceit, views, doubt, lust for existence, ignorance; sensual lust lies latent in the two pleasant and neutral feelings of the sense sphere, aversion in painful feeling, conceit in the sense sphere's two feelings and in the form and formless spheres, views in everything included in personality.

> Sattānusayā—kāmarāgānusayo, paṭighānusayo, mānānusayo, diṭṭhānusayo, vicikicchānusayo, bhavarāgānusayo, avijjānusayo. Kattha kāmarāgānusayo anuseti? Kāmadhātuyā dvīsu vedanāsu ettha kāmarāgānusayo anuseti. Kattha paṭighānusayo anuseti? Dukkhāya vedanāya ettha paṭighānusayo anuseti. Kattha mānānusayo anuseti? Kāmadhātuyā dvīsu vedanāsu rūpadhātuyā arūpadhātuyā ettha mānānusayo anuseti. Kattha diṭṭhānusayo anuseti? Sabbasakkāyapariyāpannesu dhammesu ettha diṭṭhānusayo anuseti.
