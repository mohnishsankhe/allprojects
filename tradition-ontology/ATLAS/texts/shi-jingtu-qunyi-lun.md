## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 39a01 <a id="tea-shi-jingtu-qunyi-lun-39a01"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

To the objection that the Mahāyānasaṃgraha calls birth 'by vow alone' an intention for another time: 'by vow alone' means a bare vow with no practice, which indeed brings birth only at another time; but present recollection with a sincere mind is good mental action, calling the name good verbal action and joining palms and worshipping good bodily action — with these three good actions one is born at once.

> 論言「唯由發願」者，是簡持義。簡去念佛之行，持取唯空發願，更無有行。……今時念佛至心，即意業善行也；稱佛名號，即語業善行也；合掌禮拜，即身業善行也。

### 50c07 <a id="tea-shi-jingtu-qunyi-lun-50c07"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The *Pusa chutai jing* says that west of here lies a land of sloth and pride (xieman guo), pleasant and full of music, where of millions who set out for Amitābha's land only one reaches it. Huaigan explains: this is why Shandao urged exclusive practice; those of mixed practice, whose resolve is not firm, fall into the land of sloth; of mixed practitioners not one in ten thousand is born, of exclusive practitioners not one in a thousand fails.

> 雜修之者，萬不一生；專修之人，千無一失。

### 76c02 <a id="tea-shi-jingtu-qunyi-lun-76c02"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Reciting aloud without break one attains samādhi and sees the Buddha and holy assembly clearly before one's eyes; the Sūryagarbha says 'great recollection sees a great buddha, small recollection a small buddha' — great means loud calling, small means soft; practitioners today find that with vigorous voiced recitation samādhi is easily achieved, while soft recitation lets the mind scatter. Try it rather than doubt.

> 故《大集日藏分》經言「大念見大佛，小念見小佛。」大念者，大聲稱佛也。小念者，小聲稱佛也。……現見即今諸修學者，唯須勵聲念佛，三昧易成。小聲稱佛，遂多馳散。
