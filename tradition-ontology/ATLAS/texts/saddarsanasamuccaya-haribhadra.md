# Ṣaḍdarśanasamuccaya (Haribhadra)

Haribhadra's 87-verse compendium presenting six darśanas — Buddhist, Nyāya, Sāṃkhya, Jaina, Vaiśeṣika and Jaiminīya (Mīmāṃsā) — each by its deity, categories and means of knowledge, without polemic, adding the Lokāyata at the end for those who count Nyāya and Vaiśeṣika as one. Commented by Guṇaratna (Tarkarahasyadīpikā).
**Commentaries on this text:** [Tarkarahasyadīpikā](tarkarahasyadipika.md)

### 45-58 <a id="tea-saddarsanasamuccaya-haribhadra-45-58"></a>

In the Jaina darśana the deity is the Jina, free of attachment and aversion; the realities are soul, non-soul, merit, demerit, inflow, stopping, bondage, shedding and liberation; the means of knowledge are direct and indirect; and a thing is many-sided.

### 83-85 <a id="tea-saddarsanasamuccaya-haribhadra-83-85"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Earth, water, fire and air are the four elements, the ground of consciousness; the means of knowledge is the senses alone; from the combination of the elements arise body and the rest, and the self (consciousness) is established as intoxicating power arises from the ingredients of liquor.
