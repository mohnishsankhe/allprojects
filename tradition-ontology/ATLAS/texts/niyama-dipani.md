## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### five-niyamas <a id="tea-niyama-dipani-five-niyamas"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

The universe is governed by five orders — of heat, seeds, kamma, mind and Dhamma; kamma is only one of them, and there is no creator.
