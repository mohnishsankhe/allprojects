## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-17 <a id="tea-ratana-sutta-1-17"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Let all beings gathered here be happy and listen; protect humanity day and night with love. No treasure equals the Tathāgata: by this truth may there be well-being; the destruction, dispassion and deathless reached by the Sakyan sage; the concentration without interval; the eight persons; the stream-enterer with three fetters abandoned; the arahants extinguished like a lamp — by this truth may there be well-being.
