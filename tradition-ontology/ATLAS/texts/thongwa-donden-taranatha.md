### qualifications <a id="tea-thongwa-donden-taranatha-qualifications"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The vajra-yoga may be entered only after receiving the Kālacakra empowerment, completing the preliminaries and obtaining the instructions from a qualified teacher who holds the lineage; its wind practices are not to be attempted on one's own.

### six-branches <a id="tea-thongwa-donden-taranatha-six-branches"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The vajra-yoga has six branches — withdrawal, meditative stability, vitality-control, retention, recollection and samādhi — taken in three pairs: the first pair makes the empty forms appear, the middle pair brings the winds into the central channel and stabilizes them, the last pair unites unchanging bliss with empty form. (Summary only; the practice is restricted.)

### ten-signs <a id="tea-thongwa-donden-taranatha-ten-signs"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In the branch of withdrawal, practised in darkness and against the open sky, ten signs arise in order: smoke, mirage, fireflies, a lamp, a blaze, the moon, the sun, darkness, lightning and a drop — the empty forms, which are not to be grasped as ends in themselves.
