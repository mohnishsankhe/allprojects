## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-11 <a id="tea-atanatiya-sutta-1-11"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The Four Great Kings offer a protective recitation honouring Vipassī and the other Buddhas, by which monks, nuns and lay followers may be guarded against non-human beings who are hostile to the teaching; the Buddha passes it on to the monks.
