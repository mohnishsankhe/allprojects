## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-60 <a id="tea-paramoksanirasakarikavrtti-1-60"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Rāmakaṇṭha argues that a liberation in which the soul's consciousness is lost or merged would be no gain to anyone; only the soul's own omniscience and agency, freed from mala, makes liberation a goal worth seeking.
