## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### list <a id="tea-eight-difficult-points-memorandum-list"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The eight difficult points of the Prāsaṅgika: (1) no store-consciousness distinct from the six consciousnesses; (2) no reflexive awareness; (3) autonomous syllogisms not used to generate the view in another; (4) external objects accepted (conventionally) just as consciousness is; (5) śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas realise the selflessness of phenomena; (6) grasping at the self of phenomena is an affliction; (7) disintegration (zhig pa) is a functioning thing; (8) hence a unique presentation of the three times.
