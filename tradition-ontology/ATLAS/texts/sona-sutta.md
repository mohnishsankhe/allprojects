## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 2-9 <a id="tea-sona-sutta-2-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Soṇa strove so hard in walking meditation that his feet bled, and thought of returning to lay life; the Buddha asked whether his lute sounded well when the strings were too tight or too slack — only when evenly tuned. So too much energy leads to restlessness, too little to laziness: determine on evenness of energy, balance the faculties. Soṇa did so and became an arahant.
