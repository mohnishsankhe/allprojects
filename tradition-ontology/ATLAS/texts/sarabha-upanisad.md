## Teachings (1: sourced 1)

### 1-10 <a id="tea-sarabha-upanisad-1-10"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

Paippalāda asks Brahmā which of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Maheśvara is to be meditated on; Maheśvara is supreme; in the form of Śarabha he subdued Nṛsiṃha; hymns praise his deeds — cutting Brahmā's fifth head, kicking Death, drinking the poison, burning the three cities and Kāma.
