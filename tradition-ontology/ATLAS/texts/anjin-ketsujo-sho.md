A tract teaching that the Buddha's attainment of awakening and the beings' birth were accomplished together, so that the person and the Dharma are one (kihō ittai) in Namu Amida Butsu.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### kiho-ittai <a id="tea-anjin-ketsujo-sho-kiho-ittai"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Amida's attainment of perfect awakening and our birth were accomplished at the same time; in Namu Amida Butsu the person (ki) who entrusts and the Dharma (hō) that saves are one; the Buddha's body itself is our practice, so the practitioner does nothing of his own.
