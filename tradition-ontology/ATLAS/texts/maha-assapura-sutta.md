## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3-20 <a id="tea-maha-assapura-sutta-3-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

What makes one an ascetic: conscience and fear of wrongdoing, purified conduct of body, speech, mind and livelihood, guarded senses, moderation in eating, devotion to wakefulness, mindfulness and clear comprehension, the abandoning of the hindrances (like freedom from debt, illness, prison, slavery and the desert) and the four jhānas with their similes; with the destruction of the effluents one is called an ascetic, a brahmin, one who is washed, a master of knowledge, a noble one, an arahant.
