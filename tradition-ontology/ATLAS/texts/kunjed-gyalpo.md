The root tantra of the mind series in 84 chapters: bodhicitta, the 'all-creating king', speaks to Vajrasattva in the first person, teaching that everything is its own display, that there is nothing to meditate, no samaya to keep, no ground or path to traverse, and that effort is a sickness; it includes the 'six vajra lines' and other early mind-series scriptures as chapters, and restricts the teaching to fit recipients.

## Teachings (13: skeleton 13)

### 1 <a id="tea-kunjed-gyalpo-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The setting: in Akaniṣṭha — the sky-expanse of dharmatā, the abode of mind itself, a palace of wisdom without obscuration — the all-creating king bodhicitta brings forth retinues from its own nature, its own essence and its own compassion: a dharmakāya retinue; sambhogakāya retinues called earth, water, fire, wind and space; emanation retinues of the beings of the desire, form and formless realms; and four kinds of yogins. None is separate from its nature, essence and compassion. The chapter closes: teacher, teaching, retinue, place and time arise from me and are primordially a single sphere (thig le).

### 2 <a id="tea-kunjed-gyalpo-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Asked what purpose the five causes and five wisdom-bodies serve, the all-creating king answers that there is no maker apart from itself: it sets out the three teachers, the retinues and dharmatā. Its nature has three aspects and is bodhicitta: 'byang' (purified) as the three excellences purified in dharmatā, 'chub' (pervading) as pervading everything like space, 'sems' (mind) as the unceasing all-maker that makes everything into bodhicitta.

### 4 <a id="tea-kunjed-gyalpo-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The all-creating king explains its names: 'I am primordially self-arising wisdom; I am the heart of all phenomena; I am the all-creating king, bodhicitta — understand my name and you understand all phenomena.' 'Self-arising' means without causes and conditions, beyond effort; 'wisdom' means unceasing and unobscured, revealing everything; 'byang' means primordially pure; 'chub' means pervading appearance and existence, vessel and contents, the buddhas of the three times and the beings of the six classes; 'sems' means entering and mastering all. Whoever knows its nature knows all teachers and teachings and is beyond action, effort and accomplishment, spontaneously present.

> nga ni ye nas rang 'byung ye shes te/ /nga ni ye nas chos kun snying po yin/ /nga ni kun byed rgyal po byang chub sems/ /nga yi mtshan 'di sems dpa' rtogs par gyis/

### 9 <a id="tea-kunjed-gyalpo-9"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

On deviations and obscurations: all appearances are of the essence of bodhicitta; ignorance and not-knowing are called the two obscurations, and seeking and accomplishing are called deviations. To conceive what is one's own as other is to obscure oneself by oneself. Rest happily in non-conceptual evenness: the ten great natures need no effort, and when they are not sought the three hundred and sixty deviations and obscurations have no place.

### 10 <a id="tea-kunjed-gyalpo-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The three jewels are the all-creator's body, speech and mind; as teacher it makes the three kāyas, which set out the teaching as outer, inner and secret, each threefold, making nine: by nine passes, nine scriptures and nine paths one travels to the single vehicle, the all-creator's ground of awakening — yet the all-creator, beyond action and effort, arrives at the ground of awakening without travelling.

### 14 <a id="tea-kunjed-gyalpo-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Why the nature is kept secret from the unfortunate: were the all-creator's nature taught openly to all, the three kāyas and the three teachers would lose their distinct teachings and vehicles, and nothing could be called 'excellent'; therefore the all-creating king teaches its own nature to itself, has not given this transmission to the teachers and retinues that arose from it, and it is to be shown to the Atiyoga yogin, who is the all-creator.

### 31 <a id="tea-kunjed-gyalpo-31"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The six vajra lines: 'The nature of variety is non-dual; in its particulars it is free of elaboration. Though it is not conceived as "just so", Vairocana, the all-good, appears everywhere. ### 32 <a id="tea-kunjed-gyalpo-32"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

### 33 <a id="tea-kunjed-gyalpo-33"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Definitive and provisional teaching: resting in the nature beyond conceptual examination — not meditating on samādhi, without a mind of desire or a fruit to be taken — one reaches the buddha-ground without travelling a path, finds self-arising wisdom without training awareness, accomplishes activity without seeking, and is naturally pure without guarding samaya; buddhas and beings are not seen as two. That the three-kāya teachers teach separately is a provisional teaching (drang ba'i lung) with respect to suchness.

### 44 <a id="tea-kunjed-gyalpo-44"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Nothing to be done: the all-creating king has already accomplished everything, so view need not be meditated, samaya need not be kept, the vidyādhara levels and ten grounds need not be trained, and activity need not be sought. One who, not understanding this, strives and meditates to accomplish it contradicts the meaning beyond cause and effect and is seized by the sickness of effort. Therefore, since the Great Perfection beyond cause and effect is not the domain of those without the fortune for it, they should practise the dharma of cause and result.

> de bas rgyu 'bras 'das pa'i rdzogs chen la/ /skal med rnams kyi spyod yul ma yin pas/ /rgyu dang 'bras bu'i chos la spyad par bya/

### 45 <a id="tea-kunjed-gyalpo-45"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Without view or meditation: whatever is meditated is my sickness; since bodhicitta, the heart of all, is free of grasped and grasper, there is not an atom to meditate on; as meditating has not produced the fruit for those above, I do not teach that I am attained by meditation. 'Byang' has nothing to be grasped, 'chub' is the heart of all pervading all, 'sems' is self-arising wisdom everywhere.

### 46 <a id="tea-kunjed-gyalpo-46"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

No samaya to guard: for yogins who rely on the cause-and-result scriptures of the three teachers, the two truths and the four valid means are needed; but since the all-creator made all phenomena and made them all bodhicitta, what it made needs no two truths — in bodhicitta there is neither ultimate nor conventional.

### 83 <a id="tea-kunjed-gyalpo-83"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

To whom the teaching is to be given: not to unfit vessels — those who delight in fame and worldly concerns, are proud, do not honour the excellent, are fickle, lack generosity and faith, want to force practice on their own terms, or harbour harmful intent; from them it is to be kept supremely secret. Signs and faith are first examined; the teacher takes the disciple's body and possessions so as to cut worldly attachment and test the mind-stream; to those who bear the signs, the essence, the all-creating king, is given. Teaching it to the wrong person brings untimely death and fear to both and the decline of the vehicle of the essence.
