Some seven hundred surviving songs of Tyāgarāja of Tiruvaiyāru to Rāma: surrender, the name of Rāma, the saints, and music (nāda) as a path to God when joined to devotion; they include the five 'jewel' songs (pañcaratna).

### kṛti 'Mokṣamu galadā' (rāga Sāramati) <a id="tea-tyagaraja-krtis-moksamu-galada"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Is there liberation for those who lack devotion joined with knowledge of the notes (svara)? The praṇava-nāda arising from the union of breath and fire shines as the seven notes; Śiva, the Lord, delights in the vīṇā-playing.

### kṛti 'Nāda tanum aniśam' (rāga Cittarañjani) <a id="tea-tyagaraja-krtis-nada-tanum-anisam"></a>

I bow always, with mind and head, to Śaṅkara whose body is nāda, the essence of the Sāmaveda, from whose five faces (Sadyojāta and the rest) the seven excellent notes sa ri ga ma pa dha ni arose.
