Jetsün Drakpa Gyaltsen's song of experience expanding each of the four lines: the uselessness of ethics, study and meditation done for this life; the faults of the three realms; the futility of one's own liberation alone; and resting beyond the extremes of existence and non-existence.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1 <a id="tea-parting-from-four-attachments-drakpa-gyaltsen-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Ethics, study, reflection and meditation practised for this life's sake are without essence: ethics kept for this life is the root of the lower realms, study and reflection so done are the root of pride and dispute, meditation so done is only a basis for worldly distraction — so do not be attached to this life.

### 2 <a id="tea-parting-from-four-attachments-drakpa-gyaltsen-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Reflecting on the sufferings of the three lower realms, of the gods and humans, and on the change and pervasive suffering of all saṃsāra, there is no happiness in it; so give up attachment to the three realms.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: practice_

### 3 <a id="tea-parting-from-four-attachments-drakpa-gyaltsen-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Liberating only oneself is useless when all beings of the three realms have been one's parents; generate the mind of awakening for them, taking on their suffering and giving them one's happiness.

_level: conventional · standpoint: seeker · path: general · stage: all · types: practice_

### 4 <a id="tea-parting-from-four-attachments-drakpa-gyaltsen-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

There is no liberation for one who clings to existence, no higher rebirth for one who clings to non-existence, and clinging to both is ignorance; rest free of extremes in the natural state beyond elaboration.
