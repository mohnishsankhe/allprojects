## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### exchange <a id="tea-lama-chopa-exchange"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Seeing that self-cherishing is the door to all unwanted suffering and that cherishing one's mothers is the basis of all good qualities, bless me to make the yoga of exchanging self and others the heart of my practice.

### tonglen <a id="tea-lama-chopa-tonglen"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Therefore, compassionate venerable teachers, bless me that all the wrongdoing, obscurations and suffering of mother beings ripen on me right now, and that by giving my happiness and virtue to others all beings may be happy.
