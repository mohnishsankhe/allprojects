## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### polishing-tile <a id="tea-zazenshin-polishing-tile"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

On Nanyue's polishing a tile: Dōgen reads it not as a rejection of sitting but as showing that making a mirror by polishing a tile and becoming a buddha by sitting are themselves the ancestors' practice; sitting buddha is not attaining buddhahood as a later result.
