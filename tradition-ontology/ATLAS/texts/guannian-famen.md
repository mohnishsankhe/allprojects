## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 24a26-b20 <a id="tea-guannian-famen-24a26-b20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Entering the samādhi hall: prepare a clean room with an image on the west wall; at one of four fixed times of the month, according to one's household duties, enter for one to seven days in clean clothes, taking one vegetarian meal a day; day and night bind the mind in continuous recollection of Amitābha, mind and voice together, standing or sitting, not sleeping, not whispering to others; confess the sins of this life at the three or six periods; visions that appear should not be told lightly — good ones are known to oneself, bad ones should be repented; vow off wine, meat and the five pungent roots.

> 七日之中皆須一食長齋……於道場中晝夜束心相續，專心念阿彌陀佛，心與聲相續。……所見境界不得輒說，善者自知，惡者懺悔。

### 24b21-c04 <a id="tea-guannian-famen-24b21-c04"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

When a practitioner, ill or not, is about to die, let him follow the samādhi method: set body and mind right, face west, fix the mind on contemplating Amitābha, mind and voice in accord without a break, with the conviction of birth and the thought of the lotus-dais and holy ones coming to welcome him. If he sees visions he tells his attendant, who records them; if he cannot speak the attendant keeps asking what he sees; if he tells of signs of sin, those beside him recite and repent with him until the sin is removed and the lotus-dais appears. Relatives who have taken wine, meat or pungent roots must not approach the sick one, lest he lose right mindfulness, be troubled by spirits, die deranged and fall into the evil paths.

> 又行者等若病不病欲命終時，一依上念佛三昧法正當身心，迴面向西，心亦專注觀想阿彌陀佛。心口相應聲聲莫絕，決定作往生想、華臺聖眾來迎接想。病人若見前境，即向看病人說。

### 24c18-22 <a id="tea-guannian-famen-24c18-22"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Those who call Amitābha's name aspiring to the Pure Land gain, in this life and after, five augmenting conditions: the extinction of sins; protection and long life; seeing the Buddha (by the three powers of the Buddha's vow, the samādhi and one's own merit, after the Pratyutpanna); being embraced; and the testimony of the buddhas that one will be born.

> 一者滅罪增上緣。二者護念得長命增上緣。三者見佛增上緣。四者攝生增上緣。五者證生增上緣。
