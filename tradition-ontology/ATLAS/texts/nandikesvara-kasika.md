## Teachings (2: sourced 1, skeleton 1)

### 1 <a id="tea-nandikesvara-kasika-1"></a>
`sourced` · confidence moderate

At the end of his dance the king of dancers (Śiva) sounded his drum fourteen times, wishing to uplift Sanaka and the other perfected ones; this is the net of the Śiva-sūtras.

### 1 <a id="tea-nandikesvara-kasika-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

U31 reading of the opening verse: the fourteen phoneme-sūtras are the sound of Śiva's drum at the end of his dance, given to lift up Sanaka and the perfected ones — so the grammarian's list of sounds is itself revelation for liberation.

> nṛttāvasāne naṭarājarājo nanāda ḍhakkāṃ navapañcavāram | uddhartukāmaḥ sanakādisiddhān etad vimarśe śivasūtrajālam ||
