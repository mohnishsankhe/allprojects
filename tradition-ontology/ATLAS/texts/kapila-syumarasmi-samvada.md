Seeing a cow led to sacrifice, Kapila exclaims at the Vedas; the sage Syūmaraśmi, entering the cow, challenges him, and Kapila replies that he does not censure the Vedas but that the duties of the different āśramas have one aim, and teaches the renunciant's path of knowledge.
