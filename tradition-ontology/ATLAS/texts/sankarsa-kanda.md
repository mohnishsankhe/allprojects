# Saṅkarṣa-kāṇḍa

`src:sankarsa-kanda` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Alternate titles:** Saṃkarṣa-kāṇḍa, Devatā-kāṇḍa, Saṅkarṣaṇa-kāṇḍa
**Original title:** सङ्कर्ष-काण्ड
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
**Genre:** sūtra
**Authors:** 
  - [Jaimini](../teachers/jaimini.md) — role: author; attribution: disputed
**Attribution:** tradition: Jaimini (Vṛttikāra tradition preserved by Rāmānuja); scholarly: authorship disputed; some modern accounts connect it with Kāśakṛtsna; confidence: low
**Structure:** 4 adhyāyas
**Availability:** unknown

A four-adhyāya supplement to the twelve adhyāyas of the Mīmāṃsā Sūtra, also called Devatā-kāṇḍa. The Vṛttikāra cited by Rāmānuja speaks of Jaimini's teaching in sixteen chapters (ṣoḍaśalakṣaṇa), i.e. the twelve plus these four, joined to the Śārīraka (Brahma Sūtra).
