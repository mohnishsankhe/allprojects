## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### nominal-monks <a id="tea-mappo-tomyoki-nominal-monks"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

In the last age of the Dharma there are no precept-keepers, only monks in name; to speak of breaking precepts presupposes precepts, which no longer exist; these nominal monks are nevertheless the true treasures of the age and should be honoured.
