Nāgārjuna's 'Precious Garland' of advice to a king: first the practices of higher rebirth (abhyudaya) — the ten virtuous paths, giving, non-harm — then those of definite goodness (naiḥśreyasa), the insight that 'I' and 'mine' are false; the causes of a Buddha's marks, the defence of the Mahāyāna as the Buddha's word, royal policy and the bodhisattva's deeds and grounds.

### 1.3-4 <a id="tea-ratnavali-1-3-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

First comes the dharma of higher status, afterwards definite goodness arises, for having obtained higher status one comes gradually to definite goodness. Higher status is held to be happiness, definite goodness liberation; in brief their means are faith and wisdom.
