All beings, including Brahmā and Rudra, arise from Nārāyaṇa; all this is Nārāyaṇa; the eight-syllable mantra 'oṃ namo nārāyaṇāya' leads to Vaikuṇṭha; its five short sections are styled the 'heads' of the four Vedas.

## Teachings (4: skeleton 4)

### 1 <a id="tea-narayana-upanisad-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

From Nārāyaṇa are born prāṇa, mind, the senses, the elements, Brahmā, Rudra, Indra and all beings.

_level: conventional · standpoint: cosmic · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: world-fate, ultimate_

### 2 <a id="tea-narayana-upanisad-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Nārāyaṇa is eternal; he alone is all this, what was and what will be.

_level: ultimate · standpoint: absolute · path: knowledge · stage: all · types: ultimate_

### 3 <a id="tea-narayana-upanisad-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

'Oṃ namo nārāyaṇāya' — the eight-syllable mantra; one who recites it reaches Vaikuṇṭha.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion, sound · stage: all · types: sound-language, karma-liberation_

practices: [Recitation of the eight-syllable Nārāyaṇa mantra](../practices/narayana-astaksara-japa.md)

### 4-5 <a id="tea-narayana-upanisad-4-5"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The Lord is the bliss of Brahman in the heart-lotus; 'the son of Devakī is devoted to brāhmaṇas'; reciting this Atharvaśiras frees from sins.

_level: conventional · standpoint: devotional · path: devotion · stage: all · types: ultimate, karma-liberation_
