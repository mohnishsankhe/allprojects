## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 10 <a id="tea-vyavahara-sutra-10"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

There are five grounds of judgment (vyavahāra) for monastic cases, to be used in order of availability: direct supernormal knowledge (āgama), scripture (śruta), instruction received (ājñā), retained tradition (dhāraṇā) and established practice (jīta). The same section fixes which texts a monk may be taught after how many years of monastic life.
