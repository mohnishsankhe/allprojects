## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1-20 <a id="tea-bodhisattvasamvaravimsaka-1-20"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

The bodhisattva vow is to be taken from a teacher who keeps and understands it and is competent; the four root downfalls (as in the Bodhisattvabhūmi) and forty-six lesser faults are to be avoided; a downfall committed with strong affliction destroys the vow, which may be taken again; lesser faults are confessed.
