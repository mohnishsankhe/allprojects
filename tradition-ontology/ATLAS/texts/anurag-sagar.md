# Anurāg Sāgar

Kabīr tells Dharamdās how the Sat Puruṣ emanated sixteen sons; how Kāl Nirañjan won by austerity the three worlds and the goddess Ādyā (Aṣṭāṅgī), from whom Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Maheś were born; how Kāl traps souls in the round of births; and how Kabīr, in each age, comes to lead souls (haṃs) back to Satlok through the true Name. It also prophesies the Dharamdāsī line of forty-two generations.

## Teachings (5: skeleton 5)

### Anurāg Sāgar, Kabīr's descents in the four ages <a id="tea-anurag-sagar-kabir-in-four-ages"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Kabīr (the Sat Puruṣ's messenger) came in each age to free souls from Kāl: as Sat Sukṛt in the Satya age, Munīndra in the Tretā, Karuṇāmay in the Dvāpara and Kabīr in the Kali; Kāl obtained the promise that only those who receive the true Name would be freed.

### Anurāg Sāgar, Kāl Nirañjan's austerities and the three worlds <a id="tea-anurag-sagar-kal-niranjan-austerity-and-three-worlds"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

Nirañjan performed austerities and won from the Sat Puruṣ the three worlds and the goddess Ādyā (Aṣṭāṅgī); he swallowed her, was cast out of Satlok, and with her begot Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Maheś; he became Kāl, who devours beings and hides the true Lord, and made souls forget Satlok.

### Anurāg Sāgar, the prophecy of Dharamdās's forty-two generations <a id="tea-anurag-sagar-forty-two-generations-prophecy"></a>

### Anurāg Sāgar, the release of the soul (haṃs) by the true Name <a id="tea-anurag-sagar-hams-and-the-true-name"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The soul (haṃs) that receives the true Name from the guru of Kabīr's line, with the pān-parvānā, is let pass by Kāl's messengers at death and returns to Satlok, never to be reborn.
