**Availability:** digitized-original

Vasiṣṭha teaches the despondent young Rāma that the world is a projection of mind within the one consciousness (cit), arising like a dream; that human effort (pauruṣa) guided by scripture and the good overrides fate, which is only past effort; that liberation comes by inquiry (vicāra) with the four gatekeepers (peace, inquiry, contentment, holy company), by the weakening of latent tendencies (vāsanā) and the stilling of mind and breath, through seven stages of knowledge to liberation while living. The teaching is carried by dozens of stories (Līlā, Karkaṭī, Lavaṇa, Śukra, Dāśūra, Prahlāda, Bali, Gādhi, Uddālaka, Suraghu, Vītahavya, Bhuśuṇḍa, Cūḍālā and Śikhidhvaja, the stone and others).
