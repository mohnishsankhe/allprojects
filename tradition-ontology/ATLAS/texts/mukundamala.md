Sanskrit hymn to Mukunda (Kṛṣṇa) attributed to Kulaśekhara: the devotee asks not for dharma, wealth or pleasure but only for unwavering devotion to the Lord's feet in every birth, and asks that the mind enter the Lord's feet now, since at death, with the throat choked, remembrance may fail.

## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### krsna-tvadiya <a id="tea-mukundamala-krsna-tvadiya"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

O Kṛṣṇa, let the royal swan of my mind enter the cage of your lotus feet this very day; at the time the life-breath departs, when the throat is choked by phlegm, wind and bile, how could I remember you?

> kṛṣṇa tvadīya-pada-paṅkaja-pañjarāntam adyaiva me viśatu mānasa-rāja-haṃsaḥ | prāṇa-prayāṇa-samaye kapha-vāta-pittaiḥ kaṇṭhāvarodhana-vidhau smaraṇaṃ kutas te ||

### nastha-dharme <a id="tea-mukundamala-nastha-dharme"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

I have no attachment to dharma, to heaps of wealth or to enjoyment of pleasures; let whatever must be, be, according to my former deeds; this alone I ask, O Lord, in birth after birth: let there be unwavering devotion to your two lotus feet.

> nāsthā dharme na vasu-nicaye naiva kāmopabhoge yad bhāvyaṃ tad bhavatu bhagavan pūrva-karmānurūpam | etat prārthyaṃ mama bahumataṃ janma-janmāntare 'pi tvat-pādāmbhoruha-yuga-gatā niścalā bhaktir astu ||
