### intro <a id="tea-siva-sutra-vimarsini-intro"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Paramaśiva, wishing that the secret tradition not be cut off, favoured Vasugupta in a dream, telling him of a secret on a great rock on this mountain; on waking, Vasugupta found the rock, which turned over at the touch of his hand, confirming the dream, and received the sūtras.
