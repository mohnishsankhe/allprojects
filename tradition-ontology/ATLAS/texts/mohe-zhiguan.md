## Teachings (3: skeleton 3)

### 10b12 <a id="tea-mohe-zhiguan-10b12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Because some, lacking faith, place the sage's state beyond their reach and others, lacking wisdom, grow arrogant thinking themselves equal to the Buddha, one must know the six identities: identity in principle, verbal identity, identity in contemplative practice, identity of resemblance, identity of partial truth, and ultimate identity. They begin with the ordinary person and end with the sage: beginning with the ordinary removes doubt and timidity; ending with the sage removes arrogance.

> 為此事故，須知六即，謂理即、名字即、觀行即、相似即、分真即、究竟即。此六即者，始凡終聖。始凡故，除疑怯；終聖故，除慢大。

### 10b15-10c21 <a id="tea-mohe-zhiguan-10b15"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The six identities explained: identity in principle — one moment of mind is the principle of the tathāgatagarbha (empty, provisional and middle), present in all; verbal identity — though principle is identical, in daily use one does not know it, until from a good friend or the sūtras one hears of the one real bodhi and understands in words that all dharmas are the buddha-dharma; identity in contemplative practice — mind and speech in accord, contemplation unceasing though not yet in accord with principle; identity of resemblance — the more one contemplates the clearer, like an archer nearing the target, as in the purification of the six senses, ignorance subdued and a wisdom resembling the middle way; identity of partial truth — through that power one enters the stage of the copper wheel, first breaks ignorance, sees the buddha-nature, opens the treasury and manifests suchness (the abode of arousing the mind), and so on up to equal awakening, manifesting bodies to save beings; ultimate identity — equal awakening turns into wondrous awakening, wisdom's light complete, nothing more to cut.

> 理即者，一念心即如來藏理

### 10c21 <a id="tea-mohe-zhiguan-10c21"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

The six identities in one simile: a poor man has a treasure in his house but does not know it; a knowing friend shows him and he knows; he clears the weeds and digs, gradually coming near; when near, the store is opened and he takes and uses it all.

> 譬如貧人，家有寶藏而無知者，知識示之，即得知也；耘除草穢而掘出之，漸漸得近；近已藏開，盡取用之。
