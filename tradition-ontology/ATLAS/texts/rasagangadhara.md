## Teachings (2: skeleton 2)

### 1 (on rasa) <a id="tea-rasagangadhara-1-2"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Rasa is consciousness itself, with its covering removed (bhagnāvaraṇā cit), delimited by the stable emotion such as love: when the veil of ignorance is broken through the generalized determinants, one's own blissful consciousness shines with that emotion as its object.

### 1 (opening definition) <a id="tea-rasagangadhara-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Poetry is a word that conveys a charming (ramaṇīya) meaning; charm is that which produces supermundane delight.

> ramaṇīyārthapratipādakaḥ śabdaḥ kāvyam
