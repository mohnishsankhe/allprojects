## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 3-14 <a id="tea-ganakamoggallana-sutta-3-14"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

In this Dhamma and discipline there is gradual training: first restraint by the Pātimokkha, then sense restraint, moderation in eating, wakefulness, mindfulness and clear comprehension, a secluded resting place, abandoning the hindrances, the four jhānas. Some reach nibbāna and some do not; the Tathāgata only points the way, as one who knows the road to Rājagaha.
