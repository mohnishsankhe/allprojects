### kīrtana 'ikṣvāku kula tilaka' <a id="tea-bhadracala-ramadas-kirtanas-prison"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

From prison Rāmadās reproaches Rāma: I spent the money on your ornaments — a necklace for Sītā, jewels for Lakṣmaṇa and for you — and now I suffer; why do you not answer me, O jewel of the Ikṣvākus?
