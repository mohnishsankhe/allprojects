Daṇḍin's 'mirror of poetry' in three chapters: the body of poetry, the southern (vaidarbha) and eastern (gauḍa) styles and their ten qualities, and the figures of sense and sound. Its opening praises the light called word, without which the three worlds would be blind darkness.

## Teachings (1: skeleton 1)

### 1.4 <a id="tea-kavyadarsa-1-4"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

This whole threefold world would become blind darkness if the light called 'word' did not shine to the end of saṃsāra.
