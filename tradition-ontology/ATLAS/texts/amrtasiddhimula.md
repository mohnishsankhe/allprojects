**Availability:** digitized-translation

In the Derge Tengyur (Tōh 2285, among the siddhas' works of vol. zhi) the text opens with homage to Śrī Heruka and to a three-eyed, head-severed, red deity who dispels the fear of death, then announces the 'great seals' (phyag rgya chen po) and teaches the seated seal with the heel at the perineum — the mahāmudrā of the early haṭha texts — in a Buddhist tantric frame.
