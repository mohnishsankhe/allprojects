### 1 <a id="tea-thirty-seven-practices-1"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence high

Having gained this precious human life with its freedoms and endowments, so hard to find, to free oneself and others from the ocean of saṃsāra, listening, reflecting and meditating day and night without distraction is the practice of a bodhisattva.
