### 134a06-12 <a id="tea-nianfo-sanmei-baowang-lun-134a06-12"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

A visitor asks Feixi to explain why the Lotus samādhi of Sadāparibhūta's practice and the nianfo samādhi of the Pratyutpanna are both called the unsurpassed, profound and wondrous gate of meditation and the shortcut to the way; the treatise answers in twenty gates.
