# Kālī Tantra

An eastern Kālī tantra on the worship of Dakṣiṇakālī; its meditation verse on the four-armed Dakṣiṇakālī ('karālavadanāṃ ghorāṃ muktakeśīṃ caturbhujām …') is quoted by Kṛṣṇānanda's Tantrasāra.

_Notes: Printed in Bengal (19th c.); not in the local corpus. Chapter structure not recalled. Its meditation verse is recorded as quoted: tea:tantrasara-krsnananda:2.syama-dhyana._
