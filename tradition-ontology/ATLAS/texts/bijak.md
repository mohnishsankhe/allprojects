The Bījak ('treasure-map, inventory') is the scripture of the Kabīr Chaurā line: ramainīs on creation, māyā and delusion; śabdas that satirize paṇḍit and mullā, yogī and ascetic, and call the sants to see; sākhī couplets on the Word, the guru, death and discernment (pārakh). Its tone is sharply critical and apophatic, stressing testing and discernment over devotional sweetness.

### Śabda ('santo, dekhat jag baurānā …'; no. 4 in some editions) <a id="tea-bijak-sabda-santo-dekhat-jag-baurana"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

Sants, see — the world has gone mad: tell the truth and they run to beat you, tell lies and they trust you. The Hindu says Rām is dear to him, the Turk says Rahīm; they fight each other to death and neither knows the secret. Many pīrs and holy men read their books, many practise rites and austerities, many wear sectarian marks and garlands — all are lost in pride and have not found the one within.
