**Availability:** digitized-original

The fifth and largest Aṅga, a vast collection of questions put to Mahāvīra, mostly by Gautama Indrabhūti, on souls, karma, matter, the cosmos, the gods and the path, with narrative sections: the questions of Skandaka and of the laywoman Jayantī, the schism of Jamāli, the followers of Pārśva who accept Mahāvīra, and (śataka 15) the history of Gośāla and his conflict with Mahāvīra. Its answers often distinguish respects (substance, place, time, state), foreshadowing the doctrine of standpoints.
