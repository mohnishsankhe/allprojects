### 3 <a id="tea-paramatthamanjusa-3"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

The sub-commentary identifies the anonymous 'some' who teach fourteen temperaments (Vism III) as the elder Upatissa, i.e. the Vimuttimagga.

### 18 <a id="tea-paramatthamanjusa-18"></a>
`skeleton` · confidence low

For the one whose vehicle is pure insight, momentary unification of mind (khaṇika-cittekaggatā) arising during insight serves as the concentration of the purification of mind.
