Bhīṣma's instruction on the dharma of liberation, a large anthology of dialogues and 'gītās': early forms of Sāṃkhya and Yoga (the 24 principles, the 25th puruṣa and a 26th principle), meditation (a fourfold dhyāna-yoga), the faults of yoga, the creation and dissolution of the world, time and fate, renunciation and desirelessness (Piṅgalā, Maṅki), the reciter's path (Jāpaka), non-violence (Tulādhāra, Kapila), the teachings to Śuka, and the Nārāyaṇīya.
