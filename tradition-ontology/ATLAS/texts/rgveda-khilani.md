# Ṛgveda Khilāni (the supplementary hymns)

`src:rgveda-khilani` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate titles:** Khilas, Khila-sūktas, Pariśiṣṭa hymns
**Original title:** ऋग्वेदखिलानि
**Language:** Sanskrit
**Family:** vedic
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Genre:** saṃhitā (supplementary hymns)
**Attribution:** tradition: part of the revealed hymns, not counted in the Saṃhitā; scholarly: later additions from the late Vedic period onward; confidence: moderate
**Structure:** about 5 adhyāyas of khila hymns in the Kashmiri recension (as edited by Scheftelowitz, 1906)
**Availability:** digitized-original

A collection of supplementary hymns (khila) transmitted alongside the Śākala Saṃhitā — most fully in a Kashmiri manuscript — including the Śrī Sūkta, the Medhā Sūkta, the Śivasaṅkalpa verses and other hymns used in later worship.
