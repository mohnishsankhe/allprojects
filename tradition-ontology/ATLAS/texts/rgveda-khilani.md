A collection of supplementary hymns (khila) transmitted alongside the Śākala Saṃhitā — most fully in a Kashmiri manuscript — including the Śrī Sūkta, the Medhā Sūkta, the Śivasaṅkalpa verses and other hymns used in later worship.
