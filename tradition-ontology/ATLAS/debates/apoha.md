# What does a word mean: a real universal, or the exclusion of others (apoha)?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)

A real universal (ākṛti) — MS 1.3.33; Kumārila's apohavāda rejects exclusion: an exclusion presupposes a positive grasp of what is excluded.
**Texts:**

### [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
Words signify by excluding what is other (anyāpoha); there are no real universals (Dignāga, Dharmakīrti).
