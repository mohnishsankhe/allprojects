# Are the ten practices of the Vajjian monks (including accepting gold and silver) allowable?

`dsp:ten-points-vesali` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md)
(The Vajjian monks of Vesālī, as reported by the Theravāda Vinaya) Ten practices are allowable, among them carrying salt in a horn, eating when the shadow is two fingers past noon, and accepting gold and silver.
- Cv 12.1.
**Texts:** 
  - [Cullavagga](../texts/cullavagga.md) — ref: Cv 12.1
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
(Yasa, Revata, Sabbakāmī and the council of seven hundred) None of the ten points accords with the Dhamma and discipline.
- Cv 12.2.
**Texts:** 
  - [Cullavagga](../texts/cullavagga.md) — ref: Cv 12.2
