# Can the categories and means of knowledge be coherently defined?

`dsp:definability-of-categories` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Coverage:** A5

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
No: every definition proposed by the Naiyāyikas (of valid cognition, of the means of knowledge, of the categories) fails on examination; the world of distinctions is indeterminable (Śrīharṣa's Khaṇḍanakhaṇḍakhādya), argued on the opponent's own rules.
- Khaṇḍanakhaṇḍakhādya (as usually described).
**Texts:**

### [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md)
Yes: definitions can be made exact by specifying delimitors, relations and counterpositives; Gaṅgeśa's Tattvacintāmaṇi answers such objections.
- Tattvacintāmaṇi (scholarly account of its motivation).
**Texts:**
