# Is the Abhidhamma the word of the Buddha?

`dsp:authority-of-abhidhamma` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
Yes: the Buddha taught it in the Tāvatiṃsa heaven and gave the method to Sāriputta; the Kathāvatthu was expanded from the Buddha's outline; it is teaching in ultimate terms.
- The Atthasālinī's account of its origin.
- Suttas mention monks conversing on 'abhidhamma'.
**Texts:** 
  - [Atthasālinī](../texts/atthasalini.md) — ref: nidanakatha
### [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md)
The sūtras are the authority; the Abhidharma treatises are compositions of disciples (as the Sautrāntikas are described in Abhidharma literature; U38).
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 1
### [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
Their own seven Abhidharma books are the Buddha's teaching compiled by disciples (U38).
