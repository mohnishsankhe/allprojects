# Is the Pāñcarātra a valid scripture, and does the Brahma Sūtra refute it?

`dsp:validity-of-pancaratra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A4, A9

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
The Pāñcarātra is invalid: not rooted in the Veda, practised by Sātvatas/Bhāgavatas who are a low temple-serving class, classed by smṛti with heretical systems, and refuted by Bādarāyaṇa.
- no pramāṇa establishes it
- Manu names the Sātvata among degraded classes
- BS 2.2.42–45
**Texts:** 
  - [Āgamaprāmāṇya](../texts/agamapramanya.md) — ref: purvapaksa
  - [Tantravārttika](../texts/tantravarttika.md) — ref: 1.3.4
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
The worship of Vāsudeva by the five daily practices is acceptable, but the doctrine that the soul (Saṅkarṣaṇa) is born from Vāsudeva is refuted, and the system shows contradiction and opposition to the Veda (Śāṇḍilya turning to it after not finding the highest good in the Vedas).
- an originated soul could not be liberated
- an instrument (mind) cannot arise from an agent
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 2.2.42-45
  - [Brahma Sūtra](../texts/brahma-sutra.md) — ref: 2.2.42-45
### [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md)
The Pāñcarātra is fully valid, spoken by the Lord; the Bhāgavatas are brahmins; the sūtras 42–43 state an objection that 44–45 answer, since the vyūhas are Brahman itself and the Pāñcarātra denies the origination of the soul.
- Yāmuna's Āgamaprāmāṇya
- Rāmānuja's Śrībhāṣya
- Vedānta Deśika's Pāñcarātrarakṣā
**Texts:** 
  - [Āgamaprāmāṇya](../texts/agamapramanya.md) — ref: siddhanta
  - [Śrībhāṣya](../texts/sribhasya.md) — ref: 2.2.42-45
  - [Pāñcarātrarakṣā](../texts/pancaratraraksa.md) — 

**Historical debate — Yāmuna's defence of the Pāñcarātra** (c. 10th–11th c.): Recorded as a treatise answering the objections point by point (Āgamaprāmāṇya). Śrīvaiṣṇavas hold the Pāñcarātra vindicated.

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the sides read the same sūtras and the same vyūha doctrine differently.
**The traditions' own objections:** Advaitins hold that the Pāñcarātra does teach the soul's origination; Śrīvaiṣṇavas hold that Śaṅkara misreports it.
**Candidate readings:** P2-standpoint: if the vyūhas are read as forms of the one Lord (Rāmānuja) rather than as the origination of souls (Śaṅkara's report), the Advaitin objection loses its object; Śaṅkara already accepts the worship portion.; P6-upaya: the tradition itself holds different scriptures suited to different devotees.
