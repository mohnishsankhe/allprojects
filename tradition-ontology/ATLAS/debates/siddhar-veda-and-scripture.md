# Do the Siddhars accept the Veda (and scripture generally) or reject it?

`dsp:siddhar-veda-and-scripture` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
Kaṭuveḷi: 'stand by the rule of the Veda; go the way the good follow'; climb to the open space of pure Vedānta. The Tiruvaḷḷuvar ñāṉam: when the Veda becomes true knowledge the Lord's grace gives the state; its good meaning is like ghee in milk (12).
- Scripture guides conduct and points to the goal.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kaṭuveḷic cittar: Āṉantak kaḷippu (the Joy-song)](../texts/kaduveli-ananda-kalippu.md) — ref: 8, 10
  - [Tiruvaḷḷuvar ñāṉam (the wisdom of Tiruvaḷḷuvar)](../texts/tiruvalluvar-nanam.md) — ref: 12
### [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
Pattirakiriyār: abiding alone means leaving all Vedānta and Veda; the four Vedas cry out but cannot see the secret. Sivavākkiyar and Pāmbāṭṭi scorn recitation and learning.
- The Vedas cannot see the supreme secret (Pattirakiriyār 48).
- Learning without inner knowledge is empty.
**Texts:** 
  - [Pattirakiriyār: Meyññāṉap pulampal (lament for true knowledge)](../texts/bhadragiriyar-meynana-pulampal.md) — ref: 32, 48
  - [Civavākkiyam (the songs of Sivavākkiyar)](../texts/sivavakkiyam.md) — ref: several
  - [Pāmpāṭṭic cittar pāṭalkaḷ (the songs of the snake-charmer Siddhar)](../texts/pambatti-padalgal.md) — ref: passim

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** P4-stage: scripture governs the seeker's conduct (Kaṭuveḷi 8 is addressed to the beginner) and is left behind in abiding (niṭṭai), as Pattirakiriyār 32 says.
Both voices are within the same stream and often the same poem moves from conduct to the open space; see also dsp:status-of-veda (U50).
**The traditions' own objections:** The most radical Siddhars (Sivavākkiyar, Pāmbāṭṭi) reject the Veda's authority outright, not merely for the advanced; this is recorded, not reconciled away.
