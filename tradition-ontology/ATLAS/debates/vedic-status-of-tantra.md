# Are the Śākta tantras Vedic and authoritative?

**Coverage:** A8

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md)
Only the five Śubhāgamas, which teach the Vedic Samaya way, are fully authoritative for the twice-born; the sixty-four tantras aim at worldly powers, deceive even the learned, are far from the Vedic path and meant for others; the eight Candrakalā tantras are mixed.
**Texts:** 
  - [Lakṣmīdharā (commentary on the Saundaryalaharī)](../texts/laksmidhara.md) — ref: on SL 31
### [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md)
Śrīvidyā is itself taught in śruti — the Tripurā, Bhāvanā, Kaula and Bahvṛca Upaniṣads — and the Gāyatrī is the Śrīvidyā; the Kalpasūtra calls itself a great Upaniṣad whose study makes one the performer of all sacrifices; the vidyā is hidden within all schools while the Veda is public. Bhāskararāya defends this with Mīmāṃsā reasoning.
**Texts:** 
  - [Paraśurāma Kalpasūtra](../texts/parasurama-kalpasutra.md) — ref: 1.30; 10.83
  - [Varivasyārahasya](../texts/varivasya-rahasya.md) — ref: 57-59
  - [Tripurātāpinī Upaniṣad](../texts/tripuratapani-upanisad.md) — ref: 1
### [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md)
Texts not accepted by those who know the three Vedas — including the Pāśupata and Pāñcarātra āgamas — have no authority as sources of dharma.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tantravārttika](../texts/tantravarttika.md) — ref: 1.3.4

**The traditions' own objections:** Mīmāṃsakas deny any non-Vedic source of dharma; Lakṣmīdhara denies the sixty-four tantras for the twice-born; Kaula Śrīvidyā denies that its tantras are outside the Veda.
