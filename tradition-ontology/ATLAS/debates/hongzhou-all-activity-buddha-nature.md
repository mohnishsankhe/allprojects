# Is all activity the function of buddha-nature (Hongzhou), or must numinous knowing be recognised and cultivated (Zongmi)?

`dsp:hongzhou-all-activity-buddha-nature` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Hongzhou school](../lineages/hongzhou.md)
The Way needs no cultivation — only do not defile it; the ordinary mind is the Way; walking, standing, sitting and lying, responding to things — all is the Way; this very mind is buddha.
- All dharmas are mind-dharmas; the whole of activity is the wondrous function.
**Texts:** 
  - [Record of the Transmission of the Lamp compiled in the Jingde era (Jingde chuandeng lu)](../texts/jingde-chuandeng-lu.md) — ref: juan 28 (Mazu)
### [Heze school](../lineages/heze.md)
Hongzhou equates every movement — greed and anger as much as compassion — with buddha-nature and so neglects the essential 'empty and calm knowing' and the need for gradual cultivation after awakening.
- The one word 'knowing' is the gate of all wonders; function must be distinguished from essence.
**Texts:** 
  - [General Preface to the Collection of Expressions of the Chan Source (Chanyuan zhuquanji duxu)](../texts/chanyuan-zhuquanji-duxu.md) — 
  - [Chart of the Master-Disciple Succession of the Chan Gate that Transmits the Mind-Ground in China](../texts/chanmen-shizi-chengxi-tu.md) — 
### [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
Nanyang Huizhong criticises those who take seeing, hearing and knowing as buddha-nature and the body as its perishing shell.
**Texts:** 
  - [Record of the Transmission of the Lamp compiled in the Jingde era (Jingde chuandeng lu)](../texts/jingde-chuandeng-lu.md) — ref: juan 28

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint, P4-stage
**Explanation:** Mazu speaks from the standpoint of the realized ('no cultivation, only no defilement'), Zongmi from the seeker who has awakened only in understanding and must still cultivate.
Jinul adopts Zongmi's framework while honouring Hongzhou masters as awakened.
**The traditions' own objections:** Zongmi explicitly judged the Hongzhou view deficient; the Linji tradition descending from Mazu rejected Zongmi's classification.
