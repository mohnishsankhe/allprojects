# Is the fourth Mahāpurāṇa the Śiva Purāṇa or the Vāyu Purāṇa?

`dsp:siva-or-vayu-mahapurana` · `sourced` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
The Śaiva (Śiva) Purāṇa is the fourth Mahāpurāṇa.
- VP 3.6.21 and BhP 12.7.23 name 'śaiva'
- BhP 12.13.4 gives the Śaiva 24,000 verses
**Texts:** 
  - [Viṣṇu Purāṇa](../texts/visnu-purana.md) — ref: 3.6.21
  - [Bhāgavata Purāṇa](../texts/bhagavata-purana.md) — ref: 12.7.23
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
The Vāyavīya (Vāyu) Purāṇa is the fourth Mahāpurāṇa.
- Matsya 53.18 describes the Vāyavīya of 24,000 verses with Rudra's glory
- the Nārada's index (1.95) and the Devī Bhāgavata's list count the Vāyu
**Texts:** 
  - [Matsya Purāṇa](../texts/matsya-purana.md) — ref: 53.18
  - [Nārada Purāṇa](../texts/narada-purana.md) — ref: 1.95
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
Both are named, the 'Vāyavīya' being identified with the eighteenth, the Brahmāṇḍa.
- KūP 1.1.13-15 lists Śaiva and then 'Vāyavīya ... named Brahmāṇḍa' as eighteenth
**Texts:** 
  - [Kūrma Purāṇa](../texts/kurma-purana.md) — ref: 1.1.13-15

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the lists themselves differ and none of the eight principles decides which text is meant.
**Candidate readings:** The Matsya's 'Vāyavīya of 24,000 verses with Rudra's glory' and the lists' 'Śaiva of 24,000' name one Śaiva Purāṇa proclaimed by Vāyu (the Śiva Purāṇa's own Vāyavīya Saṃhitā supports a link).; The Kūrma's identification of Vāyavīya with Brahmāṇḍa suggests the two once formed one Vāyu-proclaimed text, leaving the Śiva as fourth.; Sectarian preference (Śaiva vs other) shaped which title each list preserved.

_Notes: Scholarly note (metadata only): most modern scholars treat the Vāyu as the older Mahāpurāṇa._
