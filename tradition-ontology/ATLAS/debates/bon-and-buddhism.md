# What is Bön's relation to Buddhism: an independent older revelation, or a Tibetan religion that took over Buddhist doctrine?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md)
Bön is the teaching of Tönpa Shenrab, a fully enlightened teacher of Ölmo Lungring in Tagzig, far older than Śākyamuni; it reached Tibet through Zhang Zhung, was persecuted and hidden, and recovered as treasure; its parallels with Buddhism reflect one truth or Buddhist borrowing from Bön.
- The biographies (Dodü, Zermig, Zijid) and the histories (Shardza's Treasury of Good Sayings).
- An oral Dzogchen lineage (Zhang Zhung Nyengyü) never interrupted.

### [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md)
Buddhist historians (e.g. Thuken's Crystal Mirror of Tenets, early 19th c.) describe Bön as a pre-Buddhist religion of rites and spirits that later 'transformed' itself by adopting Buddhist scriptures; Buddhist accounts of Samye report Bön priests defeated and exiled.
- Three phases: revealed, deviant and transformed Bön.
- Parallel scriptures and terminology are taken as evidence of copying.
**Texts:** 
  - [Bashe (sba bzhed / dba' bzhed, the Testament of Ba)](../texts/bashe.md) —
