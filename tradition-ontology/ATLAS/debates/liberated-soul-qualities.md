# At liberation, are the Lord's qualities transferred to, possessed by or produced in the soul - or is the soul's own Śiva-nature manifested?

`dsp:liberated-soul-qualities` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
The liberated gains the Lord's qualities: powers of knowing and acting, the marks of perfection, union with Rudra ('attainment of the qualities' as the gain of the fifth stage).
- PS 1.21-38; PABh on PS 5.29 ('guṇāvāpti'); SDS ch. 6.
**Texts:** 
  - [Pāśupata Sūtra](../texts/pasupata-sutra.md) — ref: 1.21-38
  - [Pañcārthabhāṣya](../texts/pancarthabhasya.md) — ref: on PS 5.29
  - [Sarvadarśanasaṃgraha](../texts/sarvadarsanasangraha.md) — ref: ch. 6
### [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
Liberation is the manifestation of the soul's own Śiva-nature, not the transfer, possession or production of qualities; it comes through initiation, the Lord's act.
- Rāmakaṇṭha, Kiraṇavṛtti ch. 1.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kiraṇavṛtti](../texts/kiranavrtti.md) — ref: ch. 1

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the Siddhānta explicitly rejects accounts in which the Lord's qualities come to the soul from outside.
**The traditions' own objections:** Rāmakaṇṭha denies saṅkrānti, āveśa and samutpatti by name; which rival schools held them is not stated in the passage.
**Candidate readings:** P2-standpoint: described from the Lord's giving (Pāśupata) or from the soul's latent nature (Siddhānta).; No reconciliation: the Siddhānta's rejection is explicit.
