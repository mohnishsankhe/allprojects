# Was Mahāvīra's embryo transferred from Devānandā to Triśalā?

`dsp:mahavira-embryo-transfer` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
Yes: at Śakra's command Hariṇaigameṣin moved the embryo from the brāhmaṇī Devānandā to the kṣatriyāṇī Triśalā, since Arhats are born in kṣatriya families; the brāhmaṇa conception was the fruit of the pride Mahāvīra had felt as Marīci.
- Kalpa Sūtra, Ācārāṅga 2.15
- The Āvaśyaka-niryukti's Marīci story
- One of the ten wonders
**Texts:** 
  - [Kalpa Sūtra (Paryuṣaṇākalpa)](../texts/kalpa-sutra-jain.md) — ref: jinacaritra.mahavira
  - [Ācārāṅga Sūtra](../texts/acaranga-sutra.md) — ref: 2.15
  - [Āvaśyaka Niryukti](../texts/avasyaka-niryukti.md) — ref: marici
### [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
No: Mahāvīra was conceived by Triśalā alone; the transfer story is not accepted.
- Digambara purāṇas (Uttarapurāṇa)
