# Is there an intermediate existence (antarābhava) between death and rebirth?

`dsp:intermediate-existence` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
Yes: existence continues without interruption like the series of a grain; the gandharva at conception, the non-returner who attains nirvāṇa 'in between', and the sūtra of the destinies prove it; the intermediate being has the form of its next life and feeds on smells.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 3.10–15
### [Saṃmitīya](../lineages/sammitiya.md)
Yes (Kathāvatthu commentary on Kv 8.2 names the Pubbaseliyas and Sammitiyas).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu-aṭṭhakathā](../texts/kathavatthu-atthakatha.md) — ref: on Kv 8.2
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
No: an intermediate existence is none of the three kinds of existence; rebirth follows death immediately.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 8.2
### [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md)
No (Vasumitra).
**Texts:** 
  - [Samayabhedoparacanacakra](../texts/samayabhedoparacanacakra.md) — ref: Mahāsāṃghika tenets
### [Mahīśāsaka](../lineages/mahisasaka.md)
No (Vasumitra).
**Texts:** 
  - [Samayabhedoparacanacakra](../texts/samayabhedoparacanacakra.md) — ref: Mahīśāsaka tenets
