# Is the Yoga smṛti refuted by the Vedānta ('etena yogaḥ pratyuktaḥ', Brahma Sūtra 2.1.3)?

`dsp:vedanta-on-yoga-smrti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md)
Brahma Sūtra 2.1.3 declares that Yoga is refuted by the same reasoning as Sāṃkhya; Śaṅkara explains that Yoga is rejected where it teaches an independent pradhāna and a plurality of selves contrary to śruti, while yoga as a discipline and means is accepted.
- Where smṛtis conflict with śruti on the cause of the world, śruti prevails.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahma Sūtra](../texts/brahma-sutra.md) — ref: 2.1.3
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 2.1.3
### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
Yoga holds a real prakṛti and many puruṣas (YS 2.17–25, 2.22); commentators answer that Vyāsa's Brahma Sūtras refuted the independence and reality claimed for pradhāna and a real difference of consciousness, not yoga as meditative cultivation, which the Gītā holds higher (Nārāyaṇatīrtha).
- The yoga that is refuted is doctrinal, not practical.
- Vijñānabhikṣu likewise harmonizes Yoga with Vedānta.
**Texts:** 
  - [Yoga Sūtra of Patañjali](../texts/yoga-sutra.md) — ref: 2.17-25
  - [Yogasiddhāntacandrikā](../texts/yogasiddhantacandrika.md) — ref: intro. vv. 4-6

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path
**Explanation:** Both Śaṅkara's reading of BS 2.1.3 and the late Yoga commentators accept yoga as a path of practice while leaving the metaphysical disagreement standing (P3).
Not reconciled: the reality and independence of pradhāna and the plurality of puruṣas.
**The traditions' own objections:** Classical Yoga (Vyāsa, Vācaspati in his Yoga commentary) does not concede that its dualism is refuted; Advaita does not concede that it is true.
