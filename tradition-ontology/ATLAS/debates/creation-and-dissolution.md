# Was the world created, and does it undergo total dissolution?

`dsp:creation-and-dissolution` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A5

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md)
No means of knowledge establishes a first creation or a dissolution of everything; a bodiless Prajāpati could not will, a compassionate one would create only happiness, a purposeless one would not act; creation and dissolution are to be understood 'as today'.
- ŚV sambandhākṣepaparihāra 42–68, 113.
**Texts:** 
  - [Ślokavārttika](../texts/slokavarttika.md) — ref: sambandhākṣepaparihāra 42–116
### [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md)
Things arise and perish gradually; people were always born of parents; no Īśvara moves atoms by will.
**Texts:** 
  - [Prakaraṇapañcikā](../texts/prakaranapancika.md) — ref: ch. 7
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
Īśvara creates the world from atoms at the beginning of each cycle and dissolves it at the end; his existence is inferred from the world as an effect.
**Texts:** 
  - [Nyāyakusumāñjali](../texts/nyaya-kusumanjali.md) — ref: ch. 5
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
Creation and dissolution recur in cosmic cycles (kalpas) under the Lord.
**Texts:** 
  - [Viṣṇu Purāṇa](../texts/visnu-purana.md) — ref: book 1

**The traditions' own objections:** Mīmāṃsakas reject any creator; Naiyāyikas and Purāṇic authors hold cyclic creation by the Lord.
**Candidate readings:** P1-level: Advaita reads creation-talk as empirical (vyāvahārika) — Mīmāṃsā accepts no such levels.; Kumārila's own Tantravārttika speaks of Manus in each kalpa, suggesting that cyclic time is not denied as such, only a total destruction and first creation; unresolved within his works.
