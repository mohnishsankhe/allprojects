# Which part of the embryo forms first?

`dsp:which-limb-forms-first` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
The head, seat of the senses (Kumāraśiras Bharadvāja); the heart, seat of consciousness (Kāṅkāyana); the navel, where nourishment enters (Bhadrakāpya); the rectum, seat of wind (Bhadraśaunaka); hands and feet (Baḍiśa); the senses (Janaka of Videha); unknowable (Mārīci Kaśyapa).
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Śā 6.21
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
Dhanvantari, approved by Ātreya: all limbs form at once, since all things are mutually bound.
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Śā 6.21

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** Each sage names the seat of what he takes as primary; Ātreya adopts simultaneity because the parts are interdependent.
**The traditions' own objections:** The single-first views are rejected, not merged, by Ātreya.
