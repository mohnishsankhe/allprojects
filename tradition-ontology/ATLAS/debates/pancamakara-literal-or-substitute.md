# Are the five 'M's to be used literally, replaced by substitutes, interiorized, or rejected? (Summary only.)

`dsp:pancamakara-literal-or-substitute` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

**Coverage:** A8

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md)
Bliss is Brahman's form in the body and the five 'M's manifest it; they are used in secret worship; when unavailable the daily worship continues without them.
**Texts:** 
  - [Paraśurāma Kalpasūtra](../texts/parasurama-kalpasutra.md) — ref: 1.12, 1.24
### [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md)
Rejected: the Samaya way admits no Kaula rites or worship of a young woman's triangle.
**Texts:** 
  - [Lakṣmīdharā (commentary on the Saundaryalaharī)](../texts/laksmidhara.md) — ref: on SL 41, 31
### [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
The right-hand (dakṣiṇa) mode worships with substitutes (anukalpa) or with inner equivalents; the Goddess is worshipped on both right and left paths.
**Texts:** 
  - [Lalitā Sahasranāma](../texts/lalita-sahasranama.md) — ref: 169-171

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** The traditions themselves assign the modes by qualification and stage, and the Kalpasūtra's continuation of worship without the 'M's shows they are not strictly indispensable.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Samaya school rejects the practice outright, not as a stage; Kaula lineages hold the literal rite superior for the qualified. Restricted content: no procedure recorded.
