## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path
**Explanation:** The text itself reconciles them: both views are true and both, practised according to scripture, lead to the highest goal (12.289.7–8); the Gītā says those who see Sāṃkhya and Yoga as one see (5.4–5); the Nārāyaṇīya calls them limbs of one another (12.336.76).
The reconciliation concerns their soteriological value; it does not claim that they agree on the existence of a Lord.
**The traditions' own objections:** Classical Sāṃkhya (U09) denies a creator Lord and Yoga affirms a special puruṣa; on that point the schools remain distinct (see dsp:isvara).
