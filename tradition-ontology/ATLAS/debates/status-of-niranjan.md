# Is Nirañjan the stainless supreme or a lower, deceiving power (Kāl)?

`dsp:status-of-niranjan` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
Alakh Nirañjan, the stainless unseen, is the ultimate of the yogīs.
**Texts:** 
  - [Gorakh Bānī](../texts/gorakhbani.md) — 
### [Nirañjanī sampradāya](../lineages/niranjani.md)
Nirañjan is the formless stainless Lord, object of devotion and yoga.
**Texts:** 
  - [Vāṇī of Haridās Nirañjanī](../texts/haridas-niranjani-bani.md) — 
### [Dādū Panth](../lineages/dadu-panth.md)
Dādū bows first to Nirañjan in his Vāṇī.
**Texts:** 
  - [Dādū Vāṇī (Dādū Dayāl kī Bāṇī)](../texts/dadu-vani.md) — ref: opening invocation
### [Kabīr Panth](../lineages/kabir-panth.md)
Nirañjan is Kāl, the fallen son of the Sat Puruṣ, creator and jailer of the three worlds.
- he won the worlds by austerity and swallowed Ādyā
- he hides the true Lord
**Texts:** 
  - [Anurāg Sāgar](../texts/anurag-sagar.md) — 
### [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md)
Jot Nirañjan rules the first region (Sahasdal Kanwal) and belongs to Kāl's domain; those who stop there take a lower lord for the highest.
**Texts:** 
  - [Sār Bachan (Sār Bachan Nazm and Sār Bachan Nasr)](../texts/sar-bachan.md) — 

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the same name is the highest for some lineages and a lower power for others.
**The traditions' own objections:** Each side insists on its own valuation of the name.
**Candidate readings:** P4-stage: Sant Mat's own reading — those who reached only the first region named its lord supreme; rejected by Nāths and Nirañjanīs; P1-level: nirañjana as the absolute (stainless) vs Nirañjan as the ruler of the created worlds (cosmic function); rejected by the Kabīr Panth, for whom Kāl is not the Sat Puruṣ in any aspect; Homonymy: the name denotes different things in different lineages (so no single question); weakened if, as some scholars suggest, the Kabīr Panth myth deliberately answers the Nāth/Nirañjanī theology (scholarly suggestion, metadata only)
