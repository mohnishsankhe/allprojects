# Can valid cognition, the means of knowledge and the categories be coherently defined (and so difference established)?

`dsp:khandana-definability` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Coverage:** A6

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Śrīharṣa: every proposed definition fails; the Advaitin can argue on the basis of commonly accepted usage without committing to the reality of the categories; only self-luminous consciousness is beyond refutation.
**Texts:** 
  - [Khaṇḍanakhaṇḍakhādya](../texts/khandanakhandakhadya.md) — ref: 1
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
Nyāya (and later Navya-Nyāya, esp. Gaṅgeśa): definitions can be refined to meet the objections; the categories and difference are real and knowable.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tattvacintāmaṇi](../texts/tattvacintamani.md) — ref: (passim)

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
