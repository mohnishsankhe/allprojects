# Are revealed treasures (gter ma) genuine teachings of Padmasambhava and the buddhas?

`dsp:authenticity-of-terma` · `skeleton` · confidence low


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)
Sceptics (Sakya Paṇḍita among others) warned against texts 'dug from the earth' or received in dreams whose origin cannot be verified, and noted false revealers.
- Unverifiable origin opens the door to forgery.
- Some treasures were exposed as fabrications.
### [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)
Treasures are concealed by Padmasambhava, sealed in the revealer's mind by aspiration and entrusted to ḍākinīs; genuine revealers are prophesied and authenticated, and the tradition itself distinguishes true from false treasures.
- The six transmissions ensure a continuous lineage.
- Treasures meet the needs of later times, as intended.
- Their fruit — realization in practitioners — authenticates them.
**Texts:** 
  - [Terjung Chenmo (gter 'byung chen mo, the Great History of Treasures)](../texts/terjung-chenmo-chowang.md) — 
  - [The Nyingma History of Dudjom Rinpoche (bstan pa'i rnam gzhag and chos 'byung)](../texts/dudjom-chojung.md) — ref: terma
