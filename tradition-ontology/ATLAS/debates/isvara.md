# Is there an Īśvara — a Lord who makes the world and dispenses the fruits of action? Is he proved by reason, known only from scripture, a special puruṣa for meditation, or not to be accepted at all?

`dsp:isvara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G10

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
There is an all-knowing, imperishable maker (Īśvara): the world is an effect, like a pot, so it has an intelligent maker; the combination of atoms, the support of the world, the arts and the Veda's authority require him; he brings about the fruit of human effort according to karma (Udayana answers five objections in five clusters).
**Texts:** 
  - [Nyāyakusumāñjali](../texts/nyaya-kusumanjali.md) — ref: stabaka 1; 5.1
  - [Nyāya Sūtra](../texts/nyaya-sutra.md) — ref: 4.1.19-21
  - [Nyāyabhāṣya](../texts/nyaya-bhasya.md) — ref: 4.1.21
  - [Tattvacintāmaṇi](../texts/tattvacintamani.md) — ref: īśvarānumāna
### [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)
At the end of Brahmā's life Maheśvara, wishing to give rest to beings wearied by transmigration, dissolves the world, and at his will creation begins again from the atoms according to the unseen merit of souls.
**Texts:** 
  - [Padārthadharmasaṅgraha](../texts/padarthadharmasangraha.md) — ref: sṛṣṭi-saṃhāra
### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
Īśvara is a special puruṣa untouched by afflictions, actions, their fruition and residues, in whom the seed of omniscience is unsurpassed; devotion to him (praṇidhāna) brings samādhi near, since won over by it he favours the yogin — a model and a support, not the world's maker.
**Texts:** 
  - [Yoga Sūtra of Patañjali](../texts/yoga-sutra.md) — ref: 1.23-26
  - [Yogabhāṣya (Vyāsa's commentary on the Yoga Sūtra)](../texts/yoga-bhasya.md) — ref: 1.23-25
### [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
Īśvara is not established: whether free or bound he could not create; unconscious prakṛti acts for puruṣa's release as unconscious milk flows for the calf; the fruit of action comes from karma without a governor.
**Texts:** 
  - [Sāṃkhya Sūtra](../texts/samkhya-sutra.md) — ref: 1.92-99; 5.2-12
  - [Sāṃkhya Kārikā](../texts/samkhya-karika.md) — ref: 57
  - [Sāṃkhyakārikābhāṣya of Gauḍapāda](../texts/samkhya-karika-bhasya-gaudapada.md) — ref: 61
### [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
There is no creator: no time without action is known; with no beings yet there would be none to pity, and a compassionate creator would make only happy beings; the Veda needs no author; in the rite the sacrifice, not the deity, is principal, and dharma itself yields the fruit.
**Texts:** 
  - [Ślokavārttika](../texts/slokavarttika.md) — ref: sambandhākṣepaparihāra 42-55
  - [Mīmāṃsā Sūtra](../texts/mimamsa-sutra.md) — ref: 9.1.6-9
  - [Prakaraṇapañcikā](../texts/prakaranapancika.md) — ref: ch. 7
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Brahman as Lord is the cause of the world's origin, maintenance and dissolution, known from scripture alone — the Veda is breathed out by him; he is not partial or cruel, since like rain he gives according to the seeds of beings' karma; the fruit of works comes from him. (Īśvara belongs to Brahman as related to adjuncts.)
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahma Sūtra](../texts/brahma-sutra.md) — ref: 1.1.2-3; 2.1.34; 3.2.38-41
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 1.1.3; 2.1.34
### [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
Inference cannot prove a single omniscient maker of the world; Brahman, the Lord with infinite auspicious qualities, qualified by souls and matter, is known only from scripture (śāstrayonitvāt).
**Texts:** 
  - [Śrībhāṣya](../texts/sribhasya.md) — ref: 1.1.3
  - [Siddhitraya](../texts/siddhitraya.md) — ref: Īśvarasiddhi
  - [Tattvamuktākalāpa](../texts/tattvamuktakalapa.md) — ref: Nāyaka section
### [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
Viṣṇu alone is the independent reality from whom come creation, maintenance, dissolution, control, knowledge, ignorance, bondage and release.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya (Madhva)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-madhva.md) — ref: 1.1.2
  - [Tattvasaṅkhyāna](../texts/tattvasankhyana.md) — ref: 10-11
### [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
The world undergoes origination, maintenance and dissolution and so has an agent; unconscious māyā and karma cannot organize themselves to give each soul its due, so an omniscient Lord is required.
**Texts:** 
  - [Civañāṉa Pōtam](../texts/sivananabodham.md) — ref: sūtra 1
  - [Nareśvaraparīkṣā](../texts/naresvarapariksa.md) — ref: ch. 3
### [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
There is no permanent means of knowledge or permanent cause: what is permanent cannot produce effects successively; 'being an effect' does not prove a single intelligent maker of mountains and sprouts; the Blessed One is a means of knowledge by having become so, not as an eternal lord (Dharmakīrti, Śāntarakṣita, Ratnakīrti). Belief in Īśvara as cause arises from the error of a permanent self (Vasubandhu).
**Texts:** 
  - [Pramāṇavārttika](../texts/pramanavarttika.md) — ref: 1.9-10 (numbering as recorded)
  - [Tattvasaṅgraha of Śāntarakṣita](../texts/tattvasangraha.md) — ref: Īśvaraparīkṣā
  - [Īśvarasādhanadūṣaṇa](../texts/isvarasadhanadusana.md) — ref: whole
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 2.64
  - [Bodhicaryāvatāra](../texts/bodhicaryavatara.md) — ref: 9.119-126
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
There is no god, no Brahmā, who makes this round of rebirths; only bare dhammas occur through the conjunction of conditions; the wheel of becoming is without a maker.
**Texts:** 
  - [Visuddhimagga](../texts/visuddhimagga.md) — ref: chs. 17, 19
### [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
The maker-god of the Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika cannot be: an unembodied maker cannot act, an embodied one would be limited; if he made the world, where was he before and what was his material? — obstinate fancies. Haribhadra: the doctrine that Īśvara is the maker can be accepted in a true sense — the soul that attains the highest perfection is 'Īśvara', and every soul is the 'maker' of its own condition through its karma; a world-creating Lord is refuted.
**Texts:** 
  - [Āptaparīkṣā](../texts/aptapariksa.md) — ref: Īśvara-parīkṣā
  - [Anyayogavyavacchedikā](../texts/anyayogavyavacchedika.md) — ref: 6
  - [Ādipurāṇa of Jinasena](../texts/adipurana-jinasena.md) — ref: ch. 4
  - [Śāstravārtāsamuccaya](../texts/sastravartasamuccaya.md) — ref: stabaka 3
### [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
Some sages say inherent nature is the cause, others time — they are deluded; it is the greatness of God in the world; 'we have found him, the supreme great Lord of lords'.
**Texts:** 
  - [Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad](../texts/svetasvatara-upanisad.md) — ref: 6.1-9
### [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md)
(As reported) The only lord is the king known to the world; variety comes from own-nature.
- no unseen cause
**Texts:** 
  - [Sarvadarśanasaṃgraha](../texts/sarvadarsanasangraha.md) — ref: ch. 1
### [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
Those who say the world was made by gods, Brahmā, Īśvara, pradhāna or the self-born do not know the truth; the world never perishes.
- Sūtr 1.1.3.5–10
**Texts:** 
  - [Sūtrakṛtāṅga](../texts/sutrakrtanga.md) — ref: 1.1.3.5-10
### [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md)
If Brahmā were lord of the world, why did he make it unhappy and unjust? Creation by God is a tenet that ends in inaction.
- Ja 543; AN 3.61
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhūridatta Jātaka](../texts/bhuridatta-jataka.md) — ref: 158-160
  - [Titthāyatana Sutta](../texts/titthayatana-sutta.md) — ref: AN 3.61

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika, Vedānta and Śaiva Siddhānta affirm a Lord (by inference or by scripture), Yoga admits a special puruṣa for devotion but not as maker, and Sāṃkhya, Mīmāṃsā, the Buddhists and the Jains reject a creator.
A partial agreement cutting across the sides: Mīmāṃsā, Vedānta (BS 1.1.3; Śrībhāṣya 1.1.3) and the Buddhists concur that inference cannot establish a world-maker — for opposite ends. Haribhadra offers a Jain reinterpretation (the perfected soul as 'Īśvara').
**The traditions' own objections:** Nyāya holds the inference sound (Kusumāñjali 5.1); the Buddhists, Jains, Mīmāṃsakas and Sāṃkhyas each give explicit refutations; Yoga's Īśvara is rejected by the non-theistic Sāṃkhya (see dsp:yoga-samkhya-isvara); Advaita places the Lord at the level of Brahman with adjuncts, which the theistic schools reject.
