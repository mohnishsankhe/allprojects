# Is Īśvara real, needed, or a special puruṣa?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md)
(As reported) The only lord is the king known to the world; variety comes from own-nature.
- no unseen cause
**Texts:** 
  - [Sarvadarśanasaṃgraha](../texts/sarvadarsanasangraha.md) — ref: ch. 1
### [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
Those who say the world was made by gods, Brahmā, Īśvara, pradhāna or the self-born do not know the truth; the world never perishes.
- Sūtr 1.1.3.5–10
**Texts:** 
  - [Sūtrakṛtāṅga](../texts/sutrakrtanga.md) — ref: 1.1.3.5-10
### [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md)
If Brahmā were lord of the world, why did he make it unhappy and unjust? Creation by God is a tenet that ends in inaction.
- Ja 543; AN 3.61
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhūridatta Jātaka](../texts/bhuridatta-jataka.md) — ref: 158-160
