# What is the 'luminous mind' — the life-continuum, or the knowing heart known in meditation?

`dsp:luminous-citta-reading` · `skeleton` · confidence low · _recent (post-1800)_


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
The luminous mind is the life-continuum consciousness, luminous because pure of defilements, which are adventitious.
**Texts:** 
  - [Manorathapūraṇī](../texts/manorathapurani.md) — ref: an1.49
### [Thai Forest tradition (Kammaṭṭhāna forest tradition)](../lineages/thai-forest.md)
The luminous citta is the knowing heart met in deep practice; its radiance itself can harbour ignorance and must be transcended (Ajahn Maha Boowa).
**Texts:** 
  - [Arahattamagga, Arahattaphala: The Path to Arahantship](../texts/arahattamagga-arahattaphala.md) — ref: passim
  - [Muttodaya (A Heart Released)](../texts/muttodaya.md) — ref: citta
