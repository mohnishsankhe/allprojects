# What are the qualities and actions of vāyu (and of fire and soma in the body)?

`dsp:nature-of-vayu` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
Kuśa Sāṅkṛtyāyana: vāyu has six qualities — dry, light, cold, rough, coarse, clear.
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Sū 12.4
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
Kumāraśiras Bharadvāja and Kāṅkāyana: it is provoked by what shares its qualities and pacified by the opposite; Baḍiśa: how the formless is provoked through the body's state.
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Sū 12.5-7
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
Vāyorvida: vāyu upholds body and world and is 'the lord' himself.
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Sū 12.8-10
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
Marīci: fire within pitta does good and ill; Kāpya: soma within kapha does good and ill.
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Sū 12.11-12
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
Ātreya: all spoke rightly except for one-sidedness; vāta, pitta and kapha together, normal or disturbed, make or unmake the person.
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Sū 12.13
