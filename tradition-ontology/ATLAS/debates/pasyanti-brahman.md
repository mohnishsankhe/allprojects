# Is Speech at the level of paśyantī (the grammarians' Word-Brahman) the ultimate reality?

`dsp:pasyanti-brahman` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md)
The beginningless and endless Brahman is the principle of the Word (śabdatattva), from which the world unfolds; paśyantī, the undivided 'seeing' speech, is the highest level of speech (Bhartṛhari).
**Texts:** 
  - [Vākyapadīya](../texts/vakyapadiya.md) — ref: 1.1
### [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md)
What the grammarians call paśyantī is only the knowledge-power at the level of Sadāśiva (Śivadṛṣṭi 2.1); the ultimate is Śiva with will, knowledge and action; speech as reflexive awareness is 'supreme speech' (parā vāk) beyond paśyantī (ĪPK 1.5.13).
- paśyantī is sequential and object-related, hence limited
- a Word-absolute without the Lord's will and agency cannot account for the world
**Texts:** 
  - [Śivadṛṣṭi](../texts/sivadrsti.md) — ref: 2
  - [Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā](../texts/isvarapratyabhijna-karika.md) — ref: 1.5.13
### [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md)
Yes: Brahman is the beginningless Word-principle; paśyantī, undivided and with all sequence withdrawn, the inner self-light, is the supreme station of speech; all cognition is word-natured.
- all cognition is interpenetrated by the word (VP 1.123)
- without the word-nature of awareness there would be no illumination (VP 1.124)
**Texts:** 
  - [Vākyapadīya](../texts/vakyapadiya.md) — ref: 1.1; 1.123-124; 1.142
  - [Vākyapadīya-vṛtti (Svopajñavṛtti)](../texts/vakyapadiya-vrtti.md) — ref: on 1.142
### [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md)
No: paśyantī is only the power of knowledge at the level of Sadāśiva; the ultimate is Śiva with will, knowledge and action, and supreme speech (parā vāk) is reflexive awareness itself, beyond paśyantī.
- paśyantī still contains the seed of sequence and object
- a Word-absolute without agency cannot account for the world
**Texts:** 
  - [Śivadṛṣṭi](../texts/sivadrsti.md) — ref: 2
  - [Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā](../texts/isvarapratyabhijna-karika.md) — ref: 1.5.13
### [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md)
The mantra manuals, accepting the four levels, place parā above paśyantī and define śabda-brahman as the consciousness of all beings rather than as word or meaning.
**Texts:** 
  - [Śāradātilaka](../texts/saradatilaka.md) — ref: 1.12-14
  - [Vaiyākaraṇa-siddhānta-laghumañjūṣā of Nāgeśa](../texts/laghumanjusa.md) — ref: sphoṭa
