## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md)
Restrictive: lists of the ineligible (the impotent, fallen, deformed, heretics, the unlearned and others); the ineligible may renounce only when at the point of death.

- Sannyāsa 2.1–5

### [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md)
Open: anyone dispassionate — with or without vows or fires — may renounce; eligibility is having found desires like vomit.
- Jābāla 4
- Maitreya 2.18–20
