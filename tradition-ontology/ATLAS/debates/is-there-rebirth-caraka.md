# Is there rebirth (and another world)?

`dsp:is-there-rebirth-caraka` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md)
The deniers (nāstika) who hold perception alone valid; others who make the parents, nature, creation by another or chance the cause of birth.
- rebirth is imperceptible
- birth is from the parents
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Sū 11.6, 11.15
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
Caraka: rebirth is established by all four means — scripture of the sages of divine sight, perception (inequalities of birth, untaught infant behaviour, memory of former births), inference from karma (daiva), and yukti (no sprout without seed); denial is the worst of sins.
- perception is limited: eight causes of non-perception
- the self is partless and not transmitted
- the six dhātus' union is caused by karma
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Sū 11.7-33
