# Should one aspire to Maitreya's Tuṣita or to Amitābha's Sukhāvatī?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### `lin:faxiang`
(Aspirants to Tuṣita, as reported) Maitreya is the next buddha; being born in Tuṣita one hears him and descends with him; the ten goods suffice.
**Texts:** 
  - [Ten Doubts about the Pure Land (Jingtu shiyi lun)](../texts/jingtu-shiyi-lun.md) — ref: seventh doubt
### [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)
Tuṣita is in the desire realm, a place of retrogression and sensual attachment, without a vow to receive beings; in Sukhāvatī all are non-retrogressive and the vow and light embrace reciters.
**Texts:** 
  - [Collection on the Land of Peace and Bliss (Anle ji)](../texts/anle-ji.md) — ref: 9b22

**Historical debate — Tuṣita vs Sukhāvatī comparisons** (7th c.): Anle ji; Jingtu shiyi lun; Xifang yaojue. Pure Land: Sukhāvatī superior.

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path
**Explanation:** Both are Mahāyāna aspirations to be near a buddha; the Pure Land texts rank them by ease and non-retrogression rather than denying Tuṣita's value.
**The traditions' own objections:** Daochuo and the Shiyi lun insist that the difference is 'great in substance', not merely of temperament.

_Notes: Xuanzang and Kuiji personally aspired to Tuṣita (recalled); the Xifang yaojue attributed to Kuiji nonetheless favours the West._
