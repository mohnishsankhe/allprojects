# Is the Buddha's bodily existence supramundane, or human and impure (sāsrava)?

`dsp:nature-of-the-buddha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Lokottaravāda](../lineages/lokottaravada.md)
Supramundane: the Buddha's conduct, merit, postures, body, robes and food are beyond the world; his human acts are only conformity to the world.
**Texts:** 
  - [Mahāvastu](../texts/mahavastu.md) — ref: i.158–169
  - [Lokānuvartana-sūtra](../texts/lokanuvartana-sutra.md) — ref: whole
### [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md)
All Tathāgatas are supramundane and without outflows; body, power and life are limitless (Vasumitra).
**Texts:** 
  - [Samayabhedoparacanacakra](../texts/samayabhedoparacanacakra.md) — ref: Mahāsāṃghika tenets
### [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
The Buddha's physical body is the result of former karma and can be the object of others' defilements, hence with outflows; one takes refuge not in it but in the non-learner's dharmas; Buddhas differ in lifespan and stature.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 4.32
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 7.34
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
The Buddha lived in the human world; it is wrong to deny it (against the Vetulyakas per the commentary).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 18.1

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P1-level, P6-upaya
**Explanation:** Both sides place what is truly 'Buddha' in the pure qualities (dharmakāya / the supramundane) and treat his worldly appearance as conventional — the Lokottaravāda explicitly as skilful conformity (lokānuvartanā).
Whether the physical body itself is supramundane or sāsrava remains not yet reconciled.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Lokottaravāda insists the body and bodily acts are themselves supramundane; the Theravāda and Sarvāstivāda insist the Buddha was born, lived and died as a human.
