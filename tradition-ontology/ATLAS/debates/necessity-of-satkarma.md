## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** The HYP itself makes the acts conditional on the student's constitution (adhikāra by doṣa), which accommodates both the 'all' and 'none' positions.
The number of acts (six or eight) is a matter of enumeration of variants, not doctrine.
**The traditions' own objections:** The 'some teachers' of HYP 2.37 reject the acts outright; the reconciliation records that they would not accept them even conditionally.
