**Status:** reconciled

**Explanation:** Ātreya's own resolution: each speaker names one real factor one-sidedly; the person and disease arise from the same set of factors, in excellence or in impairment.
The text itself stops the debate (Sū 25.26-29) rather than choosing a winner; the partial views are kept as aspects of a many-factored causation.
**The traditions' own objections:** None recorded within Āyurveda; each of the single-cause views, pressed as exclusive, is what Ātreya rejects ('by taking sides').
