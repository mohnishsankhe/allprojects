# Can those who have committed the five grave offences or slandered the Dharma be born in the Pure Land?

`dsp:can-grave-offenders-be-born` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)
The eighteenth vow excludes those who commit the five grave offences and slander the true Dharma (the Sanskrit likewise).
**Texts:** 
  - [Sukhāvatīvyūha-sūtra (larger)](../texts/sukhavativyuha-larger.md) — ref: vow 18
### [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)
The Contemplation Sūtra's lowest grade: one who has committed the five offences and ten evils is born by ten recitations at death.
**Texts:** 
  - [Amitāyurdhyāna-sūtra (Guan wuliangshou jing)](../texts/amitayurdhyana-sutra.md) — ref: 16
### [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)
(Tanluan) One guilty of both offences and slander cannot be born; one who committed the offences without slandering can, since slander rejects the very Dharma.
**Texts:** 
  - [Commentary on the Rebirth Treatise (Wangsheng lun zhu)](../texts/wangsheng-lun-zhu.md) — ref: fasc. 1
### [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)
(Shandao) The vow's exclusion is a gate of restraint to deter; once committed, these too are embraced.
**Texts:** 
  - [Commentary on the Contemplation Sūtra (Guanjing shu)](../texts/guanjing-shu.md) — ref: fasc. 4
### [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md)
(Shinran) Even the 'difficult to cure' — slanderers, grave offenders, icchantikas — are healed, as Ajātaśatru was.
**Texts:** 
  - [The True Teaching, Practice and Realization of the Pure Land Way (Kyōgyōshinshō)](../texts/kyogyoshinsho.md) — ref: Shin chapter

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** reconciled
**Principles:** P6-upaya
**Explanation:** Shandao reads the exclusion as a skilful deterrent (gate of restraint) and the lower-grade birth as the gate of embrace — the tradition's own reconciliation by skilful means.
Tanluan's earlier solution (distinguishing slander) is retained as a reading of degree.
**The traditions' own objections:** Tanluan's position that slanderers cannot be born is not the same as Shandao's; the two readings are recorded side by side.
