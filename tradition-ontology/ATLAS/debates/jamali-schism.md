# Is an action 'done' while it is being done?

`dsp:jamali-schism` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
Mahāvīra: what is being done is (in a respect) done, what is moving has moved — each moment of the process accomplishes its part.
- Bhagavatī 1.1: 'calamāṇe calie'
- If nothing were accomplished in each moment, nothing would be accomplished at the end
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhagavatī Sūtra (Vyākhyāprajñapti)](../texts/bhagavati-sutra.md) — ref: 1.1
  - [Bhagavatī Sūtra (Vyākhyāprajñapti)](../texts/bhagavati-sutra.md) — ref: 9.33
### [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
Jamāli (the 'Bahurata' schism): what is being done is not yet done; only the completed act is done — as the bed being spread was not yet spread.
- Direct experience of the unfinished bed
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhagavatī Sūtra (Vyākhyāprajñapti)](../texts/bhagavati-sutra.md) — ref: 9.33
  - [Sthānāṅga Sūtra](../texts/sthananga-sutra.md) — ref: 7

**Historical debate — Jamāli's secession** (in Mahāvīra's lifetime (tradition)): Bhagavatī 9.33; Āvaśyaka commentaries. Jamāli left with followers; Priyadarśanā returned to Mahāvīra.

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** Later Jain commentators explain the dispute by standpoints: Mahāvīra's statement holds from the momentary (ṛjusūtra-type) standpoint, Jamāli's from the everyday standpoint of the whole act; Jamāli's fault was to hold his view as the only truth.
This is the tradition's own naya analysis of the first schism (moderate confidence on its formulation in the Viśeṣāvaśyaka Bhāṣya).
**The traditions' own objections:** The tradition still counts Jamāli a nihnava because he denied the Jina's word; the reconciliation does not make his secession acceptable.
