# What is the relation of the individual self to Brahman, according to the teachers named in BS 1.4.20–22?

`dsp:jiva-brahman-relation-brahma-sutra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A5

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md)
Āśmarathya: the identity statement is an indication proving the promise (by knowing one, all is known) — read by Śaṅkara as difference-and-non-difference, like sparks and fire.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahma Sūtra](../texts/brahma-sutra.md) — ref: 1.4.20
### [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md)
Auḍulomi: the self becomes Brahman when it departs in liberation; before, it is different.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahma Sūtra](../texts/brahma-sutra.md) — ref: 1.4.21
### [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md)
Kāśakṛtsna: Brahman itself abides as the individual self (accepted by Śaṅkara).
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahma Sūtra](../texts/brahma-sutra.md) — ref: 1.4.22

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
