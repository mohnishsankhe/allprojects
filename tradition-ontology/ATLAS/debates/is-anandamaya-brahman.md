# Is 'the one consisting of bliss' (ānandamaya, TU 2.5; BS 1.1.12) the supreme Brahman or a sheath?

`dsp:is-anandamaya-brahman` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A5

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Śaṅkara (in his final view, BSBh 1.1.19): it is a sheath — '-maya' means modification and it has parts; the sūtras refer to 'Brahman the tail, the support'.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 1.1.12–19
### [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md)
The Vṛttikāra (as reported by Śaṅkara), Rāmānuja and Madhva: the ānandamaya is Brahman, as the repetition of 'bliss' and the context show (BS 1.1.12).
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahma Sūtra](../texts/brahma-sutra.md) — ref: 1.1.12
