# Does the Lord relish (doṣa-bhogya) or overlook the faults of those who take refuge in him?

`dsp:dosa-bhogya` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Coverage:** A6; G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md)
He relishes them, as a cow licks the dirt from its newborn calf, out of motherly love.
- the Lord's vātsalya
- the Āḻvārs' experience
**Texts:** 
  - [Śrīvacanabhūṣaṇam](../texts/srivacana-bhusanam.md) — ref: 2
### [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md)
He overlooks them (does not see them); faults cannot be objects of relish for one opposed to all evil.
- the Lord is heya-pratyanīka
- scripture on his forgiveness
**Texts:** 
  - [Rahasyatrayasāra](../texts/rahasyatrayasara.md) — ref: Aparādhaparihāra
