# Do Brahma Sūtras 2.2.42–45 reject or uphold the Pāñcarātra?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Śaṅkara: they reject the Pāñcarātra doctrine that the jīva (Saṅkarṣaṇa) and mind originate from Vāsudeva, while accepting its worship and meditation.
