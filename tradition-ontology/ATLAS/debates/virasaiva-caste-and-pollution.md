# Does liṅga-initiation abolish caste and pollution distinctions among devotees?

`dsp:virasaiva-caste-and-pollution` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G12

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md)
Yes: among liṅga-wearers there is no caste — Basava is the son of a cobbler's house and every devotee is 'ours'; the five pollutions do not apply; the intercaste marriage of Haraḷayya's son and Madhuvarasa's daughter enacted this.
- Basava's vacanas.
- The Kalyāṇa narrative.

  - [Vacanas of Basavaṇṇa (Basavaṇṇanavara vacanagaḷu)](../texts/basavanna-vacanas.md) — ref: 'Ivanāruva…'; 'Vēdakke oregalla…'
  - [Basava Purāṇamu (Pālkuriki Sōmanātha)](../texts/basava-purana-palkuriki.md) — ref: Kalyāṇa
### [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md)
Devotion, not birth, makes the bhakta; the māheśvara drops jāti-dharmas opposed to the liṅga; at union brāhmaṇa and outcaste are alike and the realized are untouched by pollution — yet the practitioner eats with twice-born of his own jāti on the path, and the ordering of varṇa and āśrama is twofold (Śiva's and Brahmā's).
- SSM 9.4–5; 10.30–36; 14.16–20.
- SSM 9.29.
**Texts:** 
  - [Siddhāntaśikhāmaṇi](../texts/siddhantasikhamani.md) — ref: 9.4–5; 9.29; 10.30–36; 14.16–20
### [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)
Varṇa and the rules of purity bind all; marriage across varṇa in the pratiloma direction is forbidden — the objection of the orthodox in the Kalyāṇa narrative (as reported in Vīraśaiva hagiography).
- Dharmaśāstra rules of varṇa and marriage (general).
**Texts:** 
  - [Manusmṛti (Mānava Dharmaśāstra)](../texts/manusmrti.md) — ref: ch. 10 (general)

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** Within the SSM, commensality rules for practitioners (9.29) and equal vision for the realized (14.19–20) are assigned to different stages by the text itself (5.22–23); between the vacana strand, the SSM and dharmaśāstra orthodoxy the question is not yet reconciled.
**The traditions' own objections:** Basava-centred readers reject any stage-based retention of caste among devotees; dharmaśāstra orthodoxy rejects the Vīraśaiva equality claim.
