# Are precepts and practice needed once one has awakened?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Daruma school (Japan)](../lineages/daruma-shu.md)
(Reported by its opponent Eisai) There are no precepts to keep and no practice to do: the mind is buddha from the beginning.

### [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
The Platform Sūtra: 'when the mind is level, what need to keep precepts?' — yet it gives formless precepts and repentance.
**Texts:** 
  - [Platform Sūtra of the Sixth Patriarch (Liuzu tanjing)](../texts/platform-sutra.md) — ref: 6.15; 9.4

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P1-level
**Explanation:** From the ultimate standpoint there is nothing to keep; at the conventional level precepts remain the foundation — the Platform Sūtra gives both.
**The traditions' own objections:** Eisai treats the Daruma-school position as wrong view, not as a difference of level.
