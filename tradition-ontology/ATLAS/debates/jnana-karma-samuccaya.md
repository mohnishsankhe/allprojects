# Can liberating knowledge be combined with ritual action (jñāna-karma-samuccaya), or does knowledge alone liberate?

`dsp:jnana-karma-samuccaya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A6

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md)
Action enjoined by the Veda is a duty to the end and assists knowledge; liberation comes from knowledge combined with prescribed action.
- action is enjoined by the Veda as knowledge is
- scripture declares a fault in omitting it
- as a sacrifice needs its accessories, knowledge needs prescribed acts
**Texts:** 
  - [Rāma Gītā](../texts/rama-gita.md) — ref: 7.5.11-13 (stated as the objection)
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Bhāskara (Bhāskarabhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-bhaskara.md) — ref: passim (recalled)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Ignorance is the root of rebirth and only knowledge removes it; action, which depends on agent, act and instrument born of ignorance, is opposed to knowledge and cannot be combined with it; action purifies the mind beforehand and is then given up.
- knowledge destroys the whole apparatus of agency on which action depends
- 'not by action, offspring or wealth ... by renunciation' (Taittirīya Āraṇyaka)
- 'this much is immortality' (Bṛhadāraṇyaka)
**Texts:** 
  - [Rāma Gītā](../texts/rama-gita.md) — ref: 7.5.8-23
### [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md)
Bhartṛprapañca: knowledge must be combined with Vedic works throughout life; both together liberate.
- Scripture enjoins both works and knowledge for the same person.
- Brahman and world are both one and many, so both paths are real.
**Texts:** 
  - [Commentary of Bhartṛprapañca on the Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad (lost)](../texts/bhartrprapanca-bhasya.md) — ref: (lost; reported)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Maṇḍana Miśra: works performed without desire are helpful — they purify and assist the rise of knowledge — and knowledge must be made direct by contemplation; a qualified combination.
- Scripture praises sacrifice etc. as means to knowing (BĀU 4.4.22).
- Deep impressions of difference need repeated effort to remove.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasiddhi](../texts/brahmasiddhi.md) — ref: 4
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Śaṅkara and Sureśvara: knowledge alone liberates; works (without desire) only purify the mind and generate the desire to know, and are then renounced; works and knowledge cannot be combined since works presuppose agency which knowledge negates.
- Action is not opposed to ignorance; only knowledge removes it (Ātmabodha 3).
- Liberation is eternal, not produced (BSBh 1.1.4).
- Works are needed for the rise of knowledge, not for its fruit, like a horse (BS 3.4.26).
**Texts:** 
  - [Naiṣkarmyasiddhi](../texts/naiskarmyasiddhi.md) — ref: 1
  - [Gītābhāṣya of Śaṅkara](../texts/gita-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 2.11
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 3.4.26
### [Bhāmatī school (Bhāmatī-prasthāna)](../lineages/bhamati.md)
Bhāmatī: sacrifice etc. produce the desire to know (vividiṣā), not knowledge itself.
- BĀU 4.4.22 says 'they desire to know' (vividiṣanti).
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhāmatī](../texts/bhamati.md) — ref: vividiṣā
### [Vivaraṇa school (Vivaraṇa-prasthāna)](../lineages/vivarana.md)
Vivaraṇa: works, through purification, contribute to knowledge itself (vedana), not merely to the desire.
- The desire is only instrumental to the knowledge it aims at.
**Texts:** 
  - [Pañcapādikāvivaraṇa](../texts/pancapadika-vivarana.md) — ref: (sādhana)
### [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md)
Bhartṛprapañca and Bhāskara: knowledge must be combined with lifelong performance of prescribed works; works are not dropped after knowledge.
- the two Mīmāṃsās are one teaching
- scripture enjoins works for the knower too
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Bhāskara (Bhāskarabhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-bhaskara.md) — ref: 1.1.1
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Śaṅkara and Sureśvara: knowledge alone liberates; works purify the mind beforehand but cannot be combined with knowledge, whose object is the actionless self.
- knowledge and action are opposed like light and darkness
**Texts:** 
  - [Naiṣkarmyasiddhi](../texts/naiskarmyasiddhi.md) — 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 1.1.4
### [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)
Gauḍīya: neither works nor knowledge but pure devotion is the means; works and the pursuit of impersonal knowledge 'cover' devotion (BRS 1.1.11); prescribed duties may be abandoned by one devoted to Kṛṣṇa.
- BhP 10.14.3: those who give up effort for knowledge conquer him by hearing
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhaktirasāmṛtasindhu](../texts/bhaktirasamrtasindhu.md) — ref: 1.1.11
  - [Caitanya Caritāmṛta](../texts/caitanya-caritamrta.md) — ref: 2.8

**Historical debate — The debate of Śaṅkara and Maṇḍana Miśra at Māhiṣmatī** (tradition: 8th c. (Śaṅkara's lifetime)): Śaṅkaradigvijaya cantos 8–10 (the tradition's account): judged by Ubhaya Bhāratī; Maṇḍana defeated and became Sureśvara. Advaita: Śaṅkara victorious; scholarly: the event is not historically attested and the identity of Maṇḍana and Sureśvara is doubted.

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** The Rāma Gītā itself assigns action to the stage before knowledge (purification) and renunciation to the stage of knowledge.
Read by stage (P4) both sides agree that action has a place; they still disagree whether it remains necessary after knowledge, which is not reconciled.
**The traditions' own objections:** Advaita denies samuccaya at the stage of knowledge; Bhāskara's school holds combination to the end.
**Candidate readings:** P4-stage: works belong to the beginner's stage (mind purification), knowledge to the qualified (Śaṅkara's own framework).; P3-path: works and knowledge as paths for different temperaments (Īśa 1–2 as read by Śaṅkara).; Bhāmatī–Vivaraṇa difference (desire vs knowledge) as a difference of emphasis within the preparatory stage.
