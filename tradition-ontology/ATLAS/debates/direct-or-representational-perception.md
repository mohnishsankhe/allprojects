# Does sense perception grasp the external object directly, or only its form (ākāra) in cognition?

`dsp:direct-or-representational-perception` · `skeleton` · confidence low


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vaibhāṣika](../lineages/vaibhasika.md)
Directly: sense consciousness grasps a present, really existing object, without an image; consciousness always needs an existent object (the argument for three-time existence).
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 5.25
### [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md)
Through its form: cognition arises bearing the object's form; the external object, already past when cognition arises, is inferred (as summarized in later doxography; low confidence for the Indian sources).
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośabhāṣya](../texts/abhidharmakosabhasya.md) — ref: 1.42 and 9 (general)
