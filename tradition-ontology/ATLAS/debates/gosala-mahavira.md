# Was Gosāla Mahāvīra's disciple and a false Jina, or an independent Jina in his seventh reanimation?

`dsp:gosala-mahavira` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
Gosāla was Maṅkhali's son and Mahāvīra's disciple for six years; he falsely claimed to be a Jina, burnt two monks, was undone by his own fiery power, and confessed on his deathbed.
- Bhagavatī 15
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhagavatī Sūtra (Vyākhyāprajñapti)](../texts/bhagavati-sutra.md) — ref: 15
### [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md)
(Ājīvika claim, known only from the Jain report) The disciple Gosāla is dead; the one speaking is Udāyi Kuṇḍiyāyaṇa in his seventh reanimation, a Jina and the last tīrthaṅkara of the age; Mahāvīra has changed from a solitary ascetic to a teacher of crowds.
- Bhagavatī 15
- Sūtrakṛtāṅga 2.6
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhagavatī Sūtra (Vyākhyāprajñapti)](../texts/bhagavati-sutra.md) — ref: 15
