# Which 'Bhāgavata' is the Mahāpurāṇa of the lists - the Śrīmad (Viṣṇu) Bhāgavata or the Devī Bhāgavata?

`dsp:which-bhagavata-is-the-mahapurana` · `sourced` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
The Śrīmad Bhāgavata is the Mahāpurāṇa: it begins with the Gāyatrī-marked verse, contains Vṛtra's slaying and has 18,000 verses (Matsya 53.20-22), calls itself the essence of all Vedānta and the fruit of the Veda-tree.
- the Matsya definition fits it
- its own verse count (12.13.9)
- its opening 'dhīmahi' evokes the Gāyatrī
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhāgavata Purāṇa](../texts/bhagavata-purana.md) — ref: 1.1.1; 12.13.9-15
  - [Matsya Purāṇa](../texts/matsya-purana.md) — ref: 53.20-22
### [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md)
The Devī Bhāgavata is the Mahāpurāṇa 'Bhāgavata'; the Viṣṇu Bhāgavata is an Upapurāṇa (some polemicists alleged it was composed by Vopadeva).
- it too has 18,000 verses and treats the Gāyatrī and the slaying of Vṛtra
- the Devī Bhāgavata's own list of Purāṇas
**Texts:** 
  - [Devī Bhāgavata Purāṇa](../texts/devi-bhagavata-purana.md) — ref: 1.3

**Historical debate — pamphlet controversy over the Bhāgavata's status** (c. 17th-18th c. CE): Rāmāśrama (Bhānuji Dīkṣita, son of Bhaṭṭoji Dīkṣita) wrote the Durjanamukhacapeṭikā upholding the Viṣṇu-Bhāgavata as the Mahāpurāṇa; Kāśīnātha Bhaṭṭa, a Śākta paṇḍita of Vārāṇasī, replied with the Durjanamukhamahācapeṭikā for the Devī-Bhāgavata; Mitra Miśra's Vīramitrodaya (17th c.) also supported the Viṣṇu-Bhāgavata. each side claims victory

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: both sides apply the same Matsya definition to different texts; no principle decides identity of a title.
**The traditions' own objections:** Vaiṣṇava commentators insist on the Śrīmad Bhāgavata alone; Śākta authors on the Devī Bhāgavata.
**Candidate readings:** P3-path: each is the 'Bhāgavata' for its community of worshippers (Vaiṣṇava, Śākta) - both traditions would reject this as evading the question.; The lists' 'Bhāgavata' predates both extant texts and the title was later claimed by each.; Scholarly metadata: modern scholars date both later than the lists' earliest layers.
