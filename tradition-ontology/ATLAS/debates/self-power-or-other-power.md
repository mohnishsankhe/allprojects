# Is awakening reached by one's own practice (self-power) or by the power of Amitābha's vow (other-power)?

`dsp:self-power-or-other-power` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
Awakening is to see one's own nature; the Pure Land is in the purified mind and Amitābha is one's own nature; seeking a Buddha outside is delusion (as the Pure Land texts report the objection).
- The Platform Sūtra asks where the man of the West will seek birth if he does evil.
**Texts:** 
  - [Platform Sūtra of the Sixth Patriarch (Liuzu tanjing)](../texts/platform-sutra.md) — ref: Dunhuang/Zongbao chapter on doubts
  - [Resolving Doubts about the Pure Land (Jingtu yibian)](../texts/jingtu-yibian.md) — ref: 420a11
### [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)
In an age without a buddha one cannot reach non-retrogression by self-power; the easy path is to be born by riding the power of the vow; Amitābha's vow is the dominant condition.
- Nāgārjuna's water route; the lame man on another's ship; the three vows that prove other-power.
**Texts:** 
  - [Commentary on the Rebirth Treatise (Wangsheng lun zhu)](../texts/wangsheng-lun-zhu.md) — ref: fasc. 1-2
  - [Daśabhūmikavibhāṣā (Shizhu piposha lun 十住毘婆沙論)](../texts/dasabhumika-vibhasa.md) — ref: ch. 9
### [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md)
Birth is by other-power through the nenbutsu that accords with the vow; one keeps calling all one's life.
**Texts:** 
  - [Passages on the Selection of the Nenbutsu in the Original Vow (Senchakushū)](../texts/senchakushu.md) — ref: ch. 3, 16
### [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md)
Nothing is self-power: shinjin and the nenbutsu are given; the self-power good person is not the object of the vow until he turns; self-power nenbutsu leads only to the transformed land.
**Texts:** 
  - [A Record in Lament of Divergences (Tannishō)](../texts/tannisho.md) — ref: 3, 8
  - [The True Teaching, Practice and Realization of the Pure Land Way (Kyōgyōshinshō)](../texts/kyogyoshinsho.md) — ref: Transformed Lands

**Historical debate — Ōhara dialogue** (1186): Hōnen before Tendai and Nara scholars at Shōrin-in, Ōhara (Hōnen biographies). Jōdo-shū: the scholars were moved and joined the nenbutsu.

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path, P4-stage
**Explanation:** The Pure Land tradition itself presents the holy path and the Pure Land as two gates suited to different capacities and ages (Daochuo), a reconciliation by temperament and stage of the student.
Chinese masters (Yanshou, Zhuhong) accept both gates and even join them; Hōnen sets the holy path aside for himself and his age without denying its truth.
**The traditions' own objections:** Shinshū denies that self-power can bring birth in the true land and would not accept that the two are equal paths; Chan teachers who reject seeking outside would not accept the Western land as more than expedient.
