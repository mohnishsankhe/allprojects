# May (or should) a widow follow her husband onto the pyre?

`dsp:widow-anvarohana` · `skeleton` · confidence low


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)
The widow's dharma is lifelong chastity and restraint (MDh 5.156–160); Medhātithi (on Manu) holds that following the husband in death is self-destruction, not dharma, comparable to a rite performed to harm.
- the prohibition of taking one's own life
- Manu's silence on the practice

### [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)
It is permitted as an option: chastity or ascending the pyre (Viṣṇusmṛti 25.14); the Mitākṣarā (on YS 1.86) defends it as a meritorious optional act.
- an explicit smṛti option
- the promised reward
