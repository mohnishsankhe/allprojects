# Is Śiva to be worshipped in temples and installed images (the fixed), or in the personal liṅga and the living devotee (the moving)?

`dsp:virasaiva-sthavara-jangama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
Installed liṅgas in temples are proper seats of Śiva's worship for the community (temple worship as taught in the Āgamas).
- Āgamic temple ritual (general; U08/U18 supply texts).
### [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md)
The fixed shall perish, the moving endures; the body is the temple; to feed the stone snake and drive off the living devotee is hypocrisy.
- Basava's vacanas.
**Texts:** 
  - [Vacanas of Basavaṇṇa (Basavaṇṇanavara vacanagaḷu)](../texts/basavanna-vacanas.md) — ref: 'Uḷḷavaru śivālaya…'; 'Kallanāgara kaṇḍare…'
### [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md)
Graded: palm-worship of the iṣṭaliṅga excels pedestal worship; the inner liṅga excels stone liṅgas and clinging only to the outer is delusion; yet the nirmālya of fixed liṅgas is not to be used and fixed liṅgas are to be protected.
- SSM 6.48; 6.54–55; 9.33–35; 12.9–10; 15.37–44.
**Texts:** 
  - [Siddhāntaśikhāmaṇi](../texts/siddhantasikhamani.md) — ref: 6.48; 6.54–55; 9.33–35; 12.9–10; 15.37–44

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage, P3-path
**Explanation:** Within the Vīraśaiva texts the forms of worship are graded by stage (outer liṅga for all, inner for the advanced; 6.48; 15.44); vis-à-vis Āgamic temple Śaivism the question is not yet reconciled.
**The traditions' own objections:** The vacana strand does not treat temple worship as a lower stage but rejects it; temple Śaivism does not accept the iṣṭaliṅga as replacing public worship.
