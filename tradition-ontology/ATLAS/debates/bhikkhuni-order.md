# On what terms were women ordained, and can the Theravāda bhikkhunī order be restored?

`dsp:bhikkhuni-order` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

**Coverage:** G12

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
The canonical account: the Buddha, after three refusals, granted Mahāpajāpatī's going forth on condition of the eight principles of respect, affirming that women can realise all four fruits, and predicted a shorter duration of the true Dhamma; at the First Council Ānanda was charged with having urged it. (Traditionalist position, recent:) the Theravāda bhikkhunī lineage lapsed and cannot be revived, since ordination requires an existing bhikkhunī Saṅgha of the same lineage.
- Cv 10.1; AN 8.51.
- Cv 11.1.10.
- The requirement of dual ordination (garudhamma 6).
**Texts:** 
  - [Cullavagga](../texts/cullavagga.md) — ref: Cv 10.1; 11.1.10
  - [Gotamī Sutta](../texts/gotami-sutta.md) — ref: AN 8.51
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
(Revivalist position, recent:) the order can be restored with the help of bhikkhunīs of a lineage held to descend from Saṅghamittā (the Dharmaguptaka lineage of East Asia), or by monks alone under the Buddha's allowance for monks to ordain nuns; the canon affirms women's capacity for arahantship and records a flourishing Bhikkhunī Saṅgha (Thig).
- Cv 10.1.3 and the Therīgāthā.
- Revival ordinations held from 1996 (Sarnath) and 1998 (Bodh Gayā) onward (recent).
**Texts:** 
  - [Cullavagga](../texts/cullavagga.md) — ref: Cv 10.1–2
  - [Therīgāthā](../texts/therigatha.md) — ref: Thig
