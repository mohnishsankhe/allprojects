# Are the Abhidharma treatises the word of the Buddha?

`dsp:abhidharma-as-buddhavacana` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vaibhāṣika](../lineages/vaibhasika.md)
Yes: the Buddha taught the Abhidharma scattered through his discourses; Kātyāyanīputra and others gathered it, as Dharmatrāta gathered the Udāna verses.
**Texts:** 
  - [Sphuṭārthā Abhidharmakośavyākhyā](../texts/sphutartha-abhidharmakosavyakhya.md) — ref: on AK 1.3
### [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md)
No: the treatises have known human authors; the 'Abhidharma basket' consists of particular sūtras such as the Arthaviniścaya.
**Texts:** 
  - [Sphuṭārthā Abhidharmakośavyākhyā](../texts/sphutartha-abhidharmakosavyakhya.md) — ref: on AK 1.3
