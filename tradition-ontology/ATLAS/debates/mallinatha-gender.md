# Was the nineteenth Tīrthaṅkara, Malli, a woman?

`dsp:mallinatha-gender` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Coverage:** G12

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
Yes: Mallī, daughter of King Kumbha, was a woman Tīrthaṅkara — one of the ten 'wonders' of this era — born female because of a small deceit in a past life.
- Jñātādharmakathā 1.8
- Sthānāṅga's list of the ten wonders
**Texts:** 
  - [Jñātādharmakathā](../texts/jnatadharmakatha.md) — ref: 1.8
  - [Sthānāṅga Sūtra](../texts/sthananga-sutra.md) — ref: 10
### [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
No: Mallinātha was a man, like all Tīrthaṅkaras; a woman cannot reach the stages required.
- Digambara purāṇas (Uttarapurāṇa)
- The Digambara denial of women's liberation
**Texts:** 
  - [Uttarapurāṇa of Guṇabhadra](../texts/uttarapurana-gunabhadra.md) —
