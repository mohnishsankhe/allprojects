# Is Śiva the material cause of the world, or only its efficient cause?

`dsp:is-siva-the-material-cause` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
Śiva is only the efficient cause; māyā (and bindu) is the material cause and his power the instrument; if Śiva were the material he would be insentient and changing and would share the world's impurity.
**Texts:** 
  - [Ratnatraya (of Śrīkaṇṭha)](../texts/ratnatraya-srikantha.md) — ref: whole
  - [Nareśvaraparīkṣā](../texts/naresvarapariksa.md) — ref: 3
### [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md)
The same: the world arises from māyā as its material; the Lord, one with it by pervasion, is not transformed (Civañāṉa Pōtam 1 and its commentaries).
**Texts:** 
  - [Civañāṉa Pōtam](../texts/sivananabodham.md) — ref: 1
### [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md)
Śiva manifests the world within his own consciousness as a reflection (ābhāsa), by his freedom; he is both its material and efficient cause without loss (as recorded by U19).
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Brahman is both material and efficient cause, the world being its apparent transformation (vivarta) through māyā (as recorded by U13).
