# Can one be liberated while still living in the body (jīvanmukti)?

`dsp:is-jivanmukti-possible` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A6; G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Yes: with knowledge of Brahman ignorance ends; the body continues through begun karma, but the knower is liberated here.
- Chāndogya 6.14.2 ('only so long the delay')
- the Gītā's person of steady wisdom
**Texts:** 
  - [Jīvanmuktiviveka](../texts/jivanmuktiviveka.md) — ref: passim
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 4.1.15
### [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
No: while the body lasts karma and bondage remain; the knower or the surrendered is assured of release, which comes when the body falls.
- 'liberation at the end of the body' (Varadarāja's sayings)
- BS 4.1 on sins and begun karma
- Śatadūṣaṇī's vāda against jīvanmukti
**Texts:** 
  - [Śatadūṣaṇī](../texts/satadusani.md) — ref: vāda on jīvanmukti
  - [Śrībhāṣya](../texts/sribhasya.md) — ref: 4.1
