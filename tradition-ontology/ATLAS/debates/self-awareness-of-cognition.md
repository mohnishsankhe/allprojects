# Is a cognition aware of itself?

`dsp:self-awareness-of-cognition` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** B4

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
Yes: self-awareness is a kind of perception, the result of the means of knowledge; memory of having seen shows that the cognition was aware of itself (Dignāga, Dharmakīrti).
- PS 1.8–10; NB 1.10; PV 3.332 (conventional)
**Texts:** 
  - [Pramāṇasamuccaya](../texts/pramanasamuccaya.md) — ref: 1.8-10
  - [Nyāyabindu](../texts/nyayabindu.md) — ref: 1.10
  - [Pramāṇavārttika](../texts/pramanavarttika.md) — ref: 2.332 (e-text)
### [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
Cognition has a self-witnessing part — and, for Dharmapāla, a fourth part witnessing that (Cheng weishi lun).
- four parts of cognition
**Texts:** 
  - [Cheng weishi lun (*Vijñaptimātratāsiddhi)](../texts/cheng-weishi-lun.md) — ref: juan 2
### [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)
No: just as a sword does not cut itself, cognition cannot be its own object; memory does not prove reflexive awareness (Candrakīrti; Śāntideva, as recalled).
- sword and lamp examples
**Texts:** 
  - [Madhyamakāvatāra](../texts/madhyamakavatara.md) — ref: 6.72-76 (recalled)
  - [Bodhicaryāvatāra](../texts/bodhicaryavatara.md) — ref: 9.17-25 (recalled)
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
No: a cognition is known by a subsequent inner perception (anuvyavasāya) 'I know this', produced through the mind.
- Navya-Nyāya; Gaṅgeśa's treatment of the apprehension of cognition.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tattvacintāmaṇi](../texts/tattvacintamani.md) — ref: Pratyakṣa-khaṇḍa
### [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md)
Yes: every cognition reveals at once itself, its knower and its object (triple perception).
- As usually reported.
### [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md)
A cognition is not perceived but inferred from the 'knownness' it produces in the object.
- As usually reported.
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Consciousness is self-luminous (svaprakāśa) and never an object.
- As usually reported.
### [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
Cognition is reflexively aware of itself (svasaṃvedana).
- As usually reported.

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled.
**The traditions' own objections:** Nyāya's rejection of self-awareness is tied to its view that consciousness is an adventitious quality; Advaita's self-luminosity is tied to consciousness being the self. Neither accepts the other's frame.
**Candidate readings:** P2-standpoint: the schools may be answering different questions — how a cognition is manifest versus how it becomes an object of report.
