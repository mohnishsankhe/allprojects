# Do caste duties and Vedic/temple ritual bind the yogī, or are they bondage he must leave behind?

`dsp:natha-varna-and-ritual` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
For one who knows the supreme there is no caste, sacrifice, fast, pilgrimage, image or rite; worldly and pledge-conduct are 'made bonds'; knowledge alone is to be relied on; the yogī meditates where there is neither temple nor mosque.
- Akulavīra A.42–48; B.76–78; B.93–96.
- KJN 3.14–18; 21.10.
- Gorakh Bānī.
**Texts:** 
  - [Akulavīratantra](../texts/akulavira-tantra.md) — ref: a.42-48; b.93-96
  - [Kaulajñānanirṇaya](../texts/kaulajnananirnaya.md) — ref: 3.14-18; 21.10
  - [Gorakh Bānī](../texts/gorakhbani.md) — ref: sabadī
### [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)
There are four varṇas and no fifth; each life-stage has its duties, and renunciation comes in due order after the other stages.
- Manu 10.4.
- Manu 6.33–34; 6.87–90.
**Texts:** 
  - [Manusmṛti (Mānava Dharmaśāstra)](../texts/manusmrti.md) — ref: 10.4; 6.33-34; 6.87-90
### [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
Dharma is known from Vedic injunction; its enjoined acts are to be performed.
- MS 1.1.2.
**Texts:** 
  - [Mīmāṃsā Sūtra](../texts/mimamsa-sutra.md) — ref: 1.1.2

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage, P1-level
**Explanation:** The Nāth rejections are spoken of the knower (the 'one in whom this liberation abides') from the ultimate level, while Dharmaśāstra and Mīmāṃsā address the one within the social order — and Dharmaśāstra itself exempts the renouncer from ritual duties.
Within the Nāth corpus the Gorakṣaśataka (v. 3) still calls yoga 'the fruit of the Veda-tree', showing that not all Nāth texts reject the Veda outright.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Akulavīra and Gorakh Bānī call ritual and caste bondage and delusion for all (A.7–10; B.76), not merely unnecessary for the realised; Dharmaśāstra restricts renunciation by varṇa and would not accept the Nāth open recruitment.
