# Are the scriptural passages Madhva quotes from works not otherwise known (e.g. 'Bhāllaveya śruti', 'Brahmatarka', 'Paramaśruti') authentic scripture?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
They are genuine scripture — quoted from Vedic recensions, Pañcarātra texts and Purāṇa portions available to Madhva but now rare or lost; Madhva, as Vāyu's avatāra who received the Vedas' meaning from Vyāsa, had access to them.
- many old texts have been lost
- Madhva's authority as Vāyu's avatāra and Vyāsa's disciple
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya (Madhva)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-madhva.md) — ref: passim
  - [Mahābhārata-tātparya-nirṇaya](../texts/mahabharata-tatparya-nirnaya.md) — ref: 1.68-80
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Critics of Madhva have questioned the authority of quotations that cannot be traced in known texts and declined to accept them as scripture.
- the passages are not found in the known recensions
