# May the gods (devatās) be worshipped, and must caste and Vedic rites be kept, by one who seeks liberation?

`dsp:mahanubhava-and-the-devatas` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mahānubhāva panth](../lineages/mahanubhava.md)
The devatās are bound beings who give only worldly fruit; only Parameśvara and his incarnations are to be worshipped; caste and Vedic ritual have no place among seekers.
- The four realities (Sūtrapāṭh).
- Liberation from Parameśvara alone.
**Texts:** 
  - [Sūtrapāṭh](../texts/sutrapath.md) — ref: Vicāra; Ācāra
### [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)
The gods, rites and varṇa duties enjoined by śruti and smṛti bind all; a sect that rejects them is outside dharma.
- Authority of the Veda and smṛti.

**Historical debate — Opposition at the Yādava court** (c. 1270s): Līḷācaritra Cakradhar departs for the north; the community survives, keeping its texts secret.

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the Mahānubhāvas reject the plurality of worshipful gods that a P2/P3 reconciliation would assume.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Mahānubhāvas insist the devatās are not forms of Parameśvara.
**Candidate readings:** P3-path (iṣṭa-devatā): each worships the chosen form — explicitly rejected by the Mahānubhāvas.; P1-level: devatā-worship as conventional, Parameśvara as ultimate — closer to the Mahānubhāva view but it forbids, not merely ranks, such worship.
