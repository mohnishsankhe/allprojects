# Can cause and effect be the ground of birth? (Kārikā 4.11–4.23)

`dsp:gk-cause-effect` · `sourced` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Against the view that birth arises by cause and effect: if the effect is the cause the cause is born; sequence, simultaneity and mutual dependence each fail; seed-and-sprout equals what is to be proved; inability, non-knowledge and violation of sequence show non-birth.
- Sequence or simultaneity of cause and effect (4.16)
- Mutual dependence (4.17–4.18)
- Seed and sprout is sādhya-sama (4.20)
