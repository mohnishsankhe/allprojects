# Is there a person (pudgala) that is neither the same as nor different from the aggregates?

`dsp:pudgala` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pudgalavāda](../lineages/pudgalavada.md)
There is a person, real, designated in dependence on the internal, appropriated, present aggregates — neither identical with them nor different, as fire is designated in dependence on fuel; it is inexpressible, the fifth kind of knowable; it bears the burden of the aggregates and transmigrates.
- The sūtras speak of 'one person born in the world for the welfare of many' and of the burden-bearer (Bhārahāra-sūtra).
- Without a person, karma and its result, memory, and rebirth have no bearer.
- Fire is neither other than fuel (else fuel would not be hot) nor the same (else burned would be burner).
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośabhāṣya](../texts/abhidharmakosabhasya.md) — ref: 9, Pradhan p. 461–463
  - [Saṃmitīyanikāya-śāstra](../texts/sammitiyanikaya-sastra.md) — ref: whole
  - [Samayabhedoparacanacakra](../texts/samayabhedoparacanacakra.md) — ref: Vātsīputrīya tenets
### [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md)
There is no person apart from the series of aggregates; 'self' and 'person' are designations of that series. A person that is neither real nor a designation is incoherent; the five-knowables list cannot even be stated; the burden-bearer is the earlier aggregates.
- If the person were a substance it would be other than the aggregates, with a cause, or unconditioned — the outsiders' view.
- If it is designated, it is designated like milk, i.e. on the aggregates only.
- No liberation for those who imagine a self as a separate entity.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośabhāṣya](../texts/abhidharmakosabhasya.md) — ref: 9, Pradhan p. 461–470
### [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
There is no self; only aggregates conditioned by defilements and karma go from life to life through the intermediate existence, like a flame.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 3.18
  - [Vijñānakāya](../texts/vijnanakaya.md) — ref: ch. 2
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
The person is not found as a real and ultimate fact (saccikaṭṭha-paramattha).
- The Puggalavādin cannot say the person is found in the same way as real ultimate facts are.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 1.1

**Historical debate — Kathāvatthu 1.1 (the Puggalakathā)** (tradition: Third Council at Pāṭaliputra under Aśoka): Kathāvatthu and its commentary (opponents named as Vajjiputtakas and Sammitiyas) Theravāda: refuted

**Historical debate — Abhidharmakośabhāṣya ch. 9** (c. 4th–5th c. CE): AKBh ch. 9 Vasubandhu: refuted; the Saṃmitīya nevertheless flourished in the 7th c. (Xuanzang, Yijing)

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: both sides accept that the person is spoken of in dependence on the aggregates, but disagree whether it is anything beyond a designation.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Pudgalavādins insisted the person is real and neither the same as nor different from the aggregates; the Theravāda (Kathāvatthu), the Sarvāstivāda and Vasubandhu insist no person exists as a real and ultimate fact. Both reject a reconciliation that makes their position merely verbal.
