# How should Digambara image worship be performed, and are the bhaṭṭārakas authorities?

`dsp:bisapantha-terapantha` · `skeleton` · confidence low


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Bīsapantha (Digambara)](../lineages/bisapantha.md)
Worship with fresh flowers, fruits, lamps and sweets, seated; the bhaṭṭārakas are legitimate heads; the attendant deities (Padmāvatī, Kṣetrapāla) may be honoured.
- Long temple custom
- The bhaṭṭārakas preserved the tradition when naked monks were few
### [Terāpantha (Digambara)](../lineages/terapantha-digambara.md)
Offer only non-living substances (rice, dry fruits), standing; reject the bhaṭṭārakas; worship of attendant deities is wrong faith.
- Non-harm in worship
- Ṭoḍarmal's critique of wrong faith and clerical laxity
**Texts:** 
  - [Mokṣamārga Prakāśaka](../texts/moksamarga-prakasaka.md) — ref: 7

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path
**Explanation:** Both worship the same Jina images and accept Kundakunda; the manner of offering is a difference of ritual path.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Terāpantha holds worship of attendant deities to be wrong faith, a point no reconciliation of method covers.
