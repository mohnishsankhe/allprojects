# How should the Saṃhitās be interpreted — by the rite, the gods, the self, history, or one God?

`dsp:how-to-read-the-samhitas` · `sourced` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
Yāska records several schools: ritualists (yājñika), etymologists (nairukta), legend-tellers (aitihāsika), the wandering ascetics and the teachers of the self (ātmapravāda), and reads verses on the planes of the rite, the deity and the self.
- Multiple readings of the same verse (e.g. 1.164.32, 1.164.45).
**Texts:** 
  - [Nirukta](../texts/nirukta.md) — ref: 2.8; 13.9
### [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
Sāyaṇa (with Mīmāṃsā) explains each hymn chiefly by its use in the sacrifice; the Veda makes known the unseen means to the desired.
- The Veda's purpose is injunction; mantras serve the rite.
**Texts:** 
  - [Sāyaṇa's commentary on the Ṛgveda (Vedārthaprakāśa)](../texts/rgveda-bhasya-sayana.md) — ref: upodghāta
### [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
Madhva: every hymn has several levels of meaning and all ultimately praise Viṣṇu, the one denoted by all names.
- All names primarily denote Viṣṇu.
**Texts:** 
  - [Ṛgbhāṣya (Madhva)](../texts/rgbhasya-madhva.md) — ref: RV 1.1–40
### [Ārya Samāj](../lineages/arya-samaj.md)
Dayānanda (recent): the Saṃhitās teach one formless God and contain no history of persons; Agni, Indra and the rest are names of God; the ritual-mythological reading is rejected.
- Etymological reading of the divine names; RV 1.164.46.
**Texts:** 
  - [Ṛgvedādibhāṣyabhūmikā (Dayānanda Sarasvatī)](../texts/rgvedadibhasyabhumika.md) — ref: (whole)
### `lin:integral-yoga`
Sri Aurobindo (recent): the hymns are a symbolic record of inner spiritual experience (Agni the divine will, Soma delight, ṛta the truth-consciousness).
- Consistency of symbols across the hymns.
**Texts:** 
  - [The Secret of the Veda (Sri Aurobindo)](../texts/secret-of-the-veda.md) — ref: (whole)

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** The tradition's own multi-plane exegesis (rite, deity, self) lets ritual, theological and inner readings stand as readings of the same verse from different standpoints.
**The traditions' own objections:** Sāyaṇa/Mīmāṃsā hold the ritual purport primary; Madhva holds Viṣṇu the final purport of all readings; Dayānanda rejects the historical and ritual-mythological readings outright; none accepts being one standpoint among others.
