# Is the person (puggala) found as a real and ultimate fact?

`dsp:kv-puggala` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pudgalavāda](../lineages/pudgalavada.md)
The person is apprehended as a real and ultimate fact (held, per the commentary, by the Vajjiputtakas and Sammitīyas (personalists)).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 1.1
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
The person is only a conventional designation on the aggregates; it is not found as a real and ultimate fact as the aggregates are.
- If the person were real and ultimate it would have to be apprehended in the same way as other real and ultimate facts, which the opponent does not grant.
- 'There is suffering but no one who suffers' (Visuddhimagga).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 1.1

**Historical debate — Third Council at Pāṭaliputta** (3rd c. BCE (tradition: reign of Asoka)): Theravāda chronicles and the Samantapāsādikā: holders of wrong views were expelled and the Kathāvatthu was recited Theravāda: the Vibhajjavāda confirmed as the Buddha's teaching; other schools' accounts of the period differ

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P1-level
**Explanation:** Both sides accept the person at the conventional level; they differ on whether it has any further reality.
Under P1 the Theravāda's conventional person and the personalists' person agree as designations; the personalists' claim of an inexpressible real person is not reconciled.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Theravāda insists that only dhammas are ultimately real and rejects any person beyond designation; the personalists (as reported) rejected reduction of the person to the aggregates.
