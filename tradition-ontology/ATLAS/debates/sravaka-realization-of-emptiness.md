# Do śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas realize the emptiness (selflessness) of phenomena?

`dsp:sravaka-realization-of-emptiness` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Coverage:** B4

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)
Yes: without understanding that the aggregates lack own-nature they could not abandon the view of self, for as long as there is grasping at the aggregates there is grasping at 'I' (RĀ 1.35); they differ from bodhisattvas in the extent of their cultivation, not in the object understood (Candrakīrti, MAbh on 1.8).
- Grasping at aggregates grounds grasping at 'I'.
- The Hīnayāna scriptures also teach that form is like foam.
**Texts:** 
  - [Madhyamakāvatārabhāṣya](../texts/madhyamakavatara-bhasya.md) — ref: on 1.8
  - [Ratnāvalī](../texts/ratnavali.md) — ref: 1.35
### [Svātantrika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/svatantrika.md)
No: śrāvakas realize only the selflessness of persons; the selflessness of phenomena is the special realization of bodhisattvas.
- The Mahāyāna is superior because it teaches the selflessness of dharmas.
- Liberation from afflictions requires only the selflessness of persons.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tarkajvālā](../texts/tarkajvala.md) — ref: ch.4 (from memory)
