# Are the Śaiva (and Pāñcarātra) Āgamas equal in authority to the Veda?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Śiva-viśiṣṭādvaita (Śaiva Vedānta of Śrīkaṇṭha)](../lineages/siva-visistadvaita.md)
Śrīkaṇṭha: there is no difference between the Veda and the Śivāgama; both are Śiva's word.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śrīkaṇṭha (Śrīkaṇṭhabhāṣya)](../texts/srikantha-bhasya.md) — ref: 2.2.38
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Śaṅkara (BS 2.2.37–45): the Pāśupata doctrine of the Lord as mere efficient cause is refuted, and parts of the Pāñcarātra are rejected where they contradict the Veda.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 2.2.37-45
### [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
Yāmuna and Rāmānuja: the Pāñcarātra is fully Vedic and authoritative; the Pāśupata and other Śaiva systems are rejected.
**Texts:** 
  - [Śrībhāṣya](../texts/sribhasya.md) — ref: 2.2.39-42
