# Is there one prakṛti for all puruṣas, or one for each? (a debate among Sāṃkhya teachers)

**Coverage:** G (internal)

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
One prakṛti, common to all (classical).
- The manifest and pradhāna are common (SK 11); there is only one prakṛti, cause of the three worlds (Gauḍapāda on SK 66); no means of knowledge establishes many (Yuktidīpikā on SK 56).
**Texts:** 
  - [Sāṃkhya Kārikā](../texts/samkhya-karika.md) — ref: 11
  - [Sāṃkhyakārikābhāṣya of Gauḍapāda](../texts/samkhya-karika-bhasya-gaudapada.md) — ref: 66
  - [Yuktidīpikā](../texts/yuktidipika.md) — ref: 56
### [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
A separate pradhāna for each puruṣa, all following the pradhāna of the great-bodied one (Paurika).
- Reported by the Yuktidīpikā on SK 56.
**Texts:** 
  - [Yuktidīpikā](../texts/yuktidipika.md) — ref: 56
