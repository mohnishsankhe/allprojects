# Is exclusive devotion through the name, without Vedic rites, images or brahmin priests, a valid dharma?

`dsp:ekasarana-and-brahmin-orthodoxy` · `skeleton` · confidence low


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### `lin:smarta`
Rites, image worship, brahmin priesthood and the worship of the gods enjoined by smṛti are obligatory; a teaching that sets them aside and initiates all castes is heresy.
- Complaints of brahmins to the Ahom and Koch kings, as the hagiographies report them.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathā-gurucarit](../texts/katha-gurucarit.md) — ref: episodes at the Koch court
### [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md)
In the Kali age the name of Kṛṣṇa is the one refuge and the true purport of the Vedas; rites without devotion are empty (the Brahmin wives who fed Kṛṣṇa).
- Kīrtana-ghoṣā's refutation of other paths.
- Patnī-prasāda.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kīrtana-ghoṣā](../texts/kirtana-ghosa.md) — ref: Pāṣaṇḍa-mardana
  - [Patnī-prasāda](../texts/patni-prasada.md) — ref: whole

**Historical debate — Śaṅkaradeva before King Naranārāyaṇa** (mid-16th c.): Kathā-gurucarit The king takes Śaṅkaradeva's side.

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path, P4-stage
**Explanation:** Ritual and devotional paths are distinct and suit different seekers; the Bhāgavata the Ekaśaraṇa follows itself praises the name above rites for Kali.
Only the scope of each side's claim is reconciled; the Brahma saṃhati's later accommodation of some brahmanical rites shows the tradition's own range.
**The traditions' own objections:** Ekaśaraṇa rejects rites and other-deity worship even as preparation for devotion; orthodox opponents rejected initiation of all castes.
