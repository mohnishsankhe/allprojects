# Is the Tathāgata permanent (his parinirvāṇa a display), or is parinirvāṇa the final end, the Tathāgata after death being undeclared?

`dsp:permanence-of-the-tathagata` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)
The Tathāgata's lifespan is immeasurable; he awakened aeons ago and shows parinirvāṇa as skillful means; his body is the dharmakāya, the vajra body, permanent.
- Lotus 16; Golden Light ch. 2; Vimalakīrti 3.44; Mahāparinirvāṇa juan 3.
**Texts:** 
  - [Saddharmapuṇḍarīka-sūtra](../texts/saddharmapundarika.md) — ref: ch. 16
  - [Suvarṇaprabhāsottama-sūtra](../texts/suvarnaprabhasa.md) — ref: ch. 2
  - [Mahāyāna Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra](../texts/mahaparinirvana-sutra-mahayana.md) — ref: juan 3
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
At parinibbāna the Tathāgata is released from reckoning by the aggregates; whether he exists after death is not declared; like a fire gone out he cannot be said to have gone anywhere.
- The fire simile of the Aggivacchagotta Sutta (MN 72).
- The undeclared questions.
**Texts:** 
  - [Aggivacchagotta Sutta](../texts/aggivacchagotta-sutta.md) — ref: MN 72
  - [Mahāparinibbāna Sutta](../texts/mahaparinibbana-sutta.md) — ref: DN 16

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P1-level, P6-upaya
**Explanation:** Both deny that the Tathāgata can be reckoned by the aggregates; the Mahāyāna calls the undescribable dharmakāya 'permanent' at the ultimate level and the parinirvāṇa an upāya.
The Pali 'deep, immeasurable, hard to fathom' Tathāgata (MN 72) and the Vajracchedikā's Tathāgata who 'neither comes nor goes' meet under P1; the Lotus's eternal display is P6.
**The traditions' own objections:** Theravāda rejects the positive assertion of a permanent Tathāgata as a view (sassatavāda) and rejects the parinibbāna as a mere display.
