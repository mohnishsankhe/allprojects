# Is birth settled by one calling (or one thought of faith), or by lifelong many callings?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md)
(Kōsai, ichinengi) Birth is settled in a single thought of faith / one calling; further callings add nothing.

### [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md)
(Ryūkan, tanengi) One should call continuously all one's life, as Shandao taught ('until the end of life').
**Texts:**

### [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md)
Neither should be insisted on; the nenbutsu of the primal vow includes both; one thought-moment of shinjin settles birth and the callings follow in gratitude.
**Texts:** 
  - [Notes on Once-calling and Many-calling (Ichinen tanen mon'i)](../texts/ichinen-tanen-mon-i.md) — ref: conclusion

**Historical debate — One-calling / many-calling controversy in Hōnen's circle** (c. 1200–1230): Hōnen's letters; Ryūkan's tract; Shinran's Ichinen tanen mon'i. Hōnen warned against the excesses of one-calling; Shinran counselled against insisting on either.
