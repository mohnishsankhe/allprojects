# Whom does the worship of other deities (anya-devatā) really reach, and what does it yield?

`dsp:bhagavad-gita-other-deities` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### None
(As the Gītā describes them; no school is named) people moved by various desires resort to other deities, adopting their observances according to their own nature; they worship those forms with faith and obtain their desires from them.
- their faith is real and steady (7.21–22)
- they obtain their desires from the deities they propitiate (7.22)
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhagavad Gītā](../texts/bhagavad-gita.md) — ref: 7.20–22; 9.23; 9.25
### [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
Kṛṣṇa: it is he who makes their faith unwavering and who ordains the fruits they obtain; the fruit of those of small understanding is finite — worshippers of the gods go to the gods, of the ancestors to the ancestors, of spirits to spirits, his devotees to him; they too worship him, but not according to rule (avidhi-pūrvakam); not recognising him in truth as the enjoyer and lord of all sacrifices, they fall.
- the Lord grants both the faith and the fruit (7.21–22)
- he is the enjoyer and lord of all sacrifices (9.24)
- each goes to what he worships, and only the Lord's devotees come to him (7.23; 9.25)
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhagavad Gītā](../texts/bhagavad-gita.md) — ref: 7.21–23; 9.23–25
