# Did the original Jain scripture survive?

`dsp:canon-survival-jain` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
Yes, largely: the eleven Aṅgas and the other Āgamas were preserved through the councils of Pāṭaliputra, Mathurā and Valabhī and written down under Devarddhigaṇi; only the Dṛṣṭivāda with the Pūrvas was lost.
- The councils recovered what memory held
- The twelve-limbed canon is eternal in meaning and re-proclaimed by every Jina
**Texts:** 
  - [Pariśiṣṭaparvan (Sthavirāvalīcarita)](../texts/parisistaparvan.md) — ref: 9
  - [Nandī Sūtra](../texts/nandi-sutra.md) — ref: dvadasanga
  - [Kalpa Sūtra (Paryuṣaṇākalpa)](../texts/kalpa-sutra-jain.md) — ref: sthaviravali
### [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
No: knowledge of the Aṅgas and Pūrvas dwindled until 683 years after the nirvāṇa no complete Aṅga was known; only parts survive in the Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama and Kaṣāyapāhuḍa; the Śvetāmbara Āgamas are later compositions of a group that had relaxed the discipline.
- The line of 3 omniscients, 5 śrutakevalins, 11 daśapūrvins, 5 ekādaśāṅgins and 4 ācārāṅgins
- Dharasena's transmission to Puṣpadanta and Bhūtabali
- The Śvetāmbara texts contain teachings (women's liberation, the kevalin's eating) the Digambaras hold false
**Texts:** 
  - [Tiloyapaṇṇatti](../texts/tiloyapannatti.md) — ref: 4
  - [Dhavalā](../texts/dhavala.md) — ref: introduction
  - [Śrutāvatāra of Indranandi](../texts/srutavatara-indranandi.md) — ref: whole
### [Yāpanīya](../lineages/yapaniya.md)
The old canon survives in a form the Yāpanīyas accepted and cited (Āvaśyaka, Chedasūtras, niryuktis).
- Aparājita's citations
