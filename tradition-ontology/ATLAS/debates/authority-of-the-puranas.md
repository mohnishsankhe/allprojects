# What authority do the Purāṇas have beside the Veda?

`dsp:authority-of-the-puranas` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
The Purāṇa is the fifth Veda, remembered by Brahmā before the Vedas, and the Bhāgavata is the essence of all Vedānta.
- BhP 1.4.20
- Matsya 53.3
- BhP 12.13.15
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhāgavata Purāṇa](../texts/bhagavata-purana.md) — ref: 1.4.20
  - [Matsya Purāṇa](../texts/matsya-purana.md) — ref: 53.3
### [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
Smṛti, including Purāṇa, is authoritative only as far as it rests on (and does not contradict) the Veda; much of it is arthavāda.
- smṛti's authority is derived from inferred Vedic roots
**Texts:** 
  - [Mīmāṃsā Sūtra](../texts/mimamsa-sutra.md) — ref: 1.3 (smṛti-adhikaraṇa)
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
The Purāṇa was composed for those to whom the Veda is not accessible - women, śūdras and fallen twice-born.
- BhP 1.4.25
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhāgavata Purāṇa](../texts/bhagavata-purana.md) — ref: 1.4.25

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** Under P4 the Purāṇa's authority is framed by adhikāra: it carries Vedic truth to those without access to the Veda (BhP 1.4.25).
Mīmāṃsā's derivative status and the Purāṇic claim of priority (Matsya 53.3) remain not yet reconciled.
**The traditions' own objections:** Gauḍīya (Jīva's Tattva Sandarbha) and Mādhva authors treat the (sāttvika) Purāṇas as full pramāṇa, even decisive in the Kali age; Mīmāṃsakas reject independent authority.
