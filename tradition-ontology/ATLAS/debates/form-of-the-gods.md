# Are the gods person-like in form (puruṣavidha) or not?

`dsp:form-of-the-gods` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md)
Person-like: the hymns address them as conscious beings, give them names, limbs, possessions and deeds like persons.
- the manner of the praises
**Texts:** 
  - [Nirukta](../texts/nirukta.md) — ref: 7.6
### [Nairukta school (the Vedic etymologists)](../lineages/nairukta.md)
Not person-like: what is seen of them — fire, wind, sun, earth, moon — is not like a person; their actions are their selves.
- the visible deities
**Texts:** 
  - [Nirukta](../texts/nirukta.md) — ref: 7.7

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** Yāska's own conclusion allows both ('or they may be of both kinds'), and grounds the many forms in the one self of the deity (Nirukta 7.4).
