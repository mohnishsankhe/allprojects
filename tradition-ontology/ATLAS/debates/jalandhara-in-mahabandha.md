# In mahābandha, is the throat lock (jālandhara) to be applied, or the tongue pressed at the root of the front teeth instead?

`dsp:jalandhara-in-mahabandha` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
Apply the throat lock: in mahābandha the chin is set firmly on the chest while the perineum is contracted and the mind fixed in the middle (HYP 3.19-21); the Gheraṇḍa likewise holds the breath in jālandhara in mahābandha (GS 3.18-20).
- Jālandhara binds the network of channels and stops the nectar from falling into the fire (HYP 3.70-73).
- The three locks together are 'the means in all the haṭha teachings' (HYP 3.74-76).
**Texts:** 
  - [Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā (Haṭhapradīpikā)](../texts/hatha-yoga-pradipika.md) — ref: 3.19-21
  - [Gheraṇḍa Saṃhitā](../texts/gheranda-samhita.md) — ref: 3.18-20
### [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
Some teachers hold that the throat lock should be avoided in mahābandha and that the lock made with the tongue pressed at the root of the front teeth (rājadanta) is best (reported in HYP 3.22).
- The Haṭhapradīpikā reports the view without giving its reasons.
**Texts:** 
  - [Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā (Haṭhapradīpikā)](../texts/hatha-yoga-pradipika.md) — ref: 3.22
