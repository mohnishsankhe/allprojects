# Is Chan a special transmission outside the scriptural teachings, or one with them?

`dsp:chan-and-the-teachings` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
A special transmission outside the teachings, not established on words and letters; the twelve divisions of scripture are only expressions; one must not seek the meaning in words.
- Flower sermon; Linji's dismissal of names and phrases; Dahui is said to have burned the Blue Cliff blocks.
**Texts:** 
  - [Gateless Barrier (Wumen guan)](../texts/wumenguan.md) — ref: 6
  - [Record of Linji (Linji lu)](../texts/linji-lu.md) — 
  - [Blue Cliff Record (Biyan lu)](../texts/biyan-lu.md) — ref: 12
### [Heze school](../lineages/heze.md)
Chan and the teachings coincide: Zongmi correlates the three Chan tenets with three doctrinal teachings; Yanshou takes the one mind as the source of both.
**Texts:** 
  - [General Preface to the Collection of Expressions of the Chan Source (Chanyuan zhuquanji duxu)](../texts/chanyuan-zhuquanji-duxu.md) — 
  - [Record of the Source Mirror (Zongjing lu)](../texts/zongjing-lu.md) — 
### [Seon (Korean Chan)](../lineages/seon.md)
Hyujeong: Chan is the Buddha's mind and the teachings his words; first understand through the teachings, then enter Chan through the live word.
**Texts:** 
  - [Mirror for Seon Students (Seon'ga gwigam)](../texts/seonga-gwigam.md) — 
### [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
The Platform Sūtra: those who say 'do not use words' slander the scriptures, for 'not using' is itself words.
**Texts:** 
  - [Platform Sūtra of the Sixth Patriarch (Liuzu tanjing)](../texts/platform-sutra.md) — ref: 13.1

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage, P6-upaya
**Explanation:** Teachings as the provisional means and first stage, Chan as direct realization of what they point to (Hyujeong's sequence).
**The traditions' own objections:** Strict Linji voices reject subordinating Chan to doctrine; doctrinal schools (e.g. Tiantai) reject the claim of a separate transmission.
