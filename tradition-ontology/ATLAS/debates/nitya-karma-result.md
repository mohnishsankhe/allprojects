# Do obligatory (nitya) rites have a result, and why are they performed?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md)
They destroy sins done earlier and avert the future demerit that omission would cause; the seeker of release performs them to avoid that demerit.
- Tantravārttika (on MS 1.3.24–29); ŚV sambandhākṣepaparihāra 110.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tantravārttika](../texts/tantravarttika.md) — ref: on MS 1.3.24–29

### [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md)
They are performed because they are enjoined; the command itself prompts the person whose qualification is simply being alive, without a separate fruit.
**Texts:** 
  - [Prakaraṇapañcikā](../texts/prakaranapancika.md) — ref: (chapter-level; from memory)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Obligatory rites, done without desire, purify the mind and so prepare for knowledge; they do not themselves liberate.
**Texts:** 
  - [Gītābhāṣya of Śaṅkara](../texts/gita-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: (chapter-level)
