# Is Amitābha a reward-body buddha in a reward land, or a transformation body in a land where ordinary beings and sages dwell together?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)
(Jingying Huiyuan and other Sui scholars, as reported) Sukhāvatī is a land manifested for beings; the upper grades are sages of the stages; ordinary beings are born into a lower land.
**Texts:** 
  - [Commentary on the Meaning of the Contemplation Sūtra (Jingying Huiyuan)](../texts/guan-wuliangshou-jing-yishu.md) — ref: whole
### [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)
(Daochuo, Shandao) Amitābha is a reward buddha and Sukhāvatī a reward land; the old view is 'a great error'; ordinary beings enter by the vow's power; all nine grades are ordinary beings.
**Texts:** 
  - [Collection on the Land of Peace and Bliss (Anle ji)](../texts/anle-ji.md) — ref: 5c11-15
  - [Commentary on the Contemplation Sūtra (Guanjing shu)](../texts/guanjing-shu.md) — ref: fasc. 1
### `lin:tiantai`
(Tiantai four-land scheme, as later Pure Land writers use it) Sukhāvatī is chiefly the land where ordinary beings and sages dwell together, while also containing the higher lands.
**Texts:** 
  - [Resolving Doubts about the Pure Land (Jingtu yibian)](../texts/jingtu-yibian.md) — ref: 420b02
