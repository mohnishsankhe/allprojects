# Is time (kāla) a real, separate substance?

`dsp:kala-as-dravya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** B2 / G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
Yes: time is a sixth substance, made of innumerable time-atoms, one in each space-point of the universe, which assist the continuance and change of all things; conventional time (instants, muhūrtas) is measured by the luminaries.
- TS 5.39 'and time'
- TS 5.22: continuance, change, activity, before and after are the service of time.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tattvārthasūtra](../texts/tattvartha-sutra.md) — ref: 5.39
  - [Tattvārthasūtra](../texts/tattvartha-sutra.md) — ref: 5.22
### [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
Some teachers hold time to be a substance, others only the modes of souls and non-souls; the sūtra itself says 'time too, according to some'.
- Śvetāmbara TS 5.38 'kālaś cety eke'.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tattvārthasūtra](../texts/tattvartha-sutra.md) — ref: 5.38 (Śvetāmbara)

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**The traditions' own objections:** Digambara authorities treat time-atoms as a real substance, not a standpoint.
