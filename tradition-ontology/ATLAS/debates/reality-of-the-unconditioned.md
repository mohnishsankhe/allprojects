# Are the unconditioned dharmas (space, the two cessations) real entities?

`dsp:reality-of-the-unconditioned` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vaibhāṣika](../lineages/vaibhasika.md)
Yes: three real, eternal unconditioned dharmas; nirvāṇa is cessation through discernment, a real disjunction.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 1.5-6
### [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md)
No: all the unconditioned is unreal — space is absence of resistance, nirvāṇa the non-arising of further defilement and birth.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośabhāṣya](../texts/abhidharmakosabhasya.md) — ref: 2.55

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the Vaibhāṣika holds a real entity where the Sautrāntika sees a designation.
**The traditions' own objections:** Each side holds the other's account inadequate: the Vaibhāṣika that designations cannot do causal work, the Sautrāntika that the posited entities are unnecessary and unattested in the sūtras.
