# Are some Purāṇas sāttvika and authoritative, others rājasa or tāmasa and to be avoided?

`dsp:guna-ranking-of-puranas` · `sourced` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
Purāṇas are graded by the guṇas according to the deity they glorify (Matsya 53.68-69); the Padma (as cited) makes the Vaiṣṇava ones sāttvika, the Śaiva ones tāmasa; the Garuḍa's Brahmakāṇḍa forbids seekers of liberation to hear the rājasa and tāmasa ones except their sāttvika parts.
- Matsya's classification
- Padma and Garuḍa Brahmakāṇḍa lists
**Texts:** 
  - [Matsya Purāṇa](../texts/matsya-purana.md) — ref: 53.68-69
  - [Padma Purāṇa](../texts/padma-purana.md) — ref: Uttarakhaṇḍa (as cited)
  - [Garuḍa Purāṇa](../texts/garuda-purana.md) — ref: 3.1.50-56
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
All eighteen are Vyāsa's and establish dharma (KūP 1.11.279); each Purāṇa glorifies Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Sūrya and Rudra (Matsya 53.66); Śaiva Purāṇas exalt Śiva and reverse the ranking.
- the Kūrma's statement that dharma is established in all eighteen
- the Śaiva Purāṇas' own claims
**Texts:** 
  - [Kūrma Purāṇa](../texts/kurma-purana.md) — ref: 1.11.279-280
  - [Śiva Purāṇa](../texts/siva-purana.md) — ref: 1.5

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P7-arthavada, P3-path
**Explanation:** Under P7 the exaltations of one deity are praise (arthavāda) addressed to worshippers of that deity (P3), so the rankings need not deny the other Purāṇas' dharma.
This does not settle which deity is supreme; see dsp:supremacy-visnu-or-siva.
**The traditions' own objections:** Mādhva and Gauḍīya authors take the guṇa-ranking literally (sāttvika Purāṇas alone decide doctrine); Śaiva authors reject the Vaiṣṇava ranking.
