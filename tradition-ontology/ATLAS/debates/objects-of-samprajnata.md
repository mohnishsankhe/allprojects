# What are the objects of the bliss (ānanda) and I-am-ness (asmitā) forms of samprajñāta samādhi, and who are the videhas and prakṛtilayas?

`dsp:objects-of-samprajnata` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
Vācaspati Miśra: reasoning and reflection have the grasped (gross and subtle elements) as object; bliss has the grasping (the senses, sattva-dominated and pleasant) as object; I-am-ness has the grasper, the sense of 'I' in which the puruṣa is mixed with buddhi; sub-varieties 'with bliss' and 'with I-am-ness' are admitted.
- Maps YS 1.17 onto the grasper–grasping–grasped of YS 1.41.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tattvavaiśāradī](../texts/tattvavaisaradi.md) — ref: 1.17; 1.41
### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
Bhoja: sānanda is cultivation of the inner organ's sattva tinged by rajas and tamas (grasping-coalescence) — those content there are the 'videhas'; sāsmita is cultivation of pure sattva, mere being — those content there are 'prakṛtilayas'; discernment of the supreme puruṣa is grasper-coalescence.
- Asmitā is not ahaṃkāra.
**Texts:** 
  - [Rājamārtaṇḍa (Bhojavṛtti)](../texts/rajamartanda.md) — ref: 1.17
### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
Vijñānabhikṣu: bliss is a joy arising from preponderant sattva, taken as object; it is not the senses; asmitā is awareness with the self alone as object (the individual self or the Supreme Self); there are no 'with bliss / without bliss' sub-varieties.
- The sense organs are gross and would fall under reasoning.
- Sūtra and bhāṣya do not mention sub-varieties of bliss.
**Texts:** 
  - [Yogavārttika](../texts/yogavarttika.md) — ref: 1.17
### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
Vyāsa: the bodiless (videha) are gods who experience a quasi-kaivalya with a mind using its own impressions (YBh 1.19); bliss is joy, I-am-ness awareness of a single self (YBh 1.17).
**Texts:** 
  - [Yogabhāṣya (Vyāsa's commentary on the Yoga Sūtra)](../texts/yoga-bhasya.md) — ref: 1.17; 1.19
