# Do the effortful methods of haṭha (breath-retention, mudrās, mantra, meditation on objects) bring the mindless state, or does it arise only effortlessly by the guru's grace?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
(Amanaska) The laborious methods do not bring the mindless state and may cause pain and disease; it arises of itself through the guru's grace and an objectless gaze.
- Amanaska 2
**Texts:** 
  - [Amanaska (Amanaskayoga)](../texts/amanaska.md) — ref: ch. 2
### [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
(Haṭhapradīpikā) Kevala retention brings rājayoga; the great śakti awakened by postures, retentions and mudrās dissolves breath in the void, and then the natural state arises of itself; kuṇḍalinī wakes 'by the guru's grace'.
- HYP 2.73–75, 3.2, 4.10–12
**Texts:** 
  - [Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā (Haṭhapradīpikā)](../texts/hatha-yoga-pradipika.md) — ref: 2.73-75; 3.2; 4.10-12

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** Both agree the state is effortless and spontaneous (sahaja); they differ on whether preparatory effort is needed — a question of the student's stage.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Amanaska explicitly rejects the preparatory methods as useless; this reconciliation does not claim otherwise.
