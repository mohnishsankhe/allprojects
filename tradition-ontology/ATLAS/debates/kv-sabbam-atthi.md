# Do past and future dhammas exist (does 'everything exist')?

`dsp:kv-sabbam-atthi` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
Everything exists — past, future and present dhammas (held, per the commentary, by the Sabbatthivādins).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 1.6
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
Only present dhammas exist; the past has ceased and the future has not arisen.
- If the past existed as the present does, it would be present.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 1.6
