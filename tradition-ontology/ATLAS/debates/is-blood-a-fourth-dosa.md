# Is blood (rakta) a doṣa alongside vāta, pitta and kapha?

`dsp:is-blood-a-fourth-dosa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
Suśruta: the three doṣas with blood as a fourth sustain the body in origin, maintenance and dissolution; bodily diseases arise from imbalance of vāta, pitta, kapha and blood.
**Texts:** 
  - [Suśruta Saṃhitā](../texts/susruta-samhita.md) — ref: Sū 21.3-4; Sū 1.25
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
Caraka and Vāgbhaṭa: the doṣas are three; blood is among the seven dhātus (the vitiable); and Suśruta himself: blood is never provoked apart from the doṣas.
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Sū 1.57
  - [Aṣṭāṅga Hṛdaya](../texts/astanga-hrdaya.md) — ref: Sū 1.6, 1.13
  - [Suśruta Saṃhitā](../texts/susruta-samhita.md) — ref: Sū 21.26

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** In the surgical standpoint blood is counted with the doṣas because it is vitiated and let out; in the internal-medicine standpoint it is a dhātu vitiated only through the doṣas — as Suśruta also says.
