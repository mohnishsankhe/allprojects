# Is the Kāpālika way (skull vow, insignia, liquor, consort, Bhairava rites) a path to liberation?

`dsp:kapalika-path-validity` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Kāpālika (Somasiddhānta)](../lineages/kapalika.md)
As reported: knowing and wearing the insignia, meditation on the self in the yoni, and the Lord's own path of liquor and the beloved lead to release (voiced only through opponents and satirists).
- Āgamaprāmāṇya (report); Mattavilāsa v. 7 (satire); Prabodhacandrodaya act 3 (hostile).
**Texts:** 
  - [Āgamaprāmāṇya](../texts/agamapramanya.md) — ref: Māheśvara section
  - [Mattavilāsa-prahasana](../texts/mattavilasa-prahasana.md) — ref: 7
  - [Prabodhacandrodaya](../texts/prabodhacandrodaya.md) — ref: 3
### [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
Release comes only from knowing Brahman (Vāsudeva); these practices are outside the Veda.
- Yāmuna: 'tam eva viditvā atimṛtyum eti...'.
**Texts:** 
  - [Āgamaprāmāṇya](../texts/agamapramanya.md) — ref: Māheśvara section
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
The Kāpālikas are deluded opponents defeated by Śaṅkara (hagiography) and by Viveka (allegorical drama).
- Śaṅkaradigvijaya cantos 11, 15; Prabodhacandrodaya act 3.
**Texts:** 
  - [Śaṅkaradigvijaya (Mādhavīya)](../texts/sankaradigvijaya.md) — ref: 11; 15
  - [Prabodhacandrodaya](../texts/prabodhacandrodaya.md) — ref: 3

**Historical debate — Śaṅkara and the Kāpālikas (Ugrabhairava at Śrīśaila; Krakaca at Ujjayinī)** (legendary (hagiography of uncertain date)): Mādhavīya Śaṅkaradigvijaya (opponent's hagiography) Advaita hagiography: the Kāpālikas are defeated; no Kāpālika account survives.
