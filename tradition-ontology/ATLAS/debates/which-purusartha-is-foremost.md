# Which aim of life is foremost — dharma, artha, kāma, their combination, or liberation?

`dsp:which-purusartha-is-foremost` · `sourced` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)
The combination, with dharma weightiest: the settled view is that the good is the triad (MDh 2.224); dharmaśāstra prevails over arthaśāstra (YS 2.21); self-knowledge leads to the supreme good (MDh 12.85).
- dharma as the root of the others' legitimacy
- liberation after the debts are paid
**Texts:** 
  - [Manusmṛti (Mānava Dharmaśāstra)](../texts/manusmrti.md) — ref: 2.224
  - [Yājñavalkyasmṛti](../texts/yajnavalkyasmrti.md) — ref: 2.21
  - [Manusmṛti (Mānava Dharmaśāstra)](../texts/manusmrti.md) — ref: 12.83-85
### [Arthaśāstra (the science of statecraft and wealth)](../lineages/arthasastra.md)
Artha is foremost, for dharma and kāma are rooted in artha (Kauṭilya).
- the world's livelihood depends on wealth
**Texts:** 
  - [Kauṭilīya Arthaśāstra](../texts/arthasastra.md) — ref: 1.7.6-7
### [Kāmaśāstra (the science of love and pleasure)](../lineages/kamasastra.md)
The three are to be pursued in balance over the stages of life, each earlier weightier, artha first for a king; kāma needs its own śāstra.
- the allotment of aims to ages of life
**Texts:** 
  - [Kāmasūtra of Vātsyāyana](../texts/kamasutra.md) — ref: 1.2.1-15
### [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md)
Lokāyatikas (as reported by Vātsyāyana): dharma should not be practised, its fruit being future and doubtful — better a pigeon today than a peacock tomorrow.
- uncertainty of unseen fruit
**Texts:** 
  - [Kāmasūtra of Vātsyāyana](../texts/kamasutra.md) — ref: 1.2.21-24

**Historical debate — The Pāṇḍavas' discussion of the three aims** (epic narrative): In the Śāntiparvan (MBh 12.161, as usually cited) Vidura, Arjuna, Nakula–Sahadeva and Bhīma each argue for an aim and Yudhiṣṭhira points beyond them. recorded in the Mahābhārata (U05); details from memory, to be checked

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage, P3-path
**Explanation:** The texts themselves distribute the aims over the stages of life and over active and quiescent paths (Kāmasūtra 1.2.2–4; MDh 12.88–90).
**The traditions' own objections:** Kauṭilya still holds artha foremost; the Lokāyata view (known only as reported) rejects dharma altogether; for Manu the triad is subordinate to self-knowledge.
