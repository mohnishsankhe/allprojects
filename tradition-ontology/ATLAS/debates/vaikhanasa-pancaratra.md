# Which system — the Vedic Vaikhānasa or the tantric Pāñcarātra — is the proper basis of Viṣṇu temple worship, and must a Vaiṣṇava receive the branding initiation?

`dsp:vaikhanasa-pancaratra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A4

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md)
The Vaikhānasa Sūtra, composed by Vikhanas (Nārāyaṇa/Brahmā), is the first and best of sūtras; its worship is wholly Vedic; Vaikhānasas are Vaiṣṇavas from the womb through the viṣṇubali rite and need no branding; its followers are the highest.
- ten reasons (DHN)
- Vedic mantras in every rite
- prenatal consecration
**Texts:** 
  - [Daśavidhahetunirūpaṇa](../texts/dasavidhahetunirupana.md) — ref: p2.1-23
  - [Kriyādhikāra](../texts/kriyadhikara.md) — ref: 1.1
### [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md)
The Pāñcarātra is the Lord's own word, governing the chief self-manifest shrines (Śrīraṅgam, Melkote, Kāñcī), with the five sacraments as the mark of a Vaiṣṇava; only Veṅkaṭādri is excepted.
- the three gems and their temple assignments
- authority of the Lord's speech
**Texts:** 
  - [Jayākhya Saṃhitā](../texts/jayakhya-samhita.md) — ref: 1.adhika.1-14
  - [Āgamaprāmāṇya](../texts/agamapramanya.md) — ref: siddhanta

**Historical debate — Change of ritual system in Śrīvaiṣṇava temples** (11th–12th c. (tradition)): Śrīvaiṣṇava tradition credits Rāmānuja with establishing Pāñcarātra worship in some temples (e.g. Śrīraṅgam); the details are the tradition's account, not verified here. Both systems continue in different temples (e.g. Vaikhānasa at Tirumala).

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: each community holds its own system to be the better-founded.
**The traditions' own objections:** Vaikhānasas reject being classed as tantric; Pāñcarātrins reject the view that their system is non-Vedic or lower.
**Candidate readings:** P3-path: two valid ritual paths to the same Viṣṇu, suited to hereditary Vedic eligibility (Vaikhānasa) and to initiatory eligibility (Pāñcarātra).; P4-stage: the Pādma's claim that Pāñcarātra eligibility comes 'at the end' of Mīmāṃsā learning is one side's framing of adhikāra.

_Notes: Bhāskararāya (Setubandha intro) quotes a verse saying the Lord taught the Pāñcarātra, Bhāgavata and Vaikhānasa for those fallen from the Veda — a third, Śākta view (recorded in notes only)._
