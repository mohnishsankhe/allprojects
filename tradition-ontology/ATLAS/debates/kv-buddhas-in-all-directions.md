## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md)
Buddhas stand in all the directions (held, per the commentary, by the Mahāsaṅghikas).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 21.6
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
The Buddha does not say which direction or name such Buddhas; only one Buddha arises in one world-system at a time.
- Name the Buddha in the eastern direction.
- Two Buddhas cannot arise at once in one world-system (sutta).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 21.6
