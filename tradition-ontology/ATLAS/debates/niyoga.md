# Is levirate (niyoga) permitted?

`dsp:niyoga` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)
Permitted under strict rules: a sonless wife or widow, appointed by the elders, may conceive a son by the husband's brother or a sapiṇḍa (MDh 9.59–63; also earlier Dharmasūtras).
- continuity of the lineage and the ancestral offerings
**Texts:** 
  - [Manusmṛti (Mānava Dharmaśāstra)](../texts/manusmrti.md) — ref: 9.59-63
### [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)
Forbidden: the learned condemn it as fit for animals, introduced under the wicked king Vena (MDh 9.64–68).
- it confuses the classes
**Texts:** 
  - [Manusmṛti (Mānava Dharmaśāstra)](../texts/manusmrti.md) — ref: 9.64-68

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** The commentators read Manu's permission and prohibition as belonging to different ages — possible for earlier people, not for those of the Kali age (a verse ascribed to Bṛhaspati is quoted to this effect); later lists count niyoga among kalivarjya practices.
**The traditions' own objections:** Some commentators instead restrict the prohibition to particular classes or read 9.59–63 as the opponent's view; the Bṛhaspati quotation is known only through commentaries.
