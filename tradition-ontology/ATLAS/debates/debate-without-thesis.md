# May a debater refute without holding a thesis of his own (vitaṇḍā)?

`dsp:debate-without-thesis` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A5

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
Cavilling without a counter-thesis is a recognized but inferior form of debate, permitted only to protect truth; a caviller who states his purpose or meaning thereby adopts a thesis, and one who rejects all examples cannot refute anyone.
- NS 1.2.3; 4.2.50; NBh 1.1.1.
**Texts:** 
  - [Nyāya Sūtra](../texts/nyaya-sutra.md) — ref: 1.2.3; 4.2.50
  - [Nyāyabhāṣya](../texts/nyaya-bhasya.md) — ref: 1.1.1
### [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
The Mādhyamika refutes the opponent's theses by consequences without advancing a thesis of his own: 'if I had a thesis the fault would be mine; since I have none, there is no fault' (Nāgārjuna).
- Vigrahavyāvartanī v. 29 (as usually cited).
**Texts:** 
  - [Vigrahavyāvartanī](../texts/vigrahavyavartani.md) — ref: v. 29
