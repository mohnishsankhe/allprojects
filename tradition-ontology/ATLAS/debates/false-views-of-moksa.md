# What is liberation? (the Amaraughaśāsana against rival definitions)

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
Liberation is not the cutting off of karma, Veda recitation, the supportless state, meditation on form, bindu or nāda, the bliss of Kaula union, the movement of kuṇḍalinī or an even gaze; it is the mind seeing the mind in innate absorption, liberation while living.
- AmŚ 46–54.
- AmŚ 55–59: the state of living liberation.
**Texts:** 
  - [Amaraughaśāsana](../texts/amaraughasasana.md) — ref: 46-59
### [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
The Veda's injunction is the means of knowing dharma, and its enjoined acts bring their results (the position the AmŚ lists as 'liberation rests on Veda recitation').
- MS 1.1.2: dharma is what is characterised by injunction.
**Texts:** 
  - [Mīmāṃsā Sūtra](../texts/mimamsa-sutra.md) — ref: 1.1.2
### [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md)
Kaula rites of worship, wine, meat and union bring enjoyment and liberation (the position the AmŚ lists as 'bliss of union is liberation'); Kaula texts themselves speak of bhukti and mukti from the rites.
- KJN 3.27; 8.36: rites and circles give enjoyment and liberation.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kaulajñānanirṇaya](../texts/kaulajnananirnaya.md) — ref: 8.4-45
