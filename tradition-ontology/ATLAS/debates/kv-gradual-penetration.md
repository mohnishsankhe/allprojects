# Are the four truths penetrated gradually or all at once?

`dsp:kv-gradual-penetration` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
The truths are penetrated gradually (one after another) (held, per the commentary, by Andhakas, Sabbatthivādins, Sammitīyas and Bhadrayānikas).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 2.9
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
Each path penetrates all four truths in a single moment of knowledge.
- Stream-entry is not reached in stages of separate truth-seeing.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 2.9

**Historical debate — Third Council at Pāṭaliputta** (3rd c. BCE (tradition: reign of Asoka)): Theravāda chronicles and the Samantapāsādikā: holders of wrong views were expelled and the Kathāvatthu was recited Theravāda: the Vibhajjavāda confirmed as the Buddha's teaching; other schools' accounts of the period differ

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** Preliminary insight takes the truths separately; the path moment penetrates them together — the two theses may describe different phases.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Theravāda denies that the path itself is gradual; the reported opponents held that penetration proper occurs in sixteen (or several) moments.
