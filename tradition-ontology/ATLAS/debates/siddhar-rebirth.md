# Are the dead born again?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
Sivavākkiyar: as drawn milk does not return to the udder and fallen fruit does not return to the tree, the dead are not born again — 'no, no, no'.
- Irreversibility images: milk, butter, conch-sound, flower and fruit.

### [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
Paṭṭiṉattār and most Siddhar voices: we have had countless mothers, fathers and births and more are to come unless the Lord frees us; one's own deed returns to burn oneself.
- How many births before, how many more? (Tiruvēkampa viruttam).
- The Lord drives us by the rope of the two deeds (Tiruttillai 11).
**Texts:**
