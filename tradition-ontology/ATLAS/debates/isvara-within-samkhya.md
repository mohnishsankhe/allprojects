# Does Sāṃkhya deny Īśvara, or only deny that an eternal creator is proved?

`dsp:isvara-within-samkhya` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Coverage:** G10 (Sāṃkhya's side of dsp:isvara)

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
No Īśvara is established as creator (classical; the Sāṃkhya Sūtra; Aniruddha as recalled), though emergent lords exist.
- The SK is silent; Gauḍapāda argues the attributeless Īśvara cannot produce what has guṇas (on SK 61).
- 'Īśvara is not established' (SS 1.92); neither free nor bound he could not create (1.93–94); fruit comes from karma (5.2); effects depend on ahaṃkāra (6.64).
- Yet one absorbed in prakṛti becomes an all-knowing, all-doing lord in the next creation (SS 3.55–57).
**Texts:** 
  - [Sāṃkhyakārikābhāṣya of Gauḍapāda](../texts/samkhya-karika-bhasya-gaudapada.md) — ref: 61

  - [Sāṃkhyasūtravṛtti of Aniruddha](../texts/samkhya-sutra-vrtti-aniruddha.md) — ref: 1.92 (recalled)
### [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
The denial is a concessive argument; Īśvara is known from scripture (Vijñānabhikṣu, recalled).
- Vijñānabhikṣu salutes the Lord (Sāṃkhyasāra 1.1.1) and reads SS 1.92 as not denying a Lord taught in śruti (recalled, low confidence).
**Texts:** 
  - [Sāṃkhyasāra](../texts/samkhyasara.md) — ref: 1.1.1

A Lord as twenty-sixth principle above the twenty-five (epic Sāṃkhya).
