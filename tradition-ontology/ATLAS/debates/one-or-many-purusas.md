# Is the conscious self one or many?

`dsp:one-or-many-purusas` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G (Sāṃkhya–Advaita)

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
Many: each being has its own puruṣa.
- Birth, death and the instruments are allotted separately; beings do not act all at once; the guṇas differ (SK 18; SS 1.149, 6.45).
- Vāmadeva and others were liberated while others were not (SS 1.157).
- If distribution is by adjuncts, duality returns once they are established (SS 6.46); difference is known from marks (SS 5.61).
**Texts:** 
  - [Sāṃkhya Kārikā](../texts/samkhya-karika.md) — ref: 18
  - [Sāṃkhya Sūtra](../texts/samkhya-sutra.md) — ref: 1.149-157; 5.61-64; 6.45-47
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
One: the self is one; apparent plurality comes from limiting adjuncts, like space in pots.
- The self appears as individual selves like space as pot-spaces (Māṇḍūkya-kārikā 3.3–9); this is the objection the Sāṃkhya Sūtra itself states (1.150).
**Texts:** 
  - [Māṇḍūkya Kārikā (Gauḍapāda Kārikā, Āgamaśāstra)](../texts/mandukya-karika.md) — ref: 3.3-9
  - [Sāṃkhya Sūtra](../texts/samkhya-sutra.md) — ref: 1.150-153

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** Sāṃkhya's own reading (SS 1.154): non-duality texts speak of sameness of kind, plurality of number — one and many under different standpoints.
Under P2, 'one' names the single nature (pure consciousness) all puruṣas share and 'many' the number of seers; this is how the Sāṃkhya Sūtra reconciles scripture with plurality. It does not claim numerical identity.
**The traditions' own objections:** Advaita rejects the reading: the texts of non-duality teach numerical identity of the self with Brahman, and plurality belongs to adjuncts.
