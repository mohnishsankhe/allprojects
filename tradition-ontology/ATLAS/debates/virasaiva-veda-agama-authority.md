# What authority do the Veda and the Āgamas have for Vīraśaivas?

`dsp:virasaiva-veda-agama-authority` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G11

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md)
The Veda is the chief authority; the Śaiva Siddhānta Āgama is 'made of the Veda', one with it in meaning and as authoritative; the Vīraśaiva doctrine is taught in the Āgamas' later portion and agrees with Veda and Vedānta — though sacrifices and austerities without Śiva-devotion are fruitless and not worth a fraction of liṅga-worship.
- SSM 5.3–14 on authority.
- SSM 6.58–61 reads the Ṛgveda as enjoining liṅga-wearing.
- SSM 8.36–43; 11.48–49; 15.30–36 subordinate sacrifice to devotion.
**Texts:** 
  - [Siddhāntaśikhāmaṇi](../texts/siddhantasikhamani.md) — ref: 5.3–14; 6.58–61; 8.36–43; 11.48–49; 15.30–36
  - [Śrīkarabhāṣya](../texts/srikarabhasya.md) — ref: passim
### [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md)
Scripture is to be tested by experience: Basava will put the Veda to the touchstone, fetter the śāstras and cut the nose of the Āgamas; the śaraṇas' vacanas and anubhava are the authority.
- Basava's vacana 'Vēdakke oregalla kaṭṭuve'.
- Basava's refusal of the sacred-thread rite (hagiography).
**Texts:** 
  - [Vacanas of Basavaṇṇa (Basavaṇṇanavara vacanagaḷu)](../texts/basavanna-vacanas.md) — ref: 'Vēdakke oregalla kaṭṭuve…'
  - [Basavarājadēvara Ragaḷe (Harihara)](../texts/basavarajadevara-ragale.md) — ref: thread episode
### [Ārādhya Śaiva (Andhra)](../lineages/aradhya-saiva.md)
The Ārādhyas wear the liṅga and worship Śiva exclusively while keeping the sacred thread and Vedic rites.
- Tradition-account of the Ārādhya community (low confidence).
**Texts:** 
  - [Paṇḍitārādhya Caritra (Pālkuriki Sōmanātha)](../texts/panditaradhya-caritra.md) — ref: passim

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** Within the SSM, reverence for the Veda and the subordination of Vedic rites to liṅga-worship are reconciled by stage (5.22–23; 9.39–40; 15.30–36); between the SSM and the vacanas the question is not yet reconciled.
Candidate for the inter-strand question: P7-arthavada (the vacana's 'touchstone' as praise of devotion rather than doctrinal rejection) — a reading the Basava-centred strand rejects.
**The traditions' own objections:** Basava-centred readers hold the vacana's rejection of Vedic authority to be meant literally; the Ārādhya and Pañcācārya positions hold Vedic authority non-negotiable.
