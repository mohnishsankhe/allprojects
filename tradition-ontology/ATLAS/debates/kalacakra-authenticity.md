# Is the Kālacakra an authentic Buddhist tantra — and, for the Jonang, the definitive one?

`dsp:kalacakra-authenticity` · `skeleton` · confidence low


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md)
The Kālacakra with its bodhisattva commentaries is the pinnacle of the tantras and teaches the definitive meaning — the other-empty ultimate, the emptiness endowed with all supreme aspects.
- The Vimalaprabhā's reading of the tantra in the definitive sense.
- Agreement with the third-turning sūtras (Dolpopa).
**Texts:** 
  - [Mountain Doctrine: Ocean of Definitive Meaning (ri chos nges don rgya mtsho)](../texts/mountain-doctrine.md) — ref: definitive
  - [Vimalaprabhā](../texts/vimalaprabha.md) — ref: 1
### [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)
(Rendawa, early in his career, as recalled) The Kālacakra's cosmology and some of its doctrines conflict with the Abhidharma and other tantras, so it should not be accepted as an authentic Buddhist tantra.
- Cosmological differences from the Abhidharmakośa.
- Doctrinal peculiarities.

**Historical debate — Rendawa's critique of the Kālacakra** (late 14th c. (as recalled)): Reported in Tibetan histories; the Kālacakra was defended by Butön's and the Jonang traditions. The Kālacakra was accepted by all major schools; Rendawa is said to have moderated his view (low confidence).
