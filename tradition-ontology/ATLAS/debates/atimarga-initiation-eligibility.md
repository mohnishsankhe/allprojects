# Who may be initiated into the Atimārga observances: brahmin men only, or anyone?

`dsp:atimarga-initiation-eligibility` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G12

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
Only brahmin men: the disciple is a tested brahmin; 'no brahmin returns' restricts the path to brahmins; the practitioner must not speak with women or śūdras.
- PABh on PS 1.1 and 4.20; PS 1.12-17.
**Texts:** 
  - [Pañcārthabhāṣya](../texts/pancarthabhasya.md) — ref: on PS 1.1; 4.20
  - [Pāśupata Sūtra](../texts/pasupata-sutra.md) — ref: 1.12-17; 4.19-20
### [Kāpālika (Somasiddhānta)](../lineages/kapalika.md)
As reported by opponents: by entry into initiation one becomes a brahmin at once, and by the Kāpāla vow a man becomes an ascetic.
- Yāmuna, Āgamaprāmāṇya (opponent's report).
**Texts:** 
  - [Āgamaprāmāṇya](../texts/agamapramanya.md) — ref: Māheśvara section
### [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
Jain doxographers report that the Śaiva bharaṭas take the vow without regard to varṇa, and that even a slave who keeps the Śaiva initiation for twelve years attains nirvāṇa (report of the Naiyāyika-Śaivas' saying).
- Guṇaratna; Rājaśekhara.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tarkarahasyadīpikā](../texts/tarkarahasyadipika.md) — ref: Naiyāyika section
  - [Ṣaḍdarśanasamuccaya (Rājaśekhara Sūri)](../texts/saddarsanasamuccaya-rajasekhara.md) — ref: Śaiva section

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the Pāśupata restricts eligibility by birth; other Śaiva groups (as reported) do not.
**The traditions' own objections:** Kauṇḍinya's restriction to brahmins is explicit; a reconciliation must not erase it.
**Candidate readings:** P4-stage (adhikāra): each school defines qualification differently - which is itself the point at issue.; Different Śaiva orders at different periods may simply have differed in rule.
