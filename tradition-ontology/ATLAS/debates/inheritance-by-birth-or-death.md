# Does a son own ancestral property from birth or only on the father's death?

`dsp:inheritance-by-birth-or-death` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mitākṣarā school of Dharmaśāstra](../lineages/mitaksara-school.md)
By birth (janmasvatva): sons have ownership in ancestral property from birth; kinship (sapiṇḍa) is sharing particles of one body.
- ownership is known from worldly usage
- the text of Yājñavalkya on partition
**Texts:** 
  - [Mitākṣarā of Vijñāneśvara](../texts/mitaksara.md) — ref: on YS 2.114ff.
### [Dāyabhāga school of Dharmaśāstra (Bengal)](../lineages/dayabhaga-school.md)
By death (uparamasvatva): ownership passes only when the father dies, falls or renounces; heirship follows the capacity to benefit the deceased by piṇḍa offerings.
- the father's independent ownership
- spiritual benefit as the ground of heirship
**Texts:** 
  - [Dāyabhāga of Jīmūtavāhana](../texts/dayabhaga.md) — ref: ch. 1
