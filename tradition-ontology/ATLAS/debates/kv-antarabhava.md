# Is there an intermediate existence between death and rebirth?

`dsp:kv-antarabhava` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pudgalavāda](../lineages/pudgalavada.md)
There is an intermediate existence (held, per the commentary, by the Pubbaseliyas and Sammitīyas).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 8.2
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
There is no intermediate existence: rebirth-linking follows death immediately; the intermediate state is none of the three existences.
- It is neither sense-sphere, form nor formless existence.
- The death consciousness is immediately followed by rebirth-linking (Abhidhamma).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 8.2
### [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
An intermediate being (gandharva) exists between death and birth (Sarvāstivāda Abhidharma, as known from its own texts; U38).
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 3
