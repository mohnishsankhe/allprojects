# Does the funeral hymn enjoin the widow to rise to the living, or (by a variant reading) to enter the fire?

`dsp:rv-10-18-7-widow-reading` · `sourced` · confidence low


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
The transmitted text and the Padapāṭha read 'let the wives first (agre) ascend the seat' (10.18.7), and the next verse bids the widow 'rise to the world of the living' (10.18.8); the Atharvaveda describes her lying down beside the dead by 'ancient dharma' and then rising (AVŚ 18.3.1–2).
- Padapāṭha: agre; context of 10.18.8; AVŚ 18.3.1–3.

### [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)
A variant reading 'yonim agneḥ' ('the seat of fire') was cited in later times as Vedic sanction for a widow's self-immolation.
