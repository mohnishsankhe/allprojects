# Can the full ordination of nuns be introduced in the Mūlasarvāstivāda (Tibetan) tradition?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mūlasarvāstivāda](../lineages/mulasarvastivada.md)
Only by a procedure valid under the Mūlasarvāstivāda Vinaya, which requires a quorum of bhikṣuṇīs of the same lineage; such a lineage is absent in Tibet (conservative view).

**Texts:**
