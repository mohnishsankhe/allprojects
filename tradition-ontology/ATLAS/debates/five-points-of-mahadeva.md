# Does the arhat still have imperfections (the five points of Mahādeva)?

`dsp:five-points-of-mahadeva` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md)
Yes (as reported by opponents): an arhat can be tempted by others to an impure emission, still has non-knowing and doubt, is brought to understanding by others, and the path arises with an utterance.
- Reported only by the Sarvāstivāda and Theravāda sources.
**Texts:** 
  - [Samayabhedoparacanacakra](../texts/samayabhedoparacanacakra.md) — ref: first schism
  - [Abhidharma-mahāvibhāṣā-śāstra](../texts/mahavibhasa.md) — ref: fasc. 99
### [Pūrvaśaila](../lineages/purvasaila.md)
Yes (Kathāvatthu commentary on Kv 2.1–2.6 names the Pubbaseliyas and Aparaseliyas).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu-aṭṭhakathā](../texts/kathavatthu-atthakatha.md) — ref: on Kv 2.1–2.6
### [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
No: these are heresies; Mahādeva was a sinner who caused the schism (Vibhāṣā). Yet the Kośa itself holds that only the Buddha has destroyed non-defiled ignorance.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharma-mahāvibhāṣā-śāstra](../texts/mahavibhasa.md) — ref: fasc. 99
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 1.1 with bhāṣya
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
No: each of the points is refuted.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 2.1–2.6

**Historical debate — The first schism at Pāṭaliputra** (tradition: over a century after the parinirvāṇa, under Aśoka): Vasumitra; Mahāvibhāṣā (hostile) Sarvāstivāda: the majority (Mahāsāṃghika) followed Mahādeva, the elders withdrew

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** Points two and three (non-knowing, doubt) can be read as non-defiled ignorance and uncertainty about matters outside liberation, which the Sarvāstivāda itself attributes to arhats (AK 1.1 bhāṣya); the other points remain not yet reconciled.
Only the Buddha has destroyed darkness 'in every way' (AK 1.1), i.e. including non-defiled ignorance.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Vibhāṣā presents all five as heresy and would not accept that Mahādeva meant non-defiled ignorance; the Mahāsāṃghika's own wording is lost.
