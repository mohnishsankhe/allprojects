# When an Ekādaśī is 'pierced' by the Daśamī, on which day should the fast be kept?

`dsp:ekadasi-viddha` · `skeleton` · confidence low


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)
The Smārta digests decide by the extent of the tithi at sunrise and in the relevant part of the day, with their own rules on piercing (vedha).
- the tithi prevailing at sunrise governs
**Texts:** 
  - [Nirṇayasindhu of Kamalākara Bhaṭṭa](../texts/nirnayasindhu.md) — ref: ekādaśī section
  - [Kālamādhava (Kālanirṇaya)](../texts/kalamadhava.md) — ref: tithi rules
### [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)
Vaiṣṇavas reject an Ekādaśī touched by the Daśamī even at dawn (aruṇodaya) and fast on the following day (mahādvādaśī rules).
- the Daśamī-pierced Ekādaśī is impure for devotees
**Texts:** 
  - [Haribhaktivilāsa](../texts/haribhaktivilasa.md) — ref: 13

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path
**Explanation:** Each community keeps the timing rule of its own path: the Smārta rule for the general householder, the Vaiṣṇava rule for devotees who take the vow as devotion.
**The traditions' own objections:** Vaiṣṇava authorities hold their rule binding and the Smārta observance defective; Smārta digests do not accept the Vaiṣṇava extension as general law.
