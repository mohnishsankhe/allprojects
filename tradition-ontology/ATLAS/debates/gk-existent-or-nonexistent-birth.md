# Is birth of the existent or of the non-existent? (Kārikā 4.3–4.5)

`dsp:gk-existent-or-nonexistent-birth` · `sourced` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Some disputants hold birth of the existent, others (also wise) of the non-existent; the text says neither is born and that each side in refuting the other proclaims non-birth; the author approves that non-birth and does not dispute with them.
- Nothing existent is born; nothing non-existent is born (4.4).
