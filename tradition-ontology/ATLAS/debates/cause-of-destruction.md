# Does the destruction of a conditioned thing have a cause?

`dsp:cause-of-destruction` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vaibhāṣika](../lineages/vaibhasika.md)
Impermanence is one of the four real characteristics that acts on each dharma; destruction is characterized, though things are momentary.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 2.45
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 4.2-3
### [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md)
No: destruction is causeless and spontaneous; what arises perishes of itself; apparent destruction by fire is non-arising of a similar series.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośabhāṣya](../texts/abhidharmakosabhasya.md) — ref: 4.2-3
  - [Abhidharmakośabhāṣya](../texts/abhidharmakosabhasya.md) — ref: 2.46
### [Pudgalavāda](../lineages/pudgalavada.md)
Some formations are momentary, others endure for a time (Vasumitra).
**Texts:** 
  - [Samayabhedoparacanacakra](../texts/samayabhedoparacanacakra.md) — ref: Vātsīputrīya tenets

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the Vaibhāṣika holds a real entity where the Sautrāntika sees a designation.
**The traditions' own objections:** Each side holds the other's account inadequate: the Vaibhāṣika that designations cannot do causal work, the Sautrāntika that the posited entities are unnecessary and unattested in the sūtras.
