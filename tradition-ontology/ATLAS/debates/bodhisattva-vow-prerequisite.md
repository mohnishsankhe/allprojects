# Must one hold a prātimokṣa vow to take the bodhisattva vow?

`dsp:bodhisattva-vow-prerequisite` · `skeleton` · confidence low


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md)
Only those who always keep one of the seven kinds of prātimokṣa vow have the fortune for the bodhisattva vow, not others.
- The bodhisattva vow is built on the ethics of restraint.
**Texts:** 
  - [Lamp for the Path to Awakening (Bodhipathapradīpa; byang chub lam gyi sgron ma)](../texts/bodhipathapradipa.md) — ref: v20–22
### [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)
There are two bodhisattva-vow traditions: the one from Asaṅga (Cittamātra) requires a prior prātimokṣa vow; the one from Nāgārjuna and Śāntideva (Madhyamaka) can be given to anyone with the aspiration; each is to be conferred by its own rite (Sakya Paṇḍita).
- Different rites for different recipients; do not mix the procedures.
**Texts:** 
  - [Discrimination of the Three Vows (sdom pa gsum gyi rab tu dbye ba)](../texts/domsum-rabye.md) — ref: ch. 2

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage, P3-path
**Explanation:** Atiśa's condition applies to the rite of the Bodhisattvabhūmi (Asaṅga's tradition), which is the rite he names; the Madhyamaka rite has different entry conditions.
**The traditions' own objections:** A strict reading of Atiśa's verse makes the condition general.
