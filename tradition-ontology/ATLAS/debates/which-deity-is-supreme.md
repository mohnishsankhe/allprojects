# Which deity is the supreme Brahman — Śiva, Viṣṇu/Nārāyaṇa, or the Goddess?

### [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path, P7-arthavada
**Explanation:** The identity Upaniṣads read the names as one reality (Viṣṇu the heart of Śiva, Śiva of Viṣṇu), and each sectarian text's supremacy claim can be read as praise of the chosen deity.
Skanda 8–9, Rudrahṛdaya 1–5 and Kaivalya 8–10 identify the gods; the chosen-deity principle (P3) and the arthavāda reading of exaltation over rivals (P7) account for the sectarian texts.
**The traditions' own objections:** Sectarian theologians deny that their supremacy claims are mere praise; the Śarabha's subjugation of Nṛsiṃha is rejected by Vaiṣṇavas, and Śaiva authors reject the Mahā's account of Śiva's birth from Nārāyaṇa.
