# Is the material world composed of eternal partless atoms?

`dsp:atomism` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A5

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)
Yes: earth, water, fire and air consist of eternal, partless, spherical atoms which, moved by adṛṣṭa under the Lord's will, combine into dyads and larger wholes; division of wholes must end in them.
- VS 4.1.1–7; 7.1.26; NS 4.2.16–25; Praśastapāda's creation account.
**Texts:** 
  - [Vaiśeṣika Sūtra](../texts/vaisesika-sutra.md) — ref: 4.1.1–7; 7.1.26
  - [Nyāya Sūtra](../texts/nyaya-sutra.md) — ref: 4.2.16–25
  - [Padārthadharmasaṅgraha](../texts/padarthadharmasangraha.md) — ref: sṛṣṭi-saṃhāra
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
No: unconscious atoms cannot begin to move (adṛṣṭa being unconscious), inherence leads to regress, and atoms with colour and other qualities would be non-eternal and gross (Śaṅkara on Brahma Sūtra 2.2.11–17, as usually cited).
- BSBh 2.2.11–17.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 2.2.11–17
### `lin:yogacara`
No: atoms joining on six sides would have six parts; if they do not join, aggregates would be no larger than an atom (Vasubandhu).
- Viṃśatikā vv. 11–15 (range from memory).
**Texts:** 
  - `src:vimsatika` — ref: vv. 11–15
### [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
Atoms exist but are of one kind of matter (pudgala), taking the form of any element, and bond by their sticky and dry qualities.
- Tattvārtha Sūtra 5 (as usually cited).
**Texts:** 
  - `src:tattvartha-sutra` — ref: ch. 5
