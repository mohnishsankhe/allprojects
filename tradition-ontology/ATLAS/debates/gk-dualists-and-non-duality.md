# Do the dualists' positions conflict with the non-dual teaching? (Kārikā 3.17–3.18)

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Dualists, firm in their own doctrines, contradict one another; the non-dual teaching does not contradict them, since duality is said to be the difference (bheda) of the non-dual ultimate.
