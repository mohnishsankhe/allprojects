### [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md)
The self is never an object: it appears in every cognition of objects as its subject, as the goer is agent, not object, of going.
**Texts:** 
  - [Prakaraṇapañcikā](../texts/prakaranapancika.md) — ref: ch. 8
### [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
The Vṛttikāra: the self is known to itself (svasaṃvedya) and cannot be shown to another.
**Texts:** 
  - [Śābarabhāṣya](../texts/sabara-bhasya.md) — ref: 1.1.5
