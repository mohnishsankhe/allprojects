# Should Chan meditation and Pure Land recitation be practised together?

`dsp:chan-pure-land-dual-practice` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)
Yes: both return to the one source; Chan is the Chan of Pure Land and Pure Land the Pure Land of Chan; practise one gate deeply; all good practices share one end.
**Texts:** 
  - [Resolving Doubts about the Pure Land (Jingtu yibian)](../texts/jingtu-yibian.md) — ref: 420a
  - [The Myriad Good Practices Share the Same End (Wanshan tonggui ji)](../texts/wanshan-tonggui-ji.md) — ref: 966b
  - [Questions on the Pure Land (Jingtu huowen)](../texts/jingtu-huowen.md) — ref: 295a
### [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md)
Mixing practices loses birth; exclusive practice alone is right.
**Texts:** 
  - [Hymns of Worship for Birth (Wangsheng lizan ji)](../texts/wangsheng-lizan.md) — ref: 439b
### [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
Recitation is for those of lesser capacity, or a huatou; seeking a Buddha outside is contrary to seeing one's nature (as reported).
**Texts:** 
  - [Resolving Doubts about the Pure Land (Jingtu yibian)](../texts/jingtu-yibian.md) — ref: 420a11

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path
**Explanation:** Zhuhong's 'return to the source is not two, expedient gates are many' reconciles by path, while urging depth in one gate.
**The traditions' own objections:** Shandao's line (and Jōdo-shū) warns that mixed practice fails; Chan purists deny the need.
