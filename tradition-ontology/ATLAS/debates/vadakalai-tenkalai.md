# What divides the northern (Vaṭakalai) and southern (Teṅkalai) schools of Śrīvaiṣṇavism — the 'eighteen differences' (aṣṭādaśa-bheda)?

`dsp:vadakalai-tenkalai` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A6; G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md)
Surrender is a means the soul performs once, with its limbs, and the occasion of the Lord's grace ('the young monkey clings'); Śrī is all-pervading, of the Lord's rank, and with him the means and goal; the Lord overlooks faults rather than relishing them; scriptural duties bind the surrendered as the Lord's command, and deliberate offences need expiation; kaivalya is not permanent.
- scripture enjoins surrender as a means (Gītā 18.66, the Upaniṣads, Pāñcarātra)
- a grace with no occasion would make the Lord partial
- Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.8.17: as Viṣṇu is all-pervading, so is Śrī
- the Catuḥślokī: even the Lord cannot measure Śrī's greatness
**Texts:** 
  - [Rahasyatrayasāra](../texts/rahasyatrayasara.md) — ref: Upāyavibhāga; Parikaravibhāga; Paradevatāpāramārthya; Uttarakṛtya; Aparādhaparihāra
  - [Nikṣeparakṣā](../texts/niksepa-raksa.md) — ref: passim
  - [Rahasyarakṣā](../texts/rahasyaraksa.md) — ref: on the Catuḥślokī
### [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md)
The Lord alone is the means; surrender is not a means but acceptance fitting the soul's dependence ('the kitten is carried'); grace is causeless; Śrī is the mediator, not the means; the Lord relishes his devotees' faults; duties are kept as service and example, not as means or under threat; the teacher's regard saves; kaivalya is permanent.
- the carama-śloka says 'me alone' and 'abandon all dharmas', including one's own surrender as means
- the soul's nature is total dependence (pāratantrya)
- the Lord's motherly love (vātsalya) relishes faults
- the Tiruvāymoḻi and its commentaries
**Texts:** 
  - [Śrīvacanabhūṣaṇam](../texts/srivacana-bhusanam.md) — ref: 1; 2; 3; 6
  - [Mumukṣuppaṭi](../texts/mumuksuppadi.md) — ref: 3
  - [Śrīvacanabhūṣaṇa-vyākhyānam](../texts/srivacana-bhusana-vyakhyana.md) — ref: passim

**Historical debate — Formation of the two schools** (13th–15th c. (doctrine); 17th–19th c. (separate institutions, marks and temple rights)): Each school traces its view to Rāmānuja through its own teacher-line; later polemical works list eighteen points of difference, whose exact list and author are not reconstructed here. Both continue as living schools; disputes over temple rights (e.g. the forehead mark and invocations at Kāñcī) went to the courts in the colonial period (recalled; low confidence).

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the schools disagree on whether the soul's surrender is a means and on Śrī's rank; see the sub-disputes, some partially reconciled.
**The traditions' own objections:** Each school regards its points as doctrinal, not a matter of emphasis; neither accepts that the other's view is merely another standpoint.
**Candidate readings:** P2-standpoint: the Teṅkalai speak from the divine standpoint (the Lord alone saves), the Vaṭakalai from the seeker's (surrender is what the seeker does); P4-stage: the Teṅkalai stress what fits the utterly helpless; the Vaṭakalai what scripture enjoins on the qualified; shared ground: both hold the Lord as siddhopāya, surrender once, no last remembrance needed, release at the end of the body, eternal service
