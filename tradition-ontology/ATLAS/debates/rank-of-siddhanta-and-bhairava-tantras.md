# Are the dualist Siddhānta scriptures or the non-dual Bhairava tantras the higher revelation?

**Coverage:** A4, A7, I2

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md)
Śiva's teaching in ten, eighteen and sixty-four divisions has the Trika as its essence and the Mālinīvijayottara as the essence of that; the Śiva-, Rudra- and Bhairava-tantras teach difference, difference-and-non-difference and non-difference in ascending order.
- TĀ 1.18
- Jayaratha's derivation from the faces of Sadāśiva
**Texts:** 
  - [Tantrāloka](../texts/tantraloka.md) — ref: 1.18
  - [Tantrālokaviveka](../texts/tantraloka-viveka.md) — ref: 1.18
### [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
The Siddhānta of the upper stream is Śiva's definitive teaching; the soul remains distinct and in liberation becomes equal to Śiva; non-dual readings of the scriptures are rejected.
- Nārāyaṇakaṇṭha's and Rāmakaṇṭha's dualist exegesis (recorded from memory)
