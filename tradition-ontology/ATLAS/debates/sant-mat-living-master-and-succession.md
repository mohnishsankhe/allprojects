# Is a living master necessary, and how is the true successor known?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Radha Soami Satsang Beas](../lineages/radha-soami-satsang-beas.md)
A living Sant Satguru is indispensable; each master designates his successor; Jaimal Singh was authorized by Soamiji to initiate in the Punjab, and the Beas line continues by designation.
- past masters cannot initiate or guide the living

### [Radhasoami Satsang, Agra lines (Soamibagh, Peepal Mandi / Huzuri Bhawan, Dayalbagh)](../lineages/radhasoami-agra.md)
The Agra lines trace the true succession through Soamiji's own circle (Rai Salig Ram and after, or Soamiji's family at Soamibagh, or the Dayalbagh line) and have not accepted the other lines' claims.
**Texts:** 
  - [Prem Patra (Radhasoami)](../texts/prem-patra.md) — 
  - [Discourses on Radhasoami Faith](../texts/discourses-on-radhasoami-faith.md) — 
### [Dharamdāsī Kabīr Panth (Chhattisgarhī śākhā)](../lineages/dharamdasi-kabir-panth.md)
The Dharamdāsī Kabīr Panth locates authority in a hereditary line of forty-two ācāryas promised by Kabīr, not in spiritual designation.
**Texts:** 
  - [Anurāg Sāgar](../texts/anurag-sagar.md) —
