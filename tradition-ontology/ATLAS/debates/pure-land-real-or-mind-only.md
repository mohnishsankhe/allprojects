# Is the Pure Land a real land in the West, or the purified mind only?

`dsp:pure-land-real-or-mind-only` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)
The Western land is to be sought really; birth there is the 'birth of non-birth'; mind-only and the Western land are not two, and those whose power is not sufficient must be born there.
- Only those who can live in a latrine without disgust may say every place is the West (Zhuhong).
- The wise seek birth ardently while knowing its nature cannot be found (Shiyi lun).
**Texts:** 
  - [The Myriad Good Practices Share the Same End (Wanshan tonggui ji)](../texts/wanshan-tonggui-ji.md) — ref: 966b26
  - [Resolving Doubts about the Pure Land (Jingtu yibian)](../texts/jingtu-yibian.md) — ref: 420a23
  - [Ten Doubts about the Pure Land (Jingtu shiyi lun)](../texts/jingtu-shiyi-lun.md) — ref: 78a
### [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
The Pure Land is the pure mind; 'as the mind is pure the buddha-land is pure'; Amitābha is one's own nature (as reported by Pure Land writers answering the view).
**Texts:** 
  - [Platform Sūtra of the Sixth Patriarch (Liuzu tanjing)](../texts/platform-sutra.md) — ref: chapter on doubts
  - [Questions on the Pure Land (Jingtu huowen)](../texts/jingtu-huowen.md) — ref: 295a
### [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md)
The Pure Land is pointed to in a direction and given form (Shandao) for ordinary beings; it is a reward land, not merely mind.
**Texts:** 
  - [Commentary on the Contemplation Sūtra (Guanjing shu)](../texts/guanjing-shu.md) — ref: fasc. 1
### [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)
The Buddha seen in the samādhi neither comes nor goes; mind makes the buddha (Pratyutpanna; Contemplation Sūtra 8).
**Texts:** 
  - [Pratyutpannabuddhasaṃmukhāvasthitasamādhi-sūtra](../texts/pratyutpanna-samadhi-sutra.md) — ref: 3
  - [Amitāyurdhyāna-sūtra (Guan wuliangshou jing)](../texts/amitayurdhyana-sutra.md) — ref: 8

**Historical debate — Huiyuan's question to Kumārajīva** (c. 405): Dasheng dayi zhang (T1856). Kumārajīva: the vision is not false; seen by the Buddha's power, the samādhi and one's merit.

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P1-level, P6-upaya
**Explanation:** Chinese Pure Land masters reconcile mind-only (ultimate level) with the Western land (conventional level, a skilful means for those of insufficient power) — a reconciliation made within the tradition.
Tanluan's birth of non-birth, the Jingtu shiyi lun's appeal to the two truths, Yanshou and Zhuhong.
**The traditions' own objections:** Jōdo-shū and Shinshū reject dissolving the Western land into mind; Chan radicals reject the need for any land.
