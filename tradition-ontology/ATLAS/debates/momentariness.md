# Is everything that exists momentary?

`dsp:momentariness` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A5

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
Yes: whatever exists is momentary, since existence is causal efficacy and an enduring thing could produce its effects neither all at once nor successively; recognition is a construction over a series.
- The argument from causal efficacy (arthakriyā), as reported by Udayana and in NS 3.2.10.
**Texts:** 
  - [Nyāya Sūtra](../texts/nyaya-sutra.md) — ref: 3.2.10 (as pūrvapakṣa)
  - [Ātmatattvaviveka](../texts/atmatattvaviveka.md) — ref: kṣaṇabhaṅga section
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
No: there is no reason for such a universal rule; things should be accepted as they are seen; causes of arising and destruction are observed only where things arise and perish; recognition shows enduring things and an enduring self; an enduring thing produces effects successively as auxiliaries join it.
- NS 3.2.11–17; 4.1.25–28; Udayana's Ātmatattvaviveka.
**Texts:** 
  - [Nyāya Sūtra](../texts/nyaya-sutra.md) — ref: 3.2.10–17; 4.1.25–28
  - [Ātmatattvaviveka](../texts/atmatattvaviveka.md) — ref: kṣaṇabhaṅga section
### [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
Two proofs: from destruction — destruction has no cause, so a thing perishes by nature as soon as it arises (Dharmakīrti); from existence — whatever exists is momentary, since an enduring thing could be causally efficacious neither successively nor at once (Ratnakīrti: yat sat tat kṣaṇikam).
- PV svārthānumāna chapter; Kṣaṇabhaṅgasiddhi
**Texts:** 
  - [Pramāṇavārttika](../texts/pramanavarttika.md) — ref: 3.273-276 (e-text)
  - [Kṣaṇabhaṅgasiddhi (Ratnakīrti)](../texts/ksanabhangasiddhi-ratnakirti.md) — 
### [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
A real is characterized by origination, destruction and permanence together: substances endure while their modes arise and perish (Tattvārtha Sūtra 5.30, as recalled).
- anekānta
**Texts:** 
  - [Tattvārthasūtra](../texts/tattvartha-sutra.md) — ref: 5.30 (recalled)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Recognition and memory require an enduring knower; universal momentariness cannot even be stated coherently (Śaṅkara on BS 2.2.25, as recalled).
- anusmṛti
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 2.2.25 (recalled)
