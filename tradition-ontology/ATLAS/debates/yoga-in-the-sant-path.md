# What place, if any, has yogic discipline (haṭha, aṣṭāṅga, kuṇḍalinī) on the Sant path of the Name?

`dsp:yoga-in-the-sant-path` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md)
None as a means: sahaj is best — no closing of eyes, stopping of ears or mortification; the yogī dyes his clothes, not his mind; the Name and love suffice.
- forced yoga breeds pride and display
- the Lord is found by love and the Name
**Texts:** 
  - [Kabīr Granthāvalī](../texts/kabir-granthavali.md) — 
  - [The oral and popular corpus of Kabīr (sākhīs, dohās, padas and bhajans)](../texts/kabir-oral-corpus.md) — 
### [Dādū Panth](../lineages/dadu-panth.md)
Sundardās teaches aṣṭāṅga and haṭha yoga (postures, breath, cakras, the serpent power) as a stage within the Sant path, below devotion and knowledge.
**Texts:** 
  - [Jñān Samudra (Gyān Samudra)](../texts/jnana-samudra-sundardas.md) — ref: yoga ullāsa
### [Caraṇdāsī sampradāya](../lineages/charandasi.md)
Caraṇdās teaches the eight limbs of yoga to his disciples as a support of devotion to Kṛṣṇa and the guru.
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhakti Sāgar (collected works of Caraṇdās)](../texts/bhakti-sagar-charandas.md) — ref: aṣṭāṅga yoga
### [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md)
Only the yoga of the sound current, beginning at the eye centre, is needed; haṭha and kuṇḍalinī work on the lower centres is unnecessary and hazardous; surat-śabd yoga is the crown of yogas.
**Texts:** 
  - [The Crown of Life](../texts/crown-of-life.md) — 

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage, P3-path
**Explanation:** Sundardās and Caraṇdās themselves place yoga as a preparatory stage (P4) under the Name and devotion; temperaments differ (P3).
Claims only that the Sant tradition contains this graded view; it does not claim that Kabīr's verses or Sant Mat accept yoga as a stage.
**The traditions' own objections:** Kabīr's verses reject forced yoga outright; Sant Mat rejects lower-centre yoga as unnecessary; for the Nāth side of the wider question see dsp:kabir-and-the-yogis (U21).
