# How is another family's revelation to be placed — as a lower stage of one's own, as a deliberate delusion, as a subdued power, or as a partial standpoint?

**Coverage:** C

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md)
All philosophies are roles of the one conscious Lord: the Buddhists rest in the intellect-principle, the Jains in the qualities, the Vedic knowers in the Person, the Pāñcarātrikas in the unmanifest; the Trika alone knows the self as both transcendent and immanent.
**Texts:** 
  - [Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya](../texts/pratyabhijnahrdayam.md) — ref: 8 with commentary
### [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
Maheśvara and the worldly gods are subdued by Vajrapāṇi and given places in the Buddha's maṇḍala; they are worldly powers, not the goal.
**Texts:** 
  - [Sarvatathāgatatattvasaṃgraha](../texts/sarvatathagatatattvasamgraha.md) — ref: part 2
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
The Buddha and the naked ascetics' teachers are Viṣṇu's deluding descent, sent to lead the enemies of the gods away from the Veda.
**Texts:** 
  - [Viṣṇu Purāṇa](../texts/visnu-purana.md) — ref: 3.17-18
  - [Agni Purāṇa](../texts/agni-purana.md) — ref: 16
### [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
Every other doctrine is a one-sided standpoint; the Jina's word is the aggregate of all the wrong views, each true only when joined with the others.
**Texts:** 
  - [Sanmatitarka (Sammaisutta)](../texts/sanmati-tarka.md) — ref: 3.47, 3.69
### [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
Whatever is acceptable in the Vedāntins' teaching is acceptable only as the emptiness the Buddha taught.
**Texts:** 
  - [Madhyamakahṛdayakārikā](../texts/madhyamakahrdaya.md) — ref: 8
