# Does the statement 'all things are empty' refute itself?

`dsp:can-emptiness-be-asserted` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** B4

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
If all things lack own-nature, the statement 'all things lack own-nature' also lacks it and cannot negate anything; if it has own-nature, not all things are empty. Either way the thesis fails.
- An unreal statement cannot negate.
- A real statement is a counter-example.
**Texts:** 
  - [Vigrahavyāvartanī](../texts/vigrahavyavartani.md) — ref: 1-4 (the objector, as reported by Nāgārjuna)
### [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
My words are dependently arisen and so empty, yet they function, as empty carts and pots do their work; they negate as one magical man stops another. And I have no thesis, so the faults of a thesis do not touch me.
- Emptiness is not non-existence but dependent origination (VV 22).
- Empty things still function (VV 22–23).
- No thesis is advanced (VV 29).
**Texts:** 
  - [Vigrahavyāvartanī](../texts/vigrahavyavartani.md) — ref: 21-29, 70
