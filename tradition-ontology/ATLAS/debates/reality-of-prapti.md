# Is possession (prāpti) a real entity?

`dsp:reality-of-prapti` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vaibhāṣika](../lineages/vaibhasika.md)
Yes: a real dissociated force linking dharmas to a series; the state of an ordinary person is non-possession of the noble path.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 2.35-36
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 2.40
### [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md)
No: 'possession' is the intact seed in the series; the ordinary person is the series in which the noble dharmas have not arisen.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośabhāṣya](../texts/abhidharmakosabhasya.md) — ref: 2.36
  - [Abhidharmakośabhāṣya](../texts/abhidharmakosabhasya.md) — ref: 2.40

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
