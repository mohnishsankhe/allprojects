# Was haṭha yoga taught by Śiva (Ādinātha) through the Nāth siddhas, or did its earliest methods arise in a Buddhist tantric milieu?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
Haṭha was taught by Śrī Ādinātha (Śiva) and known by Matsyendra, Gorakṣa and the other great siddhas, from whom Svātmārāma received it.
**Texts:** 
  - [Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā (Haṭhapradīpikā)](../texts/hatha-yoga-pradipika.md) — ref: 1.1-9
### [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md)
The Tibetan canon places the Amṛtasiddhi among the siddhas' works, opening with homage to Heruka and ascribed to the great master Virūpa.
**Texts:** 
  - [Amṛtasiddhimūla](../texts/amrtasiddhimula.md) — ref: Tōh 2285
