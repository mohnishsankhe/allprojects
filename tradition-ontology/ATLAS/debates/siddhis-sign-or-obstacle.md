## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** The Yogaśikhā itself distinguishes contrived powers (from substances, rites, mantras — impermanent) from spontaneous ones (arising in those free of vāsanās); the former are obstacles, the latter signs.
Warnings concern powers sought or displayed; the Yogaśikhā's praise concerns powers that arise unsought, which it also says are to be kept secret.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Yogatattva makes no such distinction and treats all powers as obstacles.
