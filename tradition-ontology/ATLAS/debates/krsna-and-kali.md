# Is Kṛṣṇa a form of Kālī, or is the Goddess a power of Kṛṣṇa?

`dsp:krsna-and-kali` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md)
(Toḍala) The ten Mahāvidyās are the ten avatāras; 'Bhagavatī Kālī herself arose as the form of Kṛṣṇa' (10.11); Kālī is the source of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva (Nirvāṇa Tantra 10.42-43; Karpūrādistotra 12).
**Texts:** 
  - [Toḍala Tantra](../texts/todala-tantra.md) — ref: 10.9-11
  - [Nirvāṇa Tantra](../texts/nirvana-tantra.md) — ref: 10.42-43
### [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)
(Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava) Kṛṣṇa is the Lord himself ('kṛṣṇas tu bhagavān svayam', Bhāgavata 1.3.28); Durgā is his one power of creation, maintenance and dissolution, sustaining the worlds like his shadow and acting by his will (Brahmasaṃhitā 5.44).
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhāgavata Purāṇa](../texts/bhagavata-purana.md) — ref: 1.3.28
  - [Brahma Saṃhitā (fifth chapter)](../texts/brahma-samhita.md) — ref: 5.44

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path, P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** Each tradition describes the relation of Goddess and Kṛṣṇa from the standpoint of its chosen deity (iṣṭa-devatā); both agree the two are inseparable as power and power-holder.
Jīva Gosvāmin's commentary on the Brahmasaṃhitā itself cites 'yaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ saiva durgā' and speaks of the non-difference of power and powerful; the Toḍala makes Kṛṣṇa Kālī's form. The reconciliation records complementary standpoints only.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Gauḍīya tradition rejects any subordination of Kṛṣṇa to the Goddess; the Śākta texts reject the Goddess's subordination to Kṛṣṇa. Both reject the claim that the two views are simply the same.
