# May monks reside permanently in temples and manage their property?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Caityavāsī (temple-dwelling monks)](../lineages/caityavasi.md)
The temple-dwelling monks (as reported by their opponents): residence in and care of temples serves the tradition and the laity.
- Custom of the time; royal patronage

### [Kharatara Gaccha](../lineages/kharatara-gaccha.md)
No: monks must live in lodgings given by laypeople, wander, and own nothing, as the Āgamas prescribe.
- The canonical rules of wandering and non-possession
- Haribhadra's criticism of the temple-dwellers
**Texts:** 
  - [Prabhāvakacarita](../texts/prabhavakacarita.md) — 
  - [Uttarādhyayana Sūtra](../texts/uttaradhyayana-sutra.md) — ref: 24

**Historical debate — Debate at Aṇahilapāṭaka** (1024 CE (Vikrama 1080)): Kharatara accounts: before King Durlabharāja, Jineśvara defeated the temple-dwellers and won the epithet 'kharatara'. Vasativāsī monks allowed to reside in the capital; origin of the Kharatara Gaccha.
