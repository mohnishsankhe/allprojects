## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** The Gītā gives five causes of every action — the body, the agent, the instruments, the activities and daiva (18.14) — so each position names one causal factor from its own standpoint; Bhīṣma's seed-and-field image joins fate and effort (13.6.7).
**The traditions' own objections:** Svabhāva-vāda and kāla-vāda as held by their proponents deny that effort is a cause at all; the reconciliation does not claim they would accept being one factor among five.
