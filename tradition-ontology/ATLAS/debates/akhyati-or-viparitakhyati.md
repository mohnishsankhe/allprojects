# What appears in perceptual error ('this is silver' seen in shell)?

`dsp:akhyati-or-viparitakhyati` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A5

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md)
Nothing unreal appears: there are two cognitions, a perception of 'this' and a memory of silver that has lost its 'that', and their difference is not grasped (akhyāti); every cognition as such is valid.
- No cognition can present what is not there; error is explained by non-apprehension alone.
**Texts:** 
  - [Prakaraṇapañcikā](../texts/prakaranapancika.md) — ref: ch. 3 (Nayavīthī), ch. 6
### [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md)
Error presents a thing as other than it is: the shell and the silver are real, only the relation shown is unreal (viparītakhyāti); non-apprehension cannot explain the double moon or a mistaken direction.
- Śāstradīpikā on MS 1.1.5; Maṇḍana's Vibhramaviveka.
**Texts:** 
  - [Śāstradīpikā](../texts/sastradipika.md) — ref: 1.1.5
  - [Vibhramaviveka](../texts/vibhramaviveka.md) — ref: whole
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
Error apprehends the object as possessing a character it lacks (anyathākhyāti), the silver being known through a special contact.
