# Does a gift to the Buddha yield more merit than a gift to the Saṅgha?

`dsp:merit-of-gifts-buddha-or-sangha` · `skeleton` · confidence low


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Dharmaguptaka](../lineages/dharmaguptaka.md)
Yes: though included in the Saṅgha, the Buddha as a separate field yields greater fruit; offerings to stūpas bring great fruit (Vasumitra).
**Texts:** 
  - [Samayabhedoparacanacakra](../texts/samayabhedoparacanacakra.md) — ref: Dharmaguptaka tenets
### [Mahīśāsaka](../lineages/mahisasaka.md)
No: the Buddha is included in the Saṅgha, so a gift to the Saṅgha has great fruit (Vasumitra).
**Texts:** 
  - [Samayabhedoparacanacakra](../texts/samayabhedoparacanacakra.md) — ref: Mahīśāsaka tenets
### [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
Merit depends on giver, thing and field; merit arises even at a stūpa where no one receives.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 4.117, 4.121
