# Are the four quarters of the yuga equal, or in the proportion 4:3:2:1?

### [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)
Brahmagupta and the Sūrya Siddhānta: Kṛta, Tretā, Dvāpara and Kali are four, three, two and one tenths of the yuga, following the feet of dharma; 'not one of Āryabhaṭa's quarters agrees with the smṛti'.
- the smṛti's authority
- the decline of dharma by quarters
**Texts:** 
  - [Brāhmasphuṭasiddhānta](../texts/brahmasphuta-siddhanta.md) — ref: 1.7-9; 11
  - [Sūrya Siddhānta](../texts/surya-siddhanta.md) — ref: 1.15-17
