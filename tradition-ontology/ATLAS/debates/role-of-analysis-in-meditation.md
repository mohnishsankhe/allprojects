# Is conceptual analysis part of meditation, or must all thought be stopped?

`dsp:role-of-analysis-in-meditation` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md)
Analytical meditation is indispensable: faith, renunciation, bodhicitta and the view are produced by analysis; insight arises only when analysis itself induces pliancy; stopping all thought is not realisation of emptiness but a blank state.
- A fainted person would otherwise be realised (Kamalaśīla).
- Placement alone does not transform the mind on topics needing conviction.
**Texts:** 
  - [Great Treatise on the Stages of the Path to Enlightenment (lam rim chen mo)](../texts/lamrim-chenmo.md) — ref: intro and insight
### [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md)
Conceptualisation is great ignorance and saṃsāra itself; after ascertaining by scripture and reasoning that phenomena are unborn, one meditates without conceptualisation (Atiśa).
- Nirvāṇa is the abandoning of all conceptualisation.
- The Dhāraṇī of Entering the Non-conceptual.
**Texts:** 
  - [Lamp for the Path to Awakening (Bodhipathapradīpa; byang chub lam gyi sgron ma)](../texts/bodhipathapradipa.md) — ref: v56–60
### [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)
Sakya Paṇḍita: a Mahāmudrā of mere thoughtlessness without the wisdom of empowerment is the cause of animal or formless rebirth; genuine non-conceptual wisdom comes through empowerment and the two stages.
- The 'white self-sufficient remedy' is not the Buddha's teaching.
**Texts:** 
  - [Discrimination of the Three Vows (sdom pa gsum gyi rab tu dbye ba)](../texts/domsum-rabye.md) — ref: ch. 3

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** All three place reasoned ascertainment before non-conceptual meditation; Atiśa's non-conceptual meditation follows analysis (v60), which is the Gelug sequence of analysis leading to non-dual realisation.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Gelug denies that non-conceptuality can be produced by merely stopping thought; teachers of direct-introduction methods (outside this unit) would object that analysis is not required for every practitioner.
