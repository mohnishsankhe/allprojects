# Are the four varṇas given by birth from the cosmic Person (RV 10.90.12)?

`dsp:varna-origin-purusa-sukta` · `sourced` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
The Puruṣa Sūkta: the brāhmaṇa was the Person's mouth, the rājanya his arms, the vaiśya his thighs, the śūdra born from his feet — the order of society belongs to the cosmic sacrifice.
- The verse and its later use in the Dharmaśāstras as the charter of varṇa by birth.
**Texts:** 
  - [Ṛgveda Saṃhitā](../texts/rgveda.md) — ref: 10.90.12
### [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md)
The Buddha mocks the brahmins' claim to be 'born from Brahmā's mouth' when they are seen born from women (Aggañña Sutta), and teaches that one is a brahmin not by birth but by action (Vāseṭṭha Sutta).
- Birth from women is visible to all; conduct, not birth, makes the brahmin.
**Texts:** 
  - [Dīgha Nikāya](../texts/digha-nikaya.md) — ref: DN 27
  - [Sutta Nipāta](../texts/sutta-nipata.md) — ref: 3.9
### [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
Within the Vedic literature, the seers accepted Kavaṣa Ailūṣa, 'son of a slave woman', when the waters and gods showed him favour (Aitareya Brāhmaṇa 2.19, low confidence).
- Divine favour overrides birth in this episode.
**Texts:** 
  - [Aitareya Brāhmaṇa](../texts/aitareya-brahmana.md) — ref: 2.19
