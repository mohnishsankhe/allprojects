# Can devotion to God be a full aesthetic rasa, or only a bhāva?

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** The poeticians speak of worldly spectators' relish of poetry, Rūpa of the devotee's relish of a transcendent object — different standpoints on 'rasa'.
Rūpa himself distinguishes worldly (prākṛta) rasa from devotional rasa, so the standpoint distinction is his own; it does not settle whether the poeticians' denial is mistaken.
**The traditions' own objections:** Rūpa's school holds bhakti-rasa to be rasa in the full and highest sense; the poeticians do not grant it rasa status.
