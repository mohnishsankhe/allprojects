# Is the mantra way superior to the perfection way?

`dsp:mantra-and-paramita-ways` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
Same goal, but the mantra way is superior: unconfused, rich in means, not difficult, for the sharp; it gives buddhahood in this very life.
- Nayatrayapradīpa's four features, quoted by Advayavajra.
- Pañcakrama 3.12: buddhahood in this birth.
**Texts:** 
  - [Nayatrayapradīpa](../texts/nayatrayapradipa.md) — 
  - [Tattvaratnāvalī](../texts/tattvaratnavali.md) — ref: end
  - [Pañcakrama](../texts/pancakrama.md) — ref: 3.12
### [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md)
Neither mantra nor pāramitā as systems sees clear-light mahāmudrā; the innate is beyond both.
- Tilopa: those who speak of mantra, pāramitā and Vinaya with their tenets do not see clear light.
- Saraha: 'I have not seen a single mantra or tantra'.
**Texts:** 
  - [Mahāmudropadeśa of Tilopa (the Gaṅgā Mahāmudrā)](../texts/ganga-mahamudra.md) — ref: v21-22
  - [Dohākoṣa of Saraha (the 'People Dohā')](../texts/dohakosa-saraha.md) — ref: v41
### [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)
The perfection way is itself a complete path to buddhahood over three incalculable aeons (the sūtra position as presented in the tantric doxographies).
- The goal is one; the tantras concede the identity of the goal ('ekārthatve 'pi').
**Texts:** 
  - [Tattvaratnāvalī](../texts/tattvaratnavali.md) — ref: end

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path, P4-stage
**Explanation:** All sides agree the goal is one; the difference is in means suited to temperament and faculty (P3) and in the practitioner's stage and qualification (P4).
**The traditions' own objections:** The Vajrayāna holds the mantra way strictly superior, not merely different; the siddhas decline to rank systems at all.
