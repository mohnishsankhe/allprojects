# Is the Vrātya an exalted cosmic figure or an outsider to be brought into the Vedic fold?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Śaunaka śākhā (Atharvaveda)](../lineages/sakha-saunaka.md)
The Atharvaveda's Vrātya book exalts the Vrātya: he stirs Prajāpati, becomes Mahādeva and Īśāna, and a king should honour the knowing Vrātya above himself.
- AVŚ 15 throughout.
**Texts:** 
  - [Atharvaveda Saṃhitā (Śaunaka)](../texts/atharvaveda-saunaka.md) — ref: 15
### [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
The Sāmaveda Brāhmaṇa tradition prescribes the vrātyastoma rites by which vrātyas — people living outside Vedic rules — are admitted to Vedic society.
- Pañcaviṃśa Brāhmaṇa 17.1–4 (reference from memory).
**Texts:** 
  - [Pañcaviṃśa Brāhmaṇa](../texts/pancavimsa-brahmana.md) — ref: 17.1–4
