### [Śaunaka śākhā (Atharvaveda)](../lineages/sakha-saunaka.md)
The Atharvaveda's Vrātya book exalts the Vrātya: he stirs Prajāpati, becomes Mahādeva and Īśāna, and a king should honour the knowing Vrātya above himself.

The Sāmaveda Brāhmaṇa tradition prescribes the vrātyastoma rites by which vrātyas — people living outside Vedic rules — are admitted to Vedic society.
