# Is liberation reached by the yogī's body-discipline, or by the Name and the inner guru that the Sants set above it?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
The yogī reaches the supreme through the guru, restraint, bindu, the unstruck sound and sahaja; the Nāths themselves reject outward marks and sectarian worship ('neither temple nor mosque').
- Gorakh Bānī sayings on restraint, the guru and the supreme state.
**Texts:** 
  - [Gorakh Bānī](../texts/gorakhbani.md) — ref: sabadī
### [Kabīr Panth](../lineages/kabir-panth.md)
Earrings, ash, matted hair and bodily yoga do not bring one to Rām; only love of the Name and the true guru's word do. In the Kabīr–Gorakh Goṣṭhī Kabīr is shown overcoming Gorakh.
- Kabīr's sayings against the yogī's garb and practices (Bījak).
- Kabīr–Gorakh Goṣṭhī.
**Texts:** 
  - [Bījak](../texts/bijak.md) — ref: ramainī/sabad (refs not recalled)
  - [Kabīr–Gorakh Goṣṭhī](../texts/kabir-gorakh-gosthi.md) — ref: whole
