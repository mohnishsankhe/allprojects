# Is Siddha medicine an independent Tamil science from Śiva and Agastya, or a regional form of the Āyurvedic system?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Siddha medicine (cittā maruttuvam)](../lineages/siddha-medicine.md)
Siddha medicine was revealed by Śiva through Nandi to the Siddhars and set down by Agastya in Tamil; its 96 principles, pulse lore, 4,448 diseases, mineral-metal pharmacy and kaṟpam are its own; the Kuṟaḷ already names the three humours in Tamil.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tirukkuṟaḷ](../texts/tirukkural.md) — ref: 941
  - [Akattiyar vaittiya cintāmaṇi](../texts/akattiyar-vaittiya-cintamani.md) — ref: passim
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
Āyurveda traces medicine from Brahmā through the Aśvins and Indra to Bharadvāja and Dhanvantari; the tridoṣa, seven dhātus and rasāyana are its categories, found in Caraka and Suśruta.
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Sūtrasthāna 1
