## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
Some: non-addiction (avyasana) to sounds and other objects; some: lawful enjoyment not contrary [to dharma]; others: contact with objects at will; some: cognition of objects free of pleasure and pain in the absence of attachment and aversion.

### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
The sūtra and bhāṣya: supreme mastery is that, when the mind is stilled, the senses are stilled with it and need no further effortful means.
**Texts:**
