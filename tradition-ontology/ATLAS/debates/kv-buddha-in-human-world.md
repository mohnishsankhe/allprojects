# Did the Buddha really live in the human world and himself teach?

`dsp:kv-buddha-in-human-world` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vetulyakas (as named in the Kathāvatthu commentary and Sri Lankan chronicles)](../lineages/vetulyaka.md)
It should not be said that the Buddha lived in the human world, nor that he himself taught the Dhamma (a created form taught it) (held, per the commentary, by the Vetulyakas).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 18.1
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
The Buddha was born, lived, awakened and taught in the human world; there are shrines, parks and villages where he dwelt.
- The places where he dwelt are known.
- He is called the teacher.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 18.1
