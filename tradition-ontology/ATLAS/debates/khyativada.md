# What appears in erroneous cognition? (theories of error)

`dsp:khyativada` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G (Sāṃkhya against rival theories)

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
The existent-and-non-existent (sadasatkhyāti): the erroneous object is sublated as there and not sublated as real elsewhere.
- Error is not the appearance of the non-existent, nor of the existent, nor of the indescribable, nor appearance-as-other (SS 5.52–55); it is sadasatkhyāti (5.56).
**Texts:** 
  - [Sāṃkhya Sūtra](../texts/samkhya-sutra.md) — ref: 5.52-56
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
The indescribable (anirvacanīyakhyāti): the erroneous object is neither real nor unreal.
- The Sāṃkhya Sūtra rejects this view (5.54); Advaita's own texts to be cited by U13.
**Texts:** 
  - [Sāṃkhya Sūtra](../texts/samkhya-sutra.md) — ref: 5.54
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
Appearance-as-other (anyathākhyāti): a real thing elsewhere appears as something it is not.
- The Sāṃkhya Sūtra rejects this view (5.55); Nyāya's own texts to be cited by U11.
**Texts:** 
  - [Sāṃkhya Sūtra](../texts/samkhya-sutra.md) — ref: 5.55
