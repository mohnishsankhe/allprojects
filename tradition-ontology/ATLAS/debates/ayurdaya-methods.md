# By which method is the length of life to be computed?

`dsp:ayurdaya-methods` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [The Yavana school of horā](../lineages/yavana-jataka.md)
Maya, the Yavanas, Maṇittha and Śaktipūrva (Parāśara): each planet gives fixed years (19, 25, 15, 12, 15, 21, 20) when exalted, half when fallen, proportionately between.
**Texts:** 
  - [Bṛhajjātaka](../texts/brhat-jataka.md) — ref: 7.1
### [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)
Jīvaśarman: each planet gives an equal share of the maximum life.
**Texts:** 
  - [Bṛhajjātaka](../texts/brhat-jataka.md) — ref: 7.9
### [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)
Satya: reckon by the navāṃśas traversed; Varāhamihira calls Satya's teaching best, but warns against the many multiplications others add.
**Texts:** 
  - [Bṛhajjātaka](../texts/brhat-jataka.md) — ref: 7.9-13
