# Is there another world, and is dharma to be followed for its sake? (Rāmāyaṇa 2.108–110)

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P6-upaya
**Explanation:** The text itself says Jābāli spoke only as a means to bring Rāma back, knowing well the coming and going of the world (Vasiṣṭha, 2.110.1); the reconciliation concerns Jābāli's intention only.
The underlying disagreement between a materialist denial of the other world and the dharma of truth remains a recorded debate; the materialist schools are treated by U33.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Lokāyata, as its opponents report it, would not accept that its denial of the other world is merely a device; the Rāmāyaṇa itself does not concede any truth to the view.
