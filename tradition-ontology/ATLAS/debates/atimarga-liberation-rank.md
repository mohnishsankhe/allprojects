# Is the Atimārga's liberation final, or a lower level beneath Śiva?

`dsp:atimarga-liberation-rank` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
By this rule the brahmin reaches Rudra's proximity and does not return; the end of suffering by the Lord's grace is final, certain and absolute.
- PS 4.19-20; PS 5.35-40; PABh on PS 2.15-18 (final, non-returning proximity).
**Texts:** 
  - [Pāśupata Sūtra](../texts/pasupata-sutra.md) — ref: 4.19-20; 5.35-40
  - [Pañcārthabhāṣya](../texts/pancarthabhasya.md) — ref: on PS 2.15-18
### [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
Pāśupatas reach the level of Īśvara (Aiśvara pada) and skull-observers the Dhruva; they are not re-created, yet all that the Atimārga attains is attained with ease in Śiva-knowledge, which is higher.
- Svacchanda Tantra 10.1169-1171, 11.71-72, 11.182-185, 11.189-190.
**Texts:** 
  - [Svacchandatantra (Svacchandabhairavatantra)](../texts/svacchanda-tantra.md) — ref: 10.1169-1171; 11.71-72; 11.182-190

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** Both sides agree the Atimārga follower is not reborn; the Mantramārga's own graded scheme reads that goal as a high but not highest stage.
This records the Mantramārga's inclusivist ranking as a stage-reading; it does not claim that the Pāśupata goal is lower, only that one tradition so ranks it.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Pāśupata tradition would reject being ranked below another path: its sūtra calls all other paths bad paths (PS 4.16-18) and its end of suffering final.
