# Does the world exist only while perceived (dṛṣṭi-sṛṣṭi), or is it created by Īśvara before and apart from individual perception (sṛṣṭi-dṛṣṭi)?

`dsp:drsti-srsti-vada` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Coverage:** A6

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Dṛṣṭi-sṛṣṭi (Prakāśānanda): the world's being is its being perceived, like a dream; no creation theory is needed.
- Waking is like dream (GK 2); only the seen is known to exist.
**Texts:** 
  - [Vedāntasiddhāntamuktāvalī](../texts/vedanta-siddhanta-muktavali.md) — ref: 1
  - [Māṇḍūkya Kārikā (Gauḍapāda Kārikā, Āgamaśāstra)](../texts/mandukya-karika.md) — ref: 2.31
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Sṛṣṭi-dṛṣṭi (majority, e.g. Vedāntaparibhāṣā, Śaṅkara's BSBh 2.2.28–29): Īśvara creates the empirical world, which exists whether or not an individual perceives it; waking differs from dream.
- Objects are perceived as external and publicly shared (BS 2.2.28–29).
- Scripture teaches creation by Īśvara.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahma Sūtra](../texts/brahma-sutra.md) — ref: 2.2.28–29
  - [Vedāntaparibhāṣā](../texts/vedantaparibhasa.md) — ref: ch.7

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage, P6-upaya
**Explanation:** Later Advaitins present dṛṣṭi-sṛṣṭi as a teaching for advanced students and sṛṣṭi-dṛṣṭi for the rest; both lead to the uncreated (GK 3.15).
Both are explanatory devices within the empirical; GK 3.15 treats creation accounts as means.
**The traditions' own objections:** Critics within Advaita hold that dṛṣṭi-sṛṣṭi collapses into subjective idealism, which Śaṅkara rejected against the Buddhists (BSBh 2.2.28–32).
