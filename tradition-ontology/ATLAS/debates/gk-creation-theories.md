# Why and how does the world come to be? (theories of creation listed in Gauḍapāda Kārikā 1.6–1.9)

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
The text states a settled view (viniścaya) on the origin of existing things, that prāṇa produces all and the puruṣa the rays of consciousness (1.6), without saying whose it is, and reports the views of others (1.7–1.9): expansion or production; dream-māyā imagination; the Lord's will; time; enjoyment; sport. It answers that the fulfilled one (āptakāma) has no longing (1.9).

### None
