# What caused the first schism between the Sthaviras and the Mahāsāṃghikas?

`dsp:cause-of-the-first-schism` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
Laxity in discipline: the Vajjiputtakas' ten points were rejected at Vesālī; the defeated monks formed the Mahāsaṅgītika and altered the texts.
**Texts:** 
  - [Cullavagga](../texts/cullavagga.md) — ref: 12
  - [Dīpavaṃsa](../texts/dipavamsa.md) — ref: 5
### [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
Doctrine: Mahādeva's five points lowering the arhat divided the Saṅgha under Aśoka.
**Texts:** 
  - [Samayabhedoparacanacakra](../texts/samayabhedoparacanacakra.md) — ref: first schism
  - [Abhidharma-mahāvibhāṣā-śāstra](../texts/mahavibhasa.md) — ref: fasc. 99
### [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md)
Vinaya recension: an elder enlarged and rearranged the Vinaya; the great community kept the original.
**Texts:** 
  - [Śāriputraparipṛcchā](../texts/sariputrapariprccha.md) — ref: schism account

**Historical debate — The Council of Vaiśālī** (tradition: 100 (or 110) years after the parinirvāṇa): Cullavagga 12 and the other Vinayas The ten points judged unallowable
