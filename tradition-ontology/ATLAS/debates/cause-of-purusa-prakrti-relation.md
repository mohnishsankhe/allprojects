## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** The sūtra records the three as alternatives without rejecting any; they name the relation's cause at different removes (non-discrimination at the root, karma as its working, the subtle body as its carrier).
Non-discrimination is elsewhere called the cause of the conjunction (SS 1.55) and bondage itself (SS 6.16), which supports reading the three as compatible.
**The traditions' own objections:** None recorded in the texts consulted.
