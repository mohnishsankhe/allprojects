# Is consciousness a product of the combined elements of the body?

`dsp:consciousness-from-elements` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G (Sāṃkhya–Cārvāka)

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
No: consciousness is not found in the elements separately, nor in combination; puruṣa, other than the body, exists.
- SS 3.20–22, 5.129, 6.1–3; SK 17 (aggregates exist for another).
**Texts:** 
  - [Sāṃkhya Sūtra](../texts/samkhya-sutra.md) — ref: 3.20-22; 5.129; 6.1-3
  - [Sāṃkhya Kārikā](../texts/samkhya-karika.md) — ref: 17
### [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md)
Yes: consciousness arises from the combination of elements, as intoxicating power from ingredients.
- Stated as the opponent's view in SS 3.22; Cārvāka's own sūtras survive only in quotations.
**Texts:** 
  - [Sāṃkhya Sūtra](../texts/samkhya-sutra.md) — ref: 3.22
