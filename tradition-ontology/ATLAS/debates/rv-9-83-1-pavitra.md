# What does Ṛgveda 9.83.1 ('pavitraṃ te vitataṃ brahmaṇas pate… ataptatanūr na tad āmo aśnute') enjoin?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md)
'Pavitra' is the liṅga and 'brahman' names it; the 'unheated body' is one without initiation, who cannot attain the liṅga — so the verse enjoins wearing the liṅga after dīkṣā.
- SSM 6.58–61.
**Texts:** 
  - [Siddhāntaśikhāmaṇi](../texts/siddhantasikhamani.md) — ref: 6.58–61
  - [Liṅgadhāraṇacandrikā (Nandikeśvara)](../texts/lingadharana-candrika.md) — ref: passim
### [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md)
The verse enjoins the heating (tapta) of the body with the seals of Viṣṇu's conch and discus as part of Vaiṣṇava initiation (the SSM commentary itself names tapta-mudrā-dhāraṇa as Pāñcarātra practice; Śrīvaiṣṇava and Mādhva usage to be sourced by U08/U14/U15).
- Vaiṣṇava tāpa/tapta-mudrā initiation (low confidence on the textual loci).
