# Does the liberated self exist with Brahman's qualities or as pure consciousness only (BS 4.4.5–7)?

**Coverage:** A5

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md)
Jaimini: with the qualities of Brahman (freedom from evil, true desires etc.), because scripture mentions them.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahma Sūtra](../texts/brahma-sutra.md) — ref: 4.4.5
### [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md)
Auḍulomi: as consciousness only, that being its nature.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahma Sūtra](../texts/brahma-sutra.md) — ref: 4.4.6

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint, P1-level
**Explanation:** The sūtra itself (4.4.7, Bādarāyaṇa) declares no conflict: the qualities are stated from the empirical standpoint, pure consciousness from the ultimate (Śaṅkara's reading).
**The traditions' own objections:** Viśiṣṭādvaita reads 4.4.7 as affirming both as real simultaneously, not as two levels.
