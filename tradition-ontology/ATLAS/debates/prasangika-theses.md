# Does a Prāsaṅgika assert any thesis of his own?

`dsp:prasangika-theses` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md)
Prāsaṅgikas have no thesis asserting inherent existence, but they do assert theses conventionally — e.g. that phenomena lack inherent existence — and uphold them with valid cognition; Nāgārjuna's 'I have no thesis' means no thesis that is inherently existent.
- Without any assertion there could be no path, no teaching and no refutation.
**Texts:** 
  - [Great Treatise on the Stages of the Path to Enlightenment (lam rim chen mo)](../texts/lamrim-chenmo.md) — ref: insight
### [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)
In ultimate analysis the Mādhyamika holds no thesis at all, following Vigrahavyāvartanī 29; conventional statements are made only in accordance with others' perspective, without ultimate commitment (Gorampa and the earlier Tibetan Prāsaṅgikas).
- Any thesis is an elaboration; the view is freedom from all elaboration.
**Texts:** 
  - [Distinguishing the Views (lta ba'i shan 'byed theg mchog gnad kyi zla zer)](../texts/taway-shenje.md) — 

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P1-level
**Explanation:** Both deny any thesis at the ultimate level and allow statements at the conventional level; they differ on whether conventional assertions are the Mādhyamika's own and established by valid cognition.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Gelug holds Gorampa's 'others' perspective' reduces the Madhyamaka to having nothing to teach; the Sakya holds the Gelug's own conventional theses retain grasping.
