# Is liberation won by ritual works, by knowledge alone, by knowledge combined with works, by devotion, or by the Lord's grace — and does grace wait on the soul's surrender (the 'monkey') or carry it without cause (the 'cat')?

`dsp:works-knowledge-grace` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G9

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
Dharma, known only from the Veda, yields its fruit; liberation is not enjoined as a fruit of ritual knowledge. For one who knows the self, when earlier actions are exhausted by experience and no new ones arise — by avoiding desire-prompted and forbidden acts and performing the obligatory ones — there is no further embodiment (Kumārila). The self is subsidiary to the rite as its agent (the prior view in BS 3.4.2).
**Texts:** 
  - [Ślokavārttika](../texts/slokavarttika.md) — ref: sambandhākṣepaparihāra 102-111
  - [Śāstradīpikā](../texts/sastradipika.md) — ref: 1.1.5
  - [Arthasaṅgraha](../texts/arthasangraha.md) — ref: conclusion
  - [Brahma Sūtra](../texts/brahma-sutra.md) — ref: 3.4.2; 3.4.18 (Jaimini's views as reported)
### [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md)
One troubled by the sorrows of transmigration, not longing even for the pleasures mixed with them, turns from prohibited and desire-prompted acts, exhausts the merit and demerit already produced by experiencing them, and by self-knowledge supported by calm, restraint and the like destroys the store of karma and is released.
**Texts:** 
  - [Prakaraṇapañcikā](../texts/prakaranapancika.md) — ref: ch. 8
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Knowledge alone liberates: liberation is not something produced, reached, modified or purified by action but the eternal nature of the self; knowledge of an existing thing depends on the thing, not on the person's will; action is not opposed to ignorance and cannot remove it. Works done without desire purify the mind and produce the desire to know (sacrifice, gifts and austerity, BĀU 4.4.22) and are then renounced; knowledge cannot be combined with works, since works require the notions of agent, act and fruit that knowledge removes.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 1.1.2-4; 3.4.1-26
  - [Gītābhāṣya of Śaṅkara](../texts/gita-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: introduction to 2.11
  - [Naiṣkarmyasiddhi](../texts/naiskarmyasiddhi.md) — ref: ch. 1
  - [Upadeśasāhasrī](../texts/upadesa-sahasri.md) — ref: padya 1
  - [Bhāmatī](../texts/bhamati.md) — ref: on BS 3.4.26
### [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md)
Bhāskara (and before him Bhartṛprapañca): knowledge must be combined with the lifelong performance of prescribed works (jñāna-karma-samuccaya); 'then' in 'then the inquiry into Brahman' means after the inquiry into ritual; release does not come while the body lasts.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Bhāskara (Bhāskarabhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-bhaskara.md) — ref: 1.1.1; 4.1
  - [Bṛhadāraṇyakopaniṣadbhāṣyavārttika](../texts/brhadaranyaka-bhasya-varttika.md) — ref: Bhartṛprapañca (as reported)
### [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
Liberating knowledge is not the understanding of a sentence but meditation — continuous, loving remembrance (bhakti) — to which works and knowledge are ancillary; the inquiry into Brahman follows the inquiry into ritual. Or, for those unable, the Lord's own words 'abandoning all dharmas, take refuge in me alone' teach surrender (prapatti).
**Texts:** 
  - [Śrībhāṣya](../texts/sribhasya.md) — ref: 1.1.1
  - [Vedārthasaṅgraha](../texts/vedarthasangraha.md) — ref: on bhakti
  - [Gītābhāṣya of Rāmānuja](../texts/gita-bhasya-ramanuja.md) — ref: 18.66
### [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md)
Vedānta Deśika: surrender (prapatti) is an independent means the soul performs once, with its limbs, and is the occasion of the Lord's grace — as the young monkey clings to its mother (the 'monkey' analogy, markaṭa-nyāya).
**Texts:** 
  - [Rahasyatrayasāra](../texts/rahasyatrayasara.md) — ref: upāya-vibhāga; carama-śloka
### [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md)
Piḷḷai Lokācārya: the Lord alone is the means; surrender is not a means but the acceptance befitting the soul's dependence; his grace is without cause — as the mother cat carries the kitten (the 'cat' analogy, mārjāra-nyāya).
**Texts:** 
  - [Śrīvacanabhūṣaṇam](../texts/srivacana-bhusanam.md) — ref: 2
  - [Mumukṣuppaṭi](../texts/mumuksuppadi.md) — ref: carama-śloka section
### [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
Without Nārāyaṇa's grace there is no liberation, and without knowledge no abundant grace: grace won by ritual action is the lowest, by hearing and reflection middling, by direct vision the highest; liberation is won by his pleasure alone, with love preceded by knowledge of his greatness.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya (Madhva)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-madhva.md) — ref: 1.1.1
  - [Mahābhārata-tātparya-nirṇaya](../texts/mahabharata-tatparya-nirnaya.md) — ref: 1.76-85
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
Devotion is greater than action, knowledge and yoga, being of the nature of the fruit; neither yoga, Sāṃkhya, dharma, study, austerity nor renunciation wins the Lord as strong devotion does; devotion is not an act, since like knowledge it does not depend on effort.
**Texts:** 
  - [Nārada Bhakti Sūtra](../texts/narada-bhakti-sutra.md) — ref: 25-26
  - [Bhāgavata Purāṇa](../texts/bhagavata-purana.md) — ref: 11.14.20-21
  - [Śāṇḍilya Bhakti Sūtra](../texts/sandilya-bhakti-sutra.md) — ref: 7
### [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
The Gītā: a twofold commitment was declared of old — the yoga of knowledge for the Sāṃkhyas, the yoga of action for the yogins; renunciation and the yoga of action both bring the highest good; Janaka reached perfection by action; the sacrifice of knowledge is better than material sacrifice; not by the Vedas, austerity, gifts or sacrifice but by exclusive devotion can the Lord be seen; performing all actions, taking refuge in him, one attains by his grace; 'abandoning all dharmas, take refuge in me alone'.
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhagavad Gītā](../texts/bhagavad-gita.md) — ref: 3.3-20; 4.33; 5.2; 11.52-54; 18.56-66
### [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
The Upaniṣads cited by every side: the self is not attained by instruction, intellect or much learning but by the one whom it chooses; sacrifices are unsafe boats; those who worship ignorance (ritual) enter darkness, and greater darkness those delighting in knowledge alone — by both one crosses death; brāhmaṇas seek to know the self by Veda-study, sacrifice, gifts and austerity.
**Texts:** 
  - [Katha Upaniṣad](../texts/katha-upanisad.md) — ref: 1.2.23
  - [Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad](../texts/mundaka-upanisad.md) — ref: 1.2.7-10; 3.2.3
  - [Īśa Upaniṣad](../texts/isa-upanisad.md) — ref: 9-11
  - [Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad](../texts/brhadaranyaka-upanisad.md) — ref: 4.4.22
### [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md)
Ignorance — not mere absence of knowledge but incomplete manifestation — is the cause of transmigration and knowledge the sole cause of liberation; the descent of power (śaktipāta) depends on the Lord's freedom alone, not on a balance of karma, and is intense, middling or mild; initiation leading to nirvāṇa happens for the one who knows this, without oblation.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tantrāloka](../texts/tantraloka.md) — ref: 1.22-26; ch. 13
  - [Parātrīśikā](../texts/paratrisika.md) — ref: 25
### [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
Bondage by āṇava-mala is a real substance that knowledge alone cannot remove; when the two deeds are balanced and mala has ripened, grace descends and the guru, as Śiva, removes the bonds in initiation — the rite liberates by Śiva's power.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kiraṇavṛtti](../texts/kiranavrtti.md) — ref: 1-6
  - [Kriyākramadyotikā](../texts/kriyakramadyotika.md) — ref: dīkṣā

### [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
Right view, right knowledge and right conduct together are the path to liberation; knowledge without action is lost and the action of the ignorant is lost. No creator's grace is admitted: the soul frees itself.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tattvārthasūtra](../texts/tattvartha-sutra.md) — ref: 1.1
  - [Tattvārtharājavārttika](../texts/tattvartha-rajavarttika.md) — ref: 1.1
### [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)
In an age without a buddha one cannot reach non-retrogression by self-power; birth in the Pure Land is by the power of Amitābha's vow (other-power); Shinran: even the good are born there, how much more the evil — shinjin and the nembutsu are themselves given.
**Texts:**

**Historical debate — Śaṅkara and Maṇḍana Miśra at Māhiṣmatī** (tradition: Śaṅkara's lifetime): Śaṅkaradigvijaya 8–10 (the tradition's account): the debate on knowledge versus Vedic ritual, judged by Maṇḍana's wife Ubhaya Bhāratī, who placed a garland on each; Maṇḍana's garland withered first. Advaita tradition: Maṇḍana accepted that knowledge alone liberates, took renunciation, and became Sureśvara.

**Historical debate — The division of the Vaṭakalai and Teṅkalai** (c. 14th–15th c. CE onward): Vedānta Deśika's Rahasyatrayasāra; Piḷḷai Lokācārya's Śrīvacana Bhūṣaṇam and Mumukṣuppaḍi with Maṇavāḷa Māmuni's commentaries; the traditions list eighteen differences (see dsp:vadakalai-tenkalai). Both traditions continue; each holds its account of prapatti and grace to be Rāmānuja's.

**Historical debate — Hōnen at Ōhara** (1186 CE (tradition's account)): Hōnen Shōnin Gyōjō Ezu: before Tendai and Nara scholars Hōnen explained birth by the nembutsu of the vow for ordinary beings of this age. Jōdo tradition: the scholars were persuaded.

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path, P4-stage
**Explanation:** Reconciled in part by the texts' own ordering: works purify the mind and produce the desire to know (P4-stage — Advaita, Bhāmatī), the Gītā's two commitments for two kinds of people and the Uddhava Gītā's three yogas for different temperaments (P3-path); whether knowledge alone or knowledge with works finally liberates, whether devotion is a means or the goal beyond liberation, and whether grace takes the soul's surrender as its condition remain not yet reconciled.
P4: BSBh 3.4.26 and the Bhāmatī make works a preliminary to knowledge; Rāmānuja makes karma- and jñāna-yoga ancillary to bhakti; Madhva grades grace by the means that wins it. P3: BhG 3.3 ('a twofold commitment'), Uddhava Gītā 11.20.6-8 ('knowledge, action and devotion — there is no other'). What is not reconciled: samuccaya (Bhāskara) vs knowledge alone (Śaṅkara); Mīmāṃsā's liberation by exhausting karma vs Vedānta's knowledge; prapatti as a means (Vaṭakalai) vs no means (Teṅkalai); self-power vs other-power; grace by the Lord's freedom (Trika) vs grace occasioned by ripened mala (Siddhānta).
**The traditions' own objections:** Śaṅkara rejects samuccaya (Gītā-bhāṣya intro to 2.11; BĀU-bhāṣya 5.1.1); Bhāskara rejects knowledge alone; the bhakti schools deny that devotion is merely preparatory (NBS 25-26); the Teṅkalai deny that surrender is a means, the Vaṭakalai affirm it; Jōdo Shinshū denies any self-power; the Jains admit no grace.
