# Does the earth rotate, or do the stars revolve around a fixed earth?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)
Āryabhaṭa: as one in a moving boat sees fixed objects moving backward, so the fixed stars are seen moving westward at Laṅkā — the earth turns.
- the boat simile
**Texts:** 
  - [Āryabhaṭīya](../texts/aryabhatiya.md) — ref: 4.9
### [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)
Brahmagupta (and Lalla and others): if the earth moved a minute of arc in a prāṇa, where would it go, and why would tall things not fall?
- things would fall or be left behind
