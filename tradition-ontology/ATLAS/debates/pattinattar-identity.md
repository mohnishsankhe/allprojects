# Is the Siddhar Paṭṭiṉattār the same person as Paṭṭiṉattup Piḷḷaiyār of the eleventh Tirumuṟai?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
The tradition: one Paṭṭiṉattār (Tiruveṇkāṭar of Kāvirippūmpaṭṭiṉam) composed both the Tirumuṟai poems and the Siddhar songs; his life is one story from the eyeless needle to Tiruvoṟṟiyūr.
**Texts:** 
  - [Paṭṭiṉattār pāṭalkaḷ (Tiruppāṭal tiraṭṭu)](../texts/pattinattar-padalgal.md) — ref: tradition
### [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
The scholarly account (recorded as metadata): the Tirumuṟai poet (c. 10th c.) and the author(s) of the Siddhar songs (later, in a different idiom) are distinct; the songs may have more than one author.
**Texts:**
