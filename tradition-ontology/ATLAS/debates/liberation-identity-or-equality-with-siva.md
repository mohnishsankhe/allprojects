# Does the liberated soul become identical with Śiva, or only equal to Śiva (śivasamatva) while remaining distinct?

`dsp:liberation-identity-or-equality-with-siva` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
Sanskrit exegetes: the freed soul becomes a Śiva, equal in omniscience and omnipotence, its own powers manifested; it remains distinct and does not perform the cosmic acts; the liberations of other schools (isolation, extinction, merger) are refuted.
**Texts:** 
  - [Mokṣakārikā](../texts/moksakarika.md) — ref: whole
  - [Paramokṣanirāsakārikā](../texts/paramoksanirasakarika.md) — ref: whole
  - [Paramokṣanirāsakārikāvṛtti](../texts/paramoksanirasakarikavrtti.md) — ref: whole
### [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md)
Tamil school: release is inseparable union (advaita as ananya) — the soul, its malas powerless, abides in Śiva enjoying his bliss, 'one, other and together'; it neither becomes Śiva nor stands as a second Lord; 'Śivasamavāda' and 'Saṅkrāntavāda' are refuted.
**Texts:** 
  - [Civañāṉa Pōtam](../texts/sivananabodham.md) — ref: 10-11
  - [Civañāṉa Cittiyār](../texts/sivananasiddhiyar.md) — ref: cupakkam.11
  - [Caṅkaṟpa Nirākaraṇam](../texts/cankarpa-nirakaranam.md) — ref: whole
### [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
The liberated soul becomes equal to Śiva in omniscience and omnipotence but remains numerically distinct; the five cosmic acts remain Śiva's alone.
### [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md)
There is only one Maheśvara, the self of all beings (ĪPK 4.1); the bound individual already performs the five acts (PH 10); liberation is the recognition of that identity, culminating in lordship over the circle of one's own powers (PH 20). The Śiva Sūtra's 'he becomes like Śiva' (3.25) is read by Kṣemarāja in this sense.
**Texts:** 
  - [Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā](../texts/isvarapratyabhijna-karika.md) — ref: 4.1
  - [Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya](../texts/pratyabhijnahrdayam.md) — ref: 10, 20

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** Within the Siddhānta the two sides may be complementary standpoints (analytic: the soul's manifest powers; experiential: its enjoyment in Śiva); not yet reconciled with the non-dualists.
**The traditions' own objections:** Tamil authors reject 'equality' readings that make the soul a second agent; Sanskrit exegetes reject any merger that ends the soul's distinctness; both reject identity.
**Candidate readings:** P2-standpoint: 'equality' from the standpoint of plurality of knowers, 'identity' from the standpoint of the one consciousness.; Treat as a case of dsp:souls-one-or-distinct (U50) and reconcile there.
