# Are other mind-streams established?

`dsp:other-minds-buddhist` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** B4

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
Yes: another's mind is inferred from observed action and speech, as one's own actions are preceded by intention; the proof holds for proponents of external objects and of cognition-only alike (Dharmakīrti).
- Santānāntarasiddhi

### [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
Not ultimately: once all appearances are one's own cognition, the inference fails at the ultimate level; others are admitted conventionally (Ratnakīrti).
- Santānāntaradūṣaṇa
**Texts:** 
  - [Santānāntaradūṣaṇa](../texts/santanantaradusana.md) — 

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P1-level
**Explanation:** P1: Dharmakīrti's proof works at the conventional level; Ratnakīrti's denial is at the ultimate level and he himself allows others conventionally.
**The traditions' own objections:** Ratnakīrti presents his text as a refutation, not a complement, of the proof of other minds.
