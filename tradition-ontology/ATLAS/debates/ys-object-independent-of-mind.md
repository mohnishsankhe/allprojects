# Is the object of cognition independent of any mind, or only a construction of cognition?

`dsp:ys-object-independent-of-mind` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
The object is one, common to many minds, stands on itself, is not imagined by one or many minds, and is independent of them; minds differ though the object is the same, so the paths of object and cognition are separate; the object is common to all puruṣas and the minds work separately in each.
- bahucittālambanībhūtam ekaṃ vastu sādhāraṇaṃ, tat khalu naikacittaparikalpitaṃ nāpy anekacittaparikalpitaṃ kiṃtu svapratiṣṭham
- tasmāt svatantro 'rthaḥ sarvapuruṣasādhāraṇaḥ svatantrāṇi ca cittāni pratipuruṣaṃ pravartante
