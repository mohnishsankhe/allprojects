# Do things possess a distinct causal power (śakti)?

`dsp:sakti-causal-power` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A5

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
Yes: a distinct power in things explains efficacy — e.g. fire does not burn when a gem or mantra is present because its power is obstructed (Prābhākaras count śakti as a separate category).
- As usually reported.
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
No: an effect follows from the totality of causes, which includes the absence of counteragents; the gem or mantra is a counteragent whose absence is a cause; capacity, similarity and number are not separate categories.
- Udayana; Gaṅgeśa; Viśvanātha's list of seven categories.
**Texts:** 
  - [Nyāyakusumāñjali](../texts/nyaya-kusumanjali.md) — ref: stabaka 1
  - [Bhāṣāpariccheda](../texts/bhasapariccheda.md) — ref: 2
