# What is mastery of the senses (indriya-jaya)? (YBh 2.55)

`dsp:what-is-mastery-of-senses` · `sourced` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### None
Non-addiction to sound and other objects (attachment is addiction, since it throws one off the better).
**Texts:** 
  - [Yogabhāṣya (Vyāsa's commentary on the Yoga Sūtra)](../texts/yoga-bhasya.md) — ref: 2.55
### None
Contact with sound and the rest at one's own wish; or knowledge of them empty of pleasure and pain when attachment and aversion are absent.
**Texts:** 
  - [Yogabhāṣya (Vyāsa's commentary on the Yoga Sūtra)](../texts/yoga-bhasya.md) — ref: 2.55
### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
Jaigīṣavya: non-engagement (apratipatti) through one-pointedness of mind; Vyāsa adds that the mastery following sense-withdrawal is supreme because the senses, stilled with the mind, need no other effort-made means.
**Texts:** 
  - [Yogabhāṣya (Vyāsa's commentary on the Yoga Sūtra)](../texts/yoga-bhasya.md) — ref: 2.55
