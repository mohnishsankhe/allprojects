# What sees — the eye, consciousness, or neither?

`dsp:what-sees` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vaibhāṣika](../lineages/vaibhasika.md)
The eye sees, as the support of consciousness, since forms behind a wall are not seen.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 1.42
### [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md)
Neither: visual consciousness arises from eye and form; 'the eye sees' and 'consciousness cognizes' are conventions over a mere set of dharmas without activity.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośabhāṣya](../texts/abhidharmakosabhasya.md) — ref: 1.42, Pradhan p. 31

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P1-level
**Explanation:** The Sautrāntika itself accepts 'the eye sees' as a valid convention; disagreement remains on whether it is also true in the ultimate sense.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Vaibhāṣika holds that the eye really sees and would reject treating its thesis as merely conventional.
