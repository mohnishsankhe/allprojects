# What is the 'non-seeing' (adarśana) that is the occasion of the conjunction of seer and seen? (YBh 2.23)

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
Views listed by Vyāsa as found in the śāstra: the office of the guṇas; the non-arising of the principal mind of the master to whom objects have been shown, that is, the absence of seeing while his own seen is present; the purposiveness (arthavattā) of the guṇas; ignorance stilled with its mind as seed of the mind's arising; the manifestation of the impression of motion on the exhaustion of that of stillness.
