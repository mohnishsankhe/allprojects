## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** The sūtra's own conclusion ('both') treats the Lord-as-other and the self-as-one as two standpoints on devotion's object, each supported by scripture.
This is the text's own reconciliation, recorded as such; it is not extended here to the Vedānta schools' dispute on whether souls are one with or distinct from the Lord (dsp:souls-one-or-distinct).
**The traditions' own objections:** Dvaita rejects any identity-object for devotion; Advaita would take the other-directed devotion as provisional (P1-level) rather than an equal standpoint.
