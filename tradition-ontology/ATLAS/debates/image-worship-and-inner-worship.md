# Is worship of images and outer forms valid for one seeking liberation?

`dsp:image-worship-and-inner-worship` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
Yogins see Śiva in the Self, not in images; images are imagined for the ignorant; for the renouncer image-worship leads to rebirth.
- Darśana 4.59
- Maitreya 2.26
**Texts:** 
  - [Darśana Upaniṣad](../texts/darsana-upanisad.md) — ref: 4.48-59
  - [Maitreya Upaniṣad](../texts/maitreya-upanisad.md) — ref: 2.26
### `lin:ramanandi`
Forms are given to the formless for the worshippers' sake; the deity is worshipped in yantra and mantra, and without the yantra is not pleased.
- Rāmapūrvatāpanī 1.7; 4–5
**Texts:** 
  - [Rāmatāpanī Upaniṣad](../texts/ramatapani-upanisad.md) — ref: pūrva 1.7

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** Both texts assign outer forms to worshippers and inner worship to renouncers and knowers: the Rāmapūrvatāpanī says forms are 'for the sake of worshippers', the Maitreya restricts its rule to the seeker of liberation.
An adhikāra reading already present in both sides' own words.
**The traditions' own objections:** Devotional traditions that hold the consecrated image to be a real descent of the Lord (the arcā of Pāñcarātra and Śrīvaiṣṇava teaching) reject calling image-worship a concession to the ignorant.
