# Is every life-span fixed, or is there untimely death?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
Some: whoever dies dies at his time; the time one dies is one's fixed time.
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Śā 6.28; Vi 3.34
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
Ātreya: life rests on daiva and puruṣakāra; some karma is fixed, some not; both timely and untimely death exist — otherwise mantras, medicines, precautions and rejuvenation would be pointless.
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Vi 3.29-38; Śā 6.28

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** Fixedness belongs to strong daiva, openness to weak daiva and human effort — two standpoints on karma rather than one exclusive rule.
**The traditions' own objections:** Caraka calls 'there is no untimely death' mere words (Śā 6.28).
