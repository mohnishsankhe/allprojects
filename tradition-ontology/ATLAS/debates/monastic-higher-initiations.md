# May celibate monastics receive (and practise) the secret and wisdom empowerments?

`dsp:monastic-higher-initiations` · `skeleton` · confidence low


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md)
No: the Ādibuddha tantra strongly prohibits the secret and wisdom empowerments for those who keep celibacy; taking them breaks the vow, incurs defeat, leads to the lower realms and yields no accomplishment; with the master's empowerment the celibate may still hear and teach all tantras and perform fire offerings.
- The Ādibuddha tantra's prohibition.
- The prātimokṣa vow of celibacy would be broken.
**Texts:** 
  - [Lamp for the Path to Awakening (Bodhipathapradīpa; byang chub lam gyi sgron ma)](../texts/bodhipathapradipa.md) — ref: v66–69
### [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md)
Monastics may receive the four empowerments of Highest Yoga Tantra, the higher ones with a visualised wisdom-consort, and practise the completion stage without breaking celibacy; practice with an actual consort belongs only to a very advanced stage and the tradition's monks refrain from it.
- Tsongkhapa kept full monastic discipline while practising Highest Yoga Tantra.
- The prohibition concerns literal practice, not receiving the empowerment in visualisation.
**Texts:** 
  - [Great Treatise on the Stages of Secret Mantra (sngags rim chen mo)](../texts/ngagrim-chenmo.md) — 

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** Both forbid the celibate the literal practice; they differ on whether the empowerment itself may be taken in visualised form.
**The traditions' own objections:** Atiśa's verse states the prohibition of taking the empowerments, not only of practising them; a strict Kadam reading rejects the visualised-consort solution.

_Notes: The Gelug side is recalled from the tradition's practice; Tsongkhapa's exact wording is not given._
