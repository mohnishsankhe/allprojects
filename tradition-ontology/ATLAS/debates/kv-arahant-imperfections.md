# Does the arahant have imperfections (the 'five points')?

`dsp:kv-arahant-imperfections` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Andhakas (as named in the Kathāvatthu commentary)](../lineages/andhaka.md)
An arahant can be made to emit semen by deities, can have ignorance and doubt, can be informed by others, and one may utter speech while in jhāna (held, per the commentary, by the Pubbaseliyas and Aparaseliyas (Andhakas)).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 2.1-5
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
The arahant has abandoned lust, ignorance and doubt; none of these imperfections can belong to him.
- What would arise from lust or ignorance cannot arise where they are destroyed.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 2.1-5
