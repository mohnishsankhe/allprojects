# Are the seeds in the store-consciousness innate, newly perfumed, or both?

`dsp:seeds-innate-or-perfumed` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Coverage:** B4

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
Innate only: all seeds exist from beginningless time and are merely nourished by perfuming (Candrapāla, as reported in the Cheng weishi lun).
- Cited in the Cheng weishi lun (recalled)
**Texts:** 
  - [Cheng weishi lun (*Vijñaptimātratāsiddhi)](../texts/cheng-weishi-lun.md) — ref: juan 2
### [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
Newly perfumed only: seeds are produced by perfuming; there is no seed without prior experience (Nanda, as reported).
- Cited in the Cheng weishi lun (recalled)
**Texts:** 
  - [Cheng weishi lun (*Vijñaptimātratāsiddhi)](../texts/cheng-weishi-lun.md) — ref: juan 2
### [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
Both: some seeds are innate — including the pure seeds of the lineage — and some are newly perfumed (Dharmapāla, adopted by Xuanzang).
- Accounts both for the fixed lineages and for learning
**Texts:** 
  - [Cheng weishi lun (*Vijñaptimātratāsiddhi)](../texts/cheng-weishi-lun.md) — ref: juan 2

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** P2: Dharmapāla's own synthesis assigns innate seeds to the lineage and pure dharmas and perfumed seeds to experience; the two single-cause views each describe one standpoint.
**The traditions' own objections:** The positions of Candrapāla and Nanda are known only through the Cheng weishi lun's report; their own objections to the synthesis are not preserved.
