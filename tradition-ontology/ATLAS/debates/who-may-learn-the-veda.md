# Who may learn and recite the Veda?

`dsp:who-may-learn-the-veda` · `sourced` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
The recitation lineages transmit the Veda to the initiated (upanayana) twice-born; the Purāṇa says the triple Veda is not for the hearing of women, śūdras and lapsed twice-born, for whom Vyāsa composed the Mahābhārata (Bhāgavata 1.4.25); the Brahma Sūtra denies śūdras the right to Vedic study (1.3.34–38).
- Eligibility (adhikāra) follows initiation.
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhāgavata Purāṇa](../texts/bhagavata-purana.md) — ref: 1.4.25
  - [Brahma Sūtra](../texts/brahma-sutra.md) — ref: 1.3.34–38
### [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
The Saṃhitās themselves record women seers (ṛṣikās) of hymns, a maiden's brahmacarya (AVŚ 11.5.18), a seer 'son of a slave woman' accepted by the gods (AB 2.19), and a verse speaking auspicious speech to brahmin, rājanya, śūdra, ārya, one's own and the stranger (VS 26.2).
- The seers' own record.
**Texts:** 
  - [Bṛhaddevatā](../texts/brhaddevata.md) — ref: 2.82–84
  - [Atharvaveda Saṃhitā (Śaunaka)](../texts/atharvaveda-saunaka.md) — ref: 11.5.18
  - [Vājasaneyi Saṃhitā (Mādhyandina)](../texts/vajasaneyi-samhita.md) — ref: 26.2
  - [Aitareya Brāhmaṇa](../texts/aitareya-brahmana.md) — ref: 2.19
### [Ārya Samāj](../lineages/arya-samaj.md)
Dayānanda (recent): Vedic study and initiation are open to all, women and men of every birth, citing VS 26.2.
- VS 26.2.
**Texts:** 
  - [Vājasaneyi Saṃhitā (Mādhyandina)](../texts/vajasaneyi-samhita.md) — ref: 26.2
