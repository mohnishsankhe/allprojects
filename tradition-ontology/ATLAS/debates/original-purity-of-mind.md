# Is the mind by nature luminous and only adventitiously defiled?

`dsp:original-purity-of-mind` · `skeleton` · confidence low


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md)
Yes: the nature of mind is originally pure, defiled by adventitious defilements (Vasumitra).
**Texts:** 
  - [Samayabhedoparacanacakra](../texts/samayabhedoparacanacakra.md) — ref: Mahāsāṃghika tenets
### [Vibhajyavāda](../lineages/vibhajyavada.md)
Yes (as reported in the Vibhāṣā).
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharma-mahāvibhāṣā-śāstra](../texts/mahavibhasa.md) — ref: fasc. 27 (low confidence)
### [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
No: a mind with defilement is not pure; purity and defilement are successive states.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharma-mahāvibhāṣā-śāstra](../texts/mahavibhasa.md) — ref: fasc. 27 (low confidence)
