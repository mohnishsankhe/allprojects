# Did Śrīkaṇṭha teach qualified non-dualism as final, or as a step toward Advaita?

`dsp:srikantha-advaita-or-visistadvaita` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Śiva-viśiṣṭādvaita (Śaiva Vedānta of Śrīkaṇṭha)](../lineages/siva-visistadvaita.md)
The text of Śrīkaṇṭha's commentary: Śiva with his śakti, souls and world as his body, and liberation as equality (not identity) with Śiva — qualified non-dualism as final.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śrīkaṇṭha (Śrīkaṇṭhabhāṣya)](../texts/srikantha-bhasya.md) — ref: 4.4.17
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Appayya Dīkṣita (Śivādvaitanirṇaya): Śrīkaṇṭha's real intent is pure non-dualism; he taught the qualified form for devotees attached to Śiva with attributes.
**Texts:** 
  - [Śivādvaitanirṇaya](../texts/sivadvaitanirnaya.md) — 
### [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)
Vīraśaiva authors (e.g. Nīlakaṇṭha's Kriyāsāra) claim Śrīkaṇṭha as a Vīraśaiva teacher of Śakti-qualified non-dualism.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kriyāsāra (Nīlakaṇṭha)](../texts/kriyasara-nilakantha.md) — 

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** Appayya's own reading places the qualified doctrine at a stage for devotees (P4).
This records Appayya's reconciliation; it is not accepted by the Śaiva Viśiṣṭādvaita or Vīraśaiva readers of Śrīkaṇṭha.
**The traditions' own objections:** Vīraśaiva and Śaiva Viśiṣṭādvaita authors reject the claim that Śrīkaṇṭha's doctrine is provisional.
