## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Sowa Rigpa (gso ba rig pa), the Tibetan science of healing](../lineages/sowa-rigpa.md)
The Four Tantras is the word of the Medicine Buddha, taught through his emanations Rigpé Yeshe and Yilé Kyé in Tanaduk, brought to Tibet and translated by Vairocana, concealed and recovered as treasure, and only edited by Yuthok; its form ('Thus have I heard…') is that of a tantra.
- The opening setting and dialogue form of a Buddha-spoken tantra.
- The lineage account of translation, concealment (Samye) and recovery (1038).
- Defended by Desi Sangye Gyatso in the Blue Beryl and the history of medicine.

### [Zurkharpa (zur lugs), the Zur school of Tibetan medicine](../lineages/zurkharpa-medicine.md)
A strand of Tibetan medical scholarship held (or seriously considered) that the Four Tantras was composed in Tibet — by Yuthok — drawing on Indian and other sources, pointing to Tibetan elements in the text and the absence of an Indian original.
- No Sanskrit original or Tengyur counterpart of the whole work.
- Tibetan materia medica, terms and conditions reflected in the text.
- Examined by Zurkhar Lodrö Gyalpo (his final position not asserted here).
**Texts:** 
  - [The Oral Instructions of the Ancestors (mes po'i zhal lung)](../texts/mepo-zhallung.md) — ref: authorship
