# Is medicine effective, given that some treated patients die and some untreated recover?

`dsp:is-medicine-efficacious` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
Maitreya: medicine is no better than no medicine, since both outcomes occur with and without it.
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Sū 10.4
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
Ātreya (and Punarvasu in Vāgbhaṭa): medicine is effective for diseases curable by it; some diseases are incurable, some resolve alone and would resolve faster with help; treatment with the four pillars should not be doubted.
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Sū 10.5-6
  - [Aṣṭāṅga Hṛdaya](../texts/astanga-hrdaya.md) — ref: Utt 40.60-74

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** Maitreya's observation holds of incurable and self-limiting diseases; Ātreya's claim holds of the curable — the classes of disease are the standpoints.
**The traditions' own objections:** Ātreya rejects Maitreya's conclusion outright as 'wrong thinking' (mithyā).
