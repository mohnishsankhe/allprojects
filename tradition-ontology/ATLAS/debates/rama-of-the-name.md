# Is the Rāma whose name saves the embodied son of Daśaratha, or a formless Rām who was never born?

`dsp:rama-of-the-name` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G13

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md)
The formless and the embodied are not different: the unborn Brahman became Daśaratha's son for love of his devotees; the name stands between the two forms and makes both known; those who preach devotion while reviling the Vedas and Purāṇas are misled.
- Mānas 1.116: no difference between saguṇa and aguṇa.
- Mānas 1.21: the name as interpreter between the two.
- Bhuśuṇḍi's insistence on the embodied Rāma against Lomaśa (7.110–113).
**Texts:** 
  - [Rāmcaritmānas](../texts/ramcaritmanas.md) — ref: 1.116; 1.21; 7.110–113
  - [Dohāvalī](../texts/dohavali.md) — ref: sākhī sabadī dohā
### [Kabīr Panth](../lineages/kabir-panth.md)
Rām is formless and unborn; the Rām of the name is not the son of Daśaratha whom the world praises; avatāras and images are not the Rām within.
- Sayings attributed to Kabīr distinguishing the secret of Rām's name from Daśaratha's son (attributed; wording not reproduced here).
**Texts:**
