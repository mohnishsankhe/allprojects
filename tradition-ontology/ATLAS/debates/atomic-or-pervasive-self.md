# Is the individual self atomic or all-pervading?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md)
Atomic: the soul is minute, dwelling in the heart and pervading the body by its knowledge (Daśaślokī 1; BS 2.3.19ff read literally). Vallabha and the Gauḍīyas agree.
**Texts:** 
  - [Daśaślokī (Siddhāntaratna; Vedāntakāmadhenu)](../texts/dasasloki.md) — ref: 1
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Advaita (and Nyāya, Sāṃkhya): the self is all-pervading; atomicity is said of the self as delimited by the intellect.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 2.3.29
