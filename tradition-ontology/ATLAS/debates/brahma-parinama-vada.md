# Does Brahman itself transform into the world, and if so does it change?

`dsp:brahma-parinama-vada` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md)
Bhāskara (and Bhartṛprapañca): Brahman really transforms into the world through its power; the effect is real, different and non-different from the cause.
- BS 1.4.26 'ātmakṛteḥ pariṇāmāt'
- BS 2.1.14 teaches non-difference of a real effect
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Bhāskara (Bhāskarabhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-bhaskara.md) — ref: 1.4.26
### [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md)
Vallabha: Brahman becomes the world without change (avikṛta-pariṇāma), as gold in ornaments; creation is manifestation (āvirbhāva).
- Brahman as locus of contradictory attributes can both transform and remain unchanged
**Texts:** 
  - [Aṇubhāṣya (Brahmasūtrāṇubhāṣya)](../texts/anubhasya.md) — ref: 1.4.26
### [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)
Jīva / Caitanya: only the Lord's external power transforms (śakti-pariṇāma); the Lord remains unchanged like the touchstone that yields gold; vivarta is rejected.
- the sūtras teach pariṇāma; Śaṅkara's vivarta departs from Vyāsa
**Texts:** 
  - [Paramātmasandarbha](../texts/paramatma-sandarbha.md) — 
  - [Caitanya Caritāmṛta](../texts/caitanya-caritamrta.md) — ref: 1.7
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Advaita: a real transformation would make Brahman changeable and composite; the world is an apparent transformation (vivarta) of Brahman, a real transformation (pariṇāma) only of māyā.
- scripture calls Brahman partless and changeless
- 'the modification is only a name' (Chāndogya 6.1.4)
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 2.1.14

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** not yet reconciled: the pariṇāma schools and Advaita disagree on whether the world's reality is ultimate.
**The traditions' own objections:** Bhāskara, Vallabha and the Gauḍīyas reject any reading that assigns the world to a lower level of reality; Advaita rejects real change in Brahman.
**Candidate readings:** P1-level: Advaita itself admits pariṇāma of māyā at the vyāvahārika level — rejected by all four pariṇāma schools, for whom the world is not merely conventional; Gauḍīya śakti-pariṇāma and Vallabha's avikṛta-pariṇāma as convergent answers to Advaita's objection of change in Brahman (a partial convergence among the Vaiṣṇava schools)
