# Who is the true Sixth Patriarch — was the Northern school collateral?

`dsp:huatai-true-lineage` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Heze school](../lineages/heze.md)
Bodhidharma's robe and the true transmission passed from Hongren to Huineng; Shenxiu's line is collateral and its teaching gradual.
- The robe as token of transmission.
- Northern practice is 'freezing the mind to enter samādhi' — gradual.
**Texts:** 
  - [Treatise Establishing the True and False in the Southern School of Bodhidharma (Putidamo nanzong ding shifei lun)](../texts/nanzong-ding-shifei-lun.md) — ref: Huatai record
  - [Dunhuang Platform Sūtra](../texts/platform-sutra-dunhuang.md) — ref: 49.1
### [Northern school of Chan](../lineages/northern-chan.md)
Shenxiu was the recognised heir of Hongren, honoured by the court as 'Dharma lord of the two capitals'; Puji held the patriarchal succession in the capitals.
- Epitaphs and the Chuan fabao ji / Lengqie shizi ji present the line through Faru and Shenxiu.
**Texts:** 
  - [Annals of the Transmission of the Dharma Treasure (Chuan fabao ji)](../texts/chuan-fabao-ji.md) — 
  - [Record of the Masters and Disciples of the Laṅkāvatāra (Lengqie shizi ji)](../texts/lengqie-shizi-ji.md) — 
### [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
The Platform Sūtra itself: 'the Dharma is one school, people are northern and southern'; Shenxiu praises Huineng's 'wisdom without a teacher' and sends students to him.
**Texts:** 
  - [Platform Sūtra of the Sixth Patriarch (Liuzu tanjing)](../texts/platform-sutra.md) — ref: 11.1

**Historical debate — The Great Assembly of Huatai** (732 (Dayun temple, Huatai)): Shenhui's challenge recorded by Dugu Pei; his interlocutor was the Dharma master Chongyuan (name from memory, low confidence). An imperial commission of 796 is said to have confirmed Shenhui as seventh patriarch. All later Chan lineages trace through Huineng; the Northern school died out.
