## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** Each 'one rasa' names the ground of aesthetic experience from a different standpoint — pathos, self-regard, wonder, peace, love of God — while Bharata's list describes the variety as it appears.
The theorists themselves argue for exclusive claims; the reconciliation treats their claims as complementary perspectives, which they would not all accept.
**The traditions' own objections:** Bhoja holds that only śṛṅgāra is truly rasa; Rūpa's school holds bhakti-rasa to be rasa in the highest sense, not one perspective among others.
