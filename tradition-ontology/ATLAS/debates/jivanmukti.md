# Is liberation possible while living in the body?

`dsp:jivanmukti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md)
Bhāskara: no; while the body lasts the real adjuncts remain; liberation comes after death.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Bhāskara (Bhāskarabhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-bhaskara.md) — ref: 4.1
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Advaita: yes; knowledge destroys ignorance at once, and the body continues only by prārabdha karma (jīvanmukti).
**Texts:** 
  - [Jīvanmuktiviveka](../texts/jivanmuktiviveka.md) — 
### [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)
Gauḍīya: one who serves Hari in all conditions is called jīvanmukta; but the goal is not liberation, it is love.
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhaktirasāmṛtasindhu](../texts/bhaktirasamrtasindhu.md) — ref: 1.2.187
