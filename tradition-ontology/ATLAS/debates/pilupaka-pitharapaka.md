# When a clay pot is baked, is the change of colour in the atoms or in the whole pot?

`dsp:pilupaka-pitharapaka` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Coverage:** A5

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)
In the atoms (pīlupāka): the pot disintegrates into atoms, which change colour under heat, and a new pot is formed.
- As usually reported in the later literature.
**Texts:** 
  - [Nyāyakandalī](../texts/nyayakandali.md) — 
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
In the whole (piṭharapāka): the colour of the pot changes without its being destroyed, heat reaching the parts through the pores.
- As usually reported.
**Texts:** 
  - [Nyāyasiddhāntamuktāvalī](../texts/nyayasiddhantamuktavali.md) —
