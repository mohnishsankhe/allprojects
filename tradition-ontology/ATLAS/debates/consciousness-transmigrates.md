# Is it the same consciousness that runs and wanders through the rounds of rebirth?

`dsp:consciousness-transmigrates` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md)
(Sāti) 'As I understand the Dhamma taught by the Blessed One, it is this same consciousness that runs and wanders on, not another' — the one that speaks and feels and experiences the results of deeds.
- MN 38 (2–5).
**Texts:** 
  - [Mahātaṇhāsaṅkhaya Sutta](../texts/mahatanhasankhaya-sutta.md) — ref: MN 38 (2)
### [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md)
(The Buddha) Consciousness is dependently arisen; without a condition there is no arising of consciousness; it is reckoned by its condition as fire by its fuel.
- MN 38 (5–13).
**Texts:** 
  - [Mahātaṇhāsaṅkhaya Sutta](../texts/mahatanhasankhaya-sutta.md) — ref: MN 38
