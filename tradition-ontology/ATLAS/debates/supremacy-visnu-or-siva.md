# Is Viṣṇu or Śiva (or the Goddess) the supreme deity?

`dsp:supremacy-visnu-or-siva` · `sourced` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
Viṣṇu/Kṛṣṇa is supreme: Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān himself; Brahmā and Śiva are his functions; Śambhu is the foremost Vaiṣṇava.
- BhP 1.3.28
- VP 1.2.66
- BhP 12.13.16
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhāgavata Purāṇa](../texts/bhagavata-purana.md) — ref: 1.3.28
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
Śiva is supreme: he alone is niṣkala and worshipped in the liṅga; the column of fire whose ends Brahmā and Viṣṇu cannot find.
- ŚiP 1.5.10-13
- LiP 1.17
**Texts:** 
  - [Śiva Purāṇa](../texts/siva-purana.md) — ref: 1.5
  - [Liṅga Purāṇa](../texts/linga-purana.md) — ref: 1.17
### [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md)
The Goddess is supreme: she gives Viṣṇu, Brahmā and Śiva their bodies; beyond Hari and Hara.
- DM 1 (MkP 81.65)
- DM 4 (84.7)
- DBhP 3
**Texts:** 
  - [Devī Māhātmya](../texts/devi-mahatmya.md) — ref: 1; 4
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
Hari and Hara are one (the Kūrma's Īśvara Gītā; Gajendra's hymn reached Hari as all the gods).
- KūP 2.1-11
- BhP 8.3.30
**Texts:** 
  - [Kūrma Purāṇa](../texts/kurma-purana.md) — ref: 2.1-11

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path, P7-arthavada, P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** Each Purāṇa exalts the worshipper's chosen deity (P3) with praise-language (P7); several Purāṇas themselves state the unity of the forms (VP 1.2.66; Kūrma), a standpoint reading (P2).
The reconciliation claims only that the texts contain their own unity-passages; it does not claim the sectarian passages are non-literal for their communities.
**The traditions' own objections:** Śrīvaiṣṇava, Mādhva and Gauḍīya authors hold Viṣṇu alone supreme and Śiva a jīva/devotee; Śaiva Siddhānta and Vīraśaiva authors hold Śiva alone supreme; Śāktas the Goddess.
