### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
There is no interval; the Abhidhamma's fivefold scheme already counts a jhāna with sustained but without applied thought as a jhāna, not an interval.
- Would there then be intermediate contacts and so on?
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 18.7

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled

**The traditions' own objections:** The Kathāvatthu itself denies an 'interval' as a separate thing.
