# What is Śrī's rank: all-pervading and of the Lord's rank (Īśvara-koṭi), or atomic and of the rank of souls (jīva-koṭi)? Is she a means or only the mediator?

`dsp:status-of-sri` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A6; G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md)
Śrī is all-pervading like Viṣṇu, of the Lord's rank, and with him the means and goal; the divine couple is the object of surrender.
- Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.8.17
- Yāmuna's Catuḥślokī
- the dvaya names 'Śrīman Nārāyaṇa'
**Texts:** 
  - [Rahasyatrayasāra](../texts/rahasyatrayasara.md) — ref: Paradevatāpāramārthya
  - [Rahasyarakṣā](../texts/rahasyaraksa.md) — ref: on the Catuḥślokī
  - [Catuḥślokī](../texts/catuhsloki.md) — ref: 1-2
### [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md)
Śrī is the mediator (puruṣakāra) who reconciles soul and Lord; the Lord alone is the means; she is commonly reported as atomic in essence and of the rank of souls, though never separate from him.
- Sītā's intercession in the Rāmāyaṇa
- the first half of the dvaya shows her as mediator, the Lord's feet as means
**Texts:** 
  - [Śrīvacanabhūṣaṇam](../texts/srivacana-bhusanam.md) — ref: 1
  - [Mumukṣuppaṭi](../texts/mumuksuppadi.md) — ref: 2
