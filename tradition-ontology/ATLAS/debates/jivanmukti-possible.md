# Is liberation while living (jīvanmukti) possible, or only liberation at death?

`dsp:jivanmukti-possible` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Śaṅkara, Vidyāraṇya, most Advaitins: yes — on knowledge accumulated karma is destroyed and future karma does not cling; the body continues by prārabdha like a potter's wheel; the knower is free while living.
- BS 4.1.13–15, 19.
- Scripture's 'for him the delay lasts only till release from the body' (ChU 6.14.2).
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 4.1.13–15
  - [Jīvanmuktiviveka](../texts/jivanmuktiviveka.md) — ref: 1–5
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Maṇḍana Miśra (reported): the so-called jīvanmukta is an advanced seeker; full liberation comes only with the body's fall.
- As long as the body lasts some ignorance or its trace remains.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasiddhi](../texts/brahmasiddhi.md) — ref: 4
### [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
Viśiṣṭādvaita and Dvaita: liberation is attained only after death, when the soul reaches the Lord's abode; embodied liberation is not real liberation.
- Liberation includes the soul's full manifestation of its nature and service in Vaikuṇṭha.
**Texts:** 
  - [Śrībhāṣya](../texts/sribhasya.md) — ref: 4.1

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the Vaiṣṇava schools and Maṇḍana deny what Advaita affirms; candidate reconciliation by level is rejected by the other schools.
**The traditions' own objections:** Viśiṣṭādvaita and Dvaita reject the Advaita notion of liberation itself.
**Candidate readings:** P1-level: freedom from bondage in knowledge (ultimate) vs continued empirical embodiment (conventional).; Some Advaitins posit a 'trace of ignorance' (avidyā-leśa) to explain the continuing body, conceding part of Maṇḍana's point.
