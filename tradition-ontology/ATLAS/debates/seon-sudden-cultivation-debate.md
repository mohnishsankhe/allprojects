# Sudden awakening followed by gradual cultivation (Jinul) or sudden awakening with sudden cultivation (Seongcheol)?

`dsp:seon-sudden-cultivation-debate` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Seon (Korean Chan)](../lineages/seon.md)
Jinul: all sages first awaken, then cultivate; habit energies are removed gradually after sudden awakening; even 'sudden/sudden' rests on past-life cultivation.
**Texts:** 
  - [Secrets on Cultivating the Mind (Susim kyol)](../texts/susim-kyol.md) — ref: 1006b15; 1006c11
### [Seon (Korean Chan)](../lineages/seon.md)
Seongcheol: true Chan awakening is realization in which cultivation is complete — sudden awakening, sudden cultivation; Jinul's awakening is only understanding.
**Texts:** 
  - [The Correct Path of the Seon School (Seonmun jeongno)](../texts/seonmun-jeongno.md) — 

**Historical debate — Korean dono-jeomsu / dono-donsu controversy** (1980s–1990s): Triggered by Seongcheol's Seonmun jeongno (1981); defenders of Jinul (Songgwangsa) replied at conferences in the 1990s. Both positions remain in the Jogye Order.

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** Jinul himself allows sudden/sudden for the highest faculties and treats sudden/gradual as the path of most practitioners.
**The traditions' own objections:** Seongcheol explicitly rejects understanding-awakening as heterodox for Chan, so he would not accept the reconciliation.
