# What occasions the descent of power (śaktipāta): the equality of karma, the maturing of impurity, or the Lord's free will alone?

`dsp:cause-of-saktipata` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** D (karma and liberation); A7

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md)
Its time is the equality of the soul's karmas; Śiva waits for that time because of the soul's beginningless karma, yet remains its lord and cause, as the sun is of the opening lotus.
- figurative 'fall'
- archer and moving target
- sun and lotus
**Texts:** 
  - [Kiraṇatantra](../texts/kirana-tantra.md) — ref: 5.1-20
### [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md)
Viṣṇu's compassion — his power of grace called śaktipāta — arises for the suffering soul; by his glance its karmas become equal and fall silent, and it is lifted out of saṃsāra.
- grace is the fifth power
- robbers and the traveller
**Texts:** 
  - [Ahirbudhnya Saṃhitā](../texts/ahirbudhnya-samhita.md) — ref: 14.28-34
### [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md)
Abhinavagupta rejects karma-equality and the ripening of impurity as causes: the descent depends on the Lord's independence alone.
- Tantrāloka 13
**Texts:** 
  - [Tantrāloka](../texts/tantraloka.md) — ref: 13
### [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
The ripening of mala (malaparipāka) is the occasion; the Lord, not the mala, is the cause.
**Texts:** 
  - [Mataṅgavṛtti](../texts/matangavrtti.md) — ref: vidyāpāda
  - [Mṛgendravṛtti](../texts/mrgendravrtti.md) — ref: vidyāpāda
### [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md)
Both: when the two deeds are balanced (iruviṉaiyoppu) and āṇava has ripened (malaparipākam), grace descends in four grades.
**Texts:** 
  - [Civañāṉa Cittiyār](../texts/sivananasiddhiyar.md) — ref: cupakkam.8

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** Under P2 the Kiraṇa's own image (sun and lotus) states the event from the causal standpoint (karma-equality as the time) and the divine standpoint (the Lord as cause); the Ahirbudhnya states it from the divine standpoint, with karma-equality as effect.
The reconciliation covers the Kiraṇa and Ahirbudhnya only. It does not claim the two agree on the direction of causation, nor that the Trika position is included.
**The traditions' own objections:** Abhinavagupta explicitly rejects karma-equality as a cause of śaktipāta; the Trika therefore objects to any reading that makes it a condition.
