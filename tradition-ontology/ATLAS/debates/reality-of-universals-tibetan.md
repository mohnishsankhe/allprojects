# Are universals real entities (the Tibetan debate on Dharmakīrti)?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
Chapa Chökyi Senge and the Sangphu logicians (Kadam scholastic line) treated certain universals and relations as real (reported by their critics).
- Conceptual cognition would be baseless without real generalities.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tshad ma yid kyi mun sel](../texts/tshad-ma-yid-kyi-mun-sel.md) — 
### [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)
Sakya Paṇḍita: only particulars are real; universals are conceptual constructs formed by exclusion, as Dharmakīrti taught.
- Only what performs a function is ultimately real.
**Texts:** 
  - [Treasury of Valid Reasoning (tshad ma rigs pa'i gter)](../texts/tshad-ma-rigs-gter.md) — 
### [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md)
Universals (generally characterised phenomena) exist conventionally as permanent, non-functioning phenomena, though they are not real in the sense of functioning things (Gelug pramāṇa textbooks).
- Existence and reality (functioning) are distinguished.
**Texts:** 
  - [Illuminating the Path to Liberation (rnam 'grel thar lam gsal byed)](../texts/rnam-grel-thar-lam-gsal-byed.md) — 
  - [Bsdus grwa (Collected Topics)](../texts/bsdus-grwa.md) —
