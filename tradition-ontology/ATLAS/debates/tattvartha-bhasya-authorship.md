# Is the Tattvārthabhāṣya Umāsvāti's own commentary on the Tattvārthasūtra?

`dsp:tattvartha-bhasya-authorship` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** B2 / G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
Yes: the bhāṣya is Umāsvāti's own (svopajña); its praśasti names the author and his lineage; Siddhasenagaṇi and Haribhadra comment on it as such.
- The closing praśasti.
- Continuity of the Śvetāmbara commentarial tradition.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tattvārthādhigamabhāṣya](../texts/tattvartha-bhasya.md) — ref: praśasti
### [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
No: the Digambaras follow the sūtra text fixed in the Sarvārthasiddhi and do not accept the bhāṣya as the author's.
- The bhāṣya reflects Śvetāmbara readings (e.g. of the sūtra on the kevalin and on liberation of any sign).
**Texts:** 
  - [Sarvārthasiddhi](../texts/sarvarthasiddhi.md) —
