# Should Śrīvidyā be practised only in the inner Samaya way, or also in the Kaula way with outer worship and Kaula conduct?

`dsp:samaya-or-kaula` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Coverage:** A8

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md)
Worship only in the inner space of the heart and crown; outer worship on a physical support is 'Kaula' and is rejected, as is the worship of a young woman's triangle; the sixty-four tantras are non-Vedic and aimed at worldly powers, the Candrakalā tantras 'mixed'; only the five Śubhāgamas teach the Vedic way.
- The Vedic five Śubhāgamas teach Samaya; the Kaula tantras deceive by aiming at powers.
- Śiva and Śakti are equal (fivefold equality), worshipped together within.
**Texts:** 
  - [Lakṣmīdharā (commentary on the Saundaryalaharī)](../texts/laksmidhara.md) — ref: on SL 8, 31, 41
### [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md)
Outer worship of the cakra is performed by Śiva himself and by every knower, because it produces the conviction of non-difference; Kaula conduct, including the five 'M's, is used in secret, and daily worship continues without them when unavailable; the Goddess is both Kaulinī and intent on Samaya conduct.
- YH 3.3: 'the cakra-worship is always performed by me'.
- The Sahasranāma names her Kaulinī and Samayācāratatparā, worshipped on right and left paths.
**Texts:** 
  - [Yoginīhṛdaya](../texts/yoginihrdaya.md) — ref: 3.2-3
  - [Paraśurāma Kalpasūtra](../texts/parasurama-kalpasutra.md) — ref: 1.12, 1.24
  - [Lalitā Sahasranāma](../texts/lalita-sahasranama.md) — ref: 36-37, 169-171

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage, P3-path
**Explanation:** Both sides grade practice by the student's qualification (Lakṣmīdhara assigns modes by eligibility; the Yoginīhṛdaya grades aparā, parāparā and parā worship), so the modes can be read as different stages and temperaments of one path.
The Yoginīhṛdaya's own sequence puts outer worship below meditative and non-dual worship, which is close to the Samaya valuation of inner worship; the Sahasranāma's names hold both modes as ways to the one Goddess.
**The traditions' own objections:** Lakṣmīdhara rejects the Kaula way as outside the Veda and would not accept it as a valid stage for the twice-born; the Kaula texts treat outer worship and Kaula conduct as part of the complete path, not a concession.
