# Is Kaula practice — the Kula worship with the five tattvas, the circle and the cremation ground — a legitimate path to liberation, or a departure from dharma?

`dsp:legitimacy-of-kaula-practice` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A8

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)
The Kaula path is the highest (Kulārṇava 2.7-8), both enjoyment and yoga (2.23-24), Vedic in essence (2.85), misunderstood by the deluded as musk is taken for mud (2.109); the realized Kaula is beyond injunction and prohibition (9.55-58). But it is harder than a sword's edge (2.122), must be given only to the tested (2.36; 14.9-14), and is condemned when practised for pleasure: wine alone gives no perfection (2.117), the wilful and uninitiated go to hell (5.96-105), and abandoning ritual out of false learning is heresy (9.130).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kulārṇava Tantra](../texts/kularnava-tantra.md) — ref: 2.7-8; 2.23-24; 2.85; 2.109; 2.117; 2.122; 5.96-105; 9.55-58; 9.130
### [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md)
(Rudrayāmala, voiced by Vasiṣṭha) The Siddhas of Mahācīna drink, eat meat and live with women 'outside the Veda' — how can perfection come without Vedic rites? The text answers through the Buddha that the Kula path begins in purity and the paśu disposition and is Vedic in its own way; the question is the orthodox objection it sets out to meet.
**Texts:** 
  - [Rudrayāmala (Uttaratantra)](../texts/rudrayamala.md) — ref: 17.128-140
### [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
(Samaya Śrīvidyā) The sixty-four (Kaula) tantras beguile the world, aim at powers and are outside the Veda; the three higher classes should follow only the Vedic Samaya way (Lakṣmīdhara on Saundaryalaharī 31).
**Texts:** 
  - [Lakṣmīdharā (commentary on the Saundaryalaharī)](../texts/laksmidhara.md) — ref: 31
### [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md)
(Kālīvilāsa) In the Kali age the Kaula (vīra) disposition does not exist; worship must be pure and meatless (4.2-3).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kālīvilāsa Tantra](../texts/kalivilasa-tantra.md) — ref: 4.2-6

**Historical debate — The Vasiṣṭha–Buddha dialogue in Mahācīna (Rudrayāmala 17)** (): A narrative debate inside the text, not a recorded historical event. Vasiṣṭha accepts the Kula way and becomes a Siddha.

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage, P1-level
**Explanation:** The Kaula texts address different eligibilities (P4) and speak of freedom from injunction only for the realized, from the ultimate standpoint (P1), while binding everyone else by the samayas and warnings.
Kulārṇava 9.55-58 ('no injunction, no prohibition') is spoken of the Kaula who has reached the goal; 5.96-105 and 9.130 bind the practitioner. This reconciles the Kaula texts with their own warnings; it does not reconcile Kaula with the Samaya and orthodox Vedic rejection of the tattvas.
**The traditions' own objections:** Samaya Śrīvidyā and orthodox Vedic authorities reject the Kaula rites as such, not merely their misuse; the Kaula texts reject the claim that their way is non-Vedic.
