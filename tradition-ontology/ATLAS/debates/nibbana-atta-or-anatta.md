# Is nibbāna a true self (attā) or not-self?

`dsp:nibbana-atta-or-anatta` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Dhammakāya meditation tradition (Vijjā Dhammakāya; Wat Paknam)](../lineages/dhammakaya.md)
Nibbāna is the true self and a realm where the Dhammakāyas abide; the Dhammakāya within is the real refuge (the Dhammakāya tradition's teaching as reported by its critics and in its publications).
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
All dhammas, including nibbāna, are not-self; nibbāna is the unconditioned element, not a self: 'there is extinction but no one extinguished'.
- 'sabbe dhammā anattā' includes the unconditioned.
- The Visuddhimagga's verse on the emptiness of the truths.
