# Is the Veda threefold (trayī), or is the Atharvaveda a Veda of equal (or higher) rank?

`dsp:atharvaveda-status` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
The Veda proper of the sacrifice is threefold — ṛc, yajus, sāman — the Vedas of the hotṛ, adhvaryu and udgātṛ; 'trayī' is the standing name of Vedic knowledge.
- The śrauta liturgy uses three Vedas; the Puruṣa hymn names verses, chants, metres and formulas.
**Texts:** 
  - [Ṛgveda Saṃhitā](../texts/rgveda.md) — ref: 10.90.9
  - [Vājasaneyi Saṃhitā (Mādhyandina)](../texts/vajasaneyi-samhita.md) — ref: 36.1
  - [Bhāgavata Purāṇa](../texts/bhagavata-purana.md) — ref: 1.4.25
### [Śaunaka śākhā (Atharvaveda)](../lineages/sakha-saunaka.md)
The Atharvaveda is the fourth Veda and, as the Brahmaveda, belongs to the brahmán priest who oversees and repairs the whole rite; it is listed fourth among the Vedas and received by Sumantu from Vyāsa.
- The Chāndogya lists the Atharvaveda fourth (7.1.2); the Purāṇic account gives four Vedas; the Atharvaveda's own ancillaries claim the brahmán's office.
**Texts:** 
  - [Chāndogya Upaniṣad](../texts/chandogya-upanisad.md) — ref: 7.1.2
  - [Bhāgavata Purāṇa](../texts/bhagavata-purana.md) — ref: 1.4.19–22

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** From the standpoint of the śrauta liturgy the Veda is threefold (the three chanting and reciting priests); from the standpoint of the whole revealed corpus it is fourfold.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Atharvavedic tradition claims more than a fourth place (the Brahmaveda over the others); the reconciliation does not decide that claim.
