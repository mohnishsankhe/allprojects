# Does the Rāmānandī order descend from Rāmānuja's Śrīvaiṣṇava lineage, or is it an independent sampradāya founded by Rāmānanda with its own line from Rāma?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md)
(Older lineage lists) Rāmānanda was a disciple of Rāghavānanda in Rāmānuja's line and founded his own branch after a dispute over commensality; the order is part of the Śrī sampradāya.
- Guruparamparā lists in manuals such as the Rāmārcanapaddhati.
**Texts:** 
  - [Śrī Rāmārcana-paddhati](../texts/ramarcanapaddhati.md) — ref: guruparamparā
### [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md)
(Independent faction, 20th c.) Rāmānanda founded an independent Rāmāvat sampradāya whose line runs from Rāma through Sītā and Hanumān; he wrote his own Brahma Sūtra commentary (Ānandabhāṣya); Rāma, not Nārāyaṇa, is supreme.
- The Ānandabhāṣya as Rāmānanda's own commentary.
- Differences of worship, mantra and openness to all castes from the southern Śrīvaiṣṇavas.
**Texts:** 
  - [Ānandabhāṣya](../texts/anandabhasya.md) — ref: whole
### [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
Śrīvaiṣṇava institutions have at times claimed the northern Rāmānandīs as a branch of their sampradāya.
