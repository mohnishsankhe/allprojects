# Is Śakti supreme, is Śiva supreme, or are they equal?

`dsp:siva-sakti-primacy` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Coverage:** A8

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md)
Śakti is the power without which Śiva cannot even stir; the gods act on her command; she is the queen of the supreme Brahman.
- SL 1; SL 24; Subhagodaya: the supreme without Śakti can do nothing.
**Texts:** 
  - [Saundaryalaharī](../texts/saundarya-lahari.md) — ref: 1, 24, 98
  - [Subhagodaya](../texts/subhagodaya.md) — ref: quoted
### [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)
Uttara-Kaula: there is no Śiva-principle apart from the Śakti-principle; Śiva is included in Śakti and she alone is to be worshipped. Pūrva-Kaula: the Goddess is Śiva's body, the relation of principal and subsidiary common to both.
- SL 23 and 35 read by Lakṣmīdhara as uttara-Kaula; SL 34 as pūrva-Kaula.
**Texts:** 
  - [Lakṣmīdharā (commentary on the Saundaryalaharī)](../texts/laksmidhara.md) — ref: on SL 23, 34-35
### [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md)
Śiva and Śakti are of equal primacy, sharing a fivefold equality of seat, state, action, form and name; neither is subordinate.
- SL 41: the world has these two as father and mother.
- Tripurā Upaniṣad 14: equal in primacy, being and vigour.
**Texts:** 
  - [Lakṣmīdharā (commentary on the Saundaryalaharī)](../texts/laksmidhara.md) — ref: on SL 41
  - [Tripurā Upaniṣad](../texts/tripura-upanisad.md) — ref: 14
### [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
The ultimate is the oneness of Śiva and Śakti — light and self-awareness fused — while worship is directed to the Goddess seated on Kāmeśvara's lap.
- LSN 183: 'whose form is the oneness of Śiva and Śakti'.
- Dīpikā on YH 1.6: sāmarasya of prakāśa and vimarśa.
**Texts:** 
  - [Lalitā Sahasranāma](../texts/lalita-sahasranama.md) — ref: 182-183
  - [Yoginīhṛdayadīpikā](../texts/yoginihrdaya-dipika.md) — ref: 1.6

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P2-standpoint, P1-level
**Explanation:** From the standpoint of worship and power (śakti) the Goddess is primary; from the standpoint of the ultimate the texts speak of the fusion or equality of Śiva and Śakti — different standpoints on one union.
Even the Śākta verse that exalts Śakti (SL 1) presupposes Śiva as the one who acts with her; the Samaya equality and the Śrīvidyā 'oneness' both deny separateness.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Samaya school explicitly rejects the uttara-Kaula view that Śiva is absorbed in Śakti; Śaiva lineages outside this unit hold Śakti to be Śiva's power and not his superior. These denials are recorded, not erased.
