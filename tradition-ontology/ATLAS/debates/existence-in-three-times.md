# Do past and future dharmas exist?

`dsp:existence-in-three-times` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
Yes: all dharmas exist in all three times as real entities; the times are distinguished by activity (kāritra).
- The Buddha taught that past and future matter is to be regarded as not self — so it exists.
- Consciousness arises from two (faculty and object); a consciousness of the past needs an existent object.
- Past karma yields its result, so it exists.
- The four masters explain the distinction of the times; Vasumitra's 'state' is best.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 5.25–27
  - [Abhidharma-mahāvibhāṣā-śāstra](../texts/mahavibhasa.md) — ref: fasc. 76–77 (low confidence)
### [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md)
No: only the present exists; the conditioned 'arises having not been, and having been ceases'; results arise from the transformed series preceded by the karma.
- A remembered past thing is not seen as existing now.
- If past and future existed like the present, they would be present.
- If the result already exists, what does karma do?
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośabhāṣya](../texts/abhidharmakosabhasya.md) — ref: 5.27, Pradhan p. 295–300
### [Kāśyapīya](../lineages/kasyapiya.md)
Partly: past karma that has not yet given its fruit exists; the rest of the past and the future do not (the Vibhajyavāda position).
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośabhāṣya](../texts/abhidharmakosabhasya.md) — ref: 5.25
  - [Samayabhedoparacanacakra](../texts/samayabhedoparacanacakra.md) — ref: Kāśyapīya tenets
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 1.8
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
No: 'everything exists' is refuted.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 1.6–1.8
### [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md)
No: past and future have no real existence (Vasumitra).
**Texts:** 
  - [Samayabhedoparacanacakra](../texts/samayabhedoparacanacakra.md) — ref: Mahāsāṃghika tenets

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the Sarvāstivāda affirms the real existence of past and future dharmas; the Sautrāntika, Mahāsāṃghika, Mahīśāsaka and Theravāda deny it, the Kāśyapīya partly.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Vaibhāṣikas hold that denial of past and future makes karma, memory and cognition of the past impossible; the Sautrāntikas hold that affirming them makes everything eternal and the present unintelligible ('deep indeed is the nature of dharmas' is not an answer).
