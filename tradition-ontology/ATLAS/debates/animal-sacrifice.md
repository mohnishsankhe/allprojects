# Is the sacrifice of animals meritorious?

`dsp:animal-sacrifice` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G11

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
Animal sacrifice performed according to the Veda is meritorious and not killing in the forbidden sense.
- The śrauta ritual prescribes animal victims (paśubandha, aśvamedha).
- Manusmṛti 5.39–44: the animals were created for sacrifice; killing in sacrifice is not killing.
**Texts:** 
  - [Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa (Mādhyandina)](../texts/satapatha-brahmana.md) — ref: (paśubandha, aśvamedha sections)
  - [Manusmṛti (Mānava Dharmaśāstra)](../texts/manusmrti.md) — ref: 5.39–44
### [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md)
A sacrifice in which living beings are killed is not praised; King Mahāvijita's model sacrifice killed no animals; giving, refuge, precepts, going forth and knowledge are sacrifices of greater fruit.
- DN 5: the bloodless sacrifice with the giver glad before, during and after.
- Snp 2.7 (Brāhmaṇadhammika): the ancient brahmins did not kill cattle; sacrifice of cattle began with greed.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kūṭadanta Sutta](../texts/kutadanta-sutta.md) — ref: DN 5
  - [Sutta Nipāta](../texts/sutta-nipata.md) — ref: Snp 2.7

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the Vedic ritual prescribes what the suttas reject.
**The traditions' own objections:** Mīmāṃsā holds the Vedic injunctions to be authoritative as injunctions, not as praise; the suttas reject killing outright.
**Candidate readings:** P4-stage / P3-path: the Vedic tradition itself ranks the inner sacrifice of knowledge above material offerings (BhG 4.33; ChU 3.16) — close to DN 5's hierarchy of sacrifices; P7-arthavada: praise of animal sacrifice read as arthavāda by some later Hindu authors
