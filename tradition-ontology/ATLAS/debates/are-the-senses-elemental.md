# Are the sense and action capacities made of the elements, or products of ahaṃkāra?

`dsp:are-the-senses-elemental` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G (Sāṃkhya–Nyāya; internal)

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
Products of ahaṃkāra (classical Sāṃkhya).
- The eleven organs proceed from ahaṃkāra in its sāttvika aspect (SK 25).
- They are not elemental, since scripture speaks of them as from ahaṃkāra (SS 2.20, 5.84).
**Texts:** 
  - [Sāṃkhya Kārikā](../texts/samkhya-karika.md) — ref: 25
  - [Sāṃkhya Sūtra](../texts/samkhya-sutra.md) — ref: 2.20; 5.84
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
Elemental: the senses come from the elements.
- Nyāya Sūtra 1.1.12: smell, taste, sight, touch and hearing are the senses, from the elements.
**Texts:** 
  - [Nyāya Sūtra](../texts/nyaya-sutra.md) — ref: 1.1.12
### [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
Elemental, per the Sāṃkhya teacher Pañcādhikaraṇa (a dissenting Sāṃkhya view).
- The Yuktidīpikā on SK 22 reports that for Pañcādhikaraṇa the organs are elemental (bhautika).
**Texts:** 
  - [Yuktidīpikā](../texts/yuktidipika.md) — ref: 22
