# What does the injunctive form (liṅ etc.) convey, and what prompts a person to act?

`dsp:meaning-of-injunction` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A5

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md)
Kumārila: bhāvanā — the verbal force of the injunction (śābdī) prompting the person's activity (ārthī) toward a result, by means and procedure; the person acts from desire for the result.
- Arthasaṅgraha's analysis of the verbal suffix.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tantravārttika](../texts/tantravarttika.md) — ref: on MS 2.1.1
  - [Arthasaṅgraha](../texts/arthasangraha.md) — ref: bhāvanā
### [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md)
Prabhākara: the injunction conveys niyoga, the command / what is to be done (kārya), which itself prompts the qualified person, and is identical with the apūrva.
**Texts:** 
  - [Bṛhatī](../texts/brhati.md) — ref: on MS 2.1
  - [Prakaraṇapañcikā](../texts/prakaranapancika.md) — ref: (chapter-level)
### [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md)
Maṇḍana Miśra: the injunction conveys that the act is a means to what is desired (iṣṭasādhanatā).
**Texts:** 
  - [Vidhiviveka](../texts/vidhiviveka.md) — ref: whole
