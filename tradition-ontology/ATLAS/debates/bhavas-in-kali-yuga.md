# Are the vīra and divya dispositions (and the five tattvas) permitted in the Kali age?

`dsp:bhavas-in-kali-yuga` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A8

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md)
(Kālīvilāsa) No: the divya and vīra dispositions 'do not exist, do not exist' in the Kali age; all classes worship in the paśu disposition (4.2-3); divya belonged to Satya–Tretā, vīra to Tretā–Dvāpara (6.10-16).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kālīvilāsa Tantra](../texts/kalivilasa-tantra.md) — ref: 4.2-6; 6.10-16
### [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md)
(Mahānirvāṇa) Yes, with restrictions: the five tattvas are proper to Śakti worship (5.22-24), the tantric path is the only one for this age (2.7-15), but in the Kali age the last tattva is only with one's own wife and householders use the sweet triad (6.14; 8.170-171).
**Texts:** 
  - [Mahānirvāṇa Tantra](../texts/mahanirvana-tantra.md) — ref: 2.7-15; 5.22-24; 6.14; 8.170-171
### [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)
(Kulārṇava) The Kaula is highest without any age restriction, though only for the tested and eligible (2.7-8; 2.30-36).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kulārṇava Tantra](../texts/kularnava-tantra.md) — ref: 2.7-8; 2.30-36
