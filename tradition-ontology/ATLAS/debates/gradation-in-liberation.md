# Are the liberated graded in bliss, or equal (or one)?

`dsp:gradation-in-liberation` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
The liberated are graded: each class enjoys a hundredfold more bliss than the one below, up to Brahmā and Vāyu; the five differences and gradation are not lost in liberation.
- scripture lists the hundredfold steps of bliss
- fitness is beginningless and unalterable
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya (Madhva)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-madhva.md) — ref: 4.4.19
  - [Mahābhārata-tātparya-nirṇaya](../texts/mahabharata-tatparya-nirnaya.md) — ref: 1.68-74
  - [Nyāyāmṛta](../texts/nyayamrta.md) — ref: 4
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
In liberation there is only the one Brahman, whose bliss is without degrees; the graded blisses of scripture (e.g. Taittirīya 2.8) are portions of that bliss experienced under limiting adjuncts, not differences among the liberated.
- liberation is identity with Brahman
- gradation belongs to the conditioned state
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 4.4
  - [Advaitasiddhi](../texts/advaitasiddhi.md) — ref: pariccheda 4
### [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
The liberated are equal to Brahman in enjoyment of his bliss, differing from him only in not governing the world; there is no gradation among the released.
- sūtra 4.4.21 speaks of equality in enjoyment
- all released souls enjoy the same Brahman
**Texts:** 
  - [Śrībhāṣya](../texts/sribhasya.md) — ref: 4.4.17-21

**Historical debate — Nyāyāmṛta 4 and Advaitasiddhi 4** (16th c. CE): The fourth chapters of the two works argue whether the liberated are graded. Each side holds its own view established.
