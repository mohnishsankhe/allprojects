# Can the Lord be only the efficient cause of the world, as the Māheśvaras teach? (Brahma Sūtra 2.2.37ff, paśupati-adhikaraṇa)

**Coverage:** G2

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
The Lord is the cause; the effect - vidyā, kalā, paśu - is eternal and depends on him; Rudra is the efficient cause, his Kalā the other cause (as Yāmuna reports).
- The Lord produces, favours and conceals the effect in play (PABh on PS 1.9).
- As a potter is only the efficient cause of a pot, a conscious ruler is only the efficient cause (their reasoning as reported by Vācaspati).
**Texts:** 
  - [Pañcārthabhāṣya](../texts/pancarthabhasya.md) — ref: on PS 1.9, 2.6
  - [Bhāmatī](../texts/bhamati.md) — ref: 2.2.37
  - [Āgamaprāmāṇya](../texts/agamapramanya.md) — ref: Māheśvara section
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Brahman is both the material and the efficient cause; the view of a Lord who is only ruler is non-Vedic and inconsistent (partiality, no possible relation, no support for ruling insentient matter, finitude or non-omniscience).
- Patyur asāmañjasyāt (BS 2.2.37) and the following sūtras, as explained by Śaṅkara.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahma Sūtra](../texts/brahma-sutra.md) — ref: 2.2.37-41
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 2.2.37
### [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
The Paśupati doctrine is contrary to the Veda and internally inconsistent; its practices (insignia, skull-eating, corpse-ash) are non-Vedic.
- Rāmānuja on the paśupati-adhikaraṇa, following Yāmuna.
**Texts:** 
  - [Śrībhāṣya](../texts/sribhasya.md) — ref: 2.2.35ff
  - [Āgamaprāmāṇya](../texts/agamapramanya.md) — ref: Māheśvara section

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the disagreement concerns whether the Lord is also the material cause of the world.
**The traditions' own objections:** Both Vedānta schools reject the Pāśupata view as non-Vedic; the Pāśupatas count Vedāntic and Sāṃkhya causes among the effects (PABh 5.46).
**Candidate readings:** P2-standpoint: the Pāśupata analyses the world as eternal effect under a ruler (causal standpoint); Vedānta speaks of Brahman as the substance of all (substance standpoint).; No reconciliation: Vedānta treats the non-material-cause view as contrary to śruti.
