# Is the aspiration for awakening (bodhicitta) required for birth in the Pure Land?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)
(Tanluan; the Larger Sūtra's three grades; the three meritorious acts) All aspirants must arouse bodhicitta, which is the vow to save beings; seeking birth for pleasure does not bring birth.
**Texts:** 
  - [Commentary on the Rebirth Treatise (Wangsheng lun zhu)](../texts/wangsheng-lun-zhu.md) — ref: fasc. 2
  - [Amitāyurdhyāna-sūtra (Guan wuliangshou jing)](../texts/amitayurdhyana-sutra.md) — ref: intro
### [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md)
(Hōnen) Bodhicitta is among the practices mentioned for the three grades in order to be set aside in favour of the nenbutsu alone.
**Texts:** 
  - [Passages on the Selection of the Nenbutsu in the Original Vow (Senchakushū)](../texts/senchakushu.md) — ref: ch. 4
### `lin:huayan`
(Myōe) Discarding bodhicitta is the first great error of the Senchakushū; without it the nenbutsu is not Buddhism.
**Texts:** 
  - [Wheel Crushing Heresy (Zaijarin)](../texts/zaijarin.md) — ref: whole
### [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md)
(Shinran) The true bodhicitta is other-power: shinjin itself, given by Amida.
**Texts:** 
  - [The True Teaching, Practice and Realization of the Pure Land Way (Kyōgyōshinshō)](../texts/kyogyoshinsho.md) — ref: Shin chapter

**Historical debate — Myōe's Zaijarin against the Senchakushū** (1212–1213): Zaijarin and Zaijarin shōgonki. Jōdo and Shinshū answered through later commentaries and Shinran's other-power bodhicitta.
