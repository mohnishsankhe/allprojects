# Is the Pāśupata (and wider Atimārga) teaching within or outside the Veda?

`dsp:pasupata-vedic-status` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Coverage:** G11

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
The Pāśupata path is the best of all paths, the true path, others being bad paths; its scripture comes from Maheśvara through the teachers; it uses the Vedic brahma-mantras and is for brahmins; only from the Pañcārtha comes true ascertainment of reality.
- PS 4.16-18; PABh on PS 1.1 (āgama); SDS ch. 6 ('pañcārthād anyato nāsti yathāvat tattvaniścayaḥ').
**Texts:** 
  - [Pāśupata Sūtra](../texts/pasupata-sutra.md) — ref: 4.16-18
  - [Pañcārthabhāṣya](../texts/pancarthabhasya.md) — ref: on PS 1.1
  - [Sarvadarśanasaṃgraha](../texts/sarvadarsanasangraha.md) — ref: ch. 6
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
The Vedic Pāśupata vow of the Atharvaśiras is to be followed; the Pāśupata, Soma, Lākula, Vāma and Bhairava scriptures are non-Vedic and were taught for delusion.
- Kūrma P. 2.37.140-146; 1.15.111-113; Śiva P. 7.1.33 (the śrauta Pāśupata vow).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kūrma Purāṇa](../texts/kurma-purana.md) — ref: 2.37.140-146
  - [Kūrma Purāṇa](../texts/kurma-purana.md) — ref: 1.15.111-113
  - [Śiva Purāṇa](../texts/siva-purana.md) — ref: 7.1.33
### [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
All four Māheśvara groups are outside the Veda: they contradict the Vedic Brahman as material and efficient cause and devise āśramas outside the Vedic order.
- Yāmuna, Āgamaprāmāṇya; Rāmānuja, Śrībhāṣya 2.2.35ff.
**Texts:** 
  - [Āgamaprāmāṇya](../texts/agamapramanya.md) — ref: Māheśvara section
  - [Śrībhāṣya](../texts/sribhasya.md) — ref: 2.2.35ff
### [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
Pāśupata is one of five systems of knowledge (with Sāṃkhya, Yoga, Pañcarātra and the Vedas), taught by Śiva Śrīkaṇṭha; Nārāyaṇa is the foundation in all.
- Mahābhārata 12.337.59-64 (Nārāyaṇīya).
**Texts:** 
  - [Mahābhārata](../texts/mahabharata.md) — ref: 12.337.59-64

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the Pāśupatas claim the highest path; Purāṇic and Vaiṣṇava critics place the Pāśupata scriptures outside the Veda.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Kūrma Purāṇa and Yāmuna reject any inclusive reading; the Pāśupata Sūtra calls all other paths bad paths.
**Candidate readings:** P3-path: the Nārāyaṇīya's own stance - several systems for different temperaments with one foundation.; P4-stage: the Pāśupata presents itself as a state beyond the āśramas for qualified brahmins (atyāśrama).; No reconciliation: the Kūrma Purāṇa and Yāmuna explicitly deny Vedic authority to these scriptures.
