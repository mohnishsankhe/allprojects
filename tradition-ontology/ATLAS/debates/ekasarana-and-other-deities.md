# Must the devotee of Kṛṣṇa abstain from worshipping other deities (and from animal sacrifice to them)?

`dsp:ekasarana-and-other-deities` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md)
The Goddess is to be worshipped with the offerings vowed to her, including animal sacrifice; neglecting her brings harm.
- Mādhavadeva's initial position in the debate with Śaṅkaradeva, as the Ekaśaraṇa hagiography reports it.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathā-gurucarit](../texts/katha-gurucarit.md) — ref: Mādhavadeva episode
### [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md)
Worship of Kṛṣṇa alone suffices, as watering the root of a tree nourishes all its branches (BhP 4.31.14); worship of other gods breaks the single refuge.
- Bhāgavata 4.31.14.
- The Kīrtana-ghoṣā's refutation of other paths.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathā-gurucarit](../texts/katha-gurucarit.md) — ref: Mādhavadeva episode
  - [Kīrtana-ghoṣā](../texts/kirtana-ghosa.md) — ref: Pāṣaṇḍa-mardana

**Historical debate — Śaṅkaradeva and Mādhavadeva on the goat sacrifice** (early 16th c.): Kathā-gurucarit and other carit-puthis Mādhavadeva becomes Śaṅkaradeva's disciple.
