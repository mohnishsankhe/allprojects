# The status of the Veda: authorless, God-authored, or rejected?

`dsp:status-of-veda` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md)
(As reported) Rejected: the Veda is tainted by falsehood, inconsistency and repetition; its makers were buffoons, knaves and demons; its rites are a livelihood of priests; the Bārhaspatyas call it a mere cover.
- the slain beast and the sacrificer's father
- oil on a quenched lamp
**Texts:** 
  - [Sarvadarśanasaṃgraha](../texts/sarvadarsanasangraha.md) — ref: ch. 1
  - [Kauṭilīya Arthaśāstra](../texts/arthasastra.md) — ref: 1.2.4-5
### [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md)
(As reported) Ajita: there is no gift, no sacrifice, no offering.
- DN 2
**Texts:** 
  - [Sāmaññaphala Sutta](../texts/samannaphala-sutta.md) — ref: DN 2 §23
### [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md)
Sacrifice of animals and claims of birth-purity are refuted; the priests are cheats (Bhūridatta).
- if the slain go to heaven, let brāhmaṇas slay brāhmaṇas
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhūridatta Jātaka](../texts/bhuridatta-jataka.md) — ref: 161-173
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
The charge of falsehood, inconsistency (vyāghāta) and repetition fails: a failed rite is due to defects in the act, agent or means.
- NS 2.1.58–59
**Texts:** 
  - [Nyāya Sūtra](../texts/nyaya-sutra.md) — ref: 2.1.58-59
