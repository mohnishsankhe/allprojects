# Are space and dependent origination unconditioned?

`dsp:kv-unconditioned-dhammas` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mahīśāsaka](../lineages/mahisasaka.md)
Dependent origination and space are unconditioned (held, per the commentary, by Pubbaseliyas, Mahiṃsāsakas and Uttarāpathakas).
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 6.2
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
Only nibbāna is unconditioned; if these were unconditioned they would be a refuge, the deathless.
- Comparison with the qualities of nibbāna.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 6.2
