# Are the tantras the word of the Buddha and a legitimate Buddhist path?

`dsp:legitimacy-of-tantra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
Yes: the tantras were taught by the Buddha in the form of Vajradhara; they share the Mahāyāna goal and view and excel in means.
- The Buddha taught the Guhyasamāja in Oḍḍiyāna and the Kālacakra at Dhānyakaṭaka (tradition's account).
- Same goal as the perfection way, superior by four features (Nayatrayapradīpa, quoted by Advayavajra).
- The tantras' shocking statements are 'the pure dharmatā of the buddhas' (GST ch. 5), to be read with the explanatory tantras and the six alternatives.

### [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md)
No (as reported): the tantras are not the Buddha's word; their rites and conduct violate the Vinaya and resemble non-Buddhist practice.
- The objection is dramatized within the Guhyasamāja itself, where the bodhisattvas are astonished at the Lord's 'ill-spoken words'.
- Tibetan histories (Tāranātha) report śrāvaka monks at Vajrāsana destroying tantric images and texts (low confidence).
