### [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
No: since the organs are all-pervading, birth is their functioning at the seed-place and death its abandonment (Vindhyavāsin).
- Yuktidīpikā on SK 39; Kumārila reports that Vindhyavāsin did not accept an intermediate body (recalled).
