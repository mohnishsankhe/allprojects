# Is the mind self-luminous (illuminating itself and its objects), or is it seen (an object of the seer)?

`dsp:ys-citta-self-luminous-or-seen` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
The mind is not self-luminous because it is seen; it is grasped by puruṣa, whose consciousness, not passing over, takes the form of the intellect's modification; a mind grasped by another mind leads to regress and confusion of memory.
- na tat svābhāsaṃ dṛśyatvāt
- cittāntaradṛśye buddhibuddher atiprasaṅgaḥ smṛtisaṃkaraś ca
