# Is a stable, undecaying body required for liberation in life?

`dsp:is-bodily-immortality-required-for-liberation` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Rasa Śāstra (the science of mercury) and the Raseśvara-darśana](../lineages/rasa-sastra.md)
Rasa Śāstra: yes — liberation after the body falls cannot be seen and is 'meaningless'; knowledge requires practice and practice a stable body; a body worn by age is unfit for samādhi; so the body must first be made divine through mercury, and then yoga yields brahmanhood here.
**Texts:** 
  - [Rasārṇava](../texts/rasarnava.md) — ref: 1.8-13
  - [Rasahṛdayatantra](../texts/rasahrdayatantra.md) — ref: 1.10, 1.17-19, 1.29
  - [Sarvadarśanasaṃgraha](../texts/sarvadarsanasangraha.md) — ref: Raseśvara 1-5, 26-37
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
The 'six systems' as the Rasa texts report them: liberation is realized at or after the fall of the body; and (Advaita, the SDS objector) liberation is the manifestation of being-consciousness-bliss by knowledge, for which no divine body is needed.
**Texts:** 
  - [Sarvadarśanasaṃgraha](../texts/sarvadarsanasangraha.md) — ref: Raseśvara 4, 26
### [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
Āyurveda (Caraka): liberation is the cessation of all feeling and non-return through yoga, the recollection of truth and renunciation; rasāyana prolongs life but old age and death are natural and without remedy.
**Texts:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Śā 1.137-155; 1.115; Ci 1

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the Rasa tradition insists on the undecaying body; the others do not require it.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Rasārṇava calls post-mortem liberation 'meaningless' and the SDS rejects the knowledge-only objection as idle talk; the Advaita side holds the body irrelevant to knowledge-liberation.
**Candidate readings:** P3-path: Rasa as a body-breath path whose stated end (knowledge of identity with Śiva, the light of consciousness) overlaps the knowledge paths; P4-stage: the stable body as a preparatory condition (B2) rather than liberation itself — the Rasa texts themselves say liberation comes from knowledge (Rasahṛdaya 1.10); P1-level: bodily stability belongs to the conventional level, identity with Śiva to the ultimate
