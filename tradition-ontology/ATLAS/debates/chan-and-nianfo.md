# Is recitation of the Buddha's name compatible with Chan?

`dsp:chan-and-nianfo` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
The deluded recite the Buddha's name seeking the West; the awakened purify their own minds — the Pure Land is the pure mind (Platform Sūtra); guarding one's own mind surpasses recollecting another buddha (Xiuxin yao lun).
**Texts:** 
  - [Platform Sūtra of the Sixth Patriarch (Liuzu tanjing)](../texts/platform-sutra.md) — ref: 6.8
  - [Treatise on the Essentials of Cultivating the Mind (Xiuxin yao lun)](../texts/xiuxin-yao-lun.md) — 
### [Fayan house](../lineages/fayan.md)
Joint practice: Chan and recitation together, as advocated by Yongming Yanshou, Zhongfeng Mingben, Yunqi Zhuhong and the Ōbaku school; Musang opened his assemblies with recitation.
**Texts:** 
  - [The Myriad Good Practices Share the Same End (Wanshan tonggui ji)](../texts/wanshan-tonggui-ji.md) — 
  - [Record of the Dharma Jewel through the Generations (Lidai fabao ji)](../texts/lidai-fabao-ji.md) — 

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path, P6-upaya
**Explanation:** Recitation as a method for devotional temperaments and as a huatou ('who recites?') within Chan practice.
**The traditions' own objections:** Pure Land schools (U43) hold rebirth through Amitābha's vow to be the point, not a mind-only reading; some Chan teachers reject mixing the two.
