# Is the cause of birth the nenbutsu (with the three minds) or shinjin?

`dsp:true-cause-of-birth` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md)
The nenbutsu is the rightly determined act and cause of birth; the three minds accompany it.
**Texts:** 
  - [Passages on the Selection of the Nenbutsu in the Original Vow (Senchakushū)](../texts/senchakushu.md) — ref: ch. 2, 8, 16
### [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md)
Shinjin is the true cause (shinjin shōin); the nenbutsu is the expression of gratitude (shōmyō hōon).
**Texts:** 
  - [The True Teaching, Practice and Realization of the Pure Land Way (Kyōgyōshinshō)](../texts/kyogyoshinsho.md) — ref: Shin
  - [The Letters of Rennyo (Ofumi / Gobunshō)](../texts/rennyo-ofumi.md) — ref: 5.1

**Historical debate — The debate on shinjin in Hōnen's circle** (c. 1205): Godenshō (the 'debate on shinjin' episode, Shinran's claim that his shinjin and Hōnen's were one) Shinshū: Hōnen affirmed that shinjin, being given by the Buddha, is the same in all.
