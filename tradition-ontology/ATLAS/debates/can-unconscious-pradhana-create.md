# Can an unconscious pradhāna, without a conscious director, bring forth and order the world?

`dsp:can-unconscious-pradhana-create` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G (Sāṃkhya–Vedānta; Sāṃkhya–Nyāya)

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
Yes: prakṛti acts spontaneously for puruṣa's purpose; no Lord is needed or established.
- As unconscious milk flows for the calf, pradhāna acts for puruṣa's liberation (SK 57); as people act to satisfy a longing (SK 58).
- The conjunction of the seeing and the moving is like that of the lame and the blind (SK 21); the subtle body acts like an actor for puruṣa's purpose (SK 42); the instruments are made to act by no one (SK 31).
- Pradhāna's activity is like milk, spontaneous like a servant's, drawn on by beginningless karma (SS 3.58–63); governance by mere proximity, like a magnet (SS 1.96).
- Īśvara is not established (SS 1.92–99; 5.2–12); effects depend on ahaṃkāra, not Īśvara (SS 6.64); the attributeless cannot produce what has guṇas (Gauḍapāda on SK 61).
**Texts:** 
  - [Sāṃkhya Kārikā](../texts/samkhya-karika.md) — ref: 21, 31, 42, 57-58
  - [Sāṃkhya Sūtra](../texts/samkhya-sutra.md) — ref: 3.58-63; 5.2-12
  - [Sāṃkhyakārikābhāṣya of Gauḍapāda](../texts/samkhya-karika-bhasya-gaudapada.md) — ref: 61
### [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md)
No: the inferred pradhāna cannot be the cause, because an unconscious cause cannot produce orderly arrangement or activity.
- Orderly arrangement is impossible for the inferred cause (BS 2.2.1), and activity too (2.2.2).
- Milk and water flow under a conscious being's direction (2.2.3).
- The lame-and-blind or magnet analogies fail (2.2.7); the guṇas in equilibrium cannot become principal and subordinate (2.2.8); the doctrine is incoherent (2.2.10).
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahma Sūtra](../texts/brahma-sutra.md) — ref: 2.2.1-10
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 2.2.1-10
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
No: effects such as the world require an intelligent maker (Īśvara) as their efficient cause.
- Udayana's arguments for a world-knowing, imperishable maker from effects, arrangement, support and other marks (Nyāyakusumāñjali 5.1).
**Texts:** 
  - [Nyāyakusumāñjali](../texts/nyaya-kusumanjali.md) — ref: 5.1

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: whether a conscious agent is required is the point at issue.
**The traditions' own objections:** Classical Sāṃkhya denies that any Īśvara is established; Vedānta and Nyāya deny that the milk and lame-blind analogies apply without a conscious director.
**Candidate readings:** P2-standpoint (causal): prakṛti as material cause and a Lord as efficient cause — the theistic-Sāṃkhya reading; rejected by classical Sāṃkhya (SS 1.92) and by Advaita, for which Brahman is both material and efficient cause.; P2-standpoint (purpose): Sāṃkhya's teleology 'for puruṣa' already locates the orienting factor in consciousness (puruṣa's presence), which Vedānta places in the Lord.
