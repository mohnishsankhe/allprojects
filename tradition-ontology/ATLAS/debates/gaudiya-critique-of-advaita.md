# Is the ultimate an attributeless Brahman to be realised by 'that thou art', or the personal Bhagavān reached by devotion? (Caitanya against the Advaita renouncers)

`dsp:gaudiya-critique-of-advaita` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)
Caitanya (CC 1.7, 2.6): the Vedānta's direct sense teaches a personal Lord with transcendental qualities and powers; Brahman is his effulgence; the world is pariṇāma of his power; the great saying is Oṃ; Śaṅkara took a secondary sense; atheism that relies on the Veda is worse than Buddhism; love, not liberation, is the goal.
**Texts:** 
  - [Caitanya Caritāmṛta](../texts/caitanya-caritamrta.md) — ref: 1.7
  - [Caitanya Caritāmṛta](../texts/caitanya-caritamrta.md) — ref: 2.6
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Advaita (Sārvabhauma and Prakāśānanda before their conversion, as presented by the Gauḍīya texts; and Advaita's own texts): Brahman is attributeless; 'that thou art' teaches identity; forms and qualities belong to the conventional level.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — 

**Historical debate — Caitanya and Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya at Purī** (c. 1510 CE): CC 2.6; Caitanya Bhāgavata; Kavikarṇapūra Gauḍīya: Sārvabhauma became a devotee. Not recorded in Advaita sources.

**Historical debate — Caitanya and Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī at Vārāṇasī** (c. 1515 CE): CC 1.7 and 2.25 Gauḍīya: the renouncers accepted devotion. Known only from Gauḍīya sources.
