# Is Brahman (God) the material cause of the world, or only its efficient cause?

`dsp:brahman-material-cause` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A6; G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
Both: Brahman with souls and matter as its subtle body is the material cause, and as their self the efficient cause; the change belongs to the body, not to Brahman's essence.
- BS 1.4.23
- 'by knowing one, all is known'
**Texts:** 
  - [Śrībhāṣya](../texts/sribhasya.md) — ref: 1.4.23
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Both, but as the substrate of an apparent transformation (vivarta-upādāna): the world appears on Brahman through māyā.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Śaṅkara (Śārīrakamīmāṃsābhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: 1.4.23; 2.1.14
### [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
Only efficient: prakṛti is the material cause; Viṣṇu, eternally distinct, rules it.
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya (Madhva)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-madhva.md) — ref: 1.4 (recalled)
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
Only efficient: God arranges eternal atoms, which are the material cause.
**Texts:** 
  - [Nyāyakusumāñjali](../texts/nyaya-kusumanjali.md) — ref: passim
