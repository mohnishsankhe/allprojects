# Is the difference between Brahman and the soul due to adjuncts, natural, or inconceivable?

`dsp:aupadhika-vs-svabhavika-bhedabheda` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md)
Bhāskara: non-difference is natural, difference is due to real adjuncts (aupādhika); when the adjuncts fall away at death the soul is one with Brahman.
- scripture teaches identity as the final truth, difference as conditioned
- the adjuncts are real, so the world and bondage are real
**Texts:** 
  - [Brahmasūtrabhāṣya of Bhāskara (Bhāskarabhāṣya)](../texts/brahma-sutra-bhasya-bhaskara.md) — ref: 2.3.43
### [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md)
Yādavaprakāśa: both are natural (svābhāvika); Brahman itself becomes Īśvara, souls and matter (as reported by Rāmānuja).
- scripture declares both difference and non-difference without qualification
**Texts:** 
  - [Vedārthasaṅgraha](../texts/vedarthasangraha.md) — ref: critique of Yādavaprakāśa (section to verify)
### [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md)
Nimbārka: both difference and non-difference are natural and eternal, like snake and coils or sun and rays; not due to adjuncts.
- BS 3.2.27–28
- the soul is an eternal part and power, dependent yet real
**Texts:** 
  - [Vedāntapārijātasaurabha](../texts/vedanta-parijata-saurabha.md) — ref: 3.2.27
### [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
Rāmānuja: both forms are faulty — Bhāskara's makes Brahman itself suffer through its adjuncts, Yādava's makes Brahman itself undergo change and imperfection; the relation is that of soul and body (inseparable attribute).
- Brahman must remain free of all defects (akhila-heya-pratyanīka)
**Texts:** 
  - [Vedārthasaṅgraha](../texts/vedarthasangraha.md) — ref: (sections to verify)
  - [Śrībhāṣya](../texts/sribhasya.md) — ref: on BS 2.3.43
### [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)
Jīva: the relation is inconceivable (acintya) — neither adjunct-made nor comprehensible by logic — because the Lord's powers are inconceivable.
- reason cannot settle what revelation presents as both

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** not yet reconciled: each school's account of the cause of difference is a claim about what is real, and each rejects the others'.
**The traditions' own objections:** Every school named insists its own account is literal; Gauḍīyas object that calling the relation 'standpoint-relative' removes its inconceivability.
**Candidate readings:** P2-standpoint: part–whole (difference) vs substance (non-difference) as complementary standpoints — rejected by Bhāskara, who makes difference conditional, and by Rāmānuja, who denies non-difference in substance; P1-level: difference at the conventional level, non-difference at the ultimate — this is Advaita's reading and all these schools reject it as making difference unreal; Gauḍīya 'acintya' as a meta-position: the tension is itself affirmed as beyond reason
