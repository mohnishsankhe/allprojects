# How is a cognition (and its validity) known?

`dsp:how-cognition-is-known` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A5

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md)
A cognition is not perceived; it is inferred from the knownness (jñātatā) it produces in the object; validity belongs to it intrinsically.
- Śāstradīpikā on MS 1.1.5; the Vṛttikāra: cognition is inferred, the object perceived.
**Texts:** 
  - [Śāstradīpikā](../texts/sastradipika.md) — ref: 1.1.5
  - [Śābarabhāṣya](../texts/sabara-bhasya.md) — ref: 1.1.5
### [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md)
Every cognition is self-luminous and manifests knower, known and itself together (tripuṭī); validity is known with it.
**Texts:** 
  - [Prakaraṇapañcikā](../texts/prakaranapancika.md) — ref: ch. 8
### [Murāri Miśra's school (the 'third path' of Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/murari-mimamsa.md)
A cognition and its validity are grasped by the after-cognition (anuvyavasāya) that follows it (as reported by Gaṅgeśa).
**Texts:** 
  - [Tattvacintāmaṇi](../texts/tattvacintamani.md) — ref: prāmāṇyavāda
### [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
A cognition is known by the after-cognition; its validity is known extrinsically, by inference from successful action.
**Texts:** 
  - [Tattvacintāmaṇi](../texts/tattvacintamani.md) — ref: prāmāṇyavāda
