# Is the mind (citta) only of the size of the body, contracting and expanding, or all-pervading with only its function contracting and expanding?

`dsp:size-of-citta` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
The mind is like a lamp placed in a jar or a palace: it contracts and expands and is only of the size of the body; thus an intermediate state (antarābhāva) and saṃsāra are logical.
- ghaṭaprāsādapradīpakalpaṃ saṃkocavikāsi cittaṃ śarīraparimāṇākāramātram ity apare pratipannāḥ tathā cāntarābhāvaḥ saṃsāraś ca yukta iti
**Texts:** 
  - [Yogabhāṣya (Vyāsa's commentary on the Yoga Sūtra)](../texts/yoga-bhasya.md) — ref: 4.10
### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
It is the function (vṛtti) of the all-pervading (vibhu) mind that contracts and expands; this depends on dharma and the like as occasion, external or internal.
- vṛttir evāsya vibhunaś cittasya saṃkocavikāsinīty ācāryaḥ
**Texts:** 
  - [Yogabhāṣya (Vyāsa's commentary on the Yoga Sūtra)](../texts/yoga-bhasya.md) — ref: 4.10
### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
Some (apare): the mind, like a lamp placed in a jar or a palace, contracts and expands to the size of the body, which makes the intermediate state and transmigration intelligible.
**Texts:** 
  - [Yogabhāṣya (Vyāsa's commentary on the Yoga Sūtra)](../texts/yoga-bhasya.md) — ref: 4.10
### [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
The teacher (ācārya): the mind is all-pervading; only its activity contracts and expands, depending on merit and other causes.
**Texts:** 
  - [Yogabhāṣya (Vyāsa's commentary on the Yoga Sūtra)](../texts/yoga-bhasya.md) — ref: 4.10
