# In what sense is the living being 'a portion of me' (mamaivāṃśo jīvaloke jīvabhūtaḥ sanātanaḥ, BhG 15.7)?

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
A portion only as it were: as the sun's reflection in water, or the space enclosed by a pot, the individual appears as a part of the Lord through limiting adjuncts; when these are removed it is not other than him.
- the images of the reflected sun and the pot-space (recalled)
**Texts:** 
  - [Gītābhāṣya of Śaṅkara](../texts/gita-bhasya-sankara.md) — ref: on 15.7
### [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
A real and eternal part of the Lord, related to him as a mode or attribute to its substance; 'eternal' (sanātana) shows that the part and its distinctness are lasting.
- 'sanātanaḥ' (recalled)
- BS 2.3.43 'aṃśo nānāvyapadeśāt' (recalled)
**Texts:** 
  - [Gītābhāṣya of Rāmānuja](../texts/gita-bhasya-ramanuja.md) — ref: on 15.7
  - [Śrībhāṣya](../texts/sribhasya.md) — ref: 2.3.43
### [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
A separated portion (vibhinnāṃśa), eternally distinct from and dependent on the Lord and like him only in part, as distinct from the Lord's own forms (svāṃśa).
