### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
Not everything is due to kamma: kamma itself is not due to kamma; experiences arise also from bile, phlegm, wind, seasons, careless conduct and assault (five orders of nature).
- Would kamma then be due to kamma?
- The five niyāmas.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 17.3
