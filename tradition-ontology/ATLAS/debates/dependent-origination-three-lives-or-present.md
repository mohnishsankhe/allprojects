# Does dependent origination describe rebirth across three lives, or a process in present experience?

`dsp:dependent-origination-three-lives-or-present` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
The commentaries expound the twelve links across three lives: two past causes, eight present, two future — twenty modes and three links; the wheel has no maker or experiencer.
- Visuddhimagga XVII's analysis.
**Texts:** 
  - [Visuddhimagga](../texts/visuddhimagga.md) — ref: XVII
### [Suan Mokkh (Buddhadāsa's tradition)](../lineages/suan-mokkh.md)
The whole chain arises in daily life at every unmindful contact; 'birth' is the birth of the ego; reading it only across lives makes it useless for practice.
- The suttas' ordinary-language and Dhamma-language senses of 'birth'.
**Texts:** 
  - [Paṭiccasamuppāda: Practical Dependent Origination](../texts/paticcasamuppada-buddhadasa.md) — ref: intro
  - [Handbook for Mankind (Khu Mue Manut)](../texts/handbook-for-mankind.md) — ref: dhamma-language
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
The Vibhaṅga's Abhidhamma method already shows the links within one moment of consciousness, alongside the sutta method across lives.
**Texts:** 
  - [Vibhaṅga](../texts/vibhanga.md) — ref: 6

**Historical debate — Thai debate over Buddhadāsa's reading** (later 20th c.): Buddhadāsa's critique of the three-lives model drew conservative criticism; Payutto's Buddhadhamma presents both readings (recalled, low confidence). Both readings are taught

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P1-level, P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** The canonical Abhidhamma itself gives both an across-lives and a single-moment exposition; each reading is a standpoint the tradition already contains.
**The traditions' own objections:** Commentarial Theravāda insists that the across-lives reading, with literal rebirth, is not optional; Buddhadāsa rejected making it the primary meaning.
