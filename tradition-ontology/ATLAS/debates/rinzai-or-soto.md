# Kōan introspection and kenshō (Rinzai) or just sitting as practice-realization (Sōtō)?

`dsp:rinzai-or-soto` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** I1

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Rinzai Zen](../lineages/rinzai.md)
Kenshō must be attained through great doubt on kōans and then deepened by post-awakening training; quiet sitting without the kōan becomes dead sitting; practice in activity surpasses practice in stillness.
- Hakuin's own breakthrough through great doubt.
- Wumen: without passing the barrier one is a ghost clinging to grasses.
**Texts:** 
  - [Gateless Barrier (Wumen guan)](../texts/wumenguan.md) — ref: 1
  - [Embossed Tea Kettle (Orategama)](../texts/orategama.md) — 
  - [Wild Ivy (Itsumadegusa)](../texts/itsumadegusa.md) — 
### [Sōtō Zen](../lineages/soto.md)
Practice and realization are one; just sitting is itself the enactment of buddhahood, not a means to a later kenshō; students should just sit rather than rely on kōan stories.
- Bendōwa: even a beginner's practice is the whole of original realization.
- Rujing: just sit; body and mind dropping off.
**Texts:** 
  - [Bendōwa (Talk on Wholehearted Practice)](../texts/bendowa.md) — 
  - [Record of Things Heard from the Treasury of the True Dharma Eye (Shōbōgenzō zuimonki)](../texts/shobogenzo-zuimonki.md) — 
  - [Record of the Hōkyō Era (Hōkyōki)](../texts/hokyoki.md) — 
### [Rinzai Zen](../lineages/rinzai.md)
Bankei (within Rinzai) rejects both manufactured doubt and effortful method: simply abide in the Unborn.
**Texts:** 
  - [Sermons of Bankei (Bankei zenji seppō)](../texts/bankei-zenji-seppo.md) — 

**Historical debate — Sectarian polemics of the Tokugawa period** (17th–18th c.): Hakuin's writings attack 'silent-illumination' and 'do-nothing' Zen; Sōtō scholar-monks (Menzan, Manzan) defended Dōgen's just sitting. Specific texts of these polemics are not given here (low confidence). Each school maintains its practice; Dōgen used kōans extensively and Rinzai practises zazen, so the difference is one of emphasis.

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path, P4-stage
**Explanation:** Different methods for different temperaments and stages within one zazen-centred practice.
**The traditions' own objections:** Sōtō rejects the idea that kenshō is a goal separate from sitting; Rinzai rejects sitting without kōan as insufficient for most practitioners.
