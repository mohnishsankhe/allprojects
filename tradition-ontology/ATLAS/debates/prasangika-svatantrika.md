# Prāsaṅgika against Svātantrika

`dsp:prasangika-svatantrika` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G8

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md)
The difference is in the view, not only in method: Svātantrikas accept that phenomena exist by their own character conventionally and therefore use autonomous inferences; Prāsaṅgikas deny this even conventionally and so use consequences and 'other-acknowledged' inferences. Hence Prāsaṅgika's object of negation is subtler.
- The commonly appearing subject required for autonomous inference implies inherent existence.
**Texts:** 
  - [Great Treatise on the Stages of the Path to Enlightenment (lam rim chen mo)](../texts/lamrim-chenmo.md) — ref: insight
  - [Essence of Eloquence (drang nges legs bshad snying po)](../texts/legs-bshad-snying-po.md) — ref: Madhyamaka
### [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)
The two differ only in how the view is generated in another's mind — by autonomous inference or by consequence — not in the view realised, which is freedom from elaboration for both (Gorampa).
- The ultimate realised by both is the same freedom from extremes.
