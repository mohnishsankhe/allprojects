# Are the transgressive statements and practices of the tantras to be taken literally or as intentional language?

`dsp:literal-or-symbolic-tantric-conduct` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Ārya tradition of the Guhyasamāja](../lineages/arya-guhyasamaja.md)
Read by the six alternatives: many statements are intentional and not literal, pointing to completion-stage yoga (e.g. 'the bhagas of the vajra-women' = gnosis that destroys afflictions).
- Candrakīrti's six alternatives (provisional/definitive, intentional/not, literal/not).
- Indrabhūti's gloss of the Guhyasamāja opening.
**Texts:** 
  - [Pradīpoddyotana](../texts/pradipoddyotana.md) — ref: intro
  - [Jñānasiddhi](../texts/jnanasiddhi.md) — ref: p. 58
### [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md)
The observance is actually lived by the realized yogin — cremation ground, outcaste companions, equal vision of pure and impure — but only after realization and as a hidden observance.
- Pañcakrama 5.29–35: the yogin in union acts without hesitation, seeing mother and courtesan, food and excrement as equal.
- Queen Dohā v40–52 describes the conduct; the lives narrate transgressive acts (Virūpa, Ḍombi).
- Nāgārjuna's and Mekopa's songs: not before realization.
**Texts:** 
  - [Pañcakrama](../texts/pancakrama.md) — ref: 5.29-35
  - [Dohākoṣa-upadeśagīti of Saraha (the 'Queen Dohā')](../texts/dohakosa-queen-saraha.md) — ref: v40-52
  - [Caturaśītisiddhabodhihṛdaya (the realization songs of the eighty-four)](../texts/caturasiti-siddha-bodhihrdaya.md) — ref: 16, 43
### [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md)
For monastics the literal higher consecrations are forbidden; the master's consecration, study and ritual are allowed (Atiśa).
- The Ādibuddha tantra forbids the secret and wisdom consecrations to celibates; taking them destroys their vows.
**Texts:** 
  - [Lamp for the Path to Awakening (Bodhipathapradīpa; byang chub lam gyi sgron ma)](../texts/bodhipathapradipa.md) — ref: 65-68

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P5-neyartha, P4-stage
**Explanation:** By the tantras' own hermeneutic (P5) many statements are intentional; where conduct is meant literally it is restricted by stage and vow (P4): only the realized, and not monastics.
The six alternatives are the tradition's own version of the provisional/definitive principle; the siddha songs and the Pañcakrama tie literal observance to the stage of union; Atiśa ties it to the practitioner's vows.
**The traditions' own objections:** The siddha narratives insist that the conduct was real, not merely symbolic; the monastic tradition refuses it to monks whatever their stage.
