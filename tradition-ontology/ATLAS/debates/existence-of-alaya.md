# Is there a store-consciousness (ālaya-vijñāna) distinct from the six consciousnesses?

`dsp:existence-of-alaya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** B4

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
Yes: without a continuous, neutral consciousness holding the seeds, one could not explain the appropriation of a new body, the survival of seeds through the mindless attainments, the simultaneous occurrence of several cognitions, the karmic process and death.
- Eight proofs in the Viniścayasaṃgrahaṇī (recalled)
- Vasubandhu, Karmasiddhi: maturation-consciousness required for the attainment of cessation
- Scriptural proof: the Saṃdhinirmocana's ādāna-vijñāna verse
**Texts:** 
  - [Viniścayasaṃgrahaṇī](../texts/viniscayasamgrahani.md) — ref: opening
  - [Karmasiddhiprakaraṇa](../texts/karmasiddhiprakarana.md) — 
  - [Mahāyānasaṃgraha](../texts/mahayanasamgraha.md) — ref: ch. 1
  - [Saṃdhinirmocana-sūtra](../texts/samdhinirmocana-sutra.md) — ref: ch. 5
### [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
No: there are six consciousnesses; the continuity of karma is explained by possession (prāpti) and by the dharmas existing in the three times; mind re-arises after cessation from the prior mind.
- Abhidharmakośa (recalled)
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: ch. 2 (recalled)
### [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md)
No separate store is needed: seeds are carried by the mental series (with the body) itself, and mind re-arises after cessation from seeds in the body-mind series.
- Vasubandhu's own earlier Sautrāntika position (Abhidharmakośabhāṣya, recalled)
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośabhāṣya](../texts/abhidharmakosabhasya.md) — ref: ch. 2
### [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)
The ālaya was taught with an intention, for those who need it; it is not ultimately established and not even conventionally accepted as Yogācāra teaches it.
- Candrakīrti, Madhyamakāvatāra ch. 6 (recalled)
**Texts:** 
  - [Madhyamakāvatāra](../texts/madhyamakavatara.md) — ref: ch. 6

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Principles:** P5-neyartha, P2-standpoint
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the schools differ on whether a store-consciousness exists.
**The traditions' own objections:** Yogācāra insists the ālaya is a real eighth consciousness; Sarvāstivāda insists on real dharmas in three times; Prāsaṅgika denies both.
**Candidate readings:** P5-neyartha: Prāsaṅgika reads the ālaya teaching as provisional; Yogācāra reads it as definitive — the disagreement is about which teaching is provisional.; P2-standpoint: the ālaya answers the question of continuity from the standpoint of the series (santāna); the Sautrāntika seed-in-series may describe the same continuity without reifying a separate consciousness.
