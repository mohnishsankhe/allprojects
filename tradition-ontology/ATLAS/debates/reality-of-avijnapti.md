# Is unmanifest matter (avijñapti) a real entity?

`dsp:reality-of-avijnapti` · `skeleton` · confidence high


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vaibhāṣika](../lineages/vaibhasika.md)
Yes: unmanifest matter carries vows and non-vows through time, even when mind is absent.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 1.11
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 4.13
### [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md)
No: shape and avijñapti are not entities; the course of action is the transformation of the series produced by volition.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośabhāṣya](../texts/abhidharmakosabhasya.md) — ref: 4.3-4

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the Vaibhāṣika holds a real entity where the Sautrāntika sees a designation.
**The traditions' own objections:** Each side holds the other's account inadequate: the Vaibhāṣika that designations cannot do causal work, the Sautrāntika that the posited entities are unnecessary and unattested in the sūtras.
