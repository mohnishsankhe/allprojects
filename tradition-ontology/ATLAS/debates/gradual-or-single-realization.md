# Are the four truths realized gradually (in successive moments) or in a single moment?

`dsp:gradual-or-single-realization` · `skeleton` · confidence low


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
Gradually: sixteen moments of acceptance and knowledge, dharma- and subsequent knowledge for each truth; fifteen are the path of seeing.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 6.25–28
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
In one moment: path-knowledge penetrates the four truths simultaneously (Theravāda commentarial position; low confidence on the canonical Kathāvatthu reference).
**Texts:** 
  - [Visuddhimagga](../texts/visuddhimagga.md) — ref: XXII (low confidence)
### [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md)

**Texts:** 
  - [Samayabhedoparacanacakra](../texts/samayabhedoparacanacakra.md) — ref: Mahāsāṃghika tenets (low confidence)
