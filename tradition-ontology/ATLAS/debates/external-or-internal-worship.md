# Is outer worship (of the Śrīcakra or image) necessary, or is inner worship alone the true worship?

`dsp:external-or-internal-worship` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Coverage:** A8

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md)
Outer cakra-worship is done daily even by Śiva and by all knowers, since it produces the conviction of non-difference; the inner and the supreme follow.
**Texts:** 
  - [Yoginīhṛdaya](../texts/yoginihrdaya.md) — ref: 3.2-3
  - [Yoginīhṛdayadīpikā](../texts/yoginihrdaya-dipika.md) — ref: 3.3
### [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md)
Only inner worship in the heart-space is admitted; outer worship is Kaula and rejected.
**Texts:** 
  - [Lakṣmīdharā (commentary on the Saundaryalaharī)](../texts/laksmidhara.md) — ref: on SL 8, 41
### [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
True worship is the contemplation of the non-difference of knower, knowing and known; the offerings are inner acts (Bhāvanā Upaniṣad); supreme worship is not the offering of outer flowers but abiding in the non-dual (Saṅketapaddhati quoted in the Dīpikā).
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhāvanā Upaniṣad](../texts/bhavana-upanisad.md) — ref: sricakra-puja, upacaras
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
The Devī Gītā teaches both: outer worship, Vedic and tantric, and inner worship, which is higher.
**Texts:** 
  - [Devī Gītā](../texts/devi-gita.md) — ref: 7.39-40

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage
**Explanation:** Most of the texts themselves grade outer, meditative and non-dual worship as stages (Yoginīhṛdaya's three pūjās; the Devī Gītā), so outer and inner worship are read as suited to different stages.
**The traditions' own objections:** The Samaya school does not accept outer worship even as a lower stage for its own adherents; the Yoginīhṛdaya insists that even the knower keeps outer worship.
