# Is devotion a means to liberation, or is love of God a goal beyond liberation?

`dsp:prema-beyond-moksa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)
Gauḍīya: prema is the fifth and supreme goal; devotees refuse even the five liberations without service; liberation is made trivial by pure devotion.
**Texts:** 
  - [Prītisandarbha](../texts/priti-sandarbha.md) — 
  - [Bhaktirasāmṛtasindhu](../texts/bhaktirasamrtasindhu.md) — ref: 1.1.17
### [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md)
Puṣṭimārga: the grace-soul seeks only service and absorption; liberation (sāyujya) is a lesser fruit of the maryādā path.
**Texts:** 
  - [Sevāphala](../texts/sevaphala.md) — 
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Advaita: devotion purifies the mind and prepares for knowledge, which alone liberates; liberation is the highest end.
**Texts:** 
  - [Vivekacūḍāmaṇi](../texts/vivekacudamani.md) — 

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path, P4-stage
**Explanation:** Advaita places devotion at a stage before knowledge; the bhakti schools place knowledge of the impersonal below love — each ranks the other's goal by its own path.
The principles explain the mirror-image rankings but do not decide between them.
**The traditions' own objections:** Both sides reject being ranked as a stage of the other.
