# Is the Sahajiyā path (and those of the Bāuls and Kartābhajās) a true form of Caitanya's Vaiṣṇavism?

`dsp:sahajiya-and-gaudiya-orthodoxy` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md)
Yes: Caitanya (Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in one body) and the Gosvāmīs taught the sahaja doctrine esoterically; the Caitanya-caritāmṛta has an inner Sahajiyā meaning; parakīyā love is to be realized by human partners who see each other as Kṛṣṇa and Rādhā, turning kāma into prema.
- the kāma/prema distinction requires transformation, not suppression
- rūpa and svarūpa: the divine couple are in every human pair
**Texts:** 
  - [Vivartavilāsa](../texts/vivartavilasa.md) — 
  - [Amṛtaratnāvalī](../texts/amrtaratnavali.md) — 
### [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)
No: parakīyā belongs only to Kṛṣṇa's līlā and may never be imitated by humans; kāma (desire for one's own pleasure) and prema (desire for Kṛṣṇa's) are opposites; the Gosvāmīs were strict renunciants. Later orthodoxy lists Āul, Bāul, Kartābhajā, Neḍā, Daraveś, Sāṃi, Sahajiyā, Sakhībhekī, Smārta, Jāt-gosāñi, Ativāḍī, Cūḍādhārī and Gaurāṅga-nāgarī as deviant sects (apasampradāya).
- imitating the līlā is an offence
- Rūpa Kavirāja's abandonment of vaidhī rules was condemned (18th c.)
**Texts:** 
  - [Caitanya Caritāmṛta](../texts/caitanya-caritamrta.md) — ref: 1.4.165
  - [Ujjvalanīlamaṇi](../texts/ujjvalanilamani.md) — ref: 1.21
### [Kartābhajā](../lineages/kartabhaja.md)
Caitanya himself returned as Āulcānd to reveal the secret sahaja religion to the poor; outward conformity hides the true teaching.
**Texts:** 
  - [Sahajatattva-prakāśa (Kartābhajā dharmer ādi vṛttānta)](../texts/sahajatattva-prakasa.md) —

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: each side rejects the other's reading of the same Caitanya literature.
**The traditions' own objections:** Gauḍīya orthodoxy regards the Sahajiyā practice as a fall into kāma; the Sahajiyā regards orthodox practice as external (bāhya) and preliminary.
**Candidate readings:** P1-level: parakīyā true of the divine līlā but not of human conduct — this is the orthodox position itself and is rejected by the Sahajiyā; P5-neyartha: the Sahajiyā reading of the Caitanya-caritāmṛta as the definitive (nītārtha) meaning behind a provisional surface — the Sahajiyā's own hermeneutic, rejected by the Gauḍīyas; P3-path / P4-stage: different qualifications (adhikāra) for different practitioners — rejected by the orthodox as licensing transgression
