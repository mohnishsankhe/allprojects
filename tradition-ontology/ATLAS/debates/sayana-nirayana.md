### [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)
Nirayana: the siddhāntas and Indian horoscopy and almanacs reckon the signs and asterisms from a fixed star-point, treating the solstices' shift as an oscillation; schools differ on the amount of the correction.
- the asterisms are fixed
- the Sūrya Siddhānta's libration

### [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)
Sāyana: the seasons, the solstices and the sun's courses follow the equinox, and some authorities and reformers hold that festivals tied to seasons (and the signs) should follow it.
- uttarāyaṇa is a seasonal fact
- calendar–season drift
