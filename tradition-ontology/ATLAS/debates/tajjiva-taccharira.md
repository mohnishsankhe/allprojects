# Is the soul the same as the body, or different from it?

`dsp:tajjiva-taccharira` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md)
(As reported) The same: the soul is bounded by the skin; no soul can be drawn out and shown like a sword from its sheath; when the body is burnt nothing arises again.
- burnt seeds do not sprout (Isibh. 20)
- the sword-and-sheath challenge (Sūtr 2.1)
**Texts:** 
  - [Isibhāsiyāiṃ (Ṛṣibhāṣitāni)](../texts/isibhasiyaim.md) — ref: 20
  - [Sūtrakṛtāṅga](../texts/sutrakrtanga.md) — ref: 1.1.1.11-12; 2.1
  - [Rājapraśnīya](../texts/rajaprasniya.md) — ref: Paesi
### [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
Different: the soul is other than the body (Kesi to Paesi).
- the fire-sticks simile
**Texts:** 
  - [Rājapraśnīya](../texts/rajaprasniya.md) — ref: Paesi
### [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md)
Neither view is taught: 'soul is body' and 'soul is other than body' both make the holy life impossible; the Tathāgata teaches by the middle, dependent origination.
- SN 12.35
**Texts:** 
  - [Avijjāpaccayā Sutta](../texts/avijjapaccaya-sutta.md) — ref: SN 12.35

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the materialist and the Jain affirm opposite answers; the Buddhist declines both.

**The traditions' own objections:** Each party insists on its own answer; the Buddhist texts count both others as extremes.
**Candidate readings:** P2-standpoint: the Buddhist 'middle' is itself offered as avoiding two standpoints — but it does not accept either side's claim.; P5-neyartha: none recorded.
