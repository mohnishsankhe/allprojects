# Did Mahāvīra marry?

`dsp:mahavira-marriage` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
Yes: he married Yaśodā and had a daughter, Priyadarśanā (Anojjā), who married Jamāli.
- Kalpa Sūtra and Ācārāṅga 2.15 name his wife and daughter
**Texts:** 
  - [Kalpa Sūtra (Paryuṣaṇākalpa)](../texts/kalpa-sutra-jain.md) — ref: jinacaritra.mahavira
  - [Ācārāṅga Sūtra](../texts/acaranga-sutra.md) — ref: 2.15
### [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
No: he renounced as an unmarried prince (kumāra).
- Digambara purāṇas
**Texts:** 
  - [Uttarapurāṇa of Guṇabhadra](../texts/uttarapurana-gunabhadra.md) —
