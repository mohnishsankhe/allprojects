# Is ignorance (avidyā) a positive entity (bhāva-rūpa) that is the material cause of the world-appearance, or is it superimposition/non-apprehension itself?

`dsp:bhavarupa-avidya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** A6

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Vivaraṇa school (Vivaraṇa-prasthāna)](../lineages/vivarana.md)
Positive: ignorance is a beginningless, indescribable, positive power, proved by perception ('I am ignorant'), inference and presumption; Padmapāda calls it an insentient power.
- Mere absence cannot be a material cause.
- The report 'I knew nothing' after sleep shows positive experience of ignorance.
**Texts:** 
  - [Pañcapādikā](../texts/pancapadika.md) — ref: 1
  - [Pañcapādikāvivaraṇa](../texts/pancapadika-vivarana.md) — ref: bhava
  - [Advaitasiddhi](../texts/advaitasiddhi.md) — ref: 1
  - [Vedāntasāra](../texts/vedantasara.md) — ref: ajñāna
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Maṇḍana: ignorance is non-apprehension and misapprehension residing in the jīvas. Swami Satchidanandendra (recent): for Śaṅkara avidyā is superimposition itself (BSBh intro), not a positive root-ignorance; the later doctrine departs from Śaṅkara.
- Śaṅkara defines avidyā as adhyāsa.
- A positive ignorance would itself need explaining.

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the question concerns what Śaṅkara taught and whether a positive ignorance is required; the prakriyā principle is invoked by traditionalists but rejected by the modern critic.
**The traditions' own objections:** Satchidanandendra holds the mūlāvidyā doctrine to be an error, not a method; Vivaraṇa scholars hold the positive ignorance necessary to explain the world's material cause.
**Candidate readings:** P1-level/P6-upaya: both are empirical-level explanations of the one error (traditional compendia).; Historical reading: Śaṅkara's usage is phenomenological (adhyāsa), the Vivaraṇa's is causal-explanatory; they answer different questions.
