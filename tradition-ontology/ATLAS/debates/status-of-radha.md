# What is Rādhā's status — scriptural basis, relation to Kṛṣṇa, and place in worship?

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)
Rādhā is Kṛṣṇa's hlādinī-śakti, one in self with him, the embodiment of mahābhāva and the object of the highest service; the Bhāgavata hints at her (10.30.28 'anayārādhitaḥ'); Caitanya is Rādhā–Kṛṣṇa combined.
**Texts:** 
  - [Caitanya Caritāmṛta](../texts/caitanya-caritamrta.md) — ref: 1.1.5
  - [Bhāgavata Purāṇa](../texts/bhagavata-purana.md) — ref: 10.30.28
### [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md)
Rādhā, daughter of Vṛṣabhānu, is worshipped with Kṛṣṇa as the eternal divine couple (Daśaślokī 5).
**Texts:** 
  - [Daśaślokī (Siddhāntaratna; Vedāntakāmadhenu)](../texts/dasasloki.md) — ref: 5
### [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md)
Vallabha's own works centre on Kṛṣṇa and the gopīs as a group; Rādhā as Svāminī is emphasised by Viṭṭhalanātha.
**Texts:** 
  - [Śṛṅgārarasamaṇḍana](../texts/srngararasamandana.md) —

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path
**Explanation:** All three Vaiṣṇava schools worship Rādhā with Kṛṣṇa; their differing emphases follow their chosen devotional moods (iṣṭa and rasa).
P3 reconciles the difference of emphasis in worship. It does not settle the doctrinal questions (her marital status; whether she is named in the Bhāgavata), which remain queued under dsp:svakiya-parakiya.
**The traditions' own objections:** Each school holds its account of Rādhā as literally true, not as a matter of temperament.
