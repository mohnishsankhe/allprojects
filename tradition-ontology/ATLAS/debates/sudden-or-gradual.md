# Is awakening sudden — an all-at-once seeing of the mind's nature — or gradual, reached by stages of cultivation and analysis; and after sudden awakening, is gradual cultivation still needed?

`dsp:sudden-or-gradual` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G6

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Heze school](../lineages/heze.md)
Shenhui (Southern school): Bodhidharma's robe and the direct transmission passed to Huineng; Shenxiu's line is collateral, and its teaching — 'freezing the mind to enter samādhi, fixing the mind to view purity, activating the mind to illumine outside, concentrating the mind to realise within' — is gradual; the true teaching is sudden awakening.
**Texts:** 
  - [Treatise Establishing the True and False in the Southern School of Bodhidharma (Putidamo nanzong ding shifei lun)](../texts/nanzong-ding-shifei-lun.md) — ref: Huatai assembly
  - [Platform Sūtra of the Sixth Patriarch (Liuzu tanjing)](../texts/platform-sutra.md) — ref: 4.11
### [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
The Platform Sūtra: the Dharma has no sudden or gradual; people's natures are sharp or dull — the deluded cultivate gradually, the awakened accord suddenly. Its gate takes no-thought as its tenet, no-form as its substance, non-abiding as its root; for one awakened to self-nature there is 'sudden awakening, sudden cultivation, without gradual steps', while Shenxiu's three trainings suit smaller faculties. Guishan: a beginner who suddenly awakens to principle in one thought still has beginningless habit energies to clear — that is cultivation.
**Texts:** 
  - [Platform Sūtra of the Sixth Patriarch (Liuzu tanjing)](../texts/platform-sutra.md) — ref: 5.6; 7.2-3; 11.7
  - [Recorded Sayings of Guishan (Guishan yulu)](../texts/guishan-yulu.md) — ref: 577c
### [Northern school of Chan](../lineages/northern-chan.md)
The Northern school in its own words: the buddhas have a great expedient for entering the Way — with one thought of the pure mind one suddenly transcends to the buddha-ground; viewing the mind, if it is pure, is called the ground of the pure mind; awakening is the mind's substance free of thought, equal to space. (The Southern texts report Shenxiu's verse — the body the bodhi tree, the mind a bright mirror to be wiped — and his 'fix the mind, view stillness'.)
**Texts:** 
  - [Expedient Means of Attaining the Unborn in the Mahāyāna (Dasheng wusheng fangbian men)](../texts/dasheng-wusheng-fangbian-men.md) — ref: T85n2834 p.1273c
  - [Platform Sūtra of the Sixth Patriarch (Liuzu tanjing)](../texts/platform-sutra.md) — ref: 4.6; 11.1 (as reported)
### [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
Moheyan (Chinese party at the Tibetan court): all beings are bound by deluded, discriminating thought; when thoughts arise, do not follow them — 'not thinking, not examining' — and the originally pure mind appears; the six perfections are included in this non-examining; entry into non-conceptuality is simultaneous, not by steps.
**Texts:** 
  - [Determination of the True Principle of Sudden Awakening in the Mahāyāna (Dunwu dasheng zhengli jue)](../texts/dunwu-dasheng-zhengli-jue.md) — ref: Moheyan's replies
  - [Samten Migdrön (bsam gtan mig sgron, the Lamp for the Eye in Contemplation)](../texts/samten-migdron.md) — ref: simultaneous approach
### [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md)
Kamalaśīla: there is no way other than correct analysis (bhūta-pratyavekṣā) to reach non-recollection and non-attention; mere absence of thought cannot cause non-conceptual wisdom, or one who has fainted would have it. Seeing by analysis that no dharma has arisen in the three times, the yogin enters non-conceptual wisdom; the path is gradual — calm abiding and insight, the perfections and the grounds.
**Texts:** 
  - [Bhāvanākrama](../texts/bhavanakrama.md) — ref: third Bhāvanākrama
  - [Samten Migdrön (bsam gtan mig sgron, the Lamp for the Eye in Contemplation)](../texts/samten-migdron.md) — ref: gradual approach
### [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
The Kathāvatthu rejects the thesis that the truths are penetrated gradually (anupubbābhisamaya): each path penetrates the four truths at once.
**Texts:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — ref: 2.9
### [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
The truths are realized in sixteen successive moments of acceptance and knowledge on the path of seeing — a graded, momentary sequence.
**Texts:** 
  - [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) — ref: 6.25-28
### [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)
The Laṅkāvatāra: the stream of what is seen as one's own mind is purified gradually — as mangoes ripen — and also all at once — as a mirror shows all forms at once. The Avataṃsaka: at the first arousal of the mind one attains unsurpassed awakening. The Śūraṅgama: in principle awakening is sudden, in phenomena removal is gradual.
**Texts:** 
  - [Laṅkāvatāra-sūtra](../texts/lankavatara-sutra.md) — ref: ch. 2
  - [Buddhāvataṃsaka-sūtra](../texts/avatamsaka-sutra.md) — ref: ch. 16
  - [Śūraṅgama-sūtra (Chinese Shoulengyan jing, T945)](../texts/surangama-sutra.md) — ref: ch. 10
### [Heze school](../lineages/heze.md)
Zongmi: sudden and gradual, awakening and cultivation, seem opposed but accord and support one another; if one cultivates on the basis of awakening it is understanding-awakening, if one awakens through cultivation it is realization-awakening; 'first sudden awakening, then gradual cultivation' is like the sun rising at once while frost melts gradually. Over past lives there is only the gradual.
**Texts:** 
  - [General Preface to the Collection of Expressions of the Chan Source (Chanyuan zhuquanji duxu)](../texts/chanyuan-zhuquanji-duxu.md) — ref: T48n2015 402a; 408a
### [Seon (Korean Chan)](../lineages/seon.md)
Jinul: though there are many gates, in essence they are two — sudden awakening and gradual cultivation; even the 'sudden awakening, sudden cultivation' of the highest faculties rests on gradual cultivation in past lives; the principle is awakened suddenly, habit energies are removed step by step.
**Texts:** 
  - [Secrets on Cultivating the Mind (Susim kyol)](../texts/susim-kyol.md) — ref: T48n2020
### [Seon (Korean Chan)](../lineages/seon.md)
(Recent) Seongcheol: the patriarchs' awakening is realization-awakening in which cultivation is also complete — sudden awakening, sudden cultivation; Jinul's formula describes only understanding-awakening.
**Texts:** 
  - [The Correct Path of the Seon School (Seonmun jeongno)](../texts/seonmun-jeongno.md) — ref: whole
### [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)
Sakya Paṇḍita: the Mahāmudrā of his day and the 'Chinese Great Perfection' are essentially the same as the Hashang's teaching rejected in Tibet; true Mahāmudrā is the wisdom born of empowerment and the two stages.
**Texts:** 
  - [Discrimination of the Three Vows (sdom pa gsum gyi rab tu dbye ba)](../texts/domsum-rabye.md) — ref: ch. 3
### [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
The Ājñāsamyakpramāṇa: by the difference of persons' minds there are the gradual and the simultaneous; the medicine of one is the poison of the other — teach the simultaneous to one with the residue of former training, the gradual to beginners.
**Texts:** 
  - [Ājñāsamyakpramāṇa-nāma-ḍākinyupadeśa (the ḍākinī instruction 'True Measure of the Word')](../texts/ajnasamyakpramana.md) — ref: Derge Tengyur 271a
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
(An analogue in the Upaniṣadic yoga texts) The Varāha Upaniṣad distinguishes Śuka's path of the bird — immediate liberation through inquiry into the great sayings — from Vāmadeva's path of the ant — gradual liberation over births through yoga — and says both reach Brahman.
**Texts:** 
  - [Varāha Upaniṣad](../texts/varaha-upanisad.md) — ref: 4.34-42

**Historical debate — The Samye debate (bSam yas)** (tradition: c. 792–794 CE under Khri srong lde btsan): Tibetan accounts (the Testament of Ba / sBa bzhed; later Bu ston and others) report a debate before the king in which the Indian party of Kamalaśīla, teaching the gradual path of the perfections, prevailed over the party of the Chinese master Moheyan, teaching sudden non-conceptuality, and the king ruled that Tibetans should follow Nāgārjuna's view and the gradual path. The Chinese Dunhuang document Dunwu dasheng zhengli jue (compiled by Wangxi) records Moheyan's answers to the Indian objections and presents his teaching as upheld. Kamalaśīla's third Bhāvanākrama argues against non-thinking as the way to non-conceptuality. Tibetan tradition: Kamalaśīla prevailed and Moheyan left Tibet. Dunhuang Chinese account: Moheyan's teaching was vindicated. Nupchen's Samten Migdrön ranks the simultaneous above the gradual but below Mahāyoga and Atiyoga.

**Historical debate — Shenhui's Huatai assembly** (732 CE (Kaiyuan 20), Dayun monastery at Huatai (tradition's account)): Putidamo nanzong ding shifei lun (record of Shenhui's challenge to the Northern school's claim to be the true line and to its 'gradual' teaching; the opponent named there is the Dharma master Chongyuan) Heze tradition: Huineng established as the Sixth Patriarch and the Southern teaching as sudden and orthodox; the Northern school's own texts (e.g. Dasheng wusheng fangbian men) present their teaching otherwise.

**Historical debate — Kathāvatthu 2.9 (Anupubbābhisamayakathā)** (tradition: the Third Council under Aśoka): Kathāvatthu 2.9 and commentary Theravāda: gradual penetration of the truths rejected; each path penetrates all four truths at once.

**Historical debate — Seongcheol's critique of Jinul (recent)** (1981 CE (publication of the Seonmun jeongno)): Seonmun jeongno Contested within Korean Seon to the present.

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P4-stage, P1-level
**Explanation:** Reconciled within Chan, Seon and the tantric Tengyur by their own principle that sudden and gradual lie in the practitioner's faculties, not in the Dharma (P4-stage), and by the reading 'principle is awakened suddenly, phenomena are removed gradually' (P1-level); the Samye question — whether non-conceptuality is reached by non-examining or only through analysis — remains not yet reconciled.
Platform Sūtra 7.3 ('the Dharma has no sudden or gradual; people are sharp or dull'), Zongmi's ninth harmonisation and his sun-and-frost simile, Jinul's sudden awakening and gradual cultivation, the Ājñāsamyakpramāṇa's medicine-and-poison verse, and the Varāha Upaniṣad's bird and ant paths all assign the two to different kinds of practitioner or to different aspects (li and shi) of one path. The Śūraṅgama's 理則頓悟…事非頓除 is the P1 form.
**The traditions' own objections:** Kamalaśīla and the Tibetan tradition after Samye reject non-examining as a path (Bhāvanākrama 3; Sakya Paṇḍita, Domsum Rabye 3); Shenhui rejects the Northern teaching as gradual and collateral; Seongcheol (recent) rejects gradual cultivation after awakening as mere understanding-awakening; the Kathāvatthu rejects gradual penetration of the truths that the Sarvāstivāda teaches.
