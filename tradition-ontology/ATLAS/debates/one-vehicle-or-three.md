# Is the one vehicle the final truth and the three vehicles a skillful means — or are the three vehicles real and the 'one vehicle' spoken with an intention?

`dsp:one-vehicle-or-three` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** G

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)
One vehicle only: the three vehicles are the Buddha's skillful means to lead beings into the one buddha-vehicle (Lotus; Śrīmālā); the arhat's nirvāṇa is a conjured city.
- There is no second or third vehicle anywhere in the ten directions (Lotus 2).
- The father gives all his children the one great cart (Lotus 3).
- The two vehicles enter the great vehicle; the arhat still has the ground of ignorance (Śrīmālā 5).

  - [Saddharmapuṇḍarīka-sūtra](../texts/saddharmapundarika.md) — ref: ch. 2, 3, 7

  - [Wuliangyi jing (Sūtra of Innumerable Meanings)](../texts/wuliangyi-jing.md) — ref: ch. 2
### [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
The three vehicles correspond to real differences of lineage; the 'one vehicle' was taught with an intention (the sameness of the dharma-realm, of no-self and of liberation), to attract the indeterminate.
- The Laṅkāvatāra's five lineages.
- The Saṃdhinirmocana ch. 7: the one vehicle is taught with an intention; the fixed-lineage śrāvaka cannot reach awakening (chapter-level, from memory).
**Texts:** 
  - [Saṃdhinirmocana-sūtra](../texts/samdhinirmocana-sutra.md) — ref: ch. 7
  - [Laṅkāvatāra-sūtra](../texts/lankavatara-sutra.md) — ref: ch. 2

**Historical debate — Three carts or four carts** (6th-8th c. China): Readers of the burning-house parable divided on whether the great ox-cart given at the end is the bodhisattva's ox-cart of the three (the 'three-cart' reading of Faxiang and Sanlun exegetes) or a fourth, great white-ox cart (the 'four-cart' reading of Tiantai and Huayan). Each school maintained its reading.
