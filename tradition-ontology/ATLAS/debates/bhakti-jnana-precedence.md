# Is devotion or knowledge the higher and more direct means, and is oneness with the Lord to be desired?

`dsp:bhakti-jnana-precedence` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate


## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
Devotion alone wins the Lord; motiveless devotion is greater than liberation; pure devotees refuse even oneness without service.
- neither yoga nor Sāṃkhya nor austerity win me as does devotion (11.14.20)
- devotion dissolves the subtle body (3.25.33)
- devotees do not accept ekatva (3.29.13)
**Texts:** 
  - [Uddhava Gītā](../texts/uddhava-gita.md) — ref: 11.14.20-22
  - [Kapila Gītā](../texts/kapila-gita.md) — ref: 3.25.32-38; 3.29.11-14
### [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
Knowledge of the non-dual Self liberates; even the practice of samādhi is bondage from the absolute standpoint; the jñānī is dearest to the Lord.
- resting in awareness one is free now (Aṣṭāvakra 1.4)
- the knower is dearest (Uddhava Gītā 11.19.3)
- knowledge alone removes ignorance (Rāma Gītā 7.5.9)
**Texts:** 
  - [Aṣṭāvakra Gītā](../texts/astavakra-gita.md) — ref: 1.3-15
  - [Uddhava Gītā](../texts/uddhava-gita.md) — ref: 11.19.1-3
  - [Rāma Gītā](../texts/rama-gita.md) — ref: 7.5.8-10
### [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md)
Devotion is the lamp without which knowledge does not shine; from devotion knowledge, realization, dispassion and liberation follow quickly.
- as eyes see nothing at night without a lamp (AR 3.4.46-47)
**Texts:** 
  - [Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa](../texts/adhyatma-ramayana.md) — ref: 3.4.45-53; 1.1.51

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** partially-reconciled
**Principles:** P3-path, P4-stage
**Explanation:** The Uddhava Gītā itself assigns knowledge, action and devotion to different dispositions (11.20.6-8), and the Devī Gītā grades devotion upward into non-dual knowledge.
Under P3 the paths suit different temperaments; under P4 devotion is a means that matures into knowledge (Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa, Devī Gītā). The Kapila Gītā's refusal of oneness is not reconciled by this reading.
**The traditions' own objections:** Gauḍīya and other Vaiṣṇava readers reject the reading of bhakti as a mere means to non-dual knowledge; Advaita readers reject the claim that devotion is higher than liberation.
