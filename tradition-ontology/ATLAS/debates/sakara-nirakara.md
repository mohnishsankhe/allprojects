# Are the images (ākāra) appearing in cognition real or false?

`dsp:sakara-nirakara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Coverage:** B4

## Sides (recorded before any reconciliation)
### [Satyākāravāda (True-Aspect Yogācāra)](../lineages/satyakaravada.md)
Real: whatever appears, appears as cognition's own form; the variegated image is one non-dual cognition; even buddha-cognition has images.
- Jñānaśrīmitra's Sākārasiddhiśāstra; Ratnakīrti's Citrādvaitaprakāśavāda
**Texts:** 
  - [Sākārasiddhiśāstra](../texts/sakarasiddhisastra.md) — 
  - [Citrādvaitaprakāśavāda](../texts/citradvaitasiddhi.md) — 
### [Alīkākāravāda (False-Aspect Yogācāra)](../lineages/alikakaravada.md)
False: the images are superimposed by beginningless imprints; only image-free luminous awareness is real.
- Ratnākaraśānti's Prajñāpāramitopadeśa and Madhyamakālaṃkāropadeśa
**Texts:** 
  - [Prajñāpāramitopadeśa (Ratnākaraśānti)](../texts/prajnaparamitopadesa-ratnakarasanti.md) — 
  - [Madhyamakālaṃkāropadeśa](../texts/madhyamakalamkaropadesa.md) — 

## Reconciliation (interpretation layer)
**Status:** not yet reconciled (queued)
**Principles:** P1-level, P4-stage
**Explanation:** Not yet reconciled: the Vikramaśīla masters disagreed about the reality of cognition's images.
**The traditions' own objections:** Both sides wrote refutations of each other; Tibetan doxography keeps them as distinct sub-schools.
**Candidate readings:** P1-level: the images may be real as cognition at the level of cognition-only, false at the level of the imageless.; P4-stage: Laṅkāvatāra 10.256–258 places passing beyond the 'imageless' as a further stage, which both sides read their way.
