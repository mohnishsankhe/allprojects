# Pure lands and buddha-fields

`cpt:pure-lands` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** cosmology-time

## Names

## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Buddha-fields purified by a buddha's vows, in the ten directions: Amitābha's Sukhāvatī in the west, Akṣobhya's Abhirati and Bhaiṣajyaguru's lapis land in the east; born there beings do not retrogress.
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Sukhāvatī lies west beyond ten trillion lands; it surpasses the three realms and is not included in them; it is the reward of Dharmākara's vows; Amitābha became buddha there ten kalpas ago; its 29 adornments are the object of contemplation.
