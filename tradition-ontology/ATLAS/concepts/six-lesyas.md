## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Six colourings of the soul by passion-tinged activity — black, blue, grey (inauspicious), yellow-red, lotus-pink, white (auspicious) — found in hell-beings, gods and humans in differing degrees (TS 2.6, 3.3, 4.2, 4.22); illustrated by six travellers who would uproot, cut, lop, strip, pick or gather from a fruit tree (Gommaṭasāra Jīvakāṇḍa; the jambū-tree parable).
