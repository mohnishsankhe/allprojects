# Pre-existence of the effect (satkāryavāda)

`cpt:satkaryavada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** disputes

## Names
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): satkāryavāda

## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The effect exists in its material cause before production: the non-existent cannot be produced; a definite material is taken up; not everything comes from everything; the capable produces what it can; the effect has the nature of its cause (SK 9; SS 1.114–118). Production is manifestation, destruction is dissolution into the cause (SS 1.121). It grounds the inference of prakṛti from its effects (SK 14–16).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The effect is the cause in another state: the world is Brahman-with-body in gross state, pre-existing as Brahman-with-body in subtle state; transformation is real (pariṇāma), not apparent (vivarta).
