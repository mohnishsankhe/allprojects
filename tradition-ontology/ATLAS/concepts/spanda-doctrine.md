## Definitions
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): The ultimate as dynamic, pulsing awareness - opening and closing (unmeṣa-nimeṣa) - on which all particular movements rest, never lost in any state, and graspable in intense moments and in the gap between thoughts.
