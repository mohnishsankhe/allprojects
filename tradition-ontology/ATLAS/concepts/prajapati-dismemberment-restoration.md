## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): After emitting the creatures Prajāpati, who is the year, fell apart (vyasraṃsata) — his joints being the junctions of day and night, the moon's phases and the seasons; the gods healed him by the Agnihotra, the new- and full-moon and seasonal rites (ŚB 1.6.3.35–36), and he is rebuilt as the fire-altar whose bricks are his days and nights (ŚB 10.1.1.2–3); he put himself together with the metres, whence 'saṃhitā' (AA 3.2.6).
