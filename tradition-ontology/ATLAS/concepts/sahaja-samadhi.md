# Innate absorption (sahaja-samādhi): the mind seeing the mind

`cpt:sahaja-samadhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** consciousness-states

## Names

## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Liberation is where, by the sequence of innate absorption, the mind is seen by the mind (AmŚ 54); cf. seeing the mind by the mind in the midst of Śakti (Candrāvalokana).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Absorption that grows by the guru's grace without closing eyes or stopping ears: going about is circumambulation, work is service, lying down is prostration; the Lord's form is seen everywhere.
