## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Contemplation of body, feelings, mind and dhammas, the one-way path for purification and realisation of nibbāna (MN 10:2-3), taught in twenty-one sections.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): In MN 118 they are fulfilled by mindfulness of breathing (each tetrad of the sixteen steps is one foundation: body, feelings, mind, dhammas) and, developed, they fulfil the seven awakening factors (MN 118:23-40); a monk community 'devoted to their development' is described at mn118:13.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Contemplating body, feelings, mind and dhammas, ardent, clearly comprehending and mindful, having removed covetousness and grief for the world (MN 10); fulfilled by mindfulness of breathing and fulfilling the awakening factors (MN 118); one's own pasture (SN 47.6).
