## Definitions
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Forty sacraments (8 bodily rites, 4 Vedic vows, final bath, marriage, 5 great sacrifices, 7 cooked-food, 7 oblation and 7 Soma sacrifices) and eight virtues of the self (compassion, patience, freedom from envy, purity, calm, auspiciousness, generosity, freedom from greed); the virtues, not the sacraments, decide union with Brahman (GDh 8.14–25).
