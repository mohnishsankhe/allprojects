## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Beyond the senses; one, atomic; its presence or absence explains knowledge arising or not; it directs the senses, restrains itself, reasons and considers, and then buddhi decides (Ca Sū 8.4-7; Śā 1.18-23); unconscious though active, the self being the conscious agent (Śā 1.75-76); three kinds by guṇa (Śā 3.13; 4.36); located between head and palate, with citta in the heart (Bhela Ci 8).
