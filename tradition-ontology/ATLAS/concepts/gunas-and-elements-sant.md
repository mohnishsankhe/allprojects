## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Māyā binds with the noose of the three qualities (guṇa), from which the three gods arise; the body is woven of the five elements, which return to their source at death.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): The three guṇas and five tattvas originate in the region of Brahm (Trikuṭī) and belong to Kāl's creation.
