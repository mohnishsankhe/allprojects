## Definitions
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): The nature of one's own mind — empty, luminous, unborn, beyond speech and thought — which 'seals' all phenomena; at once the ground (mind's nature), the path (recognizing and sustaining it through the guru's pointing-out, śamatha–vipaśyanā and the four yogas) and the fruit (the dharmakāya). 'Though Mahāmudrā cannot be shown…' (Gaṅgā Mahāmudrā v1).
- [Shangpa Kagyu](../lineages/shangpa-kagyu.md): The Mahāmudrā of the amulet box: ground, path and fruit enclosed together; the three self-liberations.
