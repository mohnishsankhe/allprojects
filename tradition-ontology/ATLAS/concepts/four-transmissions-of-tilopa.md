## Definitions
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): Tilopa received the six dharmas from four Indian sources: inner heat from Caryāpa, illusory body and clear light from Nāgārjuna, dream from Lavapa, and intermediate state and transference from Sukhasiddhi (Tilopa's Ṣaḍdharmopadeśa); other Kagyu lists assign them differently (e.g. adding Kukkuripa or the Hevajra/Guhyasamāja/Mahāmāyā/Cakrasaṃvara tantras).
