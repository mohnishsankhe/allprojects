## Definitions
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): All cognitions are valid of themselves; a capacity not present in a thing cannot be given by another; validity is set aside only by a cognition of the object's being otherwise or knowledge of a defect in the cause — neither of which can arise for the authorless Veda.
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): Validity is known by inferring the cognition from the knownness it produces.
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): Validity is known with the self-luminous cognition itself.
- [Murāri Miśra's school (the 'third path' of Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/murari-mimamsa.md): Validity is known by the after-cognition (anuvyavasāya) of the cognition (as reported by Gaṅgeśa).
