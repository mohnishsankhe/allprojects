## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md): Consecrated images of the Jinas are worshipped by laity with material offerings and by all with inner worship; the image is the Jina 'placed' (sthāpanā) and a support for recalling his qualities, not a grace-giver; the canon shows gods and Draupadī worshipping Jina images.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Image worship is practised (the Bīsapantha with fresh offerings, the Terāpantha with dry, non-living offerings only).
- [Sthānakavāsī](../lineages/sthanakavasi.md): Rejected: the thirty-two Āgamas contain no injunction to it, and worship with water, flowers and lamps harms living beings; devotion is to the Jina's qualities and to living mendicants.
- [Śvetāmbara Terāpanth](../lineages/terapanthi.md): Rejected likewise.
- [Tāraṇapantha (Samaiyā)](../lineages/taranapantha.md): Rejected; the shrines keep scriptures instead of images.
