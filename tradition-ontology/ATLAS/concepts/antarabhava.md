## Definitions
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Between death and birth a being with the form of its coming existence travels to its place of birth, drawn by desire; it feeds on smells.
- [Saṃmitīya](../lineages/sammitiya.md): Accepted (Kv 8.2 commentary).
- [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md): Denied (Vasumitra).
- [Mahīśāsaka](../lineages/mahisasaka.md): Denied (Vasumitra).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Denied (Kathāvatthu 8.2).
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): U44 contribution: the intermediate state is 'blocked' by deity yoga or resting in reality when turning toward rebirth (Ṣaḍdharmopadeśa, from Sukhasiddhi).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): bar do: counted as three in the Nāropa texts (birth-death, dream, becoming); the being of the bardo of becoming, seeing its future parents, should regard them as the guru and block rebirth or choose it (Ājñāsamyakpramāṇa).
