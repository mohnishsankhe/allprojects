## Definitions
- [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md): Penances, śrāddhas including one's own, the virajā homa, placing the fires in oneself, the declaration renouncing the three desires, the gift of fearlessness, abandoning topknot and thread, and receiving praṇava and great saying from the guru — a ritual death to the world (Jābāla 4; Āruṇi 2–3; Nāradaparivrājaka 4.38; Kaṭharudra; Paramahaṃsaparivrājaka).
