## Definitions
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Every qualified cognition has a qualificand (viśeṣya), a qualifier (prakāra) and a relation (saṃsarga), with corresponding properties (viśeṣyatā, prakāratā, saṃsargatā) that determine one another (nirūpaka / nirūpita); properties and absences are specified by their delimitors, counterpositives and relations; valid cognition is apprehending in X the property X has as qualifier.
