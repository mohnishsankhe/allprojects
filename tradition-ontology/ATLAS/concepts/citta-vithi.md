## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Consciousness occurs in fixed series: the five-door process of up to seventeen mind-moments (life-continuum, adverting, sense consciousness, receiving, investigating, determining, seven impulsions, two registrations), mind-door processes, absorption and path processes; each moment conditions the next by proximity, contiguity, absence and disappearance.
