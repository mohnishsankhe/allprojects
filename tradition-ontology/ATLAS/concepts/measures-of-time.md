## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Units from the prāṇa (the time of a breath) and vināḍī to the nāḍī, the sidereal and civil day, lunar and solar months, the year which is a day of the gods (SS 1.11–14), up to the yuga and kalpa; nine kinds of measure are distinguished — of Brahmā, the gods, the fathers, Prajāpati, Jupiter, the sun, the civil day, the moon and the stars (SS 14).
