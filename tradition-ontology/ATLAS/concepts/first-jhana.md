## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Vism IV p. 139-147: the first jhāna, on the earth kasiṇa, is 'secluded from sensual desires, secluded from unwholesome states, with applied and sustained thought, rapture and happiness born of seclusion'; it has five factors abandoned (the hindrances) and five possessed (applied thought, sustained thought, rapture, happiness, one-pointedness), is good in three ways (beginning, middle, end) and has ten marks. Absorption lasts a single moment of consciousness.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Secluded from sensual pleasures and unwholesome states, with applied and sustained thought, rapture and bliss born of seclusion, pervading the body like bath powder kneaded with water (DN 2); remembered by the bodhisatta from childhood (MN 36); lust is abandoned through it (MN 44).
