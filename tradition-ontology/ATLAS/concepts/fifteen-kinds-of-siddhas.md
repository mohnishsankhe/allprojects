## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Liberated as Tīrthaṅkara or not; during or outside a Tīrthaṅkara's ford; self-enlightened, enlightened by an outer cause (pratyekabuddha) or taught; in female, male or neuter body; in Jain garb, other garb or as householder; singly or together.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Digambaras read the categories of sex and garb retrospectively or as psychological sex, not as liberation from a female or clothed body.
