## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The two goods of the Ratnāvalī: first the dharma of higher status (good rebirth through ethics and giving), then definite goodness (liberation through the insight that 'I' and 'mine' are false); faith leads to the first, wisdom to the second (RĀ 1.3–5).
