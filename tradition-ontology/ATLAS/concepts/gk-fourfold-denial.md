## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The Kārikā denies that anything is born from itself or from another, or as existent, non-existent or both (4.22); and it names four corners (koṭi), "exists", "does not exist", "exists and does not exist", "does not exist, does not exist", with moving, still, both and non-being, by which the childish cover the Lord (4.83–4.84).
