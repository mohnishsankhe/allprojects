## Definitions
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Wine, meat, fish, parched grain and ritual union, used in the Kaula worship of Śakti (Mahānirvāṇa 5.22-24). Restricted: summary only. The texts bind their use to initiation, consecration and the samayas, and condemn their use for pleasure: wine alone gives no perfection (Kulārṇava 2.117); the uninitiated, wilful or addicted go to hell (5.96-105).
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The Kālīvilāsa forbids them in the Kali age, allowing only the paśu disposition (4.2-3).
