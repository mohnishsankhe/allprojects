## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): 'This triple world is mind only' (Daśabhūmika 6); 'all is made by mind' (Avataṃsaka); 'this mind makes the Buddha, this mind is the Buddha' (Contemplation Sūtra); the image in samādhi is cognition only (Saṃdhinirmocana 8); the world is what is seen of one's own mind (Laṅkāvatāra).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): Kagyu masters teach that 'appearances are mind' (3rd Karmapa; Nāropa's conventional truth) but add that mind is empty; Nāropa criticizes mind-only proponents who take non-dual mind as truly real.
