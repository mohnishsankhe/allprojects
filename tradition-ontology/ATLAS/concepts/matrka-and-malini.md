## Definitions
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The fifty letters in natural order (mātṛkā, A to KṢA) as the emanation of the tattvas (Parātrīśikā 5-9), and in the scrambled order mālinī uniting vowels and consonants (Śiva and Śakti); the ground of limited knowledge when unrecognized (Śiva Sūtra 1.4) and of liberation when awakened by the teacher (2.7).
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): Śabda-brahman, dwelling as kuṇḍalinī, manifests as the letters (ŚT 1.14); the fifty letters from a to kṣa are the Goddess's body and the matrix of all mantras, installed on the body in mātṛkā-nyāsa and used as a rosary of letters.
