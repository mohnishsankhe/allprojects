## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Pravāhaṇa Jaivali's teaching: yonder world, the rain-god, this world, man and woman are five sacrificial fires; the offerings are faith, Soma, rain, food and semen, so that at the fifth offering water becomes 'person'; the knower of it goes by the path of the gods (ChU 5.3-10; BAU 6.2).
