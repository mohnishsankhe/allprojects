# Plurality of puruṣas

`cpt:plurality-of-purusas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** self

## Names
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): puruṣabahutva

## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Puruṣas are many, because birth, death and instruments are allotted separately, beings do not act all at once, and the guṇas are differently proportioned (SK 18; SS 1.149, 6.45); some were liberated while others were not (SS 1.157). Scriptures of non-duality refer to sameness of kind, not number (SS 1.154).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): There are many puruṣas: the seen ceases for one whose purpose is accomplished but continues for others, being common to them (2.22, YBh 2.22); the end of saṃsāra holds for the skilled, not for others (YBh 4.33).
