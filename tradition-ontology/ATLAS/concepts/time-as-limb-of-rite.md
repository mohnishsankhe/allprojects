# Time as a limb of the rite (Mīmāṃsā and Dharmaśāstra use of timing)

## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Vedic rites are enjoined for fixed times — seasons, new and full moon, asterisms — so the knowledge of time serves the rite (VJ y.3); the smṛti and digests fix the times of sacraments, vows, festivals and śrāddha, and have the planets worshipped (YājñSm 1.295–308); jyotiṣa supplies the muhūrta for elective rites, while the digests decide conflicts of times.
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): For Mīmāṃsā, dharma is known from Vedic injunction (MS 1.1.2); the time an injunction names (e.g. spring for establishing the fires) belongs to the rite as its subsidiary; the Vedāṅgas serve the performance of the enjoined rite.
