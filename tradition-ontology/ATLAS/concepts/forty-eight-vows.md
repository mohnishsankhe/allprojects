## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The vows (T360) establishing Sukhāvatī: no evil destinies, golden bodies, superknowledges, infinite light and life, birth for all who call on him (18th), welcome at death (19th), rebirth by dedicating merit (20th).
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Tanluan singles out three vows as proof of other-power — the 18th (birth by ten recitations), 11th (rightly established group), 22nd (surpassing the stages; returning to save beings); the Sanskrit recension has 47 vows.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): Shinran reads five 'true' vows (11th, 12th, 13th, 17th, 18th) and the provisional 19th and 20th, and the 22nd for the returning aspect.
