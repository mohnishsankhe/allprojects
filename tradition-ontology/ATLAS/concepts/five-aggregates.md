## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Form, feeling, perception, formations and consciousness, whatever of each is past, future or present (SN 22.48); as aggregates of clinging they are suffering in brief (SN 56.11) and identity (MN 44); like foam, a bubble, a mirage, a plantain trunk and a magic trick (SN 22.95); the burden (SN 22.22).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The conditioned dharmas grouped as matter, feeling, perception, formations and consciousness; feeling and perception are separated from the other mental factors because they are the roots of dispute and saṃsāra.
- [Lokottaravāda](../lineages/lokottaravada.md): Each aggregate is not self (Mahāvastu's version of the second sermon).
- [Pudgalavāda](../lineages/pudgalavada.md): The basis on which the person is designated, neither identical with it nor different.
