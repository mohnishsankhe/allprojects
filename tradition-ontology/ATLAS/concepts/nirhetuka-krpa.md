## Definitions
- [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md): The Lord's grace needs no cause in the soul ('the kitten carried by the cat').
- [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md): The Lord's grace is free, but he takes the soul's surrender as its occasion (vyāja) so as not to be partial ('the baby monkey clinging').
