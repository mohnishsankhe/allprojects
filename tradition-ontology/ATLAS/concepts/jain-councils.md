## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Pāṭaliputra (after the twelve-year famine, c. 160 years after the nirvāṇa, with Sthūlabhadra); Mathurā under Skandila and, at the same time, Valabhī under Nāgārjuna (c. 827–840 years after); Valabhī under Devarddhigaṇi (980 or 993 years after, 453 or 466 CE), where the canon was written down and the recensions reconciled.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The Digambaras do not accept these councils as preserving the authentic Aṅgas.
