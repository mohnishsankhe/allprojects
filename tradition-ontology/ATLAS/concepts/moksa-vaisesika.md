## Definitions
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): When adṛṣṭa is absent there is no contact with a body and no new appearance (VS 5.2.20 C); knowledge of the categories arising from dharma brings detachment, no new merit and demerit accrue, and when the old are exhausted the self, without seed, takes no body — a quiescence like fire whose fuel is burnt (Praśastapāda).
