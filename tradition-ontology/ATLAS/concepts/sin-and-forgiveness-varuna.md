## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Offence (āgas) and sin (enas) — one's own, done in the body, through wine, anger, dice or thoughtlessness, even in sleep, and those inherited from the fathers — bind as Varuṇa's fetters; the seer examines himself, asks the wise, confesses and asks to be loosened like a calf from its tether, to be sinless before Aditi (RV 1.24.15, 2.28.5, 5.85.7–8, 7.86–89).
