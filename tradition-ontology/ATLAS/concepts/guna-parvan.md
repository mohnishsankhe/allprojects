## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The guṇas exist at four levels: the particularized (sixteen: five elements, ten senses and mind), the unparticularized (five tanmātras and I-am-ness), the mere mark (mahat) and the unmarked (pradhāna) (2.19, YBh 2.19); subtlety of objects ends in the unmarked (1.45).
