## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The teacher is unmoved whether disciples listen or not (MN 137); he does not treat disciples as a potter the raw clay but repeatedly admonishes (MN 122); the Tathāgata only points the way (MN 107; Dhp 276); good friendship is the whole of the holy life (SN 45.2).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): The Kagyu lives dramatize it as trial and devotion: Tilopa and Nāropa's twelve hardships, Marpa and Milarepa's towers; the disciple needs faith, diligence, wisdom and compassion; instructions given without the root guru's consent bear no fruit.
