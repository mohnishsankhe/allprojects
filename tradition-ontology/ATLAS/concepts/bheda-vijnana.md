## Definitions
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Knowing the self as other than body, karma and passion: 'the soul is other, matter is other' (Iṣṭopadeśa 50); all who are liberated are liberated by it, all who are bound are bound by its absence (Samayasāra-kalaśa 131).
- [Adhyātma movement (Adhyātma-mata)](../lineages/adhyatma-jain.md): The central practice of the Adhyātma path, rendered in the Samayasāra Nāṭaka.
