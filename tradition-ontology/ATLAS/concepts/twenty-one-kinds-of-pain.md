## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The pain whose absolute cessation is the goal is divided into twenty-one kinds (Vācaspati); later manuals list them as the body, the six senses (with mind), their six objects, the six cognitions, pleasure and pain itself; birth itself is pain (NS 4.1.55).
