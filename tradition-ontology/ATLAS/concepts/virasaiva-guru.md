## Definitions
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The disciple, discerning and dispassionate, seeks a guru free of greed, versed in self-inquiry and the Śiva-siddhānta, righteous, of good conduct, wearing ash, rudrākṣa and the liṅga, knowing the yoga of liṅga and aṅga (6.1–6); without a perfected guru there is no end to saṃsāra, and such gurus are rare (13.24–27); the guru is Śiva and is not to be regarded as an ordinary man (11.19–24).
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): Allama tests every aspirant, including Akka Mahādēvi and Siddharāma, by questioning in the assembly.
