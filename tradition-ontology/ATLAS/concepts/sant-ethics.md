## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Truth above austerity; non-violence and a vegetarian diet (the goat eats leaves and is flayed); honest household labour and contentment (give only as much as my family needs); humility (none worse than I); keep the critic near; the sādhu known by conduct, not garb.
- [Dādū Panth](../lineages/dadu-panth.md): Erase the self, abandon the faults of body and mind, and be without enmity to all beings.
- [Ravidāsī (Ravidassia) tradition](../lineages/ravidasi.md): Low deeds, not birth, make a man low.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Four vows: lacto-vegetarian diet, no alcohol or drugs, a moral life, daily meditation.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): Speak truth and walk the right path.
