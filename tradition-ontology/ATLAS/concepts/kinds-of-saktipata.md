## Definitions
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): Grace depends on the Lord's freedom alone; intense, middling and mild, each in three degrees (nine grades); its signs are devotion to Rudra, mantra-success, control of beings, completion of undertakings and poetic power with sudden knowledge of scripture (MVT 2.14-16; TĀ 13); graded by scripture (TĀ 13.300-301).
