# Restraint of the mind and its faults (Kārikā 3.40–3.46)

## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The Kārikā makes fearlessness, ending of sorrow, awakening and imperishable peace depend on restraining the mind (3.40); it counsels patience (3.41), and treats distraction (vikṣepa), laya, kaṣāya (the mind as "sakaṣāya") and relishing of the happiness (āsvāda, named rasāsvāda by commentators) with a counsel for each (3.42–3.45); when the mind is neither absorbed nor distracted it is brahman (3.46).
