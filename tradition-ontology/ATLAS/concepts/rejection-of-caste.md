## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Caste has no standing before God: do not ask a sādhu's caste; the brahmin and the Turk came by the same way.
- [Ravidāsī (Ravidassia) tradition](../lineages/ravidasi.md): Ravidās names his Camār caste and its work without shame; the Lord raises the one whose touch the world calls polluting; in Begampura there is no second or third rank.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): Lalon has never seen the form of caste; at birth and death no sign of it remains.
- [Kartābhajā](../lineages/kartabhaja.md): In the Kartā's fellowship there is one caste — while outward custom is kept in the world.
- [Satnāmī movements](../lineages/satnami.md): Ghāsīdās's Satnāmīs reject caste hierarchy and untouchability.
- [Balarāmī sampradāya](../lineages/balarami.md): Balarām Hāḍi's followers reject brahmanical caste religion.
