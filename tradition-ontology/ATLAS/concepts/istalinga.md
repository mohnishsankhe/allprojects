# The personal liṅga worn on the body (iṣṭaliṅga)

`cpt:istalinga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ultimate

## Names
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): iṣṭaliṅga

## Definitions
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Śiva himself, given by the guru at initiation as a small liṅga worn on the body for life and worshipped on the palm; Śiva abides in it from the moment of initiation, so it needs no invocation; the outer liṅga is to be worn 'knowing it is of that same form' as the inner.
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): The body bearing the liṅga is itself the temple: the moving (jaṅgama) against the fixed (sthāvara) shrine.
