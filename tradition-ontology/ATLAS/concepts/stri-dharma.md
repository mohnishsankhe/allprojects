## Definitions
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Women are to be honoured, for where they are not all rites fail (MDh 3.56); a woman is to be under the protection of father, husband and sons (MDh 5.148; 9.3); a widow chooses chastity or (in some later texts) ascending the pyre (Viṣṇusmṛti 25.14); women are qualified for Śrauta rites with their husbands (KŚS 1.1.7); dharma may be learned from women (ĀpDh 2.11.29.15); an older tradition distinguishes women who study the Veda (brahmavādinī) from those who marry directly (sadyovadhū) (Hārīta, quoted).
