## Definitions
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): The saint calls the afflicted his own, is softer than wax yet firm as diamond, bears insult like water quenching fire, and sees the Lord in all beings.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): Rāma dwells in hearts free of lust, anger, pride and greed, that see others' wives as mothers and others' wealth as poison and rejoice in others' good.
- [Unaffiliated regional bhakti poets (classificatory grouping)](../lineages/regional-bhakti-poets.md): The true Vaiṣṇava knows others' pain, helps without pride and speaks ill of none (Narsinh Mehta).
- [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md): The best devotee sees the Lord in all and all in the Lord.
