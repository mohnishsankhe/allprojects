# Liberation (apavarga) as the end of pain

`cpt:apavarga-nyaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** karma-rebirth

## Names
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): apavarga
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): mukti

## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Absolute release from pain (NS 1.1.22), reached by removing false knowledge (NS 1.1.2). Mainstream view: the liberated self has neither pleasure nor cognition — there is no proof of an eternal pleasure, and seeking it would bind (NBh 1.1.22); liberation is peace in which all pain is cut off (NBh 1.1.2). Minority view (Bhāsarvajña): the cessation of pain is qualified by the experience of eternal bliss.
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): A destruction of pain not contemporaneous with any prior absence of pain in the same self (Muktivāda).
