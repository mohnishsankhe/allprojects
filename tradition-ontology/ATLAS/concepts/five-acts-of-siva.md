## Definitions
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Emission, maintenance, withdrawal, concealment, grace — performed by Śiva through his power, all for the souls' release; concealment ripens mala, withdrawal gives rest.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Aintoḻil, enacted in Naṭarāja's dance: drum, fearless hand, fire, planted foot, raised foot.
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): Emission, maintenance, withdrawal, concealment and grace; the bound individual performs them too - as manifesting, relishing, reflecting, planting the seed and dissolving (PH 10-11); bondage and liberation are Śiva's play (Paramārthasāra 33).
