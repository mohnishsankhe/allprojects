## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Breath is the eldest and best of the vital powers, on whose departure the body fails (BAU 6.1; ChU 5.1); the one god (BAU 3.9.9); born from the self like a shadow, dividing itself fivefold (PrU 3.3-7); brahman (KauU 2.1) and the intelligent self (KauU 3.2); the gatherer into which the senses go in sleep (ChU 4.3.3); breath is father, mother, all (ChU 7.15).
