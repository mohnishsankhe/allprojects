## Definitions
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Five āmnāyas from Śiva's five faces — Eastern, Western, Southern, Northern, Upper — the Upper supreme (Kulārṇava 3.7; 3.15-19). In the Kashmir–Nepal scheme reported by scholarship (moderate confidence): the Eastern is the Kula/Trika worship, the Southern Tripurasundarī (Śrīvidyā), the Western Kubjikā, the Northern Kālī (Krama, Guhyakālī); Nepal adds the Upper and speaks of six (ṣaḍāmnāya).
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): The Paścimāmnāya is 'the western of all paths' (Kubjikāmata 2.21).
