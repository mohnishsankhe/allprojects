## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The 'other' samādhi, preceded by practice of the cognition of cessation, with only impressions remaining (1.18); its means is higher dispassion; the mind becomes supportless, as if non-existent (YBh 1.18); nothing is cognized in it (YBh 1.2). Bhoja: reached by rejecting every arising activity 'not this, not this' (RM 1.18).
