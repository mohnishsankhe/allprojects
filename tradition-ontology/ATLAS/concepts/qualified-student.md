## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): One impartial, intelligent and eager is a vessel for hearing (CŚ 12.1); one who at the mention of emptiness feels joy, tears and horripilation is a vessel to whom the ultimate should be taught (MA 6.4–5); emptiness wrongly grasped harms the dull, so the Buddha hesitated to teach (MMK 24.11–12).
