# The sixteen categories of Nyāya (ṣoḍaśa padārtha)

## Names
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): ṣoḍaśa padārthāḥ

## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The sixteen topics whose true knowledge yields the highest good (NS 1.1.1): the means and objects of knowledge, and the elements of inquiry and debate. The Bhāṣya explains that the latter are listed separately so that Nyāya is not merely a science of the self like the Upaniṣads.
