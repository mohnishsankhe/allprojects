## Definitions
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Mahākāśyapa, Ānanda, Śāṇakavāsin, Upagupta (then Dhītika, Kṛṣṇa, Sudarśana), with Madhyāntika as Ānanda's disciple who brought the Dharma to Kashmir (Aśokāvadāna and related avadānas; the later names with low confidence).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The Vinaya lineage of the elders from Upāli to Moggaliputta Tissa (Dīpavaṃsa, Samantapāsādikā) — a different succession.
