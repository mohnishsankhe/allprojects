## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Liberation at the fall of the body when prārabdha is exhausted — like pot-space merging with space or wind becoming still (Muktikā 1 prose, 2.76; Yogakuṇḍalī 3.34); the dissolution of mind 'without form'.
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Sāṃkhya's final isolation: 'when separation from the body is attained and pradhāna ceases, its purpose fulfilled, he attains isolation both certain and final' (SK 68). The SK does not use the word videhamukti.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): At the fall of the body the knower's prāṇas do not depart; he merges, nothing new being attained.
