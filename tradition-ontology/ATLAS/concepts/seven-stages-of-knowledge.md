## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Good desire, inquiry, thinning of mind, attainment of sattva, non-attachment, non-perception of objects and the reaching of the fourth; liberation lies beyond them; the seventh belongs to the jīvanmukta and the state beyond the fourth to the bodiless liberated.
