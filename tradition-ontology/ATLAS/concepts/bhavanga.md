# The life-continuum (bhavaṅga)

`cpt:bhavanga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** consciousness-states

## Names

## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The passive resultant consciousness that flows whenever no active process occurs (as in dreamless sleep), identical in type with the rebirth-linking and death consciousness of that life.

## Relations (interpretation layer)
- contrasts-with → [Deep sleep](deep-sleep.md): Yoga treats deep sleep as a mental modification (nidrā-vṛtti); the Abhidhamma as the uninterrupted life-continuum
