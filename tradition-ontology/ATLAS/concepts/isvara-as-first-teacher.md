## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Īśvara is the teacher even of the ancients (1.26); the first knower out of compassion taught the doctrine to Āsuri through a created mind (YBh 1.25). Commentators read 'anuśāsana' (1.1) as Patañjali's re-teaching of yoga first taught by Hiraṇyagarbha and give the lineage Pitāmaha — Śeṣa (Patañjali) — Vyāsa — Vācaspati and Vijñānabhikṣu. In the stages of saṃyama 'yoga itself is the teacher' (YBh 3.6).
