## Names

## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The universe consists of six eternal, uncreated substances: souls, matter, the medium of motion, the medium of rest, space and time; all but time are 'extended' (astikāya); only matter has form (TS 5.1–5, 5.39).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Some Śvetāmbara authorities treat time only as a mode of the other substances ('time too, according to some', Śv TS 5.38).
