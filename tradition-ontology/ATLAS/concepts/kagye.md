## Definitions
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): The sādhana section of Mahāyoga: five transcendent (Mañjuśrī-Yamāntaka body, Hayagrīva speech, Yangdag mind, Chemchok-Amṛta qualities, Vajrakīla activity) and three mundane (Mamo, Jigten Chötö, Möpa Drakngak) heruka practices, entrusted to eight Indian vidyādharas and given by Padmasambhava to his disciples.
