## Definitions
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): One ever joined in non-duality succeeds; abandoning the attitude of duality one lives by non-dual conduct; the yogī takes fragrance and stench alike and is unstained by merit and sin; forms of conduct (as child, madman, king...) are whatever vow he wills; no one may be made to take the caru by force (KJN 11–12).
