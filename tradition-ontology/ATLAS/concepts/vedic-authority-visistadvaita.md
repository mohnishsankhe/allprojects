## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The Veda is authorless and eternal, re-promulgated by the Lord in each creation; it alone makes the Lord known; the Itihāsa–Purāṇas (above all the Viṣṇu Purāṇa) and the Pāñcarātra, spoken by the Lord, are authoritative, and the Tamil hymns of the Āḻvārs are a Veda in Tamil.
