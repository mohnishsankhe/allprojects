## Definitions
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): Five systems holding different views, proclaimed by Kapila, Hiraṇyagarbha, the Lord himself, Apāntaratamas and Śiva respectively, in all of which Nārāyaṇa is the culmination (MBh 12.337.59–67).
