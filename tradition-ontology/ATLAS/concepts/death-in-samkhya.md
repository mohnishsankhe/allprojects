## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): At death the body born of mother and father perishes, while the constant subtle body, perfumed by the dispositions, transmigrates to divine, animal or human birth according to virtue and non-virtue (SK 39–40, 44, 53); suffering from old age and death lasts until the subtle body ceases (SK 55). The knower's body falls when impressions are exhausted, and isolation follows (SK 67–68). The teachers disagreed on the subtle body at death and birth (Yuktidīpikā on SK 39).
