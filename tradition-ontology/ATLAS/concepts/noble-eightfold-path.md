## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Right view, intention, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness and concentration (SN 45.8); contained in the three trainings (MN 44); right view comes first and each factor has a mundane and a supramundane form (MN 117); where it is found, the four kinds of ascetic are found (DN 16).
