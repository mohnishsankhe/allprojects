## Definitions
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): The line from Mahākāśyapa to Bodhidharma (28) and from Bodhidharma to Huineng (six Chinese patriarchs), as listed in the Platform Sūtra and Jingde chuandeng lu; the Dunhuang Platform Sūtra gives a different list counting Huineng as 40th from the seven buddhas.
