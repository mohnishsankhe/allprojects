## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Taking the impermanent as permanent, the painful as pleasant, the impure as pure and the selfless as self; the afflictions arise in dependence on the errors about pleasant and unpleasant and ignorance ceases with them (MMK 23.1–2, 23.23); Catuḥśataka chs. 1–4 overcome them in turn.
