# The self as its own light (svayaṃjyotis)

## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The self is the light by which a person acts when sun, moon, fire and speech are gone (BAU 4.3.6); in dream 'the person becomes his own light' (4.3.9, 4.3.14); 'by his light all this is illumined' (KU 2.2.15; MuU 2.2.10; ŚU 6.14).
