## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Jyotiṣa rests on three branches: gaṇita or siddhānta (computation of the planets' motions), horā (nativities) and saṃhitā (omens and mundane matters); Parāśara calls the horā the best of the three; Varāhamihira composed works in all three.
- [The Kerala tradition of jyotiṣa](../lineages/kerala-jyotisa.md): Kerala authors speak of six limbs (ṣaḍaṅga): nativity (jātaka), sphere (gola), omens (nimitta), interrogation (praśna), electional timing (muhūrta) and computation (gaṇita).
