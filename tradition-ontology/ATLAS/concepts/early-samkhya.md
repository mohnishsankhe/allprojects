## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The tradition traces Sāṃkhya to Kapila, Āsuri and Pañcaśikha and to teachers such as Janaka, Vasiṣṭha and Dvaipāyana (Yuktidīpikā on SK 72). Earlier formulations are preserved in the Mahābhārata's Mokṣadharma (Pañcaśikha's teaching to Janaka; Vasiṣṭha–Karāla and Yājñavalkya–Janaka dialogues with 24/25/26 principles), in the Bhagavad Gītā's 'sāṃkhya' (knowledge) and field-knower teaching (ch. 13–14, 18.13), in Caraka's account of twenty-four principles (Śārīrasthāna 1), and in Aśvaghoṣa's report of Arāḍa's teaching (Buddhacarita 12, a Buddhist report). Upaniṣadic hierarchies (Kaṭha 3.10–11) and the word 'sāṃkhya' (Śvetāśvatara 6.13) are claimed by Sāṃkhya as its scriptural basis.
