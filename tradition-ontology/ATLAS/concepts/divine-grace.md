## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): By the Lord's grace the devotee attains the eternal abode even while acting (BhG 18.56, 18.62), crosses all obstacles (18.58), sees the universal form (11.47), and is lifted from the ocean of saṃsāra (12.7); the Lord dwelling in the heart destroys the darkness of ignorance (10.11).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: To those ever yoked who worship him with love the Lord gives the yoga of understanding (10.10) and, out of compassion, abiding in their own being or in his own (ātma-bhāva-stha), destroys the darkness born of ignorance (10.11); Arjuna's delusion is gone through the word spoken out of favour to him (11.1); the Lord gives the divine eye (11.8) and, being gracious (prasanna), shows the supreme form by his own yoga (11.47); Arjuna prays 'be gracious' (11.25, 11.31, 11.45) and seeks his grace (11.44); for those whose awareness (cetas) is set on him the Lord becomes before long the deliverer from the ocean of death and saṃsāra (12.7).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7 and 9 (without the word prasāda): the Lord makes the worshipper's faith unwavering and ordains the fruits obtained (7.21–22); for the undivided devotee he himself carries acquisition and preservation (yoga-kṣema) (9.22); for the constant rememberer he is easy to reach (8.14).
