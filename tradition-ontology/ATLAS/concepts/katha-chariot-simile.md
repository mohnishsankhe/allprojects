## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The self is the lord of the chariot, the body the chariot, the intellect (buddhi) the charioteer, the mind (manas) the reins, the senses (indriya) the horses and the objects (viṣaya) their roads (1.3.3-4). With an unyoked mind the senses are unruly like bad horses and one goes to saṃsāra; with a yoked mind they are obedient like good horses and one reaches the highest place of Viṣṇu, from which there is no rebirth (1.3.5-9).
