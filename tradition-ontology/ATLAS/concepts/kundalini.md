# Kuṇḍalinī

**Category:** body-energy

## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The coiled power lying at the base of the suṣumnā, blocking the door to Brahman; awakened by fire, breath and mind she rises through the centres and knots to unite with Śiva in the crown; 'bondage for the ignorant, liberation for yogins'; in the Varāha the supreme power from which breath, fire, bindu, nāda and mind arise.
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The coiled power at the base of the central channel, blocking its mouth; the support of all yoga (HYP 3.1); awakened by the guru's grace (3.2) and by breath, locks and mudrās (3.5, 3.66–69) she straightens like a struck snake, pierces the knots and lotuses and the mind becomes supportless; for yogins she is liberation, for fools bondage (3.107). The GŚ says she is coiled eightfold, the ṢCN three and a half times; the Ṣaṭcakranirūpaṇa describes her ascent through six centres to union with Paramaśiva.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The coiled power closing the door of Brahman, awakened by the fire of yoga with mind and breath (GŚ 30–31) or by the guru's grace (HYP 3.2), rising through suṣumṇā; asleep in the downward four-petalled lotus (AmŚ 60–64); the world-mother, pedestal of the liṅga of the void (Jñāneśvarī 6.272–273). The Amaraughaśāsana denies that its movement is itself liberation (AmŚ 52).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): One of the names of Śiva's Will (KJN 20.11); the coiled Vāmā rising from the anus to the dvādaśānta (17.23); the 'made' Kaula path as against the innate (Akulavīra B.43).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The Goddess herself, dwelling in the mūlādhāra, piercing the knots of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra, mounting the thousand-petalled lotus, showering nectar and returning to sleep in the kula-pit in three and a half coils (LSN 38-40; SL 9-10); seen by the pure-minded as a digit slender as lightning above the six lotuses (SL 21).
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tamil kuṇṭali: the coiled power at the root, in whose seat Guha is invoked; roused by the guru, it passes the six supports through the central channel to the moon of nectar.
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): The coiled power at the base, waked by posture and the locks, rising through the centres to merge in the supreme at the crown (Jñāneśvarī 6; summary only).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): Śākta view (Pūrṇānanda): the Goddess herself, coiled three and a half times round the Svayambhū liṅga, is led up through the centres to unite with Śiva in the thousand-petalled lotus, the bliss of which is liberation (Ṣaṭcakranirūpaṇa 51-53).
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): Kubjikā is praised as coiled like a sleeping serpent (Kubjikāmata 2.2).
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Kuṇḍalinī practice working up from the lower cakras is held to be unnecessary and hazardous; the Sants begin at the eye centre and rise by the sound.
- [Dādū Panth](../lineages/dadu-panth.md): Sundardās describes rousing the serpent power as part of yoga, below devotion and knowledge.
