## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Those who know (the five fires) and who venerate faith and austerity in the forest go by the flame, the day, the waxing fortnight, the northern course, the year/gods' world, the sun, the moon and lightning to brahman and do not return; those who venerate sacrifice, works and gifts go by smoke, night, the waning fortnight, the southern course, the fathers' world and the moon, and return; those who know neither become small creatures (ChU 5.10; BAU 6.2.15-16; PrU 1.9-10; KauU 1.2-3; ChU 4.15.5).
- [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md): The path of the gods (beginning with light) for meditators, without return; the path of the fathers (smoke, the moon) for ritualists, with return and residual karma.
