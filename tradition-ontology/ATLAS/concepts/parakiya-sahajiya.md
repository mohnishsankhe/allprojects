## Definitions
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): The gopīs' love outside marriage is taken as the model of the practitioner's love for a partner (not the spouse), because it is selfless, risky and full of longing; recorded as doctrine only (restricted). The path is said to be hard as holding an elephant with a spider's thread.
