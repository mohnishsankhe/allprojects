## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Giving because it ought to be given, to one who makes no return, at the right place and time to the worthy, is sāttvic; for a return or grudgingly, rājasic; at the wrong place and time, to the unworthy, with contempt, tāmasic (BhG 17.20–22).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Giving because it ought to be given, to one who makes no return, at the right place and time to a worthy recipient is sāttvic (17.20); for a return, for fruit or grudgingly, rājasic (17.21); at the wrong place and time, to the unworthy, without respect and with contempt, tāmasic (17.22).
