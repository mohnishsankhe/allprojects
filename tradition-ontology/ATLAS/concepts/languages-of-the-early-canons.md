## Definitions
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The Sarvāstivāda and Mūlasarvāstivāda transmitted their canon in Sanskrit (Turfan, Gilgit).
- [Lokottaravāda](../lineages/lokottaravada.md): The Lokottaravāda Vinaya texts are in a Prakrit-coloured 'Buddhist Hybrid Sanskrit' (Mahāvastu, Prātimokṣa, Bhikṣuṇī-Vinaya).
- [Dharmaguptaka](../lineages/dharmaguptaka.md): The earliest manuscripts, associated with the Dharmaguptaka, are in Gāndhārī written in Kharoṣṭhī.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The Theravāda transmits its canon in Pali, held to be Māgadhī, the Buddha's own language.
