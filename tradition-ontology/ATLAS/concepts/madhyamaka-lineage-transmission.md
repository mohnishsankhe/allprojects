## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The tradition's line runs from the Buddha's Perfection of Wisdom through Nāgārjuna (who received the sūtras from the nāgas in the tradition's account), Āryadeva and Rāhulabhadra, to the commentators Buddhapālita, Bhāviveka and Candrakīrti, Śāntideva, and Śrīgupta–Jñānagarbha–Śāntarakṣita–Kamalaśīla, and on to Tibet (Atiśa; Patsab Nyima Drak) and China (Kumārajīva). Commentary on a root text by a lineage master is the mode of transmission; no secret initiation is required, but emptiness is to be taught only to a fit vessel.
