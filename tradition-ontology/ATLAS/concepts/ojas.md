## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The supreme essence of the tissues, identical with strength (Su Sū 15.19; AHS Sū 11.37); seated in the heart, pervading the body, whose loss is death (Ca Sū 17.74-75); the 'supreme' ojas in the heart and the ojas carried by the ten great vessels (Ca Sū 30.7-9); three disorders: displacement, vitiation, loss (Su Sū 15.24).
