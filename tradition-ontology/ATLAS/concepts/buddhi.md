## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The first evolute (= mahat), whose nature is determination; bearer of the eight dispositions (SK 23); it accomplishes all experience for puruṣa and discriminates puruṣa from pradhāna (SK 37); unconscious, it appears conscious by taking on the shade of consciousness (Vācaspati on SK 5).
