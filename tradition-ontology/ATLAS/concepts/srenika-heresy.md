# The 'Śreṇika heresy' (eternal mind-nature in a perishing body)

## Definitions
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): The view, reported of certain southern teachers, that the knowing nature is permanent and leaves the perishing body at death; Nanyang Huizhong equates it with the heretic Śreṇika.
- [Sōtō Zen](../lineages/soto.md): Dōgen rejects it: body and mind are one, nature and form not two.
