## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Restraining the mind from unwholesome objects (Ca Sū 11.54); for mental illness: attending to the three aims, serving those who know them, and knowing oneself, place, family, time and strength (Sū 11.46-47); 'knowledge of intellect, steadiness, the self' is the best medicine for the mind (AHS Sū 1.26).
