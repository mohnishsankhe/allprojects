## Definitions
- [Rasa Śāstra (the science of mercury) and the Raseśvara-darśana](../lineages/rasa-sastra.md): A Māheśvara system that holds identity with the Supreme Lord yet requires a stable body for the liberation in life accepted by all, and makes mercury the means (SDS Raseśvara 1-5); its end is the vision of the conscious light and brahmanhood here (45-48); mercury is equated with brahman (49-51).
