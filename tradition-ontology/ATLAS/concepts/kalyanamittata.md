## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The whole of the holy life (SN 45.2); the first support for the maturing of the mind's liberation (Ud 4.1); true and false friends (DN 31).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The meditation subject is received from a good friend who is lovable, respected, esteemed, able to counsel, patient, able to give deep talk and never urges to what is useless; best is the Buddha, then the great disciples, then an arahant, and so on down to one who knows the texts; a person can be a decisive-support condition.
- [Thai Forest tradition (Kammaṭṭhāna forest tradition)](../lineages/thai-forest.md): Close personal training under an experienced forest teacher who knows the pupil's mind.
- [Borān kammaṭṭhāna (old meditation; the yogāvacara tradition)](../lineages/boran-kammatthana.md): Formal request of the meditation subject from the teacher with offerings.
