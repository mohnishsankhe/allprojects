## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Sound is a mode of matter (TS 5.24), not eternal; speech is produced from the linguistic class of matter; scripture is śruta-jñāna grounded in the omniscient's teaching; the Tīrthaṅkara's divine sound (divyadhvani) is understood by all beings in their own tongues. Words refer by name, representation, potentiality and actuality (TS 1.5).
