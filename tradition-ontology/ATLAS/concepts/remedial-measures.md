## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Because the happiness and sorrow of creatures depend on the planets, their sacrifice is to be performed for peace, prosperity, rain, long life and nourishment (BPHS 84.1–3; YājñSm 1.295–308): images, Vedic mantras, fuel-sticks, food, gifts, recitation and worship for each planet; pacification rites for births at eclipses, junctions, Mūla and Viṣṭi (BPHS 86–96); gifts, cow-milking at a Rudra shrine and the ten-million oblation for portents (BS 45.4–7); gems examined for their fortune (BS 79.1); expiations for curses of past lives (BPHS 83; Praśna Mārga). Varāhamihira warns that rites alone do not bring success (Yogayātrā 1.6).
