## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Already in the Brāhmaṇas the rite is carried into the person: the self-sacrificer knows each rite builds his own self (ŚB 11.2.6.13); fires are built of mind and knowledge (ŚB 10.5.3); the Agnihotra is 'truth offered in faith' (ŚB 11.3.1.4); feeding brāhmaṇas is 'offering into Brahman' (ĀśGS 1.1.2).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): In the Āraṇyakas and Upaniṣads: the inner Agnihotra of speech and breath (KU 2.5), the breath-oblations at meals (ChU 5.19–24), the sacrifice in the body (Prāṇāgnihotra Up.).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The renouncer deposits the sacred fires in himself (MDh 6.38) and the true self-sacrificer sees the self in all beings (MDh 12.91).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4.25–30 carries the language of the fire-offering over to inner disciplines: the senses offered into the fires of restraint, sense objects into the fires of the senses, the activities of senses and breath into the fire of the yoga of self-restraint kindled by knowledge, prāṇa into apāna and apāna into prāṇa.
