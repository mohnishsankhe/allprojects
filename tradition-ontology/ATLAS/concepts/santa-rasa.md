## Definitions
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): The rasa leading to liberation, arising from knowledge of reality, dispassion and purity of mind, acted through yama, niyama and meditation; its stable emotion is calm (NŚ śānta passage; SD 3.245), dispassion (KP 4) or the self itself as knowledge of reality (Abhinavagupta); the natural state from which the other bhāvas arise and into which they dissolve; principal rasa of the Mahābhārata (DhĀ 4.5 vṛtti); denied a place in drama by Dhanañjaya.
