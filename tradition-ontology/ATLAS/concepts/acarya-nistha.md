## Definitions
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): Madhurakavi found his teacher by following a light and tested him with a riddle; thereafter saying his teacher's name was sweeter to him than approaching Kṛṣṇa, and he knew no other god; the teacher became mother and father to him. The recitation of this devotion to the teacher is what recovered the Prabandham for Nāthamuni.
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Devotion comes chiefly by the grace of great souls, who are not different from the Lord.
