# The eighteen faults absent in the Jina

## Definitions
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Hunger, thirst, fear, anger, attachment, delusion, anxiety, old age, disease, death, sweat, fatigue, pride, pleasure, wonder, sleep, birth and distress — hence the Jina does not eat.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): A different list of eighteen: the five obstructions (to giving, gain, enjoyment, repeated enjoyment and energy), laughter, liking, disliking, fear, disgust, grief, desire, wrong view, ignorance, sleep, non-restraint, attachment and aversion — hunger and thirst are not among them (moderate confidence).
