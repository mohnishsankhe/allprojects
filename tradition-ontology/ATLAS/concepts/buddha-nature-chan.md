## Definitions
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): The nature of awakening in every being, identical with the self-nature; 'if the self-nature is awakened, beings are buddhas; if deluded, buddhas are beings'.
- [Sōtō Zen](../lineages/soto.md): 'Whole being is buddha-nature'; impermanence is buddha-nature (Dōgen).
- [Rinzai Zen](../lineages/rinzai.md): 'All beings are originally buddhas' like ice and water (Hakuin).
- [Seon (Korean Chan)](../lineages/seon.md): One's own numinous knowing is the true buddha (Jinul).
