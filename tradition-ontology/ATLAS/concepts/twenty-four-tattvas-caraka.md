## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Caraka: the eightfold prakṛti (unmanifest, buddhi, ego-sense, five elements) and sixteen modifications (ten senses, mind, five objects); the unmanifest is the knower of the field (Śā 1.61-65); creation and dissolution follow (1.66-69). Suśruta: twenty-four principles and the person as the twenty-fifth (Su Śā 1.3-9).
