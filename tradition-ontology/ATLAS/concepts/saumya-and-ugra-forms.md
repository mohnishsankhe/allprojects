## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The one Goddess appears as gentle (saumya: Kamalā, Ṣoḍaśī, Bhuvaneśvarī) and fierce (ugra: Kālī, Tārā, Chinnamastā, Dhūmāvatī) forms; the forms are imagined according to her qualities and acts for the benefit of worshippers (Mahānirvāṇa 5.140; 13.13); Satī turns dark and fearsome to show Śiva her Mahāvidyā forms (Bṛhaddharma madhya 6.78-80); Dakṣiṇakālī is terrible yet smiling and boon-giving (Tantrasāra).
