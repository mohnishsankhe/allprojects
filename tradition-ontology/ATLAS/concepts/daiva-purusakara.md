## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Life depends on both: daiva is one's own former deed, puruṣakāra present effort; weak daiva is overcome by effort and weak effort by strong daiva; some karma is fixed in time, some roused by conditions (Ca Vi 3.29-36); karma-born diseases subside only with the exhaustion of karma (Śā 1.116-117).
