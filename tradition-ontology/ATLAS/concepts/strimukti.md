# Liberation of women (strī-mukti)

## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Women can reach omniscience and liberation in a female body: the canon lists women among the fifteen kinds of siddhas, Mallī was a woman Tīrthaṅkara, Marudevī was the first liberated of this era, and nuns hold the great vows.
- [Yāpanīya](../lineages/yapaniya.md): Affirmed with arguments: nothing in a woman opposes the three jewels; the garment is not possession; incapacity for the lowest hell does not imply incapacity for liberation.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Denied: a woman cannot give up clothing, lacks the strongest bodily constitution needed for pure meditation, and her body is a site of the generation of subtle beings; she can reach the fifth stage (a nun is ranked with advanced laity) and may be reborn as a man; canonical 'female siddhas' refers to psychological sex.
