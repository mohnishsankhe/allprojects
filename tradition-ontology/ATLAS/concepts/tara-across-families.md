## Definitions
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Tārā, the saviouress who ferries beings across dangers, a female buddha or bodhisattva of compassion; forms include Green and White Tārā and the fierce Ekajaṭā and Mahācīna-Tārā, the latter crowned with Akṣobhya.
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Tārā is the second of the ten Mahāvidyās, fierce and dark blue, with Akṣobhya on her head; her Kula worship is said to have been learned in Mahācīna from the Buddha by Vasiṣṭha.
