## Definitions
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Meditating oneself as the deity — appearance inseparable from emptiness, with the deity's pride — the distinctive method of the mantra way; 'there is no meditator, no mantra, no deity: mantra and deity abide in the nature free of elaboration' (Hevajra I.5).
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The feature distinguishing mantra from the perfection vehicle: the wisdom realising emptiness itself appears as the deity's body.
