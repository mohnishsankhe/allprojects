## Definitions
- [Kabīr Panth](../lineages/kabir-panth.md): The Sat Puruṣ emanated sixteen sons; Nirañjan by austerity won the three worlds and Ādyā, begot Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Maheś, and as Kāl binds souls; Kabīr comes in every age to lead souls back to Satlok.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Bījak: from light, sound and one woman came Hari, Brahmā and Tripurāri, and the world of forms.
