## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Creation, maintenance and dissolution are the Lord's play (līlā) in the realm of prakṛti: he creates to give souls bodies to work out karma and seek him; Brahmā and Śiva are souls whom he empowers; the eternal realm is untouched by these cycles.
