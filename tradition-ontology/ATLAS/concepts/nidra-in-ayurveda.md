## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Sleep comes when the tired mind and senses withdraw from their objects; happiness, strength, knowledge and life depend on it; seven kinds — from tamas (root of sin), kapha, fatigue, adventitious, disease-following, and from night's nature (the nurse of beings) (Ca Sū 21.35-38, 21.58-59; AHS Sū 7.53-56).
