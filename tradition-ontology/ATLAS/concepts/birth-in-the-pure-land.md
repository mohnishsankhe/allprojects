## Definitions
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): At death the practitioner is born by transformation in a lotus in Sukhāvatī, where he is non-retrogressive, sees the Buddha, hears the Dharma and completes the path — then returns to save beings.
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): Birth at death through the nenbutsu, with the Buddha's welcome; awakening is attained there.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): Birth in the true land is at once the attainment of great nirvāṇa; it is settled in this life at the moment of shinjin.
