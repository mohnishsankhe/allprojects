## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The ordinary person conceives each thing — earth to nibbāna — as self, in self, from self or 'mine' and delights in it; the trainee should not conceive; the Tathāgata does not, having understood that delight is the root of suffering (MN 1); 'I am' is a conceiving (MN 140).
