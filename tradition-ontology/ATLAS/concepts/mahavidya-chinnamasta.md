## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Self-decapitated, holding her own head and a cleaver, three streams of blood rising from her neck — one into her own mouth and two into those of her attendants Ḍākinī and Varṇinī — standing on the god of love and his wife in union (standard dhyāna, recalled). Teaching: Kabandha ('the headless') is her Bhairava, by whose worship one becomes lord of all powers (Toḍala 1.12-13). A Bengali account (Prāṇatoṣiṇī, citing earlier texts) tells that she cut off her own head to feed her hungry attendants.
