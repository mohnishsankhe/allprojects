## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Events are governed by five orders — of temperature/seasons, seeds, kamma, mind, and Dhamma — so not everything experienced is due to kamma.
- [Ledi Sayadaw tradition](../lineages/ledi.md): Expounded at length in Ledi Sayadaw's Niyāma Dīpanī as the cosmic order without a creator.
