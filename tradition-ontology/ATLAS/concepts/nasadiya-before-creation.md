## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Before creation there was neither non-being nor being, death nor immortality, night nor day; That One breathed windless by its own power; darkness hidden by darkness; That One was born by tapas; desire arose as the first seed of mind; seers found the bond of being in non-being in their hearts; the gods came later; whether even the overseer in the highest heaven knows is left open (RV 10.129).
