## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The guru is Śakti; the body with nine openings is the Śrīcakra; each enclosure and its deities are constituents of body and mind — the fourteen channels, ten vital airs, ten fires, the qualities, the subtle elements, the inner organ — and the fifteen Nityās are the lunar days (Bhāvanā Upaniṣad).
- [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md): The Samayin worships the cakra only within, in the heart and the crown (Lakṣmīdhara).
