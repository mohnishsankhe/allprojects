## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Fourteen stages of the soul's purification: wrong view, the taste [of falling], mixed, right view without vows, partial restraint, careless restraint, careful restraint, unprecedented, non-returning gross passion, subtle passion, subsided delusion, destroyed delusion, omniscient with activity, omniscient without activity (Sarvārthasiddhi 1.8; Gommaṭasāra); the Tattvārthasūtra itself gives a ten-stage series of increasing shedding (9.45).
- [Adhyātma movement (Adhyātma-mata)](../lineages/adhyatma-jain.md): Banārsīdās added a chapter on the guṇasthānas to the Samayasāra Nāṭaka so that the knower of the self also knows the stages of conduct.
