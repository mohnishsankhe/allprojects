## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): From prakṛti arises mahat; from mahat ahaṃkāra; from ahaṃkāra the group of sixteen — the eleven organs from its sāttvika (vaikṛta) aspect and the five subtle elements from its tāmasa (bhūtādi) aspect, both with its taijasa aspect; from the five subtle elements the five gross elements (SK 22, 24–25; SS 1.61) — sound→space, touch→air, form→fire, taste→water, smell→earth (Gauḍapāda). In re-absorption each dissolves into its cause in reverse order; the unmanifest does not dissolve (Tattvasamāsa commentaries). The Yuktidīpikā records divergent orders taught by Vindhyavāsin and others.
