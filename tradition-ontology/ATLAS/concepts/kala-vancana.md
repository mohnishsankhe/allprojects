## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Yoga is 'the cheating of time' (GŚ 2); the yogī in samādhi is not devoured by time (GŚ 98); when kuṇḍalī wakes time is cheated (HYP 3.3); by preserving bindu one conquers death (HYP 3.88).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): Taught as most secret: the mind in the heart until unmanī turns back death (KJN 6.27–28); the nectar meditations (5.6–11).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The haṭha texts promise the conquest of time/death: the Siddhas 'broke the staff of time' (HYP 1.9); breath held, fear of death is gone (2.39–40); when kuṇḍalinī wakes, death is cheated (3.3); mudrās grant 'cheating of time' (3.130); suṣumnā devours time (4.17); the one established in rājayoga cheats time (4.103); the Haṭhatattvakaumudī has a chapter on it.
