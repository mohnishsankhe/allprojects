## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Saṃsāra full of suffering (what is to be abandoned), the conjunction of pradhāna and puruṣa (its cause), the absolute ceasing of the conjunction (the abandonment) and right seeing (the means): the śāstra is fourfold as the science of healing is fourfold (disease, cause, health, medicine) (YBh 2.15). Also named as heya (YS 2.16), heya-hetu (YS 2.17), hāna (YS 2.25) and hānopāya (YS 2.26).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): As medicine has four parts — disease, its cause, health, remedy — so the śāstra has four: saṃsāra (what is to be avoided, future suffering), its cause (the conjunction of seer and seen), liberation (avoidance, the absence of conjunction) and its means (right seeing, unwavering discernment) (YBh 2.15; YS 2.16–26).
