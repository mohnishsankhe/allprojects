## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Recollection that one will die and go alone, leaving relatives and possessions (BCA 2.32, 2.62), and that all born beings are subject to the lord of death (CŚ ch.1), turns the mind from craving toward the Dharma; nirvāṇa and the Tathāgata after death are not described by the four alternatives (MMK 25.17–18).
