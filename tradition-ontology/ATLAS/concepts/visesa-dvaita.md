# Viśeṣa — the 'substitute for difference' (Madhva)

## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): A thing has no real difference from its lasting properties (and God none from his qualities, acts and forms), yet they can be spoken of distinctly by virtue of viśeṣa; with temporary properties there is difference-and-identity. (Distinct from the Vaiśeṣika category viśeṣa.)
