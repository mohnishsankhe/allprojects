## Definitions
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): The Kālikā Purāṇa teaches offering animals to the Goddess, says it brings heaven, liberation and victory, allows substitutes and forbids brāhmaṇas certain offerings and wine (67.1-12; 67.19-23; 67.46-52; 67.113-117). Restricted: summary only. Practised at Kāmākhyā and in Bengal; opposed by Vaiṣṇava and many Śākta dakṣiṇa worshippers.
