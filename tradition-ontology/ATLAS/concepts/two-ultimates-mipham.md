## Definitions
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): The enumerated (rnam grangs pa) ultimate is emptiness as the negation of true existence, an object of reasoning; the non-enumerated (rnam grangs ma yin pa) ultimate is the union beyond the four extremes, realized in meditative equipoise.
