## Definitions

- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: desire and anger recur as a pair to be overcome: one who can withstand here, before release from the body, the surge (vega) that arises from desire and anger is yoked and a happy person (5.23); brahma-nirvāṇa is near on every side for ascetics freed from desire and anger (5.26).
