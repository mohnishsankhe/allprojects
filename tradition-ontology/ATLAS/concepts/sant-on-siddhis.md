## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Riddhi and siddhi are māyā's snares; the yogī's feats and garb do not reach the Lord; the Sant seeks only the Name (the hagiographies nevertheless narrate miracles of protection, as signs of the Lord's care).
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Inner experiences of light and sound are signposts, not the goal; the disciple is not to display or seek powers.
