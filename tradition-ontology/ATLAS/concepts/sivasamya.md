# Liberation as equality with Śiva (śivasāmya)

`cpt:sivasamya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** karma-rebirth

## Names

## Definitions
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): The freed soul becomes 'a Śiva': its own omniscience and omnipotence, covered since beginningless time, are manifested; it is equal to Śiva, not identical with him, and does not perform the five cosmic acts.
