## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The Sāmaveda sings with seven notes — kruṣṭa, prathama, dvitīya, tṛtīya, caturtha, mandra, atisvārya — which the Nāradīya Śikṣā sets against the flute's madhyama, gāndhāra, ṛṣabha, ṣaḍja, dhaivata, niṣāda and pañcama, places in the body from head to heart and shows on the fingers.
