## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The 'knowledge' that liberates is meditation — repeated, unbroken, loving remembrance of the Lord, vivid as seeing — practised daily till death and supported by the seven aids; it is bhakti, and the Lord, pleased, grants himself.
