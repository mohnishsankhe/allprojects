## Names
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): śrīcakra / cakrarāja

## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Four upward Śiva triangles and five downward Śakti triangles interlock around the central point to make forty-three angles, surrounded by eight and sixteen petals, three circles and a square of three lines (SL 11); it arises as the supreme Śakti beholds her own vibrancy (YH 1.9-10) and is ninefold with nine mantras (YH 1.13); it is the Goddess's dwelling (LSN 182) and the body itself (Bhāvanā Upaniṣad).
- [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md): For the Samayins it is worshipped within and is the moon-disc in the crown (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 10).
