## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): One whose vehicle is calm develops jhāna and then insight; one whose vehicle is pure insight (the dry-insight worker) begins with the four elements; both reach the paths, whose jhāna-level is determined by the insight leading to emergence.
- [Pa-Auk tradition (Pa-Auk Tawya)](../lineages/pa-auk.md): Both vehicles require at least access concentration strong enough to see ultimate realities.
- [Mahāsi Sayadaw tradition (the 'new Burmese method')](../lineages/mahasi.md): The pure-insight vehicle through momentary concentration is fully valid.
