## Definitions
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): Born by karma into castes and burned by threefold suffering, the soul becomes pure-minded when karma ripens and Śiva's grace arises; those who bear the inner liṅga do not return; liberation while living belongs to the one who knows the liṅga as liberation; the knower's karma-seeds are burnt, his acts leave no fruit and every act is worship.
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): Liberation is liṅgaikya, union with the liṅga, open to all who wear it.
