## Definitions
- [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md): The Śākta hymns take up the Purāṇic Mahāmāyā: she makes the universe whirl as queen of the supreme Brahman (SL 98).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The Goddess is eternal, the world is her form; she deludes even the wise and, pleased, liberates; she is the cause of all worlds, the primordial Prakṛti beyond Hari and Hara; all knowledges and women are her forms; she alone exists and her powers return into her.
