## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The siddhāntas compute the caturyuga as 4,320,000 solar years divided 4:3:2:1, 71 caturyugas to a manvantara and 1,000 to a kalpa (SS 1.15–21; BSS 1.7–9); Āryabhaṭa alone makes the four quarters equal (Āryabhaṭīya 1.5, 3.9).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Kṛta, Tretā, Dvāpara, Kali of 4,000, 3,000, 2,000, 1,000 divine years with twilights; 12,000 make a divine age; a thousand divine ages a day of Brahmā; seventy-one a Manu-period; creations and dissolutions without number (MDh 1.68–80).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The age-names appear in the Aitareya Brāhmaṇa as states of a man — lying, rising, standing, moving (AB 7.15).
