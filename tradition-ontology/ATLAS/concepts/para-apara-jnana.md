## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Lineage contribution (Upaniṣadic): MuU 1.1.4-5 is the root text — the lower knowledge is the four Vedas and six ancillary sciences, the higher that by which the imperishable is grasped; Nārada knows only mantras, not the self (ChU 7.1.3).
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Knowledge is twofold by eligibility: Śiva's knowledge, revealing the Lord, is higher; the Veda and the rest, revealing the bound soul and its bonds, are lower — different as the night-vision of a man and a cat.
