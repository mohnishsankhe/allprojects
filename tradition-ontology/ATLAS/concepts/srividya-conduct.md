## Definitions
- [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md): No censure of other schools or persons, secrecy toward the unqualified, constant immersion in Śiva-hood, avoidance of the six passions, unsanctioned violence and theft, worship of one guru without doubt, non-possessiveness, action without regard to fruit, fearlessness (PKS 1.14-25); inwardly Śākta, outwardly Śaiva, among people Vaiṣṇava (Kaula Upaniṣad).
