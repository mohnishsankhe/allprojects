## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): By the adjuncts of the ten breaths the fire is fivefold as led by prāṇa — expelling, filling, drying, burning, flooding (with nectar as their essence) — and fivefold as led by apāna — corroding, splitting, agitating, deluding, yawning; it digests the four kinds of food; these ten digits of fire are the goddesses of the inner ten triangles.
