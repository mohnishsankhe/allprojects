## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Minuteness, lightness, greatness, reach, irresistible will, control, lordship and settling things at will — arising from mastery of the elements, with perfection and invulnerability of the body; though able, the yogin does not overturn the order set by the earlier-perfected Lord (3.45, YBh 3.45).
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tamil aṭṭamā citti — aṇimā, makimā, lakimā, karimā, pirātti, pirākāmiyam, īcattuvam, vacittuvam — taught in the Tirumantiram's third tantra and claimed by the Siddhars; Tāyumāṉavar ranks them below stilling the mind and Kaṭuveḷi says they come only by Śiva's action.
