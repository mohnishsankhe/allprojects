# I-am-ness (asmitā) and the sense of 'I'

`cpt:asmita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** self

## Names
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): asmitā

## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The affliction of I-am-ness is the seeming identity of the power of seeing (buddhi) with the seer (2.6); in samprajñāta samādhi, asmitā is awareness of a single self (YBh 1.17); 'asmitā-mātra' is the sixth unparticularized principle (YBh 2.19) and the source of created minds (4.4); in the gaps of discernment the thoughts 'I am', 'mine' arise (YBh 4.27). Bhoja distinguishes asmitā (mere being shining in the inward-turned mind) from ahaṃkāra (apprehending objects as 'I') (RM 1.17).
