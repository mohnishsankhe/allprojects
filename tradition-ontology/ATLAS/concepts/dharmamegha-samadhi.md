# The cloud of dharma (dharmamegha)

**Category:** consciousness-states

## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): For one without self-interest even in the highest reflection, complete discernment brings the samādhi called cloud of dharma (4.29); from it afflictions and karma cease (4.30), knowledge becomes infinite (4.31) and the guṇas complete their sequence (4.32). Vyāsa also names the pure mind's meditation 'dharmamegha' (YBh 1.2).
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Dharmameghā ('cloud of dharma') is the name of the tenth bodhisattva ground, on which the bodhisattva is consecrated by the buddhas' rays and rains the dharma on beings like a cloud.
