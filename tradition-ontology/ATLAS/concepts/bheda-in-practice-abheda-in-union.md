## Definitions
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The SSM assigns difference and non-difference to stages: at the māheśvara stage the worshipper rejects total non-duality because worship rests on the difference of Lord and servant, and non-duality comes only with realization (10.37–43); at aikya, difference and even lordship and boundness are seen to be imagined by māyā, and one who no longer thinks 'I am servant, Śiva master' stands in non-duality (14.8–9; 14.37). The text grounds this in its own principle that its teaching differs by sthala and by qualified aspirant (5.22–23).
