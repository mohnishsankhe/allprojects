## Definitions
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Within the circle all castes are twice-born and equal to Śiva — woman, man, eunuch, caṇḍāla or brāhmaṇa; when it ends they are separate again (Kulārṇava 8.96-97). Restricted: summary only.
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): No caste distinction in giving and drinking within the circle; the Bhairavī circle may be held when occasion arises; the Tattva circle is for those who realise 'all is Brahman' (Mahānirvāṇa 8.151-154; 8.208).
