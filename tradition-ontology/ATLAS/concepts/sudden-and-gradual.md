## Definitions
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): The Dharma has no sudden or gradual; people are sharp or dull; the deluded cultivate gradually, the awakened accord suddenly (Platform Sūtra); Shenxiu's verse is presented as gradual, Huineng's as sudden.
- [Heze school](../lineages/heze.md): Sudden awakening followed by gradual cultivation (Shenhui as read by Zongmi).
- [Guiyang house](../lineages/guiyang.md): Sudden awakening to principle with gradual removal of habit energies (Guishan).
- [Seon (Korean Chan)](../lineages/seon.md): Sudden awakening, gradual cultivation as the path of all sages (Jinul); contested by Seongcheol.
