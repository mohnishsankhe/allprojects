## Definitions
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Violence is prohibited; a rite stated as a means for one who wishes to harm (the Śyena) is harmful, and the Veda does not enjoin the harming itself.
