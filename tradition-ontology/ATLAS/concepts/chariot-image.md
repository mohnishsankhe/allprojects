## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The self is the rider, the body the chariot, the intellect the charioteer, the mind the reins, the senses the horses and their objects the roads; with understanding and a yoked mind the senses are like good horses and one reaches Viṣṇu's highest step; without it one goes on in saṃsāra (KU 1.3.3-9).
