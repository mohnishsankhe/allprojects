## Definitions
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): 'Rasa arises from the union of determinants, consequents and transient states' (NŚ 6). Lollaṭa: intensification in the character; Śaṅkuka: inference in the actor; Bhaṭṭa Nāyaka: enjoyment through bhāvakatva and bhojakatva; Abhinavagupta: manifestation and relish in the spectator — the view adopted by Mammaṭa and Viśvanātha.
