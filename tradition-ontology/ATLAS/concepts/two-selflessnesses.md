# The two selflessnesses

## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): No self of persons and no self of dharmas.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): Selflessness of persons (removing the afflictive obscuration) and selflessness of dharmas (removing the cognitive obscuration); the first is entered through the teaching of aggregates and fields, the second through cognition-only.
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Selflessness of persons and of dharmas: the Buddhas taught self, no-self and neither (MMK 18.6); the aggregates, dharmas and the Tathāgata are all without own-nature.
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Both are realized by śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas as well as bodhisattvas (Candrakīrti, MAbh on 1.8).
