## Definitions
- `lin:jagannatha`: Fish, tortoise, boar, man-lion, dwarf, Paraśurāma, Rāma, Balarāma, the Buddha (who in compassion condemned animal sacrifice) and Kalki — all forms taken by Kṛṣṇa, to whom the hymn's closing verse bows as the bearer of the ten.
