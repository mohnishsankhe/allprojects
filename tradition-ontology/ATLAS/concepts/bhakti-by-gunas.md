## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Tāmasa (with violence, pride, anger in view), rājasa (for objects, fame, power), sāttvika (to remove karma, as duty) and nirguṇa devotion that flows unbroken to the Lord (BhP 3.29.7-12).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Pure devotion is nirguṇa — beyond the three guṇas — unmotivated and uninterrupted like the Gaṅgā flowing to the sea (BhP 3.29.11–12); devotion in the modes is mixed.
