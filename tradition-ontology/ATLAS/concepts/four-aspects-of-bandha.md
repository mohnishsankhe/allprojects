## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Nature (prakṛti), duration (sthiti), intensity/fruition (anubhava) and quantity (pradeśa); nature is the eight karma types (8.4), duration is given for each (8.14-8.20), fruition is experiencing and follows the name (8.21-8.22), quantity concerns the particles in all the soul's space-points (8.24).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Bondage is of nature (which karma), duration (how long), intensity (how strong its fruit) and quantity (how many particles) (TS 8.3); activity determines nature and quantity, passion determines duration and intensity (commentaries).
