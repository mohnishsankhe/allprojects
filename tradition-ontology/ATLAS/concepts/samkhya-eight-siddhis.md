## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Reasoning, hearing, study, the three destructions of suffering, gaining friends and giving are the eight accomplishments conducive to knowledge (SK 51) — not supernormal powers (those are aiśvarya). Gauḍapāda explains each as a route to knowledge of the principles (reflection, hearing, study, approaching a teacher, a knowing friend, serving the venerable); Vācaspati is recalled as making the three destructions primary and the other five their means in sequence (low confidence).
