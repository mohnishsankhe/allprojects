## Definitions
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): A teacher's word is accepted not out of respect but after examination by perception and inference, as gold is tested; the Buddha himself is shown to be authoritative by proof, and scripture is relied on only for what perception and inference cannot reach and what they do not contradict.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The bodhisattva vow is to be received from a teacher who holds the vow, is learned and capable.
