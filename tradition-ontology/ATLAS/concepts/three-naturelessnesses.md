## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Of characteristic, of arising, and ultimate — the intent behind 'all dharmas lack own-being'.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The Prajñāpāramitā's 'all dharmas are without own-nature' was spoken with the three natures in view: the imagined lacks own-characteristic, the dependent lacks self-arising, the perfected is the ultimate naturelessness.
