# Delight in the self (ātmarati): the one for whom nothing remains to be done

`cpt:atmarati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** consciousness-states

## Names
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): ātmarati

## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The person who delights in the self alone, is satisfied and content in the self, has nothing that must be done, no purpose in doing or not doing, and depends on no being for any purpose (3.17–18). Commentators divide on whether this exempts such a person from action; the next verse (3.19) still enjoins unattached action.
