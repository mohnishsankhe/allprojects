# Analytical and placement meditation

## Names

## Definitions
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): Two modes of meditation, both necessary: analytical (dpyad sgom) investigates with discernment and is required for faith, renunciation, bodhicitta and emptiness; placement ('jog sgom) holds the mind one-pointedly; in insight analysis itself induces pliancy.
