## Definitions
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The Pāśupata initiate leaves the rules of his former āśrama for the restraints of a state beyond the four āśramas; open to brahmins of any āśrama.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Yāmuna objects that Śaivas devise āśramas outside the Vedic order.
