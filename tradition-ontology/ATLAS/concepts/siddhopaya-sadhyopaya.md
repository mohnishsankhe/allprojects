## Definitions
- [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md): The Lord is the already-existent means (siddhopāya); bhakti-yoga or prapatti is the means to be performed (sādhyopāya) which occasions his grace.
- [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md): The Lord alone is the means; there is no sādhyopāya on the soul's side in the path of surrender.
