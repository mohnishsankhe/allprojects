## Definitions
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Dharma, artha and kāma, whose combination is the good (MDh 2.224), with mokṣa sought after the debts are paid (MDh 6.35–37); dharmaśāstra prevails over arthaśāstra in conflict (YS 2.21).
- [Arthaśāstra (the science of statecraft and wealth)](../lineages/arthasastra.md): Artha is foremost, dharma and kāma being rooted in it (Arthaśāstra 1.7.6–7).
- [Kāmaśāstra (the science of love and pleasure)](../lineages/kamasastra.md): The three pursued over a hundred-year life without mutual harm; dharma, artha, kāma in descending weight; mokṣa in old age (Kāmasūtra 1.2).

## Relations (interpretation layer)

_Notes: The early texts speak of three aims (trivarga); mokṣa as a fourth aim (caturvarga) is systematized later._
