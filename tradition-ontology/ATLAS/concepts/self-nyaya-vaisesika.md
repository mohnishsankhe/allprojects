# The self as a substance with qualities

**Category:** self

## Names

- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): ātman

## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): An eternal, all-pervading substance distinct from body, senses, mind and cognition; the one who sees and touches the same thing, recognizes, remembers and is born with memories (NS 3.1.1–24, 3.2.40); its qualities (cognition, desire, aversion, effort, pleasure, pain) are adventitious.
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): One of the nine substances, all-pervading (VS 7.1.29), known by marks such as breath, eye-movements, life and desire (VS 3.2.4 C), and by the distinctive use of 'I' (VS 3.2.9–13 C); perceived in yoga (VS 9.13).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): The substrate of cognition, of two kinds: the Lord and the individual selves (Tarkasaṅgraha).
