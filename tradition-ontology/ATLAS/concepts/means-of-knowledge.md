## Definitions
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): Six: perception, inference, verbal testimony, comparison, postulation and non-apprehension; a means of knowledge apprehends the previously unapprehended and is free from causal defect and from any sublating cognition.
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): Five: perception, inference, verbal testimony, comparison and postulation; every experience other than memory is a means of knowledge.
