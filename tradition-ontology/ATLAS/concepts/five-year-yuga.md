## Definitions
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): A cycle of five years (saṃvatsara, parivatsara, idāvatsara, anuvatsara, idvatsara) under Prajāpati, beginning with sun and moon together at Śraviṣṭhā in Māgha, with intercalary months to reconcile lunar and solar time.
