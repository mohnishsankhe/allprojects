## Definitions
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Mirage, smoke, fireflies, lamp and a cloudless sky arise when the mind enters 'the vajra of cessation' (GST 18.146–149).
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): In the Vajra Verses, when elements gather in meditation: earth into water — mirage; water into fire — smoke; fire into wind — fireflies; wind into consciousness — a burning lamp; consciousness into clear light — like a cloudless sky.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The eight stages of dying in the Guhyasamāja-based Gelug presentation: mirage, smoke, fireflies, flickering flame, then the white appearance, red increase, black near-attainment and the clear light of death — also re-enacted in completion-stage practice.
