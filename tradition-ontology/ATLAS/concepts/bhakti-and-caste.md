## Definitions
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): Before God birth counts for nothing: saints of every caste are equally revered; outward 'crookedness' of birth does not touch inner devotion; knowing the Veda's meaning belongs to the devotee, not to birth. The saints also record the exclusion they suffered.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): Rāmānanda's disciples include a weaver, a cobbler, a barber, a Jāṭ farmer and women (Bhaktamāl); Tulsī scorns caste-talk for himself as Rāma's slave.
- [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md): Refuge is given to every caste and people, including tribes and Muslims; in the name there is no caste.
- [Mahānubhāva panth](../lineages/mahanubhava.md): Caste does not count among seekers of Parameśvara; women and all castes may renounce.
- [Unaffiliated regional bhakti poets (classificatory grouping)](../lineages/regional-bhakti-poets.md): For high and low the Lord is one (Annamācārya).
