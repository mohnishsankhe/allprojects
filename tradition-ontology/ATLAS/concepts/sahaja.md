## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The natural state of the mind free of effort and elaboration; synonym of rājayoga and samādhi (HYP 4.4); the measure of real knowledge — until it arises all talk is hypocrisy (HYP 4.114).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The innate principle in the heart, moving by itself, delighting in others as in oneself (KJN 17.5, 17.18–26); the innate path abiding in samarasa, higher than the 'made' kuṇḍalī (Akulavīra B.43); once the Kaula is known the mind is not to be restrained (KJN 7.26).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The natural state (sahajāvasthā), a synonym of samādhi (HYP 4.4), which arises spontaneously when śakti has awakened and action is abandoned (4.11) but is hard to reach without a true guru's compassion (4.9); in paricaya 'natural bliss' arises (4.75); GS 7.17 equates it with rājayoga and unmanī.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The natural state in which the Lord is realized without forced yoga: eyes open, every act worship; Kabīr warns that everyone says 'sahaj' and no one knows it.
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): The innate beyond the two, known only by one who has crossed the darkness; the goal of the couple's discipline.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): The sahaj path and the sahaj Man, found in the body under the guru.
- [Kartābhajā](../lineages/kartabhaja.md): The sahaja religion (satya-dharma) revealed by Āulcānd.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): lhan cig skyes pa, the co-emergent: the dharmakāya inseparable from mind, appearances and thoughts; 'co-emergent wisdom, clarity-emptiness free of grasping' (Karṇatantravajrapada).
