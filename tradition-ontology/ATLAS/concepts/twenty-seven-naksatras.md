## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The moon's path is divided into 27 asterisms of 13°20′ (28 with Abhijit in muhūrta), each with a presiding deity; the Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa lists them from Kṛttikā (Agni) to Bharaṇī (Yama), later texts from Aśvinī (the Aśvins) to Revatī (Pūṣan); the Lord divided himself into them (SS 12.25).
- [Mūlasarvāstivāda](../lineages/mulasarvastivada.md): The Śārdūlakarṇāvadāna expounds the asterisms, their deities and the fortunes of those born under them, in the mouth of the outcaste king Triśaṅku.
