## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Purity of view, humility, faultless vows, constant knowledge, fear of saṃsāra, giving and austerity according to capacity, tending ascetics, service, devotion to arhats, teachers, the learned and scripture, the essential duties, promoting the path and love for co-religionists (TS 6.24); Śvetāmbara texts give twenty sthānakas.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Sixteen (ṣoḍaśa-kāraṇa-bhāvanā): purity of faith, humility, faultless vows, constant knowledge, longing for release, giving and austerity according to strength, the monks' composure and service, devotion to Arhats, teachers, the learned and scripture, the obligatory duties, spreading the path and affection for fellow believers.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Twenty 'places' (vīsa-sthānaka), from devotion to the Arhats to the propagation of the teaching (list from the Jñātādharmakathā and niryukti; low confidence on its items).
