## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Right view, right knowledge and right conduct together are the path to liberation (TS 1.1); when a later one is present the earlier ones are necessarily present (Tattvārthabhāṣya 1.1); knowledge without conduct and conduct without knowledge are both useless, like the lame and the blind in a burning forest (Rājavārttika 1.1).
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): In the ultimate sense the three jewels are the self itself — the self knowing, seeing and abiding in the self (Kundakunda; YŚ 4.2).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Yoga is the three jewels (YŚ 1.15; Yogaśataka 2).
