# Viṣṇu partless and with parts (Vaikhānasa)

## Definitions
- [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md): The supreme Brahman Nārāyaṇa has a subtle, imperishable, partless form, stood for by the fixed image, and a gross form with parts, cause of all, stood for by the movable image that receives full worship.
