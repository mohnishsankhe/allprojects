# Absorption into prakṛti (prakṛtilaya)

`cpt:prakrtilaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** karma-rebirth

## Names
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): prakṛtilaya

## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Dispassion leads to absorption into prakṛti (SK 45) — not liberation: those who take prakṛti or its evolutes for the self dissolve into them (Gauḍapāda on SK 48, 'darkness') and rise again like a diver (SS 3.54); the Kramadīpikā calls it 'bondage to prakṛti'. The one who rises may become an all-knowing lord in the next creation (SS 3.55–57).
