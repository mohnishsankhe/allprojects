## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Four alternatives — is, is not, both, neither — none of which is asserted of the ultimate (MMK 22.11; 25.17–18); in 18.8 the four statements form the Buddhas' (graded) instruction.
