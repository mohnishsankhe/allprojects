## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Lower dispassion is the consciousness of mastery of one free of thirst for seen and scriptural objects (1.15); higher dispassion is freedom from thirst for the guṇas, from discernment of the puruṣa — the utmost limit of knowledge, inseparable from kaivalya (1.16, YBh 1.16); dispassion even toward the highest powers yields kaivalya (3.50). Vācaspati divides the lower into four stages (see pth:vacaspati-four-stages-of-vairagya).
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): Worldly happiness is a barley grain, sorrow a mountain; the avadhūta's teachers teach detachment.
- [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md): No one in the world is wholly happy; inquire and see.
