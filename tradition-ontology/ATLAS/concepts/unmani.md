# Unmanī / manonmanī — the state beyond mind

**Category:** consciousness-states

## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The state beyond mind: it cannot arise while the channels are impure (HYP 2.4); it is the steadiness of mind when breath moves in the middle (2.42); a synonym of samādhi and rājayoga (4.3–4); through it duality is not perceived (4.61) and the body becomes like a log (4.106); it sprouts from tattva, haṭha and detachment (4.104).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The state in which mind has dissolved (unmanībhāva); reached by nāda and tāraka; beyond merit and sin; the supreme state of Viṣṇu (Amṛtabindu 4; Nādabindu 48; Yogaśikhā 6.61; Maṇḍalabrāhmaṇa).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Unmanī is a synonym of samādhi and rājayoga (HYP 4.3); the mind made void of void, motionless, not roused even by drums (KJN 14.83–85).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): One whose mind is beyond mind is liberated here (KJN 13.5); placing the mind in the heart until unmanī one turns back death (6.27); the 'great pervasion' in which one neither hears nor sees (17.16); the fourth mind beyond the three guṇas (Jñānakārikā 2.8).
