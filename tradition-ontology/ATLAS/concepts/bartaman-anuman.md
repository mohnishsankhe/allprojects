# The present and the inferred (bartamān and anumān)

## Definitions
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): Knowledge is of the present, verifiable in the living body (bartamān); scripture, heaven and the gods of the books are inference and hearsay (anumān).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Kabīr's parallel stance: 'I speak of what I have seen with my eyes, you of what is written on paper'.
