## Definitions
- [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md): Kalā, vidyā, rāga, kāla and niyati, which with māyā limit universal agency, knowledge, fullness, eternity and pervasion into the individual's 'only a little, only this, only now, only here' (Paramārthasāra 16-17).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Śiva clothed in body and coverings is the jīva; without coverings he is Paraśiva (PKS 1.5).
