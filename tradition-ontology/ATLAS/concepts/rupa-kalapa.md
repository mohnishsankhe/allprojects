# Material groups (rūpa-kalāpa)

`cpt:rupa-kalapa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** matter-qualities

## Names

## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Matter occurs in inseparable groups (at least the eight: four elements, colour, odour, flavour, nutritive essence) which arise and cease together.
- [Pa-Auk tradition (Pa-Auk Tawya)](../lineages/pa-auk.md): Seen directly with the light of deep concentration and analysed into their elements before insight.
- [U Ba Khin – S. N. Goenka tradition (Sayagyi U Ba Khin lineage)](../lineages/u-ba-khin-goenka.md): Their extremely rapid arising and dissolution is felt in meditation as subtle vibration.
