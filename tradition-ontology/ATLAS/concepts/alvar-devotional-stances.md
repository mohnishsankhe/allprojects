## Definitions
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The Āḻvārs approach the Lord as servant (aṭimai, Nammāḻvār), as mother of the child (Periyāḻvār as Yaśodā; Kulaśekhara as Devakī and Kausalyā), as grieving father (Kulaśekhara as Daśaratha), as cowherd companion (the Tiruppāvai girls), as bride and lover (Āṇṭāḷ; Parāṅkuśa- and Parakāla-nāyakī), and as the one who prays for the Lord's own safety (Periyāḻvār's pallāṇṭu).
