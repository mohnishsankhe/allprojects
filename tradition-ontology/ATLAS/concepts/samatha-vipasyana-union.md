# The union of calm abiding and insight

`cpt:samatha-vipasyana-union` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** consciousness-states

## Names

## Definitions
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): Calm abiding brings the superknowledges, but only the yoga of the perfection of wisdom joined with method exhausts the obscurations.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): Achieved when analysis of emptiness itself induces pliancy and stability; like a bright, unflickering lamp.
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Insight joined with calm destroys the afflictions, so calm should be sought first (BCA 8.4).
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): Calm stabilizes the mind; insight arises from analysis; the two are balanced by removing laxity and excitement (Bhāvanākrama).
