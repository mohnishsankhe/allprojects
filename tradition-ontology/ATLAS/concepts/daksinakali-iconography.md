## Definitions
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): Four-armed, terrible-faced, with loose hair and garland of heads, sword and severed head in the left hands, fearlessness and boon in the right, on Śiva as a corpse, united with Mahākāla, smiling (Kālī Tantra as quoted in Kṛṣṇānanda's Tantrasāra); the Karpūrādistotra gives the same hands (v. 4). Bengali legend ascribes the fixing of this form to Kṛṣṇānanda.
