## Definitions
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Grace is the last of Lakṣmī's (the Lord's) five acts — concealment, creation, maintenance, dissolution, grace.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Aruḷ is Śiva's own power, always with the soul like light with the eye, embodied as the guru; liberation is by grace alone, the soul's effort being itself grace-enabled.
- [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md): The Lord's grace protects in every danger and 'took me as his own' — the devotee's whole hope.
