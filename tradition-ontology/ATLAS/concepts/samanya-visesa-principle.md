# Increase by the similar, decrease by the dissimilar

`cpt:samanya-visesa-principle` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** matter-qualities

## Names

## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): At all times the general causes increase and the particular decrease (Ca Sū 1.44); hence the doṣas are allayed by substances of opposite qualities (Sū 1.59-62) and all dhātus increase by the similar (AHS Sū 1.14).
