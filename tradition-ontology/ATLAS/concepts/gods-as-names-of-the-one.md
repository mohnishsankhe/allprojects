## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Several hymns hold that the many gods are names or forms of one: 'they call it Indra, Mitra, Varuṇa, Agni … the One Being the inspired speak of in many ways' (RV 1.164.46); the poets shape the one bird into many with words (10.114.5); 'great is the one asura-ship of the gods' (3.55); the one name-giver of the gods (10.82.3); 'that is Agni, that the Sun … that brahman' (VS 32.1). Yāska: the one Self of the deity is praised in many ways, the other gods are its limbs (Nirukta 7.4). The liturgy nonetheless addresses each god as a distinct recipient.
