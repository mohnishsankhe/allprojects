## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Death comes by the fall of bindu, life by holding it (HYP 3.88); semen moved by udāna is the poison-line of death (AmŚ 38); in the nectar-filled body the seed flows upward (GŚ 65); bindu arises from mind (Candrāvalokana).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The haṭha texts make the preservation of bindu central: 'death by the fall of bindu, life by holding bindu' (HYP 3.88; DYŚ 143); bindu depends on mind, life on bindu (HYP 3.90); steadiness of mind makes breath and bindu steady (4.28); loss of bindu through women is the great danger of the ghaṭa stage (DYŚ 77–80); khecarī and vajrolī (restricted) are taught to retain or draw it up.
