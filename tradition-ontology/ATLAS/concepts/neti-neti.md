## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Lineage contribution (Upaniṣadic): the instruction (ādeśa) about brahman — 'not so, not so', for there is nothing beyond this 'not so' (BAU 2.3.6) — and the description of the self as ungraspable, indestructible, unattached, unbound (BAU 3.9.26, 4.2.4, 4.4.22, 4.5.15); akin to the negations of the imperishable (BAU 3.8.8), of the fourth (MāU 7) and 'from which words turn back' (TU 2.4).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): In the Sāṃkhya Sūtra the practice of the principles proceeds by giving up each with 'not this, not this', whence discrimination (3.76) — the renunciation of identification with each principle, as in the knowledge 'I am not, not mine, not I' (SK 64).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Brahman is taught by negating every specification; negation terminates in Brahman, the 'reality of the real', which is not negated.
