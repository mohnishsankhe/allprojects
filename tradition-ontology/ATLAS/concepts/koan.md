## Definitions
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): Old cases used for commentary, verse and contemplation; the Wumen guan, Blue Cliff Record and Book of Equanimity collect them.
- [Yangqi branch (of the Linji house)](../lineages/yangqi.md): Dahui's contemplation of the critical phrase of a case, with the doubt kept alive in daily life.
- [Rinzai Zen](../lineages/rinzai.md): A graded kōan curriculum under a teacher (Hakuin's line).
- [Sōtō Zen](../lineages/soto.md): Dōgen collected and commented on kōans but taught just sitting; the realized kōan (genjōkōan) is reality itself.
