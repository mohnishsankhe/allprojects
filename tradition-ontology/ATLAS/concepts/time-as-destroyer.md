## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): 'I am Time, the destroyer of worlds' (BhG 11.32); 'Time takes all and gives all' (MBh 12.217.25); in Vidura's parable time is the serpent, days and nights the rats (11.5–6); all accumulations end in exhaustion (Rāmāyaṇa 2.105.16).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: The Lord declares: 'I am Time, grown mighty, the maker of the destruction of worlds, engaged here in drawing in the worlds'; even without Arjuna the opposing warriors will not survive (11.32). They are already slain by him; Arjuna is to be merely the instrument (11.33–34). Earlier he names himself time among reckoners (10.30), imperishable time (10.33) and all-seizing death (10.34); Arjuna sees the warriors enter the form's mouths like rivers into the sea and moths into a flame (11.26–30).
