# Īśvara in Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika

## Names

- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Parameśvara / Īśvara

## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): A distinct self with qualities — free of demerit, false knowledge and heedlessness; with merit, knowledge and concentration; with eightfold lordship — who assists human effort and makes it fruitful, sets karma and the elements in motion, is like a reliable person and a father to beings (NS 4.1.19–21; NBh 4.1.21); the all-knowing maker proved by inference (NK 5.1; Gaṅgeśa); worshipped by all schools under different names (NK stabaka 1).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Maheśvara, by whose will creation and dissolution occur; saluted as the cause at the start of the Padārthadharmasaṅgraha. The Vaiśeṣika Sūtra itself does not name Īśvara.
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): The one omniscient supreme self with eternal cognition, desire and effort, author of the Veda, whose will fixes word-meanings (Tarkasaṅgraha).
