## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): For yogins samādhi is preceded by faith, energy, mindfulness, samādhi and insight (YS 1.20); Vyāsa: each arises from the previous — faith (serenity of mind, protecting the yogin like a kind mother), energy, mindfulness, concentration of the unagitated mind, and the discernment of insight by which one knows the thing as it is (YBh 1.20).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The means by which samādhi arises for yogins (1.20): faith (serenity of mind, protecting like a mother), from which energy, from which mindfulness, with which the mind is concentrated, to which discerning insight comes; from its practice and dispassion, asamprajñāta (YBh 1.20).
