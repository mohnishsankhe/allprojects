# Divine grace (prasāda)

**Category:** karma-rebirth

## Names
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): prasāda

## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Dvaita: liberation comes only by Viṣṇu's grace (his 'choosing'); knowledge and devotion win it; it is graded, and it reaches lower beings through the higher (Vāyu and the gods).
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The Lord's aruḷ: he graciously gave the Āḻvār knowledge-and-love free of delusion; the Lord came to Tiruppāṇ and Tirumaṅkai unasked; those who recite the Tiruppāvai receive his grace everywhere.
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Devotion is gained chiefly by the grace of great souls or a drop of the Lord's grace; even the company of the great is gained by his grace alone.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Lineage contribution (Upaniṣadic): the self is attained by the one whom it chooses (KU 1.2.23; MuU 3.2.3); 'by the grace of the creator' one sees the Lord (ŚU 3.20; KU 1.2.20 reads 'serenity of the constituents'); favoured by him the goose gains immortality (ŚU 1.6); Śvetāśvatara knew brahman by God's grace (ŚU 6.21); refuge in God (ŚU 6.18).
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): The saints' company and discernment come only by Rāma's grace; he destroys the sins of ten million births the moment a being turns to him.
- [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md): As Hari keeps me, so I remain; nothing is achieved by one's own effort.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The Lord raises the lowly by his own mercy; Rām gives to all (Malūkdās); the guru's grace awakens sahaj.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): Take me across, O merciful one — I have no boat or fare.
