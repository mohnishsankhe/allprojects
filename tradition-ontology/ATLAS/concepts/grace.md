# Divine grace (prasāda)

## Names
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): prasāda

## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Dvaita: liberation comes only by Viṣṇu's grace (his 'choosing'); knowledge and devotion win it; it is graded, and it reaches lower beings through the higher (Vāyu and the gods).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Lineage contribution (Upaniṣadic): the self is attained by the one whom it chooses (KU 1.2.23; MuU 3.2.3); 'by the grace of the creator' one sees the Lord (ŚU 3.20; KU 1.2.20 reads 'serenity of the constituents'); favoured by him the goose gains immortality (ŚU 1.6); Śvetāśvatara knew brahman by God's grace (ŚU 6.21); refuge in God (ŚU 6.18).
