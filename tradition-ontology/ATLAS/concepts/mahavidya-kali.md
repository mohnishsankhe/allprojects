## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Dark, naked, four-armed — sword and freshly severed head in the left hands, fear-dispelling and boon-giving gestures in the right — with a garland of heads, a girdle of hands, loose hair, lolling tongue and three eyes, standing on Śiva as a corpse in the cremation ground (Kālī Tantra dhyāna in the Tantrasāra). Teaching: she devours Time (Mahānirvāṇa 4.31-32); without her Śiva is a corpse (Toḍala 1.25); in the Toḍala she became Kṛṣṇa (10.11).
