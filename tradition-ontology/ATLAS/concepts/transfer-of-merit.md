# Rejoicing in and dedicating merit

## Names

## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Merit is rejoiced in and turned toward awakening for all, without apprehension of the three spheres — otherwise the dedication is 'poisoned'.
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): The fifth gate: never abandoning suffering beings, making transfer foremost; of two aspects, going and returning.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): The transfer is Amida's, not the practitioner's: the nenbutsu is 'non-transferring' practice.
