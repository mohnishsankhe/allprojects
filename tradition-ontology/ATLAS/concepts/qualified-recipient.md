## Definitions
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): One who does not revile scripture, has faith and devotion to Lakṣmī and Nārāyaṇa, and knows ritual, other tantras, yoga, Veda and Vedāṅga, Paśupati's doctrine, logic and all spiritual śāstras.
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Those fit for ritual, knowledge or conduct are joined to Śiva accordingly; the incapable receive initiation without the observances.
