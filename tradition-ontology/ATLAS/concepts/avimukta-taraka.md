## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Avimukta (Kāśī) is where Rudra gives the dying the tāraka — the mantra that carries across — so that they are freed; it is also located in the body between brows and nose (Jābāla 1–2; Muktikā 1.19–21; Rāmottaratāpanī; Tārasāra). The tāraka is identified variously as Oṃ, the Rāma mantra or the Nārāyaṇa mantra.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): Śiva frees those dying at Kāśī by the power of Rāma's name.
