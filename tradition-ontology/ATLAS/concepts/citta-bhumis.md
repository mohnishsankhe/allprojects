## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Scattered (kṣipta), stupefied (mūḍha), distracted (vikṣipta), one-pointed (ekāgra) and stilled (niruddha) (YBh 1.1). Samādhi in a distracted mind, subordinate to distraction, does not count as yoga; in the one-pointed mind the samādhi that illumines the real object, wears away the afflictions and loosens karma's bonds is samprajñāta yoga; in the stilling of all activities it is asamprajñāta (YBh 1.1).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Scattered, stupefied, distracted, one-pointed and stilled (YBh 1.1); samādhi in the distracted mind is not yoga; samprajñāta occurs in the one-pointed, asamprajñāta in the stilled mind.
