## Definitions
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): The Goddess of the yoni-pīṭha at Kāmagiri in Kāmarūpa (Kālikā Purāṇa 18.42, 18.49), 'the great vidyā in the form of the yoni' who is all four aims of life (Kāmākhyā Tantra 1.4, 1.9-13). The temple on Nīlācala was rebuilt by the Koch king Naranārāyaṇa in 1565 (historical metadata, moderate confidence).
