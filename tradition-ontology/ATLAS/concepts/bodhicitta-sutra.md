## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The resolve for buddhahood for all beings; its nature is luminous, a 'no-thought'; at its first arising perfect awakening is already attained (Avataṃsaka); even the lowest aspirants to Sukhāvatī must arouse it.
