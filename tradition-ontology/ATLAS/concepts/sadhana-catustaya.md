## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Discrimination, dispassion, the six virtues (calm, restraint, withdrawal, forbearance, faith, concentration) and longing for liberation — prerequisites for study of the Upaniṣads (Muktikā prose; Varāha 2).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Discrimination of the eternal and non-eternal, dispassion, the six virtues, and longing for liberation — the prerequisites of the inquiry into Brahman.
