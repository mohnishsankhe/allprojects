# Learning the word of the Buddha in one's own language

`cpt:own-language-rule` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** sound-language

## Names

## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The Buddha refuses to have his word put into Vedic metre (chandas) and allows it to be learned 'in one's own language' (Vin Cv 5.33); the commentary reads this as the Buddha's own language, Māgadhī, identified by the tradition with Pali.
