## Definitions
- [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md): Those who reach the Brahma-world by meditation fulfil desires by will alone, but have no power over the world's creation and are equal to the Lord only in enjoyment (BS 4.4.8, 4.4.17, 4.4.21).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Powers belong to the realm of māyā and to saguṇa meditation; they are not liberation; the knower does not relish even the bliss of absorption (GK 3.45).
