## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The seven visible planets — Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn — with the two nodes Rāhu and Ketu make the nine grahas; they are forms of Janārdana giving the fruits of karma, and from them the avatāras are born (BPHS 2.3–7); their synonyms include Greek-derived names (BJ 2.2–3); they are worshipped together in the planetary sacrifice (YājñSm 1.295–308; BPHS 84).
