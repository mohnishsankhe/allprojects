# The two aspects of the true Dharma and its decline

`cpt:decline-of-the-dharma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** cosmology-time

## Names

## Definitions
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The true Dharma is scripture and realization, upheld by those who teach and practise; since the Teacher has passed, the teaching is at its last breath and seekers must not be heedless.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): In the 'last five hundred years' of the true dharma's destruction, some will still have faith in and keep these sūtras; the Lotus's peaceful practices are for the latter age.
