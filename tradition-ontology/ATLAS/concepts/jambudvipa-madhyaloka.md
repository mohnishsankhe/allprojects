## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Concentric continents and oceans, each double the last, around round Jambūdvīpa with Meru at its navel; its seven regions (Bharata … Airāvata) divided by six ranges; humans live only within the Mānuṣottara range (2.5 continents); lands of action (Bharata, Airāvata, Videha) and lands of enjoyment (TS 3.7–39).
