## Definitions
- [Siddha medicine (cittā maruttuvam)](../lineages/siddha-medicine.md): Vaḷi (vātam), aḻal (pittam) and aiyam (kapam), the 'three reckoned by the learned beginning with wind', whose excess or deficiency causes disease (Kuṟaḷ 941). Each has subdivisions (vātam 10 — the ten winds; pittam 5; kapam 5). The Tirumantiram's rejuvenating practice removes aiyam, vātam and pittam at their times of day.
