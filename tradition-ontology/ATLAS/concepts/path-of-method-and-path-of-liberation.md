## Definitions
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): The Kagyu path has two streams: the path of method (thabs lam) — the six yogas on the basis of empowerment — and the path of liberation (grol lam) — Mahāmudrā; they are practised together, inner heat being 'the main trunk' and Mahāmudrā the 'illuminator of wisdom'.
