# Brahman in its early sense

`cpt:brahman-early-sense` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ultimate

## Names
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): brahman; jyeṣṭha brahman

## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): In the Ṛgveda brahman is the potent formulation and prayer (its lord is Bṛhaspati); in the Atharvaveda and White Yajurveda it becomes the name of the highest: 'homage to the highest Brahman' (AVŚ 10.8.1), 'those who know Brahman in man know the Supreme' (10.7.17), Brahman entered the golden city of the body (10.2.33), 'that is brahman' (VS 32.1), 'Oṃ, the void is brahman' (VS 40.17).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Brahman is the sacred formulation and Veda, and also the first principle: 'in the beginning this was Brahman', which descended into the worlds by name and form (ŚB 11.2.3); 'Brahman the self-existent' heads the teacher-lineages (ŚB 10.6.5.9), offers itself into all beings (ŚB 13.7.1.1), and as a 'great wonder' creates a second god from itself (GB 1.1.1).
