## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The highest dharma is that which produces unmotivated, uninterrupted devotion to the Lord (BhP 1.2.6); devotion beginning with the name is the supreme dharma known to the mahājanas (6.3.22); neither yoga nor Sāṃkhya wins the Lord as devotion does (11.14.20).
