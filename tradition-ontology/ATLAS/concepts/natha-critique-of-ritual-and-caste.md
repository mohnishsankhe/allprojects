## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): For one who knows the supreme there is no caste, sacrifice, fasting, pilgrimage, image or rite (Akulavīra A.42–48, B.93–96); no lunar day or fast is prescribed — rely on knowledge alone (KJN 21.10); the yogī meditates where there is neither temple nor mosque (Gorakh Bānī); the external liṅga is rejected (KJN 3.14–18).
