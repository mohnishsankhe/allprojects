# Existence of dharmas in the three times (sarvāstivāda)

**Category:** ultimate

## Definitions
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Dharmas of past, present and future all exist as real entities; proven by scripture, by consciousness arising from two, by consciousness requiring an existent object, and by the results of past karma; the times differ only by activity.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): Only the present exists; the conditioned arises having not been and ceases having been; results come from the transformed series.
- [Kāśyapīya](../lineages/kasyapiya.md): Only unripened past karma exists of the past (Vibhajyavāda position).
- [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md): Past and future do not exist as entities (Vasumitra).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Rejected in Kathāvatthu 1.6–1.8.
