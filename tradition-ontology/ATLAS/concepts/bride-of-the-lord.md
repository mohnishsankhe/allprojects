# The devotee as bride of the Lord (the bridal mood)

## Names

## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Kabīr sings as the bride whose husband, King Rām, has come home, with body as altar and mind as priest; the pain of separation (birah) is the bride's longing; the devotee as faithful wife (pativratā) of the one Lord.
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): The madhura mood realized in the human pair, each seeing the other's svarūpa as Rādhā or Kṛṣṇa (restricted context; doctrine only).
