## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The lists agree on eighteen but differ: VP 3.6.21-23 and BhP 12.7.23-24 name the Śiva (Śaiva); Matsya 53 and the Nārada's index name the Vāyu; KūP 1.1.13-15 names both, taking the 'Vāyavīya' as the Brahmāṇḍa.
