## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The Buddha taught it in the Tāvatiṃsa heaven, gave the method to Sāriputta daily, and Sāriputta taught it; it was recited at the First Council and preserved by reciters; the Kathāvatthu was expanded by Moggaliputta Tissa at the Third Council from the Buddha's outline.
