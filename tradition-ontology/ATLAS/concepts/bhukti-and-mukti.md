## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The Goddess's tantra alone accomplishes all the aims of life (SL 31); her worshipper enjoys Sarasvatī and Lakṣmī and, freed of bonds, tastes the supreme Brahman (SL 99); saubhāgya names this double blessing.
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Śiva's scriptures in all four pādas are means to enjoyment (powers, higher worlds) and to liberation; initiates are bubhukṣu or mumukṣu accordingly.
