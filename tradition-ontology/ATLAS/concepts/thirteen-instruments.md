# The thirteen instruments (karaṇa)

## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The instrument is thirteenfold — buddhi, ahaṃkāra, manas (internal) and the ten capacities (external); it seizes, holds and illuminates (SK 32–33). The internal works in three times, the external in the present; the internal is the door-keeper, the external the doors (SK 33, 35). The organs act by one another's impulse, for puruṣa's purpose alone (SK 31), and like lamps present that purpose to buddhi (SK 36).
