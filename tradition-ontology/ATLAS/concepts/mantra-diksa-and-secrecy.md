## Definitions
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): A mantra must be received from a qualified guru — pure, self-controlled, learned in the Āgamas and Veda, devoted to japa, calm, following yoga — by a qualified disciple who serves the guru and is tested one to four years according to class (ŚT 2.141–153); the mantra is kept secret (gopana is the tenth purification, ŚT 2.122); defective mantras must be purified (ŚT 2.64–123).
