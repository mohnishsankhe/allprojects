# The bodhisattva's illness from compassion

## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The bodhisattva is ill because beings are ill, as parents are ill with their only son; the sick bodhisattva sees his illness, and beings', as unreal, and so has compassion free of sentimental views.
