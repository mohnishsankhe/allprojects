## Definitions
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): Eight protections enclosing the devotee's life: guru, liṅga, jaṅgama, pādōdaka, prasāda, vibhūti, rudrākṣa and mantra.
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The SSM's bhakta-sthala includes initiation, liṅga-wearing, ash, rudrākṣa, the five-syllable mantra and the worship and prasāda of guru, liṅga and jaṅgama among its fifteen sub-sthalas.
