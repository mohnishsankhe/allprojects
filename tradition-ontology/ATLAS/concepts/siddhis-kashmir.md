## Definitions
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): Powers arise from union with power and pure knowledge (Śiva Sūtra 1.19-21; SK 3.1-7), but powers under the veil of delusion are not liberation (Śiva Sūtra 3.6; 2.4; 3.23); light, sound, form and taste arising from the unmeṣa distract (SK 3.10).
