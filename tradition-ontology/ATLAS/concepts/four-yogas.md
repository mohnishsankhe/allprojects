## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): One yoga divided in practice into four (Yogatattva 19); for practitioners of dull, middling and superior capacity (Varāha 5.10); in the Yogaśikhā four inner stages of one great yoga, each reinterpreted (mantra as reversed haṃsa, haṭha as union of sun and moon, laya as union of knower and supreme, rāja as union of rajas and retas).
