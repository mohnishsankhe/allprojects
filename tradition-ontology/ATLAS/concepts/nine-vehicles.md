## Definitions
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): Śrāvaka, pratyekabuddha and bodhisattva (the vehicles leading from the origin of suffering); kriyā, ubhaya and yoga (the vehicles of Vedic austerity, outer tantras); mahāyoga, anuyoga and atiyoga (the vehicles of overpowering means, inner tantras). Each is complete for its practitioners; the higher include the lower.
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): The Kunjed Gyalpo's nine passes, scriptures and paths lead to the single vehicle of the all-creator, which is reached without travelling.
