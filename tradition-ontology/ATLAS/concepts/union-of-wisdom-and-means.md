## Definitions
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Wisdom (emptiness, feminine) and means (compassion, bliss, masculine) joined — the meaning of 'Hevajra', of the vajra and bell, of deity couples; union is 'knowing the unity of wisdom and compassion' (Pañcakrama 5.7).
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): Emptiness without compassion is not the supreme path; compassion alone keeps one in saṃsāra; joining the two, one abides in neither (People Dohā v15–17).
