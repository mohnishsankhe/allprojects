- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The Kaula came down as Jñānanirṇiti, Mahat, Siddhāmṛta and, in Kali, Matsyodara; stolen by Kārttikeya and swallowed by a fish, it was recovered by Bhairava as fisherman; it came from Candradvīpa and is in every house of the yoginīs in Kāmarūpa (KJN 16.27–48; 22.10–12).
