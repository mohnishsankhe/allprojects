## Definitions
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Yoga is the conjunction of the self with the Lord through the mind; in action (muttering, meditation on Oṃ in the heart) or in the cessation of action; it arises for one of unclouded mind who practises.
