## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The texts explain the name in two ways: as 'force' — kuṇḍalinī rises 'by force' (haṭhāt, HYP 3.111) and is to be seized 'by force' (3.109) — and as the union of 'ha', the sun, and 'ṭha', the moon (Yogabīja; quoted by the Jyotsnā as from the Siddhasiddhāntapaddhati), i.e. of prāṇa and apāna in breath-control. The Śārṅgadhara Paddhati distinguishes Gorakṣa's haṭha from that of Mṛkaṇḍu's son.
