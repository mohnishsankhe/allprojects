## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): At the approach of inevitable death — from calamity, famine, old age or incurable disease — the practitioner gradually gives up food and passions in equanimity, with the teacher's permission and under the care of attending ascetics (TS 7.22; Ratnakaraṇḍa 122; Bhagavatī Ārādhanā). It is vitiated by wishing to live, wishing to die, affection, recalling pleasures and longing for reward (TS 7.37). The texts hold it is not self-killing because it is free of passion (Sarvārthasiddhi 7.22; Puruṣārthasiddhyupāya 177–178).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Known as saṃthārā; three forms of the final renunciation of food are distinguished (bhakta-pratyākhyāna, iṅginī, prāyopagamana).
