## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Four woeful planes (hell, animals, ghosts, asuras), the human realm and six sense-sphere heavens, sixteen fine-material Brahma realms (including the five Pure Abodes and the realm of non-percipient beings) and four immaterial realms; life-spans increase upward.
