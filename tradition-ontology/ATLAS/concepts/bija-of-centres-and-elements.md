## Definitions
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): The elements have the seeds laṃ (earth), vaṃ (water), raṃ (fire), yaṃ (air), haṃ (space) — placed in the root, svādhiṣṭhāna, maṇipūra, anāhata and viśuddhi centres, with oṃ at the ājñā; the petals of the six centres carry the fifty letters (4+6+10+12+16+2); the seeds are used in the purification of the elements (bhūtaśuddhi) and in meditation on the centres.
- [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md): The Saṅgītaratnākara's throat centre carries on its petals the praṇava, udgītha, huṃ-phaṭ, vaṣaṭ, svadhā, svāhā, namaḥ, amṛta, the seven notes and poison (ŚR 1.2.129–130).
