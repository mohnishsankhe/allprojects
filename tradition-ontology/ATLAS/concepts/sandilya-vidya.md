## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Śāṇḍilya's meditation: the person is made of resolve; meditate on the self as made of mind, with breath for body, of the form of light, like a grain of rice within yet greater than sky and earth, and resolve to attain it at death (ŚB 10.6.3).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The Chāndogya version adds 'all this is Brahman' and 'this self within the heart is Brahman' (ChU 3.14).
