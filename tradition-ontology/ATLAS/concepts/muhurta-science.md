## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Every undertaking — sacraments, marriage, journeys, building, consecration, sowing — is begun at a moment whose almanac-limbs, rising sign and planetary placements suit it (MC 1–13); an act begun under ill-placed planets destroys its doer, one begun at the right time succeeds with little effort (BS 103.59–60); yet timing alone does not guarantee success (Yogayātrā 1.5).
