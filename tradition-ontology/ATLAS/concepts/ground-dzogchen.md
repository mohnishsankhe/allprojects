## Definitions
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): The primordial state prior to saṃsāra and nirvāṇa: essence primordially pure, nature spontaneously present, compassion all-pervading; neither buddha nor being, it becomes one or the other by recognition or non-recognition of its display.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): The ground of the ground-path-fruit scheme: buddha-nature, present in all beings.
