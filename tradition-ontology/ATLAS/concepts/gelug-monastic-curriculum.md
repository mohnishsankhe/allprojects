## Definitions
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): After the Collected Topics, Types of Mind and Types of Reasons, monks debate for many years on five subjects: the Perfection of Wisdom (Abhisamayālaṅkāra), Madhyamaka (Madhyamakāvatāra), Pramāṇa (Pramāṇavārttika), Abhidharma (Abhidharmakośa) and Vinaya (Guṇaprabha's Vinayasūtra), using their college's textbooks; the course ends in the geshe examinations.
