# Śraddhā, faith

## Names

## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Faith kindles the fire and makes the offering; it is invoked morning, noon and evening (RV 10.151); against doubters, 'have faith in him' (2.12.5).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7.21–22; 9.3, 9.23: whatever form a devotee wishes to worship with faith, the Lord makes that faith unwavering, and through it the worshipper obtains desires ordained by the Lord; those without faith in this dharma return to saṃsāra; devotees of other deities worship with faith, and so worship the Lord, but not according to rule.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Faith is threefold, born of nature; a person is made of faith — as his faith, so is he (17.2–3); whatever is done without faith is 'asat', of no account here or hereafter (17.28); hearing the teaching with faith brings the worlds of the meritorious (18.71).
