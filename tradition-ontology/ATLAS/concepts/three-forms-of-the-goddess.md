## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): In the recitation tradition the presiding deities of the three caritas are Mahākālī, Mahālakṣmī and Mahāsarasvatī, derived from the primordial Mahālakṣmī (Prādhānika Rahasya); the thirteen chapters themselves do not use these three names for the caritas.
