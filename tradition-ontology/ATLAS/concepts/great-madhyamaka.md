## Names

## Definitions
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): The definitive Madhyamaka of the third wheel, the Maitreya texts, Nāgārjuna's hymns and the Kālacakra, beyond the extremes because conventional phenomena do not truly exist and the ultimate does not not exist.
- [Rimé (ris med), the non-sectarian movement](../lineages/rime.md): Kongtrul's summit of the sūtra tenets: other-emptiness Madhyamaka.

_Notes: The Nyingma (Rongzom, Mipham) use 'Great Madhyamaka' for a Madhyamaka of the union of appearance and emptiness; that usage is not merged here._
