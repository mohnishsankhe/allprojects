## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Combinations making the native a king or kingly: by exalted planets in Varāhamihira (the Yavanas say exalted malefics make a cruel king, Jīvaśarman denies they make a king at all: BJ 11.1); by the relation of the lords of angles and trines, the yogakāraka, and the cancelled fall (nīcabhaṅga) in Parāśara (BPHS 34, 39–40).
