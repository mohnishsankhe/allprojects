## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): MN 118:30-36: aroused in sequence while the four establishments are practised: mindfulness, investigation of dhammas, energy, rapture, tranquillity, concentration, equanimity, each arising in dependence on the one before; developed supported by seclusion, dispassion and cessation and ripening in relinquishment, they fulfil knowledge and liberation (mn118:42-43).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Mindfulness, investigation of dhammas, energy, rapture, tranquillity, concentration, equanimity: the fourth field of dhamma-contemplation (MN 10:42).
