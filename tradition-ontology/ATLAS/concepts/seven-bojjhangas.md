## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Mindfulness, investigation of dhammas, energy, rapture, tranquillity, concentration, equanimity; arising in sequence from the establishments of mindfulness and fulfilling true knowledge and liberation (MN 118); nourished and starved by attention (SN 46.51); to be applied according to whether the mind is sluggish or restless (SN 46.53).
