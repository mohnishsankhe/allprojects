# Emptiness of the aggregates (Heart Sūtra)

`cpt:emptiness-of-the-aggregates` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** self
**Members:** rūpa, vedanā, saṃjñā, saṃskāra, vijñāna

## Names
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): pañca-skandha-śūnyatā

## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Avalokiteśvara sees the five aggregates (form, feeling, perception, formations, consciousness) as empty (of own-being in the Sanskrit-short text); form is emptiness and emptiness is form, and likewise for the other four; all dharmas have the mark of emptiness, unarisen and unceased, and in emptiness there are no aggregates, sense-bases, realms, links of dependent origination, four truths, knowledge or attainment.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): In the shorter Sanskrit recension: Avalokiteśvara sees the five aggregates as empty of own-being (svabhāva-śūnya); form is emptiness and emptiness form, and likewise vedanā, saṃjñā, saṃskāra and vijñāna; all dharmas have the mark of emptiness, unarisen, unceased; in emptiness there are no aggregates, sense-bases, realms, links, truths, knowledge or attainment.
