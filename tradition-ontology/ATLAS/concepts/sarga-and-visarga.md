## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Primary creation (sarga) is the emergence of mahat, the elements and senses from the unmanifest; secondary creation (visarga/pratisarga) is Brahmā's fashioning of the worlds and beings - Brahmā born on the lotus from the navel of the Lord lying on the waters.
