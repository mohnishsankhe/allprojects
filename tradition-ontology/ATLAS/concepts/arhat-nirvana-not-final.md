# The arhat's nirvāṇa is not final

`cpt:arhat-nirvana-not-final` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** karma-rebirth

## Names

## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The nirvāṇa of śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas is a conjured resting place; śrāvakas are predicted to buddhahood; one who has entered the disciples' certainty cannot produce bodhicitta (Vimalakīrti).
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): Arhats still have fear, remaining birth and the ground of ignorance (Śrīmālā).

## Relations (interpretation layer)
- contrasts-with → [Nibbāna](nibbana.md): the Pali account treats arahatta as final
