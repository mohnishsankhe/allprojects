## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): In the lucidity of nirvicārā coalescence there is inner clarity (1.47); insight there is truth-bearing (1.48), with the particular as its object unlike testimony and inference (1.49); its impression obstructs other impressions (1.50). Vyāsa quotes: 'having climbed the clarity of insight, the wise one looks on the sorrowing as one on a mountain looks on those below' (YBh 1.47).
