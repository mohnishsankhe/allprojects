# The last remembrance at death

## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The bhakti-yogin must remember the Lord at death; for the surrendered the Lord himself remembers and leads them, so no last remembrance is needed (the fourth of Varadarāja's six sayings).
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): Because at death the throat will be choked and remembrance may fail, the devotee hands himself to the Lord now: 'I tell you now, for that hour' (Periyāḻvār); 'let my mind enter your feet this very day' (Mukundamālā).
