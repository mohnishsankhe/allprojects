# The imperishable (akṣara)

`cpt:aksara` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** ultimate

## Names
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): akṣara

## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): That on which space is woven, described only by negations, by whose command sun and moon stand apart; unknown, all rites are finite; the unseen seer (BAU 3.8.8-11); the object of the higher knowledge and source of beings (MuU 1.1.5-7); also the syllable Om.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 8.3, 8.11, 8.13, 8.20–21: brahman is the supreme imperishable; it is the akṣara of which the knowers of the Veda speak, which passionless ascetics enter and for which people practise brahmacarya; Oṃ is the one-syllable brahman; the eternal unmanifest beyond the unmanifest is called the akṣara, the highest goal, from which there is no return, the Lord's supreme abode.
