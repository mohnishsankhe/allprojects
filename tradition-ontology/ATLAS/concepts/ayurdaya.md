## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The span of life is computed from the planets — by the methods of Maya, the Yavanas, Maṇittha and Parāśara (exaltation-years halved in fall), of Jīvaśarman, and of Satya (by navāṃśas traversed), whom Varāhamihira prefers (BJ 7); the lords of the 2nd and 7th are the killers (māraka) whose periods bring death when life is spent (BPHS 44).
