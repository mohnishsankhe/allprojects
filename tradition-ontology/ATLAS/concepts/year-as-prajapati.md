## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Prajāpati is the year (saṃvatsara); the Veda's syllables are arranged to equal its moments (ŚB 10.4.2.22–25); the year is also Death, wearing away mortals' lives by days and nights (ŚB 10.4.3.1).
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): The Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa opens in homage to Prajāpati as lord of the five-year age whose limbs are days, seasons, half-years and months (Ṛk 1).
