## Definitions
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): Creation and dissolution take place in the body: the fourteen worlds are principles within it; dissolution runs from action into knowledge and will and into the light of Śiva; enjoyment is below, liberation above (KJN 2.4–9); the supreme is that from which all arises and into which all returns like rivers to the sea (14.101).
