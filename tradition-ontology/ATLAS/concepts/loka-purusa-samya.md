# The person is the measure of the world

## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Whatever is in the world is in the person and vice versa: earth is the person's form, water moisture, fire heat, wind breath, space cavities, brahman the inner self; Prajāpati is mind, Indra ego-sense, the sun intake, Rudra anger, Soma serenity; creation is conception and the four ages childhood, youth, age and disease (Ca Śā 5.3-5); seeing the world in oneself and oneself in the world gives rise to the true understanding that leads to release (Śā 5.7).
