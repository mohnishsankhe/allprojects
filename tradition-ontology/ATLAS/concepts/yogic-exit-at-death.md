## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 8.10–13: at the time of departure the yogin, with unmoving mind, devotion and the power of yoga, makes the breath enter between the brows and reaches the supreme divine Person; restraining all the gates, confining the mind in the heart, placing the breath in the head, established in yogic concentration, uttering Oṃ and remembering the Lord, he goes to the supreme goal.
