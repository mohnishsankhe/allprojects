## Definitions
- [Mahānubhāva panth](../lineages/mahanubhava.md): Parameśvara's five incarnations — Śrī Kṛṣṇa, Dattātreya, Cāṅgadeva Rāüḷ, Govindaprabhu, Cakradhar — through whom alone liberation is given; grace passes from one to the next.
