## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Equanimity (sāmāyika), praise of the twenty-four Jinas, salutation of the teacher, repentance (pratikramaṇa), abandonment of the body (kāyotsarga) and renunciation (pratyākhyāna) — daily obligations of ascetics and observant laity.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): For householders the Digambaras also teach six daily duties: worship, attendance on the teacher, study, restraint, austerity and giving.
