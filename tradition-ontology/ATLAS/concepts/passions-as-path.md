## Definitions
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The world is bound by passion and freed by passion (Hevajra II.2); the same deeds that bind free when joined with means (Cittaviśuddhiprakaraṇa 6); as the knower of poison eats poison unharmed (v. 18); 'great passion' is a name of the Buddha.
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): Tilopa: as one who has taken poison (knowingly) is not killed by it, the yogin is not bound by sense pleasures.
