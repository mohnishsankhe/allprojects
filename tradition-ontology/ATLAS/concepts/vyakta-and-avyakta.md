# The manifest and the unmanifest

## Names

## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The manifest is caused, impermanent, non-pervasive, active, many, dependent, merging, composite and subordinate; the unmanifest is the opposite (SK 10); both consist of the three guṇas and are non-discriminating, objects, common, unconscious and productive, unlike puruṣa (SK 11).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7.24; 8.18–21; 9.4: manifest things come forth from and dissolve into the unmanifest with Brahmā's day and night; beyond that unmanifest is another, eternal unmanifest which does not perish when beings perish, called the akṣara, the highest goal and the Lord's supreme abode; the Lord pervades the world in unmanifest form; the unintelligent think of him as unmanifest come into manifestation (7.24; whether 'unmanifest' states what he is or belongs to their mistaken view, the verse does not settle).
