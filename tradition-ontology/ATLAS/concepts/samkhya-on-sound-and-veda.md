## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Sound is the subtle element from which space arises (SK 22 with Gauḍapāda); speech has sound as its object (SK 34). Testimony is one of three means of knowledge (SK 4–6). The Sāṃkhya Sūtra holds that the Vedas are neither eternal nor authored by a person, yet valid by their own manifest power (5.45–51); sound is not eternal and there is no sphoṭa (5.57–58).
