## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The universe of eternal substances is uncreated and beginningless; a creator-God is refuted — where was he before, why would a perfect being create, an unembodied maker cannot act (Ādipurāṇa 4; Āptaparīkṣā; Anyayogavyavacchedikā 6); the omniscient Jina is worshipped but does not create or intervene.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Haribhadra reinterprets 'Īśvara' as the soul capable of perfection and each soul as the 'maker' of its own condition (Śāstravārtāsamuccaya 3).
