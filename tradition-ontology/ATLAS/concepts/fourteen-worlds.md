## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Seven upper worlds - Bhūr, Bhuvaḥ, Svar, Mahar, Janas, Tapas, Satya - and seven lower worlds (the pātālas) within the cosmic egg; the first three (trailokya) are dissolved every night of Brahmā; the yogin rises through them (BhP 2.2.22-31).
