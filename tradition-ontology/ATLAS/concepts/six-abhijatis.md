## Definitions
- [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md): (As reported) Black, blue, red, yellow, white and supremely white classes of beings — from the cruel to the three Ājīvika leaders (AN 6.57, ascribed to Pūraṇa; DN 2, to Makkhali); in the Tamil report black, dark blue, green, red, golden, white, with release in the supremely white birth.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The Buddha's six: dark or bright birth leading to dark, bright, or neither-dark-nor-bright (nibbāna) results according to conduct.
