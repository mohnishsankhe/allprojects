## Definitions
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The Kulārṇava gives the rationale: bliss is the form of Brahman residing in the body and the substance manifests it; wine is Śakti, meat Śiva, the enjoyer Bhairava, and the bliss of their union is liberation (5.79-80); those devoted to inner worship are dearest (5.70).
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The Tattva circle is truly performed only by those in whom 'all is Brahman' arises (Mahānirvāṇa 8.208); inner worship offers the mind, breath and senses in place of outer substances (5.143-146).
