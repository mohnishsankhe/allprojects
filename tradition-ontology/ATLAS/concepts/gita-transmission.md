## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Arjuna, his nature struck by pitiable weakness and his mind bewildered about dharma, asks what is decisively better, declares himself Kṛṣṇa's disciple (śiṣya) who has taken refuge (prapanna), and asks to be instructed (2.7); the teaching follows from 2.11. Duryodhana tells Droṇa that the opposing army was arrayed by Drupada's son, 'your intelligent disciple' (1.3; Dhṛṣṭadyumna in the epic).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The yoga was handed down from Vivasvat to Manu to Ikṣvāku and the royal sages, then lost and taught again (BhG 4.1–3); Arjuna becomes a disciple (2.7); knowledge is learned from seers of truth by prostration, inquiry and service (4.34); it is not to be told to one without austerity or devotion (18.67); Sañjaya heard it by Vyāsa's grace (18.75); after instruction the disciple decides (18.63); Kṛṣṇa cannot repeat it (Anugītā 14.16.11); knowledge may be received even from a śūdra (MBh 12.306.85), yet Vidura, of śūdra birth, defers to Sanatsujāta (5.41.5).
