## Definitions
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Common siddhis (pacifying, increasing, the eight great siddhis such as the good vase, flight, the sword) and the supreme siddhi (mahāmudrā/buddhahood); Tilopa lists long life, no white hair, radiance and a lion's strength among the signs.
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): The songs warn against pride in powers and against mistaking experiences for realization.
