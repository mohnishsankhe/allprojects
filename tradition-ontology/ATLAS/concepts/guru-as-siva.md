## Definitions
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): To souls who know only human forms Śiva appears in human form as the guru (as a hunter uses a decoy deer), or teaches from within to the more advanced; the guru is to be served as Śiva.
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): In initiation the ācārya becomes Śiva by ritual identification; Śiva's power acts through him.
