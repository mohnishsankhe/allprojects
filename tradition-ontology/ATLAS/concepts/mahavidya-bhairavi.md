## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Red as a thousand rising suns, wearing a garland of heads, holding rosary and book (or noose and goad) and showing fearlessness and boon (standard dhyāna, recalled). Teaching: the fierce power of dissolution and austerity; Dakṣiṇāmūrti is her Bhairava (Toḍala 1.11); in the Bṛhaddharma Satī herself is Bhairavī within Śiva's body.
