## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Beyond waking, dream and deep sleep: not inward- or outward-knowing, not a mass of cognition, unseen, ungraspable, whose essence is the certainty of the one self, the stilling of the world, peaceful, auspicious, non-dual — 'that is the self, that is to be known' (MāU 7); the measureless (amātra) of Om (MāU 12); 'the fourth is greater than these' (MaiU 7.11).
