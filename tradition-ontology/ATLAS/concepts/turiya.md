## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): In the Kārikā: ruler of the ending of all sorrows, non-dual, imperishable, all-pervading, all-seeing, without cause/effect bond, without seed-sleep. | In the Kārikā ch.3: unborn, sleepless, dreamless, nameless, formless, shining once for all, omniscient; free of speech and thought; motionless and fearless samādhi; self-abiding, peaceful, inexpressible. | In 4.81: unborn, sleepless, dreamless, dawning of itself, once-shone; in 4.80 the steady abiding of one who has turned back, the domain of the awakened; 4.100 the state hard to see, very deep, unborn, of sameness, without multiplicity.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Not knowing inward, outward or both, not a mass of cognition, not knowing, not unknowing; unseen, not to be dealt with, non-dual; it is the self and is to be known; it is without measure.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Beyond waking, dream and deep sleep: not inward- or outward-knowing, not a mass of cognition, unseen, ungraspable, whose essence is the certainty of the one self, the stilling of the world, peaceful, auspicious, non-dual — 'that is the self, that is to be known' (MāU 7); the measureless (amātra) of Om (MāU 12); 'the fourth is greater than these' (MaiU 7.11).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The haṭha texts use 'turyā/turya' as a synonym of samādhi (HYP 4.4) and locate it between the brows, 'the place of Śiva', where mind dissolves and time is not (4.48).
