## Names
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): annarasamaya ... ānandamaya ātman

## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): TU 2.1-5 teaches five selves, each within and filling the one before and each person-shaped: made of the essence of food, of breath, of mind, of understanding and of bliss, with brahman as the tail and foundation of the last; TU 3 reaches the same five as successive understandings of brahman. The TU calls them selves 'made of' (-maya); the name 'sheath' (kośa) for them is later Vedānta usage.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Food, vital air, mind, intellect and bliss sheaths, which seem to cover the self; taught progressively so that the self is known as distinct from each; the bliss-sheath is not Brahman (Śaṅkara).
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Five kōcams among the ninety-six: aṉṉamayam, pirāṇamayam, maṉōmayam, viññāṉamayam, āṉantamayam.
