# The eight dispositions of buddhi (bhāva)

**Members:** dharma (virtue), jñāna (knowledge), vairāgya (dispassion), aiśvarya (power), adharma, ajñāna, avairāgya / rāga, anaiśvarya

## Names
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): bhāva

## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Buddhi's sāttvika form is virtue, knowledge, dispassion and power; its tāmasa form their opposites (SK 23). They are innate, natural or acquired (SK 43); they perfume the subtle body (SK 40). Virtue leads upward, non-virtue downward, knowledge to liberation, error to bondage; dispassion to absorption in prakṛti, passion to saṃsāra, power to non-obstruction (SK 44–45). By seven of them prakṛti binds herself, by one (knowledge) she releases herself (SK 63).
