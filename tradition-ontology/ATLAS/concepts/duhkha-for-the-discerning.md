## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): For the discerning (vivekin) everything is suffering, because of the suffering of transformation (pariṇāma), of torment (tāpa) and of impression (saṃskāra), and because of the conflict of the functions of the guṇas (YS 2.15). Vyāsa: the yogin is the eyeball on which a strand of wool troubles, and the ordinary person is not so troubled (YBh 2.15); only suffering not yet come is to be avoided (YS 2.16, YBh 2.16).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Because of the sufferings of change, anguish and impressions, and the conflict of the guṇas, everything is suffering for the discerning (2.15); the yogin is sensitive to it like an eyeball (YBh 2.15); even the happiness of contentment is suffering compared with kaivalya (YBh 3.18).
