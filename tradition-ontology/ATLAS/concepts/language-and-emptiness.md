## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Words are dependently arisen and empty yet function (VV 22–23); 'empty' is said only for designation (MMK 22.11); the ultimate is taught only through convention (24.10); in reality the object to be named has ceased (18.7) and no dharma was taught by the Buddha to anyone (25.24).
