## Definitions
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): The tradition's account: during the three days of ambuvācī in Āṣāḍha the Earth menstruates; at Kāmākhyā the Goddess herself is in her courses, the temple is closed, and on the fourth day it reopens and pieces of red cloth are given as prasāda (the Ambubachi Melā). A Bengali ritual calendar in the Prāṇatoṣiṇī names 'ambuvācī, three days'.
