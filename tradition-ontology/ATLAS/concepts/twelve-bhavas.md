## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Counted from the ascendant, the houses signify: 1 body and self (tanu); 2 wealth, family and speech; 3 siblings and courage; 4 mother, home, happiness; 5 children, intellect and merit of past lives; 6 enemies, disease, debt; 7 spouse; 8 longevity and death; 9 fortune, dharma, teacher and father; 10 action and position; 11 gains; 12 loss, expense and liberation (BJ 1.15–16; BPHS 11–23).
