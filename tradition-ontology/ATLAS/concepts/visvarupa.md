## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The sovereign form shown to Arjuna by grace through a divine eye: the whole universe in one body, as bright as a thousand suns, and as Time devouring the warriors (BhG 11.5–34); seen only by undivided devotion (11.52–54); a similar self-manifestation occurs in the Kaurava court (MBh 5.129) and Mārkaṇḍeya sees the worlds within a divine child (3.186).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: At Arjuna's request (11.3–4) the Lord shows his sovereign form, which cannot be seen with ordinary sight and is seen with a divine eye (11.8): countless mouths, eyes, arms and ornaments, a radiance like a thousand suns, the whole world standing as one in his body (11.9–13), without beginning, middle or end (11.16, 11.19); the gods and seers praise it and the worlds tremble (11.20–22); the warriors of both armies rush into its mouths (11.26–30); it declares itself Time (11.32). It was shown by grace and the Lord's own yoga, never seen before by another (11.47), is not to be seen by Vedas, sacrifice, study, gifts, rites or austerity (11.48, 11.53), and is known, seen in truth and entered by undivided devotion (11.54).
