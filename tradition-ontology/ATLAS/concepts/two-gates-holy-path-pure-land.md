## Definitions
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Of two excellent dharmas, the holy path (awakening in this world by one's own practice) is hard to realise in the last age; only the Pure Land gate is passable (Daochuo).
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): Set aside the holy path and enter the Pure Land gate (Senchakushū 1, 16).
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): Compassion of the path of sages is limited; that of the Pure Land path is thoroughgoing (Tannishō 4); the 'two pairs and four levels' refine the division.
