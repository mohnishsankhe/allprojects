## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The HYP prescribes the six acts only for those with excess fat or phlegm, whose humours are unbalanced (2.21), keeps them secret (2.23), and reports that some teachers hold breath-control alone sufficient (2.37); the GS makes the acts the first of its seven means for everyone (1.9–12); the Haṭharatnāvalī and Haṭhatattvakaumudī count eight.
