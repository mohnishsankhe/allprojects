## Definitions
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): The Bengali Śākta songs of Rāmprasād and Kamalākānta address Kālī as Mother with intimacy — pleading, reproaching, claiming the child's right, using her name as protection, and seeing the world as her play; they draw on tantric imagery (the six centres, the cremation ground) but make devotion the path.
