## Definitions
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Without the samaya discipline there is no perfection (Kulārṇava 5.96).
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): Tāmasa disciples who ignore the samayas and slander the guru become dead while living (Kubjikāmata 12.3-9).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): Kept by practising the guru's instruction continuously 'like a river'; broken by giving the secret instructions to unfit vessels.
