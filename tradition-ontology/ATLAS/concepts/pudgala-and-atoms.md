## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Matter (pudgala) consists of partless atoms and their aggregates, formed by union and division and bound by degrees of smoothness and roughness; it has touch, taste, smell and colour and takes the forms of sound, light, shadow and bodies; body, speech, mind, breath, pleasure and pain are its services, and karma is a subtle kind of matter (TS 5.19–28, 5.33–37).
