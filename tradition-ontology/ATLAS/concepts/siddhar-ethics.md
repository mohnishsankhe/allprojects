## Definitions
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Do no sin (Kaṭuveḷi pallavi); no slander, lying, cursing, anger or cruelty, not even stones thrown at birds; no killing or flesh-eating; no ganja, opium or toddy; no false garb; give away the wealth Śiva gave; wish that all beings be happy (Tāyumāṉavar). One Siddhar also enjoins the Veda's rule (Kaṭuveḷi 8).
