## Definitions
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Smṛti is authoritative as implying a Vedic source, but is set aside when it conflicts with the Veda or shows a worldly motive; customs of the good done without visible motive are authoritative (MS 1.3.1–7).
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): Texts of Sāṃkhya, Yoga, Pāñcarātra, Pāśupata and the Buddhists have no authority for dharma, even where they agree with the Veda, because the sources of dharma are fixed in number (Tantravārttika).
