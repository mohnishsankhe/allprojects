## Definitions
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Death of the wise-among-the-wise (the omniscient), of the wise (the renunciant), of the wise-ignorant (the vowed layperson), of the ignorant (right faith without vows) and of the ignorant-among-the-ignorant (wrong faith).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The Ācārāṅga's three forms of the wise monk's death and the Prakīrṇakas on the preparation of the dying (restricted theme: the texts' conditions only).
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Five kinds of death — 'wise-wise' (of the omniscient), wise (of the ascetic), wise-foolish (of the layman), foolish (of one with right view but no vows) and foolish-foolish (of the wrong-believer); the wise death by renunciation of food is praised (Bhagavatī Ārādhanā).
