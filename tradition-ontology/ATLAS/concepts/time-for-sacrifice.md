# Time as the condition of the sacrifice

## Definitions
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): The Vedas exist for the sacrifices, which are enjoined for fixed times; hence the science of time is needed and stands at the head of the limbs (VJ Ṛk 3, 35; Yajus 3); fires are set up under the Kṛttikās because they keep to the east (ŚB 2.1.2.3).
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Jyotiṣa's later branches keep this purpose: the muhūrta texts fix the times of sacraments, fire-establishment, consecration and other rites (Muhūrtacintāmaṇi, prakaraṇas 5–13), and the planets are worshipped before great undertakings (YājñSm 1.295–308).
