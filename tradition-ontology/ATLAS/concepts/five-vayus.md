## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): prāṇa (head; breathing, swallowing; upholds intellect, heart, senses, mind), udāna (chest; speech, effort, memory), vyāna (heart; all movement), samāna (near the fire; digestion) and apāna (pelvis; elimination and birth) (AHS Sū 12.4-9); vāyu 'whose self is prāṇa, udāna, samāna, vyāna and apāna' upholds the body (Ca Sū 12.8).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Prāṇa and the others are the shared function of the three internal instruments (SK 29; SS 2.31); the Kramadīpikā places prāṇa at mouth and nose, apāna at the navel, samāna at the heart, udāna at the throat, vyāna at the joints.
