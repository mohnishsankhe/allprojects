# Reflection of consciousness in buddhi

## Names

## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Buddhi, unconscious, takes on the shade of consciousness and appears conscious, while puruṣa, reflected in it, appears to know and enjoy (Vācaspati on SK 5; cf. SK 20); the Sāṃkhya Sūtra speaks of coloring (uparāga) that is misidentification rather than real coloring, like the red flower and the crystal (1.164, 6.28). Vijñānabhikṣu is reported to teach a mutual reflection of puruṣa and buddhi (recalled, low confidence).
