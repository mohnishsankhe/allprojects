## Definitions
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The deity is that to which the offering is directed, as named in injunction or mantra; the sacrificial act, not the deity, is principal and produces the fruit; the deity's embodiment and eating are not established — mantra and arthavāda statements implying them are not meant that way (MS 9.1.6–9, Śabara).
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The Brahma Sūtra reports Jaimini as denying gods eligibility for certain meditations.
