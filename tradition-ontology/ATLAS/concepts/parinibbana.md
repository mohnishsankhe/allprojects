## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The final passing of the Buddha or an arahant; the Buddha passed up through the jhānas and formless attainments to cessation, down again, and attained final nibbāna on emerging from the fourth jhāna (DN 16); what happens to the Tathāgata after it is undeclared (MN 72).
