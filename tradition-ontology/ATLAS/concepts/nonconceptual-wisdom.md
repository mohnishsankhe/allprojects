# Non-conceptual wisdom reached through analysis

`cpt:nonconceptual-wisdom` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** consciousness-states

## Names
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): nirvikalpa-jñāna

## Definitions
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): When analysing wisdom finds no dharma arisen in the three times, the yogin enters non-conceptual wisdom pacifying all elaboration (Bhāvanākrama III).
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): When neither being nor non-being remains before the mind, it is pacified without object (BCA 9.34–35).
