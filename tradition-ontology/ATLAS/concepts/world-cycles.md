## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): An eon has four incalculable phases; the world is destroyed in turn by fire (up to the Ābhassara), water (up to the Subhakiṇha) and wind (up to the Vehapphala).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The world contracts and expands over aeons; at contraction beings are mostly reborn in the Ābhassara realm (DN 27; DN 1).
