## Definitions
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The Mūla Saṅgha (Kundakundānvaya, with the Nandi, Sena, Deva and Siṃha gaṇas and later the Balātkāra gaṇa), and the Kāṣṭhā, Drāviḍa and Māthura saṅghas — the latter three condemned as deviant in Devasena's Darśanasāra, later accepted as Digambara branches; the Yāpanīya was a separate order.
