## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The Saṃhitās name in-breath, out-breath and diffused breath (prāṇa, apāna, vyāna) with life (asu) among the goods of the Camaka (TS 4.7) and in long-life charms (AVŚ 8.1); the Vrātya has seven prāṇas, seven apānas and seven vyānas identified with fire, sun, moon, wind, waters, animals and creatures (AVŚ 15.15–17).
