## Definitions
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): The dying person faces west, fixes mind and voice on Amitābha; companions recite with him, record and repent visions; those who have taken wine or meat keep away; friends pledge in advance to help one another complete the ten recitations; do not move or weep over the dying.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): The person of shinjin does not depend on the manner of death or deathbed rites.
