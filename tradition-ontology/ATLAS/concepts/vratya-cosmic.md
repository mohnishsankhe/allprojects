## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The vow-bound wanderer of Atharvaveda 15 who stirs Prajāpati into creation, becomes the one, the great, brahman, tapas and truth, becomes Mahādeva and Īśāna, stands upright a year, is seated on a throne of seasons and Vedas, has seven prāṇas identified with fire, sun, moon, wind, waters, animals and creatures, and is to be honoured above the king when he comes as guest.
