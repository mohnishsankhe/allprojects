# Non-abiding nirvāṇa

`cpt:apratisthita-nirvana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** karma-rebirth

## Names
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): apratiṣṭhita-nirvāṇa

## Definitions
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): Because of great compassion the Buddhas do not abide in nirvāṇa but remain acting for the whole world (Bhāvanākrama I).
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The buddhas' nirvāṇa, abiding neither in saṃsāra nor in quiescence, by wisdom and compassion.
