## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): A sāman is a verse (from the ārcika) turned into song, with stretched vowels, repetitions and stobhas, divided among the chanters as hiṅkāra, prastāva, udgītha, pratihāra, upadrava and nidhana.
