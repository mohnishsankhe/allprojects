# Coalescence (samāpatti)

`cpt:samapatti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Members:** savitarkā, nirvitarkā, savicārā, nirvicārā

## Names
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): samāpatti

## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): In a mind whose activities have dwindled, like a flawless crystal, coalescence is resting on and taking the form of grasper, grasping or grasped (1.41); it is savitarkā (mixed with word, object and knowledge constructions), nirvitarkā (object alone), savicārā and nirvicārā (subtle objects) (1.42–44), extending to the unmarked (1.45); these four are samādhi with seed (1.46).
