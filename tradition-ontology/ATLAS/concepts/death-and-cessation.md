## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): At death bodily, verbal and mental formations cease, vitality is exhausted, heat dissipated and the faculties broken up; in cessation the formations cease but vitality and heat remain and the faculties are clear (MN 43).
