# The three liṅgas (iṣṭa, prāṇa, bhāva)

**Category:** ultimate

## Names
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): liṅgaṃ trividham

## Definitions
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The liṅga is threefold: gross (the iṣṭaliṅga worn on the body), subtle (the prāṇaliṅga of inner contemplation) and beyond the highest (the tṛptiliṅga).
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): The vacanas speak of iṣṭaliṅga, prāṇaliṅga and bhāvaliṅga, and the manuals correlate them with the gross, subtle and causal bodies and with the three aṅgas (tyāga, bhōga, yōga).
