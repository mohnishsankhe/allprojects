# The two material causes: bindu (pure māyā) and māyā

## Definitions
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Bindu (mahāmāyā, śuddhamāyā) produces the pure universe, the bodies of pure beings and sound; māyā (aśuddhamāyā) produces the impure universe; prakṛti with the three guṇas arises within the māyic range.
