## Definitions
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The guru tests the disciple by knowledge or action for a year, six months or three (Kulārṇava 14.19), and the disciple tests the guru by the bliss and signs of his practice (14.25); if they give and receive without testing both become piśācas (14.11); Kula knowledge never stays in the unworthy (2.36).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): The guru tests the disciple's devotion and purifies his karma through ordeals (Tilopa–Nāropa, Marpa–Milarepa); the disciple examines the guru for the three trainings and experience.
