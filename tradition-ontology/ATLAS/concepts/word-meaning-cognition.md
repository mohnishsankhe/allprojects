## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Word, object and cognition are confused through mutual superimposition; saṃyama on their distinction gives knowledge of the cries of all beings (3.17). The word is a single unit grasped by the buddhi from the letters (YBh 3.17); the relation of word and meaning is held eternal (YBh 1.27); savitarkā coalescence is mixed with these constructions (1.42).
