## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The Mahāvidyās accomplish for worshippers the acts of killing, driving away, agitation, bewitching, melting, yawning, paralysing and destroying (Bṛhaddharma madhya 6.132-133); the six acts (śānti, vaśya, stambhana, vidveṣaṇa, uccāṭana, māraṇa) are treated in the eastern tantras. Recorded only as a teaching; not described. Kṛṣṇānanda treats such claims as needing the tradition's checks.
