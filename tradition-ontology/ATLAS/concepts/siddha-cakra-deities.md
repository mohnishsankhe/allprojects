## Definitions
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Pattirakiriyār's couplets 66–72 place Gaṇapati at the base of the root support, Brahmā in the earth figure, Viṣṇu in the water crescent, Rudra in the triangle with three rings, Maheśvara in the hexagon of air, Sadāśiva in the circle, and the guru's feet at the crown; Kuṭampai 17 speaks of the triangle and the hexagon.
