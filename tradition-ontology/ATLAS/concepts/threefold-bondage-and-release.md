## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Bondage is threefold: to prakṛti (absorption from taking the eight prakṛtis as highest), to the modifications (domination by the senses and objects), and through gifts (giving with conceit); release is threefold: by increase of knowledge, by calming of the senses' passion, and by total exhaustion (of virtue and non-virtue), whence isolation (Tattvasamāsa 19–20 with the Kramadīpikā). Strictly, prakṛti is bound and released (SK 62).
