## Definitions
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): Nitya acts (lifelong) and naimittika acts (on occasions) are to be performed — they destroy past sin and avert the demerit of omission; kāmya acts bring their fruits and more births; niṣiddha acts bring demerit. The seeker of release does the first two and avoids the last two.
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): The seeker of release abstains from prohibited and desire-prompted acts, exhausts past karma by experience, and destroys it by self-knowledge with calm, restraint and celibacy.
