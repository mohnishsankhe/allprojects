## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The universe (loka) is finite world-space within infinite empty non-world, uncreated and beginningless, shaped like a man standing with arms akimbo, fourteen rajju high: the seven hells below, the disc of the middle world at the waist, the heavens above, and the abode of the liberated at the crown; mobile beings live only in the central channel (trasa-nāḍī) (TS 3–4, 5.12, 10.5–8; Tiloyapaṇṇatti; Lokaprakāśa).
