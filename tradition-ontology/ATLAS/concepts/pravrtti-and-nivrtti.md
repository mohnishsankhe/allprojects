## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Engagement (pravṛtti), rooted in delusion, desire, aversion and karma and grown into ego-sense, attachment, doubt and wrong means, is suffering and the root of evil; disengagement (nivṛtti) is release, the supreme brahman (Ca Śā 5.8-11).
