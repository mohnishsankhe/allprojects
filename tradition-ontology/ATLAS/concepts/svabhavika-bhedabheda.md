## Definitions
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): Souls and world are by their very nature both different and non-different from the Lord: different as dependent, atomic and limited; non-different as his powers without separate existence.
- [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md): Yādavaprakāśa (as reported by Rāmānuja): Brahman itself naturally transforms into Īśvara, souls and matter, and so is both one and many by nature.
