## Definitions

- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The mind is not self-illuminating because it is seen (4.19); it cannot know itself and its object at one time (4.20); if seen by another mind there would be infinite regress and confusion of memories (4.21); awareness of one's own cognition arises when unmoving consciousness takes its form (4.22).
