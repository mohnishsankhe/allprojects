## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Five small vows, three supplementary vows and four disciplinary vows (TS 7.20–21 names the seven śīlas: directions, region, purposeless harm, equanimity, holy-day fasting, limiting consumables, sharing with guests); the grouping into guṇa- and śikṣā-vratas differs between authors (Samantabhadra, Kundakunda, Hemacandra).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Guṇavratas: directions, consumables, purposeless harm; śikṣāvratas: sāmāyika, limiting region, pauṣadha, sharing with guests (Śvetāmbara standard, recalled).
