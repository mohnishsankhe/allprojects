## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Sensual desire, ill will, dullness and drowsiness, restlessness and remorse, doubt: the first field of dhamma-contemplation, known as present or absent, with the arising, abandoning and non-recurrence of each known (MN 10:36).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Vism IV p. 141-146: sensual desire, ill will, sloth and torpor, restlessness and remorse and doubt are the unwholesome states from which the first jhāna is secluded; they are the adversaries and opposites of the jhāna factors (concentration/sensual desire, rapture/ill will, applied thought/sloth and torpor, happiness/restlessness and remorse, sustained thought/doubt); the jhāna does not arise while they are unabandoned. Each obstructs in its own way (p. 146).
