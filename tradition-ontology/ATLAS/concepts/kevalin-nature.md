## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The living omniscient (13th stage) has destroyed the four destructive karmas but keeps the four others; he teaches, moves and at the end may equalise his remaining karmas by expanding his soul through the universe (kevali-samudghāta) before liberation.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The kevalin does not eat, suffer hunger or disease; the eleven hardships of TS 9.11 are figurative (Sarvārthasiddhi); his knowledge and perception are simultaneous.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The kevalin takes food, as the scriptures show of Mahāvīra; knowledge and perception alternate (Jinabhadra) or are identical (Siddhasena).
