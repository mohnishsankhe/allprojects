## Definitions
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Knowledge (vidyā/jñāna), ritual (kriyā), yoga and conduct (caryā); all of Śiva's scriptures are said to have them and to serve enjoyment and liberation.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The Pādma Saṃhitā is arranged in the four pādas jñāna, yoga, kriyā, caryā.
