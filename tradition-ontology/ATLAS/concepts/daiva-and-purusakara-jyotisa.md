# Fate and effort in jyotiṣa

## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Fate (daiva) is karma made in another birth; success comes from fate and human effort together, like a harvest from rain and ploughing; a single defect ruins all (Yogayātrā 1.3–4). Favourable planets alone do not bring success, or the astrologer would be king; rites alone do not, or the priest would be king (1.5–6). Yet an act begun under ill-placed planets destroys its doer, and a well-timed one succeeds with slight effort (BS 103.59–60). The smṛti says fate is human effort of a former body and a chariot does not move on one wheel (YājñSm 1.349–351).
