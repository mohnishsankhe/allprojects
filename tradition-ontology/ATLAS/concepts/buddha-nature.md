## Definitions
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): All beings have the Tathāgata within, covered by adventitious defilements — the dharmakāya not yet freed; the realm of beings neither increases nor decreases; it is empty of stains and not empty of qualities; all beings, even icchantikas, have it.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The Laṅkāvatāra: taught to remove the fear of no-self, meaning emptiness; the Avataṃsaka: the Tathāgata's wisdom present in all beings.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): For the Nyingma (Mipham), the sugata-essence is the union of luminosity and emptiness with the qualities spontaneously present, neither a mere absence nor a self; the Guhyagarbha makes it the source from which beings' conceptual karma emanates the realms.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): The 'cause' of awakening present in all beings, for three reasons (the dharmakāya pervades, suchness is undifferentiated, the potential is present) — Jewel Ornament ch.1; identified by Nāropa with the luminous mind obscured by adventitious stains, and by the 3rd Karmapa with mind's clarity-emptiness.
- [Karma Kagyu (Kamtsang Kagyu)](../lineages/karma-kagyu.md): Within the Karma Kagyu read both as empty of own-nature (the 8th Karmapa) and as empty only of what is other (the shentong reading of the 8th Situ and Jamgön Kongtrul): not yet reconciled.
