## Definitions
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): All beings have the Tathāgata within, covered by adventitious defilements — the dharmakāya not yet freed; the realm of beings neither increases nor decreases; it is empty of stains and not empty of qualities; all beings, even icchantikas, have it.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The Laṅkāvatāra: taught to remove the fear of no-self, meaning emptiness; the Avataṃsaka: the Tathāgata's wisdom present in all beings.
