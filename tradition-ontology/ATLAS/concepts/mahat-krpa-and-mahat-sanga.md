## Definitions
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Devotion comes chiefly by the grace of great souls or a drop of the Lord's grace; their company is hard to gain, unfathomable and unfailing, and is itself gained by his grace, for there is no difference between him and his people.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The Āḻvārs seek the company and service of devotees: the Tiruppāvai's girls wake one another before approaching the Lord; Kulaśekhara longs for the devotees of Śrīraṅgam.
