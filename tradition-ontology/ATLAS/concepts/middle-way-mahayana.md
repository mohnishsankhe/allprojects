## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): 'Is' and 'is not', permanent and impermanent, self and no-self are extremes; the middle between them — formless, unshowable, without abode — is the middle way; the wise do not take a stand even in the middle.
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): Buddha-nature is the middle way (Mahāparinirvāṇa).
