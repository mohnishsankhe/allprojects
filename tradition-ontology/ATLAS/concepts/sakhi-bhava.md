## Definitions

- [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md): The singer is a sakhī in the grove; Svāmī Haridās is Lalitā.
- [Rasik Rāma-bhakti (Rasik sampradāya of the Rāmānandīs)](../lineages/rasik-ramanandi.md): The Rāma-rasik is a companion of Sītā in the inner Ayodhyā.
