## Definitions
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): Marathi can carry the highest teaching — Jñāneśvar's words will outdo nectar; if God made Sanskrit, the vernacular was not born of thieves (Eknāth); words are the saints' wealth and weapons (Tukārām).
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): Tulsī tells Rāma's story in the vernacular (bhāṣā) for his inner joy, in accord with all scripture.
- [Mahānubhāva panth](../lineages/mahanubhava.md): Cakradhar taught in Marathi and bade his disciples keep to it; the scriptures are in Marathi.
- [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md): Scripture is sung and staged in Assamese and Brajabuli so that all may hear.
