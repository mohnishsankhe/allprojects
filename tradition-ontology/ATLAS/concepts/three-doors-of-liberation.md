## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The signless, desireless and void gateways, entered through the contemplations of impermanence, suffering and not-self.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Emptiness, wishless and signless concentrations; pure, they are the three doors of liberation.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Emptiness, the signless and the wishless, cultivated by the bodhisattva even in dreams, whose fruit he does not realize before his roots are ripe; in the Lotus's Sanskrit they are the herbs that cure the blind.
