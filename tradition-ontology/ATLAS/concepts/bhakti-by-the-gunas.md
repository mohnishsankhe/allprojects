## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Tāmasa (with violence, pride, envy), rājasa (for objects, fame, power), sāttvika (to be rid of karma or because enjoined), all seeing difference; beyond them nirguṇa devotion, motiveless and unbroken.
- [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md): Tāmasī, rājasī and sāttvikī devotion to the Goddess, and supreme devotion beyond them.
