## Definitions
- [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md): (As reported) The soul is the body, bounded by the skin; it ends with the body; no soul can be drawn out of the body and shown.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Both 'soul is body' and 'soul is other than body' are extremes; the middle is dependent origination.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The soul is distinct from the body (Kesi's answer to Paesi).
