## Definitions
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): Sciences attached to the four Vedas: medicine (āyurveda), archery/warfare (dhanurveda), music (gāndharvaveda) and statecraft or architecture (arthaśāstra / sthāpatyaveda), as listed in the Caraṇavyūha.
