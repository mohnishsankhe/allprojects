## Definitions
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): Śiva taught the doctrine to the Goddess and Nandin; the gaṇa Reṇuka, emerging from the Someśvara liṅga at Kollipāki, taught Agastya; five ācāryas appear from liṅgas in every age and found the five pīṭhas; the SSM's compiler stands in the ācārya line of Muddadeva and Siddhanātha.
