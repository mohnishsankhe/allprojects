## Definitions
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): The vacanas refuse caste among devotees: Basava calls himself the son of a cobbler's house and asks to say of every devotee 'he is ours'; the intercaste marriage at Kalyāṇa is remembered as the occasion of the upheaval.
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The SSM makes devotion, not birth, the mark of the bhakta (9.4–5), tells the māheśvara to drop jāti-dharmas opposed to the liṅga (10.30–31) and sees brāhmaṇa and outcaste alike at union (14.19–20), but also prescribes eating with twice-born of one's own jāti on the path (9.29) and distinguishes Śiva's and Brahmā's orderings of varṇa and āśrama (10.34–36).
