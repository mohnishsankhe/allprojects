## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Gain and loss, fame and disrepute, praise and blame, pleasure and pain, which turn around the world; the noble disciple reflects that they are impermanent and is not swept away (AN 8.6); a mind unshaken by them is the highest blessing (Snp 2.4).
