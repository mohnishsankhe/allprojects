## Definitions
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Read by Shandao as 'beings who call my name even ten times'; the exclusion of grave offenders is a gate of restraint.
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): The vow that selected the nenbutsu alone as the practice for birth.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): The vow of sincere mind and entrusting (shinjin), whose three minds are one mind given by Amida; the cause of birth in the true land.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Beings of the ten directions who sincerely entrust themselves, wish to be born and think of Amitābha even ten times are born there, except those who commit the five grave offences and slander the dharma.
