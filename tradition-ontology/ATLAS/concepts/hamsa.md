# Haṃsa — the swan / the self as breath-mantra

## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The inner self as the 'swan' that moves with the breath (haṃ-sa), reversed as so'ham 'I am He'; meditated as a bird with fire and moon as wings; its knowledge (haṃsa-vidyā) is called the only means to liberation (Brahmavidyā) and the inner sacrifice (Pāśupatabrahma); also the name of the third kind of renouncer.
