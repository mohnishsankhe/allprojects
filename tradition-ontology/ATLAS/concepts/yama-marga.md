## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The dead, bound by Yama's messengers in a body for suffering, is dragged south over thorns, stones and burning ground to Yama's city: in MkP in twelve days, in the Garuḍa over a year past sixteen cities where he eats the monthly piṇḍas, crossing the Vaitaraṇī; in the Bhāgavata in two or three muhūrtas. Gifts made in life (umbrellas, sandals, food, the Vaitaraṇī cow) ease the road.
