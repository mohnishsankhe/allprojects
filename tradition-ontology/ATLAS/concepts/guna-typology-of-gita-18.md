## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Knowledge, action, agent, intellect (buddhi), steadfastness (dhṛti) and happiness are each sāttvic, rājasic or tāmasic (BhG 18.19–39) — sāttvic knowledge seeing one imperishable being in all, sāttvic happiness 'poison at first, nectar at the end'.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Knowledge, action and agent are threefold by the guṇas, as declared in the enumeration of the guṇas (18.19): knowledge (18.20–22), action (18.23–25), agent (18.26–28), understanding (18.30–32), steadiness (18.33–35) and happiness (18.36–39) are each sāttvic, rājasic or tāmasic; no being on earth or among the gods is free of the three guṇas (18.40).
