# Emptiness (śūnyatā) in Madhyamaka

`cpt:sunyata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ultimate

## Names
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): śūnyatā
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): niḥsvabhāvatā

## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Every dharma is empty of own-nature because it arises dependently; emptiness is dependent origination, a dependent designation and the middle way (MMK 24.18); it is not a further entity and is itself empty (13.7–8).
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): The mere absence of own-nature, a non-implicative negation, in persons and phenomena alike (PsP 24.18; MA 6).
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): The lack of own-nature shown by the absence of true unity and plurality (MAL 1).
