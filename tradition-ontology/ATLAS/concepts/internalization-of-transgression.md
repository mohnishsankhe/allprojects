## Definitions
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The Kula substances (wine, meat and the rest) are declared Śakti and Śiva and their union bliss; inner worship is dearer than outer; without the samaya discipline the wilful fall.
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The haṭha texts quote the Kaula saying about eating cow's flesh and drinking the liquor of the immortals and read it as the tongue turned back into the skull and the nectar that flows there.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The Yoginī tantras give an intentional language of substitute words for substances, places and acts, not to be revealed to the unconsecrated; conduct in the cremation ground is for the qualified.
