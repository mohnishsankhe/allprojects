## Definitions
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The Āḻvār becomes the girl in love — Parāṅkuśa-nāyakī, Parakāla-nāyakī — spoken of by her mother and friend, sending messengers, threatening to ride the maṭal; Āṇṭāḷ, a woman, sings her own bridal longing and dreams of her wedding.
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): The gopīs of Vraja are the example of devotion — but their love never lacked knowledge of his greatness and sought his happiness, unlike a paramour's.
- `lin:jagannatha`: Rādhā passes through the heroine's states — waiting, wronged, estranged by pride, going to the tryst, holding her lover in her power.
