# The object of negation (dgag bya)

**Category:** ultimate

## Names
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): dgag bya

## Definitions
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): What reasoning must refute is inherent existence — existence from the object's own side, not posited by conceptual designation; to negate less leaves the root of saṃsāra, to negate more destroys conventional existence and falls into nihilism.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): Gorampa: in ultimate analysis the four extremes themselves are negated, without Tsongkhapa's qualifier; conventional appearances are not established even conventionally under analysis.
