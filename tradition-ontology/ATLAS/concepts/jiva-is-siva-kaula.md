## Definitions
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The body is the temple and the jīva is Sadāśiva; bound by fetters or karma it is jīva, freed it is Sadāśiva — as paddy and husked rice (Kulārṇava 9.41-43); bound by the eight fetters one is a paśu, freed one is Maheśvara (13.90-91).
