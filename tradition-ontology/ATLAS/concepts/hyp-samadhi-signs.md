## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The text states that the yogin in samādhi hears no conch or drum and his body is like wood (4.106), stays as if dead and is liberated (4.107), is not eaten by Time nor bound by karma (4.108), knows no smell, taste, form, touch or sound (4.109), whose mind is neither asleep nor awake (4.110), who knows no cold, heat, pain or pleasure (4.111), stays as if asleep in waking without breath-movement (4.112), and cannot be slain by weapons or seized by mantras (4.113).
