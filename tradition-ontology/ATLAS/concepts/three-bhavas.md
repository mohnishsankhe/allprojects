## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The worshipper's disposition determines the mode of worship: paśu (pure, without the Kaula substances), vīra (heroic, with them), divya (divine); the Kālīvilāsa assigns divya to Satya–Tretā, vīra to Tretā–Dvāpara and allows only paśu in the Kali age (4.2-3; 6.10-16); the Buddha's instruction to Vasiṣṭha begins in the paśu disposition (Rudrayāmala 17.137); drinking is threefold by divya, vīra and paśu (Kulārṇava 7.94).
