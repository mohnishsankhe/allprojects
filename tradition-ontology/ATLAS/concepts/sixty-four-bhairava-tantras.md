## Definitions
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The Śrīkaṇṭhīya's eight groups of eight (Bhairava, Yāmala, Mata, Maṅgala, Cakra, Bahurūpa, Vāgīśa, Śikhā), each group 'spoken' by one of the Bhairavas; together 'the wheel of Sadāśiva'.
- [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md): The 'eight times eight' of Śiva's teaching, dominated by non-duality.
