## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Śrī is the Lord's inseparable consort, mother of the world and mediator.
- [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md): All-pervading (vibhu) like Viṣṇu, of the Lord's rank (Īśvara-koṭi), with him the means and the goal; the couple is one object of refuge.
- [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md): The mediator (puruṣakāra), not the means; commonly reported as atomic and of the rank of souls (jīva-koṭi), though never separate from the Lord.
