## Definitions
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): Before giving a mantra the guru tests its fit with the disciple by tables of letters — the akaḍama-cakra and others dividing mantras into 'accomplished, to be accomplished, well accomplished, hostile' (siddha, sādhya, susiddha, ari) — and by the 'debt and credit' (ṛṇa-dhana) reckoning between the letters of the mantra and of the disciple's name; the Padārthādarśa on ŚT 2 gathers several such methods.
