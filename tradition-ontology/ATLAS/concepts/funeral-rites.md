## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The āhitāgni is burned with his own fires and implements (ŚB 12.5.2); a funeral mound is later built (ŚB 13.8); the funeral mantras are collected in TA 6.
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Cremation with the Yama hymn, burial of infants, water-offerings, bone-collection, ten days' impurity, the first-year offerings and the uniting with the ancestors (ĀśGS 4.1–6; YS 3.1–2; MDh 5.59–68).
