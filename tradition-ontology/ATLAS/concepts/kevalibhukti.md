# Whether the omniscient eats (kevali-bhukti)

`cpt:kevalibhukti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** disputes

## Names

## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The omniscient, while his gross body lasts, takes food; hunger and the other hardships due to feeling-karma occur in him (Tattvārthasūtra 9.11 read literally); Mahāvīra recovered from illness on food brought from Revatī.
- [Yāpanīya](../lineages/yapaniya.md): Affirmed with arguments by Śākaṭāyana.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Denied: hunger and thirst are among the eighteen faults absent in the Jina; without deluding karma feeling-karma cannot cause hunger; the omniscient's body is sustained without morsel-food; he has no bodily pleasure or pain.
