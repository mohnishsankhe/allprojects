## Definitions
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): All dharmas are by nature inexpressible; words attach to designations and imagined natures, not to the thing's own nature, which is the object of non-conceptual cognition.
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): The particular is inexpressible; words and concepts reach only exclusions.
