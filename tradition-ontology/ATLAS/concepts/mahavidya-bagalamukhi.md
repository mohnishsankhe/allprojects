## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Golden-yellow, in yellow garments, seizing an enemy's tongue with her left hand and raising a club with her right, on a throne amid an ocean (standard dhyāna, recalled). Teaching: the power of paralysing (stambhana) enemies and speech; the one-faced Mahārudra, destroyer of the world, is her Bhairava (Toḍala 1.14).
