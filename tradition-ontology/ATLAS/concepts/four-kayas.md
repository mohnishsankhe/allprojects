## Definitions
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): Mind itself, unborn and empty, is the dharmakāya; its unceasing clear radiance the nirmāṇakāya; baseless great bliss, union, the saṃbhogakāya (Karṇatantravajrapada); their inseparability the svābhāvikakāya. Death, bardo and birth are brought onto the path as the three kāyas (bka' dpe phyi ma).
