## Definitions
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Devotion is ninefold, outer and inner — setting the breath in the liṅga and the liṅga in the breath with a still mind — and arises from grace as grace arises from it; its presence, not birth, makes the bhakta; it is the power of the soul as śakti is of the liṅga (manual tradition).
