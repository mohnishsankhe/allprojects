## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The Nāth order is divided into twelve panths, traditionally set in order by Gorakhnāth; lists commonly include the Satyanāth, Dharamnāth, Rām(ke), Nāṭeśvarī (Lakṣmaṇnāth), Kanthaḍ, Kapilānī, Vairāg (Bharthari), Mannāth, Āī, Pāgal, Dhajnāth and Gaṅgānāth panths, but the lists vary.
