# Discriminating knowledge (viveka, vivekakhyāti)

## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The discriminating knowledge of the manifest, the unmanifest and the knower (SK 2), accomplished by buddhi (SK 37) and culminating, through repeated practice of the principles, in the complete, pure and single knowledge 'I am not, not mine, not I' (SK 64); it is the direct realization of the distinctness of sattva and puruṣa (Vācaspati). Liberation comes from it alone (SS 3.23–25); it is reached by renouncing each principle with 'not this, not this' (SS 3.76).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The cognition of the difference between sattva and puruṣa (YBh 2.26); unwavering, it is the means of liberation (2.26); limbs of yoga make knowledge shine up to it (2.28); from it come sovereignty and omniscience (3.49); complete, it yields the cloud of dharma (4.29); the discerning mind bends toward kaivalya (4.26).
