## Definitions
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The teaching is kept from the undevoted, the deceitful and the cruel and given only to a devoted, tested disciple, even one tested for a year; the adept keeps his mark hidden (KJN 4.20; 5.35; 6.13–14; 14.4–11; 17.11; 22.6).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The haṭha knowledge is to be kept secret: kept secret it is potent, revealed it is powerless (HYP 1.11).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Haṭha must be kept secret — potent when hidden, impotent when revealed (HYP 1.11); the mudrās like a jewel box, told to no one (3.9, 3.18; GS 3.5); the yoga śāstra only for a true devotee (SS 1.19); vajrolī only for one as dear as one's life (DYŚ 137); powers never to be displayed (DYŚ 92–93); the Jyotsnā lists who may and may not receive haṭha.
