## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The Upaniṣads restrict their teaching: Yājñavalkya and Ārtabhāga discuss karma apart, not in public (BAU 3.2.13); the honey-doctrine is for the eldest son or a worthy pupil only, not for the whole earth full of wealth (ChU 3.11.5-6); the mantha rite only for son or pupil (BAU 6.3.12); not to be given to one not tranquil, not a son or pupil (ŚU 6.22; MaiU 6.29); only to those who have performed the head-vow (MuU 3.2.10).
