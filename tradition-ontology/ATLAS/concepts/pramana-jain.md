## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The five knowledges are themselves pramāṇa: sensory and scriptural are indirect, clairvoyance, mind-reading and omniscience direct, because only knowledge by the soul alone is direct (TS 1.9–12).
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Akalaṅka's system: direct cognition is conventionally direct (sense-perception) or truly direct; indirect cognition is memory, recognition, reasoning, inference and scripture (Laghīyastraya).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Valid cognition illuminates itself and its object and is free of contradiction (Nyāyāvatāra 1); it is the right ascertainment of an object (Pramāṇamīmāṃsā 1.1.2).
