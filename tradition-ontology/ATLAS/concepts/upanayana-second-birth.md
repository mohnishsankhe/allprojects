## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The teacher becomes pregnant with the pupil and brings him forth with the Sāvitrī on the third night (ŚB 11.5.4.12).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The Vedic birth in which the Sāvitrī is mother and the teacher father (MDh 2.170); the best birth, given by knowledge (ĀpDh 1.1.1.16–17); due at eight, eleven or twelve (MDh 2.36; ĀśGS 1.19).
