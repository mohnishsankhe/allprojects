## Definitions
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): The property or relation that fixes the scope of another property — e.g. the counterpositiveness of 'absence of pot' is delimited by potness and by the relation (conjunction) through which the pot is denied; central to exact definitions such as that of pervasion.
