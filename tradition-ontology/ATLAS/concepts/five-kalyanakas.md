## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Conception (descent into the womb), birth, renunciation, omniscience and nirvāṇa of each Tīrthaṅkara, at each of which the gods come to celebrate — at birth Indra bathes the child on Mount Meru; the events are re-enacted in the consecration of images (pañcakalyāṇaka-pratiṣṭhā).
