## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Nāth-derived anatomy: the three channels (iḍā, piṅgalā, suṣumnā, or Gaṅgā, Yamunā, Sarasvatī) meeting at trikuṭī, the six cakras, the tenth door, the sky-circle where nectar drips and the unstruck sound is heard; the body as a finely woven sheet.
- [Dādū Panth](../lineages/dadu-panth.md): Sundardās's yoga ullāsa sets out the cakras and the serpent power as a subordinate stage.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Six cakras below the eyes belong to the body (piṇḍ); the Sant practice starts at the eye centre and does not work through the lower cakras; above lie the regions.
