## Definitions
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): The Veda consists of injunction (vidhi), mantra, name (nāmadheya), prohibition (niṣedha) and explanatory praise (arthavāda); all are meaningful through injunctions directly or indirectly.
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The sūtra divides the Veda into mantra and brāhmaṇa (MS 2.1.32–33) and treats injunction, arthavāda, mantra, smṛti and name in adhyāya 1.
