## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The intellectual creation of fifty kinds: five errors (darkness, delusion, great delusion, gloom, blind gloom; 62 subdivisions), twenty-eight incapacities (11 injuries of the organs, 17 of buddhi), nine contentments and eight accomplishments (SK 46–51). Error, incapacity and contentment curb accomplishment (SK 51). With the ten fundamental topics they make the sixty topics of the Ṣaṣṭitantra.
