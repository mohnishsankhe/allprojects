## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Months are lunar (new-moon-ending in the south and west, full-moon-ending in the north) and named after the asterism of the full moon (Caitra from Citrā, Vaiśākha from Viśākhā …); a lunar month without a solar ingress is intercalary (adhika) and one with two is lost (kṣaya); solar months run from ingress to ingress (SS 1.13), and some regions (Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Bengal, Odisha) keep solar calendars; the yuga, year, month and day began together at Caitra's bright first (Āryabhaṭīya 3.11).
