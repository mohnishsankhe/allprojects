## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The Purāṇas teach the eight limbs with their own contents: the ethical lists vary (MkP 41.16-17; ŚiP 7.2.37.18-19), the support of dhāraṇā and dhyāna is the deity's form (VP 6.7; BhP 3.28, 11.14) or Śiva (ŚiP 7.2.39), and the goal is joining the mind with Brahman (VP 6.7.31) or nirvāṇa, Maheśa's state (LiP 1.8.5).
