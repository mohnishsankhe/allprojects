# The eternality of Āyurveda

## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Āyurveda is eternal because beginningless, because its marks are established by nature (like the heat of fire), and because the nature of things is eternal; its 'origin' means only its being understood and taught (Ca Sū 30.27); Brahmā 'remembered' it (AHS Sū 1.3).
