# The chain from false knowledge to liberation (NS 1.1.2)

## Names

## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): False knowledge → faults (attachment, aversion, delusion) → activity (good and bad acts of body, speech and mind, producing merit and demerit) → birth → pain; this unbroken series is saṃsāra. When knowledge of truth removes false knowledge, each successive link falls away and absolute liberation results (NS 1.1.2; NBh 1.1.2).
