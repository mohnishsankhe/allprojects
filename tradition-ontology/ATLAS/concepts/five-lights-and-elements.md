## Definitions
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): The ground's display arises as five-coloured lights, the radiance of the five wisdoms; not recognized, the lights solidify into the five elements (space, air, fire, water, earth) and the body; in realization the elements return to light, as in the rainbow body.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): In the Kunjed Gyalpo's setting the sambhogakāya retinues are named earth, water, fire, wind and space; at death the elements dissolve one into another.
