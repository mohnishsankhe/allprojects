## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Everything is won by rightly applied effort; fate is only one's former effort, overcome by present effort; reliance on fate is delusion.
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): In jyotiṣa, fate is karma made in another birth, and success needs fate and human effort together like farming; neither favourable planets nor rites alone suffice (Yogayātrā 1.3–6); the smṛti likewise calls fate the effort of a former body (YājñSm 1.349–351).
