## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Gupti is the proper restraint of activity (9.4); the five samitis are īryā, bhāṣā, eṣaṇā, ādāna-nikṣepa, utsarga (9.5).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Proper restraint of mind, speech and body, and care in walking, speaking, seeking alms, handling objects and excreting (TS 9.4–5); together the eight 'mothers of the doctrine' (Uttarādhyayana 24).
