## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): All who reach Brahman travel after death by the path of light — flame, day, bright fortnight, northern course, year, wind, sun, moon, lightning, then Varuṇa, Indra and Prajāpati as escorts — cross the Virajā and enter Vaikuṇṭha.
