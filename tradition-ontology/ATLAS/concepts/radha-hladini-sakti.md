## Definitions
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Rādhā is the hlādinī-śakti, the embodiment of mahābhāva, one in self with Kṛṣṇa yet in a separate body for the play.
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): Rādhā, daughter of Vṛṣabhānu, is worshipped on Kṛṣṇa's left as his eternal consort.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): Rādhā as Svāminī (mistress), emphasised in Viṭṭhalanātha's writings.
