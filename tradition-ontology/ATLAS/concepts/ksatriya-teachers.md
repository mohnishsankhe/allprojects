## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Several teachings are placed in the mouths of kṣatriyas who teach brahmins: Ajātaśatru teaches Gārgya Bālāki, noting that this reverses the natural order (BAU 2.1); Pravāhaṇa Jaivali says the five-fires knowledge had never before gone to brahmins (ChU 5.3.7; BAU 6.2.8); Aśvapati teaches the vaiśvānara self (ChU 5.11); Citra teaches Āruṇi (KauU 1.1); Janaka is taught by and rewards Yājñavalkya (BAU 4).
