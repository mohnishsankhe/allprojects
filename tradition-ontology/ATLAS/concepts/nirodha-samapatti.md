# The attainment of cessation

`cpt:nirodha-samapatti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** consciousness-states

## Names

## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The cessation of perception and feeling, beyond the formless spheres: verbal, bodily and mental formations cease in that order, while vitality and heat remain, unlike a dead person (MN 43; MN 44); reached through the progressive cessation of perception (DN 9); emerged from with the contacts of empty, signless and undirected (MN 44).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The temporary cessation of consciousness and mental factors, attained by non-returners and arahants who have the eight attainments, after preparatory resolutions.
