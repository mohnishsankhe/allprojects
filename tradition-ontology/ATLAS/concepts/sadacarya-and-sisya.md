## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): A true teacher belongs to the unbroken lineage, knows the doctrine, lives it, and teaches out of compassion without gain; the disciple approaches with humility, is initiated by the five sacraments, serves, keeps the secret mantras from the unfit and hands them on; Rāmānuja's public disclosure of the mantra is remembered as the compassion that overrides secrecy.
