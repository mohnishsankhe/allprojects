## Definitions
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The temples where the Lord abides in his image, each 'sung' (maṅgaḷāśāsana) by one or more Āḻvārs — 106 on earth with the Milk Ocean and Paramapadam making 108; the Āḻvārs long to be born even as a step or a fish at Tiruvēṅkaṭam and see the Lord at Śrīraṅgam from feet to face.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The temples sung by the Āḻvārs, where the Lord abides as the image; visiting and praying for his welfare (maṅgaḷāśāsana) at them is a Śrīvaiṣṇava practice.
