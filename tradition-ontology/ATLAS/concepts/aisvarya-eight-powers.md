# Power (aiśvarya) and the eight powers

## Names
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): aiśvarya

## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Power is a sāttvika disposition of buddhi (SK 23) giving non-obstruction (SK 45); the commentators list becoming minute, great, light, reaching, irresistible will, lordship, control and going where one wishes (Gauḍapāda on SK 23). Warnings: attachment to the powers is the error 'delusion', through which gods like Indra fall back (Gauḍapāda on SK 48); gaining the powers is not liberation, since they end (SS 5.82; 4.32). Yogic powers are nonetheless not to be denied (SS 5.128).
