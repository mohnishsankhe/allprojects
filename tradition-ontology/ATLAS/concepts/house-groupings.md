## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Angles (kendra: 1, 4, 7, 10), succedents (paṇaphara: 2, 5, 8, 11) and cadents (āpoklima: 3, 6, 9, 12); trines (1, 5, 9); increasing houses (upacaya: 3, 6, 10, 11); bad houses (duḥsthāna: 6, 8, 12); death-dealing houses (2 and 7) (BJ 1.15–18; BPHS 44). Later practitioners also group the houses by the four aims of life (dharma 1-5-9, artha 2-6-10, kāma 3-7-11, mokṣa 4-8-12).
