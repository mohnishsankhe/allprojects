## Definitions
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): The name of the Tathāgata of Unhindered Light breaks all ignorance and fulfils all aspirations when called in accord with reality (Tanluan); hearing the name with joy even for one thought gains unsurpassed merit.
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): The name gathers all the virtues of the Buddha's inner realization and outer activity, as 'house' contains all its timbers (Senchakushū 3).
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): The auspicious name embodying all virtues is the great practice.
