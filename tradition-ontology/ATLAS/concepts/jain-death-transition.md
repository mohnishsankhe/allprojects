## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The lifespan karma for the next life is bound once during the present life; at death the soul passes in transit to the destiny so fixed; the lifespans of the spontaneously born and those in their last body cannot be cut short (TS 2.25–30, 2.53, 8.10). The soul-colouring (leśyā) at death is said to accompany rebirth (Śvetāmbara canon, recalled).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Signs foretelling the time of death are described so that the practitioner can prepare (YŚ 5).
