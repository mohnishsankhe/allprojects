## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): ŚU 1.2 lists time, inherent nature, fixed order (niyati), chance, the elements, the womb and the person as causes to be considered, and rejects them singly and in combination; ŚU 1.3 and 6.1 answer that the cause is God's own power and greatness.
