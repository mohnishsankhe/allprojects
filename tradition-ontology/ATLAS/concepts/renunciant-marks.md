## Definitions
- [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md): Staff (one or three), water-pot, sling, strainer, ochre or no cloth, shaven head, topknot and thread kept or abandoned; the texts differ on which marks are kept, and many say the true marks are inner (staff of knowledge, thread of the Self) and that marks do not liberate.
