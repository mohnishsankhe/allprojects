# The inner controller (antaryāmin)

`cpt:antaryamin` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ultimate

## Names
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): antaryāmin

## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The self who dwells in each element, faculty and being, other than it, unknown to it, having it as body and controlling it from within — the unseen seer, the immortal (BAU 3.7.3-23); 'the inner controller, the source of all' (MāU 6).
