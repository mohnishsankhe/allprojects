## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Her form is imagined according to her qualities: black because all colours — all beings — vanish into her; the crescent for immortality; three eyes as moon, sun and fire; boon and fearlessness to protect; seated on a red lotus as ruling the worlds of rajas; pure consciousness watching Time play (Mahānirvāṇa 13.5-13; 5.140).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): The devotional songs question and affirm her blackness: black to the world, she lights the lotus of the heart (Kamalākānta).
