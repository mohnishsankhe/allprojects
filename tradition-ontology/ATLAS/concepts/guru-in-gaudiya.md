## Definitions
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): One initiating guru and many instructing gurus; the Lord within (caitya-guru); the guru is to be honoured as Kṛṣṇa's representative; qualification is knowledge of Kṛṣṇa, not birth; guru and disciple should examine each other.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): Initiation is given by the ācārya's descendants (the Gosvāmī houses); the disciple's relation is to the Lord through the guru's family.
