# Aspiring and engaging bodhicitta

## Definitions
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): The awakening mind is twofold, as aspiration and as engagement, like the wish to go and actually going (BCA 1.15–16).
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): Aspiring bodhicitta does not grow without the vow of the engaging mind, which requires prātimokṣa as its basis.
