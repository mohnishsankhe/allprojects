# Physical and psychological sex (dravya-veda, bhāva-veda)

`cpt:dravya-bhava-veda` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** body-energy

## Names

## Definitions
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Sex is twofold: the bodily marks and the rise of the karma of sexual desire, which need not coincide; liberation may be reached with any psychological sex but only with the male body — the Digambara key to reading canonical passages about women among the liberated.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Śvetāmbaras accept the distinction but hold that liberation is possible with the female body.
