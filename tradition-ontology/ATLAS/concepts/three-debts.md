## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): A brāhmaṇa is born owing studentship to the seers, sacrifice to the gods and offspring to the ancestors (TS 6.3.10.5); ŚB adds hospitality to men (1.7.2.1–6).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The debts must be paid before seeking liberation (MDh 6.35–37).
