## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Each planet has its own image-material, colour, mount and weapons (BPHS 84.4–13), its fuel-stick and gift (YājñSm 1.295–306), and in later texts its gem: ruby (Sun), pearl (Moon), coral (Mars), emerald (Mercury), yellow sapphire (Jupiter), diamond (Venus), blue sapphire (Saturn), hessonite (Rāhu) and cat's-eye (Ketu).
