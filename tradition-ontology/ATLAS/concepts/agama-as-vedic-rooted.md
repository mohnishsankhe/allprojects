## Definitions
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The Pāñcarātra is the Lord's own word and of Vedic root; its followers are brahmins.
- [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md): Vaikhānasa worship follows the path of the śruti in every rite and is performed with Vedic mantras.
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Tantras that teach the worship of Rāma, Kṛṣṇa, Nṛsiṃha, Rudra, Paraśiva or Sundarī are rooted in Upaniṣads such as the Rāmatāpanī and are beyond doubt (Bhāskararāya, Setubandha introduction).
