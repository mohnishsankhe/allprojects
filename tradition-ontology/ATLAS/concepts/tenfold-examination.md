## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The patient is examined by constitution, morbid state, tissue-excellence, compactness, measure, suitability, mind, capacity for food, capacity for exercise and age (Ca Vi 8.94); distinct is the list of ten things to be examined before action — cause, instrument, source and effect, its fruit, continuation, place, time, undertaking, means (Vi 8.84).
