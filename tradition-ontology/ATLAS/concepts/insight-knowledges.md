## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The Visuddhimagga's purification by knowledge and vision of the way consists of eight knowledges and conformity; the Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha lists ten insight knowledges (adding comprehension); the later tradition counts sixteen by adding the discernment of mentality-materiality, of conditions, change-of-lineage, path, fruit and reviewing.
- [Mahāsi Sayadaw tradition (the 'new Burmese method')](../lineages/mahasi.md): The stages as they unfold in noting practice, described in The Progress of Insight.
