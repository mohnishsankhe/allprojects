## Definitions
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): No renunciation is required: grinding, gardening, potting, trading and service become worship when joined to the name; the Lord comes to the house.
- [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md): Worldly life (prapañca) is to be lived with vigilance and effort alongside the highest aim.
