# The mind (manas) as one and atomic

`cpt:manas-atomic` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mind

## Names
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): manas

## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): One per body, atomic; its mark is the non-simultaneity of cognitions; apparent simultaneity is like the firebrand circle, due to its swift movement (NS 1.1.16, 3.2.56–59); cognition belongs to the self, not the mind (NS 3.2.19).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): An eternal atomic substance, one in each body because efforts and cognitions are not simultaneous (VS 3.2.1–3, 7.1.30 C); its first motion at creation is caused by adṛṣṭa (VS 5.2.14).
