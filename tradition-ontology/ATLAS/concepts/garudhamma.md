## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The eight rules accepted by Mahāpajāpatī as her ordination: a nun ordained a century bows to a monk ordained that day; no rains without monks; fortnightly asking; invitation and penance before both Saṅghas; ordination by both after two years' training; no abuse of monks; no admonishing of monks (Cv 10.1.4; AN 8.51).
