## Definitions
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): No god is beyond the guru and no mantra beyond his sandals; the guru is to be tested and so is the disciple.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The innate is known only from the true guru; without a guru there is no supreme siddhi.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Only one who has a guru drinks the nectar in the upturned well of the sky.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The true guru (satguru) gives the Name and is honoured above God as the means to him.
