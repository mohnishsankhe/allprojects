## Definitions
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Non-duality means non-separation (ananya), not identity: the Lord is one with souls, other than them, and together with them — as the vowel 'a' in all letters, the soul in the body, the eye's light with the sun's. In release the soul, its malas powerless, abides in Śiva enjoying his bliss, neither lost nor equal as a second agent.
