## Definitions
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Dharmakīrti argues at times as though objects were external (causing images in cognition) and at times from the standpoint of cognition-only, where cognition is non-dual; later commentators (and Tibetan doxographers, who call the school 'Sautrāntika' or 'Yogācāra' accordingly) treat these as successive levels.
