## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The mind is the battlefield: restless elephant, thief, bird; defeat and victory are the mind's; the Name recited while it wanders is not remembrance; when it dies the Lord is found.
- [Dādū Panth](../lineages/dadu-panth.md): Sundardās: the mind is thief, monkey, mad elephant, tamed only by the guru's word and discrimination.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): The mind is Kāl's agent, attached to the soul up to Trikuṭī; the soul must be freed from it by the sound current.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): The mind addressed in every song ('o mind'), to be turned to truth and to the Man.
