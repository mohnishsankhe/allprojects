## Definitions
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): Four great schools founded by disciples of Gampopa and his nephew — Karma, Tsalpa, Barom, Phagdru — and eight lesser founded by disciples of Phagmodrupa — Drikung, Drukpa, Taklung, Trophu, Martsang, Yelpa, Yazang, Shugseb. 'Great' and 'lesser' refer to the generation of origin, not to size.
