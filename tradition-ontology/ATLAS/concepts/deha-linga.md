## Definitions
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The true liṅga is within the body — the all-pervading one above the lotuses in which all dissolves; liṅgas of stone, wood or clay are to be abandoned (KJN 3.9–31).
