## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The four destructive (ghātiyā) karmas — knowledge-obscuring, perception-obscuring, deluding, obstructive — obscure the soul's own qualities; their destruction yields omniscience (TS 10.1). The four non-destructive — feeling, lifespan, body-making, status — continue in the living omniscient until death.
