## Definitions
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Śiva has no body of māyā; for worship and grace he is given a body of mantras — the five brahma-mantras (Īśāna, Tatpuruṣa, Aghora, Vāmadeva, Sadyojāta) as his faces and the six limb-mantras — made of the pure śakti; the mantras themselves are conscious beings in office.
