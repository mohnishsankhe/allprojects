# Conventional and ultimate truth (sammuti- and paramattha-sacca)

**Category:** ultimate

## Names

## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The Buddha taught both in conventional terms (persons, beings) and in ultimate terms (aggregates, dhammas); both are true on their own level; the Abhidhamma is teaching in ultimate terms. 'Chariot' is a mere term; ultimately only its parts, and only mentality-materiality, are found.
- [Thai Forest tradition (Kammaṭṭhāna forest tradition)](../lineages/thai-forest.md): Conventions (sammuti) must be used and respected, but the heart that has seen through them is released (vimutti) from them.
