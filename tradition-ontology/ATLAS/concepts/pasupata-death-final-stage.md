## Definitions
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): In the fourth stage the ascetic lives in the cremation ground until the body falls, remembering Rudra; the fifth stage is without body, its place Rudra himself; the liberated brahmin does not return.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Dying at Kāyāvarohaṇa leads to Rudra's world (Kūrma P.).
