## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Karma is subtle matter drawn in through the soul's activity and bound by its passions, adhering to all its space-points (TS 8.2, 8.24); the soul's passional states are 'psychic karma', the particles 'material karma'.
- [Adhyātma movement (Adhyātma-mata)](../lineages/adhyatma-jain.md): By the ultimate standpoint the soul is not the doer of material karma; it is the doer only of its own states.
