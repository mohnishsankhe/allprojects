## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Parā: abiding in non-duality across all the senses' outflows; parāparā: meditative dissolution of outer into the inner non-dual abode; aparā: worship of the cakra, done even by Śiva and by all knowers because it yields the conviction of non-difference (YH 3.2-3 with the Dīpikā).
