## Definitions
- [Rasa Śāstra (the science of mercury) and the Raseśvara-darśana](../lineages/rasa-sastra.md): Knowledge of identity with Śiva in an ageless, deathless body (Rasārṇava 1.8); liberation after death is unseen and 'meaningless' (1.9-13); liberation from knowledge, knowledge from practice, practice in a stable body (Rasahṛdaya 1.10); siddhas have attained it in mercurial bodies (SDS 11-12).
