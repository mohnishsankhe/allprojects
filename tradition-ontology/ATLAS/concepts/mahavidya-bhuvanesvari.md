## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Radiant as the rising sun, three-eyed, crowned with the crescent, holding noose and goad and showing boon and fearlessness (standard dhyāna, recalled). Teaching: queen of the worlds and of space; Tryambaka is her Bhairava because he delights in her in heaven, earth and the underworld (Toḍala 1.9-10); in the Devī Gītā the supreme Goddess appears as Bhuvaneśvarī.
