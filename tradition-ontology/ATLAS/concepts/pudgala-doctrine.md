# The doctrine of the person (pudgalavāda)

`cpt:pudgala-doctrine` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** self

## Names

## Definitions
- [Pudgalavāda](../lineages/pudgalavada.md): A real person, neither the same as nor different from the aggregates, designated in dependence on them as fire on fuel; inexpressible; the bearer of the burden; that which transmigrates.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): No self: only aggregates conditioned by defilement and karma go on like a flame.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): The person is a designation of the series; no liberation for those who imagine a self as a separate entity.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The person is not found as a real and ultimate fact.
