## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Every being contains two portions, of the individual soul and of the supreme Self; in the planets, the gods' powers and the full avatāras (Rāma, Kṛṣṇa, Narasiṃha, Varāha) the supreme-Self portion predominates, in men the jīva-portion; the avatāras come forth from the planets and return into them, and beings finally merge into the unmanifest (BPHS 1.21–24; 2.1–10).
