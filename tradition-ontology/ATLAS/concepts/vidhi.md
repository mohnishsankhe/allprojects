## Definitions
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The Vedic statement that impels to action; the sole means of knowing dharma, able to make known the future, subtle and remote.
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): What it conveys is bhāvanā, the prompting force (Kumārila); for Maṇḍana, that the act is a means to the desired (iṣṭasādhanatā).
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): What it conveys is the command / what is to be done (niyoga, kārya).
