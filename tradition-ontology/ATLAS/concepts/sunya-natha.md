## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): In the HYP suṣumnā is 'the path of the void' (3.4); the breath dissolves in the void when śakti wakes (4.10–12); the nāda stages pass through śūnya, atiśūnya and mahāśūnya (4.70–75); 'empty within, empty without, like a pot in space; full within, full without, like a pot in the ocean' (4.56); samādhi is 'void and not void' (śūnyāśūnya, 4.3).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The void is the path of the breath become a royal road (HYP 3.3); the mind 'void of void' (KJN 14.84); the principle known as supportless and without elaboration (GŚ 92); kuṇḍalinī is the pedestal of the liṅga of the void (Jñāneśvarī 6.273).
