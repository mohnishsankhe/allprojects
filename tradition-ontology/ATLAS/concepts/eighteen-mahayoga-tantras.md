## Definitions
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): The tantra section of Mahāyoga: five root tantras of body, speech, mind, qualities and activity (Sangs rgyas mnyam sbyor, Zla gsang thig le, Gsang ba 'dus pa, Dpal mchog dang po, Karma mā le), five practice tantras, five activity tantras and two supplementary tantras (the Rnam snang sgyu 'phrul drva ba and the Noose of Method), with the Guhyagarbha as their summary root.
