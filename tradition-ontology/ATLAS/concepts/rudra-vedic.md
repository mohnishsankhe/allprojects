## Names
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Rudra, Tryambaka, Paśupati, Bhava, Śarva, Nīlagrīva, Śiva (epithet)

## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Rudra is feared as the archer whose wrath and arrows harm men and cattle and is begged to turn them away, and loved as the best of physicians with a thousand remedies and a healing hand; he is lord of cattle (Paśupati), Bhava and Śarva, blue-necked; the Śatarudrīya pays homage to him as present in all places, beings and callings and names his auspicious, non-terrible body (śivā tanūḥ) and him as 'śiva, śivatara'; the Keśin drank with him from the cup.
