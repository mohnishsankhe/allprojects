## Definitions
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The single reality, the 'hero beyond the Kula', in which all dharmas dissolve like rivers in the ocean; beyond all supports, aims, channels, breath-practices and rituals; neither near nor far; beyond 'is' and 'is not'; the one abiding in it is every god and every teacher (Akulavīra A.3–41, B.52–54).
