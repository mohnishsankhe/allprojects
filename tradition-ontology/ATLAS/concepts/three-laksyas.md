## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Inner (kuṇḍalinī, inner sound, blue light between the eyes), outer (colours and lights in space before the nose and above the head) and intermediate (the sequence of voids) — the objects of tāraka-yoga in the Advayatāraka and Maṇḍalabrāhmaṇa.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Inner, outer and middle objects of fixed attention (GŚ-Briggs 13; SSP 2).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): In the haṭha body-map the three targets (inner, outer, middle) are among the things the yogin must know in his own body (Vivekamārtaṇḍa family); śāmbhavī mudrā is attention on an inner target with the gaze outward (HYP 4.36).
