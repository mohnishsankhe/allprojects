## Definitions
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): Placing, settling firmly, re-settling, settling closely, taming, calming, fully calming, one-pointing and concentrating; with six powers (hearing, reflection, mindfulness, awareness, vigour, familiarity) and four attentions (forced, interrupted, uninterrupted, spontaneous); the five faults and eight remedies of the Madhyāntavibhāga are the obstacles and antidotes along the way.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): Placing, continuous placing, repeated placing, close placing, taming, pacifying, thorough pacifying, one-pointedness and equipoise; achieved through six powers and four attentions, with the five faults and eight antidotes, following the Śrāvakabhūmi and Madhyāntavibhāga.
