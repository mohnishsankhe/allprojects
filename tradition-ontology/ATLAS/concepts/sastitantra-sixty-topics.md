## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The SK's topics are those of the whole Ṣaṣṭitantra without its tales and polemics (SK 72). The sixty are ten fundamental topics — pradhāna's existence, oneness, purposiveness; puruṣa's distinctness; being for another; plurality of puruṣas; separation; conjunction; the remaining function; non-agency — and the fifty of the creation of cognitions (Yuktidīpikā introductory verses; Māṭhara on SK 72; Kramadīpikā; Vācaspati quoting the Rājavārttika).
