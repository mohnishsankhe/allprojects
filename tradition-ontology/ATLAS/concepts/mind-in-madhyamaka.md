## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The Abhidharma analysis of mind — consciousness arising from faculty and object (MMK 3.7), the aggregates, intention (17.2–3), the afflictions (23) — is accepted as conventional description; under analysis the mind is not found as a real entity, and the domain of mind ceases in the nature of things (18.7).
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): No storehouse consciousness and no reflexive awareness are needed even conventionally; the continuum of the aggregates suffices for karma (MA 6; BCA 9.73).
