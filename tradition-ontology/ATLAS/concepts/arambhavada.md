# New beginning (ārambha): the effect does not pre-exist

## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The effect is not existent, non-existent-and-existent etc. before production, but is known to have been non-existent (NS 4.1.48–50); existence does not arise from non-existence (NS 4.1.14–18); an effect is the counterpositive of a prior absence (Tarkasaṅgraha).
