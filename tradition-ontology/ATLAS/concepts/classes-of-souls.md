## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Bound (baddha), freed (mukta) and eternal (nitya); bound souls seek enjoyment or release, and seekers of release seek kaivalya or the Lord, by bhakti or prapatti.
- [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md): Five kinds: eternal, freed, bound, isolated (kevala, those in kaivalya) and seekers of release (mumukṣu).
