## Definitions
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Earth, water, fire and air consist of eternal, partless, imperceptible atoms of spherical size (VS 4.1.1, 4.1.6–7, 7.1.26 C); at creation, atoms set in motion by adṛṣṭa combine into dyads and so on up to the gross elements (Praśastapāda); the triad (trasareṇu) is the smallest visible thing (later manuals).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Division of wholes ends in atoms, beyond the truṭi; atoms are partless, since ākāśa's 'penetration' does not apply to non-products and the alternative is an infinite regress (NS 4.2.16–25).
