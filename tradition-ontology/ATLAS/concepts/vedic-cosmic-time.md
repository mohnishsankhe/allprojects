## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The year is the twelve-spoked unaging wheel of ṛta with 720 sons (days and nights) (RV 1.164.11, 1.164.48); it was born from the ocean after ṛta and satya (10.190.2); the Disposer fashioned sun and moon 'as before' (10.190.3); generations pass while the dawns return (1.113.11); in the Atharvaveda Time itself is the first god (19.53–54).
