## Definitions
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): Dharmakāya, saṃbhogakāya and nirmāṇakāya with the svābhāvikakāya; the two form bodies arise through aspiration and compassion (Queen Dohā v64–68).
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The generation and completion stages yield the three bodies (Tōh 2292 no. 25); some systems add the body of great bliss.
