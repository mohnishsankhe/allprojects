## Definitions
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): 'What is saṃsāra is certainly nirvāṇa' (People Dohā v122); not conceiving them as two is the mode of the dharmadhātu (Queen Dohā v10–11).
- [Ārya tradition of the Guhyasamāja](../lineages/arya-guhyasamaja.md): Union is where saṃsāra and nirvāṇa become one by abandoning the two concepts (Pañcakrama 5.2).
