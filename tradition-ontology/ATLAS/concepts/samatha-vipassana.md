## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Calm and insight developed together — insight preceded by calm, calm by insight, or yoked evenly (AN 4.170; MN 149); calm and insight are the path to the unconditioned (SN 43.12); no jhāna without wisdom, no wisdom without jhāna (Dhp 372); the jhānas as a basis for insight into the aggregates (MN 64; AN 9.36).
