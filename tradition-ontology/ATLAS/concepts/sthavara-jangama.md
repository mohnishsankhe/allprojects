# The fixed and the moving (sthāvara and jaṅgama)

`cpt:sthavara-jangama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** disputes

## Names
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): sthāvara / jaṅgama

## Definitions
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): Temples, images and installed liṅgas are the fixed, which perish; the devotee's body and the wandering devotee are the moving, which endures and is the true temple; worship of stone while despising the living devotee is hypocrisy.
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The SSM ranks worship on the palm above worship on a pedestal, the inner liṅga above stone liṅgas and calls those who cling only to the outer liṅga deluded, yet forbids using the nirmālya of fixed liṅgas and bids their protection.
