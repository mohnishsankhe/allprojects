# Neither one nor many (ekānekaviyoga)

**Category:** ultimate

## Names
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): ekānekaviyoga

## Definitions
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): Whatever is held real lacks own-nature, being neither truly one nor truly many, like a reflection (MAL 1); a thing without real unity cannot be a real plurality of such units.
