## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Configurations formed by the pattern of all the planets: the Yavanas counted 1,800; Varāhamihira gives the three āśraya yogas (rajju, musala, nala), the two dala yogas (mālā, sarpa — taught by Parāśara), and the ākṛti and saṅkhyā yogas — thirty-two in all; they give their fruits in all periods (BJ 12).
