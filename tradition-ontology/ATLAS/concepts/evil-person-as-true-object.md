## Definitions
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): The vow was made for beings full of passions who cannot free themselves; those who rely on their own good are not its object until they turn; hence 'even the good person attains birth, how much more the evil person'. This is not licence: poison is not to be taken because there is an antidote.
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Even the evil-doer of the lowest grade is born by ten recitations at death (Contemplation Sūtra, Daochuo).
