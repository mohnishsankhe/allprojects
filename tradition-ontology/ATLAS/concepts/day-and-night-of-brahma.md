## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Brahmā's day and night each last a thousand ages; at day beings emerge from the unmanifest and at night dissolve into it, again and again (BhG 8.17–19); at the end of a kalpa beings enter the Lord's prakṛti (9.7).
