# Annihilationism (ucchedavāda)

## Definitions
- [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md): (As reported) Fool and wise alike are annihilated at the breaking of the body; the person is made of four elements that return to their masses.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): One of the sixty-two grounds (seven forms) and one of the two extremes avoided by the middle.
- [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md): (As reported) Death is the end; the body burnt does not return.
