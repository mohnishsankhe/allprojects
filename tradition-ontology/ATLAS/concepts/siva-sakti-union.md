# The union of Śiva and Śakti (śiva-śakti-aikya, sāmarasya)

**Category:** ultimate
**Members:** Śiva as prakāśa, Śakti as vimarśa, their sāmarasya

## Names

## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The Goddess's form is the oneness of Śiva and Śakti (LSN 183); Tripurā is the fusion of light and self-awareness (Dīpikā on YH 1.6); Śiva joined to Śakti can create, without her he cannot stir (SL 1).
- [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md): Śiva and Śakti are equal in primacy, with fivefold equality (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 41).
- [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md): Their union is the great point and the 'I' (KKV 4-5).
