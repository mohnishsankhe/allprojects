## Definitions
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): After the Buddha's passing the Dharma declines through periods (the Candragarbha's five five-hundred-year periods; true, semblance and final ages); teaching must fit the time; in the last age calling the name is the main practice.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): The true and semblance ages are over; in the last age there are only nominal monks; the Larger Sūtra alone remains after the scriptures perish.
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): Following Daochuo, the holy path does not suit the last age; the nenbutsu alone remains after the scriptures perish (Senchakushū 1, 6).
