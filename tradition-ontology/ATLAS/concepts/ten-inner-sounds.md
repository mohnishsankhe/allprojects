## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Ciṇī, ciñciṇī, bell, conch, lute, cymbal, flute, mṛdaṅga, bherī, thunder — heard in order, each with its effect; the tenth is practised (Haṃsa 4).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The HYP gives its own sequence: first ocean, thunder, kettledrum and cymbals; in the middle drum, conch, bell and horn; at the end tinkling bells, flute, vīṇā and bee (4.84–86), and one sound for each stage (4.70–77).
