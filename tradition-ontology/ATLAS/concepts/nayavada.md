## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): A thing is known through valid knowledge and standpoints (TS 1.6); the seven standpoints — naigama, saṃgraha, vyavahāra, ṛjusūtra, śabda, samabhirūḍha, evambhūta — each grasp one aspect (1.33); the two root standpoints are substance-oriented and mode-oriented; each taken alone is false, all together right (Sanmati). Every rival doctrine is a standpoint made absolute.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Five standpoints with subdivisions (Śv TS 1.34–35); Mallavādin's wheel of twelve views; Yaśovijaya's refuge in all standpoints.
