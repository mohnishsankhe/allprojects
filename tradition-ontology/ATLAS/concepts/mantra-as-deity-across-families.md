## Definitions
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Śiva's body is made of mantras (the five brahma-mantras as his faces); the mantra is the deity, not a name for it.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The deity arises from and dissolves into its seed syllable; the mantra is the deity's speech and essence.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The Namaskāra mantra is the embodiment of the five supreme beings and is meditated on as letters on lotuses in word-based meditation (padastha).
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): The mantra-manuals treat letters and seeds as the bodies of their deities.
