## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Brahman is bliss (TU 3.6); the self made of bliss is the innermost of the five selves (TU 2.5); 'the essence' (raso vai saḥ) — who could live if this bliss were not in space? (TU 2.7); the highest bliss of the brahma-world on a fraction of which beings live (BAU 4.3.32); the knower of brahman's bliss fears nothing (TU 2.4, 2.9).
