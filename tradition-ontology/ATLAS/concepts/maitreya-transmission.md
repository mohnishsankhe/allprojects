## Definitions
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): Maitreya taught Asaṅga, who taught Vasubandhu (the 'lineage of vast conduct'); in Tibet the teachings came through Sajjana to Ngok Loden Sherab (the explanatory, analytic transmission) and to Tsen Kawoche (the meditative transmission); two texts were, by tradition, rediscovered by Maitrīpa.
