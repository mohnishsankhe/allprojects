## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Ignorance, I-am-ness, attachment, aversion and clinging to life (YS 2.3), which Vyāsa calls the five errors (viparyaya) (YBh 2.3, 1.8); ignorance is the field of the rest, each of which may be dormant, attenuated, interrupted or fully active (YS 2.4). They are the root of the karma-store (YS 2.12) and are thinned by kriyā-yoga (YS 2.2).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Ignorance, I-am-ness, attachment, aversion and clinging to life (2.3) — the five errors (YBh 2.3), called tamas, moha, mahāmoha, tāmisra and andhatāmisra (YBh 1.8); ignorance is their field (2.4); they are the root of the karmic residue (2.12); gross forms are removed by meditation, subtle ones by involution (2.10–11); they cease through the cloud of dharma (4.30).
