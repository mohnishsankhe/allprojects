## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7.4–7: the Lord's lower nature is eightfold — earth, water, fire, air, space, mind, intellect and the sense of 'I' (ahaṅkāra); his higher nature has become the living being and upholds the world. All beings have these two as their source; he is the origin and dissolution of the whole world; nothing is higher than him, and all is strung on him like gems on a thread.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The Lord's lower nature is eightfold — earth, water, fire, air, space, mind, intellect, ego — and his higher nature is the life-element that upholds the world; both are the womb of all beings (BhG 7.4–6); he sends forth beings resting on his prakṛti (9.8).
