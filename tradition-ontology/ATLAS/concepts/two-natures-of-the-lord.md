## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The Lord's lower nature is eightfold — earth, water, fire, air, space, mind, intellect, ego — and his higher nature is the life-element that upholds the world; both are the womb of all beings (BhG 7.4–6); he sends forth beings resting on his prakṛti (9.8).
