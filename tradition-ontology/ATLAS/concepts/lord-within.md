## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The Lord is in every body as musk in the deer's navel, oil in sesame, fire in flint; he is 'in the breath of the breath', not in temple, mosque, Kaaba or Kailāsa.
- [Ravidāsī (Ravidassia) tradition](../lineages/ravidasi.md): Between you and me what difference? As gold and bracelet, water and wave.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): The neighbour in the mirror-city, near yet a hundred thousand leagues away; the one who speaks but is not seen.
