## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The ancestors eat monthly, drink svadhā and have the moon as light (ŚB 2.4.2.2); the paths of the fathers lead to cremation grounds for those who desired offspring (ĀpDh 2.9.23).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Primeval deities of several classes, satisfied by śrāddha offerings for months or forever (MDh 3.192–199; 3.267–272).
