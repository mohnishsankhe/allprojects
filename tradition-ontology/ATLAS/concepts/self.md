# The self (ātman)

## Names
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): ātman

## Definitions
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): A self distinct from body and cognition exists: shown by desire, memory and recognition; known to itself; taught by 'not this, not this' (the Vṛttikāra in Śabara).
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): The eternal agent and enjoyer whose existence Śabara proved against unbelief; knowing it enables rites whose fruit lies in another world, and the Upaniṣads make that knowledge firm.
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): Distinct from cognition, senses and body; all-pervading, permanent, many; not self-luminous, it appears as subject in each cognition and does not appear in dreamless sleep.
