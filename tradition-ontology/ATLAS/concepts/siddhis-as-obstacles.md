# The powers as obstacles in samādhi (3.37)

`cpt:siddhis-as-obstacles` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Category:** obstacles

## Names
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The powers as obstacles in samādhi (3.37)

## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The powers arising from saṃyama, beginning with prātibha (3.36), are obstacles (upasarga) for a mind in samādhi and powers (siddhi) for the outgoing mind (3.37). At 3.51 the text adds a warning: when invited by those of high stations the yogin is to feel neither attachment nor pride, for heedlessness would find a gap and stir the afflictions, and then the undesirable would follow again.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The powers are obstacles in samādhi and powers only in emergence (3.37); kaivalya comes from dispassion even toward the highest (3.50); invited by the celestials, the yogin should form neither attachment nor pride (3.51, YBh 3.51); kaivalya does not require the powers (YBh 3.55).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The many powers, the nether regions and alchemy, even if obtained, are not to be grasped: they are nets leading down (Akulavīra B.83–84).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The DYŚ lists the powers that arise in the ghaṭa stage (clairaudience, clairvoyance, speed, perfect speech, invisibility, alchemy, flight) and says they are obstacles to the great accomplishment: the yogin should not delight in them, should hide them and live as if dull or deaf, lest disciples crowd round and he forget his practice (84–95). The HYP nonetheless promises the eight powers from the mudrās (3.8, 3.130).
