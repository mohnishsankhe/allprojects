## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Time imagined as a person: the signs are his limbs from head to feet (BJ 1.4), the Sun his self, the Moon his mind and so on (BJ 2.1); his ocean cannot be crossed even in mind by one who is not a seer (BS 2.4). An affliction to a sign afflicts the corresponding limb of the native.
