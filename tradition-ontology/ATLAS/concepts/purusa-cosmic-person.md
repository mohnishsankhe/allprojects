## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The thousand-headed Person who is all this, past and future, of whom one quarter is all beings and three quarters the immortal in heaven; sacrificed by the gods, he became the world, the Vedas, the varṇas, sun, moon and gods (RV 10.90); 'knowing him alone one goes beyond death' (VS 31.18).
