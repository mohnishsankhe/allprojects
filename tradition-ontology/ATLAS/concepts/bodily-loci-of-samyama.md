## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Places named in this part of the text: the navel wheel, the heart lotus and the light in the head (bhāṣya 3.1); the pit of the throat (3.30), the tortoise channel (3.31), the light in the head (3.32), the heart (3.34), the navel wheel (3.29). The text records what saṃyama on each is said to give; no instruction is given here.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Places for binding the mind: the navel wheel, the heart-lotus, the light in the head, the tips of nose and tongue (YBh 3.1); the throat-well, the tortoise channel in the chest (3.30–31); the heart, 'the small lotus-house in the city of Brahman' (3.34); on the navel wheel one knows the three humours and seven constituents (YBh 3.29).
