## Definitions
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): The bodhisattva path has a difficult way of strenuous effort (like walking overland) and an easy way of faith and calling buddhas' names (like sailing); Tanluan identifies the easy way with birth by the Buddha's vow.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): Shinran's first master Nāgārjuna 'taught the easy practice'.
