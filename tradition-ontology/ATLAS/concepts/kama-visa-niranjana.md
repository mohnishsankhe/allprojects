## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): From the threefold Śakti comes the seed, from the seed desire, from desire poison (death); the spotless dwells subtly within the mind; all three reside at the root of the brahma-staff (AmŚ 38–45); desire is longing for objects and poison its dissolution — abandoning both one resorts to the spotless (Candrāvalokana).
