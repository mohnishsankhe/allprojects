## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Love, compassion, rejoicing and equanimity pervading all directions without measure; the Buddha says other wanderers teach the same but cannot say where each culminates (the beautiful, infinite space, infinite consciousness, nothingness).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Friendliness, compassion, gladness and equanimity toward the happy, the suffering, the virtuous and the non-virtuous clear the mind.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Friendliness toward all beings, joy (pramoda) toward the more virtuous, compassion toward the afflicted and impartiality (mādhyasthya) toward the unruly, as supporting reflections of the vows.
