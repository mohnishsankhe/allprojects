## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Tāraka ('what makes one cross') is the yoga of lights and targets, with and without form; its later part is amanaska, the mind-less state in which the mind becomes 'like an udder after milking' (Maṇḍalabrāhmaṇa 1.3, 3.1–2).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): In the haṭha corpus: 'tāraka', which those deluded by Āgamas, Vedas and logic do not know (HYP 4.40), and the mindless (amanaska) state — a synonym of samādhi (4.4) and the goal of the Amanaska, reached effortlessly by the guru's grace (Amanaska 2).
