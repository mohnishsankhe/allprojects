## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Taking refuge in the Lord with Śrī as the sole refuge, by those with no other means and no other refuge; done once, it brings liberation at the end of the body.
- [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md): An independent means (svatantra-upāya), performed once with five limbs and self-offering, equal in fruit to bhakti-yoga; the occasion of the Lord's grace.
- [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md): Not a means but the soul's acceptance of the Lord, who alone is the means; it befits the soul's nature of dependence; regarding it as a means is a fault.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): Taking refuge at the Lord's feet with no other refuge, naming Śrī who never leaves his chest (TVM 6.10.10); clinging to the Lord's feet though he cause grief, like a child pushed away that cries for its mother (PT 5.1); giving one's life to the owner of release (TVM 1.2.1); the unbreakable relation pleaded by those with nothing (TP 28).
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Having offered self, world and Veda to the Lord, the devotee has no care for worldly loss; self-offering is one of love's eleven forms.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): Rāma never abandons one who comes for refuge (Vibhīṣaṇa).
- [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md): Living as Hari keeps one.
