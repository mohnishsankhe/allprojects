## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Taking refuge in the Lord with Śrī as the sole refuge, by those with no other means and no other refuge; done once, it brings liberation at the end of the body.
- [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md): An independent means (svatantra-upāya), performed once with five limbs and self-offering, equal in fruit to bhakti-yoga; the occasion of the Lord's grace.
- [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md): Not a means but the soul's acceptance of the Lord, who alone is the means; it befits the soul's nature of dependence; regarding it as a means is a fault.
