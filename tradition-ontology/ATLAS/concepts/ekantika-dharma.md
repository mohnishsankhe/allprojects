## Definitions
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): Exclusive devotion to Nārāyaṇa, practised by the ekāntins — non-violent, knowing the self, delighting in the welfare of beings — the best dharma, equal to Sāṃkhya-yoga, taught to Arjuna before the battle, handed down from the beginning of the Kṛta age (MBh 12.336).
