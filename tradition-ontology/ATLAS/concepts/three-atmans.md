## Definitions
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The self in all embodied beings is outer (identified with the body), inner (discerning itself) or supreme (utterly pure); by means of the inner one attains the supreme and abandons the outer (Samādhitantra 4–5; Paramātmaprakāśa; YŚ 12).
- [Adhyātma movement (Adhyātma-mata)](../lineages/adhyatma-jain.md): Who the supreme self is, that am I (Yogasāra 22) — every soul's own nature is the paramātman.
