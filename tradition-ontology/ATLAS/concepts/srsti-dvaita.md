## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Before creation Nārāyaṇa alone exists, holding all souls in himself with their awareness quiescent, with Lakṣmī; he creates, as his sport, so that embodied souls may reach happiness, sorrow or the mean according to their natures; creation is the real transformation of prakṛti under his control (not of Brahman itself), unfolding into the uncompounded principles and the cosmic egg.
