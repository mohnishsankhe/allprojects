## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Places of torment below the earth in the south within the three worlds, ruled by Yama, each matched to classes of sin (BhP 5.26: twenty-eight; VP 2.6 and MkP 12 other lists); release comes by exhaustion of the karma, by remembrance of the Lord, or by the transfer of a righteous person's merit (MkP 15); VP 2.6.46 teaches that heaven and hell are names for merit and sin.
