## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The twelve links are taught conventionally (MMK 26); they arise from conceiving caused things as real and cease when things are seen as empty (ŚS 64–65); grouped as afflictions, actions and suffering in the Pratītyasamutpādahṛdaya.
