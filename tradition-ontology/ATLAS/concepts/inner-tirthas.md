## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The sacred places are located within: Vārāṇasī between brows and nose, Prayāga in the heart, etc. (Darśana 4.48–59; Jābāla 2); the suṣumnā is the supreme tīrtha (Yogaśikhā 6); wandering to outer tīrthas is 'lowest of the low' (Maitreya 2.21).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The Gaṅgā is in the pure mind's bowl; the fish is thirsty in water while the Lord is in one's own house.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): The original Mecca is the human body.
