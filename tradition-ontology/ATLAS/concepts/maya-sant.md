## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Māyā is the great swindler (mahāṭhaginī) who takes a different form in every house — Kamalā, Bhavānī, the idol, the pilgrim-water — and binds with the three qualities; at the origin 'one woman' from whom the gods came; often 'gold and woman'.
