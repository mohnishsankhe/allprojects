## Definitions
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Liberation, reached by the Lord's grace: impersonal (absolute cessation of suffering) and personal (the Lord's powers of knowing and acting); it is union with and proximity to Rudra, from which there is no return.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Yāmuna reports it as mere absolute cessation of pain (opponent's report).
