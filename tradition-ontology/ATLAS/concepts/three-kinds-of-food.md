## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Sāttvic food increases life, vitality, strength, health and happiness and is savoury and wholesome; rājasic food is bitter, sour, salty, hot and burning and brings pain and disease; tāmasic food is stale, tasteless, putrid, leftover and impure (BhG 17.7–10).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Food dear to each is threefold (17.7): foods that increase life, sattva, strength, health, happiness and delight, savoury, smooth, substantial and agreeable, are dear to the sāttvic (17.8); pungent, sour, salty, very hot, sharp, dry and burning foods, bringing pain, grief and disease, are desired by the rājasic (17.9); stale, tasteless, putrid, overnight, leftover and impure food is dear to the tāmasic (17.10).
