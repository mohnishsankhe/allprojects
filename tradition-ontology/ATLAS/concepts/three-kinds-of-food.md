## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Sāttvic food increases life, vitality, strength, health and happiness and is savoury and wholesome; rājasic food is bitter, sour, salty, hot and burning and brings pain and disease; tāmasic food is stale, tasteless, putrid, leftover and impure (BhG 17.7–10).
