## Definitions
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): Holy company; talk of the Lord's stories; singing his qualities; expounding his words; guileless service of the teacher as the Lord; virtuous conduct with steady worship; worship through his mantra; honouring devotees and seeing him in all with dispassion; inquiry into his nature — open to women, men and animals alike; the first leads to the rest in order.
