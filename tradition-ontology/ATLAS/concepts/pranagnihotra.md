## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The first morsels of every meal are offered with 'svāhā' to prāṇa, vyāna, apāna, samāna and udāna, satisfying in turn the senses, their deities and regions; one who eats knowing this offers in all worlds and all selves and his evils burn up like a reed-tuft in fire (ChU 5.19–24); the mantras 'in faith, entered into the breath, I offer the immortal' frame the meal (TA 10.69–70); the whole body becomes the sacrificial ground (Prāṇāgnihotra Up.).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Prescribed in the Dharmasūtra rules for eating as the daily rite of the twice-born at meals.
