# The conjunction of seer and seen (saṃyoga)

## Names
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): saṃyoga

## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The beginningless conjunction of seer and seen is the cause of what is to be avoided (2.17); it is the cause of apprehending owner and owned (2.23); its cause is ignorance (2.24); its absence is kaivalya (2.25). Vyāsa records eight views on the 'non-seeing' that causes it (YBh 2.23).
