# Convergence of means of knowledge (pramāṇa-saṃplava)

`cpt:pramana-samplava` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** disputes

## Names

## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The same object may be known by several means (fire by testimony, inference and perception), though some objects are restricted to one (heaven to scripture); knowledge culminates in perception (NBh 1.1.3).

## Relations (interpretation layer)
- opposes → `cpt:pramana-vyavastha-buddhist`: Buddhist logicians restrict each means of knowledge to its own kind of object. — rests on [1.1.3/2](../texts/nyaya-bhasya.md#tea-nyaya-bhasya-1-1-3-2)
