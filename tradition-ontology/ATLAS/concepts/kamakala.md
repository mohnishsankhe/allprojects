## Definitions
- [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md): The white and red points (Śiva and Śakti) and the point of their union, the sun that is the 'I'; kāma because it is lovely and kalā as the points of fire and moon; this vidyā is the sequence of the Goddess's cakra, and one who knows it becomes Mahātripurasundarī (KKV 6-8).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The Goddess 'whose form is the kāmakalā' (LSN 73); the Saundaryalaharī's meditation on it (SL 19) is taught for attraction.
