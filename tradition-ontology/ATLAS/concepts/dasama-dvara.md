## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Beyond the nine openings, the tenth door: twofold as the path of nectar and the path of death, at the root of the brahma-staff; the śaṅkhinī-mouth between the royal teeth through which nectar flows (AmŚ 31–32, 69–70).
