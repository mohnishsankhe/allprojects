## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Valid cognition (pramāṇa: perception, inference, testimony), error (viparyaya), conceptual construction (vikalpa), sleep (nidrā) and memory (smṛti), each afflicted (kliṣṭa) or unafflicted (akliṣṭa) (YS 1.5–1.11). Afflicted ones are caused by the afflictions and become the field for the karma-store; unafflicted ones have discernment as object; the two arise in each other's gaps; activities and impressions produce each other in an unceasing wheel (YBh 1.5). All of them are of the nature of pleasure, pain and delusion, and are to be stilled (YBh 1.11).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The mind's activities are five — valid cognition, error, conceptual construction, sleep and memory — each afflicted or unafflicted (YS 1.5–11); activities and impressions produce one another in a ceaseless wheel (YBh 1.5); all have the nature of pleasure, pain and delusion and are to be stilled (YBh 1.11).
