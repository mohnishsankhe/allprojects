## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Lineage contribution (Upaniṣadic): MaiU 5.2 names Brahmā, Rudra and Viṣṇu as the foremost forms of the one, being its rajas-, tamas- and sattva-parts; MaiU 5.1 hails the one as Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra and all the gods.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): One Lord takes the names Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva for creation, maintenance and destruction (VP 1.2.66); in the Devī Māhātmya and Devī Bhāgavata the three receive their bodies and powers from the Goddess.
