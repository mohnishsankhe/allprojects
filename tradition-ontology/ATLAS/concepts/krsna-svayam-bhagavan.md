## Definitions
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Kṛṣṇa is the source of all avatāras, not an avatāra of Viṣṇu (BhP 1.3.28 as governing statement).
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): Puruṣottama Kṛṣṇa is the highest form of Brahman.
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): Kṛṣṇa is the supreme Brahman with Rādhā.
