## Definitions
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): For householders in the Kali age the sweet triad (milk, sugar, honey) replaces wine (Mahānirvāṇa 8.170-171); the last tattva is limited to one's own wife (6.14).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): Pumpkin, sugarcane and spirits are 'equal to' an animal victim; coconut water or honey replace wine for the twice-born (Kālikā Purāṇa 67.23; 67.114-115).
