- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The text says that when the breath flows in suṣumnā and the mind enters the void the yogin uproots all karma (4.12), that the yogin joined in samādhi is not bound by karma (4.108) and that for one in whom the awareness of śakti has arisen and who has given up all action the sahaja state arises of itself (4.11).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The body arises from good and bad deeds and turns the soul through birth and death like a water-wheel (GS 1.6–7); when breath flows in suṣumnā and mind enters the void the yogin uproots all karma (HYP 4.12); the yogin in samādhi is not bound by karma (4.108); at niṣpatti the seeds of beginningless karma are crossed (SS 3.66); in paricaya he sees the 'three peaks' of his karma (SS 3.61).
