## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Dvaita's case against Advaita: ignorance cannot belong to a self-luminous attributeless self or to the all-knower; the indeterminable is unproven; the world's falsity is unproven; 'māyā' in scripture means God's will; and an attributeless Brahman is indistinguishable from the Mādhyamika void.
