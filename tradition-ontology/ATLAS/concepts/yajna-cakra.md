## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Prajāpati created beings together with sacrifice, bidding them multiply by it; gods and humans nourish one another through it and so attain the highest good; one who enjoys the gods' gifts without offering is a thief; eaters of the sacrificial remainder are freed from faults (3.10–13); beings come from food, food from rain, rain from sacrifice, sacrifice from action, action from brahman, brahman from the imperishable (3.14–15); one who does not keep this wheel turning lives in vain (3.16). Action other than for sacrifice binds (3.9).
