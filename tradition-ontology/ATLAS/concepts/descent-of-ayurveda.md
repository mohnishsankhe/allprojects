## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Brahmā → Prajāpati (Dakṣa) → the Aśvins → Indra; then (a) Bharadvāja, sent by the assembled sages, learns from Indra and teaches the sages, and Punarvasu Ātreya teaches six disciples (Ca Sū 1.3-40); (b) Indra teaches Dhanvantari, who as Divodāsa of Kāśī teaches Suśruta and others (Su Sū 1.20-21); (c) Indra teaches Ātreya and other sages (AHS Sū 1.3; Bhāvaprakāśa, where Ātreya himself goes to Indra).
