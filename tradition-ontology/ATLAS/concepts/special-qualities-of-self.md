## Definitions
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Cognition, pleasure, pain, desire, aversion, effort, merit and demerit — eight special qualities of the self alone (Tarkasaṅgraha); memory-impression also resides only in the self; liberation is their cessation (Praśastapāda, as usually summarized).
