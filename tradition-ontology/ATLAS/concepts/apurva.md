# Apūrva (the unseen potency of ritual action)

`cpt:apurva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** karma-rebirth

## Names
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): apūrva

## Definitions
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The perishable act produces an unseen potency that yields the enjoined fruit at a later time; its existence is known from the injunction itself (MS 2.1.5, Śabara).
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): Identified with the injunction's 'what is to be done' (kārya), known only from the Veda.
