## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The tradition holds, on Madhva's own statements reading Ṛgveda 1.141, that Vāyu descended as Hanumān, as Bhīma and, in Kali 4300, as Madhva, to proclaim the truth about Hari; the hagiographies' feats are told as signs of this.
