## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): All speculation about the past (18: eternalism 4, partial eternalism 4, finite/infinite 4, eel-wriggling 4, chance origin 2) and the future (44: percipient 16, non-percipient 8, neither 8, annihilation 7, nibbāna here and now 5) falls within sixty-two grounds; each is conditioned by contact and craving, and the Tathāgata knows them and what is beyond them.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The theories about the past (eternalism, partial eternalism, finitude, eel-wriggling, chance origin) and the future (percipient, non-percipient, neither, annihilation, nibbāna here and now) listed in the Brahmajāla; all arise from contact and craving (DN 1).
