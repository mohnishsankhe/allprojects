## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Dvaita: the soul's own intrinsic faculty of awareness (its essential sense), the seventh kind of perception; it apprehends the self, mind and its states, pleasure and pain, time and space, and the validity of knowledge; its deliverances are infallible and never sublated, and it is the final guarantor of knowledge, including the reality of the world.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Lineage contribution (Upaniṣadic): 'you cannot see the seer of seeing' (BAU 3.4.2); 'by what would one know the knower?' (BAU 2.4.14); the unseen seer (BAU 3.7.23, 3.8.11); the ear of the ear (Kena 1.2); the word sākṣin itself in ŚU 6.11 (the one God, witness, knower, alone, without qualities).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Puruṣa is witness, seer and neutral (SK 19), witness through direct relation (SS 1.161) and of deep sleep and other states (SS 1.148); upon knowledge it sees prakṛti like a spectator, at ease (SK 65).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The unchanging consciousness that illumines the mind's modes, the three states and ignorance itself, never an object (DDV 1; the theatre-lamp of Pañcadaśī 10).
