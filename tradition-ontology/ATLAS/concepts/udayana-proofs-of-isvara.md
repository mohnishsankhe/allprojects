## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The all-knowing maker is proved from: (1) the world's being an effect (kārya); (2) the combining of atoms at creation (āyojana); (3) the support of the world (dhṛti) and (4) its destruction ('ādi'); (5) traditional skills and usage of words (pada); (6) the validity of the Veda (pratyaya); (7) scripture (śruti); (8) the Veda's consisting of sentences (vākya); (9) special number — the magnitude of dyads, which requires an enumerating cognition at creation (saṃkhyāviśeṣa). The five clusters first answer five objections.
