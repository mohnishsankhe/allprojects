## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Saṃvara is the stopping of inflow (9.1). Its means are the guptis, samitis, dharma, anuprekṣās, conquest of the parīṣahas and cāritra (9.2); tapas also brings shedding (9.3).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The blocking of inflow by the three controls, five carefulnesses, ten virtues, twelve reflections, conquest of the twenty-two hardships and the five conducts (TS 9.1–2); inflow is always to be abandoned and stopping adopted (Vītarāgastotra 19).
