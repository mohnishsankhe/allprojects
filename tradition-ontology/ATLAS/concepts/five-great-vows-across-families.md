## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Abstention from violence, falsehood, stealing, unchastity and possessiveness is the vow; kept in part it is the small vow, in full the great vow (mahāvrata) of the mendicant.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The same five are the yamas; unlimited by birth, place, time or occasion they are the great vow (mahāvrata).
- [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md): The Dharmasūtras give the renouncer a set of vows of the same kind (non-injury, truth, not stealing, celibacy, renunciation).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The five lay precepts overlap (non-killing, non-stealing, sexual restraint, truthful speech) but replace non-possession with abstention from intoxicants.
