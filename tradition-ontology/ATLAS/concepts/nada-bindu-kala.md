## Definitions
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): From the supreme Lord come Śakti, nāda and bindu; bindu divides into bindu (Śiva), bīja (Śakti) and nāda (their union); from them Raudrī, Jyeṣṭhā and Vāmā, Rudra, Brahmā and Viṣṇu (will, knowledge, action); from the dividing bindu the unmanifest sound, śabda-brahman; from bindu-Śambhu Sadāśiva, Īśa, Rudra, Viṣṇu and Brahmā (ŚT 1.7–16).
