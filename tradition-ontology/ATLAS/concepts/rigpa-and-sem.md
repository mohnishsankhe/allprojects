# Awareness (rig pa) and ordinary mind (sems)

`cpt:rigpa-and-sem` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** consciousness-states

## Names

## Definitions
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): Ordinary mind is dualistic, conceptual, arising from non-recognition; awareness is the non-dual knowing of the ground. Dzogchen practice begins by distinguishing them (ru shan) and then resting in awareness; mistaking sems for rig pa is the principal error.
- [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md): Bön Dzogchen likewise distinguishes the moving mind from the nature of mind (sems nyid) and awareness.
