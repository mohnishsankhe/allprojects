## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Faith born of one's nature is sāttvic, rājasic or tāmasic, accordingly worshipping gods, yakṣas and rākṣasas, or ghosts and spirits; a person is made of faith (BhG 17.2–4).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Asked about those who sacrifice with faith outside scriptural injunction (17.1), Kṛṣṇa answers that the faith of embodied beings is threefold, born of nature — sāttvic, rājasic, tāmasic (17.2); faith accords with one's sattva, and a person is made of faith: as his faith, so is he (17.3); the sāttvic worship gods, the rājasic yakṣas and rākṣasas, the tāmasic the departed and hosts of spirits (17.4); those who practise terrible unsanctioned austerity are of demonic resolve (17.5–6).
