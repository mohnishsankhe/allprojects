## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Knowledge and ignorance lead to different ends (KU 1.2.4); fools in ignorance wander like the blind led by the blind (KU 1.2.5; MuU 1.2.8); Īśa 9-11 says both those who worship ignorance and those who delight in knowledge enter darkness, and that one knowing both crosses death by ignorance and gains immortality by knowledge; ŚU 5.1 calls the perishable ignorance and the immortal knowledge; what is done with knowledge is more powerful (ChU 1.1.10).
