# Equalizing self and other

`cpt:equalizing-self-and-other` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics

## Names
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): parātmasamatā

## Definitions
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Since happiness is equally dear and suffering equally unwelcome to self and others, one protects all as oneself, as the many limbs are protected as one body (BCA 8.90–96; ŚSK 1).
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The first part of the Śāntideva method: since all equally wish happiness and not suffering, there is no reason to cherish oneself more.
