## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Kamma is intention; its source is contact, its result threefold in time, its cessation the cessation of contact, the way the eightfold path (AN 6.63); beings are owners of their deeds (MN 135); dark, bright, mixed and neither (AN 4.232); the same deed ripens differently in the developed and undeveloped (AN 3.100); not everything felt is due to past deeds (MN 101; AN 3.61).
