# Not-self (anattā)

`cpt:anatta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** self

## Names
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): anattā

## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The five aggregates are not-self since they lead to affliction and cannot be commanded (SN 22.59), impermanent and so not fit to be regarded as self (SN 22.59; MN 148); neither self nor what belongs to self is found (MN 22); 'a being' is a convention for the aggregates (SN 5.10; Mil 3.1.1); the Buddha declined 'there is a self' and 'there is no self' before Vacchagotta (SN 44.10).
