## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Whatever state one remembers when leaving the body, to that one goes, having been absorbed in it; so one should remember the Lord at all times (BhG 8.5–7); the guṇa prevailing at death determines the next birth (14.14–15).
