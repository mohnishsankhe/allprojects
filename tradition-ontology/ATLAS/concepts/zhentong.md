# Other-emptiness (gzhan stong)

`cpt:zhentong` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ultimate

## Names
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): gzhan stong
- [Rimé (ris med), the non-sectarian movement](../lineages/rime.md): gzhan stong dbu ma

## Definitions
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): The ultimate — buddha-nature, dharmakāya, dharmatā — is empty of all adventitious conventional phenomena, which are other than it, and not empty of its own nature and qualities; it is permanent and not a dependent arising.
- [Rimé (ris med), the non-sectarian movement](../lineages/rime.md): In Kongtrul, the Great Madhyamaka view suited to meditative experience; in Mipham, acceptable in the sense that unconditioned wisdom is empty of the adventitious while not established by own-nature.
