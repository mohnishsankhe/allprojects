## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Liberation is not: cutting off good and bad karma, Veda recitation, the supportless state, the attainment of form/bindu/nāda by meditation, the bliss of Kaula worship with wine, meat and union, the movement of kuṇḍalinī, or an even gaze; it is the mind seeing the mind in sahaja-samādhi (AmŚ 46–54).
