## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Reality is many-sided: a thing is permanent and impermanent, one and many, existent and non-existent, each in a definite respect (TS 5.30–32); every absolute (ekānta) assertion grasps only one side and is refuted (Āptamīmāṃsā). The Jina's word is the union of the partial views (Sanmati 3.69).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The Jain doctrine accepts all standpoints without partisanship (Anyayogavyavacchedikā 30); Haribhadra and Yaśovijaya apply it irenically to other darśanas.
