## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Initiation in stages: first the cutting of the top-knot, the mantra and the black cord (the aughaṛ); after probation the splitting of the ear-cartilages and the wearing of the earrings (the darśanī / kānphaṭa), with a new name ending in -nāth; the Gorakṣaguṭikā tells of Gorakṣa taking 'the split ear and the cut top-knot'.
