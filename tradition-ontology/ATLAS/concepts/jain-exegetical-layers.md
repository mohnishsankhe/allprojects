## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Niryukti (Prakrit verse, ascribed to Bhadrabāhu), bhāṣya (Prakrit verse, e.g. Jinabhadra, Saṅghadāsa), cūrṇi (Prakrit-Sanskrit prose, e.g. Jinadāsa), and ṭīkā or vṛtti (Sanskrit: Haribhadra, Śīlāṅka, Śāntisūri, Abhayadeva, Malayagiri); with the Aṅgas these form the 'five-limbed' (pañcāṅgī) scripture of the Mūrtipūjakas, which the Sthānakavāsīs reject beyond the root texts.
