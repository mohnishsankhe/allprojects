## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Proposition, reason, example, application, conclusion (NS 1.1.32–39): e.g. 'the mountain has fire; because it has smoke; whatever has smoke has fire, like a kitchen; so is this; therefore so'. The members embody all four means of knowledge (NBh 1.1.1); a ten-member view is rejected (NBh 1.1.32).
