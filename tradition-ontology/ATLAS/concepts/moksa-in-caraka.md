## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Separation from all conjunctions through the absence of rajas and tamas and the exhaustion of strong karma — non-return (Śā 1.142); reached by the recollection of truth and the understanding 'this is not I, not mine', after which the self, become brahman, has no mark (1.143-155); disengagement, the tranquil imperishable brahman, called nirvāṇa (Śā 5.11-23).
