## Definitions
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): The Mahāparinirvāṇa: 'self' means the tathāgatagarbha; no-self is saṃsāra, self is the Tathāgata; the Aṅgulimālīya likewise.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The Laṅkāvatāra denies that the garbha-teaching is the tīrthikas' self.
