## Definitions
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Liable to fall, able to end life at will, guarding, stable, able to perfect the faculties, unshakeable; five can fall back from what they have obtained.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): No falling back from arhatship.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): An arahant does not fall away (Kv 1.2).
