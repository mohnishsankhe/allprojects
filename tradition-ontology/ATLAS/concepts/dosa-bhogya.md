## Definitions
- [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md): Out of motherly love the Lord relishes the faults of those who come to him, as a cow licks its newborn calf.
- [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md): The Lord overlooks the faults of the surrendered (does not see them), but does not relish faults.
