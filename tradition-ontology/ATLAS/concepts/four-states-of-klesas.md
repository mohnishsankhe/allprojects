## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Prasupta (dormant), tanu (attenuated), vicchinna (interrupted), udāra (fully active) (YS 2.4). Vyāsa: dormancy is the seed-state of bare potency awakened by facing a support; attenuation is by the cultivation of the opposite; interruption is acting again and again in this or that form (anger absent at a time of passion); fully active is having gained activity toward an object; he adds a fifth, the burnt-seed (dagdha-bīja) state, in the one of the last body (YBh 2.4).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The afflictions may be dormant, thinned, interrupted or active (2.4); Vyāsa adds a fifth, 'burnt-seed' state, found only in one whose afflictions are exhausted, 'skilled' and in his last body (YBh 2.4).
