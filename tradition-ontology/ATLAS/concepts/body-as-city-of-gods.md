# The body as the city of the gods

## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The body is the eight-wheeled, nine-gated unconquerable stronghold of the gods with a golden sheath covered in light, in which dwells the self-possessed wonder (yakṣa) that the knowers of Brahman know; Brahman entered it; the person (puruṣa) is so called from this stronghold (pur) of Brahman (AVŚ 10.2.28–33); the nine-gated lotus covered with three strands holds the same yakṣa (10.8.43); all the gods dwell in the person as cows in a stall (11.8.32).
