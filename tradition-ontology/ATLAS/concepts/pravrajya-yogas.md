## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Four or more strong planets in one sign make a renunciant; the strongest decides the order — for Varāhamihira Śākya, Ājīvika, bhikṣu, vṛddha, caraka, nirgrantha or forest-dweller (BJ 15.1), for Parāśara tapasvin, kāpālika, red-robed, ekadaṇḍin, yati, cakradhara or nirgrantha (BPHS 79.2–3); a planet set in the sun gives devotion without initiation; Saturn's aspect gives initiation; certain royal yogas make a founder of a holy place (BJ 15.2–4).
