## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Perception of light and vision of forms arising in concentration and vanishing when the imperfections of concentration arise; the bodhisatta abandoned eleven imperfections to stabilise them (MN 128); the perception of light is a development of concentration leading to knowledge and vision (AN 4.41).
