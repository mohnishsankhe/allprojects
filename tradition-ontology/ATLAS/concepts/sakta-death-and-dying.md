## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The Mahānirvāṇa: cremation in the cremation ground; no burning of a wife with her husband, for woman is the Goddess's own form; death beside the Goddess or near Kaulas is praised; whoever dies contemplating the one Truth, forgetting the three worlds, abides in his own nature (10.78-83). Kula worship continues even during death-impurity (10.78).
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): The Kubjikāmata teaches knowledge of the three times and the yogic departure (ch. 23; restricted).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): Kālī dwells in the cremation ground (Kālī Tantra dhyāna); the Śākta poets make her name the refuge at death — Rāmprasād is remembered as dying singing while immersing her image in the Ganges (tradition's account).
