## Definitions
- [Siddha medicine (cittā maruttuvam)](../lineages/siddha-medicine.md): Calcined metal and mineral medicines — parpam (white calx), centūram (red calx), cuṇṇam, kaṭṭu, meḻuku — held by the tradition to be the most potent Siddha medicines. The tradition's own warnings: raw minerals and metals are poison; they must be purified (cutti) and processed exactly as the guru teaches; wrongly made they kill; they are given only with strict regimen (pattiyam) and by a qualified physician. No processes, ingredients or doses are recorded here.
