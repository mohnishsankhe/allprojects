# Consciousness from the elements (bhūta-caitanya)

`cpt:bhuta-caitanya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** consciousness-states

## Names

## Definitions
- [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md): (As reported) Consciousness, absent in each element, arises in them when combined as a body — like intoxicating power — and perishes with them, like a drum's sound dying away.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): (Jain report) From five elements a self arises and perishes with them.
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Refuted: cognition arises only from prior cognition; mind is a beginningless continuum.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Refuted: consciousness is absent in the dead body; what experiences the elements is not one of them.
