## Definitions
- [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md): Liberation is attained by knowledge of Brahman combined with the lifelong performance of one's prescribed duties; works are not abandoned upon knowledge.

_Notes: Opposed by Śaṅkara and Sureśvara, for whom knowledge alone liberates (see dsp:jnana-karma-samuccaya)._
