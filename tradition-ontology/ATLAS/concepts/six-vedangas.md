## Definitions
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): Phonetics, ritual procedure, grammar, etymology, metre and time-reckoning; the Veda's nose, hands, mouth, ear, feet and eye (PŚ 41–42), studied as a duty without motive (Mahābhāṣya); compiled by later seers for ease of learning (Nirukta 1.20); among the fourteen seats of knowledge (YS 1.3); counted as lower knowledge (MuU 1.1.5).
