## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Mind and speech are a yoked pair carrying the sacrifice, mind the greater (ŚB 1.4.4.7); the sacrifice has two tracks, mind (the silent brahman priest) and speech (the other priests) (ChU 4.16.1–2; AB 5.33); in the inner Agnihotra speech and breath are offered into each other (KU 2.5).
