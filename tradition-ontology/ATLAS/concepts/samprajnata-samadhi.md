## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Samādhi accompanied by reasoning, reflection, bliss and I-am-ness (1.17): the first has all four, the second lacks reasoning, the third lacks reflection, the fourth is I-am-ness alone; all have a support (YBh 1.17); it occurs in the one-pointed mind (YBh 1.1). Commentators differ on the objects of the bliss and I-am-ness levels (Vācaspati, Bhoja RM 1.17, Vijñānabhikṣu YV 1.17).
