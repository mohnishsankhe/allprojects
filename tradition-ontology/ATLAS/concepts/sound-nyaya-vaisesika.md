# Sound as a non-eternal quality of ākāśa

**Category:** sound-language

## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Sound has a beginning and is sense-perceived, hence non-eternal (NS 2.2.13ff.).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Sound is the mark of ākāśa, produced by conjunction, disjunction and sound (VS 2.1.24–27, 2.2.36–37 C); it is inarticulate or phonemic (Tarkasaṅgraha); later authors explain its travel to the ear on the model of waves or of kadamba buds.
