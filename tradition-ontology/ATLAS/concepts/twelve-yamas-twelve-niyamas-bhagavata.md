## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Yamas: non-violence, truth, non-stealing, non-attachment, modesty, non-accumulation, faith in dharma, celibacy, silence, steadiness, forbearance, fearlessness. Niyamas: purity (outer and inner), recitation, austerity, fire-offering, faith, hospitality, worship of the Lord, pilgrimage, acting for others, contentment, service of the teacher.
