## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The five destinations — hell, animals, ghosts, humans, gods (MN 12) — with the heavens of the sense sphere, the Brahmā worlds and the formless spheres; systematised in the commentaries as thirty-one planes (U37).
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The hells, hungry ghosts and animals (lower realms) contemplated by the small scope; gods, demigods and humans (higher realms) whose sufferings are contemplated by the middling scope.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): The six realms and their sufferings (Jewel Ornament ch.5); clinging to bliss, clarity and non-thought in meditation leads to the desire, form and formless realms (Moonbeams).
