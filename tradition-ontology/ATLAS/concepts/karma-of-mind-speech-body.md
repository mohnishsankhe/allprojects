## Definitions
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Ten wrongs — three mental, four verbal, three bodily — with mind as instigator; bodily faults lead to plant-births, verbal to bird and beast births, mental to low human births (MDh 12.3–9); the residue of deeds shapes birth, form and fortune (ĀpDh 2.1.2.3); one's own conduct raises or lowers class in the next birth (ĀpDh 2.5.11.10–11).
