## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md): In early medieval western India many monks lived permanently in temples, handling their property; Haribhadra condemned this, and Jineśvara's debate at Aṇahilapāṭaka (1024) won recognition for monks living in lodgings (vasati), the origin of the Kharatara reform.
