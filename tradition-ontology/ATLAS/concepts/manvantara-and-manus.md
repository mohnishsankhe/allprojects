## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Each day of Brahmā has fourteen Manus: Svāyambhuva, Svārociṣa, Uttama (Auttama), Tāmasa, Raivata, Cākṣuṣa, Vaivasvata (the present), then the Sāvarṇis beginning with Sūrya-Sāvarṇi (the Devī Māhātmya's Suratha); each interval has its Indra, gods, seven sages and avatāra; names of the later Manus vary between Purāṇas.
