- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): The Siddhars veil yoga, alchemy and medicine in coded names and riddles — the house of three-times-two, the horse of twelve feet, the root tamarind, the eight and the two — so that only a guru's disciple can decode them; glossaries such as the Akattiyar paripāṣai explain some.
