## Definitions
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): (1) The popular list (widely printed, with samādhi places): Nandi (Kāśī), Agastya (Anantaśayanam), Tirumūlar (Chidambaram), Bogar (Palani), Koṅkaṇar (Tirupati), Maccamuṉi (Tiruparaṅkuṉṟam), Kōrakkar (Poyyūr), Caṭṭaimuṉi (Srirangam), Cuntarāṉantar (Madurai), Rāmatēvar (Aḻakar malai), Kuṭampai (Māyavaram), Karuvūrār (Karūr), Iṭaikkāṭar (Tiruvaṇṇāmalai), Kamalamuṉi (Tiruvārūr), Patañjali (Rāmēśvaram), Taṉvantiri (Vaitīsvaraṉkōyil), Vālmīki (Eṭṭukkuṭi), Pāmbāṭṭi (Caṅkaraṉkōyil). Names moderate, places low confidence.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): (2) The Tirumantiram's own lineage lists (checked locally): Nandi's eight — the four Nandis, Śivayoga, Patañjali, Vyāghrapāda and Tirumūlar (TM 67) — and Tirumūlar's mantra-line — Mālāṅkaṉ, Intiraṉ, Cōmaṉ, Piramaṉ, Uruttiraṉ, Kanturu, Kālāṅki, Kañcamalaiyaṉ (TM 69).
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): (3) The poets of the printed Siddhar anthologies, largely absent from list (1): Sivavākkiyar, Paṭṭiṉattār, Pattirakiriyār, Aḻukaṇṇi, Kaṭuveḷi, Kuṭampai, Pāmbāṭṭi, Iṭaikkāṭar, Akappēy, with works ascribed to Tirumūlar, Caṭṭaimuṉi, Rāmatēvar and Tiruvaḷḷuvar (the local Project Madurai series checked).
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): (4) Other lists (not reconstructed here) add or substitute names such as Kākapucuṇṭar, Urōmarisi, Pulippāṇi, Tēraiyar, Yūkimuṉi, Kālāṅki, Pulastiyar, Sivavākkiyar and Paṭṭiṉattār; the number eighteen is conventional and the membership varies from source to source.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tāyumāṉavar praises the 'host of Siddhas' (cittarkaṇam) who attained the harmony of Vedānta and Siddhānta.
