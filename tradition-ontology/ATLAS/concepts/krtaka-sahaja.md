## Definitions
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The Kaula path is twofold: the 'made' (kṛtaka), in which kuṇḍalī is impelled by seals, mantras, oblations and ear-whispered formulas, and the 'innate' (sahaja), abiding in samarasa (Akulavīra B.43–45); conceptions, even of the centres and deities, keep one turning (Jñānakārikā 2.20–23).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The Amaraughaśāsana likewise refuses to call kuṇḍalinī's movement liberation and places liberation in sahaja-samādhi (AmŚ 52–54).
