## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): In the Kārikā: a-viśva (first/obtaining), u-taijasa (exaltation/"both-ness"), m-prājña (measuring/merging); the measureless Oṃ is the stilling of duality.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The self as the syllable: a is vaiśvānara, u is taijasa, m is prājña; the fourth is without measure; the quarters are the measures and the measures the quarters.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Oṃ analysed into three measures plus the half-measure (with Vedas, fires, worlds, deities, guṇas and colours), into twelve measures with destinies at death (Nādabindu), and sixteen measures ending in parā (Nāradaparivrājaka 8; Paramahaṃsaparivrājaka).
