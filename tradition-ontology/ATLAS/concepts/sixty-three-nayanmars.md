## Definitions
- [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md): The canonical company of Śiva's devotees: 63 individual saints and 9 groups (tokai aṭiyār), first listed in Cuntarar's Tiruttoṇṭattokai (Tēvāram 7.39), expanded by Nampi Āṇṭār Nampi and told at length in Cēkkiḻār's Periya Purāṇam; their images stand in Tamil Śiva temples and their days are kept as festivals. Their stories teach that love and service of Śiva and his devotees, not birth, learning or rite, win his grace; they include kings, brahmins, merchants, farmers, a hunter, a potter, a washerman, a fisherman, an outcaste, women and a former Buddhist.
