## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The heart ('mahat', 'artha') holds consciousness, the self with its qualities, mind and its objects and the supreme ojas, and is the root of the ten great vessels (Ca Sū 30.3-14); the seat of consciousness and abode of ojas (Śārṅgadhara Pūrva 5.49); Bhela places citta in the heart and manas between head and palate.
