## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The moment is time at its utmost minuteness and alone real; sequence is the flow of moments; hours and days are constructions of the buddhi (YBh 3.52); sequence is apprehended at the end of a transformation (4.33); saṃyama on moment and sequence gives discriminative knowledge (3.52). Permanence is twofold: unchanging (puruṣa) and changing (guṇas) (YBh 4.33).
