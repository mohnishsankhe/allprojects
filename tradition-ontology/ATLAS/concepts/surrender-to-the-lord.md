## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7.14, 7.19, 7.29; 9.32: those who take refuge in the Lord alone cross his guṇa-made māyā; the knower takes refuge after many births knowing 'Vāsudeva is all'; those who take refuge strive for release from old age and death; whoever takes refuge — even those of sinful birth, women, vaiśyas and śūdras — reaches the highest goal. Evil-doers of demonic disposition do not take refuge (7.15).
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): Taking refuge in the Lord alone, who frees from all sins (BhG 18.66); those who take refuge cross his māyā (7.14); Rāma protects anyone who comes once for refuge (Rāmāyaṇa 6.18.33).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Nothing in me is mine; giving back to the Lord what is his costs nothing; saying 'you, you' the 'I' disappears.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Surrender to the Master's will (mauj) and acceptance of pralabdh karma as his gift.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): The helpless prayer to be taken across by the merciful one.
