## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The afflicted, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of wealth and the knower worship the Lord; the knower is the best and is the Lord's very self (BhG 7.16–18).
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): Jñāneśvar calls the knower's devotion the 'fourth' devotion, beyond those of the afflicted, the seeker and the one who wants gain.
