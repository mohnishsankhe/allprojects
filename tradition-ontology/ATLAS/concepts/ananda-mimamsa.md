## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): A graded measure of bliss: the fullest human bliss multiplied a hundredfold at each step through classes of beings up to the bliss of brahman (TU 2.8; BAU 4.3.33 with a different list), with the refrain that the learned one untouched by desire has each of these.
