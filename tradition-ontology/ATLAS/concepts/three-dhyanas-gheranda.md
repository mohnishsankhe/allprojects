## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Gross meditation on a form (the chosen deity or the guru), meditation on light (kuṇḍalinī as a flame, the navel sun, the praṇava-light between the brows) and subtle meditation on bindu/kuṇḍalinī by śāmbhavī; each is vastly superior to the one before (GS 6.1–22).
