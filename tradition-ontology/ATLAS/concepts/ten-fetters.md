## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Vism XXII p. 682-684: the ten fetters, so called because they bind beings to aggregates, to the fruit of kamma and to suffering; five higher (lust for fine-material and immaterial existence, conceit, restlessness, ignorance) and five lower (personality view, doubt, adherence to rites and observances, sensual lust, ill will). The text says personality view, doubt, adherence to rites and observances, and the sensual lust and ill will that lead to states of loss are cut by the first path knowledge; the remaining gross sensual lust and ill will by the second; the subtle by the third; the five higher by the fourth.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Identity view, doubt, clinging to rules and vows, sensual lust, ill will (the five lower), lust for form, lust for the formless, conceit, restlessness, ignorance (the five higher); abandoned stage by stage (MN 64; MN 2).
