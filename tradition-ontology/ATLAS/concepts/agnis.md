## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Pitta is the body's only fire (Su Sū 21.9); the belly-fire, the five element-fires and the seven tissue-fires (Ca Ci 15.13-15; counted as thirteen by later authors); four states — balanced, irregular, sharp, weak (AHS Sū 1.8); ojas, tejas and the prāṇas depend on agni, 'agni is the root' (Ca Ci 15.3-4).
