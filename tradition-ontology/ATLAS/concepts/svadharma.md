## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Arjuna's own dharma as a kṣatriya is to fight a battle in accordance with dharma, the best thing for a kṣatriya and an open door to heaven; abandoning it incurs sin and disgrace (2.31–37). One's own dharma, even imperfect, is better than another's well performed; death in it is better; another's brings fear (3.35).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The duty arising from one's own nature and station, to be done even if imperfect in preference to another's (BhG 3.35, 18.47); by it, as worship of the Lord, one reaches perfection (18.46); the meat-seller holds his inherited work as his dharma (MBh 3.198).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The duty of one's class and stage, better imperfect than another's done well (MDh 10.97); each class's work is its austerity (MDh 11.235); following it brings heaven and endlessness (Arthaśāstra 1.3.14) and a good rebirth (ĀpDh 2.1.2.2–3).
