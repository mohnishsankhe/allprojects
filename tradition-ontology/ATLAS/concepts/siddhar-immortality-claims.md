## Definitions
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): The tradition holds that the Siddhars did not die: they live on in subtle or perfected bodies in the Potiyil, Caturakiri and Kolli hills, abide in their samādhi shrines, fly and travel far (Bogar to China), enter other bodies (Tirumūlar) and vanish into liṅgas (Paṭṭiṉattār).
