## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Knowledge is sensory (mati), scriptural (śruta), clairvoyant (avadhi), mind-reading (manaḥparyaya) and omniscient (kevala); the first three can be erroneous in one of wrong view; up to four can coexist, omniscience stands alone (TS 1.9–33).
