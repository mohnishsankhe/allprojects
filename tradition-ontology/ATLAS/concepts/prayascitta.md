## Definitions
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Sins — omission, commission, attachment — are removed by confession, remorse, austerity, recitation and gifts (MDh 11.44, 11.227–229), by japa, tapas, offering, fasting and giving and by purifying texts (GDh 19.11–12); sins are graded from the five great sins down (MDh 11.54ff.).
