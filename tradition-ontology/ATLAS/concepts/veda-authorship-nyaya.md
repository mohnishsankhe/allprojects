## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The Veda is valid like mantra and medicine because its seers and teachers are reliable (NS 2.1.69; NBh); its validity rests on its being rooted in the Lord's perception (Gaṅgeśa); all Vedic sentences are valid because spoken by the Lord (Tarkasaṅgraha).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): The composition of sentences in the Veda is preceded by understanding (VS 6.1.1 C); its authority follows from its being 'the statement of that' (VS 1.1.3).
