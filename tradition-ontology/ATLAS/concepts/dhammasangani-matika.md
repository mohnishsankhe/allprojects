## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Twenty-two triads and one hundred Abhidhamma dyads (in clusters: roots, taints, fetters, ties, floods, bonds, hindrances, misapprehension, clinging, defilements) plus forty-two sutta dyads, by which every dhamma is classified; the Paṭṭhāna applies its conditions to the same matrix.
