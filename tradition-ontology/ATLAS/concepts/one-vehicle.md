## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): There is only one buddha-vehicle; the three are its skillful presentations; all who hear the Lotus become buddhas.
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): The two vehicles enter the great vehicle; the arhat's nirvāṇa is not final (Śrīmālā).
