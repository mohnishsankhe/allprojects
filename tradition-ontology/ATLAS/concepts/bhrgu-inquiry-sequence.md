## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Bhṛgu, told to seek that from which beings are born, by which they live and into which they enter, knows brahman as food, then breath, then mind, then understanding, then bliss; after each he is told to seek by tapas; the sequence ends with the vidyā established in the highest space.
