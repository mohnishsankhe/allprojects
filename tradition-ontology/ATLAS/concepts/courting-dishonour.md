# Courting dishonour and the exchange of merit and sin

## Definitions
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The second-stage ascetic hides his sign and provokes contempt; those who revile him receive his demerit and he receives their merit; dishonour is the highest restraint and makes his austerity complete. Kauṇḍinya: undertaken only after gaining knowledge, with impurity reduced and the teacher's leave, without breaking the restraints.
