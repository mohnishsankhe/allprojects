## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The text says all yogas have a fourfold state: beginning (4.69). In ārambha the knot of Brahmā is pierced and bliss and tinkling sounds arise (4.70-71); in ghaṭa the breath goes in the middle and the knot of Viṣṇu is pierced (4.72-73); in paricaya the mardala sound and the great void (4.74-75); in niṣpatti the knot of Rudra is pierced and the mind becomes one, called rājayoga (4.76-77).
