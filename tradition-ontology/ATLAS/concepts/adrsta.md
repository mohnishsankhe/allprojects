## Definitions
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Merit and demerit (qualities of selves) that cause motions without visible cause — needle and magnet, ascent of sap, upward flame, the first motion of atoms and minds — and the mind's departure at death and entry at birth; its absence is liberation (VS 5.1.15, 5.2.8, 5.2.14, 5.2.19–20 C); acts done for the unseen (VS 6.2.2).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): A supersensible cause of experience must be admitted (NK stabaka 1); the Lord sets the stores of merit and demerit in motion (NBh 4.1.21).
