## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The true deity must be faultless, omniscient and the teacher of scripture (Ratnakaraṇḍa 5); outward miracles prove nothing (Āptamīmāṃsā 1); only one whose speech agrees with reason and scripture is trustworthy (Āptamīmāṃsā 6).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Śrīmad Rājacandra's marks of the sadguru: self-knowledge, equal vision, acting only as karma unfolds, unprecedented speech, supreme scriptural knowledge (Ātmasiddhi 10); the sectarian (matārthī) mistakes outward signs for the teacher (Ātmasiddhi 24–33).
