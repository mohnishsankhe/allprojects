## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Vāta, pitta and kapha, which sustain the body in balance and cause disease when disturbed.
- [Sowa Rigpa (gso ba rig pa), the Tibetan science of healing](../lineages/sowa-rigpa.md): Wind (rlung), bile (mkhris pa) and phlegm (bad kan), which the Four Tantras trace to the three mental poisons — desire, hatred and delusion — rooted in ignorance.
- [Siddha medicine (cittā maruttuvam)](../lineages/siddha-medicine.md): Vaḷi, aḻal and aiyam (the three kuṟṟam).
