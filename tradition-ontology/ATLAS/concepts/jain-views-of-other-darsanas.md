## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Every rival doctrine is a standpoint taken as absolute (Sanmati 3.47); the Jina's word is the union of the partial views (Sanmati 3.69); the Jain doctrine alone, accepting all standpoints, is not partisan (Anyayogavyavacchedikā 30). Polemical works refute other schools; doxographies present them without polemic (Ṣaḍdarśanasamuccaya).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Haribhadra: no partiality for Mahāvīra, no hatred for Kapila — accept whatever is reasoned (Lokatattvanirṇaya 38); the goal is one under many names (YDS 129–130); Ānandaghana: the six darśanas are the limbs of the Jina (Covīsī 21).
