# Single refuge (ekaśaraṇa)

## Names

## Definitions
- [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md): Refuge in Kṛṣṇa alone, excluding other gods, images and Vedic rites; as watering the root nourishes the whole tree, worship of Kṛṣṇa satisfies all.
