## Definitions
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The two inquiries (into dharma and into Brahman) are distinguished; Śabara drew his proof of the self from the Śārīraka and Upavarṣa deferred it there (as reported by Śaṅkara); Kumārila says the Upaniṣads make the knowledge of the self firm; later Bhāṭṭas read Upaniṣads on the agent-self as serving rites and those on the non-transmigrating self as serving meditations.
