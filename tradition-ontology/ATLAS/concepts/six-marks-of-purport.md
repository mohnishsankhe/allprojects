## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Opening and close, repetition, novelty, fruit, praise, demonstration — used in hearing to determine that the Upaniṣads' purport is the non-dual Brahman (from Mīmāṃsā).
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Mīmāṃsā supplies the criteria that Vedānta manuals list as six marks: the opening and close of a passage (upakrama–upasaṃhāra; their relative strength argued in the Upakramaparākrama), repetition (abhyāsa, which marks a distinct rite, MS 2.2.2), novelty (apūrvatā: an injunction is for what is otherwise unobtained), the stated result (phala, MS 4.3), praise (arthavāda, MS 1.2.7) and reasoned demonstration (upapatti); doubtful sense is settled by the complementary passage (MS 1.4.29).
