## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Anger, pride, deceit and greed, each with four varieties: anantānubandhī, apratyākhyāna, pratyākhyāna, saṃjvalana; sixteen in all, forming the conduct-deluding karma of the passions.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Anger, pride, deceit and greed, each in four grades — endless-binding (blocking right view), blocking partial renunciation, blocking full renunciation, and smouldering (blocking perfect conduct) — with nine quasi-passions (TS 8.9).
