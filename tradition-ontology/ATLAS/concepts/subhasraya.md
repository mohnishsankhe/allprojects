## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The mind in yoga needs a support: Brahman in its formed and formless aspects; since the beginner cannot hold the formless, he meditates on Viṣṇu's form, the ground of all powers, which alone purifies (VP 6.7.45-78).
