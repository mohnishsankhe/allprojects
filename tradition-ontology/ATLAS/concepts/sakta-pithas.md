## Definitions
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): The seats where parts of Satī fell, each with its goddess and guardian Bhairava; the Kālikā Purāṇa knows a small set (18.41-50); the late Pīṭhanirṇaya lists 51 (Bengali lists 51 or 52, with upapīṭhas); other texts count 108.
