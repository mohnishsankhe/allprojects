## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Vism XXII p. 694: abandoning by substitution of opposites (tad-aṅga-pahāna) is the abandoning of each state by a factor of knowledge that is its opposite; the text pairs, for instance, defining mentality-materiality with personality view, discerning conditions with the view of no cause, seeing arising with the annihilation view, seeing passing away with the eternity view, and lists eighteen principal insights each abandoning one thing (contemplation of impermanence the perception of permanence, and so on).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): By substitution of opposites (in insight), by suppression (in concentration), by cutting off (in the path), by tranquillization (in fruition), by escape (nibbāna).
