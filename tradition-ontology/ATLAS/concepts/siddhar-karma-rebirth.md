## Definitions
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Most Siddhar voices take rebirth under the two deeds for granted — 'how many mothers, how many births' (Paṭṭiṉattār), the Lord drives us by the rope of the two deeds (Tiruttillai 11), one's own deed burns oneself — while a famous Sivavākkiyar verse says the dead are not born again. Burning the old fate ends the two deeds (Tiruvaḷḷuvar ñāṉam 13).
