# The six (later seven) categories of Vaiśeṣika

## Names
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): ṣaṭ padārthāḥ / sapta padārthāḥ

## Definitions
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Substance, quality, motion, universal, particularity, inherence (Praśastapāda; Upaskāra VS 1.1.4), to which absence is added as the seventh (Udayana's Lakṣaṇāvalī, Śivāditya, Śrīvallabha); absence was not listed earlier because it is determined through its counterpositive, not because unreal (Kiraṇāvalī). Candramati's treatise counts ten.
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Seven categories (Tarkasaṅgraha, Bhāṣāpariccheda); capacity, similarity and number are not additional categories (argued from Udayana's principle of exclusive alternatives).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Accepted as further objects of knowledge besides the twelve (NBh 1.1.9).
