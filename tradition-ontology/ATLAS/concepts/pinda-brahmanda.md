# The body as microcosm (piṇḍa and brahmāṇḍa)

`cpt:pinda-brahmanda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-energy

## Names
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): piṇḍa-brahmāṇḍa

## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Everything in the cosmic egg is within the body (SSP 3); the elements, channels and doors of the body are enumerated (AmŚ 11–32); the yogī must know his own body — six centres, sixteen supports, three aims, five voids, a house of one pillar and nine doors (GŚ-Briggs 13–14); 'the swallowing of the body by the body' is the Nāth secret (Jñāneśvarī 6.291).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The fourteen worlds are within the body as principles (KJN 2.4–5); the one who knows the Akulavīra as abiding in the body is freed (Akulavīra B.93).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The Śiva Saṃhitā places Meru, the continents, rivers, oceans, seers, stars, planets, sacred fords and their deities, sun and moon and the elements within the body; 'he who knows this is a yogin' (2.1–5); the body is called brahmāṇḍa (1.91).
