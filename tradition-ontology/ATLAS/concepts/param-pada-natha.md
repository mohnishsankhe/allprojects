## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The supreme is named the 'state' (pada), the spotless (nirañjana), the invisible (alakh), the void (śūnya) and Śiva; it is stainless, motionless, sky-like knowledge and bliss, into which the yogī enters as milk into milk (GŚ 99–100); the yogī meditates on the supreme state 'where there is neither temple nor mosque' (Gorakh Bānī).
