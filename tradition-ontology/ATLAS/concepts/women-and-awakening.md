## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Women who go forth can realise stream-entry to arahantship (Cv 10.1.3); the nuns Somā and Vajirā answer Māra that womanhood is irrelevant to the concentrated mind (SN 5.2; Thig 3.8); the Therīgāthā records arahant nuns; the canon also states that a woman cannot be a fully awakened Buddha (MN 115; AN 1.279) and the Buddhavaṃsa names maleness among the conditions of the Buddha-aspiration (Bv 2.59).
