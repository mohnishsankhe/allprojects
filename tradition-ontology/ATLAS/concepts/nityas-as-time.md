## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The fifteen Nityās are the fifteen lunar days, the unfolding of time (Bhāvanā Upaniṣad); the Śrīvidyā's digits wax and wane with the moon over sixteen days, so the moon is the Śrīcakra (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 10); the Tantrarāja correlates the Nityās with days and breaths.
