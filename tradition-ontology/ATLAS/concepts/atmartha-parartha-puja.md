## Definitions
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Worship is twofold: for oneself, in the liṅga received at initiation or on a ground-altar, for one's own fruit; for others, in temples with installed liṅgas, for the king and realm.
