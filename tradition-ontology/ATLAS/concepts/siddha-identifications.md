## Definitions
- [Tantra as a movement across the traditions (Tantraśāstra; the Mantramārga / Mantranaya current)](../lineages/tantra-movement.md): Several siddhas bear the same or cognate names in both traditions and share legends: Mīnapa and Matsyendra (Mīnanātha) both learn Śiva's secret teaching inside a fish; Jālandhara is Hāḍipā of the Bengali Nāth cycle and Jālandharipā of the Buddhist list, teacher of Kāṇha (Kānipā, Kāṇhapā); Cauraṅgī is the maimed prince restored in both; Gorakṣa appears in the Buddhist list as Cauraṅgī's cowherd attendant; Virūpa/Virūpākṣa is named in the Haṭhapradīpikā and credited in Tibet with the Amṛtasiddhi. Each tradition tells the stories within its own teaching.
