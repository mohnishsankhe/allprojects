## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): There is not the least difference between saṃsāra and nirvāṇa; their limits are the same (MMK 25.19–20); there is nothing to superimpose on nirvāṇa and nothing to remove from saṃsāra (16.10); those who see reality conceive neither (YṢ 5–6).
