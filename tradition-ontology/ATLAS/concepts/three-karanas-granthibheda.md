## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Before right view first arises the soul passes through three processes of purification — the ordinary, the unprecedented and the non-returning — which break the dense knot (granthi) of attachment and aversion; right view then arises (karma literature; Haribhadra places the knot's breaking at the sthirā view).
