## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Giving, morality, patience, vigor, meditation and wisdom; wisdom leads the other five, which without it are not even called perfections; giving should be unsupported, patience free of the notion of self.
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Giving, discipline, patience, vigour, meditation and wisdom, each the subject of a chapter of the Bodhicaryāvatāra (5–9) or a ground of the Madhyamakāvatāra (1–6); all are taught for the sake of wisdom (BCA 9.1).
