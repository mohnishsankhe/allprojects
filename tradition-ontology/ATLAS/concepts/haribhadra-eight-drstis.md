## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Mitrā, tārā, balā, dīprā, sthirā, kāntā, prabhā, parā (YDS 13), with light like straw-fire, cow-dung fire, wood fire, lamp, gem, star, sun and moon (15), correlated with the eight limbs (yama … samādhi), the removal of eight faults (fatigue, anxiety, distraction, agitation, confusion, delight in other, pain, attachment) and the rise of eight qualities (non-aversion, desire to know, desire to hear, hearing, understanding, reflection, right apprehension, right practice) (16); the first four are unstable and precede the breaking of the knot, the last four are stable.
