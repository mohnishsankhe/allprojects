## Definitions
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): Four lamps: the far-reaching water lamp (the eyes), the lamp of the empty sphere, the lamp of the pure expanse, and the lamp of self-arising wisdom; six when the lamp of the flesh heart and of the smooth white channel are added. Doctrinal anatomy; their use belongs to the restricted direct crossing.
