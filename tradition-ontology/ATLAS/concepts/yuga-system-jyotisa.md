# The yugas and kalpa in the siddhāntas

## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The caturyuga of 4,320,000 solar years is divided 4:3:2:1 by the feet of dharma; 71 caturyugas and a junction make a manvantara; 14 manvantaras and junctions make a kalpa of 1,000 caturyugas, a day of Brahmā, whose life is 100 years, half now passed (SS 1.15–21). Āryabhaṭa instead makes the four quarters equal and the kalpa 14 × 72 yugas (Āryabhaṭīya 1.5), which Brahmagupta rejects as contrary to smṛti (BSS 1.9).
