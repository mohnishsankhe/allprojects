## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Brahman in itself is without attributes; the same Brahman with the adjunct of māyā is the Lord with attributes, object of worship, real at the empirical level. Texts teaching form serve meditation (BS 3.2.15).
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): No difference between the formless and the embodied; the formless becomes embodied by the devotee's love; the name mediates between them; yet for the devotee the embodied Rāma and devotion are to be preferred (Bhuśuṇḍi against Lomaśa).
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): Viṭṭhala standing on the brick is the self-known One; Jñāneśvar's non-dual teaching and the saints' love of the form are held together.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The Sants place the nirguṇa above the saguṇa: stone images, pilgrimages and the avatāra (the son of Daśaratha) do not reach the secret of the Name.
- [Caraṇdāsī sampradāya](../lineages/charandasi.md): Caraṇdās joins both: nirguṇa knowledge with devotion to Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.
