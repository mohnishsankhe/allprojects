## Definitions
- [The Kerala tradition of jyotiṣa](../lineages/kerala-jyotisa.md): A question is answered from the chart of its moment and the omens present (Ṣaṭpañcāśikā); in Kerala the aṣṭamaṅgala-praśna uses eight auspicious objects, the querist's touch and a ritual setting, and temple queries (devaprāśna) diagnose the deity's displeasure; afflictions are traced to curses and remedied (Praśna Mārga).
