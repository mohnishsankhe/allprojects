## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The sūtras record both the view that a woman's body cannot attain buddhahood (Śāriputra's objection; the nāga princess transforming into a man; Bhaiṣajyaguru's and Amitābha's vows that women may be freed from the woman's state) and the teaching that all dharmas are neither male nor female (the goddess in Vimalakīrti), together with women teachers (Śrīmālā) and women predicted to buddhahood (the Ganges goddess).
