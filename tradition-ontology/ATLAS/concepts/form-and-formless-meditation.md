## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Meditation on the Goddess is of two kinds; the formless is beyond speech and mind and reachable only by yogins through great effort; a form is imagined for the formless Kālī to steady the mind and awaken subtle meditation (Mahānirvāṇa 5.137-140); the formless Goddess produces form for the sake of worship (Nirvāṇa Tantra 1.2-5).
