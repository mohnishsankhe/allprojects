## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Auspicious activity binds merit (pleasant feeling, auspicious lifespan, name and status), inauspicious binds demerit (TS 6.3, 8.25–26).
- [Adhyātma movement (Adhyātma-mata)](../lineages/adhyatma-jain.md): Merit too binds — a golden fetter; the aim is the pure state beyond both.
