## Definitions
- [Tantra as a movement across the traditions (Tantraśāstra; the Mantramārga / Mantranaya current)](../lineages/tantra-movement.md): Across the Śaiva, Śākta, Vaiṣṇava, Buddhist and Jain tantras the same elements recur: initiation into a lineage and mantra; the mantra as the deity; the maṇḍala or yantra as the deity's seat and body; purification of the elements and installation of mantras on the body before worship; identification with the deity; the subtle body of channels and centres; the guru as the deity; secrecy and eligibility; and the twin aims of power (siddhi, bhukti) and liberation. Each family arranges them within its own view and goal.
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The Mantramārga's ritual core: dīkṣā that destroys bonds, daily and occasional worship with nyāsa and bhūtaśuddhi, mantras as Śiva's body, and the sādhaka's pursuit of siddhis beside the liberation-seeker's path.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The Pāñcarātra saṃhitās, spoken by Nārāyaṇa, teach initiation, mantra, maṇḍala, nyāsa and temple worship as the means to reach the Lord; the Sātvata, Pauṣkara and Jayākhya are called its three gems.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The Mantranaya adds to the Mahāyāna path consecration, the deity's maṇḍala and mantra, and yoga with the channels, winds and drops, holding it swifter than the perfection vehicle.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Jain mantra-kalpas (Bhairava-Padmāvatī-kalpa, Jvālāmālinī-kalpa) teach the worship of the Jinas' attendant goddesses with seed syllables, yantras, body-protection and the six rites, for protection and worldly ends under devotion to the Jina; liberation stays the Jain liberation.
