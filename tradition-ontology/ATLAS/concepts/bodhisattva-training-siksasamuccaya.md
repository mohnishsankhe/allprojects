## Definitions
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): The training is to give away one's body, enjoyments and merit of the three times to all beings, and to protect, purify and increase them (ŚSK 4), rooted in faith and the resolve for awakening (ŚSK 2).
