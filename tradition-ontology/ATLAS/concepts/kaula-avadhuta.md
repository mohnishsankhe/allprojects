## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The avadhūta stage is the saṃnyāsa of the Kali age (Mahānirvāṇa 8.221); avadhūtas are of four kinds, complete (paramahaṃsa) and incomplete; the Śaiva avadhūta is consecrated by the complete consecration (14.142-149).
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The vīra avadhūta holds 'I am Śiva, the bliss of Bhairava'; fruits are graded by caste (Nirvāṇa Tantra 14.1-10).
