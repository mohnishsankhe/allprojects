## Definitions
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Those who desire enjoyment, liberation or powers are restless; the devotee is desireless and so at peace.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): The 'supernatural capacity' given by grace is capacity to relish the Lord, not yogic power.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The Āḻvārs want neither Indra's kingdom nor dharma, wealth and pleasure, nor the 'drum' as a reward, but only service and devotion in every birth; the Tiruppallāṇṭu admits seekers of wealth and of self-realisation (kaivalya) only if they give those aims up.
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Having gained love one desires nothing and has no zeal for other ends; devotion is higher than action, knowledge and yoga.
