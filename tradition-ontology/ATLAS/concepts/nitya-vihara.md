## Definitions
- [Rādhāvallabha sampradāya](../lineages/radhavallabha.md): The pair's love-play in the groves is eternal, without beginning, separation or departure; separation is only felt within union.
- [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md): The natural pair play for ever in the nikuñja.
