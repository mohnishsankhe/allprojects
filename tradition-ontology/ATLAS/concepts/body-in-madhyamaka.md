## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The body is analysed into its parts and elements and not found as a self (BCA 9.58–60; RĀ 2.1); it is to be protected as an instrument for beings' benefit and given to them (ŚSK 4–5); bodies arise from action and afflictions, which are empty (MMK 17.27). No subtle-body anatomy of channels and centres is taught in these sūtra-path texts (that belongs to the Vajrayāna).
