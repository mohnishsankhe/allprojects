## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Tripurā is prior to every triad — abodes, principles, seats, liṅgas, letters (Dīpikā on YH 1.6); her city is threefold as triangle, circle and square and her mantra three-part (Bhāskararāya); she is 'trayī, abode of the three aims, dwelling in the three, garlanded by the three cities' (LSN 162-163) and 'trimūrti' (LSN 125); the Tripurā Upaniṣad opens with 'three cities, three paths'.
- [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md): The Bahvṛca Upaniṣad (4) identifies the three cities with the three bodies, pervaded and illumined by Mahātripurasundarī as the inward consciousness.
- [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md): She existed before the three lights (Cidvallī on KKV 2).
