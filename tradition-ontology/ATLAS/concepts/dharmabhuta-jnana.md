## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Consciousness as an attribute of the self, distinct from the self-luminous self that has it: eternal, it contracts through karma in bondage and expands to omniscience in release; it reveals objects to its owner and is manifest only to him.
