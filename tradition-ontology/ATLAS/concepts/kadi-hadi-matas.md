## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The two living forms of the vidyā: Kādi, after Manmatha (Kāmarāja), taught by the Tantrarāja ('Kādimata') and encoded in SL 32; Hādi, after Lopāmudrā. The Bhāvanā Upaniṣad teaches its contemplation 'according to the Kādi and Hādi doctrines'; the Bahvṛca Upaniṣad names the Kādi, Hādi and Sādi vidyās; the twelve upāsakas each have their own form.
