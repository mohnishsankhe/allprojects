## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Time is twofold: the time that ends the worlds, and reckonable time, gross (embodied, from the breath) and subtle (unembodied, from the truṭi) (SS 1.10–11); time is without beginning or end and is inferred from the planets and stars (Āryabhaṭīya 3.11).
