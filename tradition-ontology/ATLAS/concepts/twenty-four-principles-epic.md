## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13.6–7: the field is listed as the great elements, ahaṅkāra, buddhi, the unmanifest, the ten senses and the one, the five ranges of the senses, with desire, aversion, pleasure, pain, the aggregate, sentience and steadiness; the text does not number them.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The Sāṃkhyas enumerate twenty-four principles together with prakṛti (the unmanifest), the twenty-fifth being beyond them (MBh 12.294.42); the Gītā's field lists the great elements, ego, intellect, the unmanifest, the ten organs and mind, and the five objects (BhG 13.5).
