## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The earth-disc: Jambudvīpa with Meru at its centre, surrounded by the salt ocean; then Plakṣa, Śālmali, Kuśa, Krauñca, Śāka and Puṣkara dvīpas, each ringed by an ocean (of sugar-cane juice, wine, ghee, curd, milk and sweet water respectively), then a golden land, the Lokāloka range and darkness; Jambudvīpa has nine varṣas, of which Bhārata is the land of action.
