## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Vāsanās are manifested only in the form fitted to the ripening of karma (4.8), succeed one another without break across birth, place and time because memory and saṃskāra have one form (4.9), are beginningless because the wish to be is universal (4.10), and are held together by cause, fruit, support and object, so that they cease when these are absent (4.11).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Latent tendencies, laid down by the experience of afflictions, karma and fruition, manifest only when suited to the ripening karma (4.8), stay connected across births, places and times (4.9), are beginningless because the will to be is eternal (4.10), and cease when their cause, result, substrate and support cease (4.11); the mind, though variegated by them, exists for another (4.24).
