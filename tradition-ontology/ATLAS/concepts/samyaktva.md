## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Conviction in the realities (TS 1.2), arising spontaneously or by instruction (1.3); its transgressions are doubt, desire, disgust, praise and acquaintance of wrong-believers (7.23); its eight limbs, three kinds (subsidential, mixed, destructional) and five signs (calm, longing for release, detachment, compassion, affirmation).
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Faith in the true deity, scripture and teacher, free of three follies and eight prides, with eight limbs (Ratnakaraṇḍa 4).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): To regard the true god as god, the true teacher as teacher and pure dharma as dharma (YŚ 2.2).
- [Adhyātma movement (Adhyātma-mata)](../lineages/adhyatma-jain.md): The soul that takes refuge in the real (the pure self) is of right view (Samayasāra 11).
