## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): A thousand cycles of four ages (mahāyugas) with fourteen Manus make a day of Brahmā (kalpa), followed by a night of equal length; Brahmā lives a hundred years of such days, in two halves (parārdha); the present is the first day of his second half (Śveta-Vārāha kalpa, seventh manvantara, Kali age - tradition's reckoning).
