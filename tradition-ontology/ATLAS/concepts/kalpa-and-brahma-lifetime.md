## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 8.17, 9.7–8: the day of Brahmā extends to a thousand yugas and his night likewise (8.17); at the end of a kalpa all beings go into the Lord's prakṛti, and at the beginning of a kalpa he, taking hold of his own prakṛti, sends forth again the whole multitude of beings, helpless under prakṛti's power (9.7–8).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): A thousand cycles of four ages (mahāyugas) with fourteen Manus make a day of Brahmā (kalpa), followed by a night of equal length; Brahmā lives a hundred years of such days, in two halves (parārdha); the present is the first day of his second half (Śveta-Vārāha kalpa, seventh manvantara, Kali age - tradition's reckoning).
