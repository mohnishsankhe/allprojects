## Definitions
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): Songs speak in double meanings (sandhyā-bhāṣā) of the body, the moons, the tide and the Man, so that practice is taught only to the initiated.
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): The manuals and padas conceal the practice in the language of Vaiṣṇava rasa.
- [Kartābhajā](../lineages/kartabhaja.md): The Bhāber Gīt is deliberately obscure, using the imagery of the bazaar and the company; the teaching is gupta.
