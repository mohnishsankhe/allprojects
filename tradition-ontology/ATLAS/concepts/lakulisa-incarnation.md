## Definitions
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The tradition's accounts: Śiva enters an abandoned corpse in a cremation ground and becomes the brahmacārin Lakulī at Kāyāvatāra, with four disciples (Liṅga P.); the Lord in a brahmin's body descends at Kāyāvataraṇa and teaches Kuśika at Ujjayinī (Kauṇḍinya); Śiva is born to Viśvarūpa and Sudarśanā, enters the body of the dead infant, and at last merges into the Brahmeśvara liṅga at Kāyāvarohaṇa (Kāravaṇa-māhātmya).
