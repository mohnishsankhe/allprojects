## Definitions
- [Kālacakra tradition in India](../lineages/kalacakra.md): Smoke, mirage, fireflies, lamp, flame, moon, sun, darkness (Rāhu), the digit (kalā, lightning) and the great drop, after which the universal image of empty form appears (Sekoddeśa 26).
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): The empty forms that appear in the branch of withdrawal of the Jonang six-branch yoga: smoke, mirage, fireflies, lamp, blaze, moon, sun, darkness, lightning and drop.
