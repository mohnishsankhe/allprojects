## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Matsya 53.68-69: sāttvika Purāṇas glorify Hari, rājasa Brahmā, tāmasa Agni and Śiva, mixed ones Sarasvatī and the ancestors. The Padma (as cited) assigns six Purāṇas to each guṇa; the Garuḍa's Brahmakāṇḍa gives a different, graded list. Śaiva texts counter with lists in which most Purāṇas glorify Śiva (not checked here).
