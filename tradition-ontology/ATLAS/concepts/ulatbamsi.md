## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Riddling paradox used to point to the reversed practice — the woman enjoyed by two (GŚ 56), the inverted well in the sky (Gorakh Bānī).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Riddling songs of impossible images (the lion grazing cows, the son born before the mother, the guru at the disciple's feet) that turn ordinary perception upside down; 'whoever understands this song is liberated'.
