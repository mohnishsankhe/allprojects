## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): 'The gods love the cryptic and dislike the obvious': ritual and cosmic realities are given indirect names (indha → Indra; svāpyaya → svapna), and the teaching of correspondences is itself 'secret' (ŚB 10.5.2.20; 14.6.11.2; AB 3.43).
