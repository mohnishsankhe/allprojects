## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The person comprises body (tanū, śarīra), life-breath (asu, prāṇa), mind (manas), breath-self (ātman) and, in one reading, an 'unborn part' (RV 10.16.4); at death eye goes to the sun and breath to the wind (10.16.3); the immortal is of one origin with the mortal (1.164.30, 38); the seer does not know what he is (1.164.37) yet can say 'I was Manu and the Sun' (4.26.1); Sūrya is the self of all (1.115.1); the wise, ageless self is known without fear of death (AVŚ 10.8.44).
