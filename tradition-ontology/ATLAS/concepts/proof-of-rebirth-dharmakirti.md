## Definitions
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Breath, senses and cognition do not arise from the body alone without a prior moment of their own kind; so the first moment of mind in a new life comes from a previous mind, and cultivation can continue across lives.
