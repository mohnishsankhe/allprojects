## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The teaching descends from the omniscient Tīrthaṅkara through the gaṇadharas and the lineage of ācāryas; initiation (dīkṣā) with hair-plucking and the great vows; study under the teacher (vācanā); confession and expiation before the teacher; the teacher adapts instruction to the student; the ācārya receives the secret Sūrimantra at installation (Śvetāmbara).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Haribhadra: conventional yoga is serving the teacher and hearing scripture according to capacity (Yogaśataka 3–4); Hemacandra adds what is 'established by experience' to what was learned from scripture and teacher (YŚ 12.1).
