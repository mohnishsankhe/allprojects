# The conjunction of puruṣa and prakṛti (saṃyoga)

`cpt:conjunction-of-purusa-and-prakrti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** karma-rebirth

## Names
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): saṃyoga

## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Their conjunction, like that of the lame and the blind, produces creation (SK 21), makes the unconscious seem conscious and the indifferent seem an agent (SK 20); it is due to non-discrimination (SS 1.55) and its cause is variously given as karma, non-discrimination (Pañcaśikha) or the subtle body (Sanandana) (SS 6.67–69).
