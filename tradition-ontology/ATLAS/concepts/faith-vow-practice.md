## Definitions
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Faith, vow and holding the name are the three provisions for birth: whether one is born depends on faith and vow, the grade on the depth of practice (Ouyi); faith is the source of the way (Wang Rixiu).
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): Believe that birth is attained with one calling, yet practise with the whole of life (Hōnen).
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): Practice (gyō: the name) and entrusting (shin: shinjin) are both Amida's gift and are inseparable; shinjin is the cause and the nenbutsu its expression in gratitude.
