## Definitions
- [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md): The two orders of religious men: the śramaṇa, who has gone forth, and the brāhmaṇa by birth and ritual; grammar gives 'śramaṇa-brāhmaṇa' as the type of perpetual enmity, and Aśoka finds both everywhere and urges concord between sects.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): A true samaṇa or brāhmaṇa is made by conduct — calm, harmlessness, putting away evil — not by birth, shaving or rites.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): One is a śramaṇa by equanimity, a brāhmaṇa by celibacy, and a brāhmaṇa or kṣatriya by deeds.
