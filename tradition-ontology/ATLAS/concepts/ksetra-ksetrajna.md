# The field and the knower of the field (kṣetra–kṣetrajña)

## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The body and all of prakṛti with its modifications is the field; the one who knows it is its knower; the Lord is the knower in all fields; knowledge of both is true knowledge (BhG 13.1–6, 13.33–34).
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): Vāsudeva is the knower of the field (MBh 12.326.38).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: This body is called the field and whoever knows it the knower of the field (13.2); the Lord is also (api) to be known as the knower of the field in all fields, and knowledge of field and knower is knowledge (13.3). The field in brief: the great elements, the sense of 'I', understanding, the unmanifest, the ten faculties and the one, the five domains of the senses, desire, aversion, pleasure, pain, the aggregate, sentience and steadiness, with their modifications (13.6-13.7). Every being arises from the union of field and knower (13.27); the possessor of the field illumines the whole field as the sun the world (13.34); those who know their difference with the eye of knowledge go to the highest (13.35).
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): The body-field of elements, senses, mind and passions is known by the Lord as knower of every field (Jñāneśvarī 13).
