# The field and the knower of the field (kṣetra–kṣetrajña)

`cpt:ksetra-ksetrajna` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** self

## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The body and all of prakṛti with its modifications is the field; the one who knows it is its knower; the Lord is the knower in all fields; knowledge of both is true knowledge (BhG 13.1–6, 13.33–34).
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): Vāsudeva is the knower of the field (MBh 12.326.38).
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): The body-field of elements, senses, mind and passions is known by the Lord as knower of every field (Jñāneśvarī 13).
