## Definitions
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Student, householder, forest-hermit and renouncer (MDh 6.87); in sequence they lead to the highest state (MDh 6.88) with the householder as their support and best (MDh 3.77–78; 6.89–90); some allow free choice after studentship (GDh 3.1; VDh 7.3), 'the teachers' recognize only the householder (GDh 3.36; BDh 2.6.11.27), and Āpastamba lists them with his own order (householder first) while criticizing liberation by knowledge alone (ĀpDh 2.9.21).
