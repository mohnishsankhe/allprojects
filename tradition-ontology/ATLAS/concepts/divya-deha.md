## Definitions
- [Rasa Śāstra (the science of mercury) and the Raseśvara-darśana](../lineages/rasa-sastra.md): The body born of the creation of Hara and Gaurī, permanent though the six-sheathed body is not; those who gain it without abandoning the body are the rasa-siddhas, served by mantras (SDS 7-9; Rasahṛdaya 1.7).
