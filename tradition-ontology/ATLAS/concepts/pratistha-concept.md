## Definitions
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The series of rites from ploughing the site to installation, performed with a single root āgama; by it the deity is established in liṅga or image.
- [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md): The fixed and movable images are installed and linked; the fixed stands for the partless, the movable for the partite form.
- [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md): Installation of the seven deities, reserved to the tantri.
