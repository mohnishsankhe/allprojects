## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The seers 'saw' the hymns and had direct perception of dharma, handing them on by instruction (Nirukta 1.20, 2.11); speech was found entered into the seers (RV 10.71.3); Vāc makes whom she loves a seer (10.125.5); Varuṇa made Vasiṣṭha a seer (7.88.4); the hymns also speak of being 'fashioned' by the poets (5.2.11).
- [Nairukta school (the Vedic etymologists)](../lineages/nairukta.md): A ṛṣi is so called from seeing the hymns; Brahman the self-existent came upon the seers in their austerity; they had direct vision of dharma (Nirukta 2.11; 1.20).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Seers are no longer born because of transgression, yet some become 'seers by learning' through residual merit (ĀpDh 1.2.5.5).
