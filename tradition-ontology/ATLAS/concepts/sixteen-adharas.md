## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Sixteen places of the body on which attention is fixed (from the big toe to the brahmarandhra in the usual list), to be known by the yogī (GŚ-Briggs 13; SSP 2).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Jālandhara 'binds the sixteen supports' (HYP 3.73); the Vivekamārtaṇḍa family lists them among what the yogin must know in his own body; the Haṭhatattvakaumudī devotes a chapter to them.
