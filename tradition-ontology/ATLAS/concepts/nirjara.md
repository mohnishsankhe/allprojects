## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The falling away of karma, either after fruition (savipāka) or ahead of time through austerity (avipāka) (TS 8.23, 9.3); shedding increases innumerable-fold at each of ten stages from right view to the Jina (9.45).
