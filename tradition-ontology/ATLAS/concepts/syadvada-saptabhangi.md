## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Every predication is qualified by 'syāt' (in some respect); with respect to any attribute a thing admits seven predications: is, is not, is and is not, inexpressible, is and inexpressible, is not and inexpressible, is, is not and inexpressible (Āptamīmāṃsā 14, 104–105); statements presenting the whole belong to pramāṇa, partial ones to naya (Rājavārttika).
