# The eternality of words (śabda-nityatva)

## Definitions
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Words, i.e. the letters, are eternal and manifested by utterance: utterance is for conveying to another; the same word is recognized everywhere; utterances are counted as repetitions; there is no cause of destruction (MS 1.1.18–23, against the view of 1.1.6–11).
