## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The condition of the dead between death and union with the ancestors; it becomes fixed for those who die badly, do great wrong or lack the rites (e.g. no bull released on the eleventh day), who then suffer hunger and thirst and trouble their kin (dreams, signs); it is ended by śrāddha, gifts, nārāyaṇa-bali and hearing sacred texts (the Bhāgavata saptāha frees Dhundhukārī).
