## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Kapila, the supreme sage and first knower, gave the teaching out of compassion to Āsuri, Āsuri to Pañcaśikha, who expanded it; through a succession of pupils it reached Īśvarakṛṣṇa (SK 69–71). The Māṭhara Vṛtti names Bhārgava, Ulūka, Vālmīki, Hārīta and Devala; the Yuktidīpikā names Hārīta, Vāddhali, Kairāta, Paurika, Ṛṣabheśvara, Pañcādhikaraṇa, Patañjali, Vārṣagaṇya, Kauṇḍinya and Mūka, adding that the full lineage cannot be told because it began with the Blessed One. Gauḍapāda places Kapila among Brahmā's seven sons with Āsuri, Voḍhu and Pañcaśikha.
