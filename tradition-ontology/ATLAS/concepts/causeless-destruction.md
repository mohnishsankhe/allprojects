## Definitions
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): Destruction needs no cause; everything arisen perishes of itself at once; apparent destruction by fire is the non-arising of a similar series.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Things are momentary, but impermanence is a real characteristic acting on each dharma.
