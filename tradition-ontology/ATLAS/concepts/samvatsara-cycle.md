# The Jupiter years and the sixty-year cycle

## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Jupiter's years are named after the month-asterism of his heliacal rising (BS 8.1–2); the sixty-year cycle, beginning with Prabhava when Jupiter rises in Māgha at the start of Dhaniṣṭhā, consists of twelve five-year yugas whose years repeat the Vedic names saṃvatsara, parivatsara, idāvatsara, anuvatsara and idvatsara (BS 8.23–29).
