## Definitions
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): The manuals classify desire-driven applications of mantras into six rites — pacification (śānti), subjugation (vaśya), immobilization (stambhana), causing enmity (vidveṣaṇa), expulsion (uccāṭana) and killing (māraṇa) — assigning to each its direction, time, seat and fire-pit; they belong to the worldly (kāmya) use of mantras, distinct from their use for liberation.
