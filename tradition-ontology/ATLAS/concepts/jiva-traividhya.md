## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): By nature souls are of three classes: fit for liberation (gods, sages, ancestors, rulers, the best humans), ever-transmigrating (middling humans), and fit for blinding darkness (daityas, rākṣasas, piśācas, the lowest humans). The texts state this plainly; souls of the third class reach darkness especially through hatred of Hari.
