## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Beings who experience a state 'like kaivalya' but return: the bodiless (gods, per Vyāsa) pass through the ripening of their impressions; those merged in prakṛti remain until the mind returns by force of its unfinished task (YBh 1.19); they are placed outside the worlds, 'in the state of liberation' (YBh 3.26). Bhoja identifies the bodiless with those who rest content in the bliss-samādhi and the prakṛtilayas with those content in the I-am-ness samādhi (RM 1.17).
