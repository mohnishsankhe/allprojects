## Definitions
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Rūpa: the gopīs' paramour love is the summit of amorous rasa; Jīva: they are Kṛṣṇa's own potencies, so svakīyā in truth; Viśvanātha: parakīyā eternally. Love is defined as desire for Kṛṣṇa's pleasure, not one's own.
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): Rādhā as Kṛṣṇa's eternal consort; the school's view is generally described as svakīyā.
