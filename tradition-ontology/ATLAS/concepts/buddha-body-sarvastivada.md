## Definitions
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Refuge in the Buddha is refuge in the non-learner's dharmas that make a Buddha, not in his body; all Buddhas are equal in accumulation, dharma-body and benefit to the world, not in lifespan, caste or stature; the eighteen exclusive qualities.
