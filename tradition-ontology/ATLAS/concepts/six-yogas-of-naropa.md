## Definitions
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): Inner heat, illusory body, dream, clear light, intermediate state and transference (with 'entering another's body' and, in some lists, the consort practice) — gathered by Tilopa from four Indian transmissions and given by Nāropa to Marpa; the Kagyu 'path of method'. Lists vary between sources: Tilopa's Ṣaḍdharmopadeśa treats transference and grong 'jug together; the Karṇatantravajrapada adds karmamudrā.
- [Shangpa Kagyu](../lineages/shangpa-kagyu.md): Niguma's six dharmas, a parallel set with the same six topics, taught in the Shangpa.
