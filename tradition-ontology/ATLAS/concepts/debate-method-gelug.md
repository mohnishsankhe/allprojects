## Definitions
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): A standing challenger, opening with Mañjuśrī's syllable 'dhīḥ', poses consequences to a seated defender, clapping and stamping at each point; the defender may answer only 'I accept', 'the reason is not established', 'there is no pervasion' or ask 'why?'; debate is a meditation that sharpens analysis and cuts wrong views.
