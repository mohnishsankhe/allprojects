## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Death comes by exhaustion of life-span, of kamma, of both, or by a destructive kamma; at death a kamma, a kamma-sign or a destiny-sign presents itself; death consciousness is followed immediately by rebirth-linking with that object — there is no intermediate existence.
