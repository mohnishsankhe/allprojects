## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Life is the joint activity of the senses, fivefold: prāṇa (mouth and nose to the heart), samāna (to the navel), apāna (down to the soles), udāna (up to the head) and vyāna (pervading); prāṇa is chief (YBh 3.39); mastery of udāna and samāna yields powers (3.39–40).
