## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The six plus skillful means, vow, power and knowledge, one predominating on each of the ten grounds.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): Generosity, discipline, patience, vigour, meditation, wisdom, skill in means, aspiration, power and knowledge, matched to the ten grounds.
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): The six with method, aspiration, power and knowledge, each predominant on one of the ten grounds (MA chs. 1–10).
