## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The soul's states are subsidential, destructional, mixed, rising and inherent, with 2, 9, 18, 21 and 3 divisions (TS 2.1–7); at liberation all cease except perfect right view, knowledge, perception and siddhahood (10.3–4).
