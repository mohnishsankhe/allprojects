## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Triśikhi 119–127: the cessation of throbbing at particular points of the limbs and failure to see the tip of one's tongue foretell the time of death.
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The haṭha compendia include the signs of approaching death: the Haṭhatattvakaumudī's chapter on kālavañcana; the Śiva Svarodaya's prognostication from the breath; the Śiva Saṃhitā's gazing at one's shadow-image in the sky (seeing it whole means long life).
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): In jyotiṣa, early death is foretold by ariṣṭa yogas at birth (BJ 6; BPHS 9) and a king's death by portents such as images weeping or sweating (BS 45.8); longevity is computed and the killing planets identified (BJ 7; BPHS 44).
