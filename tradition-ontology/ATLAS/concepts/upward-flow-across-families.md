## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The moon's nectar, consumed by the sun below, is to be kept and turned back; the breath is forced into the central channel.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The fire of caṇḍālī at the navel melts the moon at the head; the drops descend through the four joys and are drawn back upward.
