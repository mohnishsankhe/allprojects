## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Kali is full of faults, and gambling, drink, illicit women, slaughter and gold are its dwellings; yet in Kali merit comes quickly: singing the name gives what earlier ages won by meditation, sacrifice and worship, and śūdras and women gain merit easily by service.
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Congregational chanting of the name is the dharma of Kali, inaugurated by Caitanya; in Kali there is no other way.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): In Kali all paths are ruined; Kṛṣṇa alone is the refuge.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): In Kali neither ritual, devotion nor discernment stands; the name alone supports; yet no age equals Kali for one with faith, since singing Rāma's qualities crosses existence without effort.
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): In Kali the name sung together suffices without sacrifice or austerity.
- [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md): In Kali rites, pilgrimages and other gods do not save; Kṛṣṇa's name is the refuge.
