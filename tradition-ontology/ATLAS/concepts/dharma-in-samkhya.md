## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Virtue is a disposition of buddhi that leads upward but not to release (SK 23, 44); Gauḍapāda defines it through compassion, giving and the Pātañjala restraints and observances. Vedic means are impure through animal killing (SK 2; Vācaspati with Pañcaśikha). The knower of the principles is liberated in any stage of life (verse quoted by Gauḍapāda); outward signs of renunciation without knowledge are a false contentment (Gauḍapāda on SK 50); gifts given with conceit bind (Kramadīpikā); one's own duty supports meditation (SS 3.35).
