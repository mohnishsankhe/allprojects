# Dharma (as known through Vedic injunction)

**Category:** ethics

## Names
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): dharma

## Definitions
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Dharma is the beneficial object (artha) marked by injunction: the enjoined act that joins a person with the highest good; perception cannot know it, and what the Veda states only as a means for harm (the Śyena) is not dharma.
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): An object with a purpose made known by the Veda — the sacrifice and the like (Arthasaṅgraha).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Lineage contribution (Upaniṣadic): created by brahman as the power of the ruling power; nothing higher; dharma is truth (BAU 1.4.14); the three branches of dharma (ChU 2.23.1); 'practise dharma' (TU 1.11); one's own dharma lifts the elemental self (MaiU 4.3); the self is beyond dharma and adharma (KU 1.2.14).
