## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Each of the eleven brāhmaṇas who became Mahāvīra's gaṇadharas held a hidden doubt, resolved by Mahāvīra: whether there is a soul (Indrabhūti), karma (Agnibhūti), a soul other than the body (Vāyubhūti), real elements (Vyakta), rebirth in like form (Sudharman), bondage and liberation (Maṇḍika), gods (Mauryaputra), hell-beings (Akampita), merit and demerit (Acalabhrātṛ), another world (Metārya), nirvāṇa (Prabhāsa).
