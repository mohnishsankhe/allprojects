## Definitions
- [Vaibhāṣika](../lineages/vaibhasika.md): The later Vaibhāṣika count of the real dharmas: 11 of matter, 1 mind, 46 mental factors, 14 dissociated formations, 3 unconditioned — built from the Kośa's classification.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The Prakaraṇapāda's fivefold division (matter, mind, mental factors, dissociated, unconditioned) is the basis of the list.
