# Work as worship (kāyaka)

`cpt:kayaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

## Definitions
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): Honest labour in one's own occupation is worship — 'work is Kailāsa' — and must not be interrupted even for guru, liṅga or jaṅgama, nor gather beyond the day's need.
