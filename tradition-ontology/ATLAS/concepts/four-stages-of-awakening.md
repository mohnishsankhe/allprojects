## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Stream-enterer (three fetters abandoned), once-returner (lust, hatred, delusion attenuated), non-returner (five lower fetters abandoned) and arahant (all ten), each with a path and a fruit — eight noble persons, four pairs (DN 16; MN 64; Ud 5.5); women and men alike can realise all four fruits (Cv 10.1.3).
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Stream-enterer to arhat do not think 'I have attained', for nothing is attained (Vajracchedikā 9).
