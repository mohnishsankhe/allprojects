## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): In the womb the jīva, from the seventh month conscious, remembers its past births and prays to the Lord, resolving to lift itself out; at birth it loses that knowledge. Death is the body's ceasing to function, birth the taking of 'I am this' toward a body.
