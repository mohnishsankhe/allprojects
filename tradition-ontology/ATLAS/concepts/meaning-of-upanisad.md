## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): In the texts 'upaniṣad' means a secret teaching or hidden name/correspondence: its upaniṣad is 'the real behind the real' (BAU 2.1.20); the upaniṣad of the asuras (ChU 8.8.4); 'we have told you the upaniṣad' (Kena 4.7); the rule 'do not beg' is the upaniṣad of the breath-brahman (KauU 2.1). The gods love the cryptic (AU 1.3.14).
