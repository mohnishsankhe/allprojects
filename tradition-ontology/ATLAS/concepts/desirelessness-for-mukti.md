# Not desiring liberation

**Category:** karma-rebirth

## Names

## Definitions
- [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md): The devotees to be honoured are those who do not desire even liberation; one asks only for devotion.
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): Tukārām asks for no liberation or wealth, only never to forget God and the saints' company.
- [Rādhāvallabha sampradāya](../lineages/radhavallabha.md): Beside the pair's rasa, Vaikuṇṭha and liberation are nothing.
