## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Souls are graded, beginninglessly and endlessly, in knowledge, bliss and office — from humans and kings up through gandharvas, ancestors, gods, Indra, Rudra, to Brahmā and Vāyu, with Lakṣmī above and Viṣṇu infinitely above; the gradation is unalterable and continues in liberation, and knowing it is required for liberation.
