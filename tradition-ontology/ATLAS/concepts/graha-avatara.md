## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Janārdana takes the form of the planets to give beings the fruits of karma; to destroy the daityas, strengthen the gods and establish dharma, the avatāras were born from the planets: Rāma from the Sun, Kṛṣṇa from the Moon, Narasiṃha from Mars, the Buddha from Mercury, Vāmana from Jupiter, Paraśurāma from Venus, the Tortoise from Saturn, the Boar from Rāhu and the Fish from Ketu (BPHS 2.3–7).
