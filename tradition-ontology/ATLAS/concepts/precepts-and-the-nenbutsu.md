## Definitions
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Chinese Pure Land joins recitation with precepts and good conduct: the three meritorious acts; Mao Ziyuan's five precepts confirmed by five recitations; Yinguang's 'fulfil your duties, avoid evil'.
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): Precepts are not the condition of birth (they are not the selected practice), yet disciples must not claim the nenbutsu has no precepts or encourages evil (Seven-Article Pledge); Jōdo-shū transmits the bodhisattva precepts.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): No precepts in the last age; licensed evil is nevertheless rejected.
