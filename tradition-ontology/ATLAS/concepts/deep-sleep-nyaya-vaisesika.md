## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): In dreamless sleep there is no affliction — the analogy for liberation's possibility (NS 4.1.63).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): No cognition arises in deep sleep because the mind rests in the channel purītat and lacks contact with the skin-sense, which every cognition requires (commentarial tradition on the Muktāvalī).
