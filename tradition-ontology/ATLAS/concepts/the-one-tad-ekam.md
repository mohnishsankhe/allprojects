## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The Saṃhitās speak of a One behind the many: That One which breathed windless by its own power before creation (RV 10.129.2), the One Being the inspired call by many names (1.164.46), the One that has become all this (8.58.2), the one-alone god in whom the gods become one (AVŚ 13.4), the Unborn that propped apart the six regions (RV 1.164.6), of whom there is no likeness (VS 32.3).
