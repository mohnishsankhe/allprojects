## Definitions
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Who may perform a rite and receive its fruit: humans, not animals or gods (MS 6.1.4–5); women as well as men, husband and wife jointly (MS 6.1.8, 6.1.17); for Vedic rites, those with Vedic learning, excluding śūdras (MS 6.1.25–38); the eligibility injunction states ownership of the fruit.
