## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Kabīr addresses Hindu and Turk alike, rejects both their rites, and uses Persian and Sufi words (pīr, the beloved, the lover's pain) beside Nāth and Vaiṣṇava ones. Recorded as context only.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): Bāuls and Fakirs share gurus, songs and practice; Lalon sings of the original Mecca in the human body. Context only.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Dārā Shikoh's Persian Upaniṣads (Sirr-i Akbar, 1657) and his comparative Majmaʿ al-Baḥrayn (1655), with his recorded questions to Bābā Lāl, are documents of the exchange. Context only.
