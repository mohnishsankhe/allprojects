## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The Jinakalpa — solitary, naked or nearly equipment-less, of extreme rigour, for those of the strongest constitution and scriptural learning — and the Sthavirakalpa — life in the community with minimal clothing and equipment; the first ended after Jambū, the second is the valid mode now.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The Digambaras recognize no such lapse: nudity remains the only mode of full renunciation.
