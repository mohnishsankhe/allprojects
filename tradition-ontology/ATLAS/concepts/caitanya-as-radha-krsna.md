## Definitions
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Caitanya is Kṛṣṇa who took Rādhā's mood and complexion to taste her love; also the yuga-avatāra of Kali inaugurating the chanting of the name.
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): Caitanya is the union of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in one body, and so the model of the sahaja union of the two principles in the human; the Gosvāmīs knew this esoteric meaning.
- [Kartābhajā](../lineages/kartabhaja.md): Caitanya returned as Āulcānd to reveal the sahaja religion.
