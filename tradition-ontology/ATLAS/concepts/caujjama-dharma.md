## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Pārśva taught restraint from violence, falsehood, theft and possessions (chastity being implied in non-possession) and allowed garments; Mahāvīra taught five great vows and nakedness; the Keśin–Gautama dialogue explains the difference by the dispositions of the people of each era, and Pārśva's followers joined Mahāvīra's order.
