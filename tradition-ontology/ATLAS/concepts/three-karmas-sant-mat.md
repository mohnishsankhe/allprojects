## Definitions
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Pralabdh (destiny of this body) must be lived out; sinchit (stored) karma is taken over by the Master and burnt through practice; kriyaman (new) karma is avoided by living by the vows; the initiate is not reborn below the human form and returns home within four lives at most.
