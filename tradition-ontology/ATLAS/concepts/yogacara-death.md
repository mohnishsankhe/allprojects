## Definitions
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): At death the store-consciousness is the last to leave the body and at conception the first to enter it; the body grows cold progressively — for those of good karma from the lower body upward, for those of bad karma from the head downward — until consciousness leaves at the heart; the intermediate existence follows.
