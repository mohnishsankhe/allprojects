# The soul as God's reflection (bimba–pratibimba, Dvaita sense)

## Names
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): bimba–pratibimba

## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The soul is a reflection (pratibimba) of God, its original (bimba): dependent on him, resembling him, and eternally different; the reflecting 'adjunct' is the soul's own nature, so the relation is eternal. The worshipper contemplates: 'You are the original, I am the reflection, though there is a difference between us.' (Not the Advaita reflection theory, in which the reflection is Brahman appearing in an adjunct.)
