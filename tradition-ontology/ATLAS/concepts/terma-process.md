## Definitions
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): Padmasambhava teaches a disciple, seals the teaching in the disciple's mind by aspiration and empowerment, has it written in symbolic script and hidden (in the earth or the mind) under a protector; at the prophesied time the disciple's rebirth, the revealer, finds the sign, decodes it and establishes the teaching, often after years of secrecy.
- [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md): Bön treasures were hidden during the persecutions and recovered by revealers such as Shenchen Luga.
