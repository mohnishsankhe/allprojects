# The tripartition (trivṛt) of heat, water and food

## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Being made heat, water and food each threefold (ChU 6.3.3); the red, white and black forms of fire, sun, moon and lightning are those of heat, water and food, and the thing itself is only a name (ChU 6.4); eaten food, water and heat divide threefold into body and mind (ChU 6.5).
