## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The Brāhmaṇas correlate the classes with Prajāpati's limbs, metres, gods and animals, declaring the śūdra unfit for sacrifice (TS 7.1.1.4–6), with the three Vedas (TB 3.12.9.2), and call learned brāhmaṇas 'human gods' (ŚB 2.2.2.6).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Four classes, three of them twice-born (MDh 10.4), created from the mouth, arms, thighs and feet (MDh 1.31), with duties — teaching and sacrificing; protecting; trade and farming; service (MDh 1.88–91); each earlier class superior by birth (ĀpDh 1.1.1.4–8); a lower class rises and a higher falls in the next birth by conduct (ĀpDh 2.5.11.10–11); a brāhmaṇa who neglects the Veda sinks to a śūdra's state (MDh 2.168); penalties for a śūdra hearing the Veda (GDh 12.4–6).
