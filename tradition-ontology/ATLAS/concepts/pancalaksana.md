## Names

## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): A Purāṇa treats creation (sarga), secondary creation (pratisarga), dynasties (vaṃśa), the Manu-intervals (manvantara) and the deeds of the dynasties (vaṃśānucarita) (VP 3.6.24; KūP 1.1.12; Matsya 53.65); the Bhāgavata calls the five-mark Purāṇas the 'small' ones (12.7.10).
