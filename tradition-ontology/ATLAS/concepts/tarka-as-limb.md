## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): In the Maitrī's six limbs reasoning stands between concentration and absorption.
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The Śaiva ṣaḍaṅga counts tarka — the discernment of what is to be taken up and left — as the highest limb ('tarko yogāṅgam uttamam', Mālinīvijayottara as quoted by Abhinavagupta — recalled); the Mṛgendra calls the corresponding scrutiny ūha.
