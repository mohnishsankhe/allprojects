## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Five vows (abstention from violence, falsehood, stealing, unchastity, possessiveness), small or great; five contemplations for each; reflection on harm and suffering; friendliness, delight, compassion and equanimity; reflection on the nature of world and body for dread of saṃsāra and detachment.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Five contemplations for each vow steady it (TS 7.3–8), with the reflections on the harm and suffering of the sins (7.9–10) and on world and body for dread and detachment (7.12).
