## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Hearing, singing, remembering, serving the feet, worship, obeisance, servitude, friendship and self-offering (BhP 7.5.23-24); the Śiva Purāṇa's three means (hearing, singing, reflection) are a Śaiva counterpart.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): Tulsī's own nine taught to Śabarī (saints' company first, guileless trust last); any one suffices.
- [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md): The Bhāgavata's nine expounded one by one, culminating in self-offering.
- [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md): Of the nine, hearing and singing are foremost.
