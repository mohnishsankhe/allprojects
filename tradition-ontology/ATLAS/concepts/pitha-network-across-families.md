## Definitions
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Oḍḍiyāna, Jālandhara, Pūrṇagiri and Kāmarūpa as the primary seats; Bhairava present with the Goddess at Kāmākhyā where the yoginīs meet.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The twenty-four sites of the Heruka maṇḍala (Pullīramalaya, Jālandhara, Oḍḍiyāna, Arbuda, Devīkoṭa, Kāmarūpa …) and the Hevajra's ten classes of sites (pīṭha, upapīṭha …), mapped onto the body.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Kāmarūpa placed between the first two centres in the Gorakṣaśataka; Jālandhara as a siddha's name and a seat.
