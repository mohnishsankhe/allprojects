# The love of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in the Gīta Govinda

## Names

## Definitions
- `lin:jagannatha`: Kṛṣṇa's spring dance with all the cowherd women; Rādhā's jealous withdrawal; the longing of both in separation, carried by the friend; Rādhā's vain waiting and her rebuke; the friend's counsel to give up pride; Kṛṣṇa's plea to place her foot on his head; her going to the tryst; union, and Kṛṣṇa adorning her. The poem offers itself to those who delight in remembering Hari and in the arts of love.
