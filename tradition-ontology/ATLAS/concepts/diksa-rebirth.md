## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The consecrated sacrificer is made an embryo again, the hut his womb, his garments the membranes; he is born at the final bath (AB 1.3); by consecration he makes his future world (ŚB 6.2.2.27).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Consecration for sacrifice is the twice-born's third birth, after the mother and initiation (MDh 2.169).
