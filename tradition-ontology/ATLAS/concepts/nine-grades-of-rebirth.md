## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Upper, middle and lower birth, each in three grades (Contemplation Sūtra 14-16), from those with the three minds to the evil-doer saved by ten recitations at death; the Larger Sūtra's three classes of aspirants.
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): For Shandao all nine grades are ordinary beings differing by good or evil conditions met, not sages of the stages; the three minds are required of all.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): The nine grades belong to the 'essential gate' of self-power good leading to the transformed land.
