## Definitions
- [Svātantrika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/svatantrika.md): Bhāviveka's chapter on the Vedānta sets out the doctrine of the one eternal self (puruṣa) and rejects a permanent agentless self as unable to act or be known (MH 8).
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): Śāntarakṣita rejects the Upaniṣadic single eternal consciousness because cognitions are many and momentary (TS, examination of the self).
