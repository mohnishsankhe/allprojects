## Definitions
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Three principal channels (lalanā, rasanā, avadhūtī) among thirty-two; four (or five, six in the Kālacakra) wheels.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Six (or nine) centres, sixteen supports, three targets and five voids; the bulb below the navel from which 72,000 channels arise.
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Eleven centres or kinds of inner knowledge in the Kaulajñānanirṇaya; the worlds located in the body as principles.
- [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md): The Netra's six wheels, sixteen supports, three targets and five voids.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Word-based meditation places the vowels and consonants on lotuses at the navel, heart and mouth; the Jain texts do not teach the Śaiva centres as such.
