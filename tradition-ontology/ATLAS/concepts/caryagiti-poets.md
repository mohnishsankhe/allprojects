## Definitions
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): About fifty songs by some two dozen siddhas, each song signed by its poet: Kāṇha (the largest number, about thirteen), Bhusuku (about eight), Saraha (four), Kukkuripa (three), Lūipa, Śānti and Śabara (two each), and Virūpa, Guṇḍarī, Cāṭila, Kambalāmbara, Ḍombī, Mahīdhara, Vīṇā, Āryadeva, Ḍheṇḍhaṇa, Dārika, Bhāde, Tāḍaka, Kaṅkaṇa, Jayanandī, Dhāma and Tantrī (one each).

## Relations (interpretation layer)

_Notes: Song counts are recalled from the standard edition; the presence of Lūipa, Kukkuripa, Virūpa, Bhusuku, Kāṇha, Ḍombi, Śānti, Saraha, Śabari, Bhadra, Tāḍaka, Dārika and Kaṅkaṇa was confirmed by name searches in the local Tibetan of Munidatta's commentary (Tōh 2293). Identification of Caryāgīti poets with the siddhas of the lives (e.g. Śānti = Ratnākaraśānti, Kambalāmbara = Kambala, Dhāma = Dhamupa) is traditional and uncertain._
