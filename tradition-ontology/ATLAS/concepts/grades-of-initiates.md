## Definitions
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Samayin (after samaya-dīkṣā), putraka (after nirvāṇa-dīkṣā, seeking liberation), sādhaka (consecrated to master a mantra for powers) and ācārya/deśika (consecrated teacher who may initiate and install).
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Samayin, putraka, sādhaka (seeker of powers, bubhukṣu) and ācārya; the mumukṣu is liberated at death.
