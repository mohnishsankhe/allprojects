## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Immortality is prayed for as full life and freedom from death (RV 7.59.12), won by soma ('we have become immortal', 8.48.3), by gifts (10.107.2), and as a place in the immortal world of light and bliss (9.113.7–11); 'knowing the Person one goes beyond death; there is no other path' (VS 31.18); 'knowing the ageless self one does not fear death' (AVŚ 10.8.44).
