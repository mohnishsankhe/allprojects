## Names

## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The Buddhas teach relying on two truths, worldly convention and the ultimate.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Gauḍapāda distinguishes what exists by imagined or dependent convention from the ultimate, and the worldly, pure-worldly and supramundane; later Advaita counts three levels (ultimate, empirical, illusory); Śrīharṣa says the Brahma-vādin differs from the Mādhyamika in exempting consciousness.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The Jains speak of the ultimate and conventional standpoints (niścaya- and vyavahāra-naya) within a many-sided view.
