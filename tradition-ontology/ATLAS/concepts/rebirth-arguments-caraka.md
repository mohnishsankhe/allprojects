## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Perception is limited (eight causes of non-perception); parental transmission, nature, creation by another and chance fail; rebirth is established by scripture (sages of divine sight), perception (inequalities of birth, untaught infant behaviour, memory of former births), inference (from past karma called daiva) and yukti (no sprout without seed) (Ca Sū 11.6-33); the mind that joins jīva to body carries memory, and with a pure mind one remembers past births (Śā 3.13).
