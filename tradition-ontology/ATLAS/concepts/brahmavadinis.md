## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Women are recorded as seers of Ṛgveda hymns and verses — Lopāmudrā, Ghoṣā, Apālā, Viśvavārā, Vāc Āmbhṛṇī, Śraddhā, Sūryā, Yamī, Indrāṇī, Śacī, Romaśā, Śaśvatī, Sārparājñī, Godhā, Agastya's sister and others — and listed together by the Bṛhaddevatā; the Atharvaveda says a maiden wins a husband by brahmacarya (AVŚ 11.5.18).
