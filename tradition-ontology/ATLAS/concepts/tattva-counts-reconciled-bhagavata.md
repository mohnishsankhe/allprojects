# The many counts of the principles, reconciled

## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The seers count the principles as nine, eleven, five, three, twenty-six, twenty-five, seven, six, four, seventeen, sixteen or thirteen; all are right, because the principles enter into one another and each speaker counts as he intends; the dispute itself arises from the Lord's powers and ceases with calm and self-control.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): Vallabha follows the Bhāgavata's count of twenty-eight principles (nine, eleven, five and three), treating them as Brahman's own real manifestation rather than as prakṛti's alone.
