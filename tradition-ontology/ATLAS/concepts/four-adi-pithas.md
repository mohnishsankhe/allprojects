- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): Uḍḍīyāna (thighs), Kāmarūpa/Kāmagiri (yoni), Jālandhara (breasts), Pūrṇagiri (shoulders and neck), with Devīkūṭa (feet) (Kālikā Purāṇa 18.41-43).
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): Kāmākhya, Pūrṇagiri, Jālandhara and Oḍra as the levels beyond form, form, word and body (Kubjikāmata 11.6-7).
