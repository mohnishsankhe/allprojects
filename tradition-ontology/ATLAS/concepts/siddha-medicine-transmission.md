## Definitions
- [Siddha medicine (cittā maruttuvam)](../lineages/siddha-medicine.md): Śiva taught Pārvatī; the knowledge passed through Nandi to the Siddhars, above all Agastya, who taught it to his disciples (Tēraiyar, Pulastiyar and others) and set it in Tamil verse; the texts were veiled in code language and passed from guru to disciple, later in palm-leaf manuscripts held by families of physicians.
