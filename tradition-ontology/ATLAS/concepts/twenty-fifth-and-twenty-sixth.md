## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The twenty-fifth is the knower, the self that is 'awakening' (budhyamāna), which on knowing itself becomes alone; the twenty-sixth is the ever-awakened (buddha), stainless and eternal, which the twenty-fifth does not know until it awakens to it and attains its state (MBh 12.294.43, 12.296.7–12; Yājñavalkya 12.306).
