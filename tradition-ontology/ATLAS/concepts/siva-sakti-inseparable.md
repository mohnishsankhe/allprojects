## Definitions
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): Without Śiva no Śakti, without Śakti no Śiva; they go together like fire and smoke, tree and shade (KJN 17.8–9); in dissolution Śakti enters Śiva (2.6).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The one Śakti, the Lord's own power, upholds the body and the cosmos; Śiva and Śakti are not two (SSP 4); if Śiva, mind, Śakti and breath are in different places there is no success (Candrāvalokana).
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): Nothing is without Bhairava and nothing without the Goddess; by cause and effect each is the other's guru (Kubjikāmata 2.17-18).
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Without Śakti Śiva is a corpse (Toḍala 1.25).
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Kula is Śakti and Akula Śiva; the Kaula joins them (Kulārṇava 17.27); their union is the bliss that is liberation (5.79).
