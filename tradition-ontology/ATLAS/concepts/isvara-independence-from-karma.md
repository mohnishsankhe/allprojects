## Definitions
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The Lord produces the effect as he wills, all at once or in sequence, depending neither on karma nor on the soul; karma bears fruit only when favoured by his will.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Rejected: a Lord who distributes unequal lots independently would be partial; the Lord's creation depends on beings' karma (BS 2.1.34), and a merely efficient Lord is inconsistent (BS 2.2.37).
