## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The Large Perfection of Wisdom's list of emptinesses in which the bodhisattva trains: of the internal, external, both, emptiness itself, the great, the ultimate, the conditioned, the unconditioned, the absolute, the beginningless, non-rejection, essential nature, all dharmas, own-marks, the non-apprehended, the own-being of non-being — commonly 'up to' this sixteenth — then of being, non-being, own-being and other-being (twenty); the Chinese tradition often counts eighteen.
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Candrakīrti expounds the emptinesses of the Perfection of Wisdom — sixteen in detail and four in brief — as the emptiness of persons and phenomena (MA 6.179–223).
