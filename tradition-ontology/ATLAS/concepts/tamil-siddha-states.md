## Definitions
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Among the ninety-six principles: waking (cākkiram), dream (coppaṉam), deep sleep (cuḻutti), the fourth (turiyam) and beyond the fourth (turiyātītam). The songs add 'sleeping without sleeping' and the blissful awareness that needs no further knowledge (Kuṭampai 15).
