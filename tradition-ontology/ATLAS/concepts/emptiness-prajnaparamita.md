# Emptiness in the Perfection of Wisdom sūtras

`cpt:emptiness-prajnaparamita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ultimate

## Names
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): śūnyatā

## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): All dharmas — aggregates, elements, links, truths, paths, fruits, buddhahood and nirvāṇa — are empty of own-being, unborn, like illusions and dreams; the bodhisattva does not apprehend even 'bodhisattva' or 'perfection of wisdom'; emptiness is not destruction, and is not itself to be grasped.
