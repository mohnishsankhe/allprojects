## Definitions
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): The prātimokṣa vow is the basis of the bodhisattva vow; celibates may not take the secret and wisdom empowerments.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): Prātimokṣa, bodhisattva and mantra vows are each to be kept by their own rules and not mixed (Sakya Paṇḍita).
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): A monk holds all three; the tantric vows and pledges are guarded as one's life.
- [Drikung Kagyu](../lineages/drikung-kagyu.md): One in essence, differing in aspect (Gongchig).
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): To be kept distinct, each with its own ritual and rules (Sakya Paṇḍita).
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The Vajrayāna practitioner holds the monastic or lay prātimokṣa, the bodhisattva vow and the tantric (vidyādhara) vow together; Atiśa forbids celibates the secret and wisdom consecrations to protect the first.
