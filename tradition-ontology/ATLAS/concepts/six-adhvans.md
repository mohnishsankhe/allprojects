## Definitions
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The cosmos as six 'paths' — letters (varṇa), words (pada), mantras, kalās, principles (tattva) and worlds (bhuvana) — traversed and purified in nirvāṇa-dīkṣā.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The paths of the denoting - letters, mantras, words - and of the denoted - kalās (nivṛtti, pratiṣṭhā, vidyā, śānti, śāntyatītā), tattvas, worlds; purified in initiation and contemplated from gross to supreme (TĀ 6-12; VBT 56-57).
