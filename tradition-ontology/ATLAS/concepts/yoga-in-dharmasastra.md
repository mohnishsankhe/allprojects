## Definitions
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Breath-control with Oṃ and the utterances, fixation, withdrawal and meditation burn away faults, sin, attachment and ungodly qualities (MDh 6.70–74); the removal of the fifteen faults is rooted in yoga (ĀpDh 1.8.23); seeing the self by yoga is the highest dharma (YS 1.8); the Yājñavalkyasmṛti describes yogic powers (3.202–203) and liberation through music (3.115).
