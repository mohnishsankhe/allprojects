## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The teaching is secret (SK 69), given out of compassion (SK 70), to a qualified student: Kapila tested Āsuri three times over thousand-year intervals before he renounced (Māṭhara). Acquired knowledge arises with the teacher as its occasioning cause (Gauḍapāda on SK 43); among the accomplishments are hearing, study, approaching a teacher and serving the venerable (Gauḍapāda on SK 51). The Sāṃkhya Sūtra: instruction must be repeated (4.3); take the essence from many teachers like a bee (4.13); hearing without reflection does not suffice, as with Virocana, while Indra attained after obeisance, continence and long approach (4.17–19); a defiled mind lets the seed of instruction fail (4.29–30); there must be liberated teachers, else the blind lead the blind (3.80–82); there is no fixed rule since students differ (3.77).
