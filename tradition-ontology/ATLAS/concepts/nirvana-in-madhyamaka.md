## Names

## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Not abandoned, not attained, neither annihilated nor eternal, neither ceased nor arisen (MMK 25.3); neither existent nor non-existent (25.4–10); what, when grasping and depending, is saṃsāra, is nirvāṇa when not grasping and not depending (25.9); the ending of the conceptions of existence and non-existence (RĀ 1.42).
