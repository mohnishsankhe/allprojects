- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: The goal named in 5.24–26: the yogin whose happiness, delight and light are within, having become Brahman, attains brahma-nirvāṇa (5.24); seers with impurities destroyed, dualities cut, self-restrained and devoted to the welfare of all beings attain it (5.25); for self-knowing ascetics free of desire and anger it is near on every side (5.26).
