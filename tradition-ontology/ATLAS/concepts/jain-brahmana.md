## Definitions
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Bharata made the vow-keeping laity who would not tread on sprouts into brāhmaṇas, gave them the sacred thread as a sign of their vows and the life-cycle rites (kriyā) — a Jain counterpart of the Brahmanical saṃskāras.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The true brāhmaṇa is made by conduct, not birth (Uttarādhyayana 25).
