## Definitions
- [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md): Samayā (the Goddess) and Samaya (Śiva) are equal in seat (adhiṣṭhāna), in state (avasthāna), in action (anuṣṭhāna), in form (rūpa) and in name (nāma), and so of equal primacy (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 41).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Bhāskararāya repeats the five equalities (seat of worship, acts such as creation, dance, names such as Bhairava, form such as redness), crediting the Subhagodaya commentator (on LSN 37).
