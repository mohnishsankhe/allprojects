## Definitions
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): The guru comes of his own accord to the one who longs, shows his own form and burns the six supports, letters and principles (Aḻukaṇṇi 33–36); the seeker must find the master and bow at his feet (Pattirakiriyār 26); guru and Lord are one, and seeing the Lord as guru is the unspoken mantra (Tirumūla ñāṉam 6); the gaze on the guru makes one a teacher (5); the silent guru teaches by stillness (Tāyumāṉavar). Alchemy and medicine are to be learned only from a guru; hence the code language.
