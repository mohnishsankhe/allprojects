## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The six internal bases (eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mind) and six external (forms, sounds, odours, tastes, tangibles, mental objects) — 'the all' (SN 35.23); with the six consciousnesses the eighteen elements (MN 115).
