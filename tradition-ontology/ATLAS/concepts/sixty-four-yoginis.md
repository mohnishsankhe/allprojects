## Definitions
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): A circle of sixty-four yoginī goddesses worshipped in open-roofed circular temples of c. 9th–11th c. — Hirapur and Ranipur-Jharial (Odisha), Bheraghat near Jabalpur, Mitaoli (Morena), Khajuraho (rectangular) and others; the yoginīs are the Kula's transmitters (Kaulajñānanirṇaya 9.11-15). Their name-lists differ widely between texts.
