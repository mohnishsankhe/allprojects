## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The world is false (mithyā): not unreal like a hare's horn, not real like Brahman, but indescribable and sublatable; defended by the inference 'because it is seen, insentient, limited' and by five definitions.
