## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The guru is Brahman, not other than the Self; 'gu' darkness, 'ru' its remover; the roots of meditation, worship, mantra and liberation are the guru's form, feet, word and grace; the teaching is secret, for the devoted only.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The avadhūta's many teachers; the self as its own teacher.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The guru is the means to God and is honoured above God for that reason (guru and Govind).
- [Kartābhajā](../lineages/kartabhaja.md): The guru is truth; the Kartā is present in the Mahāśay.
