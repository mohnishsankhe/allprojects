## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13.8–12 (vulgate 13.7–11): twenty dispositions are declared 'knowledge' (jñāna), and what is other than them 'ignorance'. The verse lists them without further grouping.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Absence of pride and pretence, non-harm, patience, uprightness, service of the teacher, purity, steadiness, self-control, dispassion, absence of ego, seeing the evil of birth, death, age, disease and pain, non-attachment, evenness, unswerving devotion, love of solitude, constancy in self-knowledge and insight into the goal of truth — these are called knowledge (BhG 13.7–11).

## Relations (interpretation layer)

_Notes: The count of twenty depends on counting asakti and anabhiṣvaṅga separately and the two items of 13.12 separately; the text itself gives no number. This edition numbers the verses 13.8–12._
