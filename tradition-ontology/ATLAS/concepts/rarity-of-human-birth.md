# The rarity of human birth

`cpt:rarity-of-human-birth` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** cosmology-time

## Names
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): dal 'byor

## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): A blind turtle surfacing once a century would put its neck through a yoke drifting on the ocean sooner than a fool gone to the lower realms would regain human birth (MN 129; SN 56.48).
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): A human life free of the eight unfree states and having the ten endowments, of great value and hard to find by cause, example and number.
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Human birth is the stairway to liberation, won only by merit; one who wastes it is a self-slayer (Kulārṇava 1.14-17).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): The field of human life left fallow could have yielded gold (Rāmprasād).
