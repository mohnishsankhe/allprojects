## Definitions
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): Guru, yidam and ḍākinī (or protector): the inner refuge and the roots of blessing, accomplishment and activity.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): The guru is the root of blessing, the yidam the root of accomplishment, the ḍākinī and protector the root of activity; for the Kagyu, the guru is placed above the deity (Marpa's lesson).
