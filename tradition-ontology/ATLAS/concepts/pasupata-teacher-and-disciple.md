## Definitions
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The disciple is a tested brahmin of good family, keen senses and desire to know, who seeks the end of suffering, not dharma, wealth, pleasure or isolation; the teacher acts from grace, knows the nine groups and consecrates; one with scripture but without faith or knowledge of the groups is a pseudo-teacher.
