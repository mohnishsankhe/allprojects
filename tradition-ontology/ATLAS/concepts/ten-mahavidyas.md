## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Ten goddesses who are forms of the one Goddess: Kālī, Tārā, Ṣoḍaśī (Tripurasundarī), Bhuvaneśvarī, Bhairavī, Chinnamastā, Dhūmāvatī, Bagalāmukhī, Mātaṅgī and Kamalā (Muṇḍamālā as quoted in the Tantrasāra); each has her Bhairava consort (Toḍala 1.3-19) and they correspond to Viṣṇu's ten avatāras (Toḍala 10.9-11). The lists differ: the Bṛhaddharma names Sundarī and Ṣoḍaśī separately and omits Kamalā.
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): Satī's ten forms, stationed in the ten directions around Śiva before she goes to Dakṣa's sacrifice; they grant liberation to worshippers and accomplish the magical acts (Bṛhaddharma madhya 6.125-134; Mahābhāgavata 8).
