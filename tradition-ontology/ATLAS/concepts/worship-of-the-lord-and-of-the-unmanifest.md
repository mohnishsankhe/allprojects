## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Arjuna asks which are the better knowers of yoga: devotees ever yoked who worship Kṛṣṇa, or those who worship the imperishable, the unmanifest (12.1). The Lord answers that those who fix manas on him and worship him with supreme faith are held the most yoked (12.2); those who worship the imperishable — indefinable, unmanifest, all-pervading, unthinkable, unchanging, immovable, constant — with senses restrained, even-minded, delighting in the welfare of all beings, also reach him (12.3–4), but their hardship is greater, since the unmanifest goal is reached with difficulty by the embodied (12.5); those who resign all actions to him and meditate on him with undivided yoga he before long lifts from the ocean of death and saṃsāra (12.6–7).
