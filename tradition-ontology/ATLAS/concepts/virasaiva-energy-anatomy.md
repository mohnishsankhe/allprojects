## Definitions
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The liṅga of light shines at the base (mūlādhāra), in the heart and between the brows — gold, coral-red and crystal; the prāṇaliṅga rises from the navel-bulb where prāṇa and apāna meet; six centres from the base to the brows, and above them the thousand-petalled lotus with the moon-disc and the subtle aperture called Kailāsa, from which nectar cools the heart.
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): The manual tradition places the six liṅgas in the six centres (low confidence).
