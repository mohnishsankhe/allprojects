## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The end of the world cannot be reached by travelling, yet there is no end of suffering without reaching it; the world, its origin, cessation and way are declared in this fathom-long body with its perception and mind (SN 2.26).
