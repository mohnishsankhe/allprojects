# Manner of death and the next world in the chart

## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The eighth house and the 22nd decan show the manner of death (BJ 25.1–11); the lord of the decan occupied by the stronger luminary shows the world one came from — gods (Jupiter), fathers (Moon, Venus), animals (Sun, Mars) or hell-beings (Saturn, Mercury) (BJ 25.14) — and the lords of the 6th/8th decans or planets in the 7th the world one goes to (BJ 25.15).
