## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Jyotiṣa passes from teacher to pupil, often father to son (Varāhamihira from Ādityadāsa; Gaṇeśa from Keśava; Rāma and Nīlakaṇṭha from Ananta), and is given only to the calm, devoted, truthful believer — never to another's pupil, an unbeliever or a cheat (BPHS 1.7–8). The secret chapter is withheld from none who is very devoted (SS 12.11). Authors bow to the former promulgators and do not discard their works (BJ 28.10; BS 105.2); counterfeiting the text destroys merit and life (Āryabhaṭīya 4.50); even foreign Yavanas who master it are honoured like seers (BS 2.14).
