# Devotion as its own fruit (phala-rūpatva)

## Definitions
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Devotion is higher than action, knowledge and yoga because it is the fruit; knowledge of a king or of food neither pleases the king nor appeases hunger; gaining devotion one becomes perfected, immortal, satisfied.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The Āḻvārs ask for nothing but service and devotion: not the drum but kinship and service through seven times seven births (TP 29); not dharma, wealth or pleasure but unwavering devotion in every birth (Mukundamālā); the name's sweetness above Indra's kingdom (Tirumālai 2).
