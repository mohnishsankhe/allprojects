## Definitions
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The one consciousness-brahman is imagined as enjoyer, enjoyed and impeller through the three guṇas; tamas and rajas are removed at the śaraṇa stage; earth and the other elements are Śiva's eight forms, his body and effect; the thirty-six tattvas are waves of Śiva's ocean.
