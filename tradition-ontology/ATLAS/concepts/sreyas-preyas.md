## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The good (śreyas) and the pleasant (preyas) both approach a person; the wise examines and chooses the good, the fool the pleasant for getting and keeping (KU 1.2.1-2); Naciketas is praised for refusing the pleasant (KU 1.1.21-29).
