## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The means of knowledge, the five-membered demonstration, pseudo-reasons and points of defeat; Uddyotakara writes to remove the errors of 'bad reasoners' (the Buddhists).
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Two means of knowledge, inference with the three characteristics of the reason; answers to the Jain single-mark theory.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The single mark of a valid reason is 'otherwise-impossibility' (anyathānupapatti); Jain logic adopts and revises the Nyāya and Buddhist frameworks.
