# The four formless attainments

## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The spheres of infinite space, infinite consciousness, nothingness and neither-perception-nor-non-perception, reached by passing beyond perceptions of form (MN 111; MN 121); the bodhisatta learned the third and fourth from Āḷāra and Uddaka and rejected them as not leading to nibbāna (MN 26); each is a basis for insight (AN 9.36).
