## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The one-syllabled Brahman to be uttered at death (BhG 8.13), the praṇava in all the Vedas (7.8), the first word of 'oṃ tat sat' with which rites begin (17.23–24), 'among words the one syllable' (10.25).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: the Lord is the praṇava in all the Vedas (7.8) and the oṃkāra along with the three Vedas (9.17); Oṃ is 'the one-syllabled Brahman', uttered at departure while remembering the Lord (8.13).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: 'Of utterances I am the single syllable' (10.25), the syllable named in 8.13 as 'om, the one-syllabled brahman'.
