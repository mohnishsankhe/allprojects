## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The goal: when discrimination arises, prakṛti — her purpose served — ceases for that puruṣa like a dancer who has shown herself (SK 59, 65–66); the knower remains embodied for a time (SK 67), and at the body's end attains isolation that is certain and final (SK 68). It is not the manifestation of bliss, nor going anywhere, nor annihilation (SS 5.74–83), but the destruction of obstacles (SS 6.20) and the complete cessation of the threefold suffering (SS 1.1, 3.85). Strictly, bondage and release belong to prakṛti (SK 62).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The absence of the conjunction of seer and seen (2.25); arising when sattva and puruṣa are equal in purity (3.55) and from dispassion even toward the highest power (3.50); the return of the guṇas, their purpose fulfilled, to their source, or the power of consciousness established in its own form (4.34). Vyāsa: then the puruṣa is the light of its own form alone, stainless, alone (YBh 2.27, 3.55).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Enjoyment of one's own self apart from the Lord; a lesser goal.
- [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md): Not permanent.
- [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md): Permanent, with no return.
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): In HYP 4.62 kaivalya is what remains when the mind dissolves by abandoning the knowable.
