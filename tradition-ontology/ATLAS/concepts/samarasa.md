## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Samādhi is the oneness of equal taste when breath and mind are gone (GŚ 94); the samarasa of body and supreme state by the guru's grace (SSP 5); the yogī becomes non-dual like milk in milk (GŚ 100).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): For one abiding in samarasa all is full, the Akulavīra alone; he is every god and every school (Akulavīra A.24–41).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The sameness (samarasatva) when breath is exhausted and mind dissolved — a definition of samādhi (HYP 4.6; GŚ 94).
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tāyumāṉavar's 'harmony' of Vedānta and Śaiva Siddhānta: the Siddhas who have attained it are praised; the Supreme is both the non-dual reality and the Lord of grace. Kaṭuveḷi likewise joins 'pure Vedānta' to Śiva devotion.
- `lin:vallalar`: Vallalar (Ramalinga Swamigal) takes up camaracam in the name of his 'Camaraca Cutta Caṉmārkkam'.
