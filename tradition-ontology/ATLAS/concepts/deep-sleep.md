# Deep sleep

`cpt:deep-sleep` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** consciousness-states

## Names
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): nidrā

## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): For Yoga, sleep is itself an activity of the mind, whose support is the cognition of absence (YS 1.10) — a cognition because it is recollected on waking ('I slept happily', 'I slept heavily') — and it must be stilled in samādhi like other cognitions (YBh 1.10); the knowledge of dream or sleep may serve as a support for steadying the mind (1.38).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Lineage contribution (Upaniṣadic): in deep sleep one has merged with being, 'gone to one's own' (ChU 6.8.1); one desires no desire and sees no dream, embraced by the intelligent self, beyond sorrow (BAU 4.3.19-22); one lies in the hitā channels (BAU 2.1.19); the mind overcome by light (PrU 4.6); Indra objects that there one does not know oneself (ChU 8.11).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): In deep sleep the self rests in the Lord within the heart, its knowledge contracted; it persists as 'I', as the memory 'I slept happily' shows.
