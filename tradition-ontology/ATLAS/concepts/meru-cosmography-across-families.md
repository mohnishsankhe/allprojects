## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Seven ring-continents and oceans around Jambudvīpa, with Meru at its centre and Bhārata as the land of action.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Jambūdvīpa, round, a hundred thousand yojanas across, with Meru as its navel, surrounded by continents and oceans; Bharata and Airāvata as regions of the six-period cycles.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The receptacle world on circles of wind, water and gold; Meru amid seven golden ranges, with four continents, Jambudvīpa to the south.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Vyāsa's cosmology of seven worlds from Avīci to the back of Meru and above.
