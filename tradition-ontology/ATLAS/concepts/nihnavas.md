## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Seven (Sthānāṅga) or eight (with the Boṭikas, in the Āvaśyaka commentaries): Jamāli (action in progress is not yet done), Tiṣyagupta (the soul is only its last space-point), Āṣāḍha (agnosticism about who is a true monk), Aśvamitra (momentariness), Gaṅga (two cognitions at once), Rohagupta (three categories), Goṣṭhāmāhila (karma touches but does not bind the soul), and Śivabhūti (nakedness required).
