## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The subtle body, produced first, unattached, constant, made of the principles from mahat down to the subtle elements (buddhi, ahaṃkāra, manas, the ten capacities and the five subtle elements), transmigrates, perfumed by the dispositions, without enjoying (SK 40); it needs a support like a picture or shadow (SK 41) and plays its parts like an actor (SK 42). The subtle bodies are constant; the bodies born of mother and father perish (SK 39). The Sāṃkhya Sūtra calls it 'seventeen, one' (3.9) and admits a conveying (ātivāhika) body (5.103). Vindhyavāsin denied the subtle body (Yuktidīpikā on SK 39).
