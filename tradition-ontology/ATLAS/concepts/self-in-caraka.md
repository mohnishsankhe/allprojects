## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The supreme self is changeless, eternal, the seer, cause of consciousness together with mind, elements and senses (Sū 1.56); beginningless, the unmanifest knower of the field, all-pervading yet feeling only through its own senses, witness of all, known by the marks of life, without which the body is an empty house (Śā 1.59-85); the empirical person (rāśi-puruṣa) is born of delusion, desire, aversion and karma (Śā 1.53); the inner self of the embryo is eternal and 'produces itself' (Śā 3.8). Caraka argues against those who teach an ownerless aggregate of momentary things (Śā 1.46-51).
