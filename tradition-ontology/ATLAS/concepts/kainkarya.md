## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The soul's fulfilment is service to the Lord with Śrī, done for his pleasure alone, here and eternally in Vaikuṇṭha; its highest form is service to his devotees.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): 'At all times without break, staying close, we must do faultless service' (TVM 3.3.1); the drum the girls asked for is to be his kin and serve him alone for seven times seven births (TP 29).
