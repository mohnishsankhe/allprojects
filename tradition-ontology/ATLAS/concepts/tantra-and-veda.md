## Definitions
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The Śaiva scriptures are a higher revelation than the Veda, which they do not contradict but surpass; the Veda is the lower, common scripture.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The Pāñcarātra is praised above all śāstras as the brahmin among the classes; its defenders (Yāmuna) argue that it is Vedic in root and authoritative.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The mantra way shares the goal of the perfection vehicle but is superior by its many means, lack of difficulty and suitability for the sharp.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Jain mantra lore is held to derive from the lost Vidyānupravāda Pūrva, part of the Jina's own canon, not from outside.
