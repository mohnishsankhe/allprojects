## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The fifteen lunar days repeat the classes nandā, bhadrā, jayā, riktā and pūrṇā; each tithi has a presiding deity from Agni (first) to the Moon (fifteenth); their quality changes with the fortnight (MC 1.3–4).
