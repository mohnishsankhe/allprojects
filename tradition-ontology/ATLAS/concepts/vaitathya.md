## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The wise say the entities are unreal, shown from dream (inner place, short time, chariots absent), and waking is one with dream by the sameness of distinctions; both what is imagined within and grasped outside are unreal in each state; the self imagines all by its own māyā. | In 4.31–4.41 and 4.61–4.67 the argument of prakaraṇa 2 is resumed: dream and waking objects are unreal, seen by mind, and objects of each other.
