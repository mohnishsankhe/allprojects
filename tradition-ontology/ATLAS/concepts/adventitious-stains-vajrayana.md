# Beings are buddhas obscured by adventitious stains

## Definitions
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Beings are buddhas but covered by adventitious stains; removing them, they are buddhas (Hevajra II.4).
- [Kālacakra tradition in India](../lineages/kalacakra.md): The stain is neither adventitious nor long-lasting, neither born without mind nor abiding in it; it is destroyed by union with emptiness while mind's gnosis-nature remains (Sekoddeśa 129–133).
