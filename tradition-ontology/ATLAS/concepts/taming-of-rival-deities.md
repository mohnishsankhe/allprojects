## Definitions
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Vajrapāṇi as Trailokyavijaya subdues Maheśvara and the worldly gods and gives them places in the maṇḍala; Heruka's taking of the twenty-four sites is told as the conversion of Bhairava's retinue.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Viṣṇu sends forth the Great Deluder, who teaches the daityas the doctrines of the naked and the red-robed ascetics so that they abandon the Veda.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The Maitrī tells that Bṛhaspati, in the form of Śukra, created a false knowledge for the asuras' destruction, and warns against the no-self doctrine's tricksters.
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The Buddha appears as a Kula-Bhairava perfected by Tārā's mantra, the teacher of Vasiṣṭha.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Kṛṣṇa and Rāma are retold as figures within the Jain universal history, subordinate to the Jinas.
