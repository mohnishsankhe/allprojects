## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The 120-year system counted from the birth-asterism: the lords, repeating thrice from Kṛttikā, are the Sun (6 years), Moon (10), Mars (7), Rāhu (18), Jupiter (16), Saturn (19), Mercury (17), Ketu (7) and Venus (20); since a full life is 120 years it is chief in the Kali age (BPHS 46.12–15).
