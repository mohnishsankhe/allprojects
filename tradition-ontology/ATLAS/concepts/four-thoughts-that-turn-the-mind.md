## Definitions
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): Contemplation of the precious human birth, of impermanence and death, of the defects of saṃsāra and of karma, which turn the mind from worldly life to the Dharma; the first part of the preliminaries.
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): The preliminaries of mind training (Seven Points 1.1): the precious human life, death and impermanence, karma, and the faults of saṃsāra.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The topics of the small and middling scopes that turn the mind from this life and from future lives.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): The topics of the impure vision.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): The precious human life, death and impermanence, the cause and effect of karma, and the faults of saṃsāra; contemplated first (Jewel Ornament ch.2–6; the common preliminaries).
