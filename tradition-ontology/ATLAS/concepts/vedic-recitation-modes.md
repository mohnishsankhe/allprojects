## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The text is learned in the continuous form (saṃhitā), word by word (pada), in overlapping pairs (krama) and in the eight modified recitations (jaṭā, mālā, śikhā, rekhā, dhvaja, daṇḍa, ratha, ghana), with hand gestures marking the accents; understanding the meaning is also enjoined (Nirukta 1.18; Pāṇinīya Śikṣā 32–33, 54–55).
