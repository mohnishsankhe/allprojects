## Definitions
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): Allama names the ultimate as the void — bayalu, open space without enclosure — in which liṅga, aṅga and every attainment cease to be two; his tests of the other śaraṇas in the Śūnyasampādane bring each attainment to this void.
