# The Tathāgata after death

## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Whether the Tathāgata exists, does not exist, both or neither after death is undeclared: liberated from reckoning by form and the other aggregates he is deep like the ocean, and 'reborn' or 'not reborn' does not apply (MN 72); since he is not apprehended in truth even here, annihilation cannot be asserted (SN 22.85–86).
