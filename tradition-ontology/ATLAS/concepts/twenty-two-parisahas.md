## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Hunger, thirst, cold, heat, insect bites, nakedness, discontent, women, wandering, sitting, lying, abuse, injury, begging, not obtaining, illness, grass-pricks, dirt, honour, intelligence, ignorance and lack of vision, to be endured for the path and for shedding (TS 9.8–9); eleven are said to occur in the Jina (9.11), a sūtra read differently by the two sects.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Eleven of them (those due to feeling-karma, including hunger and thirst) actually occur in the omniscient; 'nakedness' may be borne as scanty clothing.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): In the omniscient the eleven are only figurative; 'nakedness' means complete nudity.
