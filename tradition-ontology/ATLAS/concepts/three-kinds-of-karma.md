## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Accumulated (burnt by knowledge), to come (does not cling) and begun (exhausted by experience, like a potter's wheel's momentum).
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Cañcitam (stored), pirārattam (begun, which forms this body) and ākāmiyam (being made): dīkṣā and grace burn the first, the second is exhausted by experience, the third does not bind the freed.
