## Definitions
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): An element is either for the sake of the rite or for the sake of the person; what is for the person is what gives him delight (MS 4.1.1–2). Self-knowledge is argued by Bhāṭṭas to be both (Śāstradīpikā).
