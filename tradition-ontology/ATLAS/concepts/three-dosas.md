# The three doṣas (vāta, pitta, kapha)

## Names

## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Vāta, pitta and kapha are the bodily doṣas (Ca Sū 1.57); undisturbed they sustain the body, disturbed destroy it (AHS Sū 1.6-7); they uphold the body as Wind, Sun and Soma the world (Su Sū 21.8), like three pillars (Su Sū 21.3); each five-fold (AHS Sū 12); in their normal state they give strength and long life as the three aims rightly pursued (Ca Sū 12.13).
