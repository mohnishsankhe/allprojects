# The five categories of the Pāśupatas (pañcārtha)

## Names
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): pañcārtha

## Definitions
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The whole teaching is summed up as effect (kārya), cause (kāraṇa), union (yoga), rule (vidhi) and the end of suffering (duḥkhānta), all implied in the first sūtra.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Śaṅkara reports the same five as taught by Paśupati for release from the paśus' bond, and rejects the accompanying view of the Lord as merely efficient cause.
