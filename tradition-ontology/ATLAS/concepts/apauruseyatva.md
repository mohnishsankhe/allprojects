## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Dvaita: the Veda has no author, not even God; it is eternal and intrinsically valid.
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The Veda has no author: names of recensions come from expounders; apparent persons mentioned are sound-similarities; the unbroken chain of study has no beginning; creator-authorship is unprovable.
