## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The embodied self (ātman, dehin) is unborn, eternal, not slain when the body is slain, passing into new bodies as into new clothes, unmanifest and unthinkable (BhG 2.11–30); the knower of the field (13.1–2); an eternal portion of the Lord (15.7); Bhṛgu defends the existence of a living self in the elemental body (MBh 12.175–185).
