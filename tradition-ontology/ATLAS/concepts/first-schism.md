## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): After the Vesālī council the defeated Vajjiputtakas formed the Mahāsaṅgītika (Dīpavaṃsa).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Mahādeva's five points split the order under Aśoka (Vasumitra; hostile Vibhāṣā account).
- [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md): The elders enlarged the Vinaya; the majority kept the old text (Śāriputraparipṛcchā).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Schism proper (saṅghabheda) is a wheel- or act-schism; its lie is the gravest sin.
