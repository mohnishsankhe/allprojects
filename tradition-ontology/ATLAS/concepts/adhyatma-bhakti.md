## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Ānandaghana sings the Jina as the soul's only beloved and husband, a love without beginning or end (Covīsī 1), and the path as hidden to reason and quarrel but open to experience (Covīsī 2); devotion to the Jina is devotion to the soul's own perfected nature.
- [Adhyātma movement (Adhyātma-mata)](../lineages/adhyatma-jain.md): Banārsīdās and later Hindi Adhyātma poets (Dyānatrāy, Bhūdhardās) write devotional padas addressed to the self and the Jina.
