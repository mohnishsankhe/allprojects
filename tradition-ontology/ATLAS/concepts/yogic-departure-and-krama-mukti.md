## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Lineage contribution (Upaniṣadic): of the 101 channels one goes to the crown; going up by it one reaches immortality (KU 2.3.16; ChU 8.6.6); the person made of mind goes out through the indrayoni at the parting of the hair (TU 1.6); the self entered through the vidṛti (AU 1.3.12); through the suṣumṇā, joined with breath, Om and mind, one goes upward (MaiU 6.21); the path of the gods leads to the brahma-worlds from which there is no return (BAU 6.2.15).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The yogin may leave the body at will through the crown (BhP 2.2.15-21) and either be liberated at once or rise by the suṣumnā through Agni, Śiśumāra, Mahar- and Satyaloka and be liberated with Brahmā at the dissolution (2.2.22-31); the Vāyavīya also speaks of departure at will (ŚiP 7.2.37.26).
