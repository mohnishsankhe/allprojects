## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Śaṅkara founded four cardinal monasteries, each with a Veda, a mahāvākya, a first ācārya and Daśanāmī names: Śṛṅgeri (south; Yajur; ahaṃ brahmāsmi; Sureśvara; Sarasvatī, Bhāratī, Purī), Govardhana, Purī (east; Ṛg; prajñānaṃ brahma; Padmapāda; Vana, Araṇya), Śāradā, Dvārakā (west; Sāma; tat tvam asi; Hastāmalaka; Tīrtha, Āśrama), Jyotirmaṭh (north; Atharva; ayam ātmā brahma; Toṭaka; Giri, Parvata, Sāgara). The Kāñcī Kāmakoṭi pīṭha claims foundation by Śaṅkara too, which the others dispute. See lin:dasanami (U57) for the living order.
