## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Death (kāl) stands over every head: do today what you would do tomorrow; the clay will knead the potter; between the mill-stones none survives; yet the death the world fears is joy to the devotee; Kabīr went to Maghar to die to refute the belief that dying in Kāśī liberates; he returned the sheet of the body unstained.
- [Kabīr Panth](../lineages/kabir-panth.md): At death Kāl's messengers let pass the soul that bears the true Name and the pān-parvānā; the caukā rite is performed for the dead.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): The Master himself receives the initiate at death; meditation is daily rehearsal of death.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): The cage of green bamboo will break; if the neighbour touched me the torments of death would go; Bāuls and fakirs are commonly buried (samādhi) rather than cremated, as Lalon was at Cheuṛiyā.
