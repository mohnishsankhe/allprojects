## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Sākhī (witness couplet), pad or śabd (song in a rāga), ramainī (doctrinal caupāī stanzas), arranged in aṅgas; sung in satsaṅg and bhajan.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): The Bāul gān signed by the guru's and poet's names, sung with ektārā and dotārā; song is teaching, practice and code.
