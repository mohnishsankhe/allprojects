## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The corpse is burned by Agni asked not to consume it utterly; eye goes to sun, breath to wind, limbs to plants and waters; the unborn part is carried to the world of the well-doers; the flesh-eating fire is sent away; the widow lying beside the dead is bidden to rise to the world of the living; the living set a boundary against death and go back to dancing and laughter; the bones are laid in mother earth (RV 10.16–18); the Atharvaveda includes those buried, cast out, burned and exposed (AVŚ 18.2.34).
