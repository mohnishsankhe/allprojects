# Tsongkhapa's eight difficult points of Prāsaṅgika

`cpt:eight-difficult-points` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

## Names
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): dka' gnas brgyad

## Definitions
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): No ālaya distinct from the six consciousnesses; no reflexive awareness; no autonomous syllogisms; external objects accepted conventionally; śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas realise the selflessness of phenomena; grasping at the self of phenomena is an affliction; disintegration is a functioning thing; hence a unique account of the three times.
