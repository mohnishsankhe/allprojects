# The body–self relation of world and God (śarīra–śarīrin)

`cpt:sarira-sariri-bhava` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ultimate
**Members:** the Lord as self (śarīrin, ādhāra, niyantṛ, śeṣin), souls and matter as body (śarīra, ādheya, niyāmya, śeṣa)

## Names
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): śarīra-śarīri-bhāva

## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): All souls and matter are the Lord's body — wholly controlled, supported and existing for him — and he is their self; this relation, taught by the mediating texts, explains how Brahman is one and yet the world and souls are real and distinct.
