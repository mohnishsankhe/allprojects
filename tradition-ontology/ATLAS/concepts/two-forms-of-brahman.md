## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Brahman has two forms: formed and formless, mortal and immortal (BAU 2.3.1; MaiU 6.3); time and the timeless (MaiU 6.15); sound and non-sound, the word-brahman and the highest (MaiU 6.22); Om is the higher and lower brahman (PrU 5.2).
