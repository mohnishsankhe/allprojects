## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The Vedas exist for the sacrifices, which depend on times; so the science of time is the eye (cakṣus, nayana) of the Veda and stands at the head of the Vedāṅgas like a peacock's crest; Parāśara calls his horā the 'eye of the Veda heard from Brahmā's mouth', says none can know past and future without it, and warns that one who condemns it goes to hell.
