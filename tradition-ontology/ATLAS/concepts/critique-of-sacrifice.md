## Definitions
- [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md): (As reported) Sacrifice, śrāddha and the Veda are a livelihood of priests; the Veda was made by buffoons, knaves and demons.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Animal sacrifice, fire-worship and caste by birth are refuted; if the slain beast goes to heaven let brāhmaṇas slay brāhmaṇas.
- [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md): (As reported) 'There is no gift, no sacrifice, no offering.'
