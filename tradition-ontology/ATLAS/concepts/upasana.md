## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Venerating something as brahman or as the self: name, speech, mind and the rest (ChU 7), the sun (ChU 3.19), mind and space (ChU 3.18), the six teachers' views (BAU 4.1), 'as the self' (BAU 1.4.7); each has a stated fruit. Kena 1.5 denies that brahman is 'this that people venerate here'.
