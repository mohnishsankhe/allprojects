# The twenty-five principles (pañcaviṃśati-tattva)

**Category:** body-energy
**Members:** 1 puruṣa — neither producer nor product, 2 mūlaprakṛti (pradhāna, avyakta) — producer only, 3 mahat = buddhi — producer and product, 4 ahaṃkāra — producer and product, 5–9 the five tanmātras (sound, touch, form, taste, smell) — producers and products, 10 manas — product only, 11–15 the five buddhīndriyas (ear, skin, eye, tongue, nose) — products only, 16–20 the five karmendriyas (speech, hands, feet, anus, genitals) — products only, 21–25 the five mahābhūtas (space, air, fire, water, earth) — products only

## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Reality enumerated as twenty-five principles: puruṣa; mūlaprakṛti, which is not a product; seven principles that are both products and producers — mahat (buddhi), ahaṃkāra and the five subtle elements; and sixteen that are products only — manas, the five sense capacities, the five action capacities and the five gross elements (SK 3, 22; SS 1.61; Tattvasamāsa 1–3). 'The knower of the twenty-five principles is liberated in any stage of life' (verse quoted by Gauḍapāda and the Jayamaṅgalā).
