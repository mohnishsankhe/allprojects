## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): An old, smoky, dishevelled widow in a chariot bannered with a crow, holding a winnowing basket, hungry and quarrelsome (standard dhyāna, recalled). Teaching: she has no Bhairava because she bears the form of a widow (Toḍala 1.13); worshipped to remove enemies and misfortune.
