## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Clinging to life (abhiniveśa), a fear of death found even in the newborn worm, shows past births (2.9, YBh 2.9); karma ripening as life-span is fast or slow, and saṃyama on it or omens give knowledge of death — omens personal, from other beings and from the gods (3.22, YBh 3.22); mastery of udāna gives departure at will at death (3.39); the mass of karma manifested at death forms the next birth (YBh 2.13).
