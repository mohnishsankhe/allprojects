# Rejection of the five pollutions (pañcasūtaka)

`cpt:pancasutaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ethics

## Names
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): pañcasūtaka

## Definitions
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): Liṅga-wearers are not subject to the pollutions of birth, death, menstruation, leftovers or caste.
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The teacher who has become Śiva is not tainted by birth- and death-pollution (14.16).
