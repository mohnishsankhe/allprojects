## Definitions
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): The Buddha has become (bhūta) a means of valid cognition: he knows what is to be abandoned and adopted with its means, through compassion cultivated over many lives and the seeing of selflessness; he is the teacher, the Sugata and the protector; his authority is proved, not assumed.
