## Definitions
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Seven degrees of inner exhilaration in the Kula rite: ārambha, taruṇa, yauvana, prauḍha, tadanta, unmanā and anavastha (Kulārṇava 8.4); the first five are waking, unmanā dream, the equal state deep sleep; knowing them one is liberated (8.94-95).
