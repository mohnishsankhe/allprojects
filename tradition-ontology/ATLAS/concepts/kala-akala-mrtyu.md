## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Death at the exhaustion of life's measure is timely; death midway through wrong conduct, like an axle broken by overload, is untimely; both exist (Ca Vi 3.37-38; Śā 6.28); the life-span of this age is a hundred years (Śā 6.29).
