## Definitions
- [Lokottaravāda](../lineages/lokottaravada.md): Everything about the Buddhas is beyond the world: conduct, merit, postures, body, robes, food, even the fleshly eye.
- [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md): Tathāgatas are supramundane and without outflows; their body, power and lifespan are limitless; they never sleep and are always in concentration (Vasumitra).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Rejects that the Buddha did not live in the human world (Kv 18.1).
