## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The Arhat speaks the meaning (artha); the gaṇadharas compose it as sūtras; the scripture so formed is in meaning beginningless and eternal, re-proclaimed by every Tīrthaṅkara.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The Digambaras add that the Jina's teaching issues as the divine sound (divyadhvani), which the gaṇadharas render.
