## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Haṭha is taught solely for rājayoga (HYP 1.2), a stairway to it (1.1); rājayoga is one of sixteen names of samādhi: rājayoga, samādhi, unmanī, manonmanī, amaratva, laya, tattva, śūnyāśūnya, paraṃ padam, amanaska, advaita, nirālamba, nirañjana, jīvanmukti, sahajā, turyā (4.3–4); samādhi is the union of self and mind, the sameness when breath and mind are dissolved, the union of jīvātman and paramātman (4.5–7); the GS equates rājayoga, samādhi, unmanī and the natural state (7.17); the Jyotsnā identifies rājayoga with asamprajñāta yoga.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Haṭha is a stairway to rājayoga (HYP 1.1); rājayoga, samādhi, unmanī, manonmanī, amaratva, laya, tattva, śūnyāśūnya, param pada, amanaska, advaita, nirālamba, nirañjana, jīvanmukti, sahajā and turyā are one (HYP 4.3–4).
