## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The knots of Brahmā below, of Viṣṇu in the heart and of Rudra between the brows, released in practice (Yogaviṣaya 17); pierced when kuṇḍalī awakens (HYP 3.2).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The knots of Brahmā (after the mūlādhāra), Viṣṇu (after the maṇipūra) and Rudra (at the ājñā) pierced by the Goddess in her ascent (LSN 38-39).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The knots of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra, pierced when kuṇḍalinī awakens (HYP 3.2) and, in the nāda-stages, at ārambha, ghaṭa and niṣpatti respectively (4.70–77); bhastrikā retention is said to break them (2.67).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Knots of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra on the path of the suṣumnā, pierced by kuṇḍalinī through yoga and the grace of guru and god (Yogakuṇḍalī 1; Varāha 5; Brahmavidyā).
