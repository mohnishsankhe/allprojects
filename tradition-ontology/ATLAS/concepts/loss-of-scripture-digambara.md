## Definitions
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The knowledge of the Aṅgas and Pūrvas declined step by step after Mahāvīra — three omniscients (62 years), five śrutakevalins (100), eleven knowers of ten Pūrvas (183), five of the eleven Aṅgas (220), four of the Ācārāṅga (118) — until 683 years after the nirvāṇa no complete Aṅga was known; only portions reached Dharasena and Guṇadhara and were written as the Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama and Kaṣāyapāhuḍa. The Śvetāmbara Āgamas are therefore not the original scripture.
