## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Lineage contribution (Upaniṣadic): the self gathers the particles of energy into the heart, the tip of the heart lights up, and it departs by the eye, the head or another part, followed by breath and the functions, knowledge and action holding it (BAU 4.4.1-2); speech merges into mind, mind into breath, breath into heat, heat into the highest deity (ChU 6.8.6, 6.15); the creaking cart and the fruit loosed from its stalk (BAU 4.3.35-38); the inner sound is no longer heard (BAU 5.9); the breaths do not leave the dead man; the name remains (BAU 3.2.11-12).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): At the appointed time illness arises, senses and strength fail, the heat of the body stirred by wind breaks the vital points, udāna rises, speech fails and sight whirls, Yama's messengers are seen; the thumb-sized person leaves the body (through an upper or lower opening according to destiny), looking back at home; the good die easily, the wicked in terror.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): Senses and elements dissolve (form into sound … earth into water … into consciousness); consciousness dissolves into appearance (sign: moonrise / smoke), appearance into increase (sunrise / lamp), increase into attainment (darkness), attainment into clear light (dawn, a cloudless sky); the eighty conceptions cease and white, red and mind meet.
