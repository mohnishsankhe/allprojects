## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The inauspicious act done by one whose intellect, resolve and memory have lapsed; the provoker of all doṣas (Ca Śā 1.98-109); cause of exogenous and mental disease (Sū 7.51-52), of exogenous insanity (Ni 7.10) and, via adharma, of epidemics (Vi 3.20).
