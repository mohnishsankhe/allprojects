## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Concentration, meditation and samādhi together on one object (3.4). Mastery of it gives the light of insight (3.5); it is applied to the stage next after the one mastered (3.6); the three are inner limbs relative to the earlier five and outer relative to the seedless samādhi (3.7-3.8). Most of the powers of the Vibhūti-pāda are stated as following from saṃyama on some object.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Concentration, meditation and samādhi on one object (3.4); from its mastery the light of insight (3.5); applied by stages, 'yoga itself being the teacher' (3.6, YBh 3.6); applied to various objects it yields the powers.
