# The atomic size of the self (aṇu-jīva)

**Category:** self

## Names

## Definitions
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): The soul is atomic, a knower, one per body, infinite in number.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): The soul is atomic, a spark of Brahman.
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The soul is an infinitesimal particle of consciousness.
