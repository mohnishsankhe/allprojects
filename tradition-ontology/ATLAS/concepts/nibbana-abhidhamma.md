## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The unconditioned element, existing and apprehensible by the path, one in nature, with or without residue, void, signless and desireless; not a self and not a creator.
- [Dhammakāya meditation tradition (Vijjā Dhammakāya; Wat Paknam)](../lineages/dhammakaya.md): A realm and true self where the Dhammakāyas abide (contested).
- [Suan Mokkh (Buddhadāsa's tradition)](../lineages/suan-mokkh.md): The coolness of extinguished defilements here and now.
