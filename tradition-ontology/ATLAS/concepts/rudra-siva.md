## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The Śvetāśvatara names the one God Rudra, Śiva, Hara and Maheśvara: Rudra is one, ruling the worlds, creating and withdrawing them (3.2); his auspicious (śivā) form is prayed to (3.5); he is Śiva present everywhere (3.11), hidden in all beings like the film on ghee (4.16), 'Śiva alone' when there is neither day nor night (4.18); the māyin (4.10).
