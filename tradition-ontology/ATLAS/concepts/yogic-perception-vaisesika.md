## Definitions
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): The self is perceived through a special conjunction of self and mind, as are other substances (VS 9.13–17 C); yogins in concentration and out of it perceive the subtle, hidden and distant (Praśastapāda); seers' knowledge and the vision of the perfected arise from merit (VS 9.28).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The perception of one engaged in yoga arises from samādhi (NBh 1.1.3).
