## Definitions
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Bad activity (aśubhopayoga: passion, violence) binds demerit; good activity (śubhopayoga: devotion, vows, compassion) binds merit — a golden fetter; pure activity (śuddhopayoga: abiding in the self as equanimity) alone liberates.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Kundakunda's Pravacanasāra distinguishes the pure state (śuddhopayoga) — the soul resting in itself, cause of liberation — from the auspicious (devotion, vows; cause of merit) and the inauspicious (passion; cause of demerit).
