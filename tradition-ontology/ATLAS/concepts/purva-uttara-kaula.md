## Definitions
- [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md): Two Kaula doctrines set out by Lakṣmīdhara before the Samaya view: the pūrva-Kaula on the Goddess as Śiva's body with a shared relation of principal and subsidiary (SL 34), and the uttara-Kaula in which there is no Śiva-principle apart from Śakti and she alone is worshipped (SL 23, 35).
