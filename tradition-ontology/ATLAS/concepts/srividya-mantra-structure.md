## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The Pañcadaśī has fifteen syllables in three sections, each closed by the seed called hṛllekhā (SL 32); a further seed added makes the sixteen-syllable Ṣoḍaśī (LSN 118); in the Kādi form the first section begins with 'ka', in the Hādi form with 'ha'; the Triśatī unfolds the fifteen syllables in three hundred names. Syllables are deliberately not recorded here.
