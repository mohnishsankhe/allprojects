## Definitions
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The servants of the servants of the Lord, even if caṇḍālas of caṇḍālas, are our masters; brāhmaṇas who revile devotees become outcastes; the Āḻvārs ask to be made servants of the Lord's servants; the Lord made the pāṇar bard be carried into his shrine.
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): There is no difference between the Lord and his people; among devotees there is no distinction of caste, learning, beauty, family, wealth or occupation, because they are his; the lowest births are eligible for devotion.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): A dog-cooker devoted to the Lord is better than a brāhmaṇa with twelve virtues who is averse to him (BhP 7.9.10).
