## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): As many channels as there are embodied kinds of things; thirteen named, with roots and signs of vitiation; for the mind the whole conscious body is the channel (Ca Vi 5.3-7); vitiation is excess flow, obstruction, knotting or wrong course (Vi 5.22).
