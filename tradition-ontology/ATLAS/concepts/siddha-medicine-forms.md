## Definitions
- [Siddha medicine (cittā maruttuvam)](../lineages/siddha-medicine.md): The tradition counts thirty-two internal forms (e.g. juice, decoction kuṭinīr, powder cūraṇam, electuary lēkiyam, medicated ghee and oil, pill kuḷikai, waxy meḻuku, calcined parpam, centūram, cuṇṇam, kaṭṭu) and thirty-two external forms (e.g. poultice, fomentation, oil application, fumigation, collyrium, nasal drops, massage tokkaṇam, leech and cautery). Treatment involves patient, physician, medicine and attendant (Kuṟaḷ 950) and a regimen of restrictions (pattiyam).
