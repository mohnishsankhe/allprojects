## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The 'former' texts contained in the Dṛṣṭivāda: Utpāda, Agrāyaṇīya, Vīryapravāda, Astināstipravāda, Jñānapravāda, Satyapravāda, Ātmapravāda, Karmapravāda, Pratyākhyāna, Vidyānupravāda, Kalyāṇa (Avandhya), Prāṇāvāya, Kriyāviśāla and Lokabindusāra; lost after Bhadrabāhu (in meaning, the last four after him; in text, progressively).
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Part of the Agrāyaṇīya survives in the Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama and part of the Jñānapravāda in the Kaṣāyapāhuḍa.
