## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): From outside in: (1) bhūpura, Trailokyamohana, cakreśvarī Tripurā, Prakaṭa yoginīs (the powers Aṇimā etc., the eight Mothers, the ten mudrā-powers); (2) sixteen petals, Sarvāśāparipūraka, Tripureśī, Gupta yoginīs (the sixteen attractions); (3) eight petals, Sarvasaṃkṣobhaṇa, Tripurasundarī, Guptatara (the eight Anaṅga goddesses); (4) fourteen triangles, Sarvasaubhāgyadāyaka, Tripuravāsinī, Sampradāya yoginīs; (5) outer ten, Sarvārthasādhaka, Tripurāśrī, Kulottīrṇa; (6) inner ten, Sarvarakṣākara, Tripuramālinī, Nigarbha; (7) eight triangles, Sarvarogahara, Tripurāsiddhā, Rahasya (the eight goddesses of speech); (8) the triangle, Sarvasiddhiprada, Tripurāmbā, Atirahasya (Kāmeśvarī, Vajreśvarī, Bhagamālinī); (9) the point, Sarvānandamaya, Mahātripurasundarī, Parāparātirahasya.
