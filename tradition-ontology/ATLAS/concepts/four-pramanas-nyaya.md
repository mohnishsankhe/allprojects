## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Perception, inference, comparison and verbal testimony (NS 1.1.3); tradition, presumption, inclusion and non-apprehension are reduced to them (NS 2.2.1–2). Bhāsarvajña accepts only three (no separate comparison).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): The four means organize the Tattvacintāmaṇi's four books.
