## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The commentary identifies the luminous mind of AN 1.49–52 with the life-continuum, defiled by adventitious defilements.
- [Thai Forest tradition (Kammaṭṭhāna forest tradition)](../lineages/thai-forest.md): The radiant citta known in meditation; defilements are visitors (Ajahn Mun); ignorance can reside in its radiance (Ajahn Maha Boowa).
