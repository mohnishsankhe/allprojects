## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The opening of the Dhammapada: things (dhammā) have mind (mano) as forerunner, as chief, and are made of mind; what one says or does from a corrupted or a serene mind is followed by suffering or by happiness, as the wheel follows the foot of the ox or the shadow its owner (Dhp 1-2).
