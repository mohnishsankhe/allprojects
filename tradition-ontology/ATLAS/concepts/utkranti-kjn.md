# Departure through the skull (utkrānti)

## Definitions
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): By casting the breath upward the skull splits and the jīva goes to the spotless and becomes the all-pervading Śiva (KJN 20.1–3); at the fall of the body the skull splits for one who has the nyāsa (13.8); once the body is left the jīva is called supreme Śiva (6.7). Restricted: no method recorded.
