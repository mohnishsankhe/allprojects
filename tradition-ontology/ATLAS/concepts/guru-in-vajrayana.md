## Definitions
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The vajra master is inseparable from Vajradhara (Gurupañcāśikā 22), greater than the Buddha in kindness (Pañcakrama 4.2); attainment follows him; to despise him is to despise all buddhas.
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): The innate is found only from the true guru's mouth; the guru is the boat, the physician, the sun of gnosis.
