## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The texts say success comes to anyone who practises — young, old, sick or weak (HYP 1.64; DYŚ 37) — whatever his sect: brāhmaṇa, śramaṇa, Buddhist, Jain, Kāpālika or materialist (DYŚ 38); nāda is for the dull too (HYP 4.65, 4.80); householders can succeed (SS 5.186, 5.210–212); but some practices succeed only for the virtuous and envy-free (HYP 3.95); the SS grades aspirants and assigns them yogas; the Jyotsnā holds the dispassionate alone properly eligible and lists who may receive haṭha.
