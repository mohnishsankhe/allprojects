## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Portents by which the yogin knows his death: not seeing Arundhatī, the pole-star, the moon's shadow or the Milky Way (death within a year), a rayless sun, certain dreams, loss of shadow and bodily changes (shorter spans).
