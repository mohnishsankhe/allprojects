# The open space (veṭṭa veḷi)

## Definitions
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): The boundless open space above and beyond the body's supports, taken as the truth itself: for those who hold it true no title-deed is needed (Kuṭampai 1); the heart 'standing empty' without ritual (Aḻukaṇṇi 37); 'the open space of pure Vedānta' (Kaṭuveḷi 10).
