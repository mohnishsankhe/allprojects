## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The Black (kṛṣṇa) Yajurveda mixes formulas and explanatory prose (Taittirīya, Maitrāyaṇī, Kāṭhaka, Kapiṣṭhala); the White (śukla) keeps the formulas (Vājasaneyi Saṃhitā) apart from the Brāhmaṇa. The tradition tells that Yājñavalkya disgorged the Yajus learned from Vaiśampāyana, which his fellows took up as partridges (Taittirīya), and received new Yajus from the Sun as a horse (Vājasaneyi).
