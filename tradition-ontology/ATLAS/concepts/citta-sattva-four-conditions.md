## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Vyāsa: the mind has three guṇas; its sattva, whose form is illumination, mixed with rajas and tamas is fond of lordship and sense-objects; pierced by tamas it goes toward adharma, ignorance, non-dispassion and non-lordship; when the covering of delusion is worn away and only touched by rajas it goes toward dharma, knowledge, dispassion and lordship; freed even of the trace of rajas it is established in its own form, reduced to discernment of the difference of sattva and puruṣa, and goes to the meditation of the cloud of dharma (YBh 1.2).
