## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Secondary Purāṇas told by sages or by Vyāsa's disciples (VP 3.6.23; KūP 1.11.280); the Kūrma lists eighteen (Sanatkumāra, Nārasiṃha, Skānda, Śivadharma, Āścarya, Nāradīya, Kāpila, Mānava, Auśanasa, Brahmāṇḍa, Vāruṇa, Kālikā, Māheśvara, Sāmba, Saura, Pārāśara, Mārīca, Bhārgava); the Matsya adds that every Purāṇa apart from the eighteen issued from them (53.64).
