## Definitions
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): The power of Amitābha's original vow as the dominant condition of birth and of all that follows; the easy path rides on it (Tanluan).
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): Birth through the nenbutsu that accords with the Buddha's vow; the practitioner keeps calling.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): Wholly Amida's working: shinjin and the nenbutsu are given; the practitioner's 'no working is true working'.
