## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Every action undertaken with body, speech or mind, right or wrong, has five causes, declared in the sāṃkhya (18.13): the seat (adhiṣṭhāna), the agent (kartṛ), the various instruments (karaṇa), the many distinct activities (ceṣṭā) and the divine (daiva) as the fifth (18.14–15); this being so, one who sees one's self alone as the agent does not see (18.16), and one whose disposition is not shaped by the sense of 'I' does not slay though slaying and is not bound (18.17).
