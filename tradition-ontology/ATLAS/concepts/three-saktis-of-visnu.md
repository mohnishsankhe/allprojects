## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Parā (Viṣṇu-śakti), aparā (kṣetrajña) and avidyā-karma, which veils the kṣetrajña and causes saṃsāra; the world as the śakti of Brahman like the light of a fire.
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Internal (svarūpa/cit), marginal (the souls) and external (māyā) powers; the basis of acintya-bhedābheda.
