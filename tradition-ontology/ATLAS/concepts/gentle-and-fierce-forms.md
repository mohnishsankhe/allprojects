# The Goddess's gentle and fierce forms

## Names
- [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md): saumya and raudra rūpa

## Definitions
- [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md): One Goddess appears in gentle (saumya) forms — Lalitā, Umā, Lakṣmī, Sarasvatī — and fierce (ghora) forms — Durgā, Caṇḍikā, Kālī, Cāmuṇḍā; all goddesses and all women are her portions (Devī Māhātmya 11); knowers of the āgamas name her Speech, Padmā or Pārvatī, but she is 'a certain fourth' (SL 98).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Lalitā is the gentle, beautiful queen, but her war-names show fierce powers in her service — Vārāhī, Jvālāmālinī, the Nityās (LSN 25-33).
