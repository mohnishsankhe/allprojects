## Definitions
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): Knowledge does not end devotion: the knower's 'fourth' devotion and the natural devotion of the realised continue without a sense of two.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): Devotion is the self-luminous gem that remains when the lamp of knowledge may be blown out; knowledge and devotion both end suffering but devotion is the surer.
