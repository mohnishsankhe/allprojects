## Definitions

- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The middle channel between iḍā and piṅgalā, blocked by kuṇḍalinī and by impurities; when purified and opened the breath enters it, the mind becomes steady (manonmanī) and time is 'devoured' (HYP 2.41–42, 3.3, 4.17); synonyms: śūnyapadavī, brahmarandhra, mahāpatha, śmaśāna, śāmbhavī, madhyamārga (3.4); within it lie vajrā, citriṇī and the brahmanāḍī (SS 2.18; ṢCN 1–2).
