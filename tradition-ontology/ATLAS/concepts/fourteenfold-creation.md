# The creation of beings and the worlds by guṇa

## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Creation of beings is fourteenfold: eight divine, five animal, one human (SK 53); the upper worlds abound in sattva, the root in tamas, the middle in rajas, from Brahmā to a blade of grass (SK 54); even there suffering from old age and death is common (SK 55; SS 3.53). The Kramadīpikā names the eight divine classes (daiva, paiśāca, rākṣasa, yākṣa, gāndharva, aindra, prājāpatya, brāhma) and five animal (domestic animals, birds, wild beasts, reptiles, plants).
