## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): In the Yoga Upaniṣads: (1) the seed-essence of the body, white (Śiva) and red (Śakti), whose retention and union are sought (Dhyānabindu, Yogatattva, Yogaśikhā — summary-only); (2) the point of Oṃ; (3) identified with mind (Yogakuṇḍalī 3.5; Yogaśikhā 6.73).
