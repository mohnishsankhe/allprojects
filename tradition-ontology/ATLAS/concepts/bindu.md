## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The central point of the Śrīcakra (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 8) and the great point of the Kāmakalāvilāsa in which Śiva's light is reflected; the pair of white and red points (KKV 4-7).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): In the haṭha texts bindu is the vital essence (semen; with rajas in women), identified with the moon and the nectar in the head; life depends on it and its fall is death (Amṛtasiddhi; HYP 3.88); cosmologically bindu is Śiva and rajas Śakti (SS 1.92); Śiva's nature is nāda, bindu and kalā (HYP 4.1).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): In the Yoga Upaniṣads: (1) the seed-essence of the body, white (Śiva) and red (Śakti), whose retention and union are sought (Dhyānabindu, Yogatattva, Yogaśikhā — summary-only); (2) the point of Oṃ; (3) identified with mind (Yogakuṇḍalī 3.5; Yogaśikhā 6.73).
