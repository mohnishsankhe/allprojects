## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Powers arise from practice and concentration (far-hearing, far-seeing, invisibility, sky-travel, entering bodies); the Yogatattva calls them obstacles to be hidden; the Yogaśikhā distinguishes contrived from spontaneous powers and treats spontaneous powers as the test of a siddha.
