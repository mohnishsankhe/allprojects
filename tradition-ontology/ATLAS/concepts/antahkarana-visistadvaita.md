## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Mind (manas), intellect (mahat/buddhi) and ahaṃkāra are evolutes of prakṛti and instruments of the soul; the actual knowing is the soul's attributive consciousness working through them; ahaṃkāra as evolute is distinct from the true 'I', the self.
