## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Friendliness toward all beings, joy toward the more virtuous, compassion toward the afflicted and impartiality toward the unruly (TS 7.11); Hemacandra makes them supports of meditation (YŚ 4.117–122); recited daily in Amitagati's Sāmāyikapāṭha.
