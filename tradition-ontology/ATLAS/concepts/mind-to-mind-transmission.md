## Definitions
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): The Dharma is transmitted from mind to mind, each awakening himself; the robe is only a token (Hongren); the Platform Sūtra lists the line of transmission; in the Dunhuang version, possession of the text marks transmission.
- [Heze school](../lineages/heze.md): The silent transmission concerned only 'knowing' (Zongmi).
- [Sōtō Zen](../lineages/soto.md): Face-to-face transmission (menju) and documents of succession (shisho).
