## Definitions
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): From about the 13th c., clothed clerics (bhaṭṭārakas) presided over Digambara seats, temples, libraries and rites, filling the gap left by the near-absence of naked monks; the Bīsapantha accepts them, the Terāpantha rejected them; seats survive in the south (Śravaṇabeḷagoḷa, Mūḍabidri, Humcha).
