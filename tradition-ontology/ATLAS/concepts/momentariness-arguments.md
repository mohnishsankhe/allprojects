## Definitions
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): (1) From destruction: destruction has no cause, so a thing perishes by nature as soon as it arises (Dharmakīrti); (2) from existence: to exist is to be causally efficacious, which an enduring thing could not be either successively or at once (Dharmottara, Jñānaśrīmitra, Ratnakīrti: yat sat tat kṣaṇikam).
