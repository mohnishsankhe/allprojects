## Definitions
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): An omniscient knower of dharma is not seen now, cannot be proved to have existed, cannot be established by his own scripture without circularity, and could not be recognized by the non-omniscient; an omniscience by all six means of knowledge would not be denied, but not a perception of dharma.
