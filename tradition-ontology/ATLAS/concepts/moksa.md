# Liberation (mokṣa) in Dvaita

`cpt:moksa` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** karma-rebirth
**Members:** sālokya, sāmīpya, sārūpya, sāyujya

## Names
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): mokṣa / mukti

## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Release from saṃsāra by Viṣṇu's grace into the unobstructed enjoyment of one's own bliss, in eternal difference from and dependence on him, in forms such as sālokya, sāmīpya, sārūpya and sāyujya, graded by fitness, with bhakti continuing and the liberated desiring only in accord with the Lord's will; not the mere cessation of ignorance.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Lineage contribution (Upaniṣadic): the goal is named 'immortality' — the desireless knower's breaths do not depart; being brahman he goes to brahman (BAU 4.4.6); the serene one reaches the highest light and appears in his own form (ChU 8.12.3); the knower becomes brahman (MuU 3.2.9); freed from name and form like rivers in the ocean (MuU 3.2.8); knowing God one is freed from all fetters (ŚU 1.8, 6.13); ŚU 6.16 names saṃsāra and mokṣa.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): After the body falls, the soul goes by the path of light to Vaikuṇṭha, manifests its own nature, keeps its individuality, experiences the whole Brahman as his mode, shares his bliss and powers except cosmic functions, never returns, and serves him eternally.
- [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md): Non-difference with Brahman after death, once the adjuncts have fallen.
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): Attainment of the Lord's nature in non-separation, with the soul's distinctness preserved; by grace.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): Maryādā souls attain union (sāyujya) or Akṣara; puṣṭi souls receive service in the Lord's play, valued above liberation.
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Freedom from māyā is a by-product; the goal is prema-service in Kṛṣṇa's abode; sāyujya is rejected.
- [Śiva-viśiṣṭādvaita (Śaiva Vedānta of Śrīkaṇṭha)](../lineages/siva-visistadvaita.md): Equality with Śiva.
- [Mahānubhāva panth](../lineages/mahanubhava.md): Liberation is given by Parameśvara alone through the incarnations; the devatās cannot give it.
- [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md): The devotee does not desire liberation, only devotion.
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): Liberation is not asked for; remembrance and the saints' company are.
- [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md): Union (sāyujya) is the only lasting liberation; the accomplished is free while living.
