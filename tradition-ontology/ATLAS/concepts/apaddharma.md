## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): In extremity the rules may be set aside to preserve life, with later expiation, as in Viśvāmitra's taking the dog's haunch (MBh 12.139).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): When livelihood by one's own duty fails, one may live by the next class's means (with limits), as set out in MDh 10.81ff. — though living permanently by another's dharma causes a fall (MDh 10.97).
