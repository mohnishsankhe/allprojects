# The self common to all men (vaiśvānara)

`cpt:vaisvanara` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** ultimate

## Names

## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The self conceived as the whole cosmos — sky its head, sun its eye, wind its breath, space its trunk, water its bladder, earth its feet — of which each partial meditation grasps only one part (ChU 5.11-18); the waking quarter of the self (MāU 3); the digestive fire (BAU 5.9).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 15.14: Kṛṣṇa, becoming Vaiśvānara and dwelling in the bodies of breathing beings, joined with prāṇa and apāna, digests the fourfold food.
