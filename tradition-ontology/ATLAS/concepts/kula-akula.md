## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Kula is Śakti and akula Śiva, their relation kaula; the Goddess is Kaulinī and also akulā (Bhāskararāya on LSN 36-37).
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Akula is Śiva, Kula is Śakti; the Kaulas are adept in joining them (Kulārṇava 17.27); for Kaulas 'the non-Kula becomes Kula' (9.62).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): Kula is the totality of powers (Śakti), Akula Śiva beyond it; consciousness finally dissolves into the Akula abode (TĀ 4.170); the Kaula rule dwells in Bhairava's heart (Parātrīśikā 4).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): Kula is the domain of Śakti — the body, its centres and the yoginīs — and Akula is Śiva beyond it, where Bhairava abides at the end of aeons; the nectar is 'beyond Akula and Kula' (KJN 14.97; 16.41).
