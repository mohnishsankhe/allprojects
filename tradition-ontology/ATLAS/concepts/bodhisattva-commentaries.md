## Definitions
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): Three Indian commentaries held to be by tenth-level bodhisattvas — the Vimalaprabhā on the Kālacakra, Vajragarbha's commentary on the Hevajra, and Vajrapāṇi's commentary on the Cakrasaṃvara — which read the tantras in the definitive sense and are central authorities for the Jonang.
