## Definitions
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Six stations of the soul's progress to union with Śiva — bhakta (devotion has arisen), māheśvara (steadfastness through dispassion), prasādi (serenity of mind through grace), prāṇaliṅgi (the liṅga known within as consciousness whose power is breath), śaraṇa (bridal union with Śiva) and aikya (immersion in Śiva's bliss). The first three are marked by predominance of action, the last three by the yoga of knowledge.
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): The vacanas, above all Cennabasava's, arrange the whole of devotional life under the six sthalas, each paired with a liṅga, a kind of devotion and a mode of the soul.
