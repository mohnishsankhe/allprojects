## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Prāṇa, apāna, vyāna, udāna and samāna are all breath (BAU 1.5.3); PrU 3.5-7 assigns them places: apāna in the organs of excretion and generation, prāṇa in eye, ear, mouth and nose, samāna in the middle distributing food, vyāna in the channels, udāna leading upward at death; ChU 3.13 links them with the five openings of the heart and ChU 5.19-23 with the five first offerings of food.
