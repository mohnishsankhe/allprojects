## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The seen does not exist apart from consciousness; worlds arise from the mind's conceiving (saṅkalpa, manorājya) like dreams, whole lives occurring in a moment, worlds within a room, a rock or an atom; wiping away the seen is nirvāṇa.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): All grasped by mind and senses is māyā, a mental creation; the three states are the mind's play like a whirling firebrand.
