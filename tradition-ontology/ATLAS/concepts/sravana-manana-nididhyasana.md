## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Dvaita: hearing, reflection, meditation and devotion are the principal means to knowledge; meditation, not a mere subsidiary of hearing, produces the direct realization by which the Lord is pleased; hearing and the rest must never be reduced.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The threefold discipline of the qualified seeker (BĀU 2.4.5): hearing removes doubt about the means (scripture's purport), reflection doubt about the object, contemplation contrary habitual notions.
- [Vivaraṇa school (Vivaraṇa-prasthāna)](../lineages/vivarana.md): Hearing is principal, the other two auxiliaries.
- [Bhāmatī school (Bhāmatī-prasthāna)](../lineages/bhamati.md): Contemplation is principal; the purified mind is the instrument.
