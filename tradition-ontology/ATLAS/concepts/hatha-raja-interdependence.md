## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Haṭha is taught solely for rājayoga (1.2); its practices run up to rājayoga's fruit (1.67); without haṭha rājayoga does not succeed, and the reverse (2.76); mudrās do not shine without rājayoga (3.126); those who know only haṭha are said to lack the fruit of their toil (4.79); all haṭha and laya methods are for rājayoga (4.103); the seed, field, water image (4.104).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Without haṭha rājayoga does not succeed and without rājayoga haṭha does not; both are practised to completion (HYP 2.76; SS 5.181); those who practise only haṭha without knowing rājayoga labour without fruit (HYP 4.79); all haṭha and laya methods are for rājayoga (4.103); tattva is the seed, haṭha the field, detachment the water (4.104).
