## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The pupil approaches 'with fuel in hand' (MuU 1.2.12; PrU 1.1; ChU 8.7.2; KauU 1.1); the teacher must be learned in the Veda and established in brahman (MuU 1.2.12); pupils are tested — a year of austerity (PrU 1.2), truthfulness (ChU 4.4), 101 years of study (ChU 8.7-12); knowledge from a teacher leads most surely (ChU 4.9.3); 'one who has a teacher knows' (ChU 6.14.2); lineage lists (vaṃśa) trace teachings back to brahman (BAU 2.6, 4.6, 6.5; ChU 3.11.4, 8.15; MuU 1.1.1-2); devotion to the teacher as to God (ŚU 6.23).
