## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Only a soul in its last cycle (caramāvarta), no longer binding karma of maximum intensity (apunarbandhaka), who performs the preliminaries (pūrvasevā) — worship of teachers and gods, good conduct, austerity, non-aversion to liberation — is fit for yoga (Yogabindu; Yogaviṃśikā).
