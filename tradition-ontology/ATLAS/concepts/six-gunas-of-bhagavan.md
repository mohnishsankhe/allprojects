## Definitions
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Knowledge, power, lordship, strength, virility and splendour; knowledge is Brahman's very form and the other five its aspects; the six are also Lakṣmī's body; pairs of them manifest as the vyūhas.
