## Names

## Definitions
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): 'I am the servant; the Lord is the master in the form of all' — Hanumān's and Bharata's devotion.
- [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md): Servant-devotion alone is cultivated; the erotic mood is not.
- [Mīrābāī's devotion (the Mīrā song-tradition)](../lineages/mirabai.md): 'Keep me as your servant' — Mīrā's plea alongside her bridal love.
- [Unaffiliated regional bhakti poets (classificatory grouping)](../lineages/regional-bhakti-poets.md): Tyāgarāja prefers service near Rāma to wealth; Bhadrācala Rāmadās, Rāma's servant, reproaches his master.
