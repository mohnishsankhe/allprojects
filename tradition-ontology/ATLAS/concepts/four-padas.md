## Definitions
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Every Āgama has four sections (vidyā/jñāna, kriyā, yoga, caryā); as a path, the initiate's conduct, ritual, yoga and knowledge.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Four successive stages, each containing all four in miniature (caryā-in-caryā etc.), with fruits sālokya, sāmīpya, sārūpya, sāyujya; the four paths of servant, son, friend and the true path.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Several Pāñcarātra saṃhitās (e.g. the Pādma) are likewise divided into four quarters — knowledge, yoga, ritual and conduct.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): The Tirumantiram's four paths (caryai, kiriyai, yōkam, ñāṉam) are accepted by Tāyumāṉavar and underlie the Siddhars' view that outer worship belongs to the lower rungs, which the knower leaves behind.
