## Definitions
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): Śiva met as six liṅgas corresponding to the six sthalas: ācāraliṅga (bhakta), guruliṅga (māheśvara), śivaliṅga (prasādi), jaṅgamaliṅga (prāṇaliṅgi), prasādaliṅga (śaraṇa), mahāliṅga (aikya); in the manual tradition each is correlated with a sense (smell, taste, sight, touch, hearing, the heart) and an element.
