## Definitions
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): Five codes of conduct: liṅgācāra (exclusive worship of the liṅga), sadācāra (honest livelihood and moral life), śivācāra (equality of all devotees), bhṛtyācāra (servant-humility) and gaṇācāra (defence of the community and faith).
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The SSM's exclusivity (no other gods), its duty to protect liṅgas and devotees at the cost of life and its rule on revilers of Śiva are the textual background of liṅgācāra and gaṇācāra.
