## Definitions
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Mind, Buddha and beings are one; the Western land is born within one's own mind (Zunshi); every land is the Bliss of mind-only and every buddha the Amitābha of one's nature (Tianru); yet one must be born in a buddha-land because one's power is insufficient (Yanshou), and to deny the Western land while unable to live in a latrine without disgust is self-deception (Zhuhong).
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): Not accepted: the Pure Land is pointed to in a direction and given form for ordinary beings.
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): The Western Land is near for the wise: as the mind is pure the buddha-land is pure; bodhisattvas and realms are states of mind.
- [Rinzai Zen](../lineages/rinzai.md): 'This very place is the Lotus Land' (Hakuin).
