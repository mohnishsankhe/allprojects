## Definitions
- [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md): Six famous leaders of orders contemporary with the Buddha — Pūraṇa Kassapa, Makkhali Gosāla, Ajita Kesakambalī, Pakudha Kaccāyana, Sañjaya Belaṭṭhiputta, Nigaṇṭha Nātaputta — each praised as ford-maker and elder, each reported as answering Ajātasattu off the point; they appear together in DN 2, DN 16, MN 30, MN 36, MN 77, SN 3.1, SN 44.9, Snp 3.6, the Vinaya (bowl episode) and the Milindapañha.
