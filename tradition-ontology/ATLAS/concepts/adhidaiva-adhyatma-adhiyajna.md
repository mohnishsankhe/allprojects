## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7.29–8.4 gives a set of six things to be known with the Lord and defines each: Brahman is the supreme imperishable; adhyātma is own being (svabhāva); karma is the sending-forth (visarga) that brings about beings' states of existence; adhibhūta is the perishable state; adhidaivata is puruṣa ('person'); adhiyajña is the Lord himself here in the body. Those who know him so know him even at the time of departure.
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Correspondences are drawn in parallel series: in the realm of the gods the sun is the eater and the moon the food; in the realm of the self the breath is the eater and food is its food (ŚB 10.6.2.1–4).
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): The Āraṇyaka saṃhitā-teachings extend the planes to speech: stops, sibilants and vowels = earth, atmosphere, sky = eye, ear, mind (AA 3.2.5).
