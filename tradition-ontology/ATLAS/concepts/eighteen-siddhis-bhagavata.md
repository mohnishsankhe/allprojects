## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Eight chief siddhis residing in the Lord (aṇimā, mahimā, laghimā, prāpti, prākāmya, īśitā, vaśitā, fulfilment of desire) and ten from the guṇas (freedom from hunger and thirst, far hearing and seeing, speed of mind, taking any form, entering another body, dying at will, sporting with the gods, accomplishing one's resolve, unobstructed command), with lesser ones such as knowing the three times and others' minds; all are obstacles for one seeking the Lord.
