# The Lord's lordly yoga (yogam aiśvaram): beings in him, he not in them

## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 9.4–6 with 7.12: all this is pervaded by the Lord in unmanifest form; all beings abide in him, but he is not established in them; and yet beings do not abide in him — 'behold my lordly yoga'; sustaining beings and bringing them into being, his self does not abide in them; as the great wind moving everywhere abides in space, so all beings abide in him. States of the guṇas are from him, he not in them, they in him.
