# From the departed (preta) to ancestor (pitṛ)

## Definitions
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The newly dead remains a preta nourished by the rites of the first days and the monthly offerings of the first year, until the sapiṇḍīkaraṇa unites him with father, grandfather and great-grandfather, the most distant then passing out of the piṇḍa circle.
