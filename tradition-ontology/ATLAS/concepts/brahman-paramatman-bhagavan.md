## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The one non-dual reality called Brahman, Paramātman and Bhagavān (BhP 1.2.11); Bhagavān possesses the six excellences and is properly Vāsudeva (VP 6.5.74-79); Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān himself (BhP 1.3.28).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): One non-dual reality realised in three ways: as Brahman (effulgence, incomplete), Paramātman (indwelling partial expansion) and Bhagavān (full, with all powers); Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān himself.
