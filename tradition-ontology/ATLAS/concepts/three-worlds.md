## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Earth, atmosphere and heaven, formed from the Puruṣa's feet, navel and head (RV 10.90.14), each with its gods — Agni on earth, Vāyu or Indra in the atmosphere, Sūrya in heaven (Nirukta 7.5) — and named by the utterances bhūḥ, bhuvaḥ, svaḥ (VS 36.3).
