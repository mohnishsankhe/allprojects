## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The claim of the Yogatattva and Yogaśikhā that knowledge without yoga and yoga without knowledge cannot liberate, and that both must be practised; contrasted with the Muktikā and Mahāvākya, where knowledge alone liberates.
