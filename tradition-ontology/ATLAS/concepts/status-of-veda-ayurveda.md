## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The authoritative scripture is the Veda and teachings not contrary to it by examiners approved of the learned (Ca Sū 11.27); the āptas are free of rajas and tamas (11.18); Vāgbhaṭa both calls his text to be applied 'like a mantra, not to be questioned' (Utt 40.81) and asks that what is well said be accepted whoever says it (Utt 40.86-88).
