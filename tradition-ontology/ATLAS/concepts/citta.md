# The mind (citta) in Yoga

## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The mind is a product of the guṇas (YBh 1.2), seen and not self-luminous (4.19), variegated by countless latent tendencies yet existing for another (4.24); it is one, has many objects and endures (YBh 1.32); it has seen properties (cognitions) and seven unseen ones — stilling, merit, impressions, transformation, life, movement, capacity (YBh 3.15); coloured by seer and seen it apprehends all (4.23). Commentators equate it with the inner organ (antaḥkaraṇa).
