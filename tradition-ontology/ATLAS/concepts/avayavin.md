## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The whole is a substance over and above its parts, perceived as one, inhering in its parts (NS 2.1.31–37, 4.2.4–17); the notion of the whole is also named as the cause of passion, countered by the notion of parts (NS 4.2.3; NBh).
