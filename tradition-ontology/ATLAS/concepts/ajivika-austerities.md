## Definitions
- [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md): (As reported) Nakedness, licking the hands, refusing food brought or prepared, eating at long intervals; fourfold 'fierce, severe' austerity, abstention from tasty food, restraint of the tongue; yet (Jain report) a rule allowing cold water, seeds, food made for the ascetic and women.
