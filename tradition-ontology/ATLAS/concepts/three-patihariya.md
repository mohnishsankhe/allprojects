## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The wonders of psychic power, of revealing others' minds, and of instruction; the Buddha is horrified by the first two, which an unbeliever may ascribe to a charm, and praises only the wonder of instruction (DN 11); a monk is rebuked for displaying power to laypeople (Vin Cv 5.8).
