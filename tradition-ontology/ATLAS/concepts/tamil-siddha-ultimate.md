## Definitions
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): The Siddhars name the ultimate Civam, Parāparam, Paramporuḷ, the Light (cōti, parañcōti) and the open space (veṭṭa veḷi). It is within the body and everywhere, self-luminous, the undivided fullness (akaṇṭa paripūraṇam), identical with love (TM 270), beyond qualities and beyond what devotees can speak; it is found by inner knowledge and the guru's grace, not in stone images, temples or scripture.
