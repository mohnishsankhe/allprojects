## Definitions
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Initiation acts on the soul, not caste or body; it is the cause of the other means and, as nirvāṇa-dīkṣā, destroys past and future karma through mantra-power, the present body continuing like a potter's wheel. It is samaya- (or viśeṣa-) and nirvāṇa-dīkṣā, followed by consecrations (abhiṣeka) of sādhaka and ācārya; for the incapable the observances are purified away ('seedless', nirbīja), for the capable they remain ('with seed').
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Initiation (dīkṣā) into Bhagavān's mantras and worship; in Śrīvaiṣṇava usage the five sacraments.
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Samaya, viśeṣa and nirvāṇa dīkṣā, then consecrations (ācāryābhiṣeka, sādhakābhiṣeka); nirvāṇa-dīkṣā with or without 'seed'.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Seven modes: by look, touch, word, thought, scripture, yoga and the fire rite.
