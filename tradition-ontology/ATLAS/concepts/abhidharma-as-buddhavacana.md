## Definitions
- [Vaibhāṣika](../lineages/vaibhasika.md): The Buddha taught the Abhidharma in scattered form; Kātyāyanīputra and others gathered it, as Dharmatrāta gathered the Udāna verses.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): Not the Buddha's word; the Abhidharma basket consists of sūtras that teach dharma characteristics.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The Pali tradition holds the seven Abhidhamma books to be buddhavacana (the Kathāvatthu being set out by Moggaliputta Tissa on the Buddha's outline).
