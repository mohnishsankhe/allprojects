## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): In 4.87–4.88: duality with an object and apprehension is worldly; without an object but with apprehension is "pure worldly" (śuddha laukika); without object and apprehension is beyond the world. Knowledge, knowable and to-be-known are proclaimed; knowing them in order gives omniscience (4.89).
