## Definitions
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Incarnations come from Kāl's regions to keep order in creation; Saints come from the highest region to take souls home and are above them.
- [Kabīr Panth](../lineages/kabir-panth.md): Kabīr came in all four ages as the Sat Puruṣ's messenger; the three gods are Kāl's sons.
- [Garībdāsī panth](../lineages/garibdasi.md): Kabīr is the supreme God above the gods of the three worlds.
