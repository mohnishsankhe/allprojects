## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): In the seen there will be only the seen, in the heard only the heard, in the sensed only the sensed, in the cognised only the cognised; then 'you' will not be 'by that' or 'in that', neither here nor beyond nor between: the end of suffering (Ud 1.10; SN 35.95).
