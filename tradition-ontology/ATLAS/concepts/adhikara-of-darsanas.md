## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Bhāskararāya: the many schools are valid for their respective qualified followers; censuring them unsettles those followers, who then fall from both their own school and the Kaula, for which they are not qualified (on the Kaula Upaniṣad); hence Paraśurāma's rule of no censure (PKS 1.14).
