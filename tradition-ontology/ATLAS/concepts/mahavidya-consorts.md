- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Each Mahāvidyā is worshipped with her Bhairava at her right: Mahākāla (Dakṣiṇakālī), Akṣobhya (Tārā), five-faced Śiva (Tripurasundarī), Tryambaka (Bhuvaneśvarī), Dakṣiṇāmūrti (Bhairavī), Kabandha (Chinnamastā), none for the widow Dhūmāvatī, Mahārudra (Bagalā), Mataṅga (Mātaṅgī), Sadāśiva as Viṣṇu (Kamalā) (Toḍala 1.3-19).
