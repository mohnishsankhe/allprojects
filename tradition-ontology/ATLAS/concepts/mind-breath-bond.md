## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Mind and breath are mixed like milk and water: when breath moves mind moves, when breath is still mind is still (HYP 2.2); who binds the one binds the other; the mind has two causes, latent impressions and breath, and when one is destroyed both are (4.21–25); the mind is lord of the senses, breath lord of the mind, laya lord of breath (4.29); knowledge cannot arise while breath lives and mind is not dead (4.15); the living soul is pulled up and down by prāṇa and apāna (GŚ 26–29).
