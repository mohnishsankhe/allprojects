## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The Tattvārthasūtra's seven realities — soul, non-soul, inflow, bondage, stopping, shedding, liberation — whose right understanding is right view (TS 1.2, 1.4); the Śvetāmbara canon and Kundakunda count nine categories by adding merit and demerit.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The nine realities (nava tattva): jīva, ajīva, puṇya, pāpa, āsrava, saṃvara, nirjarā, bandha, mokṣa (Navatattvaprakaraṇa; Haribhadra's Ṣaḍdarśanasamuccaya).
