## Definitions
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): The position that awakening is reached by simply not thinking or attending to anything, criticized by Kamalaśīla as leading only to dullness (Bhāvanākrama III); in the Tibetan accounts ascribed to the Chan master Moheyan at Samye.
