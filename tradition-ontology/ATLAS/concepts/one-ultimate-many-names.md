## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Brahman, one without a second, is sung in many ways by the seers, so knowledge from a single teacher is not firm; the Veda's interpretations differ with natures.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The one from which words turn back is the Person of the Sāṃkhyas, the Brahman of the Vedāntins, the pure consciousness of the Vijñānavādins and the void of the Śūnyavādins; for the one established in the supreme it may be described with the terms no-self and emptiness; the external-object and consciousness doctrines are one for us.
- `lin:ganapatya`: The undivided understanding regarding Śiva, Viṣṇu, Śakti, the Sun and Gaṇeśa is right yoga; the one lord became fivefold.
