## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Brahman, one without a second, is sung in many ways by the seers, so knowledge from a single teacher is not firm; the Veda's interpretations differ with natures.

- `lin:ganapatya`: The undivided understanding regarding Śiva, Viṣṇu, Śakti, the Sun and Gaṇeśa is right yoga; the one lord became fivefold.
