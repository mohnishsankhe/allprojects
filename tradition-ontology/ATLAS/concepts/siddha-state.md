## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): After release the soul goes upward to the end of the universe (10.5), for four causes (10.6) illustrated by four similes (10.7), and stops for want of the medium of motion (10.8); the liberated are set out under twelve heads (10.9).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Freed of all karma, the soul rises in one instant to the summit of the universe — like a potter's wheel, a gourd freed of clay, a castor seed and a flame — and stops there for want of the medium of motion; it abides bodiless, in its last body's shape, with perfect right view, knowledge, perception and siddhahood, one among infinitely many (TS 10.3–9).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Haribhadra: the one goal, nirvāṇa, is called by others Sadāśiva, the supreme Brahman, the perfected self and suchness (YDS 129–130).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Beyond description: no sound, reasoning or mind reaches it; not long or short, of no colour, not female, male or neuter; it knows and perceives; formless being (Ācārāṅga 1.5.6); the liberated rise to the summit of the world, where the medium of motion ends, and there enjoy unobstructed happiness (Aupapātika, Uttarādhyayana 36).
