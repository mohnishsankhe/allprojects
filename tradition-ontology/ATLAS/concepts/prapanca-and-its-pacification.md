# Elaboration and its pacification

`cpt:prapanca-and-its-pacification` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mind

## Names
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): prapañca / prapañcopaśama

## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Action and afflictions come from conceptual construction, which comes from elaboration; elaboration ceases in emptiness (MMK 18.5); the ultimate and nirvāṇa are the pacification of elaboration (18.9; 25.24).
