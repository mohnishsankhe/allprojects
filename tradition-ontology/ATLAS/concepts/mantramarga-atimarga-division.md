## Definitions
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The Mantramārga's scriptures rank the Atimārga (the ascetic Pāśupata and Lākula paths, for liberation alone) below the Mantramārga, whose initiates may seek powers and enjoyments as well as liberation, and which is open to householders.
