# The three principal aspects of the path

## Definitions
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): Renunciation, bodhicitta and the correct view: without renunciation one cannot stop pursuing saṃsāra; renunciation without bodhicitta does not lead to buddhahood; neither without the view cuts the root of existence.
