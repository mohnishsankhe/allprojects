## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The text names the great adepts of haṭha (1.5-8) and says that by the power of haṭhayoga they have broken the staff of Death and roam the universe (1.9).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): HYP 1.5–8 names some thirty-three great Siddhas, beginning with Ādinātha, Matsyendra, Śābara, Ānandabhairava, Cauraṅgī, Mīna, Gorakṣa and Virūpākṣa and ending with Kāpālika, who by haṭha broke the staff of time and roam the universe (1.9). Several names recur in the Buddhist list of eighty-four Mahāsiddhas and in Nāth lists (see U21, U49).
