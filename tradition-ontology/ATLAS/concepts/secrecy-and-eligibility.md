## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The teachings are to be given only to a calm, devoted, well-tested disciple or son — never to the unbeliever, ungrateful or unqualified (Muktikā 1.40–49; Subāla 16; Nirvāṇa; Mahāvākya; Yogaśikhā 2.2–3, 'after twelve years of service').
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): See cpt:srividya-secrecy: the vidyā hidden in all schools (PKS 1.30) and guarded (YH 1.2).
