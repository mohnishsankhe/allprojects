## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: One should act with a view to holding the world together (3.20): people do what the best person does and follow the standard he sets (3.21); Kṛṣṇa himself acts for this reason (3.22–24); the wise should act unattached as the unwise act attached, and not unsettle the understanding of the ignorant attached to action (3.25–26, 3.29).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The wise act unattached for the welfare and cohesion of the world, as Janaka did and as the Lord himself acts though he has nothing to gain, without unsettling the understanding of the ignorant (BhG 3.20–26).
