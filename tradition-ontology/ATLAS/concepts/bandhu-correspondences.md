## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The Brāhmaṇas explain every element of a rite by a hidden connection (bandhu) to a cosmic and a bodily counterpart — bricks and days, metres and classes and gods, the breaths and the seers — organized on three planes: of the gods (adhidaivata), of the self (adhyātma) and of the sacrifice (adhiyajña); knowing the connection is what makes the rite work.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Carried into the Āraṇyakas and Upaniṣads as upāsanā — meditations on correspondences (e.g. breath-oblations satisfying senses, gods and regions, ChU 5.19–23; the saṃhitā-upaniṣad, TU 1.3).
