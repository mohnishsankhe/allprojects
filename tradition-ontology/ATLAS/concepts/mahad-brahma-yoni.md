## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: The Lord's womb is great brahman (mahad brahma); in it he places the germ, and from that the origin of all beings comes (14.3). Of whatever forms come into being in all wombs, great brahman is the womb and the Lord is the seed-giving father (14.4). The commentators take 'great brahman' here as prakṛti; the verses call it only 'great brahman' and 'my womb'.
