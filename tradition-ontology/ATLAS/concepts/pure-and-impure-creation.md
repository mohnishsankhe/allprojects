## Definitions
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Creation proceeds from the Lord's Śakti as a pure creation (the vyūhas from pairs of qualities) and an other-than-pure creation from prakṛti; the Jayākhya orders the Vaiṣṇava (pure), primordial-matter and Brahmā's creations.
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Śiva creates the pure universe (śuddhādhvan) directly from bindu, and delegates the impure (from māyā) to Ananta, first of the Vidyeśvaras.
