# The imperishable (avipraṇāśa) of action

## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): A realist theory stated in MMK 17.13–20: an imperishable factor, like a debt-note, connects action to its fruit, morally indeterminate and abandoned only by cultivation (17.14–15); Nāgārjuna answers from the emptiness of action (17.21ff).
