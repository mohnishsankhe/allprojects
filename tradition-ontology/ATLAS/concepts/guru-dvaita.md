## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Knowledge descends from Nārāyaṇa (as Vyāsa, author of the Brahmasūtras) through Madhva and the maṭha pontiffs; the qualified disciple gains the supreme knowledge from the teacher's grace, hears the commentaries and reflects on them with companions; the ācārya initiates the prepared devotee into Hari's worship; renunciants receive the praṇava and new names; debate (vāda) between teacher and disciple serves the truth.
