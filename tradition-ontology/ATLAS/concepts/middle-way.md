# The middle way

`cpt:middle-way` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics

## Names
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): madhyamā pratipad

## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The practice that avoids devotion to sensual pleasure and self-mortification — the noble eightfold path (SN 56.11); the balanced energy of the lute (AN 6.55); in doctrine the middle between 'all exists' and 'all does not exist', i.e. dependent origination (SN 12.15).
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The path free of the extremes of existence and non-existence, eternalism and annihilationism, identified with emptiness and dependent origination (MMK 24.18; 15.7–10; RĀ 1.61–62).
