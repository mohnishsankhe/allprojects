## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The person has sixteen parts (kalā), fifteen of which depend on food (ChU 6.7); in PrU 6 the sixteen parts arise from the person and vanish into him like rivers into the ocean, and he becomes partless and immortal; MuU 3.2.7 speaks of the fifteen parts going to their foundations.
