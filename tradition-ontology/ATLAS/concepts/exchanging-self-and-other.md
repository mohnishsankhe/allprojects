# Exchanging self and other

`cpt:exchanging-self-and-other` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics

## Names
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): parātmaparivartana

## Definitions
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): The supreme secret: giving oneself to others and taking others as oneself, since all suffering comes from desiring one's own happiness and all happiness from desiring others' (BCA 8.120–136).
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): The heart of mind training: reversing the objects of self-cherishing and other-cherishing, enacted by sending and taking (Seven Points 2.6; Eight Verses 7).
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The second of the two methods of generating bodhicitta, from Śāntideva; the Guru Pūjā makes it the heart of practice.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): Taught as the 'practice of a bodhisattva' by Tokmé Zangpo.
