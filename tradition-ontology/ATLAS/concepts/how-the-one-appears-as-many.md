## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Three explanatory theories of the jīva and Īśvara: limitation (avaccheda, Bhāmatī), reflection (pratibimba, Vivaraṇa) and semblance (ābhāsa, Sureśvara); the tradition treats them as alternative methods (prakriyā) converging on identity.
