## Definitions
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): The death of a devotee is liṅgaikya; the dead are buried seated, holding the liṅga, rather than cremated, and no ancestral rites or death-pollution follow; the saints' deaths are narrated as union at sacred places.
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): One who at death drinks water in which a rudrākṣa has been pressed, thinking of Rudra, goes to Rudra's world.
