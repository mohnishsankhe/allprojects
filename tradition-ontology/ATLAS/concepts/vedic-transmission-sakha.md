## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The Veda passes from the seers by instruction (Nirukta 1.20) to pupils who repeat the teacher's words (RV 7.103.5); the teacher initiates the student and bears him as an embryo (AVŚ 11.5.3); Vyāsa divided the one Veda into four and his pupils' lines branched into śākhās (Bhāgavata 1.4.19–23; Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.4–5); each śākhā preserves its text by exact recitation.
