## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Each sign is divided into equal parts ruled by planets, each division forming a chart for a domain of life: six in Varāhamihira (BJ 1.9), sixteen 'spoken by Brahmā' in Parāśara — sign, horā, drekkāṇa, fourth, seventh, navāṃśa, tenth, twelfth, sixteenth, twentieth, twenty-fourth, twenty-seventh, thirtieth, fortieth, forty-fifth and sixtieth (BPHS 6.2–4).
