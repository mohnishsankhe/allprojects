## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Reality is divided into the seer — pure seeing, the puruṣa (2.20) — and the seen — the guṇas as elements and senses, for experience and liberation (2.18); their conjunction, caused by ignorance, is the cause of suffering (2.17, 2.24), and I-am-ness is their seeming identity (2.6).
