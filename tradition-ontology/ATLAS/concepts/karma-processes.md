## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Karma is bound, remains (sattā), rises in fruition (udaya), can be prematurely drawn out (udīraṇā), transferred between subtypes (saṅkramaṇa), increased or decreased in duration and intensity (utkarṣaṇa, apakarṣaṇa), subsided (upaśama), fixed (nidhatti) or made unalterable (nikācanā); it is experienced by its nature and then shed (TS 8.21–23; Gommaṭasāra).
