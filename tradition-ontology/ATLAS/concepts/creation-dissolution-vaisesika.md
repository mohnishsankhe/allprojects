## Definitions
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): At the end of a Brahmā's lifespan Maheśvara wills dissolution and all products are resolved into atoms; then He wills creation for the selves' experience: through adṛṣṭa motion arises in atoms, dyads and the elements arise in order, Brahmā is born in a cosmic egg and appointed to create beings (Praśastapāda).
