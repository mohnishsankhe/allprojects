## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The Bhārata has three levels of meaning: the narrative of the Pāṇḍavas with Kṛṣṇa (āstīka), the teaching of dharma, devotion and conduct (manvādi), and the reading of all its words as names of Nārāyaṇa (auparicara).
