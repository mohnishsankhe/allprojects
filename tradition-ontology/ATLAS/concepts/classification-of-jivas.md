## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Souls are liberated or worldly; worldly souls are with or without mind, mobile or immobile; one-sensed beings are embodied in earth, water, fire, air and plants; others have two to five senses (TS 2.10–24); the lowest are the nigoda beings sharing one body.
