## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The astrologer (sāṃvatsara, daivajña) must be of good family, pleasing, learned in all three branches, pure, skilled and eloquent (BS 2); only a seer crosses the ocean of Kālapuruṣa (BS 2.4); the king must honour him, and even forest-dwelling renunciants consult him (BS 2.6–8); one who predicts by trickery, feigned possession or eavesdropping is no knower of fate (BS 2.15); the Kerala manual requires daily worship, recitation and truthfulness of him (Praśna Mārga 1).
