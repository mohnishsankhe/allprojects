## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The dead go by the ancient paths, past Yama's two four-eyed dogs, to Yama and the fathers in the highest heaven, where they are rejoined with their iṣṭāpūrta and a radiant body and rejoice in svadhā (RV 10.14); the fathers are lower, middle and higher (10.15.1); those who reached heaven by tapas, battle or great gifts are there (10.154); Kaśyapa asks for the immortal world of light and bliss (9.113).
