## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The Sahasranāma recounts the war in names: Mantriṇī and Daṇḍanāthā, the fire-wall, Gaṇeśa from Kāmeśvara's glance, the destruction of Bhaṇḍa and the revival of Kāma (LSN 25-33).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Bhaṇḍāsura, born from the ashes of Kāma burnt by Śiva, conquers the worlds; Lalitā arises from the gods' sacrificial fire, weds Kāmeśvara, defeats Bhaṇḍa with her śaktis and revives Kāma.
