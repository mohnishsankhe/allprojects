## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The Laṅkāvatāra identifies the tathāgatagarbha with the ālayavijñāna: it evolves with the seven consciousnesses yet is pure by nature; without it there would be neither saṃsāra nor nirvāṇa.
