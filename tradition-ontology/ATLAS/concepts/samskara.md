# Latent impressions (saṃskāra)

`cpt:samskara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mind

## Names

## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Activities leave impressions that produce activities again (YBh 1.5); impressions born of samādhi-insight obstruct others (1.50), and those of stilling obstruct even these (YBh 1.51); impressions of emergence and of stilling alternate in the transformation of stilling (3.9); direct perception of impressions gives knowledge of former births (3.18); memory and impressions are of one form (4.9).
