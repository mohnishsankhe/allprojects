## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Lalitā is the qualityless, partless Brahman (LSN 43-45), consciousness and bliss seated on the five Brahmas (LSN 61), 'cit' being Brahman itself (Bhāskararāya on name 4), and at once the queen on Kāmeśvara's lap; the mahātattva meaning of the mantra is the partless supreme principle (YH 2.73-74).
- [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md): The Śākta Upaniṣads have the Goddess declare herself Brahman (Devī Upaniṣad); she alone is the self, what is other is unreal, and 'the one real thing called Lalitā is the non-dual, partless supreme Brahman' (Bahvṛca 5).
