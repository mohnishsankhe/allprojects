# The validity of cognition (prāmāṇya)

## Definitions
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Validity is intrinsic (svataḥ) both in arising and in being known; invalidity is extrinsic, due to a defect in the cause or a sublating cognition.
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): Every experience is valid; error is a pair of cognitions not distinguished, not a false cognition.
