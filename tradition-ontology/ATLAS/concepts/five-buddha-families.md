## Definitions
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Tathāgata (Vairocana), vajra (Akṣobhya), jewel (Ratnasambhava), lotus (Amitābha), action (Amoghasiddhi): the families of the maṇḍala into which aggregates, elements, afflictions and gnoses are ordered; the Guhyasamāja places Akṣobhya at the centre, the yoga tantras Vairocana.
