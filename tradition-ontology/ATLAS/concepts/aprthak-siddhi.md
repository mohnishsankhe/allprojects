## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The relation between substance and mode, Brahman and its body, substance and attribute: the second can neither exist nor be known without the first, though distinct from it; it replaces both identity and the Nyāya inherence (samavāya).
