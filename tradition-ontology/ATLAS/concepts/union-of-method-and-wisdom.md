# The union of method and wisdom

## Names

## Definitions
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): Wisdom without method and method without wisdom are both bondage; method is all the perfections other than wisdom.
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): Neither method without wisdom nor wisdom without method liberates the bodhisattva (Bhāvanākrama II); the Buddha's teaching has emptiness and compassion as its essence (RĀ 4.96).
