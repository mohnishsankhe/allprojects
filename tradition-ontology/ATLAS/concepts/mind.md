## Definitions
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): The inner sense by which the self grasps its relation to objects and by which the self is known in saṃsāra; some Bhāṭṭas held it persists in release as the organ of the self's bliss.
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): The conjunction of self and mind, located in the body, is the non-inherent cause of the self's qualities; hence the self's qualities appear only where the body is.
