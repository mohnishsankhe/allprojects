## Definitions
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Joy, supreme joy, joy of cessation and innate joy, arising in consecration and completion-stage yoga as the drop moves through the cakras; the innate is known between supreme joy and cessation (Tilopa) or as the fourth (Saraha).
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): Saraha warns against identifying the experienced moments with the innate (King Dohā v27).
