## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Kṛṣṇa redefines some thirty virtues and states inwardly (calm = intellect fixed on the Lord; strength = breath control; heaven = rise of sattva; the friend = the teacher; the home = the human body) and concludes that seeing merit and fault is the fault, being beyond both the merit.
