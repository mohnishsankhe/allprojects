## Definitions
- [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md): (Tamil report) Five principles: life (uyir) and four kinds of beginningless, indestructible, unchanging atoms (earth, water, fire, air) that combine into bodies and separate; a single atom is seen only by the divine eye.
