## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The vows of good conduct: homage, offering, confession, rejoicing, requesting teaching, requesting the buddhas to stay, dedication — and, in the 40-fascicle Chinese, ten vows adding following the buddhas, according with beings and universal dedication; recited for rebirth in Sukhāvatī.
