## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Two brahmans are to be known: the word-brahman and the highest; one skilled in the word-brahman attains the highest; by sound alone the soundless is revealed (MaiU 6.22).
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): Brahman is the beginningless, endless Word-principle that appears as the world through its powers, time chief among them; all words finally denote it as Being (VP 1.1–4; 3.1–2).
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): The unmanifest sound arising from the supreme bindu, which is the consciousness of all beings, not the word or the meaning; it dwells as kuṇḍalinī and becomes the letters (ŚT 1.11–14).
