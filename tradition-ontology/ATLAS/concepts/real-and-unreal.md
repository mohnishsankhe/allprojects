- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Of what is not (asat — the non-existent or unreal) there is no being, and of what is (sat — the existent or real) no non-being; those who see the truth (tattvadarśin) have seen the conclusion (anta) about both (2.16).
