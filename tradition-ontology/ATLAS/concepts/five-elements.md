## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Space, air, fire, water and earth, arising from the five subtle elements; specific, and calm, turbulent or dull (SK 22, 38).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Lineage contribution (Upaniṣadic): from the self arose space, from space wind, from wind fire, from fire water, from water earth (TU 2.1); earth, wind, space, water and light as the five great elements (AU 3.1.3); the fivefold quality of yoga — earth, water, fire, wind, space (ŚU 2.12); ChU 6 has a threefold scheme (heat, water, food).
