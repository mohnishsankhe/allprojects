## Definitions
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Rites that purify the body from conception to death and make it 'fit for Brahman' (MDh 2.26–28); Gautama's forty, including the daily great sacrifices and the solemn rites, are of no avail without the eight virtues of the self (GDh 8.14–25); later works fix a list of sixteen from conception to cremation.
