## Definitions
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): The aspiration to become a buddha is the aspiration to save beings; one who seeks birth only for pleasure is not born (Tanluan); beginners seek birth first because they cannot yet save beings in the evil world.
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): Not a separate requirement of birth in Hōnen's reading (disputed by Myōe).
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): Other-power bodhicitta is shinjin.
