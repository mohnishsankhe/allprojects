## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Vism III p. 110-114: the forty are ten kasiṇas, ten foulnesses, ten recollections, four divine abidings, four immaterial states, one perception (repulsiveness in nutriment) and one defining (of the four elements). The text decides them in ten ways: by enumeration; by what brings access or absorption (ten bring only access); by kind of jhāna; by transcending (of factors or of object); by increase (only the ten kasiṇas are to be enlarged); by object (twenty-two have a counterpart sign, twelve intrinsic-nature states, six objects not to be described; eight moving objects at the preparatory stage); by plane (the ten foulnesses, body-mindfulness and nutriment-perception do not occur among devas); by taking (nineteen by sight, breathing by touch, the air kasiṇa by sight and touch, eighteen by hearing); by condition; and by suitability to temperament.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Ten kasiṇas, ten foulnesses, ten recollections, four divine abidings, four formless states, one perception (repulsiveness of nutriment) and one defining (of the four elements); ten lead only to access, thirty to absorption.
