## Definitions
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Consciousness is no more real than its object; reflexive awareness and the storehouse are rejected; 'mind only' denies a creator other than mind and is of provisional meaning (MA 6.45–97; BCA 9.15–25).
- [Svātantrika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/svatantrika.md): Bhāviveka rejects the real other-dependent nature and accepts external objects conventionally (MH 5).
