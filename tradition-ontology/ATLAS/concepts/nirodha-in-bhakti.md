## Definitions
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Devotion is not desire, being relinquishment — the laying down of worldly and Vedic activity in the Lord; this means single-mindedness toward him (giving up other supports) and indifference to what opposes him (doing only what is favourable to him); its means include giving up sense-objects and attachment.
