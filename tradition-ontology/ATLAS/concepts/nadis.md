## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The Bhāvanā Upaniṣad names fourteen channels — Alambusā, Kuhū, Viśvodarā, Vāruṇā, Hastijihvā, Yaśasvatī, Aśvinī, Gāndhārī, Pūṣā, Śaṅkhinī, Sarasvatī, Iḍā, Piṅgalā, Suṣumnā — as the deities of the fourteen triangles of the Śrīcakra.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Lineage contribution (Upaniṣadic): 101 channels of the heart, one going to the crown by which one reaches immortality (KU 2.3.16; ChU 8.6.6); the 72,000 hitā channels in which the sleeper rests (BAU 2.1.19); 101 × 100 × 72,000 branch channels through which vyāna moves (PrU 3.6); coloured fluids and the sun's rays in the channels (ChU 8.6.1); the suṣumṇā (MaiU 6.21).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The haṭha texts count 72,000 channels arising from the bulb (GŚ 16; HYP 1.39, 4.18) — or 350,000 (SS 2.13) — with ten (GŚ 17–22) or fourteen (SS 2.13) principal and three chief: iḍā, piṅgalā and suṣumnā; they must be purified before the breath can enter the middle (HYP 2.4–5); only suṣumnā matters, the rest are 'useless' (HYP 4.18).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): 72,000 channels spread from the kanda near the navel; ten or fourteen are principal (iḍā, piṅgalā, suṣumnā, sarasvatī, gāndhārī, hastijihvā, pūṣā, yaśasvinī, alambusā, kuhū, śaṅkhinī, vāruṇī, viśvodarī, payasvinī); the suṣumnā is the path of liberation; iḍā carries the moon, piṅgalā the sun.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Ten principal nāṭis among the ninety-six: iṭakalai, piṅkalai, cuḻumuṉai, kāntāri, atti, cikuvai, alampuṭai, puruṭaṉ, caṅkiṉi, kuku (Tamil forms; list recalled at moderate confidence).
