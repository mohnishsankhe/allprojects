## Definitions
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Creation is in three (or four) grand divisions: piṇḍ (physical, the body), aṇḍ (astral), brahmāṇḍ (causal, to Trikuṭī/Daswān Dwār) — Kāl's domain — and above them the purely spiritual Dayāl Deś; spirit, spirit-and-mind, and mind-and-matter.
