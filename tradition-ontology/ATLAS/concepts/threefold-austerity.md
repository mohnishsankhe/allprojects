## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Austerity of body (worship of gods, the twice-born, teachers and the wise; purity, uprightness, celibacy, non-harm), of speech (non-agitating, truthful, pleasant, beneficial speech and recitation) and of mind (serenity, gentleness, silence, self-restraint, purity of being); each again sāttvic, rājasic or tāmasic (BhG 17.14–19); self-tormenting austerity not enjoined by scripture is demonic (17.5–6).
