## Definitions
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Śiva dances the ānanda-tāṇḍava in the hall of Chidambaram (the 'space of consciousness'), witnessed by Patañjali and Vyāghrapāda; the dance is the five acts, the raised foot being grace; Chidambaram is also the heart within the body.
- [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md): Śiva as dancer in the cremation ground (Kāraikkāl Ammaiyār) and in the golden hall of Tillai (Tēvāram, Tiruvicaippā, Tirumantiram's ninth tantra).
