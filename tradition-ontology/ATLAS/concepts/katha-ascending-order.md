## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Above the senses are the objects, above them the mind, above it the intellect, above it the great self (mahān ātmā), above it the unmanifest (avyakta), above it the person (puruṣa); nothing is higher than the person, who is the limit and the highest goal.
