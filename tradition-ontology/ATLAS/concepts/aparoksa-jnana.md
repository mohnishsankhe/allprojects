## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Direct realization of God, which wins his highest grace; the gods are liberated when Hari becomes directly perceived to them; its scope is limited by fitness — few may see him as all-pervading, most see him within their own body.
