## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The editorial division of the non-principal Muktikā Upaniṣads into Sāmānya-Vedānta, Yoga, Sannyāsa, Śaiva, Śākta and Vaiṣṇava groups, used by the Adyar Library series and later catalogues; it is not found in the Muktikā.
