## Definitions
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): The Veda is kept by exact oral transmission from teacher to pupil in several parallel recitations (continuous, word-by-word, step and the eight 'modified' ones), with attention to accent and quantity; reading from a written text is a fault (PŚ 32); memorization without understanding is 'dry fuel without fire' (Nirukta 1.18); the Vedas are endless (TB 3.10.11).
