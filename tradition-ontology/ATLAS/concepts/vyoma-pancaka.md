## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Spaces seen in the intermediate target: ākāśa, parākāśa, mahākāśa, sūryākāśa and paramākāśa (Maṇḍalabrāhmaṇa 4.1), or the guṇa-less space, supreme space, great space, space of principles and space of the sun (Advayatāraka) — order and names differ.
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The five voids of the Nāth body-map that the yogin must know in his own body (Vivekamārtaṇḍa family); the HYP's khecarī is 'in the space-wheel (vyomacakra) between sun and moon' (4.45).
