## Definitions
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): The dohās place realization on 'the fourteenth ground'; the Queen Dohā says grounds, paths and buddhahood are all one innate gnosis.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The Hevajra correlates the ten grounds with ten classes of sacred site; later systems count twelve, thirteen or more grounds.
