## Definitions
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): No perception, however heightened, can know dharma: perception grasps only what is present, and any excellence of a sense stays within its own domain (seeing far or subtle, not hearing colours).
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Cognition born of the culmination of meditation on a real object — vivid and non-conceptual; the example of those who, disturbed by desire or fear, 'see' absent things shows that cultivation produces vividness; only cultivation of the real (the four truths) makes it a means of knowledge.
