## Definitions
- [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md): For one who can do nothing, being held as his own by a true teacher is itself enough for salvation; the teacher's embrace is the final means.
- [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md): The teacher is indispensable as the one who imparts the mantras and may perform surrender on the disciple's behalf.
