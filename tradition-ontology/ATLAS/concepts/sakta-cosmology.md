## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Countless universes made of the five elements rest like sand-grains on Ananta upon the cosmic tortoise in the ocean of compassion (Nirvāṇa Tantra 1.6-9); the gods arise from Kālī and dissolve into her (10.42-43).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): The root Nature divides the Person into jīva and the supreme and herself into māyā and vidyā; Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva receive their functions from her (Bṛhaddharma madhya 6.98-124).
