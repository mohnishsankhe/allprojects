## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The Brāhmaṇas identify the sacrifice with the Person (ŚB 1.3.2.1), with Prajāpati and the year, and with Viṣṇu; the cosmos itself arose from a sacrifice — Brahman offering itself into all beings (ŚB 13.7.1.1), the world-creators' thousand-year session with austerity, truth and breath as priests (TB 3.12.9.3–7) — and the gods live by offering to one another (ŚB 5.1.1.2).
