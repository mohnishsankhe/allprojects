## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Before breath-control the Gheraṇḍa requires, in order, a suitable place (a secluded hut in a good land, not a far country, forest, capital or crowd), the right time (beginning in spring or autumn, not in the cold, hot or rainy seasons), moderate diet, and the purification of the channels (by seed-syllables or by dhauti); only then prāṇāyāma.
