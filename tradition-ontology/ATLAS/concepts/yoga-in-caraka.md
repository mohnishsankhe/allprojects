## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): When the mind is steady and established in the self, pleasure and pain cease and mastery arises — this is yoga, which leads to liberation (Ca Śā 1.137-139); its eight powers arise from concentration of the purified mind (1.141); the path of yoga and of the Sāṃkhyas is the recollection of truth (1.150-151).
