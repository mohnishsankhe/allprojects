# Śakti as consciousness-power

**Category:** ultimate

## Names
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): citśakti
- [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md): śakti

## Definitions
- [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md): The Goddess is the power of the absolute without which Śiva cannot stir (SL 1; Subhagodaya); she abides in all beings as consciousness (Devī Māhātmya 5).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): She is citśakti and the form of sentience, yet also the power of the insentient (LSN 90); the self-awareness (vimarśa) of Śiva's light (KKV 2; LSN 112).
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): The Goddess is the supreme power of knowledge, will and action, and the power of sound (Kubjikāmata 2.2).
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Kālī is pure consciousness (cinmayī), witness of all (Mahānirvāṇa 13.12); she is the power without which Śiva is inert (Toḍala 1.25).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): The root Nature, who as vidyā commands the three gods to create, protect and dissolve (Bṛhaddharma madhya 6.98-124).
