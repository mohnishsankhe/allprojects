## Definitions
- [Aghora (Aghor panth; Aghorī and Aughaṛ ascetics)](../lineages/aghora.md): Nothing is to be shunned as impure; the practitioner overcomes disgust, fear, shame and the other fetters, since one freed from the fetters is Maheśvara (Kulārṇava 13.90–91, cited in Aghora teaching).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): Non-dual conduct: fragrance and stench alike; unstained by merit and sin (KJN 11.26–29).
