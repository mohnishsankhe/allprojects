## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The beginner (prāthamakalpika), the honey-stage yogin (madhubhūmika) with truth-bearing insight, the one whose insight is light (prajñājyotis) who has mastered elements and senses, and the one beyond what is to be cultivated (atikrāntabhāvanīya) whose aim is the mind's dissolution (YBh 3.51).
