## Definitions
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Goloka/Vraja, made of wish-fulfilling gems, is Kṛṣṇa's highest abode above Vaikuṇṭha; the earthly Vraja (Vṛndāvana, Govardhana, Rādhākuṇḍa) is non-different from it.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): Vraja and Gokul as the site of the Lord's eternal play into which grace admits the soul.
