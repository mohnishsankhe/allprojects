## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): At death the soul, with only its fiery and karmic bodies, moves along the rows of space-points to its next birth-place, straight in one instant or with up to three bends; for up to three instants it takes in no matter; the liberated soul rises straight up (TS 2.25–30).
