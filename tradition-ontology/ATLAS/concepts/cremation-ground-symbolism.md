## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Kālī dwells in the cremation ground with jackals around her (Kālī Tantra dhyāna); the vīra worships her there at night (Karpūrādistotra, restricted); corpse and skull seats (śava-sādhana, pañcamuṇḍī) belong to the same setting (restricted, summary only). In Bengali devotion the heart is made the cremation ground so that the Mother may dance there (song ascribed to Rāmprasād, low confidence).
