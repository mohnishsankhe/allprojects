## Definitions
- [Atimārga](../lineages/atimarga.md): Pāśupatas ('beyond the āśramas') and Lākulas ('beyond the world', skull observance) in the Niśvāsamukha; the Svacchanda names skull-observers and Pāśupatas, and levels for Mausulas, Kārukas, Vaimalas and 'Pramāṇa'; the Kūrma lists Pāśupata, Soma and Lākula; some sources and scholars add the Kāpālikas (Somasiddhānta) as a third division.
