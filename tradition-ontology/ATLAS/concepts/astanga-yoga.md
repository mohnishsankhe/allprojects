## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Restraints (yama), observances (niyama), posture (āsana), breath-regulation (prāṇāyāma), sense-withdrawal (pratyāhāra), concentration (dhāraṇā), meditation (dhyāna), samādhi (YS 2.29). By practising them impurity is destroyed and the light of knowledge rises to discernment (YS 2.28, YBh 2.28). Pāda 2 (this chunk) gives yama, niyama, āsana, prāṇāyāma and pratyāhāra (2.30–2.55); the last three follow in pāda 3.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Restraints, observances, posture, breath-regulation, sense-withdrawal, concentration, meditation and samādhi (2.29); their practice destroys impurity until discriminative discernment (2.28); the last three are inner limbs (3.7). Nārāyaṇatīrtha subsumes many named yogas under the eight limbs.
