## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Restraints, observances, posture, breath-regulation, sense-withdrawal, concentration, meditation and samādhi (2.29); their practice destroys impurity until discriminative discernment (2.28); the last three are inner limbs (3.7). Nārāyaṇatīrtha subsumes many named yogas under the eight limbs.
