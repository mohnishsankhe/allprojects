## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Violence is the severing of vitalities through careless, passion-driven activity (TS 7.13); life pervades earth, water, fire, air and plants (2.13); souls support one another (5.21). Inwardly, non-violence is the non-arising of passion and violence its arising; the other sins are forms of violence (Puruṣārthasiddhyupāya 42, 44). Non-violence is called the supreme Brahman (Svayambhūstotra).
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Material and psychic violence (dravya- and bhāva-hiṃsā) are distinguished; psychic violence is the root.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The pure, eternal law proclaimed by all Arhats: no being is to be killed, ruled, enslaved, tormented or driven away; all beings love life; 'you are the one you intend to kill'; the essence of wisdom is not to kill anything.
