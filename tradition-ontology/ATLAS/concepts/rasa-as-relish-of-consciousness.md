## Definitions
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): Rasa is relished in a consciousness freed from personal limits and at rest in itself, a supermundane wonder; it is the 'twin' of tasting Brahman — undivided, self-luminous, bliss and consciousness, untouched by other objects (Bhaṭṭa Nāyaka as reported; Abhinavagupta; SD 3.2–3); for Jagannātha it is consciousness itself with its veil removed.
