## Definitions
- [Rinzai Zen](../lineages/rinzai.md): Illness from excessive or unbalanced striving — rising heat, cold legs, fear, exhaustion — cured by naikan and the soft-butter method (Hakuin, Yasenkanna).
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): Wrong ways of sitting are called a 'sickness, not meditation' (Platform Sūtra); confused thought and dullness are sicknesses (Yongjia).
