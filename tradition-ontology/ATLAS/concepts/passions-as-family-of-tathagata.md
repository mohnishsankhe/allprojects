## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The body-view, ignorance, craving, the poisons and the sixty-two views are the Tathāgata's family: as lotuses grow in mud, buddha-qualities grow in beings in the mud of passions, not in those who have entered the unconditioned.
