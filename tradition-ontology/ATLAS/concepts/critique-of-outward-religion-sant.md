## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Idols, pilgrimage, ritual bathing, touch-purity, fasting and sacrifice, sectarian garb, the Veda and the Qur'ān recited without love are rejected; the yogī dyed his clothes, not his mind.
- [Ravidāsī (Ravidassia) tradition](../lineages/ravidasi.md): Every outward offering is already defiled (milk by the calf, flowers by the bee); purity is of the mind.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): Your path is covered by temple and mosque.
