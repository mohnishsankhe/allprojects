## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Meditation is fixing the mind on one object for up to a muhūrta (TS 9.27); it is sorrowful (four kinds), cruel (four kinds), virtuous (examining the command, harm, fruition and structure) and pure (four stages: with multiplicity, reasoning and shifting; with unity and reasoning; subtle activity; ceased activity); only the last two lead to liberation (9.28–44).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): A steady mind is dhyāna, a moving one is citta (Dhyānaśataka 2).
