## Definitions
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): From the arising of the thought of awakening, through the perfections, the grounds and irreversibility, to buddhahood — undertaken for all beings, with the armor of vowing to liberate countless beings who are not found; neither destroying the conditioned nor abiding in the unconditioned.
