## Definitions
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Herbal, breath, yogic and mineral-metal regimens to make the body firm, reverse grey hair and wrinkles (TM 727) and prolong life for yoga. The tradition warns that mineral and mercurial kaṟpam without a guru is deadly; and Kuṭampai asks what need of kaṟpam the true knower has (2).
- [Siddha medicine (cittā maruttuvam)](../lineages/siddha-medicine.md): In Siddha medicine kaṟpam is the rejuvenative branch (kaṟpa maruttuvam).
