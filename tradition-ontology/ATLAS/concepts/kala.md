# Time (kāla)

**Category:** cosmology-time

## Names
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): kāla

## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Dvaita: time is eternal (as a flow) and non-eternal (in its divisions), directly perceived by the witness even in deep sleep; everything seen or remembered is set in it.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Lineage contribution (Upaniṣadic): time is one of the candidate causes rejected by the Śvetāśvatara (1.2, 6.1); God is the maker of time (6.2, 6.16); brahman has two forms, time and the timeless; 'time cooks all beings in the great self' (MaiU 6.14-16).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): An eternal insentient substance without guṇas, part of the Lord's body, ruling change in the realm of play and present but not ruling in the eternal realm.
