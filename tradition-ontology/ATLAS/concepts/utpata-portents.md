## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): A portent is a change of nature: men's misdeeds displease the gods, who produce heavenly, atmospheric and earthly portents announcing calamity; the king must counter them with pacification; the earthly yield to it, the atmospheric soften, and even the heavenly are calmed by great gifts and oblations (BS 45.1–7); moving, sweating or weeping images foretell ruin (BS 45.8). The great digest of such omens is the Adbhutasāgara.
