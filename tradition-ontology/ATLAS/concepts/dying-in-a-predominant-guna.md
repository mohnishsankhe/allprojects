# Dying while a guṇa predominates (BhG 14.14–15, 14.18)

## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: The guṇa that has increased when the embodied one dies decides what follows: dying when sattva has increased, it reaches the stainless worlds of those who know the highest (14.14); dying in rajas it is born among those attached to action, and dissolved in tamas in deluded wombs (14.15). Those abiding in sattva go upward, the rājasa stay in the middle, and the tāmasa, abiding in the workings of the lowest guṇa, go downward (14.18). The fruits of the guṇas are named in 14.16: the stainless fruit of well-done action, pain, ignorance.
