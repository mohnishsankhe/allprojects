## Definitions
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): The tradition's account: Satī wishes to attend her father Dakṣa's sacrifice, to which Śiva was not invited; when Śiva refuses, she turns dark and terrible and fills the ten directions with ten forms, the Mahāvidyās; she explains that she is the root Nature who took birth to win him, and goes (Bṛhaddharma madhya 6.78-134; Mahābhāgavata 8). Her death at the sacrifice leads to the scattering of her body and the pīṭhas (Kālikā Purāṇa 18).
