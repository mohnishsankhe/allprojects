## Definitions
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): Dreams present real objects of other times and places, remembered through impressions awakened by the unseen and taken as present by a sleep-affected mind; release resembles dreamless sleep in having no cognition, but without return.
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): In dreamless sleep the self is not manifest, since there is no cognition.
