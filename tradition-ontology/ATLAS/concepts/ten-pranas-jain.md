## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): An embodied soul lives by up to ten vitalities: five senses, three powers (mind, speech, body), breath and lifespan; one-sensed beings have four; injury is the severing of these (TS 7.13; Dravyasaṃgraha).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Hemacandra also describes the five winds (prāṇa, apāna, samāna, udāna, vyāna) with their seats and colours, but does not make their control a means to liberation (YŚ 5–6).
