# Avidyā (ignorance) in Advaita

**Members:** covering of the soul's own qualities, covering of the Supreme

## Names
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): avidyā (svaguṇācchādikā, paramācchādikā)

## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): For Śaṅkara, the superimposition of self and not-self, removed by discrimination; for later Advaita a positive, beginningless, indescribable power with veiling and projecting powers, the cause of saṃsāra, removed by knowledge alone.
- [Vivaraṇa school (Vivaraṇa-prasthāna)](../lineages/vivarana.md): One ignorance located in and directed to pure consciousness.
- [Bhāmatī school (Bhāmatī-prasthāna)](../lineages/bhamati.md): Ignorances located in the jīvas, directed to Brahman.
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Dvaita: a real, beginningless covering in each soul, twofold — one hiding the soul's own nature and qualities, one hiding (its knowledge of) the Supreme; only the Lord removes them and grants liberation. It does not affect God or the self-luminous in the Advaita sense; Madhva's refutations deny ignorance of a self-luminous, attributeless Brahman.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Karma, which contracts the soul's knowledge and makes it take the body for the self; not an indescribable cosmic ignorance.
