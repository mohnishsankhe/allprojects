## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md): Eleven Aṅgas, twelve Upāṅgas, ten Prakīrṇakas, six Chedasūtras, four Mūlasūtras and two Cūlikāsūtras (Nandī, Anuyogadvāra), with the Kalpa Sūtra read as part of the Daśāśrutaskandha; the commentarial layers (niryukti, bhāṣya, cūrṇi, ṭīkā) are authoritative with them.
- [Sthānakavāsī](../lineages/sthanakavasi.md): The Sthānakavāsīs accept thirty-two of these: 11 Aṅgas, 12 Upāṅgas, 4 Chedasūtras (Niśītha, Vyavahāra, Bṛhatkalpa, Daśāśrutaskandha), 4 Mūlasūtras (Uttarādhyayana, Daśavaikālika, Nandī, Anuyogadvāra) and the Āvaśyaka; not the Prakīrṇakas, the Mahāniśītha, the Jītakalpa or the Piṇḍa-/Ogha-niryukti.
- [Śvetāmbara Terāpanth](../lineages/terapanthi.md): The Terāpanthīs accept the same thirty-two as the Sthānakavāsīs.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The Digambaras accept none of these as authentic; the original Aṅgas are lost.
