## Definitions
- [Krama (the 'Sequence'; Mahānaya, Mahārtha)](../lineages/krama.md): Twelve goddesses as the phases of cognition: Sṛṣṭikālī, Raktakālī, Sthitināśakālī, Yamakālī, Saṃhārakālī, Mṛtyukālī, Rudrakālī, Mārtaṇḍakālī, Paramārkakālī, Kālāgnirudrakālī, Mahākālakālī, Mahābhairavacaṇḍograghorakālī (TĀ 4.148-172; Kramastotra 15-27).

## Relations (interpretation layer)

_Notes: The first six names and the phases are confirmed by TĀ 4.148-154 and the Kramastotra; the forms of names 7-12 (esp. Rudrakālī vs Bhadrakālī, and the full name of the twelfth) are reconstructed - moderate confidence._
