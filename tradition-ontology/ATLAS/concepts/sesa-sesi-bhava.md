## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The soul's essential nature is to exist for the Lord, its master (śeṣin); all its good lies in his pleasure and its fulfilment in service.
- [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md): Emphasis on belonging to the Lord alone (ananyārha-śeṣatva) and on total dependence (pāratantrya), such that even one's surrender is not one's own act of means.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): 'Not knowing myself I thought I and mine; I am yours and what is mine is yours' (TVM 2.9.9); the soul's duty is faultless service at all times (TVM 3.3.1); the Lord stamped the Āḻvār and all his possessions with his discus (PAT 5.4.1).
