## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Abstention from violence, falsehood, stealing, unchastity and possessiveness — in part (small vows) for householders, in full (great vows) for ascetics (TS 7.1–2).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Śvetāmbara ascetics add not eating at night as a sixth vow (Daśavaikālika).
