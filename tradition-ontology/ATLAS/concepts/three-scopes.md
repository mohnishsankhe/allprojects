# The three scopes (persons of small, middling and great capacity)

## Names

## Definitions
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): Atiśa's division of practitioners by motivation: the lesser seeks saṃsāric happiness (higher rebirth), the middling seeks their own peace (liberation), the supreme seeks the end of all beings' suffering (buddhahood).
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The lamrim's structure: the practices of the small and middling scopes are trained as 'common' preliminaries leading to the great scope, so that every teaching becomes a step to buddhahood.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): Correlated by Sakya teachers with the first three lines of Parting from the Four Attachments.
