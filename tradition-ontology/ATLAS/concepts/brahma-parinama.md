# Brahman's real transformation into the world (pariṇāma)

**Category:** cosmology-time

## Names

## Definitions
- [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md): The world is a real transformation of Brahman through its power; effect is both different and non-different from cause.
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): The world is a real transformation of the Lord's power.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): Transformation without change (avikṛta).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Transformation of the external power only (śakti-pariṇāma).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): In dissolution Brahman has souls and matter as a subtle body without name and form; in creation, as a gross body with name and form; Brahman is thus the material and efficient cause, and the transformation is real, yet belongs to the body, leaving Brahman untouched.
