## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The great brick altar built over a year in five layers, typically falcon-shaped, sevenfold to a hundred-and-one-fold (ŚB 10.2.3.18); the builder rebuilds Prajāpati, gains immortality after death and escapes re-death (ŚB 10.4.3); its 'secret' (rahasya) in ŚB 10 interiorizes it as fires built of mind, speech, breath, eye, ear and knowledge (ŚB 10.5.3).
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): Its layout by cord and peg, and the transformation of its shapes with equal area, are the subject of the Śulbasūtras.
