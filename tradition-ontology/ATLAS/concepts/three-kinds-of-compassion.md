## Definitions
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Compassion for beings who cling to 'I' and 'mine'; compassion seeing beings as impermanent; and compassion seeing them as empty of own-nature, like the moon in rippling water (MA 1.3–4).
