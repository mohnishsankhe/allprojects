## Definitions
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): Vaibhāṣika, Sautrāntika, Cittamātra and Madhyamaka (Svātantrika, Prāsaṅgika), studied as a ladder of increasingly subtle views, each refuting the grosser object of negation of the one below.
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Vaibhāṣika, Sautrāntika, Yogācāra and Mādhyamika as a graded sequence (Mokṣākaragupta; later Tibetan grub mtha').
