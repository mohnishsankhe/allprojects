## Definitions
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): The word is a partless, sequence-less entity distinct from the sounds that manifest it; the sounds plant seeds in the intellect and with the last sound the sphoṭa is grasped; the sentence-sphoṭa is finally the only real one (VP 1.44, 1.73; Mahābhāṣya on P 1.1.70; VBhS sphoṭa-nirṇaya).
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Rejected: the letters themselves (through their impressions) convey the meaning; a separate sphoṭa is unperceived and superfluous (Upavarṣa in Śabara; ŚV sphoṭavāda).
