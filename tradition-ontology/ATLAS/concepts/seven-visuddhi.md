## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Purification of virtue, of mind, of view, by overcoming doubt, by knowledge and vision of what is and is not the path, by knowledge and vision of the way, and knowledge and vision — each serving the next like a relay of chariots, the goal being final nibbāna without clinging (MN 24); systematised in the Visuddhimagga (U37).
