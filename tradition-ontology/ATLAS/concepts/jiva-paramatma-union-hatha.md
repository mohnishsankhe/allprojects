## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The text defines the state of samādhi (sameness) as the oneness of jīvātman and paramātman with all resolves destroyed (4.7).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The haṭha texts describe the self in samādhi as the union of jīvātman and paramātman (HYP 4.7; DYŚ 82, 114), 'separating mind from body and uniting it with the supreme self' (GS 7.3) — 'I am Brahman, being-consciousness-bliss' (GS 7.4); the Śiva Saṃhitā's one eternal knowledge (1.1). The metaphysics is borrowed from Advaita; the practice texts do not argue it.
