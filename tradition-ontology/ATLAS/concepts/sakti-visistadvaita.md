## Definitions
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Śrīpati: Śiva (liṅga) qualified by Śakti and the soul (aṅga) qualified by devotion are non-dual; liberation is their union in equal essence (liṅgāṅga-sāmarasya); the Siddhāntaśikhāmaṇi works this out through the stations (sthala).
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): Śiva is one, qualified by his inseparable Śakti: the pedestal is the supreme Śakti, the liṅga Paraśiva, and the union of Śiva and Śakti — the universe — is the liṅga; the soul is qualified by devotion (bhakti) as Śiva by power (Śrīpati's formulation; philosophy owned by U16).
