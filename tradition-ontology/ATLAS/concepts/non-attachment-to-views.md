# Not clinging to views

`cpt:non-attachment-to-views` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** disputes

## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The sage of the Aṭṭhakavagga holds no formulated view, does not dispute, does not proclaim 'here alone is purity' and does not take himself as equal, superior or inferior (Snp 4.3, 4.8, 4.9); even right view is a raft (MN 22; MN 38).
