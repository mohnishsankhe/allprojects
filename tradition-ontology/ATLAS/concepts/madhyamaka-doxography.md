## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Later Indian and Tibetan doxographies divide Mādhyamikas: by method into Prāsaṅgika and Svātantrika; by the account of the conventional into Sautrāntika-Madhyamaka (external objects conventionally, Bhāviveka) and Yogācāra-Madhyamaka (mind-only conventionally, Śāntarakṣita); Maitrīpa's Tattvaratnāvalī into those holding non-duality illusion-like and those holding the non-foundation of all dharmas; the Jñānasārasamuccaya ranks Madhyamaka above the other tenet systems.
