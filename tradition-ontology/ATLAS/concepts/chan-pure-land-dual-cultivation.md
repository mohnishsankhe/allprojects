## Definitions
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Chan and Pure Land reach the same end by different roads; 'Chan is the Chan of Pure Land, Pure Land the Pure Land of Chan'; practise one gate deeply; Yanshou's myriad good practices include recitation. The 'four alternatives' ascribed to Yanshou (with Chan and Pure Land like a tiger with horns) are a later formulation.
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): In Chan contexts recitation is used as a huatou ('who is reciting the Buddha's name?') or as a practice for those of lesser capacity (as reported in the Pure Land sources).
