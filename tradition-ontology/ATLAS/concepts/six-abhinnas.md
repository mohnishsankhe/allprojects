## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Psychic powers, the divine ear, knowledge of others' minds, recollection of past lives, the divine eye and the destruction of the effluents, attainable from the fourth jhāna (DN 2; AN 3.101; MN 119).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Five mundane direct knowledges developed from the fourth jhāna — supernormal powers, the divine ear, knowledge of others' minds, recollection of past lives, the divine eye — and the sixth, supramundane, destruction of the taints; the worldling's powers are fragile and an impediment to insight.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Magical power, divine ear, others' minds, former lives, death and rebirth, destruction of outflows; three are 'knowledges'; instruction is the highest wonder; power arises from cultivation, birth, spells, herbs or karma.
