## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Forms of Sāṃkhya that admit a Lord: the epic 'twenty-sixth principle' above the twenty-five (Mahābhārata, Mokṣadharma), Purāṇic accounts where emergence from pradhāna proceeds under the Lord and Kapila is an avatāra (Māṭhara on SK 1; Bhāgavata Purāṇa), a Pāñcarātra Ṣaṣṭitantra (Ahirbudhnya Saṃhitā), Yoga as 'Sāṃkhya with Īśvara' in doxographies, and Vijñānabhikṣu, who salutes mahat as the self-born Lord and treats the Sāṃkhya Sūtra's denial of Īśvara as concessive.
