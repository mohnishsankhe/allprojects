# The nature of thought is dharmakāya

## Definitions
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): Thoughts are not obstacles to be suppressed: their essence is the dharmakāya, 'nothing whatever, yet arising as anything' (Dorje Chang Thungma); like clouds they dissolve in space when mind is seen (Gaṅgā Mahāmudrā v10).
