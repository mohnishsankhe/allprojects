# The inseparability of saṃsāra and nirvāṇa ('khor 'das dbyer med)

## Names

## Definitions
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): The Sakya view of the Lamdre: the causal continuum, the all-base as union of clarity and emptiness, holds saṃsāra and nirvāṇa inseparably; when obscured it appears as saṃsāra, when purified as nirvāṇa.
