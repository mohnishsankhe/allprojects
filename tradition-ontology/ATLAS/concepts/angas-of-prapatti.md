## Definitions
- [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md): Following the six-fold verse of the Ahirbudhnya Saṃhitā: resolve to do what pleases, avoidance of what displeases, great faith, choosing the Lord as protector, helplessness, and self-offering; Deśika makes the first five limbs and self-offering the principal.
- [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md): The same qualities are the natural adjuncts of one who knows his dependence, not limbs of a means.
