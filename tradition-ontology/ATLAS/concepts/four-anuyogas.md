## Definitions
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Digambara literature is classed as narrative (prathamānuyoga), cosmology and karma (karaṇānuyoga), conduct (caraṇānuyoga) and substances (dravyānuyoga) (Ratnakaraṇḍa 43–46).
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The Digambara arrangement of all scripture: prathamānuyoga (narratives of the great persons: the purāṇas), karaṇānuyoga (cosmology and the mathematics of karma: Tiloyapaṇṇatti, Gommaṭasāra), caraṇānuyoga (conduct: Mūlācāra, Ratnakaraṇḍa Śrāvakācāra) and dravyānuyoga (substances and the self: Kundakunda, the Tattvārthasūtra).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Āryarakṣita is said to have divided the teaching into four anuyogas — caraṇakaraṇa (conduct), dharmakathā (narrative), gaṇita (calculation, cosmology) and dravya (substances) — because pupils could no longer grasp all aspects of every sūtra at once.
