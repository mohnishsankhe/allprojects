## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Going for refuge to the Buddha, the Dhamma and the Saṅgha (Khp 1); first the form of ordination (Vin Mv 1.12); the 'jewels' of the Ratana Sutta.
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): Taken three times, kneeling with joined palms, after offerings and the seven-limb worship; the entrance to all practice.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): Arises from fear and faith; one knows the qualities of the Three Jewels and keeps the precepts of refuge.
