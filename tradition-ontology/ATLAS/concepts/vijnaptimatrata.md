## Definitions
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The three realms are only cognition: objects appearing as external are cognition appearing as objects, as in a dream; this is argued by the Viṃśatikā against objections from proponents of external objects (restriction of place and time, intersubjectivity, efficacy, atoms) and in its final sense is the perfected nature itself, realized when consciousness no longer apprehends an object.
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Dharmakīrti argues from the co-apprehension of blue and its cognition that they are not different; cognition, undivided in itself, appears divided into grasped and grasper to deluded vision.
