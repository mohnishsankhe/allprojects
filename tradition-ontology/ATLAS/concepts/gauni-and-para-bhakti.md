## Definitions
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Secondary devotion is threefold, by the guṇas or by the motive (the distressed and the others), each earlier kind better; primary devotion is love itself, one though it appears in eleven forms.
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Secondary devotion — singing, remembering, worship — is the cause of and leads to supreme devotion (Śāṇḍilya Sūtra, as recalled).
