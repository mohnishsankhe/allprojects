# Devotion to the attributeless (nirguṇa bhakti)

`cpt:nirguna-bhakti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ultimate

## Names
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): nirguṇ bhakti / nirguṇ rām kī bhakti

## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Love and remembrance directed to the formless Rām who is in every body, through the Name and the true guru, without images, avatāras or rites; the Lord is 'neither one nor two' and yet the Beloved.
- [Ravidāsī (Ravidassia) tradition](../lineages/ravidasi.md): Ravidās's devotion to the formless Lord as sandalwood to water, gold to bracelet.
- [Dādū Panth](../lineages/dadu-panth.md): Dādū's remembrance of the one stainless Rām of Hindu and Turk.

## Relations (interpretation layer)
- contrasts-with → [Attributeless and qualified Brahman](nirguna-saguna-brahman.md): The Sants love the nirguṇa as a person without accepting saguṇa forms as final; saguṇa bhaktas (e.g. Tulsīdās) reply that the two are not different. — rests on [dasrath-sut-tihun-lok-bakhana](../texts/kabir-oral-corpus.md#tea-kabir-oral-corpus-dasrath-sut-tihun-lok-bakhana)
