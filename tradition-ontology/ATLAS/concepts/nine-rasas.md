## Definitions
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): Śṛṅgāra (erotic), hāsya (comic), karuṇa (compassionate), raudra (furious), vīra (heroic), bhayānaka (terrible), bībhatsa (odious), adbhuta (marvellous) — eight in drama (NŚ 6.15); śānta (peace) as ninth in the NŚ's śānta passage, Ānandavardhana, Abhinavagupta and Mammaṭa ('lovely with nine rasas', KP 1.1); four are sources of the other four (NŚ 6.39–41). Others add preyas (Rudraṭa), vatsala (SD 3.251) or bhakti.
