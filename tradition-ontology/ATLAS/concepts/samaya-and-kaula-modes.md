## Definitions
- [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md): Samaya worship is in the inner space of the heart and crown; Kaula worship uses an external support; the Samayin rejects outer worship, the Kaula rites and the sixty-four tantras (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 8, 31, 41).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The Goddess is both Kaulinī and intent on Samaya conduct, worshipped on the right and left paths (LSN 36-37, 169-171); Bhāskararāya defines the two modes without rejecting either.
- [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md): Outer cakra worship is performed by Śiva himself and by all knowers (YH 3.3 with the Dīpikā).
