## Definitions
- [Rasa Śāstra (the science of mercury) and the Raseśvara-darśana](../lineages/rasa-sastra.md): Mercury has natural impurities (poison, fire, dirt) and adventitious 'coverings', twelve in all, each causing disease or death; with them it is poison, freed of them nectar (Rasaratnākara Rasa 1.27-30; RRS 11.20-25); impure minerals and metals likewise destroy life (Rasaratnākara Rasa 5-7; RRS 5.47-48, 5.73).
