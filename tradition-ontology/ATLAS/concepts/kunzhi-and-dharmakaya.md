# The all-base (kun gzhi) distinguished from the dharmakāya

**Category:** consciousness-states

## Definitions
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): Longchenpa distinguishes the all-base — the neutral, unaware ground of karmic traces — from the dharmakāya, and all-base consciousness from wisdom; an indifferent, blank state is the all-base, not awareness.
- [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md): In Bön the kun gzhi is itself the ultimate base, empty and clear, inseparable from awareness.
