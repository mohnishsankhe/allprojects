## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Mind-element and mind-consciousness-element occur in dependence on a material base; the canon (Paṭṭhāna) leaves it unnamed and the Dhammasaṅgaṇī does not list it; the commentaries and manuals identify it as the heart, within the blood in the heart cavity.

_Notes: The Theravāda's only 'seat of mind' teaching; no channel/cakra anatomy is taught in the Abhidhamma (contrast the borān and Dhammakāya bodily bases)._
