# The three kinds of suffering (duḥkhatraya)

## Names
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): duḥkhatraya

## Definitions
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Being struck by the three sufferings prompts the inquiry (SK 1); the commentators name them internal (bodily and mental), from other beings, and from the divine (Gauḍapāda, Vācaspati). Seen and Vedic remedies do not end them certainly and finally (SK 1–2); their complete cessation is the supreme goal (SS 1.1). Suffering from old age and death belongs to embodied existence until the subtle body ceases (SK 55); the discriminating count even pleasure as suffering (SS 6.6–8).
