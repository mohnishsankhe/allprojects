## Definitions
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Subsidiary scriptures attached to the 28 root Āgamas; the Kāmika names them one by one (206 by its stated counts; tradition usually says 207); the Kiraṇa calls any division within a division an upabheda and says the divisions differ in knowledge, not in fruit.
