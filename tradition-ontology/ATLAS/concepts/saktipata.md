# Descent of power / grace (śaktipāta)

`cpt:saktipata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** karma-rebirth

## Names

## Definitions
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Śiva's awakening of the soul by his power, spoken of figuratively as a 'fall'; it occurs at the time of the equality of karma, Śiva remaining its lord as the sun is of the lotus; its sign is a distinctive devotion.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Viṣṇu's compassion, his fifth power of grace, which āgama-knowers call śaktipāta: by his glance the soul's karmas become equal and fall silent, and it is lifted from saṃsāra.
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): The descent of Śiva's grace-power on a ripe soul, marked by signs such as devotion and the wish for release; it qualifies for dīkṣā.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Catti nipātam in four grades suited to caryā, kriyā, yoga and jñāna.
