# The self (ātman) in the Upaniṣads

## Names

- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): puruṣa

## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The innermost self: the seer of seeing, not an object of seeing (BAU 3.4.2); the light of a person when all other lights are gone (BAU 4.3.6); the person made of understanding in the heart (BAU 4.3.7); the inner controller (BAU 3.7); dearer than all (BAU 1.4.8); unborn and undying (KU 1.2.18); free from evil, old age, death, sorrow, hunger and thirst (ChU 8.7.1). It is to be seen, heard, reflected on and meditated upon (BAU 2.4.5).

- same-as-under-standpoint → [Brahman (the ultimate ground)](brahman.md) (absolute): identity is declared in the mahāvākyas; the later schools read the identity as strict (Advaita), as body-soul relation (Viśiṣṭādvaita) or deny it (Dvaita) — rests on [1.4.10](../texts/brhadaranyaka-upanisad.md#tea-brhadaranyaka-upanisad-1-4-10), [6.8.7](../texts/chandogya-upanisad.md#tea-chandogya-upanisad-6-8-7), [2](../texts/mandukya-upanisad.md#tea-mandukya-upanisad-2), [3.1.3](../texts/aitareya-upanisad.md#tea-aitareya-upanisad-3-1-3)
