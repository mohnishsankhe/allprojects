# The self as a dependent designation

`cpt:self-as-dependent-designation` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** self

## Names

## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The self is neither the aggregates nor other than them (MMK 18.1), neither the appropriated nor apart from it (27.8), related to them as fire to fuel (10.15); like a plantain tree split apart, the person analysed into elements is nothing (RĀ 2.1).
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Like a chariot sought in seven ways, the self is not found yet is designated in dependence on the aggregates (MA 6.151–160).
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The 'mere I' designated in dependence on the aggregates is the conventionally existent self that acts and experiences; the inherently existent self apprehended by innate self-grasping does not exist.

## Relations (interpretation layer)
- contrasts-with → [The self (ātman) in the Upaniṣads](atman.md): the Upaniṣadic self is a real, unchanging subject; Madhyamaka finds no such self — rests on [18.1](../texts/mulamadhyamakakarika.md#tea-mulamadhyamakakarika-18-1)
- contrasts-with → [The doctrine of the person (pudgalavāda)](pudgala-doctrine.md): the Pudgalavādins' person 'neither same as nor different from' the aggregates is inexpressible but real; Madhyamaka holds it a mere designation — rests on [16.2](../texts/mulamadhyamakakarika.md#tea-mulamadhyamakakarika-16-2)
