## Definitions
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): A later Kagyu classification of approaches to Mahāmudrā: sūtra Mahāmudrā (introduced through the pāramitā path, the Ratnagotravibhāga and the guru's blessing, without tantric empowerment), mantra Mahāmudrā (reached through empowerment and the two stages, including the six yogas), and essence Mahāmudrā (direct pointing-out of mind's nature to a ripe disciple, beyond either). The scheme answers the question raised by Gampopa's practice of teaching Mahāmudrā apart from tantra.
