## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The pot breaks and water merges with water; the one who has died while living cannot die; freedom is now, in sahaj.
- [Kabīr Panth](../lineages/kabir-panth.md): The soul (haṃs) that has received the true Name passes Kāl and returns to Satlok, never to be reborn.
- [Ravidāsī (Ravidassia) tradition](../lineages/ravidasi.md): Begampura, the city without sorrow.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Return of the soul to Sach Khand and beyond to Anāmī, within at most four lives of initiation.
