## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Mind is swiftest of flying things (RV 6.9.5), it wanders far and can be called back from Yama (10.58), it is yoked by the inspired (5.81.1; VS 11.1); the Śivasaṅkalpa verses call it the divine far-goer of waking and sleep, the one light of lights, knowing, thought and steadfastness, the immortal light within, holder of past, present and future, in which the Vedas are set and all thought woven, the heart-seated charioteer (VS 34.1–6); desire was its first seed (RV 10.129.4).
