## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Each planet signifies parts of the person and world: the Sun the self and father and kingship, the Moon mind and mother, Mars strength and siblings, Mercury speech and learning, Jupiter knowledge, children and teachers, Venus desire and spouse, Saturn sorrow, servants and longevity (BJ 2.1 and later texts).
