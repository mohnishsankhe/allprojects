## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Faith, energy, mindfulness, concentration and wisdom (SN 48.10); as powers they cannot be shaken by their opposites.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): For those not born into the state, asaṃprajñāta samādhi is preceded by faith, energy, mindfulness, samādhi and insight, each giving rise to the next.
