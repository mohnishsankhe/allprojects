## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Seven earths one below another — Ratnaprabhā to Mahātamaḥprabhā — with 8.4 million hells, where beings suffer by their own leśyā, from one another and (in the first three) from the asuras, for up to 1–33 ocean-measures (TS 3.1–6).
