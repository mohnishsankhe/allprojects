## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The first Jina's disciples were simple and slow (ṛju-jaḍa), the last Jina's crooked and slow (vakra-jaḍa), the intermediate ones simple and wise (ṛju-prājña); therefore the law is stated as five vows for the first and last, four for the middle ones, and discipline is adapted accordingly.
