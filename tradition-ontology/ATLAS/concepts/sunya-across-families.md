## Definitions
- [Tantra as a movement across the traditions (Tantraśāstra; the Mantramārga / Mantranaya current)](../lineages/tantra-movement.md): The word śūnya/śūnyatā runs through Buddhist, Nāth, haṭha, Vīraśaiva and Sant language but names different things: for the Madhyamaka, the absence of own-nature in all dharmas; in the Buddhist completion stage, four graded 'empties' leading to clear light; in the haṭha texts, the central channel (śūnyapadavī) and the voids pierced in the four stages; for the Nāths and Allama, the fullness beyond the mind; for the Sants, the sky-circle where the Name resounds.
