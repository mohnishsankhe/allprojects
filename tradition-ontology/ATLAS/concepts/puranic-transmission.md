## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Brahmā remembered the one Purāṇa first; Vyāsa condenses it in each Dvāpara; he gave the Purāṇa-saṃhitā to Romaharṣaṇa, whose disciples made saṃhitās, giving four root saṃhitās (VP 3.6.15-19; BhP 12.7.5-7 with a different list of six disciples); the Sūta Ugraśravas recites to Śaunaka at Naimiṣa; each Purāṇa also has its own chain of speakers (e.g. Viṣṇu - Garuḍa; Parāśara - Maitreya; Śuka - Parīkṣit).
