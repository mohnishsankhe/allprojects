## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The extinguishing of greed, hatred and delusion (SN 38.1), the unconditioned (SN 43.12; AN 3.47), the cessation of becoming (SN 12.68), described by negation as the sphere without elements, formless spheres, worlds, coming or going (Ud 8.1) and as the unborn, unmade (Ud 8.3); realised in this life (with residue) and at death (without residue) (Iti 44); not a self (MN 1; Dhp 279).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The one unconditioned ultimate reality (paramattha-dhamma), with the characteristic of peace; distinct from mere absence (commentarial).
