## Definitions
- [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md): The highest renouncer, 'who has shaken off' saṃsāra, beyond castes and stages, living without marks or rules, appearing child-like, mad or ghost-like (Avadhūta 1–2; Turīyātīta; Jābāla 6; Maṇḍalabrāhmaṇa 5).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The liberated wanderer who has shaken off all ties, behaves like a child, madman or dullard, is untouched by desire and learns from all beings.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The one beyond hope, vāsanā, meditation and ego, defined syllable by syllable in the Avadhūta Gītā.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The Nāth ideal of the free yogī who has shaken off all conventions and stands above all schools (SSP 6).
