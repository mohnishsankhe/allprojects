## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Each truth known as 'this is …', 'it is to be (understood / abandoned / realised / developed)', and 'it has been …' — three turnings × four truths = twelve aspects; only when this knowledge was purified did the Buddha claim awakening (SN 56.11).
