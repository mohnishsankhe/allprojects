## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Seven worlds from the Avīci hell to the world of Brahmā (bhūr, the intermediate world, Mahendra's heaven, Prajāpati's mahar, and Brahmā's jana, tapas and satya), with hells and seven netherworlds below, the earth of seven continents around Meru, and classes of gods whose highest members enjoy the meditations with reasoning, reflection, bliss and I-am-ness; the bodiless and those merged in prakṛti are outside the worlds (YBh 3.26). Known by saṃyama on the sun (3.26–28).
