## Definitions
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The fruit of rites for 'one desiring heaven', understood wherever no fruit is stated; the word denotes happiness (prīti) itself (Śabara); later described as happiness unmixed with sorrow, not followed by loss.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Heaven as the reward of the warrior slain in a dharmic battle (2.32, 2.37), and as the aim of the ritual-minded criticised in 2.43.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 9.20–21: those versed in the three Vedas, soma-drinkers purified of sin, worship with sacrifices and pray for heaven; they reach the world of the lord of the gods and enjoy the gods' divine pleasures, but when their merit is exhausted they re-enter the world of mortals — 'going and coming'. 8.16: the worlds up to Brahmā's are subject to return.
