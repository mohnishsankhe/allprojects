## Definitions
- [Dharmaguptaka](../lineages/dharmaguptaka.md): A newly ordained monk lives in dependence (niśraya) on a preceptor (upādhyāya) and teachers (ācārya), serving them as a son serves a father while they instruct him; a preceptor must have at least ten years since ordination and be competent in the Dharma and Vinaya; dependence lasts (normally) five years.
- [Mūlasarvāstivāda](../lineages/mulasarvastivada.md): The same structure of preceptor, instructor and dependence governs Tibetan monastic training, the preceptor (mkhan po) conferring ordination.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Upajjhāya and ācariya with the duties of pupil and teacher (Mahāvagga I).
