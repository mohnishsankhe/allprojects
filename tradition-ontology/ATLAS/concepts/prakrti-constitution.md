## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The doṣa predominating at conception — from semen and ovum, season and womb, the mother's diet, the elements — fixes the constitution: vāta-, pitta-, kapha-natured, mixed, or balanced (best) (Ca Sū 7.39-41; Vi 8.95-100; AHS Sū 1.9-10); it does not itself cause disease, as a poison-born worm is not harmed by poison (Su Śā 4.78-79; AHS Sū 1.9).
