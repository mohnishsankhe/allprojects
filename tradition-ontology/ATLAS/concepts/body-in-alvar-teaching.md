## Definitions
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The world and elements are the Lord's body, he their life (TVM 1.1.7); the human body is 'impure' and joined to false knowledge (Tiruviruttam 1), yet may be marked as the Lord's (PAT 5.4.1) and, in love, weeps, sleeps not and melts (TVM 7.2.1). The Āḻvār hymns give no teaching on subtle anatomy, channels or centres.
