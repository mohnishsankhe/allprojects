# The eighteen vital points (aṣṭādaśa marmasthāna) of withdrawal

## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Eighteen points of the body, from the big toes upward to the head, with fixed distances between them, through which the breath and attention are drawn and held one after another as a form of sense-withdrawal (Yoga Yājñavalkya; Vasiṣṭha Saṃhitā).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The same eighteen points in the Triśikhibrāhmaṇa, Darśana and Śāṇḍilya Upaniṣads, the fifth of their five withdrawals.
