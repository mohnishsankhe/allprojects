- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Immeasurable, through nothingness, emptiness and signless liberations of mind, which differ in name but are one in meaning in that the unshakeable liberation is empty of lust, hatred and delusion, the 'makers of measure, something and signs' (MN 43).
