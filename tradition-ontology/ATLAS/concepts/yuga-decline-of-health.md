## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): In the first age men were long-lived and free of disease; from over-eating came heaviness, idleness, hoarding and greed; with each age a quarter of dharma is lost, the qualities of the earth decline and diseases arise; life shortens by a year every hundred years (Ca Vi 3.24-26).
