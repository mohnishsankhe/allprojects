## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Activity of body, speech and mind is inflow; auspicious activity brings merit, inauspicious demerit; with passions it is sāmparāyika, without passions īryāpatha (the commentators: lasting and transient); the sāmparāyika kind has the divisions of senses (5), passions (4), non-vows (5) and actions (25); the causes of the inflow of each karma type are given at 6.10-6.27.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The activity of body, speech and mind is the channel of inflow; auspicious activity brings merit, inauspicious demerit; with passions it binds lastingly, without passions only transiently (TS 6.1–6).
