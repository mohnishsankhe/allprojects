## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Meditation in Kṛta, sacrifice in Tretā, worship in Dvāpara, singing the name in Kali (BhP 12.3.52; VP 6.2.17).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Dharma declines by a quarter in each age and its chief form changes — austerity, knowledge, sacrifice, giving (MDh 1.81–86); each age has its code — Manu, Gautama, Śaṅkha-Likhita, Parāśara (Parāśarasmṛti 1.24); litigation arose with that decline (Nāradasmṛti Mātṛkā 1.1–2); later authorities list practices forbidden in Kali (kalivarjya).
