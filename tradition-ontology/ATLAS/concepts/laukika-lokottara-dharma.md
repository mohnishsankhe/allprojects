## Definitions
- [Śvetāmbara Terāpanth](../lineages/terapanthi.md): Ācārya Bhikṣu distinguished worldly (laukika) duties — social help, saving bodily life, charity to the unrestrained — from spiritual (lokottara) dharma, which alone stops karmic inflow; the first may be socially good but is not dharma in the strict sense.
- [Sthānakavāsī](../lineages/sthanakavasi.md): Opponents hold that compassion that saves life (jīva-rakṣā) and giving are themselves religious merit and part of dharma.
