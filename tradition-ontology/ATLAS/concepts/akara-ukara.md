# The letters a and u (akāram, ukāram)

## Definitions
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): The letter 'a' is the root, foot and crown of all and the Supreme itself (taṟparam); 'u' is supreme consciousness (ciṟparam); together they are He and She. Kaṭuveḷi's 'knowing the eight and the two' is read as a and u in Tamil numerals (code language).

## Relations (interpretation layer)
- part-of → [Oṃ, the designator of Īśvara](pranava.md): a and u are the first two elements of Om
