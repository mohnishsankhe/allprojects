## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): When breath is exhausted and mind dissolves there is samarasa (GŚ 94); when breath and mind both dissolve one is liberated, not otherwise (Candrāvalokana).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): In the HYP laya is the lord of the breath, resting on nāda (4.29); 'the forgetting of objects because latent impressions do not arise' (4.34); an indescribable bliss, whether or not it is called liberation (4.30–32); Ādinātha taught a crore and a quarter kinds (4.66; DYŚ: eight crore saṅketas, 14–24).
