## Definitions
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): In the mind series the nature of mind speaks as 'the all-creating king': teacher, teaching, retinue, place and time arise from it; there is no maker apart from it; it is self-arising wisdom beyond cause and condition. It is not a creator deity separate from beings but the nature of mind itself.
