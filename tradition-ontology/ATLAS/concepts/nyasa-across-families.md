## Definitions
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Before worship the body is made divine by placing mantras, the limbs (aṅga) of the deity's mantra and the letters of the alphabet on its parts.
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Elaborate installations (e.g. the sixfold nyāsa) place the Goddess, her mantras and the letters on the body.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Syllables (oṃ āḥ hūṃ and the deity's seeds) are placed at the crown, throat and heart, and the body is seen as the deity's maṇḍala.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Jain worship begins with 'sakalīkaraṇa', protecting and consecrating the body by placing the syllables of the homage to the five supreme beings on the limbs.
