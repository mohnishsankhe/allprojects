## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Ṛṣabha, Ajita, Sambhava, Abhinandana, Sumati, Padmaprabha, Supārśva, Candraprabha, Suvidhi (Puṣpadanta), Śītala, Śreyāṃsa, Vāsupūjya, Vimala, Ananta, Dharma, Śānti, Kunthu, Ara, Malli, Munisuvrata, Nami, Nemi (Ariṣṭanemi), Pārśva and Mahāvīra (Vardhamāna); twenty-four appear in every half-cycle in Bharata and Airāvata, while in Mahāvideha Tīrthaṅkaras are always present.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The nineteenth, Mallī, was a woman.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): All were men; they reached nirvāṇa at Aṣṭāpada (Ṛṣabha), Campā (Vāsupūjya), Ūrjayanta (Nemi), Pāvā (Mahāvīra) and Sammeda Śikhara (the other twenty).
