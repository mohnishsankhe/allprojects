## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Immediately after death the being takes an airy conveyance-body (ātivāhika); over the first ten days the piṇḍas offered by the sons build, limb by limb, a hand-sized body for the preta, which joins the airy body; the preta feels hunger from the tenth day.
