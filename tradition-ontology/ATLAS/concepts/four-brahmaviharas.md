## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Vism IX p. 295, 318-319: loving-kindness, compassion, gladness and equanimity; each has a mark, function, manifestation, proximate cause, success and failure, a common and a special purpose, and a near and a far enemy (see prc:brahmavihara-bhavana). The beginner starts with loving-kindness, reflecting first on the danger in hate and the benefit of patience.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Love, compassion, rejoicing and equanimity pervading all directions without measure (DN 13; MN 62); a divine abiding here (Snp 1.8); the path to the Brahmā world (DN 13) and a basis for liberation of mind (MN 43).
