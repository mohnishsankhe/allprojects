## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): As space is threefold — the whole, the space delimited by a pond and the sky reflected in it — consciousness is the whole, the consciousness delimited by the intellect, and its reflection; agency belongs to the intellect with the reflection and is superimposed on the witness.
