## Definitions
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The Veda, the smṛti and conduct of those who know it, the practice of the good, and what satisfies oneself (MDh 2.6, 2.12); with desire born of right intention (YS 1.7); where texts are silent, the agreement of the knowers and of a learned assembly (ĀpDh 1.1.1.2; MDh 12.108–113); dharma is what the Āryas praise (ĀpDh 1.7.20.6–7); the rest may be learned from women and all classes (ĀpDh 2.11.29.15).
