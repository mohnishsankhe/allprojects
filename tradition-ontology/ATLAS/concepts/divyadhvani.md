# The divine sound of the Jina

`cpt:divyadhvani` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** sound-language

## Names

## Definitions
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): An undifferentiated sound issuing from the Jina without movement of lips, at set times of day, understood by every being in its own speech and rendered into scripture by the gaṇadharas.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The Jina speaks Ardhamāgadhī, which by a supernatural excellence becomes the speech of each listener.
