## Definitions
- [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md): The knower of the Śiva-Śakti pair beyond the principles attains, according to the degree of worship, the five liberations: equal power, same world, nearness, same form and union (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 11); union is granted even to one who says 'Bhavāni tvam' (SL 22).
