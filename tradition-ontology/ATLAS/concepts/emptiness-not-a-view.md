# Emptiness is not a view

`cpt:emptiness-not-a-view` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ultimate

## Names

## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Emptiness is the relinquishing of all views; those for whom emptiness is a view are incurable (MMK 13.8); the Buddha taught for the abandonment of all views (27.30).
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Like one who asks for the 'nothing' he was offered, or a medicine that stays in the stomach, emptiness taken as a view cannot cure (PsP on 13.8).
