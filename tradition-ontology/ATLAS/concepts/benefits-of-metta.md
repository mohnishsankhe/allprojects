## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Eleven benefits of the liberation of mind by love — sleeping and waking well, no bad dreams, dear to humans and non-humans, protected by deities, unharmed by fire, poison and weapons, quick concentration, serene complexion, unconfused death, rebirth in the Brahmā world (AN 11.15); it outshines all merit (Iti 27).
