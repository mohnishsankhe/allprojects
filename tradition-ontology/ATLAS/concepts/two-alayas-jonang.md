# The two ālayas: ālaya-consciousness and ālaya-wisdom

`cpt:two-alayas-jonang` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** consciousness-states
**Members:** ālaya-vijñāna (kun gzhi rnam shes), ālaya-jñāna (kun gzhi ye shes)

## Names
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): kun gzhi gnyis

## Definitions
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): The basis of all is twofold: the ālaya-consciousness (kun gzhi rnam shes), conventional ground of imprints, and the ālaya-wisdom (kun gzhi ye shes), the ultimate buddha-nature.
