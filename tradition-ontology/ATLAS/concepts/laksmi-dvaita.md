## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Lakṣmī is the highest dependent being: eternally untouched by sorrow, never bound, 'imperishable' (akṣara) in body, superior to all souls in every quality, yet infinitely below and wholly dependent on Viṣṇu; as Śrī, Bhū and Durgā she is the higher prakṛti.
