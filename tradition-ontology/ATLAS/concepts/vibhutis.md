## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The Lord as the best in each class — among lights the sun, among words Oṃ, among sacrifices japa, among sages Vyāsa, and so on — every glory springing from a fragment of his splendour; he sustains the world with one fragment (BhG ch. 10; 7.8–11).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Saṃyama on particular objects yields powers: knowledge of past and future, of the cries of beings, of former births, of other minds; invisibility; knowledge of death; strengths; knowledge of the worlds, stars, the body; freedom from hunger; steadiness; sight of siddhas; intuition; knowledge of the mind and of the puruṣa; divine senses; entering another body; levitation and departure at death; radiance; divine hearing; movement through space; mastery of elements and senses; the eight powers; omnipotence and omniscience (3.16–49).
