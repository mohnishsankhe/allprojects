## Definitions
- [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md): Singing the pair's play in dhrupad is worship; the image of Bāṅke Bihārī appeared in answer to Svāmī Haridās's song.
- [Unaffiliated regional bhakti poets (classificatory grouping)](../lineages/regional-bhakti-poets.md): Music without devotion does not lead to the good path; joined to devotion it is worship of Śiva as sound and of Rāma (Tyāgarāja).
