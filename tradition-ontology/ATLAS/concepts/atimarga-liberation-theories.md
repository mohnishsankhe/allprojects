## Definitions
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Liberation is the manifestation of the soul's own Śiva-nature, not transfer, possession or production of the Lord's qualities.
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The Pāśupatas speak of the attainment of the Lord's qualities and powers in liberation.
