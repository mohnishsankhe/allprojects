## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Festivals and vows are fixed by tithi, asterism or solar ingress; when a tithi spans two days the digests rule which day holds it (the tithi at sunrise, or the one covering the prescribed part of the day), and a tithi 'pierced' (viddha) by the previous one may be rejected; Vaiṣṇava and Smārta rules differ, notably for Ekādaśī (Kālamādhava; Nirṇayasindhu; Dharmasindhu; Haribhaktivilāsa).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The nibandhas (Kālamādhava, Nirṇayasindhu, Dharmasindhu) decide the right day for each rite from the extent of the tithi at sunrise and in the relevant part of the day.
