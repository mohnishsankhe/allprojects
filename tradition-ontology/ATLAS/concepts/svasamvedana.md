## Definitions
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Every cognition is aware of itself; this self-awareness is a perception and is the result of the means of knowledge; memory of the cognition proves it had two appearances (of itself and of the object).
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The self-witnessing part of cognition (Cheng weishi lun); for Sthiramati the only real part.
