## Definitions
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Above the eye centre (tīsrā til) the soul rises through Sahasdal Kanwal (thousand-petalled lotus; bell and conch), Trikuṭī (Brahm; drum/thunder), Daswān Dwār/Sunn and Mahāsunn (sārangī), Bhanwar Guphā (flute) and Sach Khand/Sat Lok (vīṇā), then Alakh, Agam and Anāmī/Radhasoami; the first two belong to Kāl's domain, the rest are purely spiritual.
