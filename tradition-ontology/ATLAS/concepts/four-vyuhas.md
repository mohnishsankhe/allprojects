## Definitions
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa (knowledge and strength), Pradyumna (lordship and virility) and Aniruddha (power and splendour), each nonetheless possessing all six qualities.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): As reported by Śaṅkara: Vāsudeva the highest Self, Saṅkarṣaṇa the soul, Pradyumna mind, Aniruddha ego-sense — a doctrine of the soul's origination he rejects.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Vāsudeva (all six qualities), Saṅkarṣaṇa (knowledge and strength), Pradyumna (lordship and virility), Aniruddha (power and splendour), with functions in creation, maintenance, dissolution and teaching, and as objects of meditation.
