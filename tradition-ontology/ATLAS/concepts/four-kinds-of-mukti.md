## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Residence in the Lord's world, likeness of form, nearness and union — reached by devotion and meditation; the Muktikā counts them as lesser than kaivalya, which comes by knowledge alone; the Kalisantaraṇa promises all four to the reciter of the names.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Dwelling in Śiva's world, nearness, sameness of form, and union — the first three 'station-liberations' (patamutti), only the fourth final.
