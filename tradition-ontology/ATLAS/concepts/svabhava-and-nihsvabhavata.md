# Own-nature and its absence

`cpt:svabhava-and-nihsvabhavata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ultimate

## Names
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): svabhāva / niḥsvabhāvatā

## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Own-nature would be unfabricated and independent (MMK 15.1–2); since everything is produced by causes and conditions, nothing has it, and without own-nature there is no other-nature, existence or non-existence (15.3–6).
