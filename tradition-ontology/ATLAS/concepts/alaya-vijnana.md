## Definitions
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The eighth consciousness: maturation of karma, receptacle of all seeds, appropriator of the body; unperceived, neutral, flowing like a torrent; proved by the need to explain rebirth, the mindless attainments, the continuity of seeds and the simultaneity of cognitions; it ceases as ālaya at arhatship and is transformed at buddhahood into mirror-like wisdom.
