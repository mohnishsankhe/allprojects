## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Jyotiṣa texts name the ultimate in their invocations and cosmogonies rather than teach it: Brahman, unthinkable and unmanifest, without and with qualities (SS 1.1); Vāsudeva the supreme Brahman, from whom Saṅkarṣaṇa, the golden egg and Aniruddha-as-Sun arise (SS 12.12–21); Ka, one and many, the supreme Brahman (Āryabhaṭīya 1.1); Viṣṇu, one and unmanifest, with his three powers (BPHS 1.9–20); the Sun as self of all and door of liberation (Yogayātrā 1.1; BJ 1.1); for the Bṛhat Saṃhitā the ultimate cause is disputed among Kapila, Kaṇāda and those who say time, nature or karma (BS 1.7).
