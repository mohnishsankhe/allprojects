## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): An eclipse is a time for bathing, gifts, oblations and recitation, whose offerings Rāhu receives by Brahmā's boon (BS 5.14); the digests prescribe a period of restriction before it (vedha/sūtaka) without cooking or eating, bathing at its start and end, and great merit for recitation and gifts during it; a child born during an eclipse needs a pacification rite (BPHS 91).
