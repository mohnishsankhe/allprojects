# Keeping scripture and worldly duty after conviction

## Definitions
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Even after conviction becomes firm, scripture is to be kept, lest one fall; worldly conduct continues so far, and eating and the like as long as the body lasts; until love is attained, worldly activity is not abandoned — only its fruit is given up.
