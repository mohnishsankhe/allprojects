## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Nammāḻvār's four works are the four Vedas in Tamil — Tiruviruttam the Ṛg, Tiruvāciriyam the Yajus, Periya Tiruvantāti the Atharva, Tiruvāymoḻi the Sāman — and Tirumaṅkai's six works the six aṅgas; the Tiruvāymoḻi is the Tamil Upaniṣad.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The Āḻvārs call their Tamil the 'Tamil of wisdom' (ñāṉat tamiḻ) and promise the fruit of the Veda to those who recite their decads.
