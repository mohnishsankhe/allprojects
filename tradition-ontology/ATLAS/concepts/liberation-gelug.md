## Definitions
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): Liberation (nirvāṇa) is the final cessation of the afflictive obscurations by the wisdom directly realising emptiness; buddhahood (omniscience) is the further removal of the obscurations to knowledge, which only bodhicitta-motivated practice accomplishes.
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): The obscurations of afflictions and of knowables are exhausted only by the perfection of wisdom joined with method.
