## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Viṣṇu is worshipped through the conduct of one's varṇa and āśrama (VP 3.8.9); the Purāṇas add vows, pilgrimage, gifts, śrāddha, image worship and temple building as dharma for all classes, and praise devotion above all rites.
