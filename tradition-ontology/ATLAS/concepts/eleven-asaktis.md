## Definitions
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Devotion, though one, appears as eleven attachments: to the glory of the Lord's qualities, to his form, to worship, to remembrance, to service, to friendship, to parental love, to love as a beloved, to self-offering, to absorption in him, and to supreme separation.
