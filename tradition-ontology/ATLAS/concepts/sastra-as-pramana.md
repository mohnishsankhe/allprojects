## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Scripture is the authority for settling what is to be done and not done; whoever sets aside its ordinance and acts from desire attains neither perfection, happiness nor the highest goal; knowing its ordinance one should act (16.23–24). Austerity not enjoined by scripture, done with hypocrisy and tormenting the body, is of demonic resolve (17.5–6). Arjuna's question about those who set the ordinance aside but sacrifice with faith (17.1) opens the teaching on threefold faith.
