## Definitions
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The haṭha yogin lives alone in a small hut in a righteous, prosperous and peaceful kingdom, free of rock, fire and water around, with a small door, no holes, smeared with cow-dung, walled, with a well (HYP 1.12–14; GS 5.5–7); not in a distant land, forest, capital or crowd (GS 5.3–4); begin in spring or autumn, not in winter, summer or the rains (GS 5.8–9).
