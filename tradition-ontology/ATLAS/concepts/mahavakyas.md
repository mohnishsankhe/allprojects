# The four great sayings (mahāvākya)

## Names
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): mahāvākya

## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): A later grouping (Advaita tradition) of four Upaniṣadic sentences, one from each Veda: prajñānaṃ brahma (Ṛgveda: AU 3.1.3), ahaṃ brahmāsmi (White Yajurveda: BAU 1.4.10), tat tvam asi (Sāmaveda: ChU 6.8.7), ayam ātmā brahma (Atharvaveda: MāU 2; also BAU 2.5.19). The Upaniṣads themselves do not use the word 'mahāvākya'.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): U04 contribution: the minor Upaniṣads treat the four sayings as a secret initiatory teaching (Śukarahasya), assign one to each Veda and maṭha (Maṭhāmnāya), give them to the renouncer at initiation (Nāradaparivrājaka 4.38; Paramahaṃsaparivrājaka) and read them as the Goddess (Bahvṛca).
