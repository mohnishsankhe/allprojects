## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Speech is measured in four quarters; the wise brahmins know them; three are hidden and do not stir; men speak the fourth (RV 1.164.45). The verse does not name them; the Nirukta records several readings (Oṃ and the three vyāhṛtis; noun, verb, preposition, particle; mantra, ritual text, brāhmaṇa and ordinary speech; ṛc, yajus, sāman and ordinary speech; speech of creatures; speech in the self), and later schools read the levels of speech.
