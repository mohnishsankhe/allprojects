## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: Having gone beyond these three guṇas, bound up with the body's arising (deha-samudbhava), the embodied one, freed from birth, death, old age and pain, attains immortality (14.20). Asked for the marks, the conduct and the means (14.21), Kṛṣṇa describes one who neither hates illumination, activity and delusion when present nor longs for them when gone; who sits as one indifferent, unshaken, knowing only that 'the guṇas are operating'; who is the same in pain and pleasure, toward clod, stone and gold, in blame and praise, honour and dishonour, toward friend and foe, and has given up all undertakings (14.22-14.25). The means named is serving the Lord with unswerving bhakti-yoga, by which one goes beyond the guṇas and becomes fit for becoming brahman (14.26); seeing no doer but the guṇas and knowing what is beyond them leads to the Lord's state (14.19).
