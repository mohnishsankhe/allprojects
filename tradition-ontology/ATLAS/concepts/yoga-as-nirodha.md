# Yoga as the stilling of the mind's activities (citta-vṛtti-nirodha)

`cpt:yoga-as-nirodha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** consciousness-states

## Names
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): yogaś cittavṛttinirodhaḥ

## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Yoga is the stilling of the mind's activities (YS 1.2), after which the seer abides in its own form (1.3); Vyāsa equates yoga with samādhi and admits it only in the one-pointed and stilled mind (YBh 1.1); commentators describe stilling as the inward dissolution of the activities into their cause.
