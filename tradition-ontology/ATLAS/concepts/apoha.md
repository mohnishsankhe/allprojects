# Exclusion (apoha) as the meaning of words

## Definitions
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Words and concepts do not reach real universals or the particulars; they apply by excluding what is other; Dharmakīrti grounds this in the similar effects of particulars, Ratnakīrti defines the word-meaning as a positive thing qualified by exclusion.
