## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Blue-black, short and pot-bellied, standing in the archer's stance on a corpse, wearing a tiger skin and a garland of heads, with a single tawny braid (ekajaṭā) bearing Akṣobhya; in her four hands sword and cleaver, skull-cup and blue lotus (standard dhyāna, recalled). Teaching: she who ferries across (tāraṇa) and goddess of speech (Nīlasarasvatī); Akṣobhya is her Bhairava because he drank the ocean's poison unagitated (Toḍala 1.4-6); linked with Mahācīna, Vasiṣṭha and the Buddha (Rudrayāmala 17; Tārā Tantra 1).
