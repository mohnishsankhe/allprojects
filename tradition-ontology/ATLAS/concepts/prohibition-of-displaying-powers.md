## Definitions
- [Dharmaguptaka](../lineages/dharmaguptaka.md): The Vinayas tell how Piṇḍola Bhāradvāja rose into the air to win a sandalwood bowl and the Buddha forbade monks to display powers before laypeople; falsely claiming superhuman states is a pārājika, and a true claim to the unordained a lesser offence (moderate confidence on the parallels in each Vinaya).
