## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Conduct followed with mindfulness that brings health and mastery of the senses; worship, purity, truthful and gentle speech, no taking or coveting what is another's, avoiding bad company; devotion to continence, knowledge, giving, friendliness, compassion, joy, equanimity and calm (Ca Sū 8.17-29); in Vāgbhaṭa, dharma as the condition of happiness, the ten sinful acts abandoned, all beings seen as oneself, the middle way, and constant mindfulness (AHS Sū 2.20-47).
