# Cognition as an adventitious quality of the self

`cpt:cognition-adventitious` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** consciousness-states

## Names

## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Cognition (buddhi = jñāna = upalabdhi) is a non-eternal quality of the self produced by the contact of self, mind, sense and object; it is not the self's nature and is absent in liberation (NS 1.1.15, 3.2.1–9; NBh 1.1.22).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): The individual self's cognition, desire and effort are non-eternal; the Lord's are eternal (Tarkasaṅgraha).
