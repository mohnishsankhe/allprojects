## Definitions
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Vyāsa: four belong to the liberation of insight (what is to be abandoned is fully known; its causes are worn away; abandonment is realised by the samādhi of stilling; the means, discernment, is cultivated) and three to the liberation of the mind (the buddhi's office is done; the guṇas dissolve into their cause; there is no further arising) (YS 2.27, YBh 2.27).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The discerning one's insight at the final stage is sevenfold (2.27): four releases regarding what is to be done (known, exhausted, realized, cultivated) and three releases of the mind (buddhi's task done; guṇas dissolving; the puruṣa alone) (YBh 2.27).
