## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Indian astronomy and horoscopy reckon longitudes sidereally (nirayana) from a fixed star-point, treating the shifting solstices as an oscillation (SS: libration of 27°); the seasons and the sun's northern and southern courses follow the equinoxes (sāyana); the difference, the ayanāṃśa, is valued differently by different schools.
