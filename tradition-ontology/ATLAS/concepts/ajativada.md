# Non-origination (ajātivāda)

`cpt:ajativada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ultimate

## Names

## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): No existent arises from itself, from another, from both or without cause (MMK 1.1); non-arising (anutpāda) is the emptiness of own-nature, not the unborn self.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): From the highest standpoint nothing is ever born — not from itself, another, both or neither; creation-teachings are means to lead the mind (GK 3.15).

## Relations (interpretation layer)
- contrasts-with → [Emptiness (śūnyatā) in Madhyamaka](sunyata.md): Gauḍapāda's non-origination is of the non-dual self; the Madhyamaka's is of all dharmas without a remaining self — the Advaita tradition insists on the difference (GK 4.99) — rests on [4.22](../texts/mandukya-karika.md#tea-mandukya-karika-4-22), [1.1](../texts/mulamadhyamakakarika.md#tea-mulamadhyamakakarika-1-1), [4.99](../texts/mandukya-karika.md#tea-mandukya-karika-4-99)
