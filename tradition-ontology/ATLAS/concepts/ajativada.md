# Ajātivāda (non-origination) — Kārikā 2.32

## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): In the Kārikā 2.32: no dissolution, no origination, none bound, no striver, none seeking liberation, none liberated: this is the ultimate. Developed in prakaraṇas 3 and 4. | In the Kārikā ch.3: nothing is born in truth; birth is by māyā alone; the immortal does not become mortal; no jīva is born; this is the highest truth (3.2, 3.19–3.28, 3.48). | In prakaraṇa 4: disputants who hold birth of the existent or non-existent proclaim non-birth (4.3–4.5); nothing is born from itself or from another, nor as existent, non-existent or both (4.22); dharmas are unborn and by nature free of old age and death (4.10, 4.29); no jīva is born (4.71); all dharmas are unborn, peaceful, equal (4.91–4.93).
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): No existent arises from itself, from another, from both or without cause (MMK 1.1); non-arising (anutpāda) is the emptiness of own-nature, not the unborn self.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): From the highest standpoint nothing is ever born — not from itself, another, both or neither; creation-teachings are means to lead the mind (GK 3.15).
