## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): A recurring story: the vital powers (speech, sight, hearing, mind and others) dispute which is best; each leaves the body in turn and it survives; when breath begins to leave, all are uprooted, and they acknowledge breath as best (BAU 6.1; ChU 5.1; PrU 2.3-4; KauU 2.14; cf. BAU 1.3 and 1.5.21).
