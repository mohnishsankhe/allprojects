## Definitions
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): The Vedas and the Vedāṅgas are 'lower knowledge'; the higher is that by which the imperishable is attained (MuU 1.1.4–5) — the Vedāṅga tradition's own placement of itself below realization.
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Correspondingly, self-knowledge is 'the foremost of all sciences' (MDh 12.85).
