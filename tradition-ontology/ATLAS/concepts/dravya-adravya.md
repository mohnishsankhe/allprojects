- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Six substances — prakṛti, time, pure sattva, attributive consciousness, soul, Lord — and ten non-substances — sattva, rajas, tamas, sound, touch, form, taste, smell, conjunction and potency.
