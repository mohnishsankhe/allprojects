## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Uttering the Lord's name, even indirectly, destroys all sin and is the best expiation (BhP 6.2.9-14); it is the fearless way for all (2.1.11), the supreme dharma (6.3.22) and the means of the Kali age (12.3.51-52; VP 6.2.17); offences against it hinder its fruit (Padma, as cited).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The Name (of Rām) remembered inwardly is the only raft; recited on beads while the mind wanders it is nothing; by the Name the Camār Ravidās is honoured by brahmins.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): The Name is the Word within, not the spoken name.
- [Kabīr Panth](../lineages/kabir-panth.md): The true Name given at initiation frees the soul from Kāl.
