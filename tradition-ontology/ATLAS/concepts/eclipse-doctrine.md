## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): In the epic, the immortal head of Rāhu swallows sun and moon in enmity (MBh 1.17; cf. Svarbhānu in RV 5.40); the siddhāntas explain that the moon covers the sun and the earth's shadow covers the moon (Āryabhaṭīya 4.37); Varāhamihira rejects the rival accounts of Rāhu, states the shadow-cause as the truth of the śāstra, and explains that Rāhu is present at eclipses by Brahmā's boon to receive the offerings, so that the name is used figuratively (BS 5.1–15).
