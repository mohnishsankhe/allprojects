## Definitions
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): Empty of the separable defilements; not empty of the inseparable buddha-qualities (Śrīmālā 9; RGV 1.154-155); the Mahāparinirvāṇa's wise see both the empty and the non-empty.
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): The locus classicus of other-emptiness: the element is empty of separable stains and not empty of inseparable qualities — read as definitive.
