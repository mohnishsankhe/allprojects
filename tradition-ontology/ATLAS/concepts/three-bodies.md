## Definitions
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Gross (quintuplicated elements, waking), subtle (seventeen members, dream) and causal (ignorance, deep sleep) bodies; the self is none of them.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): The soul is covered by the physical, astral and causal bodies, which it leaves behind at the eye centre, Sahasdal Kanwal/Trikuṭī and Daswān Dwār respectively.
- [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md): The Bahvṛca Upaniṣad (4): Mahātripurasundarī pervades the three cities, which are the three bodies, and illumines them outside and within as the inward consciousness; the Sahasranāma names her as the waker, dreamer and sleeper and as the fourth (LSN 62-63).
