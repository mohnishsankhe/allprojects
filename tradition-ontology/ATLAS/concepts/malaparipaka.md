# The ripening of mala (malaparipāka)

`cpt:malaparipaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** karma-rebirth

## Names

## Definitions
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Āṇava mala, one with many powers, ripens through embodied experience and Śiva's concealing power until it is ready to be removed; then śaktipāta.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Malaparipākam, with iruviṉaiyoppu, is the condition for the descent of grace.
