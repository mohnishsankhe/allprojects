## Definitions
- [Svātantrika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/svatantrika.md): Appearances that function as they appear are the correct conventional; those that do not (a mirage as water) the incorrect (Jñānagarbha); the correct conventional is the ladder to reality (MH 3.12).
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): Conventional things satisfy only when unexamined, arise and cease, and can function (MAL 64).
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Candrakīrti distinguishes the correct and incorrect conventional only from the world's standpoint; for the noble ones all of it is merely conventional (MA 6.25–28, from memory).
