## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): One who falls from yoga is not lost: he dwells in the worlds of the meritorious, is reborn among the pure and prosperous or in a family of yogins, recovers his former understanding and, perfected through many births, reaches the highest goal (BhG 6.37–45).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Arjuna asks what becomes of one with faith who does not strive enough and whose mind falls from yoga before perfection — does he perish like a torn cloud, fallen from both (6.37–39)? Kṛṣṇa: there is no destruction for him here or hereafter, for no doer of good goes to a bad end (6.40); he dwells for endless years in the worlds of the meritorious and is reborn in a pure and prosperous house (6.41), or — rarer — in a family of wise yogins (6.42); there he regains the understanding of his former body and strives again (6.43), carried on by his former practice even against his will; even the mere enquirer into yoga passes beyond śabda-brahman (6.44); striving, cleansed, perfected through many births, he reaches the highest course (6.45).
