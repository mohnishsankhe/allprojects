## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): One who falls from yoga is not lost: he dwells in the worlds of the meritorious, is reborn among the pure and prosperous or in a family of yogins, recovers his former understanding and, perfected through many births, reaches the highest goal (BhG 6.37–45).
