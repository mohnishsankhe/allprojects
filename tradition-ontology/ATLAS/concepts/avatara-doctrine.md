## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The Lord (or his Puruṣa form) descends age after age in innumerable forms to protect the world; all are portions and parts except Kṛṣṇa, Bhagavān himself (BhP 1.3); in the Śaiva and Śākta Purāṇas Śiva and the Goddess likewise appear repeatedly ('whenever trouble arises I will descend', DM 11).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Rūpa's classification: svayaṃ-rūpa, tad-ekātma-rūpa (vilāsa, svāṃśa), āveśa; puruṣa, guṇa, līlā, manvantara, yuga and śaktyāveśa avatāras; Kṛṣṇa is their source.
