## Definitions
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The Sant verses often name woman with gold as māyā's snare (kanak-kāminī) and warn the renunciant against her; yet women Sants (Sahajobāī, Dayābāī; Mīrā is claimed as Ravidās's disciple) taught with authority, and Kabīr speaks as the bride of Rām.
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): Every woman is Rādhā in her svarūpa; the female partner is revered and may be the guru of the practice.
- [Kartābhajā](../lineages/kartabhaja.md): Satī Mā led the community and is venerated as the Mother.
