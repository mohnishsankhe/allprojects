## Definitions
- [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md): Nāda is twofold (ŚR 1.2.3). The unstruck is worshipped by sages by the guru's path as an easy means to liberation but has no charm for people; the struck, made into music through śrutis and notes, delights the world and breaks worldly existence (ŚR 1.2.163–167).
