## Definitions
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The aggregate of twenty-four in which action, fruit, knowledge, pleasure and pain rest; born of delusion, desire, aversion and karma; endless while joined with rajas and tamas, ceasing with the increase of sattva (Ca Śā 1.35-38, 1.53).
