## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Knowledge of truth arises from repeated practice of a particular samādhi (NS 4.2.38), which is possible through prior practice despite intruding objects and hunger (4.2.39–41); yoga is to be practised in forests, caves and sandbanks (4.2.42); for liberation one purifies the self by yama and niyama and by yoga and the method of the inner self — austerity, breath-control, withdrawal, meditation, fixation, and repeated contemplation of objects (4.2.46; NBh); with study, reflection and discussion (4.2.47–49).
