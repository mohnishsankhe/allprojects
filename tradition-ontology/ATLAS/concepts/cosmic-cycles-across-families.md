## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Four ages (kṛta, tretā, dvāpara, kali) making a mahāyuga; a day of Brahmā (kalpa) and its night of dissolution.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Endless alternation of ascending and descending half-cycles (utsarpiṇī, avasarpiṇī), each of six periods, in Bharata and Airāvata.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Aeons (kalpa) of formation, duration, destruction and emptiness; the great aeon made of eighty intermediate aeons.
