## Definitions
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Relatives within the sapiṇḍa circle are impure for ten days after a death (MDh 5.59) or for periods graded by class (5.83); infants under two are buried without water-offerings (MDh 5.68; YS 3.1).
