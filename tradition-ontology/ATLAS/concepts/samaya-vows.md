## Definitions
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Pledges taken at consecration — the root and gross downfalls, the pledges of the five families, the vidyādhara vow on top of the prātimokṣa and bodhisattva vows; broken pledges bring the vajra hell.
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): Tilopa: the vow is the non-conceptual meaning; guarding vows with concepts degenerates from it; Dhūmapa: the supreme vow is not clinging to one's own realization.
