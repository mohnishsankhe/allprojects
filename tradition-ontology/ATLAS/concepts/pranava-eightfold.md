## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Om is the designator of Viṣṇu and has eight components, from 'a' to 'atiśānta', naming eight of his forms (Viśva, Taijasa, Prājña, Turīya, Ātman, Antarātman, Paramātman, Jñānātman); the other mantras derive from it.
