## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The breaths are the primordial seers who made the one Person, Prajāpati (ŚB 6.1.1.1); breath is the consecrated (KB 7.1); the one god is the breath (ŚB 11.6.3); in sleep the breaths enter the self (ŚB 10.5.2.20); the Ṛgvedic seers are forms of the breath (AA 2.2.1); the breaths are counted as five (prāṇa, apāna, vyāna, udāna, samāna) and sometimes as seven in the head.
