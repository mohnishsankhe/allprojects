## Definitions
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Āgama and śilpa texts treat the temple (prāsāda) as the body of the deity, its parts named as limbs.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Pāñcarātra saṃhitās likewise identify the temple with Bhagavān's body.
- [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md): Kerala tantra treats the temple and its installed deity as a living body whose power the tantri maintains.
