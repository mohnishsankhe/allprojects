## Definitions
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): Couplets (dohā) and rāga-set songs (caryā) in Apabhraṃśa and Old Bengali, signed by the siddha-poet, teach the innate in riddling images.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The Hindi sabadīs and pads ascribed to Gorakh and the Bengali Nāth song-cycles (Gopīcandra, Mayanāmatī) teach the yoga of the body in the same riddling register.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Kabīr's sākhīs (couplets) and pads continue the form, with the upside-down speech (ulaṭbāṃsī).
