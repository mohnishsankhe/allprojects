## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The Namaskāra mantra is held eternal and the essence of the scriptures; its shorter forms (16, 6, 5, 4, 2, 1 syllables — e.g. 'a-si-ā-u-sā', 'arhaṃ', 'oṃ' analysed as the five initials) are recited and meditated on by the teacher's instruction (Dravyasaṃgraha 49; YŚ 8). Word-based meditation places letters on lotuses in the body. Protective hymns (Uvasaggaharaṃ, Bhaktāmara, Kalyāṇamandira, Ṛṣimaṇḍala) are recited with associated yantras and mantras.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The Sūrimantra, given orally to ācāryas at installation, and the Vardhamāna-vidyā; worship of the Siddhacakra (nine padas).
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Mantra manuals of the goddesses Padmāvatī and Jvālāmālinī (Malliṣeṇa, Indranandi) for protection and worldly ends; the Jñānārṇava condemns using mantra and meditation to harm others.
