## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The eight powers Aṇimā and the rest are deities of the outermost enclosure, the gate where the gods won their powers (SL 93) and rays of the Goddess's own body (SL 96); firm contemplation brings the power of command (PKS 1.13); meditation with the goddesses of speech brings poetry (SL 17) and on the kāmakalā the power to attract (SL 19); the sixty-four tantras aim at particular powers, while the Goddess's tantra accomplishes all aims (SL 31); those who grasp the mantra without lineage are harmed by its rays (YH 2.81).
