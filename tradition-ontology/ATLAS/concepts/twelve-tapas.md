## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Six external — fasting, eating less, limiting the alms-round, giving up tasty food, lonely lodging, bodily mortification — and six internal — expiation, humility, service, study, abandonment, meditation (TS 9.19–20); austerity both stops and sheds karma (9.3).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The Śvetāmbara list has saṃlīnatā (withdrawal) for lonely lodging and places meditation before abandonment (Uttarādhyayana 30).
