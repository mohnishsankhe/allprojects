# Liṅga-initiation

**Category:** teacher-transmission

## Names
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): dīkṣā

## Definitions
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The guru bathes the disciple with consecrated water, whispers the five-syllable mantra secretly into his right ear, installs a liṅga in the disciple's breath and places it in his hand, to be worn for life; dīkṣā gives knowledge and destroys the fetters and removes the duality of guru and disciple.
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): Initiation is open to all — women, children and every occupation; Allama received the liṅga from the yogi Animiṣa.
