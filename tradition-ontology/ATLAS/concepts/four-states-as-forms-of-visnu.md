## Definitions
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Dvaita reads the Māṇḍūkya's four quarters as four forms of the Supreme Self in all beings: Viśva presiding over waking, Taijasa creating dreams in the mind, Prājña lord of deep sleep, and Turīya at the dvādaśānta, attained by the liberated. The soul experiences the states; God governs them.
