## Definitions
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The metres (gāyatrī, triṣṭubh, jagatī, anuṣṭubh and others) were born from the Puruṣa sacrifice (RV 10.90.9) and are recorded for every verse in the Anukramaṇīs.
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): Metres defined by syllable count and feet — gāyatrī (24), uṣṇih (28), anuṣṭubh (32), bṛhatī (36), paṅkti (40), triṣṭubh (44), jagatī (48) — described by Piṅgala with light/heavy syllables and three-syllable groups; the gods covered themselves with them against death (ChU 1.4.2); in the Brāhmaṇas each metre corresponds to a god, a class, a season and an animal (TS 7.1.1.4–6).
