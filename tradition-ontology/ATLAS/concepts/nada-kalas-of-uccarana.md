## Definitions
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Bindu, ardhacandra, rodhinī, nāda, nādānta, śakti, vyāpikā, samanā and unmanā, each half the length of the one before, unmanā being timeless (Varivasyārahasya with Prakāśa).
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): After a, u, m the utterance of the praṇava passes through bindu, ardhacandra, nirodhikā, nāda, nādānta, śakti, vyāpinī, samanā and unmanā, each subtler and shorter, sound dissolving into the soundless.
