## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Kṛṣṇa taught of old a twofold commitment (niṣṭhā): by jñānayoga for the sāṃkhyas and by karmayoga for the yogins (3.3); freedom from action is not reached by not acting, nor perfection by renunciation alone (3.4). Cf. the understanding 'according to sāṃkhya' and 'according to yoga' (2.39).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: the childish, not the learned, speak of Sāṃkhya and Yoga as separate; one properly established in even one of them obtains the fruit of both; the place reached by the followers of Sāṃkhya is reached by those of Yoga too, and whoever sees Sāṃkhya and Yoga as one truly sees (5.4–5). In context (5.1–6; cf. 3.3) Sāṃkhya stands with the renunciation of actions and knowledge, Yoga with the yoga of action.
