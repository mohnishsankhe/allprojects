## Definitions
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The twofold Bhairava tantra of the southern face (Kāmika: 'twofold'): the Mantrapīṭha centred on male Bhairava forms such as Svacchanda, and the Vidyāpīṭha centred on goddesses and feminine mantras, subdivided into Yāmala and Śakti tantras.

## Relations (interpretation layer)

_Notes: The Kāmika says only that the Bhairava tantra is 'twofold'; the names Mantrapīṭha/Vidyāpīṭha are from other scriptures (e.g. Jayadrathayāmala) and scholarly synthesis._
