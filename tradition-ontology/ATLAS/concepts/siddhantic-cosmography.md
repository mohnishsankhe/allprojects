## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Within the hollow egg of Brahmā the planetary orbits lie one below another (Saturn outermost, the Moon lowest); in the middle the earth-globe stands in space by Brahman's supporting power, with Meru through its axis, gods above and asuras below (SS 12.29–36); the earth needs no other support and draws heavy things to itself (Siddhānta Śiromaṇi, Gola). For the Jains the luminary gods circle Meru and make the divisions of time in the human region (TS 4.12–15).
