## Definitions
- [Rinzai Zen](../lineages/rinzai.md): The lower abdomen below the navel, where breath and energy are gathered in naikan; contemplated as one's original face and Pure Land (Hakuin).
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): Chan generally locates the ultimate in the mind, not in a subtle body; the Platform Sūtra treats the body as a city whose king is the nature.
