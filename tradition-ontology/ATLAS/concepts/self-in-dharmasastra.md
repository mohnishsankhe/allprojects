## Definitions
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The self is to be seen by yoga (YS 1.8), is its own witness (MDh 8.84), is distinguished as knower of the field, elemental self and jīva (MDh 12.12–13), is one in all like space in pots or the sun in waters (YS 3.144), and is the one called Agni, Manu, Prajāpati, Indra, breath and Brahman (MDh 12.123); self-knowledge is highest (MDh 12.85); nothing is higher than attaining the self (ĀpDh 1.8.22.2).
