## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Complete release from karma through the absence of the causes of bondage and through shedding (10.2); the states cease except perfect right view, knowledge, perception and siddhatva (10.3-10.4).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Release from all karma through the absence of the causes of bondage and through shedding (TS 10.2); it follows omniscience and the stopping of all activity; the liberated do not return.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The self conquered by passions is saṃsāra; the same self conquering them is liberation (YŚ 4.5).
