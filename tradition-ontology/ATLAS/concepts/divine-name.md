## Definitions
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): The name ('Viṭṭhala', 'Rāma Kṛṣṇa Hari') is the easy means for all, needing no rule of time, place or caste; sung together in kīrtan it lights the lamp of knowledge.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): Rāma's name is greater than both his formless and embodied forms, mediates between them, is the one support in Kali and the ferrying mantra at death in Kāśī.
- [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md): Kṛṣṇa's name is the first of the four real things; it saves even when uttered unknowingly (Ajāmila) and removes offences when sung constantly.
- [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md): Do not be lazy with Hari's name; death is ever at hand.
- [Mīrābāī's devotion (the Mīrā song-tradition)](../lineages/mirabai.md): The name is the jewel-wealth that spending does not lessen and thieves cannot steal.
- [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md): Rāma's name at dawn, in mind and speech, is the beginning of the day's discipline.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The name 'Nārāyaṇa' is the refuge found after long searching; it gives family, wealth, heaven, grace and strength, more good than a mother; its sweetness is worth more than Indra's kingdom; singing it, past and future faults burn like cotton in fire; by it the senses are guarded and Yama's servants trampled.
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Being sung of, the Lord quickly manifests himself and makes his devotees experience him; hearing and singing his qualities are means to devotion even in worldly life.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The Lord himself whispered the eight-syllable mantra into Tirumaṅkai's ear (the tradition's account).
