## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The one Lord is present as the supreme (para) in Vaikuṇṭha, the four vyūhas, the descents (vibhava), the inner controller (antaryāmin) and the consecrated image (arcā), in ascending order of accessibility; the arcā is the most accessible.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Bhagavān exists as the supreme (para), the emanations (vyūha), the descents (vibhava), the inner controller (antaryāmin) and the consecrated image (arcā).
