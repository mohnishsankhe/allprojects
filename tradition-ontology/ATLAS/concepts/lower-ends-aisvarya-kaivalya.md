## Definitions
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Wealth, lordly power (aiśvarya) and even kaivalya are ends the Lord can grant but lesser than him; the wise devotee seeks nothing but the Lord (ananya-prayojana); seeking other ends is an obstacle.
