## Definitions
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Thirty-two (or 72,000) channels with three principal — lalanā (left, moon), rasanā (right, sun), avadhūtī (centre) — and four (Hevajra) or six (Kālacakra) cakras; winds as the mount of mind; white and red drops; the yoga of drawing the winds into the central channel.
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): The dohās speak of wind and mind ceasing where sun and moon do not enter (People Dohā v26–27) and of the three channels as bellows and forge (Kamparipa).
