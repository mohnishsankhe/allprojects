## Definitions
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The substrate of movement, senses and experience (NS 1.1.11), earthy (NS 3.1.27), produced by past karma (NS 3.2.60).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Bodies of earth (ours), water (Varuṇa's world), fire (Āditya's world) and air (Vāyu's world); some beings are not womb-born (VS 4.2.9 C; Tarkasaṅgraha); breath is air moving in the body, one but named prāṇa, apāna etc. by adjuncts.
