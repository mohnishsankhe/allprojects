## Definitions
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Vasumitra: nine Mahāsāṃghika and eleven Sthavira groups arising in the second to fourth centuries after the parinirvāṇa.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Dīpavaṃsa/Mahāvaṃsa: eighteen (twelve Theravāda-derived, six Mahāsaṅgītika-derived) plus six later.
- [Mūlasarvāstivāda](../lineages/mulasarvastivada.md): Vinītadeva/Tibetan tradition: eighteen in four groups — Sarvāstivāda, Mahāsāṃghika, Saṃmitīya, Sthavira.
- [Pudgalavāda](../lineages/pudgalavada.md): Bhavya records a list according to the Saṃmitīyas (details not given here).
