## Definitions
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Unconventional conduct in cremation grounds, among outcastes, with ornaments of bone, 'mad' behaviour and equal vision — undertaken after warmth or realization, as a hidden observance.
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): Only after realizing equality (Mekopa); the unrealized who act so are like a kingdom seized by its subjects (Nāgārjuna's song); the Queen Dohā describes it and also warns against base conduct.
