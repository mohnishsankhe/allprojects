## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The horā reveals the ripening of the karma, good and bad, earned in a former birth (BJ 1.3); the science shows it as a lamp shows objects in the dark (LJ 1.3); Janārdana in the form of the planets gives beings the fruits of their karma (BPHS 2.3); sins of former births appear as childlessness and other afflictions (BPHS 83).
