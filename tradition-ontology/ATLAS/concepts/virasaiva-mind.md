## Definitions
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The mind is a mirror soiled by ignorance that the guru cleanses; serenity of mind is grace; outer impressions (vāsanā) project the world, inner ones reveal the self's bliss.
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): Māyā is not gold, woman or land but the desire standing before the mind.
