## Definitions
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The jīva is a part of the formless Śiva through beginningless ignorance, with Śiva abiding in it as fire in the sunstone; the self is eternal and distinct from body, senses and intellect (against Cārvāka and Bauddha), atomic through contact with impurity, yet in truth shining like a lamp in the heart.
