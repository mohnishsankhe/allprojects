## Definitions
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Selves are many because their states are distinct and on the authority of scripture (VS 3.2.15–17 C).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Individual selves are distinct in each body; the Lord is one (Tarkasaṅgraha).
