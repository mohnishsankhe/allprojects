## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Seven groups taught for the long endurance of the holy life: the four establishments of mindfulness, four right efforts, four bases of power, five faculties, five powers, seven factors of awakening and the noble eightfold path (DN 16; MN 149; SN 43).
