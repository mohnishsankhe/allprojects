## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The main division into Śvetāmbara and Digambara (with the extinct Yāpanīya between them), turning on monastic nudity, women's liberation, the omniscient's eating, the canon and details of Mahāvīra's life; within the Śvetāmbaras, the image-worshipping Mūrtipūjakas and the aniconic Sthānakavāsīs and Terāpanthīs; within the Digambaras, the Bīsapantha, Terāpantha and Tāraṇapantha.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The Digambaras arose from Śivabhūti's Boṭika schism, 609 years after the nirvāṇa.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The Śvetāmbaras arose from monks who stayed in the north during Bhadrabāhu's famine and adopted clothing.
