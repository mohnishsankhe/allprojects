## Names

## Definitions
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The resolve for awakening for all beings, born with compassion and non-dual understanding (MA 1.1); an elixir transforming the body (BCA 1.10); aspiring and engaging (BCA 1.15–16); relative and ultimate.
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): The second of the three points after compassion (Bhāvanākrama I).
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): Generated after refuge and love on seeing beings' suffering; the aspiring mind must be held by the engaging vow.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The only door to the Mahāyāna.
