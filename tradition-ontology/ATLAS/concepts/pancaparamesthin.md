## Definitions
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Arhats (embodied omniscients), Siddhas (liberated), Ācāryas (heads of orders), Upādhyāyas (teachers) and Sādhus (ascetics), honoured in the Namaskāra mantra; together they have 108 qualities (12 + 8 + 36 + 25 + 27). The Jina is worshipped as the model of qualities to be attained, not as a giver of grace (Sarvārthasiddhi maṅgala).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Arhats, Siddhas, Ācāryas, Upādhyāyas and Sādhus, saluted in the Namaskāra that opens the Bhagavatī (Śvetāmbara) and the Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama (Digambara); the salutation is to qualities and ranks, not to named individuals.
