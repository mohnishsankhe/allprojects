# Destiny (niyativāda)

`cpt:niyativada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** karma-rebirth

## Names
- [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md): niyati

## Definitions
- [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md): (As reported) There is no cause or condition for defilement or purification, no effort; beings are transformed by destiny, circumstance and nature; all things are fixed.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): A tenet that ends in inaction; the doctrine of cause (hetu) is the safe course; Makkhali's doctrine is the worst of all.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): One-sided niyati is a wrong view; niyati with time, own-nature, deeds and effort is right view.
