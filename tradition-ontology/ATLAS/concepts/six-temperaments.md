## Definitions
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Vism III p. 101-114: the six temperaments (cariyā) are the greedy, the hating, the deluded, the faithful, the intelligent and the speculative (fourteen counted by some with combinations, but in brief six). Faith is near-kin to greed, understanding to hate, speculation to delusion. The text gives five means of discerning temperament (posture, work, eating, seeing, the arising of states), then what suits each (lodging and the rest), and which of the forty subjects suit each; it cautions that its discernment of marks is by the teachers' opinion and not essential (p. 107), and that the assignment of subjects is by direct opposition and extreme suitability (p. 114).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Greedy, hating, deluded, faithful, intelligent and speculative; each has suitable subjects, dwellings and postures; faith parallels greed, intelligence hatred, speculation delusion.
- [Abhayagiri fraternity (Abhayagiri-vāsins)](../lineages/abhayagiri.md): The Vimuttimagga counts fourteen kinds of behaviour including mixed types (as reported).
