## Definitions
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Prosperity from pure food taken with restraint and recitation; taint lies in injury (VS 6.1.8–12, 6.2.6–10 C); the life-stages yield merit according to purity of intention (6.2.3–5); ablution, fasting, celibacy, residence with the teacher, forest-dwelling, sacrifice, giving and observances for the unseen (6.2.2); common duties such as non-injury, truthfulness and non-stealing (Praśastapāda).
