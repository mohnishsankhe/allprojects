# Union of liṅga and aṅga (liṅgāṅga-sāmarasya, aikya)

## Names

- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): sāmarasya

## Definitions
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Liberation as the soul's immersion in the ocean of Śiva's bliss, free of longing for sense-pleasure; the self contemplated as dissolved in the sky of consciousness by the nature of sāmarasya; 'like water in water' in common exposition.
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): Death of the devotee is itself called liṅgaikya: Basava at Kūḍalasaṅgama, Allama and Akka at Kadaḷi.
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): In Śrīpati's Śakti-viśiṣṭādvaita the soul (aṅga) qualified by devotion and Śiva (liṅga) qualified by Śakti attain union in equal essence; the non-duality is real, not an appearance.
