- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): The soul (āṉmā, uyir) plays the body's play and leaves it to wither (Kaṭuveḷi 21); the house of nine openings is not 'I' (Pattirakiriyār 39); the seeker asks 'who am I, who are you' (225) and longs to stand as 'I am He' (219); to know oneself is to join the Lord (Kuṭampai 31).
