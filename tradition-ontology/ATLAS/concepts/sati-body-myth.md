## Definitions
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): After Satī's death Śiva carried her corpse; Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śani entered it and dropped its parts across the land; where each fell Śiva stayed as a liṅga and the Goddess is worshipped (Kālikā Purāṇa 18.36-50). Later versions have Viṣṇu cut the body with his discus and count 51 or 52 places.
