## Definitions
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): BhP 1.3 lists twenty-two by ordinal; BhP 2.7 adds others (Hayagrīva, Haṃsa, Hari who saved Gajendra, the Manvantara-avatāras); the Agni (2-16, 49) and Garuḍa (1.142) teach ten (Matsya, Kūrma, Varāha, Narasiṃha, Vāmana, Paraśurāma, Rāma, Kṛṣṇa or Balarāma, Buddha, Kalki); a count of twenty-four is common in later tradition (its membership varies and no single Bhāgavata verse gives it).
