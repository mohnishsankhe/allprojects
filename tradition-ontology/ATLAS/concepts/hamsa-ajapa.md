## Definitions
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The haṃsa dwells in the bodies of breathing beings as the knot of prāṇa and apāna and is repeated 21,600 times a day, called so 'ham (Yogaviṣaya 28–29).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): 'Haṃsa haṃsa' is to be said always in the body (KJN 13.2); the jīva is the haṃsa (6.5) moving up and down and dissolving at the end of the twelve (17.18).
