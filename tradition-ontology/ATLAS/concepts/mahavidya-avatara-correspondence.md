## Definitions
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Toḍala 10.9-11 identifies the Mahāvidyās with Viṣṇu's avatāras — Tārā the Fish, Bagalā the Tortoise, Dhūmāvatī the Boar, Chinnamastā Nṛsiṃha, Bhuvaneśvarī Vāmana, Mātaṅgī Rāma, Tripurā Paraśurāma, Bhairavī Balabhadra, Mahālakṣmī the Buddha, Durgā Kalki, Kālī Kṛṣṇa — and promises equality with Mahādeva to their worshipper.
