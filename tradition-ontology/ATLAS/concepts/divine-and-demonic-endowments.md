## Definitions
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Two kinds of beings: the divine endowment (fearlessness, purity, non-harm, truth, compassion and the rest) leads to liberation; the demonic (hypocrisy, pride, anger, harshness, ignorance) to bondage; the demonic deny a Lord and live for desire (BhG ch. 16).
