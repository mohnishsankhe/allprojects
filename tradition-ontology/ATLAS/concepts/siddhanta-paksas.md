## Definitions
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Siddhānta astronomy runs in schools with their own constants: the Āryapakṣa (Āryabhaṭa, Bhāskara I, Lalla, Kerala), the Brāhmapakṣa (Brahmagupta, Śrīpati, Bhāskara II), the Saurapakṣa (Sūrya Siddhānta), the Ārdharātrika (midnight) school, and later the Gaṇeśapakṣa (Grahalāghava); Varāhamihira already ranked the five early siddhāntas (PS 1.4).
