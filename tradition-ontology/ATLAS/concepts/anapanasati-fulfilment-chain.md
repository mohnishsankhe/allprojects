## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): MN 118:15 announces and MN 118:16-43 explains a chain: mindfulness of breathing, developed and cultivated, fulfils the four establishments of mindfulness (via the four tetrads, 23-28); these fulfil the seven awakening factors (29-40); these fulfil knowledge and liberation (41-43).

## Relations (interpretation layer)
- leads-to → [The four establishments of mindfulness (cattāro satipaṭṭhānā)](four-satipatthanas.md): mindfulness of breathing fulfils the four establishments (tetrad 1 = body, 2 = feelings, 3 = mind, 4 = dhammas) — rests on [mn118:15](../texts/anapanasati-sutta.md#tea-anapanasati-sutta-mn118-15), [mn118:28](../texts/anapanasati-sutta.md#tea-anapanasati-sutta-mn118-28)
