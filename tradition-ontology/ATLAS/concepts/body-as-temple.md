# The body as temple

`cpt:body-as-temple` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** body-energy

## Names

## Definitions
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): 'The body is a temple and the jīva in it is Śiva' (Maitreya 2.1; Skanda 10); the Yogaśikhā calls it Śiva's or Viṣṇu's temple; the Bhāvanā makes the body the Śrīcakra.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): For the Siddhars the Lord has made the body his temple (TM 725); the heart is the sanctum and the flesh the shrine (TM 1823); temple and sacred tank are within the mind (Sivavākkiyar) — hence both the care of the body and the scorn for stone temples.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The body is the true mosque and temple; service and worship are within (Dādū); the offering is body and mind (Ravidās).
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): The original Mecca is in this human body.
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The body is the temple of the God (Kulārṇava 9.41); the guru knows the unity of the body and the cosmos (13.88).
