## Definitions
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Earth (solidity), water (cohesion), fire (heat), air (motion), internal and external, seen as 'not mine' (MN 28; MN 62); the four vipers (SN 35.238).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): In the Nāropa texts the elements are known through their dissolution: earth into water, water into fire, fire into wind, wind into consciousness at death and in yoga; inner heat does not progress unless the 'equal taste of the four elements' is known.

## Relations (interpretation layer)
