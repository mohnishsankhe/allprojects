## Definitions
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Powers arise with yoga: distant seeing, hearing, thinking, knowing and omniscience (power of knowing); swiftness of mind, taking forms at will, acting without organs (power of acting); and eight marks of perfection - subject to none, not to be entered, not to be slain, fearless, imperishable, unaging, deathless, unobstructed. They arise gradually after six months of constant yoga and make the yogin a chief of Mahādeva's troops. The texts present them as signs and as part of liberation, not as dangers.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The Skanda Purāṇa tells of Pāśupata siddhas who went to Śiva's abode in the same body.
- [Kāpālika (Somasiddhānta)](../lineages/kapalika.md): Hostile dramas attribute flight and power over gods and heavens to Kāpālikas.
