# Mahāsthāmaprāpta

`tch:mahasthamaprapta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Dashizhi 大勢至, Seishi
**Lineages:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md), [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md), [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md)
**Historicity:** mythic

The bodhisattva of great strength, attendant of Amitābha with Avalokiteśvara; object of the eleventh contemplation of the Contemplation Sūtra; in the Śūraṅgama his penetration is by recollection of the Buddha.

_Notes: U43 contribution: Hōnen was regarded in his circle as a manifestation of Seishi (Eshinni's dream); Shinran composed hymns on Seishi's penetration through recollection._
