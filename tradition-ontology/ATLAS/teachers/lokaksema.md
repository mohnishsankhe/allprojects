Kuṣāṇa monk in Luoyang (active c. 167-186) who made the first Chinese translations of Mahāyāna sūtras: the 8,000-line Perfection of Wisdom (Daoxing, 179), the Pratyutpanna-samādhi, the Akṣobhyavyūha, the Kāśyapaparivarta, the Śūraṅgamasamādhi (lost) and others.
