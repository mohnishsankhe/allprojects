# Lakṣmaṇa

`tch:laksmana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md), [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Rāma (Dāśarathi)](rama.md)

Rāma's brother: recipient of Rāma's teaching on māyā, knowledge and devotion (Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa 3.4) and of the Rāma Gītā (7.5); in the Rāmcaritmānas he himself teaches Guha (the Lakṣmaṇa Gītā).
