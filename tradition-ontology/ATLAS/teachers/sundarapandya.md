# Sundarapāṇḍya

`tch:sundarapandya` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Lineages:** [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md)

Early author to whom later commentators attribute the three verses quoted at the end of Śaṅkara's BSBh 1.1.4 (that once 'I am the existent Brahman' is known, there is nothing further to be done, and the knower-status lasts only until the self is known).
