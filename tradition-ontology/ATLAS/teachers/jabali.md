# Jābāli

`tch:jabali` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Historicity:** legendary

A brāhmaṇa counsellor who urges Rāma, with arguments that deny the other world, to return and rule; Rāma rejects this, and Vasiṣṭha says Jābāli spoke only to bring Rāma back (Rāmāyaṇa 2.108–110).
