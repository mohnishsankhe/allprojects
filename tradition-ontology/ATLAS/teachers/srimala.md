# Śrīmālā

`tch:srimala` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Śrīmālādevī, Shengman 勝鬘, Shōman
**Lineages:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md)
**Historicity:** legendary

Queen of Ayodhyā, daughter of king Prasenajit and Mallikā, who in the Śrīmālā sūtra teaches the one vehicle and the tathāgatagarbha with the Buddha's confirmation; she receives the prediction that she will become the buddha Samantaprabha.
**Realization — the tradition's account (as a figure of the sūtras):** Predicted to buddhahood as Samantaprabha (Śrīmālā ch. 1)
