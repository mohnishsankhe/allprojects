# Māṉakkañcāṟa Nāyaṉār

`tch:manakkancarar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Kañcāṟūr
