# Meypporuḷ Nāyaṉār

`tch:meypporul` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Tirukkōvalūr
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. A chieftain stabbed by his enemy Muttanātaṉ, who had come disguised as a Śaiva ascetic; dying, he ordered his guard Tattaṉ to escort the assassin safely out, because he wore Śiva's garb.
