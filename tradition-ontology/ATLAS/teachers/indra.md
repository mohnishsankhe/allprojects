# Indra (as student and teacher)

`tch:indra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Maghavan
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Teachers:** [Prajāpati (as teacher)](prajapati.md), [Umā Haimavatī](uma-haimavati.md)
**Students:** [Pratardana Daivodāsi](pratardana-daivodasi.md)

Lord of the gods: lives 101 years as Prajāpati's student and alone goes beyond the body-self (ChU 8.7-12); in the Kena he meets Umā Haimavatī and first knows the yakṣa as brahman (Kena 3.11-4.3); teaches Pratardana 'I am breath, the intelligent self' (KauU 3).
