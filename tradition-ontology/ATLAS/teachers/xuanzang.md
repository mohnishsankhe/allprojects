Chinese pilgrim-translator who studied the Yogācārabhūmi at Nālandā under Śīlabhadra, won a celebrated debate at Kanyākubja under King Harṣa, and on his return translated the Yogācāra corpus and compiled the Cheng weishi lun, founding Faxiang.
