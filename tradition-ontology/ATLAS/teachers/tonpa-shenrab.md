Founder of Yungdrung Bön. Tradition: a fully enlightened teacher who descended from the heavens and was born prince of Ölmo Lungring in Tagzig; he taught Bön, pursued into Tibet the demon Khyapa Lagring who had stolen his horses, taught there and returned; his deeds are told in the Dodü, the Zermig and the Zijid.
