Tamil Śaiva poet-saint (early 18th c.) whose songs join Siddhānta and Vedānta in a 'harmony' (camaracam), extol the silent guru and stillness (cummā iruttal), honour the Siddhars, and pray that all beings be happy. Named after Śiva as 'He who became the mother' at the Tiruchirappalli rock temple.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** An accountant-minister at the Nāyak court of Tiruchirappalli, he met the silent guru (Mauṉa kuru) of Tirumūlar's line, who told him 'be still' (cummā iru); he later left the court, married at his guru's bidding, and after his wife's death lived as a renouncer until his samādhi near Rāmanātapuram.
