# Pañcādhikaraṇa

`tch:pancadhikarana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Historicity:** historical

Sāṃkhya teacher known from the Yuktidīpikā: the organs are elemental (bhautika); the instrument is by nature empty, like a deserted village or dry river bed, its knowledges coming from pradhāna; all transformation of the organs is 'from another'; a permanent conveying (ātivāhika) subtle body enters the embryo; knowledge is natural (at the same time as the principles, innate, or flowing) or acquired. Named in the Yuktidīpikā's list of teachers through whom the doctrine passed between Pañcaśikha and Īśvarakṛṣṇa (on SK 71: Hārīta, Vāddhali, Kairāta, Paurika, Ṛṣabheśvara, Pañcādhikaraṇa, Patañjali, Vārṣagaṇya, Kauṇḍinya, Mūka and others).
