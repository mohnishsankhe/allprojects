# Hiraṇyagarbha

`tch:hiranyagarbha` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Pitāmaha (Brahmā) as first teacher of yoga
**Lineages:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md), [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md), [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Students:** [Patañjali](patanjali.md)

The tradition's founder of yoga. The Yoga commentators (Vācaspati, Vijñānabhikṣu, Rāmānanda Sarasvatī, Nāgeśa) name Hiraṇyagarbha as the original teacher and treat Patañjali's śāstra as a re-teaching; the Mahābhārata's Nārāyaṇīya likewise pairs Kapila as the teacher of Sāṃkhya with Hiraṇyagarbha as the knower of yoga.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The first and most ancient teacher of yoga: 'Hiraṇyagarbha is the speaker of yoga, no other is ancient' (verse cited by Vijñānabhikṣu from the Yoga-Yājñavalkya, YV 1.1). Commentators read 'anuśāsana' in YS 1.1 as Patañjali's re-teaching of the yoga Hiraṇyagarbha taught; Nārāyaṇatīrtha: 'Pitāmaha taught yoga, then Śeṣa'.
