# Huike 慧可 (Shenguang)

**Alternate names:** Shenguang 神光, Eka (Jp.)
**Lineages:** [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: 487–593; Scholarly account: 6th c.; (confidence moderate)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Bodhidharma (Putidamo 菩提達磨)](bodhidharma.md)
**Students:** [Sengcan 僧璨](sengcan.md), [Na Chanshi 那禪師 and Huiman 慧滿](nazhan.md)

Second Chinese patriarch. In the tradition's account he stood in the snow and cut off his own arm to show his resolve, asked Bodhidharma to pacify his mind and was told 'bring me the mind'; the earliest biography (Xu gaoseng zhuan) says bandits cut off his arm and that Bodhidharma gave him the four-fascicle Laṅkāvatāra.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** 'I have searched for the mind and cannot find it.' — 'I have pacified your mind for you.' (Jingde chuandeng lu juan 3; Wumenguan case 41).
