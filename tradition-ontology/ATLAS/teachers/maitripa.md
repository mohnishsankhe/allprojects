U44 contribution: Indian scholar-siddha (c. 1007–1085), disciple of Śavaripa (and in some accounts of Nāropa), said to have been expelled from Vikramaśīla for keeping wine and a consort; author of the some twenty-one short works of the Advayavajrasaṃgraha (amanasikāra, the four seals, the Tattvaratnāvalī) and of a Vajrayoginī sādhana lineage; teacher of Marpa and of Vajrapāṇi and Sahajavajra.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Tibetan tradition holds that Maitrīpa rediscovered the Dharmadharmatāvibhāga and the Ratnagotravibhāga, hidden in a stūpa, after a vision of Maitreya.
