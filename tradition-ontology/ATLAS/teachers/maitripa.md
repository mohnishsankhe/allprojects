# Maitrīpa

`tch:maitripa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Advayavajra, Maitreyanātha (the siddha)
**Lineages:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), `lin:mahasiddha`, [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
**Historicity:** historical
**Works:** 
  - [Tattvaratnāvalī](../texts/tattvaratnavali.md) — attribution: accepted

U40 contribution: author of the Tattvaratnāvalī, whose doxography divides Madhyamaka into the illusion-like non-dualists and the proponents of the non-foundation of all dharmas, the latter being his own view.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Tibetan tradition holds that Maitrīpa rediscovered the Dharmadharmatāvibhāga and the Ratnagotravibhāga, hidden in a stūpa, after a vision of Maitreya.
