# the Vṛttikāra (cited by Śabara)

`tch:vrttikara-mimamsa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Vṛttikāra
**Lineages:** [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
**Historicity:** historical

An earlier commentator whose exposition Śabara reproduces on MS 1.1.3–5 ('the Vṛttikāra explained this passage otherwise'): definitions of perception, inference, verbal testimony, comparison, postulation and absence as means of knowledge, a refutation of the views that cognitions have no object and that all is empty, and a proof of the self.

_Notes: Identity disputed (Upavarṣa? Bodhāyana? Bhavadāsa?). Scholarly literature calls this passage the Vṛttikāragrantha._
