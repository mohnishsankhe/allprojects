Pontiff of the Sode maṭha (Uḍupi), worshipper of Hayagrīva, poet and polemicist: author of the Yuktimallikā, the Tīrthaprabandha, the Rukmiṇīśavijaya and a commentary on the Mahābhārata, and of Kannada songs signed 'Hayavadana'. The tradition credits him with making the Uḍupi paryāya of Kṛṣṇa worship a two-year turn.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The tradition regards Vādirāja as a ṛju soul — one of the class destined for the highest offices — and as a future Vāyu (bhāvi-samīra).
