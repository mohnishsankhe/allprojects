No. 44 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A peasant digging a mountainside with a hoe whom Śāntipa taught to dig the mountain of mind; he realized before his teacher did.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A peasant digging a mountainside with a hoe whom Śāntipa taught to dig the mountain of mind; he realized before his teacher did. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 44): All pleasure and pain arise from mind; by the guru's instruction dig the mountain of mind; the wise may dig mountains of earth but will not gain innate great bliss; ordinary knowing awakens in the centre of the heart and in the six collections bliss is unbroken; all imputations are meaningless causes of suffering; rest in the innate state beyond meditation and non-meditation.

_Notes: Life from Abhayadatta's Caturaśītisiddhapravṛtti recalled from memory (not read locally); the song is read locally in Tōh 2292. Uses the term tha mal shes pa ('ordinary knowing')._
