Commentator of Badaratittha (near Kāñcīpuram in South India) who wrote the Paramatthadīpanī on seven Khuddaka books, the Visuddhimagga sub-commentary Paramatthamañjūsā and sub-commentaries on the Nikāya commentaries.

_Notes: Whether the Khuddaka commentator and the ṭīkā author are one person is debated by scholars._
