# Īśvarakṛṣṇa

`tch:isvarakrsna` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 350–450 CE; (confidence moderate)
**Historicity:** historical
**Works:** 
  - [Sāṃkhya Kārikā](../texts/samkhya-karika.md) — attribution: accepted

Author of the Sāṃkhya Kārikā, who, 'having thoroughly understood the established doctrine handed down by a succession of pupils, summarized it in āryā verses' (SK 71). The Yuktidīpikā says he condensed the Ṣaṣṭitantra out of compassion for pupils of short life. An identification with Vindhyavāsin was proposed by some scholars and is not generally accepted.
