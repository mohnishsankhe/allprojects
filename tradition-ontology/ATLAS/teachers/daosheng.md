# Daosheng

`tch:daosheng` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Zhu Daosheng 竺道生
**Lineages:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 355-434 CE; (confidence high)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Kumārajīva](kumarajiva.md)

Chinese monk (c. 355-434), disciple of Kumārajīva, who taught that even icchantikas have buddha-nature and can become buddhas and that awakening is sudden, before the full Nirvāṇa Sūtra reached the south; expelled from the Jiankang saṅgha, he was vindicated when Dharmakṣema's version arrived containing that teaching.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Chinese accounts say that on Tiger Hill the stones nodded when he preached that icchantikas have buddha-nature
