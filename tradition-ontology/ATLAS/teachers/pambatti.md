# Pāmbāṭṭi (Pāmpāṭṭic cittar)

`tch:pambatti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Pāmpāṭṭic cittar
**Lineages:** [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: undetermined; Siddhar works are usually placed after c. 1400; (confidence low)
**Places:** Marudamalai, Caṅkaraṉkōyil — samādhi in the popular list
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Caṭṭaimuṉi (Sattaimuni)](sattaimuni.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Pāmpāṭṭic cittar pāṭalkaḷ (the songs of the snake-charmer Siddhar)](../texts/pambatti-padalgal.md) — attribution: traditional

The 'snake-charmer Siddhar', whose songs with the refrain 'dance, O snake!' teach the inner ascent and scorn caste, learning and ritual. The tradition says he was a snake-catcher seeking a jewelled serpent in the hills when a Siddhar (usually Caṭṭaimuṉi) showed him that the true serpent lies coiled within.

_Notes: The meeting with Caṭṭaimuṉi is recalled at low confidence. Counted in the popular list of the eighteen Siddhars (see cpt:eighteen-siddhars); the samādhi place given follows that list and is recalled at low confidence._
