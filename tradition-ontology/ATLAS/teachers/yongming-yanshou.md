# Yongming Yanshou

**Alternate names:** 永明延壽, Zhijue chanshi 智覺禪師, Yōmyō Enju
**Lineages:** [Fayan house](../lineages/fayan.md), [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md), [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)

**Works:**

  - [Record of the Source Mirror (Zongjing lu)](../texts/zongjing-lu.md) — attribution: accepted

Fayan Chan master of Hangzhou whose Wanshan tonggui ji defends recitation and all good practices alongside Chan; counted the sixth Pure Land patriarch in the Fozu tongji. The 'four alternatives' on Chan and Pure Land ('with Chan and with Pure Land, like a tiger with horns') are traditionally ascribed to him but are not found in the Wanshan tonggui ji (checked locally) and appear only in later sources.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Tradition says he drew lots before an image, choosing between meditation and the myriad good practices, and drew the latter seven times; he recited the name 100,000 times daily (Song biographies, as recalled).
