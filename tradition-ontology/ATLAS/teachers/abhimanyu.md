# Abhimanyu

`tch:abhimanyu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Saubhadra (son of Subhadrā), Saubhadra
**Lineages:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Named in the Gītā only as Saubhadra, the strong-armed son of Subhadrā, a great chariot-warrior who blows his conch (1.6, 1.18); identified as Abhimanyu, Arjuna's son, from the epic.

_Notes: Saubhadra = Abhimanyu is the epic identification. Linked in BhG ch. 1–3 at 1.6, 1.18._
