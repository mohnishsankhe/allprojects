# Govinda (author of the Rasahṛdayatantra)

`tch:govinda-rasahrdaya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Govinda Bhagavatpāda (SDS), Govinda-bhikṣu
**Lineages:** [Rasa Śāstra (the science of mercury) and the Raseśvara-darśana](../lineages/rasa-sastra.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 10th–11th c.; (confidence low)
**Historicity:** historical
**Works:** 
  - [Rasahṛdayatantra](../texts/rasahrdayatantra.md) — attribution: accepted

Author of the Rasahṛdayatantra; the Sarvadarśanasaṃgraha calls him Govinda Bhagavatpāda and lists him among the siddhas who attained jīvanmukti in a mercurial body (Raseśvara 12); counted among the twenty-seven siddhas (Rasaratnasamuccaya 1.4).

_Notes: Tradition (SDS) calls him Govinda Bhagavatpāda; whether he is Śaṅkara's teacher (tch:govinda-bhagavatpada) is denied by scholars. Kept separate._
