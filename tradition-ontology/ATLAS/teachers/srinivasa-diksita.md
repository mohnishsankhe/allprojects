Vaikhānasa scholar ('Śrīnivāsamakhin', titled Vedāntadeśika), of the Kauśika lineage, son of Govindācārya and devotee of Veṅkaṭeśa, author of the Tātparyacintāmaṇi commentary containing the Daśavidhahetunirūpaṇa.
