# Śrīnivāsa Ācārya

`tch:srinivasa-acarya-gaudiya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī](gopala-bhatta-gosvami.md), [Jīva Gosvāmī](jiva-gosvami.md)

Second-generation leader who, with Narottama and Śyāmānanda, carried the Gosvāmīs' books from Vṛndāvana to Bengal; the tradition says they were stolen at Viṣṇupur and recovered when King Vīra Hāmvīra became his disciple.
