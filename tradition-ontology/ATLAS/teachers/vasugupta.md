The Kashmiri teacher to whom the Śiva Sūtras were revealed and from whom the Spanda tradition descends; Kṣemarāja also makes him the author of the Spandakārikā.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Paramaśiva, wishing the secret tradition not to be cut off, favoured him in a dream and told him of a secret on a great rock on Mahādeva mountain; on waking he found the rock, which turned over at the touch of his hand, and received the sūtras (Kṣemarāja). In Bhāskara's account the sūtras appeared to him 'by a siddha's command'.
