# Samartha Rāmdās

**Alternate names:** Rāmdās Svāmī, Samartha

Marathi saint (1608–1681/82), born Nārāyaṇ Ṭhosar at Jāmb, who fled his wedding at the word 'sāvadhān' ('beware'), spent twelve years in austerity and Rāma-japa at Ṭākaḷī near Nāsik and twelve years on pilgrimage, then founded maṭhs and Māruti temples, taught the Dāsbodh and Manāce Śloka and, in Marathi tradition, guided Śivājī.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Rāma and Hanumān appeared to him as a boy; Rāma initiated him.

_Notes: The extent of his relation to Śivājī is debated by historians._
