# Caṇṭēcura Nāyaṉār

`tch:cantesvara-nayanar` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Vicāracarumaṉ (birth name), Caṇḍeśvara
**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Cēyñalūr
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. A brahmin boy who, tending the village cows, made a sand liṅga and bathed it with their milk; when his father kicked over the milk pots he struck at his father's legs with the staff, which became an axe; Śiva made him Caṇṭēcuran, chief of his attendants, heir to his leftover garlands and offerings.
