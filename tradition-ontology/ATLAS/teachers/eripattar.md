# Eṟipatta Nāyaṉār

`tch:eripattar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Karuvūr
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. He cut down the royal elephant that had scattered the flowers gathered for Śiva by the devotee Civakāmi Āṇṭār, and its keepers; the king Pukaḻccōḻar offered his own life in atonement and Eṟipattar tried to take his own, until Śiva intervened and restored all.
