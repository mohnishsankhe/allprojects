# The Aśvins

`tch:asvins` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Aśvinau, Nāsatyas, Dasras, devabhiṣajau (physicians of the gods)
**Lineages:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Teachers:** [Dakṣa Prajāpati](daksa-prajapati.md)
**Students:** [Indra (as student and teacher)](indra.md)

The twin physicians of the gods (Nāsatyas, Dasras), who learnt Āyurveda from Prajāpati and taught it to Indra; they rejoined the head of the sacrifice cut off by Rudra, restored Pūṣan's teeth, Bhaga's eyes and Indra's arm, cured the Moon of consumption and made the aged Cyavana young (Su Sū 1.17; Ca Ci 1.4.41-44; Bhāvaprakāśa Pūrva 1.8-13).
