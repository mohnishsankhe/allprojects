# Maitreyī

`tch:maitreyi` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Yājñavalkya](yajnavalkya.md)

One of Yājñavalkya's two wives, described as a discusser of brahman (brahmavādinī, BAU 4.5.1); when he is about to leave the household she refuses wealth that cannot make her immortal and receives the teaching on the self (BAU 2.4, 4.5).
