No. 20 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A great scholar and gatekeeper of Nālandā (or Vikramaśīla) to whom a ḍākinī appeared as an old woman and asked whether he understood the words or the meaning; he left to seek Tilopa, underwent twelve great hardships and received the whole transmission; teacher of Marpa and of the six dharmas.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A great scholar and gatekeeper of Nālandā (or Vikramaśīla) to whom a ḍākinī appeared as an old woman and asked whether he understood the words or the meaning; he left to seek Tilopa, underwent twelve great hardships and received the whole transmission; teacher of Marpa and of the six dharmas. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 20): As a universal monarch with his armies rules the continents and subcontinents, the yogin of the one taste of the innate rules saṃsāra: that is great bliss.
