# Keśin (Keśikumāra Śramaṇa)

`tch:kesi-kumara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Kesī, Keśī Kumāraśramaṇa
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Pārśva (Pārśvanātha)](parsva.md)
**Students:** [Paesi (Pradeśin)](paesi.md)

A monk of Pārśva's line who met Gautama at Śrāvastī and, after their dialogue on the four and five vows and on clothing, accepted Mahāvīra's teaching (Uttarādhyayana 23); in the Rājapraśnīya he converts King Paesi.
