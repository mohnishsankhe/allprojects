Milarepa's close disciple, called his 'moon-like' son (Gampopa being the 'sun-like'); he went to India and brought back from Tiphupa the teachings of the formless ḍākinīs; source of the Rechung hearing lineage and, by the Drukpa account, of the Six Equal Tastes hidden as treasure.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Proud of the learning he brought from India, he was humbled by Milarepa's display (sheltering inside a yak horn); Milarepa's Life is framed as told to him.
