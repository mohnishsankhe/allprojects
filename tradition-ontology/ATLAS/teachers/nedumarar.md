# Niṉṟacīr Neṭumāṟa Nāyaṉār

`tch:nedumarar` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Kūṉ Pāṇṭiyaṉ ('the hunchbacked Pāṇṭiya'), Neṭumāṟaṉ
**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Madurai
**Historicity:** historical

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. The Pāṇṭiya king, a patron of the Jains, whose burning fever Campantar cured with sacred ash; his hunched back was straightened and he turned Śaiva; he won the battle of Nelvēli.
