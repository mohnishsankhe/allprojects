# Periyāḻvār

**Alternate names:** Viṣṇucitta, Paṭṭarpirāṉ, Bhaṭṭanātha, Periyazhvar

Brāhmaṇa priest-gardener of Śrīvilliputtūr, foster-father of Āṇṭāḷ; author of the Tiruppallāṇṭu, which opens the Prabandham and daily worship with a benediction for the Lord's own safety, and of the Periyāḻvār Tirumoḻi, the first Tamil song-cycle of Kṛṣṇa's childhood sung through Yaśodā's eyes.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Summoned when the Pāṇḍya king Vallabhadeva sought to know the supreme truth, he established from the scriptures that Nārāyaṇa is supreme; the purse of gold hung as the prize bent down to him. The Lord appeared on Garuḍa in the sky to see his devotee honoured; fearing the evil eye might fall on the Lord, the Āḻvār sang 'many years, many years' for his safety.
