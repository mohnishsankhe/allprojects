# Maṇibhadrā

`tch:manibhadra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Maṇibhadra, ma Ni b+ha dra (Tibetan)
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Kukkuripa](kukkuripa.md)

No. 65 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A young wife of a householder who received instruction from Kukkuripa and practised secretly for a year while keeping house; when she broke her water pot on the way home she realized, and later flew into the sky.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A young wife of a householder who received instruction from Kukkuripa and practised secretly for a year while keeping house; when she broke her water pot on the way home she realized, and later flew into the sky. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 65): When one is without concepts, the many concepts follow sound; therefore the nature becomes manifest: whatever appears is that nature itself.

_Notes: Life from Abhayadatta's Caturaśītisiddhapravṛtti recalled from memory (not read locally); the song is read locally in Tōh 2292. The second line is uncertain._
