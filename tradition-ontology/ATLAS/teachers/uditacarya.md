# Uditācārya

`tch:uditacarya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Ārya Uditācārya
**Lineages:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 380 CE (Gupta year 61); (confidence high)
**Places:** Mathurā
**Historicity:** historical

Pāśupata teacher of the Mathurā pillar inscription of 380 CE, described as tenth in succession from Kuśika; he installed two liṅgas named after his teachers (Kapileśvara and Upamiteśvara) in a teachers' shrine.
