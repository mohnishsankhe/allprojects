# Channa

`tch:channa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Historicity:** historical

The charioteer of the bodhisatta's renunciation, later an obstinate monk on whom the 'higher penalty' (brahmadaṇḍa) was imposed after the parinibbāna (DN 16; Cv 11.1.12).

_Notes: The Channa of SN 22.90 and MN 144 is identified with him by some commentaries; not asserted here._
