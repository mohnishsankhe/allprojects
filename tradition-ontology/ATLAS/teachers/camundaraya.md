# Cāmuṇḍarāya

Minister of the Western Gaṅga kings who erected the colossal image of Bāhubali (Gommaṭeśvara) at Śravaṇabeḷagoḷa (c. 981 CE) and wrote the Kannada Cāmuṇḍarāya Purāṇa.
