# Saṅghabhadra

`tch:sanghabhadra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Zhongxian 眾賢
**Lineages:** [Vaibhāṣika](../lineages/vaibhasika.md), [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Contemporary of Vasubandhu (Xuanzang); Scholarly account: c. 4th–5th c. CE; (confidence low)
**Places:** Kashmir, Matipura (where he died, per Xuanzang)
**Historicity:** historical
**Works:** 
  - [Nyāyānusāra](../texts/nyayanusara.md) — attribution: accepted
  - [Abhidharmasamayapradīpikā](../texts/abhidharmasamayapradipika.md) — attribution: accepted

The great Kashmiri Vaibhāṣika who defended orthodoxy against Vasubandhu's Sautrāntika criticisms in the Nyāyānusāra, developing new formulations (e.g. on the activity of dharmas).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Xuanzang relates that he spent twelve years composing a refutation of the Kośa and set out to debate Vasubandhu, who declined; Saṅghabhadra died before they met and Vasubandhu renamed his work Nyāyānusāra.
