# Śreṇika (Bimbisāra)

`tch:srenika-bimbisara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Bhaṃbhasāra, Bimbisāra
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Mahāvīra (Vardhamāna)](mahavira.md)

King of Magadha at Rājagṛha, a hearer of Mahāvīra, who (because of his faith) will be the first Tīrthaṅkara, Padmanābha, of the next ascending half-cycle, though he first suffers in hell for past deeds.
