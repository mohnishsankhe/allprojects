# Suratha

`tch:suratha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Medhas](medhas.md)

A king of the Caitra line, deposed by enemies and ministers; after hearing the Devī Māhātmya he worshipped the Goddess for three years and received his kingdom back and the boon of becoming Sāvarṇi, the eighth Manu (DM 13; MkP 93.17).
