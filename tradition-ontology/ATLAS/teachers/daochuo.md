Master who in the Anle ji taught that in the last age of the Dharma the holy path can hardly be realised and only the Pure Land gate is passable; that Amitābha is a reward-body buddha; that ten continuous recitations suffice even for evil-doers. Popularised counting recitations (with beans or a rosary). Fourth of the seven Shinshū masters.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Tradition says that at Xuanzhong si, reading the inscription on Tanluan's deeds (609), he turned to the Pure Land at forty-eight, lectured on the Contemplation Sūtra some two hundred times and recited the name 70,000 times a day, counting with beans (Xu gaoseng zhuan; later accounts).
