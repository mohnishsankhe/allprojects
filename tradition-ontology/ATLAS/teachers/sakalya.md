# Śākalya

`tch:sakalya` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Śākalya the padakāra
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Śākala śākhā (Ṛgveda)](../lineages/sakha-sakala.md), [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Ṛgveda Padapāṭha](../texts/rgveda-padapatha.md) — attribution: traditional

The teacher credited with the Padapāṭha of the Ṛgveda and eponym of the Śākala recension; cited by Yāska and Pāṇini. Tradition sometimes identifies him with Vidagdha Śākalya, Yājñavalkya's questioner on the number of the gods in the Bṛhadāraṇyaka (3.9) — an identification scholars dispute.
