# Madālasā

`tch:madalasa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Students:** [Alarka](alarka.md)

A woman teacher of self-knowledge in the Mārkaṇḍeya. Her lullaby opens 'śuddho 'si re tāta, na te 'sti nāma' in the checked e-text (MkP 25.11); the popular verse 'śuddho 'si buddho 'si nirañjano 'si ...' recited under her name was not found there.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The queen who, under pretext of lulling her crying infants, taught her first sons that the self is pure, that its name is a recent imagination, and that the body of five elements is not theirs (MkP 25.10-18), so that they renounced; she taught Alarka rājadharma and gave him a ring containing a teaching for the time of distress (MkP 26-35).
