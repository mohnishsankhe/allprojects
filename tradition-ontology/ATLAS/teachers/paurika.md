# Paurika

`tch:paurika` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Historicity:** historical

Sāṃkhya teacher who held, according to the Yuktidīpikā (on SK 56), that each puruṣa has its own pradhāna producing its body and the rest; when the pradhāna of the great-bodied one (māhātmyaśarīra) operates, the others operate, and when it ceases, they cease. The Yuktidīpikā rejects this for want of proof. Named in the Yuktidīpikā's list of teachers through whom the doctrine passed between Pañcaśikha and Īśvarakṛṣṇa (on SK 71: Hārīta, Vāddhali, Kairāta, Paurika, Ṛṣabheśvara, Pañcādhikaraṇa, Patañjali, Vārṣagaṇya, Kauṇḍinya, Mūka and others).
