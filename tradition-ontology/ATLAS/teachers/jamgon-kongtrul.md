Co-founder of the Rimé movement. Born to a family with Bön connections, trained at Shechen and at Palpung, he received and practised the transmissions of all schools and compiled them in the Five Treasuries — the Treasury of Knowledge, the Treasury of Kagyu Mantra, the Treasury of Precious Treasures, the Treasury of Instructions and the Treasury of Extensive Teachings — mapping Tibetan practice as eight great chariots of practice lineages. He presented the other-emptiness view as the Great Madhyamaka and revived the Shangpa, Chöd and Kālacakra instructions.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Regarded as an emanation of the translator Vairocana.
