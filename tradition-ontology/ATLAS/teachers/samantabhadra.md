Digambara poet-philosopher who first set out many-sidedness and the sevenfold predication systematically in the Āptamīmāṃsā, and wrote the Svayambhūstotra, Yuktyanuśāsana and (by tradition) the Ratnakaraṇḍaśrāvakācāra. The tradition tells that he was afflicted by an insatiable hunger (bhasmaka), took leave of monkhood to cure it, and at Vārāṇasī caused the image of Candraprabha to appear from a Śiva-liṅga while reciting his hymn.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The image of Candraprabha emerged from the liṅga at Vārāṇasī as he sang the hymn to the eighth Tīrthaṅkara.
