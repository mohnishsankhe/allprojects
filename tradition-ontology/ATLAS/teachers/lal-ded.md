14th-century Kashmiri yoginī whose vākhs, in the idiom of non-dual Śaivism and yoga, speak of seeking Śiva far and finding him at home in her own body, of breath-practice lighting the lamp of knowledge, and of the indifference of the realized to sectarian names.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Tradition holds that she left an unhappy married life to wander as an ascetic, dancing unclothed after her teacher's single instruction to turn from the outer to the inner; she is also revered by Kashmiri Muslims (as Lallā Ārifa).

_Notes: Her teacher is named in tradition as Siddha Śrīkaṇṭha (Sed Bayu) - low confidence, not given as an id._
