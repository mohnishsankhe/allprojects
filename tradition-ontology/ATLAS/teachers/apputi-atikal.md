# Appūti Aṭikaḷ

`tch:apputi-atikal` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Tiṅkaḷūr
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. A brahmin who, without having met Appar, named his sons, water-sheds and alms-houses after him; when Appar visited, a son died of snakebite and the parents hid it to feed their guest; Appar sang and the boy revived.
