No. 68 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A man who talked incessantly and was shunned; taught to hear all speech as echo.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A man who talked incessantly and was shunned; taught to hear all speech as echo. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 68): In the centre of the pure sky of dharmadhātu the thunder of the unborn resounds; all the deluded phenomena of appearance and existence are the beautiful fruit, the three bodies and gnosis.
