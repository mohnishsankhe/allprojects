# Aṅgiras (teacher of Śaunaka)

`tch:angiras` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Śaunaka śākhā (Atharvaveda)](../lineages/sakha-saunaka.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Satyavāha Bhāradvāja](satyavaha-bharadvaja.md)
**Students:** [Śaunaka Mahāśāla](saunaka-mahasala.md)

Teacher of the Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad: answers Śaunaka's question 'by knowing what is all this known?' with the higher and lower knowledge (MuU 1.1.3-5); 'this truth the seer Aṅgiras declared of old' (MuU 3.2.11).

_Notes: Whether tradition identifies him with the Ṛgvedic seer-family ancestor Aṅgiras is not asserted here._
