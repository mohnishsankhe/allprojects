Teacher of the Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad: answers Śaunaka's question 'by knowing what is all this known?' with the higher and lower knowledge (MuU 1.1.3-5); 'this truth the seer Aṅgiras declared of old' (MuU 3.2.11).

_Notes: Whether tradition identifies him with the Ṛgvedic seer-family ancestor Aṅgiras is not asserted here._
