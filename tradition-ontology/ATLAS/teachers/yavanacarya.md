# Yavanācārya ('the Yavana teacher')

`tch:yavanacarya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Yavana school of horā](../lineages/yavana-jataka.md), [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)
**Historicity:** unknown

The teacher or teachers called 'the Yavanas' whom Varāhamihira cites on longevity (BJ 7.1), royal yogas (BJ 11.1) and the count of nābhasa yogas (BJ 12.1), and whom he says are honoured like seers (BS 2.14).
