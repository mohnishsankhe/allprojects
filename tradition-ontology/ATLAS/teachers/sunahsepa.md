# Śunaḥśepa

`tch:sunahsepa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Devarāta Vaiśvāmitra
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Śākala śākhā (Ṛgveda)](../lineages/sakha-sakala.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Ṛgveda Saṃhitā](../texts/rgveda.md) — attribution: traditional

Śunaḥśepa Ājīgarti, later adopted as Devarāta Vaiśvāmitra, seer of RV 1.24–30, including the prayer that Varuṇa loosen the upper, middle and lower fetters so that we may be sinless before Aditi (1.24.15). His rescue from being sacrificed is told in the Aitareya Brāhmaṇa.
