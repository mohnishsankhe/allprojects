# Neminātha (Ariṣṭanemi)

`tch:neminatha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Ariṣṭanemi, Nemi, Ariṭṭhanemi (Prakrit)
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: the twenty-second Jina, preceding Pārśva by 84,000 years in the traditional chronology; (confidence low)
**Places:** Śauryapura, Dvārakā, Girnār (Ujjayanta)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Students:** [Rājīmatī (Rājul)](rajimati.md), [Rathanemi](rathanemi.md)

The twenty-second Tīrthaṅkara, emblem the conch, son of Samudravijaya and Śivā of the Yādava (Hari) clan, born at Śauryapura, cousin of Kṛṣṇa. On the way to his wedding with Rājīmatī he heard the cries of animals penned for the feast, freed them and renounced; Rājīmatī too became a nun and steadied his brother Rathanemi in his vows (Uttarādhyayana 22). He attained omniscience and nirvāṇa on Mount Girnār (Ujjayanta / Raivataka). His body is described as dark.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** He renounced on seeing the animals to be killed for his own wedding feast and attained omniscience on Girnār.

_Notes: Some scholars have argued for a historical core to Nemi; most treat him as legendary._
