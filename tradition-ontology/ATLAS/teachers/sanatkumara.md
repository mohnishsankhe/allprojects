# Sanatkumāra

`tch:sanatkumara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Students:** [Nārada](narada.md), [Vṛtra](vrtra.md)

Eldest of the four Kumāras (Sanaka, Sanandana, Sanātana, Sanatkumāra), mind-born sons of Brahmā who refused to procreate and remained celibate knowers (VP 1.7; KūP 1.10.13-14); the first avatāra in BhP 1.3.6; with his brothers he instructs Nārada in the Nārada Purāṇa; the first Upapurāṇa bears his name (KūP 1.1.17).

_Notes: U05's contribution; his teaching in Chāndogya 7 is covered by U03._
