No. 35 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A farmer with a goitre on his neck whom Nāgārjuna taught to visualize the growth enlarging and then to see it as empty.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A farmer with a goitre on his neck whom Nāgārjuna taught to visualize the growth enlarging and then to see it as empty. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 35): Where the innate is concerned, if one strains, a thorn arises; if one lets go without clinging, that is the supreme meditation; by the light of the guru's speech the extremes of existence and non-existence are not found; what is not found is seen; realizing that is the supreme meaning.
