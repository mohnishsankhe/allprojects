Physician of Dakpo who, after his wife's death, became a Kadampa monk and then Milarepa's chief disciple; he joined the Kadam graded path with Milarepa's Mahāmudrā and Six Yogas, founded Daklha Gampo (1121) and wrote the Jewel Ornament of Liberation; all the Dakpo Kagyu schools descend from his disciples.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Foretold in the Samādhirāja Sūtra (by the tradition's reading) as a physician-monk who would spread its teaching; he came to Milarepa already accomplished in Kadam meditation, reported his experiences, and was told they were neither good nor bad; Milarepa's parting instruction was to show his calloused seat from years of sitting.
