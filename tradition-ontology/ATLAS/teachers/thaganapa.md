# Thaganapa

`tch:thaganapa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Thaganapāda, tha kha na (Tibetan)
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary

No. 19 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A compulsive liar taught by a monk to meditate that all appearances are a lie; he realized emptiness through his own habit.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A compulsive liar taught by a monk to meditate that all appearances are a lie; he realized emptiness through his own habit. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 19): As water in the ear is drawn out by water, so, meditating on the false appearances of existence as false, one sees the truth.
