# Dharmatrāta

`tch:dharmatrata` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Fajiu 法救
**Lineages:** [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md), [Vaibhāṣika](../lineages/vaibhasika.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 1st c. BCE – 2nd c. CE; (confidence low)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Udānavarga](../texts/udanavarga.md) — attribution: traditional

One of the four great masters of the Vibhāṣā: dharmas passing through the three times change their mode of being (bhāva) but not their substance, as a gold vessel broken and remade (AKBh 5.26). Yaśomitra credits an elder Dharmatrāta with arranging the Udānavarga; he is also counted among the Dārṣṭāntikas.
