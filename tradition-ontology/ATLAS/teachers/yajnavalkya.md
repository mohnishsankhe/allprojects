The central teacher of the Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad: he teaches Maitreyī that the self is to be seen, heard, reflected on and meditated upon (BAU 2.4, 4.5), defeats the brahmins at Janaka's court (BAU 3) and teaches Janaka on light, dream, sleep, death and karma (BAU 4.3-4). BAU 6.5.3 names him Vājasaneya, through whom the white Yajus formulas received from the sun are proclaimed.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** BAU 6.5.3 says the white Yajus formulas come from the sun (Āditya) and are proclaimed by Yājñavalkya Vājasaneya; later Purāṇic accounts elaborate that he received them from the sun (not recorded here in detail).

_Notes: Teacher of the mantha rite lineage: Uddālaka Āruṇi taught it to his pupil Yājñavalkya (BAU 6.3.7, moderate on the kaṇḍikā). The Yājñavalkya Smṛti and Yoga-Yājñavalkya are later works attributed to him (owned by other units)._
