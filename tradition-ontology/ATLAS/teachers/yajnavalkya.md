In the Mokṣadharma (12.298–306) Yājñavalkya teaches king Daivarāti Janaka the evolution of the principles, the guṇas, yoga and dissolution, tells how he won the Yajus teaching from the Sun by austerity (12.306.3–22), and relates his answers to the Gandharva Viśvāvasu (12.306.27); he declares that knowledge may be received even from a śūdra (12.306.85).
