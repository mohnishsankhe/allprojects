# Cimin Huiri

**Alternate names:** 慈愍慧日, Cimin sanzang
**Lineages:** [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 680–748 CE; (confidence moderate)
**Historicity:** historical

Tang pilgrim to India (702–719) who on his return taught Pure Land practice joined with precepts and study and criticised Chan teachers who rejected recitation as mere form.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** After travelling to India he prayed at Gandhāra and received a vision of Avalokiteśvara urging him to the Pure Land (as recalled).
