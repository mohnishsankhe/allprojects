Digambara master of the karma scriptures ('emperor of the siddhānta') who wrote the Gommaṭasāra, Labdhisāra and Trilokasāra for the minister Cāmuṇḍarāya.

_Notes: Some scholars assign the Dravyasaṃgraha to a different Nemicandra (Siddhāntideva)._
