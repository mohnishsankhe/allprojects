No. 67 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). The younger of the two headless sisters (see Mekhalā).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The younger of the two headless sisters (see Mekhalā). Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 67): Putting on the great armour, with jewel-like courage, launching the boat of one's own mind, one surely enters the interior of the picture.

_Notes: Life from Abhayadatta's Caturaśītisiddhapravṛtti recalled from memory (not read locally); the song is read locally in Tōh 2292. Tōh 2292 reads 'nA ga ka la ka'; identified with Kanakhalā by position (low). The song's meaning is obscure._
