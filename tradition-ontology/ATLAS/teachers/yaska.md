Author of the Nirukta, the etymological commentary on the Vedic word-lists (Nighaṇṭu). He defends the meaningfulness of the mantras against Kautsa (Nirukta 1.15–16), teaches that the seers had direct vision of dharma (1.20), that one who recites the Veda without knowing its meaning is a post bearing a load (1.18), and that because of the deity's greatness the one Self is praised in many ways (7.4).
