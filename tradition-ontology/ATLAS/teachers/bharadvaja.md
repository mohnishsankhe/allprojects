# Bharadvāja

`tch:bharadvaja` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Bharadvāja Bārhaspatya
**Lineages:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md), [The school of Ātreya (Ātreya-sampradāya, the physicians' line)](../lineages/atreya-sampradaya.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Śākala śākhā (Ṛgveda)](../lineages/sakha-sakala.md), [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

**Works:** 
  - [Ṛgveda Saṃhitā](../texts/rgveda.md) — attribution: traditional

Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): the sage who, chosen by the assembly of sages in the Himalaya when disease obstructed austerity and study, went to Indra 'seeking long life', learnt the threefold Āyurveda (cause, sign, remedy), gained immeasurable life and taught it to the sages (Ca Sū 1.3-27).

_Notes: Distinct in this unit from Kumāraśiras Bharadvāja who speaks in Ca Sū 12, 26 and Śā 6 (tch:kumarasiras-bharadvaja); the objector of Ca Śā 3.4 is simply 'Bharadvāja'._
