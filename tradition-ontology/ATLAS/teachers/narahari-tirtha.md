# Narahari Tīrtha

`tch:narahari-tirtha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md), [Uttarādi Maṭha](../lineages/uttaradi-matha.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: pontificate c. 1324–1333 in maṭha lists; Scholarly account: his inscriptions at Śrīkūrmam are dated c. 1264–1293 CE; (confidence low)
**Places:** Kaliṅga (Śrīkūrmam)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Madhva](madhva.md), [Padmanābha Tīrtha](padmanabha-tirtha.md)
**Students:** [Mādhava Tīrtha](madhava-tirtha.md)

Disciple of Madhva and his successor after Padmanābha Tīrtha in the Uttarādi line. The tradition says he had served as regent of the Kaliṅga kingdom and brought from its treasury the images of Rāma and Sītā (Mūla Rāma) that Madhva had asked for; inscriptions of his at Śrīkūrmam are used by scholars to date Madhva.

_Notes: The tradition also credits him with fostering Yakṣagāna — low confidence, not verified._
