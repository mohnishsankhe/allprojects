# Sarvabhakṣa

No. 75 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A glutton with a belly like a pot whom Saraha taught to visualize his stomach empty as space and consuming the sun and moon.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A glutton with a belly like a pot whom Saraha taught to visualize his stomach empty as space and consuming the sun and moon. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 75): When not realized there is great difference among tastes; realized, tastes have no difference in essence; likewise when not realized saṃsāra and nirvāṇa appear different; realized, saṃsāra and nirvāṇa are great bliss.
