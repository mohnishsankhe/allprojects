# Garab Dorje (Prahevajra)

`tch:garab-dorje` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** dga' rab rdo rje, Pramodavajra, Surativajra
**Lineages:** [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md), [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)
**Places:** Oḍḍiyāna, the Śītavana charnel ground
**Historicity:** legendary
**Students:** [Mañjuśrīmitra](manjusrimitra.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Tsik Sum Ne Dek (tshig gsum gnad brdeg, Hitting the Vital Point in Three Words)](../texts/tsik-sum-ne-dek.md) — attribution: traditional

The first human teacher of the Great Perfection. Born in Oḍḍiyāna to a nun-princess by miraculous conception, he received all the tantras from Vajrasattva, taught the Dzogchen scriptures, and at his passing into light left his testament, the Three Statements that Hit the Vital Point, to Mañjuśrīmitra.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Realized the Great Perfection from birth; dissolved his body into light and dropped his testament in a golden casket from the sky.
