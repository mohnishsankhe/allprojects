# Ciṟuttoṇṭa Nāyaṉār

`tch:siruttontar` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Parañcōti (as general)
**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: mid-7th c. (Vātāpi taken 642); (confidence moderate)
**Places:** Tiruccheṅkāṭṭaṅkuṭi
**Historicity:** historical

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. General of the Pallava king who led the capture of Vātāpi; retiring to serve devotees, he was visited by Śiva as a Bhairava ascetic who asked for a meal cooked from an only son of five; he and his wife Veṇkāṭṭu Naṅkai cooked their son Cīrāḷaṉ; the ascetic bade them call the boy to eat with him, and the boy came running, alive.
