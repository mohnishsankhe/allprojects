Founder of the Gelug. Born in Tsongkha (Amdo), he trained with teachers of all schools — Rendawa above all in Madhyamaka — did long retreats with eight disciples at Wölkha, and through the visionary Umapa and then directly communicated with Mañjuśrī; the tradition says his realization of emptiness came while reading Buddhapālita's commentary after a dream of Nāgārjuna and his disciples. He wrote the Great Treatises on the Stages of the Path and of Mantra, founded the Great Prayer Festival at Lhasa and Ganden monastery (1409), and restored monastic discipline.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Regarded as an emanation of Mañjuśrī; after years of dialogue with Mañjuśrī through Umapa and then directly, he dreamt of Nāgārjuna and his five disciples, one of whom, Buddhapālita, touched his head with a text; next day, reading Buddhapālita's commentary, he gained complete certainty about the view (Praise of Dependent Origination closes with this gratitude).
