The tenth Tīrthaṅkara of the present descending half-cycle (avasarpiṇī). Emblem (lāñchana): the śrīvatsa mark in Śvetāmbara lists (a wish-fulfilling tree in some Digambara lists; low confidence). Parents: Dṛḍharatha and Nandā. Born at Bhadrilapura (Bhaddilapura); attained nirvāṇa at Sammeda Śikhara.

_Notes: Parents, birthplaces and emblems follow the common Śvetāmbara lists (e.g. Hemacandra); Digambara lists agree in most points and differ in some emblems and names._
