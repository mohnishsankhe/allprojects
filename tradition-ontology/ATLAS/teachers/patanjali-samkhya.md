# Patañjali (Sāṃkhya teacher)

`tch:patanjali-samkhya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)

A Sāṃkhya teacher cited by the Yuktidīpikā: mahat arises directly from pradhāna; all transformation of the organs is 'from itself'; subtle bodies arise and cease repeatedly (one leads the organs to the womb, another arises at death). Named in the Yuktidīpikā's list of teachers through whom the doctrine passed between Pañcaśikha and Īśvarakṛṣṇa (on SK 71: Hārīta, Vāddhali, Kairāta, Paurika, Ṛṣabheśvara, Pañcādhikaraṇa, Patañjali, Vārṣagaṇya, Kauṇḍinya, Mūka and others). Whether he is the author of the Yoga Sūtra (tch:patanjali) is disputed.
