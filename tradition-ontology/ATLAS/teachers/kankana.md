**Realization — the tradition's account:** A king who meditated on the light of the jewel of his bracelet (kaṅkaṇa) as the light of mind. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 29): From the wish-fulfilling jewel of thought the colour of experience arises; by the power of conduct all needs and wishes come; it is accomplished by those who have equal taste.
