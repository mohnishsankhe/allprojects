# Ajita Kesakambalī

`tch:ajita-kesakambali` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Ajita Keśakambala (Skt.), Ajita of the hair blanket, 阿夷陀翅舍欽婆羅 (Chinese, Dīrgha-āgama)
**Lineages:** [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 5th c. BCE (contemporary of the Buddha and Mahāvīra); (confidence low)
**Historicity:** historical

One of the six famous teachers, named for his blanket of human hair. DN 2 reports his annihilationism: there is no gift, sacrifice or offering, no fruit of good and bad deeds, no this world or other world, no mother or father, no spontaneously born beings, no ascetic who has realized the other world; the person is made of the four great elements, which return to their masses at death, the faculties passing into space; fool and wise alike are annihilated at the body's breaking up.

_Notes: Doctrine reported_by_opponent. Later tradition and scholars treat him as a forerunner of the Lokāyata; no text links him to that school by lineage. SN 44.9 reports (surprisingly) that he too declared where his dead disciples were reborn._
