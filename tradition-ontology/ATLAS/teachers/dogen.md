Founder of Japanese Sōtō: trained in Tendai and under Myōzen, went to China (1223–1227), received Caodong transmission from Tiantong Rujing, taught just sitting as the enactment of original realization, founded Kōshōji and Eiheiji, and wrote the Shōbōgenzō, Fukanzazengi and monastic instructions.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** During sitting at Tiantong, Rujing rebuked a dozing monk: 'practising Chan is body and mind dropping off'; hearing this Dōgen awoke and Rujing confirmed 'body and mind dropped off; dropped-off body and mind' (Sōtō biographies; Rujing xu yulu).
