# Nidāgha

`tch:nidagha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** `lin:advaita-vedanta`, [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Teachers:** [Ṛbhu](rbhu.md)

Son of Pulastya and disciple of Ṛbhu, who lived at Vīranagara on the Devikā; Ṛbhu returns twice in disguise to test and teach him (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 2.15-16); the recipient of the Ṛbhu Gītā.
