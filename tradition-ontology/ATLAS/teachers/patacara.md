# Paṭācārā

`tch:patacara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Gotama Buddha](gotama-buddha.md)

Foremost nun in knowledge of the Vinaya; her verses tell of her liberation at a lamp's going out (Thig 5.10); by the commentarial account she had lost husband, children and parents in a single day; teacher of many nuns (Thig 5.11, 6.1).
