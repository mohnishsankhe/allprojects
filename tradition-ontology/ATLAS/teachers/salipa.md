# Śalipa

**Alternate names:** Śālipa, Śṛgālapāda ('the wolf/jackal one'), sha wa ri pa (Tōh 2292 spelling)
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)

No. 21 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A low-caste man terrified by the howling of wolves (jackals) near the cremation ground where he lived; a monk taught him to meditate on all sounds as the howl, and through the fear itself he realized.
