# Śivabhūti (Sahasramalla)

`tch:sivabhuti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Historicity:** legendary

In the Śvetāmbara account the founder of the Boṭika (Digambara) schism at Rathavīrapura 609 years after the nirvāṇa: when his teacher Āryakṛṣṇa tore up a precious blanket the king had given him, he declared that the Jinakalpa nakedness was the true path and went naked; his sister Uttarā, told that women may not go naked, kept her clothes — hence the Boṭikas deny liberation to women.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** This is the Śvetāmbara (opponent's) account of the origin of the Digambaras; the Digambaras do not accept it.
