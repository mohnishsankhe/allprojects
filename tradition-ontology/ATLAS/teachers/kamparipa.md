No. 45 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A blacksmith taught to visualize his forge within: the channels as bellows and the fire of gnosis.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A blacksmith taught to visualize his forge within: the channels as bellows and the fire of gnosis. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 45): On the hearth of natural form, on the charcoal of recollection, awareness and concepts, blowing the bellows of lalanā and rasanā and kindling the fire of gnosis in the avadhūtī, the concepts of the three poisons are forged into the dharmakāya; realizing this, Kampari was freed.
