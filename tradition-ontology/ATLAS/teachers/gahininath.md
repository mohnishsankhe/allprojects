# Gahinīnāth

`tch:gahininath` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Gaiṇīnāth
**Lineages:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)
**Places:** Tryambakeśvar (Brahmagiri)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Gorakṣanātha](goraksanatha.md)
**Students:** [Nivṛttināth](nivrttinath.md)

Nāth yogī, disciple of Gorakṣanātha, who initiated Nivṛttināth in a cave on the Brahmagiri near Tryambakeśvar and charged him to spread devotion to Kṛṣṇa; through him the Vārkarī saints trace their Nāth initiation.
