A brāhmaṇa of Kāśī of the Kāśyapa gotra, son of Govindadeva and grandson of Viśvanāthadeva (colophon of the Haṭhatattvakaumudī), author of two large haṭha compendia, the Haṭhatattvakaumudī and the Haṭhasaṅketacandrikā; the colophon calls him also a physician (devavaidya, variant).
