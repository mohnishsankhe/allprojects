# Naciketas

`tch:naciketas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Nāciketa
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Yama (Mṛtyu)](yama.md)

Boy who, given to Death by his father, waits three nights in Yama's house and chooses as his third boon the knowledge of what happens after death; refusing all worldly gifts, he receives the teaching of the self and the whole method of yoga and attains brahman (KU 1.1-2.3.18).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** KU 2.3.18: having received this knowledge from Death and the whole rule of yoga, Naciketas attained brahman and became free of stain and death.
