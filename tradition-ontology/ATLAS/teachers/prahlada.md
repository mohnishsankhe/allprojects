# Prahlāda

`tch:prahlada` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Teachers:** [Nārada](narada.md)

The child-devotee who taught his demon schoolfellows the nine forms of devotion (BhP 7.5.23-24) and that devotion should be practised from childhood (7.6.1); a mahājana (6.3.20) and king of the daityas in Sutala.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Son of the demon-king Hiraṇyakaśipu, devoted to Viṣṇu from the womb; unharmed by his father's attempts to kill him; saved by Narasiṃha; refuses boons and liberation for himself alone (BhP 7.4-10; VP 1.16-20).
