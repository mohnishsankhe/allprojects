Cennabasava (Cennabasavaṇṇa), Basava's nephew, honoured as the master of ṣaṭsthala doctrine; his vacanas are signed Kūḍalacennasaṅgamadēva, and after the Kalyāṇa upheaval he led the śaraṇas with the vacana manuscripts to Uḷavi.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Son of Basava's sister Akkanāgamma; the Śūnyasampādane has Allama acknowledge his knowledge of the sthalas, and he gives liṅga-initiation to Siddharāma; he attained union at Uḷavi.
