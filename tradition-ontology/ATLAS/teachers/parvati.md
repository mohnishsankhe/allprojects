# Pārvatī

`tch:parvati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Umā, Girijā, Gaurī
**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md), [Rasa Śāstra (the science of mercury) and the Raseśvara-darśana](../lineages/rasa-sastra.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Teachers:** [Śiva](siva.md)

Śiva's consort, the questioner in the Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa (her doubt why the supreme Rāma grieved for Sītā, 1.1.13-15) and in the Guru Gītā; in Śākta texts identified with the Goddess who herself teaches (Devī Gītā, Bhagavatī Gītā).
