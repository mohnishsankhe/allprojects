# Ārdraka (Ārdrakakumāra)

`tch:ardraka-kumara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Addaya
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Mahāvīra (Vardhamāna)](mahavira.md)

A prince, in the tradition from a distant land, who became a monk of Mahāvīra and on his way to him debated Gośāla, Buddhists, brāhmaṇas, Vedāntin (ekadaṇḍin) ascetics and elephant-killing hermits (Sūtrakṛtāṅga 2.6).
