In U06's texts: the family priest of the Ikṣvākus who, at Viśvāmitra's request, teaches the despondent young Rāma the Yoga Vāsiṣṭha / Mokṣopāya; within it he recounts his own learning from Brahmā, from the crow Bhuśuṇḍa and from Śiva.
**Realization — the tradition's account (Ṛgveda):** Varuṇa took Vasiṣṭha into his boat and made him a seer by his powers (RV 7.88.3–4).

_Notes: Seer attributions follow the Sarvānukramaṇī as printed in the hymn headers of the local Ṛgveda text (checked). Parāśara is his grandson (through Śakti) in the tradition; 'students' here records that line of descent._
