# Devahūti

`tch:devahuti` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Kapila](kapila.md)

Daughter of Svāyambhuva Manu, wife of the sage Kardama and mother of Kapila; the questioner of the Kapila Gītā, who asks for a devotion suited to her and attains liberation.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Following her son's instruction she practised devotional yoga at Bindusaras, her body withered unnoticed, and she attained the Lord; the place became Siddhapada (Bhāgavata 3.33).
