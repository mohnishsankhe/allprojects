Founder of Śuddhādvaita and the Puṣṭimārga; author of the Aṇubhāṣya, Tattvārthadīpanibandha, Subodhinī and the sixteen short works; established the worship of Śrīnāthjī at Govardhana and taught the brahmasambandha initiation.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Born at Campāraṇya to the Telugu Brahmin Lakṣmaṇa Bhaṭṭa and Illammā; won a debate at the Vijayanagara court and was honoured as ācārya; travelled India three times (the 84 'seats', baiṭhak, mark where he expounded the Bhāgavata); at midnight on Śrāvaṇa śukla ekādaśī at Gokul, Kṛṣṇa appeared and gave him the brahmasambandha, and the next day he initiated Dāmodaradās Harsānī; at the end he took renunciation and entered the Ganges at Kāśī (1531), leaving the Śikṣāślokī as his last instruction.
