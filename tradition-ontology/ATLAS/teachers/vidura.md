# Vidura

`tch:vidura` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Kṣattṛ
**Lineages:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: end of the Dvāpara yuga (the Bhārata war; Kali yuga traditionally from 3102 BCE)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Students:** [Dhṛtarāṣṭra](dhrtarastra.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Viduranīti (Vidura's counsel)](../texts/viduraniti.md) — attribution: traditional

Half-brother of Dhṛtarāṣṭra and Pāṇḍu, born of a śūdra woman; Dharma incarnate through Māṇḍavya's curse (1.100.28). He counsels Dhṛtarāṣṭra (Viduranīti, 5.33–40) and, declaring that as one born of a śūdra womb he will not speak beyond this, calls Sanatsujāta to teach the higher knowledge (5.41.5); consoles him after the war (11.2–7); dies in the forest by entering Yudhiṣṭhira's body limb by limb and breath by breath (15.33.25).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** An embodiment of Dharma; his death by yoga, entering Yudhiṣṭhira, is narrated at 15.33.
