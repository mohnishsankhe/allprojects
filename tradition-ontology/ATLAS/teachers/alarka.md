# Alarka

**Teachers:** [Madālasā](madalasa.md), [Dattātreya](dattatreya.md)

Youngest son of Madālasā and King Ṛtadhvaja (Kuvalayāśva); raised as a king, he received his mother's ring-instruction and, when defeated in war, went to Dattātreya, learned yoga and renounced (MkP 25-44).
