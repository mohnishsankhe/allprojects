# The daughter of the nāga king Sāgara

`tch:naga-princess` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Longnü 龍女, Ryūnyo, Sāgaranāgarājaduhitā
**Lineages:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), `lin:tiantai`, `lin:nichiren`
**Historicity:** legendary

The eight-year-old nāga princess of Lotus ch. 12, whom Mañjuśrī taught in the sea: she offers a jewel to the Buddha, and before Śāriputra's doubt about women's bodies she instantly becomes a male bodhisattva and a buddha in the southern world Vimalā.
