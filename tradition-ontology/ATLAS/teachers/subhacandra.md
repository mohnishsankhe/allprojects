# Śubhacandra (author of the Jñānārṇava)

**Works:**

Digambara ācārya, author of the Jñānārṇava on meditation. The tradition tells that he and his brother Bhartṛhari were princes who renounced; Bhartṛhari became a yogin who gained an alchemical elixir, and Śubhacandra showed him that self-knowledge, not the elixir, is the true attainment — the Jñānārṇava being written for his instruction.

_Notes: Distinct from Bhaṭṭāraka Śubhacandra (16th c.)._
