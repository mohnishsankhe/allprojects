Gujarati jeweller, poet and lay teacher (1867–1901), author of the Ātmasiddhi, the Apūrva Avasara and the Mokṣamāḷā; he taught a non-sectarian path of self-realization under a true teacher and was a guide of M. K. Gandhi. The tradition says he recalled past lives from the age of seven, was a prodigy of memory (śatāvadhāna), and attained self-realization; his followers later formed several ashrams (e.g. Agas, Dharampur).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Memory of former lives (jātismaraṇa) at seven; later accounts in his letters of self-realization (samyagdarśana).

_Notes: Affinity with the Adhyātma current (he taught from the Samayasāra and Banārsīdās's Nāṭaka) rather than formal membership._
