**Realization — the tradition's account:** A wrestler proud of his strength, taught to wrestle with his pride. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 37): The pride of ignorance obscures the wish-fulfilling jewel of non-conception; in wishing to act, needs and wishes arise; it is accomplished by those who have equal taste.
