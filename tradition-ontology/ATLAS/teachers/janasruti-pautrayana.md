# Jānaśruti Pautrāyaṇa

**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Raikva 'with the cart' (sayugvan)](raikva.md)

A great-grandson of Janaśruta, a generous giver who kept open houses for food; overhearing geese compare him unfavourably to Raikva, he seeks Raikva and receives the saṃvarga teaching (ChU 4.1-2).
