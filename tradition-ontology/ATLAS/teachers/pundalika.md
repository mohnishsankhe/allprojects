# Puṇḍalīka

`tch:pundalika` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Puṇḍalīk
**Lineages:** [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)
**Places:** Paṇḍharpūr
**Historicity:** legendary

The devotee of Paṇḍharpūr whose service to his parents brought Kṛṣṇa to his door; asked to wait, the Lord stood on the brick Puṇḍalīka tossed him and remains there as Viṭṭhala. Vārkarīs chant 'Puṇḍalīka varade Hari Viṭṭhala'.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Kṛṣṇa himself came to see him because of his devotion to his parents.
