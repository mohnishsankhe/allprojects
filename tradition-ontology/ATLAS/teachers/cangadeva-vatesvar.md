# Cāṅgadev (Cāṅgā Vaṭeśvar)

`tch:cangadeva-vatesvar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Places:** Puṇatāmbe (on the Godāvarī)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Muktābāī](muktabai.md)

A yogī said to have lived fourteen hundred years by yogic power, who came riding a tiger with a cobra for a whip to meet Jñāneśvar and found the siblings riding a moving wall; humbled, he took Muktābāī as guru. Jñāneśvar's Cāṅgadeva Pāsaṣṭī is addressed to him.

_Notes: Distinct from the Mahānubhāva Cāṅgadeva Rāüḷ of Dvārakā (tch:cangadeva-raul)._
