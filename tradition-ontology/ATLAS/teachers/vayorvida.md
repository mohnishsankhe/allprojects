# Vāyorvida (the royal sage)

`tch:vayorvida` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md), [The school of Ātreya (Ātreya-sampradāya, the physicians' line)](../lineages/atreya-sampradaya.md)
**Historicity:** legendary

The royal sage who praised vāyu as the upholder of body and world, 'the lord Vāyu himself' (Ca Sū 12.8, 12.10); six tastes defined by qualities (Sū 26.8); in Sū 25.12-13 (as Vāryovida) he holds that beings and diseases arise from rasa (water).
