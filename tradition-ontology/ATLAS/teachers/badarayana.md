# Bādarāyaṇa

`tch:badarayana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Vyāsa (in the tradition's identification), Bādarāyaṇa Vyāsa
**Lineages:** [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md), [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: identical with Vyāsa, end of Dvāpara; Scholarly account: c. 2nd c. BCE – 5th c. CE (uncertain); (confidence low)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Brahma Sūtra](../texts/brahma-sutra.md) — attribution: traditional

Author (tradition) or final redactor (scholarly) of the Brahma Sūtras; he states his own view by name at several points (e.g. BS 1.3.33, 3.4.1, 4.4.7, 4.4.12) against Jaimini, Bādari and others.

_Notes: The identification with Vyāsa (tch:vyasa) is the tradition's; Śaṅkara refers to the sūtrakāra as Vyāsa in places. Scholars treat them as distinct._
