# Ulūka (Sāṃkhya teacher)

`tch:uluka-samkhya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Historicity:** unknown

Named in the Māṭhara Vṛtti (on SK 71) among those through whom the knowledge came from Pañcaśikha to Īśvarakṛṣṇa: Bhārgava, Ulūka, Vālmīki, Hārīta, Devala and others. 'Ulūka' is also a name of the Vaiśeṣika founder Kaṇāda; no identity is implied.
