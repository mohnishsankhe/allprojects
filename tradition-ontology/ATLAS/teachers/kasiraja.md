# the king of Kāśī

`tch:kasiraja` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Kāśirāja, Kāśya
**Lineages:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Valiant Pāṇḍava ally named as kāśirāja in Duryodhana's list (1.5) and as kāśya, 'supreme archer', among the conch-blowers (1.17); taken as the same person.

_Notes: The kāśirāja of 1.5 and the kāśya of 1.17 are taken as the same person. Linked in BhG ch. 1–3 at 1.5, 1.17._
