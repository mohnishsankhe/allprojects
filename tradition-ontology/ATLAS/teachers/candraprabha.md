The eighth Tīrthaṅkara of the present descending half-cycle (avasarpiṇī). Emblem (lāñchana): the crescent moon. Parents: Mahāsena and Lakṣmaṇā. Born at Candrapurī; attained nirvāṇa at Sammeda Śikhara. His body is described as white.

_Notes: Parents, birthplaces and emblems follow the common Śvetāmbara lists (e.g. Hemacandra); Digambara lists agree in most points and differ in some emblems and names._
