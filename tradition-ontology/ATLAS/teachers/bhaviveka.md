Madhyamaka master who introduced Dignāga's formal inference into Madhyamaka, criticized Buddhapālita, and in the Madhyamakahṛdaya and Tarkajvālā surveyed and answered the Śrāvaka, Yogācāra, Sāṃkhya, Vaiśeṣika, Vedānta and Mīmāṃsā schools; regarded in Tibet as the founder of Svātantrika.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Xuanzang records that Bhāviveka, wishing to debate the Yogācāra master Dharmapāla and failing to meet him, resolved to wait for Maitreya to settle his doubts, recited a dhāraṇī before an image of Avalokiteśvara and entered a rock palace of the asuras to await Maitreya's coming.

_Notes: Xuanzang's story from memory (moderate); the later works under the name Bhavya are assigned by scholars to other authors._
