No. 25 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A scavenger who stitched rags; taught the generation and completion stages.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A scavenger who stitched rags; taught the generation and completion stages. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 25): Meditate on the conventional generation stage and the ultimate completion stage in union as mahāmudrā: the fruit, the three bodies and gnosis, dawns.
