# Yasa Kākaṇḍakaputta

`tch:yasa-kakandakaputta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Yaśas
**Lineages:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md), [Sthavira (the Elders' group of the first schism)](../lineages/sthavira.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: a century after the parinibbāna; (confidence moderate)
**Historicity:** historical

The elder who objected to the ten points of the Vajjian monks and called the Second Council at Vesālī (Cv 12).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Finding the Vajjiputtaka monks of Vesālī accepting gold and silver, he objected, was expelled by them, and gathered the elders (Revata, Sabbakāmī and others) for the council that rejected the ten points.
