Polymath of Mithilā who commented on every Vedic school; for Nyāya he wrote the Tātparyaṭīkā — rescuing Uddyotakara's 'aged cows' from the mire of bad commentaries — and the Nyāyasūcīnibandha, which fixed the sūtra text. He reads 'avyapadeśya' and 'vyavasāyātmaka' in NS 1.1.4 as indeterminate and determinate perception, following his teacher Trilocana.

_Notes: His non-Nyāya works belong to U09, U10, U12 and U13._
