# Parīkṣit

`tch:pariksit` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Viṣṇurāta
**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Śuka](suka.md)

Grandson of Arjuna, protected in the womb by Kṛṣṇa; cursed to die in seven days, he renounced his kingdom and sat in prāyopaveśa on the Gaṅgā (BhP 1.19), where he heard the Bhāgavata from Śuka; the model hearer (śravaṇa) of the Purāṇa. He also subdued Kali and assigned him five places (BhP 1.17).
