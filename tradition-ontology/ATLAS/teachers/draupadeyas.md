# the Draupadeyas (sons of Draupadī)

`tch:draupadeyas` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** draupadeyāḥ, Draupadeyāḥ
**Lineages:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

The sons of Draupadī, named as a group among the great chariot-warriors of the Pāṇḍava army (1.6) and the conch-blowers (1.18). Group entry.

_Notes: A group, not a single person; the Gītā does not name them individually. Linked in BhG ch. 1–3 at 1.6, 1.18._
