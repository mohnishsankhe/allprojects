# Ṛṣabha

`tch:rsabha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Historicity:** legendary

In the Bhāgavata an avatāra of Viṣṇu and avadhūta-teacher; the same figure the Jains honour as the first Tīrthaṅkara. BhP 5.6.9 adds that a king named Arhat of Koṅka, Veṅka and Kuṭaka, imitating him, will spread a heterodox path in Kali (a polemical report).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Son of Nābhi and Merudevī, the eighth avatāra 'who showed the path honoured by all āśramas' (BhP 1.3.13); after teaching his sons that the human body is not for dog-like pleasures but for austerity (BhP 5.5.1-27), he lived as a naked avadhūta and gave up his body in a forest fire in Kuṭaka (BhP 5.5.28-5.6.8).
