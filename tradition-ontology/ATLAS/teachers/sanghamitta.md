# Saṅghamittā

`tch:sanghamitta` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: 3rd c. BCE, in the reign of Devānampiya Tissa; Scholarly account: c. mid-3rd c. BCE (chronicle account); (confidence moderate)
**Historicity:** historical

Daughter of Emperor Asoka and a bhikkhunī who, by the chronicles, brought the bhikkhunī ordination and a branch of the Bodhi tree to Laṅkā.

_Notes: Known from the Dīpavaṃsa and Mahāvaṃsa (not in the canon)._
