# Upavarṣa

`tch:upavarsa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md), [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: before Śabara; (confidence low)
**Historicity:** historical
**Works:** 
  - [Upavarṣa's vṛtti (lost)](../texts/upavarsa-vrtti.md) — attribution: traditional

Called 'the revered (bhagavān) Upavarṣa' by Śabara, who cites his view that the word 'gauḥ' is simply the letters g, au and ḥ; Śaṅkara (BSBh 3.3.53) reports that Upavarṣa in the first treatise deferred the proof of the self to the Śārīraka.

_Notes: Whether Upavarṣa is the same as the Vṛttikāra quoted by Śabara, or as Bodhāyana (the Vṛttikāra of Rāmānuja's tradition), is disputed._
