# Upakosala Kāmalāyana

**Teachers:** [Satyakāma Jābāla](satyakama-jabala.md)

Pupil of Satyakāma who tended his teacher's fires for twelve years; the fires themselves taught him 'breath is brahman, ka is brahman, kha is brahman' before his teacher taught him the person in the eye (ChU 4.10-15).
