# Śrīkaṇṭha(nātha)

`tch:srikanthanatha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md), [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Students:** [Durvāsas](durvasas.md)

The form of Śiva who, wandering on Mount Kailāsa when the Śaiva secrets had been lost at the start of the Kali age, descended for grace and commanded the sage Durvāsas to keep the scripture from being cut off (Śivadṛṣṭi 7.107-110).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A form of Śiva himself.

_Notes: Distinct from tch:srikantha (the Śaiva Vedāntin) and tch:srikanthabhatta (Bhāskara's teacher)._
