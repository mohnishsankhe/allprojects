# Moggaliputta Tissa

`tch:moggaliputta-tissa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: 218 years after the parinibbāna (c. 325 BCE on the tradition's count); Scholarly account: 3rd c. BCE, if historical; the council is not mentioned in Asoka's inscriptions; (confidence low)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Students:** [Mahinda](mahinda.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Kathāvatthu](../texts/kathavatthu.md) — attribution: traditional

The elder who, by the Theravāda chronicles and commentaries, presided over the Third Council at Pāṭaliputta under Asoka, purified the Saṅgha, composed the Kathāvatthu and sent missions abroad.

_Notes: Account from the Dīpavaṃsa, Mahāvaṃsa and Samantapāsādikā; U37 owns the Kathāvatthu._
