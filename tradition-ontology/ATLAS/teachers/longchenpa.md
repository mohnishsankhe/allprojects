The 'Omniscient' systematizer of the Great Perfection: after monastic studies at Sangphu he received the Heart-essence from Kumārādza and the Khandro Nyingthig, and composed the Seven Treasuries, the Trilogies of Natural Ease, Self-Liberation and Dispelling Darkness, and the Nyingthig Yabzhi; he spent years in Bhutan in exile.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Reached the final vision of the exhaustion of phenomena; at his death the earth shook and relics appeared.
