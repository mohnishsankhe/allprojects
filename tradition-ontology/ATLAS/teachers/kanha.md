# Kāṇha (Kānipā / Kānifnāth)

`tch:kanha` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Alternate names:** Kānipā, Kānupā, Kānifnāth, Kānhapā, Kṛṣṇapāda, Kānerī (HYP 1.7?)
**Lineages:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), `lin:mahasiddha`
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Jālandharanātha](jalandharanatha.md)

U21 contribution: in Nāth tradition Kānipā/Kānupā is Jālandhara's disciple (Bengali saga) and, as Kānifnāth, one of the Marathi nine Nāths, with a samādhi shrine at Madhi (Ahilyanagar/Ahmednagar district). Whether this Nāth figure is the same as the Buddhist siddha Kāṇha / Kṛṣṇācārya is not settled.

_Notes: Registry id for the Buddhist mahāsiddha Kāṇha (U44). Identification with the Nāth Kānipā is the tradition's (shared siddha lists) and is disputed; HYP 1.7 'Kānerī' may or may not be the same figure._
