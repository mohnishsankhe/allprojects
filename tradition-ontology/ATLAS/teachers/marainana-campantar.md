# Maṟaiñāṉa Campantar

`tch:marainana-campantar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 13th c.; (confidence low)
**Places:** Peṇṇākaṭam, Chidambaram
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Aruṇanti Civācāriyār](arunanti.md)
**Students:** [Umāpati Civācāriyār](umapati-sivacarya.md)

Disciple of Aruṇanti and guru of Umāpati Civācāriyār; third of the four teachers of the outer lineage. No Meykaṇṭa treatise is his.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Seeing the Chidambaram priest Umāpati carried in a palanquin with a torch in daylight, he called out 'a day-blind man riding a dead log'; stung, Umāpati followed him and became his disciple, eating food from his hand, for which the Tillai priests excluded him.

_Notes: Details of his caste and the palanquin story are given at low confidence (they come from later hagiography)._
