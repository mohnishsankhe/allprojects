# Nivṛttināth

`tch:nivrttinatha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Nivṛtti, Nivṛttidev
**Lineages:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), `lin:varkari`
**Dates:** Tradition's account: 1273–1297 (tradition); Scholarly account: late 13th c.; (confidence moderate)
**Places:** Āpegāv, Āḷandī, Tryambakeśvar
**Historicity:** historical

**Works:** 
  - [Jñāneśvarī (Bhāvārthadīpikā)](../texts/jnanesvari.md) — attribution: doubtful

Elder brother and guru of Jñāneśvar, initiated in the Nāth line by Gahinīnāth; Jñāneśvar calls himself 'Nivṛtti's servant' (nivṛttidāsu) throughout the Jñāneśvarī. Abhaṅgas are ascribed to him.

_Notes: Works field notes only that the Jñāneśvarī is dedicated to him; he is not its author._
