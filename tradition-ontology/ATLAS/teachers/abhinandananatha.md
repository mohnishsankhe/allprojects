The fourth Tīrthaṅkara of the present descending half-cycle (avasarpiṇī). Emblem (lāñchana): the monkey. Parents: Saṃvara and Siddhārthā. Born at Ayodhyā; attained nirvāṇa at Sammeda Śikhara.

_Notes: Parents, birthplaces and emblems follow the common Śvetāmbara lists (e.g. Hemacandra); Digambara lists agree in most points and differ in some emblems and names._
