First of the five founding masters of the Sakya; son of Khön Könchok Gyalpo; at twelve he received Parting from the Four Attachments from Mañjuśrī in a vision; he received the Lamdre from Zhangtön Chöbar, and later the tradition says Virūpa appeared to him and gave him the Lamdre directly (the 'short lineage'); he wrote eleven commentaries on the Vajra Verses.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Received the four lines of Parting from the Four Attachments from Mañjuśrī at twelve; later Virūpa appeared to him in visions over a month and transmitted the Lamdre and many instructions.
