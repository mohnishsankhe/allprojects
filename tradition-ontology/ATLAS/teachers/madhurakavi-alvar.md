# Madhurakavi Āḻvār

`tch:madhurakavi-alvar` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Madhurakavi
**Lineages:** [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: early Kali age (hagiographies); Scholarly account: c. 9th c. CE; (confidence low)
**Places:** Tirukkōḷūr, Kurukūr
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Nammāḻvār](nammalvar.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Kaṇṇinuṇ Ciṟuttāmpu](../texts/kanninun-ciruttampu.md) — attribution: accepted

Disciple of Nammāḻvār and author of the eleven-verse Kaṇṇinuṇ Ciṟuttāmpu, addressed to his teacher rather than to Viṣṇu: 'I know no other god'. The model of devotion to the teacher as sufficient (ācārya-niṣṭhā); he recorded Nammāḻvār's hymns.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A brāhmaṇa of Tirukkōḷūr who, while at Ayodhyā on pilgrimage, saw a light in the southern sky, followed it to Kurukūr and found the silent Nammāḻvār under the tamarind; tested him with a riddle, received his answer, and served him for the rest of his life.
