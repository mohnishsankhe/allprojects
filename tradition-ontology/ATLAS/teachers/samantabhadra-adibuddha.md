# Samantabhadra (Kun tu bzang po), the primordial buddha

`tch:samantabhadra-adibuddha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Kuntuzangpo, Kun tu bzang po, the Primordial Protector (gdod ma'i mgon po), Kuntu Zangpo (Bön)
**Lineages:** [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md), [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md), [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Works:** 
  - [Kunjed Gyalpo (chos thams cad rdzogs pa chen po byang chub kyi sems kun byed rgyal po, the All-Creating King)](../texts/kunjed-gyalpo.md) — attribution: traditional
  - [Kuntuzangpo Mönlam (kun tu bzang po'i smon lam stobs po che, the Powerful Aspiration of Samantabhadra)](../texts/kuntuzangpo-monlam.md) — attribution: traditional

In the Nyingma and Bön the dharmakāya primordial buddha, depicted naked and sky-blue in union with Samantabhadrī: the ground recognized as itself. He is the teacher of the Kunjed Gyalpo and the Seventeen Tantras and speaks the Aspiration of Samantabhadra. Distinct from the bodhisattva Samantabhadra of the sūtras (tch:samantabhadra).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Primordially liberated by recognizing the ground-appearances as his own display, 'without doing even an instant of virtue', and so never entering saṃsāra.
