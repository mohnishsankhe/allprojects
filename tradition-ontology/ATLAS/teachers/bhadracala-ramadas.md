17th-c. Telugu revenue official of Golconda who spent state revenue on the Rāma temple at Bhadrācalam, was imprisoned, and — by tradition — freed when Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa paid the sultan in gold; author of the Dāśarathi Śatakam and kīrtanas.

_Notes: A tradition that Kabīr initiated him is chronologically impossible; recorded only as the tradition's account._
