First Chinese and twenty-eighth Indian patriarch of Chan. In the tradition's account a South Indian prince and disciple of Prajñātāra who came to China, told Emperor Wu of Liang that his merit was 'no merit' and the holy truth 'vast emptiness, nothing holy', sat facing a wall for nine years at Shaolin, and transmitted robe and Dharma to Huike. The earliest source (Xu gaoseng zhuan, 645) describes a South Indian brahmin teaching 'wall-gazing' and the four practices in the Northern Wei.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Heir of Prajñātāra in the 28-generation Indian line; brought the mind-seal east; 'wall-gazing Brahmin'.
