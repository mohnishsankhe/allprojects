# Svāmī Haridās

`tch:svami-haridas` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md), [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md)

Singer-saint of Vṛndāvana (16th c.), founder of the Haridāsī (Sakhī) sampradāya, who sang the eternal pair's play in the Kelimāl; at his singing in Nidhivan the image of Bāṅke Bihārī appeared. Revered as Tānsen's teacher; the Emperor Akbar is said to have visited him in disguise to hear him. His tradition holds him to be the sakhī Lalitā.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Bāṅke Bihārī manifested before him; he is Lalitā sakhī.

_Notes: The guru Āśudhīra is given in the Nimbārkī account; the independent Haridāsī account differs._
