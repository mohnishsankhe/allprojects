# Nāgārjuna (the siddha, alchemist and physician)

`tch:nagarjuna-siddha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md), [The school of Dhanvantari (Dhānvantara-sampradāya, the surgeons' line)](../lineages/dhanvantari-sampradaya.md), [Rasa Śāstra (the science of mercury) and the Raseśvara-darśana](../lineages/rasa-sastra.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Suśruta Saṃhitā](../texts/susruta-samhita.md) — attribution: doubtful
  - [Rasendramaṅgala](../texts/rasendramangala.md) — attribution: traditional

Named by Ḍalhaṇa as the redactor of the Suśruta Saṃhitā; counted among the twenty-seven siddhas of rasa (Rasaratnasamuccaya 1.4) and cited in the Rasaratnākara (Rasa-khaṇḍa 1.17) as having taught for the sick; alchemical works (Rasendramaṅgala) are attributed to him.

_Notes: Tibetan and some Indian traditions identify him with the Madhyamaka philosopher (tch:nagarjuna); scholars usually distinguish one or more later Nāgārjunas. Kept separate here._
