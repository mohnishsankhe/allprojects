U44 contribution (the siddha of the eighty-four): No. 16 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). Ordained at Nālandā, he mastered alchemy, fed the saṅgha in famine by turning iron into gold, received the Prajñāpāramitā from the nāgas, and in the end gave his own head to a prince; the tradition identifies him with the Madhyamaka master and with the author of the Pañcakrama.

Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 16): One who has not realized should not engage in the (special) conduct: if he does, it is like a kingdom seized by its subjects; one who has realized should not act like one who has not: if he does, it is like an elephant caught in a swamp.
