# Kauṇḍinya (Sāṃkhya teacher)

`tch:kaundinya-samkhya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Historicity:** unknown

Named in the Yuktidīpikā's list of teachers through whom the doctrine passed between Pañcaśikha and Īśvarakṛṣṇa (on SK 71: Hārīta, Vāddhali, Kairāta, Paurika, Ṛṣabheśvara, Pañcādhikaraṇa, Patañjali, Vārṣagaṇya, Kauṇḍinya, Mūka and others). Distinct from the Pāśupata commentator Kauṇḍinya (tch:kaundinya).
