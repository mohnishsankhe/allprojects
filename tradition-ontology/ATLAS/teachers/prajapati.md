# Prajāpati (as teacher)

`tch:prajapati` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md), [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Teachers:** [Brahmā (as first teacher)](brahma.md)
**Students:** [Indra (as student and teacher)](indra.md), [Virocana](virocana.md), [Manu Vaivasvata](manu-vaivasvata.md)

Lord of creatures, teacher of gods, humans and asuras: teaches 'da' (BAU 5.2); teaches Indra and Virocana the self free from evil over 101 years (ChU 8.7-12); link in teacher-lines (ChU 3.11.4, 8.15.1); in MaiU 2 he teaches the Vālakhilyas.

_Notes: Linked in BhG ch. 1–3 at 3.10, 3.11, 3.12._
