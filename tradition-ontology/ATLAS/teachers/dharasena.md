# Dharasena

`tch:dharasena` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: c. 683 years after the nirvāṇa; (confidence low)
**Places:** Girinagar (Girnār)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Students:** [Puṣpadanta (ācārya)](puspadanta-acarya.md), [Bhūtabali](bhutabali.md)

Digambara teacher at the Candra cave of Girinagar (Girnār) who knew part of the Pūrvas; fearing the loss of scripture he summoned two monks, tested them with defective spells which they corrected, and taught them the Karmaprābhṛta, which they set down as the Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama.
