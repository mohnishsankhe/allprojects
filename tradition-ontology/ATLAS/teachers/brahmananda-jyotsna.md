# Brahmānanda (author of the Jyotsnā)

Author of the Jyotsnā, the standard Sanskrit commentary on the Haṭhapradīpikā. He names Meruśāstrin as the prompter of his work and reads haṭha through Pātañjala yoga and Advaita: haṭha = union of sun and moon (prāṇa and apāna); rājayoga = asamprajñāta samādhi.

_Notes: Not the Advaitin Gauḍa Brahmānanda Sarasvatī (tch:brahmananda-sarasvati)._
