# Pāyāsi

`tch:payasi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary

A chieftain (rājañña) of Setavyā in Kosala who held that there is no other world, no spontaneously born beings and no fruit of deeds, and tested it on condemned criminals; converted by Kumāra Kassapa, he was reborn as a minor deity (DN 23).
