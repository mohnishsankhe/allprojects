# Vimalanātha

**Alternate names:** Vimala
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: the thirteenth of the twenty-four Jinas of this era (tradition's chronology of immense lifespans and intervals); (confidence low)
**Places:** Kāmpilya, Sammeda Śikhara
**Historicity:** legendary

The thirteenth Tīrthaṅkara of the present descending half-cycle (avasarpiṇī). Emblem (lāñchana): the boar. Parents: Kṛtavarman and Śyāmā. Born at Kāmpilya; attained nirvāṇa at Sammeda Śikhara. The Tamil Mērumantara Purāṇam makes Meru and Mandara his gaṇadharas.

_Notes: Parents, birthplaces and emblems follow the common Śvetāmbara lists (e.g. Hemacandra); Digambara lists agree in most points and differ in some emblems and names._
