# Paramārthasamudgata

`tch:paramarthasamudgata` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Shengyi sheng 勝義生
**Lineages:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
**Historicity:** legendary

The bodhisattva who, in Saṃdhinirmocana ch. 7, asks the intent of 'all dharmas lack own-being' and hears the three naturelessnesses and the three turnings.
