# Vindhyavāsin

`tch:vindhyavasin` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Vindhyavāsa
**Lineages:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 4th–5th c. CE (uncertain); (confidence low)
**Historicity:** historical

Sāṃkhya teacher whose distinctive views the Yuktidīpikā records: from mahat arise six non-specifics (ahaṃkāra and the five subtle elements); the organs are eleven and all-pervading; the functions of saṅkalpa, abhimāna and adhyavasāya are one; there is no subtle body — birth is the organs' functioning at the seed-place and death its abandonment; even the supreme sage's knowledge is acquired, not innate. Kumārila reports that he did not accept an intermediate body. Paramārtha's Life of Vasubandhu tells that the Sāṃkhya master Vindhyavāsa, pupil of Vṛṣagaṇa, defeated Vasubandhu's teacher Buddhamitra in debate before a king at Ayodhyā.
