# Jaigīṣavya

`tch:jaigisavya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md), [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md), [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Students:** [Asita Devala](asita-devala.md)

Ancient yoga master cited in the Yoga-bhāṣya: on mastery of the senses he holds that it is non-apprehension arising from the mind's one-pointedness (YBh 2.55); questioned by Āvaṭya (YBh 3.18), he declares everything he experienced in all births to be suffering, and the unsurpassed happiness of contentment to be suffering compared with the happiness of kaivalya.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Through direct perception of his saṃskāras he saw the sequence of his births through ten great creation-cycles, and knowledge born of discrimination arose in him (YBh 3.18).
