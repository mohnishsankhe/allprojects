Kashmiri Naiyāyika, author of the Nyāyasāra and its auto-commentary Nyāyabhūṣaṇa, known for independent positions: only three means of knowledge, and liberation as the cessation of pain qualified by the experience of eternal bliss. A Śaiva of Pāśupata orientation.
