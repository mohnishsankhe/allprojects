# Śākaṭāyana (the ancient grammarian)

`tch:sakatayana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md), [Nairukta school (the Vedic etymologists)](../lineages/nairukta.md), [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary

Pre-Pāṇinian grammarian cited by Yāska and Pāṇini (e.g. 3.4.111); author of the thesis that all nouns derive from verbs (Nirukta 1.12). Distinct from the Jain grammarian Pālyakīrti who took his name.
