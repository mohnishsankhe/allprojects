Pupil of Vīrasena, preceptor of the Rāṣṭrakūṭa king Amoghavarṣa I; completed the Jayadhavalā (837 CE) and wrote the Ādipurāṇa, which defines Jain householder rites and the Jain brāhmaṇa; also the Pārśvābhyudaya.
