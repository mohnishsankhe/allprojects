# Bhaṭṭa Nāyaka

**Lineages:** [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md), [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)

**Historicity:** historical
**Works:** 
  - [Hṛdayadarpaṇa of Bhaṭṭa Nāyaka](../texts/hrdayadarpana.md) — attribution: accepted

Kashmiri theorist (Hṛdayadarpaṇa, lost) who held that rasa is neither produced, inferred nor suggested but 'enjoyed': poetic language 'makes present' (bhāvakatva) the situation in universal form and 'makes it enjoyable' (bhojakatva) in a state of rest in sattva, akin to tasting the supreme Brahman.

_Notes: Known through Abhinavagupta's and Mahimabhaṭṭa's reports._
