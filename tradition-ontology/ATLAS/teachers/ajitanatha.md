# Ajitanātha

`tch:ajitanatha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Ajita
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: the second of the twenty-four Jinas of this era (tradition's chronology of immense lifespans and intervals); (confidence low)
**Places:** Ayodhyā, Sammeda Śikhara
**Historicity:** legendary

The second Tīrthaṅkara of the present descending half-cycle (avasarpiṇī). Emblem (lāñchana): the elephant. Parents: King Jitaśatru and Queen Vijayā. Born at Ayodhyā; attained nirvāṇa at Sammeda Śikhara. His cousin Sagara was the second world-emperor (cakravartin).

_Notes: Parents, birthplaces and emblems follow the common Śvetāmbara lists (e.g. Hemacandra); Digambara lists agree in most points and differ in some emblems and names._
