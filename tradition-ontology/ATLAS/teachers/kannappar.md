# Kaṇṇappa Nāyaṉār

`tch:kannappar` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Tiṇṇaṉ (birth name), Kaṇṇappar
**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Kāḷahasti
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. A hunter who worshipped the liṅga of Kāḷahasti with meat he had tasted, water carried in his mouth and flowers from his hair, to the dismay of the temple priest Civakōcariyār; when blood flowed from the liṅga's eye he plucked out his own eye and set it there; when the other eye bled he placed his foot on it to mark the spot and began to cut out his second eye — Śiva stopped his hand crying 'Stay, Kaṇṇappa!' and took him to his side.
