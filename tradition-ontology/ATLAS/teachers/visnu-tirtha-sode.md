# Viṣṇu Tīrtha (Sode)

`tch:visnu-tirtha-sode` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md), [The eight maṭhas of Uḍupi (aṣṭa-maṭha)](../lineages/udupi-asta-matha.md)
**Places:** Uḍupi
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Madhva](madhva.md)

Disciple of Madhva and, in the tradition's account, founder of the Sode maṭha, one of the eight maṭhas of Uḍupi that take turns in the worship of Kṛṣṇa. He was Madhva's younger brother; the tradition also derives the Subrahmaṇya maṭha from him and remembers his austerities.
