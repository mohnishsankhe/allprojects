Indian paṇḍita invited to Tibet under Trisong Detsen; he translated the Guhyagarbha and mind-series texts and taught the Heart-essence (Vima Nyingthig) to the king and to Nyang Tingdzin Zangpo, then went to Wutaishan in China. Author of the Simultaneous Entry meditation text in the Tengyur (Tōh 3910).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Attained the great transference rainbow body ('ja' lus 'pho ba chen po).
