No. 72 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A father whose sons had died; he carried a skull in his grief and was taught by Kāṇha to meditate on the skull and bone ornaments as symbols.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A father whose sons had died; he carried a skull in his grief and was taught by Kāṇha to meditate on the skull and bone ornaments as symbols. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 72): All things are the non-dual Vajradhara; ornaments, the skull and the rest are adventitious; the delusion of grasping 'I', too, is not found when one searches for it oneself.
