# Linji Yixuan 臨濟義玄

**Alternate names:** Rinzai Gigen (Jp.), Imje Uihyeon (Kr.), Huizhao chanshi 慧照禪師
**Lineages:** [Linji house](../lineages/linji.md), [Hongzhou school](../lineages/hongzhou.md), [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md), [Rinzai Zen](../lineages/rinzai.md)

Founder of the Linji house in Zhenzhou (Hebei); heir of Huangbo. Taught the true person of no rank, independence from all names, 'be master wherever you are', and used the shout; his sayings are the Linji lu.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Three times he asked Huangbo the great meaning of the buddha-dharma and three times was struck; sent to Dayu and told Huangbo had been 'grandmotherly', he awoke and said 'there isn't much to Huangbo's buddha-dharma' (Linji lu, record of pilgrimage).
