# Umā Haimavatī

`tch:uma-haimavati` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Students:** [Indra (as student and teacher)](indra.md)

The very beautiful woman who appears to Indra in the sky where the yakṣa vanished and tells him it was brahman, in whose victory the gods had been glorifying themselves (Kena 3.12-4.1).

_Notes: Later Śākta and Śaiva readers identify her with the Goddess Umā/Pārvatī, daughter of Himavat._
