# Uddālaka Āruṇi

`tch:uddalaka-aruni` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Āruṇi, Gautama, Uddālaka
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md)
**Places:** Kuru-Pañcāla, Madra (studied there, BAU 3.7.1)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Patañcala Kāpya](patancala-kapya.md), [Pravāhaṇa Jaivali](pravahana-jaivali.md), [Aśvapati Kaikeya](asvapati-kaikeya.md), [Citra Gāṅgyāyani](citra-gangyayani.md)
**Students:** [Śvetaketu Āruṇeya](svetaketu.md), [Yājñavalkya](yajnavalkya.md)

Brahmin teacher of the Gautama clan, father of Śvetaketu: teaches the doctrine of being (sat) with the refrain tat tvam asi (ChU 6); questions Yājñavalkya on the thread and the inner controller (BAU 3.7); goes as a pupil to Pravāhaṇa Jaivali (BAU 6.2; ChU 5.3), to Aśvapati Kaikeya (ChU 5.11) and to Citra (KauU 1.1).

_Notes: ChU 3.11.4 says his father taught him the honey-doctrine as the eldest son._
