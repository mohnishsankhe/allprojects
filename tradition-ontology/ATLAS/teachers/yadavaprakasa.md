Advaita-leaning teacher of Kāñcīpuram under whom Rāmānuja first studied; taught svābhāvika bhedābheda (Brahman itself becomes Īśvara, souls and matter). His Brahma Sūtra commentary is lost; the Yatidharmasamuccaya and the lexicon Vaijayantī are attributed to him.
**Realization — the tradition's account (Śrīvaiṣṇava):** Śrīvaiṣṇava hagiography (Guruparamparāprabhāva) says he disagreed with Rāmānuja's readings, plotted against him on a pilgrimage, and later became his disciple as the renouncer Govinda Jīyar.
