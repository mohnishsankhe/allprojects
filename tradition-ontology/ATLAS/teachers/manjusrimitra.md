Indian scholar who came to debate Garab Dorje, was defeated, became his disciple for many years, and arranged the Dzogchen teachings into three series (mind, space, instruction); author of the Gold Refined from Ore (Tōh 2591).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Received Garab Dorje's testament; his own testament was the Six Meditation Experiences.
