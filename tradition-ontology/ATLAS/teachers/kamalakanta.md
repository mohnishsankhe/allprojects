Bengali Śākta poet and sādhaka of Ambikā-Kālnā, later at Burdwan under Mahārāja Tejcandra; author of Śyāmā-saṅgīt to Kālī and Umā in the line of Rāmprasād, and of the Sādhaka-rañjana on tantric yoga.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The Mahārāja of Burdwan became his disciple after he showed that the Goddess answered his call.
