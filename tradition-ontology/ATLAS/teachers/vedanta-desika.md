Veṅkaṭanātha of Tūppul near Kāñcī, poet, logician and theologian; the Vaṭakalai regard him as their doctrinal source. He wrote over a hundred works in Sanskrit, Maṇipravāḷa and Tamil — the Rahasyatrayasāra, Tattvamuktākalāpa, Śatadūṣaṇī, Nyāyasiddhāñjana, Nyāyapariśuddhi, Seśvaramīmāṃsā, Pādukāsahasra, Saṅkalpasūryodaya and many hymns — and lived through the sack of Śrīraṅgam.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The tradition holds him an incarnation of the bell of Veṅkaṭeśvara; the titles Vedāntācārya and Sarvatantrasvatantra were conferred on him by Raṅganātha and Raṅganāyakī; Hayagrīva, whose mantra he received from Garuḍa, granted him knowledge; he declined royal patronage.
