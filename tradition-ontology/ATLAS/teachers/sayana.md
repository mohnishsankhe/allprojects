Sāyaṇa (Sāyaṇācārya), minister of the early Vijayanagara kings and director of the great series of Vedic commentaries (Vedārthaprakāśa) on the Saṃhitās, Brāhmaṇas and Āraṇyakas, which explain the hymns chiefly by their ritual use; also author of digests (the 'Sudhānidhi' works).

_Notes: Brother of Mādhava, whom the tradition identifies with Vidyāraṇya (tch:vidyaranya) — identification disputed. Birth year unknown; 'from' is a rough estimate._
