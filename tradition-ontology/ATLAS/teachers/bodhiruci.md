North Indian monk in Luoyang (arrived 508) who translated the ten-fascicle Laṅkāvatāra, the Vajracchedikā, Vasubandhu's Daśabhūmika commentary and the Anūnatvāpūrṇatvanirdeśa.

_Notes: Distinct from the Tang translator Bodhiruci (d. 727) who compiled the Ratnakūṭa (T310)._
