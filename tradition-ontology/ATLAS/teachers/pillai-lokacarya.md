Eldest son of Vaṭakku Tiruvīti Piḷḷai, named after Nampiḷḷai (Lokācārya); a lifelong celibate who wrote the eighteen rahasya works in Maṇipravāḷa, above all the Śrīvacanabhūṣaṇam, Mumukṣuppaṭi, Tattvatrayam and Arthapañcakam; the Teṅkalai regard him as their doctrinal source.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The Teṅkalai tradition holds that Raṅganātha himself, in the form of a teacher, instructed him, and that he taught the secret doctrine openly out of compassion.
