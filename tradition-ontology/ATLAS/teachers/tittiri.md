# Tittiri

`tch:tittiri` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Taittirīya śākhā (Black Yajurveda)](../lineages/sakha-taittiriya.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Vaiśampāyana](vaisampayana.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Taittirīya Brāhmaṇa](../texts/taittiriya-brahmana.md) — attribution: traditional

Eponym of the Taittirīya school. The Purāṇa derives the name from Vaiśampāyana's pupils who became partridges (tittiri) to take up the disgorged Yajus (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.5.13); the grammatical tradition derives it from a teacher named Tittiri (Pāṇini 4.3.102).
