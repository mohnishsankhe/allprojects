# Vāsupūjya

**Alternate names:** Vāsupūjyasvāmī
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: the twelfth of the twenty-four Jinas of this era (tradition's chronology of immense lifespans and intervals); (confidence low)
**Places:** Campā, Campā
**Historicity:** legendary

The twelfth Tīrthaṅkara of the present descending half-cycle (avasarpiṇī). Emblem (lāñchana): the buffalo. Parents: Vasupūjya and Jayā. Born at Campā; attained nirvāṇa at Campā. The only Tīrthaṅkara to attain nirvāṇa at Campā; his body is described as red; Śvetāmbara accounts say he did not marry or rule.

_Notes: Parents, birthplaces and emblems follow the common Śvetāmbara lists (e.g. Hemacandra); Digambara lists agree in most points and differ in some emblems and names._
