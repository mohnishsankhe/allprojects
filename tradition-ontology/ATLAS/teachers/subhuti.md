# Subhūti

`tch:subhuti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Xuputi 須菩提, Rab 'byor
**Lineages:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Gotama Buddha](gotama-buddha.md)

Disciple of the Buddha, foremost of those who dwell in peace (araṇāvihārin); in the Perfection of Wisdom sūtras he teaches the perfection of wisdom through the Buddha's power and is the Buddha's interlocutor in the Vajracchedikā, where he weeps on hearing it.
**Realization — the tradition's account (as a figure of the sūtras):** An arhat declared foremost in dwelling without conflict; in the Vajracchedikā §9 he says he does not think 'I am an arhat free of passion'.

_Notes: AN 1 Etadaggavagga (the 'foremost' lists)_
