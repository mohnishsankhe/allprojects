# Pratardana Daivodāsi

`tch:pratardana-daivodasi` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Indra (as student and teacher)](indra.md)

Son of Divodāsa, who reached Indra's abode by fighting and valour and asked Indra for the boon most beneficial to humans; taught that Indra is breath, the intelligent self (KauU 3.1-2); the 'inner agnihotra of Pratardana' is named after him (KauU 2.5).
