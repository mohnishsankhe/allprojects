# Samādhi (the merchant)

`tch:samadhi-vaisya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Medhas](medhas.md)

A merchant cast out by his greedy family who still could not stop worrying about them; with Suratha he heard the Devī Māhātmya, and asked the Goddess not for wealth but for the knowledge that frees from attachment, which she granted (DM 13).
