# Paraśurāma (Jāmadagnya Bhārgava)

**Lineages:** [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md), [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md), [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

**Works:** 
  - [Paraśurāma Kalpasūtra](../texts/parasurama-kalpasutra.md) — attribution: traditional

The avatāra-sage, son of Jamadagni and Reṇukā, who in the Śrīvidyā tradition is the chief disciple of Mahādeva and author of the Kalpasūtra, and who in the Tripurā Rahasya receives the teaching of Tripurā from Dattātreya.
