# Sanandana

`tch:sanandana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Sanandana Ācārya, Sananda
**Lineages:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md), [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Sanandana Saṃhitā (one of the five Śubhāgamas)](../texts/sanandana-samhita-subhagama.md) — attribution: traditional

Named by the Sāṃkhya Sūtra (6.69): 'the (owner–owned relation of puruṣa and prakṛti) is caused by the subtle body, says the teacher Sanandana'. Gauḍapāda (on SK 1, 43) names Sanaka, Sanandana (Sananda), Sanātana and Sanatkumāra among Brahmā's sons, in whom the dispositions arose naturally at sixteen.

_Notes: Whether the SS's teacher is the Kumāra of Purāṇic lore is not stated in the text._
