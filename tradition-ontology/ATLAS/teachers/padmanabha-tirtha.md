Disciple of Madhva and first pontiff after him in the line that became the Uttarādi Maṭha; author of the Sattarkadīpāvalī (on the Brahmasūtrabhāṣya) and Nyāyaratnāvalī (on the Anuvyākhyāna). In the tradition's account he had been a learned opponent (named Śobhana Bhaṭṭa) before becoming Madhva's disciple.
