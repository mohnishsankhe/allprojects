# Piṅgala (Pāśupata tīrthakara)

`tch:pingala-pasupata` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Alternate names:** Piṅgalākṣa (Rājaśekhara)
**Lineages:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Historicity:** unknown

Number 12 of the eighteen avatāras / tīrthakaras of Śiva worshipped by the Śaiva (Pāśupata-Naiyāyika) ascetics according to the Jain doxographers Guṇaratna (Tarkarahasyadīpikā) and Rājaśekhara (Ṣaḍdarśanasamuccaya, 1348). Nothing else is recorded.

_Notes: Known only from the Jain lists; not to be conflated with the Vedic/Purāṇic sage of the same name._
