# Paesi (Pradeśin)

`tch:paesi` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Pradeśī, Paesī
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Keśin (Keśikumāra Śramaṇa)](kesi-kumara.md)

King of Śvetavikā who doubted the soul, tested it by experiments on condemned criminals, and was convinced by Keśin that the soul is distinct from the body (Rājapraśnīya); reborn as the god Sūryābha.

_Notes: Parallels King Pāyāsi of the Pali Pāyāsi Sutta (DN 23)._
