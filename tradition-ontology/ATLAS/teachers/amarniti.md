# Amarnīti Nāyaṉār

`tch:amarniti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Paḻaiyāṟai
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. A merchant who gave loincloths to devotees; a loincloth left with him by Śiva disguised as a celibate vanished, and he offered its weight in cloth, then all his wealth — the scale balanced only when he, his wife and son climbed on it, and they were taken to Śiva's world.
