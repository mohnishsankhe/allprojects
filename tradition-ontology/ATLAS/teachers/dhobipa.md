**Realization — the tradition's account:** A washerman taught to see his washing as the purification of mind. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 28): Though one washes it for a long time, charcoal does not turn white by nature; the nature, wholly pure emptiness, is pure; the guru's instruction is the best washer of the nature.
