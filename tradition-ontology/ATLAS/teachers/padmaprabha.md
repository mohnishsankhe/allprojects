# Padmaprabha

**Alternate names:** Padmaprabhu
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: the sixth of the twenty-four Jinas of this era (tradition's chronology of immense lifespans and intervals); (confidence low)
**Places:** Kauśāmbī, Sammeda Śikhara
**Historicity:** legendary

The sixth Tīrthaṅkara of the present descending half-cycle (avasarpiṇī). Emblem (lāñchana): the red lotus. Parents: Dhara (Śrīdhara) and Susīmā. Born at Kauśāmbī; attained nirvāṇa at Sammeda Śikhara. His body is described as red in colour.

_Notes: Parents, birthplaces and emblems follow the common Śvetāmbara lists (e.g. Hemacandra); Digambara lists agree in most points and differ in some emblems and names._
