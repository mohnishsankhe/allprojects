# Parañcōti Muṉivar (guru of Meykaṇṭār)

`tch:parancoti-munivar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Satyajñānadarśin](satyajnanadarsin.md)
**Students:** [Meykaṇṭār](meykantar.md)

The sage from Kailāsa who, in the tradition's account, taught the child Meykaṇṭār; last of the 'inner' lineage (akaccantāṉam) of Nandi, Sanatkumāra and Satyajñānadarśin.

_Notes: Distinct from Parañcōti Muṉivar, author of the Tiruviḷaiyāṭal Purāṇam (tch:parancoti-tiruvilaiyatal)._
