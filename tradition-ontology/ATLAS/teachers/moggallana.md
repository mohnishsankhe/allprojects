# Mahāmoggallāna

`tch:moggallana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Moggallāna, Kolita
**Lineages:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Gotama Buddha](gotama-buddha.md), [Sañjaya Belaṭṭhiputta](sanjaya-belatthiputta.md)

The left-hand chief disciple, foremost in psychic power; Kolita, Sāriputta's friend, converted with him; the Buddha instructed him against drowsiness (AN 7.61); visits to heavens and hells in the Vimānavatthu and Petavatthu; by the commentarial account killed by bandits shortly before the Buddha's death.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Attained arahantship seven days after ordination after the Buddha's instruction on drowsiness (AN 7.61, commentarial setting).

_Notes: AN 1 Etadaggavagga (the 'foremost' lists)_
