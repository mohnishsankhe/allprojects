# Suvidhinātha (Puṣpadanta)

**Alternate names:** Puṣpadanta (his other name), Suvidhi
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: the ninth of the twenty-four Jinas of this era (tradition's chronology of immense lifespans and intervals); (confidence low)
**Places:** Kākandī, Sammeda Śikhara
**Historicity:** legendary

The ninth Tīrthaṅkara of the present descending half-cycle (avasarpiṇī). Emblem (lāñchana): the makara (crocodile / sea-monster). Parents: Sugrīva and Rāmā. Born at Kākandī; attained nirvāṇa at Sammeda Śikhara. His body is described as white. Not to be confused with the Digambara author Puṣpadanta (tch:puspadanta-acarya).

_Notes: Parents, birthplaces and emblems follow the common Śvetāmbara lists (e.g. Hemacandra); Digambara lists agree in most points and differ in some emblems and names._
