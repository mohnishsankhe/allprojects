# Pakudha Kaccāyana

`tch:pakudha-kaccayana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Pakudha Kātiyāna / Pakudhaka Kātiyāna (SN 2.30), Kakuda Kātyāyana (Skt.)
**Lineages:** [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 5th c. BCE (contemporary of the Buddha and Mahāvīra); (confidence low)
**Historicity:** historical

One of the six famous teachers. DN 2 reports his doctrine of seven bodies (kāya) — earth, water, fire, air, pleasure, pain and the soul — unmade, barren, firm as a pillar, unmoving, not affecting one another, so that one who cuts off a head with a sharp sword takes no life: the sword only passes through the space between the seven bodies.

_Notes: Doctrine reported_by_opponent. The Pali commentary's report that he avoided cold water is recalled with low confidence. His seven bodies reappear merged with niyati in Ja 545 and in the fourth 'negation' of MN 76._
