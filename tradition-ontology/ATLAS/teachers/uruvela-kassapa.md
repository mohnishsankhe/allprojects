# Uruvela Kassapa

`tch:uruvela-kassapa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Gotama Buddha](gotama-buddha.md)

Leader of a thousand matted-hair fire-worshipping ascetics at Uruvelā, converted with his brothers Nadī Kassapa and Gayā Kassapa; they heard the Fire Sermon at Gayāsīsa (Vin Mv 1.15–21).
