# Toṇṭaraṭippoṭi Āḻvār

Garland-maker of Śrīraṅgam whose name means 'dust of the feet of the Lord's servants'; author of the Tirumālai and the dawn-awakening Tiruppaḷḷiyeḻucci. After a fall into a courtesan's household he was restored by the Lord's grace and taught that the name is sweeter than Indra's kingdom and that devotees of any birth are to be honoured.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A brāhmaṇa devoted to making garlands for Raṅganātha, he was infatuated with the courtesan Dēvadēvi and lost all his wealth. The Lord, in the guise of a servant, sent her a golden vessel of the temple in Vipranārāyaṇa's name; he was arrested for its theft until the Lord revealed the truth in a dream to the king. Humbled, he returned to service and named himself dust of the feet of the devotees.
