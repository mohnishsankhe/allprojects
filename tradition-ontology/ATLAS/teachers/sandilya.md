# Śāṇḍilya

`tch:sandilya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md)
**Historicity:** legendary

Sage who relates the Jayākhya's teaching to other sages; a Pāñcarātra account quoted by Śaṅkara (BSBh 2.2.45) says he took up this teaching after not finding the highest good in the four Vedas.
