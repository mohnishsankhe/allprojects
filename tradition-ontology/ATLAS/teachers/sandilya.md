Named author of the Śāṇḍilya-vidyā: all this is brahman; the self within the heart, smaller than a grain and greater than the worlds, is brahman, which 'I shall attain on departing' (ChU 3.14.1-4).

_Notes: The same teaching appears in Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa 10.6.3 (moderate). Later works (Śāṇḍilya Bhakti Sūtra, Śāṇḍilya Upaniṣad) are attributed to a Śāṇḍilya; identity not asserted here._
