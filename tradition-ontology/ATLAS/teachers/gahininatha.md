# Gahinīnātha

`tch:gahininatha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Gahinī, Gaibīnāth, Gaininātha
**Lineages:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Gorakṣanātha](goraksanatha.md)
**Students:** [Nivṛttināth](nivrttinatha.md)

Nāth guru of Nivṛttināth in the Marathi tradition, counted among the nine Nāths (the Marathi list); tradition places his meeting with Nivṛtti at the Brahmagiri near Tryambakeśvar and a samādhi at Gahinīnāthgaḍ.
