The principal systematizer of Viśiṣṭādvaita: born at Śrīperumbūdūr, he renounced at Kāñcī, led the Śrīraṅgam temple and community, spent years at Melkote during the Cōḻa persecution, and wrote nine works — the Śrībhāṣya, Vedārthasaṅgraha, Vedāntasāra, Vedāntadīpa, Gītābhāṣya, the three Gadyas and the Nityagrantha.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Varadarāja answered his doubts through Tirukkacci Nambi; Tirukkōṭṭiyūr Nambi gave him the secret mantra after eighteen visits, and he proclaimed its meaning to all from the temple tower, accepting hell for himself if others could be saved; he surrendered to Śrī and Raṅganātha on Paṅguni Uttiram, and the Lord's reply is recorded in the Śaraṇāgati Gadya. The Śrīvaiṣṇava tradition regards him as an incarnation of Ādiśeṣa (and Lakṣmaṇa).
