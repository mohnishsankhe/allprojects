Tamil poet of the Irāmāvatāram (Kamba Rāmāyaṇam), the great devotional retelling of Rāma's story; by tradition he also sang the Caṭakōpar Antāti in praise of Nammāḻvār so that his Rāmāyaṇa would be accepted at Śrīraṅgam.
