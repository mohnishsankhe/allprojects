# Vālmīki (Sāṃkhya teacher)

`tch:valmiki-samkhya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Historicity:** unknown

Named in the Māṭhara Vṛtti (on SK 71) among those through whom the knowledge came from Pañcaśikha to Īśvarakṛṣṇa: Bhārgava, Ulūka, Vālmīki, Hārīta, Devala and others. No identification with the poet of the Rāmāyaṇa is implied.
