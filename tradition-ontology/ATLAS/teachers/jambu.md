# Jambū (Jambūsvāmin)

`tch:jambu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Jaṃbū, Jambūkumāra
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: liberated 64 years after Mahāvīra's nirvāṇa (Śvetāmbara); 62 years for the three omniscients (Digambara); (confidence low)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Sudharman](sudharman.md)
**Students:** [Prabhava](prabhava.md)

Pupil of Sudharman and the last person of this era to attain omniscience in Bharata-kṣetra: a merchant's son who renounced on his wedding night with his eight brides (and converted the thief Prabhava who came to rob them). After him, the Śvetāmbaras say, certain things were lost — including the naked Jinakalpa mode, the highest knowledges and liberation in this region. The Aṅgas are addressed to him.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The last omniscient (kevalin) of this era in Bharata-kṣetra.
