No. 56 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A man who wished to escape saṃsāra; taught to cross by the guru.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A man who wished to escape saṃsāra; taught to cross by the guru. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 56): Having wandered long in the ocean of saṃsāra since beginningless time, today by the supreme boat, the true guru, I crossed the ocean hard to cross.
