# Sanatsujāta

**Lineages:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

**Students:** [Dhṛtarāṣṭra](dhrtarastra.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Sanatsujātīya](../texts/sanatsujatiya.md) — attribution: traditional

An ever-youthful sage (kumāra) summoned by Vidura's thought, who teaches Dhṛtarāṣṭra that heedlessness is death and heedfulness immortality, and describes the brāhmaṇa, silence, the virtues and the shining Brahman seen by yogins (5.41–45).

_Notes: The tradition counts him among Brahmā's mind-born sons (not checked here)._
