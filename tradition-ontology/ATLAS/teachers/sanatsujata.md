An ever-youthful sage (kumāra) summoned by Vidura's thought, who teaches Dhṛtarāṣṭra that heedlessness is death and heedfulness immortality, and describes the brāhmaṇa, silence, the virtues and the shining Brahman seen by yogins (5.41–45).
