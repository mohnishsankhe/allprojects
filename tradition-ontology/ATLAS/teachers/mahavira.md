The twenty-fourth and last Tīrthaṅkara of this era, emblem the lion. Born Vardhamāna to Siddhārtha of the Jñātṛ (Nāya) clan and Triśalā at Kuṇḍagrāma near Vaiśālī; renounced at thirty; for twelve and a half years practised severe austerities in silence, enduring hardships and attacks (Ācārāṅga 1.9); attained omniscience; taught for thirty years, founding the fourfold community with eleven gaṇadharas headed by Indrabhūti Gautama; attained nirvāṇa at Pāvā aged seventy-two. Śvetāmbaras add that his embryo was transferred from the brāhmaṇī Devānandā to Triśalā, that his mother saw fourteen dreams (sixteen for Digambaras), and that he married Yaśodā and had a daughter, Priyadarśanā; Digambaras deny the transfer and the marriage. Known in the Pali canon as Nigaṇṭha Nātaputta.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** In the thirteenth year of his ascetic life, on the tenth of the bright half of Vaiśākha, outside Jṛmbhikagrāma on the bank of the Ṛjupālikā, under a śāla tree, squatting in the milking posture in the heat of the sun after a two-and-a-half-day fast without water, in deep (pure) meditation, he attained the infinite, supreme, unobstructed, complete and full knowledge and perception called kevala; at Pāvā, in his seventy-second year, he passed to nirvāṇa, freed from all suffering.
