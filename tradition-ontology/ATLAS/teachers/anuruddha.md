# Anuruddha

`tch:anuruddha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Gotama Buddha](gotama-buddha.md)

The Buddha's Sakyan cousin, foremost in the divine eye; practised with Nandiya and Kimbila (MN 31, MN 128); his eight thoughts of a great person (AN 8.30); present at the Buddha's parinibbāna (DN 16) and, by commentarial account, entrusted with the Aṅguttara.

_Notes: AN 1 Etadaggavagga (the 'foremost' lists). Distinct from the later Abhidhamma author tch:anuruddha-acariya._
