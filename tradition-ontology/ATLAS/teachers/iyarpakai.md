# Iyaṟpakai Nāyaṉār

`tch:iyarpakai` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Kāvirippūmpaṭṭiṉam
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. A merchant who never refused a devotee; when Śiva came as a brahmin and asked for his wife, he gave her, fought off his kinsmen who tried to stop them, and escorted the pair out of town — whereupon Śiva revealed himself.
