Vāc, daughter of the seer Ambhṛṇa, the ṛṣikā of RV 10.125, in which Speech proclaims herself the queen and self of all, moving with the gods, the power by which beings eat, see, breathe and hear, who makes whom she loves a brahman and a seer.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The Anukramaṇī gives the deity of her hymn as 'ātman', the seer praising herself as the self of all; later commentary (Sāyaṇa) presents her as a knower of Brahman who spoke from identity with it.
