# Viṭṭhalpant

`tch:vitthalpant` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Alternate names:** Viṭṭhalpant Kulkarṇī
**Lineages:** [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)
**Places:** Āpegāv, Āḷandī, Kāśī
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Students:** [Nivṛttināth](nivrttinath.md), [Jñāneśvar](jnanesvar.md)

Father of Nivṛttināth, Jñāneśvar, Sopāndev and Muktābāī: a brahmin of Āpegāv who took sannyāsa at Kāśī, returned to household life at his guru's command, and was outcast with his family; he and his wife Rukmiṇībāī are said to have given up their lives at Prayāga as expiation so that their children might be accepted.

_Notes: Name of his sannyāsa-guru differs between accounts (some say Rāmānanda / Śrīpād); not recorded here._
