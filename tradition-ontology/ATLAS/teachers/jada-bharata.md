# Jaḍa Bharata

`tch:jada-bharata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Bharata (son of Ṛṣabha), Bharata the dullard
**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Students:** [Rahūgaṇa](rahugana.md)

The exemplar of the power of the last thought (anta-smṛti) and of the avadhūta who hides his realization; teacher of Rahūgaṇa.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The king after whom Bhāratavarṣa is named retired to the forest, became attached to an orphaned fawn, died thinking of it and was reborn as a deer; then reborn as a brahmin who, remembering, lived as if dull and dumb, and taught the king of Sindhu-Sauvīra (Rahūgaṇa) the distinction of self and body (BhP 5.7-14; VP 2.13-16).
