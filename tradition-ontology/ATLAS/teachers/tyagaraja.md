Telugu singer-saint of Tiruvaiyāru (1767–1847), devotee of Rāma, composer of some seven hundred surviving kṛtis and two musical dramas; he declined the Tanjore court's patronage ('Is wealth happiness, or service near Rāma?') and is said to have taken sannyāsa before death.
