# Prabhāsa

`tch:prabhasa-ganadhara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Pabhāsa
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Mahāvīra (Vardhamāna)](mahavira.md)

One of the eleven gaṇadharas (chief disciples) of Mahāvīra, originally a learned brāhmaṇa who came to debate him at Madhyamā Pāvā and was converted when Mahāvīra resolved his hidden doubt — his doubt was whether liberation (nirvāṇa) exists.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Attained omniscience and liberation before or soon after Mahāvīra, according to the Śvetāmbara lists.

_Notes: The doubts are those of the gaṇadharavāda in the Viśeṣāvaśyaka Bhāṣya (moderate confidence on the pairing of each doubt with each name)._
