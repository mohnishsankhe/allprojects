# Ippen

Wandering holy man who gave up home and possessions ('the saint who casts off'), distributed nenbutsu talismans to over 250,000 people and led dancing nenbutsu across Japan; before dying he burned his books, saying all the teachings of his life had become 'Namu Amida Butsu'. Founder of the Ji-shū.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** At the Kumano Hongū in 1274 the deity (as Amida's manifestation) told him that birth had been settled for all by Amida's awakening ten kalpas ago and that he should distribute talismans regardless of faith or purity (Ippen Hijiri-e).
