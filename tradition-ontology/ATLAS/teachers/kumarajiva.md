Kuchean monk-translator (344-413 or 350-409) in Chang'an, whose Chinese versions of the Lotus, Vimalakīrti, Diamond, Smaller Sukhāvatīvyūha, Large and Small Perfection of Wisdom, Śūraṅgamasamādhi, Mūlamadhyamakakārikā and the Dazhidu lun became standard in East Asia.
