# Shaokang

`tch:shaokang` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** 少康, Hou Shandao 後善導 (the later Shandao)
**Lineages:** [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: d. 805 CE; (confidence moderate)
**Places:** Muzhou (Wulong shan)
**Historicity:** historical

Tang master of Muzhou (Zhejiang) called 'the later Shandao', who taught children to recite by paying them a coin per recitation and led large recitation assemblies; fifth patriarch in the Fozu tongji list.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** On reading a text of Shandao the image emitted light and he resolved on the Pure Land; when he recited, buddhas were seen issuing from his mouth (Song biographies, as recalled).

_Notes: The coin episode is widely told; recalled, moderate._
