# Tsangpa Gyare Yeshe Dorje (the first Gyalwang Drukpa)

**Alternate names:** gtsang pa rgya ras ye shes rdo rje
**Lineages:** [Drukpa Kagyu](../lineages/drukpa-kagyu.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 1161–1211; (confidence high)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Lingje Repa Pema Dorje](lingje-repa.md)
**Students:** [Gyalwa Götsangpa Gönpo Dorje](gotsangpa.md), [Gyalwa Lorepa Wangchuk Tsöndrü](lorepa.md)
**Works:** 
  - [The Six Cycles of Equal Taste](../texts/six-equal-tastes.md) — attribution: traditional

Founder of the Drukpa Kagyu at Ralung (1180) and Druk (1189); disciple of Lingje Repa; rediscoverer (by the tradition's account) of the Six Equal Tastes hidden by Rechungpa; the first of the Gyalwang Drukpa incarnations.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** At Namdruk he heard thunder and saw nine dragons rise into the sky, and named his lineage Drukpa, 'of the Dragon'.
