# Dhruva

`tch:dhruva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Teachers:** [Nārada](narada.md)

Son of King Uttānapāda; slighted by his stepmother, he went to the forest as a child, was initiated by Nārada (BhP 4.8) - or taught by the seven sages (VP 1.11-12) - and by austere meditation on Vāsudeva won the vision of Viṣṇu and the eternal position of the pole-star (dhruva-sthāna).
