# Bāhubali (Gommaṭeśvara)

`tch:bahubali` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Gommaṭa, Gommaṭeśvara, Bhujabali
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Places:** Takṣaśilā / Podanapura (his kingdom), Śravaṇabeḷagoḷa (image)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Ṛṣabha (Ṛṣabhanātha, Ādinātha)](rsabha.md)

Son of Ṛṣabha, who defeated his elder brother Bharata in single combat for the kingdom, then renounced in the moment of victory and stood motionless in meditation (kāyotsarga) for a year until creepers grew over him. Śvetāmbaras say his omniscience was delayed by pride until his sisters Brāhmī and Sundarī told him to 'get down from the elephant'; Digambaras say a lingering thought that he stood on Bharata's land delayed it. For Digambaras the first of this era to attain liberation. His 58-foot image (981 CE) at Śravaṇabeḷagoḷa is anointed in the twelve-yearly Mahāmastakābhiṣeka.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** He stood a full year in body-abandonment; when the last obstacle (pride, or the thought of his brother's land) fell away he attained omniscience.
