Late-Ming reviver of monastic discipline at Yunqi si who joined Chan and Pure Land, taught recitation of the name as the principal practice for all, promoted releasing living beings and lay self-examination ledgers, and wrote the Amitābha Sūtra commentary; eighth patriarch in the later Pure Land list; one of the four great masters of the late Ming.
