Mīmāṃsaka whose distinct account of how validity is known (through the after-cognition, anuvyavasāya) made him proverbially the founder of a 'third path'; fragments of his Tripādīnītinayana and (attributed) Aṅgatvanirukti survive.

_Notes: Modern literature numbers several Murāris inconsistently (II/III); low confidence on dating and on the second work._
