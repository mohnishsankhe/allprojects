# The eight Vāgdevatās (Vaśinī and the others)

`tch:vagdevatas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Works:** 
  - [Lalitā Sahasranāma](../texts/lalita-sahasranama.md) — attribution: traditional

The eight goddesses of speech — Vaśinī, Kāmeśvarī, Modinī, Vimalā, Aruṇā, Jayinī, Sarveśvarī and Kaulinī — who composed the Lalitā Sahasranāma at the Goddess's command and preside over the eight-cornered enclosure of the Śrīcakra; the Saundaryalaharī (17) makes them givers of poetic speech.
