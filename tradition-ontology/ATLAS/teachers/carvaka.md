# Cārvāka (the eponymous teacher)

`tch:carvaka` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Lineages:** [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Bṛhaspati](brhaspati.md)

The legendary disciple of Bṛhaspati after whom the school is named; in the Prabodhacandrodaya (Act 2) he is the servant of Great Delusion who spreads the Lokāyata through his pupils. Guṇaratna and others derive the name from 'chewing' (carv) — those who 'swallow' merit, demerit and the unseen — or from 'pleasing speech' (cāru-vāk).

_Notes: Distinct from tch:carvaka-raksasa of the Mahābhārata; the texts do not identify the two._
