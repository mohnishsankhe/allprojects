# Vasumitra

`tch:vasumitra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Shiyou 世友
**Lineages:** [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md), [Vaibhāṣika](../lineages/vaibhasika.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Presided over the council of Kaniṣka in Kashmir (Xuanzang); Scholarly account: c. 1st–2nd c. CE; (confidence low)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Prakaraṇapāda](../texts/prakaranapada.md) — attribution: traditional
  - [Dhātukāya](../texts/dhatukaya.md) — attribution: traditional
  - [Samayabhedoparacanacakra](../texts/samayabhedoparacanacakra.md) — attribution: traditional
  - [Abhidharma-mahāvibhāṣā-śāstra](../texts/mahavibhasa.md) — attribution: traditional

One of the four great masters of the Vibhāṣā, who held that dharmas are called past, present and future according to their state (avasthā) — the view Vasubandhu judged the best (AK 5.26). The tradition also credits a Vasumitra with the Prakaraṇapāda and the Samayabhedoparacanacakra; scholars usually distinguish several Vasumitras.

_Notes: Homonyms: scholars distinguish the Vibhāṣā master, the author of the Prakaraṇapāda and the doxographer; the tradition often identifies them. One id is used here with that caveat._
