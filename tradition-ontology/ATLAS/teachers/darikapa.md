# Dārikapa

**Alternate names:** Dārika, dA ri ka pa (Tibetan), King Indrapāla (per the lives)
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)

No. 77 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A king of Pāṭaliputra who, with his minister Ḍeṅgi, became Lūipa's disciple and served a temple courtesan for twelve years; a lineage holder of Lūipa's Cakrasaṃvara and a Caryāgīti poet.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A king of Pāṭaliputra who, with his minister Ḍeṅgi, became Lūipa's disciple and served a temple courtesan for twelve years; a lineage holder of Lūipa's Cakrasaṃvara and a Caryāgīti poet. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 77): Great bliss is within oneself but unrealized, obscured by delusion; one who wants bliss and clarity should gather the accumulations of merit and gnosis; without the two accumulations, though one strives for a hundred lives, without a guru one will not realize great bliss.
