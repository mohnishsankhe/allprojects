# Mayanāmatī

`tch:mayanamati` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Maynāmatī, Mainamati
**Lineages:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), `lin:folk-natha-siddha`
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Gorakṣanātha](goraksanatha.md)
**Students:** [Gopīcandra (Gopīcand)](gopicandra.md)

Queen-mother in the Bengali Nāth cycle, a disciple of Gorakṣa who received the 'great knowledge' (mahājñāna) and with it power over death; she compels her son Gopīcandra to renounce. One of the few women siddhas of the Nāth tradition.
