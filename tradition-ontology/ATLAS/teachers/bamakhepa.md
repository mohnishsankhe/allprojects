# Bāmākhepā (Vāmākṣepā)

`tch:bamakhepa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

**Alternate names:** Vāmācaraṇa Caṭṭopādhyāya, Khepā Bābā
**Lineages:** [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md), [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 1837–1911; (confidence moderate)
**Places:** Ātalā, Tārāpīṭh (Birbhum)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Kailāspati Bābā](kailaspati-baba.md)

Śākta 'mad saint' of the Tārāpīṭh cremation ground in Birbhum, devotee of Tārā, who lived as a child of the Mother, disregarding rules of purity; remembered for his miracles and for teaching by paradox.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Taught by the ascetic Kailāspati Bābā at Tārāpīṭh, he had the vision of Tārā in the cremation ground and thereafter treated her as his mother.
