Chidambaram priest (Tillai dīkṣitar) turned disciple of Maṟaiñāṉa Campantar; author of eight of the fourteen Meykaṇṭa Śāstras (Civappirakācam, Tiruvaruṭpayaṉ, Viṉāveṇpā, Poṟṟippahṟoṭai, Koṭikkavi, Neñcuviṭu Tūtu, Uṇmai Neṟi Viḷakkam [disputed], Caṅkaṟpa Nirākaraṇam), of the Sanskrit Pauṣkarabhāṣya and Śataratnasaṅgraha, and of Tamil purāṇas of Chidambaram and the saints.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Excluded by the Tillai priests, he lived at Koṟṟavaṉkuṭi; when the festival flag would not rise he sang the Koṭikkavi and it rose. Naṭarāja is said to have sent him a verse-letter ordering liberation for the outcaste Peṟṟāṉ Cāmpāṉ, and he granted release even to a thorn bush.
