# Ānanda (lay disciple)

`tch:ananda-upasaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Mahāvīra (Vardhamāna)](mahavira.md)

A householder of Vāṇijagrāma, the first of the ten lay disciples of the Upāsakadaśā, who took the twelve lay vows and the eleven pratimās, gained clairvoyance (avadhi-jñāna) and died in peace; when Gautama doubted the extent of his clairvoyance, Mahāvīra sent Gautama to apologize to the layman.
