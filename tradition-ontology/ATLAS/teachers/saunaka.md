Head (kulapati) of the sages of the Naimiṣa forest, at whose twelve-year sacrifice Ugraśravas recites the Mahābhārata.

_Notes: A brāhmaṇa Śaunaka also instructs Yudhiṣṭhira at the start of the exile (3.2, the eightfold path of dharma at 3.2.71); the identity of the two is not asserted here._
