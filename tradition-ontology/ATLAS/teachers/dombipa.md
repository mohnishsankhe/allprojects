# Ḍombipa (Ḍombi Heruka)

**Alternate names:** Ḍombī Heruka, Dom+bi pa (Tibetan)
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)

**Works:** 
  - [Sahajasiddhi](../texts/sahajasiddhi.md) — attribution: traditional

No. 4 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A king of Magadha, disciple of Virūpa, who took a low-caste ḍombī girl as his consort; banished, he lived with her in the forest for twelve years and returned riding a tigress, holding a snake as whip; author of Hevajra works and (traditionally) the Sahajasiddhi.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A king of Magadha, disciple of Virūpa, who took a low-caste ḍombī girl as his consort; banished, he lived with her in the forest for twelve years and returned riding a tigress, holding a snake as whip; author of Hevajra works and (traditionally) the Sahajasiddhi. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 4): As the power of an alchemical elixir turns iron into gold, the power of the precious consecration transforms the afflictions into non-dual gnosis.
