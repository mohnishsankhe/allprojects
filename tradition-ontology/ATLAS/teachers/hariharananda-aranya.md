Renunciant who founded the Kāpil Maṭh at Madhupur as a monastery of Sāṃkhya-Yoga and lived much of his life in seclusion in a cave there; author of the Bhāsvatī on the Yoga Bhāṣya and of works on Sāṃkhya (e.g., Sāṃkhyatattvāloka), and of the commentary rendered in English as 'Yoga Philosophy of Patañjali'.
