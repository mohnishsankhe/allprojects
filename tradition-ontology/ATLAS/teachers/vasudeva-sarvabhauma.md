Navadvīpa scholar who, on the tradition's account, studied in Mithilā and brought the Tattvacintāmaṇi to Navadvīpa (by memory, since copying was not permitted); teacher of Raghunātha Śiromaṇi in the same account; later a court scholar at Puri, where the Gauḍīya hagiographies place his meeting with Caitanya.
