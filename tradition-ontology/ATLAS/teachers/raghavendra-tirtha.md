Born Veṅkaṭanātha at Bhuvanagiri, disciple of Sudhīndra Tīrtha, and author of the Parimala on the Nyāyasudhā (hence 'Parimalācārya'), the Bhāvadīpa on the Tattvaprakāśikā, the Tantradīpikā, the Nyāyamuktāvalī, the Candrikāprakāśa, the Bhāṭṭasaṅgraha on Mīmāṃsā, Upaniṣad khaṇḍārthas and the Gītāvivṛti. His brindāvana at Mantrālaya is the tradition's greatest pilgrimage centre.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The tradition holds that Rāghavendra was Prahlāda reborn (through Bāhlīka and Vyāsatīrtha), that he entered his brindāvana alive in 1671, and that he remains present there, answering devotees.

_Notes: The claim that he will remain in the brindāvana for 700 years is recalled at low confidence._
