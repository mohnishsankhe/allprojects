Disciple of Madhva and, in the tradition's account, founder of the Palimaru maṭha, one of the eight maṭhas of Uḍupi that take turns in the worship of Kṛṣṇa. He is remembered as the copyist of the manuscript collection of Madhva's works (the Sarvamūla) and as author of the short Sampradāyapaddhati.
