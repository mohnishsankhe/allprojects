# Aññāsi Koṇḍañña

`tch:kondanna` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Aññā Koṇḍañña, Añña Koṇḍañña
**Lineages:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Gotama Buddha](gotama-buddha.md)

The first of the five monks; the first to understand the first discourse ('whatever is subject to arising is subject to cessation', SN 56.11), hence 'Koṇḍañña who has understood'; foremost in seniority.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The Dhamma-eye arose in him during the first discourse; he became an arahant with the Anattalakkhaṇa Sutta (SN 56.11; SN 22.59; Vin Mv 1.6).
