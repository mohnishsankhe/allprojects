# Mūrtti Nāyaṉār

`tch:murtti-nayanar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Madurai
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. A merchant who supplied sandal paste to the Madurai temple; when a Jain-favouring usurper stopped the supply he ground his own elbow on the stone; made king by Śiva's will, he ruled with the sacred ash, the rudrākṣa and matted hair as his crown and insignia.
