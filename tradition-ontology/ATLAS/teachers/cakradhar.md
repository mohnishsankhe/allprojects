# Cakradhar Svāmī

`tch:cakradhar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Cakrapāṇi, Śrī Cakradhar Prabhu, Haripāla (before renunciation)
**Lineages:** [Mahānubhāva panth](../lineages/mahanubhava.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: incarnation of Parameśvara; left for the north Śaka 1196 (1274); Scholarly account: c. 1194?–after 1274; (confidence low)
**Places:** Bharuch, Ṛddhipur, Paiṭhaṇ region, Vidarbha
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Govindaprabhu (Guṇḍam Rāüḷ)](govindaprabhu.md)
**Students:** [Nāgadevācārya (Bhaṭobās)](nagadevacarya.md), [Mhāimbhaṭ](mhaimbhat.md), [Mahadaisā (Mahadamba)](mahadaisa.md), [Bāïsā](baisa.md), [Keśobās (Keśirāja Vyās)](kesobas.md)

Founder of the Mahānubhāva panth (13th c.), held by his followers to be Parameśvara's fifth incarnation. By their account the Gujarati prince Haripāla of Bharuch died and Cāṅgadeva Rāüḷ entered his body; receiving Govindaprabhu's grace at Ṛddhipur, he wandered Maharashtra teaching in Marathi, admitted women and all castes to renunciation, forbade the worship of the devatās, and, threatened by the Yādava court, departed to the north (c. 1274).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Parameśvara himself, one of the five Kṛṣṇas; entered the dead body of Haripāla; survived the executioner and left for the north.
