# Mañjuśrī

`tch:manjusri` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Wenshu 文殊, Mañjughoṣa, Monju, Jampelyang ('jam dpal dbyangs)
**Lineages:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md), `lin:huayan`
**Historicity:** mythic

The bodhisattva of wisdom, 'ever-youthful' (kumārabhūta): visits Vimalakīrti and asks about non-duality; sends Sudhana on his pilgrimage and receives him at its end; teaches the Saptaśatikā; in the Lotus recalls the past buddha Candrasūryapradīpa and brings forth the nāga princess; in the Śūraṅgama chooses Avalokiteśvara's method.
**Realization — the tradition's account (as a figure of the sūtras):** A bodhisattva who in the Śūraṅgamasamādhi-sūtra is revealed to have been a buddha in the past; Wutaishan in China is his abode (Chinese tradition).
