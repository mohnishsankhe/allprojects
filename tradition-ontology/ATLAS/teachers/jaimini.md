# Jaimini

`tch:jaimini` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Jaimini Muni
**Lineages:** [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md), [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Jaiminīya (Talavakāra) śākhā (Sāmaveda)](../lineages/sakha-jaiminiya.md), [Kauthuma śākhā (Sāmaveda)](../lineages/sakha-kauthuma.md), [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: pupil of Vyāsa, to whom Vyāsa entrusted the Sāmaveda (Purāṇic account); Scholarly account: the Mīmāṃsā Sūtra c. 3rd c. BCE – 1st c. CE; (confidence low)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Vyāsa (Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana)](vyasa.md), [Mārkaṇḍeya](markandeya.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Mīmāṃsā Sūtra](../texts/mimamsa-sutra.md) — attribution: traditional
  - [Saṅkarṣa-kāṇḍa](../texts/sankarsa-kanda.md) — attribution: disputed
  - [Jaimini Upadeśa Sūtras](../texts/jaimini-upadesa-sutra.md) — attribution: traditional
  - [Jaiminīya Brāhmaṇa](../texts/jaiminiya-brahmana.md) — attribution: traditional

Author of the Mīmāṃsā Sūtra. In the Mīmāṃsā Sūtra he is cited by name for positions (e.g. that actions too are subsidiaries, MS 3.1.4); the Brahma Sūtra reports his views on the fruit of action, on knowledge as subsidiary to rites and on the released self (BS 3.2.40, 3.4.2, 3.4.18, 4.3.12, 4.4.5).

_Notes: The Purāṇic Jaimini (Sāmaveda transmitter, narrator of the Jaimini Aśvamedha) and the sūtrakāra are identified by tradition; the identity is not established historically. The astrological Jaimini Sūtras (src:jaimini-upadesa-sutra) are a separate attribution._
