Shepherd-Siddhar (iṭaiyar) whose songs address the herdsman, cattle and flute. The tradition tells that, foreseeing a twelve-year drought, he fed his flock on erukku leaves and stored grain in mud, and when the nine planets came to him he so charmed them that they shifted their places and the rains returned.

_Notes: Distinct from the Caṅkam poet Iṭaikkāṭaṉār. The planets legend and his discipleship to Bogar are recalled at low confidence. Counted in the popular list of the eighteen Siddhars (see cpt:eighteen-siddhars); the samādhi place given follows that list and is recalled at low confidence._
