# Naminātha

**Alternate names:** Nami

**Dates:** Tradition's account: the twenty-first of the twenty-four Jinas of this era (tradition's chronology of immense lifespans and intervals); (confidence low)
**Places:** Mithilā, Sammeda Śikhara
**Historicity:** legendary

The twenty-first Tīrthaṅkara of the present descending half-cycle (avasarpiṇī). Emblem (lāñchana): the blue lotus (nīlotpala). Parents: Vijaya and Vaprā. Born at Mithilā; attained nirvāṇa at Sammeda Śikhara. Not to be confused with the royal sage Nami of Uttarādhyayana 9.

_Notes: Parents, birthplaces and emblems follow the common Śvetāmbara lists (e.g. Hemacandra); Digambara lists agree in most points and differ in some emblems and names._
