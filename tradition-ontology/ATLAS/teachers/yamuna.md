Grandson of Nāthamuni and grand-teacher of Rāmānuja; author of the Siddhitraya, Āgamaprāmāṇya, Gītārthasaṅgraha, Stotraratna and Catuḥślokī, who laid the philosophical foundation of Viśiṣṭādvaita and defended the Pāñcarātra.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** As a boy he defeated the court scholar and won half a kingdom, hence 'Āḷavantār' ('he who came to rule'); Maṇakkāl Nambi led him to renounce it. At his death three fingers of his hand stayed folded, for three unfulfilled wishes, and unfolded when Rāmānuja vowed to fulfil them.
