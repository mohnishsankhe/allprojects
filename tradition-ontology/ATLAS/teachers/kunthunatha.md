# Kunthunātha

**Alternate names:** Kunthu

**Places:** Hastināpura, Sammeda Śikhara
**Historicity:** legendary

The seventeenth Tīrthaṅkara of the present descending half-cycle (avasarpiṇī). Emblem (lāñchana): the goat. Parents: Sūra (Sūrasena) and Śrī. Born at Hastināpura; attained nirvāṇa at Sammeda Śikhara. He was also the sixth world-emperor.

_Notes: Parents, birthplaces and emblems follow the common Śvetāmbara lists (e.g. Hemacandra); Digambara lists agree in most points and differ in some emblems and names._
