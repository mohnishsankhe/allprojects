Kashmiri Naiyāyika, author of the Nyāyamañjarī, the Nyāyakalikā and the drama Āgamaḍambara, set in the reign of King Śaṅkaravarman (r. 883–902). He defends the Veda and the Lord and examines the validity of the Āgamas.

_Notes: His son is usually identified as the poet Abhinanda (moderate confidence)._
