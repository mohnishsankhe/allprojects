In the Vīraśaiva tradition, the sage to whom Reṇuka taught the Siddhāntaśikhāmaṇi on the Malaya mountain (SSM 1.29; ch. 4–5).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** When all gods and sages gathered in the north for Śiva's wedding and the earth tilted, Śiva sent the diminutive Agastya south, where he balanced the world from the Potiyil hills; he is held to live there still as a Siddhar.
