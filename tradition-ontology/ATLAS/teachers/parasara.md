Grandson of Vasiṣṭha and father of Vyāsa; the speaker of the Viṣṇu Purāṇa to Maitreya, which he received from Vasiṣṭha and Pulastya after renouncing his rite to burn the rākṣasas (VP 1.1.12-26).
