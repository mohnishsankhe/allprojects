Commentator on the Saundaryalaharī (the Lakṣmīdharā) and on the Subhagodaya; the classical exponent of the Samaya school, who restricts worship to the inner space, teaches the fivefold equality of Śiva and Śakti, and rejects the sixty-four tantras and Kaula practice as outside the Vedic path.

_Notes: Distinct from Lakṣmīdhara Bhaṭṭa, author of the Kṛtyakalpataru (tch:laksmidhara in U02/U07), and from Lakṣmīdhara Kavi of the Advaitamakaranda (tch:laksmidhara-kavi), though some identify him with the latter.
