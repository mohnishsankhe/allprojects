Śvetāmbara (Tapā Gaccha) monk who became a wandering mystic poet; author of the Covīsī hymns to the Tīrthaṅkaras and of the Bahottarī padas. He sings the soul's love for the Jina, dismisses sectarian dispute and treats the six darśanas as limbs of the Jina. The tradition identifies him with the monk Lābhānanda, says he lived and died at Meṛtā, and that Yaśovijaya sought him out and praised him.
