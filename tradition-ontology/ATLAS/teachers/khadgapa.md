No. 15 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A thief who, taught by a yogin, circumambulated an image of Avalokiteśvara and gained the siddhi of the sword; he then turned it to the deathless sword of gnosis.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A thief who, taught by a yogin, circumambulated an image of Avalokiteśvara and gained the siddhi of the sword; he then turned it to the deathless sword of gnosis. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 15): The worldly armed hero, though he fights enemies and battles without end, himself falls under suffering; with the sword of deathless gnosis Khaḍgapa defeated the enemies of the three realms and enjoys the fruit.
