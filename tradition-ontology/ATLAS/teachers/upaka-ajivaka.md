# Upaka the Ājīvaka

`tch:upaka-ajivaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary

The first person the newly awakened Buddha met on the road from Gayā; told of the Buddha's awakening he said 'may it be so' and went another way (MN 26; Vinaya Mv 1.6). The Therīgāthā verses of Cāpā tell of her husband Kāḷa, formerly an ascetic with a staff, who left her to go forth; the commentary identifies him with Upaka.
