Song Chan master, compiler of the Chanyuan qinggui (1103), who founded a Lotus assembly (Lianhua shenghui) for recitation; fifth successor of Huiyuan in Zongxiao's list. His simile of farming and banking for Chan and Pure Land is cited in the Lushan lianzong baojian preface.
