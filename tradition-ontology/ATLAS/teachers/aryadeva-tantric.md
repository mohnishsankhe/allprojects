No. 18 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). Born from a lotus in Siṃhala, disciple of Nāgārjuna at Nālandā; gave one of his eyes to a woman (or tree-goddess) who asked for it — hence 'the one-eyed'; defeated non-Buddhist debaters; the tradition identifies him with the Madhyamaka Āryadeva and the author of the Caryāmelāpakapradīpa.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Born from a lotus in Siṃhala, disciple of Nāgārjuna at Nālandā; gave one of his eyes to a woman (or tree-goddess) who asked for it — hence 'the one-eyed'; defeated non-Buddhist debaters; the tradition identifies him with the Madhyamaka Āryadeva and the author of the Caryāmelāpakapradīpa. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 18): All buddhas of the three times are one in essence; realized, that is one's own mind by nature; wishing to realize it, leave it uncontrived just as it is; habituated to it, yoga becomes manifest.
