# Dāruka

`tch:daruka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md), [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)
**Historicity:** mythic

A gaṇa dear to Śiva, paired with Reṇuka among the pramathas; Reṇuka's leap over him in Śiva's assembly is the occasion of Reṇuka's descent (SSM 2.27; 3.67–77).

_Notes: In Pañcācārya yuga-lists Dāruka is also named among the ācāryas of an earlier age (low confidence)._
