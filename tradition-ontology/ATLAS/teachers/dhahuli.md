**Realization — the tradition's account:** A rope-maker with blistered hands. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 70): Union-awareness, the treasury of jewels, the non-dual state, is not found by seeking; like a mind that has given up activity, as it is placed, so it is blissful.
