# Mūka Kavi

`tch:muka-kavi` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md), [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Mūka Pañcaśatī](../texts/muka-pancasati.md) — attribution: traditional

The 'mute poet' of Kāñcī who by tradition received speech through Kāmākṣī's grace and poured out the Mūka Pañcaśatī; the Kāñcī Kāmakoṭi tradition counts him among its ācāryas.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Mute from birth, he became a poet when the Goddess (or a devotee in her presence) gave him her grace.
