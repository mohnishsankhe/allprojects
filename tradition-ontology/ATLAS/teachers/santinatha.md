# Śāntinātha

`tch:santinatha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Śānti
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: the sixteenth of the twenty-four Jinas of this era (tradition's chronology of immense lifespans and intervals); (confidence low)
**Places:** Hastināpura, Sammeda Śikhara
**Historicity:** legendary

The sixteenth Tīrthaṅkara of the present descending half-cycle (avasarpiṇī). Emblem (lāñchana): the deer (antelope). Parents: Viśvasena and Acirā. Born at Hastināpura; attained nirvāṇa at Sammeda Śikhara. He was also the fifth world-emperor (cakravartin) before renouncing; in a previous life as King Megharatha he offered his own flesh to save a pigeon from a hawk. He is invoked for peace (śānti) and widely worshipped.

_Notes: Parents, birthplaces and emblems follow the common Śvetāmbara lists (e.g. Hemacandra); Digambara lists agree in most points and differ in some emblems and names._
