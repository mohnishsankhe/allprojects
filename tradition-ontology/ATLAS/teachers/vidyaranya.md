Pontiff of Śṛṅgeri (d. 1386), author of the Pañcadaśī, the Jīvanmuktiviveka, the Vivaraṇaprameyasaṅgraha and the Anubhūtiprakāśa; in the tradition identified with Mādhava, minister of the founders of Vijayanagara and brother of Sāyaṇa, and so credited with the Sarvadarśanasaṅgraha and the Śaṅkaradigvijaya.

_Notes: Identity with the minister Mādhava is the tradition's; scholars debate it._
