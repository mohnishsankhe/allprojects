# Maitreya (the bodhisattva, future buddha)

`tch:maitreya-bodhisattva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Metteyya (Pali), Mile 彌勒, Ajita, Miroku, Jampa (byams pa)
**Lineages:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md), [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Students:** [Asaṅga](asanga.md)

The bodhisattva who will be the next buddha, now in Tuṣita: in the Lotus he asks the questions that open the sūtra and receives the revelation of the Buddha's lifespan; in the Gaṇḍavyūha he opens the tower of Vairocana to Sudhana; in the Śālistamba he teaches dependent origination; in the Saṃdhinirmocana (ch. 8) he asks about calm and insight. Tibetan tradition makes him the teacher of Asaṅga and source of the five Maitreya treatises.
