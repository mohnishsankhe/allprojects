# Sarvānanda (Sarvānandanātha) of Mehar

**Lineages:** [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md), [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md)

Śākta adept of Mehar (Chandpur/Comilla, East Bengal), remembered as having realised all ten Mahāvidyās in a single night; the Sarvollāsa Tantra is ascribed to him and his descendants were Śākta gurus in Bengal.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** On a new-moon night at Mehar, seated on a corpse-seat with the help of his servant Pūrṇānanda, he had the vision of all ten Mahāvidyās one after another (hence 'Sarvavidyā').

_Notes: The servant Pūrṇānanda of this story is not Pūrṇānanda Giri, author of the Śrītattvacintāmaṇi._
