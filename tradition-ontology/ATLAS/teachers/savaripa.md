No. 5 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A hunter of the mountains of eastern India who, with his two wives Lokī and Guṇī, was shown by Avalokiteśvara (in the guise of a hunter who killed with a single arrow) the hell awaiting a killer; he renounced hunting and, instructed in mahāmudrā, attained realization; guru of Maitrīpa.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A hunter of the mountains of eastern India who, with his two wives Lokī and Guṇī, was shown by Avalokiteśvara (in the guise of a hunter who killed with a single arrow) the hell awaiting a killer; he renounced hunting and, instructed in mahāmudrā, attained realization; guru of Maitrīpa. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 5): In the forest of ignorance roam the deer of grasped and grasper; drawing the bow of means and wisdom I shot the single arrow of the essential meaning: what died was conceptual thought, its flesh I ate as non-duality, its taste I experienced as great bliss, and the fruit, mahāmudrā, was attained.
