# Skanda / Guha

`tch:skanda` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Kumāra, Ṣaḍānana, Kārttikeya
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Historicity:** mythic

Son of Śiva: teaches Prajāpati the rosary (Akṣamālikā); the Skanda Upaniṣad is his declaration of the identity of jīva and Śiva; in the Jābāli his line (Īśāna → Ṣaḍānana → Jābāli) transmits the Paśupati teaching.
