# Mekhalā

**Alternate names:** me kha la (Tibetan)
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Kāṇhapa (Kṛṣṇācārya)](kanha.md)

No. 66 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). The elder of two sisters of Devīkoṭa, mocked in marriage, who took instruction from Kāṇha; when he asked for a fee, the two sisters cut off their heads and offered them, and sang their realization; he restored them; they are linked to the 'headless' Vajrayoginī (Chinnamuṇḍā).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The elder of two sisters of Devīkoṭa, mocked in marriage, who took instruction from Kāṇha; when he asked for a fee, the two sisters cut off their heads and offered them, and sang their realization; he restored them; they are linked to the 'headless' Vajrayoginī (Chinnamuṇḍā). Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 66): All outer and inner are gathered in mind; without clinging there is equal taste; without striving, the supreme meditation; non-dual great bliss is complete buddhahood.
