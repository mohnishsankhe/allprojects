# Indrabhūti Gautama

**Alternate names:** Gautama Svāmī, Goyama, Gautama Gaṇadhara
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: contemporary of Mahāvīra; liberated 12 years after him (Śvetāmbara); (confidence low)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Mahāvīra (Vardhamāna)](mahavira.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Ṛṣimaṇḍala-stotra](../texts/rsimandala-stotra.md) — attribution: doubtful

The first and chief gaṇadhara of Mahāvīra, a brāhmaṇa of Gobbara near Rājagṛha, who came to refute him and was converted when Mahāvīra resolved his doubt whether a soul exists. The questioner of the Bhagavatī and the addressee of 'Gautama, be not careless for a moment' (Uttarādhyayana 10). His attachment to his teacher delayed his omniscience, which he attained on the night of Mahāvīra's nirvāṇa (Śvetāmbara account) — remembered at Dīvālī; he is invoked for success and wealth ('Gautama-svāmī').
**Realization — the tradition's account:** His love for Mahāvīra was the last bond; when he learned of Mahāvīra's nirvāṇa he grieved, reflected on the Jina's detachment, and attained omniscience that night.
