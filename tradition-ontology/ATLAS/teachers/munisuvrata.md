# Munisuvrata

`tch:munisuvrata` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Munisuvratanātha, Suvrata
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: the twentieth of the twenty-four Jinas of this era (tradition's chronology of immense lifespans and intervals); (confidence low)
**Places:** Rājagṛha, Sammeda Śikhara
**Historicity:** legendary

The twentieth Tīrthaṅkara of the present descending half-cycle (avasarpiṇī). Emblem (lāñchana): the tortoise. Parents: Sumitra and Padmāvatī. Born at Rājagṛha; attained nirvāṇa at Sammeda Śikhara. His body is described as dark; Rāma, Lakṣmaṇa and Rāvaṇa lived in his era.

_Notes: Parents, birthplaces and emblems follow the common Śvetāmbara lists (e.g. Hemacandra); Digambara lists agree in most points and differ in some emblems and names._
