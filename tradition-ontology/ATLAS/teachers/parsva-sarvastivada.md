# Pārśva (Sarvāstivāda elder)

`tch:parsva-sarvastivada` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Alternate names:** Xie zunzhe 脇尊者
**Lineages:** [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Contemporary of King Kaniṣka; Scholarly account: c. 1st–2nd c. CE; (confidence low)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary

Sarvāstivāda elder of Gandhāra credited by Xuanzang with prompting the Kashmir council that compiled the Vibhāṣā; distinct from the Jain Tīrthaṅkara Pārśva.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** An elder who vowed not to lie down until he had mastered the Tripiṭaka and attained the three knowledges ('the rib elder'); he urged Kaniṣka to convene the Kashmir council.
