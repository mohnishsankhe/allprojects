# Kuṅkiliyakkalaya Nāyaṉār

`tch:kunkiliyakkalayar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Tirukkaṭavūr
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. He burned kuṅkiliyam incense before Śiva, sold all he had for it, and spent even his wife's marriage badge on incense; at Tiruppaṉantāḷ he straightened the leaning liṅga by pulling with a rope tied to his own neck.
