# Dagmema

`tch:dagmema` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** bdag med ma (Tib., 'Nairātmyā', her name as Marpa's consort)
**Lineages:** [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Marpa Chökyi Lodrö (Marpa the Translator)](marpa.md)

Marpa's wife, remembered in the Life of Milarepa for her compassion toward Milarepa during his ordeals; in some accounts she dissolves into Marpa at death.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** She interceded for Milarepa and helped forge a letter so that Ngok Chöku Dorje would teach him; Marpa later said that without his permission the teaching could not bear fruit.
