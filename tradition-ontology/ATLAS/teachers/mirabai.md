# Mīrābāī

Rajput princess of Meṛtā (tradition c. 1498–1547), married into the Sisodia house of Mewar and early widowed, who took Kṛṣṇa as Giridhar Gopāl for her husband, danced with sādhus, survived the poison and the snake sent by the Rāṇā, went to Vṛndāvana and then Dvārakā, and — so tradition says — merged into the image of Raṇchoḍ. The Bhaktamāl praises her for casting off family honour; songs in her name name Raidās as guru.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Merged bodily into Kṛṣṇa's image at Dvārakā.

_Notes: Raidās as guru is the songs' claim; chronology makes a direct meeting doubtful._
