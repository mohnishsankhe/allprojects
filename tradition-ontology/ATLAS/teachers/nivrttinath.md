# Nivṛttināth

`tch:nivrttinath` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: 1273–1297; (confidence low)
**Places:** Āpegāv, Āḷandī, Tryambakeśvar
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Gahinīnāth](gahininath.md)
**Students:** [Jñāneśvar](jnanesvar.md), [Sopāndev](sopandev.md), [Muktābāī](muktabai.md)

Eldest of the four sibling-saints and Jñāneśvar's guru; initiated into the Nāth path by Gahinīnāth, he passed it to Jñāneśvar and bade him write the Jñāneśvarī and then an independent work (Amṛtānubhava). His own abhaṅgas are few. Samādhi at Tryambakeśvar.
