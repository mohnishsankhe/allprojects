# Gārgī Vācaknavī

`tch:gargi-vacaknavi` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Gārgī
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary

Woman participant in the debate at Janaka's court: she questions Yājñavalkya on what the worlds are woven upon, is warned not to ask too much lest her head fall off (BAU 3.6), returns with two questions 'like two arrows', hears the teaching of the imperishable (akṣara) and declares that no one will defeat him (BAU 3.8).
