# Ubhaya Bhāratī

`tch:ubhaya-bharati` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Sarasavāṇī, Śāradā (as deity), Bhāratī
**Lineages:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
**Historicity:** legendary

Wife of Maṇḍana Miśra who, in the tradition's account, judged the debate between Śaṅkara and her husband by the fading of the garlands they wore, then herself debated Śaṅkara; venerated as Sarasvatī (Śāradā), associated with the Śāradā shrine at Śṛṅgeri.

_Notes: Known only from hagiography._
