Burmese scholar-monk (1846–1923) who wrote more than a hundred works in Pali and Burmese, founded the Ledi forest monastery near Monywa, and taught insight meditation and the Abhidhamma to laypeople — the source of the U Ba Khin – Goenka lineage.
