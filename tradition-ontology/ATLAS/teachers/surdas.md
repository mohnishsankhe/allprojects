Blind Braj Bhāṣā poet-singer of Kṛṣṇa's childhood and the gopīs' love and separation (Sūrsāgar); foremost of the aṣṭachāp.
**Realization — the tradition's account (Puṣṭimārga):** The Caurāsī Vārtā says Sūrdās, blind from birth, lived at Gaughāṭ near Agra singing songs of humility (dainya) until Vallabha initiated him and told him to sing Kṛṣṇa's play instead; he then sang at Śrīnāthjī's temple.
