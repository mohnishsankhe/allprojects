Brahmin saint of Paiṭhaṇ (1533–1599), disciple of Janārdana Svāmī: corrected the Jñāneśvarī text (1584), wrote the Eknāthī Bhāgavat, the Bhāvārtha Rāmāyaṇa, bhāruḍs and abhaṅgas, and lived as a householder of legendary patience — bathing again and again without anger when spat upon, pouring Gaṅgā water meant for Rāmeśvaram into a dying donkey's mouth, feeding Mahārs at his ancestors' rite. Kṛṣṇa is said to have served in his house as Śrīkhaṇḍyā.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Received Datta's vision through Janārdana's grace; Kṛṣṇa served him as a household servant for twelve years.
