Author of the Brahmasūtrabhāṣya that is the classic statement of aupādhika bhedābheda: Brahman really transforms through its power; souls are its parts, distinguished by real adjuncts; liberation after death through knowledge combined with lifelong duty. He attacked Śaṅkara's māyāvāda as Mahāyāna Buddhism in Vedic dress; Vācaspati Miśra and later Advaitins answer him.

_Notes: Often described as a tridaṇḍin (Vaiṣṇava-style renouncer) — low confidence. Distinct from the mathematician Bhāskara and from Bhāskararāya._
