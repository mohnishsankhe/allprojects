Traditional author of the Kārikā on the Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad; the text itself does not name its author. Its four prakaraṇas restate the Upaniṣad (ch.1), argue the unreality of dream and waking (ch.2), teach non-birth by the space analogy and give counsel on restraining the mind (ch.3), and argue non-origination and the firebrand image (ch.4).

_Notes: Also credited (traditional) with Śākta works (Subhagodaya, Śrīvidyāratnasūtras) and an Uttaragītā commentary. Whether he is the same person as the author of the commentary on the Sāṃkhya Kārikā (src:samkhya-karika-bhasya-gaudapada) is doubted by most scholars._
