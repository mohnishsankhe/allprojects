# Darma Dode

`tch:darma-dode` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** dar ma mdo sde
**Lineages:** [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Marpa Chökyi Lodrö (Marpa the Translator)](marpa.md)

Marpa's son and intended heir, who died young after a fall from a horse; by the tradition's account he used the practice of 'entering another's body' (grong 'jug), first entering a dead pigeon that flew to India and then the corpse of a young brahmin, who became Tiphupa.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Knowing the transference into another body from his father, at death he transferred his consciousness into a pigeon and then into a brahmin boy's body in India (Tiphupa); after him the practice of grong 'jug is said to have ceased in Tibet.

_Notes: The claim that transference into another body ended with him is the tradition's account (low)._
