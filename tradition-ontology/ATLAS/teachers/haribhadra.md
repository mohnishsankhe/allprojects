Śvetāmbara ācārya and the great systematizer of Jain yoga (Yogadṛṣṭisamuccaya, Yogabindu, Yogaśataka, Yogaviṃśikā), doxographer of all the darśanas (Ṣaḍdarśanasamuccaya, Śāstravārtāsamuccaya) and polemicist. The tradition tells that he was a learned brahmin of Citrakūṭa who vowed to become the pupil of anyone whose words he could not understand; hearing the nun Yākinī recite a verse he could not explain, he took initiation and called himself 'Yākinī's spiritual son'. After his pupils Haṃsa and Paramahaṃsa were killed by Buddhists, his anger was calmed by his teacher; he is credited with 1,444 works, many signed with the word 'viraha' (separation).

_Notes: Several different authors named Haribhadra existed; the ascription of some of the 1,444 works is doubtful._
