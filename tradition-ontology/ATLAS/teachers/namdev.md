Tailor-caste (śimpī) saint of Paṇḍharpūr (c. 1270–1350), Viṭṭhala's intimate from childhood — the Lord ate his offering — who, after Gorā Kumbhār judged him 'unbaked', took Visobā Khecar as guru and learned to see God everywhere. Companion of Jñāneśvar, he vowed to 'dance in kīrtan and light the lamp of knowledge in the world', carried Viṭṭhala's name to Punjab in Hindi songs, and took samādhi at the temple step (Nāmdev pāyarī).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Viṭṭhala spoke and ate with him as a child; only after taking a guru did he see the Lord in all things.
