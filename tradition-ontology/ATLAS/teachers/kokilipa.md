# Kokilipa

`tch:kokilipa` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Alternate names:** Kokila, ko ki la (Tibetan)
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Historicity:** legendary

No. 80 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A pleasure-loving king who, disturbed by the song of the cuckoo (kokila), was taught to meditate on sound.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A pleasure-loving king who, disturbed by the song of the cuckoo (kokila), was taught to meditate on sound. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 80): Giving up activity is the instruction; being without clinging and craving is the realization; the bliss of no-thing is the meditation; when there is nothing to accomplish, that is the supreme fruit.
