# Düsum Khyenpa, the first Karmapa

`tch:dusum-khyenpa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** dus gsum mkhyen pa ('knower of the three times'), Karmapa I
**Lineages:** [Karma Kagyu (Kamtsang Kagyu)](../lineages/karma-kagyu.md), [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 1110–1193; (confidence high)
**Places:** Kham, Tsurphu
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Gampopa Sönam Rinchen (Dakpo Lhaje)](gampopa.md)
**Students:** [Karma Pakshi, the second Karmapa](karma-pakshi.md)

Disciple of Gampopa (after studying Madhyamaka and logic with Kadam teachers), founder of the Karma Kagyu and of the monasteries of Kampo Nenang, Karma Gön and Tsurphu; regarded as the first Karmapa.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** He attained realization at fifty; the ḍākinīs are said to have woven a crown from their hair and offered it to him — the origin of the Karmapa's black crown.
