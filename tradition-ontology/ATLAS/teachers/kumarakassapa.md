# Kumārakassapa

`tch:kumarakassapa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Gotama Buddha](gotama-buddha.md)

An elder, foremost in variegated speech, who answered Pāyāsi's denial of the other world with similes (DN 23); the Vammika Sutta (MN 23) was addressed to him.
