# Patañjali

Author (re-teacher) of the Yoga Sūtra. The tradition regards him as Śeṣa incarnate and as the same Patañjali who wrote the grammatical Mahābhāṣya; modern scholarship mostly distinguishes the two and debates whether he also wrote the bhāṣya (the 'Pātañjala Yogaśāstra' hypothesis).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Regarded as an incarnation of Śeṣa (Ananta), the serpent of Viṣṇu, who took a human form out of compassion to teach; the bhāṣya's maṅgala verse praises the 'lord of serpents, giver of yoga'. Later tradition credits him with removing the impurities of speech (grammar), mind (yoga) and body (medicine) — Bhoja's introductory verses praise Bhoja for doing the same 'like the lord of serpents'.
