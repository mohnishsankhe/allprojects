Nālandā monk-poet, author of the Bodhicaryāvatāra and the Śikṣāsamuccaya, the classic Indian manuals of the bodhisattva's awakening mind, patience, exchange of self and other and wisdom.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A prince who, on the eve of his coronation, saw Mañjuśrī seated on the throne in a dream and renounced; at Nālandā he seemed only to eat, sleep and relieve himself ('Bhusuku'); asked to recite before the assembly, he taught the Bodhicaryāvatāra and at the verse 'when neither being nor non-being remains before the mind' (9.34) rose into the sky and vanished, while his voice continued; the Śikṣāsamuccaya was later found in his cell (Bu-ston; Tāranātha; a Nepalese Sanskrit hagiography).
