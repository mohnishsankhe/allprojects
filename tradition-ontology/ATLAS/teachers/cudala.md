# Cūḍālā

`tch:cudala` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Students:** [Śikhidhvaja](sikhidhvaja.md)

Queen of Mālava who attained self-knowledge by her own inquiry and powers through breath-discipline; unable to teach her husband Śikhidhvaja as herself, she came to him in the forest disguised as the young brāhmaṇa Kumbha and led him to realization (Mokṣopāya 6.81-114; Yoga Vāsiṣṭha Nirvāṇa, pūrvārdha).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Through daily inquiry into what the self is she found the Self and 'shone' so that her husband remarked on her changed radiance (MU 6.82-83 'cūḍālā-prabodha', 'cūḍālātmalābha').
