# Caraka

`tch:caraka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Carakācārya
**Lineages:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md), [The school of Ātreya (Ātreya-sampradāya, the physicians' line)](../lineages/atreya-sampradaya.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: an incarnation of Śeṣa (Bhāvaprakāśa); Scholarly account: c. 1st c. BCE – 2nd c. CE; (confidence low)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — attribution: accepted

The redactor (pratisaṃskartā) of Agniveśa's tantra: 'the redactor expands what is said briefly and condenses what is too long, and makes an old tantra new' (Ca Si 12.36-37). In the Bhāvaprakāśa he is a portion of the thousand-headed Śeṣa who came to earth 'like a spy' (cara) and was born as a sage's son; Cakrapāṇi salutes the lord of serpents who removed the faults of mind, speech and body by the Pātañjala, the Mahābhāṣya and Caraka's redaction.

_Notes: A Buddhist narrative preserved in Chinese makes a physician Caraka attend King Kaniṣka; scholars differ on whether this is the redactor (scholarly hypothesis, recalled)._
