# Paila

`tch:paila` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Śākala śākhā (Ṛgveda)](../lineages/sakha-sakala.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Vyāsa (Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana)](vyasa.md)

Vyāsa's pupil who received the Ṛgveda (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 1.4.21; Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.4) and divided it among his own pupils, from whom the Ṛgveda śākhās descend.
