# Arivāṭṭāya Nāyaṉār

`tch:arivattayar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Tāyaṉār
**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Kaṇamaṅkalam
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. He offered red rice, greens and tender mango daily; impoverished and faint, he stumbled and the offering fell into a crack in the earth, and he began to cut his throat with a sickle (arivāḷ) — Śiva's hand rose from the crack to stop him.
