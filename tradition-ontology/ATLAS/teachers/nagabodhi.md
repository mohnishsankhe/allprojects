# Nāgabodhi

`tch:nagabodhi` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Nāgabuddhi, nA ga bo d+hi (Tibetan), klu'i byang chub
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Nāgārjuna (the siddha)](nagarjuna-siddha.md)

No. 76 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A thief who came to rob Nāgārjuna and became his disciple (the 'red-horned thief'); he is counted in the Ārya Guhyasamāja lineage and is said to live on at Śrīparvata.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A thief who came to rob Nāgārjuna and became his disciple (the 'red-horned thief'); he is counted in the Ārya Guhyasamāja lineage and is said to live on at Śrīparvata. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 76): Dwelling on earth, all is good fortune; gone to the ocean's depths, one is free of poverty; sword in hand, great enjoyment; gone to no particular direction, one meets one's friends.

_Notes: Life from Abhayadatta's Caturaśītisiddhapravṛtti recalled from memory (not read locally); the song is read locally in Tōh 2292. The images may name siddhis (earth, ocean, sword); uncertain._
