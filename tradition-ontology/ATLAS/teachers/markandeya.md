**Works:** 
  - [Mārkaṇḍeyasamāsyā (Mārkaṇḍeya's discourses)](../texts/markandeya-samasya.md) — attribution: traditional

The long-lived sage who instructs the Pāṇḍavas in the forest (3.180–221): on karma, the ages and the dissolution — in which he saw the worlds within the body of a divine child on a banyan branch (3.186.81–91) — the devoted wife and the righteous meat-seller.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The long-lived sage who, by Nara-Nārāyaṇa's boon, witnessed an illusory deluge and saw the Lord as an infant lying on a banyan leaf, entering and leaving the child's body (BhP 12.8-10).
