**Lineages:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md), [The school of Dhanvantari (Dhānvantara-sampradāya, the surgeons' line)](../lineages/dhanvantari-sampradaya.md)

**Works:** 
  - [Suśruta Saṃhitā](../texts/susruta-samhita.md) — attribution: traditional

The student who put the questions to Divodāsa Dhanvantari on behalf of his fellows, who asked that surgery be made the root of the teaching (Su Sū 1.10-14); the Suśruta Saṃhitā is addressed to him ('vatsa Suśruta').

_Notes: Later tradition makes him a son of Viśvāmitra (recalled, not checked)._
