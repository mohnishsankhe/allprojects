# Nārada

**Alternate names:** Devarṣi Nārada
**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md), [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md), [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md)

  - [Nārada Purāṇa](../texts/narada-purana.md) — attribution: traditional

The divine sage and wandering devotee: teacher of Vyāsa (urging him to write the Bhāgavata), initiator of Dhruva with the twelve-syllable mantra, teacher of Prahlāda while still in his mother's womb, pupil of the Kumāras in the Nārada Purāṇa; one of the twelve mahājanas (BhP 6.3.20).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** In a former life the son of a maidservant who served Vedāntin sages, heard Hari's glories, had a vision of the Lord and was told he would see him again only in a later body (BhP 1.5.23-1.6.38); reborn as the celestial sage who wanders singing Hari's names with his vīṇā. BhP 1.3.8 counts him the third avatāra, who 'expounded the Sātvata tantra'.
