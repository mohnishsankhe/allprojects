# Rāśīkara

`tch:rasikara` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Lineages:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Historicity:** unknown
**Works:** 
  - [Rāśīkara-bhāṣya](../texts/rasikarabhasya.md) — attribution: traditional

The seventeenth of the eighteen Pāśupata tīrthakaras in the Jain lists (Guṇaratna; Rājaśekhara); the Ratnaṭīkā has the ascetic salute 'the tīrthakaras from Lakulīśa to Rāśīkara', and the Sarvadarśanasaṃgraha cites a 'Rāśīkara-bhāṣya'. His identification with Kauṇḍinya is a scholarly suggestion.
