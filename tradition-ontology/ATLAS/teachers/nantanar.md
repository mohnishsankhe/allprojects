# Nantaṉār (Tirunāḷaippōvār)

`tch:nantanar` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Tirunāḷaippōvār ('he who will go tomorrow'), Nandanar
**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Ātaṉūr; Chidambaram
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. A Pulaiya (outcaste) labourer who supplied leather and strings for temple drums; at Tiruppuṅkūr Śiva moved the bull Nandi aside so he could see; long saying 'I will go to Tillai tomorrow', he at last reached Chidambaram where, at Śiva's command conveyed to the priests, he passed through fire, rose as a brahmin sage and entered the sanctum, never to be seen again.
