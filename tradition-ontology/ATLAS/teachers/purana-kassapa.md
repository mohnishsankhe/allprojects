# Pūraṇa Kassapa

`tch:purana-kassapa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Pūraṇa Kāśyapa (Skt.), Pūraṇaṉ (Tamil), 不蘭迦葉 (Chinese, Dīrgha-āgama)
**Lineages:** [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md), [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 5th c. BCE (contemporary of the Buddha and Mahāvīra); (confidence low)
**Places:** Rājagaha, Sāvatthī
**Historicity:** historical

One of the six famous teachers of the Pali canon, leader of an order. DN 2 reports his doctrine of non-action (akiriya): the one who kills, steals or lies does no evil, and gifts, self-control and truthfulness bring no merit. SN 22.60 and SN 46.56 ascribe to him the view that there is no cause for defilement, purification, knowing or not knowing; AN 6.57 ascribes to him the six classes of birth in which the Ājīvika leaders are 'supremely white'. In the Tamil Maṇimēkalai and Nīlakēci, Pūraṇa is the Ājīvika teacher. Buddhist legend (Dhammapada commentary, Divyāvadāna) says he drowned himself after the Buddha's Twin Miracle.
