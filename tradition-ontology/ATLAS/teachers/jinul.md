Goryeo reformer of Seon: founded the Samādhi and Prajñā Community (Suseonsa, later Songgwangsa); taught sudden awakening followed by gradual cultivation, tracing back the radiance to the empty and calm numinous awareness, the balanced cultivation of samādhi and prajñā, harmony with Huayan, and the hwadu as a shortcut.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Three awakenings through texts rather than a living teacher: reading the Platform Sūtra (the self-nature is not defiled by the senses), Li Tongxuan's Huayan commentary, and Dahui's records (freedom from lingering obstruction).
