# Uddaka Rāmaputta

`tch:uddaka-ramaputta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md)
**Historicity:** historical
**Students:** [Gotama Buddha](gotama-buddha.md)

A teacher of the bodhisatta who taught the attainment of the sphere of neither-perception-nor-non-perception realised by his father Rāma (MN 26, MN 36).

_Notes: Known only through Buddhist texts; the text distinguishes Rāma, who had attained it, from Uddaka._
