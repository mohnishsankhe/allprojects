# Vimalakīrti

`tch:vimalakirti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Weimojie 維摩詰, Yuima, Dri ma med par grags pa
**Lineages:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
**Historicity:** legendary

The Licchavi householder of Vaiśālī, a bodhisattva of inconceivable skillful means who lives as a layman, visits gambling halls and brothels to teach, feigns illness to draw the Buddha's disciples, and answers Mañjuśrī's question on non-duality with silence; he comes from Akṣobhya's world Abhirati.
**Realization — the tradition's account (as a figure of the sūtras):** Had served past buddhas, obtained the acceptance of dharmas, eloquence and dhāraṇī (Vimalakīrti 2.1)
