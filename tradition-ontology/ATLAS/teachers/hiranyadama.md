# Hiraṇyadāma

`tch:hiranyadama` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Lineages:** [Vāma stream (the left current of Tumburu)](../lineages/vama-srotas.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: early 9th c. (as narrated in an inscription of 1052 CE); (confidence low)
**Places:** Cambodia
**Historicity:** semi-legendary

Brahmin who, according to the Khmer Sdok Kak Thom inscription (1052 CE), performed rites for King Jayavarman II and taught four Vāma tantras — Vīṇāśikha, Nayottara, Sammoha and Śiraścheda — to the royal priest.
