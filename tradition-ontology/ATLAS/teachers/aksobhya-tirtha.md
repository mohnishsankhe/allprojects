# Akṣobhya Tīrtha

`tch:aksobhya-tirtha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md), [Uttarādi Maṭha](../lineages/uttaradi-matha.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 14th c. CE; (confidence low)
**Places:** Mulbagal (debate, tradition), Malkheḍ (brindāvana, tradition)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Madhva](madhva.md), [Mādhava Tīrtha](madhava-tirtha.md)
**Students:** [Jayatīrtha](jayatirtha.md)

Disciple of Madhva, pontiff after Mādhava Tīrtha, and teacher of Jayatīrtha. The Mādhva tradition records his debate with the Advaitin Vidyāraṇya on 'tat tvam asi', with Vedānta Deśika as arbiter, and a verse it ascribes to Deśika declaring Akṣobhya the victor; the Advaita tradition does not accept this account.
