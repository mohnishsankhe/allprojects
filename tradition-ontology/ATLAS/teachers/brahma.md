# Brahmā (as first teacher)

`tch:brahma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Pitāmaha, Parameṣṭhin, Padmaja, Svayambhū
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Students:** [Atharvan](atharvan.md), [Prajāpati (as teacher)](prajapati.md)

The first of the gods, maker and protector of the world, who taught the knowledge of brahman to his eldest son Atharvan (MuU 1.1.1); the first link of teacher-lines (ChU 3.11.4, 8.15.1); in KauU 1.5-7 he questions the knower arriving in the brahma-world.
