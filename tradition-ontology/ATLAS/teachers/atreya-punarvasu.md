# Punarvasu Ātreya

`tch:atreya-punarvasu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Ātreya, Punarvasu, Kṛṣṇātreya (Ca Sū 11.65), Atriputra, son of Atri (AHS Sū 1.3), Bhagavān Ātreya
**Lineages:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md), [The school of Ātreya (Ātreya-sampradāya, the physicians' line)](../lineages/atreya-sampradaya.md)
**Places:** the Himalaya, Kāmpilya in Pañcāla, by the Gaṅgā (Ca Vi 3.3), the Caitraratha forest (Ca Sū 26.6)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Bharadvāja](bharadvaja.md), [Indra (as student and teacher)](indra.md)
**Students:** [Agniveśa](agnivesa.md), [Bhela](bhela.md), [Jatūkarṇa](jatukarna.md), [Parāśara (disciple of Ātreya)](parasara-ayurveda.md), [Hārīta (disciple of Ātreya)](harita-ayurveda.md), [Kṣārapāṇi](ksarapani.md)

The teacher of the Caraka Saṃhitā: out of friendliness and compassion for all beings he taught Āyurveda to six disciples — Agniveśa, Bhela, Jatūkarṇa, Parāśara, Hārīta and Kṣārapāṇi (Ca Sū 1.30-31) — and in every chapter 'thus spoke the venerable Ātreya'. He presides over the debates of the sages and closes them (Sū 12.13, 25.26-29, 26.9).

_Notes: Caraka has Ātreya learn from Bharadvāja (implied by Sū 1.26-30); the Bhāvaprakāśa has Ātreya go to Indra himself. Bhikṣu Ātreya of Ca Sū 25.24 is a different speaker. Whether Kṛṣṇātreya (a śālākya authority in later sources) is the same person is disputed._
