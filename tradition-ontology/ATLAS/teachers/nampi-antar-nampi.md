# Nampi Āṇṭār Nampi

`tch:nampi-antar-nampi` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. late 10th–11th c.; (confidence low)
**Places:** Tirunāraiyūr, Chidambaram
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Tiruttoṇṭar Tiruvantāti](../texts/tiruttontar-tiruvantati.md) — attribution: accepted
  - [Patiṉoṉṟām Tirumuṟai (the eleventh book)](../texts/patinonram-tirumurai.md) — attribution: accepted

Priest of Tirunāraiyūr who, by the tradition's account, recovered the Tēvāram at Chidambaram and arranged the first eleven Tirumuṟai; author of ten works in the eleventh, including the Tiruttoṇṭar Tiruvantāti.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** As a boy he fed the Gaṇeśa (Poḷḷāppiḷḷaiyār) of Tirunāraiyūr, who ate and taught him; guided by that Gaṇeśa he found the hymns in a sealed chamber at Chidambaram, half eaten by termites — what remained was what the Lord wished preserved.
