# Kaṇāda

`tch:kanada` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Kaṇabhuj, Kaṇabhakṣa, Ulūka, Kāśyapa
**Lineages:** [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: an ancient ṛṣi; Scholarly account: the Vaiśeṣika Sūtra core is usually dated around the turn of the era (estimates vary widely); (confidence low)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Vaiśeṣika Sūtra](../texts/vaisesika-sutra.md) — attribution: traditional

The sage to whom the Vaiśeṣika Sūtra is attributed, founder of Vaiśeṣika; Praśastapāda salutes him after Īśvara. His names Kaṇabhuj / Kaṇabhakṣa ('grain-eater') are traditionally explained by his living on gleaned grains; Buddhist and Jain sources call the school Aulūkya after his name Ulūka.
