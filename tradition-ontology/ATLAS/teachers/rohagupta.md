# Rohagupta (Ṣaḍulūka)

`tch:rohagupta` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Alternate names:** Chaḷuya, Ṣaḍulūka
**Lineages:** [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary

The sixth schismatic in Śvetāmbara lists, who in debate asserted three categories — soul, non-soul and 'no-soul' (trairāśika) — and would not recant when refuted by his teacher Śrīgupta; the Jain tradition makes him the founder of the Vaiśeṣika system with its six categories.

_Notes: The identification with Vaiśeṣika is the Jain tradition's account (reported_by_opponent from the Vaiśeṣika point of view)._
