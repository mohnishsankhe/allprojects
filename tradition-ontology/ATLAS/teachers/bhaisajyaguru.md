# Bhaiṣajyaguru, the Medicine Buddha (sangs rgyas sman bla)

`tch:bhaisajyaguru` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Sangye Menla, Menla, Vaiḍūryaprabharāja ('king of lapis-lazuli light'), Yaoshi fo 藥師佛, Yakushi
**Lineages:** [Sowa Rigpa (gso ba rig pa), the Tibetan science of healing](../lineages/sowa-rigpa.md), [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Works:** 
  - [The Four Tantras (rgyud bzhi): the Secret Instruction Tantra on the Eight Branches, the Essence of Nectar (bdud rtsi snying po yan lag brgyad pa gsang ba man ngag gi rgyud)](../texts/gyushi.md) — attribution: traditional

In Sowa Rigpa the teacher of the Four Tantras: seated in the medicine city Tanaduk, he emanates the sage Rigpé Yeshe, who teaches, and Yilé Kyé, who requests. Physicians practise his sādhana and consecrate medicines in his name.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A buddha whose twelve vows (Bhaiṣajyaguru sūtra) include healing all beings.
