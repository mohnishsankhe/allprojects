Founder of the present Aghora lineage at Krīm Kuṇḍ (Kīnārām Sthal), Varanasi. Tradition: born at Rāmgaṛh (Chandauli), he was first initiated by the Vaiṣṇava Śivarām, then received Aghora from Bābā Kālūrām, whom he recognised as Dattātreya (after a vision at Girnar); he wandered to Hiṅglāj and elsewhere, founded monasteries and wrote Hindi works — Vivekasār, Rāmgītā, Rāmrasāl, Gītāvalī.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Received Aghora-dīkṣā from Kālūrām (Dattātreya); famed for miracles and for compassion to the poor; lived about 170 years.
