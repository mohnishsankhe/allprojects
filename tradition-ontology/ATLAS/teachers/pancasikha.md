# Pañcaśikha

**Alternate names:** Kāpileya (in the Mahābhārata), Kāpileya
**Lineages:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md), [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

  - [Pañcaśikha fragments (Pañcaśikhasūtra)](../texts/pancasikha-sutra.md) — attribution: traditional
  - [Ṣaṣṭitantra](../texts/sastitantra.md) — attribution: disputed

Pupil of Āsuri by whom 'the doctrine was expanded' (SK 70). In the Mahābhārata's Mokṣadharma he teaches King Janaka (Janadeva) of Mithilā. Sayings ascribed to him are quoted in the Yoga Bhāṣya (on YS 1.4, 1.25, 1.36, 2.13, as identified by Vācaspati Miśra) and in the Tattvakaumudī on SK 2; the Sāṃkhya Sūtra cites his views by name (5.32, 6.68).
