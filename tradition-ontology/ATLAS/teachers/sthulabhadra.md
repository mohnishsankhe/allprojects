# Sthūlabhadra

`tch:sthulabhadra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Thūlabhadda
**Lineages:** [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Sambhūtavijaya](sambhutavijaya.md), [Bhadrabāhu (the śrutakevalin)](bhadrabahu.md)
**Students:** [Mahāgiri](mahagiri.md), [Suhastin](suhastin.md)

Son of the Nanda minister Śakaṭāla, who lived twelve years with the courtesan Kośā, renounced, and later spent a rainy season in her house without falling (celebrated as the model of chastity). He presided over the council at Pāṭaliputra after the famine; he learned the fourteen Pūrvas from Bhadrabāhu, but after he displayed a magical power (turning into a lion before his sisters) Bhadrabāhu taught him the last four as text only, without their meaning — the beginning of the loss of the Pūrvas.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Invoked in the auspicious verse 'maṅgalaṃ sthūlabhadrādyā' after Mahāvīra and Gautama (Śvetāmbara form).
