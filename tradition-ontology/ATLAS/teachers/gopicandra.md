# Gopīcandra (Gopīcand)

**Alternate names:** Gopīcand, Govindacandra (identification debated)
**Lineages:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), `lin:folk-natha-siddha`
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Jālandharanātha](jalandharanatha.md)

King of Bengal in the Nāth cycle, son of Mayanāmatī, who at his mother's insistence renounced his kingdom and queens and took initiation from Hāḍipā (Jālandhara) to escape an early death; hero of Bengali, Hindi and Rajasthani sagas.
