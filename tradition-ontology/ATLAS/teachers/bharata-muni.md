The sage to whom the Nāṭyaśāstra is ascribed: Brahmā taught him the Nāṭyaveda, which he performed with his hundred sons before the gods; his sons' mockery of the sages brought a curse, and drama was brought down to earth at King Nahuṣa's request (NŚ 1, 36).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Received the Nāṭyaveda from Brahmā, who composed it by yoga from the four Vedas.
