# Bālnāth

**Lineages:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Places:** Tilla Jogian
**Historicity:** legendary

Nāth yogī associated with Tilla Jogian (Gorakh Ṭillā) in the Salt Range; in Wāris Shāh's Hīr he gives Rāñjhā the split-ear initiation.

_Notes: Distinct from the Himachali folk deity Bābā Bālaknāth (U59), with whom he is sometimes confused._
