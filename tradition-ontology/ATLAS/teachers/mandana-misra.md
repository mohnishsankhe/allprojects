Mīmāṃsaka of the Bhāṭṭa line (by tradition Kumārila's pupil): author of the Vidhiviveka (injunction means 'being a means to the desired'), Bhāvanāviveka, Vibhramaviveka, Mīmāṃsānukramaṇī, the Sphoṭasiddhi (defending sphoṭa) and the Advaita Brahmasiddhi.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** In Śaṅkara's hagiographies Maṇḍana, a ritualist householder, debates Śaṅkara with his wife Ubhaya-Bhāratī as judge, is defeated, takes sannyāsa and becomes Sureśvara.

_Notes: The identification with Sureśvara is traditional and disputed by modern scholarship._
