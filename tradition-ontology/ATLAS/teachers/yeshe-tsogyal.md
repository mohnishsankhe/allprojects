# Yeshe Tsogyal (ye shes mtsho rgyal)

**Lineages:** [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: 8th c.; Scholarly account: 8th c. (her full hagiography is a 17th-c. treasure); (confidence low)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Padmasambhava (Guru Rinpoche)](padmasambhava.md)

Tibetan noblewoman who became Padmasambhava's consort and principal disciple; she compiled and concealed his teachings as treasures, received the Khandro Nyingthig lineage, and is revered as a fully enlightened ḍākinī (the Queen of Great Bliss of the Longchen Nyingthig).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Attained buddhahood in one life and departed without leaving a body.
