# Niguma

`tch:niguma` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Vimalā, ye shes mkha' 'gro ma
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md), [Shangpa Kagyu](../lineages/shangpa-kagyu.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Students:** [Khyungpo Naljor](khyungpo-naljor.md)

Kashmiri yoginī (c. 10th–11th c.), in Tibetan accounts Nāropa's sister or consort, who taught Khyungpo Naljor the six dharmas that bear her name and the mahāmudrā of the 'amulet box'; source of the Shangpa Kagyu.

_Notes: Her 'six dharmas' and the Shangpa details belong mainly to U46._
