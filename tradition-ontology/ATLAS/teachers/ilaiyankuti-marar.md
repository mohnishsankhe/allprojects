# Iḷaiyāṉkuṭi Māṟa Nāyaṉār

**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Iḷaiyāṉkuṭi
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. A farmer who fed devotees until he was destitute; on a night of heavy rain he gathered the newly sown paddy from his field and pulled up his roof-beams for firewood to feed a guest, who was Śiva.
