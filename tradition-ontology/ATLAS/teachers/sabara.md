Author of the Śābarabhāṣya, the earliest surviving commentary on the whole Mīmāṃsā Sūtra and the base text of both later schools.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A late popular verse makes Śabarasvāmin, a brāhmaṇa, the father by wives of the four varṇas of Varāhamihira, King Vikramāditya, Bhartṛhari and others; another story says he lived disguised as a śabara (forest-dweller). These are legends, not Mīmāṃsā doctrine.
