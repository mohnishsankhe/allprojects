Founder of the Navadvīpa school of Navya-Nyāya; his Dīdhiti on the Tattvacintāmaṇi became the base of later study, and his Padārthatattvanirūpaṇa re-examined the list of categories.

_Notes: The tradition tells of his defeating Pakṣadhara in debate in Mithilā (low confidence)._
