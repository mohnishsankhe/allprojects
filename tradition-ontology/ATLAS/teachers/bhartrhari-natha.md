# Bhartṛhari (Bharthari, the Nāth king-yogī)

`tch:bhartrhari-natha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Bharthari, Bhartarīnāth, Rājā Bharthari
**Lineages:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), `lin:folk-natha-siddha`
**Places:** Ujjain (Bharthari's cave), Alwar (Bhartṛhari)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Gorakṣanātha](goraksanatha.md)

King of Ujjain in Nāth legend who renounced his throne — in the best-known telling after the fruit of immortality he gave his queen returned to him through her lover — and became a disciple of Gorakh; founder of the Bharthari (Vairāg) panth; hero of sung sagas; counted among the Marathi nine Nāths. Tradition often identifies him with the poet of the three śatakas and with Bhartṛhari the grammarian.

_Notes: Distinct id from tch:bhartrhari (the grammarian of the Vākyapadīya, U31). The tradition's identification of the three is not accepted by scholars._
