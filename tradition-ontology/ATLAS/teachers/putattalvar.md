Second of the 'first Āḻvārs'; author of the Iraṇṭām Tiruvantāti, which lights the lamp of love, longing and a melting mind for Nāraṇaṉ.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Born in a flower at Tirukkaṭalmallai; at the Tirukkōvalūr meeting he lit the lamp of love with longing as ghee and his melting mind as wick.
