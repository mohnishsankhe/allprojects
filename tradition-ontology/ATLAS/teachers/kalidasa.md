The classical poet; Śākta tradition holds that he gained his genius by the Goddess's (Kālī's) grace and ascribes to him the Śyāmalā Daṇḍaka and other hymns.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** An unlettered man who became the greatest poet when the Goddess wrote on his tongue.
