# Dakṣa Prajāpati

`tch:daksa-prajapati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Prajāpati
**Lineages:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Teachers:** [Brahmā (as first teacher)](brahma.md)
**Students:** [The Aśvins](asvins.md)

The Prajāpati who received Āyurveda from Brahmā and taught it to the Aśvins (Ca Sū 1.4; Su Sū 1.20; AHS Sū 1.3); the Bhāvaprakāśa names him Dakṣa.

_Notes: Caraka, Suśruta and Vāgbhaṭa say only 'Prajāpati'; Bhāvaprakāśa Pūrva 1.6 and the commentators say Dakṣa._
