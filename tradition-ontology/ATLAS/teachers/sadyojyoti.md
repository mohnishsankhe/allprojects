Kashmirian theologian of the Śaiva Siddhānta, the earliest datable exegete of its scriptures; he commented on the Svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgraha and wrote short doctrinal treatises (the Nareśvaraparīkṣā, Mokṣakārikā, Bhogakārikā and others).
