# Gṛtsamada

`tch:grtsamada` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Gṛtsamada Bhārgava Śaunaka, Āṅgirasa Śaunahotra
**Lineages:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Śākala śākhā (Ṛgveda)](../lineages/sakha-sakala.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Ṛgveda Saṃhitā](../texts/rgveda.md) — attribution: traditional

Seer of most of Ṛgveda book 2 (e.g. 2.12 on Indra, whom some say 'does not exist' — 'have faith in him'; 2.33 to Rudra the best of physicians). The Anukramaṇī calls him formerly Āṅgirasa Śaunahotra, later Bhārgava Śaunaka.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The tradition counts him among the seers who 'saw' the hymns of the second maṇḍala.

_Notes: Seer attributions follow the Sarvānukramaṇī as printed in the hymn headers of the local Ṛgveda text (checked)._
