# Bharata (the first world-emperor)

`tch:bharata-cakravartin` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Bharata Cakravartin
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Ṛṣabha (Ṛṣabhanātha, Ādinātha)](rsabha.md)

Eldest son of Ṛṣabha and first of the twelve world-emperors (cakravartin) of this era, after whom Bhāratavarṣa is named in Jain tradition. He conquered the six divisions of Bharata-kṣetra; Digambaras (Ādipurāṇa) say he created the brāhmaṇas from among the laity who avoided trampling sprouts; Śvetāmbaras tell that he attained omniscience while looking in a mirror in his palace, seeing the body's unreality when a ring fell from his finger.
