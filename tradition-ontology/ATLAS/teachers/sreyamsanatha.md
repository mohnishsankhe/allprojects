# Śreyāṃsanātha

**Alternate names:** Śreyāṃsa
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: the eleventh of the twenty-four Jinas of this era (tradition's chronology of immense lifespans and intervals); (confidence low)
**Places:** Siṃhapurī, Sammeda Śikhara
**Historicity:** legendary

The eleventh Tīrthaṅkara of the present descending half-cycle (avasarpiṇī). Emblem (lāñchana): the rhinoceros. Parents: Viṣṇu and Viṣṇu(-śrī) (low confidence). Born at Siṃhapurī; attained nirvāṇa at Sammeda Śikhara. The first vāsudeva, Tripṛṣṭha, lived in his time.

_Notes: Parents, birthplaces and emblems follow the common Śvetāmbara lists (e.g. Hemacandra); Digambara lists agree in most points and differ in some emblems and names._
