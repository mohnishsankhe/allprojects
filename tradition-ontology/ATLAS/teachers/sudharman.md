# Sudharman

**Alternate names:** Suhamma, Sudharmā Svāmī, Lohārya (a Digambara name; low confidence)
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary

**Works:** 
  - [Śvetāmbara Āgamas (the Jain canon)](../texts/svetambara-agamas.md) — attribution: traditional

The fifth gaṇadhara, whose doubt was whether beings are reborn in the same kind; the only gaṇadhara besides Gautama to survive Mahāvīra, he became head of the community, and the Śvetāmbara Aṅgas are his recension, addressed to his pupil Jambū ('I have heard, O long-lived one…'). All later Śvetāmbara monks trace their descent to him.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Attained omniscience twelve years after Mahāvīra's nirvāṇa and liberation eight years later (Śvetāmbara account; moderate confidence).
