The mysterious founder of the Kartābhajā, who appeared as a youth in a betel garden at Ulā (Nadia), was held to be Caitanya returned, wandered with twenty-two disciples, and chose Rāmśaraṇ Pāl as his successor.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Caitanya, who had disappeared at Puri, reappeared in Bengal as Āulcānd to reveal the secret sahaja religion to the poor and low-caste.
