# Tapihritsa (ta pi hri tsa)

**Lineages:** [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md), [Zhang Zhung Nyengyü (the Oral Transmission of Zhang Zhung)](../lineages/zhang-zhung-nyengyu.md)

**Students:** [Gyerpung Nangzher Löpo (gyer spungs snang bzher lod po)](nangzher-lopo.md)

Zhang Zhung master of the Nyengyü lineage who attained the rainbow body and later appeared as a boy to Gyerpung Nangzher Löpo to transmit the Oral Transmission; visualized as a naked, crystal-white figure in guru yoga.
