# Tirunīlakaṇṭa Nāyaṉār

`tch:tirunilakantar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Tirunīlakaṇṭar the potter
**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Chidambaram
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. A potter of Tillai who, reproached by his wife for his infidelity, vowed 'by Tirunīlakaṇṭam' never to touch her and kept the vow into old age; when Śiva as a yogī's begging bowl left in his keeping vanished, the couple had to bathe holding hands to prove their word — they held a stick between them, the vow was revealed, and they rose from the water young again.
