# Ajātaśatru of Kāśī

`tch:ajatasatru-kasi` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Ajātaśatru Kāśya
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
**Places:** Kāśī
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Students:** [Gārgya Bālāki](gargya-balaki.md)

King of Kāśī who refutes Gārgya Bālāki's twelve (KauU: sixteen) identifications of brahman and teaches the self into which the sleeping person withdraws, from which all breaths, worlds and gods come forth like sparks (BAU 2.1; KauU 4). He notes it is against the natural order for a brahmin to come to a kṣatriya to learn brahman.

_Notes: Distinct from the Magadhan king of the Buddhist texts; id disambiguated._
