# Sivavākkiyar (Civavākkiyar)

`tch:sivavakkiyar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Civavākkiyar, Śivavākyar
**Lineages:** [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: undetermined; Siddhar works are usually placed after c. 1400; (confidence low)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Civavākkiyam (the songs of Sivavākkiyar)](../texts/sivavakkiyam.md) — attribution: traditional

The most iconoclastic Siddhar poet: his Civavākkiyam mocks stone images, temple ritual, pilgrimage, Vedic recitation and caste, locates the Lord within, and in one famous verse denies the rebirth of the dead. His name is explained as 'he whose (first) utterance was Śiva'.

_Notes: The Śrīvaiṣṇava tradition identifies him with Tirumaḻicai Āḻvār in his Śaiva phase; others reject the identification. Not in the popular list of eighteen, but in other lists and in every Siddhar anthology._
