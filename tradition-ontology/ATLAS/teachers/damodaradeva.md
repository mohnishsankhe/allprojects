# Dāmodaradeva

**Lineages:** [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md), [Brahma saṃhati (Dāmodarīyā)](../lineages/brahma-samhati.md)
**Places:** Vaikuṇṭhapur (Bārpeṭā)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Śaṅkaradeva](sankaradeva.md)
**Students:** [Bhaṭṭadeva (Vaikuṇṭhanātha Bhāgavata Bhaṭṭācārya)](bhattadeva.md)

Brahmin preacher (tradition 1488–1598), founder of the Brahma saṃhati; the Mahāpuruṣīyā account makes him Śaṅkaradeva's disciple, while his own followers (Dāmodarīyā) stress his independence.
