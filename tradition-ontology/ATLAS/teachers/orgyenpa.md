# Orgyenpa Rinchen Pal

**Alternate names:** o rgyan pa rin chen dpal
**Lineages:** [Drukpa Kagyu](../lineages/drukpa-kagyu.md), [Karma Kagyu (Kamtsang Kagyu)](../lineages/karma-kagyu.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 1229/1230–1309; (confidence moderate)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Gyalwa Götsangpa Gönpo Dorje](gotsangpa.md), [Karma Pakshi, the second Karmapa](karma-pakshi.md)

Drukpa master, disciple of Götsangpa, famous for his journey to Oḍḍiyāna and the 'approach and accomplishment of the three vajras' received there; teacher of the 3rd Karmapa, whom (by the tradition's account) he recognized as the rebirth of Karma Pakshi.
