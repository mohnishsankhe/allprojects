# Garga (Pāśupata)

`tch:garga-pasupata` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Gārgya, Garbha (variant reading, Liṅga P. 1.7.52)
**Lineages:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Lakulīśa](lakulisa.md)

The second of Lakulīśa's four disciples in the Purāṇic lists (Liṅga P. 1.24.131; Kūrma P. 1.51.26) and third in the Jain lists of eighteen tīrthakaras (as Gārgya); a Pāśupata line was traced to him.
