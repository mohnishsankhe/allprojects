Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): 'I am Dhanvantari, the first god, remover of old age, disease and death for the immortals, come again to earth to teach the limb of surgery with the other limbs' (Su Sū 1.21). As King Divodāsa of Kāśī he taught Aupadhenava, Vaitaraṇa, Aurabhra, Pauṣkalāvata, Karavīrya, Gopurarakṣita, Suśruta and others (Su Sū 1.3), having learnt Āyurveda from Indra (Su Sū 1.20); the Bhāvaprakāśa tells how Indra sent him to be born in Kāśī.

_Notes: Ca Śā 6.21 cites Dhanvantari's view that all limbs of the embryo form at once._
