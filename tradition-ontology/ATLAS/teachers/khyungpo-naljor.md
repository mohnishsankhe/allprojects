# Khyungpo Naljor

**Alternate names:** khyung po rnal 'byor tshul khrims mgon po
**Lineages:** [Shangpa Kagyu](../lineages/shangpa-kagyu.md)

**Places:** Shang (Tsang), Zhong-zhong

Tibetan yogin, first trained in Bön and Dzogchen, who travelled to India and Nepal, received the six dharmas and the Amulet Mahāmudrā from Niguma and teachings from Sukhasiddhi, Maitrīpa and others, and founded the Shangpa Kagyu with its seat at Zhong-zhong in Shang.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** He met Niguma in a charnel ground (Sosaling), where she appeared in the sky as a dark-skinned ḍākinī; she taught him in dreams and waking, and bade that the teachings pass to one disciple at a time for seven generations.
