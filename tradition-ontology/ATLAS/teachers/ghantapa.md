# Ghaṇṭāpa

`tch:ghantapa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Vajraghaṇṭa, dril bu pa (Tibetan), g+haN+Ta pa
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary

No. 52 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A royal monk-scholar of strict celibacy whom the king of Pāṭaliputra tried to shame by sending a young woman; he took her as consort and, when accused, the two rose into the sky as Cakrasaṃvara and Vajravārāhī; founder of the Ghaṇṭāpa lineage of Cakrasaṃvara (the five-deity body maṇḍala).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A royal monk-scholar of strict celibacy whom the king of Pāṭaliputra tried to shame by sending a young woman; he took her as consort and, when accused, the two rose into the sky as Cakrasaṃvara and Vajravārāhī; founder of the Ghaṇṭāpa lineage of Cakrasaṃvara (the five-deity body maṇḍala). Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 52): The three — lalanā, rasanā and avadhūtī — the guru blessed and bound; the three — self, mind and appearance — the wise make firm so that realization may arise.
