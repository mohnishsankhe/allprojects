# Mārīca Kaśyapa

`tch:marica-kasyapa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Kaśyapa, Vṛddha Kāśyapa
**Lineages:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Students:** [Vṛddha Jīvaka](vrddha-jivaka.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Kāśyapa Saṃhitā](../texts/kasyapa-samhita.md) — attribution: traditional

The teacher of children's medicine whose instruction to Vṛddha Jīvaka forms the Kāśyapa Saṃhitā; in Ca Śā 6.21 'Mārīci Kaśyapa' holds that the order of the embryo's limbs cannot be known, being beyond perception.
