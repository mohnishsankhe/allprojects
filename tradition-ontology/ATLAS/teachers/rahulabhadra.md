# Rāhulabhadra

`tch:rahulabhadra` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Alternate names:** sgra gcan 'dzin bzang po (Tibetan), Rāhulata (Chinese patriarch lists)
**Lineages:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Prajñāpāramitāstotra](../texts/prajnaparamitastotra.md) — attribution: traditional

Early Madhyamaka poet to whom the Hymn to the Perfection of Wisdom is ascribed; the lineage accounts place him either as Nāgārjuna's teacher (Tibetan) or as a successor of Āryadeva (Chinese patriarch lists).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** In some Tibetan accounts the brahmin Rāhulabhadra (identified with Saraha) ordained Nāgārjuna at Nālandā; in the Chinese lineage of the Dharma-treasure he follows Kāṇadeva (Āryadeva).

_Notes: Conflicting placements are both the traditions' accounts; not reconciled here._
