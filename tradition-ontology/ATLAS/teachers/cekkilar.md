# Cēkkiḻār

**Alternate names:** Aruḷmoḻittēvar (name as minister), Toṇṭar Cīr Paravuvār
**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md), [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md)

**Places:** Kuṉṟattūr, Chidambaram
**Historicity:** historical
**Works:** 
  - [Periya Purāṇam](../texts/periya-puranam.md) — attribution: accepted

Minister of the Cōḻa king Kulōttuṅka II (Anapāyaṉ), author of the Periya Purāṇam, the twelfth Tirumuṟai.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Seeing the king delight in the Jain epic Cīvakacintāmaṇi, he turned him to the lives of Śiva's devotees; at Chidambaram Śiva gave him the first word 'ulakelām' and he completed the work in a year.
