# Ryōnin

`tch:ryonin` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** 良忍, Shōō Daishi
**Lineages:** [Yūzū Nenbutsu-shū (the interfusing nenbutsu of Ryōnin)](../lineages/yuzu-nenbutsu-shu.md), `lin:tendai`, [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 1073–1132 CE; (confidence moderate)
**Places:** Ōhara (Raigō-in)
**Historicity:** historical

Tendai monk of Ōhara, master of shōmyō chant, founder of the Yūzū nenbutsu: he enrolled nobles and commoners — and, in the tradition's account, gods — in a register of nenbutsu practitioners whose recitations interpenetrate.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** In 1117, in samādhi, Amida revealed to him the interfusing nenbutsu (Yūzū nenbutsu engi).
