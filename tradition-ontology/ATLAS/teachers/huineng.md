Sixth and last patriarch. In the tradition's account an illiterate firewood seller from Lingnan who awoke on hearing the Diamond Sūtra, laboured pounding rice at Hongren's monastery, answered Shenxiu's verse with 'originally there is not a single thing', received robe and Dharma at night, hid among hunters for fifteen years, revealed himself at the wind-and-flag debate, and taught the sudden teaching at Caoxi; his body is preserved at Nanhua temple. Scholars see him as a historical but obscure southern teacher whose image was shaped by Shenhui and the Platform Sūtra.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** On hearing 'one should produce a mind that abides nowhere' from the Diamond Sūtra he 'greatly awakened that all dharmas are not apart from the self-nature' and exclaimed that the self-nature is originally pure, unborn, complete, unmoving and able to produce all dharmas (Platform Sūtra 4.13).
