# Gotama Buddha

`tch:gotama-buddha` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Siddhattha Gotama, Sakyamuni, the Tathāgata, the Blessed One (Bhagavā), Samaṇa Gotama
**Lineages:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: born 623 BCE, awakened 588 BCE, parinibbāna 543 BCE (Theravāda Buddhist-era reckoning; Buddhist Era counted from 544/543 BCE); Scholarly account: c. 5th c. BCE; the parinibbāna is placed c. 486 BCE on the 'corrected long chronology' or c. 400 BCE (±20) on the 'short chronology' now preferred by many scholars; (confidence moderate)
**Places:** Kapilavatthu, Lumbinī, Uruvelā (Bodh Gayā), Isipatana (Sārnāth), Rājagaha, Sāvatthī, Vesālī, Kusinārā
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Āḷāra Kālāma](alara-kalama.md), [Uddaka Rāmaputta](uddaka-ramaputta.md)
**Students:** [Sāriputta](sariputta.md), [Mahāmoggallāna](moggallana.md), [Ānanda](ananda.md), [Mahākassapa](mahakassapa.md), [Upāli](upali.md), [Anuruddha](anuruddha.md), [Mahākaccāna (Mahākaccāyana)](mahakaccana.md), [Aññāsi Koṇḍañña](kondanna.md), [Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī](mahapajapati-gotami.md), [Khemā](khema.md), [Uppalavaṇṇā](uppalavanna.md), [Rāhula](rahula.md)

The Blessed One who, at Sāvatthī on the full-moon night ending the rains, praises the assembly and teaches mindfulness of breathing.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** After six years of striving and the rejection of self-mortification, he recalled the first jhāna, took food, entered the four jhānas and in the three watches of the night attained recollection of past lives, the divine eye and the destruction of the effluents with knowledge of the four truths; 'unshakeable is my liberation, this is my last birth' (MN 26, MN 36, MN 4; SN 56.11; Vin Mv 1.1).
