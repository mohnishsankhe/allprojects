The great systematizer of the Jonang and of the other-emptiness (gzhan stong) view. Born in Dolpo, he trained at Sakya, received the Kālacakra six-branch yoga at Jonang, became its abbot, built its great stūpa, oversaw a new translation of the Kālacakra tantra and Vimalaprabhā, and taught — in the Mountain Doctrine, the Fourth Council and many other works — that the ultimate, buddha-nature, is permanent and empty only of what is other than it. Known as 'the Omniscient One of Dolpo'.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** The Jonang account holds that the other-emptiness view dawned in Dolpopa's own experience through the six-branch yoga before he set it out in writing, and that he was an emanation of the Kalkī Puṇḍarīka, author of the Vimalaprabhā (emanation claim: low confidence).
