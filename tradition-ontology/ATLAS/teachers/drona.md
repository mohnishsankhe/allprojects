# Droṇa

`tch:drona` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** ācārya, dvijottama, the ācārya
**Lineages:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

The teacher (ācārya), 'best of the twice-born', to whom Duryodhana speaks (1.2–11) and whose disciple Dhṛṣṭadyumna arrayed the Pāṇḍava army (1.3); with Bhīṣma one whom Arjuna holds worthy of worship (1.25, 2.4).

_Notes: That the ācārya of 1.2–11 is Droṇa is the epic context; Droṇa is named at 1.25 and 2.4. Linked in BhG ch. 1–3 at 1.2, 1.3, 1.7, 1.8, 1.25, 2.4._
