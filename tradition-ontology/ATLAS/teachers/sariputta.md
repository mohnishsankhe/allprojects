# Sāriputta

**Alternate names:** Upatissa, Dhammasenāpati

The Buddha's right-hand chief disciple, foremost in wisdom; a brahmin (Upatissa) from Nālaka, formerly a pupil of Sañjaya, converted by Assaji's verse 'ye dhammā hetuppabhavā'; expounder of MN 9, 28, 43, 141, DN 33–34 and many Nikāya discourses, and by tradition of the Paṭisambhidāmagga and Niddesa; he died shortly before the Buddha (SN 47.13).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Became a stream-enterer on hearing Assaji's verse (Vin Mv 1.23); by the commentarial account attained arahantship a fortnight after ordination while fanning the Buddha during the discourse to Dīghanakha (MN 74).

_Notes: AN 1 Etadaggavagga (the 'foremost' lists)_
