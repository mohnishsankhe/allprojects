# Cuntarāṉantar (Sundarānandar)

`tch:sundaranandar` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Alternate names:** Cuntarāṉantar, Sundaranandar
**Lineages:** [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: undetermined; Siddhar works are usually placed after c. 1400; (confidence low)
**Places:** Madurai — samādhi in the popular list
**Historicity:** legendary

Siddhar associated with Madurai: the Siddhar shrine in the Mīṉākṣī temple is linked by tradition with him and with Śiva's sport as the 'all-powerful Siddhar' (ellām valla cittar) who made the stone elephant eat sugarcane.

_Notes: The link between the Tiruviḷaiyāṭal episode and this Siddhar is recalled at low confidence. Counted in the popular list of the eighteen Siddhars (see cpt:eighteen-siddhars); the samādhi place given follows that list and is recalled at low confidence._
