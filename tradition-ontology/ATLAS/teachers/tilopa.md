No. 22 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A brahmin who served as a royal priest, then became a monk and yogin; lived as a sesame pounder and as servant to a courtesan in Bengal; by the Tibetan account received the four instruction lineages from Caryāpa, Nāgārjuna, Lavapa and Sukhasiddhi and directly from Vajradhara; guru of Nāropa.
