# Jālandharanātha

`tch:jalandharanatha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Jālandhari, Jālandharipā, Hāḍipā, Hāḍi-siddha, Jālandharīpāv (Hindi), Jālandharapāda, dzA lan+d+ha ra (Tibetan)
**Lineages:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Students:** [Kāṇhapa (Kṛṣṇācārya)](kanha.md), [Gopīcandra (Gopīcand)](gopicandra.md), [Tantipa](tantipa.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Nāth Siddhoṁ kī Bāniyāṁ](../texts/nath-siddhon-ki-baniyan.md) — attribution: traditional

A siddha counted among the nine Nāths; in the Bengali Gopīcandra cycle he is Hāḍipā, the siddha living as a sweeper (hāḍi) who initiates King Gopīcandra at Mayanāmatī's request; his disciple is Kāṇha (Kānipā). Also in the Buddhist siddha lists as Jālandhari(pā).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A great siddha whose power humbles kings; Gopīcandra's guru.

_Notes: Life from Abhayadatta's Caturaśītisiddhapravṛtti recalled from memory (not read locally); the song is read locally in Tōh 2292._
