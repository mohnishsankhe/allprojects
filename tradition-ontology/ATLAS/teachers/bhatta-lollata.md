# Bhaṭṭa Lollaṭa

`tch:bhatta-lollata` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 8th–9th c. CE; (confidence low)
**Historicity:** historical

Early commentator on the Nāṭyaśāstra, known through Abhinavagupta's report: rasa is the stable emotion intensified (upacita) by the determinants, existing primarily in the character (Rāma) and secondarily in the actor who imitates him.

_Notes: His views are known only through opponents' reports (Abhinavabhāratī, Kāvyaprakāśa)._
