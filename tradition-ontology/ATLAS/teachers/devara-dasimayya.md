The weaver-saint (Jēḍara Dāsimayya) of Muddanūru, reckoned by the tradition among the earliest vacanakāras, before Basava; his vacanas, signed Rāmanātha, deny that the self is male or female.

_Notes: Whether 'Dēvara' and 'Jēḍara' Dāsimayya are one person is debated._
