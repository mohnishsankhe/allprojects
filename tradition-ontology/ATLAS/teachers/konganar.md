# Koṅkaṇar

**Lineages:** [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: undetermined; Siddhar works are usually placed after c. 1400; (confidence low)
**Places:** Tirupati — samādhi in the popular list
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Bogar (Pōkar)](bogar.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Koṅkaṇar vāta kāviyam](../texts/konganar-vata-kaviyam.md) — attribution: traditional

Siddhar and alchemist, in the tradition a disciple of Bogar. A popular story tells that he burnt a crane with his glance and was then rebuked by a devoted housewife who had kept him waiting: 'Did you take me for the crane, Koṅkaṇa?' — sending him to learn from her and from Tiruvaḷḷuvar.

_Notes: The crane story (kokkeṉṟu niṉaittāyō koṅkaṇavā) is recalled with moderate confidence. Counted in the popular list of the eighteen Siddhars (see cpt:eighteen-siddhars); the samādhi place given follows that list and is recalled at low confidence._
