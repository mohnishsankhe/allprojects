# Bhaṭṭa Rāmakaṇṭha (Rāmakaṇṭha II)

The foremost Kashmirian exegete of the Siddhānta: commentaries on the Mataṅgapārameśvara, Kiraṇa, Sārdhatriśatikālottara, and Sadyojyoti's Nareśvaraparīkṣā, Mokṣakārikā and Paramokṣanirāsakārikā. He argued that mala is a substance removable only by Śiva's power in initiation, that the liberated soul becomes equal to Śiva, and answered the non-dualist Śaivas.

_Notes: Correction to the unit brief: 'Rāmakaṇṭha II' and 'Bhaṭṭa Rāmakaṇṭha' are the same person (the Saiddhāntika son of Nārāyaṇakaṇṭha). He is distinct from Rājānaka Rāmakaṇṭha, author of the Spandakārikāvivṛti and a Gītā commentary (non-dualist milieu). Some scholars suggest the Śrīkaṇṭha of the Ratnatraya was his teacher (low confidence)._
