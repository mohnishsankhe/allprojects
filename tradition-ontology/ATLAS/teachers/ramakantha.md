Kashmirian Saiddhāntika exegete, son of Nārāyaṇakaṇṭha, who commented on the Kiraṇa, the Mataṅgapārameśvara and the Sārdhatriśatikālottara, and on works of Sadyojyoti, maintaining that liberation is the soul's becoming equal to Śiva, not identity with him.
