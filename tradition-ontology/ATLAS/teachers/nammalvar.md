# Nammāḻvār

`tch:nammalvar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Śaṭhakopa, Śaṭhāri, Parāṅkuśa, Māṟaṉ
**Lineages:** `lin:alvar`, [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Students:** [Nāthamuni](nathamuni.md)

(U25 owns.) For Viśiṣṭādvaita: the 'first head of the clan' (kulapati) of the Śrīvaiṣṇavas, from whom Nāthamuni received the Prabandham; his Tiruvāymoḻi is the 'Tamil Upaniṣad' (Dramiḍopaniṣad) expounded by the ācāryas, and Deśika's Pādukāsahasra identifies him with the Lord's sandals.
