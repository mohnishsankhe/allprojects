# Sena (Sena Nhāvī)

`tch:sena` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Alternate names:** Sen, Sain
**Lineages:** [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md), [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Rāmānanda](ramananda.md)

Barber-saint. In the Marathi tradition a Vārkarī poet whose place the Lord took to shave the king while Sena was absorbed in worship; in the Bhaktamāl a barber of Bāndhavgaṛh and one of Rāmānanda's twelve disciples; a hymn under the name Sain stands in the Sikh scripture (context only).

_Notes: Whether the Marathi Sena and the Hindi Sena are one person is disputed; kept as one entry with both lineages._
