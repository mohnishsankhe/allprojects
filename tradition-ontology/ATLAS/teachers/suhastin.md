# Suhastin

`tch:suhastin` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Suhatthi
**Lineages:** [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Sthūlabhadra](sthulabhadra.md)
**Students:** [Samprati](samprati.md)

Pupil of Sthūlabhadra who converted the Maurya king Samprati, grandson of Aśoka, a great patron of Jainism; the Kalpa Sūtra's list of gaṇas and kulas descends largely from his pupils.
