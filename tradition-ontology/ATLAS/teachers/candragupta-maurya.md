# Candragupta Maurya

`tch:candragupta-maurya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: reigned c. 321–297 BCE; (confidence moderate)
**Places:** Pāṭaliputra, Śravaṇabeḷagoḷa (Candragiri)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Bhadrabāhu (the śrutakevalin)](bhadrabahu.md)

Founder of the Maurya empire. Digambara tradition (and inscriptions at Śravaṇabeḷagoḷa) says he renounced, followed Bhadrabāhu south and died by sallekhanā on Candragiri; Hemacandra's Śvetāmbara account also makes him end his life as a Jain.
