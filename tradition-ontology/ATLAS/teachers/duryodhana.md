# Duryodhana

`tch:duryodhana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Dhṛtarāṣṭra's son, king of the Kaurava side (rājā, 1.2), who names the leading warriors of both armies to his teacher and urges that Bhīṣma be protected (1.2–11); Arjuna calls him the evil-minded son of Dhṛtarāṣṭra (1.23).
