# Khāṇḍikya Janaka

`tch:khandikya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Keśidhvaja](kesidhvaja.md)

A Janaka king devoted to ritual action (karma), expelled by Keśidhvaja; asked by him for the expiation for a killed cow, he chose instead to ask for the teaching of yoga, then renounced and dissolved into the Brahman called Viṣṇu (VP 6.6-7.104).
