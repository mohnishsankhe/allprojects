Author of two commentaries on the Yoga Sūtra, the Yogasiddhāntacandrikā and the Sūtrārthabodhinī; a devotee of Kṛṣṇa who interprets Yoga within Vedānta and subsumes many named yogas under the eight-limbed yoga.

_Notes: Identity with Nārāyaṇa Tīrtha, composer of the Kṛṣṇalīlātaraṅgiṇī, is sometimes asserted — not verified._
