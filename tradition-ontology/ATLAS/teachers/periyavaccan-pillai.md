# Periyavāccāṉ Piḷḷai

`tch:periyavaccan-pillai` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Kṛṣṇa (Periyavāccāṉ), Vyākhyāna-cakravarti
**Lineages:** [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md), [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md)

**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Nampiḷḷai](nampillai.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Irupattunālāyirappaṭi (the 'Twenty-four Thousand')](../texts/irupattunalayirappati.md) — attribution: accepted

Disciple of Nampiḷḷai, called the 'emperor of commentators' (vyākhyāna-cakravarti), who wrote Maṇipravāḷa commentaries on nearly the whole Divya Prabandham, including the Irupattunālāyirappaṭi on the Tiruvāymoḻi.
