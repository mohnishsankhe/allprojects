U44 contribution (the siddha of the eighty-four): No. 12 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A great scholar of Vikramaśīla invited to Siṃhala; returning, he met Koṭali, a peasant digging the mountainside to whom he had once given instruction, and found that Koṭali had realized it while he had not; he then practised twelve years and attained the fruit.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A great scholar of Vikramaśīla invited to Siṃhala; returning, he met Koṭali, a peasant digging the mountainside to whom he had once given instruction, and found that Koṭali had realized it while he had not; he then practised twelve years and attained the fruit. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 12): As a child nourished by its mother grows to a wrestler's strength, one of small mind, gradually raised by the guru's instruction, becomes of the great vehicle; as medicines cure the diseases of wind, bile, phlegm and their combination, the guru's instruction burns in an instant the clinging to 'I' and self.
