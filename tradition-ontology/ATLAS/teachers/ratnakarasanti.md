Vikramaśīla scholar (by tradition 'gate-keeper' of the eastern gate) and tantric master, counted among the eighty-four mahāsiddhas as Śāntipa; the chief defender of image-less (nirākāra / alīkākāra) Yogācāra and teacher of Atiśa.
