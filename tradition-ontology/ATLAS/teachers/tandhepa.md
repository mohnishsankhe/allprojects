No. 33 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A gambler who lost everything and was taught that the three realms are as empty as his purse.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A gambler who lost everything and was taught that the three realms are as empty as his purse. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 33): As the many recollections and awarenesses are exhausted and cease in the non-conceptual expanse, so all phenomena of appearance and existence are exhausted and cease in the expanse of emptiness.
