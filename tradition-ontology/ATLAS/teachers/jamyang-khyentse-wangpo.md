Co-founder of the Rimé movement. A monk of the Sakya (Ngor) tradition based at Dzongsar, he sought out and received the teachings of all schools from many masters, revealed treasures, and was regarded as the holder of the seven modes of transmission; he inspired and directed Kongtrul's and Loter Wangpo's collections.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Regarded as the mind-emanation of Jigme Lingpa and as an emanation of Mañjuśrī; holder of the seven transmissions (bka' babs bdun).
