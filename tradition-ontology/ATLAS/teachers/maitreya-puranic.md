# Maitreya (sage)

**Historicity:** legendary

The sage who questions Parāśara in the Viṣṇu Purāṇa (VP 1.1.1-11) and who, in the Bhāgavata's third and fourth books, instructs Vidura on creation and the Lord's deeds. Not the Buddhist Maitreya.
