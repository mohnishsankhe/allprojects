# Cēramāṉ Perumāḷ Nāyaṉār

**Alternate names:** Kaḻaṟṟaṟivār, Peruma(k)kōtaiyār
**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Koṭuṅkōḷūr
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Tirukkayilāya Ñāṉa Ulā](../texts/tirukkayilaya-nana-ula.md) — attribution: accepted

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. A Cēra king who heard the anklets of Naṭarāja's dance at the end of his daily worship; friend of Cuntarar, he rode with him to Kailāsa on a horse into whose ear he whispered the five syllables, and recited his Ulā there.
