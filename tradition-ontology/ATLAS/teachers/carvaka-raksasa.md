# Cārvāka the rākṣasa (Mahābhārata)

`tch:carvaka-raksasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md)
**Historicity:** mythic

In MBh 12.39 a rākṣasa, friend of Duryodhana, who in the guise of a mendicant (a Sāṃkhya with tuft and triple staff) denounces Yudhiṣṭhira as a kinslayer before the assembled brāhmaṇas and is burnt by their 'hum'; Kṛṣṇa explains that he had won from Brahmā fearlessness from all beings except when he insulted brāhmaṇas. He teaches no doctrine; Duryodhana (9.63.38) calls him 'the wanderer skilled in speech'.
