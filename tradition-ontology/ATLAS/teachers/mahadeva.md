# Mahādeva (of the five points)

`tch:mahadeva` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Dazhongtian 大眾天 / Datian 大天
**Lineages:** [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md), [Caitika](../lineages/caitika.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: At the time of the first schism under Aśoka (Vasumitra); a second Mahādeva at the end of the second century founded the Caitika; (confidence low)
**Historicity:** legendary

The monk to whom Vasumitra and the Mahāvibhāṣā attribute the five points on the arhat that caused the first schism; the Mahāsāṃghika sources do not present him so. The Pali tradition knows a different Mahādeva as a missionary.
**Realization — an opponent's account:** The Sarvāstivāda Mahāvibhāṣā tells that Mahādeva, a merchant's son of Mathurā, committed three deadly sins, became a monk at Pāṭaliputra, claimed arhatship and proclaimed five points lowering the arhat, which split the order; this is a hostile account.

_Notes: Reported mainly by the Sarvāstivāda (an opponent)._
