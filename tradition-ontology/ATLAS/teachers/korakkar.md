Siddhar of the popular list whom the tradition identifies with Gorakṣanātha: born from the sacred ash that Maccamuṉi gave a childless woman, who threw it away, until a boy came forth from where it fell. Herbal and alchemical works are ascribed to him; his samādhi is placed at Poyyūr (Vaṭakku Poykainallūr) near Nākappaṭṭiṉam.
