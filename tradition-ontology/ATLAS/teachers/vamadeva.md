# Vāmadeva

`tch:vamadeva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Vāmadeva Gautama
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Śākala śākhā (Ṛgveda)](../lineages/sakha-sakala.md), [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md), [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Ṛgveda Saṃhitā](../texts/rgveda.md) — attribution: traditional
  - [Janmamaraṇavicāra](../texts/janmamaranavicara.md) — attribution: accepted

The Ṛgvedic seer who, seeing that he was brahman, declared 'I was Manu, I was the sun' (BAU 1.4.10) and who, lying in the womb, knew all the births of the gods (AU 2.1.5, quoting RV 4.27.1).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** AU 2.1.5-6: while still in the womb he knew the births of the gods; knowing thus, on the body's breaking he rose up and became immortal.

_Notes: Seer attributions follow the Sarvānukramaṇī as printed in the hymn headers of the local Ṛgveda text (checked). The Anukramaṇī allows Indra (or the self, ātman) as speaker/deity of 4.26.1–3._
