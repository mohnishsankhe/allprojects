# Atipatta Nāyaṉār

`tch:atipattar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Nākappaṭṭiṉam
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. Head of a fishing community who released the first fish of every catch for Śiva; in lean days he caught only one fish and released it, and when a golden fish set with gems came into his net he released that too; Śiva appeared.
