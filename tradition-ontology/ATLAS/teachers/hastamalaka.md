# Hastāmalaka

`tch:hastamalaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Śaṅkara (Ādi Śaṅkarācārya)](sankara.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Hastāmalakīya (Hastāmalaka-stotra)](../texts/hastamalakiya.md) — attribution: traditional

Disciple of Śaṅkara; in the tradition's account a seemingly dull boy who, asked 'who are you?', answered with the verses of the Hastāmalakīya, knowing the self as clearly as 'an āmalaka fruit in the hand'; first head of the Dvārakā (Śāradā) maṭha.
