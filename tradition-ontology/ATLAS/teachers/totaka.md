# Toṭaka (Toṭakācārya)

`tch:totaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Giri (tradition), Troṭaka
**Lineages:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Śaṅkara (Ādi Śaṅkarācārya)](sankara.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Toṭakāṣṭaka](../texts/totakastaka.md) — attribution: traditional
  - [Śrutisārasamuddharaṇa](../texts/srutisara-samuddharana.md) — attribution: traditional

Disciple of Śaṅkara, author (tradition) of the Toṭakāṣṭaka in praise of his guru and the Śrutisārasamuddharaṇa; in the tradition's account a devoted servant (called Giri) whose realization came through the guru's grace, first head of Jyotirmaṭh.

_Notes: Not to be confused with Ānandagiri (Ānandajñāna), the 13th-c. commentator._
