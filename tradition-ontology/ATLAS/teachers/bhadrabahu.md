The last 'scripture-omniscient' (śrutakevalin), who knew all fourteen Pūrvas. Śvetāmbaras say that during a twelve-year famine he withdrew to Nepal to practise the 'great breath' (mahāprāṇa) meditation and there taught the Pūrvas to Sthūlabhadra; they ascribe to him the Kalpa Sūtra, three Chedasūtras and the niryuktis. Digambaras say he foresaw the famine, led the saṅgha south to Karnataka with the Maurya emperor Candragupta as his disciple, and died by sallekhanā on Candragiri at Śravaṇabeḷagoḷa; those who stayed in the north adopted clothes.

_Notes: Scholars distinguish a later Bhadrabāhu (II) to whom the niryuktis may belong._
