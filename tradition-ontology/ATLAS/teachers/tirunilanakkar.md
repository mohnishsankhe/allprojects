# Tirunīlanakka Nāyaṉār

`tch:tirunilanakkar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Tiruccāttamaṅkai
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. A brahmin priest whose wife blew away a spider that fell on the liṅga; he rebuked her for defiling it, and Śiva showed him blisters everywhere on his body except where she had blown. He attained Śiva at Campantar's wedding.
