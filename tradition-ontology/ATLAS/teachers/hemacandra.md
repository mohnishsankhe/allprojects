Śvetāmbara polymath of Gujarat ('Kalikālasarvajña', the omniscient of the Kali age), teacher of king Kumārapāla, for whom he wrote the Yogaśāstra; also author of the Pramāṇamīmāṃsā, the Vītarāgastotra, the two Vyavacchedikā hymns, the Triṣaṣṭiśalākāpuruṣacarita, and works on grammar and poetics. He declares that the last chapter of the Yogaśāstra rests on his own experience.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** He says the twelfth chapter of the Yogaśāstra sets out what is 'established by experience' (anubhavasiddha), beyond what he learned from scripture and the teacher.
