# Ākāśagarbha

`tch:akasagarbha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Xukongzang 虛空藏, Kokūzō
**Lineages:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), `lin:shingon`
**Historicity:** mythic

The bodhisattva 'womb of space', before whom bodhisattvas confess root downfalls (Ākāśagarbha-sūtra); his mantra-recitation (gumonji) was practiced by Kūkai.
