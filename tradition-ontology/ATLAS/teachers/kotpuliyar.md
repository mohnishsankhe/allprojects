# Kōṭpuli Nāyaṉār

**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Tirunāṭṭiyattāṉkuṭi
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. A Cōḻa general who stored paddy for the temple before going to war; returning to find that his kinsmen had eaten it in a famine, he put them to death; Śiva appeared and took them all to his world.
