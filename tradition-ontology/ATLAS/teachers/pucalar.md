# Pūcalā Nāyaṉār

`tch:pucalar` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Pūcalār
**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Tiruniṉṟavūr
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. A poor brahmin who built a temple in his mind stone by stone and fixed a day for its consecration; the Pallava king, who had fixed the same day for his great stone temple at Kāñcī, was told by Śiva in a dream that he would first attend Pūcalār's consecration, and went to see that temple.
