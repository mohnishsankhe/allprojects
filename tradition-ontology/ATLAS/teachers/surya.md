# Sūrya (as revealer of jyotiṣa)

`tch:surya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Vivasvant, Savitṛ, Bhāskara, Āditya
**Lineages:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)
**Historicity:** mythic
**Students:** [Maya (the asura)](maya-asura.md), [Varāhamihira](varahamihira.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Sūrya Siddhānta](../texts/surya-siddhanta.md) — attribution: traditional

The Sun as the source of astronomical knowledge: he taught the Sūrya Siddhānta to the asura Maya through a person born of his own portion (SS 1.4–9); he granted Varāhamihira a boon at Kāpitthaka (BJ 28.9); in horā he is the self (ātman) of the Kālapuruṣa (BJ 2.1) and the lord of planets to whom Parāśara bows (BPHS 1.6).
