Nāgar brahmin poet of Junāgaḍh (15th c.), the first great Gujarati poet: taunted by his sister-in-law, he fasted before a Śiva-liṅga in the forest until Śiva led him to see Kṛṣṇa's rāsa; thereafter he sang with low-caste devotees, and Kṛṣṇa rescued him in the Hāramālā, Māmeruṃ and Huṇḍī episodes. Author of 'Vaiṣṇava jana to'.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Śiva showed him the rāsa-līlā; Kṛṣṇa placed a garland on him before the king.
