# Vāseṭṭha

`tch:vasettha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md)
**Historicity:** historical

A young brahmin who, with Bhāradvāja, asked whether one is a brahmin by birth or by deeds (MN 98 / Snp 3.9) and questioned the Buddha on union with Brahmā (DN 13); in DN 27 a Vāseṭṭha is a novice.

_Notes: The Vāseṭṭhas of DN 13, DN 27 and MN 98 may not be the same person._
