# Gautama Akṣapāda

`tch:gautama-aksapada` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Akṣapāda, Gotama, Gautama
**Lineages:** [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: an ancient ṛṣi; Scholarly account: the Nyāya Sūtra is usually dated c. 2nd–3rd c. CE; nothing is known of its author(s); (confidence low)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Nyāya Sūtra](../texts/nyaya-sutra.md) — attribution: traditional

The sage to whom the Nyāya Sūtra is attributed, the founder of Nyāya. The commentators call him the best of sages who taught the śāstra for the peace of the world.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Called 'the best of sages' (Nyāyavārttika maṅgala) and 'the supremely compassionate sage' who composed the śāstra wishing to lift the world sunk in the mire of pain (Vācaspati, Tātparyaṭīkā on NS 1.1.1).

_Notes: Popular legends explain 'Akṣapāda' ('eyes in the feet'); not recorded here because the versions could not be checked._
