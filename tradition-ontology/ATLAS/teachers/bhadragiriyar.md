# Pattirakiriyār (Bhadragiri)

`tch:bhadragiriyar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Bhadragiri, Paththirakiriyar, Pattiragiri
**Lineages:** [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Places:** Ujjain, Tiruviṭaimarutūr — where he attained release (tradition)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Paṭṭiṉattār (Tiruveṇkāṭar)](pattinattar.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Pattirakiriyār: Meyññāṉap pulampal (lament for true knowledge)](../texts/bhadragiriyar-meynana-pulampal.md) — attribution: traditional

King of Ujjain turned renouncer and disciple of Paṭṭiṉattār; his 'Lament for true knowledge' asks, in 231 couplets, 'when will the day come' of renunciation, inner yoga and knowledge. The tradition identifies him with Bhartṛhari, king of Ujjain.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Seeing the stake burn when Paṭṭiṉattār was wrongly condemned, and learning the truth about his court, the king renounced and followed him; living on alms at Tiruviṭaimarutūr, he was chided by his guru for keeping a begging-bowl and a dog ('a householder!'), cast both away, and attained release before his guru.

_Notes: The identification with Bhartṛhari (tch:bhartrhari) is the tradition's; scholars do not accept it. Story recalled at low–moderate confidence._
