# Aḻukaṇṇi (Aḻukaṇic cittar)

`tch:azhukanni` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Aḻukaṇic cittar, Azhukanni
**Lineages:** [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: undetermined; Siddhar works are usually placed after c. 1400; (confidence low)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Aḻukaṇic cittar pāṭalkaḷ (the songs of Aḻukaṇṇi)](../texts/azhukanni-padalgal.md) — attribution: traditional

Siddhar poet of forty stanzas in veiled imagery of the root, the six supports and the ninety-six principles, addressed to 'my Kaṇṇammā'; he tells how the guru came, showed his own form and burnt the supports and letters.

_Notes: Nothing is recalled of his life. The name means 'weeping-eyed'; whether the poet is a man or a woman is not settled here._
