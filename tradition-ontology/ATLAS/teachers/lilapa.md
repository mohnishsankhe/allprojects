# Līlapa

**Alternate names:** Līlāpāda, li la pa (Tibetan)
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Historicity:** legendary

No. 2 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A king who would not give up his throne and pleasures; a guru taught him to meditate while seated on his lion throne, taking the jewel of his ring (or his royal ease) as the object, and he attained realization in the midst of royal life.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A king who would not give up his throne and pleasures; a guru taught him to meditate while seated on his lion throne, taking the jewel of his ring (or his royal ease) as the object, and he attained realization in the midst of royal life. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 2): In the hermitage of the four immeasurables the king of yogins is like a lion, his head adorned with a turquoise five-braided mane, bearing the yogin's insignia of the five families; as the lion's ten claws tear the flesh and bones of elephants, the yogin's ten perfections tear apart concepts, the adverse side; realizing thus, Līla was freed.
