# Janaka of Videha

`tch:janaka` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Janaka Vaideha
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md)
**Places:** Videha
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Yājñavalkya](yajnavalkya.md), [Aṣṭāvakra](astavakra.md)

King of Videha whose sacrifice is the setting of the debate at BAU 3; he reports six teachers' definitions of brahman (BAU 4.1) and is taught by Yājñavalkya on the self as light, dream, deep sleep and death (BAU 4.3-4); told 'you have reached fearlessness' (BAU 4.2.4).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** On hearing Aṣṭāvakra he exclaims that he is stainless, peaceful consciousness beyond nature, long deceived by delusion (Aṣṭāvakra Gītā 2.1); in the Yoga Vāsiṣṭha he awakens through the Siddha Gītā and rules as one liberated while living.

_Notes: 'Janaka' is a dynastic name; other units record other Janakas (e.g., tch:daivarati-janaka)._
