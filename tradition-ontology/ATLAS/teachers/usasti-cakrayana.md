# Uṣasti Cākrāyaṇa

`tch:usasti-cakrayana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Uṣasta Cākrāyaṇa (BAU 3.4)
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary

Asks Yājñavalkya to explain the brahman that is immediate and direct, the self within all (BAU 3.4, as Uṣasta); in ChU 1.10-11 he survives a famine among the Kurus by eating an elephant-keeper's leftover beans but refuses the leftover water, and then instructs priests on the deities of the chant.

_Notes: Identity of the BAU Uṣasta and the ChU Uṣasti is assumed from the shared patronymic (moderate)._
