# Sumatinātha

**Alternate names:** Sumati
**Lineages:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md), [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: the fifth of the twenty-four Jinas of this era (tradition's chronology of immense lifespans and intervals); (confidence low)
**Places:** Ayodhyā, Sammeda Śikhara
**Historicity:** legendary
**Students:** [Śambhunātha](sambhunatha.md)

The fifth Tīrthaṅkara of the present descending half-cycle (avasarpiṇī). Emblem (lāñchana): the curlew (krauñca) in Śvetāmbara lists; the ruddy goose (cakravāka) in Digambara lists. Parents: Megharatha (Megha) and Maṅgalā. Born at Ayodhyā; attained nirvāṇa at Sammeda Śikhara.

_Notes: Parents, birthplaces and emblems follow the common Śvetāmbara lists (e.g. Hemacandra); Digambara lists agree in most points and differ in some emblems and names._
