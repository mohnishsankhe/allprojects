# Śrī Siṃha

**Alternate names:** dpal gyi seng ge, Śrī Siṃha of China
**Lineages:** [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md), [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Mañjuśrīmitra](manjusrimitra.md)
**Students:** [Jñānasūtra](jnanasutra.md), [Vimalamitra](vimalamitra.md), [Padmasambhava (Guru Rinpoche)](padmasambhava.md), [Vairocana the translator (Pagor Vairocana)](vairocana-translator.md)
**Works:**

  - [Kunjed Gyalpo (chos thams cad rdzogs pa chen po byang chub kyi sems kun byed rgyal po, the All-Creating King)](../texts/kunjed-gyalpo.md) — attribution: accepted

Master of the instruction series who, by tradition, was born in China (or Khotan), studied with Mañjuśrīmitra, divided the instruction series into outer, inner, secret and innermost secret cycles, and taught Jñānasūtra, Vimalamitra, Padmasambhava and Vairocana; co-translator of the Kunjed Gyalpo according to its colophon.
