# Lalon Fakir (Lālan Sāṃi)

`tch:lalon-fakir` · `skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

**Alternate names:** Lālan Śāh, Lalon Sain, Lālan Fakir, Fakir Lalon Shah
**Lineages:** [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: his followers say he lived 116 years; birth variously 1772 or 1774; Scholarly account: died 17 October 1890 at Cheuṛiyā; birth unknown (c. 1770s by most reports); (confidence moderate)
**Places:** Cheuṛiyā (Kushtia), Nadia
**Historicity:** historical

  - [Lālan-gīti (the songs of Lalon Fakir)](../texts/lalon-giti.md) — attribution: traditional

The greatest Bāul master, of Cheuṛiyā near Kushtia: his songs of the unknown bird in the body's cage, the Man of the Heart, the mirror-city, the futility of caste and creed and the worship of the human are the classic Bāul teaching. His disciples ('Lalon's fakirs') keep his ākhṛā and festival.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Abandoned by his companions on a pilgrimage when he fell ill with smallpox, he was nursed by a Muslim household and met his guru Sirāj Sāṃi; his caste of birth (Hindu Kāyastha or Muslim) was lost and he himself refused to name it.

_Notes: Birth, origin (Hindu or Muslim) and the details of the smallpox story are disputed; flagged recent because his life extends to 1890._
