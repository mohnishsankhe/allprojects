# Kukkuripa

**Alternate names:** Kukurāja (?), ku ku ri pa (Tibetan)
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Students:** [Maṇibhadrā](manibhadra.md), [Marpa Chökyi Lodrö (Marpa the Translator)](marpa.md)

No. 34 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A brahmin yogin who, on pilgrimage, rescued a starving bitch and practised in a cave with her; invited to Indra's heaven he returned for the dog, who was revealed as a ḍākinī; associated with the Mahāmāyā tantra and poet of Caryāgīti songs; in Tibetan accounts one of Marpa's teachers.

Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 34): Worship of buddhas and the like brings no benefit; where there is desire and striving there is no buddha; the glory of experience is the true guru's blessing; the fortunate have it within themselves yet do not see it.
