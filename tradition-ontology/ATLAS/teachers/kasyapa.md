Kaśyapa Mārīca, seer of RV 1.99 and of the closing Soma hymns 9.113–114 (the prayer to be made immortal in the world of unfading light and bliss); one of the seven seers of RV 10.137; in the Atharvaveda 'self-born Kaśyapa' arises from Time (AVŚ 19.53.10).
