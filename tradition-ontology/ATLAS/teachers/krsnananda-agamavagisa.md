# Kṛṣṇānanda Āgamavāgīśa

`tch:krsnananda-agamavagisa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Kṛṣṇānanda Bhaṭṭācārya, Āgamavāgīśa
**Lineages:** [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md), [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md), [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)

**Places:** Navadvīpa (Nadia, Bengal)
**Historicity:** historical
**Works:** 
  - [Tantrasāra (Bṛhattantrasāra) of Kṛṣṇānanda Āgamavāgīśa](../texts/tantrasara-krsnananda.md) — attribution: accepted

Bengali tantric scholar of Navadvīpa, compiler of the Tantrasāra (Bṛhattantrasāra), the standard Bengali digest of tantric worship, which sets out the meditation and worship of Dakṣiṇakālī. Bengali tradition credits him with fixing the popular form of Dakṣiṇakālī and with introducing the worship of Kālī in clay images in the household.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Bengali legend: praying to know the form in which Kālī should be worshipped, he was told to adopt the first form he saw at dawn; he saw a dark village woman plastering a wall, her hair loose, who bit her tongue in embarrassment on seeing him — the pose of Dakṣiṇakālī.

_Notes: The legend is oral/popular; the Tantrasāra itself quotes the Kālī Tantra's older meditation verse (tea:tantrasara-krsnananda:2.syama-dhyana)._
