The seven 'wind-girdled' silent sages to whom the verses of the Keśin hymn (RV 10.136) are ascribed one each: Jūti, Vātajūti, Viprajūti, Vṛṣāṇaka, Karikrata, Etaśa and Ṛśyaśṛṅga.
