# Ēṉātinātar Nāyaṉār

`tch:enatinatar` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md)
**Places:** Eyiṉaṉūr
**Historicity:** legendary

One of the sixty-three Nāyaṉmārs. A master of swordsmanship whose defeated rival Atisūraṉ smeared sacred ash on his forehead before their duel; seeing the ash, Ēṉātinātar lowered his guard and let himself be killed rather than strike a wearer of Śiva's sign.
