# Bhusuku

`tch:bhusuku` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Bhusukupāda, b+hu su ku (Tibetan)
**Lineages:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary

No. 41 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A monk of Nālandā who did nothing but eat, sleep and stroll (bhu-su-ku); challenged to recite a text before the assembly he recited the Bodhicaryāvatāra and rose into the air; Abhayadatta identifies him with Śāntideva; also a poet of the Caryāgīti.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A monk of Nālandā who did nothing but eat, sleep and stroll (bhu-su-ku); challenged to recite a text before the assembly he recited the Bodhicaryāvatāra and rose into the air; Abhayadatta identifies him with Śāntideva; also a poet of the Caryāgīti. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 41): Until one realizes oneself there is great difference between tastes, and saṃsāra and the buddhas are very different; at the time of realization saṃsāra and nirvāṇa are great bliss, like a jewel blazing in the ocean.
