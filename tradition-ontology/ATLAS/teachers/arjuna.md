# Arjuna (Pārtha, Dhanaṃjaya)

**Dates:** Tradition's account: end of the Dvāpara yuga (the Bhārata war; Kali yuga traditionally from 3102 BCE)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Kṛṣṇa (Vāsudeva)](krsna.md), [Śiva](siva.md)

The third Pāṇḍava, the archer, whose refusal to fight his kinsmen occasions the Gītā; he declares himself Kṛṣṇa's disciple and takes refuge in him (2.7), receives the divine eye and the vision of the universal form (11.8ff), and after the war asks Kṛṣṇa to repeat the teaching (Anugītā 14.16).
