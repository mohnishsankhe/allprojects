**Alternate names:** Śrīraṅganāthamuni, Periya Mudali
**Lineages:** [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

First ācārya of the Śrīvaiṣṇava lineage (after Nammāḻvār), of Vīranārāyaṇapuram (Kāṭṭumannārkōyil). The tradition says he recovered and arranged the Nālāyira Divya Prabandham, set its hymns to music for temple recitation, and taught yoga; his works on logic and yoga are lost.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Hearing ten verses of the Tiruvāymoḻi ending 'these ten out of a thousand', he sought the rest at Kurukūr, recited Madhurakavi's Kaṇṇinuṇ Ciṟuttāmpu twelve thousand times in yogic absorption, and received from Nammāḻvār in vision the whole four thousand hymns and their meaning.
