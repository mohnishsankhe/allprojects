**Works:** 
  - [Kutampaic cittar pāṭalkaḷ (the songs of Kuṭampai)](../texts/kudambai-padalgal.md) — attribution: traditional

Siddhar named from the refrain of his thirty-two couplets, addressed to a girl wearing kuṭampai ear-ornaments; for the realized, he asks, what need of mantra, seals, yoga, rejuvenation or hymns?
