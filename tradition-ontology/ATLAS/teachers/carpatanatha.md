# Carpaṭanātha

`tch:carpatanatha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Carpaṭi, Carpaṭnāth, Carpaṭīnātha
**Lineages:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), `lin:mahasiddha`, `lin:rasa-sastra`
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Works:** 
  - [Nāth Siddhoṁ kī Bāniyāṁ](../texts/nath-siddhon-ki-baniyan.md) — attribution: traditional

A Nāth siddha (Carpaṭi in HYP 1.6) to whom Hindi sayings are ascribed (Nāth Siddhoṁ kī Bāniyāṁ); in Chamba tradition the guru of the founder-king of Chamba; also named among alchemical siddhas; one of the Marathi nine Nāths (Carpaṭnāth).
