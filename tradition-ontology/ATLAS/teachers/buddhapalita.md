# Buddhapālita

**Lineages:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 470–540 CE; (confidence moderate)
**Places:** Haṃsakrīḍa in South India (Tāranātha), Dantapurī (Tāranātha)
**Historicity:** historical
**Students:** [Kamalabuddhi](kamalabuddhi.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Buddhapālita-Mūlamadhyamakavṛtti](../texts/buddhapalita-vrtti.md) — attribution: accepted

Commentator on the Mūlamadhyamakakārikā whose method of drawing out the opponent's unwanted consequences was criticized by Bhāviveka and defended by Candrakīrti, and who is therefore counted as the first 'Prāsaṅgika'.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Tāranātha relates that he studied Nāgārjuna's works under Saṃgharakṣita, attained realization through the practice of Mañjuśrī, and taught at Dantapurī.
