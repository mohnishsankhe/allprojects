# Nandi (Nantīcar)

**Alternate names:** Nantīcar, Nantitēvar, Nandīśvara, Nandikeśvara, Tirunanti
**Lineages:** [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md), [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md), [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)

In the Tamil Siddha tradition Nandi (Nantīcar, Nantitēvar) is the first of the eighteen Siddhars and the channel through whom Śiva's knowledge — including medicine — reached Tirumūlar and the others (TM 67–68).
