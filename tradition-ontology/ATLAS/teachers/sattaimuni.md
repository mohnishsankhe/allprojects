# Caṭṭaimuṉi (Sattaimuni)

**Alternate names:** Caṭṭaimuṉi
**Lineages:** [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: undetermined; Siddhar works are usually placed after c. 1400; (confidence low)
**Places:** Srirangam — samādhi in the popular list
**Historicity:** legendary
**Students:** [Pāmbāṭṭi (Pāmpāṭṭic cittar)](pambatti.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Caṭṭaimuṉi ñāṉam (the wisdom of Sattaimuni)](../texts/sattaimuni-nanam.md) — attribution: traditional

Siddhar whose name is taken from the cloak (caṭṭai) he wore; his Ñāṉam contrasts outward worship with the Siddhars' worship of the goddess on the 'Meru' of triangles and with vāci yoga. Some accounts say he wrote openly what other Siddhars veiled.

_Notes: Origins and the 'open writing' story recalled at low confidence. Counted in the popular list of the eighteen Siddhars (see cpt:eighteen-siddhars); the samādhi place given follows that list and is recalled at low confidence._
