# Tukārām

`tch:tukaram` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Tukobā, Tukā, Tukārām Mahārāj
**Lineages:** [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: 1608–1650 (Śaka 1530–1571); some accounts give 1598 for his birth; Scholarly account: first half of 17th c.; (confidence moderate)
**Places:** Dehu, Bhāmnāth hill, Lohagāv, Paṇḍharpūr
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Bābājī Caitanya](babaji-caitanya.md)
**Students:** [Bahiṇābāī](bahinabai.md), [Rāmeśvar Bhaṭṭ](ramesvar-bhatt.md), [Santājī Jagnāḍe](santaji-jagnade.md), [Niḷobā](niloba.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Tukārām Gāthā](../texts/tukaram-gatha.md) — attribution: accepted

Kuṇbī grocer of Dehu (1608–1650 by the usual tradition) who, ruined by famine and bereavement, turned wholly to Viṭṭhala; initiated in a dream by Bābājī Caitanya with the mantra 'Rāma Kṛṣṇa Hari'. His abhaṅgas — thrown into the Indrāyaṇī at the order of the brahmin Rāmeśvar Bhaṭṭ and restored after thirteen days — became the Tukārām Gāthā. He is 'the pinnacle' of the Vārkarī temple; tradition says he departed bodily for Vaikuṇṭha (Tukārām Bīj).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Initiated in a dream; Viṭṭhala restored his drowned abhaṅgas; at the end he ascended to Vaikuṇṭha in his body.
