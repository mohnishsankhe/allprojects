# Avalokiteśvara

`tch:avalokitesvara` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Guanshiyin / Guanyin 觀世音, Guanzizai 觀自在, Kannon / Kanzeon, Chenrezig (spyan ras gzigs), Lokeśvara, Padmapāṇi
**Lineages:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md), `lin:tiantai`, [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
**Historicity:** mythic

The bodhisattva who, coursing in the deep perfection of wisdom, sees the five aggregates as empty and speaks the teaching to Śāriputra (Heart Sūtra 2.2-2.8).
**Realization — the tradition's account (as a figure of the sūtras):** A great bodhisattva of the tenth ground, or a buddha of the past (Zhengfa Ming) appearing as a bodhisattva; his realization is the penetration of hearing into the self-nature (Śūraṅgama juan 6)

_Notes: Gender: male in Indian and Tibetan iconography; female forms (Guanyin) prevalent in East Asia from c. 10th c._
