Author of the Tattvārthasūtra, the first Sanskrit summa of Jain doctrine, accepted by both sects. The Śvetāmbaras know him as Umāsvāti, son of Svāti and Umā, of the Uccairnāgara branch, who also wrote the bhāṣya and the Praśamarati; the Digambaras as Umāsvāmin (also called Gṛdhrapiccha), in Kundakunda's line.
