# Hita Harivaṃśa

Founder of the Rādhāvallabha sampradāya (tradition 1502–1552), born at Bād near Mathurā, who received his mantra from Rādhā herself, settled in Vṛndāvana and installed Rādhāvallabha (1535). Author of the Hita Caurāsī and, by his tradition's account, the Rādhā-sudhānidhi; the tradition regards him as the flute of Kṛṣṇa incarnate.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Rādhā gave him the mantra in a dream (or vision); he is Kṛṣṇa's flute (vaṃśī) incarnate.
