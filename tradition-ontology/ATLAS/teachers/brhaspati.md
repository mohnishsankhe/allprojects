U33 contribution: the Lokāyata is 'Bṛhaspati's doctrine'. The tradition's account makes him the preceptor of the gods who composed a deluding doctrine for the asuras (Maitrī Up. 7.9) and gave it to his disciple Cārvāka (Prabodhacandrodaya 2); the Bṛhaspati Sūtra, quoted as 'the venerable Bṛhaspati' by Jayarāśi, is ascribed to him, as are the anti-Vedic verses at the end of the Sarvadarśanasaṅgraha's first chapter. The Arthaśāstra's 'Bārhaspatyas' (1.2.4) hold that only economics and government are sciences.
