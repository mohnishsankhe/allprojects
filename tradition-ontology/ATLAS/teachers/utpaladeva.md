Son of Udayākara (ĪPK 4.18) and disciple of Somānanda; systematized the Pratyabhijñā in the Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā with a short and a long commentary, wrote the Siddhitrayī and the devotional hymns of the Śivastotrāvalī.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** His hymns present devotion as the relish of an identity with Śiva already recognized.
