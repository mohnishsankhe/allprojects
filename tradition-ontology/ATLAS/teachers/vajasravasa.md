# Vājaśravasa (father of Naciketas)

`tch:vajasravasa` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Auddālaki Āruṇi (KU 1.1.11), Gautama (KU 1.1.10), Uśan (some read uśan in KU 1.1.1 as a name)
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
**Historicity:** legendary

Naciketas's father, who in the Viśvajit sacrifice gives away all his possessions, offering worn-out cows; in anger he gives his son to Death (KU 1.1.1-4). Yama calls him Auddālaki Āruṇi (KU 1.1.11) and Naciketas calls him Gautama (KU 1.1.10).

_Notes: Whether he is the Uddālaka Āruṇi of ChU/BAU is not settled; KU 1.1.11 calls him Auddālaki ('son of Uddālaka')._
