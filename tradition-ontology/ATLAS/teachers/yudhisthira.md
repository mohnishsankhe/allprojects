# Yudhiṣṭhira (Dharmarāja)

`tch:yudhisthira` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Dharmarāja, Ajātaśatru, Kaunteya, son of Kuntī (kuntīputra)
**Lineages:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: end of the Dvāpara yuga (the Bhārata war; Kali yuga traditionally from 3102 BCE)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Teachers:** [Bhīṣma (Devavrata, Gāṅgeya)](bhisma.md), [Kṛṣṇa (Vāsudeva)](krsna.md), [Vyāsa (Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana)](vyasa.md), [Mārkaṇḍeya](markandeya.md)

Eldest Pāṇḍava, son of Dharma; answers the Yakṣa (3.297) and Nahuṣa (3.177), defends acting righteously without seeking its fruit against Draupadī (3.32.4), wishes to renounce after the war and is persuaded to rule (12.7–36), receives Bhīṣma's instruction, and at the end refuses to enter heaven without the dog who was devoted to him (17.3).

_Notes: Linked in BhG ch. 1–3 at 1.16._
