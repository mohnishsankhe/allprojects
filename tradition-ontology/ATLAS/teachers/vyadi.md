**Works:**

U31 contribution: the ancient grammarian credited with the lost Saṃgraha (said to have had a hundred thousand verses), which Bhartṛhari says declined before Patañjali; in the Mahābhāṣya he holds that a word denotes the individual substance (dravya), against Vājapyāyana's universal.
