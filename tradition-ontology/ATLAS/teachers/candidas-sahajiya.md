The Bengali poet whom the Sahajiyās claim as their master: lover of the washerwoman Rāmī, singer of the love of man above all; one of several poets called Caṇḍīdās.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A priest of Bāśulī, he loved the washerwoman Rāmī; Sahajiyā tradition makes their love the exemplary practice of prema.
