In U06's texts: the sage whose request for Rāma's help occasions the Yoga Vāsiṣṭha and who is present throughout Vasiṣṭha's teaching.
**Realization — the tradition's account (epic):** Born a kṣatriya king (son of Gādhi), he attained the status of brahmarṣi by long tapas and was at last acknowledged by Vasiṣṭha (Rāmāyaṇa, Bāla-kāṇḍa).
