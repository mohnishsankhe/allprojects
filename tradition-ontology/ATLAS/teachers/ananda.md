# Ānanda

`tch:ananda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Ānanda (Anan 阿難)
**Lineages:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md), [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Gotama Buddha](gotama-buddha.md), [Puṇṇa Mantāṇiputta](punna-mantaniputta.md)

The Buddha's cousin and personal attendant for twenty-five years, foremost in learning, memory, attentiveness and service; interceded for women's going forth (Cv 10.1); recited the Dhamma at the First Council; many suttas are his teaching (MN 52, MN 108, SN 22.83, AN 4.170).
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Attained arahantship during the night before the First Council, as he was lying down, before his head reached the pillow (Cullavagga 11).

_Notes: AN 1 Etadaggavagga (the 'foremost' lists)_
