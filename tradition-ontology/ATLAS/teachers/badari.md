# Bādari

`tch:badari` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md), [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md)
**Historicity:** unknown

Early Mīmāṃsā teacher cited in the Mīmāṃsā Sūtra: holds that only substances, qualities and purificatory acts are subsidiaries (MS 3.1.3) and that the injunctions are for all four varṇas (MS 6.1.27, a view rejected); also cited at MS 8.3.6 and in the Brahma Sūtra.
