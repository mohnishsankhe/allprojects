# Acyutaprekṣa

`tch:acyutapreksa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Acyutaprekṣa Tīrtha, Acyutaprajña, Acyutapramati (Sumadhvavijaya)
**Lineages:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
**Places:** Uḍupi
**Historicity:** historical
**Students:** [Madhva](madhva.md)

The ascetic at Uḍupi from whom Madhva took renunciation; the Sumadhvavijaya calls him Acyutapramati. In the tradition's account his ascetic line had come under the teaching of māyā, and he later accepted his disciple's doctrine.

_Notes: The earlier names of his line (the 'Haṃsa paramparā' back to Nārāyaṇa as Haṃsa and Brahmā) are not listed; see lin:dvaita notes._
