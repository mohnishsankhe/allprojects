Pre-Śaṅkara Vedāntin and commentator on the Bṛhadāraṇyaka (and reportedly the Kaṭha) Upaniṣad; taught that Brahman is both one and many like the ocean and its waves, that the world is its real modification, and that liberation needs knowledge combined with works. Known only from his critics Śaṅkara, Sureśvara and Ānandagiri.

_Notes: The list of eight 'states' (avasthā) of Brahman ascribed to him by Sureśvara is recalled with low confidence and not recorded as a teaching._
