The great commentator of Valabhī whose Sanskrit commentaries on the Thirty Verses and the Madhyāntavibhāga are the classic Indian explanations of Yogācāra; in East Asian doxography the representative of the view that the seen and seeing parts of cognition are imagined, only the self-witnessing part real.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Tāranātha tells that in a former life he was a pigeon who heard Vasubandhu recite the abhidharma daily, and was reborn human as a result.
