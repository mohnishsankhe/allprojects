Founder of the Pratyabhijñā philosophy with the Śivadṛṣṭi; he traced his descent from the mind-born siddhas of Durvāsas' Tryambaka line (Śivadṛṣṭi 7.107-122). Teacher of Utpaladeva; also wrote a (lost) commentary on the Parātrīśikā cited by Abhinavagupta.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** Heir of a line of siddhas born of the mind of Durvāsas, descended through the fifteenth who married, whose son came to Kashmir.
