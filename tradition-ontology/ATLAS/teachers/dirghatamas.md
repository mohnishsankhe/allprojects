Dīrghatamas Aucathya (Māmateya), seer of RV 1.140–164, including the riddle hymn 1.164 (the two birds, the four quarters of speech, 'the One Being the inspired call by many names'), the Viṣṇu hymn 1.154 and the horse-sacrifice hymns 1.162–163. The tradition remembers him as born blind.
