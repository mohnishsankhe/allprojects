# Vāgbhaṭa

**Lineages:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md), [The Aṣṭavaidya physician families of Kerala](../lineages/kerala-astavaidya.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 600 CE, Sindh; (confidence moderate)
**Historicity:** historical
**Works:**

  - [Aṣṭāṅga Saṅgraha](../texts/astanga-sangraha.md) — attribution: disputed
  - [Rasaratnasamuccaya](../texts/rasaratnasamuccaya.md) — attribution: doubtful

Author of the Aṣṭāṅgahṛdaya, 'son of Siṃhagupta, lord of physicians' (colophons), who made a verse 'heart' of the eight-limbed medicine from the scattered tantras of Agniveśa and the others (AHS Sū 1.3-5). He opens with homage to the unprecedented physician who destroyed the diseases of passion, and ends by asking readers to take what is well said without partisanship.

_Notes: Buddhist affiliation is a scholarly inference from AHS Sū 1.1 and 2.21-22. Some later authors distinguish an elder Vāgbhaṭa (Aṣṭāṅgasaṅgraha). The Rasaratnasamuccaya's author also calls himself son of Siṃhagupta._
