# Gārgya Bālāki

`tch:gargya-balaki` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Alternate names:** Dṛpta-Bālāki Gārgya, Bālāki
**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Ajātaśatru of Kāśī](ajatasatru-kasi.md)

A proud (dṛpta) learned brahmin who offers to explain brahman to Ajātaśatru, proposes a series of persons in the sun, moon and other phenomena, is refuted and becomes his pupil (BAU 2.1; KauU 4).
