Wife of Agastya and seer of RV 1.179.1–2: after many autumns of toil she laments that age wears away the beauty of bodies and asks that husbands come to their wives; the ancient seers too, who spoke truths with the gods, did not reach the end (of tapas).

_Notes: Named among the brahmavādinīs of the Bṛhaddevatā. The Śākta (Śrīvidyā) tradition names a Lopāmudrā as seer of one form of the Śrīvidyā mantra (reference only, low confidence)._
