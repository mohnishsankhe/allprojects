# Puṣpadanta (ācārya)

`tch:puspadanta-acarya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Historicity:** semi-legendary
**Teachers:** [Dharasena](dharasena.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama](../texts/satkhandagama.md) — attribution: traditional

Pupil of Dharasena and co-author of the Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama, of which he wrote the opening (Satprarūpaṇā); the gods are said to have straightened his uneven teeth, hence the name.
