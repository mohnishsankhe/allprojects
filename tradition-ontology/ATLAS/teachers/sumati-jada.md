# Sumati ('Jaḍa')

`tch:sumati-jada` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Historicity:** legendary

Son of a Bhārgava brahmin who behaved like a dullard (jaḍa) because he remembered past births; he told his father how beings are born, die, travel to Yama and suffer in hell (MkP 10-15), the account the wise birds relate to Jaimini.
