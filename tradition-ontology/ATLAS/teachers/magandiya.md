# Māgaṇḍiya (MN 75)

`tch:magandiya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Lineages:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md)
**Historicity:** historical

A wanderer of the Bhāradvāja clan who held the Buddha a 'destroyer of growth' and was taught that health is the highest gain and nibbāna the highest bliss (MN 75).

_Notes: Distinct from the brahmin Māgaṇḍiya of Snp 4.9._
