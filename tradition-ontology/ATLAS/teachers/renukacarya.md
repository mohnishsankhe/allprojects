# Reṇukācārya (Revaṇasiddha)

`tch:renukacarya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Alternate names:** Reṇuka, Revaṇa, Revaṇasiddha, Revaṇārādhya, Reṇukasiddha
**Lineages:** [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md), [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: appears in every yuga; in the Kali age as Revaṇa/Reṇuka
**Places:** Kollipāki (Kolanupāka, Telangana), Malaya mountain, Laṅkā, Rambhāpurī (Bāḷehonnūr)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Students:** [Agastya](agastya.md)
**Works:** 
  - [Siddhāntaśikhāmaṇi](../texts/siddhantasikhamani.md) — attribution: traditional

The first of the five ācāryas: the gaṇa Reṇuka who, cursed for leaping over his fellow gaṇa Dāruka in Śiva's assembly, emerged from the Someśvara liṅga at Kollipāki, taught Agastya the Siddhāntaśikhāmaṇi, and in the tradition founded the Rambhāpurī pīṭha at Bāḷehonnūr.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A pramatha created by Śiva (SSM 2.25–27), sent to earth for overstepping Dāruka (3.66–77), he appeared from the Someśvara liṅga at Kollipāki without human birth (3.83–84; 4.1), taught Agastya, visited Vibhīṣaṇa in Laṅkā, and returned into the liṅga (ch. 21).

_Notes: Seat: Vīra-siṃhāsana at Rambhāpurī (low confidence on the siṃhāsana name)._
