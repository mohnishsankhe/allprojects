No. 81 of the eighty-four siddhas (Tōh 2292 order). A handsome man vain of his looks, taught to see the body as illusory.
**Realization — the tradition's account:** A handsome man vain of his looks, taught to see the body as illusory. Realization song in Tōh 2292 (no. 81): Saṃsāra has no essence; in this rainbow-like body, by ignorance, clinging and pride, one holds poison as bliss; when the poison-disease of clinging is cleansed, saṃsāra itself is the dharmakāya.
