# Pippalāda

**Lineages:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Paippalāda śākhā (Atharvaveda)](../lineages/sakha-paippalada.md)
**Historicity:** legendary
**Students:** [Sukeśā Bhāradvāja](sukesa-bharadvaja.md), [Śaibya Satyakāma](saibya-satyakama.md), [Sauryāyaṇī Gārgya](sauryayani-gargya.md), [Kausalya Āśvalāyana](kausalya-asvalayana.md), [Bhārgava Vaidarbhi](bhargava-vaidarbhi.md), [Kabandhī Kātyāyana](kabandhi-katyayana.md)
**Works:**

Sage of the Praśna Upaniṣad who answers the six questions of six seekers after making them live a year with austerity, celibacy and faith (PrU 1.2).

_Notes: Tradition connects him with the Paippalāda śākhā of the Atharvaveda (moderate)._
