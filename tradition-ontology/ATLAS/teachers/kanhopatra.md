# Kānhopātrā

`tch:kanhopatra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)
**Places:** Maṅgaḷveḍhe, Paṇḍharpūr
**Historicity:** semi-legendary

Daughter of a courtesan of Maṅgaḷveḍhe (15th c.) who refused a king's summons and died at the Paṇḍharpūr temple, where she is the only saint with a memorial inside the precinct; her abhaṅgas plead for Viṭṭhala's protection.
