# Mahinda

`tch:mahinda` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Lineages:** [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: arrived in Laṅkā 236 years after the parinibbāna; Scholarly account: mid-3rd c. BCE; (confidence moderate)
**Historicity:** historical
**Teachers:** [Moggaliputta Tissa](moggaliputta-tissa.md)

Son of Asoka and pupil of Moggaliputta Tissa who, by the chronicles, brought the Dhamma to Laṅkā, converting King Devānampiya Tissa at Mihintale, and founded the Mahāvihāra tradition; the commentaries were held to have been brought by him.
