# naiṣkarmya

`trm:naiskarmya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नैष्कर्म्य
**Literal:** freedom from action, actionlessness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: A person does not attain naiṣkarmya by not undertaking actions (3.4).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Freedom from the binding power of action, not attained by abstaining from action (BhG 3.4) but, as supreme perfection (naiṣkarmya-siddhi), by an understanding unattached everywhere through renunciation (18.49).
