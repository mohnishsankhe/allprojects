# parārthānumāna

`trm:pararthanumana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** परार्थानुमान
**Literal:** inference for others

## Definitions by tradition
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Statement of the triple-marked reason so as to make another understand what one has understood; figuratively called inference because it is its cause.
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): The five-membered statement by which one who has inferred makes another infer.
