# amata

`trm:amata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** the deathless

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): An epithet of nibbāna: the doors to the deathless are open (MN 26); heedfulness is the path to the deathless (Dhp 21); mindfulness of death merges in the deathless (AN 6.19).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Epithet of nibbāna: where there is no death.
