# avaivartika / avinivartanīya

`trm:avaivartika` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** irreversible, non-retrogressing

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The bodhisattva who can no longer turn back from full awakening, known by signs (Aṣṭa ch. 17); in the Pure Land sūtras those born in Sukhāvatī attain it.
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Non-retrogression: reached quickly by the easy path of faith (Nāgārjuna); assured to all born in Sukhāvatī for five reasons (Jingtu shiyi lun); Tanluan equates it with the rightly established group.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): Attained in this life at the moment of shinjin ('immediately attain birth and dwell in non-retrogression').
