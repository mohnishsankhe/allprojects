# vāc

`trm:vac` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वाच्
**Literal:** speech; voice

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Speech as goddess and power: she proclaims herself queen and self of all (RV 10.125); four quarters of her, three hidden (1.164.45); she revealed herself to some like a bride (10.71.4); the gods begot her and all animals speak her (8.100.11).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Speech as Prajāpati's own and second, sent forth to pervade all (PB 20.14.2); yoked with mind to carry the sacrifice (ŚB 1.4.4.7); speech as consecration (KB 7.1).
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): The object of grammar and phonetics; in the Mahābhāṣya the 'great god' who enters mortals, identity with whom is the aim of grammar.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Speech, one of the seven feminine excellences that are the Lord (10.34).
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): Speech as the consciousness of beings, within and without, moving them to all activity; when it departs a being is like a log (VP 1, from memory).

## Forms in other languages
- Pali: vācā  — partial

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [śabdabrahman](sabda-brahman.md) — Bhartṛhari's 'Brahman as word' is a later philosophy of sound that draws on the Vedic exaltation of Vāc; the Saṃhitās do not use the term.
