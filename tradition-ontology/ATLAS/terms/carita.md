# carita (cariyā)

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** temperament, conduct

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The six temperaments (greedy, hating, deluded, faithful, intelligent, speculative); 'cariyā', 'pakati' and 'ussannatā' are one in meaning; discerned by posture, work, eating, seeing and the arising of states, with the text's caution that this is by the teachers' opinion (Vism III p. 101-107).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The six temperaments — greedy, hating, deluded, faithful, intelligent, speculative — determining suitable meditation subjects.
