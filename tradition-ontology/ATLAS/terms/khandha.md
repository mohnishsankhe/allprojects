# khandha

`trm:khandha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** aggregate, heap, mass

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The five aggregates — form, feeling, perception, formations, consciousness — whatever of each is past, future or present, internal or external, gross or subtle (SN 22.48).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The five aggregates: matter, feeling, perception, formations, consciousness.
