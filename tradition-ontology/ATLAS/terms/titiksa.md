# titikṣā

`trm:titiksa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तितिक्षा
**Literal:** endurance, forbearance

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Enduring the transient contacts that give pleasure and pain (BhG 2.14).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Forbearance: enduring heat and cold, pleasure and pain and all afflictions without retaliation or lament.
