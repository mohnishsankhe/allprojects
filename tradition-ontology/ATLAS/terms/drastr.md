# draṣṭṛ

`trm:drastr` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** द्रष्टृ
**Literal:** seer

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The seer, the puruṣa: seeing alone, pure, seeing by conforming to cognitions (2.20); the witness of the buddhi (YBh 2.17); its conjunction with the seen is the cause of suffering (2.17).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Puruṣa as seer (SK 19), who sees prakṛti like a spectator once knowledge arises (SK 65).
