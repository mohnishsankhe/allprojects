## Definitions by tradition
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): Congregational singing of abhaṅgas and the name with cymbals and drum, the daily and nightly practice of Vārkarī groups.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): Devotional song to Rāma sung in company.
- [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md): Worship in general; also the second, inner instruction given after śaraṇ (usage to verify).
- [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md): Devotional singing, typically congregational, of songs and names of the deity, from 'bhaj' (to share in, worship).
