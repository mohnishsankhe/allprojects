# xin (mind)

`trm:xin` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 心
**Literal:** heart-mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): In Chan the word for the ultimate itself — 'this very mind is buddha' — as well as for deluded mind; which is meant depends on context.
- [Linji house](../lineages/linji.md): The formless mind-dharma pervading the ten directions, functioning as seeing, hearing and acting (Linji).

## Forms in other languages

- Korean: sim  — exact
