# adṛṣṭa

`trm:adrsta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अदृष्ट
**Literal:** the unseen (merit and demerit)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): The unseen cause of motions without visible cause and of the mind's passage at death and birth; its absence is liberation (VS 5.1.15, 5.2.14, 5.2.19–20 C).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The residue of past action that brings about the body (NS 3.2.60); set in motion by the Lord (NBh 4.1.21).
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Unseen force; used by Śāstradīpikā for what awakens impressions in dreams, and generally for the unseen results of acts, of which apūrva is the Mīmāṃsā form.
- [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md): (As reported) Denied: there is no unseen cause; the variety of things is from own-nature.
