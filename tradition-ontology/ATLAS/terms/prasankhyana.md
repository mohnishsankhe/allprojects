# prasaṃkhyāna

`trm:prasankhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रसंख्यान
**Literal:** highest reflection, meditative discernment

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The meditation called the cloud of dharma (YBh 1.2); its fire burns the seeds of affliction (YBh 2.2); one who takes no interest even in it attains the cloud of dharma (4.29).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Repeated contemplation of objects (and then of the body) that removes false construction and the I-notion (NBh 4.2.2, 4.2.46).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Repeated meditative contemplation of the Upaniṣadic knowledge to make it direct and to destroy the impressions of difference — required by Maṇḍana, rejected as necessary by Śaṅkara (US 18) and Sureśvara.
