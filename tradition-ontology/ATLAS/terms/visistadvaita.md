# viśiṣṭādvaita

`trm:visistadvaita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विशिष्टाद्वैत
**Literal:** non-duality of the qualified

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The doctrine that Brahman, qualified by souls and matter as its inseparable modes, is one without a second; also read as the non-duality of Brahman in its causal (subtle) and effected (gross) states.
