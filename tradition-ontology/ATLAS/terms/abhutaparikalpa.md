# abhūta-parikalpa

`trm:abhutaparikalpa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अभूतपरिकल्प
**Literal:** unreal imagination

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The dependent nature as the mind and mental factors of the three realms: it exists, while the duality it projects does not; it is 'unreal' because its objects are unreal, but it is not wholly non-existent, and its extinction is liberation.
