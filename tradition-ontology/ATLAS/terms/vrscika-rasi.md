## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The eighth sign (Scorpio), 30° of the zodiac made of nine asterism-quarters; the genitals of Kālapuruṣa (BJ 1.4); ruled by Mars; fixed; of the water triplicity (later texts); even, female and gentle (BJ 1.11); Varāhamihira's alternative (Greek-derived) name: kaurpya (BJ 1.8); dignities: Moon in fall (BJ 1.13).
