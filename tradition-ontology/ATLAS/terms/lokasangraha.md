# lokasaṃgraha

`trm:lokasangraha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** लोकसंग्रह
**Literal:** holding together (maintaining) of the world

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The holding together of the world, with a view to which one should act (3.20) and the wise act unattached (3.25), since people follow the standard the best person sets (3.21).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The welfare and cohesion of the world, for whose sake the wise act unattached, as Janaka did (BhG 3.20, 3.25).
