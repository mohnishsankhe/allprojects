# satipaṭṭhāna

`trm:satipatthana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** establishment of mindfulness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Four contemplations, of body, feelings, mind and dhammas, each practised ardent, clearly comprehending and mindful, having put away covetousness and displeasure for the world; the path of purification and realisation of nibbāna (MN 10).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The four establishments of mindfulness — contemplating body, feelings, mind and dhammas, ardent, clearly comprehending, mindful, having removed covetousness and grief (MN 10); the direct path to purification and nibbāna; one's own pasture (SN 47.6).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The four foundations: body, feeling, mind, dhammas; matched by the commentary to four kinds of practitioner.
