# sallekhanā

`trm:sallekhana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सल्लेखना
**Literal:** thinning out

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The voluntary, gradual giving up of food and passions when death is inevitable, undertaken without passion, with the teacher's permission (TS 7.22; Ratnakaraṇḍa 122); held by the tradition not to be self-killing.
