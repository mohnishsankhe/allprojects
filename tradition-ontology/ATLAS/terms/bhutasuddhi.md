# bhūtaśuddhi

`trm:bhutasuddhi` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भूतशुद्धि
**Literal:** purification of the elements

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The ritual-meditative dissolution of the body's elements into their sources and the re-creation of a pure, divine body before worship (the Kāmika's 'self-purification' corresponds in function).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Purification of the elements performed before worship of Bhagavān.
- [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md): Performed by the tantri in the preliminary sequence of Kerala worship.
