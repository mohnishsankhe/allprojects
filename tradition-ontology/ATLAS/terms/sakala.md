# sakala

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सकल
**Literal:** with parts

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Śiva as having form, worshipped in the image.
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): A soul bound by all three bonds — mala, karma and māyā — living in the worlds of māyā.
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The knower with all three impurities, identifying with body and objects (waking).
