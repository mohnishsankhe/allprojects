# nigraha

`trm:nigraha` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निग्रह
**Literal:** restraint, suppression

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): In the Kārikā ch.3: restraint of the mind, on which fearlessness, the ending of sorrow, awakening and imperishable peace depend (3.40); to be achieved without weariness (3.41) and by a means (3.42).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Arjuna thinks restraining (nigraha) the restless mind as very hard as restraining the wind (6.34); Kṛṣṇa grants that the mind is hard to restrain (durnigraha) but says it is held by practice and dispassion (6.35).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Beings follow their own nature; what will suppression accomplish? (3.33).
