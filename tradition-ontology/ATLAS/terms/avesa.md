# āveśa

`trm:avesa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आवेश
**Literal:** possession, entering

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): A rival account of liberation rejected by Rāmakaṇṭha: possession [of the soul by the Lord's qualities].
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Among the siddha's powers: he 'enters all' and none can enter him (sarvāṃś cāviśati ... anāveśyaḥ).
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Viṣṇu's special presence in a soul (as distinct from an avatāra); one must know which beings are his avatāras and which are only his āveśas.
