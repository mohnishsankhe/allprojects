# srotas

`trm:srotas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्रोतस्
**Literal:** stream, channel

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): the channels that carry the tissues undergoing transformation; there are as many as there are embodied kinds of things in the person; thirteen are named (of prāṇa, water, food, rasa, blood, flesh, fat, bone, marrow, semen, urine, faeces, sweat), and for the mind the whole conscious body is its channel (Ca Vi 5.3-7); vitiated by excess flow, obstruction, knotting or wrong course (Vi 5.22).
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): A stream of Śaiva revelation issuing from one of Sadāśiva's five faces: Siddhānta (upper), Gāruḍa (east), Vāma (north), Bhūta (west) and Bhairava (south); the Kāmika also counts twenty-five by the five faces each being fivefold.
