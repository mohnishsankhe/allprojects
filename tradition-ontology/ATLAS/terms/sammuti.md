# sammuti

`trm:sammuti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** convention

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Conventional designation: 'as the word chariot is used when parts are assembled, so the convention being when aggregates exist' (SN 5.10); the world's usages the Tathāgata uses without grasping (DN 9); later the conventional truth (sammuti-sacca) of the commentaries.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Conventional truth: persons, beings, chariots and the like, designated on the basis of ultimate dhammas; true by common usage but not found on analysis.
- [Thai Forest tradition (Kammaṭṭhāna forest tradition)](../lineages/thai-forest.md): In the forest teachers' usage, the conventions of the world (names, selves, even the Dhamma's words) which the released heart still uses but is no longer bound by; contrasted with vimutti.
