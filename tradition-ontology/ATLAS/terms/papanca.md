# papañca

`trm:papanca` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** proliferation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The proliferation of concepts that follows feeling, perception and thought, by which 'perceptions and notions tinged with proliferation' beset a person (MN 18); rooted in the thought 'I am' (Snp 4.14); the Dhamma is for one who delights in non-proliferation (AN 8.30).
