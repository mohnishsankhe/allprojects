# rudrākṣa

`trm:rudraksa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** रुद्राक्ष
**Literal:** Rudra's eye

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The seed-beads born from Śiva's tears at the burning of the Triple City (SSM 7.47–48), one of the eight shields; wearing them frees brāhmaṇa and outcaste alike (7.62).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Bead from Kālāgnirudra's tears, worn with mantras.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The seed worn and used for japa by Śaivas, glorified in the Śiva Purāṇa (Vidyeśvara 25) and the Devī Bhāgavata.
