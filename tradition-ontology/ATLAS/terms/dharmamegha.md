# dharma-megha

`trm:dharmamegha` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** धर्ममेघ
**Literal:** cloud of dharma

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The samādhi that arises for one without desire even in prasaṃkhyāna, from discrimination-vision in every way (4.29); from it the cessation of afflictions and karma (4.30).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The samādhi of one who, detached even from the highest reflection, has complete discriminative discernment and no other cognitions (4.29, YBh 4.29); from it afflictions and karma cease (4.30).
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The tenth bodhisattva ground, at which the bodhisattva is consecrated by the buddhas and rains the dharma like a cloud.
