# śukra (bhṛgu, sita)

`trm:sukra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शुक्र
**Literal:** semen; the reproductive tissue

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Venus: desire (madana) of Kālapuruṣa, a minister honoured by the dānavas (BJ 2.1); female, brahmin, rajas, lord of water (BJ 2.6–7); rules Taurus and Libra, exalted in Pisces 27°; period 20 years; Paraśurāma is born from him (BPHS 2.5); Greek-derived name Āsphujit (BJ 2.3).
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The seventh tissue, whose supreme essence is ojas (Su Sū 15.19; AHS Sū 11.37); from it the embryo arises as its essence (Ca Ci 15.16).
