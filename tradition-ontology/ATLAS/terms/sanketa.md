**Language:** Sanskrit

**Literal:** agreed sign, tryst

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): A 'code' or 'tryst': the threefold saṅketa of cakra, mantra and worship (YH 1.6), explained as the meeting-place of Śiva and Śakti (Dīpikā).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): In the Dattātreyayogaśāstra, the 'indications' — points of fixed attention — by which the mind dissolves in layayoga; Ādinātha taught eight crore (DYŚ 14–24).
