**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** यति
**Literal:** striver, ascetic

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 8.11: ascetics free from passion (vīta-rāga) who enter the imperishable.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Ascetics of strict vows sacrifice by recitation and knowledge (4.28); for self-knowing ascetics free of desire and anger brahma-nirvāṇa is near (5.26). Contrast 'ayati', one who does not strive (6.37).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Ascetic, striver: the yatis who have ascertained the meaning of Vedānta (MuU 3.2.6).
- [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md): The renouncer-ascetic; the texts address him as yati.
