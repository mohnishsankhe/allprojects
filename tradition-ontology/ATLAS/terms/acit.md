# acit

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the insentient

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Insentient reality in three kinds: pure sattva, mixed sattva (prakṛti) and that devoid of sattva (time); the body of the Lord together with the souls.
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): The non-conscious reality, of three kinds: aprākṛta, prākṛta and time.
