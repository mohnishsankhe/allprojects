**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** sevenfold untenability

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Rāmānuja's seven objections to Advaita's avidyā: of locus (āśraya), concealment (tirodhāna), nature (svarūpa), indescribability (anirvacanīyatva), means of knowledge (pramāṇa), remover (nivartaka) and removal (nivṛtti).
