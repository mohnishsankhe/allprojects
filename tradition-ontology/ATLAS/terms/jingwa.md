# bying ba

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** བྱིང་བ
**Literal:** laxity; sinking

## Definitions by tradition
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The fault in which the mind's hold on the object slackens; gross laxity loses clarity, subtle laxity keeps clarity but loses its intensity and is easily mistaken for good meditation.
