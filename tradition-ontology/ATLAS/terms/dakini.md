# ḍākinī

`trm:dakini` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** sky-goer (Tibetan gloss)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Female tantric deity or realized woman, 'sky-goer', guardian and source of instructions; the dohās speak of 'the secret speech of the ḍākinīs' and warn that ḍākinīs punish breaches of pledge.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): Sky-goer (mkha' 'gro ma): female wisdom beings; the third root; keepers and entrusters of treasures; Yeshe Tsogyal as the queen of ḍākinīs.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): mkha' 'gro ma, 'sky-goer': the feminine source and guardian of the hearing-lineage instructions — the wisdom ḍākinī to whom Vajradhara speaks the Vajra Verses; Niguma and Sukhasiddhi, source of the Shangpa.
