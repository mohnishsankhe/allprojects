# sekha

`trm:sekha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** trainee

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): A noble disciple in training (stream-enterer to one on the path of arahantship), who should not conceive (MN 1).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): A noble person still in training (the first seven noble persons).
