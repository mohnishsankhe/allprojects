# maṇipūra

`trm:manipura` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मणिपूर
**Literal:** city of jewels

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The navel centre, where the Goddess rises (LSN 38); the Saundaryalaharī assigns water to it (SL 9) and there worships the dark rain-cloud with its lightning (SL 40); the Samayins see the Goddess manifest there (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 7; Bhāskararāya on LSN 37).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The navel centre, ten-petalled, of fire (RAṂ), where the bulb is strung on suṣumnā like a jewel (GŚ 15).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The navel centre, a twelve-spoked wheel where the jīva wanders.
