# vāsudeva (Jain)

`trm:vasudeva-jain` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वासुदेव

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): One of the nine half-world-conquerors of each half-cycle, always paired with an elder brother baladeva and opposed by a prativāsudeva whom he kills; Lakṣmaṇa and Kṛṣṇa are the eighth and ninth. Because of the violence of their lives vāsudevas go to hell, though Kṛṣṇa will be a future Tīrthaṅkara.
