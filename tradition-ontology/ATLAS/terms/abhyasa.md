# abhyāsa

`trm:abhyasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अभ्यास
**Literal:** repeated practice

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Practice: effort toward the mind's stability (1.13), firmly grounded when long, uninterrupted and devoted (1.14); with dispassion the means of stilling (1.12).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Practice, which together with dispassion restrains the restless mind (BhG 6.35); a step of the devotional ladder (12.9).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Practice, with dispassion a support of meditation (SS 3.36) and of the cessation of coloring (SS 6.29).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Practice: together with dispassion (vairāgya) the means by which the restless mind is held (6.35); the 'former practice' (pūrva-abhyāsa) of a previous life carries the fallen yogin onward (6.44).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Practice, repeated effort: the yoga of practice is offered to one who cannot hold the thought (citta) steadily on the Lord (12.9); one incapable even of practice is told to be intent on work for him (12.10); in the ranking of 12.12 knowledge is better than practice.
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Practice over many lives, possible because mind does not depend on the body alone; qualities of mind so cultivated become its nature and increase without limit.
