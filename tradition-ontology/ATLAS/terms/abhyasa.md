# abhyāsa

`trm:abhyasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अभ्यास
**Literal:** repeated practice

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Practice: effort toward the mind's stability (1.13), firmly grounded when long, uninterrupted and devoted (1.14); with dispassion the means of stilling (1.12).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Practice, which together with dispassion restrains the restless mind (BhG 6.35); a step of the devotional ladder (12.9).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Practice, with dispassion a support of meditation (SS 3.36) and of the cessation of coloring (SS 6.29).
