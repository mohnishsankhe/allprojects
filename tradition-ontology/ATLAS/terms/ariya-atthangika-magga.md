**Language:** Pali

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): DN 22:21: right view, right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration, each defined in the sutta.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Right view, intention, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness and concentration (SN 45.8), the middle way that avoids sensual indulgence and self-mortification (SN 56.11); grouped into the trainings of virtue, concentration and wisdom (MN 44).
