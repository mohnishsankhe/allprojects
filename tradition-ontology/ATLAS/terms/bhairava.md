# Bhairava / Mahābhairava

`trm:bhairava` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भैरव
**Literal:** the terrible

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kāpālika (Somasiddhānta)](../lineages/kapalika.md): The deity of the Kāpālikas in hostile portraits (Mahābhairava worshipped with liquor and alleged human offerings); the skull-bearing form of Śiva.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The supreme Śiva as the Trika names him; his true form is indescribable fullness identical with the Goddess, not the forms with parts described for meditation (VBT 7-16).
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): In the Śiva Sūtra the upsurge of awareness (udyama) is Bhairava (1.5).
