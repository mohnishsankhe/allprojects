# bhagavat

`trm:bhagavat` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भगवत्
**Literal:** possessing bhaga

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The one possessing the six excellences - lordship, valour, fame, fortune, knowledge and detachment - who knows the origin and end of beings, knowledge and ignorance; properly Vāsudeva; with Brahman and Paramātman one of three names of the non-dual reality.
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): The Lord (Bhagavān), speaker of the Gītā ('śrībhagavān uvāca') and object of the Bhāgavatas' devotion; the Pañcarātra's whole knower is 'the Lord himself' (12.337.63).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The Lord as possessor of the six qualities (ṣāḍguṇya).
