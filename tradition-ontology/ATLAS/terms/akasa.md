# ākāśa

`trm:akasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आकाश
**Literal:** ether; space-as-medium

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): One, all-pervading, eternal substance whose quality is sound.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: Space: all-pervading and, because of its subtlety, unstained, as the self present in the body is unstained (13.33).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Space: the final support from which all beings arise and into which they return (ChU 1.9.1); the accomplisher of name and form (ChU 8.14.1); the first element to arise from the self (TU 2.1); the space within the heart (ChU 8.1.3); 'kha' is brahman (ChU 4.10.4; BAU 5.1.1).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The substance that gives room to all others; divided into world-space (lokākāśa), where the other substances are, and infinite empty non-world space (alokākāśa) (TS 5.9, 5.12, 5.18).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Space as an unconditioned dharma, whose nature is non-obstruction; distinct from the space-element (a cavity), which is conditioned matter.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): Mere absence of tangible resistance.
