# prātimokṣa

`trm:pratimoksa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The discipline of individual liberation, eightfold by class (monk, nun, probationer, male and female novice, male and female lay follower, faster) and four in substance; given up by renouncing the training, death and other causes.
- [Dharmaguptaka](../lineages/dharmaguptaka.md): The code of 250 rules for monks and 348 for nuns recited at the poṣadha.
