## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The twenty-sixth, stainless, awakened, immeasurable and eternal, which ever knows the twenty-fifth and twenty-fourth; the twenty-fifth attains its state on awakening (MBh 12.296.7–12).
