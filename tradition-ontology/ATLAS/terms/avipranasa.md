# avipraṇāśa

`trm:avipranasa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the imperishable, non-perishing (of action)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): A realist theory stated in MMK 17: an 'imperishable' like a debt-note connects action and fruit; it is abandoned only by cultivation (17.14–15). Nāgārjuna's own answer is that action, being unarisen, does not perish (17.21).
