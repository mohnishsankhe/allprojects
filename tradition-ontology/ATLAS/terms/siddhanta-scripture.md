## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The scriptures of the upper stream — the 28 Śiva- and Rudra-tantras and their upāgamas.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Each of the four Pāñcarātra divisions (Mantra-, Āgama-, Tantra-, Tantrāntara-siddhānta), so called because those settled in Mīmāṃsā and other śāstras become eligible for it.
