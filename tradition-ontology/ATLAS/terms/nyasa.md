# nyāsa

`trm:nyasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** न्यास
**Literal:** placing, deposit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): Placing: the installation of letters, mantras and deities on parts of the body by touch and visualization (mātṛkā-nyāsa, ṛṣyādi-nyāsa, the six-limbed nyāsa), making the body divine before worship and japa.
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Placing mantras (e.g. the five brahma-mantras and six limbs) on the body and on the image to make them divine.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Placing mantras on the body (and, in another sense, 'depositing' oneself — see trm:nyasa-surrender).
- [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md): Placing of mātṛkā and deity mantras, as taught in the Prapañcasāra and Śāradātilaka.
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Placing mantras and deities on the body; Śrīvidyā uses many sets (karaśuddhi, the vāgdevatā-nyāsa, the sixfold ṣoḍhā-nyāsa and others), taught in the Vāmakeśvara and the Kalpasūtra.
