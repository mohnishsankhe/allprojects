# Terāpantha

`trm:terapantha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Rajasthani
**Native script:** तेरापंथ

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara Terāpanth](../lineages/terapanthi.md): The Śvetāmbara order founded by Ācārya Bhikṣu (1760); the name is explained as 'the path of thirteen' (after the first thirteen monks or laymen) or as 'your path, O Lord' (terā = 'yours').
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Also the name of an unconnected 17th-c. Digambara reform (against the bhaṭṭārakas and fresh offerings), contrasted with the Bīsapantha.
