# pārada

`trm:parada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पारद
**Literal:** that which carries across; mercury

## Definitions by tradition
- [Rasa Śāstra (the science of mercury) and the Raseśvara-darśana](../lineages/rasa-sastra.md): Mercury, so called because it gives the far shore (pāra) of saṃsāra (SDS Raseśvara 1-2), or because adepts proclaim it for others (para-artha) (Rasārṇava 1.35); Śiva's seed (harabīja), also called rasa, sūta, rasendra, rasarāja (Rasārṇava 1.28, 1.34-36).
