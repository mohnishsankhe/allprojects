# moha

`trm:moha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मोह
**Literal:** delusion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The second error, eightfold (SK 48): attachment to or self-assertion for the eight powers, through which even gods like Indra fall back (Gauḍapāda; Kramadīpikā).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The worst of the faults, since the others arise only in the deluded (NS 4.1.6).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Delusion born of anger (BhG 2.63) and of the pairs of opposites (7.27); destroyed by the teaching, as Arjuna declares (18.73).
