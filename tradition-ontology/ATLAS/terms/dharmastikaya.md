# dharmāstikāya

`trm:dharmastikaya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** धर्मास्तिकाय
**Literal:** the medium of motion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): A single, formless, world-pervading substance that assists (without causing) the motion of souls and matter, as water assists fish (TS 5.17); its absence beyond the world stops the liberated soul at the summit (TS 10.8).
