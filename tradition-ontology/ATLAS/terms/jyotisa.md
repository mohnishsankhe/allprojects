# jyotiṣa

`trm:jyotisa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ज्योतिष
**Literal:** (science) of the lights

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The science of the lights in three branches — computation (gaṇita/siddhānta), horoscopy (horā) and mundane omens (saṃhitā) — of which the horā is called best; the 'eye of the Veda' (BS 1.9; BPHS 1.1–6).
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): In its Vedāṅga form, time-reckoning by sun, moon and lunar mansions to fix the times of sacrifice; 'at the head of the Vedāṅgas' (Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa Ṛk 3, 35).

## Forms in other languages
- Tamil: cōtiṭam சோதிடம் — exact — loan form of jyotiṣa
- Tibetan: skar rtsis  — partial — 'star-calculation', the Indian-derived astral science (translation, not a loan)
- Pali: nakkhatta(-vijjā)  — partial — astrology as listed among the 'low arts' in DN 1; narrower than jyotiṣa
