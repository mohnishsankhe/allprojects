# vimokṣamukha

`trm:vimoksamukha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** door of liberation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The three doors — emptiness (śūnyatā), the signless (animitta) and the wishless (apraṇihita) — which the bodhisattva cultivates, even in dreams, without realizing their fruit prematurely.
