# nirvikalpaka

`trm:nirvikalpaka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निर्विकल्पक
**Literal:** indeterminate

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): The first bare awareness of an object, like an infant's or a mute's, born of the pure object (ŚV pratyakṣa 112).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Perception without a qualifier ('this is something'); read by Vācaspati into 'avyapadeśya' in NS 1.1.4.
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Not itself perceived but inferred as the first stage of determinate perception.
