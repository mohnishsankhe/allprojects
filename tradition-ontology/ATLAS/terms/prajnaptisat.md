# prajñaptisat

`trm:prajnaptisat` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** existing as designation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): Existing only as a designation over a collection (like milk over colour, smell etc., or a heap of grain); for Vasubandhu the person is such a designation over the series of aggregates.
