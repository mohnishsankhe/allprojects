**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): Awareness-holder (rig 'dzin): an accomplished tantric master; in Mahāyoga the four levels of vidyādhara (matured, mastery over life, mahāmudrā, spontaneous presence); also the eight Indian vidyādharas of the Kagye transmission.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): 'Knowledge-holder': a mantra practitioner who has attained a level of siddhi; the vidyādhara vow is the tantric vow.
