# vajradhara

`trm:vajradhara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** vajra-holder

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The 'vajra-holder', the form of the Buddha who teaches the tantras and the state of union attained at the end of the path ('the great Vajradhara', Pañcakrama 1.7); the guru is to be seen as inseparable from him.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): rdo rje 'chang: the dharmakāya buddha in sambhoga form, from whom Tilopa received the teaching directly; the speaker of the Vajra Verses; the guru is to be seen as Vajradhara.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): The thirteenth ground of the Lamdre, the final result, at which one is omniscient and awakens with a retinue.
