# dakṣiṇācāra

`trm:daksinacara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दक्षिणाचार
**Literal:** right-hand conduct

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The right-hand conduct; the Goddess 'abides on the right and left paths' and is 'worshipped by the dakṣiṇa and non-dakṣiṇa' (LSN 169-171).
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The fourth mode: 'right-hand' worship of the Goddess without the Kaula substances (or with substitutes); in Bengal the usual mode of Kālī worship.
