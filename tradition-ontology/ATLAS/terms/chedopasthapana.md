# chedopasthāpanā

`trm:chedopasthapana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** छेदोपस्थापना
**Literal:** re-establishment (in the vows)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The 'great initiation' (baḍī dīkṣā): the second, definitive ordination in which the novice is placed in the vows one by one after learning the Daśavaikālika's fourth chapter; also the name of the second of the five kinds of conduct.
