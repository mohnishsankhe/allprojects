# brahmarandhra

`trm:brahmarandhra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ब्रह्मरन्ध्र
**Literal:** aperture of Brahman

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): A name of suṣumnā (HYP 3.4) and the subtle opening within it; kuṇḍalinī sleeps at its mouth (SS 4.14); fixing the mind there frees from sin (SS 5.141, recalled).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The aperture of Brahman at the crown; place of the space-concentration that opens the door of liberation (GŚ 73) and through which the jīva departs when the skull splits (KJN 20.2).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The opening at the crown; seat of the state beyond the fourth; exit of the breath that does not return.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The opening at the crown through which the yogin departs.
