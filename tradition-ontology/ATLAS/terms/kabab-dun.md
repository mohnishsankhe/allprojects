# bka' babs bdun

`trm:kabab-dun` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tibetan
**Literal:** the seven (modes of) transmission

## Definitions by tradition
- [Rimé (ris med), the non-sectarian movement](../lineages/rime.md): The seven transmissions held by Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo: the long oral lineage, earth treasures, rediscovered treasures, mind treasures, recollection, pure vision and the hearing lineage.
