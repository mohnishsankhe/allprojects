# devatā

`trm:devata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** देवता
**Literal:** deity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): That to which an offering is directed as named in the injunction or mantra; subordinate to the sacrificial act, which alone produces the fruit (MS 9.1.9); not established as embodied or eating the offering (Śabara).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The deity addressed by a verse, recorded with its seer and metre in the Anukramaṇīs; in the person 'all deities dwell as cows in a stall' (AVŚ 11.8.32).
