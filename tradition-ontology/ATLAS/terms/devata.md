# devatā

`trm:devata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** देवता
**Literal:** deity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): That to which an offering is directed as named in the injunction or mantra; subordinate to the sacrificial act, which alone produces the fruit (MS 9.1.9); not established as embodied or eating the offering (Śabara).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The deity addressed by a verse, recorded with its seer and metre in the Anukramaṇīs; in the person 'all deities dwell as cows in a stall' (AVŚ 11.8.32).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Deities to whom people sacrifice desiring quick success of their actions in the human world (4.12).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7.20–23; 9.23, 9.25: 'other deities' (anya-devatā) resorted to by those whose knowledge desires have carried off; their worshippers go to the gods and obtain finite fruit; they too worship the Lord, but not according to rule.
- [Mahānubhāva panth](../lineages/mahanubhava.md): The gods of the Vedas and Purāṇas, one of the four realities: powerful beings who can give worldly fruit but are themselves bound and cannot liberate; not to be worshipped.
