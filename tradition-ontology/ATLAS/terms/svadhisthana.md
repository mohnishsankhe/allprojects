# svādhiṣṭhāna

`trm:svadhisthana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्वाधिष्ठान
**Literal:** own seat

## Definitions by tradition
- [Ārya tradition of the Guhyasamāja](../lineages/arya-guhyasamaja.md): Self-consecration: the illusory body arisen from wind and mind, the third stage of the Pañcakrama, 'the vision of conventional truth', obtained only by the guru's grace.
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The second centre at the genitals, six-petalled, of water (VAṂ); GŚ 14 derives the name from 'sva' = prāṇa.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The centre at the genitals.
