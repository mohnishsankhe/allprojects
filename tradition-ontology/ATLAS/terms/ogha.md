# ogha

`trm:ogha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Native script:** ओघ
**Literal:** flood

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The four floods (sensual pleasure, becoming, views, ignorance) crossed 'without halting and without straining' (SN 1.1; SN 35.238).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): A 'stream' of gurus; Śrīvidyā worship honours three: the divine (divyaugha), the perfected (siddhaugha) and the human (mānavaugha) teachers, forming the circle of gurus.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Flood: desire, existence, views, ignorance — the outflows with views separated out.
