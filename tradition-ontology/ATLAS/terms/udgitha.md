**Literal:** the up-singing

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The central portion of a sāman, sung by the udgātṛ.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The loud chant of the Sāman singer, venerated as Om and as breath (ChU 1.1-1.12; BAU 1.3).
