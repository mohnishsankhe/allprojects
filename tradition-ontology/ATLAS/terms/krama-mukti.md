# krama-mukti

`trm:krama-mukti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** क्रममुक्ति
**Literal:** liberation by stages

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Gradual liberation: meditators on the qualified Brahman reach the Brahma-world by the path of light and are liberated with its ruler at the end of the cycle (BS 4.3.10).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Liberation by stages: the yogin ascends to Satyaloka and is liberated with Brahmā at the dissolution (BhP 2.2.22-31).
