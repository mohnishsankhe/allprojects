# dbu ma chen po (Uma Chenpo)

`trm:uma-chenpo` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tibetan
**Literal:** Great Madhyamaka

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): Dolpopa's name for the definitive Madhyamaka of the third turning, the Maitreya texts, Nāgārjuna's hymns and the Kālacakra, in which the ultimate is other-empty.
- [Rimé (ris med), the non-sectarian movement](../lineages/rime.md): Kongtrul's name for the other-emptiness Madhyamaka presented as the summit of the sūtra tenets.
