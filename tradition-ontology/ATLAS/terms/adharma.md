# adharma

`trm:adharma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अधर्म
**Literal:** non-dharma, what is contrary to dharma

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: In Arjuna's argument, the disorder that overpowers a family once its family dharmas perish, leading to the corruption of its women and varṇa-mixture (1.40–41).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The tāmasa opposite of virtue (SK 23), leading to downward movement (SK 44).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): A quality of the self produced by forbidden acts (Tarkasaṅgraha).
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): What is made known by prohibition as leading to harm (anartha), e.g. the violence aimed at in the Śyena rite; also the demerit (pāpa) that results.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: The counter-order whose rise, together with the decline of dharma, is the occasion on which the Lord sends himself forth (4.7).
