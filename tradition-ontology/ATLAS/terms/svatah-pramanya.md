# svataḥ-prāmāṇya

`trm:svatah-pramanya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्वतःप्रामाण्य
**Literal:** intrinsic validity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Validity of knowledge belongs to it by itself (and is apprehended by the witness); invalidity comes from extraneous defects.
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): All means of knowledge are valid of themselves, since a capacity not present in a thing cannot be given by another (ŚV codanā 47); invalidity is established only by a cognition that the object is otherwise or by knowledge of a defect in the cause (ŚV codanā 53). The Veda, having no author, has no source of defect.
- [Murāri Miśra's school (the 'third path' of Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/murari-mimamsa.md): Validity is intrinsic but is grasped by the after-cognition (anuvyavasāya) of the first cognition (as reported by Gaṅgeśa).
