# ātmayājin

`trm:atmayajin` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आत्मयाजिन्
**Literal:** self-sacrificer

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): One who sacrifices knowing that each rite builds a limb of his own (new) self, and who so sheds the mortal body like a snake its skin (ŚB 11.2.6.13).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): One who sees the self in all beings and all beings in the self, and so attains self-rule (MDh 12.91).
