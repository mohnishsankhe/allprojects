# sthāpanā (representation)

`trm:sthapana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्थापना
**Literal:** placing

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): In the fourfold nikṣepa, a word applied to a representation of the thing — an image, painting or token — whether it resembles it or not.
- [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md): The canonical ground for venerating the Jina 'placed' in an image (sthāpanā-jina), and for the sthāpanācārya (a token of the teacher used in rites).
