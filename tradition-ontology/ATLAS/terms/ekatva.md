# ekatva

`trm:ekatva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** एकत्व
**Literal:** oneness

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Oneness with the Lord (merging), the fifth kind of liberation, which pure devotees do not accept.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Established in oneness, the yogin worships the Lord as abiding in all beings and abides in him however he lives (6.31).
