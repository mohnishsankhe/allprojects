# soma

`trm:soma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सोम
**Literal:** the pressed (plant/juice)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The plant, its pressed juice drunk in the sacrifice, and the god: 'we have drunk the soma, we have become immortal' (RV 8.48.3); but the soma the brahmins know none eats (10.85.3).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): In the haṭha texts the moon's nectar: 'drinking soma' with the tongue turned up (HYP 3.44); the body filled with the moon's digit (somakalā) is not abandoned by the soul (HYP 3.45–46; GŚ 66).
