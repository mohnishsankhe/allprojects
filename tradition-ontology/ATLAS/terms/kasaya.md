# kaṣāya

`trm:kasaya` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कषाय
**Literal:** passion (literally: what stains, colours)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Anger, pride, deceit and greed (8.9); among the rising states of the soul (2.6), a cause of bondage (8.1), the reason for the soul's taking in karma-particles (8.2), and the ground of conduct-deluding karma (6.14, 8.9); each has four varieties (8.9).
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): One of the six tastes (Ca Sū 1.65; AHS Sū 1.14); for which doṣas it pacifies or provokes see Ca Sū 1.66.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Latent attachment: the mind's stiffening under impressions of attachment even without distraction.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The four passions — anger, pride, deceit, greed — which make karma stick to the soul (TS 6.4, 8.2); each in four grades of intensity.
- [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md): The discoloured or ochre cloth of the renouncer.
