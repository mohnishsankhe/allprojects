# kaṣāya

`trm:kasaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** काषाय
**Literal:** astringent

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): One of the six tastes (Ca Sū 1.65; AHS Sū 1.14); for which doṣas it pacifies or provokes see Ca Sū 1.66.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Latent attachment: the mind's stiffening under impressions of attachment even without distraction.
- [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md): The discoloured or ochre cloth of the renouncer.
