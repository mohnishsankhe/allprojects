**Literal:** vitality

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In Jain usage the ten vitalities of an embodied soul: the five senses, the three powers (mind, speech, body), breath and lifespan; violence is the severing of these (TS 7.13).
