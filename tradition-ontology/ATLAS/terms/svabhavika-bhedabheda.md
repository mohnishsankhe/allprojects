# svābhāvika-bhedābheda

`trm:svabhavika-bhedabheda` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** natural difference-non-difference

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md): Yādavaprakāśa's form (as reported by Rāmānuja): Brahman by nature becomes Īśvara, souls and matter; difference and non-difference are both natural.
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): Name of Nimbārka's doctrine: the relation of Brahman to souls and world is naturally both difference and non-difference, not due to adjuncts.
