# pṛthivī

`trm:prthivi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पृथिवी
**Literal:** earth

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Substance possessing smell (with colour, taste, touch); eternal as atoms, non-eternal as products; our bodies are earthy.
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The Earth, mother of men (AVŚ 12.1.12), upheld by truth, order, consecration, tapas, brahman and sacrifice (12.1.1), to whom the dead are returned (RV 10.18.10).
