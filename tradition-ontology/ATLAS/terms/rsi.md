# ṛṣi

`trm:rsi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ऋषि
**Literal:** seer

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): A seer of the hymns; 'a ṛṣi is so called from seeing' (Nirukta 2.11); the seers had direct perception of dharma (1.20); Vāc makes whom she loves a ṛṣi (RV 10.125.5).
- [Nairukta school (the Vedic etymologists)](../lineages/nairukta.md): One who 'saw' the hymns; Brahman the self-existent came upon them in their austerity (Nirukta 2.11); they had direct vision of dharma (Nirukta 1.20).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The seers are the breaths (ŚB 6.1.1.1; AA 2.2.1).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Seers whose impurities are destroyed, whose dualities are cut, who are self-restrained and devoted to the welfare of all beings, attain brahma-nirvāṇa (5.25).
