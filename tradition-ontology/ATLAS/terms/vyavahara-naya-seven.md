# vyavahāra (the third of the seven nayas)

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** व्यवहार (थे थिर्द् ओफ़् थे सेवेन् नयस्)
**Literal:** the practical (distributive) standpoint

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The third of the seven standpoints: it divides the general into particulars for practical dealings (TS 1.33); distinct from vyavahāra paired with niścaya.
