# tāyin

`trm:tayin` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** protector

## Definitions by tradition
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): The Buddha as proclaiming the path he himself has seen, the four truths.
- [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md): In the Isibhāsiyāiṃ's chapter of Maṅkhaliputta, the steadfast one who is not moved and does not turn into this or that state, and so saves himself and others from saṃsāra.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Epithet in GK 4.99 of the awakened one whose knowledge does not go out to objects; Śaṅkara explains it as 'pervasive like space' or 'protector'.
