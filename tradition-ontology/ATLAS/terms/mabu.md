# ma bu

`trm:mabu` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tibetan
**Literal:** mother-son

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md): 'Mother and son': the base (mother) and awareness (son) recognized as inseparable; the meeting of mother and son clear light at death.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): Mother and child luminosity: the ground luminosity at death and the path luminosity recognized in practice, whose meeting is liberation.
