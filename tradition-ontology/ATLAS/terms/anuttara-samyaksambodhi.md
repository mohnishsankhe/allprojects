# anuttarā samyaksaṃbodhi

`trm:anuttara-samyaksambodhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** unsurpassed perfect awakening

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The full awakening of a buddha, the bodhisattva's goal; 'not even the least dharma is found in it — therefore it is called unsurpassed perfect awakening' (Vajracchedikā 22); all buddhas of the three times attain it by relying on the perfection of wisdom (Heart).
