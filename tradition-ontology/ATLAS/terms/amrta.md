# amṛta

`trm:amrta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अमृत
**Literal:** not-dying; immortality; nectar

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Immortality and the immortal: of the gods, of the fathers' world, of soma; 'may I be freed from death, not from immortality' (RV 7.59.12).

## Forms in other languages

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
