# bādha

`trm:badha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** बाध
**Literal:** sublation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): A pseudo-reason whose probandum's absence is known by another means of knowledge ('fire is not hot').
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Sublation: the cancellation of a cognition by a later one that shows its object never was as it appeared (the snake by the rope-cognition); the criterion of the three levels of reality.
