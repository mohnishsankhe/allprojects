# amanaska

`trm:amanaska` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अमनस्क
**Literal:** mindless

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The mindless state, a synonym of samādhi (HYP 4.4); the name and goal of the Amanaska's rājayoga, attained effortlessly by the guru's grace (Amanaska 2).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The later (uttara) tāraka, in which the mind is like an udder after milking.
