# pratyaya

`trm:pratyaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रत्यय
**Literal:** cognition; content of mind; cause

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): A cognition or presented content of the mind (1.10, 3.2, 3.19, 4.27); also 'cause' in upāya-/bhava-pratyaya (1.19–20).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Condition; four kinds: causal, immediately preceding, object and dominant conditions.
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The four conditions of the Abhidharma (causal, objective support, immediately preceding, dominant; MMK 1.2), which cannot contain the own-nature of the effect (1.3).
