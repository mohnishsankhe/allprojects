# mukhavastrikā

`trm:mukhavastrika` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मुखवस्त्रिका
**Literal:** mouth-cloth

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sthānakavāsī](../lineages/sthanakavasi.md): The cloth covering the mouth, worn constantly by Sthānakavāsī and Terāpanthī mendicants to avoid harming beings of the air by breath and speech.
- [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md): Mūrtipūjaka mendicants carry it in the hand and hold it before the mouth when speaking; laity use it in rites.
