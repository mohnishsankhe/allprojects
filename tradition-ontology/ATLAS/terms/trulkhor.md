**Literal:** magic wheel

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): Magical-wheel (yantra) exercises of the vajra body — in the Karṇatantravajrapada six root and thirty-nine branch movements — for clearing obstacles in the channel-and-wind practices; restricted.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): 'Magical wheel': sequences of physical movement combined with breath holding, practised with the completion stage and in Dzogchen retreats (restricted).
- [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md): The trulkhor of the Zhang Zhung Nyengyü (restricted).
