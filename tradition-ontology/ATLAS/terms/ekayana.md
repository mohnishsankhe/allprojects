# ekayāna

`trm:ekayana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** एकायन
**Literal:** the one vehicle

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The single buddha-vehicle: the three vehicles are its skillful presentation (Lotus 2-3); the śrāvaka and pratyekabuddha vehicles enter the great vehicle (Śrīmālā 5).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Name of the Veda-branch claimed as the root of the Pāñcarātra; Vedānta Deśika's Pāñcarātrarakṣā gives primary eligibility to the Ekāyanins.
