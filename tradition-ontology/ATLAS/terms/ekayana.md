# ekāyana

`trm:ekayana` · `sourced` · confidence low

**Language:** Pali
**Native script:** एकायन
**Literal:** one-going

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): In MN 10 the path (magga) of the four satipaṭṭhānas is called ekāyana, for the purification of beings, overcoming sorrow and lamentation, ending pain and displeasure, reaching the right method and realising nibbāna. The sutta does not gloss the word; its rendering is disputed.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The single buddha-vehicle: the three vehicles are its skillful presentation (Lotus 2-3); the śrāvaka and pratyekabuddha vehicles enter the great vehicle (Śrīmālā 5).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Name of the Veda-branch claimed as the root of the Pāñcarātra; Vedānta Deśika's Pāñcarātrarakṣā gives primary eligibility to the Ekāyanins.
