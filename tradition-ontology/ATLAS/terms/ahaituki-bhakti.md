# ahaitukī bhakti

`trm:ahaituki-bhakti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अहैतुकी भक्ति
**Literal:** causeless devotion

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Devotion without motive and without interruption to the Supreme Person, flowing to him like the Gaṅgā to the sea (Kapila); also 'animittā'.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Devotion without any motive of gain, which does not even seek liberation.
