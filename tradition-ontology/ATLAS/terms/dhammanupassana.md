## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Contemplation of dhammas in five sections in MN 10: the five hindrances, five aggregates of clinging, six sense bases, seven awakening factors and four noble truths.
