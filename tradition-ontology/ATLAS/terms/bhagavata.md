# bhāgavata

`trm:bhagavata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भागवत
**Literal:** belonging to Bhagavat

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): A devotee of the Lord; the Bhāgavata Purāṇa; devotees are themselves tīrthas (BhP 1.13.10).
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): A worshipper of Bhagavat (Vāsudeva); the Nārāyaṇīya says Sāṃkhyas together with Bhāgavatas enter Saṃkarṣaṇa on the path of liberation (12.332.16).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): A follower of the Pāñcarātra; opponents held the Bhāgavatas/Sātvatas to be a low temple-serving class, Yāmuna that they are brahmins so named for a quality.
