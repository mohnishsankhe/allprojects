# arthāpatti

`trm:arthapatti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अर्थापत्ति
**Literal:** postulation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Supposing an unseen fact without which a seen or heard fact would not be possible (Devadatta, alive and not at home, is outside) — the Vṛttikāra in Śabara; accepted by both Bhāṭṭas and Prābhākaras.
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Proposed as a means of knowledge; included in inference (NS 2.2.2).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Included in inference (Praśastapāda).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Presumption: knowing what must be so to account for something otherwise inexplicable (fat Devadatta who does not eat by day eats at night).
