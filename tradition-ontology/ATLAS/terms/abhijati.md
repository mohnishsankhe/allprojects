# abhijāti

`trm:abhijati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** class of birth

## Definitions by tradition
- [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md): (As reported) One of six classes — black, blue, red, yellow, white, supremely white — through which beings pass; the supremely white are the three Ājīvika leaders (AN 6.57, ascribed to Pūraṇa); in the Tamil report, black, dark blue, green, red, golden, white, with release in the supremely white birth.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The Buddha's six: dark or bright birth yielding a dark result, a bright result, or nibbāna.
