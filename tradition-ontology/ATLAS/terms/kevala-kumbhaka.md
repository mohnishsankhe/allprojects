# kevala kumbhaka

`trm:kevala-kumbhaka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** केवलकुम्भक
**Literal:** retention alone

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Spontaneous retention free of inhalation and exhalation; when mastered nothing is unattainable and rājayoga is reached (HYP 2.73–75); no retention equals it (1.43).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Spontaneous suspension of breath; its signs and the awakening of kuṇḍalinī. Summary only.
