## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The form of the Śrīvidyā beginning with 'ha', called after Lopāmudrā; the Bhāvanā Upaniṣad says it teaches according to both Kādi and Hādi doctrines; the Bahvṛca Upaniṣad names Kādi, Hādi and Sādi vidyās.
