# avasthā

`trm:avastha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अवस्था
**Literal:** stage, state

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The practitioner's fixed stations until the gain: manifest, unmanifest, conquest, cutting, completion.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tamil avattai: the five states among the ninety-six principles — cākkiram, coppaṉam, cuḻutti, turiyam, turiyātītam.
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): State of the soul: waking, dream, deep sleep, the fourth and beyond the fourth.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Avattai: the soul's states; also the three great conditions kēvalam (isolation with āṇava), cakalam (embodied) and cuttam (purified).
