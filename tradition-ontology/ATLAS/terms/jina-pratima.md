# jina-pratimā

`trm:jina-pratima` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** जिनप्रतिमा
**Literal:** likeness of the Jina

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md): A consecrated image of a Jina (always in meditation, seated or standing), a support for recalling his qualities; worshipped with material (dravya) and inner (bhāva) worship.
- [Sthānakavāsī](../lineages/sthanakavasi.md): Not to be worshipped: the canon gives no such injunction and worship harms beings.
