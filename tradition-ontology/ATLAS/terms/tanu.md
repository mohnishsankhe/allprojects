**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): A state of the afflictions (2.4): Weakened by cultivating the opposite (YBh 2.4); kriyā-yoga thins the afflictions (2.2).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: The form (of a deity) which a devotee wishes to worship with faith (7.21); the human body (mānuṣī tanu) the Lord has taken, for which the deluded disregard him (9.11).
