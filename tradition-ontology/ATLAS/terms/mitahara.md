# mitāhāra

`trm:mitahara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मिताहार
**Literal:** measured eating

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Moderate diet: smooth, sweet food eaten to please Śiva, leaving a quarter of the stomach empty (HYP 1.58); chief of the yamas (HYP 1.38; DYŚ 30); without it yoga brings disease (GS 5.16); half food, a quarter water, a quarter air (GS 5.22).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Moderate, sweet, unctuous food leaving a quarter of the stomach empty; chief of the yamas.
