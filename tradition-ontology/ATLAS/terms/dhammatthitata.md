# dhammaṭṭhitatā

`trm:dhammatthitata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Literal:** the stableness of the Dhamma

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The fixed course of things — dependent origination and the three marks — which stands whether Tathāgatas arise or not; a Tathāgata discovers and reveals it (SN 12.20; AN 3.136).
