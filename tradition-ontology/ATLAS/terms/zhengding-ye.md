# zhengding ye

**Literal:** rightly determined act

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): The rightly determined act: calling Amitābha's name single-heartedly at all times, because it accords with his vow (Shandao).
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): Shōjōgō: the calling of the name, to be chosen over the auxiliary acts (Senchakushū 16).
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): The name of the primal vow is the act of right assurance; shinjin is its cause (Shōshinge).
