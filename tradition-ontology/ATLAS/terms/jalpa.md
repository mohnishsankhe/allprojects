# jalpa

`trm:jalpa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** जल्प
**Literal:** wrangling (debate for victory)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Debate with the features of vāda in which quibbles, futile rejoinders and points of defeat are also used (NS 1.2.2); permitted to protect the ascertainment of truth (NS 4.2.50).
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): each party establishing its own thesis and attacking the other's, e.g. 'rebirth exists' against 'it does not' (Ca Vi 8.28).
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Debate aimed at victory, one of the three kinds of debate among the learned.
