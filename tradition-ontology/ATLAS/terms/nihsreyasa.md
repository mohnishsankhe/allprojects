# niḥśreyasa

`trm:nihsreyasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निःश्रेयस
**Literal:** the highest good

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Attained from knowledge of the truth of the sixteen categories (NS 1.1.1); in the science of the self it is the attainment of liberation (NBh 1.1.1).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): One of the two results of dharma, beside prosperity (VS 1.1.2).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Both renunciation (saṃnyāsa) and the yoga of action bring about the highest good (5.2).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The highest good, secured by Vedic recitation, austerity, knowledge, sense-restraint, non-injury and service of the teacher, above all by self-knowledge (MDh 12.83–85).
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The highest good with which what injunction makes known joins a person (Śabara on MS 1.1.2); for Āpadeva and Laugākṣi Bhāskara, the fruit of dharma done as an offering to Govinda/Īśvara.
