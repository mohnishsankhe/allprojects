# vikṛti

`trm:vikrti` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विकृति
**Literal:** change, deviation; morbid state

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The morbid state of the patient, examined second in the tenfold examination (Ca Vi 8.94); the deviations from normal that signal disease or approaching death (Ca In 1.6-7); disorder as imbalance of the dhātus (Sū 9.4).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): A principle that is a product only and produces no further principle: the sixteen (manas, ten capacities, five gross elements) (SK 3; Tattvasamāsa 2).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The eight 'modified' recitations built on the krama: jaṭā, mālā, śikhā, rekhā, dhvaja, daṇḍa, ratha, ghana.
