# śuddhopayoga

`trm:suddhopayoga` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शुद्धोपयोग
**Literal:** pure application (of consciousness)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Pure cognitive activity, the soul's abiding in itself beyond both good (śubha) and bad (aśubha) activity; good activity (devotion, vows) brings merit but still binds, pure activity alone liberates.
