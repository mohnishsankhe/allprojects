# arcā / arcāvatāra

`trm:arca` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अर्चा
**Literal:** image-descent

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The Lord's descent into consecrated images in temples and homes, the most accessible of his forms, where he depends even on his worshippers.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The Lord present in a consecrated image, the most accessible of his five modes.
- [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md): The image of Viṣṇu in which he is worshipped; image worship is the best means.
