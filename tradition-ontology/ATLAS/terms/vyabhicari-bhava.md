## Definitions by tradition
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): The thirty-three transient states that move within the stable emotion, from despondency (nirveda) to deliberation (NŚ 6.18–21).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The thirty-three transient emotions that move within the permanent emotion.
