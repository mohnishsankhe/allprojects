# bīja

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** बीज
**Literal:** seed

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): A potency deposited in the store-consciousness by experience and action, which later ripens as a moment of consciousness or its object; seeds are momentary, simultaneous with their fruit, continuous as a series, morally determinate, dependent on conditions and productive of their own fruit (Mahāyānasaṃgraha); Dharmapāla holds that some are innate and some newly perfumed.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): Seed: the name-and-form in a series capable of producing a result directly or mediately through the specific transformation of the series; latent defilements are such seeds.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7.10; 9.18: the Lord is the eternal seed of all beings, the imperishable seed.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Seed: the Lord is whatever is the seed of all beings; nothing moving or unmoving could exist without him (10.39).
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): Seed-syllable: a monosyllabic mantra (often ending in the nasal bindu) that contains a deity or element in seed form — e.g. laṃ vaṃ raṃ yaṃ haṃ for the elements, hrīṃ, śrīṃ, klīṃ, aiṃ for goddesses; in the threefold bindu, bīja is Śakti (ŚT 1.8–9).
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Seed syllable from which the deity arises in the generation stage; the seed syllables of the five families in dream yoga (Ṣaḍdharmopadeśa).
