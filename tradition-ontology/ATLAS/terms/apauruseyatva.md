# apauruṣeyatva

`trm:apauruseyatva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अपौरुषेयत्व
**Literal:** not being of human origin

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The Veda's having no author, human or divine; names like Kāṭhaka come from expounding, not authorship (MS 1.1.30); the unbroken chain of teachers' study has no first term (ŚV vākya 366); since defects of testimony depend on speakers, an authorless Veda is free of them (ŚV codanā 62).
