# nyams

`trm:nyam` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** ཉམས
**Literal:** experience

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): Meditative experiences — above all bliss, clarity and non-thought — which are signs of practice but, if grasped, lead into the desire, form and formless realms; they are transient, unlike realization (rtogs pa).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): Temporary meditative experiences — above all bliss, clarity and non-thought — which come and go and are to be neither sought nor clung to; distinguished from realization (rtogs pa).
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): Experiences of the yogin in concentration — the content of the 'experiential vision' of the Lamdre — which the Vajra Verses teach one to recognise without clinging.
