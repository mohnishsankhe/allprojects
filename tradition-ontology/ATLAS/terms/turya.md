# turya

`trm:turya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तुर्य
**Literal:** the fourth

## Definitions by tradition
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): The fourth state, whose enjoyment arises amid the three (Śiva Sūtra 1.7) and which is to be poured like oil into them (3.20).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The fourth abode in which emission, maintenance, withdrawal and the three states shine without covering it (Paramārthasāra 34-35).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The fourth state, a synonym of samādhi (HYP 4.4); the place between the brows where mind dissolves and time does not exist (4.48).
