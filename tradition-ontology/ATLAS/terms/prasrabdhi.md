# praśrabdhi

`trm:prasrabdhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रश्रब्धि
**Literal:** pliancy; serenity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The workability of body and mind that removes badness, arising with calm; one of the eight antidotal formations and a wholesome factor.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The fitness of body and mind for virtuous use; its arising, mental then physical, marks the achievement of calm abiding, and analysis-induced pliancy marks insight.
