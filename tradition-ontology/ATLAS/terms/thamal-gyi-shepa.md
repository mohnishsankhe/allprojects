# tha mal gyi shes pa (ordinary mind)

`trm:thamal-gyi-shepa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** ཐ་མལ་གྱི་ཤེས་པ
**Literal:** ordinary / common knowing

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): 'Ordinary mind': present awareness just as it is, uncontrived and unspoiled by conceptions of worldly or spiritual kinds; recognized, it is the dharmakāya (Gampopa); 'the great way of the ordinary' (Karṇatantravajrapada).

## Forms in other languages
