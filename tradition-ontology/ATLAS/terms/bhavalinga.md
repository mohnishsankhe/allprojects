# bhāvaliṅga

`trm:bhavalinga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भावलिङ्ग
**Literal:** the liṅga of feeling / of pure being

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The liṅga in which feeling (bhāva) itself dissolves, grasped by devotion and worshipped with flowers of feeling, superior to liṅgas of clay or stone (SSM 15.37–44); in the vacana triad iṣṭa–prāṇa–bhāva the third, beyond form.
