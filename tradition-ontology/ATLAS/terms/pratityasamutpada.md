# pratītyasamutpāda

`trm:pratityasamutpada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रतीत्यसमुत्पाद
**Literal:** arising in dependence

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The arising of things in dependence on causes and conditions, which is their non-arising by own-nature: it is called emptiness, dependent designation and the middle way (MMK 24.18; PsP); taught in the dedication as free of the eight extremes and as the pacification of elaboration; conventionally the twelve links (MMK 26).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Dependent origination in twelve limbs over three lives; the Vaibhāṣikas take it as 'of states' (āvasthika), each limb being the fivefold aggregates at a stage named by what predominates.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Seeing dependent origination is seeing the dharma and the Buddha (Śālistamba); what arises from conditions is unborn and empty (Anavatapta); the twelve links rest on mind (Daśabhūmika 6).
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The 'king of reasons': because things arise in dependence (on causes, parts and conceptual designation) they are empty of inherent existence; seeing it as unfailing while destroying all grasping completes the view.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): In the Vajra Verses, dependent arising (rten 'brel) of body and path — outer, inner and ultimate — through which the path is completed.
