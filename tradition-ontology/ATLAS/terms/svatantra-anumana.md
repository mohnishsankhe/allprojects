# svatantra-anumāna

`trm:svatantra-anumana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** autonomous (independent) inference

## Definitions by tradition
- [Svātantrika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/svatantrika.md): A formal inference with thesis, reason and example stated in one's own right, required of the Mādhyamika to prove emptiness (Bhāviveka: 'ultimately the inner sense-bases do not arise from themselves, because they exist, like consciousness').
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Not appropriate for a Mādhyamika, who has no position of his own to establish (PsP 1).
