## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Said of the embodied self: unborn, eternal, everlasting, primeval (2.20); knowing it as unborn one sees that it neither slays nor is slain (2.21).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The unborn (she-goat): one unborn female, red, white and black, producing many like herself, and two unborn males who enjoy and leave her (ŚU 4.5; cf. 1.9).
