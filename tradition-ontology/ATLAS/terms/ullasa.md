# ullāsa

`trm:ullasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उल्लास
**Literal:** exhilaration, upsurge; also 'chapter'

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The seven degrees of inner exhilaration in the Kula rite — ārambha, taruṇa, yauvana, prauḍha, tadanta, unmanā, anavastha (Kulārṇava 8.4); the first five are 'waking', unmanā is 'dream', the equal state is 'deep sleep' (8.94-95). The Kulārṇava's chapters are also called ullāsas.
