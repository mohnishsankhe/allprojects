# vimarśa

`trm:vimarsa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विमर्श
**Literal:** reflexive awareness, self-reflection

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The self-reflective awareness that is the very nature of manifestation (ĪPK 1.5.11), supreme speech and the Lord's primary freedom (1.5.13); pure knowing and acting are the Lord's vimarśa (1.8.11).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Self-awareness, the Goddess as the mirror in which Śiva knows himself (KKV 2); 'whose form is vimarśa' (LSN 112).
