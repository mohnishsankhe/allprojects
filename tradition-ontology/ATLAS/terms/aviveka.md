# aviveka

`trm:aviveka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अविवेक
**Literal:** non-discrimination

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The failure to discriminate puruṣa from prakṛti, the cause of their conjunction and so of bondage (SS 1.55); beginningless, removed by discrimination like darkness by light; bondage is non-discrimination alone (SS 6.12–16). The manifest and unmanifest are 'non-discriminating' (SK 11).
