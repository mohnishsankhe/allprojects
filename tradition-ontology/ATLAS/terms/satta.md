## Definitions by tradition
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): The highest universal, distinct from substance, quality and motion, in which the notion 'existent' arises (VS 1.2.7–8 C).
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): Being, the great universal (mahāsattā) that all words finally denote, identified with Brahman (VP 3.1).
