# Aniruddha

`trm:aniruddha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनिरुद्ध
**Literal:** the unobstructed

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): The fourth form, from whom Brahmā arises; called the great self and ahaṃkāra (MBh 12.326.69; 12.327.26).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The vyūha manifesting power and splendour; in Śaṅkara's report, identified with ego-sense (ahaṃkāra).
- [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md): The fifth of the five forms of Viṣṇu invoked in Vaikhānasa worship.
