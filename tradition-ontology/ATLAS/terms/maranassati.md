# maraṇassati

`trm:maranassati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** mindfulness of death

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Recollection of death, 'may I live for one breath', of great fruit, merging in the deathless (AN 6.19).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Recollection of death, by simple attention or in eight ways; it leads to access.
