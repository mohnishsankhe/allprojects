# saptabhaṅgī

`trm:saptabhangi` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सप्तभङ्गी
**Literal:** sevenfold predication

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The seven possible predications about any attribute: in some respect it is; is not; is and is not; is inexpressible; is and is inexpressible; is not and is inexpressible; is, is not and is inexpressible (Āptamīmāṃsā 14).
