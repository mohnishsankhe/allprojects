# vāsanākṣaya

`trm:vasana-ksaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वासनाक्षय
**Literal:** destruction of vāsanās

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Exhaustion of latent tendencies; the Yoga Vāsiṣṭha equates it with liberation ('mokṣaḥ syād vāsanākṣayaḥ').
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Destruction of latent tendencies, practised with knowledge and mind-dissolution for the stable freedom of the jīvanmukta.
