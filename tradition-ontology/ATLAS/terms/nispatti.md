# niṣpatti

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निष्पत्ति
**Literal:** consummation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The fourth stage: rudragranthi pierced, flute and vīṇā sounds, the mind one — rājayoga (HYP 4.76–77); it gives both enjoyment and liberation (DYŚ 147).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Fourth stage: natural yoga; liberation while living.
