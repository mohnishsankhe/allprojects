# kṣamāpanā

`trm:ksamapana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** क्षमापना
**Literal:** causing to forgive

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Asking and granting forgiveness — to all beings in daily and annual confession, and person to person at Paryuṣaṇa; the Kalpa Sūtra makes it the condition of being a true 'fulfiller' of the discipline.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Mutual asking and granting of forgiveness, above all at the end of Paryuṣaṇa ('micchāmi dukkaḍaṃ').
