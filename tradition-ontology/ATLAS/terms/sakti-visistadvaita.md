# Śakti-viśiṣṭādvaita

`trm:sakti-visistadvaita` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शक्तिविशिष्टाद्वैत
**Literal:** non-duality of (Śiva) qualified by Śakti

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The philosophical name of Vīraśaiva doctrine in Śrīpati's Śrīkarabhāṣya (philosophy owned by U16); the SSM's teaching that the liṅga is Śiva with Śakti as its pedestal and the universe their union (11.32) is its root image.
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Śrīpati's doctrine: non-duality of Śiva and soul, both qualified by Śakti (in Śiva as power, in the soul as devotion), realised as their union.
