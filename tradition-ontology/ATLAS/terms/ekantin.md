# ekāntin

`trm:ekantin` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** एकान्तिन्
**Literal:** one-pointed devotee

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): One devoted solely to Nārāyaṇa, dwelling in the White Island or entering Vāsudeva directly without the intermediate goals (MBh 12.323.26; 12.336.3–4; 12.332.18).
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): The one-pointed devotee, 'chief' among devotees (NBS 67), who purifies families, holy places and scriptures.
