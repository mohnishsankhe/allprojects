# yoni

`trm:yoni` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** योनि
**Literal:** womb, source

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): The Goddess's yoni fallen at Kāmagiri, worshipped at Kāmākhyā; Kāmākhyā is 'the great vidyā in the form of the yoni' (Kālikā Purāṇa 18.42; Kāmākhyā Tantra 1.4).
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): The supreme primal yoni of the supreme, fourfold (Kubjikāmata 11.6).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The triangle as womb of the universe: the triangles of the Śrīcakra are yonis; the yoni-mudrā is the ninth mudrā; the Tripurā Upaniṣad calls the Goddess's Śakti the womb of all.
