# saṃśaya

`trm:samsaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** संशय
**Literal:** doubt

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Doubt: a cognition touching both alternatives (YBh 1.30).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Deliberation awaiting a distinguishing mark (NS 1.1.23); a cognition of one thing as qualified by mutually opposed properties, e.g. 'a post or a man?' (Tarkasaṅgraha).
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The doubt about the passage that gives rise to an adhikaraṇa (also called sandeha, viśaya).
