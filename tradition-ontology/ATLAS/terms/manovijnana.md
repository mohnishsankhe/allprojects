# mano-vijñāna

`trm:manovijnana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** mental consciousness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The sixth consciousness, cognizing mental objects; it arises always except in the heaven of non-perception, the two mindless attainments, deep sleep and fainting; it is the one that conceptualizes and is first transformed (into discerning wisdom) on the path.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The sixth consciousness, cause and support of the others in the Laṅkāvatāra's account.
