# vyavahāra-naya (Kundakunda)

`trm:vyavahara-naya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** व्यवहारनय
**Literal:** the conventional standpoint

## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The conventional standpoint, which describes the soul through distinctions and relations (as having knowledge, faith, conduct, bondage, stages); 'unreal' (abhūtārtha) but indispensable for teaching, like speaking to a foreigner in his own tongue.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The conventional standpoint, which describes the soul through its relations with karma, body and practice; needed to teach the ultimate as a foreign tongue is needed to teach a foreigner (Samayasāra 8), but called unreal (abhūtārtha) (Samayasāra 11).
- [Adhyātma movement (Adhyātma-mata)](../lineages/adhyatma-jain.md): Outward conduct, ritual and the stages of practice — valid in their place, but not to be mistaken for the self (Mokṣamārga Prakāśaka).

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
