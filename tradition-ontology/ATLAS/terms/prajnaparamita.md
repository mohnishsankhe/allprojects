# prajñāpāramitā

`trm:prajnaparamita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रज्ञापारमिता
**Literal:** perfection of wisdom; wisdom gone beyond

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The sixth perfection: wisdom that apprehends no dharma, not even itself; the 'mother of the buddhas' and leader of the other five perfections; also the name of the sūtras that teach it and, personified, a goddess.
- [Chöd (gcod), 'Cutting'](../lineages/chod.md): The view of Chöd, applied as practice to cut clinging; personified as the Great Mother.
- [Shije (zhi byed), 'Pacification [of suffering]'](../lineages/shije.md): The teaching whose mantra 'pacifies all suffering', from which Pacification takes its name.
