# kāntā-bhāva

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Love as of a beloved wife — 'eternal love as a beloved' (nitya-kāntā-bhajana, NBS 66), one of the eleven attachments (NBS 82).
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The Āḻvārs' bridal love: Āṇṭāḷ's longing to wed the Lord and Nammāḻvār's and Tirumaṅkai's voice as the love-sick girl.
