**Literal:** point of defeat

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Occasion for rebuke in debate: misunderstanding or non-understanding (NS 1.2.19); twenty-two kinds (NS 5.2.1).
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Reduced by Dharmakīrti to two: failing to state what establishes the proof, and failing to point out a real fault.
