# pubbenivāsānussati

`trm:pubbenivasanussati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** recollection of past lives

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Recollecting one's many past births with their details (DN 2; MN 4); the first true knowledge.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Direct knowledge recollecting former existences; its range differs among sectarians, disciples, paccekabuddhas and Buddhas.
