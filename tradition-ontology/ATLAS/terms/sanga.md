# saṅga

`trm:sanga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सङ्ग
**Literal:** attachment, clinging

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Attachment: not to inaction (2.47); to be abandoned in action (2.48, 3.9); arises from dwelling on objects and gives rise to desire (2.62); the ignorant are attached to action and to the actions of the guṇas (3.26, 3.29); the knower is not attached (3.28).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Attachment to be abandoned in acting: abandoning attachment to the fruit of action (4.20); one whose attachment is gone (gata-saṅga) (4.23); acting having abandoned attachment (5.10–11).
