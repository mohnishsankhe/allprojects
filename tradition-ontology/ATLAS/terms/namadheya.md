## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): A word in an injunction that names the rite rather than enjoining a quality (e.g. udbhid, citrā, agnihotra, śyena) (MS 1.4.2–5).
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): Meaningful by delimiting what is enjoined; a word is taken as a name for four reasons — avoiding secondary meaning, avoiding a split sentence, another text supplying the quality, and comparison.
