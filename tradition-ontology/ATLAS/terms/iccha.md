**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** इच्छा
**Literal:** desire

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): A mark and special quality of the self (NS 1.1.10); 'desire is wanting' (Tarkasaṅgraha); eternal in the Lord.
- [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md): The power of will, first of the three powers; in the Trika the goddess Parā; the Śiva Sūtra calls it Umā, the virgin (1.13).
