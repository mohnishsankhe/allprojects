## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The empirical person, the aggregate of twenty-four, born of delusion, desire, aversion and karma, in which action, fruit, knowledge, pleasure and pain are established (Ca Śā 1.35-37, 1.53).
