# dhammavicayasambojjhaṅga

`trm:dhammavicaya-sambojjhanga` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** awakening factor of investigation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Investigation-of-dhammas awakening factor: aroused when, abiding thus mindful, he investigates that dhamma with wisdom, examines it and undertakes a scrutiny of it (MN 118:31).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Investigating states with wisdom; nourished by attending to wholesome and unwholesome states (SN 46.51). One of the thirty-seven qualities conducive to awakening (DN 16.3.50; MN 149): a member of the seven factors of awakening.
