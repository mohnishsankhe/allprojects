## Definitions by tradition
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The Lord's power of action, his creatorhood, making things appear by his will (ĪPK 2.4.1).
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Kiriyai: ritual worship of Śiva in the liṅga and image, the son's path (satputra-mārga), leading to sāmīpya.
