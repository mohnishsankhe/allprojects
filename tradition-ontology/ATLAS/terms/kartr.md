# kartṛ

`trm:kartr` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कर्तृ
**Literal:** doer, agent

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: One deluded by ahaṃkāra thinks 'I am the doer' (3.27); Kṛṣṇa says that by not acting he would be the doer (cause) of confusion (3.24).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: the Lord is the maker (kartṛ) of the fourfold class order, yet is to be known as the non-doer (akartṛ), imperishable (4.13).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13.30: one who sees actions done by prakṛti alone and his self as not the doer (akartṛ) sees; 14.19: the seer beholds no agent other than the guṇas.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Agent: the second of the five factors of action (18.14); one of untrained understanding who sees the self alone as agent does not see (18.16); the agent is one of the three constituents of action (18.18) and is sāttvic, rājasic or tāmasic (18.26–28).
