# puthujjana

`trm:puthujjana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** ordinary person, worldling

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The uninstructed ordinary person who perceives and conceives (MN 1) and feels two darts (SN 36.6).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): An ordinary person who has not reached a path.
