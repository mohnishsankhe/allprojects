# dauṣṭhulya

`trm:dausthulya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** badness; unwieldiness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The heaviness or unworkability of body and mind, the latent substrate of defilement; its twofold removal (afflictive and cognitive) is the transformation of the basis; pliancy removes it in meditation.
