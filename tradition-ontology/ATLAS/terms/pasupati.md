# paśupati

`trm:pasupati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पशुपति
**Literal:** lord of animals/cattle

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Epithet of Rudra as lord of beasts, to whom the five kinds of animals are allotted (AVŚ 11.2); also in the Śatarudrīya.
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The Lord (Rudra, Maheśvara) as master of all bound souls; he 'reaches' and 'protects' them by infinite knowledge and lordship, and releases them by grace.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The Lord of beings, all-knowing, doer of the five acts; witness who empowers the senses.
