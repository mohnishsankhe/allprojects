**Literal:** correct word

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): A correct word (as taught by grammar), whose knowing use brings merit, as opposed to a corrupt form (apaśabda); 'a single word well known and well used is a wish-granting cow in heaven' (Mahābhāṣya on 6.1.84).
