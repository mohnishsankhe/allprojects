# Puruṣottama

`trm:purusottama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पुरुषोत्तम
**Literal:** the highest person

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Viṣṇu, beyond both the perishable (souls) and the imperishable (Lakṣmī).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The Lord, beyond the perishable and higher than the imperishable, who enters and sustains the three worlds (BhG 15.16–18).
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): Nārāyaṇa, reached by the ekāntins; those on the 'fourth path' go to him (MBh 12.336.3).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The Lord as distinct from and higher than both the bound (kṣara) and the freed (akṣara) souls (Gītā 15.16–18).
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): The highest person, Kṛṣṇa, above Akṣara Brahman; the goal of grace-devotion.
