# paramaṃ dhāma

`trm:parama-dhama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** परमं धाम
**Literal:** the supreme abode

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: 'My supreme abode' — the unmanifest imperishable, the supreme goal, reaching which they do not return (8.21).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The Lord's supreme abode, not illumined by sun, moon or fire, from which there is no return (BhG 15.6, 8.21).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Arjuna calls the Lord the supreme brahman and supreme abode (10.12), and the knower, the known and the supreme abode (11.38).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: The Lord's supreme abode, which neither sun nor moon nor fire illumines, and from which those who reach it do not return (15.6).
