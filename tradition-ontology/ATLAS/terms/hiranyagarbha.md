# hiraṇyagarbha

`trm:hiranyagarbha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** हिरण्यगर्भ
**Literal:** golden germ/embryo

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The Golden Germ that arose in the beginning as the one lord of what exists (RV 10.121.1).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): In the Purāṇas the cosmic creator Brahmā as the first embodied being; in VP 6.7.51 the being whose contemplation is of both Brahman and action.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The cosmic subtle being: consciousness identified with the aggregate of subtle bodies (sūtrātman), collective counterpart of taijasa.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The golden germ, whom Rudra saw being born (ŚU 3.4, 4.12).
