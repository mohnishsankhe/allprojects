# upāsanā

`trm:upasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उपासना
**Literal:** worship, meditation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Worship of Hari with the qualities appropriate to one's fitness — Brahmā with all qualities, humans as bliss, knower and the ever-existing Self.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The 'knowledge' the Upaniṣads enjoin for liberation: repeated, continuous meditation on Brahman, which, when it becomes loving and vivid, is bhakti.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Veneration, meditation: venerating X as brahman (ChU 7; BAU 4.1); 'one should venerate it as the self' (BAU 1.4.7); 'not this that people venerate here' (Kena 1.5).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Meditation/worship: sustained mental activity directed to a prescribed object (the qualified Brahman, a symbol), an act that can be enjoined; purifies and steadies the mind and leads to the Brahma-world.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Worship (upa-√ās, pari-upa-√ās): Arjuna contrasts those who worship the Lord with those who worship the imperishable unmanifest (12.1); the Lord describes both kinds (12.2–6); the devotees 'attend to' this nectar of dharma (12.20).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13.26: others, not knowing this, worship (upāsate) having heard from others, and they too cross death.
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Meditation on a thing as something else ('let one meditate on the Real as Brahman'), by which one becomes what one meditates on (ŚB 10.6.3).
