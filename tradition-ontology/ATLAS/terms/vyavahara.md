# vyavahāra

`trm:vyavahara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** व्यवहार
**Literal:** legal procedure

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Litigation and the law of the eighteen titles, which arose when dharma declined among humans (Nāradasmṛti Mātṛkā 1.1–2).
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Conventional usage, in dependence on which alone the ultimate is taught (MMK 24.10); the Buddhas speak of arising and ceasing by worldly convention (ŚS 1).
