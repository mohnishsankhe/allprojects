## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The name derived from the birth-asterism: the Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa says the knowers of the śāstra laid down a sacrificer's name from his nakṣatra (VJ r.28); later, each asterism-quarter is assigned a syllable for naming a child.
