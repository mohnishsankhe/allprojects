# bhasma

`trm:bhasma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भस्म
**Literal:** ash

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Ash, so called because it reproaches sins; bathing in ash is the supreme 'fire-bath' (SSM 7.4–5; 7.23–25).
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Ash consecrated with the mantras, used for bathing thrice daily, lying on, re-bathing and initiation; the sign of the first stage.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The Atharvaśiras identifies all the elements with ash: 'fire is ash ... all this is ash'.
- [Kāpālika (Somasiddhānta)](../lineages/kapalika.md): The ash of a cremation, smeared by a Kāpālika (Sattasaī) and listed by Yāmuna among the six Kāpāla insignia.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Sacred ash worn by Śaivas (often as the tripuṇḍra), praised in the Śiva Purāṇa.
- [Rasa Śāstra (the science of mercury) and the Raseśvara-darśana](../lineages/rasa-sastra.md): Lineage contribution (Rasa Śāstra / later Āyurveda): the calcined preparation of a metal, mineral or gem used as medicine; the texts insist that only properly purified and processed material is used, the impure being life-destroying (RRS 5.47-48, 5.73). RESTRICTED: no processes recorded.
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Sacred ash from the sacred fire, smeared on all the limbs in the Pāśupata yoga.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Ash used in the Virajā initiation (Śiva Gītā).
- [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md): Tirunīṟu, the sacred ash: mantra, medicine and the mark of Śiva's devotee.
