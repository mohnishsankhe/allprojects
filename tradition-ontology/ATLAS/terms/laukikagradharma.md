# laukikāgra-dharma

`trm:laukikagradharma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** supreme mundane quality

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): Fourth aid to penetration: only the distraction of the grasper remains, and the immediate concentration is touched.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The fourth root of penetration, lasting one moment, immediately followed by the path of seeing.
