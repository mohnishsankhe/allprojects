# kuṭīprāveśika

`trm:kutipravesika` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कुटीप्रावेशिक
**Literal:** (rejuvenation) by entering a hut

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The principal and most difficult mode of rasāyana, taken in a specially built three-chambered hut after purification (Ca Ci 1.1.16-24, 1.4.27-28; AHS Utt 39.5-8); contrasted with vātātapika, taken amid ordinary life exposed to wind and sun.
