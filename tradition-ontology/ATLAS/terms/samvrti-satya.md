# saṃvṛti-satya

`trm:samvrti-satya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** संवृतिसत्य
**Literal:** concealing / conventional truth

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The truth of worldly convention (lokasaṃvṛti), relied on for teaching the ultimate (MMK 24.8–10).
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Saṃvṛti is ignorance that totally covers reality, or mutual dependence, or worldly convention (PsP 24.8); what delusion fabricates and presents as true (MA 6.28).
- [Svātantrika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/svatantrika.md): Divided into the correct conventional, which functions as it appears, and the incorrect conventional, which does not (Jñānagarbha); the ladder to the ultimate (Bhāviveka).
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): Twofold, false and correct; correct conventional truth is what, unexamined, arises, perishes and performs functions; it is the ladder to the ultimate and must not be disparaged.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): What is found by conventional valid cognition; existent, though not inherently; Prāsaṅgika does not divide it into correct and false from its own side, only relative to worldly cognition.

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [sammuti](sammuti.md) — Theravāda sammuti-sacca also denotes conventional designations, but it contrasts with ultimately real dhammas, which Madhyamaka does not admit
- contested: [vyāvahārika](vyavaharika.md) (P1-level: both name the level of worldly usage) — Advaita's vyāvahārika is the empirical level of a world superimposed on Brahman; Madhyamaka's saṃvṛti has no real substrate. Neither tradition accepts identity.
