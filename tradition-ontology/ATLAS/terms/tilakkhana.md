# tilakkhaṇa

`trm:tilakkhana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** the three marks

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Impermanence (anicca), suffering (dukkha) and not-self (anattā): 'all formations are impermanent, all formations are suffering, all phenomena are not-self' (AN 3.136; Dhp 277–279); the term tilakkhaṇa itself is commentarial.
