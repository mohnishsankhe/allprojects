# vijñapti

`trm:vijnapti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** information

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Manifest (informative) action: bodily vijñapti is shape (not movement, since things are momentary); vocal vijñapti is speech-sound.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): Bodily action is volition that moves the body; shape is not a real entity.
