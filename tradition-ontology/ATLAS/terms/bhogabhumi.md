# bhogabhūmi

`trm:bhogabhumi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भोगभूमि
**Literal:** land of enjoyment

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The regions other than Bhārata, where beings only enjoy the fruits of past deeds.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Regions where wish-fulfilling trees provide all needs, lifespans are long and liberation is not possible (TS 3.28–31).
