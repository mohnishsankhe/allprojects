# paramāṇu

`trm:paramanu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** परमाणु
**Literal:** atom; 'ultimate minute'

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): The eternal, partless, imperceptible ultimate constituent of earth, water, fire and air; of spherical size (VS 4.1.1, 4.1.7, 7.1.26 C).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Division ends in atoms, which lie beyond the truṭi (NS 4.2.16–17); atoms are imperceptible (NS 2.1.37).
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The supposed indivisible unit of matter, refuted: an atom joined on six sides would have six parts; without parts atoms could not aggregate, cast shadow or obstruct.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The partless unit of matter, occupying one space-point, with one taste, colour, smell and two touches; atoms bind by degrees of smoothness and roughness (TS 5.25–37).
