# vivekakhyāti

`trm:vivekakhyati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विवेकख्याति
**Literal:** discriminative discernment

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The discernment of the distinctness of puruṣa and prakṛti (sattva), culminating in the knowledge 'I am not, not mine, not I' (SK 64); called the direct realization of the distinctness of sattva and puruṣa (Vācaspati).
