## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): one hundred and seven vital points of five kinds — of flesh (11), vessels (41), ligaments (27), bone (8) and joints (20) (Su Śā 6.3-4); the three great marmas are head, heart and bladder (Ca Sū 29.3). (Marma in depth: U58.)
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The eighteen vital points of the body through which the breath is moved in one kind of withdrawal (Yoga Yājñavalkya; Vasiṣṭha Saṃhitā).
