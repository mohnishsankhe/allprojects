# catuḥṣaṣṭi-tantra

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** चतुःषष्टितन्त्र
**Literal:** the sixty-four tantras

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The sixty-four Bhairava tantras in eight groups of eight (Śrīkaṇṭhīya).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The sixty-four tantras 'of the Mothers' named in the Vāmakeśvara, by which Paśupati 'beguiled the world' before revealing the Goddess's own tantra (Saundaryalaharī 31).
