# dvandva

`trm:dvandva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** द्वन्द्व
**Literal:** pair (of opposites)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: The pairs such as cold and heat, pleasure and pain, honour and dishonour (6.7); one who has gone beyond them (dvandvātīta) is not bound though acting (4.22); one free of them (nirdvandva) is easily released from bondage (5.3).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: The 'delusion of the pairs' (dvandva-moha), arising from desire and aversion, into which all beings fall at birth (7.27); those whose sin has ended are freed from it and worship the Lord firm in vows (7.28).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Pairs such as cold and heat, which no longer assail one who has mastered posture (2.48); bearing them is austerity (YBh 2.32).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The pairs such as heat and cold, pleasure and pain, from which one should be free (BhG 2.45, 5.3); the delusion of opposites born of desire and aversion (7.27).
