# mahāsukha

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Great bliss: the bliss joined with emptiness that is the nature of the innate and of the goal; the fourth cakra at the head is 'of great bliss'.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): bde ba chen po, great bliss: the taste of mind's nature 'as it is' (Nāropa); the saṃbhogakāya aspect (Karṇatantravajrapada); the object of the method path.
