# brahmaloka

`trm:brahmaloka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ब्रह्मलोक
**Literal:** the world of Brahmā

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 8.16 (ā-brahma-bhuvanāt): the worlds up to Brahmā's realm are subject to return.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The world of brahman: reached by the path of the gods (BAU 6.2.15) and by brahmacarya (ChU 8.4-5); described with its lake, river, tree, city and palace (KauU 1.3-5; ChU 8.5.3); in BAU 4.3.32 the ocean-like non-dual seer 'is the world of brahman'.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The world of the conditioned Brahman (Hiraṇyagarbha), reached by meditators on the qualified Brahman, where they are liberated at the end of the cycle.
