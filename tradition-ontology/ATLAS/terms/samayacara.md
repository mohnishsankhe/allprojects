# samayācāra

`trm:samayacara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** समयाचार
**Literal:** the conventional/Samaya way

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): In Lakṣmīdhara, the Vedic way of Goddess worship taught in the five 'auspicious āgamas', opposed to the Kaula and 'mixed' ways.
- [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md): The Vedic, inner conduct of Śrīvidyā taught by the five Śubhāgamas and followed by Lakṣmīdhara; rejects outer and Kaula worship.
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The Goddess is 'intent on Samaya conduct' (LSN 37); Bhāskararāya notes that the Rudrayāmala teaches samayācāra in ten chapters.
