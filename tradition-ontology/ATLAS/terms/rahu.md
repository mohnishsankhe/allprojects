**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The ascending node counted as a planet: in the epic the asura whose severed head, made immortal by the nectar, swallows sun and moon (MBh 1.17); in the siddhāntas the node (pāta) that deflects the moon (SS 2.6); for Varāhamihira not the cause of eclipses but present at them by Brahmā's boon to receive the offerings (BS 5.13–15); the Boar is born from him (BPHS 2.6); period 18 years.
