# guhya

`trm:guhya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** गुह्य
**Literal:** secret

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: The teaching of ch. 9 is 'most secret' (guhyatama), to be told to one free of cavilling (9.1), and 'the royal secret' (rājaguhya) (9.2).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The secret teaching: the ancient yoga (BhG 4.3), the royal secret (9.2), the most secret (15.20, 18.63–64), not to be told to the undisciplined or undevoted (18.67).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Secret: Kṛṣṇa's teaching is 'knowledge more secret than the secret' (18.63); his supreme word is 'the most secret of all' (sarva-guhyatama, 18.64); 'this supreme secret' is to be declared among his devotees (18.68); Sañjaya heard 'this supreme secret' by Vyāsa's grace (18.75).
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The secret: the tantras are to be kept from the unconsecrated; revealing the secret to the unripe is a root downfall.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Secret: of secret things the Lord is silence (10.38); Arjuna calls the teaching he has received 'the supreme secret called adhyātma' (11.1).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: The teaching on the highest person is called the most secret teaching (guhyatama śāstra) (15.20).
