# caityavāsa

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md): The practice of monks residing permanently in temples and managing their property, attacked by Haribhadra and by the Kharatara reformers, who insisted on vasativāsa, living in lodgings offered by laypeople.
