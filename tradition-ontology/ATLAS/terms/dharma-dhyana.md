## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): TS 9.36: dharmya dhyāna is for the examination of the command (ājñā), the apāya ('going away', also 'ruin'), the ripening (vipāka) and the form (saṃsthāna); with śukla, a cause of liberation (9.29).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Meditation examining the Jina's command, the harm of wrong ways, the fruition of karma and the structure of the universe (TS 9.36); leads to heaven and toward liberation.
