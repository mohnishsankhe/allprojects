# bhūtakoṭi

`trm:bhutakoti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the reality-limit, the summit of the real

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The limit of reality, synonym of emptiness and suchness; to 'realize the reality-limit' is to enter final peace, which the bodhisattva with skillful means postpones until his roots of merit are ripe for buddhahood.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): Synonym of emptiness, meaning the non-erroneous.
