# dharmabhūta-jñāna

`trm:dharmabhuta-jnana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** knowledge that is an attribute

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Attributive consciousness: the soul's (and the Lord's) consciousness as a quality distinct from the self-luminous substrate; eternal, it contracts in bondage through karma and expands fully in liberation; it goes out to objects as a lamp's light.
