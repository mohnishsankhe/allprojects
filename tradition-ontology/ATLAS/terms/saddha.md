# saddhā

`trm:saddha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** faith, confidence

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Confidence in the Tathāgata's awakening (SN 48.10), placed after examining a teacher (MN 95; MN 47); faith arises from suffering and leads to gladness (SN 12.23); the first faculty and power.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Confidence in the Triple Gem; beautiful universal; faculty and power; the wholesome counterpart of greed in the greedy temperament.
