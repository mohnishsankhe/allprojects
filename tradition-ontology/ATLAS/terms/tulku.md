# sprul sku

`trm:tulku` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** སྤྲུལ་སྐུ
**Literal:** emanation body

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): Emanation body (nirmāṇakāya); in Tibetan usage also a recognized reincarnation of a master, a system in which many Nyingma treasure revealers and abbots are identified.
- [Karma Kagyu (Kamtsang Kagyu)](../lineages/karma-kagyu.md): A teacher recognized as the rebirth of a previous master; by the tradition's account the Karmapas were the first such line (Karma Pakshi recognized as Düsum Khyenpa's rebirth).
