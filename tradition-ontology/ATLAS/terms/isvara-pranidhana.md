# īśvara-praṇidhāna

`trm:isvara-pranidhana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ईश्वरप्रणिधान
**Literal:** devotion (setting down) toward the Lord

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Devotion to Īśvara: a special bhakti by which Īśvara, won over, favours the yogin (YBh 1.23); offering all actions to the supreme teacher or renouncing their fruit (YBh 2.1, RM 2.1); a means to samādhi (1.23, 2.45), part of kriyā-yoga (2.1) and a niyama (2.32).
