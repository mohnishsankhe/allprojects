# sphoṭa

`trm:sphota` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्फोट
**Literal:** burst (of meaning)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The grammarians' unitary word-entity distinct from the letters; rejected by Kumārila as superfluous, the letters themselves conveying meaning (ŚV sphoṭavāda); Upavarṣa already identified the word with the letters.
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): The meaning-bearing word itself, partless and without sequence, distinct from the sounds (dhvani/nāda) that manifest it; it 'bursts forth' as meaning in the intellect when the sounds have planted their seeds (VP 1.44; Mahābhāṣya on P 1.1.70). The later grammarians count eight kinds and hold the sentence-sphoṭa alone real.
