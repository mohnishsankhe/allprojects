## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Freedom from desire for what is not one's own (YBh 2.30); when established, all jewels present themselves (2.37).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): One of the common duties of all (MDh 10.63).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In Jainism abstention from taking what is not given (TS 7.15).
