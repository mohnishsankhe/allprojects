# anutpattika-dharma-kṣānti

`trm:anutpattika-dharma-ksanti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** acceptance (patience) of the non-arising of dharmas

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The bodhisattva's settled acceptance that dharmas are unborn and selfless, marking irreversibility; in the Daśabhūmika it is attained on the eighth ground; its merit surpasses gifts of jewel-filled worlds.
