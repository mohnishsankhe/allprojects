## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): One of the twelve houses counted from the ascendant, each governing a domain of life — body, family and speech, siblings, mother and home, children and intellect, enemies and disease, spouse, longevity and death, fortune and dharma, action, gains, loss and liberation (BJ 1.15–18; BPHS 11).
