# natthikavāda

`trm:natthikavada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** doctrine of 'there is not'

## Definitions by tradition
- [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md): (As reported) The view that there is no gift, no fruit of deeds, no this world or other world, no spontaneously born beings.
- [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md): The same view as held by Pāyāsi, answered by Kumāra Kassapa.
