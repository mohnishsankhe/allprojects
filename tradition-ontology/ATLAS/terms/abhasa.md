# ābhāsa

`trm:abhasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आभास
**Literal:** semblance, reflection

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The reflection of consciousness in the intellect, compared to the sky reflected in a pond; unreal, a product of ignorance; the jīva as agent (Rāma-hṛdaya, Rāma Gītā).
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): An appearance manifested by consciousness within itself as if external (ĪPK 1.5.1); the world is ābhāsa, real as manifestation; the individual too manifests (ābhāsana) as the first of his five acts (PH 11).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Semblance: the jīva as a mere appearance of consciousness in the intellect (Sureśvara); in BS 2.3.50 'reflection'.
