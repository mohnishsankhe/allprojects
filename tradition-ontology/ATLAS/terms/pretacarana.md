# pretācaraṇa

`trm:pretacarana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रेताचरण
**Literal:** going about like a wretch

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Living unwashed, unshaven and ungroomed like a poor madman (the 'preta' of the sūtra being a living man), so that caste and āśrama marks are cut off.
