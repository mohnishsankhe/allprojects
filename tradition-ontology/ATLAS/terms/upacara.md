# upacāra

`trm:upacara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उपचार
**Literal:** service, attendance

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): A service offered to the deity; the Goddess is 'rich in sixty-four services' (LSN 58); the Kalpasūtra lists them (PKS 4.5); the Bhāvanā Upaniṣad interiorizes them.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): Metaphorical use of words; all talk of self and dharmas is figurative, referring to transformations of consciousness.
