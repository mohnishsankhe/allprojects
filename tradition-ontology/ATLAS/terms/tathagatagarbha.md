# tathāgatagarbha

`trm:tathagatagarbha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तथागतगर्भ
**Literal:** embryo / womb / matrix of the Tathāgata

## Definitions by tradition
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): The buddha present in every being, covered by adventitious defilements — the dharmakāya not yet freed; empty of stains, not empty of qualities; what saṃsāra and nirvāṇa depend on.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): In the Laṅkāvatāra, taught to remove the fear of no-self and meaning emptiness, not a tīrthika self; identified with the ālayavijñāna.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): The sugata-essence (bde gshegs snying po): in the Guhyagarbha the source from which conceptual karma emanates the realms; for Mipham the union of luminosity and emptiness with qualities spontaneously present.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): In the Mahāyānasūtrālaṃkāra, the statement that all embodied beings 'have it as their embryo' because suchness, undifferentiated in all, is tathāgatahood when purified — held alongside the doctrine of differing lineages.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): bde gshegs snying po: buddha-nature, the cause of awakening present in all beings (Jewel Ornament ch.1); identified by Nāropa and the 3rd Karmapa with the luminous emptiness of mind, obscured by adventitious stains.
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): Buddha-nature as the ultimate itself: permanent, empty of adventitious stains, not empty of the buddha-qualities, present in all beings (Tibetan de bzhin gshegs pa'i snying po / bde gshegs snying po).
- [Rimé (ris med), the non-sectarian movement](../lineages/rime.md): Read by Kongtrul as the Great Madhyamaka's other-empty ground; by Mipham as the union of luminosity and emptiness.
