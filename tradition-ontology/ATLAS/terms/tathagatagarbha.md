# tathāgatagarbha

`trm:tathagatagarbha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तथागतगर्भ
**Literal:** embryo / womb / matrix of the Tathāgata

## Definitions by tradition
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): The buddha present in every being, covered by adventitious defilements — the dharmakāya not yet freed; empty of stains, not empty of qualities; what saṃsāra and nirvāṇa depend on.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): In the Laṅkāvatāra, taught to remove the fear of no-self and meaning emptiness, not a tīrthika self; identified with the ālayavijñāna.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): In the Mahāyānasūtrālaṃkāra, the statement that all embodied beings 'have it as their embryo' because suchness, undifferentiated in all, is tathāgatahood when purified — held alongside the doctrine of differing lineages.
