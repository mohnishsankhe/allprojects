# nāmāparādha

`trm:namaparadha` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नामापराध
**Literal:** offence against the name

## Definitions by tradition
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): An offence against the name; ten are listed (from the Padma Purāṇa), and they block the name's fruit of love.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): One of ten offences that obstruct the fruit of chanting the name, listed from the Padma Purāṇa by later Vaiṣṇava authors.
