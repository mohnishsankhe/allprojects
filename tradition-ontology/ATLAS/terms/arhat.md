# arhat

`trm:arhat` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अर्हत्
**Literal:** worthy one

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Arhat: the non-learner after the knowledge of destruction; six kinds, five of which can fall back (Sarvāstivāda).
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): The arhat does not fall back from arhatship.
- [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md): Per the Sarvāstivāda reports, the Mahāsāṃghika side accepted Mahādeva's five points: an arhat may be tempted by others, has non-knowing and doubt, is instructed by others; yet arhats do not fall back (Vasumitra).
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): In the Lotus and Śrīmālā the arhat's nirvāṇa is not final — he still has the ground of ignorance and is destined for buddhahood; the Vajracchedikā: an arhat who thought 'I am an arhat' would seize a self.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): One in whom the store-consciousness, as ālaya, has ceased and the afflicted mind is absent; a disciple-vehicle arhat still has the cognitive obscuration.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The embodied omniscient who has destroyed the four destructive karmas; the first of the five supreme beings (arihanta 'destroyer of enemies' in popular etymology).
