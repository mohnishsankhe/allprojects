## Definitions by tradition
- [Kāpālika (Somasiddhānta)](../lineages/kapalika.md): Ear-ornament (or necklace), rucaka, earrings, crest-jewel, ash and sacred thread, with skull and khaṭvāṅga as sub-insignia; knowing and wearing them is said to bring release (Yāmuna's report).
