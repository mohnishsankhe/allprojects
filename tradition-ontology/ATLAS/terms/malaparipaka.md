## Definitions by tradition
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): The ripening of āṇava mala, whose powers turn away until it is ready to be removed.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Malaparipākam, the ripening that accompanies the balance of deeds and precedes grace.
