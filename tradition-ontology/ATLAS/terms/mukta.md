# mukta

`trm:mukta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मुक्त
**Literal:** freed

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Souls released from saṃsāra who have reached Vaikuṇṭha and serve the Lord eternally.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: For the freed one acting for the sake of sacrifice, action dissolves entirely (4.23); the meditating sage free of desire, fear and anger is 'forever freed' (sadā mukta) (5.28).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): A soul freed of all karma (TS 2.10).
