# manu

`trm:manu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मनु
**Literal:** Manu

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Progenitor and law-giver presiding over a manvantara; the present is the seventh, Vaivasvata; the eighth will be Sāvarṇi (the Devī Māhātmya's Suratha).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: The Manus, who with the seven great seers, sharing the Lord's being (mad-bhāva), were born of mind, and from whom come the creatures of the world (10.6; the verse's grouping 'catvāro manavaḥ' is read as 'the four Manus' or as 'the four (of old) and the Manus').
