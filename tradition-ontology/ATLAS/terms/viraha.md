# viraha

`trm:viraha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विरह
**Literal:** separation

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Love in separation from the beloved Lord, which keeps the mind constantly on him (BhP 10.47.34).
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): Love in separation, which Vallabha holds to be the highest state of devotion (the gopīs' state).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Separation (vipralambha), exemplified in the Śikṣāṣṭaka 7 and in Caitanya's last years.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): Separation-longing voiced by the Āḻvārs as the love-sick girl, her mother and friend, and as the grieving parent (Kulaśekhara as Daśaratha); ended in Nammāḻvār's final union (TVM 10.10.11).
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Supreme separation (parama-viraha) is the last of love's eleven forms; supreme anguish on forgetting the Lord is Nārada's own definition of devotion (NBS 19, 82).
- `lin:jagannatha`: The mutual longing of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in separation, carried by the friend, which is most of the Gīta Govinda (GG 2–9).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The pain of separation from the Beloved, praised as 'the sultan' and as the sign of true devotion; the body without it is a cremation ground.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): The longing for the Man of the Heart who comes near and cannot be held.
- [Mīrābāī's devotion (the Mīrā song-tradition)](../lineages/mirabai.md): The anguish of separation from Giridhar, the wound only the wounded know.
- [Rādhāvallabha sampradāya](../lineages/radhavallabha.md): Not real in the eternal play, where the pair are never apart; felt only within union.
