**Literal:** left-hand conduct

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The left-hand conduct, contrasted with the right; Lakṣmīdhara assigns it to those not eligible for the Vedic way.
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The fifth mode: 'left-hand' worship with the Kaula substances, reserved for the vīra (summary only).
