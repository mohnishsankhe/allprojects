# vāta

`trm:vata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वात
**Literal:** wind (from √vā 'to move', Su Sū 21.5)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The doṣa of movement: dry, cold, light, subtle, mobile, clear and rough (Ca Sū 1.59); fivefold as prāṇa, udāna, samāna, vyāna and apāna; unprovoked it upholds the body's structure, impels all movement and guides the mind, and in the world upholds the earth and drives the seasons (Ca Sū 12.8); chief seat the colon (AHS Sū 12.1).
- [Siddha medicine (cittā maruttuvam)](../lineages/siddha-medicine.md): Tamil vaḷi or vātam: the wind humour, first of the three (Kuṟaḷ 941 names the three 'beginning with vaḷi'); built of air and ether; in Siddha pulse-reading the strongest beat.
