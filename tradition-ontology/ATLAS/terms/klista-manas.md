# kliṣṭa-manas

`trm:klista-manas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** क्लिष्टमनस्
**Literal:** afflicted mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The seventh consciousness, called 'mind' (manas), whose nature is thinking; it depends on and takes as object the store-consciousness, grasping it as a self, and is always accompanied by four afflictions — self-view, self-delusion, self-conceit and self-love; absent in the arhat, in the attainment of cessation and on the supramundane path.
