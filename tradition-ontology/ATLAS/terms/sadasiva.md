# sadāśiva

`trm:sadasiva` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सदाशिव
**Literal:** the ever-auspicious

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The five-faced form of Śiva (faces Īśāna, Tatpuruṣa, Aghora, Vāmadeva, Sadyojāta) from whom all tantras issue; the 28 Siddhānta tantras are the limbs of his mantra-body.
- [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md): Third tattva: will predominant; the inward 'nimeṣa' (ĪPK 3.1.3), 'I am this' with the 'I' prominent.
