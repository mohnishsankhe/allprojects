# tuṟavu

`trm:turavu` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tamil

## Definitions by tradition
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Renunciation (Kuṟaḷ ch. 35): cutting 'I' and 'mine'.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Renunciation: Paṭṭiṉattār ranks the renouncer far above the giver and the renouncer-in-mind higher still (Tiruvēkampamālai 1).
- [Tamil Jain tradition](../lineages/tamil-jain.md): Renunciation: in the Nālaṭiyār the wise one's giving up of attachment on seeing the body's impurity and the world's transience.
