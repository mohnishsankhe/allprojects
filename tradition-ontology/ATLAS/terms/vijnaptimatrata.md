# vijñapti-mātratā

`trm:vijnaptimatrata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** cognition-only-ness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): Both the thesis 'everything is cognition-only' and, in its final sense, the perfected nature itself: suchness, in which consciousness abides when it no longer apprehends an object (Triṃśikā 25, 28).
