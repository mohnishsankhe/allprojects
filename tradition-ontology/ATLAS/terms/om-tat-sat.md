# oṃ tat sat

`trm:om-tat-sat` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ओं तत् सत्
**Literal:** Oṃ, That, Being

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The threefold designation of Brahman by which the brāhmaṇas, Vedas and sacrifices were ordained; Oṃ begins the rites, 'tat' marks acts done without seeking fruit, 'sat' denotes reality, goodness and praiseworthy action (BhG 17.23–27).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: The threefold designation (nirdeśa) of Brahman, by which the brāhmaṇas, Vedas and sacrifices were ordained of old (17.23); the rites of sacrifice, giving and austerity of the expounders of Brahman begin with 'oṃ' (17.24); with 'tat' those seeking release perform them without aiming at fruit (17.25); 'sat' is used for reality, goodness and a praiseworthy act, for steadfastness in sacrifice, austerity and giving and for action for that purpose (17.26–27). (This edition's text of 17.23 omits 'oṃ'.)
