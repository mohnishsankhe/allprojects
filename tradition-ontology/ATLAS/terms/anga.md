# aṅga

`trm:anga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अङ्ग
**Literal:** limb, body

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The soul or devotee in relation to the liṅga: knowledge is the aṅga and the eternal knowable the liṅga, and the aṅgaliṅgī has the liṅga mounted on the aṅga and the aṅga present in the liṅga (SSM 12.42–44); the jīva is a part of Śiva through beginningless ignorance (5.34–36).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): 'Limb' or topic: a chapter of a Sant anthology gathering sākhīs on one theme (gurudev kau aṅg, birah kau aṅg …).
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): A subsidiary element of a principal rite (pradhāna), e.g. the prayājas of the new- and full-moon rites.
