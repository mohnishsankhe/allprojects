# dharmatā

`trm:dharmata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** धर्मता
**Literal:** the nature of dharmas, reality

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The true nature of things, which cannot be cognized by ordinary consciousness; the Buddha is to be seen from it; it abides whether or not buddhas appear.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): Suchness without the division of grasped and grasper, which is nirvāṇa, as distinct from dharmas — the appearance of duality and names, which is saṃsāra (Dharmadharmatāvibhāga).
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The nature of things, unarisen and unceasing, like nirvāṇa, where what is to be named and the domain of mind have ceased (MMK 18.7).
