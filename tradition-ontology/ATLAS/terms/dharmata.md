# dharmatā

`trm:dharmata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** धर्मता
**Literal:** the nature of dharmas, reality

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The true nature of things, which cannot be cognized by ordinary consciousness; the Buddha is to be seen from it; it abides whether or not buddhas appear.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): Suchness without the division of grasped and grasper, which is nirvāṇa, as distinct from dharmas — the appearance of duality and names, which is saṃsāra (Dharmadharmatāvibhāga).
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Dharma-nature (faxing), pure and ultimately unborn; 'birth' is said only from the side of those born (Tanluan, Daochuo).
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): Dharmatā 'not established as anything', one and indivisible; all the teachings flow toward it (Satyadvayāvatāra).
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): Recollected at waking and rested in, free of elaboration, before sleep (Caryāsaṃgrahapradīpa).
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): Reality (chos nyid); in the four visions the 'direct perception of dharmatā' and the 'exhaustion of phenomena in dharmatā'; the bardo of dharmatā after death.
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The nature of things, unarisen and unceasing, like nirvāṇa, where what is to be named and the domain of mind have ceased (MMK 18.7).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): chos nyid: the clear light of dharmatā without arising and ceasing, beyond speech and thought, is the path of Mahāmudrā (Tilopa).
