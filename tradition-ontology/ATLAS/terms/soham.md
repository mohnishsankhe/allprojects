# so'ham

`trm:soham` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सोऽहम्
**Literal:** 'I am He'

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): In the SSM the mantra of the supreme self (8.17–22) and the feeling with which the haṃsa-formed supreme self is contemplated in śivayoga-samādhi (12.21–24).
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): In Basava's vacana the stance the devotee asks to be spared ('do not make me say sōhaṃ'), in favour of dāsōhaṃ, 'I am your servant'.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): 'I am He': the haṃsa heard in reverse, the natural mantra of the breath (Yogaviṣaya 29).
