# lokāyata

`trm:lokayata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** लोकायत
**Literal:** extended in / directed to the world (of the people)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md): The school's name: the view that only this world, as far as the senses reach, exists; the doxographies call it the teaching most people follow.
- [Arthaśāstra (the science of statecraft and wealth)](../lineages/arthasastra.md): In the Arthaśāstra, one of the three branches of investigation (ānvīkṣikī), with Sāṃkhya and Yoga.

## Forms in other languages
- Tamil: ulōkāyatam  — exact
- Tibetan: rgyang 'phen pa  — partial — Tibetan name of the Lokāyata/Cārvāka school
- Pali: lokāyata  — partial — in the Pali canon a branch of brahmanical learning (disputation/nature-lore), not clearly the school
