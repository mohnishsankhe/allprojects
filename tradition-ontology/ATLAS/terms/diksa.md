# dīkṣā

`trm:diksa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दीक्षा
**Literal:** initiation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): That by which supreme knowledge is given (dī) and the bondage of fetters destroyed (kṣī) (SSM 15.7); in Vīraśaiva practice the liṅga-initiation, with bathing, the whispered five-syllable mantra and the giving of the liṅga into the disciple's hand (6.19–25); by it the duality of guru and disciple is removed (19.6–7).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The consecration that makes the sacrificer fit for the soma rite, with restraint, vigil and tapas; with tapas it is among the powers that uphold the earth (AVŚ 12.1.1) and was first undertaken by the seers (AVŚ 19.41.1).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The consecration of the sacrificer before a Soma rite, by which the priests 'make him an embryo again' (AB 1.3); he keeps vows and is reborn at the final bath.
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The consecration for sacrifice counted as the twice-born's third birth (MDh 2.169).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Initiation, threefold: śāktī by the entry of power, śāmbhavī by the placing of the foot, māntrī by mantra (PKS 1.32); Bālā is given first, with a name ending in '-ānandanātha' (PKS 1.39-41).
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Consecration of a tested brahmin with mantra-sanctified ash before the southern image, with the hearing of the mantra.
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Initiation by touch, sight or piercing (sparśa, dṛk, vedha), given only after mutual testing (Kulārṇava 14); there is no initiation higher than the Śākta (12.12).
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): Initiation in which a qualified guru gives the mantra to a qualified, tested disciple (ŚT 2.141–153).
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Initiation into Hari's worship and mantra by the ācārya (with the sipping of the Lord's foot-water and vows); those without it are called 'beasts among men'.
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The rite performed on the soul — not on caste or body — by which the guru, empowered by Śiva's mantras, joins the soul to Śiva and destroys its bonds; it is the cause of the other means (ritual, knowledge, conduct).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Initiation into Bhagavān's mantras and worship, in Śrīvaiṣṇava usage centred on the five sacraments (pañcasaṃskāra).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): Initiation, in many forms (TĀ 14-22); for one who knows the Heart, initiation needs no sesame and ghee (Parātrīśikā 25); Spanda calls identification with the meditated 'the initiation of nirvāṇa' (SK 2.7).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Initiation, so called because it gives divine knowledge and destroys sin; the guru gives the mantras (gopāla-mantra, kāma-gāyatrī), which are not published.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): Two initiations: the refuge mantra (nāma-nivedana) and the brahmasambandha.
