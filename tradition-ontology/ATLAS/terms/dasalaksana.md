# daśalakṣaṇa

`trm:dasalaksana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दशलक्षण
**Literal:** having ten marks

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The Bhāgavata's ten marks of a (great) Purāṇa, given in two different lists (BhP 2.10.1; 12.7.9-10); the tenth, āśraya/apāśraya, is the support for whose sake the other nine are told.
