# ādhāra

`trm:adhara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आधार
**Literal:** support, base

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): (1) The root centre (GŚ 10). (2) The sixteen supports of the body on which breath and attention are fixed; jālandhara 'binds the sixteen ādhāras' (HYP 3.73); the Haṭhatattvakaumudī devotes a chapter to them.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): A support-point of attention in the body (sixteen in the Yogacūḍāmaṇi); the base centre.
