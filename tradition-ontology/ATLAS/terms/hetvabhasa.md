# hetvābhāsa

`trm:hetvabhasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** हेत्वाभास
**Literal:** pseudo-reason; fallacy of the reason

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): A reason that only appears to be one; five in the sūtra: inconclusive, contradictory, prakaraṇasama, sādhyasama, kālātīta (NS 1.2.4).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Five in the Navya list: inconclusive, contradictory, counterbalanced, unestablished, sublated (Tarkasaṅgraha); defined generally in the Tattvacintāmaṇi's hetvābhāsa-sāmānya-nirukti.
