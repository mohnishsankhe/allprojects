# kālacakra

`trm:kalacakra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kālacakra tradition in India](../lineages/kalacakra.md): The 'wheel of time': name of the tantra and deity; time (kāla) is means/bliss, the wheel (cakra) wisdom/empty form; the outer, inner and other wheels.
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): The tantra, the deity and the primordial buddha whose teaching of the age of perfection and six-branch yoga is the heart of the Jonang; its 'emptiness endowed with all supreme aspects' names the other-empty ultimate.
