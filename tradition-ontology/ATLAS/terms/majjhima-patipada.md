# majjhimā paṭipadā

`trm:majjhima-patipada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** middle way

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The practice that avoids the two extremes of devotion to sensual pleasure and self-mortification (SN 56.11); also the teaching 'by the middle' that avoids 'all exists' and 'all does not exist' through dependent origination (SN 12.15).
