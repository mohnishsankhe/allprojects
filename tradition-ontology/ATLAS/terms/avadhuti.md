# avadhūtī

`trm:avadhuti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The central channel, 'free of the duality of grasped and grasper'; above the navel also called suṣumṇā (Sekoddeśa 50); into it the winds are drawn in the completion stage.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): dbu ma / a wa dhU tI, the central channel; entering it, wind and mind give non-conceptuality; the channel-knots released, 'all becomes the central channel'.

## Forms in other languages
- Tibetan: rtsa dbu ma / kun 'dar ma  — exact
- Tibetan: dbu ma དབུ་མ — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [suṣumnā](susumna.md) — The Sekoddeśa names the avadhūtī above the navel suṣumṇā; the Hindu haṭha suṣumṇā is the corresponding central channel in a different system.
