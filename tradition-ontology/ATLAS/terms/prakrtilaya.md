# prakṛtilaya

`trm:prakrtilaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रकृतिलय
**Literal:** absorption into prakṛti

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The result of dispassion (without discrimination) (SK 45): a dissolution into prakṛti or its evolutes, taken for liberation, from which one rises again like a diver (SS 3.54); the Kramadīpikā calls it 'bondage to prakṛti'.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Those whose mind, with its task unfinished, is dissolved in prakṛti, experiencing a quasi-kaivalya until it returns (YBh 1.19); Bhoja: those who rest content in I-am-ness samādhi (RM 1.17).
