# yogipratyakṣa

`trm:yogipratyaksa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** योगिप्रत्यक्ष
**Literal:** perception of yogins

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Perception of the self and other substances from a special conjunction of self and mind (VS 9.13–14); of yogins in concentration (yukta) and out of it (viyukta) (Praśastapāda).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The perception of one engaged in yoga arises from samādhi (NBh 1.1.3).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Yogaja, one of the three extraordinary contacts.
