# vyāna

`trm:vyana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** व्यान
**Literal:** diffused breath

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): One of the five vital winds that are the shared function of the three internal instruments (SK 29); the Kramadīpikā places it at the joints.
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): the vāyu seated in the heart and moving swiftly through the whole body, governing movement, flexion, extension, blinking — nearly all the body's actions (AHS Sū 12.6-7).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The diffused breath, moving through the channels (PrU 3.6); the right side of the self made of breath (TU 2.2).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The breath pervading the whole body (SS 3.7).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The diffused breath, the third of the breaths named in the Saṃhitās (TS 4.7; AVŚ 15.15–17).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The breath pervading the body.
