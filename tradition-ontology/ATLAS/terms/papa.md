# pāpa

`trm:papa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पाप
**Literal:** sin, evil

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Sin: Arjuna fears it would cling to them for killing kin (1.36, 1.39, 1.45); Kṛṣṇa says refusing the dharmic battle incurs it (2.33) while fighting with equanimity does not (2.38); those who cook only for themselves eat sin (agha, 3.13); Arjuna asks what drives a person to sin against his will (3.36).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: people of meritorious deeds in whom sin (pāpa) has come to an end, freed from the delusion of the pairs, worship the Lord firm in their vows (7.28).
- [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md): (As reported) Pūraṇa denies that the doer incurs it.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: whoever knows the Lord as unborn and beginningless, the great lord of the world, is undeluded among mortals and freed from all sins (sarva-pāpaiḥ) (10.3).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Inauspicious karma (TS 8.26); also the five sins — violence, falsehood, theft, unchastity, possessiveness.
