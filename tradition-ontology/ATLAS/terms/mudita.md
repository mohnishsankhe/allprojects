# muditā

`trm:mudita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मुदिता
**Literal:** gladness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Gladness cultivated toward the virtuous, one of the four attitudes that clarify the mind (1.33, YBh 1.33). Saṃyama on it yields its strength (3.23).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The third brahmavihāra, abandoning discontent (MN 62; DN 13).

## Forms in other languages
- Pali: muditā  — partial — one of the four brahmavihāras (immeasurables); in Buddhism radiated to all beings
- Sanskrit: muditā  — exact
