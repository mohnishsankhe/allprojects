# araṇāvihāra

`trm:aranavihara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** dwelling without conflict (in peace)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The samādhi of non-contention for which Subhūti is foremost; he does not think of himself as having attained it.
