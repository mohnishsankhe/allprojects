# kṣaṇikatva

`trm:ksanikatva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** momentariness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Everything existent lasts only a moment: destruction needs no cause, and an enduring thing could not be causally efficacious either successively or at once.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Momentariness: the conditioned is momentary, perishing as soon as it arises (the reason bodily manifestation is shape, not movement).
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): Destruction has no cause: what arises perishes of itself.
- [Pudgalavāda](../lineages/pudgalavada.md): Some formations are momentary, others endure for a time (Vasumitra).
