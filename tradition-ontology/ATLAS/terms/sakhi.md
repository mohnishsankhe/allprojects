## Definitions by tradition
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): 'Witness': the rhymed couplet (dohā) in which the Sants bear witness to what they have seen; arranged in aṅgas.
- `lin:jagannatha`: The heroine's woman friend and go-between, who carries the lovers' longing between Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa and counsels Rādhā (GG).
