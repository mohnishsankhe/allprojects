# ādeś

**Language:** Hindi
**Native script:** आदेश
**Literal:** command

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The Nāth greeting and response ('ādeś, ādeś'), acknowledging the command/presence of the Lord in the other.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Instruction, teaching formula: 'now the instruction: not so, not so' (BAU 2.3.6); the teaching about brahman in lightning and in the mind (Kena 4.4-5); the teaching of 'I' and of the self (ChU 7.25).
