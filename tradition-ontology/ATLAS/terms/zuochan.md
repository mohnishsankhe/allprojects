# zuochan (seated meditation, zazen)

`trm:zuochan` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 坐禪
**Literal:** sitting meditation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): Redefined in the Platform Sūtra: not attaching to mind or purity nor mere immobility; 'sitting' is thoughts not arising toward objects, 'meditation' is seeing the unmoving self-nature.
- [Sōtō Zen](../lineages/soto.md): Just sitting as the enactment of buddhahood; 'not learning meditation but the Dharma gate of ease and joy' (Dōgen).
- [Rinzai Zen](../lineages/rinzai.md): The seat of kōan introspection and of samādhi-power (jōriki).
