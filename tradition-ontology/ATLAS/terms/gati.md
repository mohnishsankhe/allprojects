# gati

**Language:** Pali
**Native script:** गति
**Literal:** destination

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The five destinations: hell, animal realm, ghost realm, humans, gods (MN 12).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): One of the four states of embodied existence — hellish, animal, human, divine — determined by karma (TS 2.6, 8.11); also motion.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Destiny: five — hell-beings, ghosts, animals, humans, gods; the intermediate existence is not a destiny.
