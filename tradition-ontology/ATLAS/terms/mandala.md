# maṇḍala

`trm:mandala` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मण्डल
**Literal:** circle; book

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): One of the ten books of the Ṛgveda.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): The maṇḍala (dkyil 'khor) of deities which the Guhyagarbha declares to be the primordial nature of appearances; also the maṇḍala offering of the preliminaries.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The sacred circle — palace and assembly of deities (e.g. the Vajradhātu, Womb, Guhyasamāja and Heruka maṇḍalas) into which the disciple is introduced and as which the world and body are seen; also the offering of the universe.
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The coloured diagram on which the deity is worshipped, especially at initiation.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Diagram for worship and initiation, richly described in the Pauṣkara.
