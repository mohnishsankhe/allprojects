# dharmakāya

`trm:dharmakaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** धर्मकाय
**Literal:** the dharma-body

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The Buddha as the dharma / suchness, not his physical form: 'the guides have the dharma for body'; not sick, not fed by food; later the first of the three bodies.
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): The dharmakāya is the tathāgatagarbha freed from defilements; it is permanent, blissful, self and pure and pervades all beings.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The buddhas' body as the purified dharmadhātu; in the three-body scheme the natural body, basis of the other two; at the Triṃśikā's close, the transformed basis 'called the dharma-body of the great sage'.
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): In Tanluan two dharma-bodies: of dharma-nature and of expedient means, different but inseparable; the Rebirth Treatise's 'uncreated dharma-body of true wisdom'.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): The formless dharma-body as suchness from which Dharmākara appeared; Amida is the means to know jinen.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): The dharma-body, personified as Samantabhadra; in Dzogchen the ground recognized, empty essence.
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): The dharma body: 'liberation in an instant is called dharmakāya' (Queen Dohā v8); appearances and sounds are primordially dharmakāya (Tōh 2292 no. 30).
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The truth body of the Buddhas, born from the collection of wisdom (RĀ 3.12–13).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): chos sku: the emptiness of mind itself, unborn; 'the essence of thought is dharmakāya'; recognized at death as the clear light by those of highest faculties.
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): The ultimate itself, permanent and the true self (perfection of self), not newly produced at awakening.
