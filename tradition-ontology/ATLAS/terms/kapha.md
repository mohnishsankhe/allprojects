# kapha

`trm:kapha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कफ
**Literal:** phlegm; also śleṣman 'that which embraces' (√śliṣ, Su Sū 21.5)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The doṣa of cohesion and stability: heavy, cold, soft, unctuous, sweet, stable and slimy (Ca Sū 1.61); it upholds the body by the action of water — binding joints, lubrication, healing, strength and firmness (Su Sū 15.4); fivefold as avalambaka, kledaka, bodhaka, tarpaka and śleṣaka (AHS Sū 12.15-18).
- [Siddha medicine (cittā maruttuvam)](../lineages/siddha-medicine.md): Tamil aiyam or kapam: the water-earth humour, third of the three.
