## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The fruition of karma: birth, life-span and experience (2.13), bearing joy or anguish (2.14).
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The taste a substance takes after digestion, threefold: sweet, sour, pungent (AHS Sū 1.17).
