# vipāka

`trm:vipaka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विपाक
**Literal:** ripening, fruition

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The fruition of karma: birth, life-span and experience (2.13), bearing joy or anguish (2.14).
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The taste a substance takes after digestion, threefold: sweet, sour, pungent (AHS Sū 1.17).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The fruit of kamma, experienced in this life, the next or later (AN 6.63); the same deed ripens heavily or lightly depending on the doer's development (AN 3.100).
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The store-consciousness as the ripened result of past action; a new maturation arises when the previous one is exhausted.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Resultant consciousness and factors produced by past kamma; kammically indeterminate.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Ripening: a neutral dharma counted as a being, arising from good or bad action, different in kind from its cause.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): The result arises from the specific transformation of the series; ripening does not itself produce further ripening.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In Jain usage the experiencing of karma's fruit according to its nature, after which it is shed (TS 8.21–23).
