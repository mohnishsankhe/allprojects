# liṅgāṅga-sāmarasya

`trm:linganga-samarasya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** लिङ्गाङ्गसामरस्य
**Literal:** equal flavour of liṅga and aṅga

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The goal of the path: the soul (aṅga) and Śiva (liṅga) in a union of equal flavour, 'like water poured into water' in common exposition; in the SSM, contemplation of the self dissolved in the sky of consciousness by the nature of sāmarasya (14.42–44).
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The union in equal essence of the liṅga (Śiva) and the aṅga (soul), the goal of Śakti-viśiṣṭādvaita (Śrīkarabhāṣya).
