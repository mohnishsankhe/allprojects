# madhyamā pratipad

**Literal:** the middle path

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The path between the extremes of existence and non-existence, eternalism and annihilationism; identified with emptiness and dependent origination (MMK 24.18).
