# prājña (Māṇḍūkya)

`trm:prajna-mandukya` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): In this text: the third quarter of the self; its station is deep sleep; it is become one, a mass of cognition, made of bliss, an enjoyer of bliss, with thought as its mouth; it is the measure m. (MU 6 calls "this one" lord of all, knower of all, inner controller and source of all; the verse does not name its referent, which commentators take to be prājña.)
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): In the Kārikā: the third, a mass of cognition (ghanaprajña); seated in the space of the heart; enjoyer of bliss; bound by cause alone; joined with seed-sleep; knows nothing of self, others, truth or untruth; equated with m.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The 'intelligent one', the third quarter of the self, whose field is deep sleep, a mass of cognition, made of bliss (MāU 5); the intelligent self that embraces the sleeper (BAU 4.3.21).
