# abhiṣeka

`trm:abhiseka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अभिषेक
**Literal:** empowerment; consecration (Tib. dbang)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): The master's empowerment, required for tantric practice after pleasing the teacher; the secret and wisdom empowerments are not to be taken by celibates.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): In the Lamdre, empowerment of cause (ripening), path (the four empowerments meditated in four sessions) and result (the four result-empowerments of omniscience).
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The necessary door to the mantra path, received from a qualified master with the vows and pledges.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Consecration (empowerment) by a vajra master that authorizes and ripens the disciple: in the highest tantras four — vase, secret, wisdom-gnosis and the fourth; in the Kālacakra, seven 'child' consecrations and the higher ones, 'three conventional, the fourth ultimate'.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): Empowerment (dbang): the vase, secret, wisdom and word empowerments (also received symbolically in guru yoga); in Dzogchen, the empowerment of awareness's expressive power.
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Consecration conferring the office of teacher (ācāryābhiṣeka) or of power-seeker (sādhakābhiṣeka).
- [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md): Bathing of the installed image; in Kerala, kalaśa-abhiṣeka rites renew the deity's power.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): dbang, empowerment: the Cakrasaṃvara four empowerments of the sixty-two-deity maṇḍala and those of Vajravārāhī's fifteen goddesses ripen the disciple for the six dharmas (Karṇatantravajrapada).
