# sāttvika-bhāva

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The eight involuntary bodily signs of ecstasy: stupor, sweating, horripilation, faltering voice, trembling, change of colour, tears, fainting.
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): The eight involuntary states arising from sattva (concentrated mind): paralysis, sweat, horripilation, change of voice, trembling, change of colour, tears, fainting (NŚ 6.22).
