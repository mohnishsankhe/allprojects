# sāṃsiddhika (prākṛtika, vaikṛta)

`trm:samsiddhika` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सांसिद्धिक
**Literal:** innate (natural, acquired)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The three origins of the dispositions (SK 43): innate, as in Kapila at the first creation; natural, as in Brahmā's sons at sixteen or in Āsuri through contact with the supreme sage; acquired, as in ordinary people, with a teacher as occasioning cause.
