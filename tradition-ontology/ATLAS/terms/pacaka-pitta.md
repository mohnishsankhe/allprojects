# pācaka pitta

`trm:pacaka-pitta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the digesting (pitta)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The pitta between the stomach and the intestine, which digests the four kinds of food and separates essence and waste and supports the other pittas; called 'fire' (Su Sū 21.10; AHS Sū 12.10-12).
