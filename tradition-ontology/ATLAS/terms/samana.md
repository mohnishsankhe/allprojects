# samāna

`trm:samana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** समान
**Literal:** equalizing breath

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): One of the five vital winds that are the shared function of the three internal instruments (SK 29); the Kramadīpikā places it at the heart.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The vital air that distributes evenly, operating up to the navel; its mastery gives radiance (3.40, YBh 3.39–40).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The equalizing breath in the middle of the body, distributing food (PrU 3.5).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The breath at the navel, distributing food.
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The equalizing breath, named with the other breaths in later Vedic lists (low confidence for particular Saṃhitā loci).
