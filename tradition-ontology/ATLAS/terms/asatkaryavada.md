# asatkāryavāda

`trm:asatkaryavada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** असत्कार्यवाद
**Literal:** doctrine that the effect is non-existent (before production)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The rival (Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika) view that the effect is new, rejected by Sāṃkhya: the non-existent cannot be produced, like a man's horn (SK 9; SS 1.114).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Before production the effect is non-existent (a prior absence of it is real) (NS 4.1.48–50; effect as counterpositive of prior absence, Tarkasaṅgraha).
