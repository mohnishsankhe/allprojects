## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Both the fifteen-syllable mantra of Lalitā (with its sixteen-syllable form) and the tradition of her worship; the Goddess is herself 'Śrīvidyā, the sixteen-syllable vidyā, three-peaked' (LSN 118).
