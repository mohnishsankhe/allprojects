# upādānakkhandha

`trm:upadanakkhandha` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** aggregate of clinging

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The five: form, feeling, perception, formations, consciousness; the monk knows each with its arising and passing away (MN 10:38).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The aggregates as affected by the effluents and subject to clinging (SN 22.48); in brief, they are suffering (SN 56.11) and identity (sakkāya) (MN 44).
