# trairūpya

`trm:trairupya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** त्रैरूप्य
**Literal:** the three characteristics (of a valid reason)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): (1) The reason is certainly present in the subject; (2) present only in similar instances; (3) certainly absent from dissimilar instances.

## Forms in other languages
- Tibetan: tshul gsum  — exact
- Chinese: 因三相 yin sanxiang  — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [avayava](avayava.md) — Nyāya's five-membered proof and the Buddhist triple-marked reason both formalize inference; the Buddhists reduce the members and put the force in the reason's marks.
