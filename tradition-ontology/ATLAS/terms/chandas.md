# chandas

`trm:chandas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** छन्दस्
**Literal:** metre

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Vedic metre; the metres came from the Puruṣa sacrifice (RV 10.90.9).
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): Metre, the 'feet' of the Veda (PŚ 41); so called because the gods covered (chad) themselves with the metres against death (ChU 1.4.2). In Pāṇini, also the Vedic language as opposed to ordinary speech.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: The metres (or the hymns in them): the field and its knower have been sung in them (13.5); they are the leaves of the aśvattha (15.1).
