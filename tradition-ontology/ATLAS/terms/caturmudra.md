**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The four seals: action seal, dharma seal, great seal and pledge seal, which Advayavajra orders as stages leading from the action seal to the pledge seal.
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): Saraha criticizes those who 'set out the four seals by distinguishing the moments' and take the experience for the innate (King Dohā v27).
