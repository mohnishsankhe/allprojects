# prāṇāgnihotra

`trm:pranagnihotra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्राणाग्निहोत्र
**Literal:** fire-offering into the breaths

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The offering of the first morsels of a meal to the five breaths (prāṇa, vyāna, apāna, samāna, udāna) with svāhā, so that eating becomes an Agnihotra offered in all worlds and selves (ChU 5.19–24; Mahānārāyaṇa Up. = TA 10.69–70; Prāṇāgnihotra Up.).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The meal-rite of the twice-born incorporating the breath-oblations, prescribed in the Dharmasūtras' rules on eating.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Offering food to the five breaths as an inner agnihotra.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The fire-offering into the breaths: in ChU 5.19-24 the first morsels of food are offered to prāṇa, vyāna, apāna, samāna and udāna as an agnihotra into the vaiśvānara self; the name is from later texts (e.g., the Prāṇāgnihotra Upaniṣad).
