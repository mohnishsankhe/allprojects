# iddhi

`trm:iddhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** psychic power, success

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The many kinds of power — becoming many, passing through walls, walking on water, flying, touching sun and moon (DN 2); the first direct knowledge; its display is disparaged as a 'wonder' (DN 11).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Supernormal power, tenfold (by resolve, transformation, mind-made, etc.); the worldling's power is fragile and an impediment to insight.

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: ṛddhi  — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [siddhi](siddhi.md) — the Yoga and haṭha texts' siddhis overlap with the iddhis; the suttas treat display of them as unfit
