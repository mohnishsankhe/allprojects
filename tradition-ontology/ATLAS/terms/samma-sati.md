## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): DN 22:21: the monk abides contemplating body in body, feelings in feelings, mind in mind, dhammas in dhammas, ardent, clearly comprehending and mindful, having put away covetousness and displeasure for the world (that is, the four establishments of mindfulness).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The four establishments of mindfulness (SN 45.8). One of the thirty-seven qualities conducive to awakening (DN 16.3.50; MN 149): a member of the noble eightfold path.
