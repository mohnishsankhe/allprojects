# visuddhi

`trm:visuddhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Native script:** विशुद्धि
**Literal:** purification

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The seven purifications of virtue, mind, view, overcoming doubt, knowledge of path and not-path, knowledge of the way, and knowledge and vision, each like a chariot in a relay (MN 24).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Purity; nibbāna; the seven purifications that make up the path.
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The absolute removal of each impurity: of ignorance, demerit, what causes attachment, falling away, and the paśu-state.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The throat centre.
