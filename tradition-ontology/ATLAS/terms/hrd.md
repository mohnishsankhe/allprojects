## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The heart as the place of inner seeing: the seers searched in the heart (RV 10.129.4); the light is set in the heart (6.9.6); the wise see with heart and mind (10.177.1); the mind is heart-seated (VS 34.6).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: The heart: the knowable is seated in the heart of all (13.18); the Lord is seated in the heart of all (15.15).
