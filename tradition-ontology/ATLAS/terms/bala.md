## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): innate, seasonal/age-born and acquired (Ca Sū 11.36); identified with ojas (Su Sū 15.19).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Bālā Tripurasundarī, the Goddess as a young girl, whose three-syllable vidyā is imparted first at initiation (PKS 1.39).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The five powers — faith, energy, mindfulness, concentration, wisdom — the faculties become unshakeable; one of the seven groups of the thirty-seven qualities.
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Devotion to the teacher, clarity of mind, conquest of opposites, merit and vigilance.
