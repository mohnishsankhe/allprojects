## Definitions by tradition
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The perfected body of the Nāth siddha — deathless, unageing, not cut by weapons — as distinct from the Gauḍīya inner devotional body of the same name.
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The inner, perfected spiritual body/identity in which the practitioner serves in Vraja, alongside service in the practitioner's body (sādhaka-rūpa).
