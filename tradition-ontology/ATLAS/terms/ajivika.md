# ājīvika

`trm:ajivika` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आजीविक
**Literal:** one who lives (by a rule of) livelihood

## Definitions by tradition
- [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md): A member of the naked ascetic order of Gosāla and his predecessors; the name appears in the Pali and Jain canons, the Arthaśāstra, Aśoka's edicts and inscriptions, and Tamil works.
