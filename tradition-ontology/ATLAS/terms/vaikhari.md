**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वैखरी
**Literal:** the articulated / 'rough' (speech)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): Articulated, audible speech, produced when breath strikes the places of articulation, whose letters are grasped by the hearer (Vṛtti on VP 1.142); for Nāgeśa, speech in the throat and mouth.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Spoken speech coming forth through the palate.
