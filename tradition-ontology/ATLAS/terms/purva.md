# pūrva

`trm:purva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पूर्व
**Literal:** former

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): One of the fourteen 'former' texts, said to have been taught before the Aṅgas and contained in the Dṛṣṭivāda; known in full last by the śrutakevalin Bhadrabāhu and then gradually lost.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Portions of the second and fifth Pūrvas survive as the Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama and Kaṣāyapāhuḍa.
