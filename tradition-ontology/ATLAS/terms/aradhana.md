# ārādhanā

`trm:aradhana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आराधना
**Literal:** propitiation; accomplishment

## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): 'Accomplishment': the perfecting of right faith, knowledge, conduct and austerity, especially as brought to completion at the end of life under a guide.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): One who has reconciled with all at Paryuṣaṇa is an 'ārādhaka' (fulfiller) of the discipline.
