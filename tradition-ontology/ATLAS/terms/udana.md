# udāna

`trm:udana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उदान
**Literal:** up-breath

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): One of the five vital winds that are the shared function of the three internal instruments (SK 29); the Kramadīpikā places it at the throat.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The vital air that leads upward, operating up to the head; its mastery gives non-adherence to water, mud and thorns and departure at death at will (3.39, YBh 3.39).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The up-breath, which through one channel leads upward at death — to a world of merit, of evil or of humans (PrU 3.7); identified with heat (PrU 3.9).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The breath in the throat.
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The up-breath, named with the other breaths in some Saṃhitā and Brāhmaṇa lists (low confidence for particular Saṃhitā loci).
