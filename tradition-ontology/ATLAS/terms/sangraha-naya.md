**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** संग्रहनय
**Literal:** the collective standpoint

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The standpoint that grasps the general, uniting all things under a common character such as 'existent' (TS 1.33); taken absolutely it becomes a monism.
