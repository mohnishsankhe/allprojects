# dhutaguṇa

`trm:dhutaguna` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dharmaguptaka](../lineages/dharmaguptaka.md): Ascetic practices (twelve or thirteen in the lists): wearing rag-robes, three robes only, alms-round, one meal, living in the forest, at the root of a tree, in the open, in a charnel ground, sitting without lying down, and others.
