# āyurveda

`trm:ayurveda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आयुर्वेद
**Literal:** the knowledge (veda) of life (āyus)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The science in which the wholesome and unwholesome, happy and unhappy life, what is good and bad for life, and its measure are taught (Ca Sū 1.41); so called because in it life is found or by it one attains life (Su Sū 1.15); it makes life known by its own marks (Ca Sū 30.23). It is beginningless and eternal (Ca Sū 30.27), an upāṅga of the Atharvaveda (Su Sū 1.6). Synonyms: śākhā, vidyā, sūtra, jñāna, śāstra, lakṣaṇa, tantra (Ca Sū 30.31).
