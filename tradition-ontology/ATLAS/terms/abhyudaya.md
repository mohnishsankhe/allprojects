# abhyudaya

`trm:abhyudaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अभ्युदय
**Literal:** higher status, elevation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Happiness in good rebirths, the fruit of the dharma of higher status practised first (RĀ 1.3–4).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): The first result of dharma (VS 1.1.2); sought through visible means where the visible result is absent, and through pure food with restraint (VS 6.1.8, 6.2.1, 6.2.9 C).
