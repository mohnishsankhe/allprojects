## Definitions by tradition
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): 'Non-meditation', the fourth yoga of Mahāmudrā — there is nothing to meditate on and no meditator; realization is complete; its greater degree is the dharmakāya; 'by familiarity with non-meditation unsurpassed awakening is attained' (Gaṅgā Mahāmudrā).
