# bhāva

`trm:bhava` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भाव
**Literal:** disposition, state of being

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The eight dispositions of buddhi — virtue, knowledge, dispassion, power and their opposites (SK 23) — innate, natural or acquired (SK 43), which perfume the subtle body (SK 40) and by seven of which prakṛti binds herself (SK 63).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Sensual, form and formless becoming, conditioned by clinging and conditioning birth (SN 12.2); 'the cessation of becoming is nibbāna' (SN 12.68).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): A name of Rudra, paired with Śarva (AVŚ 11.2).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: State, being: understanding, knowledge, forbearance, pleasure and pain, fear and fearlessness and the rest are the various states (bhāva) of beings arising from the Lord alone (10.4–5); Arjuna asks in which bhāvas the Lord is to be contemplated (10.17); the wise worship him 'endowed with bhāva' (10.8).
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): A real existent or existence; with its opposite (abhāva) one of the two extremes: whoever sees existence and non-existence does not see the truth (MMK 15.6), and nirvāṇa is neither (25.4–10).
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The practitioner's disposition that governs the mode of worship: paśu, vīra or divya; the Kālīvilāsa assigns them to the ages and allows only paśu in the Kali age (4.2-3; 6.10-16).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Emotion: the stage after attachment in Rūpa's sequence, pure sattva that softens the heart; in the Ujjvalanīlamaṇi also the name of mahābhāva's threshold.
