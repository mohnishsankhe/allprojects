# sattvadhātu

`trm:sattvadhatu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the realm (element) of beings

## Definitions by tradition
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): Neither increases nor decreases; identical with the tathāgatagarbha and the dharmakāya; the element when impure is called a being.
