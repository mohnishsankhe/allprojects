# caturvyūha

`trm:caturvyuha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** चतुर्व्यूह
**Literal:** fourfold arrangement

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The fourfold scheme of the Yoga śāstra modelled on medicine — saṃsāra, its cause, liberation, the means (YBh 2.15).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The four emanations (vyūha) of the Lord: Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna, Aniruddha.
