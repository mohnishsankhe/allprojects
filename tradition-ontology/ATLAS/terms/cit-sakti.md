# citśakti

`trm:cit-sakti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** चिच्छक्ति
**Literal:** power of consciousness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Consciousness-power: the Goddess as the power of the sentient, who is also the power of the insentient (LSN 90).
- [Śiva-viśiṣṭādvaita (Śaiva Vedānta of Śrīkaṇṭha)](../lineages/siva-visistadvaita.md): Śiva's power of consciousness, inseparable from him, identified with Umā and with the supreme ether; the world subsists in it subtly.
