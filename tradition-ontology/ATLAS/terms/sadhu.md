# sādhu (Jain mendicant)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): A fully initiated Jain monk who has taken the five great vows for life; the fifth rank saluted in the Namaskāra ('all the sādhus in the world').
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): A Jain monk (sādhvī, nun), the fifth of the supreme beings honoured in the Namaskāra mantra.
