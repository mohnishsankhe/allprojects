# kāmya-karma

`trm:kamya-karma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** काम्य-कर्म
**Literal:** desire-prompted act

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Acts enjoined for one who desires a particular fruit (e.g. heaven, cattle); the seeker of release abstains from them because they produce further bodies.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Desire-prompted actions, whose laying aside the seers call saṃnyāsa (18.2).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Desire-prompted rites for particular results (heaven etc.), to be avoided by the seeker.
