# adhiṣṭhāna

`trm:adhisthana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अधिष्ठान
**Literal:** control, presiding

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The need for a controller, a proof of puruṣa (SK 17); in the Sāṃkhya Sūtra, governance by mere proximity, like a magnet (SS 1.96).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Substratum: the real on which something unreal is superimposed (the rope for the snake; Brahman for the world).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): byin rlabs, blessing: the transforming influence that flows from the lineage through the guru to the devoted disciple, regarded as the decisive condition for realization of Mahāmudrā.

## Forms in other languages
- Tibetan: byin rlabs བྱིན་རླབས — exact
