# dgag bya

`trm:gakja` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** དགག་བྱ
**Literal:** object of negation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): What Madhyamaka reasoning refutes: inherent existence (existence from the object's own side, not posited by conceptual designation), which must be identified precisely before its absence can be ascertained.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): For Gorampa the object of negation at the ultimate level is not limited to a qualified 'inherent' existence: all four extremes, including existence as such, are negated.
