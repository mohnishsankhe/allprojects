## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The first and lowest of the four yogas: placing the mātṛkā letters on the body and repeating a mantra; fit for the mild (mṛdu) practitioner, who succeeds in twelve years (DYŚ 11–13; SS 5.11).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The first of the four yogas; repetition, or the natural haṃsa reversed.
