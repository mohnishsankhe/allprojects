# mithyātva

`trm:mithyatva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मिथ्यात्व
**Literal:** falsity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The Advaita claim that the world is false; Dvaita refutes the proposed definitions and the inferences from perceivability, insentience and limitedness.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Wrong view — the first cause of bondage (TS 8.1); five kinds in the Digambara list (one-sided, contrary, indiscriminate reverence, doubtful, ignorant).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Wrong faith; that taught by others falls into the four vāda classes, or five kinds: one-sided, perverse, doubting, vainayika, ajñānika.
