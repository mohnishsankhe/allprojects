# asaṅkhata

`trm:asankhata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** unconditioned

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): What is not made by conditions: no arising, no passing, no alteration while standing is discerned (AN 3.47); the destruction of lust, hatred and delusion (SN 43.12).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Not produced by conditions: in the Theravāda only nibbāna is unconditioned; the Kathāvatthu rejects the views that space, dependent origination or the truths are unconditioned.
