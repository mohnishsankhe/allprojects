# strī-nirvāṇa (strī-mukti)

`trm:strinirvana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्त्रीनिर्वाण
**Literal:** women's nirvāṇa

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Liberation of women in a female body — affirmed: the canon speaks of women siddhas and a woman Tīrthaṅkara.
- [Yāpanīya](../lineages/yapaniya.md): Affirmed and argued: the three jewels are not deficient in women.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Denied: a woman cannot give up clothing and lacks the bodily constitution for the highest meditation; she may be reborn as a man and then be liberated.
