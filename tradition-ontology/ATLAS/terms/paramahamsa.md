## Definitions by tradition
- [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md): Fourth kind: no topknot or thread; knowledge as staff; beyond dharma and adharma; attains the world of truth or liberation.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The highest class of renunciant, established in knowledge of the self, beyond ritual marks; the model of the vidvat-sannyāsin.
