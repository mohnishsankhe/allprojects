# amaratva

`trm:amaratva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अमरत्व
**Literal:** immortality

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Immortality, a synonym of samādhi (HYP 4.3), and the conquest of death sought through retaining bindu and nectar.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Deathlessness: a synonym of the highest state (HYP 4.3); in the Kaula texts attained by devotion to guru and yoginīs and by the nectar meditations (KJN 5.33).
