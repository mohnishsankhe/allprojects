# prāyaścitta

`trm:prayascitta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रायश्चित्त
**Literal:** expiation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Rites and penances removing the stain of sin — needed by one who omits the enjoined, does the forbidden or is attached to objects (MDh 11.44); by confession, remorse, austerity, recitation and gifts (MDh 11.227).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The rite that removes sin; the Purāṇas hold the name or remembrance of the Lord to be the best expiation.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Nine (Śvetāmbara ten) forms of correcting a fault, from confession to re-initiation (TS 9.22).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Expiatory rite for faults in ritual or conduct.
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Rites enjoined on the occasion of a fault or failure in performance; treated in Mīmāṃsā as occasional acts (e.g. MS 6.4).
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Rites that make good lapses in ritual and observance.
- [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md): Expiation for deliberate offences after surrender, which the Vaṭakalai require (or a renewed surrender) and the Teṅkalai do not regard as needed for salvation.
