# asmimāna

`trm:asmimana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** the conceit 'I am'

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The residual conceit and desire 'I am' in one who no longer takes any aggregate as self, like the smell of soap in a washed cloth (SN 22.89); abandoned by the perception of impermanence (MN 62; Ud 4.1).
