# nāstika

`trm:nastika` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नास्तिक
**Literal:** one who says 'it is not'

## Definitions by tradition
- [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md): (Opponents' designation) The Cārvāka as 'crest-jewel of the nāstikas': one who denies the other world, the fruit of deeds and the Veda.
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): One who reviles the Veda and relies on reasoning against it, to be expelled by the good (MDh 2.11).
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The nihilist who denies action, its fruit and the other world; Candrakīrti argues the Mādhyamika is not a nāstika, since he teaches dependent origination and says only that things lack own-nature (PsP 17.30; 18.7).
