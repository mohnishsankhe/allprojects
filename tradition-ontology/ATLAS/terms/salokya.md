# sālokya

`trm:salokya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सालोक्य
**Literal:** sharing the same world

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Form of liberation: dwelling in the Lord's world.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Liberation as residence in the Lord's world; one of the five that pure devotees refuse without service.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Liberation as residence in the Lord's world; one of five liberations devotees do not accept without service.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Residence in the Lord's world — first of the four kinds of liberation.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Living in Śiva's world; fruit of caryā.
- [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md): Dwelling in the Lord's world, the first of the four liberations, perishable with the worlds.
