# dāsōha

`trm:dasoha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Kannada
**Native script:** ದಾಸೋಹ
**Literal:** 'I am (your) servant' (dāsa + ahaṃ)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): (1) The devotee's self-understanding as servant of Śiva and his devotees, which Basava asks for in place of 'I am He'; (2) the giving of the fruits of one's work (kāyaka) to the jaṅgamas and the community, and in later usage free communal feeding.
