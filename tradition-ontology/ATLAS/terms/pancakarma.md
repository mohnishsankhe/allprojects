# pañcakarma

`trm:pancakarma` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पञ्चकर्म
**Literal:** the five acts

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The five purificatory therapies — emesis, purgation, the two kinds of enema (decoction and oil), and nasal evacuation (bloodletting is counted instead by the surgeons) — preceded by oleation and sudation; the best medicines for the bodily doṣas (AHS Sū 1.25; Ca Sū 2, Si).
