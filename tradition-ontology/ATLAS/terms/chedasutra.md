# chedasūtra

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** छेदसूत्र
**Literal:** text on cutting (of seniority)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): A canonical text of monastic law, fixing the atonements for transgressions; named from 'cheda' (cutting of seniority), one of the atonements. Six for Mūrtipūjakas, four for Sthānakavāsīs; traditionally studied only by mature monks.
