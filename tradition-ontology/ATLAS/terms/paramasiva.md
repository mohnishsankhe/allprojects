## Definitions by tradition
- [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md): The one reality, consciousness that is both light and reflexive awareness, free, performing the five acts, transcending and containing the thirty-six tattvas.
