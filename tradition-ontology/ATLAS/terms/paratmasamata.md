# parātmasamatā

`trm:paratmasamata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** equality of self and others

## Definitions by tradition
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): The practice, cultivated first, of regarding all beings as equal to oneself in wanting happiness and not wanting suffering, protecting them as oneself (BCA 8.90–96; ŚSK 1).
