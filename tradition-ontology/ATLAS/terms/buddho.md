## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The Thai forest parikamma word repeated with the breath ('bud-' in, '-dho' out) or alone to steady the mind; also a recollection of the Buddha.
- [Thai Forest tradition (Kammaṭṭhāna forest tradition)](../lineages/thai-forest.md): Preparatory repetition that leads the citta to converge; Ajahn Mun's and Ajahn Lee's basic instruction.
