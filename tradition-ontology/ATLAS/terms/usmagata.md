## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): First aid to penetration: the bodhisattva in concentration no longer sees objects apart from mental speech.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The first root of penetration, with the four truths in sixteen aspects as object — the sign that the fire of the noble path is near.
