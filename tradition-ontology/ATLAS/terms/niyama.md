# niyama

`trm:niyama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नियम
**Literal:** observance

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The five observances: purity, contentment, austerity, self-study, devotion to Īśvara (2.32).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The second limb: VP study, purity, contentment, austerity; ŚiP purity, contentment, austerity, japa, praṇidhi; MkP freedom from anger, service to the teacher, purity, light food, study.
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Non-anger, service of the teacher, purity, lightness of diet and vigilance.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Ten observances (austerity … vows); non-injury is called chief of the niyamas in the Yogatattva.
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The particular (means of dharma) for each life-stage (NBh 4.2.46).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): In Haribhadra's correlation the limb matching the tārā view (YDS 16).
