# puruṣakāra

`trm:purusakara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पुरुषकार
**Literal:** human effort

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Human effort, without which fate does not succeed (MBh 13.6.7); defended by Draupadī (3.28–33).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Śrī's role of intercession: she turns the soul toward the Lord and moves the Lord to forgive and receive it.
