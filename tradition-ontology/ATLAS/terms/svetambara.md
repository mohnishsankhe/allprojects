## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): 'White-clad': the tradition whose monks and nuns wear white garments, keeping clothes and bowls as instruments of restraint, not as possessions.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Called Śvetapaṭa in Digambara sources, which trace it to a famine-time relaxation of practice.
