## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): In 4.83–4.84: the four corners (koṭyaś catasraḥ, 4.84), "it exists", "it does not exist", "it exists and does not exist", "it does not exist, it does not exist", with moving, still, both and non-being, by whose graspings the Lord is covered and by which the childish cover [it]. (The alternatives denied at 4.22, from itself or another and existent, non-existent or both, are a different list.)
