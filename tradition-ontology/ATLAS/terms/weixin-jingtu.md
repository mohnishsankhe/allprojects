# weixin jingtu (mind-only Pure Land)

`trm:weixin-jingtu` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 唯心淨土
**Literal:** Pure Land that is only mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): 'As the mind is pure the buddha-land is pure': the Western Land is found in the purified mind (Platform Sūtra).
