# weixin jingtu (mind-only Pure Land)

`trm:weixin-jingtu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 唯心淨土
**Literal:** Pure Land that is only mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): The Pure Land as mind only, affirmed by Yanshou, Tianru and Zhuhong together with real birth in the West: the mind-only land is born only when the mind is understood; to purify the mind there is no method apart from Pure Land practice.
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): Rejected as a reading of the Pure Land: Shandao's 'pointing to a direction and establishing form' holds the Western land to be real for ordinary beings.
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): 'As the mind is pure the buddha-land is pure': the Western Land is found in the purified mind (Platform Sūtra).
