# navadhā bhakti

`trm:navadha-bhakti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नवधा भक्ति
**Literal:** ninefold devotion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): The nine means of devotion taught to Śabarī: holy company, talk of Rāma's stories, singing his qualities, expounding his words, guileless service of the teacher as Rāma, virtuous conduct with steady worship, worship through his mantra, honouring devotees and seeing him in all with dispassion, and inquiry into his nature.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The nine forms: hearing, singing, remembering, serving the feet, worship, obeisance, servitude, friendship and self-offering (BhP 7.5.23-24).
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): Tulsī's own list to Śabarī: saints' company, love of the Lord's stories, service to the guru, singing his qualities, mantra repetition, self-control, seeing the world full of him, contentment, and guileless trust.
- [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md): The Bhāgavata's nine, each expounded in the Dāsbodh's fourth daśaka, culminating in self-offering.
- [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md): The nine forms, of which hearing and singing are foremost.
