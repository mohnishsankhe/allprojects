# vijñāna-pariṇāma

`trm:vijnana-parinama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** transformation of consciousness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The process in which consciousness changes and appears as self and dharmas; threefold as maturation (the store), mentation (the afflicted mind) and object-cognition (the six); all figurative talk of self and dharmas refers to it, and it is itself 'imagination'.
