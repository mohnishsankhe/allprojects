# prakīrṇaka

`trm:prakirnaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रकीर्णक
**Literal:** scattered, miscellaneous

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md): A 'miscellaneous' canonical text, ten in the standard Mūrtipūjaka list (Catuḥśaraṇa, Āturapratyākhyāna and others), many on devotion and the discipline of dying; the Nandī says there were as many prakīrṇakas as disciples of a Tīrthaṅkara. Rejected by Sthānakavāsīs and Terāpanthīs.
