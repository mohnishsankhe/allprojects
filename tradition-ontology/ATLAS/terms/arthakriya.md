# arthakriyā

`trm:arthakriya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** causal efficacy; accomplishment of a purpose

## Definitions by tradition
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): The capacity to produce an effect (and to fulfil a human purpose), which is the mark of the real; only momentary particulars have it; non-deception is the continuance of it.
- [Svātantrika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/svatantrika.md): Capacity to function as it appears, distinguishing the correct from the incorrect conventional (Jñānagarbha).
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): A mark of conventional things (MAL 64), adopted from Dharmakīrti's epistemology.
