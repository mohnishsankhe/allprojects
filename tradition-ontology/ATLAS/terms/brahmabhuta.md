# brahmabhūta

`trm:brahmabhuta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ब्रह्मभूत
**Literal:** become brahman

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The liberated self after the final renunciation, no longer perceived, without mark (Ca Śā 1.155; 5.21).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Said of the yogin with inner happiness who attains brahma-nirvāṇa (5.24), and of the tranquil, stainless yogin with rajas stilled to whom supreme happiness comes (6.27).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The state of the yogin at peace who has become Brahman (BhG 5.24, 6.27); having become Brahman, serene, he attains supreme devotion (18.54); fitness for it (brahmabhūya) comes from devotion (14.26) and discipline (18.53).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Having become brahman: serene, the one who has become brahman neither grieves nor craves, is the same toward all beings and attains supreme devotion to the Lord (18.54).
