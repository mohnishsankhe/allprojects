# tapas

`trm:tapas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तपस्
**Literal:** heat; glow; austerity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Generative heat and ascetic effort: That One was born by the power of tapas (RV 10.129.3); ṛta and satya were born from kindled tapas (10.190.1); the ancients reached heaven by tapas (10.154.2); the seers first undertook tapas and dīkṣā (AVŚ 19.41.1); the one whose body is unheated does not attain soma (RV 9.83.1).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Austerity: bearing the pairs of opposites, with vows as suitable (YBh 2.32); to be practised without disturbing the mind's clarity (YBh 2.1); destroys impurity and perfects body and senses (2.43).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The creative heat by which Prajāpati and the primal waters brought forth the worlds (ŚB 11.1.6.1; AB 5.32); the seers' austerity during which Brahman came upon them (Nirukta 2.11).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Austerity as the root of all happiness; for each class its own work is its tapas — knowledge for the brāhmaṇa (MDh 11.234–235); breath-control is the highest tapas (MDh 6.70; VDh 10.5).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Austerity, heat: by tapas Prajāpati and brahman create (TU 2.6; PrU 1.4; MuU 1.1.8); Bhṛgu seeks brahman by tapas — 'tapas is brahman' (TU 3.2); a branch of dharma (ChU 2.23.1); sickness and cremation as the supreme tapas (BAU 5.11).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Austerity, threefold — of body, speech and mind (BhG 17.14–16) — and sāttvic, rājasic or tāmasic (17.17–19); terrible austerities not enjoined by scripture, tormenting the body, are demonic (17.5–6).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Austerity, for which (not for sense-pleasure) the human body is meant (BhP 5.5.1).
