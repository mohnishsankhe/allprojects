# lakuṭa / lakula / laguḍa

`trm:lakuta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** लकुट
**Literal:** club, cudgel

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The club held by Lakulīśa ('Lord with the club'), in the left hand in the Kāravaṇa-māhātmya's image.
- [Kālāmukha](../lineages/kalamukha.md): Carrying a club is among the practices Yāmuna ascribes to the Kālāmukhas.
