## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Beyond the stages of life: the Pāśupata observance so described by revelation.
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The Pāśupata state: the initiate leaves the rules of his former āśrama and follows the restraints and observances of the state beyond the āśramas.
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Name used by Śaiva scripture for the Pāśupata branch of the Atimārga.
