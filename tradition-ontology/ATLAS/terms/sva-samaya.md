# sva-samaya

`trm:sva-samaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्वसमय
**Literal:** the self in itself

## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): 'Own-doctrine / own-self': the soul abiding in its own faith, knowledge and conduct, as against para-samaya, the soul identified with karmic matter (Samayasāra 2).
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The soul abiding in its own knowing nature, as opposed to para-samaya, the soul identified with the non-self (Samayasāra 2).
