## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The emblem by which each Tīrthaṅkara's otherwise identical image is recognized, carved on the pedestal: the bull of Ṛṣabha, the conch of Nemi, the serpent of Pārśva, the lion of Mahāvīra, and so on.
