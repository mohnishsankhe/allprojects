# sadyo-mukti

`trm:sadyo-mukti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सद्योमुक्ति
**Literal:** immediate liberation

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Liberation at once at death, as opposed to the gradual ascent.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Immediate liberation of the knower of the attributeless Brahman, whose prāṇas do not depart (contrasted with krama-mukti).
