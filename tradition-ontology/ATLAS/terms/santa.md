## Definitions by tradition
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): The saint, in whom God is present; the saints' company is the true tīrtha and their feet are sought above liberation.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): The good person whose company (satsaṅg) gives discernment; greater than the Lord himself in Rāma's teaching to Śabarī.
