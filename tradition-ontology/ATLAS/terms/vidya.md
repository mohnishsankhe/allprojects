# vidyā

`trm:vidya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विद्या
**Literal:** knowledge, a knowledge-teaching

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The knowledge of the correspondences (and of the self) by which one reaches the world beyond desire where fees and uninformed austerity do not reach (ŚB 10.5.4.16).
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): The lower knowledge (aparā vidyā) includes the four Vedas and six Vedāṅgas; the higher is that by which the imperishable is reached (MuU 1.1.4–5).
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): A quality of the paśu, part of the effect: either cognitive (discriminating or not) or non-cognitive (merit and demerit).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Knowledge: what is done with knowledge is more powerful (ChU 1.1.10); the world of the gods is won by knowledge (BAU 1.5.16); paired with avidyā (Īśa 9-11; KU 1.2.4; ŚU 5.1); the higher and lower knowledge (MuU 1.1.4).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Undefective cognition (VS 9.27 C): perception, inference, memory, seers' knowledge (Praśastapāda).
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): A feminine mantra and the goddess it embodies; the ten 'great vidyās'.
