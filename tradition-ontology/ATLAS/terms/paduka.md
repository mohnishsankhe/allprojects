# pādukā

`trm:paduka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पादुका
**Literal:** sandal

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The guru's 'sandals' — the mantra of the guru's feet given before the Bālā (PKS 1.41); the feet are contemplated in the crown each morning (PKS 2.2); immersion in the Goddess's sandals is complete meditation (Bhāvanā Upaniṣad).
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The guru's sandals and the mantra of the lineage's feet; 'there is no mantra beyond the pādukā' (Kulārṇava 12.12).
