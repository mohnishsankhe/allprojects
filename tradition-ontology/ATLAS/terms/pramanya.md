# prāmāṇya

`trm:pramanya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रामाण्य
**Literal:** validity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The validity of a cognition, which arises and is known from the cognition itself; invalidity comes from outside.
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Validity of a cognition, known extrinsically (parataḥ).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Produced by excellences in the causes and known by inference, not by the cognition that apprehends the cognition (Prāmāṇyavāda).
