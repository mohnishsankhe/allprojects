# lokottara

`trm:lokottara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Lokottaravāda](../lineages/lokottaravada.md): Supramundane: the Buddha's conduct, roots of merit, postures, body, robes and food are beyond the world.
- [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md): All Tathāgatas are supramundane and without outflows (Vasumitra).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): In the Kośa, 'supramundane' qualifies the pure path (e.g. detachment by the supramundane path), not the Buddha's body.
