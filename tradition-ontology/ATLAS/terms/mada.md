**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मद
**Literal:** pride

## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Eight kinds of pride obstructing right view — in knowledge, honour, family, caste, power, wealth, austerity and body (Ratnakaraṇḍa).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The exhilaration of soma (RV 9.1.1 'most exhilarating'; 10.119).
