**Native script:** पूर्णाहन्ता
**Literal:** full I-ness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The knowledge 'I am Śiva, I am the guru, I am the whole world' (SSM 14.38–41).
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The perfect I-awareness whose essence is light and bliss, the potency of the great mantra (PH 20).
