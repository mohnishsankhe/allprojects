# parihāṇi

`trm:parihani` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Falling back: from what is obtained, what is not obtained, or enjoyment; possible for five of the six kinds of arhat, not from stream-entry; one fallen does not die before regaining the fruit.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): No falling back from arhatship, since defilements abandoned by seeing do not return.
- [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md): Stream-enterers can fall back, arhats cannot (Vasumitra).
