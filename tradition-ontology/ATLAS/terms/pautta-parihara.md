# pauṭṭa-parihāra

`trm:pautta-parihara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Prakrit
**Literal:** reanimation; changing (bodies) by turning back

## Definitions by tradition
- [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md): (Jain report) Gosāla's doctrine, drawn from the regrowing sesame plant, that beings may die and re-enter a body; he claimed to be the seventh such re-entry.
