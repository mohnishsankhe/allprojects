# paricaya

`trm:paricaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** परिचय
**Literal:** familiarity, intimacy

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The third stage: the breath reaches the great void; drum sound; natural bliss (HYP 4.74–75); the breath enters suṣumnā and pierces the centres (DYŚ 97–99; SS 3.60–61).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Third stage, in which kuṇḍalinī enters the suṣumnā.
