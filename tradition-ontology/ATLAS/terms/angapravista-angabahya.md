**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The Nandī's twofold division of scripture: what 'enters the limbs' (the twelve Aṅgas, composed by the gaṇadharas) and what is 'outside the limbs' (composed by later elders); the latter is further divided into kālika (read at set times) and utkālika (read at any time).
