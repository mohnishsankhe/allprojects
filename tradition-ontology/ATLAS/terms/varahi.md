# vārāhī / daṇḍanāthā

`trm:varahi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वाराही
**Literal:** the boar-faced goddess; the general

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Lalitā's general (Daṇḍanāthā, Daṇḍinī) on the Kiricakra chariot who slays Viśukra (LSN 26-30); her worship is the seventh section of the Kalpasūtra; in the Bhāvanā Upaniṣad she is 'the father'.
