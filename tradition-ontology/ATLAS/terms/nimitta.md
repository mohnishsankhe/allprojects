# nimitta

`trm:nimitta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** sign

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): A mental image or sign: preliminary, learning and counterpart signs; also the 'signs' of formations transcended by the signless.
- [Pa-Auk tradition (Pa-Auk Tawya)](../lineages/pa-auk.md): The light and the breath-sign that appear with deep concentration and must stabilize before jhāna.
- [Thai Forest tradition (Kammaṭṭhāna forest tradition)](../lineages/thai-forest.md): Visions and signs arising in meditation, to be investigated and not clung to.
