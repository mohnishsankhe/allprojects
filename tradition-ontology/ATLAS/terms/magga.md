# magga

`trm:magga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** path

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The fourth noble truth, the way leading to the cessation of suffering: the noble eightfold path (SN 56.11; SN 45.8); in the commentaries also the supramundane path-moment of each of the four stages.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The supramundane path consciousness, occurring once for each of the four stages, which abandons the corresponding fetters.
