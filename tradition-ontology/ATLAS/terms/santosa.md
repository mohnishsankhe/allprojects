# saṃtoṣa

`trm:santosa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** संतोष
**Literal:** contentment

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Not wanting more than the means at hand (YBh 2.32); yields unsurpassed happiness (2.42).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Contentment: the dear devotee is ever content (santuṣṭa, 12.14) and content with whatever comes (12.19).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Contentment: not craving the unobtained and enjoying what comes without elation or dejection; the third gatekeeper.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Contentment (tuṣṭi), one of the Uddhava Gītā's twelve niyamas.
