## Definitions by tradition
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): Generalization: the process by which determinants and emotions shown in the art-work are freed from belonging to a particular person, time and place, so that the spectator relishes them impersonally (Bhaṭṭa Nāyaka; Abhinavagupta).
