# paramattha

`trm:paramattha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** highest meaning; ultimate

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): What exists by its own intrinsic nature (sabhāva) and is not further reducible: consciousness, mental factors, matter and nibbāna, in contrast to concepts (paññatti).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): In the ultimate sense (paramatthato) no person is found (Mil 3.1.1); in the commentaries paramattha-sacca and the four ultimate realities (citta, cetasika, rūpa, nibbāna).
