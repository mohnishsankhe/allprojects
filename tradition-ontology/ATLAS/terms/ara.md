# ārā

`trm:ara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आरा
**Literal:** spoke; period

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): One of the six periods of each half-cycle: very happy, happy, happy-unhappy, unhappy-happy, unhappy, very unhappy (suṣama-suṣamā … duṣama-duṣamā).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The six 'spokes' (ara) of each half of the time-wheel, from 'very happy' (suṣamā-suṣamā) to 'very unhappy' (duḥṣamā-duḥṣamā); Tīrthaṅkaras appear only in the third and fourth; the present is the fifth (duḥṣamā), in which liberation from this region is no longer possible.
