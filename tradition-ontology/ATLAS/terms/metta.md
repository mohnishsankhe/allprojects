# mettā

`trm:metta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** loving-kindness, love

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): A boundless mind of love for all beings, as a mother for her only child (Snp 1.8); kept even under the saw (MN 21); its liberation of mind has eleven benefits (AN 11.15) and outshines all merit (Iti 27).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Wishing welfare to beings; developed first towards oneself and then, with the barriers broken down, to all.
