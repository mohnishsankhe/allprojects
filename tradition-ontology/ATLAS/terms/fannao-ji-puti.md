# fannao ji puti (afflictions are bodhi)

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 煩惱即菩提

## Definitions by tradition
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): The previous thought attached to objects is affliction, the next thought free of objects is bodhi.
