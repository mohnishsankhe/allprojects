# svalakṣaṇa

`trm:svalaksana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्वलक्षण
**Literal:** unique particular; own-characteristic

## Definitions by tradition
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): The real, momentary particular whose presence or absence changes the appearance of cognition; capable of causal efficacy, hence ultimately real; the object of perception, inexpressible.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The particular characteristic of a dharma (e.g. solidity of earth), as opposed to the general characteristics (impermanence etc.) shared by all conditioned dharmas; examined in the applications of mindfulness.
