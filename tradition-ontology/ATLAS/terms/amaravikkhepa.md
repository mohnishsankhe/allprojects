## Definitions by tradition
- [Ajñāna (the sceptics)](../lineages/ajnana.md): (As reported) Evasion by refusing every alternative; the fourth ground (dullness) is Sañjaya's formula.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): A wrong view arising from fear of false speech, of grasping or of cross-examination, or from dullness.
