# wuzhu (non-abiding)

`trm:wuzhu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 無住
**Literal:** not abiding

## Definitions by tradition
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): The root of the teaching and the original nature of people: moment after moment not abiding in any dharma; from the Diamond Sūtra's 'produce a mind that abides nowhere'.
- [Rinzai Zen](../lineages/rinzai.md): Takuan's mind that does not stop anywhere.
