# asura

`trm:asura` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** असुर
**Literal:** lord; possessor of power

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): In the Ṛgveda a lord of mysterious power, applied to Varuṇa and other gods ('great is the one asura-ship of the gods', RV 3.55); later the gods' opponents.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: The 'demonic state' (āsura bhāva) resorted to by evil-doers whose knowledge is carried off by māyā, who do not take refuge in the Lord (7.15); the deluding nature of rākṣasas and asuras resorted to by those who disregard him (9.12).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: The demonic (āsura) as one of the two creations of beings in this world, beside the divine (16.6), described at length in 16.7–18; 'demonic wombs' into which the hateful are cast (16.19–20); those who torment the body with unsanctioned austerities are 'of demonic resolve' (āsura-niścaya, 17.6).
