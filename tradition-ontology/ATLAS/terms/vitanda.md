# vitaṇḍā

`trm:vitanda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वितण्डा
**Literal:** cavilling (refutation without a counter-thesis)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Wrangling without establishing a counter-thesis (NS 1.2.3); the caviller who states a purpose thereby adopts a thesis (NBh 1.1.1).
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): merely finding fault with the other side (Ca Vi 8.28).
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Debate that only attacks the opponent's position, one of the three kinds of debate.
