# pulāl maṟuttal

`trm:pulal-maruttal` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tamil

## Definitions by tradition
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Refusing meat (Kuṟaḷ ch. 26): better than a thousand sacrifices.
- [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md): Taught in the Tirumantiram's first tantra.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Refusing flesh: Paṭṭiṉattār asks what flesh-eaters will do when Yama seizes them (Tiruvēkampamālai 33).
