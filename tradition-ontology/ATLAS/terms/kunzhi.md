# kun gzhi

`trm:kunzhi` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** ཀུན་གཞི
**Literal:** base of all

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): The all-base: in Longchenpa's usage the neutral, unaware basis in which karmic tendencies are stored, the root of saṃsāra; it must be distinguished from the dharmakāya — to rest in it is not to rest in awareness.
- [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md): The base of all: in Bön Dzogchen the ultimate ground, empty and clear, inseparable from awareness; not the saṃsāric storehouse.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): In the Lamdre, the all-base consciousness as the causal continuum that contains saṃsāra and nirvāṇa; when purified it is great awakening.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): Prāsaṅgika does not accept an all-base consciousness distinct from the six consciousnesses (first difficult point); the 'all-base' of the Seven Points is read as the mind's emptiness.

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: ālaya  — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [ālaya-vijñāna](alaya-vijnana.md) — the Nyingma distinguish the all-base (kun gzhi) from the all-base consciousness (kun gzhi rnam shes); both belong to the saṃsāric side
- contested: [gzhi](zhi.md) — Bön's kun gzhi functions as the ground (gzhi) of Nyingma Dzogchen, while the Nyingma kun gzhi does not
