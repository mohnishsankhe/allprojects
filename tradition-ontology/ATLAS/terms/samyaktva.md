**Native script:** सम्यक्त्व
**Literal:** rightness (of view)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The state of right view, with its eight limbs (freedom from doubt, from desire, from disgust, from delusion; upholding, stabilising, affection, illuminating the teaching), its signs (calm, longing for release, detachment, compassion, affirmation) and its kinds (subsidential, mixed, destructional).
