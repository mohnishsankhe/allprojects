# zixing (self-nature, own nature)

`trm:zixing` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 自性
**Literal:** self-nature

## Definitions by tradition
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): The originally pure, unborn, complete and unmoving nature of one's own mind that produces all dharmas (Huineng's awakening); delusion and awakening are its states.
- [Seon (Korean Chan)](../lineages/seon.md): One's own nature, the Dharma body, recognised in sudden awakening (Jinul).

## Forms in other languages
- Japanese: jishō  — exact
- Korean: jaseong  — exact
- Sanskrit: svabhāva  — partial

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- contested: [svabhāva](svabhava.md) — Same Chinese word as the svabhāva that Madhyamaka refutes; in the Platform Sūtra it names the empty, originally pure nature of mind, not an inherent own-being of dharmas; Madhyamaka would object to any reading of zixing as svabhāva.
