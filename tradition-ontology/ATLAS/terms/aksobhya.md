# Akṣobhya

`trm:aksobhya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अक्षोभ्य
**Literal:** imperturbable

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The 'Imperturbable' buddha of the east and of the vajra family; he crowns the head of Ekajaṭā and Mahācīna-Tārā.
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): In the Hindu Tārā cult, Akṣobhya (sometimes a serpent) sits on Tārā's head and is counted as her consort (recalled).
