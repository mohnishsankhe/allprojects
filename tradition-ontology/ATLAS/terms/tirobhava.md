# tirobhāva

`trm:tirobhava` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तिरोभाव
**Literal:** concealment

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The first of Lakṣmī's five acts, the concealing power by which souls are bound.
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Śiva's concealing power (rodha-śakti), called a bond by figure because it impels the bonds until the soul is ripe.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Tirōtāyi / tirōtam: fifth of the five malas; 'na' of the five letters.
