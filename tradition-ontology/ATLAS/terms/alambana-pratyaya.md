# ālambana-pratyaya

`trm:alambana-pratyaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Literal:** object-condition

## Definitions by tradition
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): The condition that is both cause of a cognition and what appears in it — which neither atoms nor their aggregate can be; it is the internal knowable form.
