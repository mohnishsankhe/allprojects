# kiriyavāda

`trm:kiriyavada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** doctrine of action / of the efficacy of deeds

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The doctrine that deeds are effective, taught by all Buddhas (kammavāda, kiriyavāda, viriyavāda); the one Ājīvaka who went to heaven held it.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The first 'assembly': 180 kinds; the true kiriyavāda is taught by one who knows self and world, influx, stopping and wearing away of karma; a rival 'kiriyāvāda' of intention alone is rejected.
