# paśyantī

`trm:pasyanti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पश्यन्ती
**Literal:** the 'seeing' level of speech

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The grammarians' highest level of speech; for Somānanda it is only the knowledge-power at the Sadāśiva level, not the ultimate (Śivadṛṣṭi 2.1).
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): The highest of Bhartṛhari's three levels of speech: undivided, all sequence withdrawn, the inner light of its own form, subtle and imperishable (Vṛtti on VP 1.142); identified by the grammarians with the Word-Brahman.
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): The second of four levels of speech, above madhyamā and below parā; placed by Nāgeśa and the tantric manuals at the navel.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The second level of speech, where it puts forth two leaves.
