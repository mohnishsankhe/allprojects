# paśyantī

`trm:pasyanti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पश्यन्ती
**Literal:** the 'seeing' level of speech

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The grammarians' highest level of speech; for Somānanda it is only the knowledge-power at the Sadāśiva level, not the ultimate (Śivadṛṣṭi 2.1).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The second level of speech, where it puts forth two leaves.
