# bhakta-parādhīnatā

`trm:bhakta-paradhinata` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The Lord's dependence on his devotees — 'I am subject to my devotees, as if not free' (BhP 9.4.63).
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The Lord who 'did as he was told' (Tirumaḻicai), who takes whatever form his devotees love (MT 44).
- `lin:jagannatha`: In the Gīta Govinda Kṛṣṇa pleads to place Rādhā's foot on his head (GG 10).
