# antaryāga

`trm:antaryaga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अन्तर्याग
**Literal:** inner sacrifice

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Inner worship, in which knower, knowing and known are offerer, fire and oblation (Bhāvanā Upaniṣad; PKS 1.26); the Yoginīhṛdaya's inner worship is held sufficient for those firm in it (Setubandha).
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Mental worship of the deity in the heart and body before external worship.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Mental worship (mānasayāga) preceding external worship.
