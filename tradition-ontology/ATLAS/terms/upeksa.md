# upekṣā

`trm:upeksa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उपेक्षा
**Literal:** equanimity, disregard

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Equanimity, disregard cultivated toward the non-virtuous, one of the four attitudes that clarify the mind (1.33, YBh 1.33). Vyāsa: equanimity toward the wicked is not a cultivation and yields no strength (YBh 3.23).

## Forms in other languages
- Pali: upekkhā  — partial — one of the four brahmavihāras (immeasurables); in Buddhism radiated to all beings
