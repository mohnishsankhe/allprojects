## Definitions by tradition
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): Lamp: in the instruction series, the channels and faculties through which awareness manifests — counted as four lamps (the far-reaching water lamp, the lamp of the empty sphere, the lamp of the pure expanse, the lamp of self-arising wisdom) or six. Doctrinal anatomy; the practices using them are restricted.
