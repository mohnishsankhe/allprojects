# pūrṇābhiṣeka

`trm:purnabhiseka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पूर्णाभिषेक
**Literal:** the complete consecration

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The full Kaula consecration; those consecrated by it are Śaiva avadhūtas (Mahānirvāṇa 14.144).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The 'full consecration' of later Śrīvidyā manuals, conferring the full authority of the tradition after the preliminary initiations.
