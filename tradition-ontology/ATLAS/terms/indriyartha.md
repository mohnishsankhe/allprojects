# indriyārtha

`trm:indriyartha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** इन्द्रियार्थ
**Literal:** object of a sense

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The objects from which the senses are to be withdrawn (2.58, 2.68), on which the hypocrite dwells in mind (3.6), and in which attraction and aversion are set (3.34).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: the yoked knower holds that the senses move among the objects of the senses (indriyārtheṣu) (5.9); one attached neither to the objects of the senses nor to actions has ascended to yoga (6.4).
