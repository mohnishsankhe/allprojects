# sandhyā

`trm:sandhya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सन्ध्या
**Literal:** junction; twilight worship

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The twilight rite in which water consecrated with the Gāyatrī is thrown up against the Mandeha demons and the sun is meditated on as Brahman (TA 2.2).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The obligatory morning and evening worship with bathing, sprinkling, breath-control and Gāyatrī recitation (YS 1.22–25).
