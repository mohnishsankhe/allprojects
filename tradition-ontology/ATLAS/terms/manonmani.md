# manonmanī

`trm:manonmani` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मनोन्मनी
**Literal:** mind-transcending

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The steadiness of mind when the breath moves in the middle (HYP 2.42); the one state (3.54); saluted as a goddess (4.64).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Synonym of unmanī and samādhi (HYP 4.3); also a name of Śakti (KJN 20.11).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The end of sound and of mind; the goal of nāda-yoga; also a measure of the sixteen-measured praṇava.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): 'Jewel of the mind' — the supreme goddess as mother and light in the Siddhar worship; in yoga texts also the name of the state beyond mind (manonmanī).
