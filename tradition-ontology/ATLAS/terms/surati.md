# surati (surat)

`trm:surati` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Hindi

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): The soul as attention or consciousness-current, a drop of the Supreme scattered through the body; it is gathered at the eye centre and rises on the sound current (surat-śabd).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): In Kabīr and the Nāth-influenced Sants: attention or recollection fixed on the Lord (surati-nirati).
