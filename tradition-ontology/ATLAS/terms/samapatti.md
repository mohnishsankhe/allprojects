# samāpatti

`trm:samapatti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** समापत्ति
**Literal:** coalescence, attainment

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The mind's taking on the form of what it rests on — grasper, grasping or grasped — like a flawless crystal (1.41); fourfold: savitarkā, nirvitarkā, savicārā, nirvicārā (1.42–44); also coalescence with the endless in posture (2.47).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Attainment: the eight fundamental attainments (four dhyānas, four formless), each tasting, pure or pure-of-outflows; also the attainments of non-perception and cessation.

## Forms in other languages
- Pali: samāpatti  — partial — Buddhist 'meditative attainment' (the jhānas and formless attainments)
- Chinese: dengzhi 等至 — exact
