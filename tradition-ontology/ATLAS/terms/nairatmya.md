# nairātmya

`trm:nairatmya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नैरात्म्य
**Literal:** selflessness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Two selflessnesses — of persons and of dharmas; the bodhisattva intent on 'dharmas are without self' is a bodhisattva.
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Selflessness, taught by the Buddhas alongside the teaching of self and of neither (MMK 18.6); in the Mahāyāna twofold, of persons (pudgala-nairātmya) and of dharmas (dharma-nairātmya).
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Its cultivation is better than the delusion of 'I'-making (BCA 9.78).

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [anattā](anatta.md) — Madhyamaka extends selflessness from persons to all dharmas, including the dhammas that Theravāda Abhidhamma treats as ultimately real
