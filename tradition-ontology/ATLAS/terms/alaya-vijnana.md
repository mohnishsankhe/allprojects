# ālaya-vijñāna

`trm:alaya-vijnana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आलयविज्ञान
**Literal:** store-consciousness; home-consciousness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The eighth, fundamental consciousness: the maturation of past action, which holds all seeds, appropriates the body, cognizes the receptacle world imperceptibly, is accompanied only by the five universal mental factors and neutral feeling, flows like a torrent, and ceases (as ālaya) at arhatship.
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): The conventional basis of all, the ground of saṃsāric imprints, to be distinguished from the ultimate ālaya-wisdom.

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [bhavaṅga](bhavanga.md) — The Mahāyānasaṃgraha (ch. 1) says the ālaya was taught by other schools under other names — the Sthaviras' bhavaṅga-vijñāna among them; the Theravāda does not accept a store of seeds.
- contested: [tathāgatagarbha](tathagatagarbha.md) — The Laṅkāvatāra relates ālaya and tathāgatagarbha; Faxiang keeps them distinct (the ālaya is defiled and ceases), Dilun/Shelun and Tathāgatagarbha readers identify its pure aspect with suchness.
