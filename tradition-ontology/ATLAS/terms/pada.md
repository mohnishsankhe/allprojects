# pada

`trm:pada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पद
**Literal:** step; foot; quarter; word

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Step, place, quarter (speech has four padas, RV 1.164.45) and, in recitation, the word (as in padapāṭha).
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): A word, defined as what ends in a nominal or verbal ending (Aṣṭādhyāyī 1.4.14); also a quarter of a verse.
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Sounds ending in a case-ending (NS 2.2.60 DCS); its meaning is individual, form and universal (NS 2.2.68).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): What possesses denotative power (Tarkasaṅgraha).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The supreme state ('the state') with which the body is brought to one taste (piṇḍa-pada-samarasa); 'param pad' in Hindi.
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): Meditation, the second of the four (KJN 3.4).
