## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The 'knower of fate': the astrologer, also sāṃvatsara, daivavid, gaṇaka or jyotiṣī; he must know the three branches and be of good conduct; the king and even forest-dwellers consult him (BS 2; BPHS 1.7–8).
