# gagan maṇḍal

`trm:gagana-mandala` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Hindi

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The 'circle of the sky' in the head, where the upside-down well of nectar is (Gorakh Bānī).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The 'sky-circle' in the head where nectar drips, light shines and the unstruck sound is heard; Nāth-derived image in Kabīr and Dādū.

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: gagana-maṇḍala  — exact
- Hindi: gagan maṇḍal  — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [sahasrāra](sahasrara.md) — Both name the highest centre in the head; the Sant image is not systematized as a lotus.
