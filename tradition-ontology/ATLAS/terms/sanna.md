# saññā

`trm:sanna` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** perception, recognition

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The third aggregate: perception of forms, sounds, odours, tastes, tangibles and mental objects; perceptions arise and cease with causes, some through training (DN 9).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Perception: the noting and recognizing of an object's marks; a universal mental factor and an aggregate.
