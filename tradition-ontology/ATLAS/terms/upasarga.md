# upasarga

`trm:upasarga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उपसर्ग
**Literal:** obstacle, affliction (of samādhi)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): What the powers are within samādhi, because they oppose seeing (3.37, YBh 3.37).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Obstacle-powers that arise after the gross obstacles are overcome: in MkP longings for rewards and five states (prātibha, śrāvaṇa, daiva, bhrama, āvarta); in LiP/ŚiP six siddhis (pratibhā, śravaṇa, vārtā, darśana, āsvāda, vedanā).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): An affliction inflicted by gods, humans or animals on a meditating ascetic, to be borne with equanimity; Mahāvīra's twelve years were full of them, and Pārśva's attacker was Kamaṭha.
