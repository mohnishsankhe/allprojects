# kasiṇa

`trm:kasina` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** totality, meditation device

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Ten kasiṇas — earth, water, fire, air, blue, yellow, red, white, space, consciousness — perceived above, below, across, non-dual, immeasurable (AN 10.25).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): A device (disk of earth, water, fire, colour, light, space) used to produce the learning and counterpart signs and the jhānas; ten are listed.
