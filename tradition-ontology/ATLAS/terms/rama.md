# Rām (Rāma)

`trm:rama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Hindi

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The Sants' most common name for the formless Lord who 'pervades every body' (ghaṭ ghaṭ rām) — explicitly distinguished from Rāma son of Daśaratha.
- [Rāmsnehī sampradāya](../lineages/ramsnehi.md): Rām as the nirguṇa object of remembrance of the 'lovers of Rām'.

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: rāma  — partial — the Sanskrit name primarily denotes the avatāra
