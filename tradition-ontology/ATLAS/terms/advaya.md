# advaya

`trm:advaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अद्वय
**Literal:** non-duality, not two

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): That paired concepts — saṃsāra and nirvāṇa, defiled and pure, self and no-self — are not two; entry into non-duality is beyond words, shown by Vimalakīrti's silence.
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Non-dual understanding (advaya-buddhi), free of the extremes of existence and non-existence, is with compassion and bodhicitta a cause of bodhisattvas (MA 1.1); the non-dual path to nirvāṇa (PsP 17.30).

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- contested: [advaita](advaita.md) — Both deny duality; the Mahāyāna sūtras deny a single substantial reality, while Advaita affirms Brahman; related by later polemics (dsp:advaita-crypto-buddhism)
