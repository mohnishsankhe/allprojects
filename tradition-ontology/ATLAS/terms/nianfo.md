# nianfo

`trm:nianfo` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 念佛
**Literal:** recollecting / invoking the Buddha

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): In the Chinese Pure Land sūtras, thinking of Amitābha or calling his name ('namo Amitābha Buddha'), even ten times, as the cause of birth in Sukhāvatī.
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Recollecting Amitābha — contemplating his marks or land, or calling his name; from Shandao onward chiefly the oral calling of 'Namo Amituofo' as the rightly determined act.
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): Nenbutsu: saying 'Namu Amida Butsu' with the conviction of being born; the practice selected in the primal vow.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): Nenbutsu: the name of Amida transferred to beings; in the person of shinjin it is an expression of gratitude, 'neither practice nor good' for the practitioner.
- [Ji-shū (the Time school of Ippen)](../lineages/ji-shu.md): Nenbutsu in which the name itself is birth, recited and danced having cast off self.

## Forms in other languages
- Japanese: nenbutsu / nembutsu 念仏 — exact
- Sanskrit: buddhānusmṛti  — partial — the Indian term means recollection; East Asian usage came to mean calling the name
- Korean: yeombul 염불 — exact
