# guṇātīta

`trm:gunatita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** गुणातीत
**Literal:** one gone beyond the guṇas

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): One who neither hates nor longs for the guṇas' effects, sits as if indifferent knowing that the guṇas act, is the same in pleasure and pain, honour and dishonour, and has renounced all undertakings (BhG 14.22–25); reached by unswerving devotion (14.26).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 14.20–26: the embodied one who goes beyond the three guṇas attains the immortal, freed from birth, death, old age and pain (14.20). Asked for his marks, conduct and means (14.21): he does not hate illumination, activity or delusion when they arise nor long for them when they cease; sitting as one indifferent he is not shaken by the guṇas, knowing 'the guṇas operate'; he is the same in pleasure and pain, toward clod, stone and gold, the pleasant and unpleasant, blame and praise, honour and dishonour, friend and foe, and relinquishes all undertakings — he is called guṇātīta (14.22–25); one who serves Kṛṣṇa with unswerving devotion goes beyond the guṇas (14.26).
