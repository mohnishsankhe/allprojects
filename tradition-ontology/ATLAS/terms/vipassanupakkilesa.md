# vipassanūpakkilesa

`trm:vipassanupakkilesa` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** imperfections of insight

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The ten imperfections of insight named in Vism XX (illumination, knowledge, rapture, tranquillity, happiness, resolve, exertion, establishment, equanimity, delight): imperfections because grounds of imperfection, not because they are unwholesome; the meditator is to see them as impermanent and not-self and not take them as the path (Vism XX p. 633-638).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Ten experiences that arise with tender insight (illumination, knowledge, rapture, tranquillity, bliss, resolution, exertion, assurance, equanimity, attachment) and are mistaken for the path.
