# tathya-saṃvṛti

`trm:tathya-samvrti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** correct (real) conventional

## Definitions by tradition
- [Svātantrika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/svatantrika.md): Conventional things that arise from causes and function as they appear (e.g. water that quenches thirst), as against the incorrect conventional (mithyā-saṃvṛti) such as a mirage taken for water (Jñānagarbha); the ladder to the ultimate (Bhāviveka).
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): What satisfies only when not analysed, arises and ceases and is capable of function (MAL 64).
