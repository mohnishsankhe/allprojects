## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Superknowledge; six — magical power, divine ear, knowledge of others' minds, recollection of former lives, knowledge of death and rebirth, destruction of outflows; the last three are the 'knowledges' (vidyā).
