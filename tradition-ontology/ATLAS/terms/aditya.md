## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The sons of Aditi (Varuṇa, Mitra, Aryaman and others), who stand by ṛta (RV 10.85.1).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: of the Ādityas the Lord is Viṣṇu (10.21); the Ādityas are among the gods Arjuna is bidden to behold in the Lord's form (11.6) and who gaze on it amazed (11.22).
