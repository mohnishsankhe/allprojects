# diṭṭhi

`trm:ditthi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** view

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): A view or theory, especially the speculative views (the sixty-two of DN 1, the ten undeclared of MN 72) that are 'a thicket, a fetter of views'; right view (sammā-diṭṭhi) is the first path factor.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): A speculative view; the sixty-two grounds are views of those who do not know or see, conditioned by contact and craving.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): View; as an unwholesome factor, wrong view, especially of self and of the world.
