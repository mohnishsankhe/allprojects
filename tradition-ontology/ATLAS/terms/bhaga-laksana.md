# bhāgalakṣaṇā

`trm:bhaga-laksana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भागलक्षणा
**Literal:** implication of a part

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Partial implication: the mode by which 'that' and 'thou' are understood as their common consciousness, dropping the opposed features, as in 'this is that Devadatta'.
