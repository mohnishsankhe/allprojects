# liṅga

`trm:linga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** लिङ्ग
**Literal:** mark, sign

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Śiva himself as the unlimited, eternal brahman in which the world dissolves (laya) and from which it arises again, delimited by his own māyā for the sake of worship (SSM 6.36–38); the liṅga is Paraśiva and its pedestal Śakti, their union the universe (11.32); worn on the body as the iṣṭaliṅga.
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): (1) The manifest, as that which 'merges' into its cause (SK 10). (2) The subtle body that transmigrates (SK 20, 40–42). (3) The inferential mark (SK 5).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Śiva's sign: the manifest (unmanifest Prakṛti) whose root is the unmarked Śiva; the formless (niṣkala) liṅga of worship; the column of fire.
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): A mark from which something is known; desire and the rest are marks of the self (NS 1.1.10).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Mark; synonymous with hetu, apadeśa, nimitta, pramāṇa, kāraṇa (VS 9.20 Candrānanda).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Three kinds: with positive and negative concomitance, positive only, negative only.
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): The aniconic form of Śiva used in worship; the Kāmika distinguishes self-manifest, divine, bāṇa, sages' and man-installed liṅgas, and the liṅga given by the guru at initiation.
