# vibhava

`trm:vibhava` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विभव
**Literal:** manifestation, descent

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The Lord's descents; the Ahirbudhnya counts thirty-nine, beginning with Padmanābha.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The Lord's descents (avatāra) — principal ones like Rāma and Kṛṣṇa in bodies of pure sattva taken by his will, and secondary ones by empowerment of souls — for the protection of the good.
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The excitants of rasa: the object and substratum (ālambana: Kṛṣṇa and his devotees) and the stimuli (uddīpana: his qualities, flute, dress, places).
