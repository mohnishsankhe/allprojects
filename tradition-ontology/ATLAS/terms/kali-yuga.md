# kali-yuga

`trm:kali-yuga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कलियुग
**Literal:** the age of strife

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The fourth and present age, an ocean of faults with one great virtue: that singing the Lord's name gives what meditation, sacrifice and worship gave in the earlier ages; reckoned by tradition from 3102 BCE.
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The age in which only the path of the Āgama avails and Vedic mantras are powerless (Mahānirvāṇa 2.7-15); in which only householder and mendicant stages remain (8.8); in which, for the Kālīvilāsa, only the paśu disposition is allowed (4.2-3).
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The present, fourth age, whose beginning (3102 BCE by the siddhāntic epoch) is the zero of calendrical computation; Āryabhaṭa was 23 when 3600 of its years had passed (Āryabhaṭīya 3.10); in it the Viṃśottarī is the chief period-system (BPHS 46.14).
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): The degenerate age in which neither ritual, devotion nor discernment stands on its own and the name is the one support — yet the best of ages for one with faith.
