# satsaṅga

`trm:satsanga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सत्सङ्ग
**Literal:** association with the good/real

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Company of the holy (sādhusaṅgama), the fourth gatekeeper, from which discrimination arises.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Association with the holy, which wins the Lord more than yoga, Sāṃkhya or austerity; attachment itself, directed to the holy, becomes the open door to liberation.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): The first of the nine means of devotion taught to Śabarī.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): The company of the good, without which there is no discernment, and which Rāma's grace grants; first of the nine devotions taught to Śabarī.
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): The saints' company, asked for above liberation.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The company of sants and gathering to sing and hear their words; the purifying association without which the Name does not take root.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): The Master's discourse and the gathering of disciples; also, inwardly, contact with the Word.
