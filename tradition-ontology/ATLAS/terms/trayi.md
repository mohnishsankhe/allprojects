**Language:** Sanskrit

**Literal:** the triple (Veda)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: The knowers of the three Vedas (traividyāḥ), soma-drinkers, win heaven by sacrifice (9.20); following the dharma of the three (trayī-dharma), desiring desires, they obtain going and coming (9.21).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The three Vedas (Ṛg, Yajus, Sāman) as the knowledge of the śrauta rite; the Atharvaveda's place beside them was debated.
