# ajo bhāgaḥ

`trm:aja-bhaga` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अजो भागः
**Literal:** the unborn part (or: the goat as portion)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): What Agni is asked to heat and carry to the world of the well-doers at cremation (RV 10.16.4); read either as the dead man's unborn part or as the goat offered as Agni's portion.
