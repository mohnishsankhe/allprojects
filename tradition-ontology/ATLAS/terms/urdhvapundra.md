# ūrdhvapuṇḍra

`trm:urdhvapundra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ऊर्ध्वपुण्ड्र
**Literal:** upright mark

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The upright forehead mark of the Vaiṣṇava, made with gopīcandana clay; praised as purifying and required for rites to bear fruit.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Vaiṣṇava vertical mark of gopīcandana.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The vertical forehead mark of white clay with the central line (śrīcūrṇa) for Śrī; U-shaped among the Vaṭakalai, Y-shaped with a line down the nose among the Teṅkalai.
