**Language:** Sanskrit

**Literal:** right knowledge

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Valid cognition: cognition of an object not already known and not contradicted.
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Experience that apprehends, in what has a property, that property as its qualifier (Tarkasaṅgraha).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Correct determination (Udayana, NK 4.5).
