**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** श्रेयस्
**Literal:** the better; the good

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Arjuna sees no good in killing kin (1.31), holds living on alms better (2.5), and asks what is better (2.7) and for the one thing by which he may reach the good (3.2); by nourishing one another gods and humans attain the highest good (3.11); one's own dharma is better (3.35); nothing is better for a kṣatriya than a dharmic battle (2.31).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The good, the better: chosen by the wise over the pleasant (KU 1.2.1-2).
