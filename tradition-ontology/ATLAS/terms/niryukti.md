## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The oldest layer of commentary on the canon: Prakrit verses (gāthā) explaining key words by the method of nikṣepa and adding narratives; ten are ascribed to Bhadrabāhu. Followed by the bhāṣya (verse), cūrṇi (Prakrit-Sanskrit prose) and ṭīkā or vṛtti (Sanskrit) layers.
