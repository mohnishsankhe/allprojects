# jñāna

`trm:jnana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ज्ञान
**Literal:** knowledge

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Knowledge is covered by desire, the constant enemy of the knower (3.38–40); desire is the destroyer of knowledge and realization (jñāna-vijñāna, 3.41); those who carp at Kṛṣṇa's teaching are deluded about all knowledge (3.32).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): A sāttvika disposition of buddhi (SK 23); through knowledge comes liberation (SK 44); in its highest form the complete, pure, single knowledge 'I am not, not mine, not I' (SK 64). Gauḍapāda distinguishes external (scriptural) from internal knowledge (of prakṛti and puruṣa), only the latter liberating.
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Synonym of buddhi (NS 1.1.15); a mark and quality of the self (NS 1.1.10), not of the mind (NS 3.2.19).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4.33–42 and 5.15–17: knowledge — learned from knowers who have seen the truth through prostration, questioning and service (4.34); by it one is not deluded again and sees all beings in the self and in the Lord (4.35); a raft across all wrong (4.36); a fire that burns all actions (4.37); the supreme purifier, found within oneself in time by one perfected in yoga (4.38); obtained by the faithful, intent and sense-restrained, and followed by supreme peace (4.39); it cuts doubt like a sword (4.42). It is covered by ignorance, which deludes creatures; where ignorance is destroyed, knowledge illumines the highest like the sun (5.15–16). 6.8 pairs it with realized knowledge (vijñāna).
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Knowledge of Viṣṇu's supremacy, the five differences and gradation, culminating in direct knowledge (aparokṣa); it wins the abundant grace that alone liberates.
- [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md): Knowledge: as the Lord's power, one of the three; as limited knowing, bondage (Śiva Sūtra 1.2); as right knowledge, the sole cause of liberation (TĀ 1.22).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Knowledge, of five kinds (mati, śruta, avadhi, manaḥparyaya, kevala), the soul's own nature; to be gained before compassion can be rightly exercised.
