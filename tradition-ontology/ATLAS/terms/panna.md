# paññā

`trm:panna` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** wisdom, understanding

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Understanding of the four truths and of rise and fall (SN 48.10); conjoined with consciousness (MN 43); the third training; born of meditation (Dhp 282), inseparable from jhāna (Dhp 372).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Understanding: insight knowledge associated with wholesome consciousness, penetrating dhammas as they are; the third training.
