# hṛdayagranthi

`trm:hrdaya-granthi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** knot of the heart

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The knot(s) of the heart: when they are cut the mortal becomes immortal (KU 2.3.15); when he is seen the knot is cut (MuU 2.2.8); the knot of ignorance (MuU 2.1.10); knots loosened by firm memory (ChU 7.26.2).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The knot of the heart: the ego-knot binding consciousness and the insentient, cut by knowledge (MU 2.2.8).
