# rang stong

`trm:rangtong` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** རང་སྟོང་
**Literal:** empty of self / of own-nature

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): Emptiness of own-nature; for the Jonang it is true of all conventional phenomena but not of the ultimate.
- [Rimé (ris med), the non-sectarian movement](../lineages/rime.md): The Madhyamaka of Nāgārjuna's reasonings, in which every phenomenon including the ultimate is empty of own-nature; in Kongtrul's presentation valid especially for cutting elaborations.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The only correct sense of emptiness: every phenomenon, including the ultimate, is empty of its own inherent existence; the Gelug rejects 'other-emptiness' as a permanent ultimate.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): Gorampa holds the self-empty view in the form of freedom from extremes; Shākya Chokden valued it for cutting conceptual elaboration while also affirming the other-empty view for meditative experience.
