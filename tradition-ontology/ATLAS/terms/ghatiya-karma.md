# ghātiyā karma

`trm:ghatiya-karma` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** घातिया कर्म
**Literal:** destructive karma

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The four karmas that obscure the soul's own qualities — knowledge-obscuring, perception-obscuring, deluding and obstructive; their destruction yields omniscience (TS 10.1).
