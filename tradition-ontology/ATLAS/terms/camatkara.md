# camatkāra

`trm:camatkara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** चमत्कार
**Literal:** wonder, relish

## Definitions by tradition
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): Wonder, the life of rasa (SD 3.3); for Abhinavagupta the self-relishing of consciousness in aesthetic experience; for Viśvanātha's ancestor Nārāyaṇa the reason why the marvellous is the one rasa.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The wondering relish of consciousness in itself; in the Śiva Sūtra 'wonder' marks the stages of yoga (1.12).
