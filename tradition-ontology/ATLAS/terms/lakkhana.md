# lakkhaṇa

`trm:lakkhana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** mark, characteristic

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): (1) The three characteristics impermanence, suffering and not-self; (2) in the commentaries' defining method, the characteristic of a dhamma, given together with its function (rasa), manifestation (paccupaṭṭhāna) and proximate cause (padaṭṭhāna).
