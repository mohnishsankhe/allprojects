# varmam

**Language:** Tamil
**Native script:** வர்மம்

## Definitions by tradition
- [Siddha medicine (cittā maruttuvam)](../lineages/siddha-medicine.md): Vital points of the body where life-breath concentrates; injury to them harms or kills and pressure on them heals. The Siddha tradition counts 108 (12 'striking' paṭu varmam and 96 'touching' toṭu varmam) and ascribes the lore to Agastya.
