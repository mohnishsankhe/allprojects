# bimba

`trm:bimba` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** बिम्ब
**Literal:** original (of a reflection)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): God as the original of which the soul is the reflection; the Lord in one's heart contemplated as 'you are the bimba, I am the reflection'.
- [Vivaraṇa school (Vivaraṇa-prasthāna)](../lineages/vivarana.md): The prototype or original of a reflection; in the Vivaraṇa, Īśvara/Brahman as the original of which the jīva is the reflection.
