# dharma-nairātmya

`trm:dharma-nairatmya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** selflessness of dharmas

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The absence of an imagined own-nature in dharmas themselves, entered through the teaching of cognition-only; knowing it removes the cognitive obscuration.
