# dukkhass' anta

`trm:dukkhassanta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** end of suffering

## Definitions by tradition
- [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md): (Ājīvika, as reported) The end that fool and wise alike reach after the fixed round of 8,400,000 great aeons.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): (Early Buddhist) The end reached by cutting off craving, not by transmigration.
