# śivayoga

`trm:sivayoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शिवयोग
**Literal:** union with Śiva

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Contemplation of Śiva as the self through inner worship of the prāṇaliṅga, culminating in samādhi (SSM 12.21–24); those who see the world as consciousness through it are Śivayogins (11.37–43).
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Yōkam: inner worship and meditation on Śiva (the eightfold or sixfold yoga), the friend's path (sakhā-mārga), leading to sārūpya.
