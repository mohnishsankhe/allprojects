# citriṇī / citrā

`trm:citrini` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** चित्रिणी
**Literal:** the variegated (channel)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): A subtle channel within suṣumnā (within vajrā, per the Ṣaṭcakranirūpaṇa), 'dear to Śiva', in which is the brahmarandhra (SS 2.18).
