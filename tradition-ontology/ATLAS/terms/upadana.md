# upādāna

`trm:upadana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Native script:** उपादान
**Literal:** clinging, fuel

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Four kinds: clinging to sensual pleasures, views, rules and observances, and a doctrine of self (SN 12.2; MN 11); also the 'fuel' by which a fire burns, used of the aggregates.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Clinging: to sense pleasures, views, rules and observances, and a doctrine of self.
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Grasping (the ninth link, fourfold: MMK 26.6) and the aggregates as what is appropriated by the appropriator; self and appropriation are explained like fire and fuel (10.15); grasping at nirvāṇa is a great grasping (16.9).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The second daily period: gathering flowers and other materials for worship.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Grasping; as the ninth limb, running about to obtain pleasures; as defilement group, four kinds.
