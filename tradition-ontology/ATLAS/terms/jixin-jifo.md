# jixin jifo (this very mind is buddha)

`trm:jixin-jifo` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 即心即佛
**Literal:** the mind itself is buddha

## Definitions by tradition
- [Hongzhou school](../lineages/hongzhou.md): Mazu's teaching that the mind right now is buddha, later complemented by 'neither mind nor buddha'.
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): Explained in the Platform Sūtra as 'the previous thought not arising is mind, the next thought not ceasing is buddha'.
- [Sōtō Zen](../lineages/soto.md): Dōgen rejects reading it as the Śreṇika view of an eternal knowing mind (Sokushin zebutsu).
