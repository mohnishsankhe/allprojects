# pratyaya-sarga

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रत्ययसर्ग
**Literal:** creation of cognitions

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The intellectual creation of fifty kinds — five errors, twenty-eight incapacities, nine contentments, eight accomplishments — arising from the inequality of the guṇas (SK 46–51); with the liṅga creation it forms the twofold creation (SK 52).
