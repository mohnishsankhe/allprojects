# Sukhāvatī

`trm:sukhavati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the Blissful (land)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Amitābha's pure land in the west, created by his vows, where beings are born from lotuses and cannot retrogress; one reaches it by aspiration, merit and holding his name.
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): The Land of Peace and Bliss (Anle, Anyang, Jile) of Amitābha, west beyond ten trillion buddha-lands; not included in the three realms; made by Dharmākara's vows; those born there are non-retrogressive.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): Gokuraku, the Pure Land of Amida; the true land of immeasurable light, where birth is at once the attainment of nirvāṇa; distinct from its transformed (provisional) lands.
