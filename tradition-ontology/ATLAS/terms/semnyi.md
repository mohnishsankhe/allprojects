# sems nyid (mind-as-such)

`trm:semnyi` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** སེམས་ཉིད
**Literal:** mind-ness, mind itself

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): Mind itself, the nature of mind, as opposed to the moving, dualistic mind (sems): unborn, empty and luminous; 'the basis of phenomena' (Nāropa); 'mind itself unborn and empty is dharmakāya' (Karṇatantravajrapada).
