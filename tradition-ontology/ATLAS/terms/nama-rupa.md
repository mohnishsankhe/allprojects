# nāma-rūpa

`trm:nama-rupa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नामरूप
**Literal:** name and form

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Name and form, which are placed in the Remainder (AVŚ 11.7.1).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Name and form: the unmanifest became manifest by name and form (BAU 1.4.7); being entered and differentiated name and form (ChU 6.3.2); space accomplishes name and form (ChU 8.14.1); the knower is freed from name and form (MuU 3.2.8).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Name and form: the empirical world as distinct from being–shining–dear; the adjuncts through which Brahman appears as many.

## Forms in other languages
- Pali: nāmarūpa  — partial — Buddhist sense: mentality-materiality
