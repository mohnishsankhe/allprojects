## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Lunar mansion dhaniṣṭhā: presiding deity the Vasus (VJ r.25–27; BS 97.4–5; MC 2.1); Viṃśottarī period-lord Mars (BPHS 46.12); muhūrta class movable (cara) (MC 2.2–8; BS 97.6–9). Vedic Śraviṣṭhā, at whose beginning the Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa places the northward turning (VJ r.6); with Jupiter's rising here the sixty-year cycle begins (BS 8.27).
