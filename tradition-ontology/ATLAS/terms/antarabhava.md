# antarābhava

`trm:antarabhava` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** intermediate existence

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): An existence between death and rebirth — rejected by the Theravāda (Kathāvatthu 8.2), which holds that rebirth-linking follows death immediately.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Intermediate existence between death and birth, having the form of the coming existence, seen by its own kind and the divine eye, feeding on smells.
- [Saṃmitīya](../lineages/sammitiya.md): Accepted (Kathāvatthu commentary on Kv 8.2).
- [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md): Denied (Vasumitra).
- [Mahīśāsaka](../lineages/mahisasaka.md): Denied (Vasumitra).
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): The intermediate state between death and rebirth, in which the yogin practises deity yoga or rests in reality to block rebirth (Tilopa, from Sukhasiddhi).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): bar do, the intermediate state: in the Nāropa system three bardos — of birth-and-death (this life), of dream, and of becoming — in which clear light, illusory body and union are to be recognized.
