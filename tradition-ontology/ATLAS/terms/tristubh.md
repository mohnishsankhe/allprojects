# triṣṭubh

`trm:tristubh` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit

**Literal:** (metre name)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The metre of four eleven-syllable lines, the commonest of the Ṛgveda.
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): A Vedic metre of 44 syllables in its standard form (Piṅgala; Ṛgveda Prātiśākhya); in the Brāhmaṇas correlated with deities and classes (TS 7.1.1.4–6).
