# doṣa

`trm:dosa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दोष
**Literal:** fault; that which vitiates

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): In Āyurveda the three bodily factors vāta, pitta and kapha, which balanced sustain the body and disturbed destroy it (AHS Sū 1.6-7; Ca Sū 1.57), and the two mental doṣas rajas and tamas (Ca Sū 1.57; AHS Sū 1.21). Suśruta counts blood with them in origin, maintenance and dissolution (Su Sū 21.3-4).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Characterized by impelling to activity (NS 1.1.18); three groups: attachment, aversion, delusion (NS 4.1.3).
- [Siddha medicine (cittā maruttuvam)](../lineages/siddha-medicine.md): In Siddha medicine the three humours (Tamil tōṣam, kuṟṟam) — vātam, pittam, kapam — which with the seven tissues make up the ninety-six principles' 'tōṣam 3'.

## Forms in other languages
- Tibetan: nyes pa  — partial — the three nyes pa of Tibetan medicine
- Tamil: tōṣam / kuṟṟam தோஷம் / குற்றம் — partial
- Pali: dosa  — partial — Pali dosa usually 'hatred'; the humours are named individually (pitta, semha, vāta)
