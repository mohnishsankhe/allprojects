# niṣkala / sakala

`trm:niskala-sakala` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निष्कल / सकल
**Literal:** without parts / with parts

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md): Viṣṇu's partless highest form (represented by the fixed image, receiving only flowers) and his partite, gross form (represented by the movable image, receiving full worship).
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Śiva as partless (niṣkala), partite (sakala) and both (sakala-niṣkala, e.g. the liṅga).
- [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md): The supreme Lord as sakala, from whom Śakti, Nāda and Bindu arise.
