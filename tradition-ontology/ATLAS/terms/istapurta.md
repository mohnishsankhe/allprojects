# iṣṭāpūrta

`trm:istapurta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** इष्टापूर्त
**Literal:** what is sacrificed and what is given

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The accumulated merit of sacrifices and gifts, with which the dead are reunited in the highest heaven (RV 10.14.8).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Sacrifices and works of merit (such as digging wells): those who venerate them win the moon and return (ChU 5.10.3; PrU 1.9); fools who think them best go to lower worlds (MuU 1.2.10).
