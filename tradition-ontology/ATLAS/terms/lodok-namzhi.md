**Literal:** the four thoughts that turn the mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): The preliminaries of mind training — the precious human life, death and impermanence, karma, and the faults of saṃsāra (Seven Points 1.1, as the commentaries explain).
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): The topics of the impure vision in the Lamdre preliminaries.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): The precious human life, death and impermanence, karma, and the faults of saṃsāra, contemplated to turn the mind from worldly concerns.
