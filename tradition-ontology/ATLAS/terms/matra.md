# mātrā

`trm:matra` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मात्रा
**Literal:** measure

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): In the Kārikā: measure of Oṃ, identified with a quarter (pāda); their commonality with viśva, taijasa, prājña is: first/obtaining, exaltation/"both-ness", measuring/merging.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): In this text: the measures of Oṃ (a, u, m), each identified with one of the first three quarters.
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): Vowel quantity: short, long and prolated (Aṣṭādhyāyī 1.2.27); one of the six topics of phonetics (TU 1.2).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): A measure of Oṃ: a, u, m and the half-measure; twelve measures in the Nādabindu, sixteen in the Nāradaparivrājaka.
