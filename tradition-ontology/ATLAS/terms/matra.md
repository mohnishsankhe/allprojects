## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): Vowel quantity: short, long and prolated (Aṣṭādhyāyī 1.2.27); one of the six topics of phonetics (TU 1.2).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): A measure of Oṃ: a, u, m and the half-measure; twelve measures in the Nādabindu, sixteen in the Nāradaparivrājaka.
