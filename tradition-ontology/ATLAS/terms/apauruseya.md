# apauruṣeya

`trm:apauruseya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अपौरुषेय
**Literal:** not of personal origin, authorless

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The Veda's character of having no author, not even God; its words and order are eternal.
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The Veda as not composed by any person; the named seers saw or first transmitted it.
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Mīmāṃsā's claim that the Veda has no author, rejected by Nyāya: the Veda is composed with understanding and its validity comes from its reliable author.
