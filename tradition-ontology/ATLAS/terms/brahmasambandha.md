## Definitions by tradition
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): 'Relation with Brahman': the initiation in which the disciple dedicates body, senses, mind, family and wealth to Kṛṣṇa, removing the five kinds of faults (Siddhāntarahasya).
