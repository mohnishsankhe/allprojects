# sandhyā-bhāṣā (sandhā-bhāṣā)

`trm:sandhya-bhasa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): The 'twilight' or intentional language of double meanings in which Bāul songs speak of body practice and the Man of the Heart, so that the uninitiated hear only devotional or everyday words.
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): The coded language of the Sahajiyā manuals and padas.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): 'Twilight' (intentional) language of the siddhas, shared by Nāth and Buddhist siddha songs; the Nāth vernacular form is the ulaṭbāṃsī.
