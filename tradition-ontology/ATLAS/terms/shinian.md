# shinian

`trm:shinian` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 十念
**Literal:** ten recollections / ten thoughts

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): The 'ten recitations' of the eighteenth vow and of the lowest grade: ten continuous thoughts of recollection without other thoughts, not needing to be counted (Tanluan, Daochuo); ten voicings of the name (Shandao); ten breaths of continuous recitation each morning (Zunshi).
