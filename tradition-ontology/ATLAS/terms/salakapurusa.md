# śalākāpuruṣa

`trm:salakapurusa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शलाकापुरुष
**Literal:** person marked (with a rod / tally)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): An 'illustrious person' of the universal history: in each half-cycle sixty-three — 24 Tīrthaṅkaras, 12 cakravartins, 9 baladevas, 9 vāsudevas and 9 prativāsudevas.
