# Taijasa

`trm:taijasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तैजस
**Literal:** the luminous

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The form of Viṣṇu that, abiding in the mind, creates the soul's dreams.
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Ahaṃkāra in its rājasa aspect, from which both groups (organs and subtle elements) proceed (SK 25).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): 'The brilliant one': the second quarter of the self, whose field is dream (MāU 4).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The self as experiencer of dream, identified with the subtle body (counterpart of Hiraṇyagarbha).
