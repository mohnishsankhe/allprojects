# prātibha

`trm:pratibha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रातिभ
**Literal:** intuition, flash of insight

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Intuition, forerunner of knowledge born of discrimination, like dawn before sunrise (3.33, YBh 3.33); with divine hearing, touch, sight, taste and smell arising from knowledge of the puruṣa (3.36).
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): Kṣemarāja glosses the 'upsurge' that is Bhairava (Śiva Sūtra 1.5) as the flash of the supreme I-awareness.
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): The seers' kind of knowledge occasionally arising in ordinary people (Praśastapāda).
