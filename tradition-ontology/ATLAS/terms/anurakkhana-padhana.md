# anurakkhaṇāppadhāna

`trm:anurakkhana-padhana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** striving to maintain

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Generating desire and effort to maintain and increase arisen wholesome states (SN 45.8). One of the thirty-seven qualities conducive to awakening (DN 16.3.50; MN 149): a member of the four right efforts.
