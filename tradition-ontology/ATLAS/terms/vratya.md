# vrātya

`trm:vratya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** व्रात्य
**Literal:** one bound by a vow / of a band

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The vow-bound wanderer of Atharvaveda book 15 who stirs Prajāpati, becomes Mahādeva and Īśāna, and is honoured as guest.
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): A member of groups living outside the Vedic sacrificial order, who may be admitted by the Vrātya rites (PB 17.1–4).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): A twice-born whose initiation has lapsed ('fallen from the Sāvitrī', MDh 2.39).
