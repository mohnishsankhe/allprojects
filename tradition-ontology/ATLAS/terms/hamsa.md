# haṃsa

`trm:hamsa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** हंस
**Literal:** swan/goose

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The swan seated in the bright, in men, in ṛta, in the sky (RV 4.40.5); in the Atharvaveda the swan rising from the waters that never lifts one foot is Prāṇa (AVŚ 11.4.21).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Goose, swan: the self or God as the goose seated in the bright (KU 2.2.2); the goose that wanders in the wheel (ŚU 1.6); flutters outward in the city of nine gates (ŚU 3.18); the one goose in the midst of the world (6.15).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The self moving with the breath (ha out, sa in), meditated as a bird and repeated naturally 21,600 times a day; also the third kind of renouncer.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The mantra the living being repeats with each breath, going out with sa and in with ha (VBT 155b).
