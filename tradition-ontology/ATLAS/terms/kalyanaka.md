# kalyāṇaka

`trm:kalyanaka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कल्याणक
**Literal:** auspicious (event)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): One of the five 'auspicious events' in every Tīrthaṅkara's life — descent into the womb (cyavana/garbha), birth (janma), renunciation (dīkṣā), omniscience (kevala-jñāna) and final liberation (nirvāṇa) — celebrated by the gods and re-enacted in temple consecrations and festivals.
