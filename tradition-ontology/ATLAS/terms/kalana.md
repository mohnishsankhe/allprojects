# kalana

`trm:kalana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कलन
**Literal:** reckoning, impelling, devouring

## Definitions by tradition
- [Krama (the 'Sequence'; Mahānaya, Mahārtha)](../lineages/krama.md): The activity of consciousness named by the root kal: projecting (kṣepa), knowing, counting, moving, sounding (TĀ 4.173).
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The act of devouring (grasana) that gives Mahākāla and Kālī their names (Mahānirvāṇa 4.31-32 with Hariharānanda's gloss).
