# tattvābhyāsa

`trm:tattvabhyasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तत्त्वाभ्यास
**Literal:** repeated practice of the principles

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The repeated contemplation of the principles from which the liberating knowledge arises (SK 64); described by Vācaspati as long, reverent, uninterrupted meditative cultivation after hearing and reasoning, and by the Sāṃkhya Sūtra as renouncing each with 'not this, not this' (SS 3.76).

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [abhyāsa](abhyasa.md) — Vācaspati's description of tattvābhyāsa (long, reverent, uninterrupted) echoes Yoga's definition of firm practice (YS 1.14); Sāṃkhya's practice has the principles as its content.
