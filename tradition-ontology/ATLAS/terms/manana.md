# manana

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मनन
**Literal:** reflection

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Reasoned reflection on what was heard, removing doubts (e.g. with companions); a principal means to knowledge.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): In the Śiva Purāṇa the third and greatest of the three means (hearing, singing, reflecting on Śiva).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Reflection: continual consideration of the teaching by reasoning in accord with it, removing doubts about the object.
