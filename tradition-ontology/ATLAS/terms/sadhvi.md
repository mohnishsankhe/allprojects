# sādhvī

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** साध्वी

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): A fully initiated Jain nun with the five great vows; in Śvetāmbara orders nuns outnumber monks, are led by a pravartinī and follow rules such as never going unclothed.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The Digambara nun is called āryikā; she keeps one white sari and is not regarded as fully without possessions.
