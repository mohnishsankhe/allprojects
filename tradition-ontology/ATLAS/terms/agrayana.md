# agrayāna

`trm:agrayana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the foremost vehicle

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The supreme vehicle for which the Vajracchedikā is taught, not hearable by those of low resolve.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Used in the Māṇḍūkya-kārikā (4.90) for the teaching from which what is to be abandoned, known, attained and matured is learned; Śaṅkara glosses it as the foremost means.
