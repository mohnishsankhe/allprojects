## Definitions by tradition
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Tantras of the yoginīs (also 'yoganiruttara', 'the unexcelled yoga'): the Cakrasaṃvara, Hevajra, Caṇḍamahāroṣaṇa and related cycles, with female deities, cremation-ground imagery and the yoga of bliss (later Tibetan 'mother tantras').
