# śānta-rasa

`trm:santa-rasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the peaceful rasa

## Definitions by tradition
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): The rasa of peace, leading to liberation, whose stable emotion is calm (śama, NŚ śānta passage; SD 3.245), dispassion (nirveda, KP 4) or knowledge of reality — the self itself (Abhinavagupta); denied as a dramatic rasa by Dhanañjaya.
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The peaceful devotional rasa, awareness of the Lord's greatness without personal intimacy (as of the sages); the first of the five primary rasas.
