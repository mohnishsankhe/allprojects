# abhihitānvaya

`trm:abhihitanvaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अभिहितान्वय
**Literal:** connection of the expressed

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): Words first express their own meanings and cease; the expressed meanings then connect to yield the sentence-meaning, as the flame is an intermediate stage in cooking with wood (Śabara on MS 1.1.25; ŚV vākya 342–343).
