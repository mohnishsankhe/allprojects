# ahaṅkāra

`trm:ahankara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अहङ्कार
**Literal:** 'I-maker'; the sense of 'I'

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The sense of 'I' by which one whose self is deluded thinks 'I am the doer', though actions are done by the guṇas of prakṛti (3.27); the one who attains peace is without it (nirahaṃkāra, 2.71).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: The sense of 'I': the devotee dear to the Lord is without it (nirahaṅkāra), as also without 'mine' (nirmama) (12.13).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: The sense of 'I', listed among the constituents of the field (13.6); its absence (anahaṃkāra) is among the items called knowledge (13.9).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: the sense of 'I' (ahaṅkāra) is the eighth member of the Lord's lower, eightfold nature, named after the five elements, mind and intellect (7.4).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The ego, the I-maker: the inner organ as appropriating ('I', 'mine'), the knot of superimposition of consciousness and the insentient.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: The sense of 'I': the demonic resort to it with force, arrogance, desire and anger (16.18); it joins hypocrisy in unsanctioned austerity (17.5); action done with it is rājasic (18.24); one without the sense 'I did it' (ahaṃkṛta bhāva) does not slay though slaying (18.17); letting go of it is part of the discipline that makes one fit to become Brahman (18.53); if from it Arjuna does not listen he will perish, and his resolve 'I will not fight' made from it is vain (18.58–59).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The false notion of 'I' regarding body and the like, which ceases through knowledge of the truth about the causes of the faults (NS 4.2.1) and through contemplation of the body within (NBh 4.2.2).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): An evolute of prakṛti; distinct from the true 'I' (aham-artha), which is the self; the false identification of body as self is the obstacle.
