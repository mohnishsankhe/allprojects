# ambuvācī

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अम्बुवाची

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): The three days in the month of Āṣāḍha (June) when the Earth — and at Kāmākhyā the Goddess — is held to menstruate; the Kāmākhyā temple is closed and reopens on the fourth day (the Ambubachi Melā). The Prāṇatoṣiṇī lists 'ambuvācī, three days' in its festival calendar.
