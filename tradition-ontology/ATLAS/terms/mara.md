# Māra

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the Killer, the Evil One

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The tempter who obstructs the bodhisattva; four Māras are named in Mahāyāna texts (of the aggregates, the defilements, death and the god-son Māra); in the Śūraṅgama demonic states arise from the aggregates.
