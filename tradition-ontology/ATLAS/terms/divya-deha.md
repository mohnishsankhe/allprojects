# divyadeha / divyatanu

`trm:divya-deha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दिव्यदेह
**Literal:** divine body

## Definitions by tradition
- [Rasa Śāstra (the science of mercury) and the Raseśvara-darśana](../lineages/rasa-sastra.md): The body 'born of the creation of Hara and Gaurī' (mercury and mica), permanent though the six-sheathed body is not; to be produced first by the yogin seeking jīvanmukti (SDS Raseśvara 7-9; Rasahṛdaya 1.7).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The divine body of the perfected yogī (a Nāth synonym of siddha-deha).
