**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The vehicle that carries all beings to full awakening; by its own account the Buddha's highest teaching, contrasted with the 'lesser' vehicle (hīnayāna) of those seeking personal nirvāṇa.
