# rang grol

`trm:rangdrol` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** རང་གྲོལ
**Literal:** self-liberation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): Self-liberation: thoughts and appearances liberate by themselves when recognized in awareness, without an antidote; with primordial liberation (ye grol), liberation on arising (shar grol), naked liberation (cer grol) and liberation from extremes (mtha' grol) it forms the modes of liberation.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): Self-liberation: thoughts and appearances liberate themselves in their own place when their nature is seen; 'the self-liberated dharmakāya' (Karṇatantravajrapada); in the Shangpa, the 'three self-liberations' of the Amulet Mahāmudrā.
