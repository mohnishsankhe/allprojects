# śruti

`trm:sruti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** श्रुति
**Literal:** what is heard

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The Veda (MDh 2.10), the supreme authority for those seeking to know dharma (MDh 2.13).
- [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md): One of the twenty-two audible pitch-divisions of the octave, produced in twenty-two channels at the heart (ŚR 1.3.8–9); the basis from which the svaras arise.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: In śrutivipratipannā (2.53): the understanding perplexed by what it has heard, which is to stand immovable in samādhi; cf. nirveda towards what is to be heard and what has been heard (2.52).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The Veda as heard revelation, transmitted by hearing from teacher to pupil.
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The Veda, as distinct from smṛti; also, in the theory of application, direct statement (see trm:sruti-mimamsa).
