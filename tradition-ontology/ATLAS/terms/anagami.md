**Language:** Pali

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): MN 118:10: a monk who, with the five lower fetters ended, is reborn spontaneously, attains final nibbāna there and is not liable to return from that world.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): One who has abandoned the five lower fetters and is reborn spontaneously in the pure abodes, attaining nibbāna there without returning (MN 64; MN 10).
