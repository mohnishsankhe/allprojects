# tattvajñāna

`trm:tattvajnana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तत्त्वज्ञान
**Literal:** knowledge of truth

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Knowledge of the true nature of the categories — in the science of the self, of the self and the rest (NBh 1.1.1); it removes false knowledge (NS 1.1.2, 4.2.35) and arises from the practice of samādhi (NS 4.2.38).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Knowledge of the similarities and dissimilarities of the categories, arising from dharma (Praśastapāda).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13.12: keeping in view the aim (artha) of the knowledge of reality is the last of the dispositions called knowledge.
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): Knowledge of reality, which Abhinavagupta makes the stable emotion of śānta: it is the self itself (Abhinavabhāratī).
