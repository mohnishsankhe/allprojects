# adhikāra

`trm:adhikara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अधिकार
**Literal:** entitlement, eligibility

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Eligibility for knowledge and scripture, in three grades (low, middle, highest) by birth and by qualities; the highest is the dispassionate renunciant devoted to Viṣṇu. Eligibility to Veda, Tantra or the Name is distinguished by class and gender.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: In 2.47: one's right or proper claim, which extends to action alone and never to its fruits.
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The qualification of the agent entitled to perform a rite and enjoy its fruit: a human (MS 6.1.4–5), man or woman — husband and wife jointly (MS 6.1.8, 6.1.17) — of the three higher varṇas, with Vedic learning (MS 6.1.25–38); the subject of adhyāya 6.
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Fitness for a practice: the Śiva Saṃhitā assigns the four yogas to four grades of aspirant (5.10–14); the Jyotsnā holds the dispassionate alone properly eligible though Purāṇic verses grant yoga to all beings; the HYP and DYŚ say anyone who practises succeeds.
