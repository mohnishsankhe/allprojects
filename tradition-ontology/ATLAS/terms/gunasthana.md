**Native script:** गुणस्थान
**Literal:** stage of quality

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): One of fourteen stages of the soul's purification from wrong view to the omniscient without activity, defined by the rise, subsidence and destruction of deluding and other karmas (Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama; Sarvārthasiddhi 1.8; Gommaṭasāra).
