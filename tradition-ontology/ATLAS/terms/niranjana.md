# nirañjana

`trm:niranjana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निरञ्जन
**Literal:** without collyrium, stainless

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): 'Spotless': the supreme free of all stain and states, abiding subtly within the mind (AmŚ 39–40); one of the synonyms of the highest state (HYP 4.4).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The spotless one beyond the centres, known by piercing them (Yogaviṣaya 10).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The stainless state reached by devotion to Śiva the guru (HYP 4.1), a synonym of samādhi (4.4), in which mind and breath dissolve (4.105).
- [Nirañjanī sampradāya](../lineages/niranjani.md): The stainless formless ultimate, the name of God for the Nirañjanīs, as for the Nāth yogīs ('alakh nirañjan').
- [Dādū Panth](../lineages/dadu-panth.md): The stainless one whom Dādū salutes first in his Vāṇī.
- [Kabīr Panth](../lineages/kabir-panth.md): In the Dharamdāsī literature, Nirañjan is Kāl, the fallen creator of the three worlds (see trm:kal-niranjan).

## Forms in other languages
- Hindi: nirañjan  — exact
- Bengali: nirañjan  — partial — in Bengal also a name of the deity Dharma

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- contested: [Kāl Nirañjan (Kāl)](kal-niranjan.md) — The same name is the highest for Nāths, Nirañjanīs and Dādū, and a lower, deceiving power for the Kabīr Panth and Sant Mat; see dsp:status-of-niranjan.
