## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: The Lord's lower nature, divided eightfold: earth, water, fire, air, space, mind (manas), intellect (buddhi) and the sense of 'I' (ahaṅkāra) (7.4–5); with the higher nature it is the source of all beings (7.6).
