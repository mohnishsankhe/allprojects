# divyadhvani

`trm:divyadhvani` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दिव्यध्वनि
**Literal:** divine sound

## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The 'divine sound' of the omniscient Jina: a single, undifferentiated sound issuing from his whole body at set times, without movement of lips, which the gaṇadharas render into scripture and all beings understand.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Śvetāmbaras speak rather of the Jina's speech in Ardhamāgadhī, understood by all beings in their own languages.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The Tīrthaṅkara's teaching-sound, understood by every being in its own language (Digambara: a sound without syllables emanating from his body).
