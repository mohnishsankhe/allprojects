# kun gzhi rgyu rgyud

`trm:kunzhi-gyu-gyu` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** ཀུན་གཞི་རྒྱུ་རྒྱུད
**Literal:** the all-base causal continuum

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): The first of the three continua: the all-base (ālaya), the union of clarity and emptiness, which contains saṃsāra and nirvāṇa and is therefore the 'root continuum'.
