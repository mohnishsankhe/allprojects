# āṇava mala

`trm:anava-mala` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आणवमल
**Literal:** the impurity of atomicity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The impurity of individuality: loss of freedom by awareness, or awareness without freedom (ĪPK 3.2.4); 'limited knowledge is bondage' (Śiva Sūtra 1.2).
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The impurity by whose contact the self, resting in the inner organ, has become atomic (aṇu) (SSM 18.5–7).
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): The innate (sahaja) impurity of the soul, one in substance with countless powers, beginningless, covering its powers of knowing and acting; it ripens and is removed by Śiva's power in initiation.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Āṇavam, the 'dark mala' (iruḷ mala) which hides even itself; never destroyed, only rendered powerless by grace.

## Forms in other languages
- Tamil: āṇavam / iruḷ  — exact
