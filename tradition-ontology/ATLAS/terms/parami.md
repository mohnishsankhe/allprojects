# pāramī

`trm:parami` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** perfection

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The ten perfections fulfilled by a bodhisatta — giving, virtue, renunciation, wisdom, energy, patience, truthfulness, determination, loving-kindness, equanimity (Bv 2.116–165).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The perfections fulfilled by a Bodhisatta (ten in the Theravāda list), treated systematically in Dhammapāla's commentary on the Cariyāpiṭaka.
