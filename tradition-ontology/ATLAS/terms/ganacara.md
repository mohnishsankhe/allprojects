# gaṇācāra

`trm:ganacara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** conduct for the community (gaṇa)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Defence of the faith and the devotees against insult and harm; the SSM bids the devotee protect liṅgas and devotees even at the cost of life and, on meeting a reviler of Śiva, to have him killed, curse him or leave (9.33–36).
