# Tārā

`trm:tara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तारा
**Literal:** she who ferries across

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The second Mahāvidyā; Akṣobhya is her Bhairava because he drank the poison without agitation (Toḍala 1.4-6); the Toḍala makes her the Fish avatāra; linked with Mahācīna and the Buddha (Rudrayāmala 17; Tārā Tantra 1).

## Forms in other languages
- Tibetan: sgrol ma (Drölma) སྒྲོལ་མ — partial — the Buddhist saviour goddess Tārā; the Hindu tantras themselves place Tārā worship in Mahācīna with the Buddha
