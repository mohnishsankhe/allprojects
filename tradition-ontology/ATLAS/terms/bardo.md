**Language:** Tibetan
**Literal:** between-two

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): Intermediate state: any interval between two states — six bardos (of life, dream, meditation, dying, dharmatā and becoming), or four in the Heart-essence (natural bardo of life, of dying, of dharmatā, of becoming).

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: antarābhava  — partial

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [antarābhava](antarabhava.md) — the bardo of becoming is the Abhidharma's intermediate existence; the Nyingma extend 'bardo' to every intermediate state
