## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The central island-continent with Meru at its centre, divided into nine varṣas, Bhārata being the southern one.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The central, round continent of the middle world, 100,000 yojanas across, with Meru at its centre (TS 3.9).
