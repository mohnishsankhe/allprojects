# tyāga

`trm:tyaga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** त्याग
**Literal:** relinquishment

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Relinquishing the fruit of all actions (BhG 18.2), threefold according to the guṇas (18.7–9); the embodied cannot give up all action, and so the one who gives up the fruit is the relinquisher (18.11); one of the 'three horses of Brahman' (MBh 11.7.19; 5.43.14).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Relinquishment: giving up the fruit of all actions, prescribed for one unable even to work for the Lord (12.11); ranked above meditation, with peace following immediately (12.12).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The eighth virtue: giving up, including giving knowledge and other gifts (TS 9.6).
