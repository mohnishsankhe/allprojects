# nitya

`trm:nitya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नित्य
**Literal:** eternal, constant

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Epithet of the embodied one (2.18, 2.20, 2.21, 2.24).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): 'The eternal ones': the fifteen goddesses of the lunar days (Kāmeśvarī to Citrā) around Tripurasundarī, the sixteenth (Mahānityā); they are the unfolding of time (Bhāvanā Upaniṣad).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): One of the fifteen lunar goddesses of Tripurā.
