# sūkṣma-śarīra

`trm:suksma-sarira` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सूक्ष्मशरीर
**Literal:** subtle body

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The subtle bodies, constant, as against the perishing bodies born of mother and father (SK 39); denied by Vindhyavāsin (Yuktidīpikā on SK 39).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The subtle body of seventeen members — five organs of knowledge, five of action, five prāṇas, mind and intellect — made of unquintuplicated elements; it transmigrates.
