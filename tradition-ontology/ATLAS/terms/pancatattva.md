# pañcatattva

`trm:pancatattva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पञ्चतत्त्व
**Literal:** the five elements/realities

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Wine, meat, fish, mudrā (parched grain) and maithuna, the five 'realities' of Śakti worship (Mahānirvāṇa 1.57; 5.22). Restricted: summary only.
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): In the Kālīvilāsa's view not to be used in the Kali age, when only the paśu disposition is allowed (Kālīvilāsa 4.2-3).
