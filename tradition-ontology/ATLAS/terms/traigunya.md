# traiguṇya

`trm:traigunya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** त्रैगुण्य
**Literal:** the triad of guṇas

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The three guṇas taken together, of which prakṛti and the manifest consist (SK 14; Tattvasamāsa 4).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The domain of the three guṇas, which is the Vedas' domain; Arjuna is told to be free of it (BhG 2.45).
