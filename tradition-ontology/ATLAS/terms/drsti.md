# dṛṣṭi

`trm:drsti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दृष्टि
**Literal:** gaze; sight

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The fixed gaze of the yogin — at the nose-tip, between the brows, or outward while attending to the inner target (śāmbhavī); in trāṭaka it is unblinking.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): View; as a latent defilement, five: satkāya-view, view of extremes, wrong view, attachment to views, attachment to rules and observances.

## Forms in other languages
- Pali: diṭṭhi  — exact
- Chinese: jian 見 — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- exact: [diṭṭhi](ditthi.md) — Sanskrit and Pali forms of the same canonical term; school definitions differ (see the per-lineage definitions).
