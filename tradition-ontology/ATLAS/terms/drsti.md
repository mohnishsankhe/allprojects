# dṛṣṭi

`trm:drsti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दृष्टि
**Literal:** view, (speculative) opinion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Speculative views such as eternalism and annihilationism, rooted in accepting real existents (YṢ 46–48); emptiness is the relinquishing of all views, and the Buddha taught for their abandonment (MMK 13.8; 27.30).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The fixed gaze of the yogin — at the nose-tip, between the brows, or outward while attending to the inner target (śāmbhavī); in trāṭaka it is unblinking.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): lta ba, the view: in the view–meditation–conduct–fruition scheme, the recognition of mind's nature; 'beyond grasper and grasped is the king of views' (Gaṅgā Mahāmudrā); a mistaken view misdirects meditation and conduct (Nāropa).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): View; as a latent defilement, five: satkāya-view, view of extremes, wrong view, attachment to views, attachment to rules and observances.
