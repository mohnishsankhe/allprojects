# yukti

`trm:yukti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** युक्ति
**Literal:** joining; reasoned application

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Caraka's fourth means of knowledge: the understanding that sees things produced by the conjunction of many causes, valid for the three times, by which the three aims are achieved (Ca Sū 11.25); also the right application of medicine to place, time and person (Sū 1.123-127).
