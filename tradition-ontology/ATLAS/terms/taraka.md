# tāraka

`trm:taraka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तारक
**Literal:** that which carries across; deliverer

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Knowledge born of discrimination as arising from one's own intuition, not from instruction (3.54, YBh 3.54); also a name of intuition (YBh 3.33).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): (1) The light between the brows and the yoga of targets that carries across saṃsāra; (2) the mantra Rudra gives the dying at Avimukta (Oṃ, the Rāma mantra or the Nārāyaṇa mantra).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): What saves (carries across), which those confused by Āgamas, Vedas and logic do not know (HYP 4.40); in the Yoga Upaniṣads a form of rājayoga.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): The Rāma name as the ferrying mantra Śiva gives those who die at Kāśī.
