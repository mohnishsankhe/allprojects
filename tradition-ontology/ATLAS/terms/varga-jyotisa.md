# varga (divisional chart)

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वर्ग
**Literal:** group; division

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): A division of each sign into equal parts, each ruled by a planet, forming a separate chart: six in Varāhamihira (sign, horā, drekkāṇa, navāṃśa, twelfth, thirtieth: BJ 1.9), sixteen in Parāśara (BPHS 6.2–4).
