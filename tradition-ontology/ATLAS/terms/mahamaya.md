# mahāmāyā

`trm:mahamaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** महामाया
**Literal:** the great māyā

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md): The Goddess as the great power of māyā, who makes the universe whirl yet is the queen of the supreme Brahman (SL 98), who drags even the wise into delusion and when pleased grants liberation (Devī Māhātmya 1).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The Goddess as the power that deludes even the wise and creates the world, and when pleased grants liberation.
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): The deluding aspect of the root Nature, by which creation proceeds (Bṛhaddharma madhya 6.102); in Bengal and Assam a common name of the Goddess.
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Another name of bindu, the pure material cause.
