# anyathākhyāti

`trm:anyathakhyati` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अन्यथाख्याति
**Literal:** apprehension otherwise; misplaced apprehension

## Definitions by tradition
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): The Nyāya theory of error: a thing is cognized as qualified by a property it lacks — the silver seen elsewhere is presented in the mother-of-pearl.
