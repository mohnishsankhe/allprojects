# satta kāyā

`trm:satta-kaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** seven bodies / substances

## Definitions by tradition
- [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md): (As reported) Pakudha's seven unmade, barren, unmoving bodies: earth, water, fire, air, pleasure, pain and soul.
- [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md): (As reported) The same seven, called eternal and uncuttable, in the Ājīvika-like doctrine of Guṇa.
