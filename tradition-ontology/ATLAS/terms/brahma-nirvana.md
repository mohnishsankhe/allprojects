# brahma-nirvāṇa

`trm:brahma-nirvana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ब्रह्मनिर्वाण
**Literal:** the extinction/bliss that is Brahman

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The goal reached by one established in the brāhmī state even at death (BhG 2.72) and by sages free of faults, delighting in the welfare of beings, for whom it lies near on every side (5.24–26).
