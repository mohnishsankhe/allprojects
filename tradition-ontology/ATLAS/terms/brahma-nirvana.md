# brahmanirvāṇa

`trm:brahma-nirvana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ब्रह्मनिर्वाण
**Literal:** the extinction/bliss that is Brahman

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: What one attains who abides in the brahmic state even at the time of death (2.72); first use of the term in the text.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The goal reached by one established in the brāhmī state even at death (BhG 2.72) and by sages free of faults, delighting in the welfare of beings, for whom it lies near on every side (5.24–26).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: The goal attained by the yogin who has inner happiness, delight and light and has become Brahman (5.24), by seers whose impurities are destroyed and dualities cut, devoted to the welfare of all beings (5.25); it is near on every side for self-knowing ascetics free of desire and anger (5.26). Cf. 'peace culminating in nirvāṇa, abiding in me' (6.15).
