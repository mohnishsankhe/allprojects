# tanmātra

`trm:tanmatra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तन्मात्र
**Literal:** 'that only'; subtle element

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The five non-specific (aviśeṣa) elements — of sound, touch, form, taste and smell — produced from ahaṃkāra as bhūtādi (SK 25, 38), from which the five gross elements arise (SK 22).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Subtle (unquintuplicated) elements arising from Brahman-with-māyā in the order space, air, fire, water, earth (TU 2.1), material of the subtle body.

## Forms in other languages
- Chinese: wuwei ('the five only-s') 五唯 — exact — as used in Chinese Buddhist accounts of Sāṃkhya (Paramārtha, Kuiji); recalled, verify
