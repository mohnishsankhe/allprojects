# pādōdaka

`trm:padodaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पादोदक
**Literal:** foot-water

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Water sanctified by washing the feet of the guru or jaṅgama, received as one of the eight shields; the SSM also reads it as the oneness of guru ('foot') and disciple ('water') through initiation (19.6–7).
