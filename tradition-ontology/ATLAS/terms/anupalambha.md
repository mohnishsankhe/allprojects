# anupalambha

`trm:anupalambha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** non-apprehension

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Not finding or grasping any dharma as an object — the way the bodhisattva courses in the perfection of wisdom; rejoicing and dedication are done 'without apprehension'.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The meditative non-apprehension of objects, and then of mind, by which the dharmadhātu is apprehended; understanding (parijñā) of the three natures is non-apprehension.
