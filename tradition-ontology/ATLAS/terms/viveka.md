# viveka

`trm:viveka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विवेक
**Literal:** discrimination

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Discriminating knowledge of the manifest, the unmanifest and the knower (SK 2), which buddhi accomplishes (SK 37); it is accomplished through practice of the principles, renouncing each with 'not this, not this' (SS 3.76).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Seclusion of body and mind; the first jhāna is born of seclusion (DN 2); a monk who delights in company cannot reach liberation of mind (MN 122).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Discrimination between the eternal and the non-eternal: the firm conviction 'Brahman is real, the world is false' (VC 20); also the discernment of self from not-self.
