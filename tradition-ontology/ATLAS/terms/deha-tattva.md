## Definitions by tradition
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): The 'truth of the body': Bāul body-theology, in which the body contains the cosmos, the gods and the Man, and is the only field of practice.
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): The Sahajiyā doctrine of the inner body with its lakes and lotuses as the eternal Vṛndāvana.
