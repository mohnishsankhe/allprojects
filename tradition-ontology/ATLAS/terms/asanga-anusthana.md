# asaṅga-anuṣṭhāna

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Religious activity that flows spontaneously without attachment, in the prabhā view; Haribhadra says others call it praśāntavāhitā, visabhāga-parikṣaya, śivavartman and dhruvādhvan (YDS).
