# pratiṣṭhā

`trm:pratistha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रतिष्ठा
**Literal:** establishment, installation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Installation and consecration of liṅga, image or temple, to be performed with a single root āgama.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Consecration of images and temples (e.g. the Hayaśīrṣa's subject).
- [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md): Installation of the fixed and movable images.
- [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md): Installation of the seven deities, performed only by the tantri.
