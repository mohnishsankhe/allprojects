## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Equanimity: the fourth brahmavihāra (abandoning aversion, MN 62), the seventh factor of awakening, the purity of the fourth jhāna; equanimity based on unity abandons that based on diversity (MN 137); the tenth perfection (Bv 2).
