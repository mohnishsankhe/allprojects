# māyādeha

`trm:mayadeha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The illusory body, of wind and mind, like a reflection or a rainbow; the conventional truth that joins with clear light (Pañcakrama; Tilopa's Ṣaḍdharmopadeśa).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): sgyu lus, illusory body: (1) the practice of seeing all appearances and one's body as illusion (mirror image, rainbow, moon in water); (2) the pure illusory body of the deity arising from clear light.
