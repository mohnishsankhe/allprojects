# sādhaka

`trm:sadhaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** साधक
**Literal:** adept, one who accomplishes

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): An initiate consecrated to master a mantra for supernatural powers and enjoyments (bubhukṣu).
- [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md): In the Dāsbodh: The practitioner who takes up hearing, reflection and devotion under the guru.
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): The practitioner's stage, relying on bhāva.
