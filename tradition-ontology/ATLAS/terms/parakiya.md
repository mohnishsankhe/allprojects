# parakīyā

`trm:parakiya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): Love for one who is not one's spouse, taken as the model of the practitioner's relation with the partner, because it is free of social motive and full of risk and longing — recorded in summary only (restricted context).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Paramour love: love for Kṛṣṇa by those married to others, held by Rūpa (UN 1.21) to be the supreme form in Vraja; its status is the subject of the svakīyā–parakīyā debate.
