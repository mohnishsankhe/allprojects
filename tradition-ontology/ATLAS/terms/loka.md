# loka

`trm:loka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** लोक
**Literal:** open space; world

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): A world or realm: the world of the living, of the fathers, of the well-doers, the immortal world (RV 9.113.7).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): A world or plane of beings; the Purāṇas count fourteen within the egg - seven above from Bhūr to Satya and seven below.
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): A sphere of existence or post-mortem state; the sacrificer constructs his future world through rites — 'a man is born into the world he has made' (ŚB 6.2.2.27).
