# yogakṣema

`trm:yogaksema` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** योगक्षेम
**Literal:** acquisition and preservation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Arjuna is told to be free of concern for acquiring and keeping (niryogakṣema) (2.45).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: What the Lord himself carries (vahāmi) for those who, with nothing else, think of him and worship him, ever devoted (9.22).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Gain and security, whose burden the Lord bears for those who worship him with undivided minds (BhG 9.22); Arjuna is told to be free of acquiring and keeping (niryogakṣema, 2.45).
