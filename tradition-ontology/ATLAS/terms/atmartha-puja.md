## Definitions by tradition
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Private worship by an initiate in the liṅga given by the guru or on a ground-altar, for one's own fruit.
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Worship of Śiva for oneself, obligatory daily for the initiate.
