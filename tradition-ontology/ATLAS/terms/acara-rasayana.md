# ācāra-rasāyana

`trm:acara-rasayana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आचाररसायन
**Literal:** rejuvenation by conduct

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The name later given to Caraka's teaching that the truthful, non-angry, non-violent, calm, generous, devout and self-controlled person 'enjoys constant rasāyana' and alone gains the full benefits of rasāyana (Ca Ci 1.4.30-35; AHS Utt 39.179-181: 'nityarasāyana').
