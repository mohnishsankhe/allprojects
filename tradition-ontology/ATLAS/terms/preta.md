# preta

`trm:preta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रेत
**Literal:** one who has gone forth; the departed

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The newly dead before being joined with the ancestors; one whose rites are lacking (e.g. no vṛṣotsarga) or who did great wrong remains a preta, hungry and thirsty, troubling kin.
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The newly dead, not yet united with the ancestors, sustained by the first-year offerings until the sapiṇḍīkaraṇa.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): Beings who, by shared karma, all see a river of pus where others see water — the Viṃśatikā's example of intersubjective agreement without external objects.
