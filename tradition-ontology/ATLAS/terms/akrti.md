# ākṛti

`trm:akrti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आकृति
**Literal:** form; universal

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The universal, which is what a word means, because Vedic action is directed to it (MS 1.3.33).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): What is called the mark of the universal (NS 2.2.70 DCS).
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): Form, universal: Vājapyāyana held that words denote the ākṛti (Mahābhāṣya on P 1.2.64).
