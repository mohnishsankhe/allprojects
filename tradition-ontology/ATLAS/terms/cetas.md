# cetas

`trm:cetas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** चेतस्
**Literal:** thought, awareness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Awareness (cetas): the hardship is greater for those whose cetas is attached to the unmanifest (12.5); the Lord soon delivers from the ocean of death and saṃsāra those whose cetas is set on him (12.7); seeing the gentle form again, Arjuna is 'sacetāḥ', with his cetas restored (11.51). Named distinctly from manas, buddhi and citta.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: Thought: those who have not formed themselves and lack thought (acetas) do not see the one abiding in themselves, even though they strive (15.11).
