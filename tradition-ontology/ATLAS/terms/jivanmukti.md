# jīvanmukti

`trm:jivanmukti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** जीवन्मुक्ति
**Literal:** liberation while living

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Liberation while living: in the Yoga Vāsiṣṭha the seventh stage, in which one acts as custom requires, unimmersed in pleasure and pain, the mind 'dead' (manonāśa).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The liberated one, though in the body, is not in it; his functions are free of intention; the body lives on while its karma lasts.
- [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md): The destruction of ignorance, the fruit of human birth and the state of liberation while living.
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The state of one who, after perfect knowledge, remains embodied through the momentum of impressions like a potter's wheel (SK 67); the Sāṃkhya Sūtra names the 'one liberated while living' and grounds it in the need for liberated teachers (SS 3.78–84).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Liberation while living, promised by knowledge of the cakra-code (YH 1.86) and of worship (YH 3.1), by three muhūrtas of the Bhāvanā contemplation, and to the steadfast practitioner who may die anywhere (PKS 10.82); the Dīpikā defines it as release from the eight bonds.
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): A synonym of samādhi (HYP 4.4); the yogin in samādhi is 'liberated' though living (4.107–112); from nirguṇa meditation and samādhi one becomes jīvanmukta (DYŚ 114).
- [Rasa Śāstra (the science of mercury) and the Raseśvara-darśana](../lineages/rasa-sastra.md): Lineage contribution (Rasa Śāstra): knowledge of identity with Śiva in an ageless, deathless body (Rasārṇava 1.8); attained through the stable body made by mercury, not after the body falls (Rasārṇava 1.9-13; SDS Raseśvara 1-5).
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): Firm realization of oneness with consciousness even while body and the rest are experienced (PH 16).
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): One who sees the whole world as play is liberated in life (SK 2.5).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): Though living he is liberated, though acting not stained (VBT 142); liberated though joined to a body (Paramārthasāra 61).
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): One whose mind is settled that the liṅga is liberation itself is liberated even while embodied (SSM 12.46); the liṅga-sthalas are directed to it (15.1–3).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Liberation while living: the knower remains embodied till prārabdha is exhausted, free of bondage, agency and sorrow.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Liberation while living: the state in which the mind is the seed and the spotless (AmŚ 57–59); a synonym of samādhi (HYP 4.4).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Liberation while the body continues: the cessation of the mind's properties through destruction of vāsanā, dissolution of mind and knowledge of truth.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Rejected: while the body lasts karma remains; liberation comes only when the body falls.
- [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md): Denied by Bhāskara: while the body lasts, real adjuncts remain.
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Redefined: one engaged in Hari's service in all conditions is jīvanmukta.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): The state of the soul freed by grace while the body, sustained by prārabdha, continues; such a one lives among devotees and acts as Śiva's servant.
