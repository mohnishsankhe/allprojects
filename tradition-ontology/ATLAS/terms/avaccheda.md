# avaccheda

`trm:avaccheda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अवच्छेद
**Literal:** cutting off, delimitation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Delimitation: consciousness delimited by the intellect, like the space enclosed by a pond; its identity with the whole is taught by the great sayings.
- [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md): The Goddess appears divided by adjuncts like the space in pots.
- [Bhāmatī school (Bhāmatī-prasthāna)](../lineages/bhamati.md): Delimitation: the jīva as consciousness delimited by the inner organ, as space by a pot.
