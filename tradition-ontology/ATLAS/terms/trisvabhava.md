# tri-svabhāva

`trm:trisvabhava` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** त्रिस्वभाव
**Literal:** the three natures

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The imagined, dependent and perfected natures under which every dharma is to be understood: how it appears, what appears, and the absence of the appearing duality.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The imagined, dependent and perfected natures (the Saṃdhinirmocana's 'three characteristics').
