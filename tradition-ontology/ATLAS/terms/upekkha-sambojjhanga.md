# upekkhāsambojjhaṅga

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** awakening factor of equanimity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Equanimity awakening factor: aroused when he looks on with equanimity at the mind thus concentrated (MN 118:36, 39).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Looking on with equanimity at the concentrated mind (MN 118). One of the thirty-seven qualities conducive to awakening (DN 16.3.50; MN 149): a member of the seven factors of awakening.
