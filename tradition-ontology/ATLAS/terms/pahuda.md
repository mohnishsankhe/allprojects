# pāhuḍa (prābhṛta)

`trm:pahuda` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Prakrit
**Native script:** पाहुड
**Literal:** gift, present

## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): A 'gift' or treatise-section: in the Pūrvas, a subdivision of a vastu (the Kaṣāyapāhuḍa is such a prābhṛta); in Kundakunda's usage, a treatise (Samayapāhuḍa, the eight Pāhuḍas). Tradition credits Kundakunda with eighty-four pāhuḍas.
