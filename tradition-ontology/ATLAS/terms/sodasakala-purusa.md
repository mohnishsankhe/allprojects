# ṣoḍaśakala puruṣa

`trm:sodasakala-purusa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The person of sixteen parts (kalā): shown by fasting to depend on food (ChU 6.7); in PrU 6 the parts arising from breath (faith, space, wind, light, water, earth, senses, mind, food, strength, austerity, mantras, rites, worlds, name) which vanish into the person as rivers into the ocean.
