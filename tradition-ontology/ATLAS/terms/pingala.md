# piṅgalā

`trm:pingala` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पिङ्गला
**Literal:** tawny

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The right channel, of the sun, identified with the Yamunā (HYP 3.110).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The channel on the right, of the sun (GŚ 20, 23).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The right channel, carrying the sun.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tamil piṅkalai: the right channel (solar), second of the ten nāṭis of the Siddha ninety-six.
