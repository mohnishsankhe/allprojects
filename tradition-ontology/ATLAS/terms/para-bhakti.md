# parā bhakti

`trm:para-bhakti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पराभक्ति
**Literal:** supreme devotion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Supreme devotion to the Lord (mad-bhakti parā), attained by one who has become Brahman (18.54); 'supreme devotion to me' (bhakti mayi parā) is shown by one who declares the secret to the devotees (18.68).
- [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md): Supreme devotion to the Goddess beyond the guṇa-graded forms, seeing her in all beings and ending in non-dual knowledge.
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Supreme devotion, to which secondary devotion leads (ŚBS, second adhyāya, as recalled).
