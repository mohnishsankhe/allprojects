# parā bhakti

`trm:para-bhakti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पराभक्ति
**Literal:** supreme devotion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md): Supreme devotion to the Goddess beyond the guṇa-graded forms, seeing her in all beings and ending in non-dual knowledge.
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Supreme devotion, to which secondary devotion leads (ŚBS, second adhyāya, as recalled).
