# sambhogakāya

`trm:sambhogakaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** enjoyment-body

## Definitions by tradition
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): The body in which the buddha, with the marks, teaches the Mahāyāna to bodhisattvas in pure fields; with the emanation body one of the two form-bodies arising from the dharmakāya.
