# sāṃbhogika-kāya

`trm:sambhogakaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** enjoyment body

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The body in which buddhas enjoy the Dharma with assemblies of bodhisattvas in their fields, differing by assemblies, fields, names and forms.
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): The body in which the buddha, with the marks, teaches the Mahāyāna to bodhisattvas in pure fields; with the emanation body one of the two form-bodies arising from the dharmakāya.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): The enjoyment body: the five buddha families and the peaceful and wrathful deities in Akaniṣṭha; in the Kunjed Gyalpo the retinues of earth, water, fire, wind and space.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The enjoyment body; with the emanation body one of the two form bodies that arise through aspiration and compassion (Queen Dohā v64–68).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): longs sku: the union of emptiness and clarity as baseless great bliss (Karṇatantravajrapada); attained in the bardo by the middling, 'like a fish in water'.
