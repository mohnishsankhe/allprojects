# upanayana

`trm:upanayana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उपनयन
**Literal:** leading near

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Initiation of the student by the teacher (AVŚ 11.5.3, 'upanayamāna').
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Initiation in which the boy gives himself to studentship and the teacher, 'becoming pregnant', brings him to birth with the Sāvitrī (ŚB 11.5.4).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The sacrament of initiation at 8, 11 or 12 years for the three classes — the second birth (MDh 2.36, 2.169; ĀpDh 1.1.1.9).
