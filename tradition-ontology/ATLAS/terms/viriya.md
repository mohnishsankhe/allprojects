# viriya

`trm:viriya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** energy, effort

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Energy, to be balanced like lute strings (AN 6.55); the faculty of energy is the four right strivings (SN 48.10); a factor of awakening, an iddhipāda and the fifth perfection.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Energy, whose efficacy all Buddhas teach against Makkhali's 'there is no energy'.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Energy, effort; occasional factor, faculty, power and awakening factor.
