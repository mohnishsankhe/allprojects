# vimutti

`trm:vimutti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** liberation, release

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Release of mind from the effluents: 'unshakeable is my liberation' (SN 56.11; MN 26); the fruit of disenchantment and dispassion (SN 22.59); with right knowledge it completes the ten factors of the arahant (MN 117).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Liberation of mind; in Thai forest usage the released heart beyond conventions.
- [Thai Forest tradition (Kammaṭṭhāna forest tradition)](../lineages/thai-forest.md): The state of the heart when all defilements are gone, contrasted with sammuti (convention).
