# tathatā

`trm:tathata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तथता
**Literal:** suchness, thusness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The way things really are, unaltered: the suchness of the Tathāgata and of all dharmas is one undivided suchness; 'Tathāgata' is a synonym of true suchness.
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): Suchness with stains (samalā tathatā) is the tathāgatagarbha, suchness without stains the dharmakāya; in the Awakening of Faith 'mind as suchness' is the one mind's unborn aspect.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The perfected nature, so called because it is always thus; the ultimate of dharmas and cognition-only-ness; undifferentiated in all beings and, when purified, tathāgatahood.
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Suchness, a synonym of emptiness and reality in Madhyamaka usage (the way things are, without own-nature).
