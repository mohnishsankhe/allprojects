# kūṭaṭṭha

`trm:kutattha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** standing like a peak; immovable

## Definitions by tradition
- [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md): (As reported) Epithet of Pakudha's seven bodies and of the eternalist's self and world: barren, peak-steady, pillar-firm.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The akiriyavādins' world is 'barren and fixed' (vañjho niyao).
