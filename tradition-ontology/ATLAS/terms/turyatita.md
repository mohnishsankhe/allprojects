# turyātīta

`trm:turyatita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तुर्यातीत
**Literal:** beyond the fourth

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The state beyond the fourth, the domain of those liberated without a body (videhamukta), lying beyond the seventh stage.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Turīyātītam: the fifth state beyond the fourth, in the Siddhānta account of the five avasthās.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The state beyond the fourth, in which the fourth pervades all states completely (Tantrāloka 10; Kṣemarāja).
