# oṃkāra

`trm:omkara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ओंकार
**Literal:** the syllable Oṃ

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: Among the Lord's self-identifications: 'the oṃkāra, and the Ṛk, the Sāman and the Yajus' (9.17).
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The object of meditation, with fixation in the heart, in the third stage.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The syllable Oṃ, explained in the Atharvaśiras as that which raises the breaths; its measures correspond to worlds, Vedas, fires and deities.
