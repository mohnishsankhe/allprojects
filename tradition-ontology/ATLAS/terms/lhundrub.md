# lhun grub

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): Spontaneous presence: the luminous nature of the ground, in which all qualities, lights and kāyas are present without being produced; brought to manifestation in direct crossing (thod rgal).
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): In Mahāyoga, the spontaneously present maṇḍala of deities (lhun grub kyi dkyil 'khor).
