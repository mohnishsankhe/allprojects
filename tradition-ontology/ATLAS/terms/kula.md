# kula

`trm:kula` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कुल
**Literal:** family, clan; the body; Śakti

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Śakti; also the body, family, country and the lower thousand-petalled lotus (Bhāskararāya on LSN 36-37); the Goddess delights in kula-nectar and guards the kula conventions.
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Śakti — the totality of powers, the 'family' of the Goddess, her yoginīs and mothers, and the manifest world; paired with akula, Śiva (Kulārṇava 17.27).
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): The Goddess's family of powers ordered in the six centres; the Kubjikāmata calls itself the Kula lineage of the Kulālikāmnāya.
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The 'family' — the domain of Śakti in the body and cosmos; the Kula aim, foundation and scripture (KJN 2.10; 3.1).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): In the Trika's Kaula dimension, the totality of the powers (Śakti) as opposed to akula (Śiva); the Kaula rule dwelling in Bhairava's heart (Parātrīśikā 4).
