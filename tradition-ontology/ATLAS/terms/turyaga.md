# turyagā

`trm:turyaga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तुर्यगा
**Literal:** the one gone to the fourth

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Seventh stage: with no difference perceived, abiding solely in one's own nature; the turya state of those liberated while living.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Seventh stage: no difference; one's own nature.
