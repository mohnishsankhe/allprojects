# yuga

`trm:yuga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** युग
**Literal:** yoke; age

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): The five-year cycle of the Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa, presided over by Prajāpati (Ṛk 1).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The four world-ages Kṛta, Tretā, Dvāpara and Kali, of 4,000, 3,000, 2,000 and 1,000 divine years with twilights, in which dharma declines foot by foot (MDh 1.68–86); named already as states of a man in AB 7.15.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): An age: the Lord comes into being age after age (BhG 4.8); Brahmā's day and night last a thousand ages each (8.17); the four ages and their decline taught by Hanumān (MBh 3.148) and Nārada (Rāmāyaṇa 7.74).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): An age or generation; 'in the earliest age of the gods being was born from non-being' (RV 10.72.2).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): An age of the world: Kṛta (Satya), Tretā, Dvāpara and Kali, of 4,000, 3,000, 2,000 and 1,000 divine years with junctions (sandhyā) of a tenth of that length before and after, so 4,800 + 3,600 + 2,400 + 1,200 divine years; dharma declines through them.
