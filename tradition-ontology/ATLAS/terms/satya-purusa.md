## Definitions by tradition
- [Kabīr Panth](../lineages/kabir-panth.md): The True Being, supreme Lord of Satlok, from whom the sixteen sons emanated; Kabīr is his incarnation or messenger.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Sat Purush, the Lord of Sach Khand / Sat Lok, the first manifestation of the Supreme in the purely spiritual region.
