# pramāda

`trm:pramada-jain` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रमाद
**Literal:** carelessness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In Jain usage heedlessness in conduct (fifteen kinds in Digambara analysis: gossip, passions, senses, sleep, affection) — the cause that makes killing 'violence' (TS 7.13, 8.1).

## Forms in other languages
- Prakrit: pamāya  — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [pramāda](pramada.md) — Yoga Sūtra 1.30 pramāda is neglect of the means of samādhi; the Jain term is broader heedlessness of conduct.
