## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): Each of the six auxiliary sciences — śikṣā, kalpa, vyākaraṇa, nirukta, chandas, jyotiṣa — pictured as the Veda's nose, hands, mouth, ear, feet and eye (PŚ 41–42).
