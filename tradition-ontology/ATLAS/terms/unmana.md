# unmanā

`trm:unmana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उन्मना
**Literal:** beyond mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The last and timeless phase in the pronunciation of the mantra's final sound, beyond samanā (Varivasyārahasya).
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The sixth ullāsa, called 'dream' (Kulārṇava 8.94).
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): The final, 'beyond-mind' phase in the subtle utterance of Oṃ, where sound dissolves into the supreme.
