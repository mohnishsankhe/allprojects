# vidhi

`trm:vidhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विधि
**Literal:** injunction

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): The part of the Veda that makes known an unknown purposeful object; fourfold as originative, applicatory, performative and eligibility injunction (Arthasaṅgraha); also classed as apūrva, niyama and parisaṅkhyā injunctions.
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): An injunction conveys a command (niyoga), 'what is to be done' (kārya), which prompts the qualified person by itself.
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Activity that produces merit: primary (the observance - vows and the six 'doors') and secondary (re-bathing and the like); it begins with ash, has censure in the middle and ends with feigned folly.
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): A Vedic statement that enjoins (NS 2.1.64 DCS).
