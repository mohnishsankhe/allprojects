**Literal:** python's mode of life

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The python's way of living: accepting whatever food comes unsought, lying still if none comes.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Living like a python on whatever comes unsought, without striving (MBh 12.172).
