# caryā

`trm:carya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** चर्या
**Literal:** conduct, observance

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The series of acts from ash-bathing to feigned folly; threefold as gift, worship and austerity; twofold as vows and doors; the second means to the gains.
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): (1) The yogin's conduct or observance (spyod pa), undertaken after realization; (2) the caryā songs (caryāgīti) sung to rāgas at the feast.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): spyod pa, conduct: 'without effort is the king of conduct' (Gaṅgā Mahāmudrā); conduct follows the view — from renunciate discipline to the 'madman's conduct' of the realized.
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Conduct: the first of the four pādas (and a section of each Āgama) — rules of life and service.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Cariyai: temple service — cleaning, lamps, garlands, singing — the servant's path (dāsa-mārga), leading to sālokya.
- [Lokottaravāda](../lineages/lokottaravada.md): Conduct of the bodhisattva; four — natural, of the vow, conforming, not turning back.
