# paṭiccasamuppāda

`trm:paticcasamuppada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** dependent origination

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The twelvefold arising of suffering from ignorance through formations, consciousness, name-and-form, the six bases, contact, feeling, craving, clinging, becoming and birth to ageing-and-death, and its cessation (SN 12.1); 'whoever sees dependent origination sees the Dhamma' (MN 28).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The twelve-linked arising of suffering from ignorance; expounded by the commentaries across three lives (three periods, twenty modes, four groups, three links), and by the Abhidhamma method within one moment.
- [Suan Mokkh (Buddhadāsa's tradition)](../lineages/suan-mokkh.md): The arising of suffering and of the 'I' in the present, from contact without mindfulness; 'birth' is the birth of the ego (Buddhadāsa).
- [Mogok Sayadaw tradition](../lineages/mogok.md): The chart of dependent origination to be understood before contemplating the arising and passing of mind and feeling.
