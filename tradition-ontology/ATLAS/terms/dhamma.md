# dhamma

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** the teaching; phenomenon; nature

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The Buddha's teaching, well proclaimed, visible here and now (AN 6.10); what is seen by seeing dependent origination (MN 28); also any phenomenon ('all dhammas are not-self'), a mental object (the fourth satipaṭṭhāna) and a quality.
