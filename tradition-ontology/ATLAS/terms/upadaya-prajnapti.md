# upādāya prajñapti

`trm:upadaya-prajnapti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** designation in dependence (on parts or bases)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Designation made in dependence on a basis, as a chariot is designated in dependence on its wheels and other parts; emptiness itself is such a designation (MMK 24.18; PsP on 24.18).
- [Pudgalavāda](../lineages/pudgalavada.md): Designation in dependence: the person is designated taking the aggregates as basis, as fire taking fuel.
