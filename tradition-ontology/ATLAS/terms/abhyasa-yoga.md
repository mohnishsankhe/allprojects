# abhyāsa-yoga

`trm:abhyasa-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अभ्यासयोग
**Literal:** the yoga of repeated practice

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 8.8: with thought (cetas) yoked by the yoga of repeated practice, not straying to anything else, one who continually contemplates (anucintayan) goes to the supreme divine Person.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Repeated practice of bringing the mind back to the Lord; the second step of the ladder of BhG 12.9, and the 'yoga of practice' of 8.8.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: The yoga of practice by which one is to seek to reach the Lord when the thought (citta) cannot be fixed steadily on him (12.9).
