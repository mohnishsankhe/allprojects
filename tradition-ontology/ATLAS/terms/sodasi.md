# ṣoḍaśī

`trm:sodasi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** षोडशी
**Literal:** the sixteen-syllabled / sixteen-year-old

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The sixteen-syllable form of the Śrīvidyā ('the sixteen-syllable vidyā', LSN 118) and the Goddess named by it; the Bahvṛca Upaniṣad names her Ṣoḍaśī.
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The third Mahāvidyā, Tripurasundarī of the sixteen-syllable mantra; the Bṛhaddharma lists Ṣoḍaśī and Sundarī separately.
