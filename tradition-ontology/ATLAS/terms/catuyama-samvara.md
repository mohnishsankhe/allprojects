# cātuyāma-saṃvara

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** fourfold restraint

## Definitions by tradition
- [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md): (Buddhist report) Nigaṇṭha Nātaputta's teaching: the Nigaṇṭha is curbed, yoked, cleansed and pervaded by all 'waters' (vāri).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): (Jain account) Cāujjāma, the law of four restraints taught by Pārśva, as against Mahāvīra's five vows.
