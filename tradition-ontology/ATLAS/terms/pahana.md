# pahāna

`trm:pahana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** abandoning

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The task regarding the second truth: its origin, craving, is to be abandoned (SN 56.11); also the second right effort.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Abandoning of defilements by substitution of opposites, suppression, cutting off, tranquillization and escape.
