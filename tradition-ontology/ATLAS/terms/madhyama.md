# madhyamā

`trm:madhyama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मध्यमा
**Literal:** the middle (speech)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): The middle level of speech: its material is the intellect alone; it follows sequence and goes beyond the working of breath (Vṛtti on VP 1.142); for Nāgeśa, speech in the heart, the level at which the sphoṭa conveys meaning.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The third level of speech, in the heart, where it buds.
