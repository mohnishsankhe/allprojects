# duḥkha

`trm:duhkha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दुःख
**Literal:** pain, suffering

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Pain, the first accompaniment of distraction, threefold — from oneself, other beings, the gods (1.31, YBh 1.31); for the discerning everything is suffering (2.15); future suffering is what is to be avoided (2.16).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Characterized by affliction (NS 1.1.21); twenty-one kinds (Vācaspati); its absolute cessation is liberation.
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Of the self (bodily and mental), from beings and from the gods; also fivefold as womb, birth, ignorance, old age and death.
