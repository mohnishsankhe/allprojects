# pratyekabuddha

`trm:pratyekabuddha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रत्येकबुद्ध
**Literal:** solitary buddha

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): One whose knowledge arises without a teacher when no Buddha appears and the śrāvakas have passed away (MMK 18.12); a 'middle buddha', born from the Buddha's teaching (MA 1.1).
