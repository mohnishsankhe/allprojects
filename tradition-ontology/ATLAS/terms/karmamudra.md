**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The 'action seal': a physical consort in the yoga of the higher consecrations; summary only (restricted).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): las kyi phyag rgya, the action seal: practice with a consort, in some lists one of Nāropa's dharmas (the 'method path'); sealed and restricted; the texts warn that the heedless and craving fall to lower realms.
