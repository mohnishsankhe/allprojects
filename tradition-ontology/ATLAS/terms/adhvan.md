# adhvan

`trm:adhvan` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अध्वन्
**Literal:** path, time-path

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The three time-paths (past, present, future) in which properties stand; past and future exist in their own form because of this difference (4.12).
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The six 'paths' of the cosmos — letters, words, mantras, kalās, principles and worlds — purified in nirvāṇa-dīkṣā.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The six paths - letters, mantras, words; kalās, tattvas, worlds - contemplated from gross to supreme (VBT 56-57; TĀ 6-12).
