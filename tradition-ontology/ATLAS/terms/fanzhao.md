# fanzhao / huiguang fanzhao (turning the light around)

`trm:fanzhao` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 返照 · 回光返照
**Literal:** turning back the illumination

## Definitions by tradition
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): Turning awareness back upon its source; 'if you turn back the light, the secret is on your side' (Huineng to Huiming).
- [Linji house](../lineages/linji.md): Linji: 'turn your light around right at the words and seek nothing else'.
- [Seon (Korean Chan)](../lineages/seon.md): Jinul's central practice of tracing back the radiance to the numinous awareness.
