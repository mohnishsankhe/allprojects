# kṛta-yuga

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कृतयुग
**Literal:** the 'accomplished' age

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The first and best age (also Satya-yuga), when dharma stands on four feet and meditation (dhyāna) on Viṣṇu is the means.
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): For Dolpopa, the age of perfection whose definitive teaching — the other-empty ultimate — he claims to restore.
