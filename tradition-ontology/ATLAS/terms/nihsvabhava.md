# niḥsvabhāva

`trm:nihsvabhava` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** without own-being

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The absence of intrinsic nature in dharmas; the Saṃdhinirmocana explains the sūtra statement 'all dharmas are without own-being' through three naturelessnesses.
