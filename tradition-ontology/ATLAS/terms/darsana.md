# darśana

`trm:darsana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दर्शन
**Literal:** seeing; perception; view

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In Jain epistemology the indeterminate, general grasp of an object preceding knowledge (visual, non-visual, clairvoyant, omniscient); in 'samyag-darśana', view or faith; also a philosophical system.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Seeing the Master, outwardly and within (the Radiant Form), which purifies and draws the soul.
