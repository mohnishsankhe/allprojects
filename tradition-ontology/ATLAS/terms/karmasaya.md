# karmāśaya

`trm:karmasaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कर्माशय
**Literal:** store (resting-place) of karma

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The residue of meritorious and demeritorious action, rooted in the afflictions, to be experienced in this or a future birth (2.12); ripening as birth, life-span and experience (2.13); normally 'of one life' (YBh 2.13).
