# abhiññā

**Language:** Pali

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Six direct knowledges: psychic power, divine ear, knowledge of others' minds, recollection of past lives, the divine eye, destruction of the effluents (DN 2; MN 119).
