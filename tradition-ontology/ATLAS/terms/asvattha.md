# aśvattha

`trm:asvattha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अश्वत्थ
**Literal:** the sacred fig

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The imperishable tree with roots above and branches below, whose leaves are the Vedic hymns, to be cut with the axe of non-attachment (BhG 15.1–4); 'among trees I am the aśvattha' (10.26).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The eternal fig tree with roots above and branches below, which is brahman, the immortal, in which all worlds rest (KU 2.3.1).
