# vivarta

`trm:vivarta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विवर्त
**Literal:** turning aside; apparent change

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Apparent transformation: a change in appearance without change in the substance, as the rope appears as a snake; the world is Brahman's vivarta and avidyā's pariṇāma.
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The Advaita doctrine that the world is an apparent transformation of Brahman; rejected because the witness establishes the world's reality.
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): The 'turning out' or unfolding by which the Word-principle appears as things (VP 1.1); whether this means an unreal appearance (as later Advaita) or a manifestation is debated.
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): Bhavabhūti: the one rasa, compassion, takes different forms (vivarta) as water takes the forms of eddies, bubbles and waves (UR 3.47).
