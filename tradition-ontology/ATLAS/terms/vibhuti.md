# vibhūti

`trm:vibhuti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विभूति
**Literal:** manifestation of power, glory

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The Lord's manifestations in the best of every class — Viṣṇu among the Ādityas, the sun among lights, Oṃ among words, japa among sacrifices, Kapila among the perfected — all sprung from a fragment of his splendour (BhG ch. 10).
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Holy ash, one of the eight shields; called vibhūti because it causes prosperity (SSM 7.4–5).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The powers treated in the third pāda (Vibhūti-pāda) that arise from saṃyama.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): One of the five names of sacred ash.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Sacred ash from the dhūnī, smeared on the body and given as blessing; in the common legend Gorakh is born from Matsyendra's ash.
- [Aghora (Aghor panth; Aghorī and Aughaṛ ascetics)](../lineages/aghora.md): Ash of the cremation ground smeared on the body.
