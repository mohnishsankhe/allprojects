# anubhava

`trm:anubhava` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनुभव
**Literal:** experience; apprehension

## Definitions by tradition
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Cognition other than memory; valid or invalid (Tarkasaṅgraha).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The outward expressions of emotion: dancing, rolling, singing, crying out, and the like.
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): In the six-bhakti scheme of the vacana and manual tradition, the devotion characteristic of the prāṇaliṅgi stage. More widely, lived experience of Śiva — the authority the śaraṇas appeal to, which gave the Anubhava Maṇṭapa its name.
