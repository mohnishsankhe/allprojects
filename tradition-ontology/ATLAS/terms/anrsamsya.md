# ānṛśaṃsya

`trm:anrsamsya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आनृशंस्य
**Literal:** non-cruelty, kindness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Non-cruelty, named the highest dharma by Yudhiṣṭhira to the Yakṣa (MBh 3.297.55, 3.297.71) and by the meat-seller (3.203.41); the reason Yudhiṣṭhira will not abandon the dog (17.3.7).
