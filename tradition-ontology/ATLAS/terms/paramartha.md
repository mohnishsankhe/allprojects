# paramārtha

`trm:paramartha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** परमार्थ
**Literal:** the ultimate

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The perfected nature as the highest object; neither existent nor non-existent, neither arising nor ceasing, neither increasing nor decreasing, not purified yet purified.
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): The highest aim — God and liberation — pursued within household life.
- [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md): The spiritual goal, which the Dāsbodh teaches alongside skilful worldly life.
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The ultimate, as in paramārtha-satya; Bhāviveka qualifies his negations as holding 'ultimately' (paramārthataḥ).
