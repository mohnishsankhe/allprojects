# bṛhaspati

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** बृहस्पति
**Literal:** lord of the formulation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Lord of sacred speech (brahmaṇaspati), who smelted the worlds like a smith (RV 10.72.2) and is addressed in the hymn on knowledge (10.71).
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Jupiter among the grahas: knowledge and happiness of Kālapuruṣa, a minister (BJ 2.1); brahmin, sattva, lord of ether (BJ 2.6–7); rules Sagittarius and Pisces, exalted in Cancer 5°; period 16 years; Vāmana is born from him (BPHS 2.5); his heliacal risings name the years of the sixty-year cycle (BS 8).
