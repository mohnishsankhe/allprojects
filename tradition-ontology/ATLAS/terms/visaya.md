# viṣaya

`trm:visaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विषय
**Literal:** object (of the senses)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The sense-objects: they turn away from one who abstains, though the taste remains (2.59); dwelling on them breeds attachment (2.62); the self-governed move among them with mastered senses (2.64).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Sense-objects, sound and the rest, are offered into the fires of the senses (4.26); the senses move among the sense-objects (indriyārtha) (5.9); the one ascended to yoga is not attached to them (6.4).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: Sense-objects: the shoots of the aśvattha's branches (15.2); the living being, presiding over the senses and the mind, partakes of them (15.9).
