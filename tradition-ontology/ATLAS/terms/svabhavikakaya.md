# svābhāvika-kāya

`trm:svabhavikakaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** natural body

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The first of the three bodies, the dharmadhātu itself, equal, subtle, the support of the enjoyment and emanation bodies.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The 'essence body', the fourth body, the inseparability of the others; its bliss is 'the great enjoyment' (Queen Dohā v65).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): ngo bo nyid sku, the 'essence body': the inseparability of the three kāyas, counted as a fourth; the Shangpa Amulet Mahāmudrā's fruit is the four kāyas.
