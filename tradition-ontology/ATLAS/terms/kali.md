# Kālī

`trm:kali` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** काली
**Literal:** the Black One; she of Time

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): She who devours (kalana) Time: because she devours Mahākāla she is the primordial Kālikā (Mahānirvāṇa 4.31-32); black because all beings enter her as colours enter black (13.5-6); standing on Śiva as a corpse (Toḍala 1.20-25).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): The Mother of the Śyāmā-saṅgīt; Dakṣiṇakālī of the Tantrasāra.
- [Krama (the 'Sequence'; Mahānaya, Mahārtha)](../lineages/krama.md): The goddess as the power of kalana - projecting, knowing, counting, moving, sounding - in twelve phases of cognition (TĀ 4.148-173).
