# vipassanā

`trm:vipassana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** insight

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Clear seeing into states as they are — their rise and fall, impermanence and not-self (MN 131; AN 4.170); paired with calm (MN 149).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Insight into the three characteristics of formations, developed through the purifications and insight knowledges.
- [Mahāsi Sayadaw tradition (the 'new Burmese method')](../lineages/mahasi.md): Continuous noting of all arising mental and physical phenomena, through which the insight knowledges unfold.
- [U Ba Khin – S. N. Goenka tradition (Sayagyi U Ba Khin lineage)](../lineages/u-ba-khin-goenka.md): Observing bodily sensations systematically and with equanimity, understanding their impermanence (the Goenka course).
- [Suan Mokkh (Buddhadāsa's tradition)](../lineages/suan-mokkh.md): Seeing the emptiness of 'I' and 'mine' in the midst of daily life.
