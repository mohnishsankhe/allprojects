# pakṣa

`trm:paksa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पक्ष
**Literal:** subject (of inference); thesis

## Definitions by tradition
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): That in which the probandum is doubted, e.g. the mountain (Tarkasaṅgraha).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): A side or thesis in debate (NS 1.1.41, 1.2.1).
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): What one wishes to prove, stated as one's own intention and not contradicted by perception, inference, one's own doctrine or common usage.
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): One who has no position — existent, non-existent or both — cannot be refuted (CŚ 16.25); the great-souled have no position and no dispute (YṢ 50).
