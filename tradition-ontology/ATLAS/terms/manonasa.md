# manonāśa

`trm:manonasa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मनोनाश
**Literal:** destruction of mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Dissolution of mind, 'with form' in the jīvanmukta and 'without form' in the videhamukta.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Dissolution of the mind: the mind's ceasing to take on forms, practised by the knower for stable abidance.
