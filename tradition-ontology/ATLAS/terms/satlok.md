# Satlok (Satya-lok)

`trm:satlok` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Hindi

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kabīr Panth](../lineages/kabir-panth.md): The true world of the Sat Puruṣ beyond the three worlds of Kāl, the home to which freed souls (haṃs) return.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Sat Lok = Sach Khand, the fifth region, the true home of the soul beyond the reach of Kāl and māyā.
- [Garībdāsī panth](../lineages/garibdasi.md): The world Kabīr showed Garībdās.

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- exact: [Sach Khaṇḍ](sach-khand.md) — Sant Mat itself identifies Sat Lok with Sach Khand.
- contested: [satyaloka](satyaloka.md) — Same name as the Purāṇic highest world of Brahmā, but the Kabīr Panth places Satlok beyond all worlds of Kāl, including Brahmā's.
