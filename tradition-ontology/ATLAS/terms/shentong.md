# gzhan stong

`trm:shentong` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** གཞན་སྟོང
**Literal:** empty of other

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): For Gorampa, Dolpopa's other-emptiness is the 'eternalist' Madhyamaka; Shākya Chokden accepted an other-empty reading of the Maitreya texts as the view for meditative experience.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): Rejected by the Gelug as positing a truly existent ultimate.
- [Karma Kagyu (Kamtsang Kagyu)](../lineages/karma-kagyu.md): In one Karma Kagyu strand (the 8th Situ, Jamgön Kongtrul reading the 3rd Karmapa), the ultimate — buddha-nature — is empty of what is other than it (adventitious stains) but not of its own qualities; contested by others in the school (the 8th Karmapa).
