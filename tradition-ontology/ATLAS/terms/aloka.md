# āloka

`trm:aloka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अलोक
**Literal:** non-world

## Definitions by tradition
- [Ārya tradition of the Guhyasamāja](../lineages/arya-guhyasamaja.md): 'Light', the first empty and first of the three appearances (light, light-appearance, light-attainment) in the Pañcakrama.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Infinite empty space beyond the universe, into which nothing can move since the medium of motion is absent (TS 10.8).
