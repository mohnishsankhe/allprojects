# nairmāṇika-kāya

`trm:nirmanakaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** emanation body

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The immeasurable emanations (craftsman, birth, awakening, parinirvāṇa and so on) by which buddhas act for beings.
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): The body that appears in the world, displays birth, awakening and parinirvāṇa to ripen beings.
