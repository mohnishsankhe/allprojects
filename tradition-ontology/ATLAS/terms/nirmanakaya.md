# nairmāṇika-kāya

`trm:nirmanakaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** emanation body

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The immeasurable emanations (craftsman, birth, awakening, parinirvāṇa and so on) by which buddhas act for beings.
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): The body that appears in the world, displays birth, awakening and parinirvāṇa to ripen beings.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): The emanation body: the six sages of the six realms who teach the vehicles (Guhyagarbha ch.3), Garab Dorje, Śākyamuni and Padmasambhava.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The emanation body, appearing variously to those to be trained.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): sprul sku: mind's unceasing clear radiance, whatever arises (Karṇatantravajrapada); taking rebirth as emanation; in Tibetan usage also a recognized reincarnate teacher (tulku).
