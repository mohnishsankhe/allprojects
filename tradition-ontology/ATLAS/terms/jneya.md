# jñeya

`trm:jneya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ज्ञेय
**Literal:** what is to be known

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: one of the topics Arjuna asks about (13.1). The knowable is brahman, beginningless and supreme, knowing which one attains the immortal, said to be neither sat nor asat (13.13); it has hands, feet, eyes everywhere, appears through the sense-qualities yet is without senses, is without guṇas yet their experiencer, is inside and outside beings, not to be known because of its subtlety, undivided yet as if divided, sustainer, devourer and generator of beings (13.14–17); light of lights beyond darkness, it is knowledge, the knowable and what is reached by knowledge, set in the heart of all (13.18). Knowing it, the devotee becomes fit for Kṛṣṇa's state (13.19).
