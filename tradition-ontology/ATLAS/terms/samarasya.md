# sāmarasya

`trm:samarasya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सामरस्य
**Literal:** equality of flavour

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Union in which the self is contemplated as dissolved in the stainless sky of consciousness; this is the supreme knowledge called liberation (SSM 14.42–44). In the six-bhakti scheme the devotion of the aikya stage is samarasa-bhakti.
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): 'Equal flavour', fusion: Tripurā is the sāmarasya of prakāśa and vimarśa (Dīpikā on YH 1.6); in the Varivasyārahasya one of the fifteen meanings of the mantra.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Equal taste, one-flavouredness: samādhi is the samarasa oneness when breath is exhausted and mind dissolved (GŚ 94); the samarasa of body and supreme state (SSP 5).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The state in which the Akulavīra is seen everywhere and 'all is samarasa' (Akulavīra A.24, A.41).
