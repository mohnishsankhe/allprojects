## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): A class of sacred narrative lore, called with itihāsa the 'fifth Veda'; remembered by Brahmā before the Vedas and condensed by Vyāsa in every Dvāpara age into eighteen Purāṇas of four lakh verses.
