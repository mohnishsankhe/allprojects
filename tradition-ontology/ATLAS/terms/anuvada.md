# anuvāda

`trm:anuvada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनुवाद
**Literal:** restatement

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): An arthavāda stating what is already known by another means of knowledge (e.g. that fire is a remedy for cold).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Repetition of what has been enjoined, distinct from mere repetition (NS 2.1.66–68 DCS).
