# śmaśāna

`trm:smasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** श्मशान
**Literal:** cremation ground

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The dwelling of the fourth stage, not left until the body falls.
- [Kāpālika (Somasiddhānta)](../lineages/kapalika.md): Setting of Kāpālika rites in literary portraits.
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): Kālī's dwelling (Kālī Tantra dhyāna in the Tantrasāra) and the place of the vīra's night worship (Karpūrādistotra). Restricted practices summary only.
- [Aghora (Aghor panth; Aghorī and Aughaṛ ascetics)](../lineages/aghora.md): The cremation ground, the Aghora ascetic's place of practice and dwelling.
