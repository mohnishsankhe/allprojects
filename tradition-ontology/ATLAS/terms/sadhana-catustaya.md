**Native script:** साधनचतुष्टय
**Literal:** fourfold means

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The four means: discrimination of eternal and non-eternal, dispassion toward enjoyments here and hereafter, the six virtues beginning with calm, and longing for liberation.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Discrimination, dispassion, the six virtues and longing for liberation.
