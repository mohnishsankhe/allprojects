# adhyāsa

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अध्यास
**Literal:** placing upon

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Superimposition: 'the appearance, of the nature of memory, of something previously seen in something else' — the mutual superimposition of self and not-self that is the basis of all empirical life, called avidyā.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Superimposition: the appearance of one thing as another through error, like a snake in a rope; ego is the first superimposition on the Self; jīva and witness appear mutually superimposed like fire and iron.
