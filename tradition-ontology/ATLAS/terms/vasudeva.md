# Vāsudeva

`trm:vasudeva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वासुदेव
**Literal:** son of Vasudeva; the indwelling god

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Name of Kṛṣṇa; 'Vāsudeva is all' is the realization of the rare great soul (BhG 7.19); 'among the Vṛṣṇis I am Vāsudeva' (10.37).
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): The supreme of the fourfold form, the knower of the field without guṇas, dwelling of all, entered by the liberated (MBh 12.326.38; 12.332.17).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The supreme Lord and first vyūha, full of all six qualities.
