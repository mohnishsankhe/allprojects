# ṛṇa

`trm:rna` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ऋण
**Literal:** debt

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The debts with which one is born — to the seers, gods and ancestors (TS 6.3.10.5), and to men (ŚB 1.7.2.1).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The three debts to be paid before seeking liberation (MDh 6.35–37).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The three debts with which, according to scripture, a brāhmaṇa is born; urged as a bar to liberation and answered as figurative (NS 4.1.59–62; NBh).
