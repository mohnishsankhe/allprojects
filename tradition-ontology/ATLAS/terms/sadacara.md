# sadācāra

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सदाचार
**Literal:** conduct of the good

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Practices of the learned (śiṣṭa) done with no visible motive, accepted as indicating Vedic injunctions (MS 1.3.7).
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Honest livelihood (kāyaka) and moral life, the fruit of which is shared (dāsōha).
