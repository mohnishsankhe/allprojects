# codanā

`trm:codana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** चोदना
**Literal:** impelling (statement)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The statement that impels to action (Śabara); the injunction, which alone makes known dharma, including what is future, subtle or remote. Śabara on MS 2.1.5 also uses 'codanā' for the apūrva the injunction makes known.
