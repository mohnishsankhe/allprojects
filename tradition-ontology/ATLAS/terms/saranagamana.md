# śaraṇagamana

`trm:saranagamana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Going for refuge: to the non-learner's dharmas that make a Buddha, the learner's and non-learner's dharmas that make the Saṅgha, and to nirvāṇa.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): skyabs 'gro: taking refuge in the Three Jewels (and in the Kagyu ngöndro also in the three roots), joined with prostrations.
