## Definitions by tradition
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): One who delights in Śiva-knowledge and keeps māyā away (SSM 5.17–18); in SSM 5.20–21 the Māheśvaras are those devoted to outer worship (the sacrifice of action), not ultimately different from the Śaivas who worship within.
