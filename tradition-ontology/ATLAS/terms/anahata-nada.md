# anāhata nāda

`trm:anahata-nada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनाहतनाद
**Literal:** unstruck sound

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The sound not produced by striking, heard within when the knot of Brahmā is pierced (HYP 4.70); within its resonance is the knowable and within that the mind, which dissolves there (4.100).
- [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md): Unstruck sound, worshipped by sages by the path taught by the guru — an easy means to liberation, but without charm (rakti) for ordinary people (ŚR 1.2.165–166).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The unstruck inner sound heard in the heart and the sky of the head; the 'bull bound threefold roars' at anāhata (GŚ 60); in Hindi 'anahad nād'.
