# animitta

`trm:animitta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** signless

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The signless concentration and liberation of mind, empty of the 'makers of signs' lust, hatred and delusion (MN 43; MN 121).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Signless: the gateway of liberation through contemplation of impermanence; an aspect of nibbāna.
