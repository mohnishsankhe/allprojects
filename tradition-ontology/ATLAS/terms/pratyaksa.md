# pratyakṣa

`trm:pratyaksa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रत्यक्ष
**Literal:** perception

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The faultless contact of sense and object; sevenfold — the witness (sākṣin) and six senses (five outer and manas).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Cognition arising from sense-object contact, non-verbalizable, non-deviating, determinate (NS 1.1.4).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Arises from the contact of self, sense, mind and object (VS 3.1.13, 9.15 Candrānanda); includes yogic perception (VS 9.13).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Cognition produced by sense-object contact, indeterminate or determinate, through six contacts; also extraordinary (alaukika) perception.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Perception: the mind's activity, coloured by an external thing through the senses, that chiefly ascertains the particular; its result is the puruṣa's awareness of the activity (YBh 1.7). The perception of nirvitarkā samāpatti is the 'highest perception', the seed of testimony and inference (YBh 1.42).
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Cognition arising in a person when the senses are in contact with an existing object; it grasps only what is present and so cannot know dharma (MS 1.1.4).
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): First an indeterminate bare awareness, then determinate cognition by universal and other features; both are perception (ŚV pratyakṣa 112).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Perception: the identity of the consciousnesses delimited by the knower, the mental mode and the object; affirmative, not grasping difference (Maṇḍana).
