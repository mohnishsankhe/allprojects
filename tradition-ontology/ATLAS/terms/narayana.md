# Nārāyaṇa

`trm:narayana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नारायण
**Literal:** resting-place of beings / of the waters

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Name of Viṣṇu most used by Madhva in his invocations: full of qualities, free of defects, known only through the true scriptures.
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): The supreme Lord of the Nārāyaṇīya, also present as the ṛṣi at Badarī; the culmination of every system of knowledge (MBh 12.337.63–67) and the goal of the ekāntins (12.336.3–4).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Named by Bhīṣma in his hymn to Kṛṣṇa (12.47) and as the one attained by the Sāṃkhya follower (12.290).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The proper name of the supreme Brahman: both the abode of all souls and things and the one who abides in them; Brahmā and Śiva are souls in his body.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): Nāraṇaṉ: the Lord's name for the Āḻvārs, the creator of Brahmā (NMT 1) and the saving name found by Tirumaṅkai (PTM 1.1.1).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): In the Vaiṣṇava and several yoga and renunciate Upaniṣads, the supreme Lord from whom Brahmā and Rudra arise, the Person in the solar orb, the one inner self.
