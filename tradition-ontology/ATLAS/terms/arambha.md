**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The first of the four stages: brahmagranthi pierced, tinkling sounds, a radiant, fragrant, healthy body (HYP 4.70–71; SS 3.29–31).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: Undertaking (the ordinary word, not the first stage of yoga, ārambha, recorded under this id): the undertaking of actions is a sign of rajas (14.12); the one beyond the guṇas has given up all undertakings (14.25).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): First of the four stages of yoga.
