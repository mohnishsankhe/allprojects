**Literal:** sky-clad

## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): 'Sky-clad': the naked monk, and the tradition that holds complete nudity necessary for the fully renounced and for liberation.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Called Boṭika (Boḍiya) in Śvetāmbara sources, which trace it to Śivabhūti's schism.
