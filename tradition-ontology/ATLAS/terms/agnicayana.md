# agnicayana

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अग्निचयन
**Literal:** piling of the fire(-altar)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The year-long building of the great brick fire-altar (often in the shape of a falcon) in five layers, explained in ŚB 6–10 as the rebuilding of Prajāpati who fell apart, so that the sacrificer builds and becomes an immortal self.
