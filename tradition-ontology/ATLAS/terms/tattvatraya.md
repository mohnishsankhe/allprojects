## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The three real categories: cit (souls), acit (insentient matter) and Īśvara (the Lord).
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): The three realities: cit, acit and Īśvara, established by scripture (Daśaślokī 7).
