# videhamukti

`trm:videhamukti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विदेहमुक्ति
**Literal:** bodiless liberation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Bodiless liberation, the domain beyond the fourth (turyātīta).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Bodiless liberation at the fall of the body, when prārabdha is exhausted; nothing is gained that was not already the case.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Liberation at the fall of the body when prārabdha is exhausted, like wind becoming still.
