# bhāva (in dramaturgy)

`trm:bhava-alankara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** state, feeling; 'that which brings into being'

## Definitions by tradition
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): Emotional state; so called because, with speech, body and sattva, it 'brings into being' (bhāvayati) the meaning of the poem (NŚ 7.1–3); of three kinds — eight stable, thirty-three transient, eight involuntary; love directed to gods, gurus and kings remains a bhāva (KP 4).
