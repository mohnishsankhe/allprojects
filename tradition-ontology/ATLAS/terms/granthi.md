# granthi

`trm:granthi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ग्रन्थि
**Literal:** knot

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The three knots — of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra — pierced in succession by the awakened power; in the HYP their piercing marks the first, second and fourth stages of nāda (4.70–77).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The three knots of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra that the Goddess pierces in her ascent (LSN 38-39).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): One of three knots (of Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra) pierced by kuṇḍalinī.
