# pavitrārohaṇa

`trm:pavitrarohana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पवित्रारोहण
**Literal:** offering of the pavitra

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Annual offering of a thread-garland (pavitra) to Śiva in Śrāvaṇa or Nabhasya, removing all defects in the year's worship.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Annual pavitra offering to Viṣṇu with the same function.
