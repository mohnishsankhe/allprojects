# savikalpaka

`trm:savikalpaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सविकल्पक
**Literal:** determinate (perception)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Perception with a qualifier, e.g. 'this is Ḍittha, a brāhmaṇa, dark' (Tarkasaṅgraha).
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): The perception that follows the bare awareness and grasps the thing with its universal and other properties; also perception.
