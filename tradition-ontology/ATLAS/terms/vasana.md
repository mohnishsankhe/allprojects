# vāsanā

`trm:vasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वासना
**Literal:** scent, impression

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Latent tendency: the grasping of an object through firm conception without reflection; one of the two seeds of mind; impure vāsanās bind to rebirth, pure ones (friendliness and the like) are to be cultivated before all are given up; bondage is bondage to vāsanā.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Latent tendencies from past experience that manifest only when suited to ripening karma (4.8), continuous across births (4.9), beginningless because the will to be is eternal (4.10), and ceasing when cause, result, substrate and support cease (4.11).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Latent tendency: the impressions of past experience and action that incline the mind; the three vāsanās (world, scripture-learning, body) obstruct knowledge; their destruction is a discipline of the jīvanmukta.
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Impressions of past experience; the material from which dreams are really produced (dreams are therefore not false).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Latent tendency that sustains mind; pure or impure; its destruction (vāsanā-kṣaya) with dissolution of mind and knowledge gives jīvanmukti.
