# 'ja' lus

**Language:** Tibetan
**Literal:** rainbow body

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): Rainbow body: the fruit in which the body's gross elements dissolve into their essence of light at death, leaving only hair and nails (or nothing); the 'great transference' ('pho ba chen po) body of Padmasambhava and Vimalamitra departs without death.
- [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md): The rainbow body attained by masters of the Zhang Zhung Nyengyü and, by Bön tradition, by Shardza Tashi Gyaltsen.
