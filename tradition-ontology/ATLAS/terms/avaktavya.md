# avaktavya

`trm:avaktavya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अवक्तव्य
**Literal:** inexpressible

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pudgalavāda](../lineages/pudgalavada.md): Inexpressible: the status of the person, which cannot be stated as other than or the same as the aggregates; a fifth category of knowables.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The fourth predication: a thing as simultaneously existent and non-existent cannot be expressed in one word.
