# mūlādhāra

`trm:muladhara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मूलाधार
**Literal:** root support

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The root centre: the Goddess's sole dwelling (LSN 38); where the Samayin worships Samayā with Navātman (SL 41).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The first centre at the perineum/anus, four-petalled, of earth (LAṂ), seat of sleeping kuṇḍalinī and of the triangular yoni with the self-born liṅga (GŚ 10–12; ṢCN 4–13).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The base centre at the anus, with the triangle where kuṇḍalinī lies.
