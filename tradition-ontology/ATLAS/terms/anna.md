# aññā

`trm:anna` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Native script:** अन्न
**Literal:** final knowledge

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Final knowledge (arahantship) in this life, one of the two fruits of satipaṭṭhāna (MN 10).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: In the cycle of 3.14: beings come from food, food from rain, rain from sacrifice.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Food: brahman is food (TU 3.2); the person is made of the essence of food (TU 2.1); vows about food (TU 3.7-10); mind is made of food (ChU 6.5.4, 6.7); 'I am food' sings the knower (TU 3.10.6).
