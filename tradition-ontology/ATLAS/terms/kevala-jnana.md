## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Perfect knowledge of all substances in all their modes, past, present and future, arising when the knowledge-obscuring and other destructive karmas are destroyed (TS 1.29, 10.1).
