# abhāva

`trm:abhava` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अभाव
**Literal:** non-existence, negation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): A dependent reality of three kinds: prior non-existence, destruction and constant (absolute) non-existence; mutual non-existence (difference) is not a fourth, being the nature of things. Known by inference and by perception.
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): The seventh category, of four kinds (prior, posterior, absolute, mutual); not listed by Praśastapāda because determined through its counterpositive, not because unreal (Udayana).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Proposed as a means of knowledge (in the sense of non-apprehension); reduced to inference (NS 2.2.2).
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Non-existence is only the alteration of an existent; where no existent is established, non-existence is not established either (MMK 15.5); nirvāṇa is not a non-existence (25.7–8).
