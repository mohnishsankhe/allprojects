# xingxing jiji (alert and calm)

`trm:xingxing-jiji` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 惺惺寂寂
**Literal:** alertness and quiescence

## Definitions by tradition
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): Balance of alertness and calm: calm without alertness is dullness, alertness without calm is scattered thought (Yongjia).
- [Seon (Korean Chan)](../lineages/seon.md): Jinul's seongjeok deungji; sequential use is the gradual method of inferior faculties.
