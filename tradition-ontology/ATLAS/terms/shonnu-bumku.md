# gzhon nu bum sku

`trm:shonnu-bumku` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tibetan
**Literal:** youthful vase body

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): The youthful vase body: the ground's luminosity enclosed within, like a lamp in a vase, not yet radiating outward; when the 'seal' of the vase breaks, the ground-appearances arise.
