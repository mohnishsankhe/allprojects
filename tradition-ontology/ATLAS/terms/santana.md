# santāna

`trm:santana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सन्तान
**Literal:** lineage (of teachers)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): Lineage (gotra, maṭhikā); lineage-based restrictions are not enjoined in the Trika and forbidden in the Mata scriptures (TĀ 4.265-270).
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): Series, continuum of momentary dharmas that is conventionally called a person; the self is a designation for it.
