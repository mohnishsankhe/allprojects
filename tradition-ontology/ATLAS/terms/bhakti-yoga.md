# bhakti-yoga

`trm:bhakti-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भक्तियोग
**Literal:** the discipline of devotion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Serving the Lord with unswerving devotion, by which one passes beyond the guṇas and becomes fit for Brahman (BhG 14.26); the theme of ch. 12.
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): Exclusive devotion (ekānta-bhakti) to Nārāyaṇa-Vāsudeva, the highest dharma (Nārāyaṇīya).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The yoga of devotion, fitting one in whom faith in the Lord's stories has arisen, who is neither disgusted with nor too attached to the world.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 14.26: one who serves Kṛṣṇa with the unswerving yoga of devotion, having gone beyond the guṇas, becomes fit for becoming brahman.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The loving meditation of the Upaniṣads, the direct means to the Lord, prepared by karma-yoga and jñāna-yoga and supported by the seven aids.
