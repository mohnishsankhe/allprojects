## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Unmanifest matter: a good or bad continuum derived from the elements, produced by manifest action or concentration, which persists even when the mind is distracted or absent; the substance of vows and non-vows.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): Not a real entity; what is called avijñapti is the specific transformation of the series produced by volition.
