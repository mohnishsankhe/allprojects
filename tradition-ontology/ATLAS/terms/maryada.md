# maryādā

`trm:maryada` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मर्यादा
**Literal:** limit, boundary, rule

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara Terāpanth](../lineages/terapanthi.md): The written code of the Terāpanth order laid down by Ācārya Bhikṣu (a single ācārya, no personal disciples, collective discipline), celebrated in the annual Maryādā Mahotsava (from Jayācārya's time).
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): The path of rule: devotion and liberation pursued through scriptural discipline and means.
