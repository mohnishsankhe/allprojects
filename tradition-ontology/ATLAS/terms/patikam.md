## Definitions by tradition
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): In the Prabandham, a decad of hymns (usually ten verses and a signature verse) — each 'tiruvāymoḻi' of the Tiruvāymoḻi is such a decad.
- [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md): A hymn of (usually) ten or eleven verses, the unit of the Tēvāram, the last verse often naming the poet and promising fruit.
