# dhī

`trm:dhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** धी
**Literal:** vision; inspired thought; insight

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The visionary thought or insight of the seer, which is 'yoked' (RV 5.81.1) and which Savitṛ is asked to impel (3.62.10).
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The discerning intellect whose lapse — seeing the eternal and non-eternal, wholesome and unwholesome wrongly — is a cause of suffering (Ca Śā 1.99).
