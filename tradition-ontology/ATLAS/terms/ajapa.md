## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The mantra 'recited' by the breath without effort (ajapā-gāyatrī).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The spontaneous japa of the breath, 21,600 per day and night, the Goddess's japa (VBT 156).
