# ajapā

`trm:ajapa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अजपा
**Literal:** unrecited

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The 'unrepeated' repetition — the haṃsa mantra made by the breath itself (Yogaviṣaya 28–29; KJN 13.2).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The mantra 'recited' by the breath without effort (ajapā-gāyatrī).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The spontaneous japa of the breath, 21,600 per day and night, the Goddess's japa (VBT 156).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): 'Unrepeated repetition': the Name going on by itself with the breath, without tongue or beads (Dādū, Kabīr and the Nāth-influenced Sants).
