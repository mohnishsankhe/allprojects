# svarga

`trm:svarga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्वर्ग
**Literal:** going to the light; heaven

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Heaven (svar), the world of light reached by sacrifice, gifts and tapas (RV 10.154.2).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: The way to heaven (svargati) prayed for by Vedic sacrificers; they reach the world of the lord of the gods and enjoy divine enjoyments, and when their merit is spent they enter the mortal world again (9.20–21).
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The fruit of rites for 'one desiring heaven'; where no fruit is stated, heaven is understood (MS 4.3.15). Śabara concludes that the word denotes happiness (prīti) itself (on MS 6.1.1–2); a later cited verse describes it as happiness unmixed with sorrow, not followed by loss, brought by longing.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Arjuna's faintness does not lead to heaven (2.2); a dharmic battle is an open door to heaven (2.32) and heaven is gained if slain (2.37); the Veda-delighters are intent on heaven (2.43).
