# tejoleśyā

`trm:tejolesya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तेजोलेश्या
**Literal:** fiery radiance

## Definitions by tradition
- [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md): (Jain report) A destructive fiery power gained by severe austerity; Gosāla learnt its method from Mahāvīra and used it to burn two monks and against Mahāvīra.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): A supernormal fiery power (labdhi) of ascetics; Mahāvīra's cool power (śītaleśyā) counters it.
