# pitṛyāna

`trm:pitryana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पितृयान
**Literal:** the path of the ancestors

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The dark path of smoke, night, the dark fortnight and the southern course, by which one reaches the lunar light and returns (BhG 8.25).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The path of the fathers: through smoke, night, the waning fortnight, the southern course, the fathers' world, space and the moon, and back as rain and food into a womb (ChU 5.10.3-6; BAU 6.2.16; PrU 1.9).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The path of the fathers, one of the two paths 'I have heard of' (RV 10.88.15).
- [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md): The path of the fathers, taken by performers of rites, leading to the moon and return to earth with residual karma (BS 3.1).
