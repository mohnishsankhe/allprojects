# khaṇika-samādhi

`trm:khanika-samadhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** momentary concentration

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Concentration lasting only for the moment, named in the Visuddhimagga among the three kinds; in the sub-commentary, the unification of mind of the pure-insight practitioner.
- [Mahāsi Sayadaw tradition (the 'new Burmese method')](../lineages/mahasi.md): Concentration arising from uninterrupted moment-to-moment noting, strong enough to suppress the hindrances and support insight without prior jhāna.
