`trm:kalasankarsini` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कालसङ्कर्षिणी
**Literal:** she who draws in (devours) time

## Definitions by tradition
- [Krama (the 'Sequence'; Mahānaya, Mahārtha)](../lineages/krama.md): The supreme Kālī of the Krama, the last of the goddesses of cognition.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): In Abhinavagupta's Trika the fourth goddess above Parā, Parāparā and Aparā.
