## Definitions by tradition
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Great passion: passion purified as great bliss and compassion; 'great, great, great passion' is a name of Mañjuśrī (Nāmasaṃgīti 30); non-abiding nirvāṇa is great passion (Sekoddeśa 82).
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): Saraha: turn the three realms into the one colour of great passion (People Dohā v28).
