# tejas

`trm:tejas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तेजस्
**Literal:** fire; light

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Substance with hot touch and colour; bodies of fire in Āditya's world; includes gold (mineral fire).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Heat, radiance: the first emanation of being, which produced water (ChU 6.2.3); its finest part is speech (6.5.4); in dying breath goes into heat (6.8.6); in deep sleep the mind is overcome by tejas (PrU 4.6).
