# ātma-dṛṣṭi

`trm:atma-drsti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** view of self

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The first of the afflicted mind's four afflictions: taking the store-consciousness as a self.
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Seeing a self, from which comes love for it, thirst for happiness, appropriation and all faults; removed by seeing selflessness.
