# vākyārtha

`trm:vakyartha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वाक्यार्थ
**Literal:** sentence-meaning

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The meaning of the whole sentence, conveyed by the word-meanings (Bhāṭṭa) or by words expressing meanings already connected (Prābhākara).
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): The meaning of the sentence, grasped as a flash of pratibhā distinct from the word-meanings (VP 2.143).
