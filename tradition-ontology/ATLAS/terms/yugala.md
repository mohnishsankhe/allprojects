# yugala

`trm:yugala` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** pair, couple

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): The divine couple Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, worshipped together (yugala-upāsanā).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Rādhā–Kṛṣṇa as the object of service in mañjarī-bhāva.
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): The divine couple (Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa) and the human couple in which they are realized.
