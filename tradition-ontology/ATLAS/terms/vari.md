## Definitions by tradition
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): The regular walking pilgrimage to Paṇḍharpūr, above all for the Āṣāḍhī and Kārtikī ekādaśīs, made in singing companies (diṇḍī) behind the saints' palanquins; keeping it for life is what makes one a Vārkarī.
