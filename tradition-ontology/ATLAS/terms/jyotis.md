# jyotis

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ज्योतिस्
**Literal:** light

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Light — the highest light reached by the worshipper (RV 1.50.10), the light set in the heart (6.9.5–6), the light reached by soma-drinkers (8.48.3), the light of lights that is mind (VS 34.1).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: The knowable is the light even of lights, beyond darkness (13.18).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Light: the light beyond heaven which is the light within a person (ChU 3.13.7); the highest light which the serene one reaches (ChU 8.3.4, 8.12.3); the light of lights (MuU 2.2.9).
