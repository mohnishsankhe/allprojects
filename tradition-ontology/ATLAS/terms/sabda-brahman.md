# śabdabrahman

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शब्दब्रह्मन्
**Literal:** word-Brahman

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): Brahman as the Word: the Word-principle (śabdatattva) that appears as the world and permeates all cognition; its highest accessible level is paśyantī (VP 1.1, 1.123, 1.142).
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): The unmanifest sound arising from the dividing supreme bindu; not the word nor the meaning (both insentient) but the consciousness of all beings, which dwells in the body as kuṇḍalinī and becomes the letters (ŚT 1.11–14).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Word-brahman: of the two brahmans, sound and non-sound, one skilled in the word-brahman attains the highest (MaiU 6.22).
