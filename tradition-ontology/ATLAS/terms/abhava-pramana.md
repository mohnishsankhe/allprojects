# abhāva-pramāṇa

`trm:abhava-pramana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अभाव-प्रमाण
**Literal:** absence as means of knowledge

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): Where the five positive means of knowledge do not arise, the non-apprehension itself is the means of knowing non-existence (ŚV abhāva 1; the Vṛttikāra in Śabara).
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): Not a separate means: the Prakaraṇapañcikā counts five means only; absence is grasped as the bare locus (as the Kāśikā on the Ślokavārttika reports the Prābhākara view).
