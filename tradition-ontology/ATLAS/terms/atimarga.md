**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अतिमार्ग
**Literal:** the path beyond

## Definitions by tradition
- [Atimārga](../lineages/atimarga.md): Name given by the Śaiva scriptures to the Pāśupata and Lākula (and skull-observing) division of Śaivism: knowledge 'beyond the states of the intellect', 'beyond the world'.
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): In the Mantramārga's own scriptures, the ascetic Śaiva path (Pāśupata and Lākula) ranked above Vedic and 'spiritual' religion but below the Mantramārga.
