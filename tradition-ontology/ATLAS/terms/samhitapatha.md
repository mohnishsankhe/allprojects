## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): The recitation of the Veda with all sandhi applied, as distinct from the word-by-word (padapāṭha) and step (kramapāṭha) recitations; its secret meaning is taught in the saṃhitā-upaniṣads (AA 3.1.1; TU 1.3).
