# leśyā

`trm:lesya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** लेश्या
**Literal:** soul-colouring

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The coloration of the soul by passion-tinged activity, in six grades — black, blue, grey (inauspicious), yellow-red, lotus-pink, white (auspicious); physical (dravya) and psychic (bhāva) (TS 2.6; Uttarādhyayana 34).
