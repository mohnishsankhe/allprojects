**Language:** Hindi

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): 'Path': an organized following of a Sant (Kabīr Panth, Dādū Panth), with initiation, seats and sādhus — which the Sants' own verses often warn against making sectarian.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): A sub-order of the Nāth yogīs; tradition counts twelve (bārah panth).
