## Definitions by tradition
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): The eleven forms in which the one love appears: attachment to the Lord's qualities and glory, form, worship, remembrance, service, friendship, parental love, conjugal love, self-offering, absorption, and supreme separation (NBS 82).
