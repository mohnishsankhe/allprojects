# Amitābha

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अमिताभ
**Literal:** immeasurable light

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): The buddha of immeasurable light (and life), Dharmākara after fulfilling his vows, presiding over Sukhāvatī; the object of recollection and calling.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): Amida Nyorai, the Tathāgata of Unhindered Light filling the ten directions; the means by which the formless reality (jinen) is made known.
