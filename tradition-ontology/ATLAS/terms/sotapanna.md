# sotāpanna

`trm:sotapanna` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** stream-enterer

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): MN 118:12: a monk who, with three fetters ended, is not liable to fall into the lower worlds, is fixed in destiny and heads for awakening.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): One who possesses the noble eightfold path (SN 55.5), has the eye of Dhamma, has abandoned identity view, doubt and clinging to rules and vows, and is no longer bound for the lower realms (DN 16).
