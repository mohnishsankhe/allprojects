# pratibimba

`trm:pratibimba` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रतिबिम्ब
**Literal:** reflection

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Puruṣa, reflected in buddhi and taking on its shade, appears to have knowledge and pleasure, while buddhi, taking on the shade of consciousness, appears conscious (Vācaspati on SK 5).
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The soul as reflection of God — dependent on its original, similar to it and eternally different from it; the 'adjunct' that makes it a reflection is its own nature, so the reflection is eternal (not produced by an external upādhi as in Advaita).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The universe as a reflection in the mirror of consciousness, appearing distinct yet undivided from it (TĀ 3; Paramārthasāra 12-13).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Reflection: the jīva (or Īśvara) as the reflection of consciousness in an adjunct, like the sun in water (BS 3.2.18).
