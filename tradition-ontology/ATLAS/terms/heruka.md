# heruka

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** (glossed) blood-drinker

## Definitions by tradition
- [Cakrasaṃvara (Saṃvara, Heruka) tradition in India](../lineages/cakrasamvara.md): The wrathful, cremation-ground form of the buddha — Cakrasaṃvara, Hevajra and others — embodying great bliss; the Hevajra glosses 'he' as compassion and 'vajra' as wisdom.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): The wrathful, blood-drinking forms of the buddhas (khrag 'thung), such as the Eight Herukas of the Kagye and the wrathful deities of the bardo.
