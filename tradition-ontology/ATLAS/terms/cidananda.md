## Definitions by tradition
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The bliss of consciousness gained by unfolding the centre, which, made firm, is liberation in life (PH 16-17).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The self as consciousness and bliss (YŚ 10.1; Jñānasāra; the pen-name of the poet Cidānanda).
