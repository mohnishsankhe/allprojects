# pitṛ

`trm:pitr` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पितृ
**Literal:** father, ancestor

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The ancestors, to whose company the dead is joined by sapiṇḍīkaraṇa and who are fed by śrāddha; they dwell in the south below the earth.
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The dead ancestors, lower, higher and middle, who have gone to the life-breath and rejoice with Yama in svadhā (RV 10.14–15).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The ancestors, who receive food monthly, svadhā as drink, the moon as their light (ŚB 2.4.2.2).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The primeval deities of several classes (Somasads, Agniṣvāttas, Barhiṣads…) (MDh 3.192–199).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The ancestors, who fall when deprived of the rice-ball and water offerings because of varṇa-mixture (1.42).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: 'of the ancestors (pitṛ) I am Aryaman' (10.29); the Lord is the father (pitā) of the world (11.43).
