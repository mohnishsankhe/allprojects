# ulṭā sādhanā

`trm:ulta-sadhana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

**Language:** Bengali

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): 'Reverse practice': turning the downward, outward flow (of desire and bodily substance) back upward and inward; recorded here as a doctrine only (restricted).
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): The Sahajiyā reversal by which kāma is turned into prema.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Reversed practice: turning the downward flows (breath, bindu, nectar, mind) upward — the viparīta-karaṇa of the Sanskrit texts (GŚ 59).

## Forms in other languages
- Hindi: ulṭā sādhanā (Nāth usage)  — partial
- Sanskrit: viparīta-karaṇa  — partial

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [ūrdhvaretas](urdhvaretas.md) — Both describe an upward reversal of the vital substance; the Bāul form is a couple's practice and is framed devotionally.
