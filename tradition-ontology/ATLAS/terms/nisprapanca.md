# niṣprapañca

`trm:nisprapanca` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** free of proliferation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): spros bral: 'freedom from elaboration' or 'simplicity', the second yoga of Mahāmudrā — the mind's nature is realized as rootless, beyond arising, abiding and ceasing, existence and non-existence; Nāropa: 'free of all elaborations'.
