# pramāda

`trm:pramada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रमाद
**Literal:** heedlessness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Heedlessness, which Sanatsujāta calls death (MBh 5.42.4); a mark of tamas (BhG 14.8, 14.13).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Carelessness: not cultivating the means of samādhi (YBh 1.30); Vyāsa warns that carelessness watches for an opening to revive the afflictions (YBh 3.51).
