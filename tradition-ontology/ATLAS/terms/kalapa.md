# kalāpa (rūpa-kalāpa)

`trm:kalapa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** group, cluster

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The smallest units of matter: groups of inseparable elements (at least eight) arising and ceasing together.
- [Pa-Auk tradition (Pa-Auk Tawya)](../lineages/pa-auk.md): Tiny particles seen with the light of access or jhāna concentration and analysed into their elements as the basis of insight.
- [U Ba Khin – S. N. Goenka tradition (Sayagyi U Ba Khin lineage)](../lineages/u-ba-khin-goenka.md): The subtle units whose rapid arising and passing is experienced as vibration in the body (U Ba Khin).
