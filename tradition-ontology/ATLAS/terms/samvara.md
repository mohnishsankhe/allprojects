# saṃvara

`trm:samvara` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** संवर
**Literal:** stopping

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In TS 9.1 the stopping (nirodha) of inflow; effected by the guptis, samitis, dharma, anuprekṣās, conquest of hardships and cāritra (9.2). 9.3 adds that by tapas there is shedding as well. That its 'ca' makes tapas a means of saṃvara too is the commentators' reading.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The blocking of inflow, achieved by the controls, carefulnesses, virtues, reflections, endurance of hardships and conduct (TS 9.1–2).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Discipline (restraint) as unmanifest matter: prātimokṣa, dhyāna-born and pure discipline; its opposite is non-discipline.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Vow (the prātimokṣa, bodhisattva and vidyādhara vows); in the Cakrasaṃvara cycle also 'binding' or 'supreme bliss' — the commentators read Saṃvara as the supreme bliss that binds the wheel of phenomena.
