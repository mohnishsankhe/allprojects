# jāṭharāgni

`trm:jatharagni` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** जाठराग्नि
**Literal:** fire of the belly

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The chief digestive fire in the abdomen, fanned by samāna, which cooks the food and on which the other fires depend (Ca Ci 15.6-8; AHS Sū 12.8, 12.11-12 as pācaka).
