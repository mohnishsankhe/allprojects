**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** སྤྲོས་བྲལ
**Literal:** free from elaborations

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): The final Madhyamaka view as Gorampa and the Sakya hold it: freedom from all four extremes — existence, non-existence, both and neither — including clinging to emptiness as a negation.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): Accepted as a description of the ultimate only in the sense of freedom from the elaborations of inherent existence and of dualistic appearance to the meditative mind; not as a negation of conventional existence.
