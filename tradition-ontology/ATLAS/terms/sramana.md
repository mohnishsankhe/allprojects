# śramaṇa

`trm:sramana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** श्रमण
**Literal:** one who toils / exerts himself

## Definitions by tradition
- [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md): A homeless ascetic outside the Vedic sacrificial order; the rival teachers of DN 2 and the Buddha and Mahāvīra are all śramaṇas; Aśoka pairs 'brāhmaṇas and śramaṇas' as the two orders found everywhere.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): A samaṇa is one who has calmed (samita) evils, lives in calm (samacariyā) and harms no one.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): One is a samaṇa not by shaving the head but by equanimity (samayā).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): In the Taittirīya Āraṇyaka, a name of the wind-girdled seers who practised austerity and celibacy (TA 2.7.1).
