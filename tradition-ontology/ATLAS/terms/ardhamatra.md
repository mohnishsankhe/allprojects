# ardhamātrā

`trm:ardhamatra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अर्धमात्रा
**Literal:** half-measure

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The fourth, half measure of Oṃ, beyond the guṇas and beyond speech, touched at the crown (MkP 42.5-6, 42.14).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The subtle fourth element of Oṃ beyond a, u and m; the 'head' of the Oṃ-bird.
