# gandharva

`trm:gandharva` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): A name of the intermediate being ('eater of smells'); the sūtra's gandharva present at conception is cited as scriptural proof of the intermediate existence.
- [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md): The sacred music of the gods (dear to the gods, NŚ 28), of notes, rhythm and words; the upaveda of the Sāmaveda.
