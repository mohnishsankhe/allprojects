# unmeṣa

`trm:unmesa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उन्मेष
**Literal:** opening (of the eyes); upsurge

## Definitions by tradition
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): The opening-out of spanda: that from which a new thought arises in one absorbed in one thought (SK 3.9); with nimeṣa, the pulse of creation and dissolution (1.1).
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): Īśvara is the outward unmeṣa, Sadāśiva the inward nimeṣa (ĪPK 3.1.3).
