# vyūha

`trm:vyuha` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** व्यूह
**Literal:** arrangement, emanated form

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): The fourfold form (caturmūrti) of the Lord — Vāsudeva, Saṃkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna, Aniruddha — each arising from the prior (MBh 12.326.68–69).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The four emanated forms of the Lord — Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna, Aniruddha — each manifesting a pair of the six qualities while possessing all six.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The four emanations Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha, each manifesting the six qualities or pairs of them, for creation, maintenance, dissolution and for the meditation of seekers.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The fourfold form of the Lord (Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna, Aniruddha) in the Triśikhi's concentrations and the Mudgala.
