# anāvṛtti

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनावृत्ति
**Literal:** non-return

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 8.15–16, 8.21, 8.23, 8.26: non-return (anāvṛtti) as against return (āvṛtti, punar-āvṛtti): the worlds up to Brahmā's realm are subject to return, but for those who reach the Lord there is no rebirth; departing yogins go by the bright course to non-return and by the dark course to return.
