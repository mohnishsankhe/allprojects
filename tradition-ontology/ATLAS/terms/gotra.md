# gotra

`trm:gotra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** lineage, family, disposition

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The spiritual lineage determining a being's vehicle: the Laṅkāvatāra lists five (śrāvaka, pratyekabuddha, Tathāgata, indeterminate, none); Vimalakīrti calls the passions the Tathāgata's family.
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): The buddha-lineage present in all beings is one of the three reasons they are tathāgatagarbhas.
