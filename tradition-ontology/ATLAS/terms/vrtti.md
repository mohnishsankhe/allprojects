# vṛtti

`trm:vrtti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वृत्ति
**Literal:** turning; activity, modification

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): An activity (modification) of the mind; five kinds — valid cognition, error, conceptual construction, sleep and memory — each afflicted or unafflicted (YS 1.5–6); the seer, when not in its own form, conforms to them (1.4).
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The three livelihoods: alms, discarded food, and what is obtained.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): A mode or modification of the inner organ; in perception it goes out and takes the form of the object; the mode in the form of Brahman removes ignorance.

## Forms in other languages
