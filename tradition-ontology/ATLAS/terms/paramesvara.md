# Parameśvara

`trm:paramesvara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** परमेश्वर
**Literal:** supreme lord

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Supreme lord: Arjuna's address as he asks to see the Lord's sovereign form (11.3).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13.28: one who sees the supreme lord standing the same in all beings, not perishing among the perishing, sees.
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): The supreme Lord, splendid with being, consciousness and bliss, with parts (sakala), source of Śakti, nāda and bindu (ŚT 1.7).
- [Mahānubhāva panth](../lineages/mahanubhava.md): The one Supreme, distinct from souls, deities and world, who alone grants liberation and incarnates as the five Kṛṣṇas.
