# ro snyoms (equal taste)

`trm:ronyom` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tibetan

**Literal:** equalized taste

## Definitions by tradition
- [Drukpa Kagyu](../lineages/drukpa-kagyu.md): Equalizing the taste: the Drukpa practice of meeting adverse circumstances as one taste with the mind's nature (the six cycles of equal taste).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): In the Karṇatantravajrapada 'blending and equal taste' are applied to the three pledges; in the bka' dpe phyi ma the 'equal taste of the four elements' is needed for inner heat to progress.
