# prameya

`trm:prameya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रमेय
**Literal:** what is to be known

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The twelve objects whose true knowledge leads to liberation: self, body, senses, objects, cognition, mind, activity, faults, rebirth, fruit, pain, liberation (NS 1.1.9); other objects (the Vaiśeṣika categories) are acknowledged (NBh 1.1.9).
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Objects of knowledge and means of knowledge are established only in mutual dependence, and so neither is established by own-nature (VV 46–51).
