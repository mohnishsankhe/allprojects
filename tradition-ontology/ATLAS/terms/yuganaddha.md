# yuganaddha

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Ārya tradition of the Guhyasamāja](../lineages/arya-guhyasamaja.md): Union: where saṃsāra and nirvāṇa, the two truths, generation and completion, wisdom and compassion become one (Pañcakrama 5); the goal of the path.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): zung 'jug, union: of emptiness and clarity, appearance and emptiness, bliss and emptiness; in the six yogas the 'body of union' that arises from clear light, first as the learner's then as the non-learner's union.
