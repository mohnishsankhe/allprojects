# vicāra

`trm:vicara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विचार
**Literal:** inquiry, reflection

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Inquiry: discriminating reflection, guided by scripture and the wise, into what one is and what the world is; the second gatekeeper and the lamp by which the real is seen.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The mind's subtle engagement with its support (YBh 1.17); coalescence with subtle objects (1.44).
