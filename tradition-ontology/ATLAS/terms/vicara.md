# vicāra

`trm:vicara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विचार
**Literal:** inquiry, reflection

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Inquiry: discriminating reflection, guided by scripture and the wise, into what one is and what the world is; the second gatekeeper and the lamp by which the real is seen.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The mind's subtle engagement with its support (YBh 1.17); coalescence with subtle objects (1.44).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Keeping the mind connected to its object; with vitakka the verbal formation (MN 44) and a factor of the first jhāna.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In Jain meditation theory (TS spelling vīcāra) the moving between object, word and activity (TS 9.44).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Sustained thought: continued pressure of the mind on the object; a factor of the first jhāna (and of the second in the fivefold scheme).

## Forms in other languages

- Sanskrit: vicāra  — exact
