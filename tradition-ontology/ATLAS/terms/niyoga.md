# niyoga

`trm:niyoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नियोग
**Literal:** appointment

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The appointment of a widow or wife to bear a son by a brother-in-law or sapiṇḍa; permitted and then condemned in Manu (9.59–68).
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): The command conveyed by the injunctive, which by itself prompts the qualified person, independent of his desire for the fruit.
