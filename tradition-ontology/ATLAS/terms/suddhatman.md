# śuddhātman

`trm:suddhatman` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शुद्धात्मन्
**Literal:** pure self

## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The soul seen by the pure standpoint as unbound, one, formless and made of knowledge and perception (Samayasāra 14, 38).
- [Adhyātma movement (Adhyātma-mata)](../lineages/adhyatma-jain.md): The object of self-experience and the refuge of right view.
