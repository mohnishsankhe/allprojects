# prajñāparādha

`trm:prajnaparadha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रज्ञापराध
**Literal:** offence against (one's own) wisdom; failure of discernment

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The inauspicious act of body, speech or mind done by one whose intellect (dhī), resolve (dhṛti) and memory (smṛti) have lapsed; the provoker of all doṣas (Ca Śā 1.102); wrong knowing and wrong acting of the intellect (Śā 1.109); one of the three causes of disease (Sū 11.43); the source of adharma and so of epidemics (Vi 3.20) and the cause of exogenous insanity (Ni 7.10); all mental disorders are born of it (Sū 7.52).
