# gzhi

`trm:zhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Literal:** ground, basis

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): The ground: the primordial state of all beings and phenomena prior to saṃsāra and nirvāṇa, whose essence is primordially pure, nature spontaneously present and compassion all-pervading; recognized it is buddhahood, unrecognized it is the basis of delusion.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): In the ground-path-fruit scheme of the Nyingma tantras, the ground is the buddha-nature present in beings, the path its recognition and cultivation, the fruit its manifestation.
