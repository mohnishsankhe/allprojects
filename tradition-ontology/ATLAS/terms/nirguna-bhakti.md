# nirguṇa bhakti

`trm:nirguna-bhakti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निर्गुणभक्ति
**Literal:** devotion beyond the guṇas

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Attributeless devotion: the fourth, highest devotion beyond the tāmasa, rājasa and sāttvika forms, by which one transcends the guṇas.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The unbroken flow of the mind to the Lord on merely hearing of his qualities, like the Gaṅgā into the sea (BhP 3.29.11-12).
