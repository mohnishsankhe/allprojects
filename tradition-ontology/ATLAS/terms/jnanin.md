# jñānin

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ज्ञानिन्
**Literal:** knower

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7.16–19: the knower, fourth of the four kinds of virtuous worshippers; ever yoked and of single devotion (eka-bhakti), he is distinguished, exceedingly dear to the Lord and the Lord to him, held to be the Lord's very self; at the end of many births he takes refuge knowing 'Vāsudeva is all'.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The knower, the fourth and highest of the devotees, whom the Lord regards as his very self (BhG 7.16–18).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: The knowers who see the truth and teach knowledge (4.34); in 6.46 the yogin is held greater than the jñānins.
