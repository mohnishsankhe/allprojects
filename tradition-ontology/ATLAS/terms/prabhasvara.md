# prabhāsvara

`trm:prabhasvara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** clear light, luminosity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Ārya tradition of the Guhyasamāja](../lineages/arya-guhyasamaja.md): Clear light, the fourth empty (sarvaśūnya), the cause of the world and the ultimate truth of the two (Pañcakrama 2.5, 3.13–15).
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): The clear light of one's own mind, never obscured by aeons of saṃsāra (Gaṅgāmā v12).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): 'od gsal, clear light: the luminous nature of all phenomena and of mind, pure from the beginning like space; fivefold (natural, wisdom, samādhi, suchness, realization) and arising at five times (death, the central channel, sleep, one taste, insight); at death, recognizing it as mother and child meeting is dharmakāya buddhahood.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): In the Vajra Verses, when consciousness dissolves into clear light there appears something like a cloudless sky.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The subtlest mind, manifested in the completion stage and at death, which when joined with the realisation of emptiness is the mantra Mahāmudrā.
