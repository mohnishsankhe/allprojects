# maṅgaḷāśāsana

`trm:mangalasasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** wishing auspiciousness

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): Praying for the Lord's own welfare out of love — Periyāḻvār's 'many years' (Tiruppallāṇṭu); the Āḻvārs' singing of each temple is called their maṅgaḷāśāsana of it.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Praying for the Lord's well-being out of love, as the Āḻvārs did, instead of asking anything for oneself.
