# videha

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विदेह
**Literal:** bodiless

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Beings who experience a state like kaivalya without a body; Vyāsa calls them gods (YBh 1.19); Bhoja: those who rest content in bliss-samādhi (RM 1.17).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The central region of Jambūdvīpa where time does not cycle and Tīrthaṅkaras (such as Sīmandhara) teach now (TS 3.10, 3.31).
