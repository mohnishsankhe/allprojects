# samatva

`trm:samatva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** समत्व
**Literal:** sameness, equanimity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Equanimity is called yoga: being the same in success and failure (2.48); making pleasure and pain, gain and loss, victory and defeat the same (2.38); the one who is the same in pain and pleasure is fit for immortality (2.15).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Evenness in success and failure, which is called yoga (BhG 2.48); equality toward friend and foe (6.9, 12.18).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Sameness (sama, sāmya): being the same in success and failure (4.22); the mind established in sameness conquers creation here, because Brahman is flawless and the same (5.19); the yukta sees clod, stone and gold as the same (6.8) and has equal regard for friend and foe (6.9); Arjuna calls the yoga taught 'yoga as sāmya' (6.33).
