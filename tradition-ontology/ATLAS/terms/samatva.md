# samatva

`trm:samatva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** समत्व
**Literal:** sameness, equanimity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Equanimity is called yoga: being the same in success and failure (2.48); making pleasure and pain, gain and loss, victory and defeat the same (2.38); the one who is the same in pain and pleasure is fit for immortality (2.15).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Evenness in success and failure, which is called yoga (BhG 2.48); equality toward friend and foe (6.9, 12.18).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Sameness (sama, sāmya): being the same in success and failure (4.22); the mind established in sameness conquers creation here, because Brahman is flawless and the same (5.19); the yukta sees clod, stone and gold as the same (6.8) and has equal regard for friend and foe (6.9); Arjuna calls the yoga taught 'yoga as sāmya' (6.33).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: Evenness: constant evenness of thought (sama-cittatva) amid the desired and the undesired (13.10); the one beyond the guṇas is the same in pain and pleasure, toward clod, stone and gold, in blame and praise, honour and dishonour (14.24-14.25); the Lord is seen abiding equally (samam) in all beings (13.28-13.29).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Evenness (samatā) is among the states that arise from the Lord (10.5); worshippers of the imperishable are even-minded (samabuddhi) everywhere (12.4); the dear devotee is the same in pain and pleasure (12.13), to enemy and friend, in honour and dishonour, cold and heat, and holds blame and praise equal (12.18–19).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Sameness: the sāttvic agent is unchanged in success and failure (18.26); the one become Brahman is the same toward all beings (18.54).
