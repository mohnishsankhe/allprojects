# arthavāda

`trm:arthavada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अर्थवाद
**Literal:** statement of purpose; explanatory praise

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Vedic passages that do not themselves enjoin; they are meaningful as praise of what is enjoined, forming one sentence with the injunctions (MS 1.2.7). A fruit stated for a subsidiary is arthavāda (MS 4.3.1).
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): A sentence whose purport is praise or blame; supplementing an injunction or a prohibition; classified as praise, blame, another's deed and ancient narrative, or as figurative (guṇavāda), restating (anuvāda) and fact-stating (bhūtārthavāda).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The explanatory portion of a Brāhmaṇa (blame, praise, the deeds of others, ancient precedent) as distinct from injunction (ĀpŚS 24.1.33).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Vedic statements of praise, blame, another's practice and ancient precedent (NS 2.1.65 DCS).
