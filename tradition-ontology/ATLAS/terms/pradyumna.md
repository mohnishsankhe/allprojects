# Pradyumna

`trm:pradyumna` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रद्युम्न
**Literal:** the pre-eminently mighty

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): The third form, born from Saṃkarṣaṇa; the liberated, 'becoming mind', enter Pradyumna (MBh 12.326.68; 12.332.15).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The vyūha manifesting lordship and virility; in Śaṅkara's report, identified with mind (manas).
