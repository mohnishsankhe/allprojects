# jālandhara (bandha)

`trm:jalandhara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** जालन्धर
**Literal:** net-holding

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The throat lock that binds the network of channels and stops the nectar falling into the fire (HYP 3.70–73; DYŚ 126–128).
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): The 'word' (pada) level of the fourfold yoni (Kubjikāmata 11.7).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): Where Satī's breasts fell; the goddess there is Caṇḍī (Kālikā Purāṇa 18.43, 18.49).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): One of the four great seats (pīṭha), which in the Yoginīhṛdaya are transformations of the four fused powers and are located in the subtle body.
