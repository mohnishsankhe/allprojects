## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The five concentrations in which breath and mind are held in the place of each element with its colour, shape, seed and deity; the GS counts them among its twenty-five mudrās, the GŚ calls them the stabilising, liquefying, burning, whirling and drying concentrations.
