# āgantuka-kleśa

`trm:agantuka-klesa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** adventitious defilement

## Definitions by tradition
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): Defilements that are not the mind's nature but come to it like dust or clouds, separable from the tathāgatagarbha and removable.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): Defilements are adventitious to the luminous mind and therefore removable.
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): The stains are adventitious; they have no support in the nature of mind and cannot survive the cultivation of their antidote.
