# āgantuka-kleśa

`trm:agantuka-klesa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** adventitious defilement

## Definitions by tradition
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): Defilements that are not the mind's nature but come to it like dust or clouds, separable from the tathāgatagarbha and removable.
