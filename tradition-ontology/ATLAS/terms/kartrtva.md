# kartṛtva

`trm:kartrtva` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कर्तृत्व
**Literal:** agency; being the doer

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: The lord creates neither agency nor actions for the world, nor the linking of action with its fruit; svabhāva is at work (5.14); the yoked knower thinks 'I do nothing at all', the senses moving among their objects (5.8–9).
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): Agency, imperishable, as opposed to effect-hood, which perishes (SK 1.14).
