# kevali-bhukti (kavalāhāra)

`trm:kevalibhukti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** केवलिभुक्ति
**Literal:** eating by the omniscient

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The omniscient takes morsel-food (kavalāhāra) while his body lasts; hunger, thirst and the other feeling-karma hardships occur in him.
- [Yāpanīya](../lineages/yapaniya.md): Affirmed and argued by Śākaṭāyana.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Denied: the omniscient is free of hunger and thirst (two of the eighteen faults) and his body is sustained without morsel-food.

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: kavalāhāra  — partial — morsel-eating
