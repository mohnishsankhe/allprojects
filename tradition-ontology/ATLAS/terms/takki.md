# takkī

`trm:takki` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** reasoner

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): One who holds a view by reasoning and inquiry rather than meditative experience; a ground in several classes of the sixty-two, and the third unreliable teacher of MN 76.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Reasoners who follow their own speculation cannot escape suffering.
