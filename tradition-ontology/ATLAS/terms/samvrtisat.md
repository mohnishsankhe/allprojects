# saṃvṛtisat

`trm:samvrtisat` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** existing by convention

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): That which exists conventionally: a thing of which the idea no longer arises when it is broken or its components are mentally removed — a pot, water.
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): What is not capable of causal efficacy — universals and conceptual objects.
