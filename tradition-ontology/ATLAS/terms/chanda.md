# chanda

`trm:chanda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** desire, zeal, wish to act

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Wholesome desire to act, the first basis of power (SN 51.20); in 'kāmacchanda' sensual desire.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Wish to act regarding the object; ethically variable; one of the four predominants.
