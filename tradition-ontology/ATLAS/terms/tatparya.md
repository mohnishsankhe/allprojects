# tātparya (mahātātparya)

`trm:tatparya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तात्पर्य
**Literal:** purport

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The intended import of a text, known by the marks (opening and close, repetition, novelty, result, praise, reasoning); the 'great purport' of all scripture is Viṣṇu's supremacy.
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): The speaker's intention, knowledge of which is a condition of verbal cognition (Tātparyavāda).
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The intended sense of a passage, settled by the rules of interpretation; the determinants listed in Vedānta manuals as six marks (upakrama–upasaṃhāra, abhyāsa, apūrvatā, phala, arthavāda, upapatti) draw on Mīmāṃsā rules.
