# satguru (sadguru)

`trm:satguru` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Hindi

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The true guru, by whose grace alone the body and the supreme state come to one taste (SSP 5); the 'guru-ful' drink the nectar, the guru-less go thirsty.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The true guru who gives the Name and the Word and opens the inner eye; praised equal to or above God (guru and Govind).
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): The Sant Satguru: a living master who has reached the highest region and is its embodiment; indispensable for initiation and inner guidance.
- [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md): The true guru who gives knowledge of the Self and removes doubt, as opposed to givers of mantras for gain or displays of power.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): The true guru, the helmsman of the boat of the human body.
