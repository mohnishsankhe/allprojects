**Literal:** Time-person

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The 'Person of Time': the zodiac as a body whose limbs are the twelve signs from the head (Aries) to the feet (Pisces) (BJ 1.4), whose self is the Sun and mind the Moon (BJ 2.1); an ocean no non-seer can cross (BS 2.4).
