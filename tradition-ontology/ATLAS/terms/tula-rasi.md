## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The seventh sign (Libra), 30° of the zodiac made of nine asterism-quarters; the bladder (lower belly) of Kālapuruṣa (BJ 1.4); ruled by Venus; movable; of the air triplicity (later texts); odd, male and cruel (BJ 1.11); Varāhamihira's alternative (Greek-derived) name: jūka (BJ 1.8); dignities: Saturn exalted (20°); Sun in fall (BJ 1.13).
