# jñānayoga

`trm:jnana-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ज्ञानयोग
**Literal:** yoga of knowledge

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The commitment (niṣṭhā) of the sāṃkhyas, one of the two taught by Kṛṣṇa of old (3.3).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The path of knowledge of the Sāṃkhyas, one of the two ancient paths named at BhG 3.3; its culmination is steadfastness in knowledge of the self.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The yoga of knowledge, fitting those disgusted with action who have renounced it.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Contemplation of the self as distinct from matter, leading to the vision of the self (ātmāvalokana), which prepares for bhakti.
