## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): A 'holder of the group': one of the chief disciples of a Tīrthaṅkara, who hears his teaching and composes it as scripture and leads a section of the community; Mahāvīra had eleven, headed by Indrabhūti Gautama.
