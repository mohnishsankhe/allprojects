# lokuttara

`trm:lokuttara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** supramundane, transcendent

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The noble, taintless path factors, as distinct from the mundane ones affected by the effluents (MN 117).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Transcending the world of the five aggregates: the four paths, four fruits and nibbāna.
