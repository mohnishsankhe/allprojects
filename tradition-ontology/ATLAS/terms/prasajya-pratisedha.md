# prasajya-pratiṣedha

`trm:prasajya-pratisedha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** non-implicative (simple) negation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Svātantrika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/svatantrika.md): A negation that merely denies without implying any other positive thing; Bhāviveka takes the negations of arising in MMK 1.1 as of this kind.
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Emptiness as the mere absence of own-nature is a negation of this kind (Tibetan exegesis of Candrakīrti).

## Forms in other languages
- Tibetan: med dgag  — exact — contrasted with ma yin dgag (paryudāsa-pratiṣedha), implicative negation
