# pāda (of an Āgama)

`trm:pada-agama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पाद
**Literal:** quarter, section

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): One of the four sections of an ideal Āgama: knowledge (vidyā/jñāna), ritual (kriyā), yoga, and conduct (caryā); all of Śiva's scriptures are said to have the four pādas.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The Pādma Saṃhitā is arranged in the same four pādas (jñāna, yoga, kriyā, caryā).
