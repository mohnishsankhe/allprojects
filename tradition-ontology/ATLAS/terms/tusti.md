# tuṣṭi

`trm:tusti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तुष्टि
**Literal:** contentment, complacency

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Nine contentments (SK 50): four internal — resting content with nature, with outward means (such as the triple staff and water-pot), with time, or with luck — and five external, from abstaining from objects for seeing their faults; they do not bring liberation and, with error and incapacity, are the curb on accomplishment (SK 51).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Contentment, among the states of beings that arise from the Lord (10.5).
