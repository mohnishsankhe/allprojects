# Vāyu

`trm:vayu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वायु
**Literal:** wind, breath

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The deity Vāyu, highest soul, giver of liberation by Viṣṇu's permission, whose three avatāras are Hanumān, Bhīma and Madhva.
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The breath as wind: ten vāyus move in the channels as the living soul's vehicles (GŚ 23–25; SS 3.4–8); when the wind moves the mind moves (HYP 2.2).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Substance with touch and without colour, inferred from touch; within the body it is breath.
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The Wind, 'the breath of the gods, germ of the world', whose sounds are heard, not his form (RV 10.168.4).
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): In the Siddha ninety-six principles, the ten vital winds (Tamil vāyu, with Tamil names pirāṇaṉ, apāṉaṉ, viyāṉaṉ, utāṉaṉ, camāṉaṉ, nākaṉ, kūrmaṉ, kirukaraṉ, tēvatattaṉ, taṉañcayaṉ).
