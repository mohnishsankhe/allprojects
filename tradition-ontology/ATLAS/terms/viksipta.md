# vikṣipta

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विक्षिप्त
**Literal:** distracted

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The distracted mind; samādhi in it, subordinate to distraction, is not yoga (YBh 1.1).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): In Hemacandra's scheme the first, restless state of mind (YŚ 12.2).
