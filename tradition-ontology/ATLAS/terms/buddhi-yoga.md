# buddhiyoga

`trm:buddhi-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** बुद्धियोग
**Literal:** the yoga of understanding

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The discipline of action yoked with understanding: action is far inferior to it (2.49); yoked with it one casts off good and evil deeds (2.50) and the wise, abandoning the fruit of action, are freed from the bondage of birth (2.51); introduced as the 'yoga' form of the teaching at 2.39.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Action guided by the resolute understanding that is free from attachment to fruits (BhG 2.49–50); the Lord gives it to his loving devotees (10.10) and Arjuna is told to resort to it (18.57).
