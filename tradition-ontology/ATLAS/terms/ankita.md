**Literal:** mark, seal (also mudrike)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): The signature name for the Lord that closes a vacanakāra's vacanas — Kūḍalasaṅgamadēva (Basava), Guhēśvara (Allama), Cennamallikārjuna (Akka), Rāmanātha (Dāsimayya) — usually received from the guru.
