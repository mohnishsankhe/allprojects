# Pūrṇagiri

`trm:purnagiri` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पूर्णगिरि
**Literal:** the full mountain

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): The 'form' (rūpa) level of the fourfold yoni (Kubjikāmata 11.7).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): Where Satī's shoulders and neck fell; the goddess there is Pūrṇeśvarī (Kālikā Purāṇa 18.43, 18.49).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): One of the four great seats (pīṭha), which in the Yoginīhṛdaya are transformations of the four fused powers and are located in the subtle body.
