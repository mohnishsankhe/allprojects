# āpaddharma

`trm:apaddharma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आपद्धर्म
**Literal:** dharma in distress

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Conduct permitted when normal dharma cannot be kept, illustrated by Viśvāmitra in famine (MBh 12.139).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The relaxed rules of livelihood and conduct permitted in emergencies (MDh 10.81ff.).
