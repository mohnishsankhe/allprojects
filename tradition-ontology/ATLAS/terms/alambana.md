# ālambana

`trm:alambana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आलम्बन
**Literal:** support; object

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The support or object on which the mind rests (1.10, 1.38); samādhis with a support are samprajñāta (YBh 1.17).
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): In the Yogaviṃśikā the fourth yoga: meditation with an external support such as the Jina's image.
