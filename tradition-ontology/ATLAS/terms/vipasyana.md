# vipaśyanā

`trm:vipasyana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विपश्यना
**Literal:** insight

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Discerning examination of the image seen in samādhi as nothing but cognition.
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): Insight arising from the correct analysis of reality (bhūtapratyavekṣā), joined with calm (Bhāvanākrama).
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Insight united with calm destroys the afflictions (BCA 8.4).
