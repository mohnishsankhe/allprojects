# vipaśyanā

`trm:vipasyana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विपश्यना
**Literal:** insight

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Discerning examination of the image seen in samādhi as nothing but cognition.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The wisdom, conjoined with pliancy induced by analysis itself, that discriminates the nature of its object — above all the selflessness of persons and phenomena; the only means of cutting the root of saṃsāra.
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): The yoga of the perfection of wisdom, joined with method, meditating without conceptualization on the absence of own-nature.
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): Insight arising from the correct analysis of reality (bhūtapratyavekṣā), joined with calm (Bhāvanākrama).
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Insight united with calm destroys the afflictions (BCA 8.4).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): lhag mthong: in Mahāmudrā, insight that looks directly at the mind in stillness and movement and recognizes its empty, luminous nature — 'looking again and again at the mind that cannot be looked at'.
