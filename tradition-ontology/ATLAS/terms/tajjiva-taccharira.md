# tajjīva-taccharīra

`trm:tajjiva-taccharira` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तज्जीवतच्छरीर
**Literal:** the soul is that, the body is that

## Definitions by tradition
- [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md): (As reported) The soul is the same as the body: bounded by the skin, it ends when the body dies.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): 'The soul is the body' and 'the soul is one thing, the body another' are both extremes that make the holy life impossible.
