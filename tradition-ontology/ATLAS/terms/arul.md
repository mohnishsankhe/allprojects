# aruḷ

`trm:arul` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tamil

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The Lord's grace: he 'graciously gave' (aruḷiṉaṉ) knowledge-and-love free of delusion (TVM 1.1.1); his grace (aruḷ) is received by all who recite the Tiruppāvai (TP 30).
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Grace: Śiva's own power (aruḷ-catti) acting toward the soul; ever present, it removes the power of mala and comes as the guru.
- [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md): The Lord's grace that saves the devotee from every danger and cuts births.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): In the Kuṟaḷ, aruḷ is compassion for all living beings (chapter 25, Aruḷuṭaimai).
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Grace: for the Siddha poets the guru's descent and self-showing (Aḻukaṇṇi 33–34) and the Lord's flood of compassion (Pattirakiriyār 62).
