**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सदसत्ख्याति
**Literal:** appearance of the existent-and-non-existent

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The Sāṃkhya Sūtra's account of error: what appears in error is both existent and non-existent, since it is sublated (as there) and not sublated (as real elsewhere) (SS 5.56), rejecting the rival accounts (5.52–55).
