# śūnyatā

`trm:sunyata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शून्यता
**Literal:** emptiness, voidness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The lack of own-being (svabhāva) of all dharmas — persons, aggregates, the path and nirvāṇa alike; not nothingness and not an entity: 'form is emptiness, emptiness is form'. Emptiness does not make dharmas empty; they are empty. It is to be realized without being grasped as a view.
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): The tathāgatagarbha is empty of the adventitious, separable defilements and not empty of the inseparable buddha-qualities; emptiness misread as mere absence is criticized.
