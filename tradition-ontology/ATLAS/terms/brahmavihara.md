# brahmavihāra

`trm:brahmavihara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** divine abiding

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Love, compassion, rejoicing and equanimity pervading all directions boundlessly — the path to companionship with Brahmā (DN 13); 'a divine abiding here' (Snp 1.8).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Loving-kindness, compassion, gladness and equanimity developed towards all beings without limit; each has a near and a far enemy.
