# nirāśīḥ

`trm:nirasih` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निराशीः
**Literal:** free from expectation, wishless

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Arjuna is to fight free from expectation and 'mine', having renounced all actions in Kṛṣṇa (3.30).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: without expectation (nirāśīḥ), restrained in thought (citta) and in himself and with all possessions given up, one who does merely bodily action incurs no fault (4.21); the meditating yogin is to be alone, restrained in thought (citta) and in himself, without expectation and without possessions (6.10).
