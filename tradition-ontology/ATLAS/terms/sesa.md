# śeṣa

`trm:sesa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शेष
**Literal:** remainder; subsidiary

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): That which exists for the sake of another (MS 3.1.2); for Bādari only substances, qualities and purificatory acts, for Jaimini also acts (MS 3.1.3–4). Its correlate is the principal (śeṣin).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): That whose nature is to exist for the purpose of another (the śeṣin) and to enhance him; every soul and thing is śeṣa of the Lord.
