# kṣara

`trm:ksara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** क्षर
**Literal:** perishable

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): All souls whose bodies perish — Brahmā, Śiva, the gods and the rest.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): All beings, the perishable puruṣa (BhG 15.16); perishable existence is the adhibhūta (8.4).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: 'The perishable state' (kṣaro bhāvaḥ) is the adhibhūta (8.4); contrasted with Brahman the supreme imperishable (8.3).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 15.16: of the two persons in the world, the perishable is 'all beings'; Kṛṣṇa is beyond the perishable (15.18).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The perishable, paired with the imperishable: primal matter (pradhāna) is the perishable, Hara the imperishable (ŚU 1.10); the perishable is ignorance (ŚU 5.1).
