# sannikarṣa

`trm:sannikarsa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सन्निकर्ष
**Literal:** contact (of sense and object)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The contact of sense and object from which perception arises (NS 1.1.4); six kinds (Uddyotakara).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Six ordinary contacts plus three extraordinary ones.
