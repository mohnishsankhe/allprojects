# sīmā

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dharmaguptaka](../lineages/dharmaguptaka.md): The ritually established boundary within which a Saṅgha performs its acts together; a wheel-schism cannot occur where no boundary is fixed.
