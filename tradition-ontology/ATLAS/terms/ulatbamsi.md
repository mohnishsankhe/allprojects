# ulaṭbāṃsī

`trm:ulatbamsi` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Hindi

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Upside-down speech: paradoxical riddles (the well upside down in the sky, the woman enjoyed by two) used by the Nāths and after them by Kabīr to point to reversed practice.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): 'Upside-down speech': riddling songs of impossible images (the lion grazing cows, the son born before the mother) whose inversion points to the reversal of ordinary perception.
