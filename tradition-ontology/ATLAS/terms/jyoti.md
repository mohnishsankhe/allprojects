# jyoti (jot)

`trm:jyoti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The inner light seen without sun or moon in the encounter with the Lord; Kabīr's first ramainī places light and sound at the origin.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): The light of the inner regions (the flame at Sahasdal Kanwal, 'Jot Nirañjan'); the soul follows the sound and sees the light.
