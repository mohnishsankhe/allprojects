# para-samaya

`trm:para-samaya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** परसमय
**Literal:** other doctrine; the self in the other

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): (1) A rival doctrine — as many as the standpoints (Sanmati 3.47); (2) in the Samayasāra, the soul dwelling in the non-self (karma, body), opposed to sva-samaya, the soul in its own nature.
