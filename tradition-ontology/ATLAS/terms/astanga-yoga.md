## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The eight limbs: restraints, observances, posture, breath-regulation, sense-withdrawal, concentration, meditation, samādhi (2.29), whose practice destroys impurity (2.28).
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tamil aṭṭāṅka yōkam: iyamam, niyamam, ātaṉam, pirāṇāyāmam, pirattiyākāram, tāraṇai, tiyāṉam, camāti (TM 552), commended by Kaṭuveḷi (31).
