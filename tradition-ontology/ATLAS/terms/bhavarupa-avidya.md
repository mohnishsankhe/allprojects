# bhāvarūpa-avidyā

`trm:bhavarupa-avidya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** ignorance of positive nature

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Ignorance as a positive entity (not a mere absence of knowledge), held by the Vivaraṇa line and later Advaita, proved by the experience 'I am ignorant'; rejected as un-Śaṅkaran by Satchidanandendra (recent).
