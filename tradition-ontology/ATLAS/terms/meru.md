# meru

`trm:meru` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मेरु
**Literal:** Mount Meru

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The mountain at the navel of Jambūdvīpa, around which the luminaries turn (TS 3.9, 4.13).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The golden axial mountain at the centre of Jambudvīpa, with the cities of the gods on its summit; the VP gives it 84,000 yojanas above ground and 16,000 below.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The central mountain, made of four jewels, rising 80,000 leagues above the water and sunk as deep, ringed by seven golden ranges and the continents; on its summit the Thirty-Three gods.
