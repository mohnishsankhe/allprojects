# ajñānavāda

`trm:ajnanavada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अज्ञानवाद
**Literal:** doctrine of not-knowing

## Definitions by tradition
- [Ajñāna (the sceptics)](../lineages/ajnana.md): (Jain report) The fourth 'assembly': 67 kinds; its adherents hold ignorance better than knowledge — 'who knows whether the soul exists, and what use is knowing it?'.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The ignorant cannot settle their inquiry in ignorance or instruct others.
