# dakṣiṇāyana

`trm:daksinayana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दक्षिणायन
**Literal:** the southern course (of the sun)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: The six months of the southern course, the last-named station of the dark course, on which the yogin reaches the lunar light and returns (8.25).
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The sun's southern course, the six months opposite to the uttarāyaṇa; in the Gītā part of the path of return (BhG 8.25).
