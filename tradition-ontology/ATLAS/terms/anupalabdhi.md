# anupalabdhi

`trm:anupalabdhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनुपलब्धि
**Literal:** non-apprehension

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): Another name for the sixth means of knowledge, by which absence is known.
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): A reason proving absence: the non-perception of something that fulfils the conditions of perceptibility; one of the three valid reasons, not a separate means of knowledge.
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Proposed (as 'abhāva') as a separate means of knowledge; not different from inference (NS 2.2.2); later, absence is perceived through the qualifier-relation contact.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Non-cognition: the means by which absence is known (a jar's absence on the ground), accepted as a separate means following Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā.
