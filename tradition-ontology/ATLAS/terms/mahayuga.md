# mahāyuga (caturyuga)

`trm:mahayuga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** महायुग
**Literal:** great age

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Twelve thousand divine years = 4,320,000 solar years, the unit in which revolutions are counted (SS 1.15; BSS 1.7).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): A cycle of the four yugas (catur-yuga) of 12,000 divine years (4,320,000 human years).
