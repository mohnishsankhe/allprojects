# cittamātra

`trm:cittamatra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** mind only

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): 'This triple world is mind only' (Daśabhūmika 6); the world is what is seen of one's own mind (Laṅkāvatāra); buddhas seen in samādhi are mind-made (Pratyutpanna).
