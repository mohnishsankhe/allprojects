# cittamātra

`trm:cittamatra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** चित्तमात्र
**Literal:** mind only

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): 'This triple world is mind only' (Daśabhūmika 6); the world is what is seen of one's own mind (Laṅkāvatāra); buddhas seen in samādhi are mind-made (Pratyutpanna).
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): Mind-only: the teaching (Daśabhūmika, Laṅkāvatāra) that the three realms are only mind; used as a synonym of cognition-only, and in the meditative sequence the stage in which the yogin sees objects as mind before abandoning even mind as an object.
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): Accepted as the account of conventional reality — external objects do not exist — before understanding that mind too lacks self (MAL 92–93).
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): The sūtra's 'mind only' denies a creator other than mind, not external form; teachings denying external objects are of provisional meaning (MA 6).
