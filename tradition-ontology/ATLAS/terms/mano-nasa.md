**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मनोनाश
**Literal:** dissolution of mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Destruction of the mind: not the loss of function but the end of the shrinking notions 'this is I, this is not I'; the state of the one liberated while living, in which pure sattva arises.
