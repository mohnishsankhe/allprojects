## Definitions by tradition
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): The attitude of holding oneself dearer than others — the 'one' into which all blame is driven, the door to all misfortune.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): Distinguished from self-grasping (a wrong view about how the self exists): self-cherishing is an attitude of concern for self over others, reversed by exchanging self and other.
