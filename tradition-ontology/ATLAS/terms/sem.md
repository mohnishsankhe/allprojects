# sems

`trm:sem` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Literal:** mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): Ordinary mind: the dualistic, conceptual consciousness that grasps subject and object and arises from not recognizing the ground; to be distinguished from awareness (rig pa). In the Kunjed Gyalpo, however, 'sems' in byang chub sems names the all-creating nature itself.
- [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md): The moving mind distinguished from the nature of mind (sems nyid) in Bön Dzogchen.
