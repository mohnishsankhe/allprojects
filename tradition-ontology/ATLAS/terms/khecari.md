# khecarī

`trm:khecari` · `skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** खेचरी
**Literal:** moving in space

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): (1) The mudrā in which the tongue, reversed, enters the cavity above the palate and the gaze is between the brows — best of mudrās (HYP 1.43, 3.32); RESTRICTED. (2) In HYP 4.43–47, the state in which the breath moves in the middle between sun and moon; the name is explained as 'the mind moves in space (kha) and the tongue moves in space' (3.41).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The seal of the tongue turned back into the cavity of the skull with the gaze between the brows (GŚ 34); a restricted practice.
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): Also a name of Śakti, 'moving in the sky' (KJN 20.10), and of the class of sky-going yoginīs.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): A secret seal learned from a guru with its mantra; bodily procedures recorded summary-only; inwardly the gaze or the renouncer's seal.
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): The state of Śiva when innate knowledge arises (Śiva Sūtra 2.5).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): A seal named among five (VBT 77) and a power-group in the pañcavāha.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tamil kēcari: the Tirumantiram's kēcari yōkam section (third tantra), revered by the Siddhars; restricted, summary only.
