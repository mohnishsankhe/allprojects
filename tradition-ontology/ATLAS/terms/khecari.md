# khecarī

`trm:khecari` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** खेचरी
**Literal:** moving in space

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): A secret seal learned from a guru with its mantra; bodily procedures recorded summary-only; inwardly the gaze or the renouncer's seal.
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): The state of Śiva when innate knowledge arises (Śiva Sūtra 2.5).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): A seal named among five (VBT 77) and a power-group in the pañcavāha.
