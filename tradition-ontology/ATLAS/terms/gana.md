**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** गान
**Literal:** song

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): A Sāmaveda song-book giving the verses transformed into melodies.
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Śiva's attendants — the pramathas such as Reṇuka and Dāruka (SSM 2.25–27; 3.67–77) — and by extension the community of devotees whose defence is gaṇācāra.
