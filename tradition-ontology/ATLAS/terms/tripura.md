# tripurā

`trm:tripura` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** त्रिपुरा
**Literal:** she of the three cities / prior to the three

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The Goddess as she who is prior (purā) to every triad — abodes, principles, seats, liṅgas, letters — the fusion of light and self-awareness (Amṛtānanda on YH 1.6); she 'whose city is threefold' (triangle, circle and square; the three-part mantra) in Bhāskararāya's gloss.
- [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md): She who existed before the three lights (Naṭanānanda's Cidvallī on Kāmakalāvilāsa 2).
