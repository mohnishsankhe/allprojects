# sūrya

`trm:surya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सूर्य
**Literal:** sun

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The sun in the body: the fire at the navel (GŚ 57) and the right channel; it swallows the nectar that drips from the moon, and so the body ages (HYP 3.77); sun and moon make time, suṣumnā devours it (4.17).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The Sun, eye of Mitra and Varuṇa, 'the self of what moves and stands' (RV 1.115.1).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The sun at the navel, of burning nature, that devours the moon's nectar (GŚ 57–58); the right side (AmŚ 71).
