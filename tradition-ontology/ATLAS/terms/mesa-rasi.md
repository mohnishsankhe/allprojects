## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The first sign (Aries), 30° of the zodiac made of nine asterism-quarters; the head of Kālapuruṣa (BJ 1.4); ruled by Mars; movable; of the fire triplicity (later texts); odd, male and cruel (BJ 1.11); Varāhamihira's alternative (Greek-derived) name: kriya (BJ 1.8); dignities: Sun exalted (10°); Saturn in fall (BJ 1.13).
