# kammaṭṭhāna

`trm:kammatthana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** place of work; meditation subject

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): A meditation subject (forty in the Visuddhimagga), given by a teacher; also meditation practice generally.
- [Thai Forest tradition (Kammaṭṭhāna forest tradition)](../lineages/thai-forest.md): In Thai usage 'kammathan' names meditation practice and the forest monks devoted to it (phra kammathan).
- [Borān kammaṭṭhāna (old meditation; the yogāvacara tradition)](../lineages/boran-kammatthana.md): The old meditation system itself (kammaṭṭhāna borān).
