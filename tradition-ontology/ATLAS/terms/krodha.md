# krodha

`trm:krodha` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** क्रोध
**Literal:** anger

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): One of the four passions (8.9); it has four varieties.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Anger: arises from desire and gives rise to delusion (2.62–63); the sage of steady thought is free from it (2.56); named with desire as the enemy born of rajas (3.37).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Anger, born of desire (BhG 2.62), twin of desire from rajas (3.37), a gate of hell (16.21).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: those free of passion, fear and anger came to the Lord's being (4.10); the surge of desire and anger is to be withstood (5.23); the ascetics free of desire and anger have brahma-nirvāṇa near (5.26); the sage from whom desire, fear and anger are gone is ever liberated (5.28).
