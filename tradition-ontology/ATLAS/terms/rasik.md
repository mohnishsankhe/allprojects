# rasik

`trm:rasik` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Hindi
**Native script:** रसिक
**Literal:** one who tastes rasa

## Definitions by tradition
- [Rasik Rāma-bhakti (Rasik sampradāya of the Rāmānandīs)](../lineages/rasik-ramanandi.md): A devotee who contemplates the sweet, intimate love-play of Sītā and Rāma as a companion (sakhī).
- [Rādhāvallabha sampradāya](../lineages/radhavallabha.md): A connoisseur of the pair's love (rasa) in Vṛndāvana; used of the Rādhāvallabha and Haridāsī poets.
- [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md): As in the Rādhāvallabha usage: one who tastes the rasa of the nikuñja.
