# iruviṉai

`trm:iruvinai` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tamil
**Literal:** the two deeds

## Definitions by tradition
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): The two deeds, good (puṇṇiyam) and bad (pāvam), both binding the soul to birth — 'golden and iron fetters'.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): In the Kuṟaḷ (v. 5) the two deeds 'born of darkness' do not cling to those who praise the Lord.
