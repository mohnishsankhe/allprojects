# rasāyana

`trm:rasayana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** रसायन
**Literal:** path/abode of rasa; rejuvenation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): the branch and the means by which the healthy gain long life, memory, intelligence, health, youth, lustre and strength — 'the means of obtaining the excellent rasa and other tissues' (Ca Ci 1.1.7-8; AHS Utt 39.1-2); taken by the hut-entering or the wind-and-sun mode (Ci 1.1.16); useless in an unpurified body (Su Ci 27.4; AHS Utt 39.4).
- [Rasa Śāstra (the science of mercury) and the Raseśvara-darśana](../lineages/rasa-sastra.md): In Rasa Śāstra: rejuvenation by processed mercury and minerals (restricted), the fourth section of the Rasaratnākara 'for the aged' (Rasakhaṇḍa 1.5).
