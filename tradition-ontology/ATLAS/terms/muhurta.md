# muhūrta

`trm:muhurta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मुहूर्त
**Literal:** moment

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): A unit of 30 kalās; thirty muhūrtas make a human day and night (VP 1.3.8).
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): (1) A thirtieth of a day-and-night (48 minutes). (2) An auspicious moment chosen by tithi, weekday, asterism, yoga, karaṇa and rising sign for an undertaking — the subject of the muhūrta texts (Muhūrtacintāmaṇi).
