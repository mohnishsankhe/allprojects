# ajñāna

`trm:ajnana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अज्ञान
**Literal:** non-knowledge

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The tāmasa opposite of knowledge (SK 23); from it (viparyaya) comes bondage (SK 44).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Ignorance, which covers knowledge and so deludes creatures (5.15); destroyed by knowledge of the self, after which knowledge illumines the highest (5.16); the source of the doubt lodged in the heart (4.42); the ignorant one (ajña) perishes (4.40).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Ignorance, synonym of avidyā: 'something positive, indescribable as being or non-being, made of the three guṇas, opposed to knowledge' (Vedāntasāra).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): Not the absence of knowledge but incomplete manifestation of the knowable (TĀ 1.25-26); twofold, in the intellect and in the person, the latter the root of saṃsāra (Tantrasāra 1).
