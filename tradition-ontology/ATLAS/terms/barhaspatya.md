**Literal:** follower of Bṛhaspati

## Definitions by tradition
- [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md): Name for the Lokāyatas as followers of Bṛhaspati; Kamalaśīla calls the deniers of inference 'Bārhaspatyas and others'.
- [Arthaśāstra (the science of statecraft and wealth)](../lineages/arthasastra.md): A school of statecraft holding that only economics and government are sciences, the Veda being a cover.
