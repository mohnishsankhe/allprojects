# pabbajjā

`trm:pabbajja` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** going forth

## Definitions by tradition
- [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md): Leaving the household for the homeless life; its first visible fruit is the honour paid even to a slave who has gone forth.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Leaving the household life for homelessness (DN 2); the going forth of a slave brings the first visible fruit of the contemplative life (DN 2).
