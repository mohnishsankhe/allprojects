# śabdabrahman

`trm:sabdabrahman` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शब्दब्रह्मन्
**Literal:** Brahman as sound/word

## Definitions by tradition
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The unstruck sound in which one is immersed to reach the supreme Brahman (VBT 38).
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The grammarians' absolute, refuted as final by Somānanda (Śivadṛṣṭi 2).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Even one who merely wishes to know yoga passes beyond śabdabrahman (6.44); the chapter does not explain the term (commentators take it as the Veda).
