# nāmarūpa

`trm:namarupa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Native script:** नामरूप
**Literal:** name-and-form, mentality-materiality

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Feeling, perception, volition, contact and attention (name) and the four elements and derived form (form) (SN 12.2); conditions and is conditioned by consciousness like two sheaves of reeds (SN 12.67).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The two 'great monsters/spirits' of Brahman by which it descended into the worlds; as far as name and form extend, so far extends the universe (ŚB 11.2.3).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The mental aggregates (or feeling, perception, volition, contact, attention) and matter; in insight, defined as all there is in place of a being.

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: nāmarūpa  — exact
- Pali: nāmarūpa  — partial — In Buddhism a link of dependent origination, a different doctrinal sense
