# āpta

`trm:apta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आप्त
**Literal:** reliable person; authority

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): One who has directly experienced the thing and speaks from the wish to communicate it as seen — the same definition for seers, āryas and mlecchas (NBh 1.1.7); the Veda is valid because its seers and teachers are reliable (NS 2.1.69).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): One who speaks of things as they are (yathārthavaktā) (Tarkasaṅgraha).
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The reliable authority: the Jina, free of the eighteen faults (including hunger and thirst), omniscient, and teacher of the good; right faith is faith in the āpta, the scripture and the teacher.
