# sāttvika-tyāga

`trm:sattvika-tyaga` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** relinquishment in the mode of goodness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Before every act, relinquishing the sense of being its agent, the sense that it is one's own and desire for its fruit, attributing all to the Lord.
