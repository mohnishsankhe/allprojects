# nitya-vihāra

`trm:nitya-vihara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Braj Bhāṣā
**Native script:** नित्य विहार
**Literal:** eternal play

## Definitions by tradition
- [Rādhāvallabha sampradāya](../lineages/radhavallabha.md): The eternal love-play of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in the groves of Vṛndāvana, without separation, birth-story or departure.
- [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md): The eternal play of the natural pair in the nikuñja.
