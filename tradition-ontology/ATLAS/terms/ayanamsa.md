# ayanāṃśa

`trm:ayanamsa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अयनांश
**Literal:** portion of the solstice

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The arc between the moving equinox and the fixed starting point of the sidereal zodiac; the siddhāntas treat the solstitial points as oscillating (the Sūrya Siddhānta's libration of 27°), and schools differ on its value.
