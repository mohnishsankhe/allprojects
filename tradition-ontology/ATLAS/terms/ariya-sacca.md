# ariyasacca

`trm:ariya-sacca` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** noble truth; truth for the noble ones

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): DN 22:17-21 gives the four in short form (17) and then expands each: suffering (18), its origin (19), its cessation (20), the way (21).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The four known 'as it really is': this is suffering; this is the origin of suffering; this is the cessation of suffering; this is the way leading to the cessation of suffering (MN 10:44).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The four noble truths (suffering, its origin, its cessation, the path), to be respectively fully understood, abandoned, realised and developed (SN 56.11); what the Buddha declared because it is beneficial (SN 56.31).
