# Turīya

`trm:turiya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तुरीय
**Literal:** the fourth

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The form of Viṣṇu at the dvādaśānta, attained by the liberated and occasionally seen by the fully concentrated with direct vision; it gives the liberated their knowledge and activity.
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The fourth quarter of speech, the only one humans speak; the other three are hidden (RV 1.164.45).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The fourth, the Lord in whom one abides beyond the intellect's states of waking, dream and sleep, of which the jīva is the witness.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The fourth: not a fourth state beside waking, dream and sleep but the self that pervades and transcends them, non-dual, peaceful, auspicious; the Lord able to end all sorrow (GK 1.10).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The fourth state beyond waking, dream and sleep; the Goddess is 'turyā' (LSN 63); in the Bhāvanā Upaniṣad the fourth state is the prostration of inner worship.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The fourth: beyond waking, dream and deep sleep; neither inward- nor outward-knowing, the stilling of the world, non-dual (MāU 7, 12, where the word is caturtha); turya in MaiU 6.19 and 7.11.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The fourth state beyond waking, dream and deep sleep; placed between the brows (Triśikhi) or at the head.
