**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अन्तराय
**Literal:** obstacle

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): An obstacle on the path of yoga; the ten of LiP 1.9 and ŚiP 7.2.38; the powers themselves are antarāyas (BhP 11.15.33).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The nine obstacles, distractions of the mind (1.30), removed through devotion to Īśvara (1.29) and practice on a single principle (1.32).
