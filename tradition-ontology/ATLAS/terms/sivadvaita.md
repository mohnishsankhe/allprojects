# śivādvaita

`trm:sivadvaita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शिवाद्वैत
**Literal:** non-duality of Śiva

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The SSM's name for its own doctrine: the śāstra devoted to Śiva's non-duality agreeing with Veda and Vedānta (3.83–87), taught by gurus devoted to Śiva-non-duality (13.24–27).
- [Śiva-viśiṣṭādvaita (Śaiva Vedānta of Śrīkaṇṭha)](../lineages/siva-visistadvaita.md): Qualified non-dualism of Śiva: Śiva with cit-śakti is one, with souls and world as his body.
