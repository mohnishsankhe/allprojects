# nāma

`trm:nama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नाम
**Literal:** name

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The Lord's name, whose utterance, even indirect, destroys sins and is the supreme expiation and the dharma of the age.
- [Nairukta school (the Vedic etymologists)](../lineages/nairukta.md): The noun, whose chief meaning is being (sattva) (Nirukta 1.1); all nouns derive from verbs according to Śākaṭāyana (1.12).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The Lord's name, non-different from him, a wish-fulfilling gem, fully empowered and manifesting itself on a tongue turned to service.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): The names of Kṛṣṇa given at the first initiation (the refuge mantra) and sung in kīrtan.
