# nāma

`trm:nama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नाम
**Literal:** name

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The Lord's name, whose utterance, even indirect, destroys sins and is the supreme expiation and the dharma of the age.
- [Nairukta school (the Vedic etymologists)](../lineages/nairukta.md): The noun, whose chief meaning is being (sattva) (Nirukta 1.1); all nouns derive from verbs according to Śākaṭāyana (1.12).
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The name 'Nārāyaṇa', found after long searching, which gives family, wealth, heaven, grace and strength and removes devotees' suffering, more than a mother (PTM 1.1); its sweetness is worth more than Indra's kingdom (Tirumālai 2).
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): The Lord, being sung of, quickly manifests himself and makes his devotees experience him (NBS 80).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The Lord's name, non-different from him, a wish-fulfilling gem, fully empowered and manifesting itself on a tongue turned to service.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): The names of Kṛṣṇa given at the first initiation (the refuge mantra) and sung in kīrtan.
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): The name of God ('Viṭṭhala', 'Rāma Kṛṣṇa Hari'), the easy means that needs no rule of time, place or caste; standing a moment at God's door with the name wins the four liberations.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): Rāma's name, greater than both his formless and embodied forms and the one support in Kali.
- [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md): The name of Kṛṣṇa, first of the four real things and the chief means of the single refuge.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The Name of the Lord (usually Rām), whose inward remembrance is the Sants' chief practice; not the spoken syllables only but the Name revealed by the guru and dwelt on with the mind.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Nām = Śabd: the creative Word, the inner sound and light, to which the names given at initiation connect the soul; distinguished from 'varṇātmak' (spoken) names.
- [Kabīr Panth](../lineages/kabir-panth.md): The true Name (sat nām) given at initiation with the pān-parvānā, by which the soul passes Kāl.
