# amanasikāra

`trm:amanasikara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** non-attention, not bringing to mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): Non-mentation, 'not engaging the mind' (yid la mi byed pa): Maitrīpa's key term for mahāmudrā, glossed as the union of clear light (a) and self-consecration (manasikāra), not mere blankness; Tilopa's 'not engaging the mind, free of all intent'.
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): Scriptural 'non-recollection and non-attention' mean the result of analysis finding nothing to recollect; mere absence of mental activity is like fainting and is not non-conceptual wisdom (Bhāvanākrama III).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): yid la mi byed pa, 'non-mentation': not engaging the mind (in grasping); the path of Mahāmudrā 'free of identification, not engaging the mind' (Tilopa); Maitrīpa's cycle of that name is a root of Kagyu Mahāmudrā.
