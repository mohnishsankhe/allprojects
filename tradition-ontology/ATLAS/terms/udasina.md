# udāsīna

`trm:udasina` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उदासीन
**Literal:** sitting apart, indifferent

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: The Lord, sending forth the beings, is 'seated as one indifferent' (udāsīnavat), unattached to those acts, and so they do not bind him (9.9).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Puruṣa, indifferent, which appears as agent through conjunction (SK 20); on knowledge it is the indifferent onlooker (SK 66).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Impartial, 'seated apart': a mark of the devotee dear to the Lord (12.16).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 14.23: the one gone beyond the guṇas sits as one indifferent (udāsīnavat), not shaken by the guṇas.
