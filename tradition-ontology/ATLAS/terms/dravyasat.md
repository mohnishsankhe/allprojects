# dravyasat

`trm:dravyasat` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** existing as substance

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Existing as a real entity (dravya), with its own nature; for the Vaibhāṣika, all dharmas, including those of past and future.
- [Pudgalavāda](../lineages/pudgalavada.md): The Vātsīputrīyas deny that the person is dravyasat, while also denying that it is a mere designation.
