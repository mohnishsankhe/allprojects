# bheda-vijñāna

`trm:bheda-vijnana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भेदविज्ञान
**Literal:** discriminative knowledge

## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Knowing the self as distinct from body, karma and passions; the cause of liberation (Samayasāra-kalaśa 131; Iṣṭopadeśa 50).
- [Adhyātma movement (Adhyātma-mata)](../lineages/adhyatma-jain.md): The central practice of the Adhyātma path: discerning the knower from all that is known.
