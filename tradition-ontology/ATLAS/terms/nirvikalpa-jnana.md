# nirvikalpa-jñāna

`trm:nirvikalpa-jnana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** non-conceptual wisdom

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The wisdom that does not construct its object — preparatory, fundamental (the direct realization of suchness) and subsequently obtained; not mere absence of thought; first gained on the path of seeing.
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): The wisdom entered when, analysing, the yogin finds no dharma arisen in the three times; it pacifies all elaboration and penetrates emptiness (Bhāvanākrama III).
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): When neither being nor non-being remains before the mind, it is pacified without object (BCA 9.35).

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [nirvikalpa-samādhi](nirvikalpa-samadhi.md) — Both name a knowing free of conceptual construction; Yogācāra insists it is a cognition of suchness, not the resting of the witness-self Advaita speaks of.
