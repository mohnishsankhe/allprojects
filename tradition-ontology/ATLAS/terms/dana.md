# dāna

`trm:dana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दान
**Literal:** giving

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Giving of wealth and food; the giver's wealth does not waste away, and 'who eats alone has only sin' (RV 10.117).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Giving, sāttvic when given because it ought to be given, to one who makes no return, at the right place and time to a worthy recipient (BhG 17.20).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Religious giving, especially to brahmins; at death the gifts (cow, land, sesame etc.) ease the road of the dead.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Giving: the humans' 'da' (BAU 5.2); 'give with faith' (TU 1.11.3); a priestly gift of the life-sacrifice (ChU 3.17.4).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The accomplishment of receiving knowledge from the venerable after serving them with shelter, medicine, staff, water-pot, food and clothing (Gauḍapāda on SK 51); Vācaspati is recalled as deriving it from the root 'dai' (to purify), the purity of discriminative knowledge (low confidence).
