# mukkuṟṟam

`trm:mukkurram` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tamil
**Native script:** முக்குற்றம்
**Literal:** the three faults

## Definitions by tradition
- [Siddha medicine (cittā maruttuvam)](../lineages/siddha-medicine.md): The three 'faults' or vital humours — vaḷi (vātam), aḻal (pittam), aiyam (kapam) — whose balance is health and whose excess or deficiency causes disease (Kuṟaḷ 941).
