# kañcuka

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कञ्चुक
**Literal:** jacket, covering

## Definitions by tradition
- [Rasa Śāstra (the science of mercury) and the Raseśvara-darśana](../lineages/rasa-sastra.md): Lineage contribution (Rasa Śāstra): the seven adventitious 'coverings' (impurities) of mercury, from earth, rock and water, causing skin disease, dullness and wind disorders (RRS 11.22-25).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The 'jackets' covering Śiva and making him a jīva; without them he is Paraśiva (PKS 1.5).
- [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md): The five (with māyā, six) sheaths that limit the individual: kalā, vidyā, rāga, kāla, niyati (Paramārthasāra 16-17).
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): The 'sheaths' from māyā — kalā (limited agency), vidyā (limited knowledge), rāga (attachment), kāla (time) and niyati (restriction) — which partly awaken and limit the soul for experience.
