# āvaraṇa

`trm:avarana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आवरण
**Literal:** covering, enclosure

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): An 'enclosure' of the Śrīcakra: each of its nine concentric parts, from the square to the point, with its presiding goddess (cakreśvarī), class of yoginīs, attendant deities and mudrā, worshipped in turn (navāvaraṇa-pūjā).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Obstruction; three — of karma (the deadly acts), of defilement (intense defilement), of ripening (bad destinies, Kuru, non-perceiving gods).
