# passaddhi

`trm:passaddhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** tranquillity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Calming of body and mind; the fifth factor of awakening; arising from rapture and leading to happiness (SN 12.23; MN 118).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Tranquillity of the mental body and of consciousness; beautiful universals; an awakening factor and an imperfection of insight when grasped.
