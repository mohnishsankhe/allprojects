**Literal:** mind training; training the mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): The Kadam instruction for transforming the mind through relative and ultimate bodhicitta — especially equalizing and exchanging self and other and sending and taking — and turning adversity into the path, transmitted in terse slogans.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): Mind training as integrated into the great scope of the lamrim and the Guru Pūjā.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): Mind training as taught in the Parting from the Four Attachments commentaries and by Tokmé Zangpo.
