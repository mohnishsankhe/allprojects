# Namu Amida Butsu

`trm:namu-amida-butsu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Japanese
**Native script:** 南無阿弥陀仏
**Literal:** homage to Amida Buddha; I take refuge in Amida Buddha

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): The six-character name: to say it with the conviction of birth is the whole of the nenbutsu.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): The name in which the person and the Dharma are one; the working of the vow calling the being; object of reverence (myōgō honzon).
- [Ji-shū (the Time school of Ippen)](../lineages/ji-shu.md): The name that is itself birth, settled for all beings ten kalpas ago; printed on the talismans.
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Namo Amituofo, the six-character name recited in Chinese Pure Land (four-character 'Amituofo' in quick recitation).
