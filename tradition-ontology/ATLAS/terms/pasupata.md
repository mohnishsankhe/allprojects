## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The observance and doctrine taught by Paśupati for the release of the paśus; also a follower of it. The Pāśupata Sūtra opens by announcing 'the Pāśupata rule of yoga'.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): In the Purāṇas also the Vedic ash-vow of the Atharvaśiras ('pāśupata vrata'), distinguished by the Kūrma Purāṇa from the non-Vedic Pāśupata scriptures.
