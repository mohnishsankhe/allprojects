# nirutti

`trm:nirutti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** language, expression

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Language; knowledge of the language of intrinsic nature (Māgadhī).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Language or dialect: the Buddha allows his word to be learned 'in one's own language' (sakāya niruttiyā) and forbids putting it into Vedic metre (Vin Cv 5.33); analytical knowledge of language is one of the four paṭisambhidās.
