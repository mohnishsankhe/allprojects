# vipakṣa

`trm:vipaksa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विपक्ष
**Literal:** dissimilar instance

## Definitions by tradition
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): What certainly lacks the probandum, e.g. the lake.
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): What is not a similar instance: something other than, contrary to, or the absence of it.
