# āśrama

`trm:asrama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आश्रम
**Literal:** stage of exertion; order of life

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): One of the four orders: student, householder, forest-hermit, renouncer (MDh 6.87); whether they are chosen freely, followed in sequence, or whether only the householder's is Vedic is disputed (GDh 3.1–3.36; BDh 2.6.11.27; VDh 7.3; ĀpDh 2.9.21.1).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Stage of life: 'proceeding through one's own āśramas' is part of the remedy (MaiU 4.3); ŚU 6.21 names those beyond the āśramas (atyāśramin); ChU 2.23.1 has three 'branches of dharma' without the word.
