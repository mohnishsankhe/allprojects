# bandhu

`trm:bandhu` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** बन्धु
**Literal:** connection, kinship

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The hidden correspondence the Brāhmaṇas establish between an element of the rite, the cosmos and the person (e.g. the bricks of the altar = the days and nights of the year = the joints of Prajāpati); knowing the bandhu is what makes the rite effective.
