## Definitions by tradition
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Vedic action done with desire, bringing prosperity and equality with the gods (MDh 12.88–90); the natural activity of beings (MDh 5.56).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The undertaking of action by speech, mind and body (NS 1.1.17), good or bad (NBh 1.1.2).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: Activity: a sign of rajas (14.12), its absence a sign of tamas (14.13); the one beyond the guṇas neither hates it nor longs for it (14.22); the ancient stream of activity flowed forth from the primal person (15.4).
