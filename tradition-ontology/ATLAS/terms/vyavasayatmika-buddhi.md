**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** व्यवसायात्मिका बुद्धि
**Literal:** understanding whose nature is resolve

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Here it is one, whereas the understandings of the irresolute are many-branched and endless (2.41); it is not established in samādhi for those carried away by enjoyment and power (2.44).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The single, resolute understanding of the yogin, contrasted with the many-branched minds of the irresolute (BhG 2.41, 2.44).
