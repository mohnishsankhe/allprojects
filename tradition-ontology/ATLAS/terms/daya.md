**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** compassion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Compassion: the asuras' 'da' — be compassionate (dayadhvam) (BAU 5.2.3).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The Lord's compassion, personified by Deśika as a goddess in the Dayāśataka; the schools define it differently.
