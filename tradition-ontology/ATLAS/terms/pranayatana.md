**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्राणायतन
**Literal:** seat of the vital breaths

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Ten seats where life resides: the temples, the three vital points (head, heart, bladder), throat, blood, semen, ojas and anus (Ca Sū 29.3); or head, throat, heart, navel, anus, bladder, ojas, semen, blood and flesh (Ca Śā 7.9).
