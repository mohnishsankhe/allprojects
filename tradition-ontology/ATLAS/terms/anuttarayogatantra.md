# anuttarayogatantra

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): 'Unsurpassed yoga tantra': the Tibetan name (rnal 'byor bla med) for the highest class, comprising the Guhyasamāja, Hevajra, Cakrasaṃvara, Kālacakra and related tantras; Indian sources more often speak of yogottara, yoganiruttara/yoginī and mahāyoga tantras.
