# wuxin (no-mind)

`trm:wuxin` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 無心
**Literal:** without mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Hongzhou school](../lineages/hongzhou.md): The state free of all mind of seeking and discriminating; 'only be without mind and that is the ultimate' (Huangbo).
- [Seon (Korean Chan)](../lineages/seon.md): Practised through ten methods of stopping deluded mind (Jinsim jikseol).
