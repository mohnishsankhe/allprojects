# pañcakṛtya

`trm:pancakrtya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पञ्चकृत्य
**Literal:** five acts

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The five cosmic acts — creation, preservation, withdrawal, concealment and grace — performed by the Goddess through Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra, Īśvara and Sadāśiva (LSN 63-64; SL 24).
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): Emission, maintenance, withdrawal, concealment and grace; the individual performs them too, as manifesting, relishing, reflecting, planting the seed and dissolving (PH 10-11).
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Śiva's five acts: emission, maintenance, withdrawal, concealment, grace.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Aintoḻil: paṭaittal, kāttal, aḻittal, maṟaittal, aruḷal — enacted in Naṭarāja's dance.
