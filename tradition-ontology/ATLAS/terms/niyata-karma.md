# niyata karman

`trm:niyata-karma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नियत कर्मन्
**Literal:** allotted / prescribed action

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Allotted action: its renunciation is not proper, and its abandonment out of delusion is tāmasic (18.7); done because it is to be done, without attachment and fruit, it is sāttvic relinquishment (18.9); allotted action done without attachment, passion or aversion by one not craving fruit is sāttvic action (18.23); 'action ordained by one's own nature' (svabhāva-niyata) incurs no fault (18.47).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The action Arjuna is told to perform, action being better than inaction (3.8); commentators take niyata as 'prescribed/obligatory'.
