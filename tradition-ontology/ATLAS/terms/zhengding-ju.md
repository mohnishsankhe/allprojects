# zhengding ju

`trm:zhengding-ju` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 正定聚
**Literal:** group rightly settled

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): The rightly established group (samyaktva-niyata-rāśi): those assured of awakening; those born in Sukhāvatī dwell in it (11th vow); Nāgārjuna's 'stage of certainty' (biding) entered by recollecting Amitābha.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): Shōjōju: entered in this life at the moment of shinjin, not first in the Pure Land.

## Forms in other languages

- Sanskrit: samyaktva-niyata-rāśi  — exact
- Chinese: biding 必定 — partial — Nāgārjuna's 'certainty'
