# skandha

`trm:skandha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्कन्ध
**Literal:** aggregate

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Aggregate, 'heap': the conditioned dharmas grouped as matter, feeling, perception, formations, consciousness; taught to those confused about mind.
- [Pudgalavāda](../lineages/pudgalavada.md): The basis (āśraya) on which the person is designated; neither identical with nor different from it.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The five aggregates, which Avalokiteśvara saw empty of own-being.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In Jain usage a molecule or compound of atoms, formed by union and division; visible bodies are aggregates (TS 5.25–28).
