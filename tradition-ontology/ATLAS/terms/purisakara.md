# purisakāra

`trm:purisakara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** human effort

## Definitions by tradition
- [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md): (As reported) Denied by Makkhali: there is no doing by self or other, no human effort, strength or vigour.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): (Prakrit purisakkāra-parakkama) Affirmed by Mahāvīra against Saddālaputta; one of the five co-causes.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Doing by oneself and by others exists, as the element of initiative shows.
