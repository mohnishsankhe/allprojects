# avadhūta

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अवधूत
**Literal:** shaken off

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): One who has 'shaken off' all worldly ties, a liberated wanderer who behaves like a child, madman or dullard; the teacher of King Yadu.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Defined syllable by syllable: a — free of the noose of hope, ever in bliss; va — free of vāsanās, living in the present; dhū — dust-grey limbs, cleansed mind, free of concentration and meditation; ta — held to truth, free of anxiety, darkness and ego.
- [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md): The renouncer who has shaken off saṃsāra, beyond castes and stages, without marks or rules; etymologized syllable by syllable.
