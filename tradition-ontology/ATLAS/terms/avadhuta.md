# avadhūta

`trm:avadhuta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अवधूत
**Literal:** shaken off

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): One who has 'shaken off' all worldly ties, a liberated wanderer who behaves like a child, madman or dullard; the teacher of King Yadu.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Defined syllable by syllable: a — free of the noose of hope, ever in bliss; va — free of vāsanās, living in the present; dhū — dust-grey limbs, cleansed mind, free of concentration and meditation; ta — held to truth, free of anxiety, darkness and ego.
- [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md): The renouncer who has shaken off saṃsāra, beyond castes and stages, without marks or rules; etymologized syllable by syllable.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The yogī who has 'shaken off' all conventions and bonds and abides in the supreme state; the ideal described in the sixth upadeśa of the Siddhasiddhāntapaddhati; addressed in Hindi as 'avadhū'.
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): In the Mahānirvāṇa, the saṃnyāsa of the Kali age (8.221); four kinds, complete (paramahaṃsa) and incomplete (14.142-149).
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The vīra renunciant who holds 'I am Śiva, the bliss of Bhairava' (Nirvāṇa Tantra 14.6).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The 'avadhū' whom Kabīr addresses in many songs — the Nāth yogī, challenged to find the Lord within rather than in yogic garb and feats.
