# unmanī

`trm:unmani` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उन्मनी
**Literal:** beyond-mind (state)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): A state of the mind named among the synonyms of samādhi (4.3); the text says duality is not perceived through the mind's becoming unmanī (4.61) and the body becomes like wood in it (4.106).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The state in which the mind is transcended; it cannot arise while the channels are impure (HYP 2.4); a synonym of samādhi (4.3); through it duality is not perceived (4.61) and the body becomes like a log (4.106).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The mind-less state beyond mind, in which one is not roused even by drums (KJN 14.83–85); a synonym of samādhi (HYP 4.3).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The mind 'beyond mind' by which one is liberated here (KJN 13.5); the fourth mind beyond the guṇas (Jñānakārikā 2.8).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The state in which the mind has dissolved; 'unmanībhāva'; beyond merit and sin.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The 'no-mind' state beyond the mind, a Nāth term used by Kabīr for absorption in the Lord.
