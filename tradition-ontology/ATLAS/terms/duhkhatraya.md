# duḥkhatraya

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दुःखत्रय
**Literal:** the triad of sufferings

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The three kinds of suffering that prompt the inquiry (SK 1): internal, from other beings, and from the divine (named by the commentaries); their complete cessation is the supreme goal (SS 1.1).
