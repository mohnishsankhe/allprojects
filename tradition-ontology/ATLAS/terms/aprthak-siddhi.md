# apṛthak-siddhi

`trm:aprthak-siddhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अपृथक्सिद्धि
**Literal:** inseparable establishment

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The relation of inseparable existence between a substance and its mode (as between Brahman and souls/matter, or a substance and its attribute): the mode can neither exist nor be known apart from the substrate, yet is distinct from it.
