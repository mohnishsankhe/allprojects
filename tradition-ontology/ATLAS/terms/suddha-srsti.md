# śuddha-sṛṣṭi

`trm:suddha-srsti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शुद्धसृष्टि
**Literal:** pure creation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The creation proceeding directly from the six qualities — the vyūhas and their realm — as distinct from the 'other-than-pure' creation from prakṛti; the Jayākhya places the Vaiṣṇava creation before the prādhānika and brāhma creations.
