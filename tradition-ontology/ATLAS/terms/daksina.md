# dakṣiṇā

`trm:daksina` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दक्षिणा
**Literal:** gift; the right (hand)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The sacrificial fee and gift to the priests; the givers of gifts stand high in heaven and partake of immortality (RV 10.107.2).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The gift to the officiating priests, the 'human gods', as oblations go to the gods (ŚB 2.2.2.6); it does not reach the world attained by knowledge (ŚB 10.5.4.16).
