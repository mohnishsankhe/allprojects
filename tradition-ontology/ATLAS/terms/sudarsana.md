**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सुदर्शन
**Literal:** the beautiful sight; Viṣṇu's discus

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The Lord's will-power (saṅkalpa) — his resolve 'may I be many' — personified as his discus; the Ahirbudhnya's central principle, of which even the Sāṃkhya is said to consist.
