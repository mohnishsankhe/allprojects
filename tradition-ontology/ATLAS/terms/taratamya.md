## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The beginningless, unalterable gradation of souls in nature, knowledge, bliss and office, from the lowest to Brahmā and Vāyu, with Lakṣmī above and Viṣṇu infinitely above; it continues in liberation.
