# satyadvaya

`trm:satyadvaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सत्यद्वय
**Literal:** the two truths

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The conventional truth of the world and the truth of the ultimate, on which the Buddhas' teaching relies (MMK 24.8–10).
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Every thing bears two natures, found by correct and by false seeing (MA 6.23); the intellect is conventional and reality is beyond its domain (BCA 9.2).
