# avasarpiṇī

`trm:avasarpini` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अवसर्पिणी
**Literal:** descending half-cycle

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The half of the time-wheel in which happiness, lifespan and virtue decline through six periods; the present age is its fifth period (TS 3.27).
