# nirmālya

`trm:nirmalya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निर्माल्य
**Literal:** remains of an offering

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): What has been offered to Śiva, which purifies the mind (SSM 11.6–11); the nirmālya of fixed (temple) liṅgas is not to be used by Vīraśaivas (9.33–35).
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Flowers taken down from the image, worn to increase devotion and show the sign.
