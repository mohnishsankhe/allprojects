# layayoga

`trm:laya-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** लययोग
**Literal:** yoga of dissolution

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The yoga in which the mind is dissolved (cittalaya) through 'indications' (saṅketa) — fixing attention on the void, the nose-tip, the brow-centre, the back of the head, the big toes, lying like a corpse — of which Ādinātha taught eight crore (DYŚ 14–24); the third of the four yogas, given to the middling aspirant (SS 5.12). The HYP holds nādānusandhāna the chief laya.
