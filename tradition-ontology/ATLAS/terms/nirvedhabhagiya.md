# nirvedha-bhāgīya

`trm:nirvedhabhagiya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** aids to penetration

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The four stages of the path of preparation — heat, peak, patience and supreme mundane quality — which the Mahāyānasūtrālaṃkāra describes as successive stages of seeing objects as mental speech, abiding in mind-only, abandoning the grasped and then the grasper.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The four roots of good conducive to penetration — heat, summits, acceptance, highest worldly dharmas — born of cultivation.
