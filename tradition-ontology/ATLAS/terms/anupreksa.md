## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In TS 9.7 the repeated thinking on twelve themes: impermanence, no refuge, saṃsāra, aloneness, otherness, impurity, inflow, stopping, shedding, the universe, rarity of enlightenment, and the well-taught dharma. Not the Buddhist or Yoga homonym.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Repeated contemplation of twelve themes — impermanence, no refuge, the round of births, aloneness, otherness, impurity, inflow, stopping, shedding, the universe, rarity of enlightenment, and the well-taught dharma (TS 9.7); also a kind of study (9.25).
