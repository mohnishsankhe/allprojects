# samudghāta

`trm:samudghata` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** समुद्घात
**Literal:** bursting forth

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The projection of the soul's space-points beyond the body without leaving it — at death, in pain, passion, or (for the kevalin) to equalise the remaining karmas before liberation.
