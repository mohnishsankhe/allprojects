# bodhisattva

`trm:bodhisattva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** awakening-being

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): One destined to Buddhahood from the time he performs the karma producing the marks; he cultivates for three incalculable aeons and a hundred aeons.
- [Lokottaravāda](../lineages/lokottaravada.md): The bodhisattva passes through four conducts and ten grounds (Mahāvastu).
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): One who has produced the thought of awakening and follows the path of the perfections to full buddhahood for all beings, refusing a merely personal nirvāṇa; yet 'no dharma called bodhisattva is found' (Aṣṭa 1).
