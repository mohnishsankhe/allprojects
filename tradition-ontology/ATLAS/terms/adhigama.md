# adhigama

`trm:adhigama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अधिगम
**Literal:** acquisition; instruction

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Right view arising through instruction or study (TS 1.3); also 'understanding' of the realities through pramāṇa and naya (TS 1.6).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Realization, the Dharma as attained (the wings of awakening), the second aspect of the true Dharma.
