# piṇḍa

`trm:pinda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पिण्ड
**Literal:** ball (of rice)

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The rice-ball offered to the dead and ancestors; the ten daily piṇḍas build the preta's body and the monthly ones feed him on the road.
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The rice-ball offered to the father, grandfather and great-grandfather in the ancestral rites.
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): The first sub-sthala: the embodied soul made fit for the path by the ripening of karma and Śiva's grace (SSM 5.27–30; cf. 5.34–36).
