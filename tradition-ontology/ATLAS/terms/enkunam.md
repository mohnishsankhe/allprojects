# eṇkuṇam

`trm:enkunam` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tamil

## Definitions by tradition
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): The eight qualities of the Lord (Kuṟaḷ 9's 'eṇkuṇattāṉ'), glossed by Parimēlaḻakar from the Śaiva Āgamas: self-dependence, pure body, natural understanding, omniscience, natural freedom from bonds, boundless grace, infinite power, boundless bliss.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Read by Jain interpreters of the Kuṟaḷ as the eight qualities of a liberated Siddha (reported here from the dispute; U34 may refine).
