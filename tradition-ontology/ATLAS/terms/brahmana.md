# brāhmaṇa

`trm:brahmana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Native script:** ब्राह्मण
**Literal:** brahmin

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Re-defined by conduct: one is a brahmin not by birth but by deeds (MN 98; Snp 1.7); 'him I call a brahmin who has nothing and takes nothing' (Dhp 396); the arahant as the true brahmin.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: In 2.46 the 'knowing brāhmaṇa', for whom all the Vedas are of as much use as a well amid a flood.
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Whatever in the Veda is not mantra (MS 2.1.33): the injunctions, arthavādas and the like.
