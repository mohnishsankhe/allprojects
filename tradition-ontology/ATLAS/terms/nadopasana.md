# nādopāsanā

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md): Worship of nāda: through it Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Maheśvara are worshipped (ŚR 1.3.2); the unstruck nāda by sages on the guru's path, the struck nāda through music (ŚR 1.2.165–167).
- [Unaffiliated regional bhakti poets (classificatory grouping)](../lineages/regional-bhakti-poets.md): Worship through sound and music, joined to devotion (Tyāgarāja).
