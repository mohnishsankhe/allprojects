# kriyāyoga

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** क्रियायोग
**Literal:** yoga of ritual action

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): In the Uddhava Gītā: ritual worship of the Lord — Vedic, tantric or mixed — in an image, ground, fire, sun, water or the heart.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Austerity, self-study and devotion to Īśvara (2.1), for cultivating samādhi and thinning the afflictions (2.2); taught for the unconcentrated mind (YBh 2.1).
