# śaktipāta

`trm:saktipata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शक्तिपात
**Literal:** descent (fall) of power

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Figurative name for Śiva's awakening of the soul by his power, like a teacher waking sleeping pupils; it occurs when the soul's karmas are in equilibrium, and its sign is a distinctive devotion.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Name used by 'those who hold to the āgamas' for Viṣṇu's compassion, his fifth power of grace, by whose glance the soul's karmas become equal and it is lifted from saṃsāra.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The descent of the Lord's power, independent of karma, intense, middling or mild in three degrees each (TĀ 13); its signs in MVT 2.14-16.
- [Rasa Śāstra (the science of mercury) and the Raseśvara-darśana](../lineages/rasa-sastra.md): Lineage contribution (Rasa Śāstra): until śaktipāta and the cutting of the fetters, understanding of mercury does not arise (Rasārṇava 1.23).
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): The descent of Śiva's power on a soul whose mala has ripened, qualifying it for initiation.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Catti nipātam, in four grades matched to the four pādas.

## Forms in other languages
- Tamil: catti nipātam  — exact
