# śāmbhavī (mudrā)

`trm:sambhavi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शाम्भवी
**Literal:** (the seal) of Śambhu

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Attention fixed on an inner target with the gaze outward and unblinking, kept secret like a woman of good family (HYP 4.35–37); also a name of suṣumnā (3.4; 4.18).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The seal of the open, unblinking gaze with attention on the inner target.
