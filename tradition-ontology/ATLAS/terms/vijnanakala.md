# vijñānākala

`trm:vijnanakala` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): A soul bound by mala alone, free of karma and māyā; the highest of these are raised by Śiva to offices as mantras, mantra-lords and Vidyeśvaras.
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The knower of pure awareness without agency (ĪPK 3.2.6-7).
