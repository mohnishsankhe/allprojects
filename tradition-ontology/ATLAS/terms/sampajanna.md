# sampajañña

`trm:sampajanna` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** clear comprehension, situational awareness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): In MN 10 the monk 'acts with clear comprehension (sampajānakārī)' in going and returning, looking, bending, eating, excreting, sleeping, speaking and keeping silent; it is also one of the qualities of every satipaṭṭhāna practice.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Acting with full awareness when going and returning, looking, bending, eating, speaking and keeping silent (DN 2; MN 10).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Clear comprehension, fourfold: of purpose, suitability, domain and non-delusion.
