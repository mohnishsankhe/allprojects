**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Aghora (Aghor panth; Aghorī and Aughaṛ ascetics)](../lineages/aghora.md): Non-discrimination, non-difference: seeing no division of pure and impure, high and low, since Śiva is in all.
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The non-dual conduct (advaitācāra) of the Kaula (KJN 11.3–4).
