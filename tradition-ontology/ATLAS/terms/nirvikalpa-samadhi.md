# nirvikalpa-samādhi

`trm:nirvikalpa-samadhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** absorption without distinctions

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Absorption in which the mental mode in the form of Brahman remains, unmanifest, with no distinction of knower and known, like salt dissolved in water; distinct from sleep because the mode persists.
