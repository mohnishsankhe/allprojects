# kalyāṇamitta

`trm:kalyanamitta` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** good friend

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The teacher who gives the meditation subject; described by a verse (dear, respected, esteemed (bhāvanīya), a speaker, patient with speech, able to speak on the profound, does not urge to what is unfit); the Buddha the best (Vism III p. 98).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The teacher who gives the meditation subject, endowed with the seven qualities; the Buddha is the best good friend.
