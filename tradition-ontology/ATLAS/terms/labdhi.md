# labdhi

`trm:labdhi` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** लब्धि
**Literal:** attainment

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): (1) A power arising from the destruction-cum-subsidence of karma through austerity — e.g. the transformable or fiery body, healing powers, sky-travel (TS 2.47–48); (2) one of the nine destructional attainments of the omniscient (TS 2.4).
