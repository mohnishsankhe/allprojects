**Native script:** श्रीपुर
**Literal:** city of Śrī

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Lalitā's city on the central peak of Meru (LSN 22), with its enclosures described in the Lalitopākhyāna and the Āryādviśatī.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Lalitā's city (Śrīnagara) with its enclosures and the Cintāmaṇi palace at the centre.
