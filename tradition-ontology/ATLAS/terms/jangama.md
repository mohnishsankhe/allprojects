# jaṅgama

`trm:jangama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** जङ्गम
**Literal:** moving

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): (1) The living, moving devotee or body as Śiva's true temple, against the fixed (Basava's vacana); (2) the wandering Śivayogin teacher whose greatness is taught in the prasādi-sthala — to see him gives accomplishment, to worship him is a means of liberation (SSM 11.37–43); (3) the order of Liṅgāyata priests and monks.
