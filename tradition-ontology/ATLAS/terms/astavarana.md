# aṣṭāvaraṇa

`trm:astavarana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अष्टावरण
**Literal:** the eight coverings (shields)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): The eight protections of the Liṅgāyata: guru, liṅga, jaṅgama, pādōdaka, prasāda, vibhūti, rudrākṣa and mantra; several appear as sub-sthalas of the bhakta-sthala in the SSM (5.27–30).
