# nirguṇa

`trm:nirguna` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निर्गुण
**Literal:** without qualities

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Without attributes: Brahman in itself, free of all qualities, relations and distinctions (BS 3.2.11, 3.2.14 as read by Śaṅkara).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Without qualities: said of the one God (ŚU 6.11), an early occurrence.
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Read by Dvaita as 'free of the qualities of prakṛti (sattva, rajas, tamas)'; the Advaita reading 'without any attributes' is rejected as against scripture and reason.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): In the Sants: the Lord beyond the three qualities and beyond form, image and incarnation, yet the object of love and remembrance ('nirguṇ rām'); not an abstraction to be merely known.
- [Dādū Panth](../lineages/dadu-panth.md): Dādū's Rām, formless and stainless (nirañjan), the one God of Hindu and Turk.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): The formless, qualityless Brahman, not different from the embodied Rāma; the name mediates between them.
- [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md): The formless Brahman beyond the elements, known by discrimination.
