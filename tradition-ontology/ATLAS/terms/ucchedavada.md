# ucchedavāda

`trm:ucchedavada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** annihilationism

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The view that the self is annihilated at death (DN 1; SN 44.10).
- [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md): (As reported) Ajita's doctrine that fool and wise alike are annihilated at the breaking of the body.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): One of the extremes: seven grounds for asserting the annihilation of an existing being, from the four-element self to the self of the highest formless sphere.
