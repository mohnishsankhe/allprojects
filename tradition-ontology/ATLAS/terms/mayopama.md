# māyopama

`trm:mayopama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** like an illusion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The standard simile of the Perfection of Wisdom: beings, dharmas, buddhahood and even nirvāṇa are like a magical illusion and a dream — appearing, yet without own-being.
