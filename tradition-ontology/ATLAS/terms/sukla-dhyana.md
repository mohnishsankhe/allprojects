**Literal:** white (bright) meditation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): TS 9.37-9.44: four kinds (9.39), the first two for one who knows the pūrvas and the last two for the kevalin (9.37-9.38), the first two with vitarka (scriptural knowledge) and the second without vīcāra (9.41-9.44).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The highest meditation in four stages, the first two for knowers of the Pūrvas, the last two for the omniscient, ending in liberation (TS 9.37–44).
