# mārga / deśī

## Definitions by tradition
- [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md): The two kinds of music: mārga, the 'sought-out' divine music fixed by Brahmā and Bharata that gives well-being when performed before Śiva, and deśī, regional music that delights people (ŚR 1.1.22–23; Bṛhaddeśī 13).
