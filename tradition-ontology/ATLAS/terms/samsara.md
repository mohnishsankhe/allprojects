# saṃsāra

`trm:samsara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** संसार
**Literal:** wandering on

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The round (of rebirth): the one without understanding goes on in saṃsāra (KU 1.3.7); God is the cause of saṃsāra and liberation (ŚU 6.16); 'the mind alone is saṃsāra' (MaiU 6.34).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The round of rebirth without discoverable beginning, in which more tears have been shed than the water of the four oceans (SN 15.3).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The round of death and rebirth, from whose ocean the Lord lifts his devotees (BhG 12.7); the forest of Vidura's parable (MBh 11.5–6).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The beginningless round of rebirths, which has no maker or experiencer but only dhammas occurring by conditions.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): The soul's own construction of 'I and mine' through ignorance; unreal and to be destroyed — distinct from the world (jagat), which is real.
