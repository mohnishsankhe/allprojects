# svārājya

`trm:svaraj` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्वाराज्य
**Literal:** self-rule

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The sovereignty over all beings attained by Brahman when it offered itself in all beings (ŚB 13.7.1.1).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The self-rule attained by the one who sacrifices to the self (MDh 12.91).
