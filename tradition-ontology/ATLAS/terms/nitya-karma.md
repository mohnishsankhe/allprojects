# nitya-karma

`trm:nitya-karma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नित्य-कर्म
**Literal:** obligatory act

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): Acts enjoined for life (e.g. the Agnihotra 'as long as one lives'); performing them destroys earlier sin and averts the demerit of omission (Tantravārttika), and the seeker of release must perform them (ŚV sambandhākṣepaparihāra 110).
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): Performed because enjoined; the command itself, not a fruit, is what prompts the performer (Prābhākara view as generally reported).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Obligatory daily rites (e.g. sandhyā), whose omission incurs sin; performed without desire they purify the mind.
