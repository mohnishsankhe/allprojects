# nāḍīśuddhi

`trm:nadi-suddhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नाडीशुद्धि
**Literal:** purification of the channels

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The cleansing of the channels, prerequisite to breath-control (HYP 2.4–10; GS 5.35–36: with seed-mantra, samanu, or by the cleansing acts, nirmanu); its signs are slimness, radiance, kindled fire, holding the breath at will and manifestation of nāda (HYP 2.19–20).
