# dṛśya

`trm:drsya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दृश्य
**Literal:** the seen

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The seen, the whole world of objects; the Yoga Vāsiṣṭha's teaching turns on the realization that the seen does not exist apart from consciousness.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The seen: of the nature of illumination, activity and inertia, consisting of elements and senses, for experience and liberation (2.18), existing for the seer's sake (2.21).
