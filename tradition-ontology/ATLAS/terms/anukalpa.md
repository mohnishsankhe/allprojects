# anukalpa

`trm:anukalpa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनुकल्प
**Literal:** secondary rule, substitute

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The substitute prescribed in place of a Kaula substance for those not eligible or in the Kali age — e.g. the 'sweet triad' of milk, sugar and honey for wine (Mahānirvāṇa 8.170-171), one's own wife only for the last tattva (6.14).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): In the Kālikā Purāṇa pumpkin, sugarcane and spirits are 'equal to' an animal victim, and coconut water or honey replace wine for the twice-born (67.23; 67.114-115).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): A substitute used in place of a prescribed substance, notably for the five 'M's in the right-hand mode.
