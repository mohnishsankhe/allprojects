## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The fourth sign (Cancer), 30° of the zodiac made of nine asterism-quarters; the heart of Kālapuruṣa (BJ 1.4); ruled by the Moon; movable; of the water triplicity (later texts); even, female and gentle (BJ 1.11); Varāhamihira's alternative (Greek-derived) name: kulīra (BJ 1.8); dignities: Jupiter exalted (5°); Mars in fall (BJ 1.13).
