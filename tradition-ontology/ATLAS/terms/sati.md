# sati

`trm:sati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** mindfulness, recollection

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Mindfulness established in the four satipaṭṭhānas (MN 10); the faculty of mindfulness (SN 48.10); the first factor of awakening, always useful (SN 46.53); dams the currents of the world (Snp 5.2).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Mindfulness: non-floating on the object, not forgetting it; a beautiful universal, faculty, power, awakening factor and path factor.
- [Mahāsi Sayadaw tradition (the 'new Burmese method')](../lineages/mahasi.md): Bare, precise noting of whatever arises in the present moment.
- [U Ba Khin – S. N. Goenka tradition (Sayagyi U Ba Khin lineage)](../lineages/u-ba-khin-goenka.md): Continuous awareness of sensations throughout the body with understanding of impermanence.
