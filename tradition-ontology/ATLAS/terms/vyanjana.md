# vyañjanā

`trm:vyanjana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** manifestation, suggestion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): The power of suggestion, a function of words beyond denotation (abhidhā) and indication (lakṣaṇā) by which the implied sense and rasa are conveyed; denied by Mahimabhaṭṭa (inference) and by Mīmāṃsakas and Naiyāyikas.
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): Accepted by Nāgeśa as a third power of words beside denotation and indication.
