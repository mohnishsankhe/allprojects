# niyati

`trm:niyati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नियति
**Literal:** restraint, fixed order, destiny

## Definitions by tradition
- [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md): (As reported) The destiny that, with circumstance (saṅgati) and nature (bhāva), transforms all beings, who have no control, power or energy; all things are fixed (niyayā savvabhāvā).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): One of five co-operating causes (with time, own-nature, former deeds and effort): wrong view when held alone, right view when held with the others.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Fixed order, necessity: listed among the candidate causes in ŚU 1.2.
- [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md): Kañcuka: restriction of cause and place ('only this, here').
