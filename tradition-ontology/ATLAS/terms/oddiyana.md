# Oḍḍiyāna

`trm:oddiyana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ओड्डियान
**Literal:** the flying-up place

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): The seat named because the Goddess 'flew up' (oḍḍitā) there (Kubjikāmata 2.40); the 'body' (piṇḍa) level of the fourfold yoni (11.7).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): Uḍḍīyāna, where Satī's thighs fell (Kālikā Purāṇa 18.41).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): One of the four great seats (pīṭha), which in the Yoginīhṛdaya are transformations of the four fused powers and are located in the subtle body.
