# upādhyāya

`trm:upadhyaya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उपाध्याय
**Literal:** teacher

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The monk who teaches the scriptures, fourth of the five supreme beings.
- [Dharmaguptaka](../lineages/dharmaguptaka.md): Preceptor: the senior monk (at least ten years ordained) who sponsors an ordination and under whom the new monk lives in dependence.
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): One who teaches a part of the Veda or its limbs for a living (MDh 2.141).
