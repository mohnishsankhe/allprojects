**Native script:** प्राणस्पन्द
**Literal:** vibration of breath

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The vibration of the life-breath, one of the two seeds of the mind: when it moves awareness arises, when it rests the mind does not arise.
