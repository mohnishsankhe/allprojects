# anicca

`trm:anicca` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** impermanent

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Arising and passing away; the first mark of all formations (AN 3.136); from the impermanence of the aggregates follows their being suffering and not fit to be taken as self (SN 22.59).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Impermanence, the first characteristic; in insight, seen as the rise and fall of formations.
- [U Ba Khin – S. N. Goenka tradition (Sayagyi U Ba Khin lineage)](../lineages/u-ba-khin-goenka.md): Experienced directly as the arising and passing of sensations throughout the body (U Ba Khin: 'anicca' as the key).
