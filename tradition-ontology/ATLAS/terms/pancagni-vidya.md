## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The knowledge of the five fires: yonder world, the rain-god, the earth, man and woman as sacrificial fires, into which faith, Soma, rain, food and semen are offered, so that the waters come to be called person at the fifth offering (ChU 5.3-10; BAU 6.2).
