# trivarga

`trm:trivarga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** त्रिवर्ग
**Literal:** the group of three (aims)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Dharma, artha and kāma, of which dharma is the root (MBh 18.5.49); each to be pursued at its time (Rāmāyaṇa 2.100).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Dharma, artha and kāma taken together (MDh 2.224); later texts add mokṣa to make the caturvarga.
