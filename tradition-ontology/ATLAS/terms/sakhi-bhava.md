# sakhī-bhāva

`trm:sakhi-bhava` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the mood of a female friend

## Definitions by tradition
- [Rādhāvallabha sampradāya](../lineages/radhavallabha.md): The devotee's stance as a companion (sahacarī) of Rādhā, serving the pair and delighting in their joy, never seeking union for herself.
- [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md): The stance of the singer-witness in the grove; Svāmī Haridās is held to be the sakhī Lalitā.
- [Rasik Rāma-bhakti (Rasik sampradāya of the Rāmānandīs)](../lineages/rasik-ramanandi.md): The Rāma-rasik devotee's identity as a companion of Sītā in the inner Ayodhyā.
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): The mood of Rādhā's female companions, in which the devotee witnesses and serves the couple's eternal play (Harivyāsa's Mahāvāṇī).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The companions' mood; the mañjarī-bhāva is a special form of it centred on service to Rādhā.
