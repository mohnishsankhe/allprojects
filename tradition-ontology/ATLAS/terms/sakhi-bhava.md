# sakhī-bhāva

`trm:sakhi-bhava` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the mood of a female friend

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): The mood of Rādhā's female companions, in which the devotee witnesses and serves the couple's eternal play (Harivyāsa's Mahāvāṇī).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The companions' mood; the mañjarī-bhāva is a special form of it centred on service to Rādhā.
