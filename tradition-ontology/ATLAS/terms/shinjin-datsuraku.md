# shinjin datsuraku (body and mind dropping off)

**Language:** Japanese
**Native script:** 身心脫落
**Literal:** casting off body and mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sōtō Zen](../lineages/soto.md): Dōgen's account of Rujing's teaching and of his own awakening; in Genjōkōan, the dropping off of the body and mind of oneself and others when verified by the myriad dharmas.
