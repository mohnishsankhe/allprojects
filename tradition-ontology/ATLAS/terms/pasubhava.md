# paśubhāva

`trm:pasubhava` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पशुभाव
**Literal:** the disposition of the 'bound beast'

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The first disposition — ritually pure worship without the Kaula substances; 'always pure' (Kālīvilāsa 6.10), the only one allowed in the Kali age by the Kālīvilāsa, and the beginning of the path in the Buddha's teaching to Vasiṣṭha (Rudrayāmala 17.137).
