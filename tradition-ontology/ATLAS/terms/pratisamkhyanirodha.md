# pratisaṃkhyānirodha

`trm:pratisamkhyanirodha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** cessation by discernment

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Cessation through discernment: disjunction (visaṃyoga) from sāsrava dharmas, obtained separately for each bond by the wisdom that discerns the truths; it is nirvāṇa.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): The non-arising of further defilement and birth once the antidote has arisen; not an entity.
