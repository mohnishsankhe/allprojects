# satkāyadṛṣṭi

`trm:satkayadrsti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सत्कायदृष्टि
**Literal:** view of the (real / transitory) collection as self

## Definitions by tradition
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): The view that takes the aggregates as self and mine; all afflictions and faults arise from it, and the yogin refutes the self, its object (MA 6.120).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The view of the existing body: grasping 'self' and 'mine' in the aggregates.
