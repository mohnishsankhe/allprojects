# hiri

`trm:hiri` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** conscience, shame of wrongdoing

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The inner sense of shame that, with ottappa, protects the world; the first quality of a true ascetic (MN 39).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Shame at wrongdoing, arising from self-respect; a beautiful universal.
