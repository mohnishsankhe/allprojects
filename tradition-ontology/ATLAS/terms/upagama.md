# upāgama

`trm:upagama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उपागम
**Literal:** subsidiary scripture

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): A subsidiary scripture attached to one of the 28 mūlāgamas; the Kāmika calls them upabheda ('sub-divisions') and lists 206 by the counts it gives (the usual traditional total is 207); the Kiraṇa says a division within the divisions is an upabheda.
