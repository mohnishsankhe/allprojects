# śaraṇāgati

`trm:saranagati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शरणागति
**Literal:** going for refuge

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): Going to the Lord alone as refuge ('mām ekaṃ śaraṇaṃ vraja', BhG 18.66; 'take refuge in him alone', 18.62); Rāma's vow to protect whoever seeks refuge (Rāmāyaṇa 6.18.33).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Surrendering one who has sought refuge is counted among the gravest sins (MBh 17.3.15).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Six-limbed refuge: resolve to do what pleases, avoidance of what displeases, faith that he will protect, choosing him as protector, laying down oneself, and helplessness.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The same as prapatti: surrender with the limbs of ānukūlya-saṅkalpa, prātikūlya-varjana, mahāviśvāsa, goptṛtva-varaṇa, kārpaṇya and ātma-nikṣepa.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): Taking refuge at the Lord's feet with no other refuge: 'I, your servant with no other refuge, have entered beneath your feet' (TVM 6.10.10); 'I have no refuge but your feet', like a child pushed away that still cries for its mother (PT 5.1).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Taking refuge in the Lord or the Goddess, as Gajendra and the gods do; the Goddess is 'intent on saving those who come for refuge'.
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Surrender, with six limbs: accepting the favourable, rejecting the unfavourable, faith in his protection, choosing him as protector, self-offering and humility.
