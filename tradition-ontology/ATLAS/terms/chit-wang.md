# chit wang (Thai)

`trm:chit-wang` · `skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

**Language:** Thai
**Literal:** void mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Buddhadāsa's term for a mind void of 'I' and 'mine', with which one should work and live.
- [Suan Mokkh (Buddhadāsa's tradition)](../lineages/suan-mokkh.md): The mind free of self-grasping, the practical form of suññatā.
