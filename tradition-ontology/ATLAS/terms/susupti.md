# suṣupti / suṣupta

`trm:susupti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सुषुप्ति
**Literal:** deep sleep

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Deep sleep: where one desires no desire and sees no dream (BAU 4.3.19; MāU 5); one has merged with being (ChU 6.8.1); one lies in the hitā channels (BAU 2.1.19).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Deep sleep: the state in which the self, as prājña, rests in ignorance without objects, experiencing bliss; the memory 'I slept happily, I knew nothing' shows the witness and ignorance.
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Dreamless sleep, in which there is no affliction — the analogy by which liberation is shown possible (NS 4.1.63); explained as the mind's contact with purītat.
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): Deep sleep is non-discrimination, māyā (Śiva Sūtra 1.10); a void absorption like deep sleep is not the goal (SK 1.12-13).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Deep sleep, in which the self rests in the Lord in the heart with knowledge contracted, persisting as 'I'.
