# anirvacanīya

`trm:anirvacaniya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनिर्वचनीय
**Literal:** indeterminable

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The Advaita category of what is neither real nor unreal; for Dvaita it is established by no means of knowledge and taught by no scripture.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Indescribable: not determinable as real (since sublated) nor as unreal (since it appears); the status of māyā, avidyā and their products.
