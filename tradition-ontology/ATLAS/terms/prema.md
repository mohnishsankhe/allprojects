**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Love for Kṛṣṇa: bhāva grown dense, melting the heart completely and marked by intense possessiveness toward him; the supreme goal, desiring only his pleasure (as opposed to kāma).
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Love for the Lord, indescribable, 'like a mute's tasting', free of guṇas and desire, ever-increasing, unbroken, subtlest, of the nature of experience (NBS 51–54).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Love of the Lord — 'the two and a half letters of prem' worth more than all books; its lane is too narrow for two.
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): Selfless divine love, the goal of the siddha stage, refined out of kāma.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): Prem: the love-practice (prem-sādhanā) of the one who has died while alive.
