# kṛpā

`trm:krpa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कृपा
**Literal:** compassion, pity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The supreme compassion that overcame Arjuna on seeing his kinsmen (1.28, 2.1).
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Grace, compassion: 'a drop of the Lord's grace' (bhagavat-kṛpā-leśa) and the grace of great souls are the chief means to devotion (NBS 38).
