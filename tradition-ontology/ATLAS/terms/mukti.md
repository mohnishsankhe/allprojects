# mukti

`trm:mukti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मुक्ति
**Literal:** liberation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The upsurge of Śiva's bliss in the Śivayogin in whom the world has dissolved (SSM 14.15); the supreme knowledge of the self dissolved in sāmarasya (14.42–44).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): The absolute cessation of pain, i.e. a destruction of pain not contemporaneous with any prior absence of pain in the same self (Muktivāda).
