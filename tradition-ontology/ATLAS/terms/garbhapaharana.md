# garbhāpaharaṇa

`trm:garbhapaharana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** गर्भापहरण
**Literal:** removal of the embryo

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The removal of Mahāvīra's embryo by Hariṇaigameṣin, at Śakra's command, from the brāhmaṇī Devānandā to the kṣatriyāṇī Triśalā; counted among the ten 'wonders' of this era.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Rejected: Mahāvīra was conceived by Triśalā alone.
