# antakāla

`trm:antakala` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अन्तकाल
**Literal:** the time of the end

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: The time of death, also called prayāṇa-kāla, 'time of departure': those with thought yoked (yukta-cetas) know the Lord even then (7.30; asked at 8.2); whoever departs remembering him then comes to his state (8.5); one goes to whatever state one remembers at the end, as habitually dwelt on (8.6); at that time the yogin sets the breath between the brows (8.10).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The hour of death, when the state remembered determines where one goes (BhG 8.5–6); being in the brāhmī state even then brings brahmanirvāṇa (2.72).
