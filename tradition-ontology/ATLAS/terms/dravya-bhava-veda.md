## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Physical sex (the bodily marks) as against psychological sex (the rise of the sexual-desire karma); Digambaras say liberation occurs from any bhāva-veda but only from the male dravya-veda, and read canonical 'female siddhas' as referring to bhāva-veda.
