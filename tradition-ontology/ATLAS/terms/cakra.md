# cakra

`trm:cakra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** चक्र
**Literal:** wheel, circle

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): A centre of the subtle body along the central channel; the texts differ in number and attributes — six plus the thousand-petalled lotus (ṢCN; GŚ), nine (Siddhasiddhāntapaddhati), with sixteen supports (ādhāra), three targets and five voids in the Nāth body-maps.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): A centre (wheel/lotus) of the body; Nāth texts count six (Yogaviṣaya, GŚ), nine (SSP) or eleven (KJN).
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Wheel of channels: in the Hevajra four — emanation (navel), dharma (heart), enjoyment (throat), great bliss (head); in the Kālacakra six.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): A centre of the subtle body; six (or nine) in the Yoga Upaniṣads; also the Śrīcakra.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): In the Tamil Siddha texts the centres are usually called ātāram (supports), six in number, with the crown-space above them; cakkaram names also the worship-diagram (the Meru of forty-three triangles, Caṭṭaimuṉi 1).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): Centres through which the power rises (VBT 29); the Netratantra names six wheels, sixteen supports, three targets and five voids (Netra 7.1).

## Forms in other languages
