# cakra

`trm:cakra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** चक्र
**Literal:** wheel

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): A centre of the subtle body; six (or nine) in the Yoga Upaniṣads; also the Śrīcakra.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): Centres through which the power rises (VBT 29); the Netratantra names six wheels, sixteen supports, three targets and five voids (Netra 7.1).
