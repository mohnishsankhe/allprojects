# sammāvācā

`trm:samma-vaca` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** right speech

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Abstaining from false, divisive, harsh speech and idle chatter (SN 45.8); speaking only the true and beneficial at the right time (MN 58). One of the thirty-seven qualities conducive to awakening (DN 16.3.50; MN 149): a member of the noble eightfold path.
