# karmabandha

`trm:karma-bandha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कर्मबन्ध
**Literal:** bondage of action

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Cast off by one joined with the understanding according to yoga (2.39); the world is bound by action (karmabandhana) except for action done for sacrifice (3.9); the wise joined with understanding are freed from the bondage of birth (janmabandha, 2.51).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: The bondage from which one free of the pairs of opposites is easily released (5.3); the unyoked one, attached to fruit through desire, is bound (5.12); actions do not bind one who knows the Lord's freedom from them (4.14), the one beyond the pairs (4.22), or the one who has renounced actions by yoga and cut doubt by knowledge (4.41).
