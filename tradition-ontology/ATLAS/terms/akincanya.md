# ākiñcanya

`trm:akincanya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आकिञ्चन्य
**Literal:** having nothing

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Destitution of any means of one's own — no ritual merit, self-knowledge or devotion — the qualification for surrender.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): Having nothing: 'your servant with no other refuge' (TVM 6.10.10); the girls 'a clan without knowledge' (TP 28); 'I have nothing' (Tirumālai 25).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The ninth virtue: the attitude 'nothing is mine' (TS 9.6).
