# benlai mianmu (original face)

`trm:benlai-mianmu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 本來面目
**Literal:** the face one had originally

## Definitions by tradition
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): One's original face 'before thinking good or evil' (Huineng to Huiming); a pointer to the self-nature.
- [Seon (Korean Chan)](../lineages/seon.md): The empty and calm numinous knowing mind is one's original face (Jinul).
- [Rinzai Zen](../lineages/rinzai.md): A standard pointer and kōan ('your original face before your parents were born').
