# vitakka

`trm:vitakka` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** thinking, applied thought

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Applied thought: thinking, directing (ūhana); its mark is directing consciousness onto the object, function striking and threshing, manifestation leading consciousness onto the object; like the striking of a bell, the first stirring like a bird spreading its wings (Vism IV p. 142-143).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Thought directed to an object; thoughts of sensuality, ill will and cruelty versus renunciation, good will and harmlessness (MN 19); verbal formation (MN 44); a factor of the first jhāna; removed by the five methods (MN 20).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Applied thought: the mounting of the mind onto the object; a factor of the first jhāna.
