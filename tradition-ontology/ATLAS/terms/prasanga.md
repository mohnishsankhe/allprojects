# prasaṅga

`trm:prasanga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रसङ्ग
**Literal:** consequence, (unwanted) implication

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Drawing out an unwanted consequence from the opponent's own position (e.g. that self-arising would be pointless and endless: Buddhapālita on MMK 1.1).
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): The proper method of the Mādhyamika, who, having no thesis, only shows the contradictions in others' positions; defended by Candrakīrti (PsP 1).
- [Svātantrika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/svatantrika.md): Insufficient by itself: a consequence-statement states no reason and example and, reversed, would imply an unwanted thesis (Bhāviveka).
