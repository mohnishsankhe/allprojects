## Definitions by tradition
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The sacred fire kept burning at a Nāth seat or by a wandering yogī; at Gorakhpur an 'unbroken' dhūnī is ascribed to Gorakhnāth; its ash is given as blessing.
- [Aghora (Aghor panth; Aghorī and Aughaṛ ascetics)](../lineages/aghora.md): The fire of the Aghora ascetic, often kept in or near the cremation ground.
