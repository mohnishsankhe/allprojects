# upamāna

`trm:upamana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उपमान
**Literal:** comparison; analogy

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Establishing what is to be established through similarity to the well known (NS 1.1.6).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Yields knowledge of the name–named relation from cognition of similarity (the gavaya example).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Not a separate means; included in inference (Praśastapāda).
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Similarity producing knowledge of an unperceived object: seeing a gavaya one cognizes the similarity in the remembered cow (the Vṛttikāra in Śabara).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Comparison: knowing the similarity of a remembered thing to one now perceived (the cow is like the gavaya).
