# gzhan stong (zhentong)

`trm:zhentong` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** གཞན་སྟོང་
**Literal:** empty of other

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): The ultimate — dharmatā, buddha-nature, the dharmakāya — is empty of everything other than itself (all adventitious conventional phenomena) but not empty of its own nature and qualities.
- [Rimé (ris med), the non-sectarian movement](../lineages/rime.md): In Kongtrul's presentation, the Great Madhyamaka view of the final turning, suited to meditative experience; in Mipham's sense, the unconditioned wisdom's emptiness of the adventitious, which does not exclude its also being empty of own-nature.
