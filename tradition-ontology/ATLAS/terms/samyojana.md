# saṁyojana

`trm:samyojana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** fetter

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): In MN 10:40 the fetter that arises dependent on a sense base and its object; known as present, how unarisen arises, how arisen is abandoned, how abandoned does not arise again.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The ten fetters: five lower (identity view, doubt, clinging to rules and vows, sensual lust, ill will) and five higher (lust for form, lust for the formless, conceit, restlessness, ignorance) (MN 64).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Fetters binding to the round; the Abhidhamma lists ten (sensual lust, aversion, conceit, view, doubt, rules and observances, lust for existence, envy, avarice, ignorance), differing from the sutta list.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Fetter: nine in the Abhidharma list; also the five lower and five higher fetters of the sūtras.
