## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Envelopment: eight (or ten) secondary defilements — shamelessness, lack of dread of blame, envy, avarice, restlessness, remorse, torpor, sleepiness (+ anger, concealment).
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): The defilement awake (manifest), as opposed to its latent seed.
