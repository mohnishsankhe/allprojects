# Prājña

`trm:prajna` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्राज्ञ
**Literal:** the knowing one

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The form of Viṣṇu that presides over deep sleep, in which the soul, covered by tamas, rests in him.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The wisdom that is 'firmly established' in the one who neither rejoices nor hates, withdraws and restrains the senses (2.57, 2.58, 2.61, 2.68); carried away when the mind follows the senses (2.67). Arjuna is said to speak prajñāvāda, words of (seeming) wisdom (2.11).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Insight: the fifth means (1.20); truth-bearing in nirvicāra lucidity (1.48); sevenfold at the final stage (2.27); its light comes from mastery of saṃyama (3.5).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Wisdom, intelligence: the wise brahmin should cultivate prajñā (BAU 4.4.21); Indra as 'breath, the intelligent self' (prajñātman, KauU 3.2); by intelligence one obtains thoughts and desires (KauU 1.7).
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The perfection of wisdom that does not apprehend any dharma; it is the mother of the buddhas and leader of the perfections; wisdom without skillful means is bondage and means without wisdom is bondage (Vimalakīrti 4.16).
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Wisdom (emptiness), the feminine pole (bell, lotus, lalanā, the consort) joined with means.
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The sixth perfection, understanding emptiness; all the other practices were taught for its sake (BCA 9.1); chief of faith and wisdom, though faith comes first (RĀ 1.5).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Intelligence or cognition, most developed in man, who says and sees what he has known and seeks the immortal (AA 2.3.2).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The self as experiencer of deep sleep, identified with the causal body (counterpart of Īśvara); bound by cause only (GK 1.11).
