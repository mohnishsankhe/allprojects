# yakṣa

`trm:yaksa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** यक्ष
**Literal:** wonder; marvellous being

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The wondrous presence, 'endowed with self' (ātmanvat), within the golden sheath or nine-gated lotus, known by the knowers of Brahman (AVŚ 10.2.32, 10.8.43); the mind is the unprecedented yakṣa within creatures (VS 34.2).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): A wondrous being, the form in which brahman appears to the proud gods (Kena 3).
