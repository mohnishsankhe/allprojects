# vākya

`trm:vakya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वाक्य
**Literal:** sentence; syntactic connection

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): A sentence is one when it has a single purpose and its parts need each other (MS 2.1.46); as the third means of subsidiarity, joint utterance (samabhivyāhāra) of subsidiary and principal words.
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): The sentence, for the grammarians the primary indivisible unit of meaning (vākya-sphoṭa); Bhartṛhari lists eight rival definitions (VP 2.1–2).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): A group of words (Tarkasaṅgraha); Vedic sentences are valid as spoken by the Lord, worldly ones when spoken by a reliable person.
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): The composition of sentences in the Veda is preceded by understanding (VS 6.1.1).
