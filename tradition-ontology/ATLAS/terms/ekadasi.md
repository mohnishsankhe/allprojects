# Ekādaśī (Hari-dina, Nārāyaṇa-dina)

`trm:ekadasi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** एकादशी
**Literal:** the eleventh (lunar day)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The eleventh lunar day of both fortnights, kept by fasting, with detailed rules for days overlapped by the tenth.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The eleventh lunar day of each fortnight, kept with fasting for Viṣṇu; so powerful by the Nārada's praise that Yama's realm was emptied.
