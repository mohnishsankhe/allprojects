**Literal:** established tenet; conclusion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): A settled position; four kinds: common to all systems, peculiar to one, implied, and provisionally accepted (NS 1.1.26–31).
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The final answer of an adhikaraṇa, reached after the prima facie view.
