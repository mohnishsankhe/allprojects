# caṇḍālī

`trm:candali` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** fierce (outcaste) woman

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Inner heat, 'the fierce woman' who blazes at the navel and melts the moon at the head (Hevajra I.1); one of Tilopa's six dharmas; restricted practice.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): gtum mo, 'the fierce one', inner heat: the yoga of kindling heat at the navel, melting the drops and bringing winds into the central channel, 'the main trunk of the path' and 'armour' of the six yogas; restricted practice.

## Forms in other languages
- Tibetan: gtum mo གཏུམ་མོ — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [kuṇḍalinī](kundalini.md) — Both are a feminine inner power at the base/navel rising through the central channel; the systems and goals differ (Buddhist: melting bliss joined with emptiness).
