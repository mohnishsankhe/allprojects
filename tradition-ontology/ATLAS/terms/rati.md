# rati (kṛṣṇa-rati)

`trm:rati` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Love or attraction for Kṛṣṇa; the seed-form of the permanent emotion; in bhāva-bhakti it first appears.
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): Love-feeling (and, in coded usage, its bodily basis) that the practitioner must stabilize and raise into prema.
