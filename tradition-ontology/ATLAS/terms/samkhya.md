# sāṃkhya

`trm:samkhya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सांख्य
**Literal:** pertaining to enumeration (saṃkhyā); discrimination

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The darśana that enumerates the twenty-five principles in order to discriminate puruṣa from prakṛti; its knowledge is the discriminating knowledge of the manifest, the unmanifest and the knower (SK 2) that removes the threefold suffering.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): In the Gītā, the understanding (buddhi) concerning the self (2.39) and the path of knowledge of the Sāṃkhyas (3.3), said to be one with Yoga (5.4–5); in the Mokṣadharma, the system that enumerates twenty-four principles with the puruṣa as twenty-fifth and relies on the teaching of the śāstra (12.289.7; 12.294), proclaimed by Kapila (12.337.60).
