## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The two-petalled centre between the brows, of mind, with the praṇava (ṢCN); HYP 4.48 calls the place between the brows the place of Śiva.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The centre in the head, between the brows.
