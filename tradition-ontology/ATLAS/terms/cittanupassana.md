## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Knowing the mind as with or without lust, hatred, delusion; contracted or scattered; exalted or unexalted; surpassable or unsurpassable; concentrated or unconcentrated; liberated or unliberated (MN 10:34).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Knowing the mind as with or without lust, hatred, delusion, contracted or scattered, exalted, concentrated, liberated (MN 10). One of the thirty-seven qualities conducive to awakening (DN 16.3.50; MN 149): a member of the four establishments of mindfulness.
