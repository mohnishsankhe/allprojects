# yi dam

`trm:yidam` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** ཡི་དམ
**Literal:** mind-bond

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): The meditational deity with whom one's mind is bound by samaya; the root of accomplishment, practised in the generation and completion stages.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): The meditational deity with whom one's mind is bound by pledge — for the Kagyu chiefly Cakrasaṃvara, Vajravārāhī and Hevajra; one of the three roots and of the fivefold Mahāmudrā.
