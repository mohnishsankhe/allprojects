# vipralambha

`trm:vipralambha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Love in separation, which nourishes and intensifies union (sambhoga); in Caitanya's last years the dominant mood.
- `lin:jagannatha`: Love-in-separation as a phase of love's play (śṛṅgāra), the ground of most of the Gīta Govinda.
