# vedanānupassanā

`trm:vedananupassana` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** contemplation of feelings

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Knowing pleasant, painful and neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling, each as worldly (sāmisa) or unworldly (nirāmisa) (MN 10:32).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Knowing pleasant, painful and neutral feelings, worldly and unworldly (MN 10). One of the thirty-seven qualities conducive to awakening (DN 16.3.50; MN 149): a member of the four establishments of mindfulness.
