# vedanānupassanā

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Knowing pleasant, painful and neutral feelings, worldly and unworldly (MN 10). One of the thirty-seven qualities conducive to awakening (DN 16.3.50; MN 149): a member of the four establishments of mindfulness.
