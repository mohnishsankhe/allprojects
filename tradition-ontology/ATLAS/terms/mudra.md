# mudrā

`trm:mudra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मुद्रा
**Literal:** seal

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): In haṭha, a bodily technique that seals and redirects breath, bindu and kuṇḍalinī — ten in the HYP (3.6–7), twenty-five in the GS (3.1–3); to be kept secret and learned from a guru (HYP 3.8–9, 3.129).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Seal: the ten mudrās of the Śrīcakra worship (sarvasaṃkṣobhiṇī to trikhaṇḍā), one for each enclosure plus the tenth; the Goddess is 'worshipped with the ten mudrās' (LSN 179).
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Seal: a hand gesture; the four seals of the Tattvasaṃgraha and of Advayavajra (karma-, dharma-, mahā-, samaya-mudrā); also the consort.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): A seal of body, gaze or attention (mahāmudrā, khecarī, śāmbhavī, vaiṣṇavī, ṣaṇmukhī); also the renouncer's inner 'seal'.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): Seals such as Karaṅkiṇī, Krodhanā, Bhairavī, Lelihānā and Khecarī (VBT 77; TĀ 32); an āṇava support (karaṇa).
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tamil muttirai: seals of yoga and ritual, declared needless for one who remains awake in awareness (Kuṭampai 6).
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Among the five tattvas, mudrā denotes parched grain eaten in the rite, not the hand-gesture (Mahānirvāṇa 5.22).
