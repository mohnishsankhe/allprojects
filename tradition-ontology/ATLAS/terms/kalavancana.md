## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The conquest or postponement of death through yoga: the Siddhas break the staff of time (HYP 1.9); mudrās give 'cheating of time' (3.130); one established in rājayoga cheats time (4.103); a chapter of the Haṭhatattvakaumudī is devoted to it.
