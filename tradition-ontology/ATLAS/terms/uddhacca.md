# uddhacca

`trm:uddhacca` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** restlessness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Restlessness as the ninth fetter and part of the fourth hindrance.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Agitation of mind; a universal unwholesome factor; a fetter abandoned only by arahantship.
