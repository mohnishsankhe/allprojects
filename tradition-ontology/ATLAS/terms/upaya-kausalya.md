# upāyakauśalya

`trm:upaya-kausalya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** skill in means

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The buddhas' and bodhisattvas' skill in adapting the teaching and their conduct to beings' capacities — teaching three vehicles for one, showing parinirvāṇa though the Buddha's life is immeasurable, living as a householder; joined with wisdom it keeps the bodhisattva from both saṃsāra and premature nirvāṇa.
