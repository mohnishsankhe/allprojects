# upāya

`trm:upaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उपाय
**Literal:** means

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The means to liberation; the five in Arthapañcaka are karma, jñāna, bhakti, prapatti and the teacher's regard.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The means to immersion: anupāya, śāmbhava, śākta, āṇava; liberation is one whatever the means (TĀ 1.165-170).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Yoga can be attained through the proper means by one whose self is controlled and who strives (6.36).
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Residence, observance, muttering-and-meditation, constant remembrance of Rudra, and grace.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Means (compassion, great bliss), the masculine pole (vajra, rasanā) joined with wisdom; 'by the very deeds that bind, joined with means, beings are freed' (Cittaviśuddhiprakaraṇa 6).
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Method — compassion and the perfections of giving and the rest — which must be united with wisdom (Bhāvanākrama II); conventional truth is the means to the ultimate (MA 6.80).
