# viññāṇa

`trm:vinnana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** consciousness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The fifth aggregate: the six classes of consciousness, each reckoned by its condition as a fire by its fuel (MN 38); dependently arisen, not a self that transmigrates (MN 38); conditioned by and conditioning name-and-form (DN 15; SN 12.67).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Consciousness as an aggregate and a link; synonymous with citta.

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: vijñāna  — exact
