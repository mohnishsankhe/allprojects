# ubhatobhāgavimutta

`trm:ubhatobhagavimutta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** freed both ways

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): One who contacts the formless liberations with the body and has destroyed the effluents by wisdom (MN 70; DN 15).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): One who touches the eight liberations with the body and whose taints are destroyed by wisdom.
