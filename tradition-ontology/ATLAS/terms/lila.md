# līlā

`trm:lila` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** लीला
**Literal:** play

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The Lord's free, purposeless-yet-gracious activity in creation and in the avatāras, above all Kṛṣṇa's play in Vraja; VP 6.7.71-72 says his activity is not caused by karma but is for the good of the worlds.
- [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md): Sport: creation is the Lord's play, without need (BS 2.1.33).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Creation undertaken as spontaneous play, like breathing; the account belongs to the empirical realm.
