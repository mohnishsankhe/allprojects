# acintya

`trm:acintya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अचिन्त्य
**Literal:** unthinkable

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Said of the embodied self: it cannot be an object of thought (2.25, with 'unmanifest' and 'unchangeable').
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Unthinkable: one of the attributes of the imperishable (akṣara) worshipped by those who worship the unmanifest (12.3).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Inconceivable: what cannot be established or refuted by reasoning and is known only from revelation — used of the Lord's powers.
