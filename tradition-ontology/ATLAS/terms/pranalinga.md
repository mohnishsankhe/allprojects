# prāṇaliṅga

`trm:pranalinga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्राणलिङ्ग
**Literal:** the liṅga of the life-breath

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The subtle liṅga, made of inner contemplation (SSM 6.49–50): the light that arises from the navel-bulb when prāṇa and apāna meet, in which breath dissolves like frost in the sun (12.6–8); the initiation formula also calls the given liṅga 'your prāṇaliṅga, to be borne like your breath' (6.26).
