# avatāra

`trm:avatara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अवतार
**Literal:** descent

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Viṣṇu's manifestation of his own eternal body of consciousness and bliss, not a birth of flesh; the avatāras are wholly identical with him and flawless.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The Lord's coming into being age after age by his own māyā to protect the good, destroy evil-doers and establish dharma (BhG 4.6–8); the noun itself is not used in the Gītā.
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): The Lord's successive manifestations (prādurbhāva), foretold by Nārāyaṇa to Nārada (MBh 12.326.72–94).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): A descent of the Lord in a form for the protection of the world; innumerable, the principal ones listed as twenty-two (BhP 1.3), ten (Agni, Garuḍa) and in other lists; all are portions and parts, but Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān himself (BhP 1.3.28).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The Lord's real descent in a body of pure sattva by his own will, not from karma.
