# vrata

`trm:vrata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** व्रत
**Literal:** ordinance; vow

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The binding ordinance of a god (Varuṇa 'of firm vratas'), and a vow undertaken by a person ('Agni, lord of vows, I shall undertake the vow', VS 1.5).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): A religious vow for a set period or day (fast, vigil, worship, gifts), with its story and fruit (e.g. Ekādaśī, Śivarātri, Navarātra, Kātyāyanī vrata).
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The primary observance of ash-bathing, lying in ash, the sixfold offering, muttering and circumambulation.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Vow, observance: the vow of breathing (BAU 1.5.23); the food vows (TU 3.7-10); the head-vow (MuU 3.2.10); Prajāpati's vow (PrU 1.13).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Abstention from violence, falsehood, stealing, unchastity and possessiveness — partial for the householder, complete for the ascetic (TS 7.1–2).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The observance taken by the sacrificer on entering a rite (ŚB 1.1.1).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The vows of the Vedic student and the observances (fasts and rules) prescribed in the smṛtis and digests.
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): In jyotiṣa, vows and fasts kept on the weekday or tithi of an afflicting planet or deity as part of the remedies (later horā and muhūrta practice).
