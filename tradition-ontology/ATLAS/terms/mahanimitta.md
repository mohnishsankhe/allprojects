# mahānimitta

`trm:mahanimitta` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** महानिमित्त
**Literal:** great (science of) signs

## Definitions by tradition
- [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md): (Jain report) The eightfold prognostication Gosāla learned from the six disācaras, drawn from the Pūrvas.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The eightfold lore (year-lore, dreams, marks, portents, bodily signs, omens and the rest) by which some claim to know the future; the Jain text says it sometimes fails and gives no release.
