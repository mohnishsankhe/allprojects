## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): In the Kārikā ch.3: distraction of the mind (vikṣipta) in desire and enjoyment (3.42), to be restrained by a means and calmed again (3.44); its absence with that of laya marks brahman (3.46).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Distraction in meditation, from which one must turn away (SS 6.30).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Distraction: the mind's turning to external things; also the projecting power of ignorance.
