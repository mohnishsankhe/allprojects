# uḍḍiyāna (bandha)

`trm:uddiyana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उड्डीयान
**Literal:** flying up

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The abdominal lock by which prāṇa 'flies up' suṣumnā; best of the locks, giving liberation naturally when firm (HYP 3.55–60).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The abdominal lock between genitals and navel, 'the lion to the elephant of death' (GŚ 35).
