# jhāna

`trm:jhana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** absorption, meditation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The four meditative absorptions: the first with applied and sustained thought, rapture and bliss born of seclusion; the second with rapture and bliss born of concentration; the third with bliss and equanimity; the fourth with purity of mindfulness through equanimity (DN 2; SN 45.8); remembered by the bodhisatta as 'the path to awakening' (MN 36); a basis for destroying the effluents (AN 9.36).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Meditative absorption with specific factors; fourfold in the suttas and fivefold in the Abhidhamma method; mundane or supramundane.
- [Pa-Auk tradition (Pa-Auk Tawya)](../lineages/pa-auk.md): Full absorption on a counterpart sign in which the mind is unbroken for long periods; required (or access) before seeing ultimate realities.
