# tīrthaṅkara

`trm:tirthankara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तीर्थङ्कर
**Literal:** ford-maker

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): A 'ford-maker': an omniscient Jina who, after attaining kevala, teaches the dharma and founds the fourfold community (the 'ford' across saṃsāra); twenty-four appear in each half of the time-cycle, by the ripening of the tīrthaṅkara-nāma-karma bound in an earlier life.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The Śvetāmbaras include a woman, Mallī, among the Tīrthaṅkaras of this era.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): All Tīrthaṅkaras are male; the Tīrthaṅkara does not eat after omniscience.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): An omniscient who establishes the fourfold community and teaches the path in a given age; twenty-four in each half-cycle.

## Forms in other languages
