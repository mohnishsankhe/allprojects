# akṣamālā

`trm:aksamala` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अक्षमाला
**Literal:** rosary of letters

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Rosary into whose beads the fifty letters a–kṣa are invoked.
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): The rosary for counting japa; also the 'garland of letters' a to kṣa (akṣa-mālā) with which mental japa is counted.
