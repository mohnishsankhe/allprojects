# pabhassara citta

`trm:pabhassara-citta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** luminous mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): 'This mind is luminous, but defiled by adventitious defilements' — understood by the noble disciple, for whom there is development of mind (AN 1.51–52).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The mind called luminous in AN 1.49–52, identified by the commentary with the life-continuum.
- [Thai Forest tradition (Kammaṭṭhāna forest tradition)](../lineages/thai-forest.md): The radiant citta of the forest teachers' experience; Ajahn Maha Boowa warns that ignorance hides in this radiance.
