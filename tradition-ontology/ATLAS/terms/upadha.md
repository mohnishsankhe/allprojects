# upadhā

`trm:upadha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** उपधा
**Literal:** grasping; craving as the basis of suffering

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The supreme cause giving suffering and the seats of suffering; its total renunciation removes all suffering (Ca Śā 1.95).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): A fault of disposition; the life-stages yield merit according to its absence (VS 6.2.3–5 C).
