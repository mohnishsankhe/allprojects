# upaniṣad

`trm:upanisad` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** sitting near; correspondence; secret

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Secret teaching, hidden connection or name: 'its upaniṣad is the real behind the real' (BAU 2.1.20); the upaniṣad of the asuras (ChU 8.8.4); 'we have told you the upaniṣad about brahman' (Kena 4.7); what is done with the upaniṣad is more powerful (ChU 1.1.10).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Knowledge of Brahman, so called because it loosens and destroys the seeds of saṃsāra and leads to Brahman; secondarily the texts.
