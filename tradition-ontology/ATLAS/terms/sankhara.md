# saṅkhāra

`trm:sankhara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** formation, volitional construction, conditioned thing

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The fourth aggregate (volition towards the six objects); in dependent origination the bodily, verbal and mental formations conditioned by ignorance (SN 12.2); in the widest sense everything conditioned ('all saṅkhāras are impermanent'); bodily formation is breathing, verbal formation applied and sustained thought, mental formation perception and feeling (MN 44).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): (1) The second link: meritorious, demeritorious and imperturbable volitions; (2) the aggregate of mental formations (fifty mental factors); (3) all conditioned things.
- [U Ba Khin – S. N. Goenka tradition (Sayagyi U Ba Khin lineage)](../lineages/u-ba-khin-goenka.md): Mental reactions of craving and aversion to sensations, and the deep conditioning they leave, which arise as sensations when observed with equanimity (Goenka).
