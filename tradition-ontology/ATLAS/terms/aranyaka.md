## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): A 'forest book' of a Vedic school, containing the dangerous or secret rites and meditations (Mahāvrata, Pravargya, saṃhitā-upaniṣads) studied away from the village, and often an Upaniṣad.
