# vikaraṇadharmitva

`trm:vikaranadharmitva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विकरणधर्मित्व
**Literal:** the capacity of acting without organs

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Unsurpassed lordship even with the instruments withdrawn; Kauṇḍinya calls it the Pāśupata's distinction over others' isolation (kaivalya). The Sūtra lists 'vikaraṇa' and 'dharmitva' separately.
