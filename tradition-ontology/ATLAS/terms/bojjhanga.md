# bojjhaṅga

`trm:bojjhanga` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** factor of awakening

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The seven factors of awakening; in MN 118:30-36 they are aroused in sequence when the four establishments are practised, and developed with the four qualifiers of seclusion, dispassion, cessation and ripening in relinquishment they fulfil knowledge and liberation (mn118:42-43).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The seven: mindfulness, investigation of dhammas, energy, rapture, tranquillity, concentration, equanimity; known as present or absent, how the unarisen arises, how the arisen comes to fulfilment by development (MN 10:42).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The seven factors: mindfulness, investigation of dhammas, energy, rapture, tranquillity, concentration, equanimity (MN 118; SN 46.51–53).
