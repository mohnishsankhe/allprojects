# pradhāna

`trm:pradhana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रधान
**Literal:** the principal, the chief (cause)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Synonym of prakṛti as the principal cause of the world (SK 11, 57); the term under which Vedānta and other critics discuss the Sāṃkhya cause.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The guṇas as the unmanifest source, 'neither being nor non-being' (YBh 2.19); mastery of pradhāna is one of the three 'honey-like' powers (3.48).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Primal matter: the perishable (ŚU 1.10); God is lord of pradhāna and of the field-knower (6.16); like a spider covering himself with threads from pradhāna (6.10).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Primal matter, one of the four forms of Viṣṇu in the VP (with puruṣa, vyakta and kāla).
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The principal act, which bears the fruit; its subsidiaries serve it (MS 9.1.1).
