# mahat

`trm:mahat` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** महत्
**Literal:** the great (one)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: 'Great brahman' (mahad brahma): the Lord's womb, in which he places the germ from which all beings come (14.3-14.4).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The first evolute of prakṛti (SK 22), identical with buddhi; Gauḍapāda lists its synonyms buddhi, āsurī, mati, khyāti, jñāna, prajñā. Vijñānabhikṣu salutes mahat as the self-born Lord, sprout of the world.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The great self: higher than the intellect, below the unmanifest (KU 1.3.10-11); speech is restrained step by step into it (KU 1.3.13).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): 'Great Brahman', the Lord's womb (BhG 14.3); in the Nārāyaṇīya Aniruddha is called the great self (12.327.26).
