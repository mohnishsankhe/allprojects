**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** व्याहृति
**Literal:** utterance

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The utterances bhūḥ, bhuvaḥ, svaḥ, recited before the Gāyatrī (VS 36.3; cf. 3.5).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The three utterances bhūḥ, bhuvaḥ, svaḥ, spoken by Prajāpati to create earth, atmosphere and sky (ŚB 11.1.6.3), drawn from the three Vedas (AB 5.32).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The utterances recited before the Sāvitrī and in breath-control (MDh 2.76–81; YS 1.23).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The calls bhūḥ, bhuvaḥ, suvaḥ (and mahaḥ): meditated on as worlds, lights, Vedas and breaths (TU 1.5); flowed from the Veda at creation (ChU 2.23.2).
