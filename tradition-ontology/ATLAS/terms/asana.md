# āsana

`trm:asana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आसन
**Literal:** seat

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Posture; the Vāyavīya names eight (svastika, padma, ardhendu, vīra, yoga, prasādhita, paryaṅka, as one likes); the MkP names padma, half and svastika.
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Posture is what is steady and pleasant (SS 3.34); there is no fixed rule (SS 6.24).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Posture, steady and comfortable (2.46), achieved by relaxing effort and coalescence with the endless (2.47); Vyāsa lists seated postures such as padmāsana, bhadrāsana and svastika (YBh 2.46).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Posture, the first limb of haṭha, giving steadiness, health and lightness (HYP 1.17); Śiva taught 84 (HYP 1.33) or 8,400,000 (GS 2.1).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Posture — four, eight, nine, eleven or seventeen named; inwardly, indifference or the seat where Brahman-thought flows.
