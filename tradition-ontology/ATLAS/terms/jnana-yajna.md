# jñāna-yajña

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ज्ञानयज्ञ
**Literal:** sacrifice of knowledge

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 9.15: others worship the Lord by sacrificing with the sacrifice of knowledge — as one, as distinct, in many ways, him who faces in all directions.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4.28, 4.33: the sacrifice of knowledge, offered by ascetics of strict vows; better than sacrifice made of material things, since all action without exception culminates in knowledge.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The sacrifice of knowledge, better than the sacrifice of material things (BhG 4.33); some worship the Lord with it (9.15); the study of the Gītā itself is worship by the sacrifice of knowledge (18.70).
