# icchantika

`trm:icchantika` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** one of (worldly) desire; the incorrigible

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): In the Laṅkāvatāra, one who has abandoned all roots of merit by rejecting the Mahāyāna — though the Tathāgata's power may revive his roots — or the bodhisattva who never enters nirvāṇa for beings' sake.
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): In the Mahāparinirvāṇa, first said to lack buddha-nature (in the sense of good qualities), then declared to have it and to be able to attain buddhahood; a fixed icchantika is denied.
