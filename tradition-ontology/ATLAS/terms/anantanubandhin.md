## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The first of the four varieties of each passion (8.9); the sūtra does not define it; the commentarial line reads it as the passion that binds for endless saṃsāra.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The most intense grade of the passions, which obstructs right view and binds to endless rebirth (TS 8.9).
