# yogamāyā

`trm:yogamaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** योगमाया
**Literal:** the māyā of (the Lord's) yoga

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: That by which the Lord is veiled, so that he is not manifest to all; through it the deluded world does not recognize him, the unborn and imperishable (7.25).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The Lord's yoga-māyā by which he is veiled and not manifest to all (BhG 7.25).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The Lord's internal power that arranges his play, e.g. making the gopīs appear married to others and veiling his majesty.
