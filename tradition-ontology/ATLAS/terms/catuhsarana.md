## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Taking the four refuges — the Arhats, the Siddhas, the Sādhus and the dharma taught by the omniscient — recited with the 'four auspicious things' and 'four highest things'.
