# buddhānusmṛti

`trm:buddhanusmrti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** recollection of the Buddha

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Calling the Buddha to mind — his body, marks, qualities or name — leading to samādhi in which buddhas are seen; understood in the sūtras as mind-made and empty.
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Recollection of Amitābha in samādhi (pratyutpanna, Contemplation Sūtra) or by calling his name; Daochuo calls the samādhi of recollection the king of samādhis.
