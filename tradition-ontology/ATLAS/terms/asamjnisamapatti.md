# asaṃjñi-samāpatti

`trm:asamjnisamapatti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** attainment of non-perception

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The mindless absorption (and the heaven of non-perceiving gods) in which mental consciousness does not arise.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Attainment of non-perception: entered in the fourth dhyāna by ordinary beings who mistake it for escape.
