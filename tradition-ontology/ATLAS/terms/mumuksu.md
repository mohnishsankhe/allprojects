# mumukṣu

`trm:mumuksu` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मुमुक्षु
**Literal:** one who desires release

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): In the Kārikā 2.32: the seeker of liberation, listed among those who are not (ultimately).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: The ancient seekers of liberation performed action, knowing the Lord's untainted relation to action (4.15).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): A bound soul seeking liberation — either the isolation of the self (kaivalya) or the Lord; the latter are bhaktas or prapannas.
- [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md): In the Dāsbodh: The seeker who has turned from the world and longs for freedom.
