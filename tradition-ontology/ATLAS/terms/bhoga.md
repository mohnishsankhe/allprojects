# bhoga

`trm:bhoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भोग
**Literal:** experience, enjoyment

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Experience, which buddhi accomplishes for puruṣa (SK 37); with liberation, one of the two purposes for which prakṛti acts (commentaries on SK 21).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Experience: a cognition that fails to distinguish sattva and puruṣa (3.35); determining the guṇas as desired or undesired without discrimination (YBh 2.18); one of the two purposes of the seen (2.18).
