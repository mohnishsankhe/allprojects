# apūrva

`trm:apurva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अपूर्व
**Literal:** the unprecedented; new (potency)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The unseen potency brought about by a perishable ritual act, postulated because the fruit (e.g. heaven) comes later: 'there is an apūrva, since the undertaking is enjoined' (Śabara on MS 2.1.5).
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): A capacity produced in the self (or the act) by the rite; later Bhāṭṭas distinguish the final apūrva of the fruit from those of the parts and subsidiaries.
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): The apūrva is the 'what is to be done' (kārya/niyoga) made known by the injunction itself, knowable only from the Veda.
