## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): One purpose of reflecting on the nature of the world and body (7.12); also named at 6.24 as a cause of Tīrthaṅkara karma.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Ardor in practice, mild, middling or intense; for the intensely ardent samādhi is near (1.21–22).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In Jainism fear of worldly existence and longing for liberation, one of the signs of right view (TS 7.12, 6.24).
