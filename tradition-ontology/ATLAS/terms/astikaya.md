# astikāya

`trm:astikaya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अस्तिकाय
**Literal:** extended existent

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The five substances that exist with extension in space-points — soul, matter, medium of motion, medium of rest, space (time excluded) (TS 5.1; Kundakunda's Pañcāstikāya).
