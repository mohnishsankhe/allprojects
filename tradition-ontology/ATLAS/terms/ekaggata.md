# ekaggatā

`trm:ekaggata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** one-pointedness, unification

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Unification of mind, which is concentration (MN 44); a factor of the jhānas (MN 111).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Unification of mind on its object; a universal factor, developed as concentration and the jhāna factor.

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: ekāgratā  — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- exact: [ekāgratā](ekagrata.md) — same word
- partial: [ekāgratā](ekagrata.md) — Yoga uses ekāgratā for a level of mind; the Abhidhamma makes it a factor present in every consciousness
