# sngon 'gro

`trm:ngondro` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** སྔོན་འགྲོ
**Literal:** going before

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): The preliminaries: the common outer preliminaries (the four thoughts that turn the mind, with the benefits of liberation and following a teacher) and the uncommon inner preliminaries (refuge with prostration, bodhicitta, Vajrasattva, maṇḍala, kusāli, guru yoga), each traditionally accumulated a hundred thousand times.
- [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md): Bön preliminaries parallel in structure (refuge, bodhicitta, purification, maṇḍala, guru yoga with Tapihritsa or Shenrab).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): The preliminaries: the four thoughts that turn the mind and the four special practices (refuge and prostration with bodhicitta, Vajrasattva, maṇḍala offering, guru yoga), usually done a hundred thousand times each, before Mahāmudrā instruction.
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): The preliminaries of mind training, the four thoughts that turn the mind (Seven Points 1.1).
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The six preparatory practices of a lamrim session and the preliminary practices (prostrations, maṇḍala offerings, Vajrasattva, guru yoga) done before retreat — as in Tsongkhapa's retreat at Wölkha.
