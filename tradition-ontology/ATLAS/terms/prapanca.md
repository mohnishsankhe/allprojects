# prapañca

`trm:prapanca` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रपञ्च
**Literal:** elaboration, conceptual proliferation, diffuseness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The verbal-conceptual elaboration from which conceptual constructions, and from them action and afflictions, arise; it ceases in emptiness (MMK 18.5); reality is not elaborated by elaborations (18.9), and those who elaborate the Buddha do not see him (22.15).
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): Worldly household life; the saints teach that prapañca and paramārtha (the highest aim) can be joined.
- [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md): Worldly life, to be conducted with vigilance and effort together with the pursuit of the highest.
- [Mahānubhāva panth](../lineages/mahanubhava.md): The world, one of the four eternal realities: made of māyā, distinct from souls, deities and Parameśvara.
