# sammāvāyāma

`trm:samma-vayama` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** right effort

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): DN 22:21: generating desire, making effort, arousing energy, exerting the mind, striving for the non-arising of unarisen evil unwholesome states, the abandoning of arisen ones, the arising of unarisen wholesome states, and the persistence and fulfilment of arisen wholesome states.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The four right strivings (SN 45.8). One of the thirty-seven qualities conducive to awakening (DN 16.3.50; MN 149): a member of the noble eightfold path.
