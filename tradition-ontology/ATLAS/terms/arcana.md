## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Ritual worship of the Lord, the fifth form of devotion; the chief means in the Dvāpara age.
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Worship: Vyāsa's definition of devotion is attachment to worship (pūjā) and the like (NBS 16); worship is one of love's eleven forms (NBS 82).
