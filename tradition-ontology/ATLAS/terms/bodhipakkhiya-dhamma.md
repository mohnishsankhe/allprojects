# bodhipakkhiyā dhammā

`trm:bodhipakkhiya-dhamma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** qualities conducive to awakening

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The thirty-seven qualities in seven groups — four establishments of mindfulness, four right efforts, four bases of power, five faculties, five powers, seven factors of awakening, the noble eightfold path — taught for the long duration of the holy life (DN 16; MN 149).
