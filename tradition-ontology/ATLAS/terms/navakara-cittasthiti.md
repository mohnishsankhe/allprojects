# navākārā citta-sthiti

`trm:navakara-cittasthiti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** ninefold settling of the mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The nine stages of calm: placing, settling firmly, re-settling, settling closely, taming, calming, calming fully, one-pointing, and concentrating; achieved by six powers and accompanied by four attentions.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The nine mental states of calm abiding — placing, continuous placing, repeated placing, close placing, taming, pacifying, thoroughly pacifying, one-pointedness, equipoise — achieved by the six powers and four attentions.
