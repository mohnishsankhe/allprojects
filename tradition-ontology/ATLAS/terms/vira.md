# vīra

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वीर
**Literal:** hero

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The 'hero' — the qualified Kaula adept, truthful, free of anger, knowing the Śakti born of the body and formed by non-dual conduct (KJN 20.18–22).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The 'hero', the qualified practitioner; the Goddess is 'mother of heroes' (LSN 156) and the knower of the mantra becomes 'lord of the circle of heroes' (YH 2.85).
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The Kaula adept of the heroic disposition; fearless, beyond shame and doubt (Kulārṇava 5.82).
