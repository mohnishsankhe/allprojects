# svastha

`trm:svastha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्वस्थ
**Literal:** established in oneself; healthy

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): One whose doṣas, fire, tissues, wastes and functions are balanced and whose self, senses and mind are clear (Su Sū 15.41).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 14.24: the one gone beyond the guṇas is svastha, 'abiding in oneself' (sva-stha, read reflexively, as the teaching renders it), alike in pain and pleasure — not the medical sense 'healthy' recorded under this id.
