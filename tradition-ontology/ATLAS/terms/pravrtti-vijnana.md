# pravṛtti-vijñāna

`trm:pravrtti-vijnana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** operative (evolving) consciousness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The consciousnesses that arise from the store as waves from the ocean: the six object-cognitions (and, in the Trisvabhāvanirdeśa's sevenfold count, the afflicted mind).
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The seven consciousnesses that arise from the ālaya like waves.
