**Literal:** deity of speech

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The eight goddesses of speech (Vaśinī, Kāmeśvarī, Modinī, Vimalā, Aruṇā, Jayinī, Sarveśvarī, Kaulinī) of the eight-cornered enclosure, composers of the Lalitā Sahasranāma; in the Bhāvanā Upaniṣad cold, heat, pleasure, pain, desire and the three qualities.
