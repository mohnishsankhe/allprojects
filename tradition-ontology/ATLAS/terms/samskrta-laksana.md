# saṃskṛta-lakṣaṇa

`trm:samskrta-laksana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The four characteristics of the conditioned — birth, duration, decay, impermanence — real forces that act on each dharma, themselves characterized by secondary characteristics.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): Designations of the series: the conditioned comes to be having not been and ceases having been.
