# mātṛkā

`trm:matrka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मातृका
**Literal:** the 'little mother': the power of the letters

## Definitions by tradition
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): The ground of limited knowledge (Śiva Sūtra 1.4); the groups of letters are presided over by Māheśvarī and the other mothers of the bound (3.19).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The letters from A to KṢA in their natural order as the emanation of the tattvas (Parātrīśikā 5-9).
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): The 'little mothers': the fifty (or fifty-one) letters from a to kṣa, manifestations of śabda-brahman through kuṇḍalinī (ŚT 1.14), the body of the Goddess and of every mantra; placed on the body in mātṛkā-nyāsa.
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The fifty letters as the 'little mothers', matrix of mantras; the Goddess is of the form of the fifty seats (LSN 156); mātṛkā is placed on the disciple's body at initiation (PKS 1.39).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Matrix, the doctrinal list from which Abhidharma analysis grew.
