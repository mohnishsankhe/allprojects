# pīṭha

`trm:pitha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पीठ
**Literal:** seat

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Seat: the four seats Kāmarūpa, Pūrṇagiri, Jālandhara and Oḍḍiyāna, located in the subtle body (YH 1.41-42; LSN 82); the Goddess is 'of the form of the fifty seats', the letters (LSN 156).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): A sacred seat of the Goddess where a part of Satī's body fell and Śiva stays as a liṅga (Kālikā Purāṇa 18.46).
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): The four seats as levels of the supreme yoni (Kubjikāmata 11.6-7).
- [Cakrasaṃvara (Saṃvara, Heruka) tradition in India](../lineages/cakrasamvara.md): Sacred seat: the twenty-four (or more) sites of the Heruka maṇḍala in India, and their inner counterparts in the body; the Hevajra correlates classes of sites with the bodhisattva grounds.
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): A seat, used in the haṭha texts as a synonym of āsana (posture): 'the eighty-four seats', 'Matsyendra's seat', 'the lord of yoga-seats'.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Four seats in the body — Kāmarūpa, Pūrṇagiri, Jālandhara, Uḍyāna.
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): A sacred seat of the Goddess and yoginīs: Kāmākhyā, Pūrṇagiri, Oḍiyāna, Arbuda with their sub-seats (KJN 8.20–22).
