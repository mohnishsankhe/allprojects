# kilesa

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** defilement

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The ten bases of defilement: greed, hatred, delusion, conceit, views, doubt, sloth, restlessness, shamelessness, fearlessness of wrongdoing.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Greed, hatred, delusion and the states born of them (a mostly commentarial collective term).
