# abhidharma

`trm:abhidharma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** 'concerning the dharma' / 'the highest dharma'

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): In the true sense, stainless wisdom with its retinue; conventionally, also the wisdom and treatises that lead to it — for the Vaibhāṣikas the seven canonical books, word of the Buddha arranged by disciples.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): For the Sautrāntikas, not a separate basket of treatises but the sūtras in which the characteristics of dharmas are taught (e.g. the Arthaviniścaya).
