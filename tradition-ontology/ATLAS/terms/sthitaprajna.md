# sthitaprajña

`trm:sthitaprajna` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्थितप्रज्ञ
**Literal:** one of steady wisdom

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The person whose wisdom is established: content in the self, free of desire, passion, fear and anger, the senses withdrawn like a tortoise's limbs, awake where others sleep, in the brāhmī state (BhG 2.54–72).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The person described in 2.55–72: gives up all desires that enter the mind, content in the self by the self (2.55); his wisdom is firmly established when he is unattached, withdraws the senses and masters them (2.57–68); his state is the brāhmī sthiti (2.72).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): One of steady wisdom (Gītā 2.55–72), identified in Advaita with the jīvanmukta, whose wisdom is the non-conceptual mode apprehending the identity of self and Brahman (VC 427).
