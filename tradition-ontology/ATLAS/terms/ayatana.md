# āyatana

`trm:ayatana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Native script:** आयतन
**Literal:** sense base, sphere, dimension

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The six internal and external bases (eye and forms, ear and sounds, nose and smells, tongue and tastes, body and tangibles, mind and dhammas), with the fetter that arises dependent on each pair (MN 10:40).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The six internal (eye … mind) and six external (forms … mental objects) bases, 'the all' (SN 35.23); also the formless 'spheres' and 'that sphere' of Ud 8.1.
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The temple (built by another) where the first-stage practitioner lives; the Ratnaṭīkā says it stands for the teacher.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The twelve bases (six internal, six external); also the formless 'spheres'.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The twelve fields were taught with a special intention: cognition's seed and its appearance as object are the 'two fields' of each cognition.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Sense-base, 'gate of arising' of mind and mental factors: twelve (six faculties, six objects).
