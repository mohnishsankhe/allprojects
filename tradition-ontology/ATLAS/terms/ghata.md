# ghaṭa

`trm:ghata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** घट
**Literal:** pot; (stage of) joining

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): (1) The body as a pot (GS 1.6–8; HYP 2.23). (2) The second stage, when the breath 'made one' enters the middle and prāṇa–apāna, mind–breath, individual and supreme self unite (HYP 4.72; DYŚ 81–83).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Second stage, in which breaths, mind and selves unite.
