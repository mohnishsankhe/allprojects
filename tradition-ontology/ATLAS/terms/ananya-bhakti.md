# ananya-bhakti

`trm:ananya-bhakti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनन्यभक्ति
**Literal:** devotion with no other (object)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 8–9: expressed as ananya-cetas (8.14), bhaktyā ananyayā (8.22), ananya-manas (9.13), ananyāḥ (9.22) and ananya-bhāk (9.30): by it the supreme Person is gained (8.22); for the one who remembers him so he is easy to reach (8.14); he carries the welfare of such devotees (9.22); even one of very bad conduct who worships so is to be considered good (9.30).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Undivided devotion (bhakti ananyā), by which alone the Lord can in the form shown be known, seen and entered in truth (11.54) — not by the Vedas, austerity, giving or sacrifice (11.53); cf. worship with 'undivided yoga' (ananya yoga) (12.6).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Devotion directed to nothing else, by which the supreme person is won (BhG 8.22), the Lord seen in truth (11.54) and gain and security borne (9.22).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13.11: 'unswerving devotion (bhakti avyabhicāriṇī) to me through a yoga that has no other (ananya-yoga)' is among the dispositions called knowledge; compare 14.26 (avyabhicāra bhakti-yoga).
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): Exclusive devotion: the unwavering understanding 'I am the servant, the Lord is the master in the form of all'.
