# śalya

`trm:salya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शल्य
**Literal:** dart, foreign body; surgery

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Surgery: removal of foreign bodies and management of wounds with instruments, knives, caustics and fire; the first and foremost limb for the Dhanvantari school (Su Sū 1.8, 1.17-18).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The three thorns that disqualify a vow-holder: deceit, longing for future reward, and wrong view (TS 7.18).
