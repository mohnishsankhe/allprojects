# brahmabhūya

`trm:brahmabhuya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ब्रह्मभूय
**Literal:** becoming brahman

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 14.26: one who serves Kṛṣṇa with the unswerving yoga of devotion, having gone beyond the guṇas, becomes fit for becoming brahman (brahmabhūyāya kalpate; the phrase recurs at 18.53).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: One who is yoked with a purified understanding, restrains himself, turns from the sense-objects, passion and aversion, lives in solitude, eats lightly, restrains speech, body and mind, is intent on the yoga of meditation, rests on dispassion and lets go of the sense of 'I', force, arrogance, desire, anger and possessions, without 'mine' and at peace, becomes fit for becoming brahman (brahmabhūyāya kalpate, 18.51–53); the same words close 14.26.
