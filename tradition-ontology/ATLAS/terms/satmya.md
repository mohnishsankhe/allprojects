# sātmya

`trm:satmya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सात्म्य
**Literal:** what has become one's own; suitability

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): That which, used continuously, agrees with a person (Ca Vi 8.118); the unsuitable is 'what does not become one with the self' (Śā 1.127); changes of habit are to be made gradually (Sū 7.36-38).
