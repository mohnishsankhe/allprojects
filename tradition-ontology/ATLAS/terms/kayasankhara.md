# kāyasaṅkhāra

`trm:kayasankhara` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** bodily formation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Step 4 of the sixteen is 'calming the bodily formation (passambhayaṁ kāyasaṅkhāraṁ)' (MN 118:18). The sutta does not gloss the term here.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): In-and-out breathing (MN 44), calmed in the fourth step of mindfulness of breathing (MN 118).
