## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): the treatise/tradition, named as the ground of Īśvara's excellence (YBh 1.24)
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Scripture/teaching: whoever casts aside its injunction (śāstra-vidhi) and acts as desire prompts attains neither perfection, happiness nor the highest course; therefore it is the authority (pramāṇa) for what is to be done and not done (16.23–24). Arjuna asks about those who set its injunction aside yet sacrifice with faith (17.1); austerity 'not enjoined by scripture' marks the demonic (17.5).
