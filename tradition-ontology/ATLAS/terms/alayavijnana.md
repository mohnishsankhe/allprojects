# ālayavijñāna

`trm:alayavijnana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** storehouse consciousness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The consciousness that holds all seeds and the body, from which the other consciousnesses arise like waves from the ocean; in the Laṅkāvatāra identified with the tathāgatagarbha.
- [Karma Kagyu (Kamtsang Kagyu)](../lineages/karma-kagyu.md): kun gzhi rnam shes: the all-ground consciousness, which recognized is mirror-like wisdom (3rd Karmapa); Nāropa lists the ālaya among the 'situations' of the one mind.
