**Literal:** extraordinary perception

## Definitions by tradition
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Perception through an extraordinary contact: through the universal (sāmānyalakṣaṇā — grasping all smoke via smoke-hood, which grounds pervasion), through a prior cognition (jñānalakṣaṇā — e.g. 'fragrant sandal' seen), and yogic (yogaja).
