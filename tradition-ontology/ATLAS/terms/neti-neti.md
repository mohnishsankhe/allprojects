# neti neti

`trm:neti-neti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नेति नेति
**Literal:** not this, not this

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): In the Sāṃkhya Sūtra, the form of the practice of the principles: discrimination comes by giving up (each principle) with 'not this, not this' (SS 3.76).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): 'Not so, not so': the instruction (ādeśa) about brahman/the self, for there is nothing beyond this 'not so' (BAU 2.3.6); the self is 'not this, not this' — ungraspable, indestructible, unattached (BAU 3.9.26, 4.2.4, 4.4.22, 4.5.15). In BAU 3.2.11 and 4.5.3 the same words are an ordinary double negative.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): 'Not so, not so' (BĀU 2.3.6): negating all specifications of Brahman by adjuncts, not Brahman itself (BS 3.2.22).
