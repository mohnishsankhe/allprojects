# sattva

`trm:sattva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सत्त्व
**Literal:** being; goodness; mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): in Caraka a name of the mind, one leg of the tripod of life (Sū 1.46; Vi 8.119); the mind named after the quality that predominates (Sū 8.6); the pure (śuddha) mind as against the rājasa and tāmasa (Śā 4.36); of superior, middling or inferior strength (Vi 8.119).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The luminous, stainless guṇa that binds by attachment to happiness and knowledge (BhG 14.6); its sign is the light of knowledge in all the gates of the body (14.11); in the Mokṣadharma also the intellect as distinct from the knower of the field (12.187.39; 12.240.21–22).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The guṇa that is light and illuminating (SK 13), of the nature of pleasure (SK 12); predominant in the upper worlds (SK 54). Vācaspati also uses 'sattva' for buddhi.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The guṇa of illumination; in YS 3.35, 3.49, 3.55 the buddhi-sattva, whose purity equal to the puruṣa's is kaivalya, and whose difference from the puruṣa is discerned in viveka-khyāti.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: sattva, being stainless, is illuminating and free of affliction; it binds by attachment to happiness and to knowledge (14.6), attaches to happiness (14.9); when illumination-knowledge arises at all the gates of the body it has increased (14.11); dying in it one reaches the stainless worlds of the knowers of the highest (14.14); knowledge arises from it (14.17); those abiding in it go upward (14.18). In 13.27 'sattva' means simply 'a being'.
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The guṇa characterized as knowledge; those in it go to the gods' state (MDh 12.26, 12.40).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: 'I am the sattva of those who have it' (10.36) — goodness or strength; in 10.41 sattva means simply 'a being'.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: In nityasattvastha, 'ever abiding in sattva' (2.45), one of the qualities of being free of the three guṇas.
