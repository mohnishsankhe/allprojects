# paryāya-paramārtha

`trm:paryaya-paramartha` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the nominal / concordant ultimate

## Definitions by tradition
- [Svātantrika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/svatantrika.md): The ultimate accessible to reasoning — the negation of arising and the wisdom that knows it — accordant with the non-nominal ultimate (aparyāya-paramārtha) that is beyond all elaboration.
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): The negation of arising is called ultimate because it accords with the ultimate (MAL 70).
