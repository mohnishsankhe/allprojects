# guṇa (poetic quality)

`trm:kavya-guna` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): The qualities of poetry — sweetness (mādhurya), vigour (ojas) and clarity (prasāda) in the later scheme, ten in Daṇḍin and Vāmana — which for Mammaṭa belong to rasa as heroism belongs to the self.
