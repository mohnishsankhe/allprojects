# vijñapti-mātra

`trm:vijnaptimatra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विज्ञप्तिमात्र
**Literal:** cognition-only; representation-only

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The thesis that the three realms are nothing but cognition: what appears as external objects is cognition appearing as objects, as in a dream or an eye-disease.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The object of meditative consciousness is nothing but cognition (Saṃdhinirmocana ch. 8).
