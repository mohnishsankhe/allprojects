# prabhāsvara-citta

`trm:prabhasvara-citta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** luminous mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): Mind is luminous by nature and its defilements are adventitious; hence emptiness is neither defiled nor pure, and only the mind that is dharmatā is luminous by nature.
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): This mind is luminous by nature; the stains are adventitious — so they can be removed by the cultivation of selflessness.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The thought of awakening is no-thought, since the nature of thought is luminous (Aṣṭa 1).
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): The self-nature pure mind that is nonetheless defiled — hard to understand (Śrīmālā); the mind's nature rests on nothing, like space (RGV).
