# gandhabba

`trm:gandhabba` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** being to be born

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The third factor (with the parents' union and the mother's season) needed for conception (MN 38); the commentaries read it as the being about to enter the womb.
