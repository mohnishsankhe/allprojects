# nirgrantha

`trm:nirgrantha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निर्ग्रन्थ
**Literal:** without knots

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): 'Knotless' — free of the knots of possessions and passions; the old name of Jain mendicants (Pali 'nigaṇṭha') and of Mahāvīra as Nigaṇṭha Nātaputta; Digambaras take it as requiring nudity.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The Jain ascetic, free of outer and inner attachments; five kinds (TS 9.46); the early name of Jain mendicants.

## Forms in other languages

- Pali: nigaṇṭha  — exact — as used in the Pali canon of Jain monks
- Prakrit: ṇiggaṃtha  — exact
