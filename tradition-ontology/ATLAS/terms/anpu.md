# aṉpu

`trm:anpu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tamil

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): Love for the Lord — the 'lamp-bowl' of Pūtattāḻvār's lamp (IT 1).
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Love, which the Siddhars, after TM 270, identify with Civam itself.
- [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md): Love for Śiva, which is itself Śiva (Tirumantiram 270).
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): In the Kuṟaḷ (ch. 8), love that makes one belong to others; without it the body is bone wrapped in skin.

## Forms in other languages
