# śauca

`trm:sauca` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शौच
**Literal:** purity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Outer purity (earth, water, pure food) and inner washing away of the mind's stains (YBh 2.32); yields disgust for one's body and non-contact with others, and purity of sattva up to fitness for seeing the self (2.40–41).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Purity of body, mind and dealings; purity in wealth is the highest; the body is purified by water, the mind by truth (MDh 5.105–109).
