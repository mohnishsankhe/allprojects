# eṣaṇā

`trm:esana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** एषणा
**Literal:** desire, pursuit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Desire, pursuit: the three desires — for sons, for wealth and for worlds — which knowers of the self rise above to live as mendicants (BAU 3.5.1, 4.4.22).
- [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md): The three desires — for wife/sons, wealth and worlds — risen above in renunciation.
