# anubhūti

`trm:anubhuti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनुभूति
**Literal:** experience

## Definitions by tradition
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): Cognition other than memory; every experience is a means of knowledge (Prakaraṇapañcikā).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Experience/awareness itself, the self as pure self-established consciousness (Iṣṭasiddhi).
