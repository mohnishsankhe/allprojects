# brahmajijñāsā

`trm:brahmajijnasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** desire to know Brahman

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md): The desire to know Brahman, i.e. the inquiry into Brahman, with which the Brahma Sūtras open.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The inquiry into Brahman undertaken after acquiring the four means, not after the inquiry into ritual.
