# śaraṇa

`trm:sarana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शरण
**Literal:** refuge; one who has taken refuge

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The fifth sthala: the aṅgaliṅgī as wife and Śiva as husband, possessed of the bliss of their union (SSM 13.2); also the name of the twelfth-century devotees of Kalyāṇa (fem. śaraṇe).
- [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md): Śaraṇ: the initiation by which one takes refuge in Kṛṣṇa, the name, the guru and the devotees, given by a sattrādhikār.
