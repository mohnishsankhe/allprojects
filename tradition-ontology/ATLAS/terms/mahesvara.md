**Literal:** of Maheśvara

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The second sthala: the bhakta in whom devotion rises through dispassion to steady discernment, steadfast in the liṅga even at the loss of life (SSM 10.2–3; 10.21–22).
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The Lord whose southern image is worshipped and whose troops the perfected yogin leads; source of the scriptural tradition.
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The Lord to be recognized as one's own self: the knower and agent, established from the beginning, full of the undivided awareness 'I am this' (ĪPK 1.1.2, 4.1).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): The name under which Praśastapāda's account of dissolution and creation speaks of the Lord.
