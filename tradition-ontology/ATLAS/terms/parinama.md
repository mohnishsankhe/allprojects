# pariṇāma

`trm:parinama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** परिणाम
**Literal:** transformation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Real transformation: prakṛti operates through the guṇas by combination and transformation, like water taking on different tastes in different receptacles (SK 16).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Real change of form in the guṇas and their products (3.9–15, 4.14, 4.32–33).
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): time, marked by the seasons, called 'transformation'; excessive, deficient or contrary seasons are the third cause of disease (Ca Sū 11.42-43); in Suśruta one of the candidate 'sources' (Su Śā 1.11).
- [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md): Transformation of a cause into an effect (BS 1.4.27).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Real transformation (as milk into curd); Advaita accepts it only for māyā/avidyā in relation to the world, never for Brahman, which undergoes only apparent transformation (vivarta).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The becoming of qualities and substances through their modes (TS 5.42); change within persistence.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The real change of Brahman's body from subtle to gross in creation, Brahman as self remaining unchanged; opposed to Advaita's apparent transformation (vivarta).
