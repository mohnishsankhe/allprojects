# asaṃskṛta

`trm:asamskrta` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Unconditioned: space, cessation through discernment and cessation not through discernment — real, eternal, without activity.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): Not real entities: space is mere absence of resistance; the cessations are mere non-arising.
- [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md): Nine unconditioned dharmas are counted, including the four formless spheres and the natures of dependent origination and of the path (Vasumitra; low confidence).
