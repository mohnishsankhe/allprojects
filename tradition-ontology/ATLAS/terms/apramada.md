# apramāda

`trm:apramada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अप्रमाद
**Literal:** heedfulness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Heedfulness, declared by Sanatsujāta to be immortality (MBh 5.42.4); one of the three 'horses of Brahman' with self-restraint and renunciation (11.7.19; 5.43.14).
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Attentiveness to what is to come, like guarding a sprout; a niyama, the fifth strength, and the state in which the end of suffering is reached.
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Heedful guarding of the awakening mind once adopted — the subject of Bodhicaryāvatāra ch.4.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Vigilance, never being careless for a moment ('samayaṃ goyama mā pamāyae'); the seventh guṇasthāna is named 'apramatta-saṃyata'.

## Forms in other languages
- Pali: appamāda  — partial — cognate; the Dhammapada's 'appamādo amatapadaṃ' is recorded by the Buddhist units
- Tibetan: bag yod pa  — exact
- Prakrit: appamāya  — exact
