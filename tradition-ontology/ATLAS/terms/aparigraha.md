# aparigraha

`trm:aparigraha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अपरिग्रह
**Literal:** non-possessiveness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Not appropriating objects, seeing the faults of acquiring, keeping, losing, attachment and harm (YBh 2.30); when steady, knowledge of the how of births (2.39).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: the meditating yogin is to be without possessions (aparigraha) (6.10); cf. the one who has given up all possessions (tyakta-sarva-parigraha) and incurs no fault (4.21).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In Jainism abstention from possessiveness, defined as infatuation (mūrcchā) (TS 7.17).

## Forms in other languages
