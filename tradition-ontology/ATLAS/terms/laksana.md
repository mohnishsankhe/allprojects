# lakṣaṇā

`trm:laksana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** लक्षण / लक्षणा
**Literal:** indication; mark

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Implication (secondary signification), used when the direct meaning of words is incompatible; in 'tat tvam asi' the words indicate pure consciousness by implication.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Also: a definition or defining mark (as svarūpa- and taṭastha-lakṣaṇa).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Lakṣaṇa: the distinguishing property by which what has been named is set apart from others — the second of the three activities of the śāstra (NBh 1.1.3).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Lakṣaṇā: secondary signification, when the primary meaning does not fit (e.g. 'the village on the Ganges').
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): Indication of a related meaning; for the Bhāṭṭas the sentence-meaning is always 'indicated' by the word-meanings (as the Prakaraṇapañcikā reports their saying), and arthavādas indicate praise.
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): Indication, the secondary power by which a word conveys a meaning connected with its primary sense when the primary sense does not fit ('a village on the Ganges').
