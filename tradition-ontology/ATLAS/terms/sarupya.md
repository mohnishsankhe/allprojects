# sārūpya

`trm:sarupya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सारूप्य
**Literal:** likeness of form

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Form of liberation: bearing a form like the Lord's.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Liberation as having a form like the Lord's.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Likeness of form with the Lord — one of the four kinds of liberation.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Having Śiva's form; fruit of yoga.
