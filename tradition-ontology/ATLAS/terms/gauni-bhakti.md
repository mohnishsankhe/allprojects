# gauṇī bhakti

`trm:gauni-bhakti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Secondary devotion: threefold by the guṇas or by the seeker's motive (the distressed etc.), each earlier better than the later (NBS 56–57); in the Śāṇḍilya Sūtra the practices that lead to supreme devotion.
