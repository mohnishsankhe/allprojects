# avijjā

`trm:avijja` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** ignorance

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Not knowing suffering, its origin, its cessation and the way (SN 12.2); the first link of dependent origination, the last fetter and one of the effluents; it has the effluents as its origin (MN 9).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Not knowing suffering, its origin, its cessation and the way; the first link.

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: avidyā  — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [avidyā](avidya.md) — same word; in the suttas not knowing the four truths, not a cosmic power or the locus of an illusory world as in Advaita
