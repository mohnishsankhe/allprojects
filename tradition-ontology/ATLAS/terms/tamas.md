# tamas

`trm:tamas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तमस्
**Literal:** darkness, inertia

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The guṇa born of ignorance, deluding all beings, binding by negligence, sloth and sleep (BhG 14.8).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): (1) The guṇa that is heavy and enveloping (SK 13), of the nature of delusion (SK 12), predominant at the root of creation (SK 54). (2) The first of the five kinds of error (viparyaya), eightfold (SK 48): taking the eight non-selves (prakṛti, buddhi, ahaṃkāra, the five subtle elements) for the self (Kramadīpikā; Gauḍapāda).
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): the second doṣa of the mind (Ca Sū 1.57); the tāmasa mind has the part of delusion (Śā 4.36); sleep born of tamas is the root of sin (Sū 21.59).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: tamas is born of ignorance and deludes all embodied beings; it binds by heedlessness, idleness and sleep (14.8); covering knowledge it attaches to heedlessness (14.9); when it increases, absence of illumination, inactivity, heedlessness and delusion arise (14.13); dying in it one is born in deluded wombs (14.15); its fruit is ignorance (14.16); heedlessness, delusion and ignorance come from it (14.17); the tāmasa go downward (14.18). In 13.18 'tamas' is darkness (the knowable is beyond darkness).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The guṇa characterized as ignorance; those in it go to the animal state (MDh 12.26, 12.40).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Not a substance but merely the absence of light (VS 5.2.21 C).
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The guṇa of darkness: one who hates all people, always makes 'I' and holds untrue notions is tāmasa, and its removal by the guru is a sub-stage of the śaraṇa (SSM 13.17–19).
