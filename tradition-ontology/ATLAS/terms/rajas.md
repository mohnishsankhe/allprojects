# rajas

`trm:rajas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** रजस्
**Literal:** region; atmosphere; (later) passion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): A region of space, especially the atmosphere; 'there was no rajas' before creation (RV 10.129.1); the six rajāṃsi propped apart (1.164.6).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The guṇa of passion, arising from thirst and attachment, binding by attachment to action (BhG 14.7); desire and anger are born of it (3.37).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The guṇa that is stimulating and mobile (SK 13), of the nature of pain (SK 12); predominant in the middle world (SK 54); suffering is a modification of rajas (Vācaspati).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The guṇa characterized as attraction and aversion; those in it go to the human state (MDh 12.26, 12.40).
