# smaraṇa

`trm:smarana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्मरण
**Literal:** remembering

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Remembering the Lord, the third form of devotion, and the supreme expiation (VP 2.6.39-40).
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Remembrance: the forgetting of the Lord is supreme anguish (NBS 19); remembrance is one of love's eleven forms (NBS 82).
- `lin:jagannatha`: Hari-smaraṇa: the Gīta Govinda is offered to those whose minds delight in remembering Hari (GG 1.3).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Remembrance of the Name, inward and constant, with the breath; repetition on beads without the mind is not sumiran.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Simran: mental repetition of the names given at initiation, to gather the attention at the eye centre.
