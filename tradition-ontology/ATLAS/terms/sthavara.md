# sthāvara

`trm:sthavara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Kannada
**Native script:** ಸ್ಥಾವರ
**Literal:** the standing, the fixed

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): The fixed and built — temple, image, installed liṅga — which in Basava's vacana 'shall perish', against the moving (jaṅgama) that endures.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): One-sensed souls embodied in earth, water, fire, air and plants (Digambara TS 2.13; the Śvetāmbara recension counts fire and air as mobile).
