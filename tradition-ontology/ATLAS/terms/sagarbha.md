# sagarbha / nirgarbha

`trm:sagarbha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सगर्भ / निर्गर्भ
**Literal:** with / without seed (garbha)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The two kinds of sahita retention in the GS: with the seed-syllables a, u, m and meditation on Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra, or without seed.
