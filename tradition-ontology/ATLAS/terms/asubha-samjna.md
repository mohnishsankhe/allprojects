**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अशुभसंज्ञा
**Literal:** notion of the unlovely

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Viewing the body as hair, flesh, blood, bone, bile, excrement and so on, cultivated to abandon lustful passion (NBh 4.2.3).
