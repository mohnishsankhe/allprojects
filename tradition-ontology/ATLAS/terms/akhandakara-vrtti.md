# akhaṇḍākāra-vṛtti

`trm:akhandakara-vrtti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** mode in the form of the undivided

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The mental mode in the form of the partless Brahman arising from the mahāvākya; it destroys ignorance and then subsides, like a clearing-nut in water.
