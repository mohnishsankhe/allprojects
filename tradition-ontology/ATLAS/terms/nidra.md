# nidrā

`trm:nidra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निद्रा
**Literal:** sleep

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Sleep: the activity whose support is the cognition of absence (1.10); a cognition, since it is recollected on waking, and to be stilled in samādhi (YBh 1.10).
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): one of the three supports; one sleeps when the tired mind and senses withdraw from their objects; happiness, strength, knowledge and life depend on it (Ca Sū 21.35-36); seven kinds, of which night-born sleep is the 'nurse of beings' and tamas-born sleep the root of sin (Sū 21.58-59).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In Jainism an effect of perception-obscuring karma, in five grades from light sleep to somnambulism (TS 8.7).
