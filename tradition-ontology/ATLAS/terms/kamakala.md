**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md): The 'digit of desire': the white and red points, Śiva and Śakti, and the point of their union (the 'I'); kāma because it is lovely, kalā the two points of fire and moon; knowing this vidyā one becomes Mahātripurasundarī (KKV 6-8); the Goddess 'whose form is the kāmakalā' (LSN 73).
