# sabhāva

`trm:sabhava` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** own-being, intrinsic nature

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The particular nature by which a dhamma is what it is (e.g. hardness for the earth element); dhammas are defined as bearing their own intrinsic nature.
