# pitta

`trm:pitta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पित्त
**Literal:** bile (from √tap 'to heat', Su Sū 21.5)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The doṣa of heat and transformation: slightly unctuous, hot, sharp, liquid, sour, flowing and pungent (Ca Sū 1.60); there is no fire in the body apart from pitta (Su Sū 21.9); fivefold as pācaka, rañjaka, sādhaka, ālocaka and bhrājaka (Su Sū 21.10; AHS Sū 12.10-14).
- [Siddha medicine (cittā maruttuvam)](../lineages/siddha-medicine.md): Tamil aḻal or pittam: the fire humour, second of the three.
