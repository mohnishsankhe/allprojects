# caitya (Jain)

`trm:caitya-jain` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** चैत्य

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md): A shrine or image of the Jina (the Mūrtipūjaka reading of 'ceiya' in the canon).
- [Sthānakavāsī](../lineages/sthanakavasi.md): The Sthānakavāsīs read canonical 'ceiya' in many places as knowledge, a sage or a sacred tree rather than an image-shrine (a disputed reading).
