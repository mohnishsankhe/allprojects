# karmabhūmi

`trm:karmabhumi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कर्मभूमि
**Literal:** land of action

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Bhārata, where deeds are done and bear fruit and liberation can be won; the other regions are lands of enjoyment.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Regions where people live by work, where religious practice and liberation are possible and Tīrthaṅkaras arise (TS 3.37).
