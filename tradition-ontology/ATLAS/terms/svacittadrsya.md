# svacittadṛśya

`trm:svacittadrsya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** what is seen as one's own mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The Laṅkāvatāra's key term: all that appears is the perception of one's own mind; its stream is purified gradually and suddenly.
