**Language:** Sanskrit

**Literal:** the far-shining; sovereign (feminine)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The feminine principle from whom the Puruṣa was born and who was born from him (RV 10.90.5); in the Atharvaveda she was all this in the beginning and was milked by gods, men and others (AVŚ 8.10).
