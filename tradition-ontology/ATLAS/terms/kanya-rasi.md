## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The sixth sign (Virgo), 30° of the zodiac made of nine asterism-quarters; the waist of Kālapuruṣa (BJ 1.4); ruled by Mercury; dual; of the earth triplicity (later texts); even, female and gentle (BJ 1.11); Varāhamihira's alternative (Greek-derived) name: pāthona (BJ 1.8); dignities: Mercury exalted (15°); Venus in fall (BJ 1.13).
