# foxing (buddha-nature)

`trm:foxing` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 佛性
**Literal:** buddha-nature

## Definitions by tradition
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): The nature of awakening present in all beings without distinction of north and south, identical with the self-nature; seeing it is becoming buddha.
- [Sōtō Zen](../lineages/soto.md): For Dōgen, 'whole being is buddha-nature' and impermanence itself is buddha-nature; not a seed or potential.
- [Rinzai Zen](../lineages/rinzai.md): 'All beings are originally buddhas', like ice that is water (Hakuin).
