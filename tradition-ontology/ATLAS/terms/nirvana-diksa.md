# nirvāṇa-dīkṣā

`trm:nirvana-diksa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निर्वाणदीक्षा
**Literal:** initiation for liberation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The liberating initiation that purifies the six paths (adhvan) and burns past and future karma, leaving only the karma that sustains the present body, which is exhausted by experience.
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): The liberating initiation: the guru, as Śiva, purifies the six paths and burns the disciple's bonds; liberation follows at death, prārabdha continuing till then.
