# kārpaṇya

`trm:karpanya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कार्पण्य
**Literal:** wretchedness, faint-heartedness, meanness of spirit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The fault (kārpaṇya-doṣa) by which Arjuna says his very nature is struck when he asks for instruction (2.7).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The humble awareness of one's inability to adopt any other means; fifth limb of prapatti.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The fault of pity or faint-heartedness that strikes Arjuna's nature (BhG 2.7).
