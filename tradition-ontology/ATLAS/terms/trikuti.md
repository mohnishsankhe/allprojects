# trikuṭī

`trm:trikuti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): The second inner region ('three prominences'), seat of Brahm/the universal mind and source of the Veda, sounding like thunder or drum; the causal region.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): In Kabīr and the Nāth-influenced Sants, the meeting place between the brows (trikuṭī) where Gaṅgā, Yamunā and Sarasvatī meet.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The 'triple peak' between the brows where the three channels meet (Hindi Nāth and Sant usage).
