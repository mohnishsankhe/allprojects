## Definitions by tradition
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): An injunction that, where something is obtained both in the stated case and elsewhere, serves to exclude the others (e.g. 'five five-nailed animals may be eaten'); explicit (śrautī) or implied (lākṣaṇikī).
