# puruṣa

`trm:purusa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पुरुष
**Literal:** person, man; the conscious self

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The conscious principle: neither producer nor product (SK 3); the opposite of the manifest and the unmanifest in being without guṇas, discriminating, subject, individual and conscious (SK 11); witness, isolated, neutral, seer and non-agent (SK 19); many (SK 18). Its existence is proved because aggregates are for another, etc. (SK 17). It is not truly bound or released (SK 62).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The cosmic Person, thousand-headed, who is all this, of whom one quarter is all beings and three quarters the immortal in heaven, and from whose sacrifice the world, Vedas and varṇas came (RV 10.90); the person in whom the gods dwell 'as cows in a stall' (AVŚ 11.8.32).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The seer, pure consciousness, unchanging, not self-luminous objects' owner but always knowing the mind's activities (4.18); utterly unmixed with sattva (3.35); many (2.22).
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): the conjunction of mind, self and body, subject-matter of Āyurveda (Ca Sū 1.47); the six dhātus (five elements and consciousness), consciousness alone, or the aggregate of twenty-four (Śā 1.16-17); 'the measure of the world' (Śā 5.3); in Suśruta the union of the five elements and the embodied one (Su Sū 1.22).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 8: the adhidaivata is puruṣa, 'person' (8.4; the verse does not say which); continual contemplation (8.8) and remembrance at departure (8.10) lead to 'the supreme divine Person' (paramaṃ puruṣaṃ divyam); that supreme Person, in whom beings abide and by whom all is pervaded, is gained by undivided devotion (8.22).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The person: the self-born person at creation (BAU 1.4.1; AU 1.1.3); the inner person in the eye or heart; in KU 1.3.11 the highest principle beyond the unmanifest; in MuU 2.1.2 the divine formless person; in ŚU 3.8-15 the great person of the Puruṣa Sūkta.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The beginningless principle that experiences pleasure and pain, seated in prakṛti and bound by attachment to the guṇas (BhG 13.19–21); the perishable and imperishable puruṣas are surpassed by the highest (15.16–18); in the Mokṣadharma the twenty-fifth principle, many according to the Sāṃkhya-Yoga thinkers, one in source according to the Nārāyaṇīya's close (12.338).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The cosmic Person with sixteen parts, source of the avatāras (BhP 1.3.1); spirit as one of the four forms of Viṣṇu (VP 1.2).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The Person with whom the sacrifice is identical (ŚB 1.3.2.1); Puruṣa Nārāyaṇa who surpassed all beings by the human sacrifice (ŚB 13.6.1.1).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The supreme Person, ruler of all, subtler than the subtle, called by many names (MDh 12.122–123).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The person, beginningless, beyond prakṛti, not touched by its qualities, like the sun reflected in water.

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [ātman](atman.md) — Both name the conscious self; Sāṃkhya's puruṣa is plural, inactive and without properties, whereas Advaita's ātman is one (with Brahman) and Nyāya's ātman bears qualities.
