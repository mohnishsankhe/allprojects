## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Souls whose intrinsic nature fits them for liberation: gods, sages, ancestors, rulers and the best humans (the 'sāttvika' souls).
