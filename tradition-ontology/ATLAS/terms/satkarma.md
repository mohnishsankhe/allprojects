# ṣaṭkarma

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** षट्कर्म
**Literal:** the six acts

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The six cleansing acts — dhauti, basti, neti, trāṭaka, nauli, kapālabhāti (HYP 2.22) — needed only by those with excess fat or phlegm (2.21); some teachers hold breath-control alone suffices (2.37); the GS gives them to all as the first means (1.12); later texts count eight.
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The six rites of worldly power — pacifying, subjugating, paralysing, dissension, driving away and killing — which the Mahāvidyās are said to accomplish for worshippers (Bṛhaddharma madhya 6.132-133). Recorded as a teaching only.
