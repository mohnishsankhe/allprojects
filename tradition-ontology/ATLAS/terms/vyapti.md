# vyāpti

`trm:vyapti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** व्याप्ति
**Literal:** pervasion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): The invariable concomitance of reason and property to be proved, grounded in identity or causality.
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): The rule of co-presence 'wherever there is smoke there is fire' (Tarkasaṅgraha); defined precisely after rejecting five provisional definitions (Tattvacintāmaṇi).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The relation between mark and marked on which inference rests (term prominent from Uddyotakara and Vācaspati on).
- [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md): (As reported) Cannot be known by perception, inference, testimony or comparison; hence inference fails.
