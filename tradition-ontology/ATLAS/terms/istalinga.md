# iṣṭaliṅga

`trm:istalinga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** इष्टलिङ्ग
**Literal:** the chosen / desired liṅga

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The gross liṅga given by the guru at initiation and worn outwardly on the body for life (SSM 6.49–50), worshipped on the palm (6.54–55) without invocation or dismissal because Śiva abides in it from the moment of initiation (10.44–47).

## Forms in other languages
- Kannada: iṣṭaliṅga ಇಷ್ಟಲಿಂಗ — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [iṣṭa-devatā](ista-devata.md) — both name the devotee's own chosen form of the divine; the iṣṭaliṅga is a physical sacrament worn on the body, which the iṣṭa-devatā need not be
