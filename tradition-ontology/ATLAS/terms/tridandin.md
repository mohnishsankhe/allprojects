# tridaṇḍin

`trm:tridandin` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** त्रिदण्डिन्
**Literal:** bearer of the triple staff

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): One who has laid down the rods of speech, mind and body (MDh 12.10); outwardly, a renouncer carrying three staves.
- [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md): Renouncer with the triple staff (Vaiṣṇava mark); inwardly, restraint of speech, body and mind.
