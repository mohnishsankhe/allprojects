# vīṭu

`trm:vitu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tamil

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): Release, liberation: the Lord is 'the one who owns release' (TVM 1.2.1); Nammāḻvār, his longing ended, 'attained release' (TVM 10.10.11).
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Release, liberation — the fourth aim, not a separate book in the Kuṟaḷ but implied in its chapters on renunciation and truth.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Release, 'the house': Kaṭuveḷi's pun — burn the forest of the senses and you will see the house (14); to know the root is the house of liberation (13).
