# 'pho ba

`trm:phowa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** འཕོ་བ
**Literal:** transference

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): Transference of consciousness at death to a pure land or to the dharmakāya; five kinds are distinguished (dharmakāya, sambhogakāya, nirmāṇakāya, ordinary, and the transference for the dead by compassion). Restricted: never applied before the time.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): Transference of consciousness at death through the upper aperture to a pure land or to the dharmakāya; 'buddhahood without meditation'; many kinds; to be done only when the time has come — untimely transference 'kills the deities'. Restricted practice.
- [Chöd (gcod), 'Cutting'](../lineages/chod.md): In Chöd, the transference of consciousness into the sky as the wrathful ḍākinī at the start of the body-offering.

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: saṃkrānti  — partial — Tengyur titles render 'pho ba with saṃkrānti; utkrānti is also used
- Tibetan: 'pho ba འཕོ་བ — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [utkrānti](utkranti.md) — Hindu yogic departure of the soul at death through the crown; the Buddhist practice transfers consciousness (not a soul) to a pure land
