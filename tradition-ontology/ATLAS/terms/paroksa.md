# parokṣa

`trm:paroksa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** परोक्ष
**Literal:** beyond sight, cryptic

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The indirect, hidden name or form preferred by the gods — 'the gods love the cryptic and dislike the obvious' (ŚB 10.5.2.20; 14.6.11.2; AB 3.43).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Knowledge through senses, mind or words — sensory and scriptural knowledge (TS 1.11); later memory, recognition, reasoning, inference and testimony.
