# attā

`trm:atta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** self; oneself

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): In ordinary usage the reflexive 'oneself' (Dhp 160); as a doctrine, a self that would be permanent, controllable and 'mine' — the suttas examine every candidate (the aggregates, feeling, consciousness, the world) and find none (MN 22; DN 15; MN 148).

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: ātman  — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- contested: [ātman](atman.md) — the same word; what the Upaniṣadic traditions affirm as ātman the suttas examine and do not find; Theravāda rejects the identification
