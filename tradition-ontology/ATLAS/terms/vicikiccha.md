# vicikicchā

`trm:vicikiccha` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** doubt

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The fifth of the five hindrances (MN 10:36).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The fifth hindrance and second fetter, fed by things that are the basis for doubt and starved by wise attention to wholesome and unwholesome states (SN 46.51).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Sceptical doubt about the Buddha, Dhamma, Saṅgha, training and conditionality; a hindrance and fetter abandoned at stream-entry.
