# ātma-nivedana

`trm:atmanivedana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आत्मनिवेदन
**Literal:** offering of oneself

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Complete self-surrender to the Lord, the ninth form of devotion.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): Giving one's life over to the Lord who owns release (TVM 1.2.1).
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Having offered self, world and Veda to the Lord, the devotee has no anxiety at worldly loss (NBS 61); self-offering is one of love's eleven forms (NBS 82).
