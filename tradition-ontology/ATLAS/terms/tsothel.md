# btso thal

**Language:** Tibetan

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sowa Rigpa (gso ba rig pa), the Tibetan science of healing](../lineages/sowa-rigpa.md): Mercury detoxified and processed into an ash used as an ingredient of the precious pills. Restricted: the tradition holds that improperly processed mercury is a poison and that the process belongs only to qualified masters; no procedure recorded.
