# pariṇāmanā

`trm:parinamana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** dedication (turning over) of merit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Turning one's own and others' merit toward full awakening for all beings; supreme when done without apprehending merit, dedicator or goal.
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Transfer of merit (huixiang), fifth of the five gates; of two aspects, going (to be born with all beings) and returning (to teach them) (Tanluan).
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): Ekō is Amida's transfer of his merit to beings, not the practitioner's; the nenbutsu is 'non-transferring' practice for the practitioner.
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Turning one's merit to the welfare and awakening of all beings — the tenth chapter of the Bodhicaryāvatāra (10.55–56).
