# cākāmal cātal

`trm:cakamal-catal` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tamil
**Native script:** சாகாமல் சாதல்
**Literal:** to die without dying

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): 'Dying without dying': the death of ego and the mortal body while living, by which death is conquered (Aḻukaṇṇi 9; Rāmatēvar 2; Kuṭampai 13).
