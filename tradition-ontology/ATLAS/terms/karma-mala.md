# kārma mala

`trm:karma-mala` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The impurity of action in an unconscious agent (ĪPK 3.2.5).
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): The soul's beginningless stream of good and bad deeds (dharma and adharma) that determines birth, lifespan and experience; distributed by the Lord.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Kaṉmam / iruviṉai: the two deeds, good and bad, both binding.

## Forms in other languages
- Tamil: kaṉmam / viṉai  — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)

_Notes: Same name as in the Śaiva Siddhānta, where the impurity is held to be a real substance._
