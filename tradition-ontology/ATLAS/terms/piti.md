# pīti

`trm:piti` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** rapture

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Rapture: so called because it refreshes; mark endearing, function refreshing body and mind or pervading, manifestation elation; fivefold: minor, momentary, showering, uplifting, pervading (Vism IV p. 143-144).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Step 5 'experiencing rapture' (MN 118:19); in the awakening-factor sequence the unworldly (nirāmisā) rapture that arises in one whose energy is aroused (mn118:33).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Rapture born of seclusion or concentration, factor of the first two jhānas (DN 2); the fourth factor of awakening; arises from gladness and leads to tranquillity (SN 12.23).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Rapture: delight in the object; fivefold (minor, momentary, showering, uplifting, pervading); a jhāna factor.
