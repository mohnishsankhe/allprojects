# kośa

`trm:kosa` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): In the Kārikā 3.11: the sheaths beginning with rasa, explained in the Taittirīya; their self, the supreme, the jīva, is shown like space.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Sheath, case: the golden sheath in which brahman is (MuU 2.2.9); the cosmic chest (ChU 3.15). The later Vedānta name 'pañca-kośa' for the five selves of TU 2 is not used in the TU itself.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tamil kōcam: the five sheaths among the ninety-six principles.
