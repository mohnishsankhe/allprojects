# jīta (established practice)

`trm:jita` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The fifth and last ground of monastic judgment: the practice established by accepted teachers, used when no higher authority (supernormal knowledge, scripture, instruction, tradition) is available; the Jītakalpa fixes atonements by it.
