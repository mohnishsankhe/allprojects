# adhidaivata

`trm:adhidaivata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अधिदैवत
**Literal:** what pertains to the deity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): With respect to the gods (the cosmos), paired with adhyātma (ChU 3.18; Kena 4.4).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 8.4: 'the adhidaivata is puruṣa' ('person'; the verse does not say which) — the answer to Arjuna's question about the adhidaiva (8.1).
