# aikaśāstrya

`trm:aikasastrya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** unity of the science

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The doctrine that the Pūrva Mīmāṃsā (on ritual) and the Uttara Mīmāṃsā (on Brahman), with the Saṅkarṣa-kāṇḍa, form one continuous science.
