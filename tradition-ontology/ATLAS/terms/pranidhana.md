# praṇidhāna

`trm:pranidhana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** vow, aspiration

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The bodhisattva's vows — Dharmākara's forty-eight, Bhaiṣajyaguru's twelve, Śrīmālā's ten, Samantabhadra's; the perfection of vow predominates on the eighth ground.
- [Lokottaravāda](../lineages/lokottaravada.md): The vow to become a Buddha, first made by Śākyamuni before a former Buddha Śākyamuni; the second of the four conducts.
