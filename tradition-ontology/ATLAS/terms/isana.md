# īśāna

`trm:isana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ईशान
**Literal:** ruler

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Lord; the Vrātya 'became Īśāna' (AVŚ 15.1.5); a name of Rudra.
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The Lord as 'Īśāna of all knowledges, lord of all beings', invoked in the fifth brahma-mantra that closes the Sūtra.
