# phassa

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** contact

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The meeting of sense faculty, object and consciousness, condition for feeling (MN 18; SN 12.2); one of the four nutriments (MN 9).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Contact: the coming together of object, sense faculty and consciousness; the first of the universal factors.
