# samādhisambojjhaṅga

`trm:samadhi-sambojjhanga` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** awakening factor of concentration

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Concentration awakening factor: aroused when, for one whose body is tranquil and who is happy, the mind is concentrated (MN 118:35).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The concentration of one tranquil and happy (MN 118). One of the thirty-seven qualities conducive to awakening (DN 16.3.50; MN 149): a member of the seven factors of awakening.
