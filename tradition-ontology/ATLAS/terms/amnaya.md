# āmnāya

`trm:amnaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आम्नाय
**Literal:** transmission, tradition

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): A 'transmission' of teaching; Paramaśiva taught five āmnāyas through his five faces (PKS 1.2); the four doors of the Goddess's palace are the four āmnāyas (PKS 3.10).
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): A Kaula transmission: five from Śiva's five faces — Eastern, Western, Southern, Northern and Upper (Kulārṇava 3.7); the Upper is supreme (3.15-19).
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): The Paścimāmnāya is the Kubjikā transmission, 'the western of all paths' (Kubjikāmata 2.21).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Its authority follows from its being 'the statement of that' (VS 1.1.3).
