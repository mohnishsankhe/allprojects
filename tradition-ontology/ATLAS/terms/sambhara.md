# saṃbhāra

`trm:sambhara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Literal:** collection, accumulation (of merit and wisdom)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The two collections: merit (puṇya), from which the form bodies arise, and wisdom (jñāna), from which the truth body arises (RĀ 3.12–13).
