## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The mutual need of the parts of a sentence, which makes them one sentence (MS 2.1.46).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): A word's inability to produce a connected cognition without another word (Tarkasaṅgraha).
