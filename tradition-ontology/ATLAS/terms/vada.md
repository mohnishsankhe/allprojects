# vāda

`trm:vada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वाद
**Literal:** debate

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): disputation with another based on the science, of two kinds, jalpa and vitaṇḍā (Ca Vi 8.28).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Debate in which both sides prove and refute with the means of knowledge and tarka, without contradicting tenets, using the five members (NS 1.2.1); undertaken with teachers, pupils and seekers of the good (NS 4.2.48).
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Debate aimed only at ascertaining the truth, as between teacher and disciple.
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Debate conducted to establish truth, governed by rules of proof and defeat.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Discussion: 'of those who debate (pravadatām) I am vāda' (10.32).
