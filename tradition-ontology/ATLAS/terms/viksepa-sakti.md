# vikṣepaśakti

`trm:viksepa-sakti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विक्षेपशक्ति
**Literal:** projecting power

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The projecting power of māyā that creates the gross and subtle world.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The projecting power of ignorance, which projects the world and the subtle and gross bodies on the self, as ignorance of the rope projects the snake.
