# mithyājñāna

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मिथ्याज्ञान
**Literal:** false knowledge

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The root of the series leading to pain (NS 1.1.2); its forms include 'there is no self', taking the non-self for the self, pain for pleasure, the impermanent for the permanent (NBh 1.1.2).
