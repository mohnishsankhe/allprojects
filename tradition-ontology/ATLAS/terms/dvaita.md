# dvaita

`trm:dvaita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** द्वैत
**Literal:** duality

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Duality: 'where there is duality, as it were, one sees another' (BAU 2.4.14, 4.5.15).
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The usual outside name for Madhva's Vedānta, stressing real difference; Madhva himself reads 'dvaita' in the Māṇḍūkya as what is 'known otherwise' (wrongly) and 'advaita' as the Lord who has no equal.
