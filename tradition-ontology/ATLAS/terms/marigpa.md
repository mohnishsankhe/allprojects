# ma rig pa

`trm:marigpa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Literal:** non-awareness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): Ignorance as non-recognition of the ground-appearances as one's own display; in three kinds: the ignorance of single identity (bdag nyid gcig pu), co-emergent ignorance (lhan cig skyes pa) and conceptualizing ignorance (kun tu brtags pa). From it dualistic grasping and saṃsāra unfold.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): In the Guhyagarbha, the conceptuality by which beings, of one nature with the buddhas, grasp 'I' and 'mine' and wander in the five classes.
- [Sowa Rigpa (gso ba rig pa), the Tibetan science of healing](../lineages/sowa-rigpa.md): The not-understanding of selflessness that is the distant cause of all disease.
