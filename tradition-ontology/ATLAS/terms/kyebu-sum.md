# skyes bu gsum

`trm:kyebu-sum` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** སྐྱེས་བུ་གསུམ
**Literal:** the three (kinds of) persons; the three scopes

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): Persons of lesser, middling and supreme capacity, who seek respectively the happiness of saṃsāra (higher rebirth), their own peace (liberation), and the end of all others' suffering (buddhahood).
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The organising principle of the lamrim: teachings 'common to' the small and middling scopes are practised as preliminaries to the great scope, not as ends in themselves.
