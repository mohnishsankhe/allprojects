# sammādiṭṭhi

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Knowledge of the four truths (SN 45.8); understanding the wholesome and its roots (MN 9); mundane and supramundane, and first of the factors (MN 117). One of the thirty-seven qualities conducive to awakening (DN 16.3.50; MN 149): a member of the noble eightfold path.
