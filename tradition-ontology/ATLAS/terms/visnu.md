# Viṣṇu

`trm:visnu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विष्णु
**Literal:** the pervader

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The one independent reality, full of infinite auspicious qualities and free of defects, the Brahman of the Upaniṣads and the primary meaning of all words.
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The god of the three strides, whose highest step the wise ever see (RV 1.22.20, 1.154).
