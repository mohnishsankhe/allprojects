# yukta

`trm:yukta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** युक्त
**Literal:** yoked, disciplined, joined

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: One should sit disciplined, intent on Kṛṣṇa (2.61); the undisciplined (ayukta) have no understanding or cultivation (2.66); the knowing one acts disciplined (3.26); one joined with understanding (buddhiyukta) casts off good and evil deeds (2.50).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Used as a defining title: the one who sees inaction in action is yukta (4.18); the yukta knower thinks 'I do nothing' (5.8); gives up fruit and attains peace (5.12); withstands the surge of desire and anger (5.23); is satisfied with knowledge and sees clod and gold alike (6.8); his well-restrained mind (citta) rests in the self (6.18); 'measured' in food, rest, sleep and effort (6.17); the one who worships the Lord with faith is 'most yoked' (yukta-tama) (6.47).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Yoked: those ever yoked who worship the Lord with love (10.10); Arjuna asks about the devotees ever yoked (12.1); those who fix the mind on him are 'the most yoked' (yuktatama) (12.2).
