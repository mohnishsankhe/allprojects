# śabda

`trm:sabda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शब्द
**Literal:** sound; word

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): (1) The subtle element of sound and the quality of space (SK 22, 28). (2) The accomplishment of knowledge arising from hearing words of instruction (SK 51). (3) In the Sāṃkhya Sūtra, verbal testimony = reliable instruction (SS 1.101); sound is not eternal and not sphoṭa (SS 5.57–58).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): (1) Verbal testimony: the instruction of a reliable person (NS 1.1.7), of seen or unseen objects (NS 1.1.8). (2) Sound, the object of hearing and a non-eternal quality of ākāśa (NS 1.1.14, 2.2.13).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Sound: a quality of ākāśa, its mark, produced by conjunction, disjunction and sound, non-eternal (VS 2.1.24–27, 2.2.36–37 Candrānanda); verbal knowledge is inference (VS 9.19).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Verbal testimony is a statement produced by true knowledge of the meaning that causes its utterance (Tattvacintāmaṇi); sound is of two kinds, inarticulate and phonemic (Tarkasaṅgraha).
- `lin:vyakarana`: The word, defined in the Mahābhāṣya as that by which, when uttered, the understanding of the thing arises.
