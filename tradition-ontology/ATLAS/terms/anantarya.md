# ānantarya

`trm:anantarya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): The five grave offences (wuni): excluded, with slander of the Dharma, in the eighteenth vow; yet the Contemplation Sūtra admits five-offence evil-doers to the lowest grade; Tanluan and Shandao reconcile the two.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The five deadly acts ripening immediately (in the next life) in hell: killing mother, father or an arhat, dividing the Saṅgha, wounding a Buddha.
