# anaiśvarya

`trm:anaisvarya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनैश्वर्य
**Literal:** powerlessness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The tāmasa opposite of power (SK 23), bringing obstruction (SK 45).
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The essence of bondage for Kauṇḍinya: the soul's lack of lordship, the obstruction of its powers; also listed by him as the fifth impurity.
