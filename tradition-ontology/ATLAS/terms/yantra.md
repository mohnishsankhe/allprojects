# yantra

`trm:yantra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** यन्त्र
**Literal:** instrument; device that holds or restrains

## Definitions by tradition
- [Tantra as a movement across the traditions (Tantraśāstra; the Mantramārga / Mantranaya current)](../lineages/tantra-movement.md): A diagram (drawn, engraved or built) in which a deity and its mantra are installed and worshipped; used across the Śākta, Śaiva, Vaiṣṇava and Jain tantric traditions beside or instead of the maṇḍala.
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The Śrīcakra: the Goddess's own form as nine interlocking triangles and enclosures.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Engraved diagrams such as the Siddhacakra and the Ṛṣimaṇḍala worshipped in Jain temples and homes.

## Forms in other languages
- Tibetan: 'khrul 'khor འཁྲུལ་འཁོར — partial — the Tibetan word renders yantra as 'machine' and names physical yoga exercises (trulkhor), not a worship diagram
