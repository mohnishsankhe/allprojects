# anumāna

`trm:anumana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनुमान
**Literal:** inference

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Faultless reasoning (upapatti) based on invariable concomitance; includes presumption and comparison.
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Threefold inference preceded by knowledge of the mark and the marked (SK 5); by inference from general correlation supersensible things (prakṛti, puruṣa) are known (SK 6).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Inference: an activity that chiefly ascertains the general through a relation present in like and absent from unlike cases (YBh 1.7); it reaches only the general (1.49, YBh 1.25).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Knowledge preceded by perception, of three kinds (NS 1.1.5); resting on perception and scripture it is anvīkṣā (NBh 1.1.1).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Knowledge from a mark (laiṅgika): effect, cause, connected, co-inherent, opposed (VS 9.18); includes verbal knowledge.
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): The instrument of inferential cognition, i.e. consideration (parāmarśa); for oneself or for others.
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Cognition of an unperceived part from seeing another part whose relation is known; of two kinds, by a relation seen directly or by one seen in general (the Vṛttikāra in Śabara).
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): In Bāul usage: inference, hearsay and scripture — everything not verifiable in the living body — set aside in favour of bartamān.
