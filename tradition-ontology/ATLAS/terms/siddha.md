# siddha

`trm:siddha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सिद्ध
**Literal:** accomplished, perfected

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): A perfected one — master of yoga who has conquered time and roams the cosmos (HYP 1.9); the Nāths count nine Nāths and eighty-four siddhas.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): A perfected one: in Tamil, cittar — the yogin-poets, physicians and alchemists who have attained the powers (citti) and the deathless state, and who stand outside caste and temple ritual.
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The perfected masters of haṭha — Ādinātha, Matsyendra, Gorakṣa and the others — who by haṭha broke the staff of time (HYP 1.5–9).
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): One who has attained siddhi; the tantric adept of the lives and songs, often living in a lay trade or as a wandering yogin.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In Jainism a soul freed of all eight karmas, bodiless, abiding at the summit of the universe in infinite knowledge, perception, bliss and energy (TS 10.4–5).
- [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md): In the Dāsbodh: The accomplished one who abides in the Self, free of doubt, still acting rightly.
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): The perfected stage, resting in prema and the sahaja state.
