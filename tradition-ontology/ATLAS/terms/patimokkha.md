# pātimokkha

`trm:patimokkha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** monastic code

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The code of rules (227 for monks, 311 for nuns) recited on the uposatha; restraint by it is the training in higher virtue (AN 3.89).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Restraint by the Pātimokkha as the first step of the gradual training (DN 2; MN 107).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The monastic code; restraint by it is the first of the four purities of virtue.
