# kṣullaka / ailaka

`trm:ksullaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** क्षुल्लक / ऐलक
**Literal:** junior (one)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The advanced Digambara lay renunciants of the eleventh pratimā: the kṣullaka keeps two cloths and a bowl, the ailaka a single loincloth and eats from the hand; the next step is muni-initiation into nudity. Women may become kṣullikās.
