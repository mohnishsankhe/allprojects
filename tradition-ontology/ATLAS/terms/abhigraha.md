# abhigraha

`trm:abhigraha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अभिग्रह
**Literal:** taking hold; resolve

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): A secret resolve limiting the conditions under which a mendicant will accept alms — like Mahāvīra's vow, fulfilled by the shackled Candanā, to accept food only from a captive princess with shaven head, one foot inside the threshold, weeping.
