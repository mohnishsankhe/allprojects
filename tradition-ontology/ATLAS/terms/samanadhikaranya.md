# sāmānādhikaraṇya

`trm:samanadhikaranya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सामानाधिकरण्य
**Literal:** co-reference, having the same substrate

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The application of several words, with different grounds of application, to one and the same object ('the blue lotus'); identity statements like 'tat tvam asi' name one Brahman under different modes, keeping the primary sense of each word.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Co-reference: two words in the same case referring to one thing, the grammatical relation of 'tat' and 'tvam'.
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Occurring in the same locus; pervasion is a kind of co-location of probans and probandum.
