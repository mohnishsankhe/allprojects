# anusaya

`trm:anusaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** underlying tendency

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Latent tendencies — to sensual lust, aversion, views, doubt, conceit, lust for becoming and ignorance (MN 18); lust underlies pleasant feeling, aversion painful, ignorance neutral (MN 44; MN 148).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Seven latent tendencies lying dormant in the continuum until conditions arouse them.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Latent defilement, the root of existence: six (seven, ten), ninety-eight by realm and mode of abandonment; subtle, adhering, growing and binding; for the Vaibhāṣika identical with the defilement.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): The defilement asleep, i.e. a seed-capacity in the series; awake it is an envelopment.
