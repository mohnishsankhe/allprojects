# viśeṣaṇa

`trm:visesana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विशेषण
**Literal:** qualifier, attribute

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): That which qualifies and distinguishes a substrate (viśeṣya); the soul is a qualifier of Brahman and so an 'aṃśa' of it.
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): A property qualifying a thing; the qualifier–qualified relation (viśeṣaṇatā) is the contact by which absence is perceived.
