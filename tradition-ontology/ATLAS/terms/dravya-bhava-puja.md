# dravya-pūjā / bhāva-pūjā

`trm:dravya-bhava-puja` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** द्रव्यपूजा / भावपूजा
**Literal:** material worship / inner worship

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md): Material worship with substances (water, sandal, flowers, incense, lamp, rice, sweets, fruit — the eightfold pūjā), proper to householders, and inner worship by praise and meditation, proper to all; the first is a means to the second.
