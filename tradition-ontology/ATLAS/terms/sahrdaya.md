## Definitions by tradition
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): 'One with heart': the sensitive, cultivated spectator or reader whose mind, polished by repeated study of poetry, can become one with the work and relish rasa (NŚ 6.32–33; DhĀ 1.1).
