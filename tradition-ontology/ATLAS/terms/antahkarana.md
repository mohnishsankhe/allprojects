# antaḥkaraṇa

`trm:antahkarana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अन्तःकरण
**Literal:** internal instrument

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Buddhi, ahaṃkāra and manas, the threefold internal instrument operating in all three times; the door-keeper, of which the ten external instruments are the doors (SK 33, 35).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The inner organ, one in substance and fourfold in function — mind (manas, doubting), intellect (buddhi, deciding), memory-store (citta) and ego (ahaṅkāra); the adjunct through which consciousness becomes the knower.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tamil antakkaraṇam: the four inner faculties (maṉam, putti, cittam, akaṅkāram) among the ninety-six principles.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): Mind and the rest, which one contemplates as absent in oneself (VBT 94).
