# jyotirliṅga

`trm:jyotirlinga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ज्योतिर्लिङ्ग
**Literal:** liṅga of light

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md): In the SSM, the liṅga of light that always shines in the base, the heart and between the brows of all beings, called brahman in the Āgamas, contemplated as the inner wearing of the liṅga (6.35; 6.39–41).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The twelve self-manifest liṅgas of Śiva's radiance in sacred sites, told in the Śiva Purāṇa's Koṭirudra Saṃhitā.
