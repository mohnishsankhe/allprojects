# bartamān (vartamāna)

`trm:bartaman` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Bengali

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): 'The present': what is here and now, verifiable in the living body; the Bāuls call themselves bartamān-panthī, followers of the present.

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: vartamāna  — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [pratyakṣa](pratyaksa.md) — Like perception, bartamān is direct knowledge, but it is specifically the body's own experience and is set against scripture as such.
