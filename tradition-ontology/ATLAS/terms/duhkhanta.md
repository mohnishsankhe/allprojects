# duḥkhānta

`trm:duhkhanta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दुःखान्त
**Literal:** end of suffering

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Liberation: twofold - the absolute cessation of all suffering, and the attainment of lordship consisting in the powers of knowing and acting; reached through the Lord's grace.
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Yāmuna reports it as the absolute cessation of pain understood as the destruction of the soul's special qualities (opponent's report).

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [mokṣa](moksa.md) — the Pāśupata name for liberation; it adds the Lord's powers to cessation
- contested: [apavarga](apavarga.md) — Nyāya's apavarga is cessation only; the Pāśupatas insist theirs is more (SDS 6/3)
