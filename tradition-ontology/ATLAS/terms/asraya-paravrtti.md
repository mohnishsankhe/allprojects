# āśraya-parāvṛtti

`trm:asraya-paravrtti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** transformation of the basis

## Definitions by tradition
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): Awakening as the revelation of the stainless element when the stains are removed.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): In the Saṃdhinirmocana the dharmakāya is the transformation of the basis.
