# āśraya-parāvṛtti

`trm:asraya-paravrtti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आश्रयपरावृत्ति
**Literal:** transformation (revolution) of the basis

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The turning of the basis — the store-consciousness, or the dependent nature — from its defiled to its purified state through the abandonment of the twofold badness; begun at the first ground and completed at buddhahood, where the consciousnesses become the four wisdoms.
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): Awakening as the revelation of the stainless element when the stains are removed.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): In the Saṃdhinirmocana the dharmakāya is the transformation of the basis.
