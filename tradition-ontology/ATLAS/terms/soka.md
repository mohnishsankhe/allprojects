**Language:** Pali

**Literal:** grief, sorrow

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): DN 22:18: sorrowing, grieving, inner sorrow in one struck by some misfortune or painful state.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Arjuna, overwhelmed by grief, casts aside his bow (1.47) and sees nothing that would dispel his grief (2.8); Kṛṣṇa: the wise do not grieve (2.11), and since the embodied one is imperishable and death certain for the born, Arjuna should not grieve (2.25–30).
