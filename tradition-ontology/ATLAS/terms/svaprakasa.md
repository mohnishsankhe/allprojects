# svaprakāśa

`trm:svaprakasa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्वप्रकाश
**Literal:** self-luminous

## Definitions by tradition
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): Cognition (saṃvid) manifests itself without another cognition; the self and the object are manifested by it.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Self-luminous: not requiring another cognition to be revealed; defined by Citsukha as being fit to be called immediate while not being an object of cognition.
