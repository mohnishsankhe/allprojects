# kratu

`trm:kratu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** क्रतु
**Literal:** will; resolve; mental power

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Effective will and resolve; 'may auspicious intentions come from all sides' (RV 1.89.1); the gods go toward one kratu (6.9.5).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The purpose or resolve that constitutes a person: as his resolve at departure, so he becomes in the next world (ŚB 10.6.3.1).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Resolve, will: 'a person is made of resolve; as his resolve here, so he becomes on departing' (ChU 3.14.1; cf. BAU 4.4.5); 'O resolve (mind), remember' (Īśa 17).
