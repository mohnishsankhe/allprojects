# pañcarātra

`trm:pancaratra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पञ्चरात्र
**Literal:** the 'five nights' (system)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): The system of Nārāyaṇa worship called Pañcarātra in the Nārāyaṇīya, 'this great upaniṣad made by Sāṃkhya-yoga' (12.326.100), whose whole knower is the Lord himself (12.337.63); one of the five systems of knowledge (12.337.59).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The scripture and tradition of Bhagavān; the Pādma derives the name from the five other śāstras becoming 'night' before it; other explanations link it to Nārāyaṇa's five-day rite (ŚB 13.6.1).
