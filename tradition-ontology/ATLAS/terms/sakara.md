# sākāra

`trm:sakara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** with images

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The position that cognition bears images (ākāra) of its objects; in late Indian debate, that these images are real (satyākāra, Jñānaśrīmitra) or false (alīkākāra, Ratnākaraśānti).
