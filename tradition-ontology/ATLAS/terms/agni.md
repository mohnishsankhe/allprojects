# agni

`trm:agni` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अग्नि
**Literal:** fire

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): the digestive-metabolic fire; no fire in the body exists apart from pitta (Su Sū 21.9); life, complexion, strength, health, ojas, tejas and the prāṇas depend on it — extinguished one dies, balanced one lives long, disturbed one falls ill; 'agni is the root' (Ca Ci 15.3-4); its states are balanced, irregular (vāta), sharp (pitta) and weak (kapha) (AHS Sū 1.8); later counted as thirteen: the belly-fire, the five element-fires and the seven tissue-fires (Ca Ci 15.13-15).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Fire and the god of fire: household priest and messenger between men and gods, carrier of oblations, present in all fires, guardian of ṛta (RV 1.1).
