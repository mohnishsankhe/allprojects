**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शब्द
**Literal:** word; verbal testimony

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): Cognition of an unperceived object from words (śāstra); the Vedic word is independently valid; a human word only when the speaker is reliable and the matter within reach of other means.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Verbal testimony: the Veda, authorless and reproduced by Īśvara at each creation; the only means of knowing Brahman.
