**Literal:** seedless initiation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Initiation in which the post-initiatory observances are purified away for those unable to keep them (children, the foolish, women, the old, the sick), their liberation coming through devotion; contrasted with sabīja ('with seed') initiation for the capable.
