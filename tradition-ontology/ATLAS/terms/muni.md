# muni

`trm:muni` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मुनि
**Literal:** silent one; ecstatic

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The silent sage of the Keśin hymn, wind-girdled and clad in soiled brown, ecstatic with munihood (RV 10.136).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The sage of steady mind is not agitated in sorrows and is free of passion, fear and anger (2.56); the seeing sage is awake in what is night for all beings (2.69).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The sage at peace, not born, not ageing, not dying, beyond conceiving (MN 140); the sage of the Aṭṭhakavagga who holds no views (Snp 4).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Silent sage: 'having done with learning and childhood he becomes a muni' (BAU 3.5.1); 'knowing it one becomes a muni' (BAU 4.4.22).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 5–6: the sage yoked in yoga reaches Brahman soon (5.6); the sage intent on liberation, restrained in senses, mind and intellect, is ever free (5.28); the sage wishing to ascend to yoga has action as the means (6.3).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: of the perfected the Lord is the sage (muni) Kapila (10.26), and of the munis Vyāsa (10.37); the devotee dear to him is maunin, 'silent' (12.19).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In Jainism the homeless ascetic under the great vows.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The sage of steady mind whom sorrow does not agitate (BhG 2.56); 'among sages I am Vyāsa' (10.37).
