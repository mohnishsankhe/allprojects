# vīriyasambojjhaṅga

`trm:viriya-sambojjhanga` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** awakening factor of energy

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Energy awakening factor: aroused when, in one investigating thus, energy is aroused and unflagging (MN 118:32).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Unflagging energy; nourished by the elements of arousal, exertion and endeavour (SN 46.51). One of the thirty-seven qualities conducive to awakening (DN 16.3.50; MN 149): a member of the seven factors of awakening.
