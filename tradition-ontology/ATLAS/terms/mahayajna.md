## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The five great sacrifices to beings, men, ancestors, gods and Brahman (ŚB 11.5.6.1; TA 2.10).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The householder's daily five sacrifices, expiating the 'five slaughter-places' (MDh 3.68–70).
