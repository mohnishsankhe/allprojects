# sahaja mānuṣa (sahaj mānuṣ)

`trm:sahaja-manusa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Bengali

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): The innate Man, the divine as present in the human person (Kṛṣṇa in man, Rādhā in woman); 'the truth of man is above all'.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): The natural Man within, to be worshipped in the human (mānuṣ bhajan).
- [Kartābhajā](../lineages/kartabhaja.md): The Master hidden in the human form, revealed by the Kartā.
