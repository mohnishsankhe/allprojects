# paramārthasat

`trm:paramarthasat` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** existing in the ultimate sense

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): That which exists ultimately: what does not lose its identity when broken or analysed into parts (like matter or feeling), as opposed to a pot or water.
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): What is capable of causal efficacy, i.e. the particular.
