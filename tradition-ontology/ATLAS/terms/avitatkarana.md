# avitatkaraṇa

`trm:avitatkarana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अवितत्करण
**Literal:** doing what is senseless

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Acting as one who cannot tell pure from impure, fit from unfit (e.g. handling sticks and clods), without breaking the restraints.
