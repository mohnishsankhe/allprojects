**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the teaching that all exists

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The doctrine that everything (the dharmas of past, present and future) exists; argued from scripture, from consciousness arising from two, from consciousness needing an existent object, and from the result of past karma.
