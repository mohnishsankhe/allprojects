# shinjin

`trm:shinjin` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Japanese
**Native script:** 信心
**Literal:** faith-mind; entrusting heart

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): True entrusting: the mind free of doubt about the primal vow, given (transferred) by Amida; the true cause of birth, buddha-nature itself, arising in one thought-moment and settling birth.

## Forms in other languages
- Chinese: xinxin 信心 — partial — in Chinese texts ordinary 'faith'; in Shinran a technical term

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- contested: [śraddhā](sraddha.md) — Shinshū insists shinjin is not the practitioner's faith but the Buddha's mind given; general Buddhist śraddhā is a faculty of the practitioner
