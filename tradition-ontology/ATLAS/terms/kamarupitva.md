# kāmarūpitva

`trm:kamarupitva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कामरूपित्व
**Literal:** taking forms at will

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The power to govern at will endless forms and instruments, independent of karma.
- [Kāpālika (Somasiddhānta)](../lineages/kapalika.md): In the Mattavilāsa the Kāpālika claims that austerity gives the power to take any form (satire).
