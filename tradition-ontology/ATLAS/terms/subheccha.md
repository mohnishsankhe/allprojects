## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): First stage of knowledge: the wish, preceded by dispassion, to leave foolishness and turn to scripture and the good.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): First stage of knowledge: the wish for liberation.
