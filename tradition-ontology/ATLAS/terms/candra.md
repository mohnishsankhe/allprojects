## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The moon in the head / at the root of the palate, made of nectar, raining downward (GŚ 57–58; SS 2.6–9); the left channel; in the Amṛtasiddhi identified with bindu.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The moon at the root of the palate, made of nectar, raining downward (GŚ 57–58); the left side and left nostril (AmŚ 71).
