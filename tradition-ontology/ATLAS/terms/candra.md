# candra / śaśin / soma

`trm:candra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** चन्द्र
**Literal:** moon

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The moon in the head / at the root of the palate, made of nectar, raining downward (GŚ 57–58; SS 2.6–9); the left channel; in the Amṛtasiddhi identified with bindu.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The moon at the root of the palate, made of nectar, raining downward (GŚ 57–58); the left side and left nostril (AmŚ 71).
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The Moon among the grahas: the mind (manas) of Kālapuruṣa and a king (BJ 2.1); female, vaiśya, sattva, of vāta–kapha constitution (BJ 2.6–8); rules Cancer, exalted in Taurus 3°; period 10 years; Kṛṣṇa is born from him (BPHS 2.5); the natal Moon is the base for transits (BS 103).
