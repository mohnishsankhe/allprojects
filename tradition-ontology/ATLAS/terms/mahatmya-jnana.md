**Literal:** knowledge of (God's) greatness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Understanding of Viṣṇu's supremacy and perfection, which must precede and ground bhakti.
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Knowledge of the Lord's greatness, which the gopīs never lacked; love without it is a paramour's love (NBS 22–23).
