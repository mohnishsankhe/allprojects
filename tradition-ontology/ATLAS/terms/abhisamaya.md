# abhisamaya

`trm:abhisamaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** penetration

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Penetration of the truths; the Theravāda holds that each path penetrates all four truths at once.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Clear realization of the truths: gradual (anupūrva), in sixteen moments.
- [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md): Realization of the four truths in a single moment (ekābhisamaya) — attributed to the Mahāsāṃghika and Vibhajyavādin side in the doxographies (low confidence).
