# anukampā (Jain)

`trm:anukampa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनुकम्पा
**Literal:** trembling-with (compassion)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Compassion, one of the marks of right faith; the elephant's compassion for the hare won Megha human birth.
- [Śvetāmbara Terāpanth](../lineages/terapanthi.md): Distinguished into worldly (laukika) compassion that preserves bodily life and spiritual (lokottara) compassion that leads beings to restraint; only the second is dharma.
