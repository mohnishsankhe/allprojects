## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): a seizing being — of eight classes: gods, their enemies, gandharvas, yakṣas, ancestors, serpents, rākṣasas, piśācas — whose seizure shows as superhuman knowledge and action (Su Utt 60.4-7); the subject of bhūtavidyā. Summary only.
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): A 'seizer': the nine planets — Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rāhu, Ketu — which 'moving, grasp the asterisms' (BPHS 3.4); forms of Janārdana giving the fruits of karma (BPHS 2.3).
