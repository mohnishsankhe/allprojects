# vīraśaiva

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वीरशैव
**Literal:** 'heroic' Śaiva; by the tradition's derivation, one who delights (ram) in the knowledge (vī) of Śiva–jīva oneness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Those who delight in the knowledge that awakens the oneness of Śiva and the jīva (SSM 5.15–16); the name of the tradition as a whole and the preferred self-designation of its Pañcācārya strand.
