# iruviṉaiyoppu

`trm:iruvinaiyoppu` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tamil
**Literal:** balance of the two deeds

## Definitions by tradition
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): The 'equality of the two deeds': the state in which the soul's good and bad karma balance, so that it turns with equal indifference from both, a precondition, with the ripening of mala, of the descent of grace.

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: karmasāmya  — same-under-standpoint — the Sanskrit Āgamic term, with a debated sense

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [karmasāmya](karmasamya.md) — Tamil usage stresses the soul's equanimity toward both deeds; the Sanskrit term the timing of śaktipāta
