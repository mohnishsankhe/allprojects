# kainkarya (Tamil kaiṅkaryam)

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** servanthood, service

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Service to the Lord (and his devotees) that expresses the soul's nature as his śeṣa; the goal both here and in Vaikuṇṭha.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): Service to the Lord 'at all times without break' (TVM 3.3.1) — the true meaning of the drum the girls ask for (TP 29).
