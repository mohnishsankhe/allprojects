# yoni-mudrā

`trm:yoni-mudra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** योनिमुद्रा
**Literal:** the womb-seal

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): In the GS, closing the senses, joining prāṇa and apāna and raising the goddess through the centres to union with Śiva; in the ŚS the contraction of the 'yoni' between anus and genitals with meditation on the flame in the brahma-yoni; also the name of a hand-gesture in Śākta worship.
