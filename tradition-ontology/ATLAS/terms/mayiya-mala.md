# māyīya mala

`trm:mayiya-mala` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The impurity of difference: the appearance of the knowable as separate (ĪPK 3.2.5).
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): The bond of māyā: the body, instruments, worlds and objects given to the soul from māyā, which both bind and help it by awakening its powers partly.

_Notes: Same name as in the Śaiva Siddhānta, where the impurity is held to be a real substance._
