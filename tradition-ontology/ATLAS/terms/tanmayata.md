# tanmayatā

`trm:tanmayata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तन्मयता
**Literal:** being made of That

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Absorption in or identity with the object of constant attention; reached by any strong emotion directed at Hari.
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Being full of him: the one-pointed devotees are 'tanmaya' (NBS 70); absorption is one of the eleven attachments (NBS 82); having love one sees, hears, speaks and thinks only of him (NBS 55).
