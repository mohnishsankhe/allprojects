# dvādaśānta

`trm:dvadasanta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** द्वादशान्त
**Literal:** the end of twelve [finger-breadths]

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): 'The end of the twelve' above the head, where the haṃsa dissolves and a throbbing garland of jewels is seen (KJN 13.6; 17.18).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The point twelve finger-breadths beyond (above or outside) the body where the breath ends and the mind is to be cast (VBT 28, 50-51).
