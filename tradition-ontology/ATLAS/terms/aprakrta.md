# aprākṛta

`trm:aprakrta` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** not of prakṛti

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): Non-material 'matter': the substance of the Lord's form and abode, beyond prakṛti.
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Transcendental, not of māyā: the Lord's body, name and abode and the devotee's perfected body.
