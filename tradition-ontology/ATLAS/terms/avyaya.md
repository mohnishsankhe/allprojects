# avyaya

`trm:avyaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अव्यय
**Literal:** imperishable, undecaying

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Said of that by which all this is pervaded, which no one can destroy (2.17), and of the self known as indestructible and unborn (2.21).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Arjuna asks to be shown the Lord's self, the imperishable (ātmānam avyayam) (11.4), and calls him the imperishable guardian of the eternal dharma (11.18); he has heard of the Lord's imperishable greatness (11.2).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: Imperishable: said of the embodied one (14.5), the supreme self (13.32), the aśvattha (15.1), the place reached by the undeluded (15.5) and the lord who sustains the three worlds (15.17).
