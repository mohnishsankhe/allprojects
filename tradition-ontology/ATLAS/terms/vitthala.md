# Viṭṭhala

`trm:vitthala` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Marathi
**Native script:** विठ्ठल
**Literal:** (popularly) 'standing on a brick' (viṭ + ṭhala); other derivations exist

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): The Lord of Paṇḍharpūr (also Viṭhobā, Pāṇḍuraṅga, Viṭhāī 'mother Viṭṭhala'), a form of Kṛṣṇa who stands on the brick Puṇḍalīka threw him, hands on hips; for some saints one with Śiva as well.
