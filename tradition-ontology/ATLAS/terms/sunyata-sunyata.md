# śūnyatā-śūnyatā

`trm:sunyata-sunyata` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the emptiness of emptiness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Emptiness is itself empty: if nothing were non-empty nothing could be empty (MMK 13.7); one of the sixteen emptinesses (MA 6), taught so that emptiness is not grasped as an entity.
