# samatha

`trm:samatha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** calm, serenity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Calm of mind, developed with insight (MN 43; MN 149); the path to the unconditioned (SN 43.12); calm preceding, following or yoked with insight (AN 4.170).
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Meditation on images without discrimination, paired with insight (Saṃdhinirmocana ch. 8).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Tranquillity of mind through concentration on one of the forty subjects.
