# samatha

`trm:samatha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Native script:** शमथ
**Literal:** calm, serenity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Calm of mind, developed with insight (MN 43; MN 149); the path to the unconditioned (SN 43.12); calm preceding, following or yoked with insight (AN 4.170).
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Meditation on images without discrimination, paired with insight (Saṃdhinirmocana ch. 8).
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): The concentration without which the superknowledges do not arise, achieved by abiding in its limbs and placing the mind on any one virtuous object.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The concentration, conjoined with the bliss of mental and physical pliancy, in which the mind stays on its object at will; achieved through the nine mental states by abandoning the five faults with the eight antidotes.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Tranquillity of mind through concentration on one of the forty subjects.
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Calm, to be sought first, through solitude and delight free of worldly attachment; insight together with calm destroys the afflictions (BCA 8.4).
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): One-pointed calm, developed by removing laxity and excitement, on which insight is based (Bhāvanākrama).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): zhi gnas: in Mahāmudrā, calm abiding first with support (object, breath, deity) and then without support, the waves of thought subsiding in their own place.
