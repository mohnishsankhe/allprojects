# bhakta

`trm:bhakta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भक्त
**Literal:** devotee

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The first sthala: one in whom devotion to Śiva has arisen; its dharma is taught in fifteen sub-sthalas (SSM 5.26–30).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): One devoted to the Lord; the four kinds of devotee (BhG 7.16) and the devotee dear to him (12.13–20); 'my devotee never perishes' (9.31).
