# bhakta

`trm:bhakta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भक्त
**Literal:** devotee

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The first sthala: one in whom devotion to Śiva has arisen; its dharma is taught in fifteen sub-sthalas (SSM 5.26–30).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): One devoted to the Lord; the four kinds of devotee (BhG 7.16) and the devotee dear to him (12.13–20); 'my devotee never perishes' (9.31).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Arjuna's being Kṛṣṇa's devotee and friend is the stated reason he receives the ancient yoga, the highest secret (4.3).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Devotee: one who does the Lord's work, holds him supreme, is unattached and without enmity toward any being comes to him (11.55); the devotee dear to him bears the marks of 12.13–19; those who with faith attend to this 'nectar of dharma' are exceedingly dear (12.20).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: The Lord's devotee who understands the field, knowledge and the knowable comes to the Lord's state (13.19).
