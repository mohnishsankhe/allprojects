# tathāgata

`trm:tathagata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Native script:** तथागत
**Literal:** the one thus gone / thus come

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The Buddha's term for himself and for the liberated one, who cannot be described as existing, not existing, both or neither after death, being deep like the ocean (MN 72; SN 22.86).
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): 'One who has not gone anywhere and has not come from anywhere' (Vajracchedikā 29); a synonym of true suchness; he knows the world's suchness through the perfection of wisdom.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Epithet of the Buddha; the Kathāvatthu rejects views that he did not really live in the human world or that Buddhas stand in all directions.
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The Buddha, who is not found in the fivefold search as identical with or different from the aggregates (MMK 22.1, 22.8); he is without own-nature, as the world is without own-nature (22.16); nothing is said of his existence after death in any of the four ways (25.17–18).
