# ojas

`trm:ojas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ओजस्
**Literal:** vigour; vital essence

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The supreme essence (tejas) of the tissues from rasa to semen, identical with strength (bala) (Su Sū 15.19; AHS Sū 11.37); seated in the heart, pure, slightly reddish-yellow, ghee-coloured, sweet, smelling of parched rice, the first thing produced in the body, whose loss is death (Ca Sū 17.74-75); the 'supreme' (para) ojas in the heart as distinct from the ojas carried by the ten great vessels (Ca Sū 30.7-9, as the commentators read it).
