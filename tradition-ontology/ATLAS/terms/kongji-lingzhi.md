# kongji lingzhi (empty and calm, numinous awareness)

`trm:kongji-lingzhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 空寂靈知
**Literal:** empty-quiescent numinous knowing

## Definitions by tradition
- [Heze school](../lineages/heze.md): The empty, quiescent mind whose numinous knowing is never obscured — one's true nature; 'the one word knowing is the gate of all wonders' (Zongmi on Heze).
- [Seon (Korean Chan)](../lineages/seon.md): One's original face and the Dharma-seal of the buddhas; the questioner himself (Jinul).

## Forms in other languages
- Japanese: kūjaku reichi  — exact
- Korean: gongjeok yeongji  — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [prakṛti-prabhāsvara-citta](prakrti-prabhasvara-citta.md) — Both name an unobscured knowing nature of mind; the Chan term comes through Zongmi's Heze and Huayan synthesis, not from the Indian luminous-mind passages.
