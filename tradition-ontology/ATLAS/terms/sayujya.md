# sāyujya ('yoga')

`trm:sayujya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सायुज्य
**Literal:** union

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Form of liberation in which the liberated enter the Lord's body and come out again, enjoying with him — without identity.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Liberation as union/oneness (ekatva) with the Lord; reached even by Śiśupāla through enmity (VP 4.15.2).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Union with the Goddess, granted to one who merely says 'Bhavāni tvam' (SL 22); the highest of the five liberations in Lakṣmīdhara.
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Direct conjunction with Rudra, attained by the ascetic dwelling in the cremation ground.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Union with the Lord — the highest of the four kinds of liberation; Śiva-sāyujya by the tripuṇḍra.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Inseparable union with Śiva; fruit of jñāna; the only final release.
- [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md): Union with the Lord, the only one of the four liberations that does not perish.
