# sahasrāra

`trm:sahasrara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सहस्रार
**Literal:** thousand-spoked

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The thousand-petalled lotus of the crown where the Goddess unites with her consort (SL 9; LSN 39); for the Samayins the Śrīcakra is the moon-disc there (Lakṣmīdhara).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The thousand-petalled lotus at or above the crown, seat of Śiva (Kailāsa, SS 5.151–157; Paramaśiva, ṢCN 40–49), where kuṇḍalinī dissolves and nectar flows.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The thousand-petalled lotus above the centres where Śakti unites with Śiva.
