# ṣaḍaṅga yoga

`trm:sadanga-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The six-limbed yoga of the Maitrī: breath-control, withdrawal, meditation, concentration, contemplative reasoning and absorption (MaiU 6.18).
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The six-branch yoga — withdrawal, meditation, breath control, retention, recollection, absorption — the supreme service in GST ch. 18 and the completion stage of the Kālacakra.
- [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md): In the Jonang, the Kālacakra six-branch vajra-yoga, the main practice for realizing the other-empty ultimate (restricted practice).
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The Śaiva six-limbed yoga, which includes reasoning (tarka) as a limb (Mālinīvijayottara, recalled); the Mṛgendra's yoga section has the scrutiny ūha in the corresponding place.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Six limbs — posture, breath-control, withdrawal, concentration, meditation, absorption — in the Gorakṣaśataka and Vivekamārtaṇḍa.
