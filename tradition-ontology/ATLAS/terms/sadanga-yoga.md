## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The six-limbed yoga of the Maitrī: breath-control, withdrawal, meditation, concentration, contemplative reasoning and absorption (MaiU 6.18).
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The six-branch yoga — withdrawal, meditation, breath control, retention, recollection, absorption — the supreme service in GST ch. 18 and the completion stage of the Kālacakra.
