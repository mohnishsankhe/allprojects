# āyus

`trm:ayus` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आयुस्
**Literal:** life; span of life

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The union of body, senses, mind and self; its synonyms are dhāri, jīvita, nityaga and anubandha (Ca Sū 1.42); the continuance of consciousness (Sū 30.22); fourfold as happy or unhappy, wholesome or unwholesome (Sū 1.41, 30.24); in Śārṅgadhara the union of body and breath (Pūrva 5.52).
