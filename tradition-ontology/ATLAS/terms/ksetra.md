## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The body and the whole of prakṛti with its modifications — elements, ego, intellect, the unmanifest, the organs, the objects, desire, aversion, pleasure, pain, the aggregate, consciousness and steadfastness (BhG 13.1, 13.5–6).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): A sacred territory such as Kāśī or Puruṣottama; seven cities are liberating (GP 2.38.5).
