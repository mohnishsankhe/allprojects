# niścaya-naya

`trm:niscaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निश्चयनय
**Literal:** standpoint of certainty

## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The standpoint of ultimate reality, which describes the self as it is in itself — pure, undivided, unbound; called 'real' (bhūtārtha) by Kundakunda. Right faith relies on it, while the conventional standpoint is needed to teach.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The ultimate standpoint, which views the soul in its own pure nature, unbound and unconnected with matter (Samayasāra 11, 14); said to be the real (bhūtārtha).
- [Adhyātma movement (Adhyātma-mata)](../lineages/adhyatma-jain.md): For the Adhyātma movement the standpoint of one's own pure self, known in self-experience; the refuge of right view, to be held together with vyavahāra (Samayasāra Nāṭaka).

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [pāramārthika](paramarthika.md) (level of truth) — both name the ultimate level from which the conventional description is transcended; Kundakunda's niścaya describes an individual pure soul, not a single Brahman
