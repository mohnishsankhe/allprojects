## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Knowledge, power, lordship, strength, virility and splendour, together constituting the supreme Brahman; knowledge is its very form, the other five its aspects; they are also Lakṣmī's body.
