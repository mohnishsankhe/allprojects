# anattā

`trm:anatta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** not-self

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): What is not a self nor belonging to a self: each aggregate is not-self, since it leads to affliction and cannot be commanded (SN 22.59); 'all phenomena (sabbe dhammā) are not-self' (AN 3.136; Dhp 279); seeing 'this is not mine, I am not this, this is not my self' (MN 22).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Not-self: no dhamma, conditioned or unconditioned, is a self; there is suffering but no sufferer.
