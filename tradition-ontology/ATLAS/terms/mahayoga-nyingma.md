# mahāyoga (Nyingma)

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** great yoga

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): The seventh vehicle, first of the inner tantras: emphasis on the generation stage and on the view that all phenomena are the great purity and equality of the deities; its tantras divide into a tantra section (the eighteen tantras, chief the Guhyagarbha) and a sādhana section (the Eight Herukas).
