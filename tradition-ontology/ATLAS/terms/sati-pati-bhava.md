# satī-pati-bhāva

`trm:sati-pati-bhava` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सतीपतिभाव
**Literal:** the relation of wife and husband

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The śaraṇa as wife (satī) and Śiva as husband (pati), the bliss of their union defining the śaraṇa-sthala (SSM 13.2; 13.5–7); lived in Akka Mahādēvi's vacanas; in the Kannada maxim, 'the śaraṇa is the wife, the liṅga the husband'.
