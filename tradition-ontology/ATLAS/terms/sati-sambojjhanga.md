# satisambojjhaṅga

`trm:sati-sambojjhanga` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** awakening factor of mindfulness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Mindfulness awakening factor: when a monk abides contemplating (body, feelings, mind or dhammas), unmuddled mindfulness is established; at that time this factor is aroused, developed and goes to fulfilment by development (MN 118:30).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Mindfulness established, always useful (MN 118; SN 46.53). One of the thirty-seven qualities conducive to awakening (DN 16.3.50; MN 149): a member of the seven factors of awakening.
