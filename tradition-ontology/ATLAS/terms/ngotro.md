# ngo sprod

`trm:ngotro` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** ངོ་སྤྲོད
**Literal:** introduction face to face

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): Direct introduction: the master's pointing out of awareness to the disciple, the first of Garab Dorje's three statements.
- [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md): The introduction to the natural state in the Zhang Zhung Nyengyü.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): The pointing-out or introduction: the guru's direct indication of the nature of mind to the disciple, by which the disciple recognizes it; given in stages in the manuals (mind in stillness and movement, appearances as mind, mind as empty, emptiness as self-liberated).
