## Definitions by tradition
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Possession: a real dissociated force that links a dharma to a particular series (acquisition and endowment); it explains why a defilement 'belongs' to someone even when not manifest.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): Not a real entity; 'possession' is the presence of an intact seed in the series.
