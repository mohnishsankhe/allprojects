# ādāna-vijñāna

`trm:adana-vijnana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** appropriating consciousness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): A name of the store-consciousness as that which appropriates and sustains the body and the seeds; the Saṃdhinirmocana says it is deep and subtle and was not taught to the childish lest they take it for a self.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Name of the base-consciousness as appropriating the body; not taught to the foolish lest they take it for a self.
