# jāgarita / jāgrat

`trm:jagrat` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** जाग्रत्
**Literal:** waking

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Waking: the field of the first quarter, vaiśvānara, outward-knowing, enjoying the gross (MāU 3).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Waking: the state in which the self, as viśva, experiences gross objects through the senses; empirically real.
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): Waking is [external] cognition (Śiva Sūtra 1.8).
