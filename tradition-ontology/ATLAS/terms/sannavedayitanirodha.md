# saññāvedayitanirodha

`trm:sannavedayitanirodha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** cessation of perception and feeling

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The attainment of cessation beyond the formless spheres, in which verbal, bodily and mental formations cease but vitality and heat remain (MN 43; MN 44); reached progressively 'with full awareness' (DN 9).
