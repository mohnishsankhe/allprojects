# bhedābheda

`trm:bhedabheda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भेदाभेद
**Literal:** difference-non-difference

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md): Difference-and-non-difference: Brahman is at once one with and different from souls and world (one as cause, many as effect).
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): Natural difference-and-non-difference of cit and acit from Brahman, like snake and coils.
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Held to be inconceivable (acintya) in the relation of the Lord and his powers.
