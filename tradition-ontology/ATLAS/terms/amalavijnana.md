# amala-vijñāna

`trm:amalavijnana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** stainless consciousness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): In Paramārtha's teaching (and the Shelun school) a ninth, pure consciousness beyond the ālaya, identified with suchness.
