# veṭṭa veḷi

`trm:vetta-veli` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tamil
**Native script:** வெட்ட வெளி
**Literal:** bare open space

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): The open, empty space — the supreme as boundless void-expanse beyond the crown; 'for those who hold the open space to be the truth, what need of a title-deed?' (Kuṭampai 1); Kaṭuveḷi speaks of 'the open space of pure Vedānta'.

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [ākāśa](akasa.md) — space as image of the supreme
- contested: [śūnya](sunya.md) — the Siddhar 'void' is not the Buddhist emptiness; resemblance only
