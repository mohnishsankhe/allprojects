## Definitions by tradition
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): The ten goddesses — Kālī, Tārā, Ṣoḍaśī, Bhuvaneśvarī, Bhairavī, Chinnamastā, Dhūmāvatī, Bagalā, Mātaṅgī, Kamalā — as forms of the one Goddess (Muṇḍamālā in the Tantrasāra; Toḍala 1 and 10).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): Satī's ten forms shown to Śiva before Dakṣa's sacrifice (Bṛhaddharma madhya 6.125-134).
