## Definitions by tradition
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): The three minds of the Contemplation Sūtra — sincere, deep, and dedicating-aspiring — which Shandao makes the settled mind for birth.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): Shinran distinguishes these (self-power, of the Contemplation Sūtra) from the three minds of the eighteenth vow, which are one mind given by Amida.
