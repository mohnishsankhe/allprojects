# iḍā

`trm:ida` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** इडा
**Literal:** (name of a channel; 'refreshment')

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The left channel, of the moon, identified with the Gaṅgā (HYP 3.110); inhalation 'through the moon' begins alternate-nostril purification (HYP 2.7).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The channel on the left, of the moon (GŚ 20, 23).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The left channel, carrying the moon.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tamil iṭakalai: the left channel (lunar), first of the ten nāṭis of the Siddha ninety-six.
