# bodhicitta

`trm:bodhicitta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** बोधिचित्त
**Literal:** the thought (mind) of awakening

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The resolve to attain full buddhahood for the sake of all beings that makes one a bodhisattva; the Aṣṭasāhasrikā adds that the thought is 'no-thought', its nature luminous; the Avataṃsaka that at its first arising one already attains perfect awakening.
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The resolve to attain awakening for the sake of all beings; with compassion and non-dual understanding the cause of bodhisattvas (MA 1.1); twofold as aspiration and engagement (BCA 1.15–16); the elixir that turns the impure body into a Victor's image (BCA 1.10).
- [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md): One of the three things in which the aspirant to omniscience must strive, after compassion (Bhāvanākrama I).
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The wish for perfect awakening for the sake of others (Abhisamayālaṃkāra 1.19); its first arising is the bodhisattva's first vow, with awakening and beings' welfare as its two objects.
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): The aspiration to become a buddha, which is the aspiration to save beings (Tanluan); required of all three grades; Shandao's and later Chinese teachers keep it with recitation.
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): Not required as a separate condition of birth in Hōnen's reading of the three grades (a point Myōe attacked).
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): The other-power aspiration for awakening, crosswise transcending: it is shinjin itself, given by Amida.
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): The mind wishing for complete awakening for the sake of all beings, generated after love and the sight of beings' suffering; aspiring bodhicitta grows only with the engaging vow.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The second principal aspect: renunciation held by bodhicitta becomes the cause of buddhahood; generated by the seven-point cause-and-effect instruction or by equalizing and exchanging self and other.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): Absence of attachment to one's own purpose (Parting 3); relative and ultimate bodhicitta under the experiential vision.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The mind of awakening: conventional (the aspiration) and ultimate (emptiness and compassion inseparable, 'free of all entities … one's own mind unborn', GST 2); in the completion stage also the white drop.
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): In the mind series, bodhicitta (byang chub kyi sems) is the primordial nature itself, the 'all-creating king': 'byang' purified, 'chub' all-pervading, 'sems' the unceasing all-maker.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): In Mahāyoga, the ultimate and relative bodhicitta generated as wisdom (Guhyagarbha ch.2); in the preliminaries, the aspiring and applying mind of awakening.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): byang chub kyi sems: (1) the aspiration for awakening for others' sake, aspiring and engaging, relative and ultimate (Jewel Ornament); (2) mind's nature itself, 'the root of all phenomena' (Tilopa); (3) in the completion stage, the white element of the subtle body.
