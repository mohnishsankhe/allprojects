# puraścaraṇa

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): The preliminary 'going before': a course of a fixed number of repetitions of a mantra (commonly counted by its syllables), with fire offering, libations, sprinkling and feeding brāhmaṇas in fixed proportions, by which the mantra is mastered before use.
