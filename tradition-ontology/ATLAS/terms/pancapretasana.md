**Literal:** seat of five corpses

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The seat of five 'corpses' — Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra and Īśvara as the legs and Sadāśiva as the plank of the Goddess's couch, lifeless without her (LSN 61; PKS 3.10); in SL 94 the four gods become the couch and Śiva its coverlet.
