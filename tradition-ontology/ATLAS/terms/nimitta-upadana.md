**Literal:** occasion / material cause

## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Instrumental and substantial cause: in Kundakunda's analysis the soul's impure states and karmic matter are occasions (nimitta) for each other, but each is the substantial cause (upādāna) only of its own modifications.
