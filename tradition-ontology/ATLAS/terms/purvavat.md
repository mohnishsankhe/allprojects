# pūrvavat

`trm:purvavat` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पूर्ववत्
**Literal:** 'as before'

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Inference whose object's class has been seen before (e.g., fire from smoke); with śeṣavat and sāmānyatodṛṣṭa one of the three kinds (commentaries on SK 5).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): One of the three kinds of inference named in NS 1.1.5; the Bhāṣya offers more than one explanation.
