# samaya-dīkṣā

`trm:samaya-diksa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** समयदीक्षा
**Literal:** initiation into the observances

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The first Śaiva initiation, making one a samayin, entitled to worship and study under the post-initiatory observances (samaya).
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): The first initiation, making one a samayin, entitled to the rules (samaya) and to hear the scriptures.
