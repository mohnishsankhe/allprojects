## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The task regarding the first truth: suffering is to be fully understood (SN 56.11).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Three full understandings: as the known, as investigation, as abandoning.
