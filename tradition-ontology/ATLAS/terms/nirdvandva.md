# nirdvandva

`trm:nirdvandva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निर्द्वन्द्व
**Literal:** free from the pairs of opposites

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: One of the qualities Arjuna is told to have when free of the three guṇas (2.45).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: free of the pairs of opposites (nirdvandva), the constant renouncer is easily released from bondage (5.3); cf. dvandvātīta, 'gone beyond the pairs' (4.22).
