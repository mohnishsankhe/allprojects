# śarīra

`trm:sarira` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शरीर
**Literal:** body

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The embodied one casts off worn-out bodies like clothes (2.22); it is not slain when the body is slain (2.20); without action even the maintenance of the body (śarīrayātrā) is impossible (3.8).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The substrate of purposive movement, senses and experienced objects (NS 1.1.11); earthy (NS 3.1.27).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Bodies of each element; some beings are not womb-born (VS 4.2.9 C; Tarkasaṅgraha).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Any substance that a conscious being wholly controls and supports for its own purposes and that exists for it alone (as its śeṣa); in this sense all souls and all matter are the body of the Lord, who is their self (śarīrin).
