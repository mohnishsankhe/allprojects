# prajāpati

`trm:prajapati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रजापति
**Literal:** lord of creatures

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The lord of creatures, named in the last verse of the Hiraṇyagarbha hymn (RV 10.121.10) and in the White Yajurveda as moving unborn within the womb and born in many ways (VS 31.19); in the Atharvaveda Time is his father (AVŚ 19.53.8).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The creator who, alone in the beginning, desired to multiply, heated himself with austerity and emitted the worlds and beings; exhausted and fallen apart after creating, he is the year and is rebuilt in the fire-altar and the sacrifice.
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): In Manu, the creator-lord who milked the syllables a-u-m and the Sāvitrī from the Vedas (MDh 2.76–77); one of the names by which the one supreme Person is called (MDh 12.123).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The creator who in the beginning created beings together with sacrifice and told them to prosper by it (3.10).
