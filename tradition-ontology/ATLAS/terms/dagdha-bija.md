# dagdha-bīja

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** दग्धबीज
**Literal:** burnt seed

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The fifth state of the afflictions, in one whose affliction-seeds are burnt by reflection, who is 'skilled' and in the last body (YBh 2.4); burnt-seed impressions give rise to no cognitions (4.28, YBh 4.28).
