# dharmadhātu

`trm:dharmadhatu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the element / realm of dharmas

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The totality and true nature of dharmas; in the Avataṃsaka the boundless realm filled by Vairocana in which all worlds interpenetrate; synonym of suchness.
