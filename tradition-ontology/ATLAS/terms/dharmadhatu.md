# dharmadhātu

`trm:dharmadhatu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the element / realm of dharmas

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The totality and true nature of dharmas; in the Avataṃsaka the boundless realm filled by Vairocana in which all worlds interpenetrate; synonym of suchness.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): A synonym of emptiness, so called because it is the cause of the noble dharmas; directly perceived when mind-only is itself abandoned; in its purity it is the buddhas' body operating as the three bodies.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): The expanse of reality (chos dbyings), in which all phenomena arise as display — the theme of Longchenpa's Chöying Dzöd.
- [Karma Kagyu (Kamtsang Kagyu)](../lineages/karma-kagyu.md): chos dbyings: the expanse; its stainless wisdom is the fifth wisdom into which the eight consciousnesses resolve (3rd Karmapa).
