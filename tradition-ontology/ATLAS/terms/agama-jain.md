# āgama (Jain canon)

`trm:agama-jain` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आगम
**Literal:** what has come down; tradition

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The canonical scripture: the words of the Tīrthaṅkara as composed by the gaṇadharas and elders, fixed at Valabhī — forty-five texts for Mūrtipūjakas, thirty-two for Sthānakavāsīs and Terāpanthīs.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The Digambaras hold the original āgama (the Aṅgas and Pūrvas) lost; the Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama and Kaṣāyapāhuḍa preserve parts of it and are called 'āgama', and the works of later ācāryas in the four anuyogas serve as scripture.

## Forms in other languages
- Prakrit: āgama  — exact
- Sanskrit: siddhānta  — partial — Śvetāmbara synonym for the canon
- Sanskrit: sūtra  — partial — common name for canonical texts
