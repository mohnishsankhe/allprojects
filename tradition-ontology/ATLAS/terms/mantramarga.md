# mantramārga

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** मन्त्रमार्ग
**Literal:** the path of mantras

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The initiatory division of Śaivism whose scriptures (Siddhānta and non-Saiddhāntika tantras) give both powers/enjoyment and liberation through initiation, mantra-worship and yoga; the fifth and highest stream after the worldly, Vedic, spiritual and Atimārga paths.
- [Atimārga](../lineages/atimarga.md): The other great division of Śaivism, beside the Atimārga, whose scriptures (Siddhānta and Bhairava tantras) describe and outrank the Atimārga.
