# viññāṇaṃ anidassanaṃ

`trm:vinnana-anidassana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** consciousness that does not manifest

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): 'Consciousness non-manifesting, infinite, radiant all round', where the elements and name-and-form find no footing and cease (DN 11; MN 49); read by the commentaries as nibbāna.
