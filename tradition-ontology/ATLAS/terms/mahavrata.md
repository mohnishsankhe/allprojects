# mahāvrata

`trm:mahavrata` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** महाव्रत
**Literal:** great vow

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The vow kept wholly (7.2).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The restraints when unlimited by class, place, time or occasion (2.31, YBh 2.31).
- [Kāpālika (Somasiddhānta)](../lineages/kapalika.md): The skull-bearer's great vow: living with a skull-bowl and skull-staff in imitation of Bhairava's penance; its keepers are 'mahāvratins'. Yāmuna reports 'by taking the Kāpāla vow a man becomes an ascetic'.
- [Lākula](../lineages/lakula.md): The observance of the Lākula division (lokātīta), with skull and khaṭvāṅga.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In Jainism the five vows taken in full by ascetics, to be kept in thought, word and deed, doing, causing and approving (TS 7.2).

## Forms in other languages
