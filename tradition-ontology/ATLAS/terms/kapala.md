# kapāla

`trm:kapala` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कपाल
**Literal:** skull; skull-bowl

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kāpālika (Somasiddhānta)](../lineages/kapalika.md): The human skull carried as begging-bowl; with the khaṭvāṅga a sub-insignia (upamudrā) of the Kāpālas (Yāmuna); eating from a skull is ascribed to the Kālāmukhas.
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The skull, 'best of all vessels' for the Kaula rite (KJN 12.13).
- [Aghora (Aghor panth; Aghorī and Aughaṛ ascetics)](../lineages/aghora.md): The skull bowl carried by the Aghora ascetic.
