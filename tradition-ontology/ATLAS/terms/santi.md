# śānti

`trm:santi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शान्ति
**Literal:** peace

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Without cultivation there is no peace, and without peace no happiness (2.66); the one whom desires enter as rivers the ocean, and who abandons all desires, attains peace (2.70–71).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Peace and the appeasing of what is harmful; the peace chants (VS 36.17; AVŚ 19.9).
- [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md): Resident priests who perform the daily pūjās in a Kerala temple under the tantri's authority.
