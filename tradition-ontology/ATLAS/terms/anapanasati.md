# ānāpānasati

`trm:anapanasati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** mindfulness of breathing

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Mindfulness of in- and out-breathing in sixteen steps, which fulfils the four establishments of mindfulness, the seven factors of awakening, and true knowledge and liberation (MN 118; SN 54.1).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Mindfulness of in- and out-breathing in sixteen bases, worked by counting, following, contact, fixing and the later stages; leads to all four jhānas and, with insight, to arahantship.
- [Suan Mokkh (Buddhadāsa's tradition)](../lineages/suan-mokkh.md): The complete practice in four tetrads covering all four foundations of mindfulness (Buddhadāsa).
- [U Ba Khin – S. N. Goenka tradition (Sayagyi U Ba Khin lineage)](../lineages/u-ba-khin-goenka.md): Awareness of natural respiration at the entrance of the nostrils, the first days of a course.
