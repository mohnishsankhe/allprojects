# taṇhā

`trm:tanha` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** craving, thirst

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): DN 22:19: the craving that leads to renewed becoming, accompanied by delight and passion, delighting here and there; threefold: kāma-, bhava-, vibhava-taṇhā. It arises and settles wherever in the world there is the dear-looking and pleasant-looking (eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mind; their objects; consciousnesses; contacts; feelings; perceptions; volitions; cravings; thoughts; sustained thoughts). It is abandoned and ceases in the same places (dn22:20).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Craving for sensual pleasures, for becoming and for non-becoming, which leads to renewed existence (SN 56.11); the six classes of craving for the six objects (SN 12.2); the taking up of the burden (SN 22.22).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Craving for sense pleasures, existence and non-existence; the origin of suffering; analysed into 108 modes.
