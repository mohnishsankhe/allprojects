# mūlam

`trm:mulam` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Tamil
**Native script:** மூலம்
**Literal:** root, origin

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): The root: the lowest support (mūlātāram) where the fire and the goddess Vālai are placed; in the Tirumūla ñāṉam also the letter 'a' as the ground of all. 'To know that root is liberation' (Kaṭuveḷi 13).
