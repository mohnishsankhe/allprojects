# kanda

`trm:kanda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कन्द
**Literal:** bulb, tuber

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The egg-shaped bulb between the genitals and navel from which the channels arise (GŚ 16); kuṇḍalinī sleeps above it (HYP 3.107).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The bulb near the navel from which the channels spread.
