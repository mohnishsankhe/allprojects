# nidāna

`trm:nidana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निदान
**Literal:** cause; diagnosis

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The cause of disease, the first of the five means of knowing it; synonyms occasion, cause, basis, condition, origin (Mādhava Nidāna 1.4-5).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The wish that one's austerities bring a particular worldly or heavenly reward; a thorn, a form of sorrowful meditation and a fault of sallekhanā (TS 7.37, 9.33).
