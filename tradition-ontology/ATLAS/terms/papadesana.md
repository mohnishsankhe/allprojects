# pāpadeśanā

`trm:papadesana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** confession of wrongdoing

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Declaring one's evil deeds before the buddhas, with regret and resolve, to purify karma — as in the Golden Light's confession, the Triskandhaka and Samantabhadra's vows.
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): Confessing before the Buddhas the evil done in beginningless saṃsāra and this life, with remorse and resolve (BCA 2.28ff).
