# yixing sanmei (one-practice samādhi)

`trm:yixing-sanmei` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 一行三昧
**Literal:** samādhi of one practice

## Definitions by tradition
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): Always practising a straight mind in all activities, not sitting motionless (Platform Sūtra).
- [East Mountain teaching](../lineages/east-mountain.md): Daoxin's samādhi of one practice drawn from the Wenshu shuo bore jing (Lengqie shizi ji).
