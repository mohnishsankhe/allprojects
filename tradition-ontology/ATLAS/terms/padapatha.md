**Literal:** word-recitation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The recitation of a Saṃhitā word by word, with sandhi undone.
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): The analysed text in which each word is recited separately without sandhi (for the Ṛgveda, Śākalya's).
