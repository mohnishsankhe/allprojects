# yoginī

`trm:yogini` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** योगिनी
**Literal:** female yogin / goddess of yoga

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): Female practitioner or goddess of the maṇḍala; the Yoginī tantras are 'their' tantras.
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): In Śrīvidyā the classes of attendant goddesses of the nine enclosures — Prakaṭa, Gupta, Guptatara, Sampradāya, Kulottīrṇa, Nigarbha, Rahasya, Atirahasya and Parāparātirahasya yoginīs; the Tripurā Upaniṣad (2) names nine yoginīs.
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The goddesses of the Kula — sky-going, mother and earth-going — through whom Kaula knowledge descended; in Kāmarūpa the Kaula scripture is 'in every yoginī's house' (Kaulajñānanirṇaya 9.11-15; 22.10).
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): Goddesses of the six centres of the Kubjikā system (Kubjikāmata 15).
