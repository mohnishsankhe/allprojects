## Definitions by tradition

- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Formal veneration of the teacher; one of the six essential duties.
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): Bowing, adoration with joined palms (Tamil toḻutal): 'O mind, worship his feet and rise' (TVM 1.1.1).
