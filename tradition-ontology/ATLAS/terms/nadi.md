# nāḍī

`trm:nadi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नाडी
**Literal:** channel, tube

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The channels of the subtle body carrying the breath: seventy-two thousand arise from the bulb (GŚ 16; HYP 1.39, 4.18), of which ten (or fourteen, SS 2.13, which counts 350,000 in all) are principal and three chief — iḍā, piṅgalā, suṣumnā. When clogged with impurities the breath cannot enter the middle (HYP 2.4).
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The hundred and one channels of the heart, one (suṣumnā) going to the crown, by which the knower departs at death with the help of Hari in the heart; the suṣumnā 'shines' in the seated worshipper.
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): a synonym of srotas (Ca Vi 5.7); in Śārṅgadhara the pulse-vessel at the root of the thumb, 'witness of life' (Pūrva 3.1).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Channel: 101 channels of the heart, one leading to the crown (KU 2.3.16; ChU 8.6.6); 72,000 branch channels (PrU 3.6); the suṣumnā (MaiU 6.21).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Channel of breath; 72,000 arise from the kanda; ten or fourteen are principal.
- [Siddha medicine (cittā maruttuvam)](../lineages/siddha-medicine.md): Tamil nāṭi means both the channels of the subtle body (ten principal ones in the ninety-six) and the pulse, the chief of the eight examinations, read at the wrist for the three humours.
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): Channels whose withdrawal is a yogic attainment (Śiva Sūtra 3.5).
