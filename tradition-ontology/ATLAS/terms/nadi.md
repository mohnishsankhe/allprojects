# nāḍī

`trm:nadi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नाडी
**Literal:** channel

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The hundred and one channels of the heart, one (suṣumnā) going to the crown, by which the knower departs at death with the help of Hari in the heart; the suṣumnā 'shines' in the seated worshipper.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Channel: 101 channels of the heart, one leading to the crown (KU 2.3.16; ChU 8.6.6); 72,000 branch channels (PrU 3.6); the suṣumnā (MaiU 6.21).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Channel of breath; 72,000 arise from the kanda; ten or fourteen are principal.
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): Channels whose withdrawal is a yogic attainment (Śiva Sūtra 3.5).
