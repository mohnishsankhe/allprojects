# kalyāṇamitra

`trm:kalyanamitra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कल्याणमित्र
**Literal:** good (spiritual) friend

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The teacher or companion who leads to awakening; the Aṣṭa says the buddhas and the perfections are the bodhisattva's good friends; Sudhana's pilgrimage and Sadāprarudita's search model devotion to them.
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The teacher, reliance on whom in thought and deed is the root of the path; qualified by the ten qualities of the Mahāyānasūtrālaṅkāra.
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): To be relied on while bad friends are abandoned (Jewel Garland 12).
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): The teacher who guides the bodhisattva; not abandoning the spiritual friend is part of protecting the body for beings' benefit (ŚSK 6).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): dge ba'i bshes gnyen: the spiritual friend, the 'condition' of the path, of four kinds according to the disciple's stage (Jewel Ornament ch.3).
