# akarman

`trm:akarma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अकर्मन्
**Literal:** non-action, inaction

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Inaction: one should not be attached to it (2.47); action is better than inaction, and without action even the body cannot be maintained (3.8).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Inaction, which the wise see in action and whose presence in inaction they also see (BhG 4.18); attachment to inaction is forbidden (2.47).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: One of the three things to be understood along with action (karma) and vikarma (4.17); even sages are bewildered about it (4.16); the wise one sees inaction in action and action in inaction (4.18); the unattached one 'does nothing at all' though engaged in action (4.20).
