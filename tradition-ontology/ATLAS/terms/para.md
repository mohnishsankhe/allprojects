# para (form of God)

`trm:para` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पर
**Literal:** the supreme

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The supreme form of Bhagavān (Para Vāsudeva) in his highest realm, first of the five modes.
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The supreme Goddess; in the Kalpasūtra one of the vidyās worshipped (the eighth section, parākrama); the commentators call Mahātripurasundarī Parābhaṭṭārikā.
