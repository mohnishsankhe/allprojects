# suññatā

`trm:sunnata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** emptiness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Emptiness as a dwelling — what is absent is seen as empty and what remains as present (MN 121); the world is empty of self and what belongs to self (SN 35.85); emptiness concentration (SN 43.12).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Voidness: all dhammas void of self; the gateway through contemplation of not-self; an aspect of nibbāna.
- [Suan Mokkh (Buddhadāsa's tradition)](../lineages/suan-mokkh.md): The mind void of 'I' and 'mine' — the heart of Buddhism, to be lived in daily work (Buddhadāsa).

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: śūnyatā  — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [śūnyatā](sunyata.md) — same word; Madhyamaka universalises emptiness as absence of own-nature
