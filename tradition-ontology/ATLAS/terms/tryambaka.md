# tryambaka

`trm:tryambaka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** त्र्यम्बक
**Literal:** three-mothered or three-eyed

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The god (Rudra) of the verse 'we worship Tryambaka … may I be freed from death, not from immortality' (RV 7.59.12; TS 1.8.6; VS 3.60).
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): The 'three-eyed' Śiva of the Mahāmṛtyuñjaya verse (RV 7.59.12), whose mantra, with added seed-syllables, is repeated for protection from death and disease.
