# akṛtābhyāgama

`trm:akrtabhyagama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अकृताभ्यागम
**Literal:** receiving (the fruit of) what was not done

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The absurdity of reaping results of acts one did not do — used to refute rival accounts of embodiment and of the agent (NS 3.2.38, 3.2.72 DCS).
