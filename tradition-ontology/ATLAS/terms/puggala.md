# puggala

`trm:puggala` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** person, individual

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The conventional person: 'the bearer of the burden is the person' (SN 22.22); the seven and eight noble persons (MN 70); in the ultimate sense not found (Mil 3.1.1).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): A person: a conventional designation (paññatti), not an ultimate reality; the Puggalapaññatti classifies persons, and the Kathāvatthu refutes the view that the person is a real and ultimate fact.
