# anāhata

`trm:anahata` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनाहत
**Literal:** unstruck

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The heart centre, twelve-petalled, of air (YAṂ), where the unstruck sound is heard (ṢCN; GŚ 60).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The unstruck sound heard within; also the heart centre.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The unstruck, unbroken inner sound, the Sound-Brahman (VBT 38).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The unstruck sound (anhad nād, anhad bājā) heard within in the encounter with the Lord; a Nāth-derived image used by Kabīr and Dādū.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): The inner sound heard in meditation, distinguished into the sounds of the successive regions.
