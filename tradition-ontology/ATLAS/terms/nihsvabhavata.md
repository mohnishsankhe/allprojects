# niḥsvabhāvatā

`trm:nihsvabhavata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निःस्वभावता
**Literal:** lack of own-nature

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The absence of own-nature in all existents, synonymous with emptiness; existents without own-nature are dependently arisen (MMK 1.10; 17.21; 22.16).
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The threefold naturelessness intended by the Prajñāpāramitā's 'all dharmas are without own-nature': of characteristic (the imagined), of arising (the dependent, which does not arise by itself) and ultimate (the perfected, which is naturelessness itself).
