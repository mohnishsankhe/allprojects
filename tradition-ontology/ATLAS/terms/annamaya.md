## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The first self: the person made of the essence of food (annarasamaya), the outermost, person-shaped; head, wings, self and tail-support indicated as "this" (Taittirīya 2.1.1).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Made of (the essence of) food: the outermost self, the person formed from food (TU 2.1).
