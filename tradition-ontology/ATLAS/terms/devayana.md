# devayāna

`trm:devayana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** देवयान
**Literal:** the path of the gods

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The bright path of fire, light, day, the bright fortnight and the northern course, by which knowers of Brahman go to Brahman and do not return (BhG 8.24).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The path of the gods, distinct from death's own path (RV 10.18.1) and from the fathers' (10.88.15).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The path of the gods: through flame, day, the waxing fortnight, the northern course, the year (or the gods' world), the sun, the moon and lightning, where a non-human person leads to brahman; those who go by it do not return (ChU 4.15.5, 5.10.1-2; BAU 6.2.15; PrU 1.10; KauU 1.3).
- [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md): The path of the gods, beginning with light (arcis), by which meditators go to Brahman/the Brahma-world without return (BS 4.3.1).
