# padārtha

`trm:padartha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पदार्थ
**Literal:** category; 'meaning of a word'

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): The categories: substance, quality, motion, universal, particularity, inherence (six), with absence as seventh in later works.
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): The sixteen topics of NS 1.1.1; also 'word-meaning' (NS 2.2.68).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Seven categories (Tarkasaṅgraha, Bhāṣāpariccheda); Raghunātha re-examines the list.
- [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md): The meaning of a word; the word-meanings, once conveyed, convey the sentence-meaning (Śabara on MS 1.1.25). Also 'category' in ontology (Bhāṭṭas five, Prābhākaras eight).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In Jain usage the nine categories (nava padārtha): the seven realities plus merit and demerit (Kundakunda, Śvetāmbara canon).
