# parā vāk

`trm:para-vak` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** परा वाक्
**Literal:** supreme speech

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): Consciousness as reflexive awareness is supreme speech, arising of itself (ĪPK 1.5.13); higher than paśyantī.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The first, unmanifest level of speech, where it 'sprouts'.
