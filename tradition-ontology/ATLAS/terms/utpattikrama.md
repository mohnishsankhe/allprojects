# utpattikrama

`trm:utpattikrama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** generation stage

## Definitions by tradition
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The stage of visualising oneself as the deity in the maṇḍala to purify ordinary appearance and conception, ripening the continuum for the completion stage.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): Part of the path of the method continuum in the Vajra Verses ('the generation stage and the rest').
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The generation stage: arising as the deity in the maṇḍala from emptiness through seed syllables, with clarity and divine pride; the basis for the completion stage ('a staircase', Pañcakrama 1.2).
