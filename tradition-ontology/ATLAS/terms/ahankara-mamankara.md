# ahaṅkāra-mamaṅkāra

`trm:ahankara-mamankara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** I-making and mine-making

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The making of 'I' and 'mine' and the underlying tendency to conceit, absent in the liberated one (AN 3.33).
