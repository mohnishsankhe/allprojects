## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): (1) The descending node, the headless trunk of the asura, counted as the ninth graha (BJ 2.3 'Śikhin'); the Fish avatāra is born from him (BPHS 2.6); period 7 years; in the twelfth from the kārakāṃśa he gives kaivalya (Jaimini 1.2.69). (2) A comet (the Ketus of BS 11).
