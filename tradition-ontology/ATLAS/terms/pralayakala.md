# pralayākala

`trm:pralayakala` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): A soul bound by mala and karma, whose māyic body is dissolved in the cosmic dissolution.
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The knower resting in the void, as in deep sleep or cosmic dissolution, with the kārma impurity (ĪPK 3.2.8).
