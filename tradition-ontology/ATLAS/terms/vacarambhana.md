# vācārambhaṇa

`trm:vacarambhana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** origination in speech

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): 'A verbal handle': the modification is a verbal handle, a name; the reality is clay (ChU 6.1.4).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): 'A matter of speech': the Chāndogya's description of modifications as mere names (the clay alone being real), the basis of the non-difference of effect from cause (BS 2.1.14).
