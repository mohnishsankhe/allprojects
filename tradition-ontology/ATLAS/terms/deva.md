## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The gods, nourished by sacrifice, nourish human beings in turn and give the enjoyments they desire (3.11–12).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): A god; thirty-three in heaven, earth and waters (RV 1.139.11) or 3,339 (3.9.9).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: The gods do not know the Lord's origin, for he is their beginning (10.2, 10.14); they are seen within the universal form (11.15) and long for a sight of it (11.52).
