**Literal:** path of preparation (application)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The second path, of the four aids to penetration (heat, peak, patience, supreme mundane quality), in which the yogin realizes that objects are mental speech and mind-only and abandons first the distraction of the grasped, then of the grasper.
