# anutpāda

`trm:anutpada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** non-arising, the unborn

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): That dharmas do not truly arise or cease; what is born from conditions is unborn by own-nature; non-arising, like suchness, neither comes nor goes and is the Tathāgata.
