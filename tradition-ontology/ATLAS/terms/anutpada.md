# anutpāda

`trm:anutpada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनुत्पाद
**Literal:** non-arising

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): Things do not arise from self, other, both or without cause (MMK 1.1); dependent origination is 'without arising' (dedication); non-arising taught in the Mahāyāna equals the extinction taught to others (RĀ 4.86).
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): That dharmas do not truly arise or cease; what is born from conditions is unborn by own-nature; non-arising, like suchness, neither comes nor goes and is the Tathāgata.

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [ajātivāda](ajativada.md) — Gauḍapāda's doctrine of non-origination (Māṇḍūkya Kārikā 4) uses the same fourfold negation of arising; for Advaita it rests on the unborn Brahman, for Madhyamaka on emptiness — both would reject identity
