**Literal:** five-faced

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Sadāśiva's five faces, identified with the five brahma-mantras (Īśāna, Tatpuruṣa, Aghora, Vāmadeva, Sadyojāta), whose combinations yield the Śiva-, Rudra- and Bhairava-tantras.
