# rudra

`trm:rudra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** रुद्र
**Literal:** the roarer (or: the red one)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The fierce archer-god feared for his arrows and loved as the best physician, lord of cattle, 'auspicious' (śiva), praised in hundreds of forms in the Śatarudrīya.
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The Lord of the Pāśupata Sūtra, near whom the liberated brahmin does not return and with whom he attains union; gods and ancestors are both in him.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Rudra: 'Rudra is one, they do not stand for a second' (ŚU 3.2); source of the gods, who saw Hiraṇyagarbha born (3.4); his auspicious form (3.5); in MaiU 5.2 the tamas-part of the one.
