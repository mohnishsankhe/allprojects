# saṃvit

`trm:samvit` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** संवित्
**Literal:** knowledge, awareness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The knowledge aspect of the internal power, by which the Lord knows and makes himself known.
- [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md): Consciousness as the one reality and as each act of awareness; in the Krama the goddess is one's own consciousness (svasaṃvid).
