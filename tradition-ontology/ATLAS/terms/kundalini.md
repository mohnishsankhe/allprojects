# kuṇḍalinī

`trm:kundalini` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कुण्डलिनी
**Literal:** the coiled one (she-serpent)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The power (śakti) coiled at the base of the central channel, blocking its mouth; the support of all yoga (HYP 3.1); awakened by the guru's grace (3.2) and by breath, locks and mudrās (3.5, 3.66–69), she pierces the lotuses and knots and makes the mind supportless. Synonyms: kuṭilāṅgī, bhujaṅgī, śakti, īśvarī, kuṇḍalī, arundhatī (3.104); coiled eightfold (GŚ 30) or three and a half times (ṢCN). For yogins she is for liberation, for fools for bondage (HYP 3.107).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The Goddess herself as the coiled power: dwelling in the mūlādhāra she pierces the three knots, mounts the thousand-petalled lotus, showers nectar, and returns to sleep in three and a half coils (LSN 38-40; SL 9-10).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The coiled power lying eightfold above the bulb, closing the door of Brahman, awakened by the fire of yoga or by the guru's grace to rise through suṣumṇā (GŚ 30–31; HYP 3.2).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): One of the names of Śiva's Will-power (KJN 20.11); in the Akulavīra the kuṇḍalī is the 'made' (kṛtaka) path, the innate abiding in samarasa (B.43).
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): The world-mother, pedestal of the liṅga of the void (Jñāneśvarī 6.272–273), coiled three and a half times (6.223).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): The Goddess coiled three and a half times around the Svayambhū liṅga at the base, led up through the centres to unite with Śiva in the thousand-petalled lotus (Ṣaṭcakranirūpaṇa 51-53, Pūrṇānanda).
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): Kubjikā praised as 'coiled like a sleeping serpent' (Kubjikāmata 2.2).
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): Śabda-brahman in the body: consciousness dwelling coiled in the middle of the body, from which the letters arise (ŚT 1.14).
- [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md): The power of Brahman in the root centre which, straightened up to the brahmarandhra, bestows nectar (ŚR 1.2.121–122).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The coiled power at the base that blocks the door of Brahman and, awakened, rises to Śiva.
- [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md): The coiled power in the root centre, meditated upon at dawn as moving up and down the inner path and led through the six centres to union with the supreme.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The power rising from the root through the wheels to the dvādaśānta (VBT 28-29), 'curved in form' as the breath (VBT 154); the Trika distinguishes its forms as power of breath and of consciousness.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tamil kuṇṭali: the coiled power at the root, roused by the guru's grace to pass the six supports (Vināyakar akaval; Tiruvaḷḷuvar ñāṉam invocation).
- [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md): The coiled power whose waking and ascent Jñāneśvar describes in Jñāneśvarī 6 as the Nāths' royal road (summary only).
