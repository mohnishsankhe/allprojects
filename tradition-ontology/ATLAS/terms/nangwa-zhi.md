# snang ba bzhi

`trm:nangwa-zhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Literal:** four appearances

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): The four visions of direct crossing: the direct perception of dharmatā (chos nyid mngon sum), the increase of experience (nyams gong 'phel), awareness reaching its full measure (rig pa tshad phebs), and the exhaustion of phenomena in dharmatā (chos nyid du zad pa).
