# abhidhā

`trm:abhidha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** naming, denotation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): Denotation, the primary power of a word to convey its conventional meaning; the first of the three powers (abhidhā, lakṣaṇā, vyañjanā).
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): The primary power (śakti) of words; for the grammarians grounded in the eternal relation of word and meaning.
