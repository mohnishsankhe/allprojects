# bhavaṅga

`trm:bhavanga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** factor of existence; life-continuum

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The resultant consciousness that maintains the continuity of an existence when no active process occurs; the same type as the rebirth-linking and death consciousness; identified by the commentary with the 'luminous mind'.
