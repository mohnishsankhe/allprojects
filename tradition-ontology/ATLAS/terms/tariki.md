# tariki

`trm:tariki` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Japanese
**Native script:** 他力
**Literal:** other power

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md): Other-power (tali): the power of the Buddha's vow as the dominant condition of birth; the easy path rides on it like a lame man on a ship (Tanluan).
- [Jōdo-shū (the Pure Land school of Hōnen)](../lineages/jodo-shu.md): Birth by the Buddha's power, through the nenbutsu that accords with the vow.
- [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](../lineages/jodo-shinshu.md): Other-power is Amida's primal vow-power; the practitioner's 'no working' is true working; shinjin itself is other-power.

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [prasāda](prasada.md) (divine) — both name salvation by the power of another (the Buddha's vow / the Lord's grace); the Pure Land tradition does not posit a creator Lord
