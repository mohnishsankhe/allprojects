# suṣumnā

`trm:susumna` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सुषुम्ना
**Literal:** (name of the central channel)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The middle channel: its synonyms are śūnyapadavī, brahmarandhra, mahāpatha, śmaśāna, śāmbhavī and madhyamārga (3.4); the text calls it the only fruitful one of 72,000 channel-doors (4.18) and the devourer of Time (4.17).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The central channel through which prāṇa and awakened kuṇḍalinī rise; its synonyms are śūnyapadavī, brahmarandhra, mahāpatha, śmaśāna, śāmbhavī and madhyamārga (HYP 3.4); of the 72,000 channels it alone is the śāmbhavī śakti (4.18); it 'devours time' (4.17).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The upward channel carrying breath, divided within the palate, through which, joined with breath, Om and mind, one goes upward (MaiU 6.21) — the earliest use of the name in these texts.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The central channel of fire, through which awakened kuṇḍalinī rises (GŚ 31); the 'middle path' the breath must enter (HYP 4.114).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The central channel, path of liberation, 'devourer of time', the supreme tīrtha.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): In BhP 2.2.24 the brilliant path by which the departing yogin rises to Vaiśvānara.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tamil cuḻumuṉai: the central channel, between iṭakalai and piṅkalai, named in the Siddha invocations and among the ten nāṭis.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The central channel meditated as fine as a lotus fibre (VBT 35).
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): The path in which the in- and out-breaths set (SK 1.24); Śiva Sūtra 3.44 makes the inner centre prior to the three channels.
