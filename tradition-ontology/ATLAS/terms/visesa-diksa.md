# viśeṣa-dīkṣā

`trm:visesa-diksa` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विशेषदीक्षा
**Literal:** special initiation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): In several Siddhānta sources a name for the initiation (often identified with or following samaya-dīkṣā) that qualifies for personal Śiva-worship.
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): The 'special' initiation qualifying the initiate for the daily worship of Śiva (details vary among manuals).
