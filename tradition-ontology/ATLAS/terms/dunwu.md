# dunwu (sudden awakening)

`trm:dunwu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 頓悟
**Literal:** sudden awakening

## Definitions by tradition
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): Awakening all at once to the self-nature; the Platform Sūtra says the Dharma has no sudden or gradual, only people are sharp or dull.
- [Seon (Korean Chan)](../lineages/seon.md): Recognising in one turning of the light that one's own nature is buddha (Jinul).
