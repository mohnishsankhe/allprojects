# alakh

`trm:alakh` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Hindi
**Native script:** अलख
**Literal:** invisible, without mark

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The 'unmarked, invisible' — a Nāth name for the supreme, called out in the begging cry 'alakh!' and joined with nirañjan ('alakh nirañjan').
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): 'The unseen, unmarked' — a name of the ultimate shared with Nāth usage ('alakh nirañjan'); in Sant Mat, Alakh Lok is a region above Sach Khand.
