# svādhyāya

`trm:svadhyaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्वाध्याय
**Literal:** self-study, recitation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Repetition of purifying formulas such as the praṇava, or study of scriptures on liberation (YBh 2.1, 2.32); brings contact with the chosen deity (2.44).
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): The Veda of one's own branch; the injunction to study it is the starting point of Mīmāṃsā in the Prābhākara account.
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Daily Vedic self-study, the sacrifice to Brahman, praised as bringing concentration and joy (ŚB 11.5.6.3; 11.5.7.1).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Recitation (study) of one's Veda: 'do not neglect study' (TU 1.11); all duties 'with study and teaching' (TU 1.9); the householder keeps reciting (ChU 8.15).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The fourth daily period: study of scripture and recitation.
