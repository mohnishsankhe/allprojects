# gtong len

`trm:tonglen` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** གཏོང་ལེན
**Literal:** sending and taking; giving and taking

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md): The meditation of taking others' suffering and its causes upon oneself and giving them one's happiness and virtue, alternately and mounted on the breath (Seven Points 2.6).
- [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md): The practice of taking and giving that completes the exchange of self and other, made the heart of practice in the Guru Pūjā.
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): Taught in commentaries on the third line of Parting from the Four Attachments and in the Thirty-Seven Practices.
