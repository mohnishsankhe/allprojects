# pītisambojjhaṅga

`trm:piti-sambojjhanga` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** awakening factor of rapture

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Rapture awakening factor: aroused when unworldly rapture arises in one whose energy is aroused (MN 118:33).
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Spiritual rapture arising in one who has aroused energy (MN 118). One of the thirty-seven qualities conducive to awakening (DN 16.3.50; MN 149): a member of the seven factors of awakening.
