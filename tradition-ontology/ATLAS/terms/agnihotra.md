# agnihotra

`trm:agnihotra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अग्निहोत्र
**Literal:** offering into the fire

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The twice-daily milk offering into the sacred fires by one who has set them up; the Brāhmaṇas explain it as healing the junction of day and night (ŚB 1.6.3.36), as an offering of 'truth in faith' when nothing else exists (ŚB 11.3.1.4), and as a cycle through the worlds and the body (ŚB 11.6.2).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Interiorized as the offering into the breaths at meals (ChU 5.19–24) and as the continuous 'inner Agnihotra' of speech and breath (KU 2.5).
