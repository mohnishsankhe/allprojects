## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The monastic discipline laid down for ten reasons (Vin Pār 1) and recited by Upāli at the First Council (Cv 11).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Reverence toward knowledge, view, conduct and the worthy (TS 9.23).
