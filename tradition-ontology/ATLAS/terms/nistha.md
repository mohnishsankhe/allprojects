# niṣṭhā

`trm:nistha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निष्ठा
**Literal:** commitment, standing, steadfast devotion to

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The twofold commitment declared by Kṛṣṇa of old: by the yoga of knowledge for the sāṃkhyas and by the yoga of action for the yogins (3.3).
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Steadiness in practice, free from the obstacles of sleepiness, distraction and the like — the fifth stage.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 16–18: Standing: Arjuna asks the standing (niṣṭhā) of those who sacrifice with faith outside scriptural injunction (17.1); attaining Brahman is 'the highest culmination (niṣṭhā) of knowledge' (18.50).
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): In the six-bhakti scheme of the vacana and manual tradition, the devotion characteristic of the māheśvara stage. The SSM's māheśvara is marked by steadfastness in the liṅga (liṅganiṣṭhā, 10.21–22).
