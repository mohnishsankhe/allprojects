# sthāyibhāva

`trm:sthayibhava` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** permanent emotion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The permanent emotion underlying a rasa; in bhakti-rasa, love for Kṛṣṇa (kṛṣṇa-rati) in one of five primary or seven secondary forms.
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): The stable emotion that becomes rasa: love, laughter, grief, anger, energy, fear, disgust, wonder (NŚ 6.17), and for śānta calm, dispassion or knowledge of reality.
