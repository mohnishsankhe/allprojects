**Literal:** the dharma of one-pointed devotion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md): The dharma of the ekāntins, the best and dear to Nārāyaṇa, equal to Sāṃkhya-yoga, by which one goes to the imperishable Hari (MBh 12.336.4, 12.336.69); sung by the Lord to Arjuna before the battle (12.336.8).
