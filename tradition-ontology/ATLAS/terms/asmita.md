# asmitā

`trm:asmita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अस्मिता
**Literal:** I-am-ness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): (1) The affliction: the seeming identity of the power of the seer and the power of seeing (2.6). (2) The fourth level of samprajñāta samādhi, awareness of a single self (YBh 1.17). (3) 'Asmitā-mātra', the sixth unparticularized principle (YBh 2.19) and the source of created minds (4.4).

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
