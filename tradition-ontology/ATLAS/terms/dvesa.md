# dveṣa

`trm:dvesa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** द्वेष
**Literal:** aversion, hatred

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: With rāga, set in each sense towards its object — a waylayer not to be yielded to (3.34); senses free of rāga and dveṣa lead to serenity (2.64); the one of steady wisdom neither rejoices nor hates (2.57).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Aversion, which follows on pain (2.8).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): A mark and special quality of the self; as a fault, one of the three groups (NS 4.1.3); 'aversion is anger' (Tarkasaṅgraha).
