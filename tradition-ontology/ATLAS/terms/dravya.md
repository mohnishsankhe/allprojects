# dravya

`trm:dravya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** द्रव्य
**Literal:** substance

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): What possesses motion and qualities and is an inherent cause (VS 1.1.14 C); nine (VS 1.1.4 C).
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Lineage contribution (Āyurveda): the nine substances — five elements, self, mind, time, directions; with senses conscious, without them unconscious (Ca Sū 1.48); the inherent cause in which qualities and action reside (Sū 1.51); every substance is made of the five elements, and nothing in the world is not a medicine when rightly used (Sū 26.10-12).
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): Substance: synonymous with self, thing, own nature, body and reality; ultimately the Real that all words denote, as gold is denoted by all ornament-words (VP 3.2); Vyāḍi held that words denote the individual substance.
