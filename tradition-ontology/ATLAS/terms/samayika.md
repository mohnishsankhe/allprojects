# sāmāyika

`trm:samayika` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सामायिक
**Literal:** equanimity

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The practice of sitting in equanimity, renouncing all harmful activity for a fixed period (commonly 48 minutes); a lay vow (TS 7.21), the first of the five conducts (TS 9.18) and the first of the six essential duties.
