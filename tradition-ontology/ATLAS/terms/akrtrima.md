# akṛtrima

`trm:akrtrima` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** not made, uncontrived

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): ma bcos pa, 'uncontrived': the mind left as it is, without fabrication, correction or spoiling — the key of Mahāmudrā resting ('relax in the uncontrived natural state', Gaṅgā Mahāmudrā; 'rest undistracted, without fabricating or spoiling', Tilopa's Acintya).
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): One of the two defining marks of own-nature (with independence): what is not produced by causes and conditions (MMK 15.2).
