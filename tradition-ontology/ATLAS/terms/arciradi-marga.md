# arcirādi-mārga

`trm:arciradi-marga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अर्चिरादिमार्ग
**Literal:** the path beginning with light

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The path of the gods by which the freed soul travels from the body to Vaikuṇṭha, escorted by the deities of light, day, the bright fortnight, the northern course, the year and the rest.
- [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md): The path 'beginning with light': the stations of the path of the gods (BS 4.3.1–6).
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The path by which the knowers go to Brahmā's world, from which they reach the Supreme with Brahmā at the great dissolution.
