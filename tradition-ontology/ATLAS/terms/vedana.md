# vedanā

`trm:vedana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** feeling

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Pleasant, painful or neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling born of the six kinds of contact (SN 12.2); the second aggregate; the uninstructed adds a 'second dart' of mental pain (SN 36.6).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Feeling: the experiencing of the object's flavour as pleasant, painful or neutral (bodily or mental); a universal mental factor and an aggregate.
- [U Ba Khin – S. N. Goenka tradition (Sayagyi U Ba Khin lineage)](../lineages/u-ba-khin-goenka.md): Bodily sensation, observed objectively as the meeting-point of mind and matter; reaction to it with craving or aversion generates saṅkhāras (Goenka).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Feeling, the aggregate of experience; pleasant, painful or neutral.
