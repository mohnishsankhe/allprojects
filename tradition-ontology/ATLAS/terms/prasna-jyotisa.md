# praśna (interrogation)

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रश्न
**Literal:** question

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): Answering a question from the chart of the moment it is asked, together with the omens then present (Ṣaṭpañcāśikā; Praśna Mārga).
- [The Kerala tradition of jyotiṣa](../lineages/kerala-jyotisa.md): In Kerala the query is an elaborate rite (aṣṭamaṅgala-praśna, devaprāśna) diagnosing afflictions from curses and spirits and prescribing remedies (Praśna Mārga).
