**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अपुनरावृत्ति
**Literal:** non-return

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: The state of not coming back (to birth), reached by those whose intellect and self are set on That, who are established in That and have That as their goal and whose impurities are shaken off by knowledge (5.17).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Non-return to rebirth, reached by those who come to the Lord (BhG 8.15–16), by knowers who depart on the bright path (8.24), and by the knowers of the self (5.17).
