# prakāra

`trm:prakara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रकार
**Literal:** mode

## Definitions by tradition
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): A mode: an entity that exists only as the attribute of another (its prakārin) and cannot be known apart from it; souls and matter are modes of Brahman.
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): What appears in a cognition as qualifying something; its property is prakāratā.
