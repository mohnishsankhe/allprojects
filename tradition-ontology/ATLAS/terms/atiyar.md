# aṭiyār

`trm:atiyar` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tamil

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md): The Lord's servants, his devotees; the Āḻvārs hold the servants of his servants, of any birth, to be their masters.
- [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md): 'Those at the feet': Śiva's devotees, to be served and revered as Śiva himself.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): The freed soul should join the true devotees (meyyaṭiyār) and revere their garb as Śiva.
