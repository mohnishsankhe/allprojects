# kārya

`trm:karya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कार्य
**Literal:** effect

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): All that is dependent: vidyā, kalā and paśu - eternal, yet wholly subject to the Lord's will.
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): The meaning of Vedic sentences and of the injunction: the act as what is to be done, known only from the Veda; all words mean in connection with it (kāryānvitābhidhāna).
