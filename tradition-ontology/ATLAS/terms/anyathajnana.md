# anyathā-jñāna

`trm:anyathajnana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अन्यथाज्ञान
**Literal:** knowing otherwise; wrong cognition

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Wrong knowledge — for Madhva the real sense of 'dvaita' in the Māṇḍūkya: the soul's misapprehension (of its relation to the body and to God), which ceases by God's will when he is known.
