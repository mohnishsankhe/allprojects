# paryāya

`trm:paryaya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पर्याय
**Literal:** mode

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): A changing state of a substance; modes arise and cease while the substance persists (TS 1.26, 1.29, 5.38).
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The rotation of the worship of Kṛṣṇa at Uḍupi among the eight maṭhas; now two years per maṭha (a change credited to Vādirāja Tīrtha).
