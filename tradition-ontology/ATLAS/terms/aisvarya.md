# aiśvarya

`trm:aisvarya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ऐश्वर्य
**Literal:** power, lordship

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): A sāttvika disposition of buddhi (SK 23) giving non-obstruction (SK 45); the commentaries list eight powers — becoming minute (aṇimā), great, light, reaching, irresistible will, lordship, control and going wherever one wishes. In Sāṃkhya these powers are aiśvarya, not 'siddhi'; attachment to them is the error called moha.
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The eight lordly powers — minuteness, lightness, greatness, reach, irresistible will, control, lordship, and settling things at will (YBh 3.45); Īśvara's lordship, which has no equal or superior (YBh 1.24).
