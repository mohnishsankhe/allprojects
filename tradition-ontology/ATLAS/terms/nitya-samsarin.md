# nitya-saṃsārin

`trm:nitya-samsarin` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नित्यसंसारिन्
**Literal:** ever-transmigrating

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Souls unfit both for liberation and for blinding darkness, who remain forever in transmigration (the middling humans); Tattvasaṅkhyāna calls them 'sṛti-saṃsthita', Tattvaviveka 'nityāvarta'.

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [abhavya](abhavya.md) — Jain 'abhavya' souls, never able to attain liberation, are analogous to Dvaita's souls unfit for liberation; the grounds (karma-theory vs innate fitness) differ.
