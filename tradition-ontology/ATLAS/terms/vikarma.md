# vikarma

**Language:** Sanskrit

**Literal:** wrong or prohibited action

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Action of a contrary or forbidden kind, which must be understood along with action and inaction (BhG 4.17).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Named in 4.17 as one of three things to be understood along with action and inaction; the text does not define it (commentators: prohibited action, or action of various kinds).
