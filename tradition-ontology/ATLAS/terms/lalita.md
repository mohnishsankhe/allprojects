# lalitā

`trm:lalita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ललिता
**Literal:** the playful / lovely one

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The supreme Goddess of Śrīvidyā, 'she who plays': Mahātripurasundarī, seated on Kāmeśvara's lap in the Śrīcakra, whose form is the oneness of Śiva and Śakti (LSN 183).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The Goddess Tripurasundarī of the Lalitopākhyāna, who arises from the gods' sacrificial fire to slay Bhaṇḍāsura.
