# saṃsāra-suddhi

`trm:samsara-suddhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** purification through transmigration

## Definitions by tradition
- [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md): (As reported) The Ājīvika doctrine that beings are purified simply by passing through the whole fixed round of births.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): A doctrine of 'some ascetics and brahmins' rejected by the Buddha: purity does not come through transmigration.
