# kāmarūpa

`trm:kamarupa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कामरूप
**Literal:** form of desire

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The yoni-place between the root and svādhiṣṭhāna centres, called Kāmarūpa/Kāmākhyā (GŚ 10–11).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): The land (Assam) where the Kaula scripture is 'in every house of the yoginīs' and the seat Kāmākhyā (KJN 8.20; 22.10–12).
- [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md): Assam; the seat where Satī's yoni fell at Kāmagiri and Kāmākhyā dwells (Kālikā Purāṇa 18.42, 18.49).
- [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md): Kāmākhya is the 'beyond form' level of the fourfold yoni (Kubjikāmata 11.7).
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Where the Kaula scripture is found in every yoginī's house (Kaulajñānanirṇaya 22.10).
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): One of the four great seats (pīṭha), which in the Yoginīhṛdaya are transformations of the four fused powers and are located in the subtle body.
