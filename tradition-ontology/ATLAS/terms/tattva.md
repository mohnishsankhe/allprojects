# tattva

`trm:tattva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तत्त्व
**Literal:** that-ness; reality

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Whatever is real, i.e. not superimposed (anāropita) and an object of valid knowledge; twofold, independent (Viṣṇu) and dependent (everything else).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Each of the twenty-five principles: mūlaprakṛti, mahat (buddhi), ahaṃkāra, manas, the five sense capacities, the five action capacities, the five subtle elements, the five gross elements, and puruṣa (SK 3, 22; SS 1.61). Knowledge of them liberates.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): A principle of the Sāṃkhya enumeration: twenty-four together with prakṛti, the twenty-fifth beyond them (MBh 12.294.42); listed in the field of BhG 13.5.
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): The thirty-six principles, which are the waves and bubbles of Śiva as the ocean of consciousness (SSM 1.3).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): (1) Reality, to which the mind is joined and dissolves (HYP 4.59). (2) In the svara science, the five elements present in the flow of breath (Śiva Svarodaya).
- [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md): One of the thirty-six levels of manifestation from Śiva to earth (Paramārthasāra 11; PH 7).
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tamil tattuvam: the principles of the embodied person, counted in the Siddha tradition as ninety-six; realization burns them away (Aḻukaṇṇi 36).
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): A principle or level of reality in the ascending scale of 36 from earth to Śiva; the lower 31 (or so) are products of māyā, the pure five of bindu.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Tattuvam: 36 tattvas (24 ātma, 7 vidyā, 5 śiva), elaborated to 96 in the Tamil lists.

## Forms in other languages

- Tamil: tattuvam தத்துவம் — exact
