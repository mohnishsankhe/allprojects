# pralaya

`trm:pralaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रलय
**Literal:** dissolution

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The dissolution of beings or worlds, fourfold: constant, occasional, elemental and final.
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): A dissolution destroying everything, for which there is no means of knowledge; creation and dissolution are to be understood 'as today' (ŚV sambandhākṣepaparihāra 68, 113).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): The periodic dissolution of all products into atoms at the end of a Brahmā's lifespan by Maheśvara's desire (Praśastapāda).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Division of wholes does not end in nothingness, because atoms exist (NS 4.2.16).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Dissolution, fourfold: daily (deep sleep), occasional, elemental, and final (liberation).
