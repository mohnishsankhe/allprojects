**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विश्व
**Literal:** the all

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The form of Viṣṇu that presides over the soul's waking state.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The self as experiencer of waking, identified with the gross body (microcosmic counterpart of Virāj/Vaiśvānara).
