# kumbhaka

`trm:kumbhaka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कुम्भक
**Literal:** pot-like (holding)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): Breath-retention: of two kinds, sahita (with inhalation and exhalation) and kevala (alone) (HYP 2.71); eight named varieties in HYP 2.44 and a different eight in GS 5.47. Extreme retention is restricted in this ontology (summary only).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Retention of breath; sahita (with inhalation and exhalation) or kevala (spontaneous). Methods recorded summary-only.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): Retention, at whose end, as at the end of exhalation or inhalation, the Peaceful shines (VBT 27).
