# kumbhaka

`trm:kumbhaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कुम्भक
**Literal:** pot-like (retention)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Retention of breath; sahita (with inhalation and exhalation) or kevala (spontaneous). Methods recorded summary-only.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): Retention, at whose end, as at the end of exhalation or inhalation, the Peaceful shines (VBT 27).
