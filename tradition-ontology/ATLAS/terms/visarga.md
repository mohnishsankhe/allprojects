# visarga

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** विसर्ग
**Literal:** secondary emission

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): In the Bhāgavata, the secondary creation by Brahmā (the Puruṣa's creation) from the imbalance of the guṇas (BhP 2.10.3).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The emissive power of the Goddess (VBT 24) and the letter ḥ at whose end awareness is placed (VBT 91).
