# wunian (no-thought)

`trm:wunian` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 無念
**Literal:** no thought

## Definitions by tradition
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): The tenet of the Platform Sūtra: being amid thoughts yet not thinking, the mind not stained by objects; not a blank absence of thought.
- [Heze school](../lineages/heze.md): The tenet of Shenhui's teaching.
- [Jingzhong school](../lineages/jingzhong.md): Musang's 'no thinking' = samādhi.
- [Baotang school](../lineages/baotang.md): For Wuzhu, no-thought includes precepts, samādhi and wisdom and replaces all ritual.

## Forms in other languages
- Japanese: munen  — exact
- Korean: munyeom  — exact
- Sanskrit: asmṛti / nirvikalpa (no single equivalent)  — partial

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- contested: [nirvikalpa](nirvikalpa.md) — Both mean the absence of conceptual thought, but the Platform Sūtra insists no-thought is thought of suchness, not a cessation of thought; Advaita and Yogācāra uses of nirvikalpa have different bases.
