# pratyavāya

`trm:pratyavaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रत्यवाय
**Literal:** falling back; demerit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md): The demerit incurred by omitting enjoined (obligatory, occasional) acts; the seeker of release performs them to avoid it (ŚV sambandhākṣepaparihāra 110; Tantravārttika).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The adverse result which is not found in the yoga of understanding (2.40).
