# āsava

`trm:asava` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** effluent, taint, canker

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The effluents of sensuality, becoming and ignorance (MN 9; DN 2), with views as a fourth in some lists; their destruction is arahantship.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Taints: of sensual desire, existence, views and ignorance (four in the Abhidhamma).

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: āsrava  — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [āsrava](asrava.md) — same word; in Jainism āsrava is the influx of karmic matter
