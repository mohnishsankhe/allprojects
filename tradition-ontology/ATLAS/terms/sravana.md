# śravaṇa

`trm:sravana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** श्रवण
**Literal:** hearing

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Study of the true scriptures and their correct interpretation from a teacher; the first principal means to knowledge, never to be reduced.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Hearing the Lord's names, qualities and deeds, the first form of devotion and the beginning of the path (BhP 1.2.16-21); for the Śiva Purāṇa the first of the three great means.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Hearing: ascertaining, under a teacher and by the six marks of purport, that the Upaniṣads teach the non-dual Brahman; for the Vivaraṇa the principal means.
- [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md): Hearing the Lord's qualities and stories: with singing, a means to devotion even in worldly life (NBS 37); Garga's definition of devotion is attachment to hearing his stories (NBS 17).
