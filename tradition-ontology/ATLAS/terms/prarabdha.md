# prārabdha

`trm:prarabdha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** प्रारब्ध
**Literal:** begun (karma)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Karma that has begun to bear fruit; for the knower it moves the body but does not bind; its exhaustion brings bodiless liberation.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Karma that has begun to fruit and keeps the body of the liberated alive until exhausted (BhP 3.28.38: 'the body ... lives on while its karma lasts').
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): Karma that has begun to bear fruit in the present body and is exhausted by experiencing it; for the surrendered the Lord may end it at once.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): The destiny karma that made the present body; not altered by the Master, only borne with detachment.
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): In jyotiṣa (interpretive gloss): the karma 'earned in a former birth' whose ripening the horā reveals as a lamp reveals objects (BJ 1.3; LJ 1.3). The jyotiṣa texts say 'karma of a former birth'; the Vedāntic word prārabdha is applied by later practitioners.
