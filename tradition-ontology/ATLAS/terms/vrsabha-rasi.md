## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The second sign (Taurus), 30° of the zodiac made of nine asterism-quarters; the face of Kālapuruṣa (BJ 1.4); ruled by Venus; fixed; of the earth triplicity (later texts); even, female and gentle (BJ 1.11); Varāhamihira's alternative (Greek-derived) name: tāvuri (BJ 1.8); dignities: Moon exalted (3°) (BJ 1.13).
