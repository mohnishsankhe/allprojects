# śiva

`trm:siva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शिव
**Literal:** auspicious; kind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): In the Saṃhitās an adjective of Rudra's auspicious, non-terrible form (TS 4.5.1) and a name in 'namaḥ śivāya ca śivatarāya ca' (TS 4.5.8); Rudra 'auspicious, go beyond' (VS 3.61).
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Śambhu, dense with consciousness and bliss, so called because pure from the beginning, free of the three impurities and the abode of all auspiciousness; the two syllables reveal supreme brahman in their primary sense (SSM 8.8–11); the ocean of consciousness whose waves are the thirty-six tattvas (1.3).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Auspicious; Śiva: 'therefore he is Śiva, present everywhere' (ŚU 3.11); 'knowing Śiva hidden in all beings' (4.14-16); 'Śiva alone' (4.18); the fourth is śiva, auspicious (MāU 7).
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Name of the Lord in the closing prayer 'may Śiva be mine, always Śiva'.
- [Kālāmukha](../lineages/kalamukha.md): The deity of the Kālāmukha temples (e.g. Kedāreśvara at Balligāve).
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Pati, the one Lord, eternally free; the efficient cause; not the material of the world; the guru of all.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): In the Śaiva and yoga Upaniṣads the supreme Lord and the jīva's true nature: 'the jīva in the body is Śiva alone'; the jīva freed of the twenty faults is called Śiva.
