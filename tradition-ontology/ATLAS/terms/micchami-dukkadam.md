# micchāmi dukkaḍaṃ

`trm:micchami-dukkadam` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Prakrit
**Native script:** मिच्छामि दुक्कडं
**Literal:** may my misdeed be in vain

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): 'May my wrongdoing be fruitless' — the formula of repentance in confession, and the greeting by which Jains ask one another's forgiveness at Saṃvatsarī.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The Prakrit formula of repentance and request for forgiveness spoken in pratikramaṇa and at Paryuṣaṇa.
