# dahara

`trm:dahara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** small, subtle

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The small (space): 'in this city of brahman is a small lotus-dwelling, within it a small space; what is within that is to be sought' (ChU 8.1.1).
- [Śiva-viśiṣṭādvaita (Śaiva Vedānta of Śrīkaṇṭha)](../lineages/siva-visistadvaita.md): The small space in the lotus of the heart (Chāndogya 8.1), identified with Śiva as the supreme ether and the seat of meditation.
