# śava-sādhana

`trm:sava-sadhana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शवसाधन
**Literal:** practice on a corpse

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): A vīra rite performed seated on a corpse in the cremation ground to win the Goddess's vision (as in the account of Sarvānanda). Restricted: named only.
- [Aghora (Aghor panth; Aghorī and Aughaṛ ascetics)](../lineages/aghora.md): Practice seated on a corpse in the cremation ground, attributed to Aghora and Śākta adepts; restricted — summary only.
