# āyurdāya

`trm:ayurdaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आयुर्दाय
**Literal:** allotment of life

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The 'gift of life': methods of computing the length of life from the planets' positions — the rival methods of Maya, the Yavanas, Maṇittha, Parāśara, Jīvaśarman and Satya, of which Varāhamihira prefers Satya's (BJ 7).
