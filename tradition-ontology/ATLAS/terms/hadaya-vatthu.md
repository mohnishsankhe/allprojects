# hadaya-vatthu

`trm:hadaya-vatthu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** heart-base

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The material base of the mind-element and mind-consciousness-element, identified by the commentaries as the heart; the Paṭṭhāna speaks only of 'the matter in dependence on which' they occur, and the Dhammasaṅgaṇī does not list it.
