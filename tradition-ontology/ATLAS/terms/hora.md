**Language:** Sanskrit

**Literal:** hour; half-sign

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): (1) Horoscopy, the branch revealing the ripening of karma of former births; the word is said to come from aho-rātra by dropping first and last syllables (BJ 1.3). (2) Half of a sign, ruled by the Sun or Moon (BJ 1.11). (3) A planetary hour.
