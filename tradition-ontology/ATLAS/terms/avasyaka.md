# āvaśyaka

`trm:avasyaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** आवश्यक
**Literal:** essential [duty]

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The six obligatory daily practices: equanimity, praise of the twenty-four, salutation, repentance, body-abandonment and renunciation (for householders the Digambaras also teach six daily duties: worship, service of the teacher, study, restraint, austerity, giving).
