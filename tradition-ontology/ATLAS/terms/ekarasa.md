# ekarasa

`trm:ekarasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** single taste

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): One taste (ro gcig): the realization that all dharmas are of one taste in the innate (Queen Dohā v10; Tōh 2292 no. 20).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): ro gcig: 'one taste', the third yoga of Mahāmudrā — appearances and mind, saṃsāra and nirvāṇa, meditation and post-meditation are realized as of one taste.
