# sāmīpya

`trm:samipya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सामीप्य
**Literal:** nearness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Form of liberation: dwelling near the Lord.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Liberation as nearness to the Lord.
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Liberation as proximity to the Lord.
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Nearness to the Lord — one of the four kinds of liberation.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Nearness to Śiva; fruit of kriyā.
