# sāmānyalakṣaṇa

`trm:samanyalaksana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सामान्यलक्षणा
**Literal:** general characteristic; universal

## Definitions by tradition
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): Anything other than the particular: a conceptual construction not capable of causal efficacy, conventionally real, the object of inference and of words, consisting in exclusion of others.
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Extraordinary contact by which all instances of a universal (all smoke) are grasped, grounding knowledge of pervasion.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The general characteristics of conditioned dharmas — impermanent, suffering, empty, non-self — contemplated in the combined application of mindfulness.

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- contested: [jāti](jati.md) — Vaiśeṣika and Nyāya hold universals (jāti) real, eternal and inherent in individuals; the Buddhists hold the general characteristic a conceptual construction.
