# sāmānyalakṣaṇā (pratyāsatti)

`trm:samanyalaksana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सामान्यलक्षणा
**Literal:** contact through the universal

## Definitions by tradition
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): Extraordinary contact by which all instances of a universal (all smoke) are grasped, grounding knowledge of pervasion.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): The general characteristics of conditioned dharmas — impermanent, suffering, empty, non-self — contemplated in the combined application of mindfulness.
