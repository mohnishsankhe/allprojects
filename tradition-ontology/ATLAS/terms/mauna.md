# mauna

**Language:** Sanskrit

**Literal:** silence

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Silence, part of mental austerity (BhG 17.16) and mark of the dear devotee (12.19); 'of secrets I am silence' (10.38); for Sanatsujāta, the true muni's silence (MBh 5.42–43).
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tamil mauṉam: silence — Pattirakiriyār's 'taking the form of a mute' (19); the silent guru of Tāyumāṉavar.
