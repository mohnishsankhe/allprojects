- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Silence: of secret things the Lord is silence (10.38); the dear devotee is silent (maunin) (12.19).
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): Tamil mauṉam: silence — Pattirakiriyār's 'taking the form of a mute' (19); the silent guru of Tāyumāṉavar.
