# karmasāmya

`trm:karmasamya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कर्मसाम्य
**Literal:** equality (balance) of karma

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): The state in which opposed karmas are equal, neither exceeding the other, marking the time of śaktipāta.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The equality of karmas that the soul attains when looked upon by Viṣṇu with grace; the two karmas fall silent.
