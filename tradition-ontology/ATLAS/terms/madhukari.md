# mādhukarī

`trm:madhukari` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** माधुकरी
**Literal:** bee-like (begging)

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The bee's way of begging: taking a little food from many houses without troubling any.
- [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md): Alms gathered a little from many houses, after the cooking fires are out.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The bee's way of alms: taking a little from each house as the bee from flowers, harming none (Daśavaikālika 1).
