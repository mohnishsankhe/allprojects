## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The deity with whom self-study brings contact (2.44); Vyāsa: gods, sages and perfected beings appear to the one devoted to self-study (YBh 2.44).
- [Tantra as a movement across the traditions (Tantraśāstra; the Mantramārga / Mantranaya current)](../lineages/tantra-movement.md): The chosen deity of an initiate, whose mantra he receives and with whom he identifies in worship.
