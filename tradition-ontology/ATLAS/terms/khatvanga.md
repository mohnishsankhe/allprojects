# khaṭvāṅga

`trm:khatvanga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** खट्वाङ्ग
**Literal:** skull-topped staff

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kāpālika (Somasiddhānta)](../lineages/kapalika.md): Staff crowned with a skull, a sub-insignia of the Kāpālas; its bell rings as Kapālakuṇḍalā flies.
- [Lākula](../lineages/lakula.md): Carried with the skull in the lokātīta observance.
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The skull-topped staff carried by herukas and yogins (Queen Dohā v50–51).
