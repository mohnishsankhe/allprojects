# sāmānyatodṛṣṭa

`trm:samanyatodrsta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सामान्यतोदृष्ट
**Literal:** 'seen in general'

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Inference from general correlation, by which things beyond the senses are known (SK 6), such as the senses as instruments (Vācaspati), and both prakṛti and puruṣa (SS 1.103).
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): One of the three kinds of inference named in NS 1.1.5; the Bhāṣya offers more than one explanation.
