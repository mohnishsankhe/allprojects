# saṃvatsara

`trm:samvatsara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** संवत्सर
**Literal:** year

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The year, born from the ocean (RV 10.190.2), the twelve-spoked wheel with 720 sons (1.164.11) or 360 spokes (1.164.48).
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): In jyotiṣa, (1) the first year of the Vedic five-year yuga (VJ; BS 8.24); (2) a year of the sixty-year Jupiter cycle beginning with Prabhava (BS 8.27–29); (3) 'sāṃvatsara' also names the astrologer (BS 2).
