# mahāmudrā

`trm:mahamudra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** महामुद्रा
**Literal:** great seal

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The first of the ten mudrās; kuṇḍalinī becomes straight like a snake struck with a stick (HYP 3.10–14); first taught in the Amṛtasiddhi. (Not the Buddhist Mahāmudrā realization.)
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): The result continuum of the Lamdre, reached through the four result-empowerments; for Sakya Paṇḍita, only the wisdom born of empowerment and the two stages is the Mahāmudrā the Buddha taught.
- [Ganden Oral Lineage of Mahāmudrā (Ganden Kagyu / Ensa Hearing Lineage)](../lineages/ganden-oral-lineage.md): Approached through sūtra (emptiness of mind as taught by Nāgārjuna) and through mantra (the clear light of great bliss).
- [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md): The 'great seal': the innate nature of mind, realized through the guru's instruction, beyond meditation and non-meditation; 'mind without aim is mahāmudrā' (Gaṅgāmā v17).
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The third of the four seals: without own-being, like the clear autumn midday sky, the single nature of existence and nirvāṇa, whose body is objectless compassion and whose form is great bliss (Advayavajra); the supreme siddhi reached by the buddhas (Pañcakrama 5.28).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): The 'great seal': the nature of one's own mind — empty, luminous, unborn — and its realization, which 'seals' all phenomena; also the path and instructions leading to it. Phyag is non-dual wisdom seizing (it), rgya is the release of saṃsāra's knot, chen po is union, the self-liberated dharmakāya (Karṇatantravajrapada). Distinct from the haṭha-yoga seal of the same name.
- [Karma Kagyu (Kamtsang Kagyu)](../lineages/karma-kagyu.md): Taught as ground, path and fruit: ground — mind itself, clarity-emptiness; path — the vajra yoga of Mahāmudrā; fruit — the stainless dharmakāya (3rd Karmapa).
- [Shangpa Kagyu](../lineages/shangpa-kagyu.md): The Mahāmudrā 'of the amulet box': ground, path and fruit enclosed together; the mind left uncontrived is self-liberated.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): In the haṭha sense, the 'great seal', a posture-and-breath practice destroying sins (GŚ 32–33).
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): In Mahāyoga the third vidyādhara level (the mahāmudrā vidyādhara, body as the deity's form); the 'great seal' of the deity in generation-stage practice.
- [Chöd (gcod), 'Cutting'](../lineages/chod.md): 'Mahāmudrā Chöd': Machig's Chöd understood as resting in the mind's nature once clinging is cut.

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- contested: [rig pa](rigpa.md) — Kagyu authors (Karma Chagmé, Tselé) treat Mahāmudrā's mind-nature and Dzogchen's rigpa as one in essence; Nyingma and Kagyu authors also distinguish their methods; not an agreed equivalence.
