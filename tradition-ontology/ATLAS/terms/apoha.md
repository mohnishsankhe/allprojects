**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): The meaning of words and object of inference: not a real universal but the exclusion of what is other (anyāpoha) — for Ratnakīrti a positive thing qualified by the exclusion of others; explains how concepts group particulars without universals.
