- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The self as what the word and awareness 'I' refer to; not the ego-evolute of prakṛti but the soul itself, which persists as 'I' in deep sleep and liberation.
