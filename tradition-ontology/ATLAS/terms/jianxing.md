# jianxing / kenshō (seeing the nature)

`trm:jianxing` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Chinese
**Native script:** 見性
**Literal:** seeing (one's) nature

## Definitions by tradition
- [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md): Directly seeing one's own nature at the words, the aim of Hongren's test and Huineng's teaching.
- [Rinzai Zen](../lineages/rinzai.md): The breakthrough awakening of kōan practice, to be followed by post-awakening training.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): In the Śūraṅgama the seeing-nature which does not move, age or perish, shown as the true mind.
