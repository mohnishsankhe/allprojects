# cāujjāma

`trm:caujjama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Prakrit
**Native script:** चाउज्जाम
**Literal:** four restraints

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The 'fourfold restraint' taught by Pārśva — from violence, falsehood, theft and possessions — which Mahāvīra expanded into five great vows by making chastity explicit.

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: cāturyāma  — exact
- Pali: cātuyāma-saṃvara  — partial — the Pali canon attributes a 'fourfold restraint' to Nigaṇṭha Nātaputta
