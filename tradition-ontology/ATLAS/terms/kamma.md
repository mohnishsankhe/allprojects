# kamma

`trm:kamma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** deed, action

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Intention (cetanā): 'having intended, one acts by body, speech and mind' (AN 6.63); beings are owners and heirs of their deeds (MN 135); kamma ripens in this life, the next or later (AN 3.34; AN 6.63) and is one of the four unthinkables in its details (AN 4.77); four kinds, dark, bright, mixed and neither, the last leading to kamma's end (AN 4.232).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Volition (cetanā) and the wholesome or unwholesome action it motivates; kamma condition for results and kamma-born matter; classified by function, weight, time and place.

## Forms in other languages

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
