# cāturmāsa

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** चातुर्मास
**Literal:** four-month period

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The four months of the rains during which mendicants stop wandering and stay in one place to avoid harming the many beings that appear, the season of teaching, austerities and Paryuṣaṇa for the laity.
