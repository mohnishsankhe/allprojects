# apratiṣṭhita-citta

`trm:apratisthita-citta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** the unsupported mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Mind (or giving) not resting on any object — form, sound, dharma — as the Tathāgata's mind stands nowhere; Kumārajīva: 'produce a mind that abides nowhere'.
