# yoniso manasikāra

`trm:yoniso-manasikara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** wise (appropriate) attention

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Attention that goes to the source: by it effluents do not arise and are abandoned (MN 2); one of the two conditions for right view (MN 43) and a factor of stream-entry (SN 55.5); the bodhisatta's means of penetrating dependent origination (DN 14).
