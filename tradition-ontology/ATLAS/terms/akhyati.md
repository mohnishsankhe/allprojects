## Definitions by tradition
- [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md): Error is not the appearance of an unreal object but the non-apprehension of the difference between a perception ('this') and a memory ('silver'); ignorance itself is non-apprehension.
