**Literal:** mistress of the cakra

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The presiding goddess of each enclosure of the Śrīcakra: Tripurā, Tripureśī, Tripurasundarī, Tripuravāsinī, Tripurāśrī, Tripuramālinī, Tripurāsiddhā, Tripurāmbā and, at the centre, Mahātripurasundarī.
