# anuvyavasāya

`trm:anuvyavasaya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अनुव्यवसाय
**Literal:** after-cognition; introspective cognition

## Definitions by tradition
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): A mental perception, following a cognition, of the form 'I know this'; the means by which a cognition is known, since a cognition does not reveal itself.
- [Murāri Miśra's school (the 'third path' of Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/murari-mimamsa.md): The reflective cognition 'I know this' that follows a cognition; for Murāri Miśra it grasps the first cognition's validity (as reported by Gaṅgeśa).
