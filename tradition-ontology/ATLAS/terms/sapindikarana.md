# sapiṇḍīkaraṇa

`trm:sapindikarana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सपिण्डीकरण
**Literal:** making co-piṇḍa

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The rite that joins the preta to the three generations of ancestors, ending the preta state; done on the twelfth day or up to a year after death.
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The rite (on the twelfth day or after a year) that unites the deceased with his three forefathers, turning the preta into a pitṛ.
