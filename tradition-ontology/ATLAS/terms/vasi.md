# vasī

`trm:vasi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Native script:** வாசி
**Literal:** mastery

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The five masteries of a jhāna: adverting, attaining, resolving, emerging, reviewing.
- [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md): The breath treated as a horse to be mastered: the Siddhar breath-yoga (vāci yōkam) that stills and turns the breath inward and is held to perfect the body and lengthen life (summary; see prc:vasi-yoga).

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: vāyu / prāṇa  — partial
- Sanskrit: vājin  — partial — horse

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [prāṇa](prana.md) — vāci is the breath as the object of mastery
