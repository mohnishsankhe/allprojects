**Language:** Sanskrit

**Literal:** beautiful one of the three cities

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): 'The beauty of the three cities': the Goddess of Śrīvidyā; as Mahātripurasundarī the queen of Mahākāmeśa (LSN 57).
- [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md): As a Mahāvidyā, worshipped with the five-faced Śiva as her Bhairava (Toḍala 1.7-9) and matched with Paraśurāma (10.10); her full cult is Śrīvidyā (U23).
