# saṃyoga

`trm:samyoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** संयोग
**Literal:** conjunction

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The conjunction of puruṣa and prakṛti, like that of the lame and the blind, from which creation proceeds (SK 21) and the unconscious appears conscious (SK 20); due to non-discrimination (SS 1.55); though it remains after knowledge, there is no motive for creation (SK 66).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The conjunction of seer and seen, cause of what is to be avoided (2.17), caused by ignorance (2.24), the cause of apprehending owner and owned (2.23); its absence is kaivalya (2.25).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: Union: every being, stationary or moving, arises from the union of the field and the knower of the field (13.27).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Cause of the usage 'conjoined'; produced by the motion of one or both, or by another conjunction (VS 7.2.10 C).
