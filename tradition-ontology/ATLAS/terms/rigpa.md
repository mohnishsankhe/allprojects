# rig pa

`trm:rigpa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** རིག་པ
**Literal:** knowing, awareness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md): Awareness: the knowing, luminous nature of mind recognized as the ground itself — empty, clear, unobstructed and unfabricated. It is distinguished from ordinary mind (sems), which grasps at objects; recognizing rig pa through the master's introduction is the entry to the Great Perfection.
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): In the Nyingma generally, the awareness that is the fruit of the inner tantras and the object of the introduction; 'awareness-holder' (rig 'dzin, vidyādhara) names those who hold it.
- [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md): In Bön Dzogchen, awareness inseparable from the base of all (kun gzhi); their union is the natural state.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): Awareness: the knowing, clear aspect of mind's nature; in Kagyu Mahāmudrā texts used of self-aware mind (rang rig) that is clear because it is awareness and non-conceptual (Nāropa); 'leave awareness naked' (Gaṅgā Mahāmudrā).

## Forms in other languages
