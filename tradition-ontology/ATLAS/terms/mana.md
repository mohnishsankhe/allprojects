# māna

`trm:mana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** conceit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Conceit — 'I am better, equal, worse' (Snp 4.9); the eighth fetter, abandoned only at arahantship.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Conceit: measuring oneself as better, equal or worse; a fetter abandoned only by arahantship.
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Love-pique: love so intense it takes the form of feigned anger or sulking, a stage above sneha.
- `lin:jagannatha`: Rādhā's jealous pride that makes her withdraw from Kṛṣṇa; the friend counsels her to give it up, and Kṛṣṇa pleads with her (GG 2, 9, 10).
