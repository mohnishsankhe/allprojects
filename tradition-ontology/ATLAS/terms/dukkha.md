# dukkha

`trm:dukkha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** suffering, unsatisfactoriness, pain

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): The first noble truth: birth, ageing, illness, death, union with the disliked, separation from the liked, not getting what one wants — in brief the five aggregates of clinging (SN 56.11); also one of the three marks, applying to all formations (AN 3.136).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The second characteristic and first truth; 'there is suffering but no one who suffers'.

## Forms in other languages
- Sanskrit: duḥkha  — exact

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- exact: [duḥkha](duhkha.md) — same word; Sāṃkhya and Yoga also begin from duḥkha, with a different account of its cause and end
