# paryuṣaṇa

`trm:paryusana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पर्युषण
**Literal:** abiding (for the rains)

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The eight-day festival at the height of the rains (Bhādrapada), of fasting, confession and forgiveness, with the public reading of the Kalpa Sūtra, ending in Saṃvatsarī.
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): The Digambaras keep the ten days of Das Lakṣaṇa (the ten dharmas) at the same season.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The chief Jain festival at the end of the rains-retreat season — eight days for Śvetāmbaras ending with forgiveness, ten days of the ten virtues (Daśalakṣaṇa) for Digambaras.
