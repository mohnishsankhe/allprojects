# nirveda

`trm:nirveda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निर्वेद
**Literal:** weariness, disenchantment

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Disgust or disenchantment with worldly aims, the sword that cuts the noose of hope; for the Bhikṣu a raft sent by the Lord.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: When understanding crosses the thicket of delusion, one reaches nirveda towards what is to be heard and what has been heard (2.52).
- [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md): Despondency, world-weariness: the first of the thirty-three transient states (NŚ 6.18), made by Mammaṭa the stable emotion of śānta.
