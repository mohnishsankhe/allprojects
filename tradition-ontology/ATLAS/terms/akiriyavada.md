# akiriyavāda

`trm:akiriyavada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** doctrine of non-action

## Definitions by tradition
- [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md): (As reported) Pūraṇa's doctrine that the doer does no evil and the giver gains no merit.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): One of the four 'assemblies' of disputants: those who deny action; 84 kinds; they hold the world barren and fixed.
