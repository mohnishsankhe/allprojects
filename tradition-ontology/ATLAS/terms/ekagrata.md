# ekāgratā

`trm:ekagrata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** एकाग्रता
**Literal:** one-pointedness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): One-pointedness of mind, a mental property that rises as all-objectness dwindles (3.11–12); a fruit of purity (2.41).
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): rtse gcig: 'one-pointedness', the first of the four yogas of Mahāmudrā — the mind rests one-pointedly in clarity and emptiness, with experiences of bliss, clarity and non-thought, in lesser, middling and greater degrees.
