# ākāra

`trm:akara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** image; aspect; form

## Definitions by tradition
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): The form of the object borne by cognition; the cognition's likeness to the object (arthasārūpya) is the means of knowledge.
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): Whether the images of cognition are real or false divides late Yogācāra (sākāra/nirākāra, satyākāra/alīkākāra).
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The first letter, in which the whole perfection of wisdom is contained (Ekākṣarī PP).
