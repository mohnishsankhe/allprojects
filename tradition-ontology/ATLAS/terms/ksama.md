# kṣamā

`trm:ksama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** क्षमा
**Literal:** forgiveness, forbearance

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Forgiveness, 'the highest peace' (MBh 5.33.48) and 'the highest strength' (3.203.41); a mark of the dear devotee (BhG 12.13).
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Forbearance, the first of the ten dharmas ('supreme forbearance', uttama-kṣamā), the virtue by which anger is conquered.
