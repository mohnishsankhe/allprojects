# paññatti

`trm:pannatti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** making known; designation, concept

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): A concept or designation: either the concept as meaning, which is made known (e.g. 'person', 'direction', a kasiṇa sign), or the concept as name, which makes it known; it lacks intrinsic nature and is not an ultimate reality.
