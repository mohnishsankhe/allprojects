## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 15.15: from Kṛṣṇa, seated in the heart of all, come memory, knowledge and apohana — taken by most commentators as their loss, by some as reasoning.
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): One of the Lord's three powers with cognition and memory (ĪPK 1.3.7); every determinate awareness arises by exclusion of an opposite (1.6.5) - a reworking of the Buddhist apoha.
