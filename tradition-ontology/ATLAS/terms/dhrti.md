# dhṛti

`trm:dhrti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** धृति
**Literal:** resolve, steadiness

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The restraining resolve whose lapse leaves the mind unable to turn from the harmful (Ca Śā 1.100); with smṛti and dhī the means of stopping the stream of body and mind (Śā 2.42).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Steadfastness, threefold by the guṇas; the sāttvic holds mind, breath and senses by unswerving yoga (BhG 18.33–35).
