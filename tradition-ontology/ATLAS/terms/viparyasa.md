# viparyāsa

`trm:viparyasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** inversion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The four inversions (taking the impermanent as permanent, etc.); the Mahāparinirvāṇa and Śrīmālā add a second set — taking the Tathāgata's permanence, bliss, self and purity as their opposites.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Perversion; four — taking the impermanent as permanent, the painful as pleasant, the impure as pure, the non-self as self.
