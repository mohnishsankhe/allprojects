# saguṇa

`trm:saguna` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** with qualities

## Definitions by tradition
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): With attributes: Brahman as associated with māyā — Īśvara, omniscient and omnipotent, object of worship and meditation; real at the empirical level.
- [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md): The embodied Rāma: the formless becomes embodied, overcome by the devotee's love; not different from the formless.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): The Lord with qualities and form — the avatāras and the images of temple worship; for the Sants not the final object of devotion (Kabīr: the son of Daśaratha is praised, but the secret of Rām's name is other).
