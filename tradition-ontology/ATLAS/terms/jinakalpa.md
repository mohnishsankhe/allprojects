# jinakalpa

`trm:jinakalpa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** जिनकल्प
**Literal:** the Jinas' mode

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): The 'mode of the Jinas': the solitary, naked (or nearly equipment-less), hand-eating ascetic life of highest rigour, open in the Śvetāmbara view only to those with special capacities and discontinued after Jambū.
