## Definitions by tradition
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): A medical image for a remedy that alone cures all illnesses: recognition of mind's nature as by itself sufficient for awakening (Gampopa, Lama Zhang).
- [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md): In Sakya Paṇḍita's critique: a revival of the Chinese Hashang's teaching, not Mahāmudrā.
