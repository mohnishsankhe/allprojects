# tīrtha

`trm:tirtha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तीर्थ
**Literal:** ford, crossing

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): A holy place of pilgrimage and bathing; the virtues are inner tīrthas and the purest mind the supreme tīrtha; devotees are living tīrthas.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): A sacred ford; Vidura calls the self a river with sacred fords whose water is truth, in which one is purified (MBh 5.40.19).
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Pilgrim place; the Sants deny that bathing at tīrthas purifies — the true Gaṅgā is in a pure mind.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): The pilgrimage is within: 'the original Mecca is in this human body'.
