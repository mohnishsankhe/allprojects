# arahant

`trm:arahant` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** worthy one

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): MN 118:9: a monk with the taints destroyed, who has lived the holy life, done what was to be done, laid down the burden, reached his own goal, destroyed the fetters of becoming, and is liberated by right knowledge.
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): One whose effluents are destroyed, who has lived the holy life and done what was to be done (DN 2); has ten factors (MN 117).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): One who has destroyed the taints; the Kathāvatthu denies that an arahant can fall away, have ignorance or doubt, be defiled by deities, or remain a householder.
