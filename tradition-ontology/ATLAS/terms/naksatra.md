# nakṣatra

`trm:naksatra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नक्षत्र
**Literal:** lunar mansion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): One of the twenty-seven (or twenty-eight) star-groups through which the moon passes, used to fix the times of rites (Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa; ŚB 2.1.2.3 on the Kṛttikās).
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): In horā and muhūrta, one of the 27 lunar mansions of 13°20′ (with Abhijit as 28th in muhūrta), each with a deity, a Viṃśottarī lord and a class of suitable actions (BS 97; MC 2.1–8); in the siddhāntas, the Lord's own twenty-sevenfold division (SS 12.25).
