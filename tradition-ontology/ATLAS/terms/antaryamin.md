# antaryāmin

`trm:antaryamin` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अन्तर्यामिन्
**Literal:** the one who controls from within

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): In this text: inner controller, one of the names of "this one" (eṣaḥ) in MU 6, together with lord of all, knower of all and source of all; the verse does not name its referent (commentators: prājña).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The inner controller: 'he who dwells in the earth yet is other than the earth, whom the earth does not know, whose body the earth is, who controls it from within' — the self, the immortal (BAU 3.7).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The Lord dwelling within every soul and thing as its self and controller, and, in a special form, in the heart of the meditator.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The Lord dwelling within beings, one of his five modes.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): The inner controller: Īśvara as the consciousness governing each being from within (BĀU 3.7), the 'starched canvas' of the Citradīpa.
