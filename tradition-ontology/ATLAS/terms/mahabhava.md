# mahābhāva

`trm:mahabhava` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Great emotion, the summit of love, found only in the gopīs and fully in Rādhā; divided into rūḍha and adhirūḍha (with modana and mādana, the latter in Rādhā alone).
- [Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā](../lineages/vaisnava-sahajiya.md): The supreme love of Rādhā, which the Sahajiyā sees embodied in Caitanya and attainable in the sahaja state of the couple.
