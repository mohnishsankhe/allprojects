# naraka

`trm:naraka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नरक
**Literal:** hell

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: In Arjuna's argument: varṇa-mixture leads family-destroyers and the family to hell (1.42); those whose family dharmas are uprooted dwell in hell for an indefinite time, as 'we have heard' (1.44).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): A place of torment for the dead, below the earth in the south within the three worlds, ruled by Yama; twenty-eight are named in BhP 5.26; the VP also calls heaven what pleases the mind and hell its opposite.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The seven infernal earths below the middle world and their beings, who suffer by their own leśyā, one another, and (in the first three) the asuras (TS 3.1–6).
