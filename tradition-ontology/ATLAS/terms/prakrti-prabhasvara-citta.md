# prakṛti-prabhāsvara-citta

`trm:prakrti-prabhasvara-citta` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** naturally luminous mind

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāsāṃghika](../lineages/mahasanghika.md): Mind luminous by nature, defiled by adventitious defilements (Vasumitra).
- [Vibhajyavāda](../lineages/vibhajyavada.md): Attributed to the Vibhajyavādins and rejected by the Vibhāṣā.
