# basti / vasti

`trm:basti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** बस्ति
**Literal:** bladder; enema

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The second cleansing act: washing the colon by drawing in water while squatting in navel-deep water (jala-basti); the GS adds a 'dry' basti on the ground.
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Medicated enema (decoction or oil), the chief remedy for vāta (AHS Sū 1.25; Ca Sū 25.40 'best of vāta-removers').
