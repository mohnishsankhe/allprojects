# pāśa

`trm:pasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पाश
**Literal:** noose

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The noose of māyā with which the Lord binds the paśus in play.
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The bonds are the kalās called effects and instruments, by which the paśus are bound and made subject to sense-objects.
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Bond; the eight bonds beginning with disgust whose destruction is liberation while living (Dīpikā on YH 1.86); burned away by the guru at initiation (PKS 1.36); in the Goddess's hand the noose is attachment (LSN 2).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The fetter of Varuṇa (or of death, Nirṛti) that binds the offender (RV 1.24.15, 7.88.7; AVŚ 4.16.6).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The bond of the paśu, loosened by the pāśupata vow.
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): The bonds: āṇava mala, karma and māyā (with Śiva's concealing power counted as a bond, and bindu for souls in the pure worlds); eternal realities, not appearances.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Pācam: the three (or five) malas that bind the soul; eternal, their power quelled in release.
