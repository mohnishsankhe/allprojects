**Native script:** महाव्रतिन्

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kāpālika (Somasiddhānta)](../lineages/kapalika.md): Name for skull-observing Śaiva ascetics; Guṇaratna counts the Mahāvratadharas as the third of four kinds of Śaiva ascetics, the Śiva Purāṇa honours them among Śaivas.
