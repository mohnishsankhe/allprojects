# Kāl Nirañjan (Kāl)

`trm:kal-niranjan` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Hindi

## Definitions by tradition
- [Kabīr Panth](../lineages/kabir-panth.md): The son of the Sat Puruṣ who by austerity won the three worlds, begot Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Maheś, and as Kāl (time/death) devours souls and keeps them from Satlok; the adversary from whom Kabīr frees souls.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Kāl, the 'negative power' (also Brahm, Jot Nirañjan at the lower levels), ruler of the three worlds up to Trikuṭī, the universal mind, who administers karma; contrasted with Dayāl, the merciful supreme.
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): Kāl as death, who stands over every head and grinds the world.
