# kaula

`trm:kaula` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कौल
**Literal:** relating to kula

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Kula is Śakti and akula Śiva; their relation is kaula; worship of the cakra drawn in outer space is conventionally called Kaula (Bhāskararāya on LSN 37).
- [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md): Outer worship on a physical support, and the Kaula doctrines of the pūrva and uttara schools, which the Samaya way rejects (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 8, 23, 34-35).
- [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md): The Kaula Upaniṣad's teaching: knowledge is the sole cause of liberation, which is being the self of all; inwardly Śākta, outwardly Śaiva, among people Vaiṣṇava.
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): The Kula path and its initiate; the highest of the seven modes of conduct (Kulārṇava 2.7-8), of the nature of both enjoyment and yoga (2.23).
