# indriyasaṃvara

`trm:indriya-samvara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** sense restraint

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Guarding the doors of the senses: seeing a form one does not grasp at its signs and features through which covetousness and grief might invade (DN 2; MN 27).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Guarding the sense doors so that covetousness and grief do not invade; the second purity of virtue.
