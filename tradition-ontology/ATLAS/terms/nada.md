# nāda

`trm:nada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नाद
**Literal:** sound

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The inner sound heard in the purified suṣumnā; laya rests on it (HYP 4.29); its stages and sounds mark the four stages of yoga (4.69–77, 4.84–86); whatever is heard as nāda is śakti, the soundless is the supreme Brahman (4.101–102).
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Inner sound, manifesting when the channels are purified (GŚ-Briggs 101); with bindu and Śakti one of three objects of meditation (KJN 5.31).
- [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md): Sound, on which song, instrument, dance, speech and the world depend (ŚR 1.2.1–2; Bṛhaddeśī 16–17); born of the union of breath (na) and fire (da) (ŚR 1.3.6); twofold, struck and unstruck, and fivefold by its places in the body.
- [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md): The sounds that manifest the sphoṭa and plant their seeds in the intellect (VP 1).
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): The second emanation after Śakti (ŚT 1.7), and in the threefold bindu the union of Śiva and Śakti (ŚT 1.8–9).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The unstruck inner sound, heard in stages, in which the mind dissolves; 'no mantra higher than nāda'.
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Musical sound; one who knows the vīṇā, the microtones and rhythm reaches liberation without effort (YS 3.115).
- [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md): The resonance arising from Śakti in the unfolding from the supreme Lord, from which Bindu arises.
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Resonance: one of the subtle phases of the mantra's final sound, between rodhinī and nādānta (Varivasyārahasya).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The subtle resonance of a mantra and the self-arising sound which is itself the mantra repeated (VBT 42, 145).
