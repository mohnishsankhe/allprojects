# grāhya-grāhaka

`trm:grahya-grahaka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ग्राह्यग्राहक
**Literal:** grasped and grasper

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The duality of object and subject, which does not exist in unreal imagination though it appears there.
- [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md): The appearance of cognition as divided into object, subject and awareness, seen by those whose vision is distorted, although cognition itself is undivided.
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The correlated objects and subjects that make the universe manifold (PH 3).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The yogin's distinction is attentiveness to their relation (VBT 106).
