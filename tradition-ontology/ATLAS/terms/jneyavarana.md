# jñeyāvaraṇa

`trm:jneyavarana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** cognitive obscuration; obscuration of the knowable

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The subtler obscuration that prevents omniscience, removed by knowledge of the selflessness of dharmas; a buddha alone is free of both obscurations.
