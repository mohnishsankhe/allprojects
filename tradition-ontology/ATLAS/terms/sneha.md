# sneha

`trm:sneha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्नेह
**Literal:** affection, love

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): The love that, grounded in knowledge of God's greatness, firm and greater than all other love, constitutes bhakti.
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): Cause of the cohering of powder etc.; in water only.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): Affection/love, the first growth from the seed of devotion, destroying other attachments.
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The stage of love above prema in which the heart melts completely.
