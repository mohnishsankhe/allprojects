# vega

`trm:vega` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वेग
**Literal:** urge, impulse

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Natural urges (of urine, faeces, semen, flatus, vomiting, sneezing, belching, yawning, hunger, thirst, tears, sleep, breath after exertion) which must not be suppressed; and the urges of greed, grief, fear, anger, pride, envy, passion and rash acts which must be restrained (Ca Sū 7.3-4, 7.26-29; AHS Sū 4.1, 4.24).
- [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md): The impression that keeps a moving thing moving (Tarkasaṅgraha; VS 5.1.17 C on the arrow).
