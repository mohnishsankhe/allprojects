**Literal:** good conduct

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The conduct of the good, to be followed always with mindfulness; it yields health and mastery of the senses at once (Ca Sū 8.17-18); it includes worship, cleanliness, truthful and gentle speech, non-covetousness, avoiding bad company and being devoted to continence, knowledge, giving, friendliness, compassion, joy, equanimity and calm (Sū 8.18-29; AHS Sū 2.20-47).
