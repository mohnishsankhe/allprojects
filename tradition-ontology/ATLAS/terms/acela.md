# acela / acelaka

`trm:acela` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अचेल
**Literal:** without cloth

## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): Nakedness, the first of the Digambara monk's special root qualities; required for full renunciation and liberation.
- [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md): Either nakedness (the Jinakalpa mode, followed by Mahāvīra and early ascetics) or wearing few, poor garments; one of the twenty-two hardships; not required now.
- [Yāpanīya](../lineages/yapaniya.md): Naked monasticism kept as the rule, with clothing allowed by exception (apavāda-liṅga) and for women.
