# parātmaparivartana

`trm:paratmaparivartana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Literal:** exchange of self and others

## Definitions by tradition
- [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md): The 'supreme secret' by which one quickly protects oneself and others: taking others as oneself and giving oneself to others (BCA 8.120, 8.136).
