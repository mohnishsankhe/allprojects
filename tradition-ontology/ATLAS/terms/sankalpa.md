# saṅkalpa

`trm:sankalpa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** संकल्प
**Literal:** construction, intention

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): The function of manas (SK 27).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Intention: a rung of the ladder (ChU 7.4); the self's intentions are real (ChU 8.1.5, 8.7.1); by intention alone the fathers' world arises (8.2.1); 'intention' is the teaching about brahman with respect to the self (Kena 4.5).
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Conceiving or willing by the mind, by which worlds are spun; its cessation is the end of saṃsāra.
