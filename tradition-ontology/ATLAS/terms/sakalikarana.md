# sakalīkaraṇa

`trm:sakalikarana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** सकलीकरण
**Literal:** making (the deity) complete in parts

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md): The placing of the six limbs (ṣaḍaṅga) on the deity's body, counted with the invocation gestures.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): In Jain ritual manuals, the consecration and protection of the worshipper's body by placing the syllables of the homage to the five supreme beings on the limbs before worship.
