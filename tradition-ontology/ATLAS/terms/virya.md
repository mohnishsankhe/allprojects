# vīrya

`trm:virya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वीर्य
**Literal:** energy, vigour

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): (1) Energy, the second means (1.20); (2) the vigour gained by one established in celibacy (2.38).
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): The active potency of a substance, twofold as hot or cold (AHS Sū 1.17).
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The fourth perfection: delight in the good, opposed by laziness, attachment to the base, despondency and self-contempt (BCA 7.2).
