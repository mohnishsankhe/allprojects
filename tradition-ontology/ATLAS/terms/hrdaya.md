# hṛdaya

`trm:hrdaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** हृदय
**Literal:** heart

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): The Heart of the Supreme: consciousness as vibrancy and great being (ĪPK 1.5.14).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): The Heart of Bhairava, the seed in which the universe lies like a tree in a banyan seed (Parātrīśikā 24); also the heart-centre of meditation (VBT 49).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The heart: seat of the self and of the space within it (ChU 3.14.3, 8.1.1-3); analysed as 'hṛdi ayam', 'this is in the heart' (ChU 8.3.3) and as hṛ-da-yam (BAU 5.3.1); where the knots and desires dwell (KU 2.3.14-15).
