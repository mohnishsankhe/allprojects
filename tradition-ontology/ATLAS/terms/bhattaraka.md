**Native script:** भट्टारक

## Definitions by tradition
- [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md): A clothed Digambara cleric heading a seat (maṭha), who administers temples, institutions and learning and conducts rites; authority of the Bīsapantha, rejected by the Digambara Terāpantha; seats at Śravaṇabeḷagoḷa, Mūḍabidri, Kolhapur and elsewhere.
