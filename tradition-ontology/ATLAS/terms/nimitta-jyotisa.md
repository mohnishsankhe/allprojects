# nimitta (omen)

`trm:nimitta-jyotisa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** निमित्त
**Literal:** sign, cause

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): A sign or omen from which future good or ill is inferred — celestial, atmospheric, terrestrial, bodily, dream, sound; for the Jains the eight 'great omens' (aṣṭāṅga-mahānimitta) are a science a monk must not live by.

## Forms in other languages

- Prakrit: nimitta  — exact
