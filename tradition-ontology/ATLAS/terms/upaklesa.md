# upakleśa

`trm:upaklesa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** secondary affliction

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The derivative afflictions (anger, enmity, concealment, spite, envy, avarice, deceit, dishonesty, pride, harmfulness, shamelessness, non-embarrassment, torpor, agitation, lack of faith, laziness, heedlessness, forgetfulness, distraction, non-awareness) listed in the Triṃśikā.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Secondary defilement: defiled mental factors other than the root defilements, including the envelopments and the six stains.
