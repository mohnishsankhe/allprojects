# aparokṣa-jñāna

`trm:aparoksa-jnana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अपरोक्षज्ञान
**Literal:** direct (non-mediate) knowledge

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Direct realization (sākṣātkāra) of God — in the form of one's own original (bimba), to the extent one's fitness allows — produced by meditation built on hearing and reflection.
- [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md): Immediate knowledge: knowledge of the self as oneself ('you are the tenth'); for the Vivaraṇa produced by the mahāvākya directly.
