## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Attention, a component of name (SN 12.2) and a factor defined in each jhāna (MN 111).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Attention: the turning of the mind to the object; a universal factor.
