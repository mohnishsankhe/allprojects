# śakti

`trm:sakti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** शक्ति
**Literal:** power

## Definitions by tradition
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): Power: the world as the power of Brahman like light from a fire (VP 1.22.56); Viṣṇu's threefold śakti (VP 6.7.61); the Goddess herself (DM).
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Śiva's power: the pedestal of the liṅga (SSM 11.32), the breath as the liṅga's power (12.2–3), the great power named Umā that lights the supreme self (12.21–24).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): Power, never different from its possessor; the door (mukha) through which Śiva is known (VBT 18-21).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Power: the self-power of God (devātmaśakti) hidden by its qualities (ŚU 1.3); his supreme power is manifold, his innate action of knowledge and strength (6.8).
- [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md): The denotative power of a word, which is the Lord's convention 'from this word this meaning is to be understood' (Tarkasaṅgraha); causal power as a separate category is rejected, efficacy being explained by the totality of causes and the absence of counteragents.
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): Śiva's single, conscious power, the instrument (not the material) of his acts; called grace (aruḷ) when it frees, concealment when it drives the bonds.
