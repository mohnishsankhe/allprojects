# pāramitā

`trm:paramita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** perfection

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The bodhisattva's six (giving, morality, patience, vigor, meditation, wisdom) or ten (adding skillful means, vow, power, knowledge) perfections, each perfected only when informed by the perfection of wisdom.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Perfection; the Kośa names the fulfilment of giving, patience, morality, vigour, meditation and wisdom in the bodhisattva career.
