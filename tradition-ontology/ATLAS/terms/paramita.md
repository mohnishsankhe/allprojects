# pāramitā

`trm:paramita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पारमिता
**Literal:** perfection

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): The ten perfections: generosity, discipline, patience, vigour, meditation, wisdom, skill in means, aspiration, power and knowledge, the last four added to the six to match the ten grounds.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The bodhisattva's six (giving, morality, patience, vigor, meditation, wisdom) or ten (adding skillful means, vow, power, knowledge) perfections, each perfected only when informed by the perfection of wisdom.
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The six perfections — giving, discipline, patience, vigour, meditation and wisdom — completed as ten with method, aspiration, power and knowledge and paired with the ten grounds (MA); wisdom is the purpose of the others (BCA 9.1).
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Perfection; the Kośa names the fulfilment of giving, patience, morality, vigour, meditation and wisdom in the bodhisattva career.
- [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md): phar phyin: the six perfections as the heart of the 'method' in the Jewel Ornament (ch.12–17).

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [pāramī](parami.md) — the Theravāda ten pāramīs list renunciation, truth, resolution, loving-kindness and equanimity where the Mahāyāna has meditation, method, aspiration, power and knowledge
- exact: [pāramī](parami.md) — Sanskrit and Pali forms of the same canonical term; school definitions differ (see the per-lineage definitions).
