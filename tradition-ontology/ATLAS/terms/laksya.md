# lakṣya

`trm:laksya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** लक्ष्य
**Literal:** target, mark

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): An object of fixed attention; the Nāth body-map counts three (inner, outer, middle); śāmbhavī mudrā is attention on an inner target with the gaze outward (HYP 4.36).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Object of attention in tāraka-yoga: inner, outer or intermediate.
