**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अवधिज्ञान
**Literal:** limited direct knowledge; clairvoyance

## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Direct knowledge by the soul of material things within a range, innate in gods and hell-beings and acquired by others (TS 1.21–22, 1.27).
