# pati

`trm:pati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पति
**Literal:** lord

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): Śiva as Paśupati, lord of the bound selves, who binds them and alone frees them.
- [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md): The Lord as possessor of unsurpassed powers of seeing and acting, eternally related to that lordship and not acquiring it (SDS ch. 6); he alone is not a paśu.
- [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md): Mahādeva as the Lord on whom the bound soul depends (SSM 10.40); at union lordship and boundness are seen as imagined by māyā (14.8–9).
- [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md): The Lord, Śiva: one, eternally free (anādimukta), omniscient and omnipotent, the efficient cause who by his single power performs the five acts for the souls' release; distinct from souls and bonds.
- [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md): Pati (Tamil pati, iṟai): the Lord who is one with, other than and together with souls; the Real (cat).

## Forms in other languages
