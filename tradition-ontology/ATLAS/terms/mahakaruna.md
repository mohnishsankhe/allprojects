# mahākaruṇā

`trm:mahakaruna` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** great compassion

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): The compassion that embraces all beings as an only child and from which the bodhisattva's illness arises; eating meat cuts off its seed.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Great compassion, one of the Buddha's exclusive qualities: a conventional knowledge differing from ordinary compassion in eight ways.
