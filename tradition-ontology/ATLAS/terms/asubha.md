# asubha

`trm:asubha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** foul, unattractive

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): The ten kinds of corpse as meditation subjects, and the perception of the body's foulness, counteracting lust.
- [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md): Contemplation of the unlovely (skeleton contemplation), entry to cultivation for those dominated by passion; it is non-greed.
