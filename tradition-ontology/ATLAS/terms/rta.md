# ṛta

`trm:rta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ऋत
**Literal:** what is fitting; the true course

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The true order that holds together the cosmos (the courses of sun, dawn, seasons and waters), the sacrifice and right conduct; guarded by Varuṇa and Mitra; born with satya from kindled tapas (RV 10.190.1); the twelve-spoked wheel of the year is 'the wheel of ṛta' (1.164.11).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The order of which food, in the Taittirīya Brāhmaṇa, calls itself the firstborn (TB 2.8.8.1).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): What is right, the order: spoken alongside truth (TU 1.1, 1.9); the right side of the self made of understanding (TU 2.4); the knower is 'first-born of ṛta' (TU 3.10.6).
