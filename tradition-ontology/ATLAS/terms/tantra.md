# tantra

`trm:tantra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तन्त्र
**Literal:** loom, warp; system; treatise

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): A revealed scripture, so called because it expands (tanoti) matters of the principles and mantras and protects (trāyate).
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): A text revealed by the Buddha (as Vajradhara) teaching mantra, maṇḍala, consecration and yoga; explained as 'continuum' (prabandha) — of ground, path and fruit (cause, method and result).
- [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md): Continuum (rgyud): the ground continuum (buddha-nature), path continuum and fruit continuum; also the scriptures of the secret mantra, in the Nyingma arranged as the six tantra-vehicles.
- [Tantra as a movement across the traditions (Tantraśāstra; the Mantramārga / Mantranaya current)](../lineages/tantra-movement.md): Across the families, a revealed scripture of the mantra path (or, for the Buddhists, also the continuum of ground, path and fruit); the Jains speak rather of kalpas (mantra manuals).
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): The Pāñcarātra calls its revelations saṃhitās or tantras (the Lakṣmī Tantra), spoken by Nārāyaṇa.
