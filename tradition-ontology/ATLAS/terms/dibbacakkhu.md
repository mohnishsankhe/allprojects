# dibbacakkhu

`trm:dibbacakkhu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** divine eye

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): Seeing beings passing away and reappearing according to their deeds (DN 2; MN 4); the second true knowledge.
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): Knowledge of the passing away and reappearance of beings according to their kamma; a mundane direct knowledge.
