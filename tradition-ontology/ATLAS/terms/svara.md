# svara

`trm:svara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्वर
**Literal:** tone; accent; note

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Vedic pitch-accent (udātta, anudātta, svarita) and, in the Sāmaveda, the seven notes of chant; a mantra wrong in svara harms the sacrificer (Pāṇinīya Śikṣā 52).
- [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md): The Vedic pitch accents — udātta (high), anudātta (low), svarita (combined) (Aṣṭādhyāyī 1.2.29–31); also 'vowel'. Error in accent turns a mantra against the sacrificer (PŚ 52).
- [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md): A note: that which arises right after the śruti, smooth and resonant, and of itself delights the listener (ŚR 1.3.24–25); seven — ṣaḍja, ṛṣabha, gāndhāra, madhyama, pañcama, dhaivata, niṣāda (sa ri ga ma pa dha ni).
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): In the svara science, the flow of breath through the left, right or both nostrils and the elements arising in it (Śiva Svarodaya).
