# aṇu / aṇīyān

`trm:anu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** atom, minute

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Minute, 'smaller than the small': the self in the heart smaller than a grain yet greater than the worlds (ChU 3.14.3; KU 1.2.20; ŚU 3.20).
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): The soul is atomic in size, dwelling in the heart and pervading the body by its quality of knowledge.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): The soul is atomic, a spark of Brahman.
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): The soul is an infinitesimal particle of consciousness (cit-kaṇa).
