# asakta

`trm:asakta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** असक्त
**Literal:** unattached

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: The manner of action taught in chapter 3: the karma-yogin acts unattached (3.7); one should always perform the action to be done unattached, thereby reaching the highest (3.19); the wise act unattached for the holding together of the world (3.25).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: non-attachment (asakti, the abstract noun) is among the dispositions called knowledge (13.10); the knowable is unattached (asaktam), yet supports all (13.15). Not to be confused with aśakti, 'incapacity' (trm:asakti).
