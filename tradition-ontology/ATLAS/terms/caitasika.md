# caitasika

`trm:caitasika` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** mental factor

## Definitions by tradition
- [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md): A factor that arises associated with a consciousness and apprehends the particular features of its object, while consciousness apprehends the object itself; the Triṃśikā lists universal, particular, wholesome, afflicted, secondary-afflicted and variable factors (fifty-one in the later count).
