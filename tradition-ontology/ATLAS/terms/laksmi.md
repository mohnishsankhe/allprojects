# Lakṣmī (Śrī, Ramā)

`trm:laksmi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** लक्ष्मी
**Literal:** fortune; the goddess

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): Viṣṇu's consort, eternally free and dependent, the 'akṣara' whose body never perishes, higher than all souls in every quality; as Śrī, Bhū and Durgā she is the higher prakṛti (śakti).
- [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md): The Lord's eternal consort, inseparable from him, mother of the world and mediator; her rank is disputed between the northern and southern schools.
- [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md): All-pervading like Viṣṇu and of the Lord's rank; with him the means and the goal; the divine couple is the object of surrender.
- [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md): The mediator (puruṣakāra) who reconciles soul and Lord; not herself the means; commonly reported as atomic in essence and of the rank of souls, though ever inseparable from the Lord.
