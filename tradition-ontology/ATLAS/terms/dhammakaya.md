# dhammakāya

`trm:dhammakaya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** Dhamma-body

## Definitions by tradition
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): In the canon, an epithet of the Buddha as embodying the Dhamma; in the Dhammakāya tradition, the luminous Buddha-form found within the body at the seventh base.
- [Dhammakāya meditation tradition (Vijjā Dhammakāya; Wat Paknam)](../lineages/dhammakaya.md): The inner Buddha-body reached through successive refined bodies at the centre of the body.
