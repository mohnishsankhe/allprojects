# ānanda-mīmāṃsā

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** inquiry into bliss

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The scale of Taittirīya 2.8: one human bliss (of a young, good, learned, strongest man owning the whole earth), each step a hundred times the last, through the human gandharvas, divine gandharvas, fathers, born-gods, karma-gods, gods, Indra, Bṛhaspati, Prajāpati to brahman; each step is also the bliss of a śrotriya not struck by desire.
