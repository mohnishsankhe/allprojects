# svadharma

`trm:svadharma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्वधर्म
**Literal:** one's own dharma

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The duty proper to one's own nature and station, better even when imperfect than another's dharma well performed (BhG 3.35, 18.47); for Arjuna, the kṣatriya's duty to fight a righteous war (2.31); worship of the Lord by one's own work leads to perfection (18.46). In the Vyādha Gītā, the inherited occupation of the meat-seller done rightly (3.198).
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): The duty proper to one's class and stage; better done imperfectly than another's done well (MDh 10.97); it leads to heaven and endlessness (Arthaśāstra 1.3.14).
