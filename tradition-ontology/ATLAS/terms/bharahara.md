# bhārahāra

`trm:bharahara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** burden-bearer

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pudgalavāda](../lineages/pudgalavada.md): The bearer of the burden (Bhārahāra-sūtra), read as the person distinct from the burden of the aggregates.
- [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md): The earlier aggregates, which harm the later, are spoken of as the bearer.
