## Definitions by tradition
- [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md): The twelfth sign (Pisces), 30° of the zodiac made of nine asterism-quarters; the feet of Kālapuruṣa (BJ 1.4); ruled by Jupiter; dual; of the water triplicity (later texts); even, female and gentle (BJ 1.11); Varāhamihira's alternative (Greek-derived) name: ittha (BJ 1.8); dignities: Venus exalted (27°); Mercury in fall (BJ 1.13).
