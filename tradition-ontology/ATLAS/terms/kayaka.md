# kāyaka

`trm:kayaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Kannada
**Native script:** ಕಾಯಕ
**Literal:** bodily work, occupation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): Honest work in one's occupation undertaken as worship; 'work is Kailāsa' (Āydakki Mārayya's vacana), not to be interrupted even for the guru, the liṅga or a jaṅgama, and not to gather more than the day's need.

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
