# piṇḍagrāha

`trm:pindagraha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** seizing on a lump / composite

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): Grasping a composite (a world, a heap of atoms) as a real unity; the Vajracchedikā says it is inexpressible yet seized by common people.
