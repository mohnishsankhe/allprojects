# bhoktṛ

`trm:bhoktr` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** भोक्तृ
**Literal:** enjoyer, experiencer

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Puruṣa as experiencer, one proof of its existence (SK 17); experience culminates in consciousness (SS 1.104).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: The Lord is the enjoyer and lord of all sacrifices; not recognizing him in truth, worshippers of other deities fall (9.24).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: the knowable is without guṇas yet their experiencer (guṇabhoktṛ, 13.15); puruṣa is the cause in the experiencing (bhoktṛtva) of pleasure and pain (13.21) and, situated in prakṛti, experiences the guṇas (13.22); 'experiencer' is among the names of the highest puruṣa in the body (13.23).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The enjoyer: the self joined with body, senses and mind (KU 1.3.4); enjoyer, enjoyed and impeller as the threefold brahman (ŚU 1.12).
