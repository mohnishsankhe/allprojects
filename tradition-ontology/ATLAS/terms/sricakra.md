# śrīcakra

`trm:sricakra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** श्रीचक्र
**Literal:** the auspicious wheel

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The 'king of cakras', Lalitā's dwelling (LSN 182): nine interlocking triangles — four of Śiva, five of Śakti — with the central point, forty-three angles, the eight- and sixteen-petalled lotuses, three circles and the square of three lines (SL 11); it arises when the supreme Śakti beholds her own vibrancy (YH 1.9-10); the body itself is the Śrīcakra (Bhāvanā Upaniṣad).
- [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md): For the Samayins the Śrīcakra is worshipped only in the inner space and is the moon-disc in the crown lotus (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 10).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): The yantra of Tripurā; the body contemplated as it.
