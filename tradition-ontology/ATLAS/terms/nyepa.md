# nyes pa (nyepa)

`trm:nyepa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Tibetan
**Native script:** ཉེས་པ་
**Literal:** fault; the three humours

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sowa Rigpa (gso ba rig pa), the Tibetan science of healing](../lineages/sowa-rigpa.md): The three principles rlung, mkhris pa and bad kan that sustain the body in balance and cause disease when disturbed; they arise from desire, hatred and delusion.
