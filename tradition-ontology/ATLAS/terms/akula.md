# akula

`trm:akula` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अकुल
**Literal:** without kula

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): Śiva (in contrast with kula, Śakti); the Goddess is 'akulā', beyond kula (LSN 37).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): What is beyond the Kula, Śiva's own abode, where Bhairava abides at the end of aeons (KJN 16.41); the nectar is 'beyond Akula and Kula' (14.97).
- [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md): Śiva, beyond the totality of powers (Kulārṇava 17.27).
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): Śiva as beyond the kula; the Akula abode into which full consciousness finally dissolves (TĀ 4.170).
