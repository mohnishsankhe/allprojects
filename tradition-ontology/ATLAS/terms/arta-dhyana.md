## Definitions by tradition
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): TS 9.30-9.34: continuous bringing to mind on separation from the disagreeable, the reverse for the agreeable, the same in the case of feeling (vedanā), and nidāna; it belongs to the unrestrained, the partly restrained and the careless ascetic.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): Brooding on getting rid of the disagreeable, keeping the agreeable, removing pain, and future rewards (TS 9.30–33).
