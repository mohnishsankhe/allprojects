## Definitions by tradition
- [Yāpanīya](../lineages/yapaniya.md): Name of the extinct order that combined naked monasticism with acceptance of women's liberation and the kevalin's eating; the name has been explained from 'yāpanīya' ('that which sustains / keeps going'), a word in the formula of monastic greeting (low confidence).
