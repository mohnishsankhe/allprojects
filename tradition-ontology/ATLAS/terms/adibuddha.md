## Definitions by tradition
- [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md): The primordial buddha 'without beginning or end' (Nāmasaṃgīti 100), gnosis as its only eye.
- [Kālacakra tradition in India](../lineages/kalacakra.md): The Kālacakra's name for the non-dual gnosis of immutable bliss and empty form, source of the root tantra ('Paramādibuddha'); not a creator.
