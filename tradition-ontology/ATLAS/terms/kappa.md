# kappa

`trm:kappa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** aeon

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): A cosmic period of contraction and expansion of the world (DN 27); the seven Buddhas arose within ninety-one aeons (DN 14).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): A world-age with four incalculable phases (contraction, contracted, expansion, expanded).
