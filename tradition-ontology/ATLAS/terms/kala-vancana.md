## Definitions by tradition
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): 'Cheating time (death)': the yoga that overcomes death (GŚ 2; HYP 3.3).
- [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md): Taught by Bhairava as the most secret practice; placing the mind in the heart until unmanī destroys death (KJN 6.16–28).
