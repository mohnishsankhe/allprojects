# nātha

`trm:natha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नाथ
**Literal:** lord, protector

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): 'Lord'; the title of the perfected masters of the tradition (Ādinātha, Matsyendranātha, Gorakṣanātha) and the name-ending taken by initiated yogīs; in the Kaulajñānanirṇaya the jīva itself is called the lord (nātha), the all-pervading Śiva.
