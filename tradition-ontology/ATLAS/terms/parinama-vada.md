# pariṇāma-vāda

`trm:parinama-vada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** doctrine of transformation

## Definitions by tradition
- [Bhedābheda Vedānta (Bhāskara's school and the early difference-and-non-difference Vedāntins)](../lineages/bhedabheda.md): The doctrine that the world is a real transformation of Brahman (through its śakti).
- [Dvaitādvaita (Nimbārka Sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaitadvaita.md): The world is a real transformation of the Lord's power.
- [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md): Accepted in the form avikṛta-pariṇāma: transformation without change in Brahman.
- [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md): Accepted as śakti-pariṇāma: the external power transforms, the Lord remains unchanged.
