# pūjā

`trm:puja` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** पूजा
**Literal:** worship, honouring

## Definitions by tradition
- [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md): Worship of Śiva with mantras, twofold: for oneself (in the liṅga received at initiation) and for others (in temples, for king and realm), preceded by the five purifications.
- [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md): Ijyā, worship of Bhagavān, the third of the five daily periods.
- [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md): Arcana of Viṣṇu in a consecrated image with Vedic mantras; the best of the means.
- [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md): The daily sequence of temple pūjās performed by the śānti under the tantri's authority.
- [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md): Not with flowers but the firm mind fixed on the great void (VBT 147); the liberated worships Bhairava with the substances of self-awareness (Paramārthasāra 75).
