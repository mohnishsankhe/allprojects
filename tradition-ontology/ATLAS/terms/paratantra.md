# paratantra (asvatantra)

`trm:paratantra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** परतन्त्र
**Literal:** dependent on another

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md): All reality other than Viṣṇu — Lakṣmī, souls, matter, time, the Veda, negations — which exists, knows and acts only as dependent on and controlled by him.
- [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md): In the Saṃdhinirmocana, the dependently arisen nature of dharmas, basis of the imagined.
