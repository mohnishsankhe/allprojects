**Language:** Kannada

## Definitions by tradition
- [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md): A member of the community that wears the personal liṅga; the preferred name of the Basava-centred strand, some of whose writers distinguish it from 'Vīraśaiva' while others treat the two as synonyms.
