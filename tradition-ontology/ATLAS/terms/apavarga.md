# apavarga

`trm:apavarga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** अपवर्ग
**Literal:** liberation; release

## Definitions by tradition
- [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md): Absolute release from pain (NS 1.1.22), reached when false knowledge and the rest are removed (NS 1.1.2); on the mainstream view without pleasure or cognition (NBh 1.1.22).
- [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md): Liberation, which comes through knowledge (SK 44); defined in the Sāṃkhya Sūtra as the indifference of one of the two (SS 3.66).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Liberation as the second purpose of the seen: determining the enjoyer's own nature (2.18, YBh 2.18).

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- exact: [mokṣa](moksa.md) — Within Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika, apavarga, mokṣa, mukti and niḥśreyasa (in its final sense) name the same state.
- contested: [kaivalya](kaivalya.md) — Sāṃkhya-Yoga's isolation of puruṣa is also a state without pain and without objects; but Nyāya's self is a substance whose consciousness is adventitious, while puruṣa is consciousness itself.
