# paññāvimutta

`trm:pannavimutta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Pali
**Literal:** freed by wisdom

## Definitions by tradition
- [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md): One whose effluents are destroyed by wisdom without contacting the formless liberations (MN 70).
- [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md): One whose taints are destroyed by wisdom without having touched the eight liberations.
