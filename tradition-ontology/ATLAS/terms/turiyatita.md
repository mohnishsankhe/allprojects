# turīyātīta

`trm:turiyatita` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** तुरीयातीत
**Literal:** beyond the fourth

## Definitions by tradition
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): (1) A fifth state beyond turīya, located at the brahmarandhra; (2) the name of the highest renouncer who lives like a cow, naked, the body as a corpse.
- [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md): The state beyond the fourth, reached by firmness in the fourth; the Goddess is 'devoid of all states' (Bhāskararāya on LSN 63).
