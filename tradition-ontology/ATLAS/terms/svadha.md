# svadhā

`trm:svadha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्वधा
**Literal:** own power; self-sustenance

## Definitions by tradition
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): Inherent power by which That One breathed (RV 10.129.2) and the immortal moves (1.164.38); also the call and food offered to the fathers, who rejoice in svadhā (10.14.7).
- [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md): The exclamation with which offerings go to the ancestors, their 'drink' (ŚB 2.4.2.2; 11.5.6.2).
