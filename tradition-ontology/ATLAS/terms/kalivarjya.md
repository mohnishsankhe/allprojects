# kalivarjya

`trm:kalivarjya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** कलिवर्ज्य
**Literal:** to be avoided in the Kali age

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md): Practices once permitted by the smṛtis but declared forbidden in the Kali age by later authorities (e.g. levirate, the great departure, cow-sacrifice), justified by the principle that dharma differs by age.
