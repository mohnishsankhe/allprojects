## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: The rare one who at the end of many births takes refuge knowing 'Vāsudeva is all' (7.19); those who have reached the highest perfection and are not reborn (8.15); those of divine nature who worship the Lord with undivided mind (9.13).
- [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md): In the Mahāparinirvāṇa and Aṅgulimālīya, the Tathāgata's true self, identified with the tathāgatagarbha, in contrast to the imagined self of the aggregates.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The rare one who knows 'Vāsudeva is all' (BhG 7.19); great souls resort to the divine nature and worship with undistracted minds (9.13).
