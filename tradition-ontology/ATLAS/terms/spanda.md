# spanda

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** स्पन्द
**Literal:** vibration, throb, subtle movement

## Definitions by tradition
- [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md): The dynamic, vibrant nature of the ultimate - Śaṅkara's opening and closing of the eyes (SK 1.1); the universal vibration on which all particular vibrations rest (SK 1.19); discoverable in intense states (1.22).
- [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md): Utpaladeva calls consciousness 'sphurattā', the great being, the Heart of the Supreme (ĪPK 1.5.14).
