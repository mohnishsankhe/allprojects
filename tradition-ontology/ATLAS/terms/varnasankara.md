# varṇasaṅkara

`trm:varnasankara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** वर्णसङ्कर
**Literal:** mixing of the classes

## Definitions by tradition
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: In Arjuna's argument it arises when the women of a family are corrupted after adharma prevails (1.41), leads the family and its destroyers to hell (1.42) and uproots the jāti- and kula-dharmas (1.43); Kṛṣṇa says that if he did not act he would be the maker of saṅkara (3.24).
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): The mixture of varṇas that Arjuna fears will follow the ruin of families (BhG 1.41) and that the Lord says would follow if he did not act (3.24).
