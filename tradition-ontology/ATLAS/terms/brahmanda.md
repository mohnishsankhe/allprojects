# brahmāṇḍa

`trm:brahmanda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** ब्रह्माण्ड
**Literal:** Brahmā's egg; the cosmos

## Definitions by tradition
- [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md): The cosmos, and the body so named because it contains the cosmos (SS 1.91, 2.6; the thousand-petalled lotus is outside the body called brahmāṇḍa, SS 5.151).
- [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md): The cosmic egg within which the fourteen worlds lie, enclosed by sheaths of the elements; broken up in the elemental dissolution.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): The cosmic egg — the universe, all of which is found within the body (piṇḍa).
