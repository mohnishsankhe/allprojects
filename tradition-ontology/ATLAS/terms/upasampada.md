# upasaṃpadā

**Language:** Sanskrit

## Definitions by tradition
- [Dharmaguptaka](../lineages/dharmaguptaka.md): Full ordination as bhikṣu or bhikṣuṇī by a formal act of motion and three proclamations before a quorum (ten, or five in border lands).
