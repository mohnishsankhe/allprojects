# jīvan-mṛtak (jīvat mṛtak)

`trm:jivan-mrtak` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Hindi

## Definitions by tradition
- [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md): 'Dead while living': the devotee who has let the self, hope and pride die while still alive; death has nothing left to take.
- [Sant Mat (Radhasoami and its successor lines)](../lineages/sant-mat.md): Dying while living: daily withdrawal of the attention from the body to the eye centre as at death.
- [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md): jyānte marā: the lover who has died to lust and self while alive.

## Forms in other languages
- Bengali: jyānte marā  — partial

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- analogous: [jīvanmukti](jivanmukti.md) — Both describe freedom in the living body; the Sant term stresses the death of the self rather than the dawning of knowledge.
