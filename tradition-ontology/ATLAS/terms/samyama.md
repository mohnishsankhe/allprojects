# saṃyama

`trm:samyama` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** संयम
**Literal:** holding together

## Definitions by tradition
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): The three, concentration (dhāraṇā), meditation (dhyāna) and samādhi, on one object; 'saṃyama' is the treatise's own technical term for the three (3.4 and bhāṣya).
- [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md): Concentration, meditation and samādhi together on one object (3.4), the 'technical term' of the śāstra (YBh 3.4); its mastery gives the light of insight (3.5) and the powers.
- [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md): Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: Restraint of the senses: the senses are offered into the fires of restraint (4.26), and the actions of senses and breath into the fire of the yoga of self-restraint (ātma-saṃyama-yoga) kindled by knowledge (4.27). Not the technical saṃyama of the Yoga Sūtra.
- [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md): The sixth virtue: restraint of senses and protection of beings (TS 9.6); also self-discipline generally.
- [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md): Restraint and moderation — the Nāth middle way ('one crosses over by restraint alone', Gorakh Bānī).
- [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md): Concentration on an object yielding a power (Śāṇḍilya).
