# nāḍīparīkṣā

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Native script:** नाडीपरीक्षा
**Literal:** examination of the pulse

## Definitions by tradition
- [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md): Diagnosis by the gait of the pulse — leech or snake for vāta, sparrow, crow or frog for pitta, swan or pigeon for kapha (Śārṅgadhara Pūrva 3.1-5); absent from the classical saṃhitās' lists of examinations.
