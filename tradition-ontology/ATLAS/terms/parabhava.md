# parabhāva

`trm:parabhava` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Language:** Sanskrit
**Literal:** other-nature, other-being

## Definitions by tradition
- [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md): The nature a thing would have in relation to another; since own-nature is not found, other-nature — which is only the own-nature of another — is not found either (MMK 1.3; 15.3).
