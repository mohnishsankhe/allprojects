# The sun's swallowing of the moon's nectar (the cause of ageing)

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)

The nectar that drips from the moon at the palate or crown is swallowed by the sun, the fire at the navel, and so the body grows old and falls under death's power; the throat lock, the inverted posture and khecarī are taught to prevent this.
**Antidotes:** [Viparītakaraṇī (the inverting technique)](../practices/viparita-karani.md), [Jālandharabandha (the throat lock)](../practices/jalandhara-bandha.md), [Khecarī mudrā ('moving in space')](../practices/khecari-mudra.md)
