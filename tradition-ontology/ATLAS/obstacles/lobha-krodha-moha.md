# Greed, anger and delusion as roots of harmful thoughts

`obs:lobha-krodha-moha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** poison
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

Harmful thoughts are preceded by greed, anger or delusion (2.34): violence for meat or skin, from 'he wronged me', or from 'it will be merit for me' (YBh 2.34); the karmic residue arises from desire, greed, delusion and anger (YBh 2.12).
