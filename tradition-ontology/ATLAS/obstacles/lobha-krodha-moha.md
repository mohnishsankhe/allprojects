# Greed, anger and delusion as roots of harmful thoughts

`obs:lobha-krodha-moha` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** passion
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

The three sources of violence and the rest: greed (e.g. for meat or skin), anger ('he wronged me'), delusion ('it will be dharma for me'), each mild, middling or intense (YS 2.34, YBh 2.34); with desire (kāma) they are also named as the source of the meritorious and demeritorious karma-store (YBh 2.12).
