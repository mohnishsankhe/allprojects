# Incompatible food (viruddha āhāra)

`obs:viruddha-ahara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Foods that conflict with the body's tissues by opposed qualities, combination, preparation, place, time, dose or nature — e.g. fish with milk — vitiating the blood and sometimes fatal (Ca Sū 26.81-92).
