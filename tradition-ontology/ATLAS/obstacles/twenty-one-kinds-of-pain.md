# The twenty-one kinds of pain

`obs:twenty-one-kinds-of-pain` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
**Taught in:** [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)

What is to be abandoned: pain in twenty-one kinds (body, six senses, six objects, six cognitions, pleasure and pain), whose absolute cessation is liberation.
