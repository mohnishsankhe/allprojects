# Self-grasping as the root of faults

`obs:self-grasping-pramana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
**Taught in:** [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)

Seeing a self produces love for it, thirst for happiness, appropriation of means as 'mine', the division of self and other, clinging and aversion, and all faults; as long as it lasts one is in saṃsāra.
