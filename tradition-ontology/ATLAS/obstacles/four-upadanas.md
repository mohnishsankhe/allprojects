# The four kinds of clinging (upādāna)

**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Vism XVII, in the treatment of 'feeling as condition of craving': clinging to sensual pleasures, to views, to rites and observances, and to a doctrine of self; 'clinging' is firm grasping (the prefix upa- meaning firm). In brief, clinging to sensual pleasures is the firmness of craving, and the other three are just views; in detail, the firmness of craving of a hundred and eight kinds is the first, tenfold wrong view the second, the grasping that purity comes from rites and observances the third, and the twenty-fold view of a personal existence the fourth. The orders: in arising, the doctrine of self first; in abandoning, views and the rest first (cut by the stream-entry path), sensual clinging last (arahat path); in teaching, sensual clinging first.
