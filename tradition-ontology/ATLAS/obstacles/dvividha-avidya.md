# Twofold ignorance (the two coverings)

`obs:dvividha-avidya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)

Two beginningless coverings in each soul — one hiding the soul's own nature and qualities, the other hiding its knowledge of the Supreme; the Lord destroys the first and removes the second when he grants liberation.
**Antidotes:** the Lord's grace, [Hearing the scriptures (śravaṇa)](../practices/sravana.md), [Meditation (nididhyāsana / dhyāna)](../practices/nididhyasana.md)
**Members:** covering of one's own qualities, covering of the Supreme
