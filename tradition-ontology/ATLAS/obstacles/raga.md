# Passion, attachment (rāga)

Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Passion or attraction (rāga), set together with aversion in each sense towards its object, is a waylayer; one should not come under its sway (3.34). The self-governed person moves among objects with senses free of attraction and aversion and attains serenity (2.64); the sage of steady mind is free from passion, fear and anger (vītarāgabhayakrodha, 2.56).
**Antidotes:** [Restraint and withdrawal of the senses (indriya-saṃyama)](../practices/indriya-samyama.md), Move among objects with senses free of passion and aversion, under control (2.64), [Meditation as objectless mind (dhyāna)](../practices/nirvisaya-dhyana.md), [Seeing the faults (doṣa-darśana)](../practices/dosa-darsana.md), [Dispassion (vairāgya)](../practices/vairagya.md)
