# Evil (pāpman)

`obs:papman` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** impurity
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Evil pierced the senses in the gods' contest, so that one speaks, sees and thinks what is improper (BAU 1.3; ChU 1.2); evils turn back from the self, the dam (ChU 8.4); the knower's evils burn like a reed tuft (ChU 5.24.3), do not cling like water on a lotus leaf (ChU 4.14.3); the self is free from evil (ChU 8.1.5).
