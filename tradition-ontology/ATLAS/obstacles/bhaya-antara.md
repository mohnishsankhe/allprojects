# Fear from making a gap (Taittirīya 2.7.1)

`obs:bhaya-antara` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Fear arises for one who makes even a small gap or difference in that which is invisible, unexpressed and unsupported; it is the fear of the knower who does not reflect (2.7.1). Fearlessness is found in that support.
