# The envelopments (paryavasthāna)

`obs:paryavasthanas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** passion
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
**Taught in:** [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md), [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md)

Eight (or ten) secondary defilements: shamelessness, lack of dread of blame, envy, avarice, restlessness, remorse, torpor, sleepiness (+ anger, concealment); for the Sautrāntika, 'envelopment' is the awakened defilement.
**Members:** āhrīkya, anapatrāpya, īrṣyā, mātsarya, auddhatya, kaukṛtya, styāna, middha, krodha, mrakṣa
