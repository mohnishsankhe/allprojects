# The conceit of identity with God

`obs:abheda-buddhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)

The thought 'I am Brahman / I am ever the Lord, and the world is unreal', which the texts attribute to the asuras and describe as leading to blinding darkness; it contradicts experience and scripture.
**Antidotes:** knowledge of the five differences, [Hearing the scriptures (śravaṇa)](../practices/sravana.md)
