# The ten imperfections of insight (vipassanūpakkilesa)

`obs:ten-vipassanupakkilesas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāsi Sayadaw tradition (the 'new Burmese method')](../lineages/mahasi.md), [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Experiences arising with tender insight into rise and fall that are mistaken for the path; grasped by view, conceit and craving they become thirty.
