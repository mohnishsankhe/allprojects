# Attachment (rāga)

`obs:raga-nyaya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** passion
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md), [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)
**Taught in:** [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md), [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)

Attachment to the agreeable, arising from false knowledge and from the notion of the whole; in Vaiśeṣika it arises from pleasure, absorption, satisfaction, the unseen and particular birth.
**Antidotes:** [Cultivating the notion of the unlovely (aśubhasaṃjñā)](../practices/asubha-samjna-nyaya.md), [Repeated contemplation of objects and of the body (prasaṅkhyāna)](../practices/prasankhyana-nyaya.md)
