# Conceptual thought (vikalpa) as the adverse side

`obs:conceptual-thought` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Taught in:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)

Conceptual thought (rnam rtog) is the enemy destroyed by the siddhas' realization — the deer of grasped and grasper shot by Śavaripa, the adverse side torn by Līlapa's ten perfections, the waves stilled by seeing one's mind.
