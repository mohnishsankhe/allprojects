# Practice learned from texts without a guru

`obs:practice-without-guru` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

The haṭha texts hold that the techniques succeed only when received from a guru: knowledge from the guru's mouth is potent, otherwise it is fruitless, powerless and brings great suffering (ŚS 3.11); the inverting technique is known from the guru's instruction, not from millions of treatises (HYP 3.78); khecarī requires the guru's mantra (Khecarīvidyā).
**Antidotes:** receiving instruction from a qualified guru and serving him (ŚS 3.12-15)
