# Hunger, thirst, sorrow, delusion, old age and death

`obs:sad-urmi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

The self within all is beyond hunger and thirst, sorrow and delusion, old age and death (BAU 3.5.1); the self free from evil is without old age, death, sorrow, hunger and thirst (ChU 8.1.5, 8.7.1).
**Members:** aśanāyā (hunger), pipāsā (thirst), śoka (sorrow), moha (delusion), jarā (old age), mṛtyu (death)
