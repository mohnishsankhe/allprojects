# The three bonds (prakṛti-, vaikārika-, dakṣiṇā-bandha)

`obs:threefold-bondage` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** bond
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Taught in:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)

Bondage to prakṛti (absorption from taking the eight prakṛtis as highest), to the modifications (renunciants and worldly people ruled by senses and objects) and through gifts (given with conceit by students, householders, mendicants and forest-dwellers).
**Members:** prakṛtibandha, vaikārikabandha, dakṣiṇābandha
