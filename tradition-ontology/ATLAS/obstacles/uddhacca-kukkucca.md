# Restlessness and remorse (uddhaccakukkucca)

`obs:uddhacca-kukkucca` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** hindrance
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Fourth hindrance. In Vism IV happiness (sukha) is its opposite (p. 141). How it obstructs, in the text's words: consciousness overtaken by restlessness and remorse is unquiet and wanders about. Vism XIV defines restlessness (uddhacca) by the mark of non-calming, function of unsteadiness like water stirred by wind, manifestation of bewilderment, proximate cause unwise attention to the non-calming of mind; remorse (kukkucca) by the mark of later regret, function of sorrow over what has been done and not done, manifestation of remorse, proximate cause what has been done and not done ('to be regarded as slavery').
**Antidotes:** happiness as the opposed jhāna factor (Vism IV p. 141), developing tranquillity, concentration and equanimity (SN 46.53), [Mindfulness of breathing (ānāpānassati)](../practices/anapanasati.md)
