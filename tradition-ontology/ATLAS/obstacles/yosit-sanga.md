# Sexual association, as the renunciant texts frame it (strī-saṅga)

`obs:yosit-sanga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** bond
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

Texts addressed to male renunciants warn that attachment to women and to men attached to women binds more than any other (the moth, the elephant, Purūravas); the Kapila Gītā applies the parallel warning to a woman's attachment to husband, children and home; the Avadhūta Gītā's eighth chapter states it in harsh terms. Recorded as the texts state it.
**Antidotes:** solitude, holy company, dispassion
