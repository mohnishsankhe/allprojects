# The five sheaths of limitation (kañcuka)

Limited agency (kalā), limited knowledge (vidyā), attachment (rāga), time (kāla) and restriction (niyati), which with māyā make the inner covering of the individual: 'only now, only a little, only this, I know with all of myself' (Paramārthasāra 16-17).
