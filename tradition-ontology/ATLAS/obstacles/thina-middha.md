# Sloth and torpor (thīnamiddha)

`obs:thina-middha` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** hindrance
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Third hindrance. In Vism IV applied thought (vitakka) is its opposite (p. 141). How it obstructs, in the text's words: consciousness overcome by sloth and torpor becomes unwieldy (akammañña). Vism XIV defines sloth (thīna) by the mark of lack of energy, function of dispelling energy, manifestation of sinking; torpor (middha) by the mark of unworkableness, function of smothering, manifestation of drooping or drowsy sleep; proximate cause of both, unwise attention to discontent, lazy stretching and the like.
**Antidotes:** applied thought as the opposed jhāna factor (Vism IV p. 141), [Perception of light](../practices/aloka-sanna.md), [Walking meditation (caṅkama)](../practices/cankama.md), arousing energy (SN 46.53)
