# Hate (dosa)

`obs:dosa` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** poison
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Vism XIV: hate (dosa) is that by which one hates, or which hates, or mere hating; its mark is ferocity, like a struck snake; its function is to spread, like a fall of poison, or to burn its own support, like a forest fire; its manifestation is fury, like a foe who has found his chance; its proximate cause is the grounds of resentment; to be regarded as urine mixed with poison. Vism IX opens with reflection on the danger in hate and the benefit of patience before loving-kindness is developed.
**Antidotes:** loving-kindness; the divine abidings (Vism III p. 114)
