# The eight intrusive circumstances and eight incompatible propensities

`obs:eight-intrusive-circumstances` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)

Circumstances that suddenly destroy the freedoms (disturbance by the five poisons, stupidity, a false teacher, laziness, a flood of bad karma, servitude, practice only for protection, hypocrisy) and propensities that cut off the chance of liberation (worldly ties, bad character, no disgust with saṃsāra, no faith, delight in wrongdoing, no interest in Dharma, broken vows, broken samaya).
