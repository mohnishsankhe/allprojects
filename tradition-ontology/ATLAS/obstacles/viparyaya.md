# Error (viparyaya): the five-jointed avidyā

`obs:viparyaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Taught in:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)

Five kinds of error with sixty-two subdivisions (SK 47–48): darkness (8), delusion (8), great delusion (10), gloom (18), blind gloom (18) — taking the eight prakṛtis for the self and thinking oneself liberated, attachment to the eight powers, attachment to the ten objects, resentment over powers and objects, and despair at losing them in death.
**Antidotes:** knowledge of the principles (SK 44, 64), [Repeated practice of the principles (tattvābhyāsa)](../practices/tattvabhyasa.md)
**Members:** tamas, moha, mahāmoha, tāmisra, andhatāmisra
