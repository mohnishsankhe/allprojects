# The fourteen unwholesome mental factors

`obs:fourteen-akusala-cetasikas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

The unwholesome factors of the Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha: delusion, shamelessness, fearlessness of wrongdoing, restlessness; greed, wrong view, conceit; hatred, envy, avarice, remorse; sloth, torpor; doubt.
