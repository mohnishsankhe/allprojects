# The five deadly acts (ānantarya)

**Category:** karma-type
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Mūlasarvāstivāda](../lineages/mulasarvastivada.md), [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Killing mother, father or an arhat, dividing the Saṅgha, wounding a Buddha with evil intent; they ripen in hell in the next life; the schism-lie is the gravest.
