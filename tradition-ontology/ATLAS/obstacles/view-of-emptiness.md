# The view of emptiness (śūnyatādṛṣṭi)

`obs:view-of-emptiness` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md)

Taking emptiness itself as a view or as annihilation — 'incurable', worse than a view of self as large as Sumeru; the RGV criticizes those 'distracted about emptiness'.
