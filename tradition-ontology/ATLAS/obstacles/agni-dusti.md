# Disorders of the digestive fire

`obs:agni-dusti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** dosa-imbalance
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

The fire becomes irregular (by vāta), sharp (by pitta) or weak (by kapha); balanced doṣas give a balanced fire (AHS Sū 1.8); when the fire is extinguished one dies (Ca Ci 15.4).
**Antidotes:** measured diet (Ca Sū 5.3-8), digestives
