# The marks of tamas and rajas (Maitrī 3.5)

**Category:** guna

**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

MaiU 3.5 lists the marks of tamas (delusion, fear, despondency, sleep, sloth, heedlessness, old age, grief, hunger, thirst...) and of rajas (craving, affection, passion, greed, violence, lust, hatred, deceit...), with which the elemental self is filled.
**Antidotes:** knowledge, austerity and one's own dharma (MaiU 4.3)
