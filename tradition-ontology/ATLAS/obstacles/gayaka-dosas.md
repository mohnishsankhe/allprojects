# The twenty-five faults of singers

`obs:gayaka-dosas` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md)
**Taught in:** [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md)

Faults that make a singer censured — biting the teeth, shouting, hissing, fear, hesitation, trembling, gaping, a crow-like voice, losing the tāla, off-pitch notes, inattention, nasality and the rest (ŚR 3.24–26); in the body, certain petals on which the jīva rests destroy success in music (ŚR 1.2.140–144).
