# Acting with desire for fruits

`obs:sakamata` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)

Action done for its results; desirelessness, flawless service and fitness are the conditions for seeing Hari, and ritual works done for results win only the lowest grace (heaven).
