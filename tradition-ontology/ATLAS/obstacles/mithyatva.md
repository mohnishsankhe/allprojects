# Wrong view (mithyātva)

`obs:mithyatva` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
**Taught in:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)

Belief contrary to the realities, the first and root cause of bondage; five kinds (one-sided, contrary, indiscriminate reverence, doubt, ignorance — Digambara list) or innate and acquired.
**Antidotes:** right view through the three processes and the breaking of the knot
