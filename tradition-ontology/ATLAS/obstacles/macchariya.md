# Avarice (macchariya)

`obs:macchariya` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Vism XIV, an indeterminate factor in the hate-rooted consciousness: avarice is the state of the miser; its mark is hiding one's own success, gained or to be gained; its function is not bearing to share it with others; its manifestation is shrinking or a bitter mood; its proximate cause is one's own success; to be regarded as an ugly state of mind. Vism XXII counts it fivefold: over dwelling, family, gain, teaching and praise.
