# Attachment and pride at the celestials' invitation

`obs:sanga-smaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

At the honey stage the gods invite the yogin with pleasures, elixirs, sky-cars and nymphs; attachment would make him fuel for saṃsāra again, and pride ('even the gods desire me') lets carelessness revive the afflictions (3.51, YBh 3.51).
