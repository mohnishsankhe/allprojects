# Doubt and scruple (śaṅkā), especially about purity

`obs:sanka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** bond
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md), [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md)
**Taught in:** [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md), [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md)

Doubt and scruples about purity and impurity bind: the purity prescribed by those of little knowledge is impurity in Śambhu's vision (VBT 123); in the Krama phases, the Yama Kālī gives rise to and removes doubt (TĀ 4.151-152).
