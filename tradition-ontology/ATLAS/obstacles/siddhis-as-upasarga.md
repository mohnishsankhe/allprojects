# Powers as obstacles in samādhi (upasarga)

`obs:siddhis-as-upasarga` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

3.37: the arisings that follow saṃyama (prātibha, divine hearing, touch, sight, taste, smell, 3.36) are obstacles (upasarga) for a mind in samādhi and powers (siddhi) for the outgoing mind; the bhāṣya says they are opposed to the seeing of it. 3.51 adds the warning about attachment and pride when invited by those of high stations.
