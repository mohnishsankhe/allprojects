# The imperfections of concentration

`obs:samadhi-upakkilesa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Doubt, inattention, sloth and torpor, fear, elation, inertia, excess energy, slack energy, longing, perception of diversity and excessive meditation on forms, which make the light and vision of forms vanish (MN 128).
