# The upasargas of the Mārkaṇḍeya

After the self is seen: longings for ritual rewards, women, heaven, divinity, elixirs and supernatural feats; then five states - prātibha, śrāvaṇa, daiva, bhrama, āvarta - by which even the gods lose their yoga and return.
**Antidotes:** turning the mind to Brahman, the mental white blanket and contemplation of Brahman (MkP 40.14-15)
