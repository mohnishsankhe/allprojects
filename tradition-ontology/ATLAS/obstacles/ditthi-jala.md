# The net of views (diṭṭhijāla)

`obs:ditthi-jala` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** fetter
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md)

Speculative views about past and future, in sixty-two grounds, in which every theorist is caught; they are only the feeling of those who do not know, conditioned by contact and craving.
**Antidotes:** understanding the arising, passing, gratification, danger and escape of feeling and contact (DN 1)
