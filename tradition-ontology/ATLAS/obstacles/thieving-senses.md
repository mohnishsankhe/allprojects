# The thieving senses (kaḷḷap pulaṉ)

`obs:thieving-senses` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Taught in:** [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)

The five senses as thieves and a forest to be cut down and burnt before the 'house' of release can be seen (Kaṭuveḷi 13–14); cut off by the guru (Aḻukaṇṇi 34); burnt by the seeker (Pattirakiriyār 1); TM 1823 calls them the thieving senses that become the temple's lamps once tamed.
**Antidotes:** [Silence of the muni (mauna, mauneya)](../practices/mauna.md), [Seeking and worshipping the guru](../practices/siddha-guru-worship.md), the guru's grace
