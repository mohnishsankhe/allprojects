# The false I-notion (ahaṅkāra)

`obs:ahankara-nyaya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
**Taught in:** [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)

The mistaken sense of 'I' regarding body and the like, which ceases through knowledge of the truth about the causes of the faults and through contemplation of the body within.
**Antidotes:** [Repeated contemplation of objects and of the body (prasaṅkhyāna)](../practices/prasankhyana-nyaya.md)
