# The diseases of the mind (Rāmcaritmānas)

`obs:manasa-roga` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md)
**Taught in:** [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md)

Delusion (moha) is the root; lust is wind, greed phlegm, anger bile; jealousy, pride, hypocrisy and the rest follow; cured only when Rāma's grace brings the true guru as physician, faith, restraint and devotion.
**Antidotes:** the true guru's word, faith, restraint, [The nine forms of devotion (navadhā bhakti)](../practices/navadha-bhakti.md)
