# One-sided dogmatism (ekānta)

`obs:ekanta-view` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
**Taught in:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)

Holding one aspect or standpoint as the whole truth — the root of rival doctrines and of sectarian quarrel; the Adhyātma critics apply it also to one-sided niścaya and one-sided vyavahāra (Āptamīmāṃsā; Jñānasāra 16; Mokṣamārga Prakāśaka).
**Antidotes:** anekānta; impartiality (mādhyasthya)
