# Taking the body to be the self

`obs:dehatmabuddhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Virocana concludes from the teaching of the reflected person that the body is the self and teaches the asuras to worship and adorn the body — 'the doctrine of the asuras'; Indra sees that such a self perishes with the body (ChU 8.7-8.9).
**Antidotes:** continued inquiry with a teacher (Indra's 101 years, ChU 8.7-12)
**Sources:** 
  - [Chāndogya Upaniṣad](../texts/chandogya-upanisad.md) — ref: 8.7.4-8.9.1; rests_on: ["tea:chandogya-upanisad:8.7.4-8.8.5", "tea:chandogya-upanisad:8.9.1-8.10.4"]
