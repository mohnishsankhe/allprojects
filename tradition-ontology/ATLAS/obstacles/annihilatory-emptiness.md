# Clinging to an emptiness of annihilation (chad stong)

`obs:annihilatory-emptiness` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md)
**Taught in:** [Jonang (jo nang pa)](../lineages/jonang.md)

Taking the ultimate to be a mere nothingness, a non-affirming negation; for the Jonang an extreme that misses the emptiness endowed with all supreme aspects.
**Antidotes:** recognition of the other-empty ultimate through the vajra-yoga
**Sources:**
