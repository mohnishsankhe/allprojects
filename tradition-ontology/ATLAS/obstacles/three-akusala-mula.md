# The three unwholesome roots

`obs:three-akusala-mula` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** poison
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Greed, hatred and delusion — the roots of the unwholesome (MN 9), the three fires (SN 35.28) and the sources of kamma (AN 3.34); their destruction is nibbāna (SN 38.1).
**Antidotes:** non-greed, non-hatred, non-delusion (AN 3.34)
**Members:** obs:lobha, obs:dosa, obs:moha
