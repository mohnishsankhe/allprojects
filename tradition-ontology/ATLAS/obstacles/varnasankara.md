# Confusion of the classes (varṇasaṅkara)

`obs:varnasankara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Arthaśāstra (the science of statecraft and wealth)](../lineages/arthasastra.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Arthaśāstra (the science of statecraft and wealth)](../lineages/arthasastra.md), [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)

As the tradition states it: the mixing of classes through unions and abandonment of one's own dharma, which the texts regard as destroying the social order (Arthaśāstra 1.3.15; MDh 10).
