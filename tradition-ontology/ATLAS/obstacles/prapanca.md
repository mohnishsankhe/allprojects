# Conceptual elaboration (prapañca)

`obs:prapanca` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)

Verbal-conceptual elaboration from which conceptual construction, action and afflictions arise; those who elaborate the Buddha do not see him.
**Antidotes:** emptiness, in which elaboration ceases (MMK 18.5)
