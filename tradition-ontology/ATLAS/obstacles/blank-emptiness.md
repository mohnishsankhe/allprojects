# Indeterminate (blank) emptiness

`obs:blank-emptiness` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)
**Taught in:** [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md)

Sitting with an empty mind, thinking of nothing, and taking this for the vastness of prajñā or for no-thought; cutting off all thought is 'a great error' and leads to rebirth elsewhere.
**Antidotes:** no-thought as thinking of suchness
