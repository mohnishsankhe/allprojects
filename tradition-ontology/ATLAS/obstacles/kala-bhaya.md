# Fear of Time (death)

`obs:kala-bhaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Taught in:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)

Kāla (Time/death) devours the unpractised; out of fear of it Brahmā, yogīs and sages practise breath-control; the yogī in samādhi is not devoured by time (GŚ 38, 98).
