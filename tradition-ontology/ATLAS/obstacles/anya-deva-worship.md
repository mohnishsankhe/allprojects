Bhagavad Gītā 7.20–23, 9.23–25, as the text states it: those whose knowledge is carried off by desires resort to other deities, constrained by their nature; the Lord makes their faith firm and it is he who ordains the fruits they obtain, but the fruit is finite — worshippers of the gods go to the gods. Devotees of other deities who sacrifice with faith sacrifice to him, but not according to the rule; not recognizing him in truth as enjoyer and lord of all sacrifices, they fall.
**Antidotes:** recognizing the Lord in truth as enjoyer of all sacrifices (9.24), worship of the Lord himself (7.23, 9.25)
