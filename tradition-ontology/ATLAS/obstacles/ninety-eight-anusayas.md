# The ninety-eight latent defilements (anuśaya)

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mūlasarvāstivāda](../lineages/mulasarvastivada.md), [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md), [Vaibhāṣika](../lineages/vaibhasika.md)

The root of existence: six anuśayas (passion, aversion, pride, ignorance, view, doubt), seven with passion split into sensual and existence-passion, ten with the five views; distributed by realm and by what abandons them — the seeing of each truth, or cultivation — they number ninety-eight.
