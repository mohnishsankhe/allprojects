# I-am-ness (asmitā)

`obs:asmita` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

The seeming identity of the power of the seer (puruṣa) and the power of seeing (buddhi) (YS 2.6). Vyāsa: enjoyment is possible only while the two, utterly distinct, seem undivided; one who does not see the puruṣa as distinct from the buddhi makes a self-notion (ātma-buddhi) there through delusion (YBh 2.6). (Also the name of the fourth samādhi accompaniment, YS 1.17.)
