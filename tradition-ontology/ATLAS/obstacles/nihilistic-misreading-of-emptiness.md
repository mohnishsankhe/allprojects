# Misreading emptiness as nothingness

`obs:nihilistic-misreading-of-emptiness` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md), [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md), [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)

Taking emptiness to mean that nothing exists, so that karma, the path and the Buddha are denied; wrongly seen emptiness destroys the slow-witted like a snake wrongly grasped.
**Antidotes:** understanding the two truths (MMK 24.8–10), understanding emptiness as dependent origination (MMK 24.18)
