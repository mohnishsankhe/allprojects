# The three desires (for sons, wealth and worlds)

`obs:esana-traya` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** bond
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)

Knowers of the self rise above the desire for sons, the desire for wealth and the desire for worlds and live as mendicants; 'the desire for sons is the desire for wealth, and the desire for wealth is the desire for worlds' (BAU 3.5.1, 4.4.22).
