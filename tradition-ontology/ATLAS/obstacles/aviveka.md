# Non-discrimination (aviveka)

`obs:aviveka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** bond
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Taught in:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)

The failure to discriminate puruṣa from prakṛti, cause of their conjunction and so of bondage; beginningless, removed by discrimination as darkness by light; 'bondage is non-discrimination alone'.
