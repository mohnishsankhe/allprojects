# Censure of other schools

`obs:sarvadarsana-ninda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Taught in:** [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md), [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)

Censuring any school is forbidden (PKS 1.14; Kaula Upaniṣad): the schools are valid for their own qualified followers, and censure makes them doubt their own way without qualifying them for the Kaula (Bhāskararāya).
