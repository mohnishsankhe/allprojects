# Rejecting the true dharma (saddharmapratikṣepa)

`obs:rejecting-the-true-dharma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** karma-type
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md)

Denying that the Mahāyāna sūtras are the Buddha's word; a karma worse than the five deadly acts, excluded from the eighteenth vow, and the mark of the icchantika.
