# Grasping at inherent existence (true-grasping)

`obs:svabhava-graha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md), [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md)
