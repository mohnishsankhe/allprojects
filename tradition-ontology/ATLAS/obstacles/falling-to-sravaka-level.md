# Falling to the level of śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas

`obs:falling-to-sravaka-level` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)
