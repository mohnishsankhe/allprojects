# Choosing the pleasant over the good

`obs:preyas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** passion
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

The fool chooses the pleasant (preyas) for the sake of getting and keeping; the child deluded by wealth thinks 'this is the world, there is no other' and falls again into Death's power (KU 1.2.2, 1.2.6).
**Antidotes:** discerning the good from the pleasant, as Naciketas did (KU 1.1.21-29)
