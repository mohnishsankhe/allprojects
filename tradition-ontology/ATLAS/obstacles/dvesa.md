Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Aversion (dveṣa), set together with passion in each sense towards its object, is a waylayer; one should not come under its sway (3.34). The self-governed person moves among objects with senses free of attraction and aversion and attains serenity (2.64); the one of steady wisdom neither rejoices nor hates (na dveṣṭi) on meeting the pleasant or unpleasant (2.57).
