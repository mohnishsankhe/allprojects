# Greed (lobha)

Vism XIV, in the consciousness rooted in greed: greed (lobha) is that by which one is greedy, or which is greedy, or mere greed; its mark is grasping an object, like a monkey-lime; its function is sticking, like a piece of meat in a hot pan; its manifestation is non-relinquishing, like lamp-black dye; its proximate cause is seeing enjoyment in things that fetter. Craving growing as a river is to be regarded as a fast stream to the ocean, taking one to the states of loss. Vism III gives the greedy temperament (rāgacarita) its own marks (phn:vism-carita-raga).
**Antidotes:** the foulness contemplations for the greedy temperament (named only; summary-only) - Vism III p. 114
