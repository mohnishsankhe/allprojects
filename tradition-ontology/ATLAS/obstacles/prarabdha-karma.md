# Begun karma (prārabdha)

`obs:prarabdha-karma` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** karma-type
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

Karma that has begun to bear fruit continues until the body falls; for this reason there is no liberation while living. For the distressed surrenderer the Lord ends it at once.
