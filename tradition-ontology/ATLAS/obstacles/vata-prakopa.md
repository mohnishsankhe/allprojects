# Aggravation of vāta

`obs:vata-prakopa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** dosa-imbalance
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Vāta increases by the repeated use of dry, light, cold, rough and clear things, exertion, fasting, waking, grief and the like, especially in the rains, at dawn and afternoon and after digestion; increased, it brings roughness of voice, emaciation, darkness, tremor, desire for warmth, sleeplessness, weakness and hard stools; provoked, it afflicts body and mind, causing fear, grief, delusion and confusion.
**Antidotes:** substances of opposite qualities (Ca Sū 1.59-62), [The five purificatory therapies (pañcakarma)](../practices/pancakarma.md), [Seasonal routine (ṛtucaryā)](../practices/rtucarya.md), [Daily routine (dinacaryā)](../practices/dinacarya.md)
