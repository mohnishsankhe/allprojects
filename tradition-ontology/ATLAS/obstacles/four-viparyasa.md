# The four inversions (and the Mahāyāna double set)

`obs:four-viparyasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Tathāgatagarbha (Buddha-nature) tradition](../lineages/tathagatagarbha.md)

Taking the impermanent, suffering, selfless and impure as permanent, blissful, self and pure; in the Mahāparinirvāṇa and Śrīmālā also taking the Tathāgata's permanence, bliss, self and purity as their opposites.
