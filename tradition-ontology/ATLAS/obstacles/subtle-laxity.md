# Subtle laxity (bying ba phra mo)

`obs:subtle-laxity` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md)
**Taught in:** [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md)

Laxity in which the object remains with some clarity but the intensity of clarity has slackened; the most insidious obstacle to calm abiding because it is mistaken for good meditation.
**Antidotes:** [Calm abiding according to the lamrim](../practices/calm-abiding-lamrim.md), introspective alertness; uplifting the mind (e.g. by recalling the value of the human life)
**Sources:**
