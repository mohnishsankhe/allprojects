# The nine obstacles (antarāya), distractions of the mind

`obs:nine-antarayas` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

Illness (vyādhi), dullness (styāna), doubt (saṃśaya), heedlessness (pramāda), laziness (ālasya), non-abstention (avirati), erroneous vision (bhrānti-darśana), failure to gain a stage (alabdha-bhūmikatva) and instability (anavasthitatva): the distractions (vikṣepa) of the mind, which occur together with its activities and are absent when they are absent; Vyāsa also calls them the taints (mala) of yoga, its enemies (pratipakṣa) and obstacles (antarāya) (YS 1.30, YBh 1.30). They do not arise through devotion to Īśvara (YBh 1.29). They are accompanied by pain, dejection, trembling of the limbs and disturbed inhalation and exhalation (YS 1.31).
