# The urges to be restrained

`obs:dharaniya-vegas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** passion
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Greed, grief, fear, anger, pride, shamelessness, envy, excessive passion and covetousness, harsh or false speech and acts that harm others — urges which, unlike the natural ones, must be restrained (Ca Sū 7.26-29; AHS Sū 4.24).
