# The faults that burn beings (bhūtadāhīya doṣa)

`obs:bhutadahiya-dosas` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)

Anger, exultation, wrath, greed, delusion, hypocrisy, malice, lying, gluttony, slander, envy, lust, rage, lack of self-control and lack of yoga; their removal is rooted in yoga (ĀpDh 1.8.23.3–5).
