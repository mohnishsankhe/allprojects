# Breaking the samaya pledges

`obs:samaya-bhanga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md), [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)

Without the samaya discipline there is no perfection and one falls from the Kula (Kulārṇava 5.96); disciples who do not honour the samayas become tāmasa (Kubjikāmata 12.3-9).
**Antidotes:** asking the guru's pardon and worshipping the transmission (Kubjikāmata 12.10), [Prāyaścitta (expiation)](../practices/prayascitta.md)
