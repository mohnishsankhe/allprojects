# False knowledge (mithyājñāna)

`obs:mithyajnana-nyaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md), [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)
**Taught in:** [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md), [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)

The root of the series leading to birth and pain: taking the non-self as self, denying the self, taking pain as pleasure, the impermanent as permanent, the unprotecting as protection, the fearful as safe, the loathsome as desirable; denying karma, its fruit and rebirth; fearing liberation.
**Antidotes:** knowledge of truth (tattvajñāna), [Yoga and the practice of samādhi (Nyāya)](../practices/nyaya-yoga.md), [Reflection (manana) by reasoning](../practices/manana-nyaya.md)
