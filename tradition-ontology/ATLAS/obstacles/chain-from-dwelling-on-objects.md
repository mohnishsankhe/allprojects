# The chain of ruin from dwelling on objects

`obs:chain-from-dwelling-on-objects` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Taught in:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Dwelling on objects breeds attachment; attachment desire; desire anger; anger delusion; delusion loss of memory; loss of memory destruction of the intellect; and so one perishes (BhG 2.62–63).
**Antidotes:** [Restraint and withdrawal of the senses](../practices/indriya-nigraha.md), moving among objects free of attraction and aversion (2.64)
**Members:** dhyāna of objects, saṅga, kāma, krodha, saṃmoha, smṛti-vibhrama, buddhi-nāśa
