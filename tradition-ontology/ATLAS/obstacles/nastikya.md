# Unbelief (nāstikya) and the 'Lokāyata' reading of Mīmāṃsā

`obs:nastikya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** other

**Taught in:** [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md)

Denial of the self, the afterlife and the Veda's authority; Kumārila writes to lead Mīmāṃsā, 'mostly made Lokāyata', back onto the āstika path, and presents Śabara's proof of the self as its refutation.
