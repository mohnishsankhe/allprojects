# Desire and anger, the enemy (kāma–krodha)

`obs:kama-the-enemy` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** passion
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Taught in:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Desire, and anger with it, born of rajas, all-devouring, is the constant enemy of the wise; it covers knowledge as smoke covers fire and has the senses, mind and intellect as its seat (BhG 3.36–43).
**Antidotes:** [Restraint and withdrawal of the senses](../practices/indriya-nigraha.md), knowing the self beyond the intellect (3.43)
