# The five accompaniments of distraction (vikṣepa-sahabhuva)

`obs:viksepa-sahabhuva` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

Pain (duḥkha, threefold: of oneself, of other beings, of the divine), dejection (daurmanasya: a disturbance of the mind through the frustration of a wish), trembling of the limbs (aṅgamejayatva), inhalation (śvāsa, when the prāṇa takes in outer air) and exhalation (praśvāsa, when it expels air from the belly). Vyāsa: they occur in a distracted mind and do not occur in a concentrated one (YS 1.31, YBh 1.31).
**Antidotes:** [Practice on a single principle (ekatattva-abhyāsa)](../practices/ekatattva-abhyasa.md), practice and dispassion (YBh 1.31)
**Members:** duḥkha, daurmanasya, aṅgamejayatva, śvāsa, praśvāsa
