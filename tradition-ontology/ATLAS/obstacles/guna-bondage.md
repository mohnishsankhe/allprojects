# Bondage by the three guṇas

`obs:guna-bondage` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** guna
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Taught in:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

The guṇas bind the embodied one: sattva by attachment to happiness and knowledge, rajas by attachment to action, tamas by negligence, sloth and sleep (BhG 14.5–8); attachment to the guṇas causes birth in good and evil wombs (13.21).
**Antidotes:** seeing no agent but the guṇas (14.19), unswerving devotion (14.26), [Taking refuge in the Lord alone (śaraṇāgati)](../practices/saranagati.md), taking refuge in the Lord alone (7.14), [Seeing that the guṇas act: non-doership](../practices/guna-witnessing.md), [Undivided devotion (ananya-bhakti)](../practices/ananya-bhakti.md), seeing no agent but the guṇas and knowing what is higher than them (14.19), serving the Lord with the unswerving yoga of devotion (14.26)
**Members:** sattva: attachment to happiness and knowledge, rajas: attachment to action, tamas: negligence, sloth, sleep, sattva: attachment to happiness (sukha-saṅga) and to knowledge (jñāna-saṅga), rajas: attachment to action (karma-saṅga), tamas: heedlessness (pramāda), idleness (ālasya), sleep (nidrā)
