# The three follies (mūḍhatā)

`obs:three-mudhatas` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)

Folly regarding the world (bathing in rivers, heaping stones for merit), regarding gods (worshipping passionate deities for favours) and regarding false ascetics — faults of right view (Ratnakaraṇḍa).
