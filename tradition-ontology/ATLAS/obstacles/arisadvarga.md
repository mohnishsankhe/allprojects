# The six inner enemies (kāma, krodha, lobha, moha, mada, mātsarya)

`obs:arisadvarga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** passion
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)

Desire, anger, greed, delusion, pride and envy are to be avoided (PKS 1.19); in the Bhāvanā Upaniṣad they, with merit and demerit, are the eight Mothers (Brāhmī and the others) of the first enclosure — to be recognized as powers of the Goddess.
