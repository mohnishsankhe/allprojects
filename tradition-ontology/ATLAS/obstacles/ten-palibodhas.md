# The ten impediments (paḷibodha)

`obs:ten-palibodhas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Things to be cut off before developing concentration; supernormal power is an impediment to insight, not to concentration.
**Antidotes:** severing or settling each (e.g. medicine for affliction)
**Members:** āvāsa (dwelling), kula (family), lābha (gain), gaṇa (following), kamma (building work), addhāna (travel), ñāti (kin), ābādha (affliction), gantha (books), iddhi (supernormal power)
