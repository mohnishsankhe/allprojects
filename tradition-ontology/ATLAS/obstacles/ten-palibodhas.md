# The ten impediments (paḷibodha)

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Vism III: the impediments to be cut before taking a meditation subject are dwelling, family, gain, class of pupils, building work, travel, relatives, illness, books, and supernormal power. The text gives conditions for each: dwelling is an impediment only for one zealous over building or hoarding belongings or bound to it; family only for one who is bound up with the supporters (parents may not be); gain for the monk of merit who has no time for his work, cut by wandering where unknown; class for the teacher who gives recitation, cut by finishing or handing on; building work is cut by finishing, handing over or giving up; travel by doing the thing awaited first; relatives by nursing them back to health; illness by treatment, and if it does not subside, by rebuking the body and practising; books only for one constantly busy reciting; supernormal power is an impediment to insight, not to concentration.
**Antidotes:** severing or settling each (e.g. medicine for affliction)
