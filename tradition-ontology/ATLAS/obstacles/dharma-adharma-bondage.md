# Merit and demerit as binding (adṛṣṭa)

`obs:dharma-adharma-bondage` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** karma-type
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md), [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)
**Taught in:** [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md), [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)

Both merit and demerit bind: engaged in through desire and aversion, they bring about connection with bodies; liberation requires that no new ones arise and the accumulated ones be exhausted.
**Antidotes:** knowledge of the categories, detachment
