# The three kinds of suffering (Pāśupata account)

`obs:duhkha-traya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

Suffering of the self (bodily illnesses; mental states such as anger, greed, delusion, fear, despondency, envy, hatred, pride), from beings and from the gods; also womb, birth, ignorance, old age and death. Their final removal is the end of suffering.
