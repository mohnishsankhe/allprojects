# The restless mind as demon (akappēy)

`obs:akappey-restless-mind` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** hindrance
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Taught in:** [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)

The mind that runs like a demon or whirls like a fish in a net, to be stilled (Akappēy's refrain; Pattirakiriyār 26); stilling it is the hardest skill (Tāyumāṉavar).
