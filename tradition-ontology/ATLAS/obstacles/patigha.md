# Resentment and aversion (paṭigha, āghāta)

`obs:patigha` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** fetter
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

In Vism IX, resentment (āghāta) arising in the meditator when loving-kindness is directed to an enemy is to be removed by a sequence of reflections given by the text (see prc:patigha-vinodana-vism). Vism XXII lists sensual lust and ill will among the lower fetters, cut in gross form by the second path knowledge and in subtle form by the third.
