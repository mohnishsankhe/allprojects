# Ego (ahaṅkāra)

`obs:ahankara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

The sense 'I am the doer', first superimposition on the Self; in the story of Dāma, Vyāla and Kaṭa its arising makes the invincible weak.
**Antidotes:** the conviction 'I am not the doer', [Relinquishment before action (sāttvika-tyāga)](../practices/sattvika-tyaga.md)
