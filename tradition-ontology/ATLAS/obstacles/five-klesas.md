# The five afflictions (kleśa)

`obs:five-klesas` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

Ignorance (avidyā), I-am-ness (asmitā), attachment (rāga), aversion (dveṣa) and clinging to life (abhiniveśa) (YS 2.3); Vyāsa: 'the five errors' (viparyaya), which he names darkness, delusion, great delusion, gloom and blind gloom (YBh 1.8). Active, they make firm the office of the guṇas, keep transformation going, raise the stream of cause and effect and, interdependent, bring karma to ripening (YBh 2.3). Ignorance is their field; each is dormant, attenuated, interrupted or fully active, and the burnt-seed state belongs to the last body only (YS 2.4, YBh 2.4). The store of karma is rooted in them (YS 2.12) and ripens while they exist (YS 2.13).
**Antidotes:** [Kriyā-yoga (austerity, self-study, devotion to Īśvara)](../practices/kriya-yoga.md), [Cultivating the opposite (pratipakṣa-bhāvanā)](../practices/pratipaksa-bhavana.md), [Meditation (dhyāna) as the means of abandoning the afflictions' activities](../practices/dhyana.md), [Cultivating discriminative discernment (viveka-khyāti)](../practices/viveka-khyati.md), thinned by kriyā-yoga (YS 2.2), abandoned in the gross form by meditation (YS 2.11), in the subtle form by involution (YS 2.10), `prc:kriya-yoga (thins them, 2.2)`, `prc:dhyana (removes their gross activities, 2.11)`, involution (pratiprasava) for the subtle (2.10)
