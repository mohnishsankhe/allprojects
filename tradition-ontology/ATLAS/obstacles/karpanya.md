# The fault of kārpaṇya (pitiable weakness)

Bhagavad Gītā 1–3: Arjuna says his very nature is struck by the fault of kārpaṇya (kārpaṇya-doṣa, 'pitiable weakness') and his mind bewildered about dharma, and so he asks Kṛṣṇa for instruction as his disciple (2.7). (In 2.49 those whose motive is the fruit are called kṛpaṇa, 'pitiable'.)
