# Conceptual construction (kalpanā, vikalpa)

`obs:kalpana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)
**Taught in:** [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md)

All conceptions — even meditations on sun, moon and fire and on the gods — keep beings turning like a water-wheel; the schools full of vikalpa do not free from saṃsāra (Jñānakārikā 2.20–23; Akulavīra A.7–10).
