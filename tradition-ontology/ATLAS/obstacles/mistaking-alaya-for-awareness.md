# Mistaking the all-base for awareness

`obs:mistaking-alaya-for-awareness` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)
**Taught in:** [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md)

Resting in a blank, indifferent, thoughtless state — the all-base (kun gzhi) — and taking it for the dharmakāya; Longchenpa warns that this leads to the formless realms, not to liberation.
**Antidotes:** [Separating saṃsāra and nirvāṇa ('khor 'das ru shan)](../practices/rushen.md), [Cutting through (khregs chod)](../practices/trekcho.md)
