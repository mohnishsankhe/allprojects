# The three impurities (mala-traya)

`obs:three-malas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** impurity
**Convergence:** 6 independent lineage(s): [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md), [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md), [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md), [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)

In the Pratyabhijñā: the āṇava impurity (loss of freedom by awareness, or awareness without freedom), the māyīya (appearance of the knowable as separate) and the kārma (agency in an unconscious agent); all three arise through the power of māyā (ĪPK 3.2.4-5). The root impurity is ignorance, not a substance (TĀ 1.22-23, MVT 1.23).
**Antidotes:** knowledge (jñāna), descent of grace (śaktipāta), dīkṣā, [Recognition (pratyabhijñā) of one's self as the Lord](../practices/pratyabhijna-recognition.md), [Liberating initiation (nirvāṇa-dīkṣā)](../practices/nirvana-diksa.md), [Jñāna (knowledge of Śiva)](../practices/saiva-jnana.md), Śiva's grace, [Liṅga-initiation (liṅgadīkṣā)](../practices/linga-diksa.md), [Śivayoga-samādhi](../practices/sivayoga-samadhi.md)
