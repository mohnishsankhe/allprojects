# Clinging to life (abhiniveśa)

`obs:abhinivesa` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

Flowing by its own momentum, rooted thus even in the learned (YS 2.9). Vyāsa: in every living being there is the constant wish 'may I not cease to be; may I be'; even a worm just born shows fear of death, of the nature of the view of annihilation; both the deluded and the wise have this tendency (vāsanā) from the experience of the pain of death (YBh 2.9).
