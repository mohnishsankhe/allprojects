# Shame before people and family honour (lok-lāj)

`obs:loka-lajja` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** bond
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Mīrābāī's devotion (the Mīrā song-tradition)](../lineages/mirabai.md), [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md)
**Taught in:** [Mīrābāī's devotion (the Mīrā song-tradition)](../lineages/mirabai.md), [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md)

Concern for public opinion and family honour that keeps one from wholehearted devotion; Mīrā casts it off, and Tulsī counsels giving up even dear ones who do not love Rāma.
**Antidotes:** [Holy company (satsaṅga)](../practices/satsanga.md), total refuge in Giridhar
