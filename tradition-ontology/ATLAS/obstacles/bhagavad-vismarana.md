# Forgetting the Lord

`obs:bhagavad-vismarana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md), [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md)
**Taught in:** [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md), [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md)

Forgetting the Lord is supreme anguish for the devotee (NBS 19); the Āḻvārs fear that at death, with the throat choked, remembrance will fail, and so entrust that hour to the Lord now (Mukundamālā; PAT 4.10.1).
