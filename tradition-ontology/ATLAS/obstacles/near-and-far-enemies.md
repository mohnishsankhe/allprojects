# The near and far enemies of the divine abidings

`obs:near-and-far-enemies` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Each divine abiding is threatened by a near enemy resembling it and a far enemy opposed to it.
**Members:** mettā: greed (near), ill will (far), karuṇā: home-based grief (near), cruelty (far), muditā: home-based joy (near), aversion (far), upekkhā: unknowing equanimity (near), greed and resentment (far)
