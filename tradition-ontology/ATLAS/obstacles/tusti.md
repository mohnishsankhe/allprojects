# Contentment (tuṣṭi): the nine complacencies

`obs:tusti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Taught in:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)

Nine false contentments (SK 50): resting content with knowing prakṛti; with outward means such as the triple staff and water-pot; with the thought that time will bring liberation; with luck; and five from abstaining from objects for seeing their faults — none brings liberation, lacking knowledge of the principles; with error and incapacity they curb accomplishment (SK 51).
