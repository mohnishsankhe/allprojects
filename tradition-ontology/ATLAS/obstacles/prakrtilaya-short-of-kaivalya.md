# Absorption in prakṛti or the bodiless state short of kaivalya

`obs:prakrtilaya-short-of-kaivalya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

The bodiless and those merged in prakṛti experience a state 'as if' of kaivalya but return when their impressions ripen or the mind's unfinished task draws it back (1.19, YBh 1.19); lower dispassion includes giving up the wish for these states (YBh 1.15).
**Antidotes:** [Dispassion (vairāgya)](../practices/vairagya.md), [Cultivating discriminative discernment (viveka-khyāti)](../practices/viveka-khyati.md)
