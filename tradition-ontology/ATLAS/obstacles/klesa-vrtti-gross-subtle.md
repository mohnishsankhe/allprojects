# Gross and subtle activities of the afflictions

`obs:klesa-vrtti-gross-subtle` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

The gross activities of the afflictions are thinned by kriyā-yoga and abandoned by meditation as discerning insight; the subtle ones (seeds) are abandoned by involution (pratiprasava). As gross dirt is shaken from cloth first and fine dirt removed later with effort, the gross activities have small opposition and the subtle ones great (YS 2.10–2.11, YBh 2.11).
