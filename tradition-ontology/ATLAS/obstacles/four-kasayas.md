# The four passions (krodha, māna, māyā, lobha) with their four varieties

`obs:four-kasayas` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** passion
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
**Taught in:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)

Anger, pride, deceit and greed (8.9): counted among the rising states of the soul (2.6), a division of the sāmparāyika inflow (6.5), the ground of conduct-deluding karma (6.14, 8.9), a cause of bondage (8.1) and the reason the soul takes in karma-particles (8.2); each has four varieties, anantānubandhī, apratyākhyāna, pratyākhyāna and saṃjvalana (8.9), which the sūtra names but does not define.
**Antidotes:** forbearance, humility, straightforwardness, contentment (the first four of the ten virtues), [Anuprekṣā (the twelve reflections)](../practices/anupreksa.md), [Sāmāyika (equanimity practice)](../practices/samayika.md)
