# Desiring what one is unfit for

`obs:ayogya-iccha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)

Aspiring to an office, a form of worship or a vision of God beyond one's intrinsic fitness; the one who does so falls, even from what he had.
**Antidotes:** serving Viṣṇu according to one's fitness
