# Slandering and turning from the guru

`obs:guru-apavada` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)
**Taught in:** [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md), [Kubjikā tradition (Paścimāmnāya, the 'Western Transmission')](../lineages/kubjika.md)

The tāmasa disciple slanders the guru, looks for faults and turns away, and is 'dead while living' (Kubjikāmata 12.5-9); when the guru is angry no one can protect (Kulārṇava 12.49).
