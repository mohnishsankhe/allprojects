# The effluents (āsava)

**Category:** impurity
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

The effluents of sensuality, becoming and ignorance — the usual sutta list of three — with views as a fourth in some lists, abandoned by seven methods (MN 2); their destruction is the knowledge of the arahant (DN 2).
**Antidotes:** the seven methods of MN 2, the four jhānas and the knowledge of the four truths, the four paths (destruction of the taints)
**Members:** kāmāsava, bhavāsava, avijjāsava, (diṭṭhāsava), diṭṭhāsava
