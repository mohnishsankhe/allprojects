# Craving (taṇhā)

`obs:tanha` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

In DN 22:19-20 craving is the noble truth of the origin of suffering: threefold (for sensual pleasure, for becoming, for non-becoming), leading to renewed becoming, accompanied by delight and passion; it arises and takes root wherever in the world there is the dear-looking and pleasant-looking, and is abandoned and ceases in the same places; its cessation is the complete fading away, giving up, relinquishment, release and non-reliance.
