# Void, sleep-like absorption mistaken for the goal

`obs:susupta-like-absorption` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md), [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md), [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md)
**Taught in:** [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md), [Pratyabhijñā (the philosophy of recognition)](../lineages/pratyabhijna.md), [Spanda (the doctrine of vibration)](../lineages/spanda.md)

A void state, like deep sleep, is artificial and not the principle (SK 1.12-13); in the great space where the breaths dissolve, the deluded yogin remains as in deep sleep while the awakened remains unveiled (SK 1.25); identification with the void is subordinate to consciousness (ĪPK 3.2.12-15).
