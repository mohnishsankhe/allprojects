# Reliance on other supports or other gods (anyāśraya)

`obs:anyasraya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md), [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md), [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md)
