# The four kinds of unwanted things (anartha)

`obs:anartha-four-kinds` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Category:** impurity
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)
**Taught in:** [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)

Unwanted tendencies arising from past sins, from pious acts (desire for their fruits), from offences, and from devotion itself (desire for gain, honour, prestige); removed in the anartha-nivṛtti stage.
**Members:** duṣkṛtottha, sukṛtottha, aparādhottha, bhakty-uttha
**Sources:** 
  - [Mādhuryakādambinī](../texts/madhurya-kadambini.md) — ref: 3; rests_on: ["tea:madhurya-kadambini:3"]

_Notes: A different fourfold list (svarūpa-bhrama, asat-tṛṣṇā, aparādha, hṛdaya-daurbalya) is given by Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura (recent)._
