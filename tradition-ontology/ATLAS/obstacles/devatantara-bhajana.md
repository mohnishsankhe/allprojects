# Worship of other deities as ends

`obs:devatantara-bhajana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md), [Mahānubhāva panth](../lineages/mahanubhava.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md), [Mahānubhāva panth](../lineages/mahanubhava.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

Resorting to other gods for ends or as the supreme, which the surrendered avoid, since Brahmā, Śiva and the rest are souls in the Lord's body.
