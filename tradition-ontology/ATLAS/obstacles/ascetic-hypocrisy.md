# Renunciation without knowledge or for gain

`obs:ascetic-hypocrisy` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md)

Bearing the wooden staff without knowledge or renouncing for food or fame; such a renouncer 'falls from both worlds' (Paramahaṃsa; Maitreya 2.18–20; Nāradaparivrājaka 5).
