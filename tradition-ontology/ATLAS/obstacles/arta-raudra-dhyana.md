# Sorrowful and cruel meditation

`obs:arta-raudra-dhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
