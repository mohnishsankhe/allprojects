# Beginningless karma as bondage (avidyā-karma)

`obs:karma-bondage` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** karma-type
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

Merit and demerit from beginningless time bind the soul to bodies in prakṛti and contract its knowledge; this karma is what scripture calls avidyā.
