# The ten offences against the name

`obs:nama-aparadha` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md), [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md), [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md), [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md), [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

Blaspheming devotees; regarding Śiva's names and qualities as independent of Viṣṇu's; disregarding the guru; deriding scripture; taking the name's glories as exaggeration; interpreting the name fancifully; sinning on its strength; equating it with other pious acts; teaching it to the faithless; lacking love for it despite hearing its glories (clinging to 'I and mine'); inattention is often added.
**Antidotes:** [Repetition of the divine name on beads (nāma-japa)](../practices/nama-japa.md), continuous chanting with humility (Śikṣāṣṭaka 3)
**Members:** sādhu-nindā, Śiva's names as independent, guru-avajñā, śruti-śāstra-nindā, arthavāda, kalpana, nāmno balāt pāpa-buddhiḥ, equating with other pious acts, instructing the faithless, no love despite hearing its glory
