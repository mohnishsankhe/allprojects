# Powers as obstacles

`obs:siddhis-as-obstacles` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle

**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

The powers arising from yoga are obstacles for one practising the highest yoga, a waste of time for one attaining the Lord; the devotee does not desire them.
**Antidotes:** devotion that desires nothing but the Lord, dispassion, concealment; living as if dull or deaf (DYŚ 93)
