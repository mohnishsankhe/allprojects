# The three groups of faults (rāga, dveṣa, moha)

`obs:three-dosas-nyaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
**Taught in:** [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)

Faults are what impel to activity; they fall into three groups — attachment, aversion, delusion — delusion being the worst; they arise from false knowledge and from falsely constructed objects.
**Antidotes:** knowledge of truth about their causes (NS 4.2.1), [Repeated contemplation of objects and of the body (prasaṅkhyāna)](../practices/prasankhyana-nyaya.md)
**Members:** obs:raga-nyaya, obs:dvesa-nyaya, obs:moha-nyaya
**Sources:**
