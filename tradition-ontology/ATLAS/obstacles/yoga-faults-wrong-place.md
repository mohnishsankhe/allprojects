# Faults from practising yoga at the wrong time or place

`obs:yoga-faults-wrong-place` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

Practising overfed, hungry, tired or agitated, in extreme cold, heat or wind, or in noisy, fiery, dangerous and impure places brings deafness, dullness, loss of memory, dumbness, blindness and fever; remedies are prescribed.
**Antidotes:** the text's remedial dhāraṇās, care of the body as the means of the four aims
