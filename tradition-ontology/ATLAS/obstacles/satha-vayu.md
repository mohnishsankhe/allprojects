# The 'śaṭha' wind of forgetting

`obs:satha-vayu` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md)
**Taught in:** [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md)

In the hagiographies, the wind (śaṭha-vāyu) that touches every newborn and makes it forget its knowledge; Nammāḻvār repelled it at birth, hence his name Śaṭhakopa ('angry at śaṭha').
