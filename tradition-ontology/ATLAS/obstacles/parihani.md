# Falling back (parihāṇi)

`obs:parihani` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Pudgalavāda](../lineages/pudgalavada.md), [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
**Taught in:** [Saṃmitīya](../lineages/sammitiya.md), [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md), [Sautrāntika](../lineages/sautrantika.md)

Loss of an attained state: for the Sarvāstivāda possible for five of the six kinds of arhat (not from stream-entry), in three forms; the Sautrāntika denies falling back from arhatship.
**Antidotes:** perfection of the faculties (to the unshakeable lineage)
