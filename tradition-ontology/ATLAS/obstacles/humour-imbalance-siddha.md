# Imbalance of the three humours

`obs:humour-imbalance-siddha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** dosa-imbalance
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Taught in:** [Siddha medicine (cittā maruttuvam)](../lineages/siddha-medicine.md)

Excess or deficiency of vaḷi, aḻal and aiyam causes disease (Kuṟaḷ 941); the tradition counts 4,448 diseases arising from it. Remedied by regimen, medicine and kaṟpam.
