# The four māras of Chöd

`obs:four-maras-chod` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Chöd (gcod), 'Cutting'](../lineages/chod.md)

The obstructive māra (sense objects that provoke attachment and aversion), the unobstructive māra (arising thoughts), the māra of elation (attachment to attainments and good fortune) and the māra of conceit (self-fixation), the root of the others.
