# Meditation as mere absence of thought

`obs:blank-meditation` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)
**Taught in:** [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)

Resting in a thought-free blankness mistaken for realisation; Sakya Paṇḍita says a fool's Mahāmudrā of this kind leads to animal rebirth, the formless realm or the śrāvaka cessation; the Gelug holds that without analysis it cannot become insight.
**Antidotes:** [Analytical meditation (dpyad sgom)](../practices/analytical-meditation.md), the wisdom born of empowerment and the two stages (Sakya)
