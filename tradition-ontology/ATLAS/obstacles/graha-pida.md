# Planetary affliction (graha-pīḍā)

`obs:graha-pida` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** karma-type
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)

The trouble a badly placed planet brings in its period or transit — the ripening of past karma which the planets, as forms of Janārdana, deliver; happiness and sorrow of all creatures depend on them.
