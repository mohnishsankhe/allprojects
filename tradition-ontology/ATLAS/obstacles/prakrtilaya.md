# Absorption into prakṛti mistaken for liberation

`obs:prakrtilaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Taught in:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)

Dispassion without discrimination leads to absorption into prakṛti or its evolutes, taken for liberation ('darkness': thinking 'I am liberated'); one rises again like a diver; the Kramadīpikā calls it bondage to prakṛti.
**Antidotes:** discrimination (viveka)
