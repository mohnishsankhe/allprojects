# The five evils, five pains and five burnings

The Larger Sūtra's account of the evils of this world and their painful and burning retributions, among which the Buddha teaches: (1) the strong oppress the weak, killing and devouring one another; (2) family members and officials without righteousness deceive, cheat and covet; (3) lust and illicit desire; (4) double tongue, harsh words, lies, frivolous talk, slander of the good, unfilial conduct; (5) idleness, neglect of family and parents, and indulgence in drink and food.
