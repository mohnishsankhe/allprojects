# The three defects of the mind: impurity, distraction, veiling

`obs:mala-viksepa-avarana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** impurity
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)

Impurity (mala) removed by selfless action, distraction (vikṣepa) removed by meditation/worship (upāsanā), and veiling (āvaraṇa) removed by knowledge — the rationale of the ordered sections of the Veda and Gītā.
