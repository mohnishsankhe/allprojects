# Fear (bhaya)

`obs:bhaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** passion
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Fear arises from a second (BAU 1.4.2); when one makes the slightest gap in brahman there is fear (TU 2.7); the knower of brahman's bliss is never afraid (TU 2.4, 2.9); brahman is fearlessness (BAU 4.4.25).
**Antidotes:** knowing the one without a second
