# Obstacles on the method and wisdom sides (Lamdre)

`obs:obstacles-to-meditation-lamdre` · `skeleton` · confidence low · _restricted: summary only_

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)
**Taught in:** [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)

The Vajra Verses list obstacles for the yogin of the method side (guarded against by faith, the protection wheel, mantra and the understanding of phenomena as mind's appearance) and of the wisdom side (outer and inner demon-paths, wrong views, failure to understand the signs, loss of the drop).
**Antidotes:** firm faith, protection wheel, mantra recitation and knots, not separating from the meaning of reality
