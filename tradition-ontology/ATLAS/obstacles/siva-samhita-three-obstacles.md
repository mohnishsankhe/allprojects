# The three kinds of obstacle (Śiva Saṃhitā)

`obs:siva-samhita-three-obstacles` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

Obstacles to liberation in the form of enjoyment (learning, arts, wealth, family, sense-objects), of dharma (meditation-objects, mantra, gifts, fame, public works, sacrifices, vows, pilgrimages) and of knowledge (SS 5.2–8); many terrible obstacles, to be overcome by perseverance and the praṇava (3.47–48).
**Antidotes:** repetition of the long praṇava in solitude (SS 3.48)
**Members:** bhoga-rūpa, dharma-rūpa, jñāna-rūpa
