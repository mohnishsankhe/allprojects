# Other cognitions in the gaps of discernment

`obs:pratyayantara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

In the gaps of a mind flowing with discernment, cognitions such as 'I am', 'mine', 'I know', 'I do not know' arise from dwindling impressions (4.27, YBh 4.27); they are removed as the afflictions are (4.28).
