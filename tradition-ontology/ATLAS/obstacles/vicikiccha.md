# Doubt (vicikicchā)

`obs:vicikiccha` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** hindrance
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Fifth hindrance. In Vism IV sustained thought (vicāra) is its opposite (p. 141). How it obstructs, in the text's words: consciousness harmed by doubt does not climb the way that leads to attaining the jhāna. Vism XIV defines it as 'the absence of cure' (vigatā cikicchā): mark uncertainty, function wavering, manifestation indecision or taking hold of no one side, proximate cause unwise attention; to be regarded as an obstacle to practice.
**Antidotes:** sustained thought as the opposed jhāna factor (Vism IV p. 141), [Wise attention](../practices/yoniso-manasikara.md), [Recollection of the Dhamma](../practices/dhammanussati.md)
