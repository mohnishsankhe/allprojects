# The four tāmasa dispositions

`obs:tamasa-bhavas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)

Non-virtue, non-knowledge, passion and powerlessness, the tāmasa form of buddhi (SK 23), leading respectively to downward movement, bondage, saṃsāra and obstruction (SK 44–45).
**Antidotes:** virtue, knowledge, dispassion, power (the sāttvika dispositions)
**Members:** adharma, ajñāna, avairāgya / rāga, anaiśvarya
