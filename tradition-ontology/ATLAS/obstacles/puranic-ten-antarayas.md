# The ten obstacles of yoga (Liṅga/Śiva Purāṇa)

`obs:puranic-ten-antarayas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

Sloth, illness, heedlessness, doubt about the ground, unsteadiness of mind, faithlessness, erroneous views, threefold suffering, dejection and restless craving for objects; they perish for one of great zeal.
**Antidotes:** great zeal (atyantotsāha, LiP 1.9.13), supreme dispassion
