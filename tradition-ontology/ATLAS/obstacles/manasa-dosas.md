# The mental doṣas rajas and tamas

`obs:manasa-dosas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** guna
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Rajas and tamas corrupt the mind as the three doṣas corrupt the body; they bind the person to rebirth; they are allayed by knowledge, understanding, steadiness, memory and concentration (Ca Sū 1.57-58; Śā 1.36, 4.34).
