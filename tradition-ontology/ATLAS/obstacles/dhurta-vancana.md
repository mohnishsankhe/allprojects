# Being cheated by those who live off the unseen

`obs:dhurta-vancana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Cārvāka / Lokāyata](../lineages/carvaka.md)

(As reported) The Lokāyata's obstacle to a happy life: fear of an unseen world and of the fruit of deeds, instilled by priests for whom sacrifice and funeral rites are a livelihood.
**Antidotes:** trusting perception alone; living happily while life lasts
