# Untimely death (apamṛtyu)

`obs:apamrtyu` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** karma-type
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)

The danger of death before one's time shown when a period-lord rules the 2nd or 7th house; removed by recitation of the Mṛtyuñjaya mantra and pacification (BPHS 52.2–3).
