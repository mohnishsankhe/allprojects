# Seduction to wrong: wine, anger, dice, thoughtlessness (dhruti)

`obs:dhruti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

Vasiṣṭha's list of what leads one astray against one's own will: surā, manyu, dice, acitti (thoughtlessness); the elder shares the younger's fault; even sleep does not ward off wrong.
**Antidotes:** 'do not play with dice — till your field' (RV 10.34.13)
**Members:** surā (intoxicating drink), manyu (anger), vibhīdaka (dice, gambling), acitti (thoughtlessness), svapna (sleep/dream)
