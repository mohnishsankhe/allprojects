# Improper breath-control

`obs:improper-pranayama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** dosa-imbalance
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Wrongly practised breath-control produces hiccup, asthma, cough and pains of head, ears and eyes, and can kill (Yogacūḍāmaṇi 116–118; Śāṇḍilya 1.23).
**Antidotes:** gradual practice under a guru
