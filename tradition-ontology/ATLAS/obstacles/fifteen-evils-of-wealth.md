# The fifteen evils rooted in wealth

`obs:fifteen-evils-of-wealth` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** passion
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

Theft, violence, falsehood, hypocrisy, lust, anger, arrogance, pride, divisiveness, enmity, distrust, rivalry and the vices (women, gambling, drink), all rooted in wealth.
**Members:** steya, hiṃsā, anṛta, dambha, kāma, krodha, smaya, mada, bheda, vaira, aviśvāsa, saṃspardhā, vyasana (×3)
