# Making the secret public (prākaṭya)

`obs:prakatya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Taught in:** [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md), [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)

Exposure of the secret worship leads to hell (PKS 1.12); the Kaula Upaniṣad forbids telling one's secret except to a disciple; Bhāskararāya calls non-exposure the chief of Kaula conducts.
