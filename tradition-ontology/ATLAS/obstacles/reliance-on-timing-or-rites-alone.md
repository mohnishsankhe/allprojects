# Relying on favourable planets or on rites alone

`obs:reliance-on-timing-or-rites-alone` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)

Expecting success from favourable planets, asterisms and times alone, or from mantras, amulets, pacifications and oblations alone, without human effort and the other means: 'the astrologer would be king… the priest would be king' (Yogayātrā 1.5–6).
**Antidotes:** human effort (puruṣakāra) joined with fate, like farming (Yogayātrā 1.3–4)
**Sources:** 
  - [Yogayātrā](../texts/yogayatra.md) — ref: 1.5-6; rests_on: ["tea:yogayatra:1.5-6"]

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [Reliance on fate](daiva-paratva.md) — both warn against leaning on fate instead of effort; Varāhamihira still requires fate and good timing alongside effort, the Yoga Vāsiṣṭha denies fate any power of its own
