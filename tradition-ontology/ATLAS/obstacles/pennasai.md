# Desire for women (peṇṇācai)

`obs:pennasai` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** passion
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Taught in:** [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)

In the renouncer-poets' own terms, desire for women is the snare that binds to birth; the seeker longs to think of woman as mother (Pattirakiriyār 8, 9–11) and Paṭṭiṉattār laments the women who bewitch. Recorded faithfully as the texts' view.
