# Knowing otherwise (wrong cognition)

`obs:anyathajnana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)

Misapprehension — the real sense of 'dvaita' in the Māṇḍūkya for Madhva: the soul's wrong knowledge (of its relation to body and Lord), produced by God's will and ceasing by his will when Brahman is known.
**Antidotes:** knowledge of Brahman; the Lord's will
