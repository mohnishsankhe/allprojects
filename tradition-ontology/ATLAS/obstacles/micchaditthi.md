# Wrong view (micchādiṭṭhi)

`obs:micchaditthi` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Vism XIV: wrong view (micchādiṭṭhi) is that by which one sees wrongly; its mark is unwise insistence; its function is clinging; its manifestation is wrong adherence; its proximate cause is the wish not to see the noble ones and the like; to be regarded as the most blameworthy. Vism XXII lists it among the wrongnesses (micchatta) and as the sense of 'adherence' (parāmāsa).
