# Pride of birth; judging devotees by caste (jāti-abhimāna)

Measuring devotees by caste, learning or family: the Āḻvārs teach that the servants of the Lord's servants, even caṇḍālas, are one's masters, and that Veda-learned brāhmaṇas who revile devotees become outcastes; the Nārada Sūtra that no such distinction exists among devotees because they are the Lord's own; the Bhāgavata that a devoted dog-cooker is better than a brāhmaṇa averse to the Lord.
**Antidotes:** [Service to devotees (bhāgavata-kainkarya)](../practices/bhagavata-kainkarya.md), remembering that outward birth does not touch devotion (Cokhāmeḷā's sugarcane)
