# Attachment, pride and heedlessness when invited (YS 3.51)

`obs:sanga-smaya-pramada` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

Attachment (saṅga) to the offered stations and pride (smaya) at being sought even by gods; the bhāṣya says pride leads him to think himself well established and not to regard himself as seized by death by the hair, and that heedlessness (pramāda), watching for a gap, would stir up the afflictions again.
