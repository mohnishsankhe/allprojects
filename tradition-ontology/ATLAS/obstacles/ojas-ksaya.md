# Diminution and disorders of ojas

`obs:ojas-ksaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Ojas is diminished by injury, wasting, anger, grief, worry, fatigue and hunger; its disorders are displacement, vitiation and loss; in loss one is fearful, weak, anxious, dull, dejected and emaciated, and complete loss is death.
**Antidotes:** life-giving herbs, milk, meat-juices (AHS Sū 11.41), avoiding the causes of mental suffering; calm and knowledge (Ca Sū 30.13-14)
