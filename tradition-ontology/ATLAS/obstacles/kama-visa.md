# Desire and poison (kāma-viṣa)

`obs:kama-visa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** passion
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Taught in:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)

From the seed comes desire, which is creation, and from desire poison, which is death (AmŚ 43–44); desire is longing for objects (Candrāvalokana).
