# Desire for lower ends (wealth, power, kaivalya)

`obs:kaivalya-desire` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

Seeking from the Lord anything other than himself — wealth, lordly powers or the isolated enjoyment of one's own self — obstructs the highest goal.
