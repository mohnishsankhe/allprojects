# Intrusion of objects and bodily urges in samādhi

`obs:samadhi-intrusions` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
**Taught in:** [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)

Objects intrude by their force, and hunger and the like drive one away, so that samādhi seems impossible; overcome through the continuing effect of previous practice.
