# Mortification of the body (kāyakleśa)

`obs:kayaklesa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

Ascetic practices that torment the body — early-morning (cold) bathing, fasting, eating once a day or not at all — which the haṭha texts tell the beginner to avoid (Gorakṣa's saying in HYP 1.61; GS 5.31; ŚS 3.33 lists early bathing and fasting among what obstructs yoga). Brahmānanda glosses the 'clinging to rules' (niyamāgraha) of HYP 1.15 with the same examples.
**Antidotes:** [Moderate diet (mitāhāra) in the haṭha texts](../practices/mitahara.md), moderate, regular practice as the guru teaches
