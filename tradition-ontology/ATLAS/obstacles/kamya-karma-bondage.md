# Bondage through desire-prompted acts

`obs:kamya-karma-bondage` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** bond
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
**Taught in:** [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md), [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md)

Desire-prompted (kāmya) acts produce fruits that require further bodies; the seeker of release therefore abstains from them.
