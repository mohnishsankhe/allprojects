# Failure of discernment (prajñāparādha)

`obs:prajnaparadha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

The unwholesome act done when intellect, resolve and memory lapse: the provoker of all doṣas, source of mental and exogenous disease, insanity and epidemics (Ca Śā 1.98-109; Sū 7.51-52; Ni 7.10; Vi 3.20).
**Antidotes:** abandoning prajñāparādha, calming the senses, memory, knowledge of place, time and self, good conduct (Ca Sū 7.53), [Good conduct (sadvṛtta)](../practices/sadvrtta.md), [Conquest of the mind (sattvāvajaya)](../practices/sattvavajaya.md)
