# The passion-producing notion of the whole

`obs:avayavy-abhimana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
**Taught in:** [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
