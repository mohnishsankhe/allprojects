# Ignorance veiling knowledge (ajñāna)

`obs:ajnana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** affliction
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Taught in:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Knowledge is veiled by ignorance, by which beings are deluded (BhG 5.15); the ignorant, faithless doubter perishes (4.40); tamas is born of ignorance (14.8).
**Antidotes:** knowledge of the self (5.16), knowledge (jñāna) (5.16, 4.42), [The sacrifice of knowledge (jñāna-yajña)](../practices/jnana-yajna.md), the Lord's lamp of knowledge, given to those ever yoked who worship him with love (10.10–11), the dispositions called knowledge (13.8–12)
