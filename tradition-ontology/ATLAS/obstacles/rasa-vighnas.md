# The seven obstacles to aesthetic relish (rasa-vighna)

`obs:rasa-vighnas` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** obstacle
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md)
**Taught in:** [Alaṅkāraśāstra and Nāṭyaśāstra (poetics, dramaturgy and the theory of rasa)](../lineages/alankara.md)

Lack of credibility; being caught in the particular time and place of oneself or another; being overwhelmed by one's own pleasure or pain; lack of the means of perception; lack of clarity; non-predominance of the stable emotion; and doubt (Abhinavagupta).
**Antidotes:** generalization (sādhāraṇīkaraṇa) by the art-work, the preliminaries, music and conventions of the stage, [The preliminaries of performance (pūrvaraṅga)](../practices/purvaranga.md)
**Members:** sambhāvanā-viraha, svagata-paragata-deśakāla-āveśa, nija-sukhādi-vivaśībhāva, pratīty-upāya-vaikalya, sphuṭatva-abhāva, apradhānatā, saṃśaya-yoga
