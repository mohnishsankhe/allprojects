# Stain, impurity, fault (kalmaṣa, kilbiṣa, pāpa)

**Category:** impurity
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Bhagavad Gītā 4–6: impurities are destroyed by sacrifice (4.30); the worst sinner crosses all wrong by the raft of knowledge (4.36); stains are shaken off by knowledge (5.17) and destroyed in the seers who attain brahma-nirvāṇa (5.25); the one acting with actions placed in Brahman is not stained by sin (5.10); one doing merely bodily action incurs no fault (4.21); the calm yogin is stainless (6.27–28); the striving yogin is cleansed of faults over many births (6.45).
