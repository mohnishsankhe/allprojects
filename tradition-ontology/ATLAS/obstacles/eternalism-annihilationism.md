# The two extremes: eternalism and annihilationism

`obs:eternalism-annihilationism` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** other
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)

'It is' is the grasp of permanence and 'it is not' the view of annihilation; whoever accepts real existents falls into one or the other.
**Antidotes:** the middle way of dependent origination
