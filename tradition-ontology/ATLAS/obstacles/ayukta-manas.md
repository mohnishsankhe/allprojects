# The unyoked mind (ayukta manas)

`obs:ayukta-manas` · `sourced` · confidence high

In the chariot simile: one without discriminating understanding, whose mind is always unyoked, has uncontrolled senses like bad horses, is impure, does not reach that place and goes to saṃsāra (Kaṭha 1.3.5, 1.3.7).
