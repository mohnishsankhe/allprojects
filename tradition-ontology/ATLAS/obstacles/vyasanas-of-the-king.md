# The eighteen vices of a king

**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)

Ten born of desire (hunting, gambling, day-sleep, censoriousness, women, drink, singing, music, dancing, idle roaming) and eight born of anger (slander, violence, treachery, envy, calumny, seizure of property, abuse, assault) (MDh 7.45–48).
