# The restless mind

`obs:cancala-citta` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)
**Taught in:** [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md)

The mind agitated by objects wanders in saṃsāra; restlessness is 'being', steadiness the mark of liberation (Jñānakārikā 1.9–10; 2.12–14).
