# The four deviations from emptiness (shor sa bzhi)

`obs:four-deviations-mahamudra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation-fault
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md)
**Taught in:** [Drukpa Kagyu](../lineages/drukpa-kagyu.md), [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md), [Karma Kagyu (Kamtsang Kagyu)](../lineages/karma-kagyu.md)

Deviating from the essence of emptiness (not recognizing it), into 'sealing' (labelling things empty), into the antidote (using emptiness as a remedy), and into the path (treating emptiness as something to reach later).
**Antidotes:** [Receiving the pointing-out instruction](../practices/pointing-out.md), recognition of mind's nature
