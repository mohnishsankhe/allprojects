# The alms-round (gocarī)

`prc:gocari` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)

The mendicant goes from house to house taking a little food 'like a bee from flowers', accepting only what was not prepared for him and is free of the faults of alms; Śvetāmbara mendicants collect in bowls and eat in their lodging; the Digambara monk eats once a day, standing, from his cupped hands, at a house where he is ceremonially received.
**Stage:** advanced
