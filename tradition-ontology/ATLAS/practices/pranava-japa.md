Repeating Oṃ, the designator of the Lord (YS 1.27), while contemplating its meaning (1.28); meditating on its measures a, u, m as waking, dream and deep sleep and on the measureless fourth as the self (Māṇḍūkya 8–12); fixing the mind on Oṃ as the fearless Brahman (MK 1.25); taking Oṃ as the bow, the self as the arrow and Brahman as the target (Muṇḍaka 2.2.3–4); prolonging its sound into the subtle phases beyond the three letters until it dissolves in the soundless; uttering it at death while remembering the Lord (BhG 8.13).
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** ['realization of the inward consciousness and removal of obstacles (YS 1.29)', 'fearlessness (MK 1.25)']
