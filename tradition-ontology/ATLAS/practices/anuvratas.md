# The small vows and lay vows

`prc:anuvratas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
**Taught in:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)

The householder keeps the five vows partially — no intentional violence to mobile beings, no gross falsehood or theft, fidelity to one's spouse, limits on possessions — with three supplementary and four disciplinary vows.
