# Remembering Hari as the doer; action as worship

`prc:hari-kartrtva-anusandhana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)

Doing the prescribed duties and avoiding the forbidden, without desire for results, while remembering that Hari within moves one's limbs and that all acts of mind, speech and body are his worship — the Yogadīpikā's definition of yoga.
