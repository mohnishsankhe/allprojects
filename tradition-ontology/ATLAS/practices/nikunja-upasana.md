# Worship in the grove (nikuñja-upāsanā)

`prc:nikunja-upasana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** visualization-deity
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md), [Rādhāvallabha sampradāya](../lineages/radhavallabha.md)
**Taught in:** [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md), [Rādhāvallabha sampradāya](../lineages/radhavallabha.md)

The devotee, as a sakhī, contemplates and inwardly serves the eternal pair at play in the grove through the founders' songs, temple service and meditation on the daily round of their play; no personal union is sought.
**Stage:** advanced
