# Wrangling and cavilling to protect truth

`prc:jalpa-vitanda-protective` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
**Taught in:** [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)

Using contentious debate — including quibbles and futile rejoinders, or pure refutation without a counter-thesis — only to protect an ascertained truth from opponents, as a fence of thorny branches protects seedlings.
**Sources:** 
  - [Nyāya Sūtra](../texts/nyaya-sutra.md) — ref: 4.2.50–51; rests_on: ["tea:nyaya-sutra:4.2.50", "tea:nyaya-sutra:4.2.51"]

## The texts' own warnings
- Quibbles, futile rejoinders and points of defeat are defined so that they can be avoided in one's own speech and exposed in the opponent's (NBh 1.1.1); vitaṇḍā without any purpose collapses into prattle. — [Nyāyabhāṣya](../texts/nyaya-bhasya.md) 1.1.1
