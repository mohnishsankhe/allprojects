# Inner worship: japa, meditation, pūjā, homa as acts of consciousness

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md), [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md)
**Taught in:** [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md), [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md)

Outer ritual is for the gross; japa is the repeated contemplation of the supreme state, meditation the formless steady understanding, worship the firm mind in the great void, oblation the offering of elements, senses and objects with the mind into the fire of consciousness, bathing the entry into one's own nature (VBT 144-153; Paramārthasāra 74-80).
