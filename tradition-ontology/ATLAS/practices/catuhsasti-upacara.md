# The sixty-four services (catuḥṣaṣṭy-upacāra)

`prc:catuhsasti-upacara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Taught in:** [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md), [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)

The Goddess is served in imagination and act as a queen with sixty-four royal services — bath, anointing, garments, jewels, weapons, mounting the cakra, sitting on Kāmeśvara's lap, drink, betel, lights, parasol, fans, mirror and the rest (PKS 4.5; LSN 58); the Bhāvanā Upaniṣad interiorizes the services.
**Stage:** intermediate
