# The four attitudes (maitrī, pramoda, kāruṇya, mādhyasthya)

`prc:maitri-adi-bhavana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
**Taught in:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)

Cultivating friendliness to all beings ('may no one do evil, may no one suffer, may the world be freed'), joy at the virtues of others, compassion for the afflicted, and impartiality toward the incorrigible; recited in the Sāmāyikapāṭha and used to support meditation.
