# Bhujaṅgāsana (the serpent)

`prc:bhujangasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** posture
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

From the toes to the navel the body rests on the ground; supporting the ground with the palms one raises the head like a serpent (GS 2.42-43).
**Stage:** intermediate
**Signs of progress:** ['the bodily fire increases, all diseases are destroyed and the serpent goddess (kuṇḍalinī) awakens (GS 2.43)']
