# Dissolving the syllables of Oṃ into the Self (Rāma Gītā)

`prc:pranava-laya-rama-gita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md)

Before samādhi, contemplating the world as Oṃ; dissolving A (the waking viśva) into U, U (the dreaming taijasa) into M, and M (the sleeping prājña) into the Self, dense consciousness: 'I am the supreme Brahman'.
