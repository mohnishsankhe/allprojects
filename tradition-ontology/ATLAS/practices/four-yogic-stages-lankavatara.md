# The four yogic stages of the Laṅkāvatāra

`prc:four-yogic-stages-lankavatara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
**Taught in:** [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md), [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md)

Mount to mind-only and stop imagining external objects; rest in suchness and pass beyond mind-only; pass beyond the imageless (even non-dual awareness as an image); abiding in the imageless, see the Mahāyāna.
