# Meditation on combinations (saṃhitā)

`prc:samhita-upasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Meditate on the five great combinations — of worlds, lights, knowledge, progeny and the body — each as prior form, latter form, junction and link (TU 1.3).
