# Repetition of the Gāyatrī (Sāvitrī)

At the morning, noon and evening twilights (sandhyā), after sipping water and breath-restraint, the practitioner recites Oṃ, the three utterances (bhūr bhuvaḥ svaḥ) and the Sāvitrī verse to Savitṛ — 'we meditate on that excellent radiance of the god Savitṛ; may he impel our thoughts' (RV 3.62.10) — a fixed number of times, meditating on its seer (Viśvāmitra), metre (gāyatrī) and deity (Savitṛ) and on the Goddess Gāyatrī; it is imparted at the initiation (upanayana). The Upaniṣads meditate on its three feet as the worlds, the Vedas and the breaths and on a fourth, 'visible' foot beyond them.
