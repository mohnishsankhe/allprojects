# Bhrāmarī ('the bee')

`prc:bhramari` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

In the Haṭhapradīpikā, rapid inhalation with the sound of a male bee and very slow exhalation with the hum of a female bee (HYP 2.68). In the Gheraṇḍa, practised after midnight in silence with the ears closed, it becomes a practice of listening: the inner sounds are heard in the right ear, from crickets and flute to drums and vīṇā, and the mind dissolves in the light within the unstruck sound (GS 5.79-83); it is the Gheraṇḍa's means to nāda-samādhi (GS 7.5-6, 7.10-11).
**Stage:** intermediate
**Signs of progress:** ['a play of indescribable bliss in the minds of the best yogins (HYP 2.68)', "the inner sounds, a light within the sound, dissolution of the mind — 'Viṣṇu's supreme state' (GS 5.80-83)"]

## The texts' own warnings
- As a lion, an elephant or a tiger is tamed only slowly, so the breath must be brought under control gradually; otherwise it kills the practitioner. — [Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā (Haṭhapradīpikā)](../texts/hatha-yoga-pradipika.md) 2.15
- Proper breath-control destroys all disease; improper practice produces all disease — hiccup, asthma, cough and pains in the head, ears and eyes arise from the disturbed breath. — [Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā (Haṭhapradīpikā)](../texts/hatha-yoga-pradipika.md) 2.16-17
- Draw the breath in slowly and release it slowly; do not hold it excessively nor release it quickly. — [Gorakṣaśataka](../texts/goraksasataka.md) 51
