# Removing resentment in the development of loving-kindness (Vism IX)

`prc:patigha-vinodana-vism` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Vism IX gives an ordered set of remedies for resentment (āghāta, paṭigha) that arises on turning loving-kindness to an enemy, each to be used if the one before does not calm the mind: return repeatedly to loving-kindness for the earlier persons; recall the Buddha's advice (the saw simile, the verse on not returning anger, the seven things that please an enemy); recall a calm quality in the person (bodily, verbal or mental conduct), or set up compassion if none is calm; admonish oneself in verse; reflect on ownership of kamma (his and one's own); recall the Teacher's earlier patience in the Jātakas; reflect that in the beginningless round the person has been mother, father, relative; reflect on the eleven benefits of loving-kindness; analyse the person into elements, aggregates and bases; and share gifts. The text refers to the Āghātapaṭivinaya-sutta for five other ways.
