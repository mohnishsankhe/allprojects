# Listening to the inner sound

`prc:nadanusandhana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): `lin:hatha-yoga`, [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** `lin:hatha-yoga`, [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

The Upaniṣads mention hearing an inner sound with the ears closed: the roar or crackling of fire heard when one closes the ears is the light within (ChU 3.13.8); the sound of the digestive fire, no longer heard when one is about to depart (BAU 5.9.1); closing the ears with the thumbs one hears the sound of the space within the heart in seven forms and passes beyond it into the soundless brahman (MaiU 6.22).
**Signs of progress:** the seven sounds of MaiU 6.22: like rivers, a small bell, a bronze vessel, a wheel, frogs, rain, a voice in a sheltered place
