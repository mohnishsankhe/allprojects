# Sallekhanā / saṃthārā (the final fast)

`prc:sallekhana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 4 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Yāpanīya](../lineages/yapaniya.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Yāpanīya](../lineages/yapaniya.md)

At the approach of death that cannot be averted (calamity, famine, old age, incurable illness), the practitioner, with the teacher's permission and under the care of attending ascetics, gradually gives up food and passions, forgives and asks forgiveness, and dies in equanimity reciting the homage to the five supreme beings.
**Stage:** advanced; the culmination of the lay vows and of ascetic life

## The texts' own warnings
- Only when death cannot be averted: calamity, famine, old age or incurable disease. — [Ratnakaraṇḍaśrāvakācāra](../texts/ratnakarandasravakacara.md) 122
- Vitiated by wishing to live, wishing to die, affection for friends, recalling pleasures and longing for future reward. — [Tattvārthasūtra](../texts/tattvartha-sutra.md) 7.37
- One who ends life by water, fire, poison or weapon under the sway of passion commits self-killing; only passionless thinning of the passions is sallekhanā. — [Puruṣārthasiddhyupāya](../texts/purusarthasiddhyupaya.md) 177-178
- To be undertaken with the teacher's permission, gradually, under attending ascetics, and abandoned faults of fear and attachment. — [Bhagavatī Ārādhanā](../texts/bhagavati-aradhana.md) conditions
- Only when the body can no longer sustain restraint (old age, incurable illness, famine, calamity); never out of passion, despair or wish for death. — [Ācārāṅga Sūtra](../texts/acaranga-sutra.md) 1.8
- One must not long for life or for death, nor wish for a reward (nidāna) in a future life. — [Tattvārthasūtra](../texts/tattvartha-sutra.md) 7.37
- It requires the permission and supervision of the teacher. — [Bhagavatī Ārādhanā](../texts/bhagavati-aradhana.md)
