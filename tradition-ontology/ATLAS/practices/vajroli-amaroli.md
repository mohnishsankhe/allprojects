# Vajrolī, amarolī and sahajolī

`prc:vajroli-amaroli` · `sourced` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

**Category:** lock-seal
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

RESTRICTED — summary only: practices for the retention and 'drawing back' of bindu (semen, and in women rajas) through upward contraction, which the texts say let even one living as he pleases, outside the yogic rules, succeed; amarolī is ascribed to the Kāpālikas of the Khaṇḍa school and sahajolī involves smearing with ash (HYP 3.83-103; DYŚ 137-145). The Dattātreyayogaśāstra places them in the haṭha of Kapila and his followers (DYŚ 29). No procedure, material or quantity is reproduced. The Gheraṇḍa's 'vajrolī' is a different, posture-like practice (see prc:vajroli-gheranda).
**Stage:** advanced

## The texts' own warnings
- Most secret; not to be given to anyone, only to one as dear as one's own life. — [Dattātreyayogaśāstra](../texts/dattatreyayogasastra.md) 137
