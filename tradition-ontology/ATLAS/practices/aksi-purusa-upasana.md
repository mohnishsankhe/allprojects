# Meditation on the person in the eye

`prc:aksi-purusa-upasana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Venerate the person seen in the eye as the self, the immortal, the fearless, brahman.
**Sources:** 
  - [Chāndogya Upaniṣad](../texts/chandogya-upanisad.md) — ref: 4.15.1; rests_on: ["tea:chandogya-upanisad:4.15.1-5"]
  - [Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad](../texts/brhadaranyaka-upanisad.md) — ref: 2.3.5; 5.5.2; rests_on: ["tea:brhadaranyaka-upanisad:2.3.1-5"]

## The texts' own warnings
- Virocana misunderstood 'the person seen in the eye' as the body and its reflection (ChU 8.7-8). — [Chāndogya Upaniṣad](../texts/chandogya-upanisad.md) 8.7.4-8.8.5
