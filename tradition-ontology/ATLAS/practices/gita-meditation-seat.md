# The meditation seat and posture of the Gītā

`prc:gita-meditation-seat` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** posture
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Taught in:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

A firm seat of one's own in a clean place, neither too high nor too low, spread with kuśa grass, a deerskin and cloth; sitting there, hold body, head and neck erect, motionless and steady.
**Stage:** meditator (6.10)
**Sources:**
