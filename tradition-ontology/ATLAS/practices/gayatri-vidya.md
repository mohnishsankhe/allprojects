# Meditation on the Gāyatrī

`prc:gayatri-vidya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Venerate the Gāyatrī as all this — speech, earth, body, heart — whose four feet are the worlds, the Vedas, the breaths and the sun; and the space within the heart as identical with the space outside (ChU 3.12; BAU 5.14).
