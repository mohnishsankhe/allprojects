# Repetition of Rāma's name and mantra

`prc:rama-nama-japa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md), [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md)
**Taught in:** [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md), [Rāmdāsī sampradāya (Samartha sampradāya)](../lineages/ramdasi.md)

Repeating the Rāma mantra received at initiation (Rāmānandī: the six-syllable mantra) or the name 'Rāma' aloud, softly or mentally, often on tulsī beads; the Samartha sampradāya uses the thirteen-syllable 'śrī rām jay rām jay jay rām'; tradition credits Samartha Rāmdās with thirteen crore repetitions at Ṭākaḷī.
