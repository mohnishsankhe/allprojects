# Breath as sacrifice: offering prāṇa and apāna

`prc:prana-apana-offering` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Taught in:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

As a form of sacrifice, some offer the in-breath into the out-breath and the out-breath into the in-breath, restraining their movement (4.29); in meditation the two breaths moving in the nostrils are made equal (5.27). The Gītā gives no counts or durations.
