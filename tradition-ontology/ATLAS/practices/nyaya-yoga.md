# Yoga and the practice of samādhi (Nyāya)

`prc:nyaya-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
**Taught in:** [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)

Repeated practice of a particular samādhi as the means to knowledge of truth, in solitary places such as forests, caves and sandbanks, with the method of the inner self learned from the Yoga śāstra — austerity, breath-control, sense-withdrawal, meditation and fixation — and repeated contemplation of sense-objects to abandon attachment and aversion.
**Stage:** after purification by yama and niyama
**Sources:** 
  - [Nyāya Sūtra](../texts/nyaya-sutra.md) — ref: 4.2.38–46; rests_on: ["tea:nyaya-sutra:4.2.38", "tea:nyaya-sutra:4.2.42", "tea:nyaya-sutra:4.2.46", "tea:nyaya-bhasya:4.2.46"]
**Sequences:** [The Nyāya path to liberation (apavarga)](../paths/nyaya-path-to-apavarga.md)

## The texts' own warnings
- Objects intrude by their force and hunger and the like drive one away from samādhi; its arising depends on the continuing effect of previous practice. — [Nyāya Sūtra](../texts/nyaya-sutra.md) 4.2.39–41
