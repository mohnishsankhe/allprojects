# Worship of the nine planets (navagraha-pūjā)

`prc:navagraha-puja` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)

Daily or occasional worship of the nine planets as deities, each with its image or emblem, colour, mount and weapons as described by Parāśara (Sun on a lotus in a seven-horse chariot, Saturn on a vulture, and so on), with flowers, lamps, food and circumambulation; South Indian temples keep a navagraha shrine for this.
