# Vedic study (vedādhyayana / svādhyāya)

`prc:vedadhyayana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
**Taught in:** [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md), [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md), [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md)

Learning one's own Vedic recension by recitation from a teacher, as enjoined by 'one's own Veda is to be studied'; in the Mīmāṃsā account it precedes, and makes possible, the inquiry into the meaning of Vedic sentences.
**Stage:** beginner (student in the teacher's house)
**Prerequisites:** initiation into Vedic study (upanayana), per Dharmaśāstra
