# Moderation in food, sleep, recreation and effort

For one measured in food and recreation, measured in effort in actions, measured in sleep and waking, yoga becomes the destroyer of sorrow (6.17); yoga is not for one who eats too much, nor for one who does not eat at all, nor for one given to too much sleep, nor for one who keeps awake (6.16).
