# Gajakaraṇī ('the elephant's act')

`prc:gajakarani` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** cleansing
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

Raising the apāna breath to the throat, one vomits what is in the stomach; by gradual practice the wheel of channels comes under control (HYP 2.38). The Haṭharatnāvalī counts it among its eight acts.
