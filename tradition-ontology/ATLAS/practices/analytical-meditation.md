# Analytical meditation (dpyad sgom)

`prc:analytical-meditation` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md)
**Taught in:** [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md)

Meditation that repeatedly investigates the object with discernment and reasoning — e.g. the reasons for the certainty of death, the kindness of beings, the one-or-many analysis of the self — to produce and strengthen conviction; alternated with placement meditation on the conclusion reached.
**Sources:** 
  - [Great Treatise on the Stages of the Path to Enlightenment (lam rim chen mo)](../texts/lamrim-chenmo.md) — ref: intro.analytical-and-placement; rests_on: ["tea:lamrim-chenmo:intro.analytical-and-placement", "tea:lamrim-chenmo:insight.union"]

## The texts' own warnings
- If analysis is overdone before calm is stable, stability declines; if placement alone is done on topics needing conviction, no transformation occurs — both are needed in balance. — [Great Treatise on the Stages of the Path to Enlightenment (lam rim chen mo)](../texts/lamrim-chenmo.md) insight.union
