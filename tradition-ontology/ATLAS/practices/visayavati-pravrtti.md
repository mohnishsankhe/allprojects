# Concentration giving rise to subtle sense-perception (1.35)

`prc:visayavati-pravrtti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

Concentrating on the tip of the nose, tip, middle and root of the tongue, or the palate, until divine smell, taste, touch, sound or form is perceived; such experiences bind the mind to steadiness and confirm what scripture and the teacher say (YBh 1.35).
**Stage:** intermediate
**Signs of progress:** awareness of divine smell, taste, form, touch or sound (YBh 1.35)
