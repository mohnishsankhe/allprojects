# Concentration giving rise to subtle sense-perception (YS 1.35)

Holding the mind at the tip of the nose, tip of tongue, palate, middle of tongue or root of tongue gives awareness of divine smell, taste, form, touch or sound; such activities bind the mind to steadiness and dispel doubt; purpose: to confirm scripture, inference and teacher by some one directly known thing (YBh 1.35).
