# Reflecting on the scriptures of devotion

**Category:** devotion-service

The scriptures of devotion are to be reflected upon and the acts that awaken devotion done (NBS 76); no half-moment wasted waiting for a better time (NBS 77); but disputation is avoided (NBS 74–75).
