# Meditation on the udgītha

`prc:udgitha-upasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Venerate the syllable Om as the udgītha, the highest essence, and the udgītha as the breath in the mouth untouched by evil; what is done with knowledge, faith and the upaniṣad is more powerful (ChU 1.1-1.12; BAU 1.3).
