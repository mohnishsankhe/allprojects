# Worship of one's image in the sky (pratīkopāsana)

`prc:pratikopasana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

Gazing with wide-open eyes in strong sunlight at one's own reflected image (shadow) and then seeing one's image in the sky; practised daily, and at journeys, weddings, auspicious acts and in danger (ŚS 5.15-20).
**Stage:** intermediate
**Signs of progress:** ['longer life and freedom from death; when the whole image is seen, mastery of the breath and free movement; seeing it within, liberation (ŚS 5.17-21)']
