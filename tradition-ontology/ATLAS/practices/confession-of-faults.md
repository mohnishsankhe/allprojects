# Confession of faults

`prc:confession-of-faults` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)

Recalling the evil done in beginningless saṃsāra and this life, natural and prescribed faults alike, one confesses them before the Buddhas with joined palms, in fear of their result, asks that they be accepted as faults, and resolves not to do them again.
