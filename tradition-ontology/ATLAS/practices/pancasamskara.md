# The five sacraments (pañcasaṃskāra, samāśrayaṇa)

`prc:pancasamskara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md), [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md), [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

Śrīvaiṣṇava initiation by the teacher: the marks of discus and conch on the shoulders (tāpa), the upright forehead mark (puṇḍra), a servant-name (nāma), the three secret mantras (mantra) and instruction in worship (yāga). Summary only.
