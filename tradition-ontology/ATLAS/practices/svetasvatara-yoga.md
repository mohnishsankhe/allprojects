# The yoga of the Śvetāśvatara (posture, breath, place)

`prc:svetasvatara-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Hold the body steady with chest, neck and head erect; draw the senses with the mind into the heart; restrain the breaths, with movements controlled, and breathe out gently through the nose when the breath is diminished; hold the mind attentively like a chariot yoked to unruly horses; practise in a level, clean place free of pebbles, fire, sand, noise and damp, pleasing to the mind and sheltered from wind.
**Prerequisites:** the invocations of Savitṛ to yoke the mind (ŚU 2.1-7)
**Signs of progress:** forms like fog, smoke, sun, wind, fire, fireflies, lightning, crystal and moon precede brahman's manifestation; lightness, health, freedom from craving, clear complexion, pleasant voice and smell, scant excreta (ŚU 2.11-13)

## The texts' own warnings
- The place must be free of disturbances (pebbles, fire, sand, noise, water-places) and not offend the eye (ŚU 2.10). — [Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad](../texts/svetasvatara-upanisad.md) 2.10
- The breath is to be let out gently through the nose when diminished (ŚU 2.9). — [Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad](../texts/svetasvatara-upanisad.md) 2.9
