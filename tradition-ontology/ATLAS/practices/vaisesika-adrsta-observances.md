# Observances for the unseen (Vaiśeṣika)

`prc:vaisesika-adrsta-observances` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)
**Taught in:** [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)

Ablution, fasting, celibacy, residence in the teacher's household, forest-dwelling, sacrifice, giving, sprinkling, and observances regarding directions, lunar mansions, mantras and times — undertaken for the unseen result (adṛṣṭa).
**Sources:** 
  - [Vaiśeṣika Sūtra](../texts/vaisesika-sutra.md) — ref: 6.2.2 (Candrānanda); rests_on: ["tea:vaisesika-sutra:6.2.1-2"]
**Sequences:** [The Vaiśeṣika path to liberation (mokṣa)](../paths/vaisesika-path-to-moksa.md)

## The texts' own warnings
- Fasting is listed without further prescription; no durations are given in the sūtra (prolonged fasting is not described here). — [Vaiśeṣika Sūtra](../texts/vaisesika-sutra.md) 6.2.2
