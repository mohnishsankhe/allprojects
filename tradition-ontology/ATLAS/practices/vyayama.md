# Exercise (vyāyāma)

`prc:vyayama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Bodily exertion for steadiness and strength, done in measure — to half one's strength by the strong in the cold season and spring, less at other times — followed by gentle massage.
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** ['lightness, capacity for work, kindled fire, reduced fat, firm well-divided limbs']

## The texts' own warnings
- Excess causes fatigue, wasting, thirst, bleeding disorders, cough, fever and vomiting; one who overdoes exercise, talk, travel, sex or waking perishes like a lion dragging an elephant. — [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) Sū 7.33-35
- To be avoided by the vāta- and pitta-afflicted, children, the old and those with indigestion. — [Aṣṭāṅga Hṛdaya](../texts/astanga-hrdaya.md) Sū 2.11
