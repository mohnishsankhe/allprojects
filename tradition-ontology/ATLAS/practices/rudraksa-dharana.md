# Wearing rudrākṣa

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md), [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)
