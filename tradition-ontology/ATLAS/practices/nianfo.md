# Calling the name of Amitābha (nianfo / nembutsu)

Thinking of Amitābha and calling 'namo Amitābha Buddha' — ten times even at death (Contemplation Sūtra), holding the name one to seven days with undistracted mind (Smaller Sūtra), with faith in the eighteenth vow (Larger Sūtra).
**Stage:** all
**Prerequisites:** ['the three minds (Shandao); faith and vow (Ouyi); for Shinshū shinjin, of which the nenbutsu is the expression']
**Duration:** whenever walking, standing, sitting or lying, 'until the end of life' (Shandao); counted daily quotas in China and Japan (tens of thousands) or uncounted
**Signs of progress:** ['seeing the Buddha in samādhi (Huaigan: loud recitation brings samādhi)', "the Buddha's welcome at death", 'for Shinshū: joy and gratitude, though its absence is not a sign of failure (Tannishō 9)']
