# Daily home worship of the Lord (tiruvārādhanam)

`prc:tiruvaradhana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

Worship of the Lord's image (arcā) at home each day, with purification, meditation on the teachers, offerings of water, flowers, food, recitation and prostration, done as service and not for any fruit.
