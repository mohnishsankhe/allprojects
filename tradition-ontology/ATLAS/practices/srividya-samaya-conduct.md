# Keeping the initiate's conduct (samaya)

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)

Observing the rules of the Kalpasūtra: no censure of other schools or persons, secrecy, constant remembrance of the vidyā, avoidance of the six passions, unsanctioned violence and theft, one guru, non-possessiveness, action without regard to fruit, fearlessness; outwardly Śaiva and among people Vaiṣṇava (Kaula Upaniṣad).
