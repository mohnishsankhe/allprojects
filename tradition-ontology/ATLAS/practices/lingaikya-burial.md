# Burial of the dead as liṅgaikya

`prc:lingaikya-burial` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)
**Taught in:** [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md), [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)

The dead devotee, having become one with the liṅga, is buried seated with the liṅga in hand rather than cremated; no death-pollution or ancestral rites are observed.
