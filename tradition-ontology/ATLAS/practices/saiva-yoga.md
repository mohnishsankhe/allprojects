# Yoga (Śivayoga)

`prc:saiva-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
**Taught in:** [The Nāyaṉmārs and the Tirumuṟai (Tamil Śaiva bhakti)](../lineages/nayanmar.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md)

Inner worship and meditation on Śiva: the Āgamas' six-limbed yoga (breath control, withdrawal, holding, meditation, reasoning [tarka], absorption) or the Tirumantiram's eight limbs, fixing the mind on Śiva in the heart or at the centres — the friend's attitude (sakhā-mārga), as Cuntarar's.
**Stage:** third pāda
**Signs of progress:** ["fruit: sārūpya, Śiva's form"]

## The texts' own warnings
