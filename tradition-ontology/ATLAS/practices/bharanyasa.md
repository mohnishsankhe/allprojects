# Bharanyāsa: the rite of laying down one's burden

`prc:bharanyasa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

The formal performance of surrender, in which one places the burden of one's protection and its fruit on the Lord, with the sāttvika-tyāga; performed by oneself, by repeating after the teacher, or by the teacher on one's behalf.
