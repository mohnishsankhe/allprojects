# Body scanning (observation of sensations)

`prc:body-scanning` · `skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [U Ba Khin – S. N. Goenka tradition (Sayagyi U Ba Khin lineage)](../lineages/u-ba-khin-goenka.md)

Moving attention systematically through the body, part by part, from head to feet and back, observing whatever sensation is present — gross or subtle — with equanimity and the understanding of impermanence; later sweeping the body in free flow.
**Stage:** all
**Prerequisites:** ['five precepts', 'three days of mindfulness of respiration']
