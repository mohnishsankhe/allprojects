# The natural discipline (sahaj): every act as worship

`prc:sahaj-sadhana-sant` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Dādū Panth](../lineages/dadu-panth.md), [Kabīr Panth](../lineages/kabir-panth.md), [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md)
**Taught in:** [Dādū Panth](../lineages/dadu-panth.md), [Kabīr Panth](../lineages/kabir-panth.md), [Sant tradition (nirguṇa bhakti of North India)](../lineages/sant.md)

Living in constant awareness of the Lord without forced austerities: eyes open, going about as circumambulation, work as service, lying down as prostration; no closing of eyes, stopping of ears or mortification.
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** ["the Lord's form seen everywhere with open eyes (Kabīr)"]
