# Dream yoga (rmi lam)

`prc:dream-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 6 independent lineage(s): [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md), [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md), [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md), [Shangpa Kagyu](../lineages/shangpa-kagyu.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)

Setting the intention before sleep and watching with mindfulness, the practitioner recognizes dreams as dreams, then trains in them — increasing, transforming, emanating, travelling to pure lands — knows them as illusion, abandons fear, and meditates on suchness; recognition in dream prepares recognition in the bardo.
**Stage:** advanced
**Prerequisites:** ['stable mindfulness', 'illusory-body contemplation']
**Signs of progress:** ['recognizing dreams while dreaming', 'fearlessness and lightness in dreams (Ājñāsamyakpramāṇa)', 'travelling to pure lands; riding sun and moon (Karṇatantravajrapada)']
