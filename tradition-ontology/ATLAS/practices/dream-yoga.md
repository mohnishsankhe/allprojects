# Dream yoga

`prc:dream-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 4 independent lineage(s): [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md), [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Taught in:** [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md), [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)

Recognizing the dream as dream and meditating on the profound meaning; focusing on nāda, bindu and the seed syllables of the five families, one sees buddha-fields and buddhas — 'the means of great bliss in sleep' (Tilopa, from Lavapa).
