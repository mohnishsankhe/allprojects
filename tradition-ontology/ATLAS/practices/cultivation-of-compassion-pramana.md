# Cultivation of compassion over many lives

`prc:cultivation-of-compassion-pramana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
**Taught in:** [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)

Compassion, repeatedly cultivated, becomes the mind's nature and grows by its own momentum without limit, because mind continues across lives; perfected, it moves one to remove others' suffering and is the root of a buddha's authority.
