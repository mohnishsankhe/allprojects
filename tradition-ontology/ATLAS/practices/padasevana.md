# Serving the Lord's feet (pāda-sevana)

`prc:padasevana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md), [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md), [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

Serving, touching, contemplating and dwelling at the Lord's feet — in the temple image and at his holy places. Fourth of the Bhāgavata's nine forms (BhP 7.5.23). The Āḻvārs make the Lord's feet (tiruvaṭi) their constant refuge: Kulaśekhara asks to lie as a step at the threshold of the sanctum at Tiruvēṅkaṭam; Tiruppāṇ sees the Lord from his feet upward; in Śrīvaiṣṇava temples the Lord's feet (the Śaṭhāri, named after Nammāḻvār) are placed on the worshipper's head.
