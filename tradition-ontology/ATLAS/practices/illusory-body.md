# Illusory-body yoga

`prc:illusory-body` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Ārya tradition of the Guhyasamāja](../lineages/arya-guhyasamaja.md), [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md)
**Taught in:** [Ārya tradition of the Guhyasamāja](../lineages/arya-guhyasamaja.md), [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md)

Training to see oneself, the deity and the whole world as illusion, dream, mirror-image and painted Vajrasattva (Tilopa, from Nāgārjuna); in the Ārya completion stage, the arising of the self-consecration body of wind and mind after the empties, 'the vision of conventional truth', obtained by the guru's grace.
**Stage:** advanced
**Prerequisites:** ['consecration', 'mind isolation (for the completion-stage form)']
