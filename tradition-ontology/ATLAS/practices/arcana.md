Worship of the Lord in his image or symbol with flowers, water, lamps, food and praise, according to rule or with simple love. Fifth of the Bhāgavata's nine forms; Vyāsa's definition of devotion is attachment to worship (NBS 16) and worship is one of love's eleven forms (NBS 82); the Tiruppāvai's girls come pure and strew pure flowers (TP 5).
