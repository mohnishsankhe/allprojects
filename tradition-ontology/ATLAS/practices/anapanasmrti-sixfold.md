# Mindfulness of breathing in six phases (Abhidharmakośa)

`prc:anapanasmrti-sixfold` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
**Taught in:** [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)

For one in whom discursive thought predominates: attention to the in- and out-breath in six phases — counting (gaṇanā), following (anugama), fixing (sthāna), observing (upalakṣaṇā), turning (vivarta) and purifying (pariśuddhi); it is wisdom, has wind as object, and is not found among outsiders. Natural breathing only; no retention.
**Stage:** beginner
