# Dwelling in the cremation ground

`prc:smasana-vasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

In the fourth stage he lives in the cremation ground, in the open or at the foot of a tree, not leaving it until the body falls, living on what comes unasked, practising remembrance.
