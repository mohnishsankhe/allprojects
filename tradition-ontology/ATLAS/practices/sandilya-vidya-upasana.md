# Śāṇḍilya-vidyā (meditation on the self made of mind)

`prc:sandilya-vidya-upasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)

Resolving that one becomes at death what one's resolve is, one meditates on the self as made of mind, with breath for body, of the form of light, small as a grain within and greater than all worlds, and resolves to attain it on departing (ŚB 10.6.3; ChU 3.14).
