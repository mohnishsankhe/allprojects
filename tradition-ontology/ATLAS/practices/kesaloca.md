# Plucking out the hair (keśa-loca)

`prc:kesaloca` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)

At initiation and periodically thereafter (for Digambara monks every two to four months) the mendicant's hair and beard are plucked out by hand, as an exercise of endurance and detachment from the body.
**Stage:** advanced
**Sources:**
