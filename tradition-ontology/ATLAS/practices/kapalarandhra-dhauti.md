# Kapālarandhra-dhauti (rubbing the forehead hollow)

`prc:kapalarandhra-dhauti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** cleansing
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

The hollow at the forehead (bhālarandhra) is rubbed with the right thumb, on waking, after meals and at the end of the day (GS 1.34-35).
**Stage:** beginner
**Signs of progress:** ['removes phlegm; the channels become clean and divine sight arises (GS 1.34-35)']
