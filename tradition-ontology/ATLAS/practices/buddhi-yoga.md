# Buddhiyoga, the yoga of understanding (BhG 2.39–53)

`prc:buddhi-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Taught in:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Hold the understanding 'according to yoga' in acting (2.39): a single, resolute understanding (2.41); take refuge in understanding rather than making the fruit the motive (2.49); be the same in success and failure — equanimity is yoga, and yoga is skill in actions (2.48, 2.50); let the understanding cross the thicket of delusion and stand immovable in samādhi (2.52–53).
**Stage:** all; culminates in understanding immovable in samādhi (2.53)
**Signs of progress:** No effort is lost and even a little protects from great fear (2.40); casting off good and evil deeds (2.50); freedom from the bondage of birth (2.51); nirveda towards what is heard (2.52); attaining yoga (2.53).
