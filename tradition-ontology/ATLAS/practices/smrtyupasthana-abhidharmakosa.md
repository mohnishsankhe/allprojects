# The four applications of mindfulness (Kośa method)

`prc:smrtyupasthana-abhidharmakosa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
**Taught in:** [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)

After calm is achieved: examining body, feeling, mind and dharmas first by their particular characteristics, then — in the application to dharmas with all as object — seeing them all as impermanent, suffering, empty and non-self; this leads into 'heat'.
**Stage:** intermediate
**Prerequisites:** ['calm (śamatha) through aśubhā or ānāpānasmṛti']
