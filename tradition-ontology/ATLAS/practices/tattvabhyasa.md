# Repeated practice of the principles (tattvābhyāsa)

`prc:tattvabhyasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Taught in:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)

After hearing the principles (manifest, unmanifest, knower) from scripture and teacher and establishing them by reasoning, one contemplates them repeatedly — long, reverently and without interruption — discriminating puruṣa from each principle and renouncing identification with each ('not this, not this'), until the knowledge 'I am not, not mine, not I' arises.
**Stage:** intermediate to advanced
**Prerequisites:** hearing and reasoning about the twenty-five principles; dispassion
**Duration:** long and uninterrupted; Vācaspati speaks of many births of practice
**Signs of progress:** the complete, pure, single knowledge 'I am not, not mine, not I' (SK 64); prakṛti seen as by a spectator, having ceased (SK 65–66)

## The texts' own warnings
- Liberation is not attained by mere hearing, because beginningless latent tendencies are strong. — [Sāṃkhya Sūtra](../texts/samkhya-sutra.md) 2.3
- Dispassion without discrimination leads only to absorption in prakṛti, from which one rises again. — [Sāṃkhya Sūtra](../texts/samkhya-sutra.md) 3.54
