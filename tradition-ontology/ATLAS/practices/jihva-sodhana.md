# Jihvā-śodhana (cleansing — and lengthening — the tongue)

`prc:jihva-sodhana` · `skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

**Category:** cleansing
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

The root of the tongue is cleaned with three fingers inserted into the throat to remove phlegm (GS 1.30). RESTRICTED in part: the text goes on to describe rubbing the tongue with butter, 'milking' it and drawing it out with an iron instrument daily at sunrise so that it lengthens (GS 1.31-32) — a preparation for khecarī; that procedure is not reproduced.
**Stage:** intermediate
**Signs of progress:** ['the text says a long tongue destroys old age, death and disease (GS 1.29)']
