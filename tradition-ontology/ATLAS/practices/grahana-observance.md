# Observances at an eclipse

`prc:grahana-observance` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md), [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)

From the onset of the restricted period before an eclipse no food is cooked or eaten; at the eclipse one bathes, recites mantras, makes oblations and gifts (whose offering Rāhu receives by Brahmā's boon), and bathes again at its end; mantra-recitation then is held especially fruitful.
**Stage:** all
**Sources:**

## The texts' own warnings
- A child born during an eclipse faces disease and danger and requires pacification. — [Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra](../texts/brhat-parasara-hora-sastra.md) 91.1

_Notes: The fast is limited to the eclipse period; not a prolonged fast._
