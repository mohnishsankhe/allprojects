Restricted-adjacent (breath retention): summary only. The sūtra offers expulsion and retention of the breath as one means to steadiness of mind; Vyāsa only glosses expulsion as expulsion of the belly-air through the nostrils by a special effort and retention as prāṇāyāma (YBh 1.34); Vyāsa relates it to fitness for concentration (YBh 2.53).
