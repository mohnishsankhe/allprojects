# The meditation on plenitude (bhūma-vidyā)

`prc:bhuma-vidya` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Venerate in ascending order name, speech, mind, intention, thought, meditation, understanding, strength, food, water, heat, space, memory, hope and breath as brahman, each greater than the one before; then seek to understand truth, understanding, thinking, faith, steadfastness, action and happiness, up to plenitude (bhūman), where one sees nothing else; then the teaching of 'I' and of the self.
**Signs of progress:** freedom of movement as far as each object reaches; the self-ruled moves at will in all worlds (ChU 7.25.2)
