# The eight-limbed fast-day vow (upavāsa)

`prc:upavasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mūlasarvāstivāda](../lineages/mulasarvastivada.md), [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

A lay person takes eight precepts from another in the morning, in a humble posture, repeating the formula, without ornaments, for one day and night: four limbs of morality (not killing, stealing, unchastity, lying), one of heedfulness (no intoxicants), three of the vow (no high beds, no ornaments/entertainment, no food after noon).
**Stage:** beginner
**Duration:** until the next sunrise
