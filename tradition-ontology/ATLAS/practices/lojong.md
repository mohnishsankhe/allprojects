Training the mind in relative and ultimate bodhicitta by means of short slogans applied throughout the day: after the preliminaries, meditating on phenomena as dream-like and resting in the mind's nature (ultimate), practising sending and taking on the breath (relative), turning every adversity into the path, applying the five powers in life and at death, checking one's progress, and keeping the commitments and precepts.
**Stage:** all (after the preliminaries)
