# Pacification for an inauspicious birth

`prc:janma-santi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)

When a child is born at an eclipse, at the new moon, at a solar ingress, in Mūla or at a junction (gaṇḍānta) of asterisms, under Viṣṭi, or with other ominous features, a pacification is performed: images of the ruling deities in gold, silver or lead are installed on a vessel, worshipped with the mantras of one's own domestic ritual, bathed over the family and honoured with oblations, followed by feeding brahmins.
