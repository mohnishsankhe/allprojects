# Liberating initiation (nirvāṇa-dīkṣā)

`prc:nirvana-diksa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)

The guru ritually traverses and purifies the six paths of the disciple's bondage, burning accumulated and future karma, leaving only the karma that sustains the present body; the disciple becomes a putraka assured of liberation at death. Summary only.
**Prerequisites:** samaya-dīkṣā
