# Rejuvenation therapy (rasāyana) — herbal

`prc:rasayana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Taken in youth or middle age after purification, by the self-controlled, either in a hut (kuṭīprāveśika) or amid ordinary life (vātātapika), with herbal preparations; its fruits are long life, memory, intelligence, health, youth, lustre and strength. No formulations recorded.
**Stage:** all
**Prerequisites:** purification of body; purity and restraint of mind (Ci 1.4.36-37)
**Signs of progress:** ['long life, memory, intelligence, health, youth, lustre, voice, strength, success of speech (Ca Ci 1.1.7-8)']

## The texts' own warnings
- Rasāyana applied to an unpurified body is fruitless, like dye on a dirty cloth. — [Aṣṭāṅga Hṛdaya](../texts/astanga-hrdaya.md) Utt 39.4
- One who has not removed his bodily and mental doṣas never attains its benefits. — [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) Ci 1.4.36
- If disease arises from a lapse in the regimen, treat it with its own remedies, setting rasāyana aside. — [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) Ci 1.4.29
