# Dispassion (vairāgya)

Lower: the consciousness of mastery (vaśīkāra-saṃjñā) of one free of thirst for seen and scripture-taught objects; by the power of discerning insight the mind sees the faults of objects, and has a consciousness of mastery without engagement and empty of rejecting and taking (YS 1.15, YBh 1.15). Higher: freedom from thirst for the guṇas themselves, from knowing the puruṣa (YS 1.16); Vyāsa: 'only the clearness of knowledge', of which kaivalya is inseparable (YBh 1.16). Dispassion makes the stream toward objects barren (YBh 1.12).
**Stage:** intermediate
**Signs of progress:** ["The yogin's own thought at the higher dispassion: 'What was to be attained is attained; the afflictions to be destroyed are destroyed; the linked chain of becoming is cut' (YBh 1.16)."]
