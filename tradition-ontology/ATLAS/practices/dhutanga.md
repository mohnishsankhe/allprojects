# The thirteen ascetic practices (dhutaṅga)

Voluntary austerities undertaken for effacement: refuse-rag robes, three robes only, alms-food, house-to-house alms, one session, bowl-food, refusing later food, forest dwelling, tree-root, open air, charnel ground, any bed, the sitter's practice; each has strict, medium and mild grades.

## The texts' own warnings
- They suit the greedy and deluded temperaments; undertaken with the wish to be admired they become a basis of evil desire. — [Visuddhimagga](../texts/visuddhimagga.md) II
- The Buddha refused Devadatta's demand to make forest dwelling, alms-food, rag robes, tree-root dwelling and abstaining from fish and meat compulsory for life; each is allowed only for those who wish. — [Cullavagga](../texts/cullavagga.md) Cv 7.3.15
