# The ten-day Vipassana course (Goenka)

`prc:ten-day-vipassana-course` · `skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [U Ba Khin – S. N. Goenka tradition (Sayagyi U Ba Khin lineage)](../lineages/u-ba-khin-goenka.md)

A residential course in noble silence: taking refuge and the five precepts, surrender to the teacher and the technique; mindfulness of respiration at the nostrils for three and a half days; body scanning from the fourth day; loving-kindness on the last day; recorded evening discourses; given free on donation.
**Stage:** beginner
**Duration:** ten full days
