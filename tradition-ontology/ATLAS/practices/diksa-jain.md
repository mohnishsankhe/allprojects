# Jain initiation (dīkṣā)

`prc:diksa-jain` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)

The candidate, having received the teacher's permission (and, for Śvetāmbaras, the family's), renounces household life, plucks out the hair, receives the great vows and the ascetic's requisites (Śvetāmbara: white robes, broom, bowls, mouth-cloth; Digambara monks: nudity, peacock-feather whisk, water-gourd) and a new name.
**Stage:** guṇasthāna 6
