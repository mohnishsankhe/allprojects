# Taking the tuḷsī necklace (Vārkarī initiation)

`prc:tulasi-mala-dharana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)
**Taught in:** [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)

A senior Vārkarī places the tuḷsī-bead necklace (māḷ) on the new member, who vows to keep the vārī, to be vegetarian and abstain from intoxicants, to observe ekādaśī and to remember the name.
**Stage:** beginner
