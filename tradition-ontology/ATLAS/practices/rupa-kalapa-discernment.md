# Discerning material groups (Pa-Auk)

`prc:rupa-kalapa-discernment` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Pa-Auk tradition (Pa-Auk Tawya)](../lineages/pa-auk.md)

With concentration from four-elements meditation or jhāna, the body is seen to break into tiny particles (kalāpa); each kind is analysed into its elements (at least eight), across the six doors and four origins, before mentality is discerned.
