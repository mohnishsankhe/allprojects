# Contemplation of the three natures

`prc:three-nature-contemplation` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
**Taught in:** [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)

The dependent is entered first as the absence of duality, then as mere imagination with non-existent duality, then the perfected as the being of that absence; the magical elephant is the model: seeing no elephant, the shape vanishing and the wood seen, all at once — understanding, abandonment and attainment of the three natures are simultaneous.
**Stage:** intermediate to advanced
