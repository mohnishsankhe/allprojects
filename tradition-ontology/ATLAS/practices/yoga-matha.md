# The practice-hut (maṭha, kuṭīra) and the place of practice

`prc:yoga-matha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

The haṭha yogin lives and practises alone in a small hut with a small door and no windows or holes, neither too high nor too low, smeared with cow-dung, clean and free of creatures, walled, with water nearby, in a righteous, well-governed and prosperous land free of trouble (HYP 1.12-14; GS 5.5-7; DYŚ 49-54; ŚS 3.20, 3.35). Practice is not to be begun in a far country, a forest, a capital or a crowd (GS 5.3-4).
