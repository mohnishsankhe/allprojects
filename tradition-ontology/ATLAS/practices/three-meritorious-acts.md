# The three meritorious acts (sanfu)

`prc:three-meritorious-acts` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)
**Taught in:** [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)

Filial care, service of teachers, non-killing and the ten good deeds; taking refuge, keeping the precepts and deportment; arousing bodhicitta, believing in cause and effect, reciting the Mahāyāna sūtras and encouraging others — 'the right cause of the pure acts of the buddhas of the three times'.
