# Worship through nāda (music as spiritual practice)

`prc:nadopasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 5 independent lineage(s): [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md), [Unaffiliated regional bhakti poets (classificatory grouping)](../lineages/regional-bhakti-poets.md), [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md), [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md), [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md), [Unaffiliated regional bhakti poets (classificatory grouping)](../lineages/regional-bhakti-poets.md), [Saṅgītaśāstra (the science of music: song, instrument and dance)](../lineages/sangita.md), [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md)

Disciplined practice of notes, rāga and rhythm, offered to the deity and attended to until the mind is absorbed in the sound: the struck nāda of music, which 'delights the world and breaks worldly existence', as an easier path than the sages' worship of the unstruck nāda; by nāda-worship the three gods are worshipped. The vīṇā-player versed in śrutis and tāla goes the way of liberation without effort.
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** ['success in song when the jīva rests on certain petals of the heart, throat and lalanā centres or in the brahmarandhra (ŚR 1.2.140–144)']
