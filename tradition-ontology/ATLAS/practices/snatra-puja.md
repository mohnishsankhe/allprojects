# The bathing rite (snātra-pūjā)

`prc:snatra-puja` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md)

A congregational re-enactment of Indra's bathing of the newborn Tīrthaṅkara on Mount Meru: a small image is bathed with water and other substances to hymns, recalling the birth-kalyāṇaka.
