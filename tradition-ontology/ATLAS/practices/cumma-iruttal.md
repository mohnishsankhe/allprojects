# Being still (cummā iruttal)

`prc:cumma-iruttal` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Taught in:** [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md), [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)

The silent guru's instruction 'be still': resting without the mind's movement, which Tāyumāṉavar holds harder and higher than every power.
**Stage:** advanced
**Sources:** 
  - [Tāyumāṉavar pāṭalkaḷ (the songs of Tāyumāṉavar)](../texts/tayumanavar-padalgal.md) — ref: passim; rests_on: ["tea:tayumanavar-padalgal:cumma-iru", "tea:tayumanavar-padalgal:kantuk-katankata"]

## The texts' own warnings
- All the powers — even transmuting metals and entering another's body — are easier than stilling the mind; the powers are not the goal. — [Tāyumāṉavar pāṭalkaḷ (the songs of Tāyumāṉavar)](../texts/tayumanavar-padalgal.md) kantuk kaṭaṅkā
