# Seed-syllables of the centres and elements

Meditating on, or softly intoning, the seed-syllable of each centre with its element — laṃ (earth, root centre), vaṃ (water, svādhiṣṭhāna), raṃ (fire, maṇipūra), yaṃ (air, anāhata), haṃ (space, viśuddhi), oṃ (ājñā) — together with its shape, colour, deity and the letters on its petals; the same seeds are used in the purification of the elements (bhūtaśuddhi) before worship, where the elements of the body are dissolved upward into their sources and a divine body is re-created.
