# Mahāyoga generation stage (bskyed rim)

`prc:generation-stage-mahayoga` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** visualization-deity
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)
**Taught in:** [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)

From the three samādhis (suchness, all-illuminating compassion, the seed syllable) the practitioner generates the maṇḍala and deity, with clear appearance, vajra pride and recollection of purity, purifying the habits of birth, and recites the mantra; everything is seen as deity, sound as mantra, thought as wisdom.
**Stage:** intermediate
**Prerequisites:** ['empowerment', 'preliminaries']
