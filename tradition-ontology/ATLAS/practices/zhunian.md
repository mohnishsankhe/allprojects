# Assisted recitation for the dying (zhunian)

`prc:zhunian` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)
**Taught in:** [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)

Companions recite Amitābha's name beside the dying, in turns, voice following voice, to support his recollection until the ten recitations are complete; the body is not moved and no one weeps.
