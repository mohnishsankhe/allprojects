# Contemplating death and impermanence

`prc:contemplating-death-and-impermanence` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)

Recollecting that death may come soon, before one's evil is exhausted, that one will go alone leaving relatives and possessions, and that the lord of death rules all who are born, one turns from craving to refuge and the Dharma.
