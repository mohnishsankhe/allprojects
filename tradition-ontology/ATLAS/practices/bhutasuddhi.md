# Purification of the elements (bhūtaśuddhi)

**Taught in:** [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)

In the preliminaries to worship the practitioner mentally dissolves the elements of the body, from earth upward, into their sources, burns away the impure body, and re-creates a pure divine body with mantras before placing mantras on it. Summary only.
