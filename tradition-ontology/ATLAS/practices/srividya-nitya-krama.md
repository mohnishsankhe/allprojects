# The daily routine of the Śrīvidyā initiate

`prc:srividya-nitya-krama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Taught in:** [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md), [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)

Rising before dawn, contemplating the guru's feet in the crown with nectar flooding the body, meditating on Gaṇapati in the heart, the morning ablutions and offerings, libations, and the day's worship (PKS 2.2); constant holding of the vidyā and immersion in Śiva-hood (PKS 1.17-18).
