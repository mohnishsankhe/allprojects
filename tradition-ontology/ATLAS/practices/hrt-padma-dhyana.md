# Meditation in the heart-lotus (Uddhava's instruction)

`prc:hrt-padma-dhyana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

Seated evenly, gaze at the nose-tip, purify the breath-channels, raise Oṃ in the heart like a bell; visualise the upturned eight-petalled heart-lotus with sun, moon and fire in its pericarp and the Lord's form in the fire; meditate on all its limbs, then on the smile, then fix the mind in space, then think of nothing; the self is seen in the Lord, light in light (BhP 11.14.32-46).
