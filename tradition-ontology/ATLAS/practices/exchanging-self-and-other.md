# Exchanging self and other

`prc:exchanging-self-and-other` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)

Having equalized self and other, one exchanges them: seeing that all suffering comes from self-concern and all happiness from concern for others, one gives oneself to others and takes others as oneself, regarding one's former self from the standpoint of others (with envy, rivalry and pride directed at it).
**Stage:** advanced
**Prerequisites:** ['equalizing self and other']
