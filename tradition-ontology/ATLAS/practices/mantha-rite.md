# The mantha rite

`prc:mantha-rite` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

A rite for one who desires greatness, performed after an observance, in which offerings of ghee are made to the breaths and other deities and a prepared mixture is drunk with formulas (BAU 6.3; ChU 5.2.4-8). Summary only.
**Sources:**

## The texts' own warnings
- It should not be taught to anyone but a son or a pupil (BAU 6.3.12). — [Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad](../texts/brhadaranyaka-upanisad.md) 6.3.12
