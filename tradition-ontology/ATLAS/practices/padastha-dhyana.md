# Padastha-dhyāna (meditation on sacred words)

`prc:padastha-dhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)

Meditation on letters and mantras: the sixteen vowels on a lotus at the navel, the consonants on a lotus at the heart, the syllable 'arhaṃ', the five-line homage and its shorter forms, visualised and silently recited according to the teacher's instruction.

## The texts' own warnings
- Mantras are to be meditated on for purification and liberation; using them to harm, attract or subjugate is evil meditation. — [Jñānārṇava](../texts/jnanarnava.md) asad-dhyana
