# Solitude and contemplation of the body

`prc:solitude-and-body-contemplation` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)

To settle the mind in concentration one withdraws from worldly company and discursive thought into solitude, reflects on the impermanence of loved ones and the impurity of the body to cut attachment, and on the pleasures of the forest.
**Stage:** intermediate
**Sources:**
