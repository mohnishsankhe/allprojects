# Temple festival (mahotsava)

`prc:mahotsava` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 4 independent lineage(s): [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md), [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md)
**Taught in:** [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md), [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md)

The annual festival begun by hoisting the flag, with daily processions of the festival image, offerings to the attendant beings and a concluding bath (avabhṛtha/ārāṭṭu), conducted by the priest or tantri.
