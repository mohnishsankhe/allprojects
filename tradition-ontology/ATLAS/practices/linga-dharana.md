# Wearing the liṅga (liṅgadhāraṇa)

`prc:linga-dharana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)
**Taught in:** [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md), [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md), [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)

The iṣṭaliṅga, kept in a small casket (karaḍige) on a cord, is worn on the body at all times — at the head, throat, chest, armpit, belly or in the hand, never below the navel; inwardly the liṅga of light is held in the mind at the base, heart or brows.
**Stage:** bhakta onward
**Sources:** 
  - [Siddhāntaśikhāmaṇi](../texts/siddhantasikhamani.md) — ref: 6.26; 6.30–33; 6.48; 6.52–53; rests_on: ["tea:siddhantasikhamani:6.26", "tea:siddhantasikhamani:6.30-33", "tea:siddhantasikhamani:6.48", "tea:siddhantasikhamani:6.52-53"]
**Sequences:** [The six stages (ṣaṭsthala) of the Vīraśaiva path](../paths/virasaiva-satsthala.md)

## The texts' own warnings
- Never separate the liṅga from the body at any time or place. — [Siddhāntaśikhāmaṇi](../texts/siddhantasikhamani.md) 6.26
- Wearing it below the navel is a cause of sin; not on the tip of matted hair, lower back or an unclean place. — [Siddhāntaśikhāmaṇi](../texts/siddhantasikhamani.md) 6.53
- The text treats the liṅga's accidental fall with the utmost gravity (recorded as restricted). — [Siddhāntaśikhāmaṇi](../texts/siddhantasikhamani.md) 6.27
