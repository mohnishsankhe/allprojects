# Relinquishment before action (sāttvika-tyāga)

`prc:sattvika-tyaga` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

Before any act, relinquishing the sense of agency, of ownership and desire for its fruit: 'the Lord himself, for his own sake, has this done through me'.
