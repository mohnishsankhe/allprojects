# Śakticālana (moving the power)

`prc:sakticalana` · `sourced` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

**Category:** energy
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

RESTRICTED — summary only: rousing kuṇḍalinī, who sleeps coiled above the kanda covering the door of Brahman with her mouth, so that she leaves the mouth of the suṣumnā and the breath enters it (HYP 3.104-123; GS 3.49-60; ŚS 4.53-57). The texts describe it with a particular seat, pressure at the kanda, bhastrikā and contraction, or (in the Gheraṇḍa) with the navel bound in cloth and aśvinī mudrā; they call it the only means to cleanse the channels and say the yoni mudrā does not succeed without it (GS 3.57). No procedure, measure or time is reproduced.
