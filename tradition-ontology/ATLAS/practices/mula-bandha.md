# Mūlabandha (the root lock)

`prc:mula-bandha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** lock-seal
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Pressing the perineum with the heel and contracting the anus, the downward-moving apāna is forcibly drawn upward, again and again (HYP 3.61-63; GS 3.14-15; ŚS 4.41; DYŚ 131; GŚ 37). The Gheraṇḍa adds pressing the navel-knot toward the spine and binding the genitals with the other heel (GS 3.14-15). In breath-control it is the contraction 'below' that accompanies the throat lock (HYP 2.46).
**Stage:** intermediate
**Signs of progress:** ['prāṇa and apāna, nāda and bindu become one; urine and faeces diminish; the old become young (HYP 3.64-65)', 'the heated kuṇḍalinī awakens and enters the brahma-channel (HYP 3.66-69)', 'mastery of breath (GS 3.17); the yogin in padmāsana leaves the ground (ŚS 4.43)']
