# Five-tone recitation (wuhui nianfo) of Fazhao

**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md), `lin:tendai`
**Taught in:** [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md), `lin:tendai`

The name is chanted by a group in five 'assemblies': slow level tone, slow level-rising tone, neither slow nor fast, gradually faster, and the four syllables 'Amituofo' ever faster; followed by hymns.
