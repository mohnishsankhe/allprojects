# Cultivating non-discrimination (abheda)

`prc:abheda-bhava` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Aghora (Aghor panth; Aghorī and Aughaṛ ascetics)](../lineages/aghora.md), [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)

Freeing oneself from the eight fetters — disgust, doubt, fear, shame, loathing, pride of family, of conduct and of caste — by treating nothing as untouchable; the freed one is Maheśvara.
