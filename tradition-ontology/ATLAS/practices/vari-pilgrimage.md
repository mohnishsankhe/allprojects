# The vārī: walking pilgrimage to Paṇḍharpūr

`prc:vari-pilgrimage` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)
**Taught in:** [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)

Twice a year (above all for Āṣāḍhī ekādaśī, again for Kārtikī) Vārkarīs walk to Paṇḍharpūr in singing companies (diṇḍī) behind the palanquins bearing the sandals of Jñāneśvar from Āḷandī and Tukārām from Dehu, singing abhaṅgas and the name by day, holding kīrtan and bhajan at the halts, keeping the ekādaśī fast, bathing in the Candrabhāgā and taking darśan of Viṭṭhala (or of the temple's pinnacle when the crowds are too great). Those who keep it for life are Vārkarīs.
**Stage:** all
**Prerequisites:** ['wearing the tuḷsī necklace (for Vārkarīs proper)', 'vegetarian diet and abstinence on the road']
**Duration:** about three weeks on foot for the Āṣāḍhī vārī from Āḷandī/Dehu; lifelong observance
**Signs of progress:** ["the saints' company experienced as the tīrtha", "longing for Paṇḍharpūr as for a mother's home"]
