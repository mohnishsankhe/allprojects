# The six essential duties

**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)

Daily performance of sāmāyika, praise of the twenty-four Jinas, salutation of the teacher, pratikramaṇa, kāyotsarga and a vow of renunciation (pratyākhyāna); for Digambara householders also the six daily duties of worship, attendance on the teacher, study, restraint, austerity and giving.
