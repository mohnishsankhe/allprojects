Vajrasattva is visualized above the head; with the four powers (support, regret, resolution, antidote) one recites the hundred-syllable mantra while nectar flows down, washing out wrongdoing and obscurations; Vajrasattva declares them purified and dissolves into oneself.
**Stage:** beginner
**Signs of progress:** ['dreams of bathing, vomiting impurities, wearing white, flying (purification signs)']
