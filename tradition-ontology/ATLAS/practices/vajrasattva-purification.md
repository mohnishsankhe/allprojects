# Vajrasattva meditation and recitation (the hundred-syllable mantra)

`prc:vajrasattva-purification` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** visualization-deity
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Taught in:** [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)

Vajrasattva is visualized above the head; with the four powers (support, regret, resolution, antidote) one recites the hundred-syllable mantra while nectar flows down, washing out wrongdoing and obscurations; Vajrasattva declares them purified and dissolves into oneself.
**Stage:** beginner
**Signs of progress:** ['dreams of bathing, vomiting impurities, wearing white, flying (purification signs)']
