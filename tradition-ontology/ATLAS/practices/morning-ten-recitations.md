# The morning ten recitations (Zunshi's shinian fa)

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md), `lin:tiantai`
**Taught in:** [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md), `lin:tiantai`

Each morning, facing west with joined palms, one recites the name continuously for the length of a natural breath — one 'recitation' — ten times, voice neither high nor low, then makes the vow of dedication; the breath is used to bind the mind (not a retention practice).
