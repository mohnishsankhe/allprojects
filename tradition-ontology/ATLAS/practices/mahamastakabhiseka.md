# The great head-anointing of Bāhubali

`prc:mahamastakabhiseka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)

About every twelve years the 58-foot image of Bāhubali at Śravaṇabeḷagoḷa is anointed from a scaffold with water, milk, sugarcane juice, sandal, saffron and other substances, in memory of his renunciation and standing meditation.
