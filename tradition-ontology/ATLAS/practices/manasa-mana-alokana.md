# Seeing the mind with the mind

`prc:manasa-mana-alokana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Taught in:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)

Placing the mind within Śakti and Śakti within the mind, the practitioner beholds the mind by the mind; in the Amaraughaśāsana this, in sahaja-samādhi, is liberation itself.
