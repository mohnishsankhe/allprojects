# The meditation taught to Bālāki

`prc:balaki-vidya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Pass beyond venerating the persons in the sun, moon and other phenomena as brahman to the self into which the sleeper withdraws and from which all breaths, worlds and gods come forth.
