# Going about like a wretch (pretācaraṇa)

`prc:pretacarana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

He lives unwashed, dirt-smeared, with hair, beard and nails grown and without grooming, like a poor madman, so that the marks of caste and āśrama are cut off and dispassion grows.
**Stage:** avyakta (second) stage
