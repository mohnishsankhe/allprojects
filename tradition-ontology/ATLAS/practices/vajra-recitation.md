# Vajra recitation (vajrajāpa)

**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Ārya tradition of the Guhyasamāja](../lineages/arya-guhyasamaja.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
