# Supporting the dying with wholesome objects

`prc:deathbed-recollection` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Relatives and monks help the dying recollect their good deeds, chant, and bring offerings or Buddha-images into view, so that a wholesome kamma or sign becomes the object of the last cognitive process, which conditions rebirth.
