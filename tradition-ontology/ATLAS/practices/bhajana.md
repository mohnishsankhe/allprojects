# Devotional singing (bhajana, bhajan)

Singing devotional songs and names of the deity, usually in a group led by a lead singer, with simple rhythm instruments; in the South Indian bhajana tradition an ordered sequence of songs and names to many deities culminating in the Lord's name; in the Vārkarī tradition singing the abhaṅgas of the saints; in the Sant tradition singing the śabdas of Kabīr and others.
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** ['melting of the heart and tears (BhP 11.14.24)']
