# Slaying desire, the enemy

**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

First restrain the senses and slay desire, the destroyer of knowledge and discernment (3.41); knowing that which is beyond the understanding, steady the self by the self and slay the enemy in the form of desire (3.43). Desire's seat is the senses, mind and understanding (3.40).

## The texts' own warnings

- The enemy in the form of desire is hard to overcome (durāsada). — [Bhagavad Gītā](../texts/bhagavad-gita.md) 3.43
