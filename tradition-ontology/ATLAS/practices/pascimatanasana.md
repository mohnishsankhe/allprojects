# Paścimatānāsana (the stretch of the back; paścimottāna, ugrāsana)

`prc:pascimatanasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** posture
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

The legs stretched on the ground like a staff, the tips of the feet held with the hands and the forehead brought to the knees (HYP 1.28; GS 2.26). The Śivasaṃhitā's 'fierce posture' (ugrāsana), with the legs apart and the head on the knees, is expressly identified with paścimottāna (ŚS 3.92). It is also the base for dry basti and taḍāgī mudrā (GS 1.48, 3.61).
**Stage:** intermediate
**Signs of progress:** ['makes the breath flow in the back (central) channel, kindles the digestive fire, slims the belly, gives health (HYP 1.29)', 'kindles the breath and removes bodily decline (ŚS 3.92)']
