# Recognition (pratyabhijñā) of one's self as the Lord

Recognizing, through the revealed powers of knowing and acting present in every experience, that one's own self is Maheśvara; established once with firm conviction, recognition itself is liberation; the mind turned inward ascends to consciousness (ĪPK 1.1.3, 4.16-17; PH 13).
**Signs of progress:** bliss of consciousness; lordship persisting amid thought (ĪPK 4.12)
