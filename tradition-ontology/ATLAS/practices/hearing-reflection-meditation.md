# Hearing, reflection and cultivation

`prc:hearing-reflection-meditation` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md), [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
**Taught in:** [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md), [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)

Wisdom arises in three stages — from hearing the teaching, from reflecting on it with reasoning, and from meditative cultivation — which are three of the seventeen stages of the Yogācārabhūmi; for the pramāṇa school, reasoning (cintā) establishes what cultivation then makes vivid.
