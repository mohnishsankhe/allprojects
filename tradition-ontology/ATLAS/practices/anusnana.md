# Re-bathing in ash (anusnāna)

`prc:anusnana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

Between the three ash-baths, after impurity from eating, sneezing, spitting, urinating or evacuating, he bathes again with ash, for purity and to show the sign.
**Stage:** vyakta (first) stage
**Sources:**
