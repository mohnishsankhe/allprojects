# Meditation objects that purify temperament

`prc:carita-visodhana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
**Taught in:** [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)

The meditation object is prescribed by temperament: the unattractive for the lustful, loving-kindness for the hateful, dependent origination for the deluded, analysis of the elements for the proud, mindfulness of breathing for the discursive.
**Stage:** beginner
**Prerequisites:** a teacher who can discern the practitioner's temperament
