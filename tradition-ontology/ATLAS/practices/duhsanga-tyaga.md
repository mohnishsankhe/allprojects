# Shunning bad company (duḥsaṅga-tyāga)

`prc:duhsanga-tyaga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md)
**Taught in:** [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md)

Avoiding in every way the company that breeds desire, anger, delusion, loss of memory and of understanding and utter ruin — small ripples that become an ocean through association (NBS 43–45); likewise not listening to talk of lust, wealth, unbelievers and enemies (NBS 63).
