# Repeated contemplation of objects and of the body (prasaṅkhyāna)

`prc:prasankhyana-nyaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
**Taught in:** [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)

First contemplating the objects of desire whose false construction breeds attachment, aversion and delusion, until that false construction ceases; then contemplating the body and the rest within, until the I-notion ceases; one who dwells detached within and without is called liberated.
**Signs of progress:** ['false construction ceases', 'I-notion ceases', 'mind detached within and without']
