# The sixty-four limbs of devotional practice

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)
**Taught in:** [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md)

Rūpa's list of practices: the first ten (taking shelter of a guru, initiation and instruction, serving the guru with trust, following the saints, inquiring into true dharma, renouncing enjoyment for Kṛṣṇa, residence at holy places, accepting only what is needed, honouring Ekādaśī, honouring the sacred trees, cows and brāhmaṇas) and ten avoidances (of non-devotees' company, many disciples, great undertakings, many books for argument, miserliness, grief, disrespect of other gods, harming beings, offences in worship and to the name, tolerating blasphemy) are the gateway; then hearing, chanting, remembering, worship, bowing, prayer, service, friendship, self-offering, wearing Vaiṣṇava marks, honouring prasāda, tulasī, festivals and more, ending with the five most potent limbs.
