# Pratardana's meditation on breath as the intelligent self

`prc:pratardana-vidya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Venerate Indra/breath as the intelligent self (prajñātman), as life and immortality; know not speech but the speaker, not the object but the knower.
**Sources:**
