## The texts' own warnings
- asubhāni ca na vaḍḍhetabbāni| — [Visuddhimagga](../texts/visuddhimagga.md) III p. 111
- ḍhitesu kuṇaparāsi yeva vaḍḍhati, na koci ānisaṃso atthi| — [Visuddhimagga](../texts/visuddhimagga.md) III p. 112
- The Vinaya origin story of the third pārājika tells that monks who practised the contemplation of the unlovely became disgusted with their bodies and took their lives; the Buddha then taught the peaceful mindfulness of breathing (low confidence on the parallels in each school's Vinaya). — [Vinaya Piṭaka](../texts/vinaya-pitaka.md) Pārājika 3, origin story; cf. SN 54.9
- The Kośa prescribes it specifically for those dominated by passion, and mindfulness of breathing for those dominated by discursive thought. — [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) 6.9
- Monks at Vesālī who practised foulness became repelled by their own bodies and many took their lives or had others kill them; the Buddha laid down the third pārājika and taught mindfulness of breathing as a peaceful, sublime dwelling. — [Mahāvibhaṅga (Bhikkhuvibhaṅga)](../texts/mahavibhanga.md) Vin Pār 3; SN 54.9
