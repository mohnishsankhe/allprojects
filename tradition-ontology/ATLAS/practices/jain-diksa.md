# Initiation into mendicancy (dīkṣā)

`prc:jain-diksa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)

The candidate, with family consent, renounces possessions in a public ceremony, receives the mendicant's equipment (whisk, bowls or water-pot) and a new name, takes the vow of equanimity for life, and has the hair plucked; Śvetāmbaras later confer the 'great initiation' (chedopasthāpanā) with the five vows; Digambara men become naked monks after the stages of kṣullaka and ailaka.
**Stage:** advanced
