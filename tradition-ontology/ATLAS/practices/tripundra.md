# Tripuṇḍra (three lines of ash)

`prc:tripundra` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Ash taken with the five brahma-mantras and applied in three horizontal lines, each line standing for a fire, a letter of Oṃ, a guṇa, a world, a power, a Veda and a deity (Kālāgnirudra); for all four stages of life.
