# Viśvajit (the rite of giving away all)

`prc:visvajit` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
**Taught in:** [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)

A rite in which the sacrificer gives away all he owns; no fruit is stated, so heaven is understood (MS 4.3.15); what one is master of is given, but not the land, which is common to all (MS 6.7.1–3).
