# Inner and outer purity (Basava's seven restraints)

`prc:antaranga-bahiranga-suddhi` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ethics

**Taught in:** [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md), [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)

Not stealing, not killing, not lying, not raging, not despising others, not praising oneself and not blaming others — Basava's definition of inner and outer purity.
