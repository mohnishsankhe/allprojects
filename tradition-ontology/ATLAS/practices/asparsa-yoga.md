# Asparśa-yoga ('contactless' yoga)

`prc:asparsa-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)

Gauḍapāda's discipline: realizing the unborn, controlling the mind without weariness (like emptying the ocean with a blade of grass), rousing it from dullness, calming distraction, recognizing latent attachment, not disturbing equilibrium, not relishing the bliss, until the mind becomes no-mind.
**Signs of progress:** ['the mind no longer imagines; no-mind (GK 3.32)']
**Sources:**
