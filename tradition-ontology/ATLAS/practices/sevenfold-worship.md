# The supreme worship (sevenfold service)

`prc:sevenfold-worship` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)

Worship of the Buddhas, the Dharma and the bodhisattvas with offerings (actual and imagined), praise and prostration, refuge, confession of faults, rejoicing in merit, requesting teaching, requesting the Buddhas to remain, and dedication — as the preparation for adopting the awakening mind.
