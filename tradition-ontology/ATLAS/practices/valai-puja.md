# Worship of Vālai / Maṉōṉmaṇi at the root and on the Meru

`prc:valai-puja` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** visualization-deity
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Taught in:** [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)

The Siddhars' Śākta worship: the goddess Vālai (Bālā), mother Maṉōṉmaṇi, is worshipped at the root centre with her three syllables and on the Meru of forty-three triangles (the Śrīcakra), with the syllables of Tripurā, Bhuvanā and Yāmaḷā, after the five initiations (Rāmatēvar; Caṭṭaimuṉi). Mantras and procedure not recorded.
**Stage:** advanced
**Prerequisites:** initiation (tīṭcai)
