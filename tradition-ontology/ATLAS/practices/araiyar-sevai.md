# Sung and enacted recitation of the Prabandham (araiyar cēvai)

`prc:araiyar-sevai` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

Hereditary temple singers (araiyars) sing the Āḻvārs' verses with gesture and dance before the Lord, above all during the recitation festival; the tradition traces the service to Nāthamuni, who set the hymns to music.
