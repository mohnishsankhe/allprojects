# Dhutaṅga wandering (tudong)

`prc:tudong` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Thai Forest tradition (Kammaṭṭhāna forest tradition)](../lineages/thai-forest.md)

Wandering alone or in small groups through forests and mountains, dwelling under a klot (umbrella tent) in forests, caves and charnel grounds, living on alms and practising meditation, facing fear and hardship.
