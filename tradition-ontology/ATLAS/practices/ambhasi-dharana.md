# Āmbhasī dhāraṇā (water-concentration)

`prc:ambhasi-dharana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)

The breath held with the mind in the water-principle, white as conch, moon or jasmine, with the nectar-seed va and Viṣṇu (GS 3.72); in the throat, 'liquefying' (GŚ 70, 74). Durations are not reproduced.
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** ['removes unbearable heat and sin; one does not die in deep water (GS 3.72-73)']
