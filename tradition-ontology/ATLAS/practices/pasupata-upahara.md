# The six-limbed offering (upahāra)

`prc:pasupata-upahara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

Kneeling south of the image after the ash-bath, the practitioner mutters, then laughs loudly again and again, sings and dances, makes the bull-like ḍuṇḍuṅ sound, prostrates and mutters, then reports the offering to the Lord, circumambulates and takes a closing bath.
**Stage:** vyakta (first) stage
**Sources:**

## The texts' own warnings
- Where worldly people are present, all this is to be done secretly. — [Sarvadarśanasaṃgraha](../texts/sarvadarsanasangraha.md) ch. 6

_Notes: The Ratnaṭīkā classes muttering and prostration as mental, dance as bodily, and laughter, song and the ḍuṇḍuṅ sound as vocal._
