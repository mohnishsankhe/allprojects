# The seven subtle dhāraṇās (MkP 40)

`prc:sapta-suksma-dharana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

Holding the mind at the head on earth, water, fire, air, space, mind and intellect in turn, experiencing and then abandoning the subtlety of each, without attachment to any.
