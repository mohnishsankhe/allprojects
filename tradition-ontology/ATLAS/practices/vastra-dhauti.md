# Vastra-dhauti (washing with a cloth; vāso-dhauti)

`prc:vastra-dhauti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** cleansing
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

A moistened strip of fine cloth is slowly swallowed as the guru teaches and then drawn out again (HYP 2.24; GS 1.40). The measurements the texts give are not reproduced.
**Stage:** beginner
**Signs of progress:** ['cough, asthma, spleen disorders, skin disease and the twenty diseases of phlegm vanish (HYP 2.25)', 'gulma, fever, spleen disorders, skin disease, phlegm and bile destroyed; health, strength and nourishment increase (GS 1.41)']
