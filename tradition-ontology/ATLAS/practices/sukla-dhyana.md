# Śukla-dhyāna (pure meditation)

`prc:sukla-dhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
**Taught in:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)

Four stages: scripture-based meditation shifting among aspects, words and activities; fixed on one aspect without shifting (which yields omniscience); the omniscient's meditation in which only subtle activity remains; and the stopping of all activity before liberation.
**Stage:** guṇasthānas 8–14
**Prerequisites:** ['best bodily frame', 'knowledge of the Pūrvas (first two stages)']
