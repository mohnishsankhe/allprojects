# Agnyādheya (establishing the sacred fires)

`prc:agnyadheya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)

The rite by which a householder sets up his three Śrauta fires; the Brāhmaṇas fix its times by season and lunar mansion (the Kṛttikās, ŚB 2.1.2.3).
