# Entering the mind-less state (unmanī-bhāva)

`prc:unmani-bhava` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Taught in:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md)

Thinking of no element, no above or below, being like wood, making the mind 'void of void', free of thought and motionless, until the jīva is dissolved and not roused by any sound.
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** insensibility to outer sound; freedom from decay (KJN 14.85–86)
