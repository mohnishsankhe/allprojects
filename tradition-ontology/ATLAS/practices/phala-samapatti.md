# Attainment of fruition (phala-samāpatti)

`prc:phala-samapatti` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

A noble person enters the fruition of his attainment, with nibbāna as object, resolving on its duration, for a pleasant abiding here and now.
