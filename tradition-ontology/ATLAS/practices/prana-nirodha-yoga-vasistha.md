# Stilling mind through the breath (Yoga Vāsiṣṭha)

`prc:prana-nirodha-yoga-vasistha` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)

Because the vibration of prāṇa is one seed of the mind, yogins calm the mind by restraining the breath through prāṇāyāma, meditation and skilful methods; the other seed, vāsanā, must also be given up.
**Stage:** intermediate
**Sources:** 
  - [Mokṣopāya](../texts/moksopaya.md) — ref: 5.92.26; rests_on: ["tea:moksopaya:5.92.6-27"]

## The texts' own warnings
