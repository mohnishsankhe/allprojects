# Service of the Lord's form (puṣṭi sevā)

`prc:pusti-seva` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md)
**Taught in:** [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md)

Loving, householder service of Kṛṣṇa's image (svarūpa) as a living child and lord — waking, feeding, dressing, playing, resting him — with body, wealth and above all the mind; the fruit of service is more service.
