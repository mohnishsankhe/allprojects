# Meditation (dhyāna) as the means of abandoning the afflictions' activities

`prc:dhyana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

The gross activities of the afflictions, thinned by kriyā-yoga, are abandoned by meditation as discerning insight (prasaṃkhyāna), until they are subtle, like burnt seeds (YS 2.11, YBh 2.11).
