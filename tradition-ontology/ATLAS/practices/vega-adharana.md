# Not suppressing the natural urges

`prc:vega-adharana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Do not hold back the arisen urges of urine, faeces, semen, flatus, vomiting, sneezing, belching, yawning, hunger, thirst, tears, sleep and breath after exertion; nor force them.
**Stage:** all
