# The neither-one-nor-many reasoning

`prc:neither-one-nor-many-reasoning` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md)

Examining whether a thing — a partless atom, a moment of consciousness, a whole — has a truly single nature or a truly manifold nature, and finding neither, one understands that it lacks own-nature, like a reflection.
