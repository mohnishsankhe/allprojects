# Breath-control with Oṃ, the utterances and the Gāyatrī

`prc:vedic-pranayama` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)

Breath-control performed while mentally reciting the Gāyatrī with its 'head', preceded by the utterances and with Oṃ, as part of the twilight worship and as the renouncer's austerity; it burns away the faults of the senses (MDh 6.70–72; YS 1.23; VDh 10.5).
