# Practice on a single principle (ekatattva-abhyāsa)

`prc:ekatattva-abhyasa` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

To remove the distractions, the mind is practised with a single principle as its support (YS 1.32, YBh 1.32). Vyāsa argues that the mind is one and has many objects and abides, not merely momentary cognitions each fixed to one object.
