# Meditation (nididhyāsana / dhyāna)

**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Sustained meditation on the Lord. In Dvaita: meditation on Viṣṇu with the qualities suited to one's fitness, within one's heart as the original (bimba) of one's self; it is an independent means (not a subsidiary of hearing) and yields the direct realization that pleases the Lord.
**Stage:** after hearing and reflection
**Prerequisites:** ['śravaṇa', 'manana', 'bhakti']
**Signs of progress:** ["direct realization (aparokṣa) of the Lord, within the scope one's fitness allows", 'contrary notions (viparīta-bhāvanā) weaken', 'savikalpa then nirvikalpa samādhi']
