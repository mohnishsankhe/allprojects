# The eight antidotal formations

`prc:eight-antidotal-formations` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
**Taught in:** [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)

Against laziness: desire-to-act, effort, faith and pliancy; against forgetting the instruction: mindfulness; against sinking and agitation: awareness that notices them; against non-application: the intention that applies the remedy; against over-application: equanimity that lets the mind flow when they have ceased.
