# Self-inquiry (ātma-vicāra)

`prc:atma-vicara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Inquiring 'Who am I? How did this world arise? Who is its maker? What is its material?' and discriminating the self from all that is seen; inquiry, not action, attains the Real.
