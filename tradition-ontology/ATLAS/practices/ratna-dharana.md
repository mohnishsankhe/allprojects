# Wearing the planets' gems (ratna-dhāraṇa)

`prc:ratna-dharana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)

Wearing, usually set in a ring or amulet touching the skin, the gem of a planet to be strengthened — ruby for the Sun, pearl for the Moon, coral for Mars, emerald for Mercury, yellow sapphire for Jupiter, diamond for Venus, blue sapphire for Saturn, hessonite for Rāhu, cat's-eye for Ketu — chosen by the chart and worn at a chosen time after consecration.
**Stage:** all
**Sources:** 
  - [Bṛhat Saṃhitā](../texts/brhat-samhita.md) — ref: 79.1; rests_on: ["tea:brhat-samhita:79.1"]
  - [Phaladīpikā](../texts/phaladipika.md) — ref: ch. 2 (gem list; from memory)

## The texts' own warnings
- A good gem brings good to kings and a bad one harm; the fate that depends on gems must be examined by experts. — [Bṛhat Saṃhitā](../texts/brhat-samhita.md) 79.1
- Rites alone — mantra, consecration, amulets, pacification, oblation, fasting, worship, recitation — do not bring success, or the king's priest would be king. — [Yogayātrā](../texts/yogayatra.md) 1.6

_Notes: The planet–gem assignment is from later texts; Varāhamihira's gem chapters judge gems by their own qualities._
