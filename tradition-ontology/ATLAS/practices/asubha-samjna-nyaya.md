# Cultivating the notion of the unlovely (aśubhasaṃjñā)

`prc:asubha-samjna-nyaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
**Taught in:** [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)

Against the passion-producing notion of a body as a beautiful whole ('woman', 'man', with its attractive details), one cultivates the notion of its parts — hair, body-hair, flesh, blood, bone, sinew, veins, phlegm, bile, excrement and so on; as this is cultivated, lustful passion is abandoned.
