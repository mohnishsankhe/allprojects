# The fourfold accomplishment (ārādhanā) at life's end

**Category:** sleep-dream-death

**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Yāpanīya](../lineages/yapaniya.md)

Under an experienced guide (niryāpaka), the practitioner perfects faith, knowledge, conduct and austerity as death approaches, with confession, forgiveness, renunciation and meditation; it culminates in sallekhanā where the conditions hold (restricted: summary only).
