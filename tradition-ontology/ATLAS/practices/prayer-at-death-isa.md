# The prayer of the dying (Īśa 15-18)

`prc:prayer-at-death-isa` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

At death one asks Pūṣan to uncover the face of truth covered by the golden vessel, affirms 'that person yonder, I am he', commits breath to the wind and body to ashes, bids the mind remember what was done, and asks Agni to lead by the good path (Īśa 15-18; BAU 5.15).
