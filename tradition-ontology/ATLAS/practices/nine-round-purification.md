# The nine-round purification breath (rlung ro dgu bsal)

`prc:nine-round-purification` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md), [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)
**Taught in:** [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md), [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)

Expelling stale air in three sets of three exhalations — through one nostril, the other and both — while visualizing the three poisons (desire, anger, ignorance) leaving through the side channels and central channel; done at the start of sessions to calm the mind.
**Stage:** beginner
