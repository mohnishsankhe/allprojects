# Placing the letters on the body (mātṛkā-nyāsa)

The fifty letters from a to kṣa, the manifestations of śabda-brahman, are installed by touch and visualization on the parts of the body (outer nyāsa) and on the petals of the inner centres (inner nyāsa), with or without the nasal bindu, so that the body becomes made of mantra before japa and worship.
