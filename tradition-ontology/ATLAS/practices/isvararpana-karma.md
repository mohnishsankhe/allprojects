# Performing dharma as an offering to the Lord (īśvarārpaṇa)

`prc:isvararpana-karma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
**Taught in:** [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md)

Doing the enjoined acts with the thought of offering them to Śrī Govinda (Āpadeva) or Īśvara (Laugākṣi Bhāskara), which makes dharma a cause of the highest good; the Gītā's 'whatever you do, do it as an offering to me' is cited as authority.
