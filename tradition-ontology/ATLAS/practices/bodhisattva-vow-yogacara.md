# Taking and keeping the bodhisattva vow (Yogācāra rite)

`prc:bodhisattva-vow-yogacara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)

The vow of the threefold discipline is taken before a teacher who holds it and is learned and capable (or, lacking one, before the buddhas); the four offences equivalent to defeat and the lesser faults are avoided; a lost vow may be retaken; lesser faults are confessed.
