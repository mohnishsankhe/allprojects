# Sending and taking (gtong len)

**Convergence:** 5 independent lineage(s): [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md), [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md), [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md), [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)

Visualising the suffering of others (beginning with one's own future suffering) and its causes, one takes it in on the in-breath, dissolving it into one's own self-cherishing; on the out-breath one sends one's body, happiness and virtue to them, imagining them satisfied; applied to the three objects and three poisons, and used when suffering or witnessing suffering.
**Stage:** intermediate (after love and compassion are developed)
**Prerequisites:** ['equalizing self and other', 'love and compassion']
