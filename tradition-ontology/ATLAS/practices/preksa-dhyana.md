# Prekṣā-dhyāna (the Terāpanthī meditation system)

`prc:preksa-dhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence low · _recent (post-1800)_

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Śvetāmbara Terāpanth](../lineages/terapanthi.md)

A twentieth-century system presented as a revival of Jain meditation: body-abandonment (kāyotsarga) as relaxation, perception of the breath and of the body, perception of 'psychic centres' (caitanya-kendra), meditation on the colours of the leśyās, and the reflections (anuprekṣā), aimed at self-perception ('see the self by the self').
**Stage:** all
