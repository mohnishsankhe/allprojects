# Kāyaka — work as worship

`prc:kayaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)
**Taught in:** [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md), [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)

Each devotee earns a living by honest work in his or her own occupation, done as worship, not interrupted even for worship, and not gathering beyond the day's need.
