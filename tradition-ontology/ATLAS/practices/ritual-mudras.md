# Ritual hand-gestures (hasta-mudrā) in worship

**Taught in:** [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md), [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)

Gestures of the hands made at fixed points of worship, mantra-repetition and meditation, which 'give joy' (mudaṃ rāti) to the deities and make them present; the fingers are held to be the five elements (little finger earth … thumb space) whose joinings produce the gestures. The Śāradātilaka gives the invocation series (āvāhanī and the rest), avaguṇṭhana, dhenu and mahāmudrā (ŚT 23.106-114) and its commentary many more (aṅkuśa, kumbha, śaṅkha, garuḍa, jñāna, weapon-gestures of the deities).
