# Muttering the five brahma-mantras

`prc:pancabrahma-japa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

Muttering of the Sadyojāta, Vāmadeva, Aghora, Tatpuruṣa and Īśāna mantras (the five purifiers) during the ash-bath, the offering and circumambulation; muttering the five alone is also a cause of merit.
