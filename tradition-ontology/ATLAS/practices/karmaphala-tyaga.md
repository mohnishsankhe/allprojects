# Relinquishing the fruit of all actions

Giving up the fruit of all actions, self-controlled and taking refuge in the Lord's yoga — the step for one unable even to work for the Lord (12.11), praised above meditation because peace follows immediately (12.12), and what the wise call tyāga (18.2, 18.11).
