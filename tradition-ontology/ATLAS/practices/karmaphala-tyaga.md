# Relinquishing the fruit of all actions

`prc:karmaphala-tyaga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Taught in:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Do sacrifice, giving, austerity and allotted action because they ought to be done, giving up attachment and fruit (18.6, 18.9); since the embodied cannot give up action entirely, relinquishing the fruit of action makes one a relinquisher (18.2, 18.11).
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** ['The relinquisher, pervaded by sattva, intelligent, doubts cut, neither hates disagreeable action nor clings to agreeable action (18.10).', 'Peace immediately (tyāgāc chāntir anantaram, 12.12).']

## The texts' own warnings
- Abandoning allotted action out of delusion is tāmasic relinquishment. — [Bhagavad Gītā](../texts/bhagavad-gita.md) 18.7
- Abandoning action because it is painful, from fear of bodily hardship, is rājasic and gains no fruit of relinquishment. — [Bhagavad Gītā](../texts/bhagavad-gita.md) 18.8
