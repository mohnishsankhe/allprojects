# Bheda-vijñāna (discriminating self from non-self)

`prc:bhedavijnana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Adhyātma movement (Adhyātma-mata)](../lineages/adhyatma-jain.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
**Taught in:** [Adhyātma movement (Adhyātma-mata)](../lineages/adhyatma-jain.md), [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)

Repeatedly discerning that the knowing self is other than body, karma, passions and thoughts — 'I am one, pure, made of perception and knowledge; nothing else, not even an atom, is mine' — and resting in self-experience; the core practice of the Adhyātma path.
**Stage:** from right view onward
**Signs of progress:** ['self-experience (svānubhūti)', 'calm of the passions']
