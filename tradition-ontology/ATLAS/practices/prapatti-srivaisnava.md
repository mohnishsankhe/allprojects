# Surrender (prapatti / śaraṇāgati) in the Śrīvaiṣṇava tradition

`prc:prapatti-srivaisnava` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Teṅkalai (southern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/tenkalai.md), [Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)](../lineages/vadakalai.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

Taking refuge, once and for all, at the feet of Nārāyaṇa with Śrī as the sole means, through a teacher, typically by reciting the dvaya: with resolve to please, avoidance of displeasing, great faith, choosing him as protector, helplessness, and laying down the burden of one's protection; thereafter living in service, free from anxiety about salvation. The Vaṭakalai treat it as a means performed; the Teṅkalai as acceptance of the Lord as the means.
**Stage:** open to all, of any class or gender
**Prerequisites:** ['ākiñcanya and ananyagatitva', 'initiation by a teacher (pañcasaṃskāra)', 'knowledge of the artha-pañcaka']
**Duration:** performed once
**Signs of progress:** ["freedom from anxiety (the Lord's 'do not grieve')", 'desire for service only']

## The texts' own warnings
- To regard one's own surrender as the means is a fault (Teṅkalai). — [Śrīvacanabhūṣaṇam](../texts/srivacana-bhusanam.md) 2
- Surrender must not be repeated as a means; repetition is allowed only as expiation (Vaṭakalai). — [Rahasyatrayasāra](../texts/rahasyatrayasara.md) Aparādhaparihāra
