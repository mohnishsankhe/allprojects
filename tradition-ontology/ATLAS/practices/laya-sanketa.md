# The 'signals' of laya-yoga (saṅketa)

`prc:laya-sanketa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

Laya-yoga is the dissolution of the mind brought about by any of the 'signals' Ādinātha taught — eight crore of them: meditating on the void while walking, standing, sleeping and eating; gazing at the nose-tip; meditating on the back of the head (said to conquer death); gazing between the brows; attending to the forehead; to the big toes; lying like a corpse, relaxed, in a solitary place (DYŚ 14-24). The Haṭhapradīpikā similarly speaks of a crore and a quarter ways of laya, nāda being chief (HYP 4.66).
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** ['dissolution of the mind (cittalaya) (DYŚ 14, 24)']
