# Dhammakāya meditation (Wat Paknam method)

`prc:dhammakaya-meditation` · `skeleton` · confidence low · _recent (post-1800)_

**Category:** visualization-deity
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Dhammakāya meditation tradition (Vijjā Dhammakāya; Wat Paknam)](../lineages/dhammakaya.md)

The mind is brought down through a series of bases (nostril, eye-corner, centre of the head, palate, throat, navel level) to the seventh base at the centre of the body, while visualizing a clear crystal sphere and repeating 'sammā arahaṃ', until a bright sphere and then successive inner bodies appear.
