# Sāman chanting (sāmagāna)

`prc:sama-gana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Jaiminīya (Talavakāra) śākhā (Sāmaveda)](../lineages/sakha-jaiminiya.md), [Kauthuma śākhā (Sāmaveda)](../lineages/sakha-kauthuma.md), [Rāṇāyanīya śākhā (Sāmaveda)](../lineages/sakha-ranayaniya.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)

Singing the verses of the Sāmaveda to their melodies as given in the song-books — village and forest songs and their ritual applications — with stretched vowels, repetitions and stobhas, the notes shown on the fingers; at the soma rite the udgātṛ, prastotṛ and pratihartṛ divide the parts.

## The texts' own warnings
- A mantra wrong in accent or letter does not convey its meaning; as a verbal thunderbolt it harms the sacrificer, as 'Indra-śatru' did through a fault of accent. — [Pāṇinīya Śikṣā](../texts/paniniya-siksa.md) 52
- The forest (āraṇyageya) melodies are regarded as potent and are learned apart, outside the village (as reported). — [Āraṇyageya-gāna (Araṇyegāna)](../texts/aranyageya-gana.md) (tradition)
