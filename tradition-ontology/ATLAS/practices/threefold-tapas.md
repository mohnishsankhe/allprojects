# Austerity of body, speech and mind

**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Taught in:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Bodily austerity: worship of gods, the twice-born, teachers and the wise; purity, uprightness, celibacy, non-harm. Verbal: speech that does not agitate, true, pleasant and beneficial, and recitation of scripture. Mental: serenity, gentleness, silence, self-restraint, purity of being — practised with faith and without desire for fruit (17.14–17).

## The texts' own warnings
- Terrible austerities not enjoined by scripture, done with hypocrisy and egoism, tormenting the elements of the body and the Lord within, are demonic. — [Bhagavad Gītā](../texts/bhagavad-gita.md) 17.5–6
- Austerity done with foolish obstinacy, by torturing oneself or to harm another, is tāmasic. — [Bhagavad Gītā](../texts/bhagavad-gita.md) 17.19
