# Recitation of 'buddho'

`prc:buddho-recitation` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Thai Forest tradition (Kammaṭṭhāna forest tradition)](../lineages/thai-forest.md)

Silent repetition of 'buddho' — alone or 'bud-' with the in-breath and '-dho' with the out-breath — until the mind settles; then the word is dropped and the breath or body is contemplated.
**Stage:** beginner
