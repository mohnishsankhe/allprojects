# Meditation with and without qualities (Gorakṣaśataka)

**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)

With the gaze at the nose-tip, meditating on the centres in their colours (with qualities) and on the attributeless Śiva in the sky and the all-pervading self (without qualities); adjuncts are removed by practice.
