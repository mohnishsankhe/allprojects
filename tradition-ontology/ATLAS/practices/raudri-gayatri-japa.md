# Expiatory and yogic recitation of the Raudrī Gāyatrī

`prc:raudri-gayatri-japa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

After seeing urine or faeces or speaking with women or śūdras, he touches water, performs breath-control and mutters the Raudrī Gāyatrī or the Bahurūpī; in the third stage recitation of the chosen Gāyatrī is the ground from which yoga proceeds.
