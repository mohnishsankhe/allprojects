# Kālī-pūjā

`prc:kali-puja` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md), [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md)
**Taught in:** [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md), [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md)

The worship of Dakṣiṇakālī on the new-moon night of Kārttika (Dīpānvitā) in Bengal, in homes and public pandals and at temples such as Dakshineswar and Kalighat, with the Tantrasāra's meditation and mantras; also weekly and monthly worship.
**Sources:** 
  - [Tantrasāra (Bṛhattantrasāra) of Kṛṣṇānanda Āgamavāgīśa](../texts/tantrasara-krsnananda.md) — ref: 2 (Śyāmā section); rests_on: ["tea:tantrasara-krsnananda:2.syama-dhyana"]

_Notes: The spread of public Kālī-pūjā is traditionally credited to Rājā Kṛṣṇacandra of Nadia (18th c.) — tradition's account, not checked._
