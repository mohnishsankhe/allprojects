# Defining the four elements (catudhātuvavatthāna)

`prc:catudhatuvavatthana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md), [Pa-Auk tradition (Pa-Auk Tawya)](../lineages/pa-auk.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Discerning the body as earth, water, fire and air — briefly for the quick-witted, or by forty-two aspects; the perception of a being disappears; reaches access; in the Pa-Auk tradition by twelve characteristics, leading to the sight of material groups.
