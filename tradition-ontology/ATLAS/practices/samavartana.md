# Samāvartana (return home, final bath)

`prc:samavartana` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)

The ceremonial bath (snāna) ending studentship, after which the graduate (snātaka) may marry; he keeps the snātaka rules (MDh 4).
