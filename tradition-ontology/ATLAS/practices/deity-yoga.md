# Deity yoga (generation-stage sādhana)

After refuge, bodhicitta and the protection circle, the practitioner dissolves ordinary appearance into emptiness, generates the maṇḍala and himself as the deity from seed syllables on sun and moon discs, invites the gnosis beings to merge with the visualized pledge beings, receives consecration and the seal of the family lord, maintains the deity's clarity and pride while reciting mantra, and finally dissolves and re-arises for activity.
**Stage:** intermediate
