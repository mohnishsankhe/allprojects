# Vedic recitation (vedapāṭha, svādhyāya of the Saṃhitā)

**Taught in:** [Jaiminīya (Talavakāra) śākhā (Sāmaveda)](../lineages/sakha-jaiminiya.md), [Kāṇva śākhā (White Yajurveda)](../lineages/sakha-kanva.md), [Kauthuma śākhā (Sāmaveda)](../lineages/sakha-kauthuma.md), [Mādhyandina śākhā (White Yajurveda)](../lineages/sakha-madhyandina.md), [Paippalāda śākhā (Atharvaveda)](../lineages/sakha-paippalada.md), [Śākala śākhā (Ṛgveda)](../lineages/sakha-sakala.md), [Śaunaka śākhā (Atharvaveda)](../lineages/sakha-saunaka.md), [Taittirīya śākhā (Black Yajurveda)](../lineages/sakha-taittiriya.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)

Learning and reciting one's śākhā's Saṃhitā orally from the teacher's mouth, phrase by phrase, with exact accents marked by hand movements, first in the continuous form, then word by word and in the interwoven recitations; daily recitation of the Veda is itself counted a sacrifice (brahmayajña). The Soma book says whoever studies the purifying verses eats purified food (RV 9.67.31).
**Stage:** beginner (entered after initiation) and lifelong
**Prerequisites:** ['upanayana (initiation) by a teacher of the śākhā']
**Duration:** years of daily study; the complete Saṃhitā with its modified recitations traditionally takes a decade or more (as reported; low confidence)
**Signs of progress:** ['mastery of the Saṃhitā, then pada, krama and the vikṛtis', 'the six virtues of a reciter (sweetness, distinctness, word-division, accent, composure, tempo)']

## The texts' own warnings
- A mantra wrong in accent or letter does not convey its meaning; as a verbal thunderbolt it harms the sacrificer, as 'Indra-śatru' did through a fault of accent. — [Pāṇinīya Śikṣā](../texts/paniniya-siksa.md) 52
- One who recites the Veda without understanding its meaning is a post bearing a load; what is repeated without understanding never blazes, like dry fuel without fire. — [Nirukta](../texts/nirukta.md) 1.18
- Studying without the hand-gestures, lacking accent and letters, one is 'burned' by the Ṛg, Yajus and Sāman. — [Pāṇinīya Śikṣā](../texts/paniniya-siksa.md) 54
