# Initiation into the observances (samaya-dīkṣā)

`prc:samaya-diksa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)

First Śaiva initiation making the disciple a samayin, bound by post-initiatory rules and entitled to worship and study; for the incapable, the observances may be purified away (seedless initiation).
