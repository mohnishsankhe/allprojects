# Yoni mudrā (the womb seal)

`prc:yoni-mudra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** lock-seal
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

In the Gheraṇḍa: seated in siddhāsana, the ears, eyes, nostrils and mouth are closed with the fingers, prāṇa is drawn in by kākī and joined with apāna, the six centres are contemplated in order with the mantra 'huṃ haṃsa', the sleeping serpent-goddess is roused and led with the jīva to the supreme lotus, and one contemplates union with Śiva — 'I am Brahman' (GS 3.37-42); it is the means to laya-samādhi (GS 7.12-13). In the Śivasaṃhitā it is the contraction of the yoni between anus and genitals with meditation on the flame rising from the brahma-yoni (ŚS 4.1-11), perfected by mūlabandha (ŚS 4.42). The Haṭhapradīpikā mentions it as holding bindu (HYP 3.43).
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** ['one established in it even once is in samādhi (GS 3.42)', 'makes defective mantras effective (ŚS 4.6)']

## The texts' own warnings
- Yoni mudrā does not succeed without first practising śakticālana. — [Gheraṇḍa Saṃhitā](../texts/gheranda-samhita.md) 3.57
- It is most secret; it is not to be given to anyone, not even with one's life in one's throat. — [Śiva Saṃhitā](../texts/siva-samhita.md) 4.11

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [Ṣaṇmukhī mudrā (closing the six openings)](sanmukhi-mudra.md) — The Gheraṇḍa's yoni mudrā includes the closing of the six openings that is elsewhere the whole of ṣaṇmukhī; the Śivasaṃhitā's yoni mudrā is a perineal contraction.

_Notes: The texts' claims that it removes even the great sins are arthavāda in their own manner._
