# Śavāsana (the corpse; mṛtāsana)

`prc:savasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** posture
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

Lying supine on the ground like a corpse (HYP 1.32; GS 2.19); the Dattātreyayogaśāstra counts lying like a corpse, relaxed in solitude, among the 'signals' of laya-yoga (DYŚ 22-23).
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** ['removes fatigue and brings rest to the mind (HYP 1.32; GS 2.19)']
