# Bhuśuṇḍa's contemplation of the breath

`prc:prana-cinta-bhusunda` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)

Continuous contemplation of the natural movement of prāṇa and apāna from and to the heart, attending to the points where each subsides and resting in the Self in the pauses between them.
**Stage:** advanced
