# Meditation on the centres (Kaula)

`prc:kjn-cakra-dhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)
**Taught in:** [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md)

Fixing the mind on a centre — the root, the brahma-knot, the heart, the throat, the palate, the forehead, the brahmarandhra — or on a lamp-flame in the sixty-four-petalled lotus, each with its own signs and powers.
**Signs of progress:** trembling, shaking, speech in tongues, spontaneous seals, frog-hopping, levitation, knowledge of past and future (KJN 14.16–19)
