# Cultivating the opposite (pratipakṣa-bhāvanā)

`prc:pratipaksa-bhavana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

When harmful thoughts arise, reflect on their opposites: that violence and the rest, done, caused or approved, from greed, anger or delusion, mild to intense, bear endless suffering and ignorance; that having taken refuge in yoga by granting fearlessness to all beings, to take such thoughts up again is to be like a dog eating its vomit (2.33–34, YBh 2.33–34).
