# Cultivating the opposite (pratipakṣa-bhāvanā)

`prc:pratipaksa-bhavana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

When harmful thoughts oppress, one cultivates their opposites (YS 2.33): Vyāsa gives the thought 'I took refuge in the dharma of yoga by giving safety to all beings; taking the harmful thoughts up again I am like a dog licking its vomit' (YBh 2.33), and contemplates the unwanted ripening that follows harmful thoughts — unending suffering and ignorance (YS 2.34, YBh 2.34). Also the way the afflictions are thinned (YBh 2.4).
