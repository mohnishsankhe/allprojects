# Mahāvedha (the great piercing)

`prc:mahavedha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** lock-seal
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Seated in mahābandha with the breath filled and held by the throat seal, the hands placed evenly on the ground, the buttocks are gently struck on the ground so that the breath leaves the two side channels and throbs in the middle (HYP 3.26-28; ŚS 4.23; DYŚ 124, where the vulgate calls it mahābandha); in the Gheraṇḍa, a retention with uḍḍīyāna made in mahābandha (GS 3.22). Without it mahāmudrā and mahābandha are fruitless (HYP 3.25; GS 3.21; ŚS 4.27).
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** ['a death-like state; the union of moon, sun and fire (HYP 3.28)', 'removes wrinkles, grey hair and trembling; gives powers such as aṇimā (HYP 3.29-30)', 'the brahma-knot is pierced; the gods in the centres tremble; kuṇḍalinī dissolves in Kailāsa (ŚS 4.24-26)']
