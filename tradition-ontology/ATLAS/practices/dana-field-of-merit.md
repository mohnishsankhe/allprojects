# Giving and the field of merit

`prc:dana-field-of-merit` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Dharmaguptaka](../lineages/dharmaguptaka.md), [Mahīśāsaka](../lineages/mahisasaka.md), [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaguptaka](../lineages/dharmaguptaka.md), [Mahīśāsaka](../lineages/mahisasaka.md), [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md)

Giving whose merit depends on the giver (faith etc.), the thing and the field (destiny, suffering, benefit, qualities); the highest gift is from one liberated to one liberated, or the bodhisattva's gift. The schools differ on whether gifts to the Buddha or to the Saṅgha yield more fruit.
