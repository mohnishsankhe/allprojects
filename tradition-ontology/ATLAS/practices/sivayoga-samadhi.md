# Śivayoga-samādhi

`prc:sivayoga-samadhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)
**Taught in:** [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md), [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)

Through inner worship, meditation on Śiva as one's self: the supreme self is contemplated in the haṃsa form with the feeling 'I am He' until attention is undivided; the six centres are contemplated from the base to the brows and Śambhu in the aperture called Kailāsa within the thousand-petalled lotus.
