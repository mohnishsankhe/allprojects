# Drinking the breath (vāyu-pāna)

`prc:vayu-pana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)

A group of practices in the Śivasaṃhitā for removing afflictions: drinking the breath with the tongue at the root of the palate; drinking cool air through the crow's-beak mouth; drinking with the tongue pressed at the cavity of the front teeth while contemplating kuṇḍalinī; drinking with the teeth pressed together and the tongue turned up (ŚS 3.68-83); the Gorakṣaśataka similarly has the yogin press the tongue-tip at the cavity of the front teeth and contemplate the goddess of nectar (GŚ 63-64).
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** ['destroys fatigue, burning, old age and disease; claims of conquering death, becoming a poet, becoming like Bhairava with the powers (ŚS 3.71-78; GŚ 64)']
**Sources:** 
  - [Śiva Saṃhitā](../texts/siva-samhita.md) — ref: 3.68-83; rests_on: ["tea:siva-samhita:3.68-83"]
  - [Gorakṣaśataka](../texts/goraksasataka.md) — ref: 63-65

_Notes: Where these practices merge into khecarī (the tongue turned up into the cavity) the restrictions of prc:khecari-mudra apply._
