# Resigning all actions to the Lord (mayi sarvāṇi karmāṇi saṃnyasya)

`prc:isvararpana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md), [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md), [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Renouncing all actions in Kṛṣṇa, with a mind directed to the self, free of expectation and of 'mine', and with the fever gone, act (fight) (3.30); those who follow this teaching constantly, with faith and without carping, are freed from actions (3.31).
**Stage:** all
**Prerequisites:** Faith (śraddhā) and freedom from carping (3.31).
**Signs of progress:** ['release from actions (3.31)', 'release from the bonds of action with auspicious and inauspicious fruits (9.28)', 'Deliverance before long by the Lord from the ocean of death and saṃsāra (12.7).']
