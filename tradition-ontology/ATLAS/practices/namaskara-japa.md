# Recitation of the Namaskāra mantra

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Sthānakavāsī](../lineages/sthanakavasi.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Śvetāmbara Terāpanth](../lineages/terapanthi.md)

Reciting the five-line homage (with the cūlikā among Śvetāmbaras) aloud, whispered or mentally, often on a rosary of 108 beads (the 108 qualities of the five supreme beings), at waking, before meals and journeys and at death; meditating on its shorter forms (a-si-ā-u-sā, arhaṃ, oṃ) by the teacher's instruction; the preparatory austerities (upadhāna) for learning it are prescribed in Śvetāmbara texts.
