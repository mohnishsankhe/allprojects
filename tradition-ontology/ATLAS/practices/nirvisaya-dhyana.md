# Meditation as objectless mind (dhyāna)

`prc:nirvisaya-dhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Taught in:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)

Meditation is the removal of passion, accomplished through the cessation of the mind's modifications; meditation is mind without object; supported by concentration, posture, one's own duty, dispassion and practice, turning away from torpor and distraction, in any place where the mind is serene.
**Stage:** intermediate
**Prerequisites:** dispassion, practice, one's own duty (SS 3.32–36)
**Signs of progress:** coloring of puruṣa calmed, puruṣa abiding in itself like a crystal (SS 2.34–35)
