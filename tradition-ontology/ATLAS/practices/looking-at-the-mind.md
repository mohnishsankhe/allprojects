# Looking at the mind (Mahāmudrā vipaśyanā)

`prc:looking-at-the-mind` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md)
**Taught in:** [Drukpa Kagyu](../lineages/drukpa-kagyu.md), [Kagyu (the Kagyu transmissions: Marpa / Dakpo Kagyu, with the Shangpa grouped by name)](../lineages/kagyu.md), [Karma Kagyu (Kamtsang Kagyu)](../lineages/karma-kagyu.md)

In stillness and in movement the meditator looks for the mind: its colour, shape, location, where it arises, abides and goes, whether the still and moving mind are one or different; not finding anything, recognizes the unfindable, luminous nature.
