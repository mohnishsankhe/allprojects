# Bāul initiation and instruction (dīkṣā and śikṣā)

`prc:baul-diksa-siksa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md)
**Taught in:** [Bāul](../lineages/baul.md)

A first initiation (dīkṣā) by a guru with a mantra, and later instruction (śikṣā), often from a second guru, into the body practice; the teaching is given individually and kept secret.

## The texts' own warnings
- The practice is not to be learned from books or songs without a guru. — [Bāul gān (the corpus of Bāul songs of other masters)](../texts/baul-gan.md)
