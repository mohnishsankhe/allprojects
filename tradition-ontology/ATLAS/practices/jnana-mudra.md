# Jñāna mudrā / cin-mudrā (the gesture of knowledge)

`prc:jnana-mudra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md), [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md)
**Taught in:** [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md), [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md)

The tips of thumb and index finger joined and the other fingers extended, turned to the side — the gesture of knowledge held by teaching deities such as Sarasvatī and Dakṣiṇāmūrti; the commentary distinguishes a related 'exposition gesture' (vyākhyāna-mudrā) (Śāradātilaka, commentary on ch. 6 and ch. 7).
