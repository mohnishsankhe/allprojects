# Antyeṣṭi (funeral rites)

`prc:antyesti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)

The dying āhitāgni goes out with his fires; the body is washed, carried to the cremation ground and burned (an āhitāgni with his sacred fires and ritual implements placed on his limbs), with the Yama hymn and funeral mantras; infants under two are buried; the relatives bathe and offer water, observe ten days' impurity, collect the bones, and later a funeral mound may be built (ĀśGS 4.1–6; YS 3.1–2; MDh 5.59–68; ŚB 12.5.2; 13.8; TA 6).
