# Jyotiṣṭoma (the model soma sacrifice)

`prc:jyotistoma` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
**Taught in:** [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)

The archetypal soma rite ('one desiring heaven should sacrifice with the Jyotiṣṭoma'), whose basic form is the Agniṣṭoma; Śabara names it as the paradigm of what leads to the highest good.
**Stage:** householder with the sacred fires
