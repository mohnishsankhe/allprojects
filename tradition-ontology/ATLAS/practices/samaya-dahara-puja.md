# Samaya worship in the heart-space (dahara)

`prc:samaya-dahara-puja` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** visualization-deity
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Taught in:** [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md), [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)

The Samayin imagines the Śrīcakra in the small space of the heart (or identifies it with the moon-disc in the crown lotus) and worships the Goddess there without any external support; he also worships the Śiva-Śakti pair in each cakra, Samayā with Samaya in the base and the thousand-petalled lotus (SL 36-41 with Lakṣmīdhara).
**Stage:** advanced
