# Karma-yoga: performing one's duty without attachment to the fruit

`prc:karma-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Taught in:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Perform the prescribed action (one's own dharma) as duty, with evenness in success and failure, abandoning attachment and the desire for the fruit; action so done 'for the sake of sacrifice' does not bind and purifies the self.
**Stage:** all; for the one wishing to ascend to yoga action is the means (6.3)
**Prerequisites:** knowledge of one's own dharma (3.35)
**Signs of progress:** evenness (samatva) in success and failure; freedom from the sense of doership (5.8–9)
