# The five purificatory therapies (pañcakarma)

`prc:pancakarma` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** cleansing
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

After oleation and sudation, the doṣas are expelled by emesis (kapha), purgation (pitta), decoction and oil enemas (vāta) and nasal evacuation (head); then a graded diet. Summary only.
**Stage:** all
**Prerequisites:** examination of the patient's strength (Ca Vi 8.94)
**Sources:**

## The texts' own warnings
- A strong medicine given without examination to a weak patient can kill him; a weak one to a strong patient is useless. — [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) Vi 8.94
- Strong purgative, cauterizing and surgical measures cannot be borne by the weak and may take life at once. — [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) Vi 8.94
