# Dīkṣā (consecration for sacrifice)

`prc:diksa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 5 independent lineage(s): [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md), [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)

Before a Soma sacrifice the sacrificer is consecrated in a hut with vows of fasting and restraint; the priests make him an embryo again (AB 1.3); he is born anew at the final bath.
**Stage:** entry (initiation)
**Prerequisites:** the descent of power (śaktipāta), shown by devotion (Kiraṇa 5); a qualified guru
