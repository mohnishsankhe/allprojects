# Dīvālī as Mahāvīra's nirvāṇa

`prc:divali-jain` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)

Jains keep Dīvālī as the night of Mahāvīra's nirvāṇa at Pāvā, when the eighteen kings lit lamps 'since the light of knowledge is gone'; the next morning Gautama's omniscience is celebrated and the Jain new year (Vīra era) begins.
