# Going forth into homelessness (pabbajjā)

Leaving household and family, shaving, taking the mendicant's life of alms and wandering under a teacher who leads an order; common to all the śramaṇa orders.

**Signs of progress:** ['honour from the king himself, even for one who was a slave (DN 2)']
