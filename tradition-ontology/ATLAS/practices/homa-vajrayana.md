# Tantric fire offering (homa)

`prc:homa-vajrayana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Taught in:** [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)

Offerings into the fire for the four activities (pacifying, increasing, subjugating, destroying) as part of sādhana and consecration rites (Vajrāvalī); the siddhas deride it when done without knowledge.
**Stage:** intermediate
**Sources:**
