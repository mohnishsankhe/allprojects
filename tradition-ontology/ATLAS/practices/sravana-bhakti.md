# Hearing the Lord's names, qualities and deeds (śravaṇa)

Listening to the names, qualities, deeds and stories of the Lord — from the Bhāgavata and the epics, from devotees and in kathā — with love. First of the Bhāgavata's nine forms (BhP 7.5.23); with singing, a means to devotion even for those living in the world (NBS 37); Garga defines devotion as attachment to hearing his stories (NBS 17); the love of Rāma's stories is the second of the nine in the Rāmāyaṇa tradition.
**Stage:** all; the first of the nine
**Signs of progress:** ['the inauspicious in the heart is shaken off and steady devotion arises (BhP 1.2.16–21)']

## The texts' own warnings
