# Remembering the Lord at death

`prc:anta-smarana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Directing the mind to the Lord (Nārāyaṇa, Kṛṣṇa, Śiva) at the time of death, prepared by lifelong remembrance, since the last thought shapes the next state; the name uttered at death protects (Ajāmila).
