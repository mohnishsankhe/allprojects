# Connecting (anubandhanā), following the breath, in mindfulness of breathing

`prc:anapanasati-connecting` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Second stage of attention (Vism VIII p. 280-281): counting withdrawn, one follows the in- and out-breaths continuously with mindfulness, not by following their beginning, middle and end (for the out-breath navel, heart, nose-tip; for the in-breath the reverse) but by touching and fixing: attending to the place touched. The text gives three similes: the lame man seated at the foot of a swing who sees both ends and the middle of the plank without going after them; the doorkeeper who examines only those who reach the gate; and the saw (a man sawing a log fixes mindfulness on the saw-teeth where they touch the log).
**Stage:** beginner
