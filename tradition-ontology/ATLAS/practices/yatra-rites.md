# The rites of the royal expedition (yātrā)

`prc:yatra-rites` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)

For a king's march: choosing the day, asterism, moment and rising sign; examining the purity of the planets and bodily omens; worship of the guhyakas; observing dreams; the bathing rite; the sacrifice to the planets; the departure; and the omens on the way (BJ 28.4–5 listing the Yātrā's contents).
