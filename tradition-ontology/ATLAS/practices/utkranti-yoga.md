Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: Summary of the Gītā's account only: at the time of departure, with unmoving mind, devotion and the power of yoga, the breath is settled between the brows (8.10); the gates (of the body) are restrained, the mind confined in the heart, the breath placed in the head, and, established in yogic concentration (yoga-dhāraṇā), one utters Oṃ, remembers the Lord and leaves the body, going to the supreme goal (8.12–13). The Gītā gives no further method.
