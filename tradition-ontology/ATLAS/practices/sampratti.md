# The father-to-son transfer at death (sampratti)

`prc:sampratti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

A dying father transfers his roles and faculties to his son: 'you are brahman, the sacrifice, the world' (BAU 1.5.17); or lying on the son and placing speech, breath, sight and the other faculties in him one by one, the son receiving each (KauU 2.15).
