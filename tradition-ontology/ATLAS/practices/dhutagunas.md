Optional austerities: rag-robes, only three robes, alms-round without skipping houses, one meal, living in the forest, at a tree's root, in the open, in a charnel ground, the sitter's practice (not lying down), and others; twelve in the Sanskrit lists, thirteen in the Pali.

## The texts' own warnings
- They are optional; the Vinaya accounts show the Buddha refusing to make forest-dwelling and similar austerities compulsory (Devadatta's demands). — [Saṅghabhedavastu](../texts/sanghabhedavastu.md) Devadatta episode (location not given)
