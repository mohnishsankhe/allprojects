# Dharma-dhyāna (virtuous meditation)

`prc:dharma-dhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
**Taught in:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)

Fixing the mind on one of four objects: the Jina's command (the realities as taught), the harm and misery of wrong ways, the fruition of karma, and the structure of the universe; supported by the reflections, the four attitudes and equanimity; also practised through the four objects (piṇḍastha …).
**Stage:** guṇasthānas 4–7 (Digambara); up to 12 in some Śvetāmbara accounts
**Prerequisites:** ['right view', 'equanimity', 'a qualified meditator and suitable place (Jñānārṇava)']
**Duration:** up to one muhūrta at a time
