# The Ambubachi observance

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md)
**Taught in:** [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md)

During the three days of the Earth's/Goddess's menstruation (ambuvācī) the Kāmākhyā temple is closed and agricultural work and certain rites cease; the temple reopens on the fourth day and red cloth is distributed (the Ambubachi Melā).
