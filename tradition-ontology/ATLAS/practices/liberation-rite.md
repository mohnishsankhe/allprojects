# The 'liberation' rite (sgrol ba)

A wrathful tantric rite in which the consciousness of a being causing great harm is, by tradition, liberated into a pure realm, performed on an effigy (liṅga). Recorded as a summary only.

## The texts' own warnings
- Tradition allows it only to one who can actually transfer the consciousness to a pure realm and acts out of compassion; performed otherwise it is a grave downfall that harms the performer.
