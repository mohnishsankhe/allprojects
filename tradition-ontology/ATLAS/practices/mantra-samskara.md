# The ten purifications of a mantra (mantra-saṃskāra)

`prc:mantra-samskara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md)
**Taught in:** [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md)

Summary only: a defective or newly received mantra is made effective by ten rites — generation, enlivening, striking, awakening, consecration, cleansing, strengthening, satiation, kindling and guarding (secrecy) — performed according to the teacher's tradition (ŚT 2.112–123).
**Stage:** intermediate
**Prerequisites:** ['initiation']
**Sources:** 
  - [Śāradātilaka](../texts/saradatilaka.md) — ref: 2.112-123; rests_on: ["tea:saradatilaka:2.112-123"]

## The texts' own warnings
- These are to be done according to tradition (sampradāya); they are kept secret in all mantras. — [Śāradātilaka](../texts/saradatilaka.md) 2.123
