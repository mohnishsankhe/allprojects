# Regimen of sleep

`prc:nidra-vidhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Sleep at night; day-sleep is wholesome in summer and for the exhausted, weak, old, children and certain patients, but not for the fat, kapha-natured or those who eat unctuous food; induce sleep by massage, bathing, pleasant food and surroundings; drive away excess sleep by purification, fasting, anxiety and exercise.

## The texts' own warnings
- Sleep untimely or in excess takes away happiness and life like another night of doom; day-sleep in the wrong season causes many kapha disorders. — [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) Sū 21.37, 21.44-49
