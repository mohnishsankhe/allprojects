# Kevala-kumbhaka (retention without inhalation or exhalation)

`prc:kevala-kumbhaka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): `lin:hatha-yoga`, [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** `lin:hatha-yoga`, [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

The spontaneous suspension of breath that arises after long practice; the Yoga Upaniṣads associate it with awakening of kuṇḍalinī and with powers. Summary only.
