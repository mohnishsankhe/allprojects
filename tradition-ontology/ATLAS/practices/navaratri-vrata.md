# The Navarātri observance

`prc:navaratri-vrata` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md)

Nine nights of worship of the Goddess in autumn and spring, with recitation, worship of young girls and — in many traditions — one of the nine Durgās on each night; accompanied by vows and fasts according to ability.
