# Annual pavitra offering (pavitrārohaṇa)

**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)

In Śrāvaṇa (or Nabhasya) the worshipper offers a consecrated thread-garland (pavitra) to the deity to make good all defects in the year's worship and complete the annual observance.
