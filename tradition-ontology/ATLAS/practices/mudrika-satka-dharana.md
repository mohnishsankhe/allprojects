# Wearing the six insignia (reported)

`prc:mudrika-satka-dharana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Kāpālika (Somasiddhānta)](../lineages/kapalika.md)
**Taught in:** [Kāpālika (Somasiddhānta)](../lineages/kapalika.md)

As reported by Yāmuna and Rāmānuja: knowing and wearing six insignia (ear-ornament or necklace, rucaka, earrings, crest-jewel, ash, sacred thread) with skull and khaṭvāṅga, claimed to prevent rebirth.
