# Guru yoga (bla ma'i rnal 'byor)

**Taught in:** [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md), [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md), [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)

Visualizing one's teacher as Padmasambhava (or in Bön as Tapihritsa or Shenrab), making the seven-branch offering, supplicating with the Seven-line prayer and Vajra Guru mantra, receiving the four empowerments by lights from the guru's three places, and mingling one's mind with the guru's wisdom mind, then resting in that state; the root of blessing and realization in Dzogchen.
