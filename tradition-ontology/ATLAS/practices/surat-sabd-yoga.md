Given only at initiation by a living master: the attention is withdrawn from the body and gathered at the eye centre by simran, held there by dhyān of the master's form, and joined to the inner sound (bhajan), which draws the soul up through the inner regions; practised daily (two and a half hours in the Beas line) with the vows.

## The texts' own warnings
- Without a living master and initiation the practice is not to be attempted; the lower centres and kuṇḍalinī are not to be worked on. — [The Crown of Life](../texts/crown-of-life.md) 
- Inner lights and sounds are not to be sought for their own sake or spoken of; the disciple should not display powers. — [Spiritual Gems](../texts/spiritual-gems.md)
