# Atiśa's daily conduct of a bodhisattva

`prc:kadam-daily-conduct` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md)
**Taught in:** [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md)

Rising in the last part of the night and recollecting the nature of phenomena; seeing appearances as illusion; seven-limb practice and aspiration; eating moderately with the body regarded as a boat and food divided in four offerings; circumambulation and recitation; the ten dharma practices with mindfulness; resting the mind free of elaboration in the first part of the night; sleeping in the lion posture with the notion of light.
