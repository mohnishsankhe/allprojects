# The five element concentrations (pañca-dhāraṇā)

`prc:element-dharanas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): `lin:hatha-yoga`, [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** `lin:hatha-yoga`, [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Holding breath and mind in five regions of the body, each with its element, shape, colour, seed-syllable and deity (Brahmā, Viṣṇu/Nārāyaṇa, Rudra, Īśvara, Sadāśiva; in the Triśikhi the four vyūhas and Vāsudeva); each conquers its element. Durations omitted.
