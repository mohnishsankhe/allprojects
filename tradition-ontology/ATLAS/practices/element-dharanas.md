# The five element-concentrations (pañca-dhāraṇā) in the haṭha texts

`prc:element-dharanas` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

The breath is held with the mind for a set period (not reproduced) in the place of each element, contemplated with its colour, shape, seed-syllable and deity: earth (yellow, square, la, Brahmā), water (white, crescent, va, Viṣṇu), fire (red, triangle, ra, Rudra), air (smoky/black, ya, Īśvara) and space (clear, ha, Sadāśiva) (GS 3.68-81; GŚ 68-75). The texts place them differently: in the heart, throat, palate, between the brows and in the brahmarandhra (GŚ 69-73); at the anus-region, navel, above the navel, between navel and brows and above the brows (DYŚ 100-110; ŚS 3.63-65); from feet to knees, knees to anus, anus to heart, heart to brows and brows to crown (Yogatattva). The Gorakṣaśataka names them the stabilising, liquefying, burning, whirling and drying concentrations (GŚ 74). The Gheraṇḍa counts them as five of its twenty-five mudrās.
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** ['conquest of each element: not harmed by earth, water, fire or wind; moving in the sky; the door of liberation opened (GS 3.70-81; DYŚ 102-110)', "no death even at Brahmā's dissolution (DYŚ 110; ŚS 3.65)", 'mastery of the element: not harmed by earth, water, fire, air; moving in space (Yogatattva 83–102)']

## The texts' own warnings
- The dhāraṇās are to be kept secret; revealing them brings loss of success. — [Gheraṇḍa Saṃhitā](../texts/gheranda-samhita.md) 3.74
- They are not to be given to the deceitful or those without devotion. — [Gheraṇḍa Saṃhitā](../texts/gheranda-samhita.md) 3.79
