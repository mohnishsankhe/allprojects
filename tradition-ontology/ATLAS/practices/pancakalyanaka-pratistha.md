# Consecration of an image (pañcakalyāṇaka-pratiṣṭhā)

`prc:pancakalyanaka-pratistha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md)

A new image is consecrated by a festival re-enacting the five auspicious events of a Tīrthaṅkara's life — conception, birth (with the bath on Meru), renunciation, omniscience (the samavasaraṇa) and nirvāṇa — after which it is installed as the Jina.
**Stage:** all
