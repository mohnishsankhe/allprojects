# Wandering alone like a madman

`prc:unmatta-carana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

Having closed the doors of the senses with the intellect, he wanders alone as if mad, taking discarded food; people call him mad and foolish. Kauṇḍinya: the madness is a feint to delude worldly examiners.
**Stage:** avyakta (second) stage
