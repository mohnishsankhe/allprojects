# The householder's daily round (āhnika)

`prc:ahnika-daily-routine` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)

Waking at the hour of Brahmā to reflect on dharma, artha, bodily strain and the meaning of the Veda (MDh 4.92), purification, twilight worship, study, the five great sacrifices, work and meals with the breath-offerings, and evening worship.
