# Congregational worship of the name (nām-prasaṅga)

`prc:nam-prasanga` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md)
**Taught in:** [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md)

In the nāmghar, before the book on the guru-āsana, a leader sings verses of the Kīrtana-ghoṣā and Nām-ghoṣā and the congregation responds with the name, with cymbals and drum, at set times of day and on festivals.
