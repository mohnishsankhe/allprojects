# Worship with substitutes (anukalpa)

`prc:anukalpa-substitution` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md), [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md), [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)
**Taught in:** [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md), [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md), [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)

Offering permitted substitutes in place of the Kaula substances or of an animal victim — milk, sugar and honey for wine; coconut water or honey for the twice-born; a pumpkin or sugarcane in place of a victim — as taught by the texts for householders, brāhmaṇas and the Kali age.
**Stage:** all (householders especially)
