# Separating saṃsāra and nirvāṇa ('khor 'das ru shan)

`prc:rushen` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)
**Taught in:** [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md), [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)

The extraordinary preliminaries of the Heart-essence: outer (acting out the behaviour of the six realms in solitude and then resting), inner (purifying the seeds of the six realms with syllables) and secret (training body, speech and mind: the vajra posture, the syllable hūṃ, and examining mind's arising, abiding and going), to distinguish ordinary mind from awareness.
**Stage:** advanced
**Sources:** 
  - [Yeshe Lama (ye shes bla ma, the Supreme Wisdom)](../texts/yeshe-lama.md) — ref: rushen; rests_on: ["tea:yeshe-lama:rushen"]

## The texts' own warnings
