# Gross, luminous and subtle meditation (Gheraṇḍa)

`prc:gheranda-three-dhyanas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** visualization-deity
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

Gross: visualize a jewel island in an ocean of nectar in the heart with the chosen deity (or the guru in the lotus of the crown); luminous: meditate on kuṇḍalinī as a flame at the root, the navel sun, or the praṇava-light between the brows; subtle: by śāmbhavī, on awakened kuṇḍalinī (GS 6.1–22).
**Stage:** intermediate
**Sources:**
