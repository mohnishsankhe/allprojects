# The annual festival of reciting the Prabandham (adhyayana-utsava)

An annual festival of about twenty days in the month of Mārkaḻi, around Vaikuṇṭha Ekādaśī, in which the whole Prabandham is recited before the Lord — the 'ten days' and 'ten nights' (pakal pattu, rā pattu) — ending with the Tiruvāymoḻi and the enactment of Nammāḻvār's liberation. The tradition says Tirumaṅkai began it at Śrīraṅgam and Nāthamuni restored it.
