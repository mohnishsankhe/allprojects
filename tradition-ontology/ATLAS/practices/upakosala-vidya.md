# Upakosala's meditation (upakosala-vidyā)

`prc:upakosala-vidya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Venerate breath, joy (ka) and space (kha) as brahman, the fires as its forms, and the person seen in the eye as the self, the immortal, the fearless, brahman; the knower goes by the path of light and does not return.
