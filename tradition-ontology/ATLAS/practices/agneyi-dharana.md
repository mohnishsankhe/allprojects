# Āgneyī / vaiśvānarī dhāraṇā (fire-concentration)

**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)

The breath held with the mind in the fire-principle at the navel, red as the indragopa insect, triangular, with its seed and Rudra (GS 3.75); at the palate, 'burning' (GŚ 71, 74). Durations are not reproduced.
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** ['removes the fear of death; one falling into blazing fire survives (GS 3.75-76)']
