# Jñāna (knowledge of Śiva)

`prc:saiva-jnana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta (the Meykaṇṭa school)](../lineages/tamil-saiva-siddhanta.md)

Knowledge given by grace through the guru, cultivated in four steps: hearing (kēṭṭal), reflecting (cintittal), clarity (teḷital) and abiding (niṭṭai); seeing the tattvas and the soul as not-Śiva and the world as a mirage, meditating the five letters, abiding at Śiva's feet — the true path (san-mārga).
**Stage:** fourth pāda
