# Devotion to the vajra master

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)
**Taught in:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md), [Vajrayāna (Mantranaya) in India](../lineages/vajrayana.md)

After mutual examination, the disciple regards the guru as inseparable from Vajradhara, bows three times a day, offers gifts, follows instructions and observes the etiquette of the Gurupañcāśikā (not stepping on his shadow, rising when he arrives, speaking softly); Saraha's songs praise the guru as boat, physician and sun.
