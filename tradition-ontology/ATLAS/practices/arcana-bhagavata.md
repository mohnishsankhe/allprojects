# Image worship (kriyā-yoga, Uddhava Gītā)

`prc:arcana-bhagavata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

Worship of the Lord in Vedic, tantric or mixed mode, in an image (of stone, wood, metal, paste, painting, sand, mind or jewel), on prepared ground, in fire, sun, water or the heart; with love even water suffices.
**Stage:** beginner

## The texts' own warnings
- Worship in images while despising the Lord present in all beings is 'offering into ashes'. — [Kapila Gītā](../texts/kapila-gita.md) 3.29.21-25
