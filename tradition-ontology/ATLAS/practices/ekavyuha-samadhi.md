# The single-practice samādhi (yixing sanmei)

`prc:ekavyuha-samadhi` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 4 independent lineage(s): `lin:chan`, [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), `lin:pure-land`, `lin:tiantai`
**Taught in:** `lin:chan`, [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md), `lin:pure-land`, `lin:tiantai`

Sitting in solitude facing the direction of a buddha and reciting his name continuously until one sees all buddhas in that one and knows the sameness of the dharmadhātu.
