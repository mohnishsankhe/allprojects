# Casting and reading the horoscope

`prc:horoscope-reading` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md), [The Kerala tradition of jyotiṣa](../lineages/kerala-jyotisa.md)

From the moment and place of birth the astrologer computes the rising sign, the planets, the divisional charts, strengths and the running periods, then judges the houses, yogas and periods to declare the ripening of past karma, with remedies where evil is shown; the Kerala practice begins with the astrologer's own worship and the omens at the time of consultation.
