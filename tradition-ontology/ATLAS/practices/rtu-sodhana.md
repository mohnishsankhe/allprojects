# Seasonal purification

`prc:rtu-sodhana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** cleansing
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Removal of the doṣas accumulated in a season at its proper time (e.g. kapha of winter in spring), by the purificatory therapies, followed by rejuvenatives; purified doṣas do not recur. Summary only.
**Stage:** all
**Sources:**

## The texts' own warnings
- Doṣas that have accumulated too much, when provoked, can cut off life; doṣas merely subdued by fasting and digestives may flare again. — [Aṣṭāṅga Hṛdaya](../texts/astanga-hrdaya.md) Sū 4.25-26
