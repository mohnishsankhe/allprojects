# Mindfulness of breathing (Śrāvakabhūmi)

`prc:anapanasmrti-sravakabhumi` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
**Taught in:** [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)

Mindfulness whose object is the in- and out-breath, prescribed for the discursive temperament; the Śrāvakabhūmi treats it in detail (including counting) and notes that it is not practised above the third absorption, where breathing ceases.
