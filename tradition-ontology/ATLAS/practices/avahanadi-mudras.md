# The invocation gestures (āvāhanī and the rest)

The series by which a deity is invited and settled in worship: āvāhanī (the añjali filled with flowers), sthāpanī (the same turned downward), sannidhāpanī (the fists joined, thumbs raised), sannirodhanī (the same with thumbs inside), sammukhīkaraṇī (the fists turned up), with sakalīkaraṇa (placing the six limbs on the deity), avaguṇṭhana (the right fist with the index finger extended, turned around), dhenu (amṛtīkaraṇa) and mahāmudrā (paramīkaraṇa) (ŚT 23.106-114).
**Stage:** all
**Members:** āvāhanī, sthāpanī, sannidhāpanī, sannirodhanī, sammukhīkaraṇī, sakalīkaraṇa, avaguṇṭhana, dhenu, mahāmudrā
