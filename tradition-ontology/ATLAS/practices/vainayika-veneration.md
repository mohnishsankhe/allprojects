# Veneration as the whole path (vinaya)

`prc:vainayika-veneration` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
**Taught in:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)

(Jain report) The vainayikas venerate gods, the king, ascetics, kin, elders, the lowly, mother and father, by mind, speech, body and gifts, holding this discipline alone to lead to the good.
