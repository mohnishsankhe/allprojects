# Making the body adamantine (vajrīkaraṇa)

`prc:vajrikarana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

**Category:** energy
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)
**Taught in:** [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md)

Bhairava's own practice by which he survived dissolution: meditation on the innate centre in the bone-circle and on nested lotuses flooded with milk-white nectar, so that the body stands 'like a thunderbolt'. Summary only.
