# Bhakti-yoga: loving meditation (upāsana, dhruvānusmṛti)

`prc:bhakti-yoga-upasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

Daily, repeated meditation on the Lord (in one of the Upaniṣadic forms, the vidyās) as one's inner self, until it becomes unbroken, vivid as sight and full of love; supported by the seven aids and by duties done as worship, continued until death, with remembrance at the last moment.
**Stage:** for those qualified by birth, learning and capacity (traivarṇika) after karma- and jñāna-yoga
**Prerequisites:** ['the seven aids (sādhana-saptaka)', 'karma-yoga and jñāna-yoga leading to the vision of the self']
**Duration:** daily until death
**Signs of progress:** ['vividness equal to seeing (darśana-samānākāra)', 'parabhakti, parajñāna, paramabhakti']
**Sources:** 
  - [Śrībhāṣya](../texts/sribhasya.md) — ref: 1.1.1; 4.1; rests_on: ["tea:sribhasya:1.1.1/2", "tea:sribhasya:4.1"]
**Sequences:** [The way of bhakti-yoga (Rāmānuja)](../paths/visistadvaita-bhakti-yoga.md)

## The texts' own warnings
