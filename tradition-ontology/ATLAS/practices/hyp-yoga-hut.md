The haṭha yogin lives alone in a small hut in a well-governed, righteous land with plentiful alms and no disturbance (1.12); the hut has a small door, no holes or cracks, is neither too high nor too low, is smeared with cow-dung, clean and free of creatures, with a pavilion, raised seat and well outside and an enclosure wall (1.13); he lives there free of all worries and practises yoga by the guru's path (1.14).
