# Placing the syllables for liberation (Kaula nyāsa)

`prc:kjn-nyasa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)
**Taught in:** [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md)

Placing seed-syllables on the anus, genitals, navel, mouth, nostrils, ears, eyes and forehead; for one in whom it is established, liberation is certain and at death the skull splits.
