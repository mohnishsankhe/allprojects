# The four establishments of mindfulness (satipaṭṭhāna)

`prc:satipatthana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

The monk abides contemplating the body in the body, feelings in feelings, mind in mind and dhammas in dhammas, ardent, clearly comprehending and mindful, having put away covetousness and displeasure for the world; twenty-one sections in all (MN 10). Stated fruit: final knowledge in this life or, with a residue, non-return, expected for one who develops them for seven years down to seven days (MN 10:46). Each section closes with the refrain (internal, external, both; arising, passing away; bare knowledge and recollection; independence; clinging to nothing in the world).
**Stage:** unmarked
**Prerequisites:** ['virtue', 'sense restraint (in the gradual training)']
**Duration:** seven years … seven days (MN 10.46)
**Signs of progress:** The text states only the fruit (mn10:46), not intermediate signs.
