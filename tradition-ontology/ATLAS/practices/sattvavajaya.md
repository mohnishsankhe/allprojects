# Conquest of the mind (sattvāvajaya)

`prc:sattvavajaya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Restraining the mind from unwholesome objects; for mental disorder, examining with understanding what is wholesome in dharma, artha and kāma, serving those who know, and cultivating knowledge of self, place, family, time and strength; knowledge, understanding, steadiness, memory and concentration are the medicines of the mind.
