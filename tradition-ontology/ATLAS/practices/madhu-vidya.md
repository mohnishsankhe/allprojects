# The honey-doctrine (madhu-vidyā)

`prc:madhu-vidya` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

ChU 3.1-11: meditate on the sun as the honey of the gods, the Vedas as flowers and the rays as honeycomb cells; for one who knows it the sun neither rises nor sets. BAU 2.5 (also called the madhu-brāhmaṇa): meditate on earth, waters, fire and all things as 'honey' for all beings and all beings as honey for them, culminating in 'this self is brahman'.
