# Self-knowledge supported by calm, restraint and celibacy (Prābhākara)

`prc:atmajnana-sama-dama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
**Taught in:** [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md), [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md)

Knowing the self as distinct from body, senses and cognition, cultivated together with calm (śama), restraint (dama), celibacy and the like; enjoined for non-return, it destroys the store of karma (Prakaraṇapañcikā). For Kumārila, self-knowledge removes the desire through which fruits accrue.
