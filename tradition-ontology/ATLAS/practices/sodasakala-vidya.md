# Satyakāma's meditation on the four quarters of brahman

`prc:sodasakala-vidya` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Venerate brahman as having four quarters of four parts each — the quarters of space ('shining'), earth, mid-space, sky and ocean ('endless'), fire, sun, moon and lightning ('luminous'), breath, sight, hearing and mind ('having an abode') — as taught to Satyakāma by a bull, fire, a goose and a diver-bird.
