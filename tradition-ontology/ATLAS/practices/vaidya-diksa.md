# Initiation of the medical student

`prc:vaidya-diksa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

At an auspicious time the fasting, bathed, ochre-clad student brings the requisites; the teacher kindles the fire, offers to Brahmā, Agni, Dhanvantari, Prajāpati, the Aśvins, Indra and the sages, and before fire, brahmins and physicians instructs him in the vows (celibacy, truth, no meat, no weapons, obedience, service of all beings, never harming the sick); the student assents. Mantras not recorded.
**Stage:** beginner
**Prerequisites:** examination of the student's qualities (Vi 8.8)
