# The five daily periods (pañcakāla)

`prc:pancakala` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

The Pāñcarātrin's day is divided into approach (abhigamana: purification and going to the Lord), gathering (upādāna: collecting materials for worship), worship (ijyā), study (svādhyāya) and meditation (yoga).
