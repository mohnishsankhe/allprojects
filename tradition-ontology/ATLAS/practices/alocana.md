# Ālocanā (confession to the teacher)

`prc:alocana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
**Taught in:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)

The practitioner discloses his faults to the teacher fully and without concealment or self-justification, as a child tells its mother, and accepts the expiation assigned.
**Stage:** all
**Sources:** 
  - [Tattvārthasūtra](../texts/tattvartha-sutra.md) — ref: 9.22; rests_on: ["tea:tattvartha-sutra:9.22"]

## The texts' own warnings
- Confession with concealment, or choosing a lenient teacher, is itself a fault (ten faults of confession in the ācāra texts, recalled).
