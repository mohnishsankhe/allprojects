# Examining the mind day and night (Kadam self-watching)

`prc:kadam-self-examination` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md)

Checking one's mind-stream three times by day and three by night, proclaiming one's own faults, watching speech in company and mind when alone; the Kadam hermit Ben Gungyal counted his virtuous and non-virtuous thoughts with white and black pebbles.
