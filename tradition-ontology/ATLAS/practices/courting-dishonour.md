# Courting dishonour (avamāna, paribhava)

`prc:courting-dishonour` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

Hiding his sign and learning, the ascetic goes among people behaving so as to be despised and reviled; he regards censure as no censure. The text states that his demerit then passes to his revilers and their merit to him.
**Stage:** avyakta (second) stage
**Signs of progress:** ['being thought mad or sinful by people (the intended result)']
