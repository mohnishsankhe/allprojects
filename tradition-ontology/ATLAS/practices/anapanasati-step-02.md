# Ānāpānasati step 2: short breath (rassaṁ assasāmī)

**Category:** breath

**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Breathing in short he knows 'I breathe in short'; breathing out short he knows 'I breathe out short'. Step 2 of the sixteen, in tetrad 1, which the sutta identifies with the contemplation of body (mn118:24). The sutta numbers no steps; they are counted from the four tetrads.
