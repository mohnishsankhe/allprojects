Being constantly intent on discerning the spanda principle, one attains one's own nature even while awake; spanda is to be grasped in intense states (great anger, joy, perplexity, running) and in the opening (unmeṣa) from which a new thought arises in one absorbed in a single thought (SK 1.21-22, 3.9).
