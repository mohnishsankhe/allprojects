# The daily round of a Kerala temple

`prc:kerala-daily-worship` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md)
**Taught in:** [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md)

From the opening of the sanctum at dawn, a fixed series of pūjās through the day (dawn, morning, mid-morning, noon and night pūjās) and processional offerings (śīveli) around the shrine, performed by the śānti; the tantri intervenes for installations, festivals and purifications.
