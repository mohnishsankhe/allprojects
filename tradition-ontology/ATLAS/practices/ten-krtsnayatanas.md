# The ten totalities (kṛtsnāyatana)

`prc:ten-krtsnayatanas` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Earth, water, fire, wind, blue, yellow, red, white, space and consciousness contemplated as all-pervading; the first eight in the fourth dhyāna with desire-realm objects, the last two formless.
**Stage:** advanced
