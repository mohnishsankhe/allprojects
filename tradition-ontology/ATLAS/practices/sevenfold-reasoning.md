# The sevenfold reasoning (chariot analysis)

`prc:sevenfold-reasoning` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)

The self is sought as a chariot is sought: is it other than its parts, identical with them, possessing them, in them, containing them, their collection or their shape? Not found in any of the seven ways, it is understood as a designation dependent on the aggregates, cutting the view of the transitory collection.
