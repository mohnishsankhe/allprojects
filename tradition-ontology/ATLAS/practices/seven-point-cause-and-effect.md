# The seven-point cause-and-effect meditation for bodhicitta

`prc:seven-point-cause-and-effect` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md)
**Taught in:** [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md)

Establishing equanimity toward friends, enemies and strangers, then meditating in sequence: all beings have been one's mother; their kindness as mothers; the wish to repay it; affectionate love; great compassion; the extraordinary resolve to free them oneself; and bodhicitta.
**Stage:** intermediate
