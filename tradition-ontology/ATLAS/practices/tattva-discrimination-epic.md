# Discriminating the principles (Sāṃkhya in the Mokṣadharma)

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Enumerating the twenty-four principles of prakṛti and knowing the twenty-fifth as other than them, the knower awakens and becomes alone (MBh 12.294); knowing the faults of body, senses and guṇas, the Sāṃkhya follower is freed (12.290).
