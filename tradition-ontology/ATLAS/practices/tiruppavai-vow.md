# The Mārkaḻi vow of the Tiruppāvai (pāvai nōṉpu)

In the month of Mārkaḻi the girls rise before dawn, wake one another, bathe, and go singing the Lord's names to his house; during the vow they take no ghee or milk, wear no kohl or flowers, do nothing forbidden, speak no evil, and give alms; they worship with pure flowers, singing and meditation. In the tradition the Tiruppāvai's thirty verses are sung daily throughout Mārkaḻi, one for each day, in temples and homes.
