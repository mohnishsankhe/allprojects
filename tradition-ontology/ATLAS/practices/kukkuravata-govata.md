# The dog-vow and the cow-vow

`prc:kukkuravata-govata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md)
**Taught in:** [The śramaṇa movement](../lineages/sramana.md)

Ascetics who live in the manner of a dog (eating food thrown on the ground, curling up like a dog) or of a cow, hoping for heavenly rebirth.
**Sources:** 
  - [Kukkuravatika Sutta](../texts/kukkuravatika-sutta.md) — ref: MN 57; rests_on: ["tea:kukkuravatika-sutta:2-5"]

## The texts' own warnings
- Perfected, the vow leads to rebirth among dogs or cattle; joined with the belief that it leads to godhood, it is wrong view leading to hell or the animal realm. — [Kukkuravatika Sutta](../texts/kukkuravatika-sutta.md) MN 57
