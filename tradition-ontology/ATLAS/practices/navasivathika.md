# The nine charnel-ground contemplations (navasivathikā)

`prc:navasivathika` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

As if seeing a discarded body in a charnel ground in nine stages (swollen, blue-black, festering; eaten by animals; skeleton with flesh and blood; without flesh, smeared with blood; without flesh and blood; scattered bones; white bones; heaped bones; rotten bones crumbled to powder), the monk compares this body with it: 'this body too is of such a nature, will become like that, and is not exempt from it' (MN 10:14-31).
**Stage:** unmarked
