# Pure food taken with restraint

`prc:pure-food-with-restraint` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)
**Taught in:** [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)

Eating food that is pure — of desirable colour, taste, smell and touch, sprinkled and consecrated, given by the untainted and taken with the proper recitation — together with self-restraint, since without restraint pure food yields no prosperity; taint lies in injury.
