# The nine forms of devotion (navadhā bhakti)

`prc:navadha-bhakti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

Devotion practised as hearing, singing and remembering the Lord's names and deeds, serving his feet, ritual worship, obeisance, servitude, friendship and complete self-offering, performed directly toward the Lord (BhP 7.5.23-24); the Śiva Purāṇa gives Śaivas the three means of hearing, singing and reflection.
**Stage:** all; from childhood (BhP 7.6.1)
**Signs of progress:** ['melting of the heart, tears, laughter, song and dance (BhP 11.14.24; 11.2.40)']
