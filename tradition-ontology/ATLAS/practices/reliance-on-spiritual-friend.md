# Reliance on the spiritual friend

`prc:reliance-on-spiritual-friend` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)
