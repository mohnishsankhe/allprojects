# Developing the direct knowledges (abhiññā)

`prc:abhinna-development` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** energy
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

From the fourth jhāna on the kasiṇas, after taming the mind in fourteen ways (in order, reverse order, skipping kasiṇas and jhānas, etc.), the meditator resolves with the direct-knowledge consciousness to produce supernormal powers, the divine ear and eye, knowledge of minds and recollection of past lives — summary only.
**Stage:** advanced
**Prerequisites:** ['mastery of all eight attainments on the eight kasiṇas']
**Sources:**
