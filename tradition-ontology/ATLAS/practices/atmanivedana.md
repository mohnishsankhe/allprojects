# Offering oneself to the Lord (ātma-nivedana)

Handing over oneself and all one has to the Lord. Ninth and last of the Bhāgavata's nine forms. 'Give up everything; give your life to the one who owns release' (TVM 1.2.1); 'I, your servant with no other refuge, have entered beneath your feet' (TVM 6.10.10); having offered self, world and Veda the devotee has no care for worldly loss (NBS 61).
