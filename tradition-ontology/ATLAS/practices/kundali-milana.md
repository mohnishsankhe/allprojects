# Matching horoscopes for marriage

`prc:kundali-milana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)

The asterisms and signs of bride and groom are compared under the eight heads (varṇa, vaśya, tārā, yoni, grahamaitra, gaṇa, bhakūṭa, nāḍī) worth 1 to 8 points, 36 in all, and faults such as nāḍī-doṣa and the fault of Mars are checked and their cancellations considered before the match and the wedding-moment are fixed.
