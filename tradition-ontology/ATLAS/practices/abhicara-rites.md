## The texts' own warnings
- Mīmāṃsā's standard example: the Veda describes the śyena rite for harming an enemy, yet such harm is not dharma — the rite is described, not commended (Śabara on Mīmāṃsā Sūtra 1.1.2, recalled with moderate confidence). — [Śābarabhāṣya](../texts/sabara-bhasya.md) 1.1.2
- Vasiṣṭha's oath 'may I die today if I am a sorcerer' shows the gravity of the charge of sorcery. — [Ṛgveda Saṃhitā](../texts/rgveda.md) 7.104.15
