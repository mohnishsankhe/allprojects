# Svara practice: attending to the flow of breath in the nostrils (svarodaya)

`prc:svara-sadhana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

The practice of the Śiva Svarodaya: noting which channel the breath is flowing in — iḍā (left, moon), piṅgalā (right, sun) or suṣumnā (both) — as the flows alternate through the day, and timing actions accordingly: steady and auspicious acts when the moon flows, active and harsh acts when the sun flows, and only meditation and yoga when the suṣumnā flows, since worldly acts then fail. The text treats this knowledge as most secret and also teaches ways of changing the flow (not detailed here).
