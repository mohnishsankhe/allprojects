# Kapālabhāti / bhālabhāti (the skull-shining)

`prc:kapalabhati` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** cleansing
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

In the Haṭhapradīpikā, rapid exhalation and inhalation like a blacksmith's bellows (HYP 2.35). In the Gheraṇḍa bhālabhāti is threefold: vāmakrama (breathing through alternate nostrils, not forcefully), vyutkrama (water drawn in through the nose and expelled through the mouth) and śītkrama (water sucked in through the mouth and expelled through the nose) (GS 1.55-60).
**Stage:** beginner
**Signs of progress:** ['dries up the disorders of phlegm (HYP 2.35; GS 1.55)']
