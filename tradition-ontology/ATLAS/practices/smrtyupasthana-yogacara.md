# The four applications of mindfulness (Yogācāra)

`prc:smrtyupasthana-yogacara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
**Taught in:** [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)

Mindfulness of body, feeling, mind and dharmas, cultivated for entry into the four truths: body because of badness, feeling as the cause of craving, mind as the basis (of the self-notion), dharmas for non-delusion about defilement and purification.
**Stage:** path of accumulation
