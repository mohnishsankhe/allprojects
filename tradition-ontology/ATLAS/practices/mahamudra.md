# Mahāmudrā (the great seal)

`prc:mahamudra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** lock-seal
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Pressing the perineum with the left heel, the right leg stretched out and its foot held with the hands, the chin on the chest (throat lock), the breath filled and held upward, then released slowly; practised on the left side and then equally on the right (HYP 3.10-15; GS 3.6-8; ŚS 4.16-20; DYŚ 121-122; GŚ 33). The texts say kuṇḍalinī then straightens like a snake struck with a stick and a death-like state arises as the breath leaves the two channels (HYP 3.11-12). It is the first of the trio (with mahābandha and mahāvedha) taught already in the Amṛtasiddhi.
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** ['destroys the great afflictions and death; all tastes become tasteless and even poison is digested like nectar; consumption, skin disease, constipation, gulma and indigestion vanish (HYP 3.14-17)', 'removes wrinkles, grey hair, old age and death, consumption, cough, fever (GS 3.8)']
