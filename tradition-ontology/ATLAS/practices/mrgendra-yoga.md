# Yoga of the Mṛgendra (seven auxiliaries)

`prc:mrgendra-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)

Breath-control (propelling, drawing in, holding), withdrawal of the mind from objects once it tastes a little ease, fixation on a chosen place, meditation as repeated thought on it, examination (ūha) of what is to be rejected and accepted, recitation bringing the object near, and absorption as unbroken flow; practised singly or together, they lead to yoga and its powers.
**Prerequisites:** initiation; one's office as teacher or adept
**Sources:**
