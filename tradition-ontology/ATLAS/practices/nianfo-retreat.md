# The seven-day nianfo samādhi retreat (Shandao's practice hall)

`prc:nianfo-retreat` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)
**Taught in:** [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)

In a cleaned room with an image on the west wall, at a chosen period of the month, the practitioner enters for one to seven days in clean clothes, one vegetarian meal a day, recollecting and reciting Amitābha day and night, standing or sitting, confessing at the three or six periods, abstaining from wine, meat and pungent roots. Summary of the text's prescription only.
**Stage:** intermediate
**Duration:** one to seven days
**Signs of progress:** ["seeing Amitābha's golden body before the mind's eye"]
