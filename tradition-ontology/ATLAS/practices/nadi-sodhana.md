# Purification of the channels (nāḍī-śodhana)

`prc:nadi-sodhana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): `lin:hatha-yoga`, [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** `lin:hatha-yoga`, [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Alternate-nostril breathing in regular sessions to purify the channels, or purification by knowledge of the Self (Darśana 5). Counts and schedules given by the texts are not reproduced.
**Signs of progress:** ['lightness, radiance, stronger digestive fire, leanness, inner sound manifest (Yogatattva 44–45; Yogacūḍāmaṇi 98–99)']
