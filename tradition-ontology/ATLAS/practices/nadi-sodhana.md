# Purification of the channels (nāḍī-śodhana, nāḍī-śuddhi)

`prc:nadi-sodhana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Alternate-nostril breathing with retention: inhaling through the moon (left) nostril, holding as far as one is able, exhaling through the sun (right), then inhaling through the right and exhaling through the left, always inhaling through the nostril just used for exhaling, slowly and never forcefully (HYP 2.7-10; ŚS 3.20-26; DYŚ 55-61); the Gorakṣaśataka adds contemplation of the moon-disc and of the blazing sun-disc (GŚ 41-46). The Gheraṇḍa distinguishes samanu purification, done with seed-syllables of air, fire and moon, from nirmanu purification by the cleansing act of dhauti (GS 5.35-46). It is practised at four junctures of the day for a period of months (the counts the texts give are not reproduced).
**Stage:** intermediate
**Prerequisites:** ['firm seat in (bound) padmāsana', 'for those with excess fat or phlegm, first the six acts (HYP 2.21)']
**Duration:** the texts say the channels are purified after about three months of regular practice (HYP 2.10; ŚS 3.26; DYŚ 61)
**Signs of progress:** ['slimness and radiance of body, breath held at will, kindled digestive fire, manifestation of the inner sound, health (HYP 2.19-20)', 'lightness, radiance, increased fire, slimness (DYŚ 62-63)', 'even body, fragrance, beauty, mastery of voice — the beginning stage (ŚS 3.29-31)', 'lightness, radiance, stronger digestive fire, leanness, inner sound manifest (Yogatattva 44–45; Yogacūḍāmaṇi 98–99)']

## The texts' own warnings
- As a lion, an elephant or a tiger is tamed only slowly, so the breath must be brought under control gradually; otherwise it kills the practitioner. — [Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā (Haṭhapradīpikā)](../texts/hatha-yoga-pradipika.md) 2.15
- Proper breath-control destroys all disease; improper practice produces all disease — hiccup, asthma, cough and pains in the head, ears and eyes arise from the disturbed breath. — [Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā (Haṭhapradīpikā)](../texts/hatha-yoga-pradipika.md) 2.16-17
- Draw the breath in slowly and release it slowly; do not hold it excessively nor release it quickly. — [Gorakṣaśataka](../texts/goraksasataka.md) 51
- Release the breath slowly, never with force. — [Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā (Haṭhapradīpikā)](../texts/hatha-yoga-pradipika.md) 2.9
- Breath must be tamed gradually, like a lion, elephant or tiger; otherwise it kills the practitioner; improper practice causes hiccup, asthma, cough and pains of head, ears and eyes. — [Yogacūḍāmaṇi Upaniṣad](../texts/yogacudamani-upanisad.md) 116-118
