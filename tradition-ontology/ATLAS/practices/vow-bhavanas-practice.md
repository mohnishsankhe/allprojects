# The contemplations for steadiness of the vows (Tattvārtha 7.4-7.8)

`prc:vow-bhavanas-practice` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
**Taught in:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)

For non-violence: restraint of speech and mind, careful walking, careful handling, looking at food and drink; for truthfulness: giving up anger, greed, fear, laughter and speaking in conformity; for non-stealing: empty and abandoned dwellings, not obstructing others, pure alms, no dispute with co-religionists; for chastity: giving up talk and sight that awaken attachment, recalling past enjoyment, stimulating foods and adorning the body; for non-possession: giving up attachment and aversion towards pleasant and unpleasant objects of the senses.
