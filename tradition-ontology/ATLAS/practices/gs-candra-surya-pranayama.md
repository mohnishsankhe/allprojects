# Breath-control through moon and sun (Gorakṣaśataka)

`prc:gs-candra-surya-pranayama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)

In the bound lotus, filling through the left (moon) and releasing through the right (sun) while meditating on the moon-disc, then the reverse while meditating on the sun-disc at the navel; this purifies the channels and manifests the inner sound.
**Signs of progress:** sweat, then trembling, then rising up (GŚ 49); purified channels, kindled fire, inner sound, health (GŚ-Briggs 101)
