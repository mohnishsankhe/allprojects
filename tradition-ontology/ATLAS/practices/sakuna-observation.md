# Observing omens (śakuna)

`prc:sakuna-observation` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)

Before and during an undertaking one observes the cries, movements and directions of birds and animals and other signs, judging them by direction, time and quarter, as taught in the augury chapters.
