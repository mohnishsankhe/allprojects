# Bathing and gifts at the solar ingress

`prc:samkranti-punyakala` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md), [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)

The period around the sun's entry into a sign, above all into Capricorn and the equinoctial and solstitial signs, is a holy time (puṇyakāla) for bathing in sacred waters, gifts and ancestral offerings.
