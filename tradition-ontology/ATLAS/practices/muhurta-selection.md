# Choosing the auspicious moment (muhūrta)

`prc:muhurta-selection` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)

Before a sacrament, marriage, journey, building, consecration, sowing or other undertaking, the astrologer examines the almanac — lunar day and its class, weekday, asterism and its class, yoga and karaṇa — the rising sign and the planets' placement, and fixes a moment suited to that kind of act, avoiding faults such as Viṣṭi, eclipses, the combustion of Jupiter or Venus and the intercalary month.
