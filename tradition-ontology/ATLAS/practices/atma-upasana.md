# Venerating it as the self

`prc:atma-upasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

'One should venerate it as the self alone, for in it all these become one' — not as any single function (breathing, speaking, seeing), each of which is incomplete; venerate the self alone as dear (BAU 1.4.7-8).
