# Kapila's eight-limbed yoga with meditation on the Lord's form

Duty, contentment, service of self-knowers, moderate pure food, solitude, the yamas and niyamas, posture, gradual mastery of breath, withdrawal, holding breath in one of its seats with the mind, meditation on the Lord's form limb by limb, and samādhi.
