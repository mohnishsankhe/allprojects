# Brahmasambandha initiation

`prc:brahmasambandha-diksa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md)
**Taught in:** [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md)

The second initiation of the Puṣṭimārga, following the refuge mantra: the disciple, holding tulasī, dedicates body, senses, life, mind and possessions to Kṛṣṇa through a formula given privately by a Gosvāmī ācārya; thereafter everything is offered before use.
**Stage:** beginner
