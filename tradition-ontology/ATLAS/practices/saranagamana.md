# Going for refuge (Sarvāstivāda definition)

`prc:saranagamana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Going for refuge to the Buddha, Dharma and Saṅgha, understood as the non-learner's dharmas that make a Buddha, the learner's and non-learner's dharmas that make the Saṅgha, and nirvāṇa; the entry to the lay discipline.
