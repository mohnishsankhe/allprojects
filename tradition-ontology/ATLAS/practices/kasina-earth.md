# The earth kasiṇa (pathavī-kasiṇa)

`prc:kasina-earth` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Gazing at a prepared disk of dawn-coloured clay (or a ploughed field/threshing floor for one with past practice), the meditator repeats its name and adverts to it with eyes open and closed until the learning sign arises, then develops it away from the device until the counterpart sign and access appear, extends the sign, and reaches the four (Abhidhamma: five) jhānas; used as a basis for supernormal power (the basis for powers such as becoming many and walking on water).
**Stage:** intermediate
**Prerequisites:** ['purified virtue', 'impediments severed', 'subject received from a teacher']
**Signs of progress:** ['learning sign', 'counterpart sign', 'access', 'jhāna']
