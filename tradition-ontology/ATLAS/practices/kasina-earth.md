# The earth kasiṇa (pathavī-kasiṇa), taking the learning sign (Vism IV p. 125)

`prc:kasina-earth` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Vism IV p. 125: the meditator looks at the prepared disk with the eyes opened evenly (as when looking at one's face in a mirror), not too wide and not too little; does not reflect on the colour or the mark but sets the mind on the concept 'earth' (pathavī), repeating it, opening and closing the eyes by turns until the learning sign arises (seen with the eyes shut as clearly as with them open). After that he develops it in his own dwelling; if the young concentration is lost he returns to the place, takes the sign again and continues. As he does so the hindrances are suppressed, the defilements subside, the mind is concentrated by access concentration and the counterpart sign arises.
**Stage:** beginner
**Prerequisites:** ['purified virtue', 'impediments severed', 'subject received from a teacher']
**Signs of progress:** The learning sign (uggahanimitta) arises when the disk with eyes closed comes into focus as when open; then, with the hindrances suppressed and access reached, the counterpart sign (paṭibhāganimitta).
