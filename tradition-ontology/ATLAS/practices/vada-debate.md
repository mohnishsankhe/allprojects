# Discussion for truth (vāda)

`prc:vada-debate` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)
**Taught in:** [Navya-Nyāya](../lineages/navya-nyaya.md), [Nyāya](../lineages/nyaya.md)

Examining a question with a partner by thesis and counter-thesis, proving and refuting only with the means of knowledge and tarka, within accepted tenets, in the five-membered form, aiming at ascertainment rather than victory.
