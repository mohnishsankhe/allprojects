# Illusory body yoga (sgyu lus)

Seeing one's body, appearances and all beings as illusory — like a mirror image, a rainbow, the moon in water — first by contemplation and in daily life, then (in the completion stage) arising as the pure illusory body of the deity.
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** ['the eight worldly concerns self-liberated', 'attachment and aversion subside']
