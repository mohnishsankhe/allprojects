# Study of grammar as purification of speech

`prc:vyakarana-study` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Vyākaraṇa (the grammarian-philosophers; śabdādvaita)](../lineages/vyakarana.md)

Learning the Pāṇinian grammar with its commentaries so as to use only correct words (sādhu-śabda), which removes the impurities of speech, brings merit and, understood to its root, leads to the Word-principle (VP 1.11–16; Mahābhāṣya on 6.1.84).
