# Concentration on the eighteen vital points (marma)

`prc:marmasthana-dharana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Holding breath and attention successively at eighteen vital points from the toes upward and back (Triśikhi 128–133; Darśana 7; Śāṇḍilya 1.69).
