# The Kātyāyanī vow of the gopīs

`prc:katyayani-vrata` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

A month-long observance in Mārgaśīrṣa by unmarried girls: early bathing in the river, worship of the goddess Kātyāyanī in an image of sand, and prayer to obtain the desired husband (for the gopīs, Kṛṣṇa).
