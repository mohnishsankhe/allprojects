# Worship of Viṣṇu according to the Tantrasārasaṅgraha and Yogadīpikā

`prc:visnu-puja-dvaita` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)

Daily and occasional worship of Viṣṇu (and his forms, especially Kṛṣṇa, Narasiṃha and Rāma) with mantras derived from the praṇava, placing of letters and forms on the body, purifications, invocation into the image and offerings, remembering that Hari in the worshipper and Hari in the image is the one Lord who accepts the worship.
**Prerequisites:** ['dīkṣā']
