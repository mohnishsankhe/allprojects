# Remedies for affliction from the ancestors

`prc:pitr-dosa-parihara` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md), [The Kerala tradition of jyotiṣa](../lineages/kerala-jyotisa.md)

When a chart or query shows the ancestors' displeasure (childlessness, repeated misfortune), expiatory ancestral rites are prescribed — śrāddha and offerings at holy places, and in later practice rites such as nārāyaṇa-bali and tripiṇḍī-śrāddha.
