# The great vows of the ascetic

`prc:mahavratas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
**Taught in:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)

Lifelong, total abstention from violence, falsehood, stealing, sexual activity and possession, in thought, word and deed, by doing, causing and approving, supported by the twenty-five contemplations; Śvetāmbaras add not eating at night.
