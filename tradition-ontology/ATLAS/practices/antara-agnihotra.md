# The inner fire-offering of Pratardana

`prc:antara-agnihotra` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Recognizing that while one speaks one cannot breathe and while one breathes one cannot speak, one regards speaking as offering breath into speech and breathing as offering speech into breath — two endless offerings made continually, waking or asleep, in place of the outer agnihotra.
