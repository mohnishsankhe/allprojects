# Worship of Hari with qualities according to fitness

`prc:guna-upasana-dvaita` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)

Meditative worship (upāsanā) of Hari under the qualities fit for the worshipper: Brahmā with all qualities, humans as bliss (ānanda), knower (jña) and the ever-existing Self (sadātmā); the gods are liberated when Hari becomes directly perceived through such worship.
