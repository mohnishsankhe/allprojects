# Initiation (dīkṣā) into Hari's worship

`prc:diksa-dvaita` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)

The ācārya initiates a qualified devotee who has undergone the saṃskāras: the disciple sips the Lord's foot-water, receives mantra and vows, and thereafter worships; the Kṛṣṇāmṛtamahārṇava calls those without Hari's initiation 'beasts among men'.
**Prerequisites:** ['devotion', 'saṃskāras', 'a qualified ācārya']
