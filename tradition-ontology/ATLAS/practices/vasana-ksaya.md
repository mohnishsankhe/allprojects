# Cessation of vāsanās and saṃskāras as the text states it

`prc:vasana-ksaya` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

The text states that vāsanās are held together by cause, fruit, support and object and cease when these are absent (4.11); that the earlier saṃskāra whose seed is burnt by the fire of knowledge no longer yields cognition (bhāṣya 4.28); and that with the destruction of the seeds of saṃskāra no other cognitions arise and dharma-megha follows (4.29). It gives no separate method here.
