# Destruction of latent tendencies (vāsanā-kṣaya)

`prc:vasana-ksaya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
