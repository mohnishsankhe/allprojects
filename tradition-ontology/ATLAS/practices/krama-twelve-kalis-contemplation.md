# Krama contemplation of the twelve Kālīs

`prc:krama-twelve-kalis-contemplation` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Krama (the 'Sequence'; Mahānaya, Mahārtha)](../lineages/krama.md), [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md)
**Taught in:** [Krama (the 'Sequence'; Mahānaya, Mahārtha)](../lineages/krama.md), [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md)

Contemplating every act of cognition as the sequence of the twelve Kālīs - emission, persistence, withdrawal and the nameless across object, means of knowledge and subject - until the sequence dissolves into the sequenceless (TĀ 4.148-172; Abhinavagupta's Kramastotra).
**Stage:** śāktopāya (Abhinavagupta)
