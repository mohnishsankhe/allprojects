# Nectar meditations for the conquest of death (mṛtyuñjaya)

`prc:amrta-candra-dhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** visualization-deity
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md)
**Taught in:** [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md)

Contemplating white, cool nectar pouring from the sky through the openings of the body, or oneself seated on a white lotus in the ocean of milk sprinkled from a lotus above, or the full moon at navel, head and heart; said to remove disease, old age and death.
**Stage:** intermediate
**Signs of progress:** freedom from disease; youthfulness; forms at will (KJN 5)
**Sources:**
