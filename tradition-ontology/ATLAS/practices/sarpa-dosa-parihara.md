# Remedies for the serpents' displeasure

`prc:sarpa-dosa-parihara` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [The Kerala tradition of jyotiṣa](../lineages/kerala-jyotisa.md)

When a query or chart shows affliction from harm to serpents or neglect of serpent-shrines, the Kerala texts prescribe worship and offerings to the serpents, restoring their groves and shrines, installing serpent images, and rites such as sarpa-bali.
