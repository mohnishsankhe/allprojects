# Enduring the sense-contacts (titikṣā)

**Taught in:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

The contacts of the senses give cold and heat, pleasure and pain; they come and go and are impermanent — endure them (2.14). The one they do not disturb is fit for immortality (2.15).
