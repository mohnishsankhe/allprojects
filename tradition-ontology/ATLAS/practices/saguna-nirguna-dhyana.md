# Meditation with and without qualities

`prc:saguna-nirguna-dhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Meditation on a form (the Lord in the heart, Maheśvara as oneself) or on the Self's nature without form, leading to samādhi (Yogatattva 104; Śāṇḍilya 1.71; Darśana 9).
