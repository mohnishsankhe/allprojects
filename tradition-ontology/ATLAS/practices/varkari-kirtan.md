# Vārkarī kīrtan

`prc:varkari-kirtan` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)
**Taught in:** [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)

A kīrtankār takes an abhaṅga as text and expounds it in song and speech, illustrated by the saints' lives, while a chorus with cymbals (ṭāḷ) and drum repeats the refrain and the name ('Jñānobā Tukārām', 'Rāma Kṛṣṇa Hari'); it ends with the name and the Pasāydān. Nāmdev is remembered as its founder.
