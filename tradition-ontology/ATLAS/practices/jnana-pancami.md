# Veneration of scripture (Jñāna Pañcamī / Śruta Pañcamī)

`prc:jnana-pancami` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md)

Once a year manuscripts and books are cleaned, displayed and worshipped: Śvetāmbaras on the fifth of bright Kārttika (Jñāna Pañcamī), Digambaras on the fifth of bright Jyeṣṭha (Śruta Pañcamī), the day the Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama was completed and worshipped.
**Stage:** all
