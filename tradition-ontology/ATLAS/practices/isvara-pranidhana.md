# Devotion to Īśvara (īśvara-praṇidhāna)

`prc:isvara-pranidhana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

Devotion, a special kind of bhakti, by which Īśvara favours the yogin by mere intent thought (YS 1.23, YBh 1.23); in kriyā-yoga it is the offering of all actions to the supreme teacher or the renouncing of their fruit (YBh 2.1, 2.32). Through it the obstacles do not arise and the yogin sees his own form (YS 1.29); it brings the perfection of samādhi (YS 2.45).
