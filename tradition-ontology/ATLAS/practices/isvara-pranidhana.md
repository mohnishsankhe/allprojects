# Devotion to Īśvara (īśvara-praṇidhāna)

`prc:isvara-pranidhana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

A special devotion (bhakti) to Īśvara, offering all actions to the supreme teacher or renouncing their fruit (YBh 1.23, 2.1; RM 1.23); Īśvara, won over, favours the yogin by mere intention and samādhi is near (1.23); a niyama and part of kriyā-yoga (2.1, 2.32).
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** realization of the inward consciousness and absence of obstacles (1.29); perfection of samādhi (2.45)
