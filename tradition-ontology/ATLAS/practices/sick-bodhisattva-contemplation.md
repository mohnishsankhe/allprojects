# The sick bodhisattva's contemplation

`prc:sick-bodhisattva-contemplation` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)

When ill, seeing the illness as arising from clinging to 'I' and 'mine', removing the illness but not the dharmas, and extending the insight that all beings' illness is likewise unreal into compassion.

  - [Vimalakīrtinirdeśa](../texts/vimalakirtinirdesa.md) — ref: 4.12-15; rests_on: ["tea:vimalakirtinirdesa:4.12"]
