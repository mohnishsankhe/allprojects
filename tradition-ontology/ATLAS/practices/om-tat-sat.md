# Performing rites with 'oṃ tat sat'

**Taught in:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Summary as the text states it: the acts of sacrifice, giving and austerity of the expounders of Brahman begin with the utterance of 'oṃ' (17.24); with 'tat', those seeking release perform sacrifice, austerity and giving without aiming at fruit (17.25); 'sat' is applied to what is real and good, to praiseworthy acts, to steadfastness in sacrifice, austerity and giving, and to action for that purpose (17.26–27). The three words are the threefold designation of Brahman (17.23).
