# Prasaṅkhyāna (repeated contemplation after verbal knowledge)

`prc:prasankhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Bhāmatī school (Bhāmatī-prasthāna)](../lineages/bhamati.md)

In Maṇḍana's view, repeatedly contemplating the knowledge obtained from the Upaniṣads until it becomes direct and destroys the impressions of difference; regarded as unnecessary for the fully qualified by Śaṅkara (US 18) and Sureśvara.
