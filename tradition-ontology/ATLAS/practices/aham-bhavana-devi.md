# Contemplation 'I am you' (identity with the Goddess)

`prc:aham-bhavana-devi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md), [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Taught in:** [Śākta traditions](../lineages/sakta.md), [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)

The worshipper contemplates himself as the Goddess ('I am you'), surrounded by her powers as rays of his own body (SL 96); in worship with the names the principal thing is to contemplate the identity of oneself and the deity (Bhāskararāya).
