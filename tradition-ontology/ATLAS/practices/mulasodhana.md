# Mūlaśodhana (cleansing the root)

The anus is washed with water again and again, using a stalk of turmeric or the middle finger (GS 1.42-44).
**Stage:** beginner
**Signs of progress:** ["removes the 'harshness' of apāna, hardness of the bowels and indigestion; gives beauty and nourishment; kindles the fire (GS 1.42-44)"]
