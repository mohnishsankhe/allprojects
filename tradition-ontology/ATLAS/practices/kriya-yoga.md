# Kriyā-yoga (austerity, self-study, devotion to Īśvara)

`prc:kriya-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

For the unconcentrated mind: austerity, self-study and devotion to Īśvara together (2.1), to cultivate samādhi and thin the afflictions (2.2); the yogin should first be intent on it (RM 2.1).
**Stage:** beginner (the vyutthita-citta)
**Signs of progress:** afflictions thinned, like seeds burnt (YBh 2.2)
