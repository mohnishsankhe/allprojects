# Kriyā-yoga (austerity, self-study, devotion to Īśvara)

`prc:kriya-yoga` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

Austerity (tapas), self-study (svādhyāya) and devotion to Īśvara (YS 2.1): for the practitioner whose mind is not yet concentrated. Practised, it brings samādhi into being and thins the afflictions, making them like burnt seeds (YS 2.2, YBh 2.2).
**Stage:** beginner
**Signs of progress:** afflictions thinned, like seeds burnt (YBh 2.2)
