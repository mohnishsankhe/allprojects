## The texts' own warnings
- The deluded who, attached to the bliss of union, call it the ultimate are like one who asks for news of Kāmarūpa at his own door. — [Dohākoṣa-nāma-caryāgīti of Saraha (the 'King Dohā')](../texts/dohakosa-king-saraha.md) v21
- The secret and wisdom consecrations are not to be taken by celibates; doing so destroys their vows and leads to bad destinies. — [Lamp for the Path to Awakening (Bodhipathapradīpa; byang chub lam gyi sgron ma)](../texts/bodhipathapradipa.md) 65-67
- Tilopa: the gnosis arises only when there is no attachment (de la zhen med). — [Mahāmudropadeśa of Tilopa (the Gaṅgā Mahāmudrā)](../texts/ganga-mahamudra.md) v27
- The heedless, those driven by craving, dependent on another and intoxicated fall from the meaning and again to the lower realms. — [Karṇatantravajrapada (the Vajra Verses of the Whispered Lineage)](../texts/karnatantravajrapada.md) 303b.5
- The method-path instruction is not given to all; it is sealed three times. — [bka' dpe phyi ma (Nāropa's instructions received from Tilopa)](../texts/bka-dpe-phyi-ma.md) 276a.6
- The secret and wisdom empowerments must not be taken by celibates; one who does breaks the vow of austerity, incurs defeat, falls to the lower realms and gains no accomplishment. — [Lamp for the Path to Awakening (Bodhipathapradīpa; byang chub lam gyi sgron ma)](../texts/bodhipathapradipa.md) v66-68
