# Mind-training transference at death (the five powers)

`prc:lojong-at-death` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md)
**Taught in:** [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md)

At death one gives away one's possessions (white seed), confesses and purifies, and applies the five powers — resolve, familiarity, white seed, repudiation, aspiration — lying in the lion posture and practising sending and taking, then resting in the nature of mind.
