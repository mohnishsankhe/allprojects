# Meditation on the deity's form

`prc:rupa-dhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** visualization-deity
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md), [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)

Holding the mind on the form of the Lord in the heart or before the mind's eye, limb by limb from the feet to the smiling face, until it does not waver; then simplifying the form (without weapons, without ornaments, one limb) or dwelling on the face (BhP 2.2.8-14; 3.28.12-33; VP 6.7.75-90).
**Stage:** intermediate; after ethical preparation, posture and breath
**Prerequisites:** ['yama and niyama', 'mastery of posture and breath', 'withdrawal of the senses']
**Signs of progress:** ['the form stays in mind whether walking, standing or acting (VP 6.7.87)']
