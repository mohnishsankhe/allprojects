# Anupāya: realization without means

For one of the most intense grace, a single hearing of the teacher's word, or the conviction arising from scripture or reasoning, reveals one's ever-present nature; nothing further is to be done - no instrument and not even contemplation (Śivadṛṣṭi 7.5-6; TĀ 2).
**Stage:** the highest grade of śaktipāta
