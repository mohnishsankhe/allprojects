# Installation and consecration (pratiṣṭhā)

`prc:pratistha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 5 independent lineage(s): [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md)
**Taught in:** [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Pāñcarātra](../lineages/pancaratra.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md), [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md)

The series of rites that establish the deity in a liṅga or image and its temple: preparatory rites on the image, its immersion and rest, installation on the pedestal, infusion of the deity through mantras, and the first worship; to be performed throughout with a single root scripture (Śaiva rule), by the tantri alone (Kerala).

## The texts' own warnings
- Installation done with a sub-division (upabheda) instead of a root āgama destroys the performer and the patron. — [Kāmikāgama](../texts/kamika-agama.md) purva.1.104-107
