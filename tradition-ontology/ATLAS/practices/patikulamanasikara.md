# Reflection on the parts of the body (paṭikūlamanasikāra)

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Reviewing the body from the soles up and the hair down as full of impurities — the thirty-one/thirty-two parts — like a bag of mixed grains (MN 10; Khp 3); counters lust (MN 62).
