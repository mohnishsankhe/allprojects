# Contemplation of oneself as the reflection of Nārāyaṇa

`prc:bimba-pratibimba-dhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)

Seated upright during the purifications before worship, one contemplates Nārāyaṇa in the lotus of the heart and one's own self before him as his reflection, and prays: 'You are the original, I am the reflection, though there is a difference between us; flawless Lord, separate my faults from me.'
