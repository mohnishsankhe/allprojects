# The kusāli's accumulation (offering the body)

`prc:kusali-offering` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)

The renunciant's offering: visualizing one's consciousness as a wrathful ḍākinī who cuts off the body's skull as a cauldron, transforms the body into nectar and offers it to the buddhas and to all guests, cutting attachment to the body. A visualization only; related to Chöd (owned by U48).
