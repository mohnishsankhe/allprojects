Bhagavad Gītā 10–12: Asking how he may know the Lord while constantly contemplating him, Arjuna asks in which beings and states the Lord is to be thought of (10.17). The Lord names himself as the self in all beings and as the chief of each class of gods, seers, beings, things, sounds, times and qualities (10.20–39), and gives a rule for all else: whatever being is glorious or mighty is to be known as sprung from a portion of his splendour (10.41). Whoever knows his manifestation and yoga in truth is joined with unwavering yoga (10.7).
