# Seeking and serving the spiritual friend

`prc:serving-the-spiritual-friend` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): `lin:huayan`, [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)
**Taught in:** `lin:huayan`, [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)

Seeking out good friends without weariness and honoring them — Sudhana's pilgrimage and Sadāprarudita's sacrifice are the models; the buddhas and perfections are the bodhisattva's true friends.
**Sources:**

## The texts' own warnings
- New bodhisattvas in the hands of bad friends become frightened of the profound teaching. — [Aṣṭasāhasrikā Prajñāpāramitā](../texts/astasahasrika-prajnaparamita.md) ch. 1 (Vaidya p. 9)
