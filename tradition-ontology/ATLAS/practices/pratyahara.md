The senses' seeming imitation of the mind's own form when not in contact with their own objects (YS 2.54). Vyāsa: when the mind is stilled the senses are stilled like it, needing no other means, as bees follow the king of the bees (madhukara-rāja) (YBh 2.54); from it, supreme mastery of the senses (YS 2.55), of which Vyāsa gives four views (YBh 2.55).
