# The five great daily sacrifices

`prc:panca-mahayajna` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)

Each day the householder studies/teaches the Veda (to Brahman), offers water to the ancestors, an oblation into fire to the gods, food-offerings (bali) to beings and hospitality to guests (to men) (ŚB 11.5.6.1–3; TA 2.10; MDh 3.68–70).
