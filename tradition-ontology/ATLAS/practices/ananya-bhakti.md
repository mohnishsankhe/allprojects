# Undivided devotion (ananya-bhakti)

Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: worship of the Lord with mind on nothing else — the knower 'of single devotion' (eka-bhakti, 7.17); constant remembrance with thought on nothing else (ananya-cetas, 8.14); the supreme Person is attainable by undivided devotion (8.22); the great souls worship him with undistracted mind, knowing him as the imperishable origin of beings (9.13); for those who think of him with no other and worship him he carries gain and security (9.22); even one of very bad conduct who worships him with undivided devotion is to be deemed good, having resolved rightly (9.30); summed up in 9.34: mind on him, devoted to him, sacrificing to him, bowing to him, with him as the supreme aim.
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** ['quickly becomes righteous (dharmātman) and attains lasting peace (9.31)', 'the Lord bears his gain and security (9.22)']
