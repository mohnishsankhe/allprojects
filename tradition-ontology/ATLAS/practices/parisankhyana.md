# Parisaṃkhyāna meditation (Upadeśasāhasrī)

`prc:parisankhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)

Reviewing each object of the senses as not-self and changing and oneself as pure, unrelated, changeless awareness, so as to cease identifying with them and to stop generating merit and demerit.
