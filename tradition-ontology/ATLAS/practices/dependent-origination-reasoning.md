# The reasoning of dependent origination

`prc:dependent-origination-reasoning` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)

Because a thing arises in dependence it is empty of own-nature, and because it is empty it can arise: reflecting that nothing is not dependently arisen and so nothing is non-empty, one avoids both eternalism and annihilationism.
