# Meditation on the qualified Brahman (saguṇa-upāsanā)

`prc:saguna-upasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md)

Sustained meditation on Brahman with attributes or through a symbol (Oṃ, the sun, the mind), seated, one-pointed, in any suitable place, continued until death; leads to the Brahma-world and gradual liberation, and prepares the mind for knowledge.
