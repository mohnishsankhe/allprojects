# Purity of food (āhāraśuddhi)

`prc:ahara-suddhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

When food (what is taken in) is pure, the mind is pure; when the mind is pure, memory is firm; with firm memory all knots are loosened (ChU 7.26.2).
**Signs of progress:** firm memory (dhruvā smṛti); loosening of all knots
