# Naming by the birth-asterism

`prc:naksatra-namakarana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md), [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)

At the naming sacrament a name is given — often a secret or ritual name besides the public one — beginning with the syllable assigned to the quarter of the asterism in which the child was born; the Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa already speaks of the sacrificer's asterism-name.
