# Seeing the faults (doṣa-darśana)

`prc:dosa-darsana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Taught in:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)

Reflecting on the faults in acquiring, guarding and losing objects, in attachment, and in the harm enjoyment does to beings, so that passion subsides; passion is not quieted by enjoyment but by seeing the faults of both.
**Stage:** all
