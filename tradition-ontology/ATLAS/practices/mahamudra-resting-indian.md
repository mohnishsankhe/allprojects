# Resting in mahāmudrā (the Indian siddha instruction)

`prc:mahamudra-resting-indian` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md)
**Taught in:** [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md)

Cutting the root of one's own mind and leaving awareness naked; letting the muddy water of concepts clear; neither blocking nor affirming appearances; relaxing body, speech and mind; mind without aim; looking at the sky-like nature until 'seeing' ceases; amanasikāra — not engaging concepts.
**Stage:** advanced
**Prerequisites:** ["the true guru's instruction", 'renunciation (Gaṅgāmā v2–3)']
**Signs of progress:** ['torrent, gentle river, ocean (Gaṅgāmā v21)', 'thoughts dissolve like clouds in space (v10)']
