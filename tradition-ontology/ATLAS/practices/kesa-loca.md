# Keśa-loca (plucking the hair)

`prc:kesa-loca` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)
**Taught in:** [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md)

At initiation and periodically thereafter the ascetic plucks out the hair of head and face by hand, as endurance of pain and freedom from bodily care; one of the twenty-eight basic qualities of the Digambara monk.
**Stage:** ascetics
**Sources:** 
  - [Mūlācāra](../texts/mulacara.md) — ref: 1.2-3; rests_on: ["tea:mulacara:1.2-3"]
