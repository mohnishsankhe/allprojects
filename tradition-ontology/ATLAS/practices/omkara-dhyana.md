# Meditation on Oṃ (MkP 42)

`prc:omkara-dhyana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

Repeat and contemplate the one syllable Oṃ: its three measures (a, u, m) and the half-measure beyond the guṇas felt at the crown; breath the bow, self the arrow, Brahman the target; become one with the imperishable.
