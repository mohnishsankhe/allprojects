# The yellow kasiṇa (pīta-kasiṇa)

`prc:kasina-yellow` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Gazing at yellow flowers, cloth or a coloured disk, the meditator repeats its name and adverts to it with eyes open and closed until the learning sign arises, then develops it away from the device until the counterpart sign and access appear, extends the sign, and reaches the four (Abhidhamma: five) jhānas; used as a basis for supernormal power (turning things to gold).
**Stage:** intermediate
**Prerequisites:** ['purified virtue', 'impediments severed', 'subject received from a teacher']
