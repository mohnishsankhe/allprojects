# Contemplation of the body as the Śrīcakra (Bhāvanā Upaniṣad)

`prc:bhavana-sricakra-deha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Taught in:** [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md), [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md), [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)

Each part of the Śrīcakra and each deity is identified with a constituent of one's body and mind (channels, vital airs, fires, qualities, subtle elements, inner organ, lunar days); the offerings are made as inner acts; the worship is the contemplation of the non-difference of knower, knowing and known. Bhāskararāya wrote a procedure (prayoga) for it.
**Stage:** advanced
**Prerequisites:** ['initiation (dīkṣā) from a qualified guru into the relevant vidyā']
**Duration:** three muhūrtas of absorption (as the Upaniṣad states)
**Signs of progress:** ['identity with the deity; intentions fulfilled without effort; called a Śiva-yogin (Bhāvanā Upaniṣad)']
