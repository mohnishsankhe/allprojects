# Worship of the iṣṭaliṅga on the palm

`prc:istalinga-puja` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)
**Taught in:** [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md), [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md), [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)

Daily worship of one's own liṅga held on the left palm (karapīṭha) with water, ash, flowers and the like, without invocation or dismissal because Śiva already abides in it; the palm-worship is ranked above worship on an outer pedestal.
**Stage:** all
