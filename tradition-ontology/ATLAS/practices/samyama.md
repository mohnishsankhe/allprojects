Saṃyama is concentration, meditation and samādhi on one object (3.4). The text says it is applied to the stage next after the one mastered, not skipping to far stages (bhāṣya 3.6), and gives the objects on which saṃyama is said to bring various results (3.16 onward). The text gives no procedure beyond this.
