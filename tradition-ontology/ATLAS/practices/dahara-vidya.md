# Meditation on the small space in the heart (dahara-vidyā)

`prc:dahara-vidya` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Śiva-viśiṣṭādvaita (Śaiva Vedānta of Śrīkaṇṭha)](../lineages/siva-visistadvaita.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Śiva-viśiṣṭādvaita (Śaiva Vedānta of Śrīkaṇṭha)](../lineages/siva-visistadvaita.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Seek and understand what is within the small space in the lotus of the heart, in this 'city of brahman': as large as outer space, containing heaven and earth; the self free from evil, old age, death, sorrow, hunger and thirst, whose desires and intentions are real.
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** freedom of movement in all worlds; whatever one desires arises by intention (ChU 8.1.6-8.2)
