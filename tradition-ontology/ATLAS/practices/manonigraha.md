# Restraint of the mind (manonigraha) in the Kārikā

`prc:manonigraha` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)

The Kārikā makes fearlessness, ending of sorrow, awakening and peace depend on restraint of the mind (3.40); to be done patiently, without weariness (3.41), by a means; distraction in desire and enjoyment is restrained and laya treated like desire (3.42); remember that all is sorrow and that all is unborn (3.43); awaken the mind in laya, calm it when distracted, know the mind as sakaṣāya, do not stir it at evenness (3.44); do not relish the happiness but be unattached by discernment and unify the mind (3.45); when the mind is neither absorbed nor distracted, that is brahman (3.46).
**Stage:** intermediate
**Signs of progress:** When the mind is neither absorbed nor distracted, motionless and without appearance, that is brahman (3.46).
