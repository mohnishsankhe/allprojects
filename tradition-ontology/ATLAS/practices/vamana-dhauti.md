# Vamana-dhauti (washing by vomiting)

`prc:vamana-dhauti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** cleansing
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

After a meal water is drunk to the throat, the gaze turned upward for a moment, and the water vomited (GS 1.39).
**Stage:** beginner
**Signs of progress:** ['removes phlegm and bile (GS 1.39)']
**Sources:** 
  - [Gheraṇḍa Saṃhitā](../texts/gheranda-samhita.md) — ref: 1.39; rests_on: ["tea:gheranda-samhita:1.39"]

## Equivalents (interpretation layer)
- partial: [Gajakaraṇī ('the elephant's act')](gajakarani.md) — Both empty the stomach upward; gajakaraṇī does it by raising apāna to the throat, vamana-dhauti after drinking water.

_Notes: Recent teaching calls a comparable practice kuñjala; that name is not in the Gheraṇḍa._
