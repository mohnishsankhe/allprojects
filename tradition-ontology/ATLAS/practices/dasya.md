# Servitude to the Lord (dāsya)

Relating to the Lord as his servant and serving him. Seventh of the Bhāgavata's nine forms. For the Āḻvārs servitude (aṭimai) is the soul's nature: 'at all times without break we must do faultless service' (TVM 3.3.1); the drum the girls ask is service for seven times seven births (TP 29); the Āḻvārs ask to serve the Lord's servants. The Nārada Sūtra: love as an eternal servant (NBS 66).
