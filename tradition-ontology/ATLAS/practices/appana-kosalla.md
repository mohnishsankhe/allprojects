# The ten skills in absorption (appanā-kosalla)

`prc:appana-kosalla` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Making the basis clean, balancing the faculties (faith with wisdom, energy with concentration; mindfulness everywhere), skill in the sign, exerting, restraining and encouraging the mind when needed, equanimity when it runs evenly, avoiding distracted people, cultivating concentrated people, resolving upon concentration.
