# Ajahn Lee's breath meditation (Method 2)

`prc:ajahn-lee-breath-method` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Thai Forest tradition (Kammaṭṭhāna forest tradition)](../lineages/thai-forest.md)

Begin with 'buddho' and a few long breaths; observe and adjust the breath until comfortable; attend to its resting spots (nose tip, middle of the head, palate, base of the throat, breastbone, navel); spread the breath energy through the whole body; keep the mind still in the breath and develop discernment.
**Stage:** beginner
