# Praying for the Lord's welfare (maṅgaḷāśāsana)

`prc:mangalasasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)
**Taught in:** [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

Out of overflowing love, the devotee prays for the Lord's own long life and safety — 'many years, many years' (Tiruppallāṇṭu) — rather than for anything for himself, and invites other devotees to join. The Āḻvārs' hymns to each temple are called their maṅgaḷāśāsana of that site; the Tiruppallāṇṭu opens Śrīvaiṣṇava daily worship.
