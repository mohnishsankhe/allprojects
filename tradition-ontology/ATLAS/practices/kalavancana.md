# Cheating death (kālavañcana)

`prc:kalavancana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

The texts' means of postponing or conquering death: breath held in the body (HYP 2.39–40), preserving bindu and nectar, the element-concentrations that remove fear of each element (DYŚ 100–110), and (HTK) knowledge of the signs of approaching death to anticipate it.
