# Hut-entering rejuvenation (kuṭīprāveśika)

`prc:kutipravesika-rasayana` · `skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

The principal mode of rasāyana: after preliminary rites, with shaved head, steadied resolve and a mind freed of its doṣas and turned to friendliness toward beings, the person enters a specially built secluded hut, is purified by cleansing therapies and then takes the rejuvenative under a physician's care. Summary only: construction, diet, preparations and durations are not recorded.
**Stage:** advanced
**Prerequisites:** capacity, health, wisdom, self-control, leisure and means (Ci 1.4.27)
**Sources:**

## The texts' own warnings
- The better mode but very hard to carry out; only for the capable and self-controlled. — [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) Ci 1.4.27-28
- Diseases arising from lapses in the regimen are to be treated apart from the rasāyana. — [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) Ci 1.4.29
