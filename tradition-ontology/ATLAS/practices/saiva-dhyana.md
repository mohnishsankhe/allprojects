# Śaiva meditation with and without object

`prc:saiva-dhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

Meditation on Śiva's form (Śrīkaṇṭha) first, with an object and a seed, and at the end without object or seed, as awareness of the formless self; knowing the meditator, the act, the object and the purpose.
