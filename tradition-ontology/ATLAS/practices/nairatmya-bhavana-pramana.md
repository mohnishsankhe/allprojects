# Cultivation of the vision of selflessness

`prc:nairatmya-bhavana-pramana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
**Taught in:** [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)

Seeing selflessness is the antidote that uproots self-love and its faults; cultivated long, in many ways, it makes virtues and faults clear and removes the imprints; liberation comes from seeing emptiness.
