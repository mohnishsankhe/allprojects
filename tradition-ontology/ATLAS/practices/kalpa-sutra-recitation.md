# Public reading of the Kalpa Sūtra

`prc:kalpa-sutra-recitation` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md)

During Paryuṣaṇa a mendicant reads and explains the Kalpa Sūtra (with the Subodhikā or similar commentary) to the congregation over several days; the reading of Mahāvīra's birth, with the display of the fourteen dreams, is a festive high point; on Saṃvatsarī the Prakrit 'Bārasa Sūtra' is recited.
