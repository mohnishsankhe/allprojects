# Counting the breath (gaṇanā), first attention of mindfulness of breathing

`prc:anapanasati-counting` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

First of the stages of attention (Vism VIII p. 278-280). The beginner counts the breaths as they are noticed: first slowly, like a grain-measurer, then, when the breaths are evident, quickly, like a herdsman counting cattle at the gate, taking only the breaths at the place they touch (the gate), not following them inside or outside; for a subject bound by counting, consciousness becomes one-pointed by the power of counting, like a boat held in a strong current by a rudder. The counting continues until, even without counting, mindfulness stays on the in- and out-breath; its purpose is only to cut off scattered outside thoughts and establish mindfulness on the object.
