# Jātakarma (birth rite)

`prc:jatakarma` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)

At birth, before the cord is cut, the father gives the child honey and ghee, whispers 'speech' in its ear and wishes it long life and intelligence.
