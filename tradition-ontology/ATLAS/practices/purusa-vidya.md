# The meditation on the person as sacrifice (puruṣa-vidyā)

`prc:purusa-vidya` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Regard one's life as a soma sacrifice: the first twenty-four years as the morning pressing, the next forty-four as the midday, the next forty-eight as the evening; hunger and thirst as consecration; austerity, giving, uprightness, non-injury and truthfulness as the priestly gifts; death as the final bath.
