# Śāṇḍilya's meditation (Śāṇḍilya-vidyā)

`prc:sandilya-vidya` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

In tranquillity, venerate all this as brahman — that from which it arises, into which it dissolves and by which it breathes; form one's resolve, since one becomes on departing what one resolves here; meditate on the self in the heart, made of mind, with breath as body and light as form, smaller than a grain and greater than the worlds, as brahman to be attained on departing, without doubt.
