# The six preparatory practices of a lamrim session

`prc:six-preparatory-practices` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md)
**Taught in:** [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md)

Cleaning the room and setting up representations of body, speech and mind; arranging offerings; sitting in the proper posture and taking refuge and generating bodhicitta; visualising the field of merit; offering the seven limbs and the maṇḍala; making requests to the lineage.
