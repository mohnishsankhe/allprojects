# Gifts for the planets (graha-dāna)

`prc:graha-dana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md), [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)

Giving to brahmins the gift proper to the afflicting planet — objects, grains, cloth, metals or animals of its colour and nature — as part of the planetary sacrifice or on its own; also great gifts of gold, food, cows and land to pacify heavenly portents.
