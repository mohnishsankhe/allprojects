# Performance of obligatory duties for purification

`prc:nitya-naimittika-karma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [Vedānta (Uttara Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/vedanta.md)

Performing the daily and occasional rites and one's duties without desire for their fruits, offered to the Lord, avoiding desire-prompted and forbidden acts, for the purification of mind that makes one fit for knowledge.
