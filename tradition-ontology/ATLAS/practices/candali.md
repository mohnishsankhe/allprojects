## The texts' own warnings
- To be received only from the guru after consecration; revealing it to the unripe is a root downfall. — [Advayavajrasaṃgraha](../texts/advayavajrasamgraha.md) 2.mulapatti
- Saraha mocks those who force the winds and 'call themselves yogins out of pride' (King Dohā v24) and those who take experiences for the ultimate (v25–26). — [Dohākoṣa-nāma-caryāgīti of Saraha (the 'King Dohā')](../texts/dohakosa-king-saraha.md) v24
- To be practised only after the common path, a complete empowerment and the generation stage, under a teacher; wind practices done wrongly cause wind disorders. — [A Book of Three Inspirations (zab lam nā ro'i chos drug gi khrid rim yid ches gsum ldan)](../texts/three-inspirations-naropa.md) warning
