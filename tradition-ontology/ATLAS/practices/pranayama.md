# Breath-regulation (prāṇāyāma)

`prc:pranayama` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md), [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md), [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

With posture mastered, cutting off the movement of inhalation and exhalation (2.49): external, internal or suspended, observed by place, time and number, becoming long and subtle (2.50, YBh 2.50). Summary only; no measures or counts.
**Stage:** intermediate
**Prerequisites:** mastery of posture (2.49)
**Signs of progress:** the covering of the light dwindles (2.52); the mind becomes fit for concentration (2.53)
