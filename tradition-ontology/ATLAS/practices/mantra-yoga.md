# Mantrayoga of the haṭha texts

`prc:mantra-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

Placing the mātṛkā letters on the limbs and repeating a mantra (DYŚ 11); the Śiva Saṃhitā adds a mantra practice with seed-syllables meditated in the centres (5.188ff). Counts of repetition are not recorded here.

## The texts' own warnings
- Mantrayoga is the lowest yoga, for the dull; mantra-sādhana is listed among the obstacles at the start of practice. — [Dattātreyayogaśāstra](../texts/dattatreyayogasastra.md) 13; 47
