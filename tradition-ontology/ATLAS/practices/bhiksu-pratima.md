# The twelve special vows of the monk (bhikṣu-pratimā)

`prc:bhiksu-pratima` · `skeleton` · confidence low · _restricted: summary only_

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)

A series of twelve progressively severe undertakings for qualified monks, restricting alms to one or a few 'portions' of food and drink for a month or more at a time and ending in intense night-long standing meditation (restricted: prolonged fasting — summary only).
**Stage:** advanced
**Sources:** 
  - [Daśāśrutaskandha](../texts/dasasrutaskandha.md) — ref: 7

## The texts' own warnings
