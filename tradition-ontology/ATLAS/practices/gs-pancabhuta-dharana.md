# The five element-concentrations (Gorakṣaśataka)

**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)

Holding the mind with the breath on earth (heart, yellow, square, la), water (throat, white crescent, va), fire (palate, red triangle, ra), air (between the brows, smoky circle, ya) and space (brahmarandhra, clear, ha, with Sadāśiva); they stabilise, dissolve, burn, whirl and dry, and the space-concentration opens the door of liberation.
