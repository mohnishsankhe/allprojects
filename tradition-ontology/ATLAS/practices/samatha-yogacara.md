# Calm abiding (śamatha) in Yogācāra sources

`prc:samatha-yogacara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
**Taught in:** [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)

Calm is the path of gathering the names of the teachings into one; lifting the sunken mind, calming the agitated one, and leaving it in equanimity when calm; it culminates in pliancy and the basic absorption.
