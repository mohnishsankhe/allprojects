# The mindless yoga (amanaska / rājayoga of the Amanaska)

Letting the mind rest without object, with a steady gaze that fixes on nothing, by the guru's grace, until the mind dissolves and the breath stops of itself (Amanaska 2); without the laborious methods of haṭha.
