# Seasonal routine (ṛtucaryā)

`prc:rtucarya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Adjust diet and conduct to the six seasons: in the 'taking' half (late winter to summer) strength declines and the dry tastes grow; in the 'releasing' half (rains to early winter) the moon restores strength; in winter the strong fire needs heavy, unctuous, sweet-sour-salt food; accumulated doṣas are cleared in the following season.
**Stage:** all

## The texts' own warnings
- In winter the strong fire, finding no fuel, consumes the tissues; hence heavy food then (not a general licence). — [Aṣṭāṅga Hṛdaya](../texts/astanga-hrdaya.md) Sū 3.7-8
