# Das Lakṣaṇa Parva

`prc:das-laksana-parva` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md)

Ten days of the Digambara rains festival, each devoted to one of the ten dharmas (supreme forbearance, humility, straightforwardness, purity, truth, restraint, austerity, renunciation, non-possession, chastity), with readings of the Tattvārthasūtra, worship and fasting according to capacity, ending with forgiveness.
**Stage:** all
**Duration:** ten days
