Summary only (restricted). After refuge, bodhicitta and supplication to Machig and the lineage, the practitioner transfers consciousness into the sky as the wrathful ḍākinī, visualizes the body left behind as offered and transformed into nectar, and distributes it to the four guests, then rests in the view in which giver, gift and recipient are empty; sung liturgy with drum, bell and thighbone trumpet. Its purpose is to cut self-clinging, not to subdue spirits.
**Stage:** advanced
