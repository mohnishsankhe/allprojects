# Diet by measure, doṣa and quality

`prc:ahara-vidhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Eat in the measure the fire can digest in due time; eat what is wholesome — what keeps balanced tissues balanced and restores the unbalanced — chosen by the twenty qualities (guṇa) and six tastes opposite to the provoked doṣa, and suited to constitution, season and habit; observe the eight factors (nature, preparation, combination, quantity, place, time, rules of eating, the eater); avoid incompatible foods. The mind's qualities also follow diet: the tāmasa and rājasa mental types are marked by greed for food.

## The texts' own warnings
- Incompatible food (e.g. fish with milk) vitiates the blood and can be fatal; whatever provokes the doṣas without expelling them is unwholesome. — [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) Sū 26.82-85
