# Feigned sleep and snoring (krāthana)

`prc:krathana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

While awake he acts as if asleep, making a snoring sound in the throat with the out-breath, so that people despise him.
**Stage:** avyakta (second) stage
