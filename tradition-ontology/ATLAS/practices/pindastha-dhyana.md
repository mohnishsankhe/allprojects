# Piṇḍastha-dhyāna (embodied meditation)

`prc:pindastha-dhyana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** visualization-deity
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)

Meditation through the five concentrations: visualising the world as an ocean of milk with a lotus on which one sits; fire arising from 'arhaṃ' burning the lotus of the eight karmas and the body; wind scattering the ashes; rain washing them away; and seeing oneself as the pure, omniscient Siddha.
**Stage:** intermediate–advanced
