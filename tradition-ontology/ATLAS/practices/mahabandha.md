# Mahābandha (the great lock)

`prc:mahabandha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** lock-seal
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

With the left heel at the perineum and the right foot on the left thigh, the breath is filled, the chin set firmly on the chest, the perineum contracted and the mind fixed in the middle; the breath is held as far as able and released slowly, on both sides (HYP 3.19-21; ŚS 4.21-22; DYŚ 123). In the Gheraṇḍa the left ankle blocks the anus and the right foot presses that ankle, with jālandhara (GS 3.18-20). Some teachers replace the throat lock here with the tongue pressed at the root of the front teeth (HYP 3.22).
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** ["stops the upward movement in all channels; releases death's noose; brings about the confluence of the three rivers and leads the mind to Kedāra (HYP 3.23-24)", 'the breath enters the middle of the suṣumnā; the body is nourished (ŚS 4.22)']
