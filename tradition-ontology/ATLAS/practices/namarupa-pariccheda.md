# Defining mentality-materiality (nāmarūpa-pariccheda)

`prc:namarupa-pariccheda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāsi Sayadaw tradition (the 'new Burmese method')](../lineages/mahasi.md), [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md), [Pa-Auk tradition (Pa-Auk Tawya)](../lineages/pa-auk.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Purification of view: discerning the jhāna factors and their associates (calm vehicle) or the four elements (insight vehicle) by characteristic, function, manifestation and proximate cause, until only mentality-materiality is seen and no being or person.
