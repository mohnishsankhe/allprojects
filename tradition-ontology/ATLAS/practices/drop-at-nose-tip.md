# Meditation on the drop at the nose tip

**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Ārya tradition of the Guhyasamāja](../lineages/arya-guhyasamaja.md), [The Mahāsiddhas (the eighty-four great adepts)](../lineages/mahasiddha.md)
