# Holding the mind on the auspicious support (VP 6.7)

`prc:subhasraya-dharana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

With breath and senses mastered, fix the mind on Viṣṇu's form as the ground of all powers, simplifying it stage by stage, until dhyāna (a single continuous flow) and samādhi (grasping the object's own nature free of imagination) arise.
