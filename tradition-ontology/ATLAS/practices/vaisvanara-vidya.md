# Meditation on the self common to all men (vaiśvānara-vidyā)

`prc:vaisvanara-vidya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Venerate the vaiśvānara self as the whole — sky as head, sun as eye, wind as breath, space as trunk, water as bladder, earth as feet — 'a span in size and beyond measure', not as any one of its parts; the first morsels of food are then offered into it as an agnihotra.
**Sources:** 
  - [Chāndogya Upaniṣad](../texts/chandogya-upanisad.md) — ref: 5.11-5.24; rests_on: ["tea:chandogya-upanisad:5.11.1-7", "tea:chandogya-upanisad:5.12.1-5.18.2", "tea:chandogya-upanisad:5.19.1-5.24.4"]

## The texts' own warnings
- Each partial meditation is incomplete, and the king says each would have come to harm (losing head, sight, breath...) without the whole teaching (ChU 5.12-17). — [Chāndogya Upaniṣad](../texts/chandogya-upanisad.md) 5.12-5.17

_Notes: Upaniṣadic 'vidyā' (meditation-knowledge); the Brahma Sūtra (3.3) discusses which vidyās are to be combined._
