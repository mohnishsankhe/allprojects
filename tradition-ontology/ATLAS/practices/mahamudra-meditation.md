After the preliminaries and the guru's pointing-out, the meditator settles the mind (śamatha), looks directly at it (vipaśyanā), recognizes its empty and luminous nature, and sustains that recognition without contrivance or distraction in and between sessions, progressing through the four yogas.
**Stage:** all (graded by the four yogas)
**Prerequisites:** ['the preliminaries (ngöndro)', "the guru's pointing-out and blessing"]
**Signs of progress:** ['experiences of bliss, clarity and non-thought (not to be clung to)', "recognition of thoughts as mind's own display", 'the four yogas: one-pointedness, simplicity, one taste, non-meditation']
