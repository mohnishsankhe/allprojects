# Gorakṣāsana (Gorakṣa's seat)

`prc:goraksasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** posture
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

The feet turned up between the knees and thighs, the heels covered with the upturned hands, the throat contracted and the gaze on the nose-tip (GS 2.24-25). In HYP 1.54 'gorakṣāsana' is the siddhas' name for bhadrāsana.
**Stage:** intermediate
**Signs of progress:** ['a cause of success for yogins (GS 2.25)']
