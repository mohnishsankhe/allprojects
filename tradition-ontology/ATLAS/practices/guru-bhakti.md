# Devotion to the teacher (guru-bhakti)

`prc:guru-bhakti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Hold the highest devotion to God and to one's teacher as to God (ŚU 6.23); honour the teacher as a god (TU 1.11.2); the pupils worship Pippalāda as the father who takes them across (PrU 6.8).
