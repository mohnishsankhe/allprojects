# Kāyotsarga (abandonment of the body)

`prc:kayotsarga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)

Standing upright (or sitting) motionless with arms hanging, eyes on the tip of the nose or closed, indifferent to the body and to hardships, for a period measured by silent recitations (e.g. of the Logassa or Namaskāra) or breaths; part of every liturgy and a form of internal austerity; the Jinas are depicted in this posture.
**Stage:** all
**Duration:** from a few breaths to long periods (ascetics)
**Signs of progress:** ['loosening of identification with the body', 'endurance of hardships']
