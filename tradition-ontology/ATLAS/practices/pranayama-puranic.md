# Purāṇic prāṇāyāma

`prc:pranayama-puranic` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

Breath-mastery in exhalation, inhalation and retention, neither hurried nor slow, after purifying the channels, practised gradually and often joined with mantra (with 'seed') or without; it burns faults and brings calm, the dispelling of darkness, inner light and clarity.

## The texts' own warnings
- Practise neither fast nor slow, step by step. — [Śiva Purāṇa](../texts/siva-purana.md) 7.2.37.25
- Do not practise overfed, hungry, tired or in unsuitable places and times; faults such as deafness, dullness and fever follow. — [Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa](../texts/markandeya-purana.md) 39.47-53
