# Upanayana (initiation into Vedic study)

`prc:upanayana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)

The boy asks to become a student; the teacher takes his hand, invests him with girdle (of muñja grass for a brāhmaṇa), staff, deerskin and (later) sacred thread, and teaches him the Sāvitrī; he is now 'twice-born' with the Sāvitrī as mother and teacher as father. Due at eight, eleven or twelve years for the three classes (ŚB 11.5.4; ĀśGS 1.19–22; MDh 2.36–40; 2.169–171).
