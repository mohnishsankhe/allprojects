# Contemplation of the worm infested corpse (puḷuvaka)

`prc:asubha-worm-infested` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāvihāra fraternity (Mahāvihāra-vāsins)](../lineages/mahavihara.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

The meditator goes to a corpse of this kind, having informed the senior monk and noted the route, grasps the sign in eleven ways with open eyes, develops the learning and counterpart signs and reaches the first jhāna; it counteracts lust and suits the greedy temperament.
**Stage:** intermediate
