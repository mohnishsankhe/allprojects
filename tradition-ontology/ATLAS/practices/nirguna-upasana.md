# Meditation on the attributeless (nirguṇa-upāsanā)

`prc:nirguna-upasana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)

For those unable to inquire: meditating on the attributeless Brahman as 'I am Brahman', which, like following a gem's light, leads to knowledge; also separating being–shining–dear from name and form in all things.
