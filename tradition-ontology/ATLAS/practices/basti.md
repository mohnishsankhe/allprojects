# Basti / jala-basti (the water enema)

`prc:basti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** cleansing
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

Squatting in utkaṭāsana in water up to the navel with a tube in the anus, one contracts the base (ādhāra) and washes (HYP 2.26-28); the Gheraṇḍa's water-basti is done likewise in navel-deep water with contraction and expansion of the anus (GS 1.45-47).
**Stage:** beginner
**Signs of progress:** ['gulma, spleen disorders, dropsy and diseases of vāta, pitta and kapha are destroyed; clarity of tissues, senses and mind; lustre; kindled fire (HYP 2.27-28)', 'removes urinary disorders, gudāvarta and harsh wind (GS 1.47)']
