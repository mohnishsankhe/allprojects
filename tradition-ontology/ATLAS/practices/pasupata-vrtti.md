# The Pāśupata livelihoods

`prc:pasupata-vrtti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

Alms (first and third stages), discarded food (second), whatever comes (fourth); in the fifth there is none. Alms are gathered with purity, avoiding certain houses, and offered to Śiva before eating; he lives after the manner of cows and deer and is purified by water alone.
