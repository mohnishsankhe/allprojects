# Insight (vipaśyanā) in Yogācāra sources

Insight investigates the meanings of the teachings and the reflection-images of meditation: discerning, thoroughly investigating, considering and examining dharmas in their extent and reality, through the four reasonings; united with calm it is the joined path.
