# Śrāddha (offering to the ancestors)

`prc:sraddha-ancestral-rite` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)

Qualified brāhmaṇas are invited and fed as the ancestors' representatives, rice-balls (piṇḍa) are offered to the father, grandfather and great-grandfather, and water and sesame given; performed monthly at the new moon and on death-anniversaries and other occasions (MDh 3.122–286).
