# Prāṇāgnihotra (offering food into the breaths)

`prc:pranagnihotra` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)

At each meal the eater sips water ('immortal under-layer'), offers the first five morsels with 'svāhā' to prāṇa, apāna, vyāna, udāna and samāna, meditating that each breath, sense, deity and region is satisfied, and ends with water ('immortal covering'); eating is thus an Agnihotra offered in the self (ChU 5.19–24; TA 10.69–70).
**Stage:** all twice-born; the knowing eater
**Duration:** daily, at meals
**Signs of progress:** ["'his evils are burned up like a reed-tuft in fire' (ChU 5.24.3)"]
