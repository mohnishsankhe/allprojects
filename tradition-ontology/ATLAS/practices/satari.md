# Receiving the Lord's feet (caṭāri / śaṭhāri)

In Śrīvaiṣṇava temples, the priest places on the worshipper's head a crown-like vessel bearing the Lord's feet, called after Śaṭhakopa (Nammāḻvār), who is held to be the Lord's feet — refuge at the feet received through the Āḻvār.
