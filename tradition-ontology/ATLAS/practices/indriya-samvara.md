# Sense restraint (indriyasaṃvara)

`prc:indriya-samvara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Seeing a form with the eye (and so for each sense) one does not grasp at its signs and features, through which covetousness and grief might invade, but guards the faculty (DN 2; MN 27; MN 39); not blindness but quick equanimity (MN 152).
**Stage:** beginner
