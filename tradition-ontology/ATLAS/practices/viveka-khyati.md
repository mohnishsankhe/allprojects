# Cultivating discriminative discernment (viveka-khyāti)

`prc:viveka-khyati` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry

**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

The cognition of the difference between sattva and puruṣa; unwavering (aviplava) discernment is the means of abandonment (YS 2.26). While false knowledge has not ceased it 'floats'; when false knowledge has come to the burnt-seed state, the stream of discerning cognition becomes stainless (YBh 2.26).
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** the sevenfold final insight (2.27); the mind bends toward kaivalya (4.26)
