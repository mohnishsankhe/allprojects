# Worship with the thousand names (nāma-arcana, kuṅkumārcana)

`prc:sahasranama-arcana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Taught in:** [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)

Offering one flower (or vermilion) into the bindu with each name in the dative with 'namaḥ', after reciting the introductory part and the placements, contemplating the identity of oneself and the Goddess as the principal thing (Bhāskararāya on the phalaśruti).
