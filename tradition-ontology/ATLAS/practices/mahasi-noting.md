# Noting (the Mahāsi method)

`prc:mahasi-noting` · `skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahāsi Sayadaw tradition (the 'new Burmese method')](../lineages/mahasi.md)

Making a light mental note of every object as it arises — 'rising', 'falling', 'thinking', 'hearing', 'pain', 'intending' — in sitting, walking and all daily activities, to build continuous mindfulness and momentary concentration and see arising and passing.
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** ['the insight knowledges in sequence (Progress of Insight)']

**Sequences:** `pth:sixteen-insight-knowledges`

## The texts' own warnings
- Lights, rapture and other experiences at the stage of arising and passing are to be noted, not taken for attainment. — [The Progress of Insight (Visuddhiñāṇakathā)](../texts/progress-of-insight.md) 4
