# Method of agreement and difference (anvaya-vyatireka)

`prc:anvaya-vyatireka` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)

Reasoning that what persists when other things are absent (consciousness through the states) is distinct from what is absent when it persists (body, senses, mind); used by Sureśvara to separate self from not-self.
