# The rainy-season retreat (cāturmāsa)

`prc:caturmasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)

Mendicants stop wandering for the four months of the rains and stay in one place chosen with the local community, to avoid harming the many beings that appear; it is a season of teaching, study and austerity.
**Stage:** advanced
