# Taking refuge (śaraṇ)

`prc:sarana-initiation` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md)
**Taught in:** [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md)

Initiation by a sattrādhikār: the disciple takes refuge in the four real things — the name, Kṛṣṇa, the guru and the devotees — and vows exclusive devotion; open to all castes and peoples.
