# Nāth initiation (dīkṣā)

`prc:natha-diksa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Taught in:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)

Entry into the order from a guru in stages: the cutting of the top-knot with the giving of a mantra and the black woollen cord (the aughaṛ stage); after a period of service and probation, the ear-splitting and earrings (darśanī stage), with a new name ending in -nāth and affiliation to a panth.
**Stage:** entry
**Prerequisites:** acceptance by a guru; service; the guru's testing
**Sources:**

**Sequences:** [The stages of Nāth initiation](../paths/natha-initiation-stages.md)

## The texts' own warnings
- The would-be disciple is warned of the hardship of the yogī's life before initiation (as depicted in Hīr). — [Hīr (Wāris Shāh)](../texts/hir-waris-shah.md) Tilla episode
