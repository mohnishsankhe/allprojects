# Expiation by confession, remorse, austerity and recitation

`prc:prayascitta-confession` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)

The sinner declares his deed, repents, resolves not to repeat it, performs austerity and Vedic recitation (and gives gifts in distress); as the mind condemns the deed, so the body is freed from it (MDh 11.227–232).
