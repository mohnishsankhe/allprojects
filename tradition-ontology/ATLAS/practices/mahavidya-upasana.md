# Worship of a Mahāvidyā

`prc:mahavidya-upasana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** visualization-deity
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md), [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md)
**Taught in:** [Bengali and Assamese Śākta tantra](../lineages/bengal-assam-sakta.md), [Kālīkula (the Kālī lineages)](../lineages/kalikula.md)

Worship of one of the ten Mahāvidyās by her mantra (received in initiation), yantra, meditation verse, placing of the mantra on the body and preparatory repetition (puraścaraṇa), with her Bhairava at her right (Toḍala 1.3-19; Tantrasāra).
