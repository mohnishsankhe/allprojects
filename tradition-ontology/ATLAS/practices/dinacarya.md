# Daily routine (dinacaryā)

`prc:dinacarya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Rise at the brāhma muhūrta (before dawn) to protect life; attend to the body and cleanliness; clean the teeth with a twig suited to season and doṣa; apply collyrium to the eyes; nasal oil, gargling and (for some) medicated smoke; daily oil-massage, especially of head, ears and feet; exercise to half one's strength; bathing; clean clothes, fragrance; eat in measure when the previous meal is digested; follow good conduct through the day; at night, after a watch has passed, sleep remembering the gods, sages and teachers.
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** ['life, health, power and fame (AHS Sū 2.49)']

## The texts' own warnings
- Tooth-twig, massage and bathing each have conditions in which they are to be avoided (indigestion, fever, eye and ear disease, kapha affliction, just after purification). — [Aṣṭāṅga Hṛdaya](../texts/astanga-hrdaya.md) Sū 2.4, 2.9, 2.18
