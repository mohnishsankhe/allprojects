# Sandhyā (twilight worship)

`prc:sandhyavandana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md), [Jaiminīya (Talavakāra) śākhā (Sāmaveda)](../lineages/sakha-jaiminiya.md), [Kāṇva śākhā (White Yajurveda)](../lineages/sakha-kanva.md), [Kauthuma śākhā (Sāmaveda)](../lineages/sakha-kauthuma.md), [Mādhyandina śākhā (White Yajurveda)](../lineages/sakha-madhyandina.md), [Paippalāda śākhā (Atharvaveda)](../lineages/sakha-paippalada.md), [Śākala śākhā (Ṛgveda)](../lineages/sakha-sakala.md), [Śaunaka śākhā (Atharvaveda)](../lineages/sakha-saunaka.md), [Taittirīya śākhā (Black Yajurveda)](../lineages/sakha-taittiriya.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)

At dawn and dusk: sipping and bathing, sprinkling with the water-verses, breath-control with the Gāyatrī, offering water to the sun (which scatters the Mandeha demons, TA 2.2), and reciting the Sāvitrī standing till sunrise or sitting till the stars appear (YS 1.22–25); the sun is meditated on as Brahman.
**Duration:** twice daily, lifelong
