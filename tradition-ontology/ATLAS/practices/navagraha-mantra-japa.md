# Recitation of the planets' mantras

`prc:navagraha-mantra-japa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](../lineages/jyotisa.md)

Repetition of the mantra proper to an afflicting planet — the Vedic verses assigned to each in the planetary sacrifice, hymns such as the Navagraha-stotra ascribed to Vyāsa and the Āditya-hṛdaya for the Sun, and in later practice seed-syllable mantras — a set number of times, often completed by a fire-offering of a tenth.
