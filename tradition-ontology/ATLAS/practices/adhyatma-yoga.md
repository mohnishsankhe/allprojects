# Yoga of the inner self (Kaṭha)

Realize the hidden god in the cave by the yoga of the inner self (KU 1.2.12); turn the gaze inward (2.1.1); restrain speech into mind, mind into the self that is knowledge, that into the great self, that into the peaceful self (1.3.13); yoga is the firm holding of the senses, when the five senses and mind stand still and the intellect does not stir (2.3.10-11); draw the thumb-sized person out of the body like the stalk from muñja grass (2.3.17).
**Signs of progress:** the highest state: senses and mind still, intellect not stirring (KU 2.3.10)
