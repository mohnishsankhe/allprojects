# Japa — audible, whispered, mental

`prc:japa-three-kinds` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)

Repetition of a mantra, which surpasses ritual sacrifice tenfold when audible, a hundredfold when whispered and a thousandfold when mental; by japa alone a brāhmaṇa can reach perfection (MDh 2.85–87).
