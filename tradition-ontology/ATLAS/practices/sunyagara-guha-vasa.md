# Dwelling in an empty house or cave

`prc:sunyagara-guha-vasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

In the stage of conquest he lives in an empty house or cave free from distraction, constantly with the god, senses conquered, studying and meditating.
**Stage:** jaya (third) stage
**Duration:** six months of constant yoga are named for the powers to arise (PS 5.12-13)
**Signs of progress:** ['powers such as distant sight arise gradually']
