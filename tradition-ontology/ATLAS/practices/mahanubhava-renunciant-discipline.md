# The Mahānubhāva renunciant discipline

`prc:mahanubhava-renunciant-discipline` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Mahānubhāva panth](../lineages/mahanubhava.md)
**Taught in:** [Mahānubhāva panth](../lineages/mahanubhava.md)

Monks and nuns keep moving (aṭana), live by begging, keep no possessions, harm no creature, avoid worldly company and remain in remembrance of Parameśvara and his incarnations.
