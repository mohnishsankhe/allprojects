# Gelug Mahāmudrā meditation on the mind

`prc:gelug-mahamudra-meditation` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md)
**Taught in:** [Ganden Oral Lineage of Mahāmudrā (Ganden Kagyu / Ensa Hearing Lineage)](../lineages/ganden-oral-lineage.md), [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md)

After preliminaries and guru yoga, one takes the mind itself — its mere clarity and knowing — as the object of calm abiding; then, with that calm, investigates the mind's way of existing to realise its emptiness; alternately in the mantra approach through the clear light.
