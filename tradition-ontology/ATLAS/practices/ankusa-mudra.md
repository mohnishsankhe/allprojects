# Aṅkuśa mudrā (the goad gesture)

`prc:ankusa-mudra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md), [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md)
**Taught in:** [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md), [Mantraśāstra (the science of mantras: the smārta-tantric mantra manuals)](../lineages/mantrasastra.md)

The middle finger of the left fist, held in the right fist, extended and the index bent like a goad; used to draw the sacred waters (tīrtha) from the sun's disc into the bathing water (Śāradātilaka ch. 4, in Rāghavabhaṭṭa's commentary).
**Stage:** all
