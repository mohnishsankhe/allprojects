# Cultivating compassion (Kamalaśīla)

`prc:compassion-meditation-bhavanakrama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md)

Compassion is cultivated first, seeing all beings as tormented by the three kinds of suffering: first toward those dear, then toward the indifferent, then toward enemies, until it is equal toward all beings in the ten directions and arises spontaneously.
**Stage:** beginner
**Sources:**
