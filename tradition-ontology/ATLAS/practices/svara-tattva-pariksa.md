# Discerning the elements in the breath (tattva in svara)

`prc:svara-tattva-pariksa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

Within each nostril-flow the five elements (earth, water, fire, air, space) arise in turn; the Śiva Svarodaya teaches how to recognise the element present by the length, direction, colour and taste (and shape) of the breath, and uses this knowledge, with the flows, to foretell outcomes, illness and the approach of death.
