# Uccāra: contemplation of the breath (āṇava means)

Āṇava contemplation of the vital breath in its five forms as it moves between the heart, the object and the dvādaśānta, recognizing seven successive blisses - one's own, the bliss-less, supreme, Brahman's, great, of consciousness and of the world (TĀ 5.43-52).
