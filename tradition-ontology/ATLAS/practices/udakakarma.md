# Udakakarma (water-offerings for the dead)

`prc:udakakarma` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Dharmaśāstra (the science of dharma; Dharmasūtra and Smṛti)](../lineages/dharmasastra.md)

Relatives offer water (with sesame) to the deceased after the cremation and during the days of impurity; not done for infants under two (YS 3.1).
