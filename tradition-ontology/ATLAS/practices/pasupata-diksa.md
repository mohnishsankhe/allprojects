# Pāśupata initiation

`prc:pasupata-diksa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

The teacher tests a brahmin, consecrates him in the forenoon before the southern image of Mahādeva with ash sanctified by the five mantras, and makes him hear the mantra; material, time, rite, image and teacher are the five factors.
**Prerequisites:** brahmin male, tested by the teacher; freedom from debts (Kuśika's declaration)
