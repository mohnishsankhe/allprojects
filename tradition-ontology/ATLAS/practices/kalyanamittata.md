# Good friendship (kalyāṇamittatā), the teacher who gives the subject

`prc:kalyanamittata` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Vism III: the meditation subject is taken from a good friend (kalyāṇamitta), described by a verse as dear, respected, esteemed (bhāvanīya), a speaker, patient with speech, able to speak on the profound and not urging to what is unfit; the Buddha is the best, then an arahant of the eighty great disciples, then a non-returner down to a one-Piṭaka holder who is conscientious (lajjī) and holds the teachers' tradition. Approach with the proper duties, ask at the proper time, state one's own temperament truthfully, and surrender oneself to the Buddha or the teacher; the teacher, knowing or asking the pupil's temperament, teaches the subject that suits it.
