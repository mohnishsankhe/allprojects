# Calm abiding (Kamalaśīla)

`prc:samatha-bhavanakrama` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md)

Seated in a suitable place with a suitable posture, the practitioner fixes the mind on an object and brings it through the nine stages from placing to equipoise, remedying laziness, forgetfulness of the object, laxity and excitement, non-application and over-application by faith, aspiration, effort, pliancy, mindfulness, introspection, application and equanimity.
**Stage:** intermediate
