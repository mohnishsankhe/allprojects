# The eleven lay stages (pratimā)

`prc:pratimas` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)

The householder progresses through eleven stages of renunciation, each adding a discipline — right view, vows, regular sāmāyika, holy-day fasts, not eating living food, not eating at night, celibacy, giving up business, giving up possessions, not approving household acts, not accepting food prepared for oneself — until close to ascetic life.
**Stage:** guṇasthāna 5
