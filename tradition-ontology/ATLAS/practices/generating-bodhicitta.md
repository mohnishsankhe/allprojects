# Generating the awakening mind

`prc:generating-bodhicitta` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)

After worship, refuge and confession, one rejoices in the virtue of all beings, requests the Buddhas to teach and not to pass away, dedicates merit, and then generates the awakening mind for the good of the world, afterwards rejoicing and inviting all beings as guests to happiness.
**Stage:** beginner
