# Breath purification with Oṃ (Uddhava Gītā)

`prc:pranava-pranayama-bhagavata` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

Purifying the breath's path by inhalation, retention and exhalation and their reverse, raising the unbroken Oṃ from the heart like a bell's sound; the text gives it as done ten times at each of the three junctions of the day, mastering the breath within a month.
