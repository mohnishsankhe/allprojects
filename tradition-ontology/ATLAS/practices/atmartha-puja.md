# Ātmārtha-pūjā (daily private worship of Śiva)

`prc:atmartha-puja` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)

The initiate's daily worship: bath with water and ash, twilight rites, bhūtaśuddhi, nyāsa, inner worship, worship of the liṅga with the five brahma-mantras and six limb-mantras, fire offering and japa.
**Prerequisites:** ['samaya-dīkṣā at least']
