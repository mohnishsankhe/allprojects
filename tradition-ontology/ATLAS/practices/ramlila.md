# Rāmlīlā (enactment of the Rāma story)

`prc:ramlila` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md)
**Taught in:** [Rāmānandī sampradāya](../lineages/ramanandi.md)

The Rāmcaritmānas is recited while its episodes are enacted over many nights, above all at Navarātri–Daśaharā; the boy-actors playing Rāma, Sītā and Lakṣmaṇa are honoured as the deities during the performance. Tradition links its beginning at Vārāṇasī to Tulsīdās and his circle.
