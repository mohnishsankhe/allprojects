# The four dhyānas

**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Early Buddhism (the Dhamma-Vinaya of the Nikāyas)](../lineages/early-buddhism.md), [Sarvāstivāda](../lineages/sarvastivada.md), [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)

Good one-pointedness of mind in four levels, with five, four, five and four factors (reasoning, investigation, joy, pleasure, concentration → … → equanimity, mindfulness, concentration, neither pain nor pleasure); each may be 'tasting' (with craving), pure, or without outflows.
**Stage:** advanced

## The texts' own warnings
- Tasting (āsvādana) dhyāna is associated with craving and defiled; the pure and outflow-free kinds are to be cultivated. — [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) 8.5-6
- The attainment of non-perception in the fourth dhyāna is entered by ordinary beings who mistake it for liberation. — [Abhidharmakośa (Abhidharmakośakārikā)](../texts/abhidharmakosa.md) 2.42
