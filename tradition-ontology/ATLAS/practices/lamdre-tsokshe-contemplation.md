# Lamdre contemplation of the three visions

`prc:lamdre-tsokshe-contemplation` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)
**Taught in:** [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)

Contemplating in order the impure vision (sufferings of saṃsāra, the difficulty of leisure and endowment, death, karma), the experiential vision (love, compassion, relative and ultimate bodhicitta, and the experiences of meditation) and the pure vision (the qualities of buddhahood) as the preliminary of the Lamdre.
