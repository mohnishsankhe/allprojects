# The seven attentions (sapta manaskārāḥ)

`prc:seven-attentions` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
**Taught in:** [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)

The yogin frees himself from desire for sense-pleasures by (1) discerning the coarseness of the lower and the peace of the higher level, (2) resolve, (3) seclusion (abandoning the stronger afflictions), (4) gathering delight, (5) examination (checking for remaining desire), (6) the completion of practice, (7) its fruit — the absorption.
