# Repetition of the divine name on beads (nāma-japa)

`prc:nama-japa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 4 independent lineage(s): [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md), [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md), [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md), [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)
**Taught in:** [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md), [Haridāsī sampradāya (Sakhī sampradāya)](../lineages/haridasi.md), [Puṣṭimārga (Vallabha Sampradāya; Śuddhādvaita Brahmavāda)](../lineages/pustimarga.md), [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)

Softly repeating the names of Kṛṣṇa (in Gauḍīya practice, above all the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahāmantra) on a string of tulasī beads, attentively and free of the ten offences; Haridāsa Ṭhākura's daily vow of three hundred thousand names is the model; later institutions fix a daily minimum (e.g. ISKCON's sixteen rounds, recent). In the Puṣṭimārga the refuge mantra 'śrī kṛṣṇaḥ śaraṇaṃ mama' is repeated.
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** ['taste for the name (ruci)', 'tears, faltering voice, horripilation while chanting (Śikṣāṣṭaka 6)']

## The texts' own warnings
- Chanting while committing the ten offences does not yield love; sinning on the strength of the name is itself an offence. — [Padma Purāṇa](../texts/padma-purana.md) nama-aparadha
