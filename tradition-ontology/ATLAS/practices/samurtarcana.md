# Vaikhānasa image worship (samūrtārcana)

`prc:samurtarcana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md)
**Taught in:** [Vaikhānasa](../lineages/vaikhanasa.md)

Daily worship of Viṣṇu with Vedic mantras: eight services to the fixed image by flowers only, thirty-two services to the movable (kautuka) image, invoking the five forms, with fire offering.
