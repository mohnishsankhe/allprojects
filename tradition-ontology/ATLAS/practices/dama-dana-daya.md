# The three 'da': self-restraint, giving, compassion

`prc:dama-dana-daya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Practise the three that the thunder repeats — da, da, da: be restrained (dāmyata), give (datta), be compassionate (dayadhvam) (BAU 5.2).
