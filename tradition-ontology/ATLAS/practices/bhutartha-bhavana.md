# Meditative cultivation of a real object (to yogic perception)

`prc:bhutartha-bhavana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)
**Taught in:** [Buddhist epistemology and logic (pramāṇa school)](../lineages/pramana-buddhist.md)

A real object (above all the four truths) is cultivated until, at its culmination, it appears vividly and without conceptual construction — yogic perception.
**Sources:** 
  - [Nyāyabindu](../texts/nyayabindu.md) — ref: 1.11; rests_on: ["tea:nyayabindu:1.7-11"]

## The texts' own warnings
- Vividness alone does not make a cognition valid: those overcome by desire, grief, fear, madness or dream also see unreal things as if before them. — [Pramāṇavārttika](../texts/pramanavarttika.md) 2.282
