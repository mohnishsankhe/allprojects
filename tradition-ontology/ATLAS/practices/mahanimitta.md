# The eightfold prognostication (mahānimitta)

`prc:mahanimitta` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md)
**Taught in:** [Ājīvika](../lineages/ajivika.md)

Divination from the year, dreams, bodily marks, portents, signs of the body and omens, to foretell gain and loss, pleasure and pain, life and death; Gosāla learned it from the six disācaras.
