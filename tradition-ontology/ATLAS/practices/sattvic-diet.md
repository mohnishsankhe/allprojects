# Sāttvic food

`prc:sattvic-diet` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Taught in:** [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Prefer foods that increase life, vitality, strength, health, happiness and cheerfulness, that are savoury, oily, substantial and agreeable; avoid the bitter, sour, salty, very hot, pungent, dry and burning (rājasic) and the stale, tasteless, putrid, leftover and impure (tāmasic) (17.7–10).
