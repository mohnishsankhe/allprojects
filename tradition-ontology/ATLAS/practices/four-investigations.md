# The four investigations and four exact knowledges

`prc:four-investigations` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
**Taught in:** [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)

Investigating names, things, designations of own-nature and designations of particulars as mere designations, and then knowing each exactly as it is; by these the bodhisattva at the stage of practice by resolve enters cognition-only.
