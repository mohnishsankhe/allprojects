# Renunciation of everything (sarva-tyāga)

`prc:sarva-tyaga` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md), [The Tamil Siddhars (cittar)](../lineages/tamil-siddha.md)

True renunciation is the giving up of the mind itself, the root of all; outer renunciation of kingdom or belongings does not suffice while the notion 'I renounce' remains.
**Stage:** advanced
