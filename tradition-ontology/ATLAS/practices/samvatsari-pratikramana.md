# The annual confession (saṃvatsarī pratikramaṇa)

`prc:samvatsari-pratikramana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](../lineages/murtipujaka.md), [Sthānakavāsī](../lineages/sthanakavasi.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Śvetāmbara Terāpanth](../lineages/terapanthi.md)

The longest form of confession, performed on the last day of Paryuṣaṇa: reviewing the year's transgressions against beings and vows, repenting with 'micchāmi dukkaḍaṃ', and asking forgiveness of all living beings and of each person one has wronged.
