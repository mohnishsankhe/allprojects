# Khecarī mudrā ('moving in space')

`prc:khecari-mudra` · `skeleton` · confidence high · _restricted: summary only_

RESTRICTED — summary only: the tongue, turned back, enters the cavity above the palate (the 'skull-cavity' or 'well of nectar') while the gaze rests between the brows (HYP 3.32; GS 3.27; ŚS 4.31; DYŚ 125; GŚ 34). The texts describe a gradual, months-long preparation of the tongue that involves cutting — not reproduced here. They also give an inner khecarī, the breath held in the void between iḍā and piṅgalā (HYP 4.43-45), and an esoteric reading of 'eating cow's flesh and drinking the liquor of the immortals' as the tongue entering the palate and drinking the moon's nectar (HYP 3.47-49). The Khecarīvidyā makes a secret mantra received from the guru essential to it.
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** ['freedom from disease, death, sloth, sleep, hunger, thirst and swoon; not stained by karma nor bound by time (HYP 3.38-40)', 'bindu does not fall (HYP 3.42)', 'the tongue tastes a succession of flavours ending in nectar (GS 3.30-32; HYP 3.50)', 'samādhi of the bliss of taste (GS 7.9)']
