## The texts' own warnings
- Khecarī, like all the ten mudrās, is to be guarded like a jewel-box and told to no one. — [Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā (Haṭhapradīpikā)](../texts/hatha-yoga-pradipika.md) 3.8-9
- The preparation is gradual, over months; never all at once, or the body perishes. — [Yogakuṇḍalī Upaniṣad](../texts/yogakundali-upanisad.md) 2.28-49
- Learn it only from a guru who knows it from text, meaning and practice, with the mantra from the guru's mouth; keep it secret. — [Yogakuṇḍalī Upaniṣad](../texts/yogakundali-upanisad.md) 2.1-13
- The practice succeeds only through the guru and the mantra together and is not to be attempted otherwise. — [Khecarīvidyā](../texts/khecarividya.md) 
- It is not to be given to anyone; it is to be concealed with effort. — [Śiva Saṃhitā](../texts/siva-samhita.md) 4.37
- Called 'most secret'; to be guarded. — [Kaulajñānanirṇaya](../texts/kaulajnananirnaya.md) 6.28
- Learned from the guru's mouth. — [Gorakṣaśataka](../texts/goraksasataka.md) 59
- Learn only from a guru who knows it from text, meaning and practice; keep it secret. — [Yogakuṇḍalī Upaniṣad](../texts/yogakundali-upanisad.md) 2.1-13
