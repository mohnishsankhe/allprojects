# Khecarī mudrā

`prc:khecari-mudra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

**Category:** lock-seal
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): `lin:hatha-yoga`, [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** `lin:hatha-yoga`, [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

The 'moving in space' seal of the tongue, taught as a secret vidyā with a mantra (melana) received from the guru's mouth; the bodily procedures described by the texts are not reproduced. Also given inner meanings (the gaze; the renouncer's 'mudrā').
