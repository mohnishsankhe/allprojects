# The renunciation rite (saṃnyāsa-dīkṣā)

`prc:sannyasa-diksa` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Penances, śrāddhas (including for oneself), the virajā homa, placing the fires in oneself, the declaration of renunciation said three times, the gift of fearlessness, abandoning topknot and thread (or not, in the Vaiṣṇava rite), receiving staff and cloth, and the teaching of praṇava and great saying.
