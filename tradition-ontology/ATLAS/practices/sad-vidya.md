# The teaching of being (sad-vidyā)

`prc:sad-vidya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Uddālaka's method: by one lump of clay all clay is known; in the beginning was being alone; it made heat, water and food threefold; illustrated by sleep, dying, honey, rivers, the tree, the banyan seed, salt, the blindfolded man and the axe ordeal, each ending 'that you are'.
