# The Nyingma preliminaries (sngon 'gro)

`prc:ngondro-nyingma` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)
**Taught in:** [Dzogchen (the Great Perfection, Atiyoga)](../lineages/dzogchen.md), [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)

The complete foundation of the Longchen Nyingthig and other cycles: the outer preliminaries (reflection on the freedoms and advantages, impermanence, the defects of saṃsāra, karma, the benefits of liberation, following a teacher) and the inner preliminaries (refuge with prostrations, bodhicitta, Vajrasattva, maṇḍala, kusāli, guru yoga), traditionally each accumulated a hundred thousand times, followed by the transference of consciousness.
**Stage:** beginner
**Duration:** traditionally completed over months or years (five sets of 100,000)
**Signs of progress:** ['turning away from worldly concerns', 'devotion and compassion growing', 'dreams of purification (see phn:purification-dream-signs)']
