# Discrimination of the five sheaths (pañca-kośa-viveka)

`prc:panca-kosa-viveka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)

Examining each sheath in turn — food, vital air, mind, intellect, bliss — as changing and known, hence not the self, until the self as their witness and support is recognized.
