# The Uḍupi paryāya of Kṛṣṇa worship

**Category:** devotion-service

**Taught in:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](../lineages/dvaita.md)

The worship of the Kṛṣṇa image installed by Madhva at Uḍupi, performed in rotation by the heads of the eight maṭhas, each holding the turn (paryāya) for two years.
