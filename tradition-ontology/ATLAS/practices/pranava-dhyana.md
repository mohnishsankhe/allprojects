# Meditation on Oṃ (praṇava)

`prc:pranava-dhyana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Contemplation and repetition of Oṃ through its measures to the half-measure and the soundless; Oṃ as bow, chariot or fire-stick; also joined with breath-regulation.
**Signs of progress:** ['Brahman shines within twelve months for one who repeats 12,000 praṇavas daily (Sannyāsa 2.103–104)']
