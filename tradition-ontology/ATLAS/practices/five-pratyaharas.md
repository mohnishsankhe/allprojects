# Five kinds of withdrawal (pratyāhāra)

`prc:five-pratyaharas` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Forcibly withdrawing the senses; seeing all that is seen as the Self; offering the fruits of acts; turning from all objects; and moving/holding the breath through the eighteen vital points (Darśana 7; Śāṇḍilya 1.69); the Yogatattva's withdrawal is regarding all perceived as the Self.
