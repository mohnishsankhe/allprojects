# Renunciation (saṃnyāsa)

`prc:sannyasa` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Rising above the desires for sons, wealth and worlds, living as a mendicant on alms, wandering forth for the sake of the self (BAU 3.5.1, 4.4.22); ascetics purified by the yoga of renunciation (MuU 3.2.6); forest-dwelling with austerity and faith on alms (MuU 1.2.11); after the father-son transfer the recovered father may wander as a mendicant (KauU 2.15).
