# Coursing in the perfection of wisdom by non-apprehension

`prc:non-apprehending-contemplation` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md), [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)
**Taught in:** [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](../lineages/chan.md), [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Mahāyāna](../lineages/mahayana.md)

Not taking hold of form or any dharma, not even of 'bodhisattva', 'perfection of wisdom' or 'nirvāṇa', and not being frightened by this; standing nowhere as the Tathāgata stands.
**Stage:** advanced
**Sources:** 
  - [Aṣṭasāhasrikā Prajñāpāramitā](../texts/astasahasrika-prajnaparamita.md) — ref: ch. 1-2; rests_on: ["tea:astasahasrika-prajnaparamita:1", "tea:astasahasrika-prajnaparamita:1/4", "tea:astasahasrika-prajnaparamita:2/2"]
