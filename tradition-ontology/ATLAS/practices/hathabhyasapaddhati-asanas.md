# The postures of the Haṭhābhyāsapaddhati (group entry)

A prose haṭha manual that describes more than a hundred postures (about 112 are counted), set in a sequence after the yamas, niyamas and cleansing acts and before breath-control and mudrā; many are dynamic or repeated movements and some use a rope. Its postures reappear, illustrated, in the Mysore Śrītattvanidhi. The individual names are not recalled with enough confidence to be entered here (see REPORT).
