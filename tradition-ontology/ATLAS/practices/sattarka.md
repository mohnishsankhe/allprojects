# Sattarka: purification of thought by pure reasoning (śākta means)

`prc:sattarka` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md), [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md)
**Taught in:** [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md), [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md)

Thought-constructs are purified (vikalpa-saṃskāra) by pure reasoning (sattarka), the teacher's instruction and scripture, repeatedly affirming the pure thought 'I am Śiva, all this is my glory' until it becomes thought-free (TĀ 4; MVT 2.22).
**Stage:** middling grace (śāktopāya)
