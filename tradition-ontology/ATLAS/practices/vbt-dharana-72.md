# Vijñāna Bhairava dhāraṇā 72: 'Who am I before desire or knowledge arises?'

The practitioner is asked to contemplate: 'when no desire of mine has arisen, or knowledge, who am I then? In truth I am that (tathābhūta)', and to become absorbed in it with the mind on it.
