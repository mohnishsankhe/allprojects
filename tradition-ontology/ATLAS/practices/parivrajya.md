# Wandering (pārivrājya) and the rains retreat

`prc:parivrajya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md)

Wandering alone or with one companion; one night in a village, five in a town, seven at a sacred place; staying in one place during the four rainy months; dwelling in empty houses, temples, tree-roots, caves.
