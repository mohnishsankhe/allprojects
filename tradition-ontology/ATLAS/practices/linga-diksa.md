# Liṅga-initiation (liṅgadīkṣā)

`prc:linga-diksa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)
**Taught in:** [Pañcācārya (Pañcapīṭha) tradition](../lineages/pancacarya.md), [Śaraṇa–vacana tradition](../lineages/sarana-vacana.md), [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)

The guru, with assistants, bathes the disciple with water from pots consecrated by the five brahma-mantras, whispers the five-syllable mantra secretly into the right ear, installs a purified liṅga in the disciple's life-breath and places it in his hand to be worn for life. In Liṅgāyata practice it is given to all, women and children included.
**Stage:** bhakta (gurukāruṇya)
**Prerequisites:** a qualified guru (SSM 6.1–6); the disciple's discernment and dispassion
