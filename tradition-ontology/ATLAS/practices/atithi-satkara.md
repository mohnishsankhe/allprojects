# Honouring the guest (atithi-satkāra)

`prc:atithi-satkara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Śaunaka śākhā (Atharvaveda)](../lineages/sakha-saunaka.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)

Receiving a guest with water, seat and food, which the Atharvaveda equates point by point with performing the sacrifice; the Vrātya who knows is to be honoured above oneself.
