# Neti (the nasal thread)

`prc:neti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** cleansing
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

A smooth, fine thread is passed into a nostril and drawn out through the mouth (HYP 2.29; GS 1.50). The measurement the texts give is not reproduced.
**Stage:** beginner
**Signs of progress:** ['cleanses the skull, gives divine sight, destroys diseases above the collar-bones (HYP 2.30)', 'gives khecarī-siddhi, removes phlegm disorders, gives divine sight (GS 1.51)']

## The texts' own warnings
- One with excess fat or phlegm should first practise the six acts; others should not do them, their doṣas being in balance. — [Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā (Haṭhapradīpikā)](../texts/hatha-yoga-pradipika.md) 2.21
- Some teachers hold that all impurities are dried up by breath-control alone and do not approve of the other acts. — [Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā (Haṭhapradīpikā)](../texts/hatha-yoga-pradipika.md) 2.37
- The six acts, which purify the body, are to be kept secret. — [Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā (Haṭhapradīpikā)](../texts/hatha-yoga-pradipika.md) 2.23

_Notes: 'Sūtra-neti' and 'jala-neti' (with water) are later names; the Gheraṇḍa's vyutkrama kapālabhāti draws water through the nose._
