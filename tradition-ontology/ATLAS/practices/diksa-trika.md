# Initiation (dīkṣā) in the Trika

`prc:diksa-trika` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md)
**Taught in:** [Kashmir Śaivism (non-dual Śaiva tantra of Kashmir)](../lineages/kashmir-saivism.md), [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](../lineages/mantramarga.md), [Trika ('the Triad')](../lineages/trika.md)

Initiation in its many forms - samaya, putraka, abbreviated, by the balance, of the absent and the dead - purifying the paths (adhvan) and removing the bonds; the teacher observes signs of immersion in the disciple (ānanda, udbhava, kampa, nidrā, ghūrṇi; MVT 11.35). For one who knows the Heart, initiation needs no oblation (Parātrīśikā 25).
