# The Kerala eight-auspicious-objects query (aṣṭamaṅgala-praśna)

`prc:astamangala-prasna` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [The Kerala tradition of jyotiṣa](../lineages/kerala-jyotisa.md)

In Kerala an elaborate query in a ritually prepared space: the eight auspicious objects, a lamp and the astrologer's worship; the querist's touch of objects, the rising sign, the placement of cowries and the omens reveal past and present afflictions — including curses of deities, serpents and ancestors — and the remedies; in temples the same procedure (devaprāśna) asks the deity's will.
