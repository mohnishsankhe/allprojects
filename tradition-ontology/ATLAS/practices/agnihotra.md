# Agnihotra (daily fire-offering)

Twice daily, at sunrise and sunset, the householder who has established the sacred fires offers milk into the āhavanīya fire. The Brāhmaṇas explain it as healing the junction of day and night in Prajāpati (ŚB 1.6.3.36), as an offering of 'truth in faith' (ŚB 11.3.1), as a cycle of the oblations through the worlds into man and woman (ŚB 11.6.2), and as protection from retribution in the other world (ŚB 11.6.1).
**Stage:** householder who has set up the fires (āhitāgni)
**Prerequisites:** ['prc:agnyadheya']
**Duration:** lifelong, twice daily
