# Trāṭaka (the fixed gaze)

`prc:trataka` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** cleansing
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

With steady eyes and concentrated mind, one gazes without blinking at a small target until tears fall (HYP 2.31; GS 1.53). Listed by the texts among the six acts, it is also a practice of concentration: in the Gheraṇḍa it leads to śāmbhavī (GS 1.54).
**Stage:** beginner
**Signs of progress:** ['frees from eye diseases and bars sloth (HYP 2.32)', 'śāmbhavī arises; no eye disease; divine sight (GS 1.54)']
