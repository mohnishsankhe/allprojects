# Mātaṅginī mudrā ('the elephant')

`prc:matangini-mudra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** cleansing
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

Standing in water up to the neck, water is drawn in through the nostrils and expelled through the mouth, then drawn in through the mouth and expelled through the nostrils, again and again (GS 3.88-91). Counted by the Gheraṇḍa among its twenty-five mudrās.
**Stage:** intermediate
**Signs of progress:** ['destroys old age and death; one becomes strong like an elephant and enjoys great happiness (GS 3.89-91)']
**Sources:**

## The texts' own warnings
