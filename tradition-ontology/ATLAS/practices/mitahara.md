# Moderate diet (mitāhāra) in the haṭha texts

`prc:mitahara` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Agreeable, sweet, unctuous food leaving a quarter of the stomach empty, offered to Śiva (HYP 1.58); half the stomach filled with food, a quarter with water and a quarter left for the air (GS 5.21-22); avoiding the bitter, sour, pungent, salty and hot, reheated or dry food, liquor, fish and meat, garlic and asafoetida (HYP 1.59-61; GS 5.23-25; ŚS 3.33; DYŚ 64-65); favouring wheat, rice, barley, milk, ghee, honey, mung and pure water (HYP 1.62-63; GS 5.17-20, 5.27-29). At the start of practice milk and ghee are recommended (HYP 2.14; ŚS 3.37; GŚ 50). It is called the chief of the restraints (HYP 1.38).

## The texts' own warnings
- One who begins yoga without moderate diet gets various diseases and no success. — [Gheraṇḍa Saṃhitā](../texts/gheranda-samhita.md) 5.16
- Early bathing, fasting and other mortifications of the body are to be avoided. — [Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā (Haṭhapradīpikā)](../texts/hatha-yoga-pradipika.md) 1.61
- Do not practise immediately after eating nor when very hungry. — [Śiva Saṃhitā](../texts/siva-samhita.md) 3.37
- Whoever begins yoga without moderate diet gets various diseases and his yoga does not succeed. — [Gheraṇḍa Saṃhitā](../texts/gheranda-samhita.md) 5.16
- Overeating is the first of the six things that destroy yoga. — [Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā (Haṭhapradīpikā)](../texts/hatha-yoga-pradipika.md) 1.15
