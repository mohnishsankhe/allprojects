# Moderate diet (mitāhāra)

`prc:mitahara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): `lin:hatha-yoga`, [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** `lin:hatha-yoga`, [Saṃnyāsa — the renunciate ideal of the Saṃnyāsa Upaniṣads](../lineages/sannyasa.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Sweet, unctuous food eaten leaving a quarter of the stomach empty; avoiding the hot, sour, salty, pungent; wholesome milk, ghee, wheat, mung, rice; called the chief of the yamas (Yogatattva 28–29, 46–49; Yogacūḍāmaṇi 43).
