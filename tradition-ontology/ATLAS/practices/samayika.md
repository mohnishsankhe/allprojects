# Sāmāyika (equanimity practice)

`prc:samayika` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Sthānakavāsī](../lineages/sthanakavasi.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md), [Śvetāmbara Terāpanth](../lineages/terapanthi.md)

For a fixed period (a muhūrta, 48 minutes) the practitioner renounces all harmful activity of mind, speech and body and sits (or stands) in equanimity, reciting the vow of sāmāyika, praising the Jinas, reflecting on the realities and cultivating friendliness toward all beings (Amitagati's Sāmāyikapāṭha), treating friend and foe, gain and loss alike. For the layman it is a disciplinary vow and a temporary taste of the ascetic life; for the ascetic it is lifelong conduct.
**Stage:** all; lay vow from guṇasthāna 5
**Prerequisites:** ['right view', 'the small vows (for the lay vow)']
**Duration:** one muhūrta (48 minutes) per session, one or more times a day
**Signs of progress:** ['equanimity toward the agreeable and disagreeable', 'steadiness of mind that supports meditation (YŚ 4.114)']

## The texts' own warnings
