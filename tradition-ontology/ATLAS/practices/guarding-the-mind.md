# Guarding the mind with mindfulness and introspection

`prc:guarding-the-mind` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)

Because the training cannot be kept without guarding the mind, the mind is bound with the rope of mindfulness and its states of body and mind are repeatedly examined by introspection, before acting, speaking or thinking.
