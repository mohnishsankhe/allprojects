# Uḍḍīyānabandha (the flying-up lock)

`prc:uddiyana-bandha` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** lock-seal
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Drawing the belly back (paścimatāna) above and below the navel, so that the breath, bound, 'flies up' in the suṣumnā like a great bird (HYP 3.55-59; GS 3.10-11; ŚS 4.48; DYŚ 129-130; GŚ 35 places it above the penis and below the navel). In breath-control it is applied at the end of retention and the beginning of exhalation (HYP 2.45); the Gheraṇḍa applies it at the end of inhalation in sagarbha retention (GS 5.50).
**Stage:** intermediate
**Signs of progress:** ['the lion to the elephant of death; even the old become young; practised six months it conquers death (HYP 3.57-59; DYŚ 129-130)', 'the best of locks; when firm, liberation comes naturally (HYP 3.60; GS 3.11)', 'the navel purified, the belly-fire blazes (ŚS 4.49-50)']
