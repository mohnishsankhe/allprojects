# Contemplation of the inner liṅga of light

`prc:pranalinga-anusandhana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** visualization-deity
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyata](../lineages/virasaiva.md)
