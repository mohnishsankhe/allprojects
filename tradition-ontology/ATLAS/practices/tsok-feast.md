# Feast offering (tshogs 'khor, gaṇacakra)

`prc:tsok-feast` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md), [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)
**Taught in:** [Bön (Yungdrung Bön)](../lineages/bon.md), [Nyingma (the Ancient / Old Translation school)](../lineages/nyingma.md)

A gathering in which food and drink are consecrated as nectar and offered to the deities and guests, with confession and restoration of samaya; held on the tenth and twenty-fifth days of the lunar month.
**Sources:**
