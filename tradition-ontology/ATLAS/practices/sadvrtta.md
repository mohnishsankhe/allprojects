# Good conduct (sadvṛtta)

`prc:sadvrtta` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ethics
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Follow, with constant mindfulness, the conduct of the good: honour gods, teachers and elders; keep clean; speak truly, gently and first; do not take or covet what is another's; restrain greed, envy, anger and pride; avoid the wicked and serve the elders; abandon the ten sinful acts; see all beings, even insects, as oneself; follow the middle way; reflect each night on how one's days are passing; be devoted to continence, knowledge, giving, friendliness, compassion, joy, equanimity and calm.
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** ['health and mastery of the senses at once (Ca Sū 8.18); a hundred years free of disease (8.31)']
