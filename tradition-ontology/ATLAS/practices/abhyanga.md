# Oil massage (abhyaṅga)

`prc:abhyanga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** body-daily-rhythm
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Daily anointing of the body with oil, especially head, ears and feet; it makes the body firm like an oiled pot or axle, calms vāta, improves skin, sight and sleep and delays ageing.
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** ['firmness, good skin, freedom from vāta-pain, good sleep (Ca Sū 5.86-89)']
**Sources:** 
  - [Caraka Saṃhitā](../texts/caraka-samhita.md) — ref: Sū 5.81-92; rests_on: ["tea:caraka-samhita:su.5.85-89"]

## The texts' own warnings
- To be avoided by those afflicted with kapha, just after purificatory therapy, and with indigestion. — [Aṣṭāṅga Hṛdaya](../texts/astanga-hrdaya.md) Sū 2.9
