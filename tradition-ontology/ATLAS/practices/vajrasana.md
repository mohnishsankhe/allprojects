# Vajrāsana (the thunderbolt seat)

`prc:vajrasana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** posture
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

The thighs made firm like a thunderbolt and the feet placed by the sides of the anus (GS 2.12); it is the seat for khecarī in the Śivasaṃhitā (4.31) and for śakticālana in the Haṭhapradīpikā (3.114-115). HYP 1.37 records vajrāsana as another name for siddhāsana.
**Stage:** beginner
**Signs of progress:** ['gives success to yogins (GS 2.12)']
