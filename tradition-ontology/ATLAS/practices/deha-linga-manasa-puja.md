# Mental worship of the body-liṅga with eight flowers

`prc:deha-linga-manasa-puja` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Kaula (the Kula tradition)](../lineages/kaula.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Taught in:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Yoginī Kaula (Yoginīkaula) of Matsyendra](../lineages/yogini-kaula.md)

Instead of an external liṅga, the practitioner worships the liṅga within the body mentally, offering the eight inner flowers — non-harming, sense-restraint, compassion, feeling, patience, conquest of anger, meditation and knowledge — and, in the Yogaviṣaya, bathing the inner Lord with the moon's nectar and offering the flower of the mind.
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** enjoyment and liberation (KJN 3.27)
