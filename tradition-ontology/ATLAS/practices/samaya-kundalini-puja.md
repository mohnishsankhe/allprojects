# Worship of the Goddess led through the cakras (Samaya procedure described by Bhāskararāya)

`prc:samaya-kundalini-puja` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** energy
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Taught in:** [Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)](../lineages/samaya-srividya.md), [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)

When the initiate's contemplation of unity is firm and the guru's rite of 'great piercing' (mahāvedha) has been done, the Goddess, risen from the base, is said to become manifest in the maṇipūra; there she is worshipped with offerings, then led to the heart, the throat and the brow with further offerings, and united with Sadāśiva in the thousand-petalled lotus. Summary only — no method of raising the power is recorded.
**Stage:** advanced
