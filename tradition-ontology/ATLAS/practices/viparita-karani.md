# Viparītakaraṇī (the inverting technique)

`prc:viparita-karani` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** lock-seal
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md), [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md), [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Because the sun at the navel swallows the nectar that drips from the moon at the palate, causing old age, the body is inverted — 'navel above, palate below; sun above, moon below' — the head on the ground (with the hands supporting) and the legs raised (HYP 3.77-79; GS 3.33-35; ŚS 4.45; DYŚ 133-135; GŚ 55-59). It is begun for a moment and lengthened a little day by day (HYP 3.81-82; DYŚ 135). In the Gorakṣaśataka and Vivekamārtaṇḍa it is taught as pratyāhāra, the 'withdrawal' of the nectar from the sun.
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** ['after six months wrinkles and grey hair are not seen; one who practises for a watch conquers time (HYP 3.82; DYŚ 136)', 'the digestive fire grows (HYP 3.80)', 'one does not perish even at the dissolution (GS 3.36; ŚS 4.46)']
