# Akṣaya Tṛtīyā (Ṛṣabha's first alms)

`prc:aksaya-trtiya-jain` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)

Commemorates the day Prince Śreyāṃsa offered sugarcane juice to Ṛṣabha, ending his long fast — the first alms of this era; those completing the year-long fast break it on this day.
