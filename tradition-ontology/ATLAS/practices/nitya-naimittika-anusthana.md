# Performing obligatory and occasional rites while abstaining from desire-prompted and prohibited acts

`prc:nitya-naimittika-anusthana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Mīmāṃsā (Pūrva Mīmāṃsā)](../lineages/mimamsa.md)
**Taught in:** [Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/bhatta-mimamsa.md), [Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā](../lineages/prabhakara-mimamsa.md)

The discipline of the one seeking release: continue the obligatory and occasional rites to avoid the demerit of omission (and, for Kumārila, to destroy past sin), abstain from desire-prompted acts that would produce new bodies and from prohibited acts; past karma is exhausted by experience.
**Stage:** advanced (mokṣārthin)
**Prerequisites:** eligibility for Vedic rites; aversion to saṃsāra (Śāstradīpikā, Prakaraṇapañcikā)
