# Taking adversity as the path

`prc:taking-adversity-as-path` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md), [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)
**Taught in:** [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md), [Sakya](../lineages/sakya.md)

When harmed or in bad circumstances, blaming self-cherishing rather than others, contemplating the harmer's kindness as a teacher of patience, seeing the situation as empty and like a dream, and doing the four practices — so that illness, enemies and loss become aids to bodhicitta.
