# Śrīvidyā initiation (śāktī, śāmbhavī, māntrī)

`prc:srividya-diksa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Taught in:** [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md), [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)

The guru, after his own worship, purifies the blindfolded disciple with the special water, contemplates the red and white feet on his head and a wave of light burning away his bonds, bathes him from a consecrated vessel, places the letters on his body, whispers the Bālā mantra and then the chosen mantra, gives his own pādukā and a name ending in '-ānandanātha', teaches the rules of conduct and makes the disciple his own self (PKS 1.34-43). Summary only.
