# Approaching a teacher with fuel in hand

`prc:guru-upasadana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Having become disgusted with the worlds won by action, go 'fuel in hand' to a teacher learned in the Veda and established in brahman (MuU 1.2.12); live with the teacher a set time in austerity, celibacy and faith before asking (PrU 1.2); serve the teacher (ChU 4.4-9, 8.7-12, 8.15).
