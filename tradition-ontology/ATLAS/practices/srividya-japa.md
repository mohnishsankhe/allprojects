# Japa of the Śrīvidyā mantra

Repetition of the fifteen- or sixteen-syllable mantra received at initiation, with attention to its subtle final phases and its meanings; the Varivasyārahasya insists that japa without knowledge of meaning bears no fruit. (No syllables recorded.)

## The texts' own warnings
- Recitation without knowing the meaning bears no fruit and may do harm. — [Varivasyārahasya](../texts/varivasya-rahasya.md) 54-56
- Those without the succession of teachers, proud of mere knowledge, are harmed by the mantra's rays through their breach of the samaya. — [Yoginīhṛdaya](../texts/yoginihrdaya.md) 2.81
