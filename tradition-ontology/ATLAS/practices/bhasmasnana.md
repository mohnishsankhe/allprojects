# Bathing in ash (bhasmasnāna)

`prc:bhasmasnana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

At each of the three junctures of the day the practitioner purifies himself, takes ash consecrated with the mantras and rubs it over the whole body while muttering, then enters the temple.
**Stage:** vyakta (first) stage
**Prerequisites:** Pāśupata initiation
