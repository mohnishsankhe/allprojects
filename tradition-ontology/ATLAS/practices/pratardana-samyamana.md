# Pratardana's inner Agnihotra (saṃyamana)

`prc:pratardana-samyamana` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Knowing that while speaking one offers breath into speech and while breathing offers speech into breath, one treats these as two endless oblations offered continuously, waking and sleeping (KU 2.5 = ŚĀ 4.5).
