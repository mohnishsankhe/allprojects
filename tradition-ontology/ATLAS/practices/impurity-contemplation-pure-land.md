# Loathing practice: contemplating the body's impurity (Jingtu shiyi lun)

**Category:** meditation

**Taught in:** [Pure Land (Jingtu / Jōdo): birth in Amitābha's land](../lineages/pure-land.md)

To turn from the five desires, the aspirant contemplates the body as impure in seven ways (seed, conception, dwelling in the womb, nourishment, birth, the body's contents, death), gradually, even if he cannot yet leave his family; paired with joyful aspiration for the Pure Land.
