# Offering all conduct to the Lord (tad-arpitākhilācāratā)

`prc:tadarpita-akhilacara` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md)
**Taught in:** [Bhakti-śāstra: the sūtra tradition of devotional theory (Nārada, Śāṇḍilya)](../lineages/bhakti-sastra.md)

Dedicating every action to the Lord (Nārada's own definition of devotion, NBS 19); having so offered all conduct, even desire, anger and pride are to be turned toward him alone (NBS 65); until love is attained, action continues with its fruit given up (NBS 62).
