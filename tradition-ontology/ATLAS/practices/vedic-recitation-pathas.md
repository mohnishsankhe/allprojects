# The Vedic recitations (pāṭha): continuous, word-by-word, step and the eight modified ones

`prc:vedic-recitation-pathas` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](../lineages/vedanga.md)

The pupil learns the text orally from the teacher in the continuous form (saṃhitā), word by word (pada), in overlapping pairs (krama) and in the elaborate 'modified' patterns (jaṭā, ghana and others), with exact accent and quantity, pronouncing each sound 'as a tigress carries her cubs' (PŚ 25) and avoiding the six faults of reciters (PŚ 32).
**Stage:** Vedic students and reciters
**Duration:** years of study from initiation

## The texts' own warnings
- A mantra faulty in accent or sound harms the sacrificer (PŚ 52). — [Pāṇinīya Śikṣā](../texts/paniniya-siksa.md) 52
- Reciting without understanding is 'dry fuel without fire' (Nirukta 1.18). — [Nirukta](../texts/nirukta.md) 1.18
