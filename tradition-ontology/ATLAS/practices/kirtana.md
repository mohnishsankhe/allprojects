Singing or reciting the names and deeds of the Lord, alone or together; declared the fearless way for all (BhP 2.1.11) and the means of the Kali age that gives what meditation, sacrifice and worship gave in earlier ages (BhP 12.3.51-52; VP 6.2.17).
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** ["love born, heart melted, laughing, weeping and dancing 'like one mad' (BhP 11.2.40)", 'the Lord manifests and makes the devotee experience him (NBS 80)']
