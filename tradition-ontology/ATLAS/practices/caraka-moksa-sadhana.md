# Caraka's means to liberation

`prc:caraka-moksa-sadhana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)
**Taught in:** [Āyurveda](../lineages/ayurveda.md)

Serving the good and avoiding the wicked; vows, fasts and restraints; study of the dharma treatises; delight in solitude and aversion to objects; approaching a teacher; renunciation of possessions (the renunciant's requisites); acting with mindfulness in all movements; bearing opposites; withdrawing the senses into the mind and the mind into the self; counting the body's parts by dhātus; contemplating all that is caused as suffering, not one's own and impermanent; all flowing from the recollection of truth (tattvasmṛti), culminating in the understanding 'this is not I, not mine' and final renunciation.
