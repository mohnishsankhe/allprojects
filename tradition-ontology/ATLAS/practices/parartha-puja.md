# Temple worship for others (parārtha-pūjā)

`prc:parartha-puja` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Kerala tantra (temple-tantra tradition of Kerala)](../lineages/kerala-tantra.md), [Śaiva Siddhānta](../lineages/saiva-siddhanta.md)

Public worship in temples with installed liṅgas or images, performed by qualified priests on behalf of the king, the realm and devotees; its rules are the subject of most South Indian Āgama redactions.
