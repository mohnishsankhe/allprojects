# Kākī mudrā ('the crow')

`prc:kaki-mudra` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** breath
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

The mouth is shaped like a crow's beak and air is drunk in slowly (GS 3.86-87). The same 'crow's-beak' drinking of cool air is taught in the Śivasaṃhitā at the two twilights and day and night (ŚS 3.70, 3.74-75), and kākī is the means of filling the belly with air in vātasāra and bahiṣkṛta dhauti (GS 1.15, 1.22) and of drawing in prāṇa in yoni mudrā (GS 3.38).
**Stage:** intermediate
**Signs of progress:** ['destroys all diseases; one becomes free of disease like a crow (GS 3.86-87)', 'destroys consumption (ŚS 3.74)']
