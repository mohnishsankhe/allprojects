A qualified guru gives a mantra — chosen and tested for the disciple — to a qualified disciple who has served and been tested (a year for a brāhmaṇa up to four for a śūdra, ŚT 2.153), with worship of the deity and the teaching of its viniyoga and nyāsa; the mantra is thereafter kept secret.
