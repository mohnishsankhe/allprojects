# Calm abiding according to the lamrim

`prc:calm-abiding-lamrim` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sense-withdrawal-concentration
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md)
**Taught in:** [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md)

Having gathered the prerequisites, one sits in the eight-featured posture, takes an object (often the visualised body of the Buddha), and progresses through the nine mental states by the six powers and four attentions, applying the eight antidotes to the five faults — above all recognising gross and subtle laxity and excitement with alertness — until pliancy of mind and body arises.
**Stage:** intermediate
**Prerequisites:** ['the six prerequisites (suitable place, few desires, contentment, few activities, pure ethics, abandoning desire-thoughts)']
**Duration:** months to years of continuous practice (the texts give no fixed measure)
**Signs of progress:** ['the nine mental states in order', 'mental then physical pliancy with joy', 'the mind stays on its object at will']

## The texts' own warnings
- Subtle laxity is easily mistaken for faultless meditation; long meditation in it dulls the mind. — [Great Treatise on the Stages of the Path to Enlightenment (lam rim chen mo)](../texts/lamrim-chenmo.md) calm.subtle-laxity
- If the limbs of calm are lost, even thousands of years of effort will not produce concentration. — [Lamp for the Path to Awakening (Bodhipathapradīpa; byang chub lam gyi sgron ma)](../texts/bodhipathapradipa.md) v39-41
