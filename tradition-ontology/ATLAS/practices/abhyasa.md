The effort toward stability of the mind (YS 1.13): the carrying out of the means to the peaceful flow of a mind without activities, from the wish to accomplish it, with endeavour, vigour and enthusiasm (YBh 1.13). It becomes firmly grounded when followed for a long time, without a break, and with earnestness (satkāra), accomplished through austerity, continence/studentship, knowledge and faith (YS 1.14, YBh 1.14).
**Stage:** all
**Prerequisites:** ['The text addresses it to one not able to fix citta steadily on the Lord (12.9).']
**Duration:** 'for a long time, without interruption' (1.14)
**Signs of progress:** ["Firmly grounded practice is 'not suddenly overcome by the impression of emergence' (YBh 1.14).", 'result stated: one goes to the supreme divine Person (8.8)']
