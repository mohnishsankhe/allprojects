# Practice (abhyāsa)

`prc:abhyasa` · `skeleton` · confidence high

Repeated effort to hold the mind in stability (1.13), cultivated for a long time, without interruption and with devotion — with austerity, celibacy, knowledge and faith — so that it becomes firmly grounded (1.14, YBh 1.14); it opens the stream of discrimination (YBh 1.12).
**Stage:** all
**Duration:** 'for a long time, without interruption' (1.14)
**Signs of progress:** not quickly overpowered by the impressions of emergence (YBh 1.14)
