# Vārisāra (inner washing with water)

`prc:varisara-dhauti` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** cleansing
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

Water is drunk slowly until the body is filled to the throat, moved through the belly and expelled downward (GS 1.17-19).
**Stage:** beginner
**Signs of progress:** ['cleans the body; one who practises it with effort gains a divine body (GS 1.18-19)']
**Sources:** 
  - [Gheraṇḍa Saṃhitā](../texts/gheranda-samhita.md) — ref: 1.17-19; rests_on: ["tea:gheranda-samhita:1.17-19"]

## The texts' own warnings
- Vārisāra is most secret. — [Gheraṇḍa Saṃhitā](../texts/gheranda-samhita.md) 1.18

_Notes: Later (recent) teaching calls a comparable practice śaṅkhaprakṣālana; that name is not in the Gheraṇḍa._
