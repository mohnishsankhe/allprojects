# The meditation on the gatherer (saṃvarga-vidyā)

`prc:samvarga-vidya` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Venerate wind as the gatherer into which fire, sun, moon and waters go (with respect to the gods) and breath as the gatherer into which speech, sight, hearing and mind go in sleep (with respect to the self).
