# The means-accomplishments: study, hearing, reasoning, friends, giving

`prc:samkhya-means-of-knowledge` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)
**Taught in:** [Sāṃkhya](../lineages/samkhya.md)

Five of the eight accomplishments of SK 51 serve as means to knowledge of the principles: studying the texts, hearing instruction, examining by reasoning ('what is true, what is highest, what is the final good'), gaining a friend or fellow student who knows, and serving the venerable with gifts (Gauḍapāda) — Vācaspati (recalled) reads dāna as the purification of discriminative knowledge. Approaching a teacher to end the three sufferings is itself an accomplishment.
**Stage:** beginner to intermediate
**Prerequisites:** desire to know born of suffering (SK 1)
**Signs of progress:** knowledge of the twenty-five principles arises
