# Awakening the Lord at dawn (tiruppaḷḷiyeḻucci)

**Taught in:** [The Āḻvārs (Tamil Vaiṣṇava saint-poets) and their hymn tradition](../lineages/alvar.md), [Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta (Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya)](../lineages/visistadvaita.md)

At dawn the Lord in the temple is roused with song — the sun has risen, the gods and sages wait at his door — and the singer asks to be made servant of his servants. Toṇṭaraṭippoṭi's ten verses are sung daily at Śrīraṅgam and other Śrīvaiṣṇava temples.
