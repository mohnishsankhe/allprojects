# Entry into cognition-only (vijñaptimātratā-praveśa)

`prc:entry-into-cognition-only` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): `lin:faxiang`, [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)
**Taught in:** `lin:faxiang`, [Yogācāra](../lineages/yogacara.md)

Having gathered merit and knowledge and reflected on the teachings, the bodhisattva in concentration sees objects as nothing but mental speech, rests in mind-only that appears as them, then no longer apprehends the object (the grasped is abandoned), then abandons the apprehension of mind-only itself (the grasper), and non-conceptual wisdom free of duality arises: 'where there is nothing grasped there is no grasping'.
**Stage:** prayoga-mārga (the four aids to penetration) leading to darśana-mārga
**Prerequisites:** accumulation of merit and knowledge; hearing and reflection on the Mahāyāna teaching; stable calm
**Signs of progress:** objects no longer seen apart from mental speech (heat); increase of the light of Dharma (peak); abiding in mind-only (patience); only the grasper remains (supreme mundane quality); non-conceptual wisdom (first ground)

## The texts' own warnings
- Even the thought 'all this is cognition-only' is an apprehension that places something before oneself; one who holds it does not yet abide in cognition-only. — [Triṃśikā (Triṃśikāvijñaptikārikā)](../texts/trimsika.md) 27
