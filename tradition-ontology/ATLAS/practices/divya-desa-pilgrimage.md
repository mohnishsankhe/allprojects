# Pilgrimage to the divine sites and maṅgaḷāśāsana

Lineage contribution (Āḻvārs): the Āḻvārs travelled to the Lord's temples and sang each one — Tirumaṅkai some eighty — longing to be born even as a fish or a step at Tiruvēṅkaṭam, seeing the Lord at Śrīraṅgam from feet to face, calling the reclining Lord at Kuṭantai to rise and speak; the sites they sang became the 108 divya-deśas.
