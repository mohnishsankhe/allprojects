# Discerning the field and its knower (kṣetra-kṣetrajña)

Bhagavad Gītā 13–15: Knowing the field (the body and its constituents, 13.2, 13.6-13.7) and its knower is called knowledge (13.3). Whoever knows puruṣa and prakṛti with the guṇas thus is not born again (13.24). The one who truly sees sees the supreme lord abiding equally in all beings (13.28), actions as done by prakṛti alone and oneself as not the doer (13.30), and the separate existence of beings as resting in the one (13.31). Those who know the difference between field and knower with the eye of knowledge go to the highest (13.35).
