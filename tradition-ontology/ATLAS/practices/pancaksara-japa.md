# Repetition of the five-syllable mantra

Repetition of 'namaḥ śivāya' (six-syllabled with Oṃ) received at initiation, spoken, whispered or — best — mentally, after three breath-restraints, facing east or north and contemplating Śiva in the heart; also used in worshipping the liṅga.
