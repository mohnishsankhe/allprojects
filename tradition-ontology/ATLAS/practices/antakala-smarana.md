# Remembering the Lord at the hour of death

`prc:antakala-smarana` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** sleep-dream-death
**Convergence:** 2 independent lineage(s): [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md), [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)
**Taught in:** [Early Bhāgavata devotion (the Nārāyaṇīya / Sātvata–Ekāntika dharma)](../lineages/bhagavata-early.md), [Epic teaching (the Mahābhārata's Sāṃkhya-Yoga and Mokṣadharma milieu)](../lineages/epic-teaching.md)

Remember the Lord at all times so that at death one departs remembering him (8.5–7); the yogin at departure, with unmoving mind, closes the gates of the body, holds the mind in the heart, sets the breath in the head (or between the brows), is established in concentration, utters Oṃ, the one-syllabled Brahman, and remembers the Lord (8.10–13).
**Stage:** lifelong preparation; applied at death
