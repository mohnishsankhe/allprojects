# Remembering the Lord at the hour of death

Bhagavad Gītā 7–9: at the time of the end, leave the body remembering the Lord alone (8.5); because one goes to whatever state one remembers at the end, having always been shaped by it, remember him at all times — for Arjuna, while fighting — with mind and intellect offered to him (8.6–7). The yogin's departure is described twice: with unmoving mind, devotion and the power of yoga, the breath made to enter between the brows (8.9–10); and with the gates restrained, the mind confined in the heart, the breath placed in the head, uttering Oṃ and remembering the Lord (8.12–13).
**Stage:** all (8.5); the yogic form advanced (8.10–13)
**Prerequisites:** ["lifelong remembrance, having 'always been shaped' by the state remembered (8.6–7, 8.14)", 'knowing the Lord with the adhibhūta, adhidaiva and adhiyajña (7.30)']
**Signs of progress:** ["result stated: one comes to the Lord's state / the supreme goal, without doubt (8.5, 8.7, 8.13)"]
