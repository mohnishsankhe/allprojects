# Worship of the liṅga

`prc:linga-puja` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)
**Taught in:** [The Purāṇic tradition (paurāṇika)](../lineages/puranic.md)

Worship of Śiva in the formless liṅga (including liṅgas made of earth for the occasion), with bathing, bilva leaves, lamps and offerings; the Śiva Purāṇa teaches that Śiva alone may be worshipped in the liṅga because he alone is niṣkala.
