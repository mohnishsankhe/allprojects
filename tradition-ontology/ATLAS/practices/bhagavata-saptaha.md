# The seven-day hearing of the Bhāgavata (saptāha)

A formal recital of the whole Bhāgavata over seven days, modelled on Śuka's teaching to Parīkṣit, with vows for the hearers; the Māhātmya praises it above sacrifice, vows, yoga and meditation and tells how it freed the preta Dhundhukārī.
