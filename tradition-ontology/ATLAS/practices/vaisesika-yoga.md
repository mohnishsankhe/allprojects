# Yoga: the mind abiding in the self (Vaiśeṣika)

`prc:vaisesika-yoga` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)
**Taught in:** [Vaiśeṣika](../lineages/vaisesika.md)

Withdrawing the mind from contact with the senses so that it abides in the self, where the embodied one has neither pleasure nor pain; through a particular conjunction of self and mind the yogin perceives the self.
**Signs of progress:** ['absence of pleasure and pain while embodied', 'perception of the self']
