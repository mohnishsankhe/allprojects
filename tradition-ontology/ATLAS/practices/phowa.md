## The texts' own warnings
- When the time has come, transfer; transferring at the wrong time kills the deities. — [bka' dpe phyi ma (Nāropa's instructions received from Tilopa)](../texts/bka-dpe-phyi-ma.md) 274b.7
- Transference is to be done when the signs of death appear, with joy and without attachment. — [Karṇatantravajrapada (the Vajra Verses of the Whispered Lineage)](../texts/karnatantravajrapada.md) 303b.1
- Transference must never be applied before the signs of death are complete: to transfer untimely is like killing the deities. — [Kunzang Lamai Shelung (kun bzang bla ma'i zhal lung, the Words of My Perfect Teacher)](../texts/kunzang-lamai-shelung.md) 3
