Worship of the Goddess and Bhairava with consecrated wine, meat, fish, parched grain and ritual union, within the Kaula rite, by the initiated vīra under a guru; the texts present it as manifesting the bliss of Brahman in the body. ## The texts' own warnings
- If a man could reach perfection merely by drinking wine, all drunkards would be perfected. — [Kulārṇava Tantra](../texts/kularnava-tantra.md) 2.117
- Walking the Kula path is harder than walking a sword's edge, clinging to a tiger's neck or holding a snake. — [Kulārṇava Tantra](../texts/kularnava-tantra.md) 2.122
- The wilful, the uninitiated, one who drinks the unconsecrated substance, takes a woman by force or kills for his own pleasure, and the addict without Kaula instruction go to hell; the intoxicated Kaula knows neither meditation nor God nor guru and falls. — [Kulārṇava Tantra](../texts/kularnava-tantra.md) 5.96-105
- Those who abandon ritual action out of half-learning are heretics and go to hell. — [Kulārṇava Tantra](../texts/kularnava-tantra.md) 9.130
- In the Kali age the last tattva only with one's own wife; the sweet triad replaces wine for householders. — [Mahānirvāṇa Tantra](../texts/mahanirvana-tantra.md) 6.14; 8.170-171
- In the Kali age the vīra and divya dispositions do not exist; worship without meat, in the paśu disposition. — [Kālīvilāsa Tantra](../texts/kalivilasa-tantra.md) 4.2-6
- A brāhmaṇa must never offer wine to the Goddess; where wine is prescribed he offers coconut water or honey. — [Kālikā Purāṇa](../texts/kalika-purana.md) 67.49-51; 67.114-115
