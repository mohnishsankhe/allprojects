# Circumambulation (pradakṣiṇa)

`prc:pradaksina` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)
**Taught in:** [Pāśupata (Pāñcārthika Pāśupata)](../lineages/pasupata.md)

Clockwise circling of the image while muttering, after the offering; one of the vows. The Sūtra adds that in Rudra's worship the counter-clockwise too is clockwise.
