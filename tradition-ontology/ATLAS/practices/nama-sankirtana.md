# Congregational chanting of the name (nāma-saṅkīrtana)

`prc:nama-sankirtana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mantra-sound
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md), [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md), [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)
**Taught in:** [Ekaśaraṇa Dharma](../lineages/ekasarana.md), [Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism (Caitanya Sampradāya; Acintya Bhedābheda)](../lineages/gaudiya-vaisnava.md), [Vārkarī sampradāya](../lineages/varkari.md)

Singing the Lord's names together, aloud, with drums (khol/mṛdaṅga) and cymbals, in homes, temples and street processions (nagara-saṅkīrtana), led by a lead singer with response; held to be the dharma of Kali and the most potent limb; to be done with humility (Śikṣāṣṭaka 3).
**Stage:** all
**Signs of progress:** ['dancing and ecstatic signs', 'humility and tolerance']
