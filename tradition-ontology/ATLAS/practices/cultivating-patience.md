# Cultivating patience

`prc:cultivating-patience` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)

Seeing that anger destroys the merit of aeons, one cultivates patience by reflection: if a thing can be remedied, why be unhappy, and if not, why be unhappy; suffering has virtues (humility, compassion, dread of evil); harm arises from causes and conditions, and the enemy is a helper in the practice of patience.
