# Vajrolī in the Gheraṇḍasaṃhitā (the arm-supported lift)

`prc:vajroli-gheranda` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** lock-seal
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Haṭha Yoga (the haṭha and rājayoga textual tradition)](../lineages/hatha-yoga.md)

Supporting the ground with both hands, both legs and the head are thrown up into the air; the sages call it vajrolī, for awakening the power and for long life (GS 3.45-48).
**Stage:** advanced
**Signs of progress:** ['mastery of bindu; the cause of liberation; success even for one given to enjoyment (GS 3.46-48)']
**Sources:**
