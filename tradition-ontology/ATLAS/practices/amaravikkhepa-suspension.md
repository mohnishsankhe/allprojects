# Withholding assertion (the eel-wriggler's refusal)

`prc:amaravikkhepa-suspension` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Ajñāna (the sceptics)](../lineages/ajnana.md)
**Taught in:** [Ajñāna (the sceptics)](../lineages/ajnana.md)

(As reported) Declining to affirm or deny any of the four alternatives on questions beyond experience, to avoid false speech, grasping, or being refuted.
**Sources:** 
  - [Brahmajāla Sutta](../texts/brahmajala-sutta.md) — ref: DN 1 §2.23–2.29; rests_on: ["tea:brahmajala-sutta:2.23-2.29"]
  - [Sāmaññaphala Sutta](../texts/samannaphala-sutta.md) — ref: DN 2 §32; rests_on: ["tea:samannaphala-sutta:32"]

## The texts' own warnings
- The Buddhist texts count it a wrong view, 'the feeling of those who do not know or see'. — [Brahmajāla Sutta](../texts/brahmajala-sutta.md) DN 1
- The ignorant cannot even instruct themselves, let alone others. — [Sūtrakṛtāṅga](../texts/sutrakrtanga.md) 1.1.2.17
