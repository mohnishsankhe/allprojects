# Hearing, reflection and meditation

`prc:three-wisdoms` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md)

Wisdom from hearing grasps the meaning of scripture; wisdom from reflection discerns definitive from provisional meaning; wisdom from meditation cultivates what has been ascertained as real — so that doubt is removed and correct knowledge arises.
