# Nirvikalpa samādhi (Vedānta)

`prc:nirvikalpa-samadhi` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)
**Taught in:** [Advaita Vedānta](../lineages/advaita-vedanta.md)

Absorption in which the mode in the form of Brahman remains without distinction of knower and known, like salt dissolved in water; its eight auxiliaries are the yoga limbs and its obstacles dullness, distraction, latent attachment and relishing.
