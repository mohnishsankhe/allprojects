# Ear-splitting (kān-phaṛāī)

`prc:karna-chedana` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate · _restricted: summary only_

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)
**Taught in:** [Nāth tradition (Nātha Siddhas; Nāth / Kānphaṭa Yogīs)](../lineages/natha.md)

At the second initiation the cartilage of both ears is split by a specialist guru and, after healing, the large earrings (darśan/mudrā) are inserted — the defining mark of the Kānphaṭa yogī. Summary only.
**Stage:** second initiation
**Prerequisites:** first initiation and probation
**Sources:** 
  - [Gorakṣaguṭikā](../texts/goraksa-gutika.md) — ref: 1.17-22; rests_on: ["tea:goraksa-gutika:1.4-24"]
**Sequences:** [The stages of Nāth initiation](../paths/natha-initiation-stages.md)

## The texts' own warnings
- A bodily rite performed only by the guru within the order; not a practice to be undertaken by oneself (tradition's rule as reported). — [Hīr (Wāris Shāh)](../texts/hir-waris-shah.md) Tilla episode
