# Worship of the nine enclosures of the Śrīcakra (navāvaraṇa-pūjā)

`prc:sricakra-navavarana-puja` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** ritual
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)
**Taught in:** [Kaula Śrīvidyā (the Vāmakeśvara–Yoginīhṛdaya exegetical line)](../lineages/kaula-srividya.md), [Śrīvidyā](../lineages/srividya.md)

After the preliminaries (Gaṇapati worship, purification, placements on the body, preparation of the special water), the Goddess is invoked into the central point and the deities of the nine enclosures are worshipped from the outer square inward — each enclosure with its presiding goddess, class of yoginīs, attendant deities and mudrā — followed by the sixty-four services, libations into the bindu, japa and the offering of all to the Goddess. The Yoginīhṛdaya calls this cakra-worship 'aparā', performed even by Śiva; the Samaya school performs it only inwardly. (Summary; no mantras recorded.)
