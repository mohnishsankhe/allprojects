# The Sunlun method

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Theravāda](../lineages/theravada.md)
**Taught in:** [Sunlun Sayadaw tradition](../lineages/sunlun.md)

A sitting begins with strong, deliberate breathing to build concentration, after which the breathing is released and attention is fixed on the touch sensation at the nostrils and then on all bodily sensations, especially pain, which is watched without moving until it dissolves — summary only.
