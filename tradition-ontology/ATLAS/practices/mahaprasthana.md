## The texts' own warnings
- The epic presents the departure as the close of a completed life after renunciation of kingship; each of the fallen is said to fall by his own fault, and only Yudhiṣṭhira reaches heaven in the body. — [Mahābhārata](../texts/mahabharata.md) 17.2
- Later Dharmaśāstra authorities list the great departure among practices forbidden in the Kali age (kalivarjya). — [Nirṇayasindhu of Kamalākara Bhaṭṭa](../texts/nirnayasindhu.md) kalivarjya lists
