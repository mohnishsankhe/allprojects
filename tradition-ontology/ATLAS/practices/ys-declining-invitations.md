# Declining the invitations of the high stations (YS 3.51)

`prc:ys-declining-invitations` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)
**Taught in:** [Pātañjala Yoga (the Yoga darśana)](../lineages/patanjala-yoga.md)

As the bhāṣya on 3.51 states it: when gods of high stations invite the yogin who has realised the madhumatī stage, he reflects on the faults of attachment (that he has obtained the lamp of yoga in the darkness of saṃsāra and would be deceived by a mirage of sense-objects), dismisses the offers as dream-like, and cultivates samādhi; having no attachment he also feels no pride, for pride would make him think himself secure, and heedlessness watching for a gap would stir the afflictions again.
