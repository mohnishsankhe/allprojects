# Pilgrimage to the tīrthas

`prc:jain-tirtha-yatra` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** devotion-service
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)
**Taught in:** [Digambara](../lineages/digambara.md), [Jainism (Jaina dharma)](../lineages/jainism.md), [Śvetāmbara](../lineages/svetambara.md)

Pilgrimage on foot to the places of the kalyāṇakas and nirvāṇas and to great temple-hills — Sammeda Śikhara (Pārasnāth), Śatruñjaya (Pālitāṇā), Girnār, Pāvāpurī, Aṣṭāpada (in memory), Śravaṇabeḷagoḷa, Ābu, Rāṇakpur — often with vows and fasting; the 'six-rule' (cha'rī pālit) sangha pilgrimage imitates mendicant discipline.
**Stage:** all
