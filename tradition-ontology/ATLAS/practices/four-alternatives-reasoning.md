# Examining arising by the four alternatives

`prc:four-alternatives-reasoning` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)

Examining whether a thing arises from itself, from another, from both or without cause, and finding each impossible, one understands that it does not arise by own-nature (the reasoning Tibetan tradition calls the 'diamond slivers').
**Stage:** intermediate
