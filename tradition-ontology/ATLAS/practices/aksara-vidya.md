# Meditation on the imperishable (akṣara-vidyā)

`prc:aksara-vidya` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](../lineages/vedic-srauta.md)
**Taught in:** [Upaniṣadic tradition (the teaching of the early Upaniṣads)](../lineages/upanisadic.md)

Know the imperishable by the negations 'not gross, not fine, not short, not long...' and as that by whose command sun and moon stand apart; without this knowledge all rites are finite.
