# Equalizing self and other

`prc:equalizing-self-and-other` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** mind-training
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md)

One first reflects that others want happiness and fear suffering exactly as oneself, so there is no reason to protect oneself alone; the world is to be protected as one's own body with its many limbs.
