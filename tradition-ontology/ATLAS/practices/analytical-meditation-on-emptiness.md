# Analytical meditation on emptiness

`prc:analytical-meditation-on-emptiness` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Category:** inquiry
**Convergence:** 3 independent lineage(s): [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md), [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Gelug](../lineages/gelug.md), [Kadam](../lineages/kadam.md), [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md), [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md)

In the Gelug: identifying the object of negation as it appears to innate self-grasping, then applying the sevenfold or four-point analysis until its absence is ascertained, and alternating this with placement on that absence; Atiśa: ascertaining by scripture and reasoning that phenomena are unborn, then meditating non-conceptually.
**Stage:** intermediate
**Prerequisites:** ['the awakening mind', 'calm abiding', 'hearing and reflecting on the texts (three wisdoms)']
**Signs of progress:** ['joy, tears and horripilation on hearing of emptiness (MA 6.4–5)', "fear of 'I am not' giving way to its end (RĀ 1.26; BCA 9.56–57)", 'the mind pacified without object when neither being nor non-being remains (BCA 9.35)']
