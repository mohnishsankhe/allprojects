# Analytical meditation on emptiness

`prc:analytical-meditation-on-emptiness` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Category:** meditation
**Convergence:** 1 independent lineage(s): [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md)
**Taught in:** [Madhyamaka](../lineages/madhyamaka.md), [Prāsaṅgika-Madhyamaka](../lineages/prasangika.md), [Yogācāra-Madhyamaka](../lineages/yogacara-madhyamaka.md)

With calm established, the practitioner examines a chosen object — the self, the body, arising, a thing — with the reasonings of the Madhyamaka (the four alternatives of arising, the fivefold or sevenfold search, neither one nor many, dependent origination) until it is not found; when nothing is found, not even non-existence, the mind rests without support in that non-finding, then analysis is resumed. Hearing and reflection precede it.
