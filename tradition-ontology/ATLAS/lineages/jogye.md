# Jogye order

`lin:jogye` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Jogye-jong 曹溪宗
**Parent:** [Seon (Korean Chan)](seon.md)
**Founders:** [Jinul 知訥 (Bojo)](../teachers/jinul.md)
**Key teachers:** [Jinul 知訥 (Bojo)](../teachers/jinul.md), [Hyesim 慧諶 (Jingak guksa)](../teachers/hyesim.md), [Taego Bou 太古普愚](../teachers/taego-bou.md), [Hyujeong 休靜 (Seosan daesa)](../teachers/hyujeong.md), [Toeong Seongcheol 退翁性徹](../teachers/seongcheol.md)
**Regions:** Korea (Songgwangsa, Haeinsa, Jogyesa)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: the name recalls Caoxi (Jogye), Huineng's mountain; Jinul's Suseonsa community (Songgwangsa) is taken as its heartland; the modern order counts Doui as founder and Taego as reviver; Scholarly account: a Goryeo Jogye school existed from the 12th c.; the modern Jogye Order was constituted in 1962; (confidence moderate)
**Status:** living

The Korean Seon order named after Huineng's Caoxi, which regards Jinul and Taego as its great masters; today the largest order of Korean Buddhism.

## Distinctive positions
- Ganhwa Seon combined with doctrinal study (the four-text curriculum, sajip).
