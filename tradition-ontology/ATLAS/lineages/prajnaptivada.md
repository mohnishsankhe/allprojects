**Parent:** [Mahāsāṃghika](mahasanghika.md)

A Mahāsāṃghika branch distinguishing what is real from what is designation (prajñapti).

## Distinctive positions
- Suffering is not an aggregate; the twelve sense-bases are not real (designations); conditioned things are designated in mutual dependence (Vasumitra; low confidence).
