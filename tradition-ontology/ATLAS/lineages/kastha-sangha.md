# Kāṣṭhā Saṅgha

`lin:kastha-sangha` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Family:** ascetic
**Parent:** [Digambara](digambara.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: founded (per Devasena's Darśanasāra) by Kumārasena; dated there in the Vikrama era (low confidence); Scholarly account: attested c. 10th–17th c. CE; (confidence low)
**Status:** absorbed

A Digambara lineage of north and west India (named after the town Kāṣṭhā) condemned as 'false' by Devasena but later accepted as a Digambara branch with its own bhaṭṭārakas.

## Distinctive positions
- uses a whisk of yak-tail hair (gopuccha) instead of peacock feathers; accused in the Darśanasāra of permitting women's initiation and other deviations (low confidence)
