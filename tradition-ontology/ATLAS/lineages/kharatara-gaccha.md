**Status:** living

A Mūrtipūjaka gaccha tracing itself to Jineśvarasūri's 1024 debate against the temple-dwelling monks at the Caulukya court; known for its Dādāguru cult and strong presence in Rajasthan.

## Distinctive positions
- born of the reform against temple-dwelling (caityavāsa) monks, insisting that monks live in lodgings (vasati) given by laypeople
- veneration of the four 'Dādāgurus' (Jinadatta, Maṇidhārī Jinacandra, Jinakuśala, Jinacandra) at shrines called dādābāḍīs
