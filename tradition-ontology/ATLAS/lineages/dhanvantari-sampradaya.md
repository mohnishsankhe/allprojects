The lineage of Dhanvantari, born as King Divodāsa of Kāśī, who taught Suśruta and his fellow students with surgery as the root (Su Sū 1.3-21). Caraka names its specialists 'the Dhānvantarīyas' (Ca Ci 5.44, 5.63).

## Distinctive positions
- Surgery (śalya) is the first and foremost of the eight limbs, being quick in effect and using instruments, knives, caustics and cautery (Su Sū 1.17-18).
- The person treated is the union of the five elements with the embodied one (Su Sū 1.22).
- Knowledge must be both textual and practical; the surgeon learns by practice on models (Su Sū 9).
