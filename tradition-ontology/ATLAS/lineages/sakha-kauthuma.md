# Kauthuma śākhā (Sāmaveda)

`lin:sakha-kauthuma` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** Kauthumīya
**Parent:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](vedic-srauta.md)
**Key teachers:** [Jaimini](../teachers/jaimini.md)
**Regions:** Gujarat, North India, Tamil Nadu and elsewhere
**Status:** living

The principal living Sāmaveda recension, preserving the verse-books and the melodies (gānas) sung by the udgātṛ priests.

## Distinctive positions
- Its Saṃhitā (Pūrvārcika and Uttarārcika) and four song-books (grāmageya, āraṇyageya, ūha, ūhya) are the standard Sāmaveda.
- The Pañcaviṃśa (Tāṇḍya) and Ṣaḍviṃśa Brāhmaṇas and the Chāndogya Brāhmaṇa and Upaniṣad belong to its tradition.
