The meditation movement founded by Maharishi Mahesh Yogi (d. 2008), a disciple of Brahmānanda Sarasvatī of Jyotirmaṭha, teaching a mantra technique to reach transcendental consciousness and a map of seven states of consciousness.

## Distinctive positions
- An effortless technique of mental repetition of a mantra given by a trained teacher, practised about twenty minutes twice a day, lets the mind settle to transcendental consciousness (the fourth state).
- Seven states of consciousness: waking, dreaming, sleeping, transcendental, cosmic, God and unity consciousness.
- Being is the ultimate reality; action is to be done established in Being (BhG 2.48 as read by Maharishi).
- The mantras are kept confidential and are not recorded here.

## The ultimate in this lineage

**Names:** Being, pure consciousness (transcendental consciousness)
**Descriptions:** Being is the absolute, unmanifest, eternal field of pure consciousness underlying all creation.
**Relation to self:** The Self is Being; in unity consciousness everything is perceived in terms of the Self.
**Relation to world:** The world is the expression of Being; in God consciousness it is perceived in its finest values.
**Caveat:** The movement presents Being in its own vocabulary; the Advaita reading it claims is its own account.
