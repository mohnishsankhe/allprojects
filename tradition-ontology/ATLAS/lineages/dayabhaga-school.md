# Dāyabhāga school of Dharmaśāstra (Bengal)

The Bengal tradition following Jīmūtavāhana's Dāyabhāga (c. 12th c.), which argues against the Mitākṣarā on inheritance and the basis of kinship.

## Distinctive positions
- Ownership of the father's property passes to the heirs only on the father's death or fall (uparamasvatva), not by birth.
- Heirship follows capacity to benefit the deceased by ancestral offerings (piṇḍa).
