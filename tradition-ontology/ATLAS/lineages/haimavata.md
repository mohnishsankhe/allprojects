# Haimavata

`lin:haimavata` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Hemavatika (Pali), Xueshan bu 雪山部
**Parent:** [Sthavira (the Elders' group of the first schism)](sthavira.md)
**Status:** extinct

A Sthavira branch that Vasumitra identifies with the original Elders displaced to the Himalaya by the Sarvāstivāda; listed among the later schools in the Pali chronicles.

## Distinctive positions
- Vasumitra identifies the Haimavatas with the original Sthaviras, who withdrew to the Himalaya when the Sarvāstivāda became dominant.
- The bodhisattva is an ordinary being (pṛthagjana) (Vasumitra; low confidence).
