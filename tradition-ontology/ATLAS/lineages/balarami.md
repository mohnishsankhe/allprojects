# Balarāmī sampradāya

**Family:** vedic

**Regions:** Meherpur (Nadia, now Bangladesh)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: early 19th c.; Balarām Hāḍi c. 1785–1850; (confidence low)
**Status:** living

A small Bengali sect founded by Balarām Hāḍi of Meherpur, of 'untouchable' birth, which rejected caste and brahmanical religion and venerated its founder as God.

## Distinctive positions
- Founded by a man of the Hāḍi (sweeper) caste, held by his followers to be the supreme God (Hāḍirām).
- Rejection of brahmanical ritual, caste and the Vedic gods; worship of the human.
