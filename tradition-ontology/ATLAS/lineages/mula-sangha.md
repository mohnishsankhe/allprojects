# Mūla Saṅgha (Digambara)

`lin:mula-sangha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** with the Nandi, Sena, Deva and Siṃha gaṇas, Balātkāra gaṇa, Sarasvatī gaccha, Kundakundānvaya
**Parent:** [Digambara](digambara.md)
**Status:** living

The principal historical Digambara monastic lineage, subdivided into gaṇas and gacchas, whose inscriptions name Kundakunda as ancestor; most present Digambara monks and bhaṭṭārakas belong to it.

## Distinctive positions
- the 'root' Digambara lineage, claiming descent through Kundakunda (Kundakundānvaya); its bhaṭṭāraka seats and monks use the peacock-feather whisk
