# Caitika

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Caitya / Cetiyavāda (Pali), Zhiduo shan bu 制多山部
**Parent:** [Mahāsāṃghika](mahasanghika.md)
**Founders:** [Mahādeva (of the five points)](../teachers/mahadeva.md)
**Status:** extinct

A Mahāsāṃghika branch in all lists; in Vasumitra's account founded by a Mahādeva at a caitya hill; connected with the Andhra Śaila schools.

## Distinctive positions
- Founded by a (second) Mahādeva who revived the five points at the end of the second century (Vasumitra); associated with caitya worship.
