# Saṃmitīya

`lin:sammitiya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Sāṃmatīya, Sammitiya (Pali), Zhengliang bu 正量部
**Parent:** [Pudgalavāda](pudgalavada.md)
**Status:** extinct

The largest Pudgalavāda school, prominent in western and eastern India in the 7th century; one of the four nikāyas of Yijing and of the Tibetan four-group classification.

## Distinctive positions
- The person is real, designated on the basis of the aggregates, neither the same as nor different from them (Saṃmitīyanikāya-śāstra).
- Counted by Yijing among the four great nikāyas of India; by Xuanzang as present in very many monasteries (low confidence on figures).
- Kathāvatthu commentary ascribes to the Sammitiyas the person (Kv 1.1) and the intermediate existence (Kv 8.2).
