**Regions:** Nalendra (Phenpo), Dar Drangmochen
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 16th c. CE; (confidence moderate)
**Status:** living

Sub-school stemming from Tsarchen Losal Gyatso (1502–1566), who re-transmitted the Lamdre Lobshé as a hidden oral instruction and the 'thirteen golden dharmas'; associated with Nalendra monastery.

## Distinctive positions
- Holds the Sakya Path with its Result and the Khön family's teaching lineages; distinguished from the other Sakya lines by its own transmission and monastic seat.
