# Vetulyakas (as named in the Kathāvatthu commentary and Sri Lankan chronicles)

`lin:vetulyaka` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Vaitulyaka, Mahāsuññatāvādins (commentary)
**Status:** extinct

A group whose 'Vetulya' teachings were rejected by the Mahāvihāra; the chronicles report their suppression by Sri Lankan kings; the commentary calls them Mahāsuññatāvādins.

## Distinctive positions
- Reported (by the Kathāvatthu commentary) to hold that the Buddha did not really live in the human world and did not himself teach the Dhamma (a created form taught it), and that the Saṅgha does not really accept offerings.
