# Vaibhāṣika

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Kāśmīra-Vaibhāṣika, the followers of the Vibhāṣā

**Status:** extinct

The Vaibhāṣikas ('followers of the Vibhāṣā') were the Kashmiri orthodoxy of the Sarvāstivāda, taking the Mahāvibhāṣā as authoritative. Saṅghabhadra's Nyāyānusāra defended their positions against the Sautrāntika criticisms of the Abhidharmakośabhāṣya.

## Distinctive positions
- The Abhidharma, arranged by Kātyāyanīputra and others from the Buddha's scattered teaching, is the word of the Buddha (Yaśomitra's report).
- The orthodoxy of the Mahāvibhāṣā: real existence of dharmas in three times, prāpti, avijñapti, the four characteristics as real forces.
- Vasubandhu presented the Kośa as 'mostly the Abhidharma as established by the Kashmiri Vaibhāṣikas' (AK 8.40).
