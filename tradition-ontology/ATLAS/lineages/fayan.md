# Fayan house

`lin:fayan` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Fayan zong 法眼宗
**Parent:** [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](chan.md)
**Founders:** [Fayan Wenyi 法眼文益](../teachers/fayan-wenyi.md)
**Key teachers:** [Luohan Guichen (Dizang) 羅漢桂琛](../teachers/luohan-guichen.md), [Fayan Wenyi 法眼文益](../teachers/fayan-wenyi.md), [Tiantai Deshao 天台德韶](../teachers/tiantai-deshao.md), [Yongming Yanshou](../teachers/yongming-yanshou.md)
**Regions:** China (Nanjing, Zhejiang); Goryeo Korea
**Dates:** Tradition's account: founded by Fayan Wenyi (885–958); Scholarly account: 10th c.; faded in the Song (11th c.); (confidence moderate)
**Status:** extinct

The last of the Five Houses to form, from Fayan Wenyi (885–958) in the line of Xuansha and Luohan Guichen; it drew on Huayan doctrine, produced Yongming Yanshou's synthesis of Chan and the teachings, and faded by the 11th century.

## Distinctive positions
- Use of Huayan categories (the six characteristics) within Chan (Rentian yanmu).
- 'Not knowing is most intimate' (Luohan Guichen to Fayan).
- Harmony of Chan with the scriptural teachings and with Pure Land practice (Yongming Yanshou).

**Transmissions given:** 
  - [Seon (Korean Chan)](seon.md) — what: Goryeo monks trained under Yongming Yanshou (tradition says 36)

## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence low

**Names:** the one mind (yixin)
**Descriptions:** 'The three realms are only mind; the myriad dharmas are only consciousness' as Fayan's guiding principle (Rentian yanmu).
**Negations:** 'Not knowing is most intimate' (Luohan Guichen to Fayan).
**Relation to world:** Every dharma bears the six characteristics of the Huayan teaching, total and particular at once.
**Caveat:** Fayan uses Huayan categories but as Chan pointers.
