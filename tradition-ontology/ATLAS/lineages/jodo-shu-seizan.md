The line of Hōnen's disciple Shōkū (Zenne-bō, 1177–1247), who had Tendai esoteric training and taught at Nishiyama (Seizan) near Kyoto; Ippen's teacher Shōtatsu belonged to it. Today three Seizan schools (Jōdo-shū Seizan, Seizan Zenrinji-ha, Seizan Fukakusa-ha).

## Distinctive positions
- All practices are taken up into (and have their meaning in) the nenbutsu; the Buddha's attainment ten kalpas ago and the beings' birth are one (as usually summarised for Shōkū's line).
