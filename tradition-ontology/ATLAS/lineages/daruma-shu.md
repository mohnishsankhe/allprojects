# Daruma school (Japan)

`lin:daruma-shu` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Daruma-shū 達磨宗
**Parent:** [Zen (Japanese Chan)](zen.md)
**Founders:** [Dainichi Nōnin 大日能忍](../teachers/dainichi-nonin.md)
**Key teachers:** [Dainichi Nōnin 大日能忍](../teachers/dainichi-nonin.md), [Kakuan 覺晏](../teachers/kakuan.md), [Koun Ejō 孤雲懷奘](../teachers/koun-ejo.md), [Tettsū Gikai 徹通義介](../teachers/tettsu-gikai.md)
**Regions:** Japan (Settsu, Tōnomine, Hajakuji in Echizen)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: late 12th c.; proscribed 1194 together with Eisai's movement; its members joined Dōgen's community in the 1240s; (confidence moderate)
**Status:** absorbed

The first independent Zen movement in Japan, founded by Dainichi Nōnin, whose certificate came by proxy from Dahui's disciple Zhuoan Deguang; its monks later became leading disciples of Dōgen.

## Distinctive positions
- Awakening without a teacher (mushi dokugo); criticised by Eisai for rejecting precepts and practice (Kōzen gokoku ron).

**Transmissions received:** 
  - [Linji house](linji.md) — what: Nōnin sent disciples to China (1189) who obtained a certificate from Zhuoan Deguang, an heir of Dahui; evidence: tradition records; scholarly accounts
