The saṃhati of the Ekaśaraṇa sattras descended from Dāmodaradeva and Harideva, with brahmin leadership and greater accommodation of brahmanical practice; it includes several of the great Majuli sattras patronised by the Ahom kings (identification of individual sattras to verify).

## Distinctive positions
- Traces itself to the brahmins Dāmodaradeva and Harideva; brahmin sattrādhikārs.
- More accommodating of image worship and of brahmanical rites than the other saṃhatis.
- Bhaṭṭadeva's prose renderings of the Bhāgavata and Gītā belong to it.
