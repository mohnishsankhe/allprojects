A classificatory grouping: the 'Elders' who formed one side of the first schism of the Saṅgha against the Mahāsāṃghikas, and from whom the Sarvāstivāda, the Vibhajyavāda and Theravāda, the Mahīśāsaka, the Dharmaguptaka, the Kāśyapīya and the Pudgalavāda derived.

## Distinctive positions
- At the first schism the elders rejected the relaxations of Vinaya (Pali account) or the five points on the arhat (Vasumitra); the Mahāsāṃghika account says the elders enlarged the Vinaya.
- All later Sthavira branches (Sarvāstivāda, Vibhajyavāda/Theravāda, Mahīśāsaka, Dharmaguptaka, Kāśyapīya, Vātsīputrīya) trace themselves to this side of the split.
