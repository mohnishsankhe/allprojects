# Vibhajyavāda

`lin:vibhajyavada` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Vibhajjavāda (Pali), 'those who teach by distinguishing'
**Parent:** [Sthavira (the Elders' group of the first schism)](sthavira.md)
**Sub-lineages:** [Theravāda](theravada.md), [Mahīśāsaka](mahisasaka.md), [Dharmaguptaka](dharmaguptaka.md), [Kāśyapīya](kasyapiya.md)
**Status:** absorbed

A designation used in two ways: by the Sarvāstivāda for opponents who 'distinguish' which past and future things exist; by the Theravāda for itself. Modern accounts group the Theravāda, Mahīśāsaka, Dharmaguptaka and Kāśyapīya as Vibhajyavādins (scholarly hypothesis).

## Distinctive positions
- Some of the past and future exist and some do not — distinguishing rather than affirming that everything exists (AKBh 5.25).
- The mind is by nature luminous (reported and refuted in the Mahāvibhāṣā; low confidence on the attribution).
- The Theravāda calls itself Vibhajjavāda (the Buddha is a 'vibhajjavādī', Mahāvaṃsa; Kathāvatthu commentary).
