The line of Kirpal Singh (1894–1974), a disciple of Sawan Singh, who founded Ruhani Satsang in Delhi in 1951; after him the line divided among Darshan Singh (then Rajinder Singh), Thakar Singh, Ajaib Singh and others.

## Distinctive positions
- The same surat-śabd yoga, with emphasis on the first-hand inner experience given at initiation and on self-introspection (the daily diary).
- Unity of man; the teaching presented as the science of the soul.


## The ultimate in this lineage

**Names:** God / the Word (Naam)
**Descriptions:** the Word heard as sound and seen as light, in all scriptures
**Negations:** not the spoken name
**Relation to self:** The soul is of the same essence.
**Relation to world:** The Word creates and sustains all.
**Caveat:** Presented as the common core of all religions — a claim the religions named do not themselves make.
