The Mādhva maṭha of Vyāsatīrtha (Vyāsarāja), disciple of Brahmaṇya Tīrtha; seated at Sosale (Mysuru district). Vyāsatīrtha's brindāvana is at Nava Brindāvana near Ānegondi (Hampi).

## Distinctive positions
- Shares the Dvaita doctrine of lin:dvaita without doctrinal difference; distinct as an institutional line of ascetic pontiffs (guru-paramparā), with its own images, brindāvanas and observances.
