# Kaṭha śākhā (Black Yajurveda)

`lin:sakha-katha` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** Kāṭhaka, Caraka-Kaṭha
**Parent:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](vedic-srauta.md)
**Key teachers:** [Kaṭha](../teachers/katha.md)
**Regions:** formerly Kashmir and the north-west
**Status:** absorbed

A Black Yajurveda tradition of the north-west whose Saṃhitā survives in manuscript; living recitation is largely lost.

## Distinctive positions
- Its Kāṭhaka Saṃhitā survives; its Brāhmaṇa survives only in portions; the Kaṭha Upaniṣad is associated with it by name.
- Its domestic ritual (Laugākṣi/Kāṭhaka Gṛhya Sūtra) long remained in use among Kashmiri Brahmins while recitation of the Saṃhitā lapsed.
