# Shelun school (Chinese 'Mahāyānasaṃgraha' school)

`lin:shelun` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic

**Parent:** [Yogācāra](yogacara.md)
**Key teachers:** [Paramārtha](../teachers/paramartha.md)
**Regions:** Southern China
**Dates:** Scholarly account: founded on Paramārtha's translation of the Mahāyānasaṃgraha (563); absorbed into Faxiang in the 7th c.; (confidence moderate)
**Status:** absorbed

The Chinese tradition of studying Paramārtha's translation of Asaṅga's Mahāyānasaṃgraha, notable for the doctrine of a ninth, pure consciousness; superseded by Xuanzang's Faxiang.

## Distinctive positions
- Studied Asaṅga's Mahāyānasaṃgraha in Paramārtha's version.
- Taught (following Paramārtha) a ninth, 'stainless consciousness' (amalavijñāna) beyond the ālaya, identified with suchness.
