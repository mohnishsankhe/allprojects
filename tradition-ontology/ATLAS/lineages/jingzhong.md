# Jingzhong school

**Family:** ascetic

**Parent:** [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](chan.md)

**Dates:** Scholarly account: 8th c.; (confidence moderate)
**Status:** extinct

The Sichuan lineage of Zhishen, Chuji and the Korean master Musang (Kim heshang, 684–762), known from the Lidai fabao ji.

## Distinctive positions
- Musang's three phrases: no remembering (wuyi) = precepts, no thinking (wunian) = samādhi, no forgetting/no delusion (mowang) = wisdom (Lidai fabao ji).
- Mass ordination-and-teaching assemblies opened with drawn-out recitation of the Buddha's name (Lidai fabao ji).
