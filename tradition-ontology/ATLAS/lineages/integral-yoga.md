The yoga taught by Sri Aurobindo (1872–1950) and the Mother (Mirra Alfassa, 1878–1973) at Pondicherry, aiming at the transformation of earthly life by a supramental consciousness.

## Distinctive positions
- Spirit has involved itself in matter and evolves through it; the goal is not escape from the world but its transformation by the descent of the Supermind.
- Ascent of consciousness through Higher Mind, Illumined Mind, Intuition and Overmind to Supermind; a triple transformation — psychic, spiritual, supramental.
- 'All life is Yoga': works, knowledge, devotion and self-perfection integrated; aspiration, rejection and surrender to the Mother's force.
- Rejection of the doctrine that the world is illusion (māyāvāda) and of the ascetic refusal of life.

## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence high · _recent (post-1800)_

**Names:** Sachchidananda, the Supermind, the Divine Mother
**Descriptions:** The Absolute is Sachchidananda, which has involved itself in matter; the Supermind (Truth-Consciousness) is its creative power.; Both the materialist's denial of spirit and the ascetic's denial of matter are rejected.
**Relation to self:** The psychic being is the evolving divine spark in the individual; the Self is one with the Divine.
**Relation to world:** The world is real, a manifestation of the Divine, to be transformed rather than escaped.
**Caveat:** Explicitly rejects Śaṅkara's reading of the world as illusion; reconciliation with Advaita must record that rejection.
