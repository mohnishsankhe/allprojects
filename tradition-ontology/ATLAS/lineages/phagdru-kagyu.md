One of the 'four great' Dakpo Kagyu schools, founded by Phagmodrupa at Densa Thil; the eight 'lesser' schools (Drikung, Drukpa, Taklung, Trophu, Martsang, Yelpa, Yazang, Shugseb) were founded by his disciples.

## Distinctive positions
- Phagmodrupa joined Gampopa's Mahāmudrā with the Sakya Lamdre he had studied under Sachen.
