The saṃhati founded by Gopāla Ātā, a disciple of Mādhavadeva, marked by intense devotion to the guru and wide recruitment among low castes and tribes.

## Distinctive positions
- Founded by Gopāla Ātā of Bhavānīpur (seat at Kālajhār).
- Strongly guru-centred and egalitarian, drawing many low-caste and tribal followers; the Māyāmarā (Moāmoriyā) sattra of Aniruddhadeva belonged to it.
