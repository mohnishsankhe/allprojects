A branch of the Rāmānandīs, traced to Agradās of Raivāsā, that practises the sweet (mādhurya) contemplation of Sītā and Rāma's eternal love in the manner of the Kṛṣṇa rasik traditions, the devotee taking the role of a sakhī.

## Distinctive positions
- Contemplates the eternal, private love-play of Sītā and Rāma in the inner Ayodhyā (Sāketa) rather than the heroic epic deeds.
- The practitioner takes an inner identity as a female companion (sakhī) of Sītā, serving the divine couple.
- Sītā's grace is the gate to Rāma.
