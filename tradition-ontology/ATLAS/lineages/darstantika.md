# Dārṣṭāntika

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** 'those who teach by examples'

**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 1st c. BCE – 3rd c. CE (named in the Mahāvibhāṣā); (confidence low)
**Status:** absorbed

The Dārṣṭāntikas are a dissenting current within the Sarvāstivāda named and refuted in the Mahāvibhāṣā; they are generally regarded as forerunners of (or identical with) the later Sautrāntikas.

## Distinctive positions
- Mental karma is volition together with covetousness, malice and wrong view themselves (AKBh 4, Pradhan p. 237: 'abhidhyādaya eva manaskarmeti dārṣṭāntikāḥ').
- Rejection of several Vaibhāṣika real entities (reported in the Mahāvibhāṣā).
- Teaching by illustrative stories and examples (dṛṣṭānta), as in Kumāralāta's Kalpanāmaṇḍitikā.
