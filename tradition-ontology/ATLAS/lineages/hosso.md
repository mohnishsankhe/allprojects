# Hossō school (Japanese Faxiang)

**Family:** ascetic

**Parent:** `lin:faxiang`
**Key teachers:** [Dōshō](../teachers/dosho.md)
**Regions:** Nara, Japan (Kōfukuji, Yakushiji)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: transmitted to Japan from 653 (Dōshō) onward; one of the six Nara schools; continues at Kōfukuji and Yakushiji; (confidence moderate)
**Status:** living

The Japanese continuation of the Chinese Faxiang (Yogācāra) school, one of the six Nara schools, still maintained at Kōfukuji and Yakushiji.

## Distinctive positions
- The Cheng weishi lun system of eight consciousnesses and five gotras, as transmitted from Xuanzang and Kuiji.
