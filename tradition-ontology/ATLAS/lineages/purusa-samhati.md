The saṃhati of the sattras led by Śaṅkaradeva's descendants, above all his grandsons Puruṣottama and Caturbhuja Ṭhākur.

## Distinctive positions
- Led by Śaṅkaradeva's grandsons Puruṣottama and Caturbhuja Ṭhākur.
- Emphasis on the name and on Śaṅkaradeva's own line.
