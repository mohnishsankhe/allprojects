The panth of Garībdās of Chhuḍānī (1717–1778), a Jat householder saint who held Kabīr to be the supreme God and left a large collection of Sant verse.

## Distinctive positions
- Kabīr is the supreme God, who met Garībdās in person as a child.
- Garībdās's Bāṇī (his 'Granth Sāhib') is the scripture.
