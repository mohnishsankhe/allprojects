The śāstra of the third aim of life (kāma), represented by Vātsyāyana Mallanāga's Kāmasūtra; recorded here only for its teaching on the three aims (trivarga) and its reported debate with Lokāyatikas.

## Distinctive positions
- The three aims are to be pursued so that each supports and does not harm the others, distributed over a life of a hundred years; of dharma, artha and kāma each earlier one is weightier (Kāmasūtra 1.2.1–14).
- Kāma needs a śāstra because its right means must be learned; the text records and answers the Mīmāṃsaka and Lokāyatika objections (Kāmasūtra 1.2).
