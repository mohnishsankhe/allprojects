The Sōtō Zen community founded around Shunryu Suzuki (1904–1971) in San Francisco, with the Tassajara training monastery; known through Zen Mind, Beginner's Mind.

## Distinctive positions
- Zazen as just sitting (shikantaza): the posture itself expresses buddha-nature; practice is not a means to a later enlightenment.
- Beginner's mind (shoshin): 'in the beginner's mind there are many possibilities, in the expert's few'.
- Monastic training adapted for lay Western practitioners (Tassajara, the first Sōtō training monastery outside Asia).

## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

**Names:** Big Mind, buddha-nature
**Descriptions:** Big Mind, including everything; buddha-nature expressed in the posture of zazen.
**Relation to self:** No separate self; 'I' is a swinging door moved by breath.
**Relation to world:** Things as it is; practice is enlightenment.
**Caveat:** Zen would reject treating 'Big Mind' as a substantial absolute.
