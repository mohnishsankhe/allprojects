# Drāviḍa Saṅgha

`lin:dravida-sangha` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Family:** ascetic
**Parent:** [Digambara](digambara.md)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: founded by Vajranandi, a pupil of Pūjyapāda, at Madhurā (Madurai) according to the Darśanasāra; Scholarly account: c. 5th–13th c. CE in Tamil country and Karnataka; (confidence low)
**Status:** absorbed

A southern Digambara lineage associated with Vajranandi and the Tamil country, criticized in the Darśanasāra; later inscriptions treat it as a respected Digambara saṅgha.

## Distinctive positions
- charged by the Darśanasāra with allowing monks to eat seeds and to engage in trade and agriculture for temples (low confidence)
