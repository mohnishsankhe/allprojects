# Pūrvaśaila

`lin:purvasaila` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Pubbaseliya (Pali), Dongshan zhu bu 東山住部
**Parent:** [Mahāsāṃghika](mahasanghika.md)
**Status:** extinct

An Andhra branch of the Mahāsāṃghika (Amarāvatī, Nāgārjunakoṇḍa inscriptions), one of the Andhakas of the Pali commentary.

## Distinctive positions
- Kathāvatthu commentary ascribes to the Pubbaseliyas (with the Aparaseliyas) that others can impute impurity to an arhat, that an arhat has ignorance and doubt, and is instructed by others (Kv 2.1–2.4) — the five points.
- The Lokānuvartana-sūtra on the Buddha's conformity to the world is attributed to the Pūrvaśailas (low confidence).
