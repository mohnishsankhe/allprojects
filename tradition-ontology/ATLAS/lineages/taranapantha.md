# Tāraṇapantha (Samaiyā)

`lin:taranapantha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Parent:** [Digambara](digambara.md)
**Founders:** `tch:taranasvami`
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Tāraṇa Svāmī (1448–1515 CE); Scholarly account: 15th–16th c. CE, Bundelkhand and Malwa; (confidence moderate)
**Status:** living

An aniconic Digambara-derived community of central India founded by Tāraṇa Svāmī, whose shrines keep books instead of images.

## Distinctive positions
- no image worship: its shrines (caityālayas) house scriptures, which are venerated
- open to members of all castes; emphasis on the soul's inner nature in Tāraṇa Svāmī's fourteen works
