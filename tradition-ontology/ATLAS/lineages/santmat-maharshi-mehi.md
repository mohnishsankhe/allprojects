A Bihar Santmat lineage founded by Maharshi Mehi Paramhans, independent of the Radhasoami organizations, teaching the inner light and sound in a Vedāntic-Sant synthesis.

## Distinctive positions
- Santmat as the common teaching of the Upaniṣads, the Gītā and the Sants, compiled in the Satsang Yog.
- Graded practice: mental repetition (mānas jap), mental contemplation of form (mānas dhyān), point-focus (dṛṣṭi yoga) and then the inner sound (nādānusandhān).
