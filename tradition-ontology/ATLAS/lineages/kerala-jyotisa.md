The Kerala stream of jyotiṣa: astronomers of the Āryabhaṭa school (Haridatta's Parahita, Parameśvara's Dṛggaṇita, the Saṅgamagrāma lineage of Mādhava, Parameśvara, Nīlakaṇṭha Somayājī and Jyeṣṭhadeva) and a strong horā and praśna tradition carried in Nambūtiri and Piṣāraṭi families, with the Praśna Mārga as its manual of interrogation and remedies.

## Distinctive positions
- Follows the Āryabhaṭa school (āryapakṣa) in astronomy, corrected by observation (Parahita, Dṛggaṇita).
- Cultivates horā through Malayalam-country commentaries on the Bṛhajjātaka (Rudra's Vivaraṇa, Govinda Bhaṭṭatiri's Daśādhyāyī).
- Developed an elaborate interrogation ritual (aṣṭamaṅgala-praśna; devaprāśna for temples) codified in the Praśna Mārga.
