The line of Hōnen's disciple Benchō (Shōkō-bō, 1162–1238), active in Kyushu (Chinzei), continued by Ryōchū (Nen'a); it became the mainstream of Jōdo-shū, with Chion-in and Zōjō-ji, and systematised the fivefold transmission (gojū sōden) under Shōgei.

## Distinctive positions
- Nenbutsu is the practice of the primal vow; other practices can also lead to birth (shogyō ōjō) but are not the vow's own practice (as usually stated for Benchō's line).
- Many-calling: continue the nenbutsu throughout life.
