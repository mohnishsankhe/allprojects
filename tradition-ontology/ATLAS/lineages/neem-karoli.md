## Distinctive positions
- Love everyone, serve everyone, remember God, tell the truth.
- Devotion to Hanumān and Rāma; recitation of the Hanumān Cālīsā; feeding people.
- No formal doctrine or initiation system; the teacher's presence and stories of his powers (tradition's account) as the centre.
