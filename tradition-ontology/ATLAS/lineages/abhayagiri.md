# Abhayagiri fraternity (Abhayagiri-vāsins)

`lin:abhayagiri` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Dhammarucika, Abhayagiri vihāra
**Parent:** [Theravāda](theravada.md)
**Regions:** Anurādhapura (Sri Lanka)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: founded when King Vaṭṭagāmaṇī Abhaya built the Abhayagiri monastery (1st c. BCE, per the Mahāvaṃsa); absorbed in the 12th-c. unification; Scholarly account: 1st c. BCE – 12th c. CE; (confidence moderate)
**Status:** absorbed

The rival Sri Lankan fraternity to the Mahāvihāra, known mainly through Mahāvihāra chronicles and foreign reports; its own literature is largely lost, which is why the Mahāvihāra commentaries became the Theravāda norm.

## Distinctive positions
- Reported (by the Mahāvihāra chronicles) to have accepted the Vetulya texts and to have had contacts with Mahāyāna teachers.
- The Vimuttimagga is associated with this fraternity by some scholars (a scholarly hypothesis, not a tradition's claim).
