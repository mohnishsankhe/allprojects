# Satyākāravāda (True-Aspect Yogācāra)

`lin:satyakaravada` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Sākāravāda (in part), rnam bden pa (Tibetan doxography)
**Parent:** [Yogācāra](yogacara.md)
**Key teachers:** [Jñānaśrīmitra](../teachers/jnanasrimitra.md), [Ratnakīrti](../teachers/ratnakirti.md), [Prajñākaragupta](../teachers/prajnakaragupta.md)
**Status:** extinct

A late Indian Yogācāra position (and Tibetan doxographic class) holding that cognition's appearing aspects are real; associated especially with Jñānaśrīmitra and Ratnakīrti of Vikramaśīla.

## Distinctive positions
- The images (ākāra) that appear in cognition are real as cognition itself; the manifold image is one non-dual variegated cognition (citrādvaita).
- Only the duality of grasped and grasper, not the images themselves, is false.
