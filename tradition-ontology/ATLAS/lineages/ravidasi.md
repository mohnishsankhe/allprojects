**Status:** living

The community and religious tradition of the followers of Guru Ravidās (Raidās), predominantly of the Camār (leatherworker) caste. It venerates Ravidās's hymns (forty preserved in the Ādi Granth, more in Rajasthani manuscripts), his vision of Begampura and his rejection of caste; in the 20th c. it organized around deras in Punjab and declared a separate Ravidassia Dharam in 2010.

## Distinctive positions
- Ravidās, a leatherworker (Camār) of Banaras, is the guru; his hymns are the scripture.
- Begampura, the 'city without sorrow', where there is no tax, no fear, no second or third class, is the goal and the ideal.
- Rejection of caste and untouchability; dignity of the Camār community.
- The relation of self and God is like gold and bracelet, water and wave: 'between you and me, me and you, what difference?'
- Since 2010 the Dera Sachkhand Ballan line has a separate scripture (Amritbāṇī) and symbol (Har), distinct from the Sikh Ādi Granth.
