# Kāśyapīya

`lin:kasyapiya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Suvarṣaka (Vasumitra), Kassapiya (Pali), Yinguang bu 飲光部
**Parent:** [Sthavira (the Elders' group of the first schism)](sthavira.md)
**Status:** extinct

A Sthavira branch (from the Sarvāstivāda in Vasumitra's list, from the Sabbatthivāda in the Pali list) holding that only unripened past karma exists; the Chinese Bieyi Saṃyuktāgama (T100) has been attributed to it.

## Distinctive positions
- Past karma whose fruit has not yet ripened exists; past karma that has given its fruit, and the rest of the past, do not (Vasumitra; the Vibhajyavādin position of AKBh 5.25; Kathāvatthu 1.8 'some exist').
- A Prātimokṣa survives in Chinese (T1460).
