**Status:** absorbed

The lineage of Punarvasu Ātreya and his six disciples (Agniveśa, Bhela, Jatūkarṇa, Parāśara, Hārīta, Kṣārapāṇi) whose tantras were approved by the assembly of sages (Ca Sū 1.30-40); Agniveśa's, redacted by Caraka and completed by Dṛḍhabala, survives as the Caraka Saṃhitā.

## Distinctive positions
- Primacy of internal medicine (kāyacikitsā).
- Ātreya taught six disciples, each of whom composed a tantra; Agniveśa's was first (Ca Sū 1.30-33).
- Surgical procedures are left to the Dhānvantarīyas (Ca Ci 5.44).
