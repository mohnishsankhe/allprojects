# Jōdo Shinshū Honganji-ha

`lin:shinshu-honganji-ha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** 浄土真宗本願寺派, Nishi Honganji, Hompa Hongwanji
**Parent:** [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](jodo-shinshu.md)
**Regions:** Japan (Nishi Honganji, Kyoto)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 1602 (split of the Honganji); (confidence high)
**Status:** living

The western branch of the Honganji after the split of 1602, when Kyōnyo was given land for a separate Honganji; head temple Nishi Honganji. The largest Shinshū body; led historically by Shinran's descendants.

## Distinctive positions
- Shares the Shinshū teaching of shinjin as the true cause of birth; differs institutionally, liturgically and in doctrinal-academy lineages rather than in core doctrine.
