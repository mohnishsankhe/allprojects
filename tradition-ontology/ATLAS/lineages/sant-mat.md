The modern Sant Mat, begun in 1861 at Agra by Shiv Dayal Singh (Soamiji Maharaj) as Radhasoami, teaches the ascent of the soul on the inner sound current under a living master. It divided into the Agra lines (Soamibagh, Peepal Mandi, Dayalbagh), Radha Soami Satsang Beas, Kirpal Singh's Ruhani Satsang and its successors, Maharshi Mehi's Santmat of Bihar and others.

## Distinctive positions
- Surat-śabd yoga: the soul (surat) is withdrawn to the eye centre and rises by attaching itself to the audible life stream (śabd, dhun) through inner regions to its source.
- The necessity of a living Sant Satguru, who initiates (nām-dān) and guides the soul inwardly.
- Creation in grand divisions — piṇḍ (physical), aṇḍ, brahmāṇḍ (ruled by Kāl, the negative power), above which are the purely spiritual regions of Dayāl up to Anāmī / Radhasoami Dhām.
- Practice as simran (repetition of the names given at initiation), dhyān (contemplation of the Master's form) and bhajan (listening to the sound).
- Vows at initiation: lacto-vegetarian diet, no alcohol or drugs, a moral life, daily meditation (two and a half hours in the Beas line).

## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

**Names:** Radhasoami, Anāmī, Sat Purush, Shabd / Nām
**Descriptions:** the ocean of consciousness and love from which the sound current flows; reached beyond Sach Khand
**Negations:** nameless (Anāmī); beyond Brahm, the god of the Vedas; beyond mind and matter
**Relation to self:** The soul (surat) is a drop of that ocean; it returns and merges while retaining (on most accounts) the bliss of love.
**Relation to world:** The lower creation is Kāl's domain; the Supreme's mercy (Dayāl) acts through the Saints.
**Caveat:** Sant Mat ranks other traditions' ultimates (Brahm, Nirañjan, Pārbrahm) as lower regions — a hierarchy that those traditions do not accept.
