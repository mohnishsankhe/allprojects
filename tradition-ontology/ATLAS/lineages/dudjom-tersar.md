The recent treasure lineage of Dudjom Lingpa (1835-1904) and Dudjom Rinpoche Jigdral Yeshe Dorje (1904-1987), widely practised in exile communities.

## Distinctive positions
- Treasures of Dudjom Lingpa and of his reincarnation Dudjom Jigdral Yeshe Dorje, including Dzogchen instructions on 'buddhahood without meditation'.
