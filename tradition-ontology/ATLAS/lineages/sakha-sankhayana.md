# Śāṅkhāyana / Kauṣītaki (Ṛgveda school)

`lin:sakha-sankhayana` · `sourced` · confidence low

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** Kauṣītaki, Śāṅkhāyana caraṇa
**Parent:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](vedic-srauta.md)
**Regions:** Kerala (Nambūtiri Ṛgvedins), Gujarat (as reported)
**Status:** living

A Ṛgveda ritual school with its own Brāhmaṇa, Āraṇyaka and Sūtras, surviving notably among Kerala's Nambūtiri reciters.

## Distinctive positions
- Uses the Kauṣītaki (Śāṅkhāyana) Brāhmaṇa and Āraṇyaka and the Śāṅkhāyana Śrauta and Gṛhya Sūtras.
- The Kauṣītaki Upaniṣad belongs to its Āraṇyaka.
