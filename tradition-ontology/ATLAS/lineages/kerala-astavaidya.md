Hereditary Nampūtiri Brahmin physician families of Kerala who transmit Āyurveda through Vāgbhaṭa's Aṣṭāṅgahṛdaya, with their own Malayalam and Sanskrit commentaries and treatment specialities.

## Distinctive positions
- Hereditary transmission of the Aṣṭāṅgahṛdaya as the root text, learnt by heart.
- Family lineages each master of the eight branches ('aṣṭa-vaidya').
