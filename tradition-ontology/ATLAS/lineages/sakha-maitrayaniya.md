# Maitrāyaṇīya śākhā (Black Yajurveda)

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** Maitrāyaṇī, Mānava
**Parent:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](vedic-srauta.md)
**Regions:** Maharashtra (Nashik region) and Gujarat (as reported)
**Status:** living

A Black Yajurveda tradition surviving in a small community of reciters.

## Distinctive positions
- Its Saṃhitā in four books mixes mantra and brāhmaṇa prose; the Mānava and Vārāha sūtras belong to it.
- The Maitrī (Maitrāyaṇīya) Upaniṣad belongs to its tradition.
