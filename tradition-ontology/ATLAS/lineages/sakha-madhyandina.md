# Mādhyandina śākhā (White Yajurveda)

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** Vājasaneyi Mādhyandina
**Parent:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](vedic-srauta.md)
**Key teachers:** [Yājñavalkya](../teachers/yajnavalkya.md), `tch:uvata`, `tch:mahidhara`
**Regions:** North India above all
**Status:** living

The dominant White Yajurveda recension of North India, with the Kātyāyana and Pāraskara sūtras.

## Distinctive positions
- Keeps mantra (Saṃhitā) and explanation (Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa) apart — the mark of the 'White' (clear) Yajurveda.
- Tradition: Yājñavalkya received these Yajus fresh from the Sun in the form of a horse (vājin), hence 'Vājasaneyi' (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.5.26–29).
- Its Saṃhitā ends with the Īśa Upaniṣad (VS 40).
