# Baotang school

`lin:baotang` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Baotang zong 保唐宗
**Parent:** [Chan (Chinese Meditation school)](chan.md)
**Founders:** [Wuzhu 無住](../teachers/wuzhu.md)
**Key teachers:** [Wuzhu 無住](../teachers/wuzhu.md)
**Regions:** Chengdu, Sichuan
**Dates:** Scholarly account: later 8th c.; (confidence moderate)
**Status:** extinct

The radical Sichuan community of Wuzhu (714–774), which rejected all formal practice in favour of no-thought and claimed the patriarchal robe; its record is the Dunhuang Lidai fabao ji.

## Distinctive positions
- No-thought alone; rejection of ritual, recitation and conventional observances (Lidai fabao ji; Zongmi's criticism).
- Claim that Bodhidharma's robe passed from Huineng via Empress Wu to Zhishen and on to Wuzhu (Lidai fabao ji).


## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence low

**Names:** no-thought (wunian 無念)
**Descriptions:** When no thought arises, that itself is seeing the Buddha; no-thought includes precepts, samādhi and wisdom (Lidai fabao ji).
**Negations:** Thought arising is like the back of a mirror, which reflects nothing (Lidai fabao ji, Musang's saying).
**Caveat:** Baotang's own record; Zongmi reported it as rejecting all practice. Moderate-low confidence in the formulation.
