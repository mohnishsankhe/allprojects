The 'Northern' school of commentary and practice on the Four Tantras associated with the lord of Jang, Namgyal Drakzang (15th c.). Details of its distinctive doctrines are not recalled with confidence.

## Distinctive positions
- One of the two classical commentarial schools on the Four Tantras; its readings were weighed against the Zur school in Desi Sangye Gyatso's standardization.
