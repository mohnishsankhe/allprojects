# Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā

`lin:vaisnava-sahajiya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** Sahajiyā Vaiṣṇavism, Sahajiyā, the Sahaja cult (older scholarly label)
**Key teachers:** [Caṇḍīdās (the Sahajiyā Caṇḍīdās)](../teachers/candidas-sahajiya.md), [Rāmī (Rajakinī)](../teachers/rami-rajakini.md), [Mukunda Dāsa (Sahajiyā)](../teachers/mukunda-dasa-sahajiya.md), [Akiñcana Dāsa](../teachers/akincana-dasa.md)
**Regions:** Bengal (Nadia, Birbhum, Bardhaman, Hooghly), Orissa (related)
**Dates:** Tradition's account: the tradition claims Jayadeva, Vidyāpati, Caṇḍīdās, Caitanya, Rūpa, Sanātana and Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja as Sahajiyā masters; Scholarly account: Sahajiyā texts are post-Caitanya, mostly 17th–18th c.; continuity with the Buddhist Sahajiyā of the Caryāgīti is a scholarly hypothesis; (confidence moderate)
**Status:** living

The Vaiṣṇava Sahajiyā is a Bengali tradition that interiorizes the love of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa: the divine couple dwell as the innate natures of every woman and man, and through a guru-guided discipline culminating in the ritual union of partners (summary only) human love is refined into divine prema and the sahaja state. Its texts are 17th–18th-century Bengali manuals; its most famous voice is the Sahajiyā Caṇḍīdās.

## Distinctive positions
- Every man and woman has two natures: the outward form (rūpa) and the divine nature (svarūpa) — Kṛṣṇa in the man, Rādhā in the woman.
- By superimposition (āropa) of svarūpa on rūpa, human love (kāma) is transformed into divine love (prema), 'as gold is purified from ore'.
- The parakīyā (love outside marriage) of the gopīs is taken as the model for human practice between partners — recorded in summary only (restricted).
- The sahaja mānuṣa, the innate Man, dwells in the body; the goal is the sahaja state beyond the play of the two.
- A graded path: pravarta (beginner, with name and mantra), sādhaka (practitioner, with bhāva), siddha (perfected, with prema).
- Caitanya and the Gosvāmīs are read as secret Sahajiyās; orthodox Gauḍīyas reject this (dsp:sahajiya-and-gaudiya-orthodoxy).

## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Names:** the innate Man (sahaja mānuṣa), Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa as svarūpa, sahaja
**Descriptions:** the truth of man, above which there is nothing; the union of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa within the human pair
**Negations:** not known by the one who has not crossed the darkness
**Relation to self:** Every person's true form (svarūpa) is Kṛṣṇa or Rādhā.
**Relation to world:** The eternal Vṛndāvana is within the body.
**Caveat:** Orthodox Gauḍīyas reject the identification of human partners with Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa; the Sahajiyā rejects the orthodox reading as merely outward.
