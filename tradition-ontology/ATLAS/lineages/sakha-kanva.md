# Kāṇva śākhā (White Yajurveda)

`lin:sakha-kanva` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** Vājasaneyi Kāṇva
**Parent:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](vedic-srauta.md)
**Key teachers:** [Yājñavalkya](../teachers/yajnavalkya.md)
**Regions:** Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra, Odisha (as reported)
**Status:** living

The second White Yajurveda recension, living chiefly in the Deccan and Odisha.

## Distinctive positions
- Its Saṃhitā and Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa differ in arrangement and wording from the Mādhyandina; the Kāṇva Bṛhadāraṇyaka is the text Śaṅkara comments on.
