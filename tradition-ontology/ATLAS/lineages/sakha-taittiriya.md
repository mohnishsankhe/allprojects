# Taittirīya śākhā (Black Yajurveda)

`lin:sakha-taittiriya` · `skeleton` · confidence high

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** Taittirīya, Āpastamba, Baudhāyana, Hiraṇyakeśin, Bhāradvāja, Vādhūla, Vaikhānasa (its sūtra schools)
**Parent:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](vedic-srauta.md)
**Key teachers:** [Vaiśampāyana](../teachers/vaisampayana.md), `tch:tittiri`, `tch:bhatta-bhaskara`, `tch:sayana`
**Regions:** South India above all (Tamil Nadu, Andhra, Karnataka, Kerala), Maharashtra
**Status:** living

The most widespread Black Yajurveda tradition, with a complete Saṃhitā, Brāhmaṇa and Āraṇyaka and several living sūtra schools.

## Distinctive positions
- Mixes mantra and explanatory prose (brāhmaṇa) in one Saṃhitā — the mark of the 'Black' Yajurveda.
- Its Saṃhitā contains the Śatarudrīya (TS 4.5) and Camaka (TS 4.7); its Āraṇyaka contains the Taittirīya and Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣads.
- Tradition derives its name from Vaiśampāyana's pupils who, as partridges (tittiri), picked up the Yajus disgorged by Yājñavalkya (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.5.13).
