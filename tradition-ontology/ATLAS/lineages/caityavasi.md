# Caityavāsī (temple-dwelling monks)

`lin:caityavasi` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Parent:** [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](murtipujaka.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: prominent c. 5th–11th c. CE in western India; opposed by Haribhadra and defeated in debate by Jineśvarasūri (1024); (confidence moderate)
**Status:** absorbed

The temple-dwelling Śvetāmbara monks of early medieval western India, against whom Haribhadra's Sambodhaprakaraṇa and the Kharatara reform (vasativāsa) were directed; their clothed resident successors are sometimes seen in the later yatis and śrīpūjyas.

## Distinctive positions
- monks resided permanently in temples, administered their property and handled money — criticized by reformers as contrary to the Āgamas
