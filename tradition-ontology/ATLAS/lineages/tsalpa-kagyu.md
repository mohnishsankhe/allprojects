One of the 'four great' Dakpo Kagyu schools, founded by Lama Zhang, author of the Ultimate Supreme Path of Mahāmudrā; the school later merged into others.

## Distinctive positions
- Lama Zhang's Mahāmudrā instruction, including the 'white self-sufficient remedy' (dkar po chig thub) idiom that Sakya Paṇḍita attacked.
