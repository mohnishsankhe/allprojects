The Japanese Vinaya school established by Jianzhen (Ganjin), with its head temple Tōshōdaiji.

## Distinctive positions
- Formal ordination on an ordination platform (kaidan) by the Four-Part Vinaya, introduced by Jianzhen; one of the six Nara schools.
