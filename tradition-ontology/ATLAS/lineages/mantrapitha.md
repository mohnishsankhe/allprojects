# Mantrapīṭha (the Seat of Mantras)

`lin:mantrapitha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** mantrapīṭha, the Svacchandabhairava cult, Bhairavāṣṭaka tradition (in the Śrīkaṇṭhīya's scheme)
**Parent:** [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](mantramarga.md)
**Key teachers:** [Kṣemarāja](../teachers/ksemaraja.md)
**Regions:** Kashmir, Nepal
**Dates:** Scholarly account: the Svacchandatantra predates the 10th c. (commented on by Kṣemarāja, 11th c.; known to Abhinavagupta); (confidence low)
**Status:** unknown

Division of the Bhairava tantras centred on a male Bhairava (chiefly Svacchandabhairava). Its main surviving scripture, the Svacchandatantra with Kṣemarāja's Uddyota, shaped Kashmirian Śaiva ritual.

## Distinctive positions
- The Bhairava tantras of the southern stream divide into the Mantrapīṭha and the Vidyāpīṭha; in the Mantrapīṭha the central deity is a male Bhairava, above all Svacchandabhairava with his consort Aghoreśvarī.
- The first group of the 64 Bhairava tantras in the Śrīkaṇṭhīya list (the Bhairavāṣṭaka) opens with the Svacchanda and was 'spoken by Bahurūpa' (Jayaratha on TĀ 1.18).
