# Bīsapantha (Digambara)

`lin:bisapantha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Parent:** [Digambara](digambara.md)
**Status:** living

The 'older' mode of Digambara temple practice, loyal to the bhaṭṭāraka institution and to the attendant deities, as distinguished from the reforming Terāpantha.

## Distinctive positions
- worship of Jina images with fresh flowers, fruits and lamps and seated worship
- acceptance of the bhaṭṭārakas as religious authorities
- worship of guardian and attendant deities (Padmāvatī, Kṣetrapāla)
