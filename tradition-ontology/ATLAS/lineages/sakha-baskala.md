A Ṛgveda recension known by name and by reports of its differences from the Śākala; not transmitted as a separate complete text.

## Distinctive positions
- Named by the tradition (e.g. the Caraṇavyūha) as the second Ṛgveda recension.
- Later reports say it differed from the Śākala chiefly in the number and order of a few hymns (e.g. including some supplementary material); no complete Bāṣkala Saṃhitā survives.
