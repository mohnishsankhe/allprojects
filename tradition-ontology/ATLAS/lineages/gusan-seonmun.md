**Dates:** Scholarly account: 9th–10th c.; (confidence moderate)
**Status:** absorbed

The nine Seon monastic lineages founded in late Silla and early Goryeo by monks returning from Tang China, the roots of all later Korean Seon.

## Distinctive positions
- Mostly Hongzhou-line transmissions from Mazu's heirs (Xitang Zhizang, Magu Baoche, Nanquan); one (Sumisan) from the Caodong line.
