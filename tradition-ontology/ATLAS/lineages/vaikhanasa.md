# Vaikhānasa

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** Vaikhānasāgama, the Vikhanas tradition, Vaikhānasa sampradāya
**Founders:** [Vikhanas](../teachers/vikhanas.md)
**Key teachers:** [Marīci](../teachers/marici.md), [Atri](../teachers/atri.md), [Kaśyapa](../teachers/kasyapa.md), [Bhṛgu Vāruṇi](../teachers/bhrgu.md), [Śrīnivāsa Dīkṣita](../teachers/srinivasa-diksita.md)
**Regions:** Andhra Pradesh (Tirumala–Tirupati), Tamil Nadu, Karnataka
**Dates:** Tradition's account: the Vaikhānasa Sūtra was composed by Vikhanas — identified with Brahmā born of Nārāyaṇa, or with Nārāyaṇa himself — and is the first of all sūtras; his disciples Bhṛgu, Atri, Marīci and Kāśyapa taught the rules of temple worship (Daśavidhahetunirūpaṇa; Kriyādhikāra 1.1); Scholarly account: the Vaikhānasa Sūtras belong to the late Taittirīya sūtra literature; the temple-worship saṃhitās are later; exact dates not established here; (confidence low)
**Status:** living

The Vaikhānasas are a hereditary community of Vedic (Taittirīya) brahmins, followers of the Sūtra of Vikhanas, whose saṃhitās (ascribed to Marīci, Atri, Kāśyapa and Bhṛgu) govern image worship of Viṣṇu with Vedic mantras. They serve as priests in many South Indian Viṣṇu temples, above all Tirumala–Tirupati, and distinguish their Vedic worship from the tantric Pāñcarātra.

## Distinctive positions
- Vaikhānasa worship of Viṣṇu is Vedic (vaidika): performed with Vedic mantras by brahmins of the Vaikhānasa Sūtra.
- Worship is twofold: formless (amūrta) through the fire and with form (samūrta) in an image; image worship (arcana) is praised as the best means.
- Viṣṇu is partless (niṣkala) and with parts (sakala): the fixed image (dhruva-bera) stands for the niṣkala and receives only flowers; the movable image (kautuka-bera) stands for the sakala and receives full worship (Kriyādhikāra 11.148–152).
- Viṣṇu is invoked in five forms: Viṣṇu, Puruṣa, Satya, Acyuta, Aniruddha (Bhṛgu Saṃhitā 13.1).
- Vaikhānasas are Vaiṣṇavas from the womb (garbha-vaiṣṇava) through the prenatal viṣṇubali rite and so do not take the Pāñcarātra branding initiation.
- The Vaikhānasa Sūtra is the best of sūtras and its followers the highest, for ten reasons (Daśavidhahetunirūpaṇa).

## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Names:** Nārāyaṇa, the supreme Brahman (nārāyaṇaparaṃ brahma), Viṣṇu, the five forms: Viṣṇu, Puruṣa, Satya, Acyuta, Aniruddha
**Descriptions:** Nārāyaṇa is the supreme Brahman, the highest Self; his highest form is subtle, imperishable and partless (niṣkala); his gross form with parts (sakala) is the cause of all (Kriyādhikāra 11.148–150).
**Relation to self:** The worshipper attains Viṣṇu's world and nearness to him in graded forms of liberation (sālokya, sāmīpya, sārūpya, sāyujya).
**Relation to world:** Nārāyaṇa is the cause of the whole world; Vikhanas, author of the Sūtra, is born of him (Daśavidhahetunirūpaṇa).
**Caveat:** The Vaikhānasas insist on the Vedic identity of their worship and would reject being classed with tantric Āgama theologies.
