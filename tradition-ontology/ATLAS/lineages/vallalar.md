The universal 'true path of equality' founded by Ramalinga Swamigal (Vallalar, 1823–1874) at Vadalur: worship of God as Grace-Light, compassion to all beings, free feeding, and rejection of caste and sectarian religion.

## Distinctive positions
- God is the Aruṭperuñjōti, the Vast Grace-Light, to be worshipped as light, not in images.
- Compassion to living beings (jīvakāruṇyam) — above all feeding the hungry — is the key to God's grace; killing and eating animals is rejected.
- Rejection of caste, of sectarian religion and of scriptural and ritual distinctions in his later teaching.
- The goal is the deathless life (maraṇamilāp peruvāḻvu), with the body transformed into a body of light (tradition's account).

## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

**Names:** Aruṭperuñjōti (the Vast Grace-Light), Taṉipperuṅkaruṇai (the unique great compassion)
**Descriptions:** God is the One Grace-Light, formless and beyond all religions, to be worshipped as light.
**Relation to self:** Souls are God's own; compassion to them is the way to God's grace.
**Relation to world:** The world is real; the body can be transformed into an imperishable body of light (tradition's account).
**Caveat:** Vallalar rejected all sectarian religions including Śaivism in his late teaching; a one-truth reading suits his universalism but he himself spoke of a single true path (sanmārgam).
