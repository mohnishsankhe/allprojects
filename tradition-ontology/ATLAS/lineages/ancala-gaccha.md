A Mūrtipūjaka gaccha founded in the 12th c. by Āryarakṣitasūri as a ritual reform, concentrated in Kacch and among Kacchī Jains.

## Distinctive positions
- uses the hem (añcala) of the garment instead of the mouth-cloth when laymen perform rites (hence the name, per the usual explanation)
- 'Vidhipakṣa' — the party of the correct rite; strongest in Kacch
