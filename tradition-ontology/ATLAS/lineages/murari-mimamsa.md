A minor third school of Mīmāṃsā associated with Murāri Miśra, proverbially 'Murāri's third path' (murāres tṛtīyaḥ panthāḥ). Only fragments of his works survive; his account of how validity is known is discussed by Navya-Nyāya.

## Distinctive positions
- Validity of a cognition is intrinsic but is grasped by the reflective after-cognition (anuvyavasāya) that apprehends the first cognition — a third account beside the Bhāṭṭa (inference from knownness) and the Prābhākara (self-luminosity) ones, as reported by Gaṅgeśa's Tattvacintāmaṇi.
