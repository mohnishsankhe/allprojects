Kubjikā ('the Bent One', Kujā), consort of Navātman/Kujeśa Bhairava, is the goddess of the Paścimāmnāya, the western of the Kaula transmissions. Its root scripture, the Kubjikāmata (25 paṭalas, c. 3,500 verses in the Kulālikāmnāya recension), with the Ṣaṭsāhasrasaṃhitā and the vast Manthānabhairava, teaches the Goddess's descent through the sacred seats, a mantra-maṇḍala system of six centres and their yoginīs, and strict samaya discipline. Absorbed into the Kālī and Śrīvidyā cults elsewhere, it survives as a living cult in Nepal.

## Distinctive positions
- The Goddess Kubjikā ('the Bent One') emerged from the liṅga, dark and hunch-backed, and became bent out of shame (lajjā) when Bhairava asked for the transmission (Kubjikāmata 2.3-5, 2.14).
- Śiva and the Goddess are mutually dependent: 'nothing is without me, nothing without you'; Bhairava calls her his guru (Kubjikāmata 2.17-18).
- The four seats are four levels of the supreme yoni: Kāmākhya (beyond form), Pūrṇagiri (form), Jālandhara (word), Oḍra/Oḍḍiyāna (body) (Kubjikāmata 11.6-7).
- The six centres (ṣaṭprakāra, with their goddesses) are the body of the Goddess; Kubjikā is the coiled power (Kubjikāmata 2.2 hymn; chs. 11-15).
- The disciple who breaks the samayas and slanders the guru becomes 'dead while living' (Kubjikāmata 12.3-9).

## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Names:** Kubjikā (Kujā, Kujeśvarī), Navātman / Kujeśa Bhairava, the supreme Yoni
**Descriptions:** the supreme power of knowledge, will and action, straight as a line and again coiled like a sleeping serpent, the power of sound (Kubjikāmata 2.2, hymn); the supreme primal yoni of the supreme, fourfold as beyond form, form, word and body (Kubjikāmata 11.6-7); dark as collyrium, bent, both beautiful and deformed, of many forms (Kubjikāmata 2.4-5)
**Negations:** beyond form (rūpātīta) at its highest level (Kubjikāmata 11.7)
**Relation to self:** The Goddess is the coiled power within the practitioner's body, located in the six centres; the initiate receives her command (ājñā) through the guru lineage.
**Relation to world:** The world is born within the sixfold order of the Goddess (Kubjikāmata 11.13-16); Bhairava and she are cause and effect of one another (2.17-18).
**Caveat:** The Kubjikā ultimate is expressed through a secret mantra-maṇḍala system and lineage command; the tradition treats this knowledge as unshareable outside initiation, so any one-truth equation is at most an outer reading.
