# Āśvalāyana (Ṛgveda school)

`lin:sakha-asvalayana` · `sourced` · confidence moderate

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** Āśvalāyana caraṇa
**Parent:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](vedic-srauta.md)
**Key teachers:** [Śaunaka (of Naimiṣa)](../teachers/saunaka.md)
**Regions:** Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra and elsewhere
**Status:** living

The most widespread living Ṛgveda ritual school, reciting the Śākala Saṃhitā with the Āśvalāyana sūtras; listed among the Ṛgveda śākhās by the Caraṇavyūha.

## Distinctive positions
- Recites the Śākala Saṃhitā and follows the Āśvalāyana Śrauta and Gṛhya Sūtras (Aitareya tradition).
- Tradition makes Āśvalāyana a pupil of Śaunaka.
