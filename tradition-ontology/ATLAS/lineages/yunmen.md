One of the Five Houses of Chan, from Yunmen Wenyan (864–949) in Xuefeng's line; famed for single-word responses, it produced Xuedou Chongxian, whose verses on a hundred cases became the Blue Cliff Record.

## Distinctive positions
- One-word barriers (yizi guan 一字關) and terse replies.
- Yunmen's three phrases as systematised by Deshan Yuanmi: containing heaven and earth, cutting off the myriad streams, following the waves.
- 'Every day is a good day' (Biyan lu case 6).
