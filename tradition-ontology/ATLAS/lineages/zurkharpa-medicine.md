# Zurkharpa (zur lugs), the Zur school of Tibetan medicine

`lin:zurkharpa-medicine` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Family:** shared
**Alternate names:** zur lugs, Zurkhar tradition
**Parent:** [Sowa Rigpa (gso ba rig pa), the Tibetan science of healing](sowa-rigpa.md)
**Founders:** [Zurkhar Nyamnyi Dorje (zur mkhar mnyam nyid rdo rje)](../teachers/zurkhar-nyamnyi-dorje.md)
**Key teachers:** [Zurkhar Nyamnyi Dorje (zur mkhar mnyam nyid rdo rje)](../teachers/zurkhar-nyamnyi-dorje.md), [Zurkhar Lodrö Gyalpo (zur mkhar blo gros rgyal po)](../teachers/zurkhar-lodro-gyalpo.md)
**Regions:** Lhokha (Zurkhar), southern Tibet
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 15th–16th c. (Zurkhar Nyamnyi Dorje 1439–1475; Zurkhar Lodrö Gyalpo 1509–1579); (confidence low)
**Status:** absorbed

The southern commentarial school on the Four Tantras founded by Zurkhar Nyamnyi Dorje and continued by Zurkhar Lodrö Gyalpo, whose commentary shaped later classical medicine.

## Distinctive positions
- Its commentary, the Oral Instructions of the Ancestors (mes po'i zhal lung), became a principal reference behind the Blue Beryl; Zurkhar Lodrö Gyalpo discussed whether the Four Tantras is the Buddha's word.
