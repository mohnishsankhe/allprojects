# Bochasanwasi Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha (BAPS)

**Family:** vedic

**Dates:** Tradition's account: BAPS holds that Akshar-Purushottam upāsanā was Svāminārāyaṇa's own teaching, revealed through Guṇātītānanda Svāmī; Scholarly account: founded by Shastriji Maharaj in 1907 after he left the Vadtal diocese (1905); (confidence high)
**Status:** living

The Svāminārāyaṇa organization founded by Shastriji Maharaj in 1907 on the doctrine that Guṇātītānanda Svāmī was Akṣarabrahman and that a succession of living gurus manifests Akṣara.

## Distinctive positions
- Akṣarabrahman is a distinct eternal entity manifest in a living guru (Guṇātītānanda and his successors); the devotee becomes brahmarūpa and worships Parabrahman (Svāminārāyaṇa).
- A Sanskrit commentary on the prasthāna-traya (Svāminārāyaṇa Bhāṣyam) presents Akṣara-Puruṣottama Darśana as a Vedānta school.


## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

**Names:** Parabrahman (Svāminārāyaṇa), Akṣarabrahman
**Descriptions:** Two distinct eternal entities: Parabrahman the supreme, and Akṣarabrahman his ideal devotee and abode, manifest in the guru.
**Relation to self:** The liberated soul becomes brahmarūpa (like Akṣara) and eternally serves Parabrahman.
**Relation to world:** The world is real, created by Parabrahman through Akṣara and māyā.
**Caveat:** The Ahmedabad and Vadtal dioceses reject the identification of Akṣara with Guṇātītānanda and his successors; see dsp:akshar-purushottam.
