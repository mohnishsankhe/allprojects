The Chinese Vinaya school founded by Daoxuan on Zhongnan mountain, which made the Dharmaguptaka Four-Part Vinaya the monastic code of East Asia.

## Distinctive positions
- The Four-Part (Dharmaguptaka) Vinaya is the standard code for all Chinese monastics.
- Daoxuan interpreted the Vinaya in Mahāyāna terms (precepts grounded in the 'seed' of the mind; low confidence on details).
