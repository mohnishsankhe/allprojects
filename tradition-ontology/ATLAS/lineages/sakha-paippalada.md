**Status:** living

The second Atharvaveda recension, surviving in Odisha and in a Kashmiri manuscript.

## Distinctive positions
- Its Saṃhitā, also in twenty books, overlaps the Śaunaka only in part and was long known only from a single Kashmiri birch-bark manuscript before Odisha manuscripts and reciters were found.
- The Praśna Upaniṣad's teacher Pippalāda is its eponym.
