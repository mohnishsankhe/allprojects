The branch of the Linji house from Huanglong Huinan, prominent in the Northern Song and the source of Eisai's Rinzai transmission.

## Distinctive positions
- Huanglong's 'three barriers' (Buddha's hand, donkey's leg, one's birth-conditions).
- Lettered Chan (wenzi Chan) of Juefan Huihong.

**Transmissions given:** 
  - [Rinzai Zen](rinzai.md) — what: Eisai's transmission from Xu'an Huaichang (1191)
