**Status:** living

The householder path of grace founded by Vallabhācārya: a non-dualism in which the world is Brahman's real, unchanged self-manifestation and the soul, a spark of Brahman with its bliss concealed, is restored by Kṛṣṇa's grace to loving service (sevā) of his child and youthful forms, above all Śrīnāthjī. Organised by his son Viṭṭhalanātha into the eight daily periods of temple (havelī) service and the music of the aṣṭachāp poets, it is led by Vallabha's descendants (the Gosvāmī 'houses').

## Distinctive positions
- Pure non-dualism (śuddhādvaita): Brahman, untouched by māyā, is the sole reality; the world is Brahman's own real form.
- Unmodified transformation (avikṛta-pariṇāma-vāda): Brahman becomes the world without undergoing change.
- The world (jagat) is real; only the soul's 'I and mine' construction (saṃsāra) is unreal and to be ended.
- Brahman manifests or conceals (āvirbhāva/tirobhāva) its being, consciousness and bliss: matter shows only sat, souls sat and cit, the Lord all three.
- Three forms of the ultimate: Puruṣottama Kṛṣṇa (highest), Akṣara Brahman (his abode, the goal of knowers) and Antaryāmin.
- Brahman is the locus of contradictory attributes (viruddha-dharmāśraya).
- Four authoritative scriptures: Veda, the Gītā, the Brahma Sūtra and the Bhāgavata (the samādhi-bhāṣā of Vyāsa).
- Grace (puṣṭi = poṣaṇa, BhP 2.10.4) is the sole cause of the highest devotion, independent of means; the maryādā path follows scriptural discipline.
- Three kinds of souls: puṣṭi, maryādā and pravāha.
- Initiation by brahmasambandha removes the five kinds of faults; everything is used only after being offered (samarpaṇa).
- Loving service (sevā) of the Lord's form in the home is the path and the fruit; mental sevā is highest; the goal is absorption (nirodha) and love in separation (viraha), not liberation.

## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Names:** Puruṣottama Śrī Kṛṣṇa, Brahman, Akṣara Brahman, Antaryāmin, Śrīnāthjī (Govardhananāthajī), the self-manifest form
**Descriptions:** Being-consciousness-bliss (sat-cit-ānanda), pure (śuddha) — untouched by māyā.; The locus of contradictory attributes (viruddha-dharmāśraya): with and without qualities, moving and unmoving.; Puruṣottama is the highest; Akṣara Brahman is his abode and the goal of knowers; Antaryāmin dwells within.; Madhurāṣṭaka: everything of the lord of sweetness is sweet.
**Negations:** Denies that māyā has any part in Brahman or in the world's reality (against Śaṅkara's māyāvāda).
**Relation to self:** The soul is a spark-like part (aṃśa) of Brahman in which bliss (ānanda) is concealed by the Lord's will; by grace it is manifested in service.
**Relation to world:** The world is Brahman itself, transformed without change (avikṛta-pariṇāma); real and to be honoured; only saṃsāra — the soul's 'I and mine' — is unreal.
**Caveat:** The impersonal Akṣara is held lower than Puruṣottama; Puṣṭimārga would reject any equalising of the knower's Brahman and the devotee's Kṛṣṇa.
