# Vaṭakalai (northern school of Śrīvaiṣṇavism)

**Dates:** Tradition's account: from Vedānta Deśika (1268–1369) and the Kāñcī teachers before him; Scholarly account: doctrinal differences take shape 13th–14th c.; separate institutions and marks by c. 17th–18th c.; (confidence low)
**Status:** living

The Śrīvaiṣṇava school following Vedānta Deśika, stronger in Sanskrit scholastic writing and associated with Kāñcī; it holds that surrender is a means the soul performs and that Śrī shares the Lord's supremacy. It wears the U-shaped mark (tiruman) without the line on the nose.

## Distinctive positions
- Prapatti (surrender) is a real means (upāya) to liberation, an alternative to bhakti-yoga for those unable to undertake it; it must be performed once, with its five limbs (aṅga) and self-offering as the principal (aṅgin).
- The Lord's grace, though free, takes the soul's act of surrender as its occasion (vyāja); 'markaṭa-kiśora-nyāya': the baby monkey clings to its mother.
- Śrī (Lakṣmī) is, with Viṣṇu, all-pervading (vibhu), of the rank of the Lord (Īśvara-koṭi), and together with him the means and the goal; the divine couple is the object of surrender.
- The Lord overlooks (does not see) the faults of the surrendered; he does not relish them.
- After prapatti the surrendered must keep the scriptural duties as the Lord's command (ājñā), and must expiate or renew surrender for deliberate offences.
- Kaivalya (enjoyment of one's own self apart from the Lord) is not a permanent state.


## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Names:** Śrīman Nārāyaṇa (the divine couple, Śrī and Nārāyaṇa), Brahman
**Descriptions:** as in Viśiṣṭādvaita generally; with the emphasis that Śrī, all-pervading like Viṣṇu, shares his supremacy and is, with him, the means and the goal (the divine pair, mithuna, is the object of surrender)
**Negations:** as for Viśiṣṭādvaita
**Relation to self:** The soul is the servant of the divine couple; it must, by the Lord's grace, perform surrender as the means.
**Relation to world:** As for Viśiṣṭādvaita: the world is the real body of the Lord.
**Caveat:** Same caveat as ult:visistadvaita; in addition the Vaṭakalai insist that Śrī is not of the rank of souls.
