The earliest of the Five Houses, from Guishan Lingyou and his heir Yangshan Huiji in the line of Baizhang; known for the harmony of master and disciple and for circle-figures, it disappeared as a distinct line by the Song.

## Distinctive positions
- Circle-figures (yuanxiang 圓相) as a teaching device, said to come from Nanyang Huizhong through Danyuan (Rentian yanmu).
- Sudden awakening to principle with gradual removal of habit energies (Guishan).


## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence low

**Names:** the original mind shown as a circle (yuanxiang)
**Descriptions:** 'In the ground of true reality not a single dust is accepted; in the gate of the myriad practices not a single dharma is discarded' (Guishan yulu).
**Negations:** The circle-figures are to be burned once understood (Yangshan).
**Relation to world:** Principle is realised suddenly; habit energies are removed gradually.
**Caveat:** The circle is a pointer, not the thing (Yangshan burned the book of 97 circles).
