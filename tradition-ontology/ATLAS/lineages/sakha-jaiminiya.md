# Jaiminīya (Talavakāra) śākhā (Sāmaveda)

`lin:sakha-jaiminiya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** Talavakāra, Jaiminīya
**Parent:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](vedic-srauta.md)
**Key teachers:** [Jaimini](../teachers/jaimini.md)
**Regions:** Kerala (Nambūtiri Sāmavedins), Tamil Nadu
**Status:** living

The third surviving Sāmaveda recension, named after Jaimini, preserved chiefly in Kerala and Tamil Nadu.

## Distinctive positions
- Has its own Saṃhitā and song-books, the Jaiminīya Brāhmaṇa and the Jaiminīya Upaniṣad Brāhmaṇa, which contains the Kena Upaniṣad.
- Its melodies and style of singing differ from the Kauthuma tradition.
