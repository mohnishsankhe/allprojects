The movement founded by Nirmala Srivastava (Shri Mataji Nirmala Devi, 1923–2011) teaching en masse kuṇḍalinī awakening ('Self-realization') and meditation on a subtle system of channels and cakras.

## Distinctive positions
- Self-realization is the spontaneous (sahaja) awakening of kuṇḍalinī, a birthright given freely and not for payment; felt as a cool breeze (paramacaitanya) on the hands and above the head (claims of the tradition).
- A subtle system of three channels (left, right, centre) and seven cakras, each with presiding deities.
- Thoughtless awareness (nirvicāra) as the first state after realization.
- The tradition holds its founder to be the Ādi Śakti (tradition's account).
