The largest Sant Mat organization, founded at Beas in the Punjab by Baba Jaimal Singh (a disciple of Soamiji), which spread the teaching worldwide under Sawan Singh and Charan Singh.

## Distinctive positions
- The five names given at initiation (held confidential) and the five sounds of the inner regions.
- Strict separation from ritual; the teaching presented as the universal core of all religions.
- Seva (voluntary service) and the Dera as a working community.
