# Śākala śākhā (Ṛgveda)

`lin:sakha-sakala` · `sourced` · confidence high

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** Śākala recension, Śākalya's recension
**Parent:** [Vedic Śrauta tradition (the Saṃhitā lineages)](vedic-srauta.md)
**Key teachers:** [Śākalya](../teachers/sakalya.md), [Paila](../teachers/paila.md), [Sāyaṇa](../teachers/sayana.md)
**Regions:** all India; the standard Ṛgveda of every living Ṛgvedin
**Status:** living

The Ṛgveda recension named after Śākalya, the only complete Ṛgveda Saṃhitā transmitted; the sūtra schools of Āśvalāyana and Śāṅkhāyana recite it.

## Distinctive positions
- Its Saṃhitā is the only fully preserved Ṛgveda: 10 maṇḍalas, 1,028 hymns counting the 11 Vālakhilya hymns (8.49–59).
- Its word-by-word text (Padapāṭha) is ascribed to Śākalya and guards the continuous text.
- The Aitareya Brāhmaṇa and Āraṇyaka belong to the Ṛgveda tradition that recites this Saṃhitā.
