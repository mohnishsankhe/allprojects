# Gāruḍa stream (tantras of Garuḍa)

`lin:garuda-tantra` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** gāruḍa-tantra, Gāruḍa tantras, eastern stream
**Parent:** [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](mantramarga.md)
**Regions:** North India, Nepal, Kerala (later viṣa-vaidya practice)
**Status:** absorbed

The Śaiva scriptural stream dealing with the neutralisation of poison by Garuḍa mantras; its lore survives partly in later compilations and in regional poison-healing traditions.

## Distinctive positions
- Mantras of Garuḍa and associated goddesses (e.g. Tvaritā) used against snake-poison and other poisons.
- Issued from the eastern (Tatpuruṣa) face of Sadāśiva (Kāmika pūrva 1.21, 1.24; Śrīkaṇṭhīya as quoted by Jayaratha).
