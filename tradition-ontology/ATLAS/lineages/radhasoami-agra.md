The Agra branches of Radhasoami after Soamiji: Soamibagh (Soamiji's family and followers), Peepal Mandi / Huzuri Bhawan (Rai Salig Ram's line) and Dayalbagh (from 1915 under Anand Swarup).

## Distinctive positions
- 'Radhasoami' is the true name of the supreme being, revealed by Soamiji.
- Soamiji and his successors as the incarnation of the Supreme (Sant Satguru).
- Dayalbagh: a planned community joining spiritual practice with work, industry and education.
