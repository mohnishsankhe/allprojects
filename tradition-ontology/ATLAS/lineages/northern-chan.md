**Status:** extinct

The metropolitan Chan of Shenxiu (d. 706) and his heirs Puji and Yifu, favoured by the Tang court; Shenhui attacked it in 730–732 as collateral and gradual, a characterization later Chan histories adopted.

## Distinctive positions
- Contemplating the mind (guanxin) and 'viewing purity' (kanjing).
- The five expedient means (wu fangbian) of the Dasheng wusheng fangbian men.
- The mind is originally pure but obscured by adventitious dust; practice removes it (the view the Platform Sūtra ascribes to Shenxiu's verse).


## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence low

**Names:** the pure mind / buddha-nature
**Descriptions:** The mind is like a bright mirror on its stand, to be wiped constantly (the verse ascribed to Shenxiu, Platform Sūtra 4.6).
**Relation to self:** The pure mind is one's own, obscured by dust.
**Relation to world:** Deluded thoughts are adventitious dust.
**Caveat:** This is the Northern view as the Platform Sūtra presents it (an opponent's report); Northern texts themselves (Guanxin lun) are only summarised here from memory.
