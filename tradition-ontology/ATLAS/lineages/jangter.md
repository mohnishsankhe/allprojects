# Jangter (the Northern Treasures)

**Status:** living

The tradition of the Northern Treasures revealed by Rigdzin Gödem (1337-1409), including the Dzogchen cycle Kuntuzangpo Gongpa Zangthal, later centred on Dorje Drak monastery.

## Distinctive positions
- Holds the Gongpa Zangthal ('Unobstructed Intention of Samantabhadra'), a Dzogchen treasure containing the Aspiration of Samantabhadra.
- Seat: Dorje Drak monastery, one of the principal Nyingma monasteries.
