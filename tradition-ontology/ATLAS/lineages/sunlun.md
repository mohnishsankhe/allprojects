A Burmese insight lineage from Sunlun Sayadaw (U Kawi, 1878–1952), known for its strong-breathing opening and its unflinching mindfulness of touch and painful sensation.

## Distinctive positions
- Vigorous, strong breathing at the start of a sitting to build concentration quickly, followed by unmoving attention to the sensation of touch (at the nostrils and then throughout the body).
- Painful feeling is not avoided: the meditator sits without moving and watches pain with great energy until it breaks up — effort (viriya) is emphasized.
- Practice without reliance on scriptural learning; mindfulness of touch as the direct way.
