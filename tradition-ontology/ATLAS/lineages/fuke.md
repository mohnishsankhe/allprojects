**Dates:** Tradition's account: traced to Puhua, Linji's eccentric companion, and brought to Japan by Shinchi Kakushin (13th c.); Scholarly account: organised as a Rinzai-affiliated order of mendicant shakuhachi players (komusō) in the Edo period; abolished 1871; revived by lay players; (confidence low)
**Status:** revived

A Japanese order of mendicant flute-playing monks (komusō) that traced itself to the Tang eccentric Puhua; its origin legend is regarded by scholars as late.

## Distinctive positions
- Blowing the bamboo flute (suizen 吹禪) as meditation.
