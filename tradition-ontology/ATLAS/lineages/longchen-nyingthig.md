The mind-treasure cycle of Jigme Lingpa, received in visions of Longchenpa, which became the main Nyingma framework for preliminaries, guru yoga and Dzogchen, spread by Jigme Lingpa's disciples and Patrul Rinpoche.

## Distinctive positions
- The most widely practised Nyingma cycle: ngöndro, guru sādhana (Rigdzin Düpa), ḍākinī practice (Yumka) and Dzogchen instruction (Yeshe Lama) in one mind-treasure.
- Received by Jigme Lingpa from Longchenpa in three visions; it condenses the Nyingthig Yabzhi.
