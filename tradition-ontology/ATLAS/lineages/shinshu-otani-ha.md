# Shinshū Ōtani-ha

`lin:shinshu-otani-ha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** 真宗大谷派, Higashi Honganji
**Parent:** [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](jodo-shinshu.md)
**Regions:** Japan (Higashi Honganji, Kyoto)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 1602; (confidence high)
**Status:** living

The eastern branch of the Honganji, from Kyōnyo (1558–1614), who received land from Tokugawa Ieyasu in 1602; head temple Higashi Honganji; home of the Meiji reform movement of Kiyozawa Manshi.

## Distinctive positions
- Shares the Shinshū teaching of shinjin as the true cause of birth; differs institutionally, liturgically and in doctrinal-academy lineages rather than in core doctrine.
