**Dates:** Scholarly account: late 11th – 14th c. CE; (confidence moderate)

The Kadam line that transmitted the graded path (lamrim) as a meditation sequence; traced by Tibetan historians to Gönpawa and Neusurpa. Tsongkhapa received it through Lhodrak Namkha Gyaltsen and others.

## Distinctive positions
- One of the three Kadam transmission lines distinguished by Tibetan historians (textual, lamrim, oral-instruction).
