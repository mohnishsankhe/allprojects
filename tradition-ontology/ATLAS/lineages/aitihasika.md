# Aitihāsikas (the legend-school of Vedic interpretation)

`lin:aitihasika` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** aitihāsikāḥ
**Parent:** [Vedāṅga (the six limbs of the Veda)](vedanga.md)
**Status:** extinct

Interpreters named in Yāska's Nirukta who read the hymns through old stories (itihāsa); known only from Yāska's reports.

## Distinctive positions
- Vedic figures are persons of old narratives: Vṛtra is an asura, son of Tvaṣṭṛ (Nirukta 2.16); the Aśvins are two pious kings (Nirukta 12.1); Yama and Yamī are the twins born to Saraṇyū (Nirukta 12.10).
