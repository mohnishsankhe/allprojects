A Rajasthani Sant sampradāya founded by Haridās Nirañjanī that names the ultimate Nirañjan and stands between the Nāth yogīs and the Sants.

## Distinctive positions
- Worship of Nirañjan, the stainless formless one, joining Nāth yogic language with Sant devotion.
- Praised by Rajasthani anthologists alongside the Dādūpanthīs.
