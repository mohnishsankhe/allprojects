# Terāpantha (Digambara)

`lin:terapantha-digambara` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Parent:** [Digambara](digambara.md)
**Key teachers:** `tch:todarmal`, `tch:banarsidas`
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 17th c. CE (Agra, Sanganer and Jaipur region), growing out of Banārsīdās's Adhyātma circle; (confidence moderate)
**Status:** living

A lay-led Digambara reform movement of the 17th–18th c. (not connected with the Śvetāmbara Terāpanth) that rejected the bhaṭṭārakas and reduced image worship to non-harming offerings; Paṇḍit Ṭoḍarmal's Mokṣamārga Prakāśaka is its classic.

## Distinctive positions
- rejects the authority of the bhaṭṭārakas
- offers only dry and non-living substances (e.g. rice, dry fruits) in image worship, no fresh flowers or lamps, and standing worship
- rejects the worship of attendant deities (śāsanadevatās) as wrong faith
