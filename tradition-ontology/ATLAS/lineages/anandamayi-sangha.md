The circle of devotees and ashrams that formed around Anandamayi Ma (1896–1982), a Bengali woman saint without a human guru, teaching devotion, remembrance of God and Self-realization across sects.

## Distinctive positions
- Anandamayi Ma had no human guru: in the tradition's account she initiated herself (1922).
- Only talk of God is worthwhile; all other talk is futile and painful.
- The supreme duty of human life is to seek Self-realization; all paths and forms of God are accepted.
- Annual week of self-discipline (saṃyama vrata) for devotees.


## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence low · _recent (post-1800)_

**Names:** God (Bhagavān, Hari), the Self (ātman)
**Descriptions:** There is only One; all forms of God and all paths are that One.
**Relation to self:** Self-realization is realizing that One as one's own Self.
**Relation to world:** Everything that happens is God's play; nothing is apart from the One.
**Caveat:** Devotees hold Anandamayi Ma herself to be a manifestation of the Divine; the one-truth reading fits her teaching but not every devotee's view of her person.
