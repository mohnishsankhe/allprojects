# Bhūta stream (tantras for spirits)

`lin:bhuta-tantra` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** bhūta-tantra, Bhūta tantras, western stream
**Parent:** [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](mantramarga.md)
**Status:** extinct

The Śaiva scriptural stream concerned with spirit-possession and exorcism; known mostly from lists and fragments.

## Distinctive positions
- Tantras for the control and exorcism of spirits (bhūta, graha), issued from the western face (Sadyojāta) (Kāmika pūrva 1.26; the Śrīkaṇṭhīya as quoted by Jayaratha: 'in the west, the Bhūta tantras').
