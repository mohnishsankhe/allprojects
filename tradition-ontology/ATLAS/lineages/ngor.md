# Ngor (Ngorpa) Sakya

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** ngor pa, Ngor Evaṃ Chöden lineage
**Parent:** [Sakya](sakya.md)

**Regions:** Ngor, Tsang, Kham (Derge)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: Ngor monastery founded 1429 CE; (confidence high)
**Status:** living

Sub-school founded by Ngorchen Kunga Zangpo at Ngor Evaṃ Chöden (1429), keeper of the Lamdre Lobshé ('explanation for disciples') and of strict monastic discipline; famed for its mandala rituals and Vajrayoginī and Hevajra transmissions.

## Distinctive positions
- Holds the Sakya Path with its Result and the Khön family's teaching lineages; distinguished from the other Sakya lines by its own transmission and monastic seat.
