A Vedic-revival movement founded by Dayānanda Sarasvatī that reads the Saṃhitās as the sole revelation of one God; recorded here only for its reading of the Saṃhitās (recent, post-1800).

## Distinctive positions
- Only the four Saṃhitās are revealed (the Brāhmaṇas, Purāṇas and later texts are human works to be accepted only where they agree with them).
- The Vedas teach one formless God; Agni, Indra and the other names denote that one God.
- The Vedas contain no history of persons or places; their knowledge (and the sacred thread and Vedic study) is open to all, women and men of every birth.
- Image worship and birth-based caste are rejected; the daily fire offering (havana/agnihotra) and Vedic study are kept.
