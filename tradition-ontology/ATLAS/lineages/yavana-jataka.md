# The Yavana school of horā

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** yavana-jātaka, the Yavanas (as cited by Varāhamihira)
**Parent:** [Jyotiṣa (the science of the lights)](jyotisa.md)
**Key teachers:** [Yavaneśvara](../teachers/yavanesvara.md), [Sphujidhvaja](../teachers/sphujidhvaja.md), [Mīnarāja](../teachers/minaraja.md), [Yavanācārya ('the Yavana teacher')](../teachers/yavanacarya.md)

**Dates:** Scholarly account: Yavaneśvara's prose rendering 149/150 CE and Sphujidhvaja's verse version 269/270 CE (Pingree's reading; the dates are debated); (confidence low)
**Status:** absorbed

The 'Yavanas' are the teachers of horā whom Varāhamihira cites and honours: 'the Yavanas are mlecchas, yet this science is well established among them, and they are honoured like seers' (BS 2.14). Their texts (the Yavanajātaka of Sphujidhvaja, Mīnarāja's Vṛddhayavanajātaka) were absorbed into mainstream horā.

## Distinctive positions
- Horoscopy by signs, houses (kendra, paṇaphara, āpoklima), decans and planetary rulers.
- Its views are quoted and sometimes rejected by Varāhamihira (BJ 7.1, 11.1, 12.1).
