A recent Aghora organisation founded by Aghoreśvar Bhagavān Rām at Varanasi, known for its leprosy service and for presenting Aghora as naturalness and service rather than as spectacular ascetic practice.

## Distinctive positions
- Aghora expressed as service (especially of leprosy patients) and as rejection of caste and social evils, open to householders; the cremation-ground practices are not required of lay members.
