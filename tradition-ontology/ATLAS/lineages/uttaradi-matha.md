**Regions:** Karnataka, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh
**Status:** living

One of the three principal Mādhva maṭhas (with the Rāghavendra and Vyāsarāja maṭhas). It traces its pontiffs from Madhva through Padmanābha Tīrtha, Narahari Tīrtha, Mādhava Tīrtha and Akṣobhya Tīrtha to Jayatīrtha and Vidyādhirāja Tīrtha; its later pontiffs include Raghūttama Tīrtha, Satyanātha Tīrtha and Satyadharma Tīrtha, most taking names beginning with 'Satya-'. It venerates Jayatīrtha's brindāvana at Malkheḍ.

## Distinctive positions
- Shares the Dvaita doctrine of lin:dvaita without doctrinal difference; distinct as an institutional line of ascetic pontiffs (guru-paramparā), with its own images, brindāvanas and observances.
