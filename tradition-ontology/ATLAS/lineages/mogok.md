Burmese insight lineage of Mogok Sayadaw (U Vimala, 1899–1962), stressing an intellectual grasp of dependent origination followed by contemplation of the arising and passing of mind and feeling.

## Distinctive positions
- Understanding dependent origination (taught with a chart) is the foundation for insight.
- Contemplation of mind and feeling: seeing each arising consciousness and feeling vanish (seeing arising and passing away) as the direct road to the path.
