# Dilun school (Chinese 'Daśabhūmika treatise' school)

An early Chinese Yogācāra-derived exegetical tradition centred on Vasubandhu's Daśabhūmika commentary, later absorbed into Huayan.

## Distinctive positions
- Founded on Vasubandhu's commentary on the Daśabhūmika translated by Bodhiruci and Ratnamati (508–511).
- Split (as usually reported) into a northern branch holding the ālaya to be defiled and a southern branch (Huiguang) identifying the pure aspect of the ālaya with true suchness / buddha-nature.
