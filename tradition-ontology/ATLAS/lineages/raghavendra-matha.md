# Rāghavendra Maṭha (Mantrālaya)

`lin:raghavendra-matha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** Śrī Rāghavendra Svāmī Maṭha, Rāyara Maṭha, the Vijayīndra / Kumbhakoṇam maṭha, Dakṣiṇādi maṭha
**Parent:** [Dvaita Vedānta (Tattvavāda; the Mādhva sampradāya)](dvaita.md)
**Key teachers:** [Vijayīndra Tīrtha](../teachers/vijayindra-tirtha.md), [Sudhīndra Tīrtha](../teachers/sudhindra-tirtha.md), [Rāghavendra Tīrtha](../teachers/raghavendra-tirtha.md), [Surendra Tīrtha](../teachers/surendra-tirtha.md)
**Regions:** Andhra Pradesh (Mantrālaya), Karnataka, Tamil Nadu (Kumbhakonam)
**Status:** living

The Mādhva maṭha whose pontiffs included Surendra Tīrtha, Vijayīndra Tīrtha (Kumbhakonam), Sudhīndra Tīrtha and Rāghavendra Tīrtha; since Rāghavendra entered his brindāvana at Mantrālaya on the Tuṅgabhadrā (1671) the seat and pilgrimage centre have been there.

## Distinctive positions
- Shares the Dvaita doctrine of lin:dvaita without doctrinal difference; distinct as an institutional line of ascetic pontiffs (guru-paramparā), with its own images, brindāvanas and observances.
