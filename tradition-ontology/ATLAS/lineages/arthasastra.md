# Arthaśāstra (the science of statecraft and wealth)

The śāstra of the second aim of life (artha): kingship, administration, law, diplomacy and wealth, represented by Kauṭilya's Arthaśāstra, which cites earlier schools (Mānavas, Bārhaspatyas, Auśanasas and others). Recorded here for its teaching on the aims of life and the order of the sciences.

## Distinctive positions
- Artha is foremost among the three aims, since dharma and kāma are rooted in it (Arthaśāstra 1.7.6–7).
- The four sciences are investigation (ānvīkṣikī — Sāṃkhya, Yoga, Lokāyata), the triple Veda, economics (vārttā) and governance (daṇḍanīti); investigation is 'the lamp of all sciences' (Arthaśāstra 1.2.1–12).
