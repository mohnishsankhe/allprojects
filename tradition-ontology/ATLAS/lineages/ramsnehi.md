An eighteenth-century Rajasthani Sant sampradāya of 'lovers of Rām', with seats at Shāhpurā (Rāmcaraṇ), Rain (Dariyāv Sāhib), Sinhthal and Kheṛāpā.

## Distinctive positions
- Remembrance of the nirguṇa Rām as the sole practice; no images.
- Several independent seats (Shāhpurā, Rain, Sinhthal, Kheṛāpā), each with its own founder.
- Renunciant sādhus in distinctive (pink/ochre) dress and householder followers.
