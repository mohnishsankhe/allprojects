The Vietnamese Thiền tradition of Thích Nhất Hạnh (1926–2022), with the Order of Interbeing and monastic practice centres led from Plum Village in France, teaching mindfulness in daily life and interbeing.

## Distinctive positions
- Interbeing (tiếp hiện): nothing exists by itself alone; each thing contains all others.
- Mindfulness in daily life: conscious breathing, walking meditation, the bell of mindfulness, short verses (gāthās) for daily acts.
- Engaged Buddhism: practice carried into social suffering and peace work.
- The Five and the Fourteen Mindfulness Trainings as reformulated precepts; non-attachment to views first.

## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

**Names:** nirvāṇa, the ultimate dimension
**Descriptions:** The ultimate dimension (nirvāṇa) is not separate from the historical dimension, as water is not separate from waves; it is the extinction of notions such as birth and death, being and non-being.
**Relation to self:** No separate self; interbeing.
**Relation to world:** Things inter-are; the world is to be touched deeply in the present moment.
**Caveat:** As a Buddhist tradition it does not posit a creator or a substantial absolute; the one-truth reading would be accepted only as 'the ultimate dimension', not as a Self or God.
