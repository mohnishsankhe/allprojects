# Shinshū Takada-ha

`lin:shinshu-takada-ha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** 真宗高田派, Senju-ji line
**Parent:** [Jōdo Shinshū (the True Pure Land school of Shinran)](jodo-shinshu.md)
**Regions:** Japan (Senju-ji, Tsu (Mie))
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 13th c. community; separate school; (confidence moderate)
**Status:** living

Branch descending from Shinran's Kantō disciple Shinbutsu of Takada (Shimotsuke); head temple Senju-ji, moved to Isshinden (Ise) in the 15th c.; preserves early manuscripts of Shinran.

## Distinctive positions
- Shares the Shinshū teaching of shinjin as the true cause of birth; differs institutionally, liturgically and in doctrinal-academy lineages rather than in core doctrine.
