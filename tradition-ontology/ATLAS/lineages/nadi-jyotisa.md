**Status:** living

The nāḍī tradition claims palm-leaf manuscripts written by ancient seers that record the life, past births and remedies of the individual who comes to consult them. Recorded here as the tradition's claim; the documented practice and manuscripts are recent.

## Distinctive positions
- The tradition's claim: the seers foresaw every individual who would come to consult them and wrote their lives on palm leaves.
- Leaves are located by thumb-impression classes and read aloud in Tamil verse, in chapters (kāṇḍa) including past-birth karma and remedies (śānti-parihāra).
