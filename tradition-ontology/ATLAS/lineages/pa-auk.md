The Burmese forest tradition of Pa-Auk Sayadaw (b. 1934), which teaches the Visuddhimagga's full sequence of calm and insight, including direct discernment of Abhidhamma ultimate realities.

## Distinctive positions
- Close adherence to the Visuddhimagga and Abhidhamma as a practical manual.
- Samatha-first route: mindfulness of breathing to the fourth jhāna with a bright counterpart sign, then the kasiṇas, the formless attainments and the immeasurables; or, for the pure-insight vehicle, four-elements meditation to access concentration.
- Insight requires seeing ultimate realities directly: the tiny material groups (rūpa-kalāpa) and their elements, the mind-moments of cognitive processes with their mental factors, and dependent origination across past and future lives, before contemplating the three characteristics.


## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Names:** nibbāna, asaṅkhata-dhātu
**Descriptions:** the unconditioned element, object of the path and fruition knowledges, realized after the ultimate realities of mentality-materiality and their causes have been directly seen
**Relation to self:** only mentality-materiality and their causes are found; no being or self
**Caveat:** Follows the Visuddhimagga's account strictly; no ground-of-being reading.
