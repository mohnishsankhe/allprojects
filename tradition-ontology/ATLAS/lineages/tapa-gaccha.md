**Status:** living

The largest Mūrtipūjaka monastic lineage, named after Jagaccandrasūri's austerities; its monks include the Mughal-era Hīravijayasūri and the philosopher Yaśovijaya.

## Distinctive positions
- the largest Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka gaccha; celebrates Saṃvatsarī on the fourth of Bhādrapada (bright half)
- reformist in origin, stressing austerity and strict monastic discipline
