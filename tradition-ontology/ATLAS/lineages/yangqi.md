The branch of the Linji house descending from Yangqi Fanghui, which produced Wuzu Fayan, Yuanwu Keqin and Dahui Zonggao and became the principal source of later Chinese, Korean and Japanese Rinzai lineages.

## Distinctive positions
- Kōan commentary (Yuanwu) and the critical-phrase practice (Dahui).

**Transmissions given:** 
  - [Rinzai Zen](rinzai.md) — what: most Japanese Rinzai lines (via Wuzhun Shifan, Xutang Zhiyu, Lanxi Daolong, etc.)
