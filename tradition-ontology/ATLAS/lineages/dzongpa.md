Sub-school from Dzongpa Kunga Namgyal (1432–1496) at Gongkar Dorjeden, known for its own transmission lines of Sakya tantric instructions; small and largely absorbed today.

## Distinctive positions
- Holds the Sakya Path with its Result and the Khön family's teaching lineages; distinguished from the other Sakya lines by its own transmission and monastic seat.
