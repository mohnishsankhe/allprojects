The monastic seat of the Kabīr Panth in Varanasi, at the place where Kabīr is said to have lived and taught; its line traces itself to Surat Gopāl.

## Distinctive positions
- Celibate succession of mahants at the site of Kabīr's teaching in Varanasi.
- The Bījak is the scripture; the Dharamdāsī cosmogony is less central here.
