Several distinct Sant movements named after the 'true name': the Satnāmīs of Narnaul (Bīrbhān, 17th c.), the Satnāmī sampradāya of Jagjīvandās in Awadh (18th c.), and the Satnāmī panth of Ghāsīdās in Chhattisgarh (19th c., flagged recent).

## Distinctive positions
- The 'true name' (sat nām) of the formless God as the centre of practice.
- Rejection of images and (in Chhattisgarh) of caste hierarchy; the Chhattisgarh Satnāmīs are largely of Camār origin.
- Vegetarianism and abstention from intoxicants (Ghāsīdās's rules).
