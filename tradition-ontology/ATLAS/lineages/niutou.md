**Regions:** Mount Niutou near Nanjing; Jiangnan
**Dates:** Tradition's account: founded by Farong (594–657), said to have been instructed by the Fourth Patriarch Daoxin; Scholarly account: the lineage is attested from the 8th c.; the link to Daoxin is regarded by scholars as a later construction; (confidence low)
**Status:** extinct

A Tang Chan lineage of the Jiangnan region traced to Niutou Farong, which stressed emptiness and non-attachment and was classed by Zongmi as the school that 'eliminates traces'.

## Distinctive positions
- The Way is fundamentally empty; 'forgetting feelings' (wangqing) rather than guarding mind (Zongmi's report).
- Insentient things possess the Way (Jueguan lun, as generally described).


## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence low

**Names:** the Way as fundamentally empty
**Descriptions:** Mind is fundamentally quiescent; 'forgetting feelings' is practice (Zongmi's report).; 'The nature of mind is unborn — what need of knowing and seeing?' (Xinming).
**Negations:** 'Originally there is not one dharma — who speaks of cultivation?' (Xinming).
**Caveat:** Known mostly through others' reports; the Xinming and Jueguan lun attributions are disputed. Low confidence.
