# Tristuti Gaccha

**Parent:** [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](murtipujaka.md)

**Dates:** Scholarly account: revived in the 19th c. by Rājendrasūri (1827–1906); (confidence moderate)
**Status:** living

A Mūrtipūjaka gaccha revived by Rājendrasūri, compiler of the Prakrit encyclopaedia Abhidhāna Rājendra, which rejects worship of the guardian deities in the monastic rite.

## Distinctive positions
- recites only three praise-verses (stuti) in caityavandana, omitting the fourth addressed to the attendant deities (śruta- and śāsana-devatās)
