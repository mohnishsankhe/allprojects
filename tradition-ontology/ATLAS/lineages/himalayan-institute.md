The organization founded by Swami Rama (1925–1996) to teach yoga, meditation and Vedānta as practical science, claiming descent from a Himalayan line of sages.

## Distinctive positions
- Yoga science as a system: diaphragmatic breathing, systematic relaxation, meditation with mantra and the application of suṣumnā (breath flowing evenly in both nostrils).
- Claimed lineage from Himalayan sages of Śaṅkara's tradition and Śrīvidyā (tradition's account).
- The four primitive urges (food, sleep, sex, self-preservation) to be regulated.
