# Jushe school (Abhidharmakośa school)

`lin:jushe-zong` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Jushe zong 俱舍宗, Kusha-shū (Japan)
**Parent:** [Sarvāstivāda](sarvastivada.md)
**Key teachers:** [Paramārtha](../teachers/paramartha.md), [Xuanzang](../teachers/xuanzang.md)
**Regions:** China, Japan (one of the six Nara schools)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 6th–8th c. CE; (confidence low)
**Status:** absorbed

The East Asian scholastic tradition of the Abhidharmakośa; in Japan the Kusha-shū, attached to the Hossō school.

## Distinctive positions
- Study of the Abhidharmakośa in Paramārtha's (563–567) and Xuanzang's (651–654) translations as a preparatory discipline, later absorbed into Faxiang/Hossō study.
