The communities of the Fourth and Fifth Patriarchs Daoxin and Hongren at Huangmei, from which both the Northern school and Huineng's line claimed descent.

## Distinctive positions
- Guarding the one (shouyi 守一) and 'one-practice samādhi' (Daoxin).
- Guarding the original true mind (shou benzhen xin 守本真心) (Hongren).
- Large settled communities combining meditation and labour.
