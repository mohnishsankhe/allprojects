# Chengshi school (Satyasiddhi school)

`lin:chengshi-zong` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Chengshi zong 成實宗, Jōjitsu-shū (Japan)
**Key teachers:** [Harivarman](../teachers/harivarman.md), [Kumārajīva](../teachers/kumarajiva.md)
**Regions:** China (5th–6th c.), Japan (one of the six Nara schools, attached to Sanron)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: 5th–7th c. CE; (confidence low)
**Status:** absorbed

The Chinese exegetical tradition centred on Harivarman's Satyasiddhiśāstra (translated by Kumārajīva, 411–412).

## Distinctive positions
- The Satyasiddhiśāstra teaches the emptiness of persons and of dharmas and was studied in China as a bridge to Mahāyāna until Jizang classed it as 'Hīnayāna'.
