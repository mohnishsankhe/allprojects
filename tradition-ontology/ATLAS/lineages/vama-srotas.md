# Vāma stream (the left current of Tumburu)

`lin:vama-srotas` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** vedic
**Alternate names:** vāmasrotas, Vāma tantras, Tumburu cult
**Parent:** [Mantramārga (the Path of Mantras)](mantramarga.md)
**Key teachers:** [Hiraṇyadāma](../teachers/hiranyadama.md)
**Regions:** North India, Nepal, Cambodia (Sdok Kak Thom inscription)
**Status:** extinct

The left current of Śaiva revelation, centred on Tumburu and four goddesses, known chiefly from the surviving Vīṇāśikhatantra and from Cambodian epigraphy.

## Distinctive positions
- Worship of Tumburu (a form of Śiva) with his four sister-goddesses (Jayā, Vijayā, Jayantī/Ajitā, Aparājitā).
- The Kāmika names the left (vāma) stream as issuing from the northern face, in 24 tantras beginning with the Nayasūtra (Kāmika pūrva 1.25–26).
- A Khmer inscription (Sdok Kak Thom, 1052 CE) recounts that the brahmin Hiraṇyadāma taught King Jayavarman II the Vīṇāśikha, Nayottara, Sammoha and Śiraścheda (early 9th c.).
