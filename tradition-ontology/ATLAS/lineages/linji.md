One of the Five Houses of Chan, stemming from Linji Yixuan (d. 866) in the line of Mazu and Huangbo, known for its shouts and devices; through its Yangqi branch it became the main Chinese Chan lineage and the source of Japanese Rinzai and Ōbaku.

## Distinctive positions
- The true person of no rank (wuwei zhenren) and the 'person of the Way who depends on nothing' (wuyi daoren) listening to the Dharma here and now.
- Shouts (he 喝) and blows as means; the four classifications (siliaojian), four guest-host relations, four illumination-function relations, three mysteries and three essentials.
- 'Be master wherever you are; wherever you stand is true'; nothing to do (wushi 無事).
- In the Song, kōan commentary (Yuanwu) and the critical phrase (Dahui's kanhua Chan).

**Transmissions given:**

## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence high

**Names:** true person of no rank (wuwei zhenren 無位真人), person of the Way who depends on nothing (wuyi daoren 無依道人), mind-dharma (xinfa 心法)
**Descriptions:** 'On the lump of red flesh there is a true person of no rank, always going in and out of the gates of your face' (Linji lu).; 'The mind-dharma has no form and pervades the ten directions; in the eye it is called seeing, in the ear hearing…' (Linji lu).; 'Only the person of the Way who depends on nothing, listening to the Dharma, is the mother of all buddhas' (Linji lu).
**Negations:** 'What kind of dried shit-stick is the true person of no rank?' (Linji lu) — no fixation even of this.
**Relation to self:** It is 'just you, listening to the Dharma right in front of me'.
**Relation to world:** Entering all realms — ordinary and holy, pure and defiled — without being defined by them; 'be master wherever you are'.
**Caveat:** Linji denies that any name — buddha, patriarch, bodhi, nirvāṇa — is the reality; they are 'hitching posts for donkeys'. The tradition would reject reifying the true person as a soul.
