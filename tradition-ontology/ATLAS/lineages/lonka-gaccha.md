# Loṅkā Gaccha

`lin:lonka-gaccha` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Parent:** [Sthānakavāsī](sthanakavasi.md)
**Founders:** [Loṅkā Śāh](../teachers/lonka-sah.md)
**Dates:** Scholarly account: from c. 1470s CE; (confidence moderate)
**Status:** absorbed

The aniconic Śvetāmbara movement that arose from the teaching of Loṅkā Śāh of Ahmedabad; its later lax 'yatis' were in turn reformed by the founders of the Sthānakavāsī orders.

## Distinctive positions
- rejection of image worship and of monastic laxity on the basis of the Āgamas as copied and read by Loṅkā Śāh
