A Telugu-speaking Vīraśaiva group, traditionally brāhmaṇa, tracing itself to Mallikārjuna Paṇḍitārādhya; it combines liṅga-wearing and exclusive Śiva devotion with retention of Vedic rites and the sacred thread.

## Distinctive positions
- Wear the liṅga and worship Śiva exclusively while retaining the sacred thread and Vedic rites.
- Look to Mallikārjuna Paṇḍitārādhya, contemporary of Basava, as their founding teacher.
