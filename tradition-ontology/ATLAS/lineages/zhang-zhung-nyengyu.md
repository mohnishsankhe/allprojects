**Regions:** Zhang Zhung (Western Tibet), Tsang, Dolpo
**Dates:** Tradition's account: an unbroken oral lineage never hidden as treasure; Tapihritsa taught Gyerpung Nangzher Löpo in the 8th c.; Scholarly account: written down by the 11th-13th c.; (confidence low)
**Status:** living

The principal Dzogchen lineage of Bön, whose teaching passed from the Nine Buddhas of the mind transmission to Tapihritsa and Gyerpung Nangzher Löpo and was set down in four cycles (outer, inner, secret, innermost) plus the experiential transmission.

## Distinctive positions
- Bön Dzogchen held to have been transmitted orally without interruption (not a treasure); twenty-four masters of the lineage are said to have attained the rainbow body.
