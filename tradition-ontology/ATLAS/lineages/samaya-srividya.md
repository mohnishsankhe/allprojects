# Samaya school of Śrīvidyā (Samayācāra)

**Alternate names:** Samaya-mata, Samayins (samayinaḥ), the Vedic way of the five Śubhāgamas
**Parent:** [Śrīvidyā](srividya.md)
**Key teachers:** `tch:lolla-laksmidhara`, [Śaṅkara (Ādi Śaṅkarācārya)](../teachers/sankara.md), [Gauḍapāda](../teachers/gaudapada.md), [Vasiṣṭha](../teachers/vasistha.md), [Sanaka](../teachers/sanaka.md), [Śuka](../teachers/suka.md), [Sanandana](../teachers/sanandana.md), [Sanatkumāra](../teachers/sanatkumara.md)
**Regions:** Orissa (Lakṣmīdhara), South India
**Dates:** Tradition's account: Taught by the five sages Vasiṣṭha, Sanaka, Śuka, Sanandana and Sanatkumāra in the five auspicious āgamas; followed by Śaṅkara (Bhagavatpāda), whose view Lakṣmīdhara claims to follow.; Scholarly account: Known chiefly from Lakṣmīdhara's commentary on the Saundaryalaharī (c. 16th c.); the five Śubhāgama saṃhitās are known only through his citations.; (confidence low)
**Status:** living

The branch of Śrīvidyā that restricts worship to internal contemplation of the Śrīcakra and the Goddess in the body's cakras, holds Śiva and Śakti to be of equal primacy, and rejects Kaula and left-hand practices as non-Vedic. Its classical statement is Lakṣmīdhara's commentary on the Saundaryalaharī.

## Distinctive positions
- Worship of the Śrīcakra only in the inner space (dahara-ākāśa, heart / the thousand-petalled lotus); external worship on a plate or cloth is 'Kaula worship' (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 8).
- The Śrīcakra is the moon-disc in the crown lotus (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 10).
- Samayā (the Goddess) and Samaya (Śiva) share a fivefold equality: of seat, of state, of action, of form and of name (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 41).
- Rejects external worship of a young woman's triangle and the Kaula rites; rejects the sixty-four tantras as aimed at worldly powers and outside the Vedic path; the eight Candrakalā tantras are 'mixed' (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 31).
- Follows the Vedic way of the five auspicious āgamas; eligibility tied to the three higher classes (Lakṣmīdhara).
- Śiva is not subordinate to Śakti nor Śakti to Śiva: they are equal in primacy (sama-prādhānya).


## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Names:** Samayā (the Goddess) with Samaya (Śiva), Bhagavatī / Brahman
**Descriptions:** Śiva and Śakti are equal in primacy, sharing a fivefold equality of seat, state, action, form and name (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 41).; The Goddess worshipped in the thousand-petalled lotus, where the Śrīcakra is the moon-disc (Lakṣmīdhara on SL 10).
**Relation to self:** The Goddess is realized within, in the space of the heart and the crown; the Samayin identifies the cakra with his own inner space.
**Relation to world:** The world is produced by the Śiva-Śakti pair acting together as father and mother (SL 41).
**Caveat:** The Samaya school rejects the uttara-Kaula view that only the Śakti principle exists and the pūrva-Kaula view that makes Śiva her body alone; any reconciliation with Kaula views must record this objection.
