The interpretive tradition following Vijñāneśvara's Mitākṣarā (late 11th–early 12th c.) on the Yājñavalkyasmṛti, the most widely followed authority in Dharmaśāstra, especially for inheritance and family law.

## Distinctive positions
- Ownership of ancestral property arises by birth (janmasvatva): sons have a share in the family property from birth.
- Sapiṇḍa relationship is defined by sharing particles of the same body (avayavānvaya).
