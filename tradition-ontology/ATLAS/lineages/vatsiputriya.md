# Vātsīputrīya

`lin:vatsiputriya` · `skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Vajjiputtaka (Pali), Duzizi bu 犢子部
**Parent:** [Pudgalavāda](pudgalavada.md)
**Founders:** [Vātsīputra](../teachers/vatsiputra.md)
**Status:** extinct

The root Pudgalavāda school, named after Vātsīputra; in Vasumitra's list it splits from the Sarvāstivāda and gives rise to the Dharmottarīya, Bhadrayānīya, Saṃmitīya and Ṣaṇṇagarika.

## Distinctive positions
- The person is neither the aggregates nor other than them; it is designated in dependence on the aggregates (Vasumitra; AKBh 9).
- Named in AKBh 9 as the holders of the pudgala doctrine ('vātsīputrīyāḥ pudgalaṃ santam icchanti', Pradhan p. 461).
