The recent treasure lineage of Chokgyur Dechen Lingpa (1829-1870), closely tied to the Rimé masters.

## Distinctive positions
- Treasures revealed by Chokgyur Lingpa, often jointly with Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo and codified by Jamgön Kongtrul, notably the Tukdrub Barche Künsel guru sādhana.
