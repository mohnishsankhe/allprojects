# Bahuśrutīya

`lin:bahusrutiya` · `skeleton` · confidence low

**Family:** ascetic
**Alternate names:** Bahussutaka / Bahulika (Pali), Duowen bu 多聞部
**Parent:** [Mahāsāṃghika](mahasanghika.md)
**Status:** extinct

A Mahāsāṃghika branch distinguishing the Buddha's supramundane from his mundane teachings.

## Distinctive positions
- The Buddha's teachings on impermanence, suffering, emptiness, non-self and nirvāṇa are supramundane, the rest mundane (Vasumitra; low confidence).
- Harivarman's Satyasiddhiśāstra is associated with this school in some Chinese accounts (disputed).
