# Upakeśa Gaccha

**Parent:** [Śvetāmbara Mūrtipūjaka](murtipujaka.md)

A historical Śvetāmbara gaccha of Marwar that traced itself to Pārśva's lineage via Keśin and is linked to the conversion of the Osvāls at Osiyā; its present status is uncertain.

## Distinctive positions
- claims descent from Pārśva's disciple-line through Keśin, i.e. from before Mahāvīra (the tradition's account)
- associated with Osiyā (Upakeśapura) in Marwar and the Osvāl caste
