# Heze school

**Regions:** Luoyang, Nanyang
**Dates:** Scholarly account: c. 730–c. 850; (confidence moderate)
**Status:** extinct

The line of Heze Shenhui (684–758), who championed Huineng and sudden awakening; Zongmi counted himself its heir and systematised its teaching of numinous knowing.

## Distinctive positions
- Sudden awakening followed by gradual cultivation (as later summarised by Zongmi).
- Knowing (zhi 知) — 'the one word knowing is the gate of all wonders' — as the substance of mind (Zongmi's report of Heze teaching).
- No-thought (wunian) as the tenet; Huineng as the true Sixth Patriarch.


## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence moderate

**Names:** numinous knowing (lingzhi 靈知)
**Descriptions:** 'Of the empty and calm mind, the numinous knowing is never obscured… the one word knowing is the gate of all wonders' (Zongmi reporting Heze teaching, Chan Preface).
**Negations:** 'The true suchness is without thought' (Xianzong ji).
**Relation to self:** It is one's true nature, known whether deluded or awakened.
**Caveat:** Knowing is not discursive cognition; Zongmi insists it is not the same as seeing and hearing.
