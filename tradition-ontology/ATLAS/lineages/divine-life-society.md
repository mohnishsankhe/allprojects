The ashram and society founded at Rishikesh in 1936 by Swami Sivananda, a former physician, teaching a 'yoga of synthesis' on an Advaita basis; many modern teachers of yoga and Vedānta were trained there.

## Distinctive positions
- The Yoga of Synthesis: karma, bhakti, rāja and jñāna yoga practised together ('head, heart and hand').
- Motto: Serve, Love, Give, Purify, Meditate, Realize; 'Be good, do good'.
- Daily discipline: rising at brahmamuhūrta, japa, meditation, study, silence, and a spiritual diary.
- Wide free distribution of short practical books in English.

## The ultimate in this lineage
`skeleton` · confidence moderate · _recent (post-1800)_

**Names:** Brahman, Saccidānanda
**Descriptions:** Brahman as existence-consciousness-bliss, reached by the synthesis of all yogas.
**Relation to self:** The Self is Brahman (Advaita).
**Relation to world:** The world is māyā from the standpoint of the ultimate; to be served as God in practice.
**Caveat:** DLS also worships personal deities and teaches devotion; its Advaita is practised within a synthesis.
